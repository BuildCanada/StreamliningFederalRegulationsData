## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-366830 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Northwest Territories Act](/eng/acts/N-27.05).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-366833 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Aboriginal party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Aboriginal party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an Aboriginal
    organization that is a party to the Agreement. ([partie
    autochtone]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Northwest Territories
    Lands and Resources Devolution Agreement that was made on June 25,
    2013, as amended from time to time. ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[federal appurtenant undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal appurtenant undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    undertaking in relation to which a use of waters or a deposit of
    waste is permitted by a licence and that is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} related to remediation that is performed by or on
      behalf of Her Majesty in right of Canada

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} under the Agreement, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an Excepted Waste Site as
        defined in section 1.1 of the Agreement; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} located on public lands that are under the
      administration of a federal minister. ([entreprise fédérale en
      cause]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[former Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[former Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Northwest Territories
    Act](/eng/acts/N-27.05), chapter N-27 of the Revised Statutes of
    Canada, 1985. ([ancienne loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[gas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means natural gas --- including coal-bed
    methane --- and all substances other than oil that are produced in
    association with natural gas. ([gaz]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Gwich'in Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Gwich'in Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement as
    defined in section 2 of the [Gwich'in Land Claim Settlement
    Act](/eng/acts/G-11.8). ([accord gwichin]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Inuvialuit Final Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Inuvialuit Final Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Agreement as defined in section 2 of the [Western Arctic
    (Inuvialuit) Claims Settlement Act](/eng/acts/W-6.7). ([Convention
    définitive des Inuvialuits]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[line of delimitation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[line of delimitation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Line of
    Delimitation that is described in Schedule 18 to the
    Agreement. ([ligne de démarcation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[minerals]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minerals]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means precious or base metals or
    other non-living naturally occurring substances --- including coal,
    but not including oil, gas or water --- that are, or were before
    their production, part of the land, whether solid, liquid or
    gaseous. ([ressources minérales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Northern
    Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Northwest Territories]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Northwest Territories]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that part of
    Canada that is north of the 60th parallel of north latitude, west of
    the boundary described in Schedule I to the [Nunavut
    Act](/eng/acts/N-28.6) and not within Yukon. ([Territoires du
    Nord-Ouest]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} crude petroleum --- regardless of gravity ---
      that is produced at a well-head in liquid form; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other hydrocarbons --- except coal and gas
      --- including hydrocarbons that may be extracted or recovered from
      surface or subsurface deposits, including deposits of oil sand,
      bitumen, bituminous sand or oil shale and other types of
      deposits. ([pétrole]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[onshore]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[onshore]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that part of Canada that is
    north of the 60th parallel of north latitude, west of the boundary
    described in Schedule I to the [Nunavut Act](/eng/acts/N-28.6) and
    not within Yukon that consists of the following lands:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} lands, including lands under water, that lie
      landward of the low-water line --- or in respect of Inuvialuit
      lands as defined in section 2 of the Inuvialuit Final Agreement,
      landward of the mean or ordinary high-water mark --- of the sea
      coast of the mainland or of the sea coast of any naturally
      occurring permanent island;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} lands under water that are within small enclosed
      bays along the sea coast of the mainland or the sea coast of any
      naturally occurring permanent island; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} lands, including lands under water, that lie
      landward of the line of delimitation and seaward of the low-water
      line --- or in respect of lands that are contiguous with the sea
      coast of Inuvialuit lands as defined in section 2 of the
      Inuvialuit Final Agreement, seaward of the mean or ordinary
      high-water mark --- of the sea coast of the mainland.

    It does not include lands that lie seaward of the line of
    delimitation, other than those lands --- including lands under water
    --- that lie landward of the low-water line of the sea coast of
    those naturally occurring permanent islands and those lands under
    water that are within small enclosed bays along the sea coast of
    those islands. ([région intracôtière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[public lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} lands --- including minerals, oil, gas, timber or
      wood and buildings, structures, improvements or other fixtures ---
      that are situated in the onshore and that belong to Her Majesty in
      right of Canada; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} interests --- in lands that are situated in the
      onshore --- that belong to Her Majesty in right of
      Canada. ([terres domaniales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Sahtu Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Sahtu Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement as
    defined in section 2 of the [Sahtu Dene and Metis Land Claim
    Settlement Act](/eng/acts/S-1.5). ([accord du
    Sahtu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[self-government agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[self-government agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement
    between Her Majesty in right of Canada and an Aboriginal people of
    the Northwest Territories that is implemented by an Act of
    Parliament and that recognizes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the legal status and capacity of a governing body
      to represent that Aboriginal people; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the authority of that governing body to enact
      laws. ([accord sur l'autonomie gouvernementale]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[settlement agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[settlement agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement that
    is listed in Schedule 1 to the Agreement. ([entente de
    règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[settlement lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[settlement lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means lands in the
    Northwest Territories whose title is vested in an Aboriginal
    organization under a settlement agreement. ([terres visées par un
    règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[small enclosed bay]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[small enclosed bay]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any coastal
    indentation that meets the following conditions:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the distance of a straight line across the
      entrance of the indentation at the low-water line measures four
      kilometres or less; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the area of the indentation, including any
      islands or parts of islands lying within the indentation, is
      greater than that of a semicircle whose diameter is the distance
      of the straight line referred to in paragraph (a). ([petite baie
      fermée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Tlicho Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Tlicho Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement as
    defined in section 2 of the [Tlicho Land Claims and Self-Government
    Act](/eng/acts/T-11.3). ([accord tlicho]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any inland waters --- whether
    in a liquid or frozen state --- that are on or below the surface of
    lands that are situated in the onshore. ([eaux]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Wherever in this Act a
reference is made, in relation to any matter, to a duty to consult, that
duty must be exercised

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by providing the person to be consulted with the
  following:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} notice of the matter in sufficient form and detail
    to allow the person to prepare their views on the matter,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a reasonable period for the person to prepare
    those views, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an opportunity to present those views to the
    person having that duty; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by considering, fully and impartially, any views so
  presented.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oaths

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Before assuming office, the
Commissioner and the Deputy Commissioner must take and subscribe the
oath of office and the oath of allegiance prescribed by the Governor in
Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salaries

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The salary of the
Commissioner and of the Deputy Commissioner are to be fixed by the
Governor in Council and paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund of
Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Executive Council

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An Executive Council of the
Northwest Territories is established, the members of which are to be
appointed by the Commissioner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seat of government

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The seat of the Government
of the Northwest Territories is at Yellowknife or at another place in
the Northwest Territories that is designated by the Legislature.

## [Legislative Power]{.HTitleText1} {#h-366905 .Part}

### [Legislative Assembly of the Northwest Territories]{.HTitleText2} {#h-366906 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuance

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Council of the
Northwest Territories established under the former Act is continued as
the Legislative Assembly of the Northwest Territories. Each member of
the Legislative Assembly is elected to represent an electoral district
in the Northwest Territories.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  Legislative Assembly is to continue for longer than five years after
  the day on which the writs are returned for a general election, but
  the Commissioner may dissolve it before then.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Writs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Writs for the election of members of the Legislative
  Assembly are to be issued on the Commissioner's instructions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oaths

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Before assuming office,
each member of the Legislative Assembly must take and subscribe before
the Commissioner the oath of office prescribed by the Legislature of the
Northwest Territories and the oath of allegiance set out in the Fifth
Schedule to the [Constitution Act, 1867](/eng/Const/).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sittings

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Legislative Assembly
must sit at least once every 12 months.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Speaker

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Legislative Assembly must elect one member as Speaker to preside over
  the Legislative Assembly when it is sitting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Speaker's vote

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Speaker may vote in the Legislative Assembly only
  in the case of a tie.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A majority of the members
of the Legislative Assembly, including the Speaker, constitutes a
quorum.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Legislative Assembly
may make rules for its operations and procedures, except in relation to
the subjects set out in paragraph 18(1)(b).

### [Legislature of the Northwest Territories]{.HTitleText2} {#h-366927 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuance

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner in
Council as defined in section 2 of the former Act is continued as the
Legislature of the Northwest Territories; the Legislature consists of
the Commissioner and the Legislative Assembly.

### [Legislative Powers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-366930 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subjects

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Legislature may make laws in relation to the following subjects in
  respect of the Northwest Territories:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the election of members of the Legislative
    Assembly, including the name and number of electoral districts and
    the qualifications of electors and candidates;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the disqualification of persons from sitting or
    voting as members of the Legislative Assembly and the privileges,
    indemnities and expenses of those members;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Executive Council;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the establishment and tenure of public offices and
    the appointment, conditions of employment and payment of
    office-holders;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} municipal and local institutions;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} direct taxation and licensing in order to raise
    revenue for territorial, municipal or local purposes;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the levying of a tax on furs --- or any other parts
    of fur-bearing animals --- that are to be shipped or taken from the
    Northwest Territories to any place outside the Northwest
    Territories;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the incorporation of companies with territorial
    objects, except railway --- other than street railway ---,
    steamship, air transport, telegraph and telephone companies;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the solemnization of marriage;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} property and civil rights;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the administration of justice, including the
    constitution, maintenance and organization of territorial courts ---
    of both civil and criminal jurisdiction --- and procedure in civil
    matters in those courts;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the establishment, maintenance and management of
    prisons and other places of confinement;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} the conservation of wildlife and its habitat;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} waters --- the rights in respect of which are under
    the administration and control of the Commissioner --- including the
    disposition under subsection 52(3) of those rights, the deposit of
    waste in those waters and what constitutes waste;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} education, but any law respecting education must
    provide that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a majority of the ratepayers of any part of the
      Northwest Territories may establish any school in that part that
      they think fit and make the necessary assessment and collection of
      rates for it, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the minority of the ratepayers of that part of
      the Northwest Territories --- whether Protestant or Roman Catholic
      --- may establish separate schools in that part and, if they do
      so, are liable to assessments of only the rates that they impose
      on themselves in respect of those schools;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} immigration;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} those public lands that are under the
    administration and control of the Commissioner, including their
    disposition under subsection 51(1);

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} intoxicants, including what constitutes an
    intoxicant;

  - [(s)]{.lawlabel} hospitals and charities;

  - [(t)]{.lawlabel} agriculture;

  - [(u)]{.lawlabel} the entering into of intergovernmental agreements
    by the Commissioner or any other official of the Government of the
    Northwest Territories;

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the expenditure of money for territorial purposes;

  - [(w)]{.lawlabel} the adoption and use of an official seal;

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} generally, all matters of a merely local or private
    nature;

  - [(y)]{.lawlabel} the imposition of fines, penalties, imprisonment or
    other punishments in respect of a contravention of a provision of a
    law of the Legislature; and

  - [(z)]{.lawlabel} any other subject that may be designated by order
    of the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laws --- intoxicants

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Legislature may make laws relating to the
  importation of intoxicants into the Northwest Territories from another
  part of Canada or elsewhere and defining what constitutes an
  intoxicant for the purposes of those laws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laws --- natural resources

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Legislature may make laws in relation to the following subjects in
  respect of the onshore:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exploration for non-renewable natural resources;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the development, conservation and management of
    non-renewable natural resources and forestry resources, including
    the rate of primary production from those resources;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} oil and gas pipelines that are situated entirely in
    the onshore;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the development, conservation and management of
    sites and facilities for the production of electrical energy; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the export, from the onshore to another part of
    Canada, of the primary production from non-renewable natural
    resources and forestry resources and of the electrical energy that
    is produced in the onshore.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- no discrimination

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A law that is made under paragraph (1)(e) is not to
  authorize or provide for discrimination in prices or in supplies that
  are exported.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laws --- taxation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Legislature may make laws in relation to the
  raising of money by any mode of taxation in respect of resources
  referred to in paragraph (1)(b) and the primary production from those
  resources and in respect of sites and facilities referred to in
  paragraph (1)(d) and the production of electrical energy from those
  sites and facilities. The Legislature may make those laws even if the
  production is exported.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- no differentiation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A law that is made under subsection (3) is not to
  authorize or provide for taxation that differentiates between
  production that is exported and that which is not.

- Definition of [[primary production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[primary
  production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} production from a non-renewable natural resource if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is in the form in which the resource exists on
      its recovery or severance from its natural state, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it results from processing or refining the
      resource and is not

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a manufactured product, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a product that results from refining crude oil,
        refining upgraded heavy crude oil, refining gases or liquids
        derived from coal or refining a synthetic equivalent of crude
        oil; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} production from a forestry resource if the product
    consists of sawlogs, poles, lumber, wood chips, sawdust or any other
    primary wood product --- or of wood pulp --- and is not manufactured
    from wood.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No derogation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in subsections (1) to (5) derogates from any
  powers that the Legislature has under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laws --- access to lands and waters

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The Legislature may make
laws in relation to access to the public lands that are under the
administration of a federal minister and to the waters overlying those
lands, including the compensation that is to be paid in respect of that
access.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Roads on Tlicho lands

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Laws of the Legislature
that are made in relation to public highways apply to roads identified
in the Tlicho Agreement --- as if they were on public lands --- if the
Tlicho Agreement provides that those laws apply to those roads.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unitization of straddling resources

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite sections 18 and 19, the
  Legislature must not amend a law of the Legislature without the
  consent of the Governor in Council if the law as amended would

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} affect the unitization of those straddling
    resources that are referred to in the Agreement for Coordination and
    Cooperation in the Management and Administration of Petroleum
    Resources in the Inuvialuit Settlement Region that was made on June
    25, 2013, as amended from time to time; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} limit how that agreement applies to or is
    implemented by the Government of the Northwest Territories.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian Energy Regulator

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite sections 18 and 19, during the period of 20
  years beginning on the day on which section 1 comes into force, the
  Legislature must not amend a law of the Legislature without the
  consent of the Governor in Council if the law as amended would affect
  the regulatory functions of the Canadian Energy Regulator in that part
  of the Inuvialuit Settlement Region --- as defined in section 2 of the
  Inuvialuit Final Agreement --- that is situated in the onshore.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Federal appurtenant undertakings

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Only a federal minister
may, in relation to a federal appurtenant undertaking, exercise the
following powers and functions under a law of the Legislature:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} approve the issuance, renewal or amendment of a
  licence permitting the use of waters or the deposit of waste in
  waters;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} consent to a declaration by a water board that an
  amendment to such a licence --- as a result of which the use, flow or
  quality of waters would be altered --- is required on an emergency
  basis;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} approve the form of any security posted in respect of
  such a licence;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} hold and apply the security;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} exercise powers that are substantially the same as
  those set out in section 39 of the [Northwest Territories Waters
  Act](/eng/acts/N-27.3), as it read immediately before the coming into
  force of section 1;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} issue policy directions to a water board that may
  issue, renew or amend such a licence; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} designate inspectors and grant them powers that are
  substantially the same as those set out in section 37 or 44.02 of the
  [Northwest Territories Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-27.3), as they read
  immediately before the coming into force of section 1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition of water board

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** For every five members
who are appointed to a water board that may --- under a law of the
Legislature --- issue, renew or amend a licence permitting the use of
waters or the deposit of waste in waters, one must be nominated by a
federal minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrictions on powers

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in subsection 18(1) or
  section 19 must be construed as giving the Legislature greater powers
  than are given to legislatures of provinces under sections 92, 92A and
  95 of the [Constitution Act, 1867](/eng/Const/).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Water power

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 18(1) and sections 19 and 20, the
  Legislature must not make laws in respect of the right to the use and
  flow of waters for the production or generation of water power to
  which the [Dominion Water Power Act](/eng/acts/W-4) applies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement implementation Acts

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsection 25(1),
the Legislature may, in exercising its powers under sections 18 and 19
for the purpose of implementing an Aboriginal land claim agreement or a
self-government agreement, make laws that are in relation to the matters
coming within class 24 of section 91 of the [Constitution Act,
1867](/eng/Const/).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laws --- conservation of wildlife

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsection 25(1),
any law of the Legislature that is in relation to the conservation of
wildlife applies, unless the contrary intention appears in it, to and in
respect of Aboriginal people.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laws --- borrowing, making loans and
investing

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Legislature may make laws for the

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} borrowing of money by the Commissioner on behalf of
    the Northwest Territories for territorial, municipal or local
    purposes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} making of loans to persons; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} investing by the Commissioner of surplus money
    standing to the credit of the Consolidated Revenue Fund of the
    Northwest Territories.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The aggregate of all borrowings is not to exceed the
  maximum amount set under subsection (4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charge on Consolidated Revenue Fund

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The repayment of money borrowed under a law made
  under paragraph (1)(a) --- and the payment of interest on that money
  --- is a charge on and is payable out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund
  of the Northwest Territories.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amount --- borrowings

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of
  the Minister of Finance, set the maximum amount of the aggregate of
  all borrowings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of
  the Minister of Finance, make regulations respecting borrowings for
  the purposes of subsections (2) and (4), including regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting what constitutes, or is deemed to
    constitute, borrowing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the entities, or classes of entities,
    whose borrowings are to be taken into account; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the manner in which the value of a
    borrowing must be determined.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withholding assent

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, in writing, direct the Commissioner to
  withhold his or her assent to a bill that has been introduced in the
  Legislative Assembly.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assent of Governor in Council

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A bill in respect of which a direction is given must
  not become law without the Governor in Council's assent, which is not
  to be given later than one year after the day on which the bill is
  adopted by the Legislative Assembly.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transmittal of laws

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A copy
  of every law of the Legislature must be transmitted by the Clerk of
  the Legislative Assembly to the Governor in Council within 30 days
  after the day on which it is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disallowance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may disallow any law of the
  Legislature or any provision of such a law at any time within one year
  after the day on which it is made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflicting laws

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** In the event of a
conflict between a law of the Legislature and a federal enactment, the
federal enactment prevails to the extent of the conflict.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Official Languages
Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01)

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  ordinance entitled the [Official Languages Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01) ---
  made on June 28, 1984 by the Commissioner in Council, as amended on
  June 26, 1986 or by an Act referred to in section 33 --- must not be
  amended, repealed or rendered inoperable by the Legislature without
  the concurrence of Parliament by way of an amendment to this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional rights and services

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in subsection (1) is to be construed as
  preventing the Commissioner, the Legislature or the Government of the
  Northwest Territories from granting rights in respect of, or providing
  services in, English, French or a language of an Aboriginal people of
  Canada --- in addition to the rights and services provided for in the
  [Official Languages Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01) referred to in
  subsection (1) --- whether by amendment, without the concurrence of
  Parliament, or by any other means.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments concurred in

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Parliament concurs in An Act to
  amend the Official Languages Act, made on October 29, 1990 by the
  Commissioner in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}March 12, 1992

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Parliament concurs in An Act to amend the Official
  Languages Act, made on March 12, 1992 by the Commissioner in Council.

## [Consolidated Revenue Fund of the Northwest Territories]{.HTitleText1} {#h-367058 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  public moneys over which the Legislature has the power of
  appropriation are to form a fund to be known as the Consolidated
  Revenue Fund of the Northwest Territories.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of bank accounts

  of the Government of the Northwest Territories, accounts for the
  deposit of public moneys with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} banks as defined in section 2 of the [Bank

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} authorized foreign banks, as defined in section 2
    of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), that are not subject to the
    restrictions and requirements referred to in subsection 524(2) of
    that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendation of Commissioner

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** The Legislative Assembly
must not adopt or pass any vote, resolution, address or bill --- for the
appropriation of public moneys, or of any tax, for any purpose --- that
has not been first recommended to the Legislative Assembly by message of
the Commissioner in the session in which the vote, resolution, address
or bill is proposed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriation of moneys granted by

granted to Her Majesty in right of Canada by Parliament to defray
expenses for a specified public service in the Northwest Territories,
the power of appropriation by the Legislature over that sum is subject
to the purpose for which it is granted.

## [Public Accounts of the Northwest Territories]{.HTitleText1} {#h-367070 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fiscal year

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** The fiscal year of the
Government of the Northwest Territories is the period beginning on April
1 in one year and ending on March 31 in the next year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission to Legislative Assembly

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner, with
the consent of the Executive Council, must lay before the Legislative
Assembly --- on or before the day of each fiscal year that the
Legislative Assembly fixes --- a report called the Public Accounts of
the Northwest Territories for the preceding fiscal year; the Legislative
Assembly must consider the report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and contents

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** The Public Accounts of
the Northwest Territories must be prepared in any form that the
Commissioner, with the consent of the Executive Council, directs and are
to include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} consolidated financial statements for the Government
  of the Northwest Territories, prepared in accordance with Canadian
  public sector accounting standards;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other information or statements that are required
  in support of those consolidated financial statements under any law of
  the Legislature; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the opinion of the Auditor General of Canada that is
  referred to in subsection 40(1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual audit

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Auditor General of Canada must audit the accounts --- including those
  related to the Consolidated Revenue Fund of the Northwest Territories
  --- and financial transactions of the Government of the Northwest
  Territories in each fiscal year in accordance with Canadian generally
  accepted auditing standards and must express his or her opinion as to
  whether

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the consolidated financial statements present
    fairly --- in all material respects and in accordance with Canadian
    public sector accounting standards --- the financial situation of
    the Government of the Northwest Territories as at the end of the
    fiscal year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the transactions of the Government of the Northwest
    Territories that have come to the notice of the Auditor General in
    the course of the audit are within the powers of that Government
    under this or any other Act of Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Auditor General must report to the Legislative
  Assembly any matter falling within the scope of the audit that, in his
  or her opinion, ought to be reported to the Assembly.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supplementary report

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** The Auditor General of
Canada may, at any time, inquire into and submit a supplementary report
to the Legislative Assembly about any matter relating to the activities
of the Government of the Northwest Territories, including whether

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} accounts have not been faithfully and properly
  maintained or public money has not been fully accounted for or paid,
  if so required by law, into the Consolidated Revenue Fund;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} essential records have not been maintained or the
  rules and procedures applied have been insufficient to safeguard and
  control public property, to secure an effective check on the
  assessment, collection and proper allocation of the revenue and to
  ensure that expenditures have been made only as authorized;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} money has been expended for purposes other than those
  for which it was appropriated by the Legislature or has been expended
  without due regard to economy or efficiency; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} satisfactory procedures have not been established to
  measure and report the effectiveness of programs, if such procedures
  could appropriately and reasonably be implemented.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report at Commissioner's request

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** At the Commissioner's
request, made with the consent of the Executive Council, the Auditor
General of Canada may --- if in his or her opinion it does not interfere
with the Auditor General's primary responsibilities --- inquire into and
report to the Legislative Assembly on any

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} matter relating to the financial affairs of the
  Government of the Northwest Territories or to public property in the
  Northwest Territories; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} person or organization that has received or is
  seeking financial aid from the Government of the Northwest
  Territories.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Auditor General's powers

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of carrying out his or her functions under this Act, the
  Auditor General of Canada has all the powers that he or she has under
  the [Auditor General Act](/eng/acts/A-17).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as provided by any law of the Legislature that
  expressly refers to this subsection, the Auditor General is entitled
  to free access at all convenient times to information that relates to
  the fulfilment of his or her responsibilities and is entitled to
  require and receive from the public service of the Northwest
  Territories any information, reports and explanations that he or she
  considers necessary for that purpose.

## [Administration of Justice]{.HTitleText1} {#h-367102 .Part}

### [Judicature]{.HTitleText2} {#h-367103 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of judges

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
must appoint the judges of any superior, district or county courts in
the Northwest Territories.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure of judges

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** The judges of the
superior, district and county courts in the Northwest Territories hold
office during good behaviour but are removable by the Governor General
on address of the Senate and House of Commons and cease to hold office
on attaining the age of 75 years.

### [Supreme Court of the Northwest Territories]{.HTitleText2} {#h-367108 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judges

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** A judge --- other than a
deputy judge --- of the Yukon Supreme Court or the Nunavut Court of
Justice is, by reason of holding that office, a judge of the Supreme
Court of the Northwest Territories.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy judges

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may appoint any person who is or has been a judge
  of a superior, district or county court of a province or a barrister
  or advocate of at least 10 years standing at the bar of a province to
  be a deputy judge of the Supreme Court of the Northwest Territories
  and fix his or her remuneration and allowances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of appointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A deputy judge may be appointed for any particular
  case or cases or for any specified period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure of office

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A deputy judge holds office during good behaviour but
  is removable by the Governor General on address of the Senate and
  House of Commons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A deputy judge must be sworn to the faithful
  performance of his or her duties in the same manner as a judge of the
  Supreme Court of the Northwest Territories and, during his or her
  appointment, has and may exercise and perform all the powers, duties
  and functions of a judge of that Court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction --- civil cases

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** The Supreme Court of the
Northwest Territories has and may exercise and perform --- in Yukon or
Nunavut --- all of its powers, duties and functions with respect to a
civil case other than one for which the Court sits with a jury.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction --- criminal cases

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  judge of the Supreme Court of the Northwest Territories has and may
  exercise and perform --- anywhere in Canada --- all of its powers,
  duties and functions with respect to any criminal offence committed or
  charged to have been committed in the Northwest Territories.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of laws

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All laws applicable to criminal proceedings held in
  the Northwest Territories apply in like manner to proceedings held
  under this section elsewhere in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any judgment, conviction, sentence or other order
  pronounced or made in any proceedings held under this section outside
  the Northwest Territories may be enforced at the place at which it is
  pronounced or made --- or elsewhere, either in or outside the
  Northwest Territories, as the judge may direct --- and the proper
  officers of the Northwest Territories have and may exercise all powers
  and authority necessary for its enforcement at the place where it is
  directed to be enforced, even if that place is not in the Northwest
  Territories.

### [Court of Appeal of the Northwest Territories]{.HTitleText2} {#h-367129 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sittings

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** The Court of Appeal of
the Northwest Territories may sit in the Northwest Territories or,
unless a law of the Legislature provides otherwise, elsewhere in Canada.

## [Public Lands and Waters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-367132 .Part}

### [Administration and Control]{.HTitleText2} {#h-367133 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public lands --- Commissioner

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner has the administration and control of public lands and
  may use, sell or otherwise dispose of those lands and retain the
  proceeds of the disposition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Commissioner does not
  have the administration and control of the following public lands
  unless they are transferred to him or her under section 54:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} those listed under subsection (3);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} those in respect of which the administration and
    control is relinquished by the Commissioner under section 53;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} those in respect of which the administration and
    control is taken by the Governor in Council under section 55; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} those acquired by Her Majesty in right of Canada
    after the coming into force of section 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council must, on the day on which
  section 1 comes into force, list the public lands that are excluded
  from the administration and control of the Commissioner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights in respect of waters

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  rights in respect of waters belong to Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) is subject to any rights granted by or
  under an Act of Parliament in respect of waters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissioner

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner has the administration and control
  of all rights in respect of waters and may exercise those rights or
  sell or otherwise dispose of them and may retain the proceeds of the
  disposition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (3), the Commissioner does not
  have the administration and control of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the right to the use and flow of waters for the
    production or generation of water power to which the [Dominion Water
    Power Act](/eng/acts/W-4) applies; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the following rights in respect of waters unless
    the administration and control of them is transferred to him or her
    under section 54:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} those listed under subsection (5),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} those in respect of which the administration and
      control is relinquished by the Commissioner under section 53,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} those in respect of which the administration
      and control is taken by the Governor in Council under section 55,
      and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} those acquired by Her Majesty in right of Canada
      after the coming into force of section 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council must, on the day on which
  section 1 comes into force, list the rights in respect of waters that
  are excluded from the administration and control of the Commissioner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Relinquishment by Commissioner

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner may,
with the Governor in Council's consent, relinquish --- in perpetuity or
for any lesser term --- the administration and control of public lands
and of rights in respect of waters.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer to Commissioner

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, with the Commissioner's consent, transfer to the Commissioner ---
in perpetuity or for any lesser term --- the administration and control
of public lands and of rights in respect of waters.

### [Restrictions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-367166 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taking of administration and control by
Governor in Council

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the Minister's recommendation, take from
  the Commissioner the administration and control of public lands and
  rights in respect of waters if, subject to subsection (2), the
  Governor in Council considers it necessary to do so for the purposes
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the national interest, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} national defence or security,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} establishing --- or changing the boundaries of
      --- a national park as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Parks
      Canada Agency Act](/eng/acts/P-0.4), a national historic site as
      defined in that subsection or another area that is protected under
      an Act of Parliament, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the creation of the infrastructure required for
      initiatives in respect of transportation or energy;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fulfilment of an obligation in respect of an
    Aboriginal or treaty right that is referred to in section 35 of the
    [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the settlement of an Aboriginal land claim or the
    implementation of an Aboriginal land claim agreement or other
    treaty, a settlement agreement or a self-government agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must --- before recommending the taking
  of the administration and control other than for a purpose related to
  national defence or security --- consult the member of the Executive
  Council who is responsible for those public lands or those rights in
  respect of waters and any affected Aboriginal party on the boundaries
  of the lands that, and the location of the waters the rights in
  respect of which, are subject to the taking.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition order --- public lands

**[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order and on the Minister's recommendation, prohibit the
issuance under a law of the Legislature of interests in --- or the
authorization under a law of the Legislature of the conduct of
activities on --- the public lands that are specified in the order if
the Governor in Council considers that the prohibition is required

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the taking of the administration and control
  of those lands under paragraph 55(1)(a) or (b); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of the settlement of an Aboriginal
  land claim or the implementation of an Aboriginal land claim agreement
  or other treaty, a settlement agreement or a self-government
  agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition order --- waters

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order and on the Minister's recommendation, prohibit any use of
waters that is specified in the order --- or the deposit of waste
directly or indirectly into those waters --- if the Governor in Council
considers

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the use or deposit would be incompatible with or
  would interfere with a particular undertaking that is in the national
  interest; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the prohibition is required for the purposes of
  the settlement of an Aboriginal land claim or the implementation of an
  Aboriginal land claim agreement or other treaty, a settlement
  agreement or a self-government agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation --- prohibition orders

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must ---
before recommending that the Governor in Council make a prohibition
order under section 56 or 57 --- consult the member of the Executive
Council who is responsible for those public lands or those waters and
any affected Aboriginal party on

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the boundaries of the lands --- and the interests or
  activities --- that are the subject of the order to be made under
  section 56; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the location of the waters that are the subject of
  the order to be made under section 57.

### [Compensation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-367190 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No expenditure or compensation

- **[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  following are to be done without expenditures by or compensation to
  the Government of the Northwest Territories:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a relinquishment under section 53;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a taking under section 55; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the making of a prohibition order under section 56
    or 57.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- improvements to public
  lands

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Commissioner relinquishes to the Governor in
  Council, or the Governor in Council takes, the administration and
  control of public lands, the Government of Canada must compensate the
  Government of the Northwest Territories for any improvements that it
  has made to those lands.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Negotiation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} As soon as practicable after the relinquishment or
  the taking, the Government of Canada and the Government of the
  Northwest Territories are to attempt to reach an agreement on the
  amount of compensation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appraisal expert

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Government of Canada and the Government of the
  Northwest Territories are unable to reach an agreement, they are to
  refer the matter to an agreed upon person with expertise in
  determining the value of improvements to land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of compensation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} That person must determine that value on a basis
  similar to the determination of the fair actual value of the
  improvements --- at the time the Governor in Council takes or has
  relinquished to it the administration and control of the lands ---
  calculated in accordance with the method for calculating the fair
  actual value of improvements to land set out in a law of general
  application of the Legislature relating to the assessment of taxes on
  real property. That value is the amount of compensation.

### [Agreements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-367207 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Management of waters

**[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** With the approval of the
Governor in Council and subject to any agreement entered into under
section 5 or 11 of the [Canada Water Act](/eng/acts/C-11), the Minister
may, on behalf of the Government of Canada, enter into an agreement with
a provincial government providing for the management of any waters that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} are

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} partially on lands situated in the Northwest
    Territories that are under the administration of a federal minister,
    and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} partially on lands that are not under the
    administration of a federal minister; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} flow between

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} lands situated in the Northwest Territories that
    are under the administration of a federal minister, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} lands that are not under the administration of a
    federal minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in sections 69 to 71, 73 and 74.

[[encumbering right]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[encumbering right]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a right referred to
    in section 7(94) of the Inuvialuit Final Agreement, an interest
    referred to in section 18.5 of the Gwich'in Agreement, section 19.5
    of the Sahtu Agreement or section 18.6 of the Tlicho Agreement or a
    similar right or interest referred to in another settlement
    agreement. ([charge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[existing interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[existing interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a right or interest that exists --- immediately
      before the coming into force of section 1 --- under an Act of
      Parliament that is repealed or rendered inapplicable to such a
      right or interest by an Act of Parliament that implements the
      Agreement;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a right or interest that exists --- immediately
      before that coming into force --- under an access order, a permit,
      licence or other authorization, a lease or an agreement for lease
      or sale that is issued, granted or otherwise obtained under an Act
      of Parliament that is repealed or rendered inapplicable to such a
      right or interest by an Act of Parliament that implements the
      Agreement;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a right or interest that exists immediately
      before that coming into force under a licence --- as defined in
      section 51 of the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management
      Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2) as it read immediately before that coming
      into force --- other than a licence that is in relation to a
      federal area as defined in section 51 of the [Mackenzie Valley
      Resource Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2);

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a right or interest that is a renewal,
      replacement or successor of a right or interest referred to in
      paragraph (a), (b) or (c) if a right to that renewal, replacement
      or successor exists immediately before that coming into force; or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a licence that

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is valid immediately before the coming into
        force of section 1 and the giving of which was subject to the
        [Federal Real Property and Federal Immovables
        Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4), or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is a renewal, replacement or successor of such
        a licence if a right to that renewal, replacement or successor
        exists immediately before that coming into force.

    For greater certainty, it includes a right or interest referred to
    in paragraph (a), (b), (c), (d) or (e) that is an encumbering right,
    as well as an interest referred to in subsection 117.2(1) of the
    [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5) that is in respect
    of the portion of those lands situated in the onshore. ([intérêt
    existant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing interests

- **[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  existing interest must, subject to subsections (2) to (4), be
  administered and governed in accordance with any law of the
  Legislature.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- additional conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A law of the Legislature may provide for additional
  conditions in respect of the exercise of an existing interest only if
  those conditions are applicable to similar rights or interests that
  are issued, granted or otherwise obtained under a law of the
  Legislature.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- limitation, suspension
  or cancellation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A law of the Legislature may, in respect of an
  existing interest, provide for its limitation, suspension or
  cancellation only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the circumstances for its limitation, suspension or
    cancellation are identical to those that would have applied
    immediately before the coming into force of section 1; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (4), its limitation,
    suspension or cancellation is for a failure to comply with a
    condition in respect of the exercise of the existing interest and
    the law applies to all similar rights or interests.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- paragraph (3)(b)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A law of the Legislature must not provide for the
  limitation, suspension or cancellation of an existing interest under
  paragraph (3)(b) if it arose from

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a recorded claim, a lease or a permit, as those
    terms are defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Northwest Territories
    and Nunavut Mining Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1516);
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an interest as defined in section 2 of the [Canada
    Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An existing interest --- other than one described in
  paragraph (c) of the definition [[existing
  interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 68 --- continues in full
  force and effect until the earliest of the following takes place:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it expires or is surrendered,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with the agreement of the holder, it is cancelled
    and replaced by a right or interest issued or granted by the
    Government of the Northwest Territories,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an encumbering right, it is --- with
    the agreement of the holder and the relevant Aboriginal organization
    --- cancelled under a settlement agreement,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} it is limited, suspended or cancelled under a law
    of the Legislature referred to in subsection (3), or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} it is expropriated and its holder is compensated
    under a law of the Legislature.

existing interest must be read as a reference to the Government of the
Northwest Territories.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pending proceedings

**[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** Every proceeding ---

(b), (c) or (e) of the definition [[existing
interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 68 that is in progress
immediately before the coming into force of section 1 must be taken up
and continued under and in conformity with the laws of the Legislature
without any further formality.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity of laws of Legislature

**[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** A law of the Legislature
that specifically provides that it applies to matters governed by
section 45, 46, 47, 57, 58 or 59 of the [Northwest Territories
Act](/eng/acts/N-27.05), the [Canada Oil and Gas Operations
Act](/eng/acts/O-7), the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), the
[Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5), the [Federal Real
Property and Federal Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4), the [Northwest
Territories Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-27.3) or the [Northwest Territories
Surface Rights Board Act](/eng/acts/N-27.1) --- as they read immediately
before the coming into force of section 1 --- is considered for all
purposes to have been validly made if it is made before that coming into
force and would have been valid if made after that coming into force.
However, it has no force and effect before that coming into force.

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Communication under the Agreement by

  Territories of information, however recorded, that is subject to
  solicitor-client privilege does not constitute a waiver of that
  privilege.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No employee or agent of the Government of the
  Northwest Territories is, except with the Minister's written
  permission, to knowingly communicate or make available any information
  referred to in subsection (1) --- or permit it to be communicated or
  made available --- to a person who is not an employee or agent of that
  Government.

  to the Government of the Northwest Territories under the Agreement,
  the contract must be assigned even if
