## Fighting Against Forced Labour and Child Labour in Supply Chains Act {#fighting-against-forced-labour-and-child-labour-in-supply-chains-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2023, c. 9
:::

Assented to 2023-05-11

An Act to enact the Fighting Against Forced Labour and Child Labour in
Supply Chains Act and to amend the Customs Tariff
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas forced labour and child labour are forms of modern slavery;

Whereas Canada, as a party to the eight fundamental conventions of the
International Labour Organization on fundamental labour rights ---
including the Forced Labour Convention, 1930, adopted in Geneva on June
28, 1930; the Abolition of Forced Labour Convention, 1957, adopted in
Geneva on June 25, 1957; and the Worst Forms of Child Labour Convention,
1999, adopted at Geneva on June 17, 1999 --- is determined to contribute
to the fight against modern slavery;

And whereas Parliament considers that it is essential to contribute to
fighting modern slavery, including by imposing reporting obligations on
government institutions involved in producing, purchasing or
distributing goods in Canada or elsewhere and on entities involved in
manufacturing, producing, growing, extracting or processing goods in
Canada or elsewhere or in importing goods manufactured, produced, grown,
extracted or processed outside Canada;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1397896 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Fighting Against Forced Labour and Child Labour in Supply Chains
Act](/eng/acts/F-10.6).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1397899 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[child labour]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[child labour]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means labour or services
    provided or offered to be provided by persons under the age of 18
    years and that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are provided or offered to be provided in Canada
      under circumstances that are contrary to the laws applicable in
      Canada;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are provided or offered to be provided under
      circumstances that are mentally, physically, socially or morally
      dangerous to them;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} interfere with their schooling by depriving them
      of the opportunity to attend school, obliging them to leave school
      prematurely or requiring them to attempt to combine school
      attendance with excessively long and heavy work; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} constitute the worst forms of child labour as
      defined in article 3 of the Worst Forms of Child Labour
      Convention, 1999, adopted at Geneva on June 17, 1999. ([travail
      des enfants]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation or a trust,
    partnership or other unincorporated organization that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is listed on a stock exchange in Canada;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a place of business in Canada, does business
      in Canada or has assets in Canada and that, based on its
      consolidated financial statements, meets at least two of the
      following conditions for at least one of its two most recent
      financial years:

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it has at least \$20 million in assets,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it has generated at least \$40 million in
        revenue, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} it employs an average of at least 250
        employees; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is prescribed by
      regulations. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[forced labour]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[forced labour]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means labour or service
    provided or offered to be provided by a person under circumstances
    that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} could reasonably be expected to cause the person
      to believe their safety or the safety of a person known to them
      would be threatened if they failed to provide or offer to provide
      the labour or service; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} constitute forced or compulsory labour as defined
      in article 2 of the Forced Labour Convention, 1930, adopted in
      Geneva on June 28, 1930. ([travail forcé]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[governing body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[governing body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the body or group of
    members of the entity with primary responsibility for the governance
    of the entity. ([corps dirigeant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[government institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[government institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in section 3 of the [Access to Information
    Act](/eng/acts/A-1). ([institution fédérale]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 3 of
    the [Access to Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1). ([responsable
    d'institution fédérale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Public Safety
    and Emergency Preparedness. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[production of goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[production of goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the
    manufacturing, growing, extracting and processing of
    goods. ([production de marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Purpose of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1397922 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to implement Canada's international commitment to contribute to the
fight against forced labour and child labour through the imposition of
reporting obligations on

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} government institutions producing, purchasing or
  distributing goods in Canada or elsewhere; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} entities producing goods in Canada or elsewhere or in
  importing goods produced outside Canada.

## [His Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1397927 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on His Majesty

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on His
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Reporting Obligation --- Government Institution]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1397930 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1397931 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Government institutions

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to any
government institution producing, purchasing or distributing goods in
Canada or elsewhere.

### [Annual Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1397934 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The head
  of every government institution must, on or before May 31 of each
  year, report to the Minister on the steps the government institution
  has taken during its previous financial year to prevent and reduce the
  risk that forced labour or child labour is used at any step of the
  production of goods produced, purchased or distributed by the
  government institution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supplementary information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must also include the following
  information in respect of the government institution:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its structure, activities and supply chains;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its policies and due diligence processes in
    relation to forced labour and child labour;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the parts of its activities and supply chains that
    carry a risk of forced labour or child labour being used and the
    steps it has taken to assess and manage that risk;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any measures taken to remediate any forced labour
    or child labour;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any measures taken to remediate the loss of income
    to the most vulnerable families that results from any measure taken
    to eliminate the use of forced labour or child labour in its
    activities and supply chains;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the training provided to employees on forced labour
    and child labour; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} how the government institution assesses its
    effectiveness in ensuring that forced labour and child labour are
    not being used in its activities and supply chains.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and manner

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may specify, in writing, the form and
  manner in which a report is to be provided. The requirements must be
  made available to the public in the manner that the Minister considers
  appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revised report

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The head
  of a government institution may provide the Minister with a revised
  version of the report provided under section 6.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of revised report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The revised version of the report must, in addition
  to the information required under subsection 6(2), indicate the date
  of the revision and include a description of the changes made to the
  original report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessibility of report

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The head of every
government institution must, on providing the Minister with a report
under section 6 or a revised report under section 7, make the report
available to the public, including by publishing it in a prominent place
on the government institution's website.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Reporting Obligation --- Entities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1397956 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1397957 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entities

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to any
entity

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} producing, selling or distributing goods in Canada or
  elsewhere;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} importing into Canada goods produced outside Canada;
  or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} controlling an entity engaged in any activity
  described in paragraph (a) or (b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of this Part and subject to the regulations, an entity is
  controlled by another entity if it is directly or indirectly
  controlled by that other entity in any manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed control

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An entity that controls another entity is deemed to
  control any entity that is controlled or deemed to be controlled by
  the other entity.

### [Annual Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1397968 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  entity must, on or before May 31 of each year, report to the Minister
  on the steps the entity has taken during its previous financial year
  to prevent and reduce the risk that forced labour or child labour is
  used at any step of the production of goods in Canada or elsewhere by
  the entity or of goods imported into Canada by the entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Single or joint report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An entity may comply with subsection (1) either

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by providing a report in respect of the entity; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by being party to a joint report in respect of more
    than one entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supplementary information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The report must also include the following
  information in respect of each entity subject to the report:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its structure, activities and supply chains;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its policies and its due diligence processes in
    relation to forced labour and child labour;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the parts of its business and supply chains that
    carry a risk of forced labour or child labour being used and the
    steps it has taken to assess and manage that risk;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any measures taken to remediate any forced labour
    or child labour;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any measures taken to remediate the loss of income
    to the most vulnerable families that results from any measure taken
    to eliminate the use of forced labour or child labour in its
    activities and supply chains;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the training provided to employees on forced labour
    and child labour; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} how the entity assesses its effectiveness in
    ensuring that forced labour and child labour are not being used in
    its business and supply chains.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of report

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The report must be approved,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a report in respect of a single
    entity, by its governing body; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a joint report, either

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by the governing body of each entity included in
      the report, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by the governing body of the entity, if any,
      that controls each entity included in the report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attestation of the report

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The approval of the report must be evidenced by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement that sets out whether it was approved
    pursuant to paragraph (4)(a) or subparagraph (4)(b)(i) or (ii); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the signature of one or more members of the
    governing body of each entity that approved the report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and manner

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may specify, in writing, the form and
  manner in which a report is to be provided. The requirements must be
  made available to the public in the manner that the Minister considers
  appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revised report

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  entity may provide the Minister with a revised version of the report
  provided under section 11.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of revised report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The revised version of the report must, in addition
  to the information required under subsection 11(3), indicate the date
  of the revision and include a description of the changes made to the
  original report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval and attestation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 11(4) and (5) apply in respect of a
  revised report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessibility of report

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  entity must, on providing the Minister with a report under section 11
  or a revised report under section 12, make the report available to the
  public, including by publishing it in a prominent place on its
  website.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Federal corporations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any entity that is incorporated under the [Canada
  Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44) or any other Act of
  Parliament must provide the report or revised report to each
  shareholder, along with its annual financial statements.

### [Administration and Enforcement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1398009 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
designate persons or classes of persons for the purposes of the
administration and enforcement of this Part.

### [Designated Person's Powers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1398012 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entry into a place

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  designated person may, for the purpose of verifying compliance with
  this Part, enter any place where they have reasonable grounds to
  believe there is anything to which this Part applies or any document
  relating to the administration of this Part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers on entry

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designated person may, for the purpose referred
  to in subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine anything in the place, including any
    document;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} use any means of communication in the place, or
    cause it to be used;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} use any computer system in the place --- or cause
    it to be used --- to examine data contained in or available to it,
    or reproduce the data --- or cause it to be reproduced --- in the
    form of a printout or other intelligible output and remove any
    printout or output for examination or copying;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prepare a document based on the data, or cause one
    to be prepared;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} use any copying equipment in the place, or cause it
    to be used;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} take photographs or make recordings or sketches of
    anything in the place;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} direct any person to put any equipment in the place
    into operation or to cease operating it;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} prohibit or limit access to all or part of the
    place or to anything in the place; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} remove anything from the place for the purpose of
    examination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons accompanying designated person

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The designated person may be accompanied by any
  person whom the designated person believes is necessary to help them
  exercise their powers or perform their duties or functions under this
  section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The owner or person in charge of the place and every
  person in the place must give all assistance that is reasonably
  required to enable the designated person to exercise their powers or
  perform their duties or functions under this section and is to provide
  any documents, information or access to any data that is reasonably
  required for that purpose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant to enter dwelling-house

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  place referred to in subsection 11(1) is a dwelling-house, the
  designated person may enter it without the occupant's consent only
  under the authority of a warrant issued under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application, a
  justice of the peace may issue a warrant authorizing the designated
  person to enter a dwelling-house, subject to any conditions specified
  in the warrant, if the justice is satisfied by information on oath
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dwelling-house is a place referred to in
    subsection 15(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entry to the dwelling-house is necessary for a
    purpose related to verifying compliance with this Part; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} entry was refused by the occupant or there are
    reasonable grounds to believe that entry will be refused by or that
    consent to entry cannot be obtained from the occupant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A person must not
obstruct or hinder a designated person who is exercising powers or
performing duties or functions under this Part.

### [Order --- Corrective Measures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1398041 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's power

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** If, on the basis of
information obtained under section 15, the Minister is of the opinion
that an entity is not in compliance with section 11 or 13, the Minister
may, by order, require the entity to take any measures that the Minister
considers to be necessary to ensure compliance with those provisions.

### [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1398044 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person or entity that fails to comply with section 11 or 13,
  subsection 15(4) or an order made under section 18, or that
  contravenes section 17, is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
  conviction and liable to a fine of not more than \$250,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False or misleading statement or
  information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person or entity that knowingly makes any false
  or misleading statement or knowingly provides false or misleading
  information to the Minister or a person designated under section 14 is
  guilty of an offence punishable on summary conviction and liable to a
  fine of not more than \$250,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of directors, officers, etc.

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** If a person or an entity
commits an offence under this Part, any director, officer or agent or
mandatary of the person or entity who directed, authorized, assented to,
acquiesced in or participated in its commission is a party to and guilty
of the offence and liable on conviction to the punishment provided for
the offence, whether or not the person or entity has been prosecuted or
convicted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence by employee or agent or mandatary

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** In a prosecution for an
offence under subsection 19(1), it is sufficient proof of the offence to
establish that it was committed by an employee or agent or mandatary of
the accused, whether or not the employee or agent or mandatary is
identified or has been prosecuted for the offence, unless the accused
establishes that they exercised due diligence to prevent its commission.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1398054 .Part}

### [Registry]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1398055 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic registry

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must maintain an electronic registry containing a copy of
  every report or revised report provided to the Minister under section
  6, 7, 11 or 12.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessibility of registry

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The registry must be made available to the public on
  the Department of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness website.

### [Regulations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1398061 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations for carrying out the purposes and provisions of
this Act, including regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing other entities for the purpose of the
  definition [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef};

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the circumstances in which an entity is
  controlled by another entity; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that may, by this Act, be
  prescribed.

### [Report to Parliament]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1398067 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must cause to be tabled in each House of Parliament, on or
  before September 30 of each year or, if a House is not then sitting,
  on any of the next 30 days on which that House is sitting, a report
  containing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a general summary of the activities of government
    institutions and entities that provided a report under this Act for
    their previous financial year that carry a risk of forced labour or
    child labour being used;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the steps that government institutions and entities
    have taken to assess and manage that risk;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, measures taken by government
    institutions and entities to remediate any forced labour or child
    labour;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any order made pursuant to section 18;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the particulars of any charge laid against a person
    or entity under section 19.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must publish the report in a prominent
  place on the Department of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness
  website within 30 days after it is tabled in both Houses of
  Parliament.

### [Review of the Act]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1398078 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review by committee

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  start of the fifth year after the day on which this section comes into
  force, a comprehensive review of this Act must be undertaken by the
  committee of the Senate, of the House of Commons or of both Houses of
  Parliament that may be designated or established for that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The committee referred to in subsection (1) must,
  within one year after the review is undertaken under that subsection,
  submit a report to the House or Houses of Parliament of which it is a
  committee, including a statement setting out any changes to the Act
  that the committee recommends.
