## Federal Prompt Payment for Construction Work Act {#federal-prompt-payment-for-construction-work-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2019, c. 29, s. 387
:::

Assented to 2019-06-21

An Act to establish a regime for prompt payment for construction work
performed for the purposes of a construction project in respect of
federal real property or federal immovables

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 387 of chapter 29 of the Statutes of Canada, 2019,
in force December 9, 2023, see SI/2023-77.\]
:::
:::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169462 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Federal Prompt Payment for Construction Work Act](/eng/acts/F-7.7).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169465 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[adjudicator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[adjudicator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual designated
      as an adjudicator by the Adjudicator Authority. ([intervenant
      expert]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Adjudicator Authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Adjudicator Authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the entity,
      designated under section 15, responsible for the designation of
      adjudicators. ([autorité des intervenants
      experts]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[capital repair]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[capital repair]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any repair intended
      to extend the normal useful life or to improve the value or
      productivity of any real property or immovable, but does not
      include maintenance or repair work performed to prevent the normal
      deterioration or to maintain the normal, functional state of the
      real property or immovable. ([réparation majeure]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[construction project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[construction project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means one or more of
      the following elements required by a contract between a contractor
      and Her Majesty or a service provider: 

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the addition, alteration or capital repair to,
        or restoration of, any federal real property or federal
        immovable;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the construction, erection or installation on
        any federal real property or federal immovable, including the
        installation of equipment that is essential to the normal or
        intended use of the federal real property or federal immovable;
        or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the complete or partial demolition or removal
        of any federal real property or federal immovable. ([projet de
        construction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[construction work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[construction work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the supply of
      materials or services, including the rental of equipment, for the
      purposes of a construction project located in Canada. ([travaux de
      construction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a party to a contract
      with Her Majesty or a service provider under which that party is
      to perform construction work, but does not include such a party if
      they are the lessor or lessee of the real property or immovable to
      which the construction work
      relates. ([entrepreneur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[federal immovable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal immovable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in section 2 of the [Federal Real Property and Federal Immovables
      Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4). ([immeuble fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[federal real property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal real property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
      as in section 2 of the [Federal Real Property and Federal
      Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4). ([bien réel
      fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Her Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Her Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Her Majesty in right of
      Canada. ([Sa Majesté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the member of the Queen's
      Privy Council for Canada who is designated as the Minister under
      section 3. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[notice of adjudication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[notice of adjudication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a written
      notice provided under subsection 16(2). ([avis de
      renvoi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[notice of non-payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[notice of non-payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a written
      notice provided under subsection 9(3), 10(3) or 11(3). ([avis de
      non-paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[proper invoice]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[proper invoice]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an invoice submitted
      under subsection 9(1) that meets the requirements under this Act
      as well as any other requirements set out in the contract between
      the contractor and Her Majesty or a service provider that do not
      conflict with the requirements under this Act. ([facture en
      règle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[service provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[service provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a party to a
      contract with Her Majesty under which that party is to provide Her
      Majesty with services related to federal real property or a
      federal immovable and may, for the purposes of fulfilling its
      obligations under that contract, enter into a contract with a
      person for the carrying out of a construction project, but does
      not include a party to such a contract if they are the lessor or
      lessee of the federal real property or federal
      immovable. ([fournisseur de services]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[subcontractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[subcontractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a party to a contract with a contractor under
        which that party is to perform construction work; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a party to a contract with any person --- other
        than Her Majesty, a service provider or the contractor --- under
        which that party is to perform construction work for the
        purposes of the same construction project for which the
        construction work referred to in paragraph (a) is to be
        performed. ([sous-traitant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employment contract

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The references to a contract in the definitions
  [[contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[service
  provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
  [[subcontractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection (1) do not
  include a contract under which a party is to perform construction work
  as an employee.

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169491 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate a member of the Queen's Privy Council for
Canada as the Minister for the purpose of this Act.

## [Purpose of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169494 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to promote the orderly and timely carrying out of construction projects
in respect of any federal real property or federal immovable by
addressing the non-payment of contractors and subcontractors who perform
construction work for the purposes of those projects.

## [Application of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169497 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Act applies to Her
Majesty, to any service provider and to any contractor or subcontractor
that is to perform construction work for the purposes of a construction
project located in Canada in respect of any federal real property or
federal immovable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of province

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, by order, designate any province if the
  Governor in Council is, taking into account any criteria set out in
  the regulations, of the opinion that the province has, by or under the
  laws of that province, enacted

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a regime for the payment of contractors and
    subcontractors that is reasonably similar to the one set out in this
    Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in case of non-payment of contractors or
    subcontractors, an adjudication regime that is reasonably similar to
    the one set out in this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- designated province

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Governor in Council designates a province
  under subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subsections 8(2) and 9(5), sections 10 to 14 and
    subsection 16(5) no longer apply to any contractor that is to
    perform construction work for the purposes of a construction project
    located in that province;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the provisions of this Act no longer apply to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any subcontractor that is to perform construction
      work for the purposes of that construction project, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any service provider that is to pay for
      construction work performed for the purposes of that construction
      project; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Governor in Council may, with respect to all
    construction projects located in that province, adapt any provisions
    of this Act that apply to Her Majesty or continue to apply to
    contractors for the purpose of addressing any inconsistency or
    conflict between those provisions and the provincial law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply to a construction
  project if any part of that project straddles the border between two
  or more provinces.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, exempt any construction project from the application of
this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to inform potential contractor

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  Her Majesty or a service provider enters into a contract with a person
  under which that person is to perform construction work, Her Majesty
  or that service provider must inform the person that they will be
  subject to this Act if they enter into the contract and provide them
  with any information prescribed by regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to inform subcontractor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before a contractor or subcontractor enters into a
  contract with a person, under which that person is to perform
  construction work for the purposes of the same construction project
  for which the construction work referred to in subsection (1) is to be
  performed, that contractor or subcontractor must inform the person
  that they will be subject to this Act if they enter into the contract
  and provide them with any information prescribed by regulation.

## [Payments Between Parties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169521 .Part}

### [Her Majesty or Service Provider to Contractor]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1169522 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission of proper invoice

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  contractor must, on a monthly basis or as specified by its contract,
  submit a proper invoice in writing to Her Majesty or a service
  provider, as the case may be, with respect to any construction work
  that was performed by the contractor, or performed and invoiced by any
  subcontractor in the subcontracting chain, up to the date of the
  proper invoice and not yet paid for by Her Majesty or that service
  provider.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to pay

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty or the service provider must pay the
  contractor for all of the construction work referred to in
  subsection (1) no later than the 28th day after the day on which the
  proper invoice is received.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-payment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), Her Majesty or the service
  provider may decline to pay for some or all of the construction work
  referred to in subsection (1) if, no later than the 21st day after the
  day on which the proper invoice is received, Her Majesty or the
  service provider provides the contractor with a notice of non-payment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No prior verification

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A proper invoice is not subject to any requirement
  respecting prior verification of the construction work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to information --- proper invoice

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} On request, a contractor must inform any
  subcontractor in the subcontracting chain of the date on which Her
  Majesty or the service provider received a proper invoice from the
  contractor under subsection (1).

### [Contractor to Subcontractor]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1169534 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to pay

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  contractor that is paid by Her Majesty or a service provider under
  subsection 9(2) must, no later than the 35th day after the day on
  which the proper invoice is received by Her Majesty or a service
  provider, pay each of its subcontractors for the construction work
  that they invoiced and that was covered by the proper invoice and paid
  for by Her Majesty or the service provider.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Partial payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), if the contractor
  is only paid in part, it must distribute the amount received from Her
  Majesty or the service provider in the following manner:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on a rateable basis, the contractor must pay any of
    its subcontractors whose construction work is not covered by a
    notice of non-payment provided under subsection 9(3) and may retain
    any amount owed to the contractor by Her Majesty or the service
    provider for any of the contractor's own construction work that is
    not covered by such a notice; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} then, from any remaining amount and on a rateable
    basis, the contractor must pay any of its subcontractors whose work
    is partly covered by a notice of non-payment provided under
    subsection 9(3) and may retain any amount owed to the contractor by
    Her Majesty or the service provider for any of the contractor's own
    construction work that is partly covered by such a notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-payment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the contractor may decline to
  pay for some or all of the construction work if, no later than the
  28th day after the day on which the proper invoice is received, the
  contractor provides the subcontractor with a notice of non-payment.

### [Subcontractor to Subcontractor]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1169544 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to pay

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  subcontractor that is paid by a contractor under subsection 10(1)
  must, no later than the 42nd day after the day on which the proper
  invoice is received by Her Majesty or the service provider, pay each
  of its subcontractors for the construction work that they invoiced and
  that was covered by the proper invoice and paid for by Her Majesty or
  the service provider.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Partial payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), if the
  subcontractor is only paid in part, it must distribute the amount
  received from the contractor in the following manner:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on a rateable basis, the subcontractor must pay any
    of its subcontractors whose construction work is not covered by a
    notice of non-payment provided under subsection 10(3) and may retain
    any amount owed to the subcontractor by the contractor for any of
    the subcontractor's own construction work that is not covered by
    such a notice; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} then, from any remaining amount and on a rateable
    basis, the subcontractor must pay any of its subcontractors whose
    work is partly covered by the notice of non-payment provided under
    subsection 10(3) and may retain any amount owed to the subcontractor
    by the contractor for any of the subcontractor's own construction
    work that is partly covered by such a notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-payment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the subcontractor may decline
  to pay for some or all of the construction work if, no later than the
  35th day after the day on which the proper invoice is received, the
  subcontractor provides its subcontractor with a notice of non-payment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further application --- subcontracting
  chain

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} This section applies to any other subcontractor in
  the subcontracting chain, with any necessary modifications, so that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any subcontractor that is paid under subsection (1)
    must pay its subcontractors no later than the 49th day after the day
    on which the proper invoice is received by Her Majesty or the
    service provider and those subcontractors must pay any of their
    subcontractors no later than the 56th day after the day on which the
    proper invoice is received and so on, in increments of seven days,
    until the end of the subcontracting chain; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any subcontractor that is paid under subsection (1)
    may decline to pay any of its subcontractors if it provides them
    with a notice of non-payment no later than the 42nd day after the
    day on which the proper invoice is received by Her Majesty or the
    service provider and those subcontractors may decline to pay any of
    their subcontractors if they provide them with a notice of
    non-payment no later than the 49th day after the day on which the
    proper invoice is received and so on, in increments of seven days,
    until the end of the subcontracting chain.

### [Holdback]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1169558 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment subject to holdback

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  payment for construction work made to a party under subsection 9(2),
  10(1) or 11(1) may be subject to a holdback provided for by the
  contract entered into between that party and the party that is to pay
  them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, the amount of a holdback made under the
  contract cannot exceed what that amount would be if the holdback was
  made under the construction law of the province in which the
  construction project is located.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of holdback

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the holdback must be paid by no later
  than the day on which that payment would be made under the
  construction law of the province in which the construction project is
  located.

### [Non-payment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1169566 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of non-payment

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A notice of non-payment
must include the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the construction work covered by the
  notice of non-payment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount that will not be paid;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for the non-payment, including whether
  the party that must pay does not have the necessary funds to do so as
  a result of also receiving a notice of non-payment that covers the
  construction work referred to in paragraph (a); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other information prescribed by regulation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest on unpaid amounts

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Interest must be paid and begins to
  accrue on the following unpaid amounts from the expiry of the time
  limit for payment provided under this Act or, when applicable, the
  shorter time limit set out in the contract between the parties:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any amount that is not covered by a notice of
    non-payment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any amount that is covered by a notice of
    non-payment, if the only reason for non-payment of that amount is
    that the party that must pay does not have the necessary funds to do
    so as a result of also receiving a notice of non-payment that covers
    the construction work that must be paid for; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any amount that is covered by a notice of
    non-payment, if there is a reason for non-payment of that amount
    other than the reason referred to in paragraph (b), but only for the
    portion of that amount that an adjudicator orders to be paid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicable interest rate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The interest rate applicable to the amounts referred
  to in subsection (1) is the rate prescribed by regulation or, if the
  contract between the parties sets out an interest rate, the greater of
  the rate prescribed by regulation and the rate set out in the
  contract.

### [Dispute Resolution]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1169581 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, in
accordance with any criteria prescribed by regulations, designate the
Adjudicator Authority.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjudication

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  contractor or subcontractor has not been fully paid for its
  construction work within the time limit for payment provided under
  this Act or, when applicable, the shorter time limit set out in its
  contract, that contractor or subcontractor may obtain from an
  adjudicator a determination respecting any dispute over the
  non-payment by the party that is to pay it under the contract.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to other party

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A contractor or subcontractor that seeks to have the
  dispute referred to in subsection (1) determined by an adjudicator
  must provide the party that is to pay it under the contract with a
  notice of adjudication no later than the 21st day after the later of
  the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the contractor receives a
    certificate of completion with respect to the construction project
    from Her Majesty or a service provider; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if any of its construction work is covered by the
    last proper invoice submitted with respect to the construction
    project, the expiry of the time limit provided under this Act for
    payment for that work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of adjudication

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice of adjudication must include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the names of the parties to the dispute;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a brief description of the dispute, including
    details of how and when it arose;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount requested to be paid;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of a proposed adjudicator; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other information prescribed by regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proposed adjudicator

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (3)(d), the name must
  be selected from a list of adjudicators established by the Adjudicator
  Authority and that selection cannot be restricted by contract.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to information --- certificate of
  completion

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} On request, a contractor must inform any
  subcontractor in the subcontracting chain of the date on which the
  contractor received a certificate of completion with respect to the
  construction project from Her Majesty or the service provider.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint appointment

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  parties to a dispute must jointly appoint an adjudicator to determine
  the dispute.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for appointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the parties are unable to jointly appoint an
  adjudicator, either party may request that the Adjudicator Authority
  appoint an adjudicator for them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On request, the Adjudicator Authority must appoint an
  adjudicator from a list of adjudicators it has established.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The appointment of an adjudicator is subject to that
  adjudicator's consent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  adjudicator must, except in the cases provided for in the regulations,
  determine the dispute.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination binding

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The adjudicator's determination is binding on the
  parties to the dispute unless they come to a written agreement or the
  determination is set aside by a court order or arbitral award.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority of court or arbitrator

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act restricts the authority of a
  court or of an arbitrator to consider the merits of a matter
  determined by an adjudicator.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with determination

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  party that is to pay for construction work under its contract is
  ordered by the adjudicator to make a payment, the party must make the
  payment no later than the 10th day after the day on which it received
  the adjudicator's determination, or within any other time limit set
  out in the determination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the party that is ordered to make the payment
  fails to do so within the applicable time limit, the party that is to
  be paid may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend further construction work without this
    constituting a breach of the terms of its contract; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} file in the Federal Court or a superior court of a
    province, as the case may be, a certified copy of the adjudicator's
    determination within two years after the day on which that
    determination was received.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of filing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On the certified copy being filed, the determination
  becomes and may be enforced as an order of the Federal Court or
  superior court of a province, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs, fees and expenses

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), each
  party to the adjudication must pay its own costs and an equal portion
  of the adjudicator's fees and expenses.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the adjudicator is of the opinion that a party has
  acted in an abusive, scandalous or vexatious manner or in bad faith,
  the adjudicator may order that party to pay some or all of the other
  party's costs or some or all of the other party's portion of the
  adjudicator's fees and expenses.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No summons

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The adjudicator is not to
be required to produce or give evidence in respect of any matter that
comes to their knowledge as a result of exercising their powers or
performing their duties or functions under this Act.

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169634 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations --- Minister

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may make
regulations prescribing

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information referred to in section 8 and the form
  and manner in which it is to be submitted;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the form and content of a proper invoice and the
  manner in which it is to be submitted;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the form and any additional content of a notice of
  non-payment and the manner in which it is to be submitted;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the powers, duties and functions of the Adjudicator
  Authority;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the eligibility and qualifications of individuals who
  may be designated as adjudicators;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the powers, duties and functions of adjudicators;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the maximum fees for the services of an adjudicator;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the form and any additional content of a notice of
  adjudication and the manner in which it is to be submitted; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the procedure and time limits governing the
  adjudication.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations --- Governor in Council

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations generally for carrying out the purposes and
provisions of this Act, including regulations prescribing

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} criteria for the purpose of subsection 6(1); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any province, any days that are to be
  excluded from the computation of time for the purposes of the time
  limits referred to in sections 9 to 11.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Section 126 of [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46)

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Section 126 of the
[Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) does not apply to or in respect of any
contravention or alleged contravention of any provision of this Act or
its regulations.

## [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169652 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application for one year

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** For one year after the
day on which this Act comes into force, this Act does not apply in
respect of the following contracts to perform construction work:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a contract entered into by a contractor, before the
  day on which this Act comes into force, with Her Majesty or a service
  provider; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a contract entered into by a subcontractor, before
  the day on which this Act comes into force or within one year after
  that day, with a contractor referred to in paragraph (a) or with any
  other subcontractor.
