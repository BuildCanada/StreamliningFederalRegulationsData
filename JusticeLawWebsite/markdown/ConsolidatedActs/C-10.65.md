## Canada--United States--Mexico Agreement Implementation Act {#canadaunited-statesmexico-agreement-implementation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2020, c. 1
:::

Assented to 2020-03-13

An Act to implement the Agreement between Canada, the United States of
America and the United Mexican States
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1237380 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada--United States--Mexico Agreement Implementation
Act](/eng/acts/C-10.65).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1237383 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this section and sections 3 to 20.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement between
    Canada, the United States of America and the United Mexican States,
    done at Buenos Aires on November 30, 2018, as amended by the
    Protocol of Amendment to the Agreement between Canada, the United
    States of America and the United Mexican States, done at Mexico City
    on December 10, 2019. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Free Trade Commission
    established under the Agreement and whose powers, functions and
    duties are set out in Chapter 30 of the
    Agreement. ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the whole or any portion
    of an Act of Parliament or a regulation, order or other instrument
    issued, made or established in the exercise of a power conferred by
    or under an Act of Parliament. ([texte législatif
    fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} for the purposes of any provision
    of this Act, means the member of the Queen's Privy Council for
    Canada designated as the Minister for the purposes of that provision
    under section 10. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[North American Free Trade Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[North American Free Trade Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    the North American Free Trade Agreement between Canada, the United
    States of America and the United Mexican States, done at Mexico
    City, Ottawa and Washington, on December 17, 1992. ([Accord de
    libre-échange nord-américain]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation consistent with Agreement

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, this
Act and any federal law that implements a provision of the Agreement or
fulfils an obligation of the Government of Canada under the Agreement is
to be interpreted in a manner consistent with the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of Act and Agreement to
water

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act or the Agreement applies to natural surface or
ground water in liquid, gaseous or solid state.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act, by specific mention or omission, is to be construed
to affect in any manner the right of Parliament to enact legislation to
implement any provision of the Agreement or fulfil any of the
obligations of the Government of Canada under the Agreement.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1237397 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1237400 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The principal purpose of
this Act is to implement the Agreement, the objectives of which, as
elaborated more specifically through its provisions, are to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} replace the North American Free Trade Agreement;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish a free trade area in accordance with the
  Agreement;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} further support mutually beneficial trade between the
  parties to the Agreement as well as their economic growth;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} preserve and expand regional trade and production by
  further incentivizing the production and sourcing of goods and
  materials in the territories of the parties to the Agreement;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} establish a clear, transparent, and predictable legal
  and commercial framework for business planning that supports further
  expansion of trade and investment, including in the online environment
  and in creative and innovative sectors;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} promote efficient and transparent customs procedures
  that reduce costs and ensure predictability for importers and
  exporters;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} recognize the right of the parties to the Agreement
  to regulate, in accordance with the rights and obligations provided
  for in the Agreement, in order to protect legitimate public welfare
  objectives;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} recognize the right of the parties to the Agreement
  to adopt or maintain measures with respect to cultural industries in
  accordance with the rights and obligations provided for in the
  Agreement;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} facilitate trade in goods and services between Canada
  and the other parties to the Agreement by preventing, identifying, and
  eliminating unnecessary technical barriers to trade, by enhancing
  transparency, and by promoting good regulatory practices;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} support the growth and development of small and
  medium-sized enterprises by enhancing their ability to participate in
  and benefit from the opportunities created by the Agreement;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} promote high levels of environmental protection
  through, among other means, the effective enforcement of environmental
  laws, enhanced environmental cooperation and mutually supportive trade
  and environmental policies and practices;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} promote the protection and enforcement of labour
  rights and the improvement of working conditions;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} promote transparency, good governance and the rule of
  law, while eliminating bribery and corruption in trade and investment;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} recognize the importance of increased engagement by
  Indigenous peoples in trade and investment; and

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} facilitate women's and men's equal access to and
  ability to benefit from the opportunities created by the Agreement and
  support the conditions for women's full participation in domestic,
  regional, and international trade and investment.

## [Causes of Action]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1237418 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Causes of action under sections 9 to 20

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person has any cause of action and no proceedings of any kind are to
  be taken, without the consent of the Attorney General of Canada, to
  enforce or determine any right or obligation that is claimed or arises
  solely under or by virtue of sections 9 to 20 or an order made under
  any of those sections.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Causes of action under Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person has any cause of action and no proceedings
  of any kind are to be taken, without the consent of the Attorney
  General of Canada, to enforce or determine any right or obligation
  that is claimed or arises solely under or by virtue of the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply with respect to causes
  of action arising out of, and proceedings taken under, Annex 14-C of
  the Agreement.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Implementation of Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1237426 .Part}

### [Approval, Designation of Minister and Representation on Commission]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1237427 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement is approved.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order designating Minister

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate any member of the Queen's Privy Council for
Canada to be the Minister for the purposes of any provision of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian representative on Commission

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is the
principal representative of Canada on the Commission.

### [Secretariat]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1237434 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of Secretariat

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Secretariat
established under paragraph 1 of Article 2002 of the North American Free
Trade Agreement is continued as the Secretariat that is to be
established under Article 30.6 of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of Canadian Section of
Secretariat

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Canadian Section of
the Secretariat that is referred to in section 14 of the [North American
Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act](/eng/acts/N-23.8) is continued
within the Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development for the
purpose of performing the functions set out in paragraph 3 of Article
30.6 of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Secretary

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  is to be a Secretary of the Canadian Section of the Secretariat to be
  appointed in accordance with the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Secretary is responsible for fulfilling the
  Section's mandate. In doing so, the Secretary is to perform the
  functions set out in paragraph 3 of Article 30.6 of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Officers and employees
who are required for the proper conduct of the work of the Canadian
Section of the Secretariat are to be appointed in accordance with the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

### [Panels and Committees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1237446 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister --- Chapter 10 of
Agreement

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister, with the concurrence of the Minister of Finance, may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint panellists in accordance with paragraphs 2
    and 3 of Annex 10-B.1 of the Agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} appoint a committee member in accordance with
    paragraph 1 of Annex 10-B.3 of the Agreement; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} propose the names of individuals for a roster
    referred to in Annex 10-B.1 or 10-B.3 of the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister --- Chapter 31 of
  Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint panellists in accordance with Article 31.9
    of the Agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} propose the names of individuals for a roster
    referred to in Article 31.8 of the Agreement; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} propose the names of individuals for the lists
    referred to in Article 31-B.3 of Annex 31-B of the Agreement.

### [Labour Council]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1237453 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister of Labour

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Labour
may act as Canada's senior governmental representative on the Labour
Council referred to in Article 23.14 of the Agreement or may designate
that representative.

### [Expenses]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1237456 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of expenditures

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
is to pay its appropriate share of the aggregate of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any expenditures incurred by or on behalf of the
  Commission,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the general expenses incurred by the committees,
  working groups and other subsidiary bodies established under the
  Agreement and the remuneration and expenses payable to representatives
  on the Commission and those committees and to members of those working
  groups and other subsidiary bodies,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the general expenses incurred by the committee
  referred to in paragraph 1 of Annex 10-B.3 of the Agreement and the
  remuneration and expenses payable to the members of that committee,
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the expenses incurred by panels established under the
  Agreement and the remuneration and expenses payable to the panellists
  on those panels, and to any experts retained by those panels.

### [Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1237463 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Article 31.19 of Agreement

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, for the purpose of suspending benefits in
  accordance with Article 31.19 of the Agreement, by order, do any of
  the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend rights or privileges granted by Canada to
    another party to the Agreement or to goods, service suppliers,
    investors or investments of investors of that party under the
    Agreement or any federal law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} modify or suspend the application of any federal
    law, with respect to a party to the Agreement other than Canada or
    to goods, service suppliers, investors or investments of investors
    of that party;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} extend the application of any federal law to a
    party to the Agreement other than Canada or to goods, service
    suppliers, investors or investments of investors of that party; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} take any other measure that the Governor in Council
    considers necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless repealed, an order made under subsection (1)
  has effect for the period specified in the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- paragraph (1)(a)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(a) does not apply in respect of rights
  and privileges that are referred to in Section D of Chapter 10 of the
  Agreement or granted under the [Special Import Measures
  Act](/eng/acts/S-15).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- paragraph (1)(b)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(b) does not apply in respect of any
  provision of the [Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15) or of
  the regulations made under that Act that implements a right or
  privilege referred to in Section D of Chapter 10 of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Article 31-B.10 of Annex 31-B of
Agreement

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  recommendation of the Minister, the Governor in Council may, for the
  purpose of imposing remedies in accordance with Article 31-B.10 of
  Annex 31-B of the Agreement, by order, do any of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend rights or privileges granted by Canada to
    another party to the Agreement or to service suppliers or goods of
    that party under the Agreement or any federal law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} modify or suspend the application of any federal
    law, with respect to a party to the Agreement other than Canada or
    to service suppliers or goods of that party;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} extend the application of any federal law to a
    party to the Agreement other than Canada or to service suppliers or
    goods of that party; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} take any other measure that the Governor in Council
    considers necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before making a recommendation to the Governor in
  Council, the Minister must consult the Minister of Finance if the
  proposed remedies include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspending or withdrawing rights or privileges
    granted by Canada under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a measure described in paragraph 53(2)(b) or (d) of
    that Act; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a measure described in paragraph 53(2)(c) of that
    Act that is related to a measure referred to in paragraph (a) or (b)
    of this subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless repealed, an order made under subsection (1)
  has effect for the period specified in the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retroactive effect

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An order made under subsection (1) may, if it so
  provides, have retroactive effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- paragraph (1)(a)

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(a) does not apply in respect of rights
  and privileges that are referred to in Section D of Chapter 10 of the
  Agreement or granted under the [Special Import Measures
  Act](/eng/acts/S-15).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- paragraph (1)(b)

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(b) does not apply in respect of any
  provision of the [Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15) or of
  the regulations made under that Act that implements a right or
  privilege referred to in Section D of Chapter 10 of the Agreement.
