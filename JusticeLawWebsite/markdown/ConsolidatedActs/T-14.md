## Trade Unions Act {#trade-unions-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. T-14
:::

An Act respecting trade unions
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451463 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Trade Unions Act](/eng/acts/T-14).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451468 .Part}

Definition of [[trade union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act, [[trade
union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such combination, whether temporary or
permanent, for regulating the relations between workmen and masters, or
for imposing restrictive conditions on the conduct of any trade or
business, as would, but for this Act, have been deemed to be an unlawful
combination by reason of some one or more of its purposes being in
restraint of trade.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 2
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451473 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements not affected

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act does not affect

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any agreement between partners relating to their own
  business;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any agreement between an employer and those employed
  by him relating to employment; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any agreement in consideration of the sale of the
  goodwill of a business or of instruction in any profession, trade or
  handicraft.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal proceedings not authorized by this
Act

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Nothing
  in this Act enables any court to entertain any legal proceeding
  instituted with the object of directly enforcing or recovering damages
  for the breach of any agreement

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} between members of a trade union, as such,
    concerning the conditions on which any members of the trade union
    shall, or shall not, sell their goods, transact business, employ or
    be employed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the payment by any person of any subscription
    or penalty to a trade union;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the application of the funds of a trade union

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to provide benefits to members,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to furnish contributions to any employer or
      workman, who is not a member of the trade union, in consideration
      of the employer or workman acting in conformity with the rules or
      resolutions of the trade union, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to discharge any fine imposed on any person by
      sentence of a court of justice;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} made between one trade union and another; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} to secure by bond or suretyship the performance of
    any of the agreements mentioned in paragraphs (a) to (d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements not unlawful

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section shall be deemed to constitute
  as unlawful any of the agreements mentioned in subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-14, s. 4
- 2001, c. 4, s. 124(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of certain Acts

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No Act
  in force in Canada providing for the constitution and incorporation of
  charitable, benevolent or provident institutions applies to trade
  unions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Act does not apply to any trade union not
  registered under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 5
:::

## [Constitution and Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451503 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Trade union may be registered

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Any seven or more members
of a trade union may, by subscribing their names to the rules of the
union and otherwise complying with the provisions of this Act with
respect to registration, register the trade union under this Act, but if
any one of the purposes of the trade union is unlawful, the registration
is void.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registrar

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar General of
Canada shall be the Registrar under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following provisions apply with respect to registration of trade
  unions under this Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application to register the trade union and
    printed copies of its rules, together with a list of the titles and
    names of its officers, shall be sent to the Registrar;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Registrar, on being satisfied that the trade
    union has complied with the regulations respecting registration,
    shall register the trade union and its rules;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} no trade union shall be registered under a name
    identical with that under which any other trade union has been
    registered, or so nearly resembling that name as to be likely to
    deceive the members or the public; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if a trade union that applies to be registered has
    been in operation for more than a year before the date of
    application, there shall be delivered to the Registrar, before the
    registration of the trade union, a general statement of the
    receipts, funds, effects and expenditure of the trade union, in the
    same form and showing the same particulars as if it were the annual
    general statement required, under this Act, to be transmitted
    annually to the Registrar.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of registration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar, on registering a trade union, shall
  issue a certificate of registration, and the certificate, unless it is
  proved to have been withdrawn or cancelled, is conclusive proof that
  the regulations with respect to registration have been complied with.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting registration under this Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the seal, if any, to be used for the
  purpose of registration;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the inspection of documents kept by the
  Registrar under this Act;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the fees, if any, to be paid on
  registration, not exceeding the fees specified in Schedule I; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} generally, for carrying into effect the provisions of
  this Act relating to registration of trade unions.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules of registered union

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The following provisions
apply with respect to the rules of a trade union registered under this
Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the rules shall contain provisions in respect of the
  several matters mentioned in Schedule II; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the rules shall be delivered by the trade
  union to any person, on demand, on payment of a sum not exceeding
  twenty-five cents.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 10
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registered office

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every trade union
registered under this Act shall have a registered office to which all
communications and notices may be addressed.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of office

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Notice of the location of
the registered office of a trade union and of any change therein shall
be given to the Registrar and recorded by him, and until notice of the
location of the registered office is given to the Registrar, a trade
union shall be deemed not to have complied with this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 12
:::

## [Annual Statement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451546 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General statement of affairs

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  general statement of the receipts, funds, effects and expenditure of
  every trade union registered under this Act shall be transmitted to
  the Registrar, before June 1 in each year, and shall show fully the
  assets and liabilities at the date and the receipts and expenditure of
  the trade union during the year preceding the date, to which it is
  made out, and separately, the expenditure in respect of the several
  objects of the trade union, and such statement shall be prepared and
  made out to such date, in such form and shall comprise such
  particulars as the Registrar requires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members and depositors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every member of and depositor in any trade union that
  is registered under this Act is entitled to receive, on application to
  the secretary or treasurer of the trade union, a copy of the general
  statement described in subsection (1) without cost.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 13
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies of rules to accompany statement

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** There shall be sent to
the Registrar, together with the general statement described in
section 13, a copy of all new rules and of all alterations of rules, and
a statement showing the changes of officers, made by the trade union
during the year preceding the date to which the general statement is
made out, and a copy of the rules of the trade union as they exist at
that date.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 14
:::

## [Respecting Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451558 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers relating to land

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  trade union registered under this Act may purchase, or take on lease,
  in the names of the trustees of the trade union, any land not
  exceeding one acre, and may sell, exchange, mortgage, hypothecate or
  lease the land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority of trustees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No purchaser, assignee, mortgagee, hypothecary
  creditor or tenant is bound to inquire whether the trustees of a trade
  union registered under this Act have authority for any sale, exchange,
  mortgage, hypothec or lease, and the receipt of the trustees is a
  discharge for the money arising from the sale, exchange, mortgage,
  hypothec or lease.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}What constitutes distinct union

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, every branch of a
  trade union shall be considered a distinct union.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-14, s. 15
- 2001, c. 4, s. 125(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property vested in trustees

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  real and personal property belonging to any trade union registered
  under this Act shall be vested in the trustees of the trade union,
  appointed as provided by this Act, for the use and benefit of the
  trade union and the members thereof.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Real and personal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The real or personal property of any branch of a
  trade union shall be vested in the trustees of the branch and be under
  the control of those trustees, or their respective executors or
  administrators, according to their respective claims or interests.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}On death of trustee

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On the death or removal of any trustees, real or
  personal property described in this section shall vest in the
  succeeding trustees for the same estate and interest as the former
  trustees had therein, and subject to the same trusts, without any
  conveyance or assignment whatever, except in the case of Dominion
  stock, which shall be transferred into the names of any new trustees.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 16
:::

## [Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451577 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forms of action

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** In all actions, suits or
indictments or summary proceedings before any court of summary
jurisdiction, concerning any property of a trade union or branch, the
property shall be stated to be the property of the persons holding the
office of trustee, in their proper names, as trustees of the trade union
without any further description.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 17
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of trustees

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  trustees of any trade union registered under this Act, or any other
  officer of such trade union who is authorized to do so by the order
  thereof, may bring or defend, or cause to be brought or defended, any
  action, suit, prosecution or complaint, in any court of competent
  jurisdiction, concerning the property, right or claim to property of
  the trade union, and may, in all cases concerning the property, real
  or personal, of the trade union, sue and be sued, and plead and be
  impleaded, in that court, in their proper names, without other
  description than the title of their office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not abated by vacation of office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No action, suit, prosecution or complaint shall be
  discontinued or abated by the death or removal from office of any
  person referred to in subsection (1), but the action, suit,
  prosecution or complaint shall be proceeded in, by or against their
  successor or successors as if the death, resignation or removal had
  not taken place, and those successors shall pay and receive the like
  costs as if the action, suit, prosecution or complaint had been
  commenced in their names for the benefit of, or to be reimbursed from
  the funds of, the trade union.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of process

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any summons to any trustee or other officer of a
  trade union registered under this Act may be served by leaving the
  summons at the registered office of the trade union.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 18
:::

## [Accounting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451591 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of trustee

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  trustee of a trade union registered under this Act is liable to make
  good any deficiency that arises or happens in the funds of the trade
  union.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A trustee of a trade union registered under this Act
  is liable only for the moneys actually received by him on account of
  the trade union.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 19
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Account to be rendered

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Every treasurer or other
officer of a trade union registered under this Act shall, at such times
as he is required by the rules of the trade union, or at any other time,
when called on by the trade union to do so, render to the trustees of
the trade union, or the members, at a meeting thereof, a just and true
account of all moneys received and paid by him since he last rendered a
like account, of the balance then remaining in his hands and of all
bonds or securities of the trade union.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 20
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audit

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  trustees shall cause an account rendered under section 20 to be
  audited by a fit and proper person appointed by them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of treasurer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On an audit under subsection (1), the treasurer, if
  required, shall forthwith

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} hand over to the trustees the balance that appears
    to be due by him; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} hand over to the trustees all securities and
    effects, books, papers and property of the trade union in his hands
    or custody.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to comply

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the treasurer fails to comply with subsection (2),
  the trustees may sue the treasurer, in any court of competent
  jurisdiction, for the balance appearing to have been due from him on
  the last account rendered by him, for all moneys since received by him
  on account of the trade union and for the securities and effects,
  books, papers and property in his hands or custody, leaving him to set
  off in the action the sums, if any, that he has since paid on account
  of the trade union.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In an action under subsection (3), the trustees are
  entitled to recover their full costs of suit, to be taxed as between
  solicitor and client.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 21
:::

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451616 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fraudulently obtaining, misapplying
funds, books, etc.

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If any
  officer, member or other person who is or represents himself to be a
  member of a trade union registered under this Act, or the nominee,
  executor, administrator or assignee of a member thereof, or any person
  whatever,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by false representation or imposition, obtains
    possession of any moneys, securities, books, papers or other effects
    of the trade union,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} having the effects of the trade union in his
    possession, wilfully withholds or fraudulently misapplies the
    effects, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} wilfully applies any part of the effects of the
    trade union to purposes other than those expressed or directed in
    the rules of the trade union,

  the provincial court judge or justices having jurisdiction in cases of
  complaint for offences under this Act for the place in which the
  registered office of the trade union is situated may, by summary
  order, on a complaint made by any person on behalf of the trade union,
  or by the Registrar, order the officer, member or other person,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to deliver up all those moneys, securities, books,
    papers or other effects to the trade union, or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} to repay the amount of money paid improperly and to
    pay, if the provincial court judge or justices think fit, a further
    sum of money not exceeding one hundred dollars, together with costs
    not exceeding five dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to comply

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In default of delivery of effects or payment of an
  amount of money, or payment of a penalty and costs ordered under
  subsection (1), the provincial court judge or justices may order the
  person convicted to be imprisoned, with or without hard labour, for
  any term not exceeding three months.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings by indictment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act prevents a trade union from
  proceeding by indictment against a person described in subsection (1),
  but no person shall be proceeded against by indictment if a conviction
  has been previously obtained for the same offence under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-14, s. 22
- R.S., 1985, c. 27 (1st Supp.), s. 203
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to have a registered office

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** If any trade union
registered under this Act is in operation for seven days without having
a registered office to which all communications and notices may be
addressed, the trade union and every officer thereof shall each incur a
penalty not exceeding twenty-five dollars for every day during which it
is so in operation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 23
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to transmit general statement

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  trade union registered under this Act that fails to transmit to the
  Registrar, before June 1 in each year, a general statement of its
  receipts, funds, effects and expenditure,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} showing fully the assets and liabilities at that
    date,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the receipts and expenditure of the trade union
    during the year immediately preceding, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} showing separately the expenditure in respect of
    the several objects of the trade union, prepared and made out to
    such date, and in such form, and comprising such particulars as the
    Registrar requires, together with a copy of all alterations of rules
    and changes of officers made, and a copy of the rules as they exist
    at that date,

  is guilty of an offence and liable to a fine not exceeding twenty-five
  dollars for each offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officer failing to transmit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every officer of a trade union registered under this
  Act whose duty it is to transmit a general statement required under
  subsection (1) who fails to do so, is guilty of an offence and liable
  to a fine not exceeding twenty-five dollars for each offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failing to furnish copies

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the secretary or treasurer of a trade union
  registered under this Act refuses or fails to furnish any member
  thereof or depositor therein, on application, with a copy of a general
  statement required under subsection (1), the secretary or treasurer is
  guilty of an offence and liable to a fine not exceeding twenty-five
  dollars for each offence.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 24
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Making false entries

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Every person who wilfully
makes, or orders to be made, any false entry in or any omission from a
general statement required under subsection 24(1), or in or from the
return of any copies of rules or alterations of rules required under
this Act is guilty of an offence and liable to a fine not exceeding two
hundred dollars for each offence.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 25
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Circulating false copies of rules of a
union

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Every person who, with
intent to mislead or defraud,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} gives to any member of a trade union registered under
  this Act, or to any person intending or applying to become a member of
  that trade union, a copy of any rules or of any alterations of the
  rules falsely pretending that they are the existing rules of the trade
  union, or that there are no other rules of the trade union, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} gives a copy of any rules of any trade union not
  registered under this Act to any person under the pretence that the
  rules are the rules of a trade union registered under this Act,

is guilty of an indictable offence and liable to a fine not exceeding
two hundred dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
months or to both.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 26
:::

## [Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451660 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summary conviction

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  offences and penalties under this Act may be prosecuted and recovered
  on summary conviction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Description of offence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The description of any offence against this Act in
  the words of this Act is sufficient.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proving exception, exemption, etc.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any exception, exemption, proviso, excuse or
  qualification, whether it accompanies or does not accompany the
  description of any offence charged under this Act, may be proved by
  the defendant, but need not be specified in the information, and if it
  is specified and negatived in the information, no proof in relation to
  the matters specified and negatived shall be required on the part of
  the informant or prosecutor.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 27
:::

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, 2000, c. 12,
s. 297\]]{.Repealed}

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451671 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purposes of trade union not unlawful

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The purposes of any trade
union shall not, by reason merely that they are in restraint of trade,
be deemed to be unlawful so as to render any member of the trade union
liable to criminal prosecution for conspiracy or otherwise, or so as to
render void or voidable any agreement or trust.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 29
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report for Parliament

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar shall lay
before Parliament annual reports with respect to the matters transacted
by him as Registrar under this Act and in pursuance thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, s. 30
:::

## [SCHEDULE II]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 10)]{.OriginatingRef}[Matters To Be Provided for by the Rules of Trade Unions Registered Under This Act]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-451688 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The name of the trade union and the place of meeting for the
  business of the trade union.

- **2** Every object for which the trade union is to be established, the
  purposes for which the funds thereof shall be applicable, the
  conditions under which any member may become entitled to any benefit
  assured thereby and the fines and forfeitures that may be imposed on
  any member of the trade union.

- **3** The manner of making, altering, amending and rescinding rules.

- **4** A provision for the appointment and removal of a general
  committee of management and of a trustee or trustees, treasurer and
  other officers of the trade union.

- **5** A provision for the investment of the funds of the trade union
  and for an annual or periodical audit of accounts.

- **6** The inspection of the books and names of members of the trade
  union by every person having an interest in the funds of the trade
  union.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-11, Sch. II
