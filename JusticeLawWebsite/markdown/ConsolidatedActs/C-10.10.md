## Canada Recovery Benefits Act {#canada-recovery-benefits-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2020, c. 12, s. 2
:::

Assented to 2020-10-02

An Act establishing the Canada recovery benefit, the Canada recovery
sickness benefit and the Canada recovery caregiving benefit to support
Canada's economic recovery in response to COVID-19

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 2 of chapter 12 of the Statutes of Canada, 2020, in
force on assent October 2, 2020.\]
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1260494 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada Recovery Benefits Act](/eng/acts/C-10.10).

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1260497 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[COVID-19]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[COVID-19]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the coronavirus disease
    2019. ([COVID-19]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Her Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Her Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Her Majesty in right of
    Canada. ([Sa Majesté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[medical practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[medical practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is
    entitled to practise medicine under the laws of a
    province. ([médecin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Employment
    and Social Development. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nurse practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nurse practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registered nurse
    who, under the laws of a province, is entitled to practise as a
    nurse practitioner --- or under an equivalent designation --- and to
    autonomously make diagnoses, order and interpret diagnostic tests,
    prescribe substances and treat patients. ([infirmier
    praticien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period of seven consecutive
    days beginning on and including Sunday. ([semaine]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Canada Recovery Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1260506 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  is eligible for a Canada recovery benefit for any two-week period
  falling within the period beginning on September 27, 2020 and ending
  on October 23, 2021 if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they have a valid social insurance number;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they were at least 15 years of age on the first day
    of the two-week period;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} they were resident and present in Canada during the
    two-week period;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section 4
    in respect of a two-week period beginning in 2020, they had, for
    2019 or in the 12-month period preceding the day on which they make
    the application, a total income of at least \$5,000 from the
    following sources:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} employment,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} self-employment,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} benefits paid to the person under any of
      subsections 22(1), 23(1), 152.04(1) and 152.05(1) of the
      [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6),

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} allowances, money or other benefits paid to the
      person under a provincial plan because of pregnancy or in respect
      of the care by the person of one or more of their new-born
      children or one or more children placed with them for the purpose
      of adoption, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any other source of income that is prescribed by
      regulation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section 4
    by a person other than a person referred to in paragraph (e.1) in
    respect of a two-week period beginning in 2021, they had, for 2019
    or for 2020 or in the 12-month period preceding the day on which
    they make the application, a total income of at least \$5,000 from
    the sources referred to in subparagraphs (d)(i) to (v);

  - [(e.1)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section
    4 by a person referred to in paragraph (g) whose benefit period was
    established on or after September 27, 2020 in respect of a two-week
    period beginning in 2021, they had, for 2019 or for 2020 or in the
    12-month period preceding the day on which they make the
    application, a total income of at least \$5,000 from the sources
    referred to in subparagraphs (d)(i), (ii), (iv) and (v) and from
    [[regular benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[special
    benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of
    the [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} during the two-week period, for reasons related to
    COVID-19, other than for reasons referred to in subparagraph
    17(1)(f)(i) and (ii), they were not employed or self-employed or
    they had a reduction of at least 50% or, if a lower percentage is
    fixed by regulation, that percentage, in their average weekly
    employment income or self-employment income for the two-week period
    relative to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section
      4 in respect of a two-week period beginning in 2020, their total
      average weekly employment income and self-employment income for
      2019 or in the 12-month period preceding the day on which they
      make the application, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section
      4 in respect of a two-week period beginning in 2021, their total
      average weekly employment income and self-employment income for
      2019 or for 2020 or in the 12-month period preceding the day on
      which they make the application;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} no [[benefit period]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
    defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Employment Insurance
    Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6), was established or could have been
    established in respect of the person in respect of any week that
    falls within the two-week period or, if such a benefit period was
    established on or after September 27, 2020 in respect of the person
    in respect of any week that falls within the two-week period,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the person was paid [[regular
      benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in that subsection,
      for the maximum number of weeks for which those benefits may be
      paid in that benefit period under Part I of that Act, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the person was paid [[regular
      benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[special
      benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in that subsection,
      for the maximum number of weeks for which both those benefits may
      be paid in that benefit period under Part I of that Act;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} no income referred to in any of the following
    subparagraphs was paid or was payable to the person in respect of
    any week that falls within the two-week period:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} allowances, money or other benefits paid to the
      person under a provincial plan because of pregnancy or in respect
      of the care by the person of one or more of their new-born
      children or one or more children placed with them for the purpose
      of adoption,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a Canada recovery sickness benefit or a Canada
      recovery caregiving benefit,

    - [(ii.1)]{.lawlabel} [[benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined
      in subsection 2(1) of the [Employment Insurance
      Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6), and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any other income that is prescribed by
      regulation;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} they sought work during the two-week period,
    whether as an employee or in self-employment;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} they did not place undue restrictions on their
    availability for work during the two-week period, whether as an
    employee or in self-employment;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} if they have not previously received any benefits
    under this Part, they have not,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on or after September 27, 2020, quit their
      employment or voluntarily ceased to work, unless it was reasonable
      to do so, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the two-week period in respect of which their
      application under section 4 relates and in any of the four
      two-week periods beginning on September 27, 2020 that are
      immediately before that two-week period

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} failed to return to their employment when it
        was reasonable to do so if their employer had made a request,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} failed to resume self-employment when it was
        reasonable to do so, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} declined a reasonable offer to work in respect
        of work that would have started during the two-week period;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} if they have previously received any benefits under
    this Part, they have not,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on or after the first day of the first two-week
      period for which any benefits were paid to them under this Part,
      quit their employment or voluntarily ceased to work, unless it was
      reasonable to do so, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the two-week period in respect of which their
      application under section 4 relates and in any of the four
      two-week periods beginning on September 27, 2020 that are
      immediately before that two-week period

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} failed to return to their employment when it
        was reasonable to do so if their employer had made a request,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} failed to resume self-employment when it was
        reasonable to do so, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} declined a reasonable offer to work;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} they were not, at any time during the two-week
    period, required to quarantine or isolate themselves under any order
    made under the [Quarantine Act](/eng/acts/Q-1.1) as a result of
    entering into Canada or

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if they were required to do so at any time during
      the two-week period, the only reason for their having been outside
      Canada was to

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} receive a medical treatment that has been
        certified by a medical practitioner to be necessary, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} accompany a person who has been certified by a
        medical practitioner to be incapable of travelling without the
        assistance of an attendant and whose only reason for having been
        outside Canada was to receive a medical treatment that has been
        certified by a medical practitioner to be necessary, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if, as a result of entering into Canada, they
      were required to isolate themselves under such an order at any
      time during the two-week period, they are a person to whom the
      requirement to quarantine themselves under the order would not
      have applied had they not been required to isolate themselves; and

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} they have filed a return of income under Part I of
    the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) in respect of the 2019 or 2020
    taxation year, other than a return of income filed under subsection
    70(2) or 104(23), paragraph 128(2)(e) or subsection 150(4) of that
    Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Income from self-employment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraphs (1)(d) to (f), income
  from self-employment is revenue from the self-employment less expenses
  incurred to earn that revenue.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Program of instruction or training

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(j), a person has not
  placed undue restrictions on their availability for work if they
  attended during the two-week period a course or program of instruction
  or training that they were referred to by a provincial government or
  body.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of paragraph (1)(n)

  [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(n) applies only in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who has already been paid a Canada
    recovery benefit for 42 weeks or more; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who has never applied under section 4 for
    any two-week period beginning before July 18, 2021.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by regulation, fix a
  lower percentage for the purpose of paragraph (1)(f).

  period beginning on September 27, 2020 and ending on October 23, 2021.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of benefit

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must pay a
Canada recovery benefit to a person who makes an application under
section 4 and who is eligible for the benefit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of payment

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the amount of a Canada recovery benefit for a week
  is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a person who applies or has applied
    under section 4 for any two-week period beginning before July 18,
    2021, \$500 for a maximum of 42 weeks and \$300 for every subsequent
    week; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a person who has never applied under
    section 4 for any two-week period beginning before July 18, 2021,
    \$300 for a week beginning on or after that date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), if a person referred to in
  paragraph (1)(b) subsequently applies under section 4 for any two-week
  period beginning before July 18, 2021, the person is deemed to be a
  person referred to in paragraph (1)(a) except for every two-week
  period for which the person was paid \$300 for each week.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repayment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person who has received a Canada recovery
  benefit or the benefit referred to in section 9.1 has income of more
  than \$38,000 for 2020 or for 2021, the person must repay an amount
  equal to 50 cents for every dollar of income earned in that year above
  \$38,000 of income, up to the total amount of those benefits received
  by them in the year, which total amount is calculated without taking
  into account any erroneous payment or overpayment, and that amount
  constitutes a debt due to Her Majesty and the debt is payable and may
  be recovered by the Minister as of the [[balance-due
  day]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 248(1) of the
  [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), for the year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[income]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (2), [[income]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Canada Recovery Sickness Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1260590 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person is eligible for a Canada recovery sickness benefit for any week
  falling within the period beginning on September 27, 2020 and ending
  on May 7, 2022 if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they have a valid social insurance number;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they were at least 15 years of age on the first day
    of the week;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} they were resident and present in Canada during the
    week;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section 11
    in respect of a week beginning in 2020, they had, for 2019 or in the
    12-month period preceding the day on which they make the
    application, a total income of at least \$5,000 from the following
    sources:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} employment,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} self-employment,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} benefits paid to the person under any of
      subsections 22(1), 23(1), 152.04(1) and 152.05(1) of the
      [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6),

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} allowances, money or other benefits paid to the
      person under a provincial plan because of pregnancy or in respect
      of the care by the person of one or more of their new-born
      children or one or more children placed with them for the purpose
      of adoption, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any other source of income that is prescribed by
      regulation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section 11
    in respect of a week beginning in 2021, they had, for 2019 or for
    2020 or in the 12-month period preceding the day on which they make
    the application, a total income of at least \$5,000 from the sources
    referred to in subparagraphs (d)(i) to (v);

    11 in respect of a week beginning in 2022, they had, for 2019, 2020
    or 2021 or in the 12-month period preceding the day on which they
    make the application, a total income of at least \$5,000 from the
    sources referred to in subparagraphs (d)(i) to (v);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} they have as an employee been unable to work for at
    least 50% of the time they would have otherwise worked in the week,
    or they have as a self-employed person reduced the time devoted to
    their work by at least 50% of the time they would have otherwise
    worked in the week, because

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} they contracted or might have contracted
      COVID-19,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} they have underlying conditions, are undergoing
      treatments or have contracted other sicknesses that, in the
      opinion of a medical practitioner, nurse practitioner, person in
      authority, government or public health authority, would make them
      more susceptible to COVID-19, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} they isolated themselves on the advice of their
      employer, a medical practitioner, nurse practitioner, person in

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} no income referred to in any of the following

    - [(iii.1)]{.lawlabel} a [[lockdown benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} they have not, in respect of the week, been granted

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} they were not, at any time during the week,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if they were required to do so at any time during
      the week, the only reason for their having been outside Canada was
      to

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} receive a medical treatment that has been
        certified by a medical practitioner to be necessary, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} accompany a person who has been certified by a
        medical practitioner to be incapable of travelling without the
        assistance of an attendant and whose only reason for having been
        outside Canada was to receive a medical treatment that has been
        certified by a medical practitioner to be necessary, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if, as a result of entering into Canada, they
      were required to isolate themselves under such an order at any

      quarantine themselves under the order would not have applied had
      they not been required to isolate themselves.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Canada Recovery Caregiving Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1260637 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person is eligible for a Canada recovery caregiving benefit for any
  week falling within the period beginning on September 27, 2020 and
  ending on May 7, 2022 if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they have a valid social insurance number;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they were at least 15 years of age on the first day
    of the week;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} they were resident and present in Canada during the
    week;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section 18
    in respect of a week beginning in 2020, they had, for 2019 or in the
    12-month period preceding the day on which they make the
    application, a total income of at least \$5,000 from the following
    sources:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} employment,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} self-employment,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} benefits paid to the person under any of
      subsections 22(1), 23(1), 152.04(1) and 152.05(1) of the
      [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6),

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} allowances, money or other benefits paid to the
      person under a provincial plan because of pregnancy or in respect
      of the care by the person of one or more of their new-born
      children or one or more children placed with them for the purpose
      of adoption, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any other source of income that is prescribed by
      regulation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section 18
    in respect of a week beginning in 2021, they had, for 2019 or for
    2020 or in the 12-month period preceding the day on which they make
    the application, a total income of at least \$5,000 from the sources
    referred to in subparagraphs (d)(i) to (v);

  - [(e.1)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section
    18 in respect of a week beginning in 2022, they had, for 2019, 2020
    or 2021 or in the 12-month period preceding the day on which they
    make the application, a total income of at least \$5,000 from the
    sources referred to in subparagraphs (d)(i) to (v);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} they have, as an employee, been unable to work for
    at least 50% of the time they would have otherwise worked in that
    week --- or they have, as a self-employed person, reduced the time
    devoted to their work as a self-employed person by at least 50% of
    the time they would have otherwise worked in that week --- because

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} they cared for a child who was under 12 years of
      age on the first day of the week because

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the school or other facility that the child
        normally attended was, for reasons related to COVID-19, closed,
        open only at certain times or open only for certain children,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the child could not attend the school or other
        facility because

        - [(I)]{.lawlabel} the child contracted or might have contracted
          COVID-19,

        - [(II)]{.lawlabel} the child was in isolation on the advice of
          a medical practitioner, nurse practitioner, person in
          authority, government or public health authority for reasons
          related to COVID-19, or

        - [(lll)]{.lawlabel} the child would, in the opinion of a
          medical practitioner or nurse practitioner, be at risk of
          having serious health complications if the child contracted
          COVID-19, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the person who usually cared for the child was
        not available for reasons related to COVID-19, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} they cared for a family member who requires
      supervised care because

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the day program or facility that the family
        member normally attended was, for reasons related to COVID-19,
        unavailable or closed, available or open only at certain times
        or available or open only for certain persons,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the family member could not attend the day
        program or facility because

        - [(I)]{.lawlabel} the family member contracted or might have
          contracted COVID-19,

        - [(II)]{.lawlabel} the family member was in isolation on the
          advice of their employer, a medical practitioner, nurse
          practitioner, person in authority, government or public health
          authority for reasons related to COVID-19, or

        - [(lll)]{.lawlabel} the family member would, in the opinion of
          a medical practitioner or nurse practitioner, be at risk of
          having serious health complications if the family member
          contracted COVID-19, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the care services that are normally provided to
        the family member at their place of residence were not available
        for reasons related to COVID-19;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} no income referred to in any of the following
    subparagraphs was paid or was payable to the person in respect of
    the week:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} [[benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Employment Insurance
      Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} allowances, money or other benefits paid to the
      person under a provincial plan because of pregnancy or in respect
      of the care by the person of one or more of their new-born
      children or one or more children placed with them for the purpose
      of adoption,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a Canada recovery benefit or a Canada recovery
      sickness benefit,

    - [(iii.1)]{.lawlabel} a [[lockdown benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
      as defined in section 2 of the [Canada Worker Lockdown Benefit
      Act](/eng/acts/C-11.01), and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any other income that is prescribed by
      regulation;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} they have not, in respect of the week, been granted
    paid leave or been paid under a plan that provides for payment for
    the care or support of another person; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} they were not, at any time during the week,
    required to quarantine or isolate themselves under any order made
    under the [Quarantine Act](/eng/acts/Q-1.1) as a result of entering
    into Canada or

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if they were required to do so at any time during

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} receive a medical treatment that has been

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments cannot be charged, etc.

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** A benefit under this Act

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not subject to the operation of any law relating
  to bankruptcy or insolvency;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cannot be assigned, charged, attached or given as
  security;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} cannot be retained by way of deduction, set-off or
  compensation under any Act of Parliament other than this Act; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} is not garnishable moneys for the purposes of the
  [Family Orders and Agreements Enforcement Assistance
  Act](/eng/acts/F-1.4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of erroneous payment or
overpayment

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister determines that a person has received a benefit under this
  Act to which they are not entitled, or an amount in excess of the
  amount of a benefit under this Act to which they are entitled, the
  person must repay the amount of the payment or the excess amount, as
  the case may be, as soon as is feasible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery as a debt to Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the erroneous payment or overpayment,
  as determined by the Minister, constitutes a debt due to Her Majesty
  as of the day on which the amount was paid and the debt is payable and
  may be recovered by the Minister as of the day the Minister determined
  the amount of the erroneous payment or overpayment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Garnishment --- financial institution

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by a notice served personally or by a confirmed delivery
  service, order a [[financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
  defined in section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), that holds
  or maintains a deposit account for a person who is indebted to Her
  Majesty under this Act to pay to the Receiver General from the deposit
  account all or part of the amount in respect of which the person is
  indebted to Her Majesty under this Act on account of the person's
  liability to Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Garnishment --- employer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by a notice served personally or by
  a confirmed delivery service, order the employer of a person who is
  indebted to Her Majesty under this Act to pay to the Receiver General
  from the remuneration that would be otherwise payable by the employer
  to the person all or part of the amount in respect of which the person
  is indebted to Her Majesty under this Act on account of the person's
  liability to Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt to Her Majesty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An amount not paid as required by a notice under
  subsection (1) or (2) is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a day is specified in the notice as being the
    day on which the amount is to be paid, a debt due to Her Majesty as
    of the day after the day specified in the notice and the debt is
    payable and may be recovered by the Minister as of the day after the
    day specified in the notice; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if no day is specified in the notice as being the
    day on which the amount is to be paid, a debt due to Her Majesty as
    of the day after the day the notice is served and the debt is
    payable and may be recovered by the Minister as of the day after the
    day the notice is served.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discharge of liability

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The receipt of the Receiver General of money paid as
  required under subsection (1) or (2) is a good and sufficient
  discharge of the original liability of the person to the extent of the
  payment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reconsideration of application

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), the
  Minister may reconsider an application for benefits under this Act
  within 36 months after the benefits have been paid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister decides that a person has received
  money by way of benefits under this Act to which they were not
  entitled, or has not received money to which they were entitled, the
  Minister must calculate the amount of the money and notify the person
  of the Minister's decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount repayable

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Section 28 applies if the Minister decides that a
  person has received money by way of benefits to which they were not
  entitled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount payable

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister decides that a person was entitled to
  receive money by way of benefits under this Act, and the money was not
  paid, the amount calculated under subsection (2) is payable to the
  person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extended time to reconsider claim

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If, in the opinion of the Minister, a false or
  misleading statement or representation has been made in connection
  with an application for benefits under this Act, the Minister has 72
  months within which to reconsider the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for review

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is the subject of a decision of the Minister made under
  this Act may make a request, in the form and manner established by the
  Minister, to the Minister for a review of that decision at any time
  within 30 days after the day on which they are notified of the
  decision or any further time that the Minister may allow.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of this section, a decision of the
  Minister includes the issuance of an order under subsection 29(1)
  or (2) and the person to whom the order is served and the person who
  would not be paid an amount as the result of the issuance of the order
  are each deemed to be a person who is the subject of a decision of the
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must review the decision if a request
  for its review is made under subsection (1). On completion of the
  review, the Minister must confirm, vary or rescind the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must notify the person who made the
  request of the Minister's decision under subsection (3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of default

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The amount of any debt
due to Her Majesty under this Act may be certified by the Minister, and
registration of the certificate in the Federal Court has the same effect
as a judgment of that Court for the amount specified in the certificate
and all related registration costs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation or prescription period

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) to (7),
  no action or proceedings are to be taken to recover money owing under
  this Act after the expiry of the six-year limitation or prescription
  period that begins on the day on which the money becomes payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction, set-off or compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Money owing by a person under this Act may be
  recovered at any time by way of deduction from, set-off against or
  compensation against any sum of money, including any benefit under
  this Act, that may be payable by Her Majesty to the person, other than
  an amount payable under section 122.61 of the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment of liability

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person's liability for money owing under this
  Act is acknowledged in accordance with subsection (5), the time during
  which the limitation or prescription period has run before the
  acknowledgment does not count in the calculation of that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment after expiry of
  limitation or prescription period

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a person's liability for money owing under this
  Act is acknowledged in accordance with subsection (5) after the expiry
  of the limitation or prescription period, an action or proceedings to
  recover the money may, subject to subsections (3) and (6), be brought
  within six years after the date of the acknowledgment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of acknowledgment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An acknowledgment of liability means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a promise to pay the money owing, made by the
    person or their agent or mandatary or other representative;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgment of the money owing, made by the
    person or their agent or mandatary or other representative, whether
    or not a promise to pay can be implied from it and whether or not it
    contains a refusal to pay;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a part payment by the person or their agent or
    mandatary or other representative of any money owing; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgment of the money owing, made in the
    course of proceedings under the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency
    Act](/eng/acts/B-3) or any other legislation dealing with the
    payment of debts, by the person, their agent or mandatary or other
    representative or the trustee or administrator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation or prescription period
  suspended

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The running of a limitation or prescription period in
  respect of money owing under this Act is suspended

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} during any period in which it is prohibited to
    commence or continue an action or other proceedings against the
    person to recover money owing under this Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during any period in which any review of a decision
    establishing the liability in respect of money owing under this Act
    is pending.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement proceedings

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply in respect of an action
  or proceedings relating to the execution, renewal or enforcement of a
  judgment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest payable

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Interest is payable on any debt due
  to Her Majesty under subsection 8(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No interest payable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No interest is payable on any debt due to Her Majesty
  under subsection 29(3), under section 38 and under any provision of
  this Act as a result of an erroneous payment or overpayment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violations

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person commits a violation if they

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} knowingly make, in relation to an application for a
    benefit under this Act, a representation that is false or
    misleading; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make an application for, and receive, a benefit
    under this Act knowing that they are not eligible to receive it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may impose a penalty on a person if the
  Minister is of the opinion that the person has committed a violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of penalty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may set the amount of the penalty for
  each violation at not more than 50% of the benefit that was or would
  have been paid as a result of committing the violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The maximum amount of all penalties that may be
  imposed on a particular person under this section is \$5,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, no penalty may be imposed on a
  person if they mistakenly believe that a representation is true or
  that they were eligible to receive the benefit, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of penalty

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of a penalty is to promote compliance
  with this Act and not to punish.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation of imposition of penalties

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** A penalty must not be
imposed under section 35 if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a prosecution for the act that would constitute the
  violation has been instituted against the person; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} more than three years have passed after the day on
  which that act occurred.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rescission, etc., of penalty

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may rescind
the imposition of a penalty under section 35, or reduce the penalty, on
the presentation of new facts or on being of the opinion that the
penalty was imposed without knowledge of, or on the basis of a mistake
as to, some material fact.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery as debt due to Her Majesty

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** A penalty imposed under
section 35 constitutes a debt due to Her Majesty and the debt is payable
and may be recovered by the Minister as of the day on which the penalty
is imposed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person commits an offence who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of obtaining a benefit for
    themselves under this Act, knowingly uses false identity information
    or another person's identity information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with intent to steal all or a substantial part of
    another person's benefit under this Act, counsels that person to
    apply for the benefit; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} knowingly makes, in relation to one or more
    applications under this Act, three or more representations that are
    false or misleading, if the total amount of the benefits that were
    or would have been paid as a result of the applications is at least
    \$5,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a person does not commit an
  offence under paragraph (1)(a) in relation to false identity
  information if they mistakenly believe that the identity information
  is not false and does not commit an offence under paragraph (1)(c) if
  they mistakenly believe that the representations are true.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prosecution

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No prosecution for an offence under this section may
  be instituted if a penalty for the act that would constitute the
  offence has been imposed under section 35.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is guilty of an offence under
  subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction to a fine of not more
  than the sum of \$5,000 plus an amount of not more than double the
  amount of the benefit that was or would have been paid as a result of
  committing the offence, to imprisonment for a term of not more than
  six months, or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[identity
  information]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In paragraph (1)(a), [[identity
  information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
  402.1 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- investigators

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate as an investigator any person or class of
  persons for the purpose of enforcing section 39.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may authorize the
  [[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the
  [Canada Revenue Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-10.11), to designate as an
  investigator any employee or class of employees of the
  [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in that section 2, for
  the purpose of enforcing section 39.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Proceedings in respect of an offence under this Act
  may be instituted at any time within, but not later than, five years
  after the day on which the Minister became aware of the subject matter
  of the prosecution.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consolidated Revenue Fund

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** All money required to do
anything in relation to this Act, including all money required by the
Minister to administer and enforce this Act or by the
[[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the [Canada
Revenue Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-10.11), to administer and enforce this
Act on behalf of the Minister, may, until March 31, 2026, be paid out of
the Consolidated Revenue Fund.
