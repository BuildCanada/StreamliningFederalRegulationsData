## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6389 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Part may be cited as
the [Atlantic Canada Opportunities Agency Act](/eng/acts/A-13.7).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6392 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

[[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Atlantic Canada
    Opportunities Agency established by section
    10; ([Agence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Atlantic Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Atlantic Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Provinces of Nova
    Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island and Newfoundland and
    Labrador; ([Canada atlantique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2014, c. 20, s.
    175\]]{.Repealed}

[[designated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area of Atlantic
    Canada designated by order of the Minister pursuant to section
    7; ([zone désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada as is appointed by Commission under the
    Great Seal to be the Minister for the purposes of this
    Part; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[President]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[President]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the President of the Agency
    appointed pursuant to subsection
    11(1). ([président]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6403 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Part is
to increase opportunity for economic development in Atlantic Canada and,
more particularly, to enhance the growth of earned incomes and
employment opportunities in that region.

## [Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6406 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions of the
Minister

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  furtherance of the purpose of this Part, the Minister may exercise
  powers and perform duties and functions that affect economic
  opportunity and development in Atlantic Canada over which Parliament
  has jurisdiction and that are not by or pursuant to law assigned to
  any other member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada or to any
  department, board or agency of the Government of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coordination

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall coordinate the policies and
  programs of the Government of Canada in relation to opportunity for
  economic development in Atlantic Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advisory boards

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may establish advisory boards and
  provide for their membership, duties, functions and operation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the approval of the Governor in Council,
  the Minister may provide for the payment of reasonable travel and
  living expenses incurred in the performance of their duties and
  functions by members of advisory boards established under subsection
  (3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsible for Agency

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister is responsible for the Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements with provinces

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may enter into agreements with the
  government of any province or provinces in Atlantic Canada respecting
  the carrying out of any program or project of the Agency.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 41 (4th Supp.), s. 6
- 2002, c. 17, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of areas

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, by order,
establish as a designated area, for the period set out in the order, any
area in Atlantic Canada where, in the opinion of the Minister,
exceptional circumstances provide opportunities for locally based
improvements in productive employment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shares and related instruments

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Subject to any regulations,
the Minister may acquire, exercise, assign or sell a stock option, share
warrant, share or other similar financial instrument obtained as a
condition of a loan or contribution made, a guarantee given or loan
insurance or credit insurance provided under section 13 or in the course
of the collection or enforcement of a debtor's obligation to the Agency.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 41 (4th Supp.), s. 8
- 2001, c. 34, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Support for programs and projects under
other Acts

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Minister is satisfied that the implementation of a program or
  project authorized by any other Act would further the purpose of this
  Part, the Minister may, conditionally or unconditionally, undertake to
  transfer funds appropriated for the purposes of this Part to
  supplement funds that the minister responsible for the program or
  project has undertaken to expend in connection therewith.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of undertaking

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to compliance with any conditions attached to
  an undertaking given by the Minister pursuant to subsection (1) and to
  the approval of Parliament, funds undertaken to be transferred may be
  expended by the minister responsible for the implementation of the
  program or project on the same terms and conditions as funds that that
  minister has undertaken to expend in connection therewith.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information in annual report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each annual report under subsection 21(3) shall
  include full and complete information relating to funds undertaken to
  be transferred under this section in the fiscal year to which the
  report relates.

## [Atlantic Canada Opportunities Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6436 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of Agency

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby
established an agency of the Government of Canada to be known as the
Atlantic Canada Opportunities Agency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall appoint an officer to be the deputy of the
  Minister, who shall be called the President of the Agency and who
  shall hold office during pleasure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chief executive officer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The President is the chief executive officer of the
  Agency and, under the direction of the Minister, has control and
  supervision over the work, officers and employees of the Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting President

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  President or a vacancy in that office, the Minister shall appoint
  another person to act as the President, but no person may act as
  President for a period exceeding ninety days without the approval of
  the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The President shall be paid such remuneration as may
  be fixed by the Governor in Council.

## [Object, Powers and Duties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6448 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Object

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The object of the Agency
is to support and promote opportunity for economic development of
Atlantic Canada, with particular emphasis on small and medium-sized
enterprises, through policy, program and project development and
implementation and through advocacy of the interests of Atlantic Canada
in national economic policy, program and project development and
implementation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** In carrying out its
object, the Agency may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in concert with other concerned departments and
  agencies of the Government of Canada, formulate plans and integrated
  federal approaches to support opportunity for economic development of
  Atlantic Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} plan, direct, manage and implement programs and
  projects intended to contribute directly or indirectly to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the establishment, development, support and
    promotion of enterprises, and more particularly small and
    medium-sized enterprises, in Atlantic Canada,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the development of entrepreneurial talent in that
    region, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the economic prosperity of that region;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} plan, direct, manage and implement programs and
  projects to improve the business environment in Atlantic Canada,
  including programs and projects

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} of support to business associations, conferences,
    studies, consultations, trade shows, demonstration projects and
    market research,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} related to the development of business opportunity
    data banks and networks,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to improve business communication and
    cooperation, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} to promote scholarship related to business and
    investment;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} assist investors to locate enterprises, and more
  particularly small and medium-sized enterprises, in Atlantic Canada,
  consistent with Atlantic Canada and federal investment requirements;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} make loans to any person with respect to the
  establishment and development of enterprises, and more particularly
  small and medium-sized enterprises, in Atlantic Canada;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} guarantee the repayment of, or provide loan insurance
  or credit insurance in respect of, any financial obligation undertaken
  by any person in respect of the establishment and development of
  enterprises, and more particularly small and medium-sized enterprises,
  in Atlantic Canada;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with terms and conditions approved by
  Treasury Board, make grants and contributions in support of programs
  and projects undertaken by the Agency or the Minister;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} enter into contracts, memoranda of understanding or
  other arrangements in the name of Her Majesty in right of Canada or in
  the name of the Agency;

- [(h.1)]{.lawlabel} acquire the assets and assume the obligations of
  the Enterprise Cape Breton Corporation or any of that Corporation's
  subsidiaries;

- [(h.2)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any security interest that it
  acquires from the Enterprise Cape Breton Corporation or any of that
  Corporation's subsidiaries and that was held by that Corporation or
  any of those subsidiaries in respect of a loan, investment or
  agreement that was acquired by that Corporation or any of those
  subsidiaries,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} hold any security interest,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} surrender any security interest,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} realize any security interest, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} exchange, sell, assign or otherwise dispose of any
    security interest;

- [(h.3)]{.lawlabel} hold or exercise, or surrender, sell, assign or
  otherwise dispose of, a stock option, share or other similar financial
  instrument that it acquires from the Enterprise Cape Breton
  Corporation or any of that Corporation's subsidiaries;

- [(h.4)]{.lawlabel} do all things, other than those mentioned in
  paragraphs (h.2) and (h.3), that are necessary for or incidental to
  the administration, management, control or disposal of the assets and
  obligations that it acquires or assumes from the Enterprise Cape
  Breton Corporation or any of that Corporation's subsidiaries; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} do all such other things as are necessary or
  incidental to the attainment of the object of the Agency.

- 2002, c. 17, s. 3

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Agency shall assist
the Minister

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} generally, in the exercise of powers and the
  performance of duties and functions under this Part and any other Act
  in relation to which powers, duties and functions are assigned to the
  Minister in relation to opportunity for economic development of
  Atlantic Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the coordination of policies and programs of the
  Government of Canada in relation to opportunity for economic
  development of Atlantic Canada;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} by administering any agreements entered into by the
  Minister with a province or provinces;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the exercise of powers conferred on the Minister
  by section 8; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} by compiling detailed information on all programs and
  projects undertaken by the Agency or the Minister for the purpose of
  measuring trends, development and progress in the economic development
  of Atlantic Canada.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6486 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers and employees

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Such
  officers and employees as are necessary for the proper conduct of the
  work of the Agency shall be appointed in accordance with the [Public
  Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Government services and facilities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In exercising its powers and performing its duties
  and functions under this Part, the Agency shall, where appropriate,
  make use of the services and facilities of departments, boards and
  agencies of the Government of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offices

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The principal office of
the Agency shall be in Moncton, New Brunswick, but the Agency shall
maintain at least one office in each other province in Atlantic Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contracts binding on Her Majesty

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  contract, memorandum of understanding and arrangement entered into by
  the Agency in its own name is binding on Her Majesty in right of
  Canada to the same extent as it is binding on the Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal proceedings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Actions, suits or other legal proceedings in respect
  of any right or obligation acquired or incurred by the Agency, whether
  in its own name or in the name of Her Majesty, may be brought or taken
  by or against the Agency in the name of the Agency in any court that
  would have jurisdiction if the Agency were a corporation that is not
  an agent of Her Majesty.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6501 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} specifying programs and projects in addition to
    those referred to in paragraph 13(c) to improve the business
    environment in Atlantic Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} defining, for the purposes of this Part, the
    expressions "small and medium-sized enterprises", "project" and
    "demonstration projects" and the class or classes of small and
    medium-sized enterprises and of operations and activities eligible
    for support by the Agency under this Part; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} generally for carrying out the purposes and
    provisions of this Part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, with the approval of the Minister
  of Finance, make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} relating to loans that may be made, guarantees that
    may be given and loan insurance and credit insurance that may be
    provided under this Part; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specifying the circumstances in which and the
    manner in which the Minister may acquire, exercise, assign or sell a
    stock option obtained as a condition under which a contribution or
    loan was made, a guarantee given or loan insurance or credit
    insurance provided under this Part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated area regulations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In order to exploit the opportunities for locally
  based improvements in productive employment identified in a designated
  area, regulations specially applicable to that area may be made under
  the authority of this section that vary from regulations of general
  application to Atlantic Canada.

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6514 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report of Agency

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President shall, within four months after the end of each fiscal year,
  submit a report on the operations of the Agency in that year to the
  Minister.

- [(2) and (2.1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2014, c. 20, s.
  177\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report by Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause to be laid before each House
  of Parliament, on any of the first fifteen days on which that House is
  sitting after September 30 next following the end of each fiscal year,
  a report, including a copy of the annual report submitted to the
  Minister under subsection (1), on the administration of this Part for
  that fiscal year.

## [Transitional]{.HTitleText1} {#h-6523 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriations based on Estimates

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The provisions made by
any appropriation Act for the fiscal year in which this section comes
into force, based on the Estimates for that year to defray the charges
and expenses of the public service of Canada within the Department of
Regional Industrial Expansion in relation to any matter in Atlantic
Canada to which the powers, duties or functions of the Minister or the
Agency extend by law, shall be applied to such classifications of the
public service within the Agency as the Governor in Council may
determine.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of powers, duties and functions

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Wherever under any Act,
order, rule or regulation, or any contract, lease, licence or other
document, any power, duty or function is vested in or exercisable by the
Minister of Regional Industrial Expansion, the Deputy Minister of
Regional Industrial Expansion or any other officer of the Department of
Regional Industrial Expansion in relation to any matter in Atlantic
Canada to which the powers, duties or functions of the Minister or of
the Agency extend by law, the power, duty or function is vested in and
shall be exercised by the Minister, the President or the appropriate
officer of the Agency, as the case may be, unless the Governor in
Council by order designates another Minister, deputy minister or officer
of a department or a portion of the public service of Canada to exercise
that power, duty or function.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed appointment

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Every indeterminate
employee in the public service of Canada who is transferred to a
position in the Agency from the public service of Canada within ninety
days after the day on which this section comes into force is deemed to
have been appointed to the Agency in accordance with subsection 15(1).
