## International River Improvements Act {#international-river-improvements-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. I-20
:::

An Act respecting the construction, operation and maintenance of
international river improvements
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-277612 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [International River Improvements Act](/eng/acts/I-20).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-22, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-277617 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[boundary waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[boundary waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    the Preliminary Article of the treaty relating to the boundary
    waters and to questions arising along the boundary between Canada
    and the United States signed at Washington on January 11, 1909 and
    set out in Schedule 1 to the [International Boundary Waters Treaty
    Act](/eng/acts/I-17). ([eaux limitrophes]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Chief Review Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chief Review Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the review
    officer appointed as Chief Review Officer under subsection 244(1) of
    the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)
    and includes any review officer designated under subsection 244(3)
    of that Act to perform the functions of the Chief Review
    Officer; ([réviseur-chef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[international river]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international river]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means water flowing from
    any place in Canada to any place outside Canada; ([cours d'eau
    international]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[international river improvement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international river improvement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a dam,
    obstruction, canal, reservoir, pipeline or other work the purpose or
    effect of which is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to increase, decrease or alter the natural flow
      of an international river, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to interfere with, alter or affect the actual or
      potential use of the international river outside Canada; ([ouvrage
      destiné à l'amélioration d'un cours d'eau
      international]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada as is designated by the Governor in Council
    as the Minister for the purposes of this
    Act. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transboundary waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transboundary waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in section 10 of the [International Boundary Waters Treaty
    Act](/eng/acts/I-17). ([eaux transfrontalières]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-20, s. 2
- 2009, c. 14, s. 89
- 2013, c. 12, s. 13

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-277630 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, for the purpose of developing and utilizing the water resources of
Canada in the national interest, make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the construction, operation and
  maintenance of international river improvements;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the issue, cancellation and suspension of
  licences for the construction, operation and maintenance of
  international river improvements;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing fees for licences issued under this Act;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the exemption of international river
  improvements from the operation of this Act;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} authorizing the conduct of inspections under this Act
  in respect of any improvement exempted from the operation of this Act
  under the authority of regulations made under paragraph (d) and
  setting out the purposes for which those inspections may be carried
  out; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} designating provisions of the regulations for the
  purpose of paragraph 33(1)(b).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-20, s. 3
- 2009, c. 14, s. 90

## [Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-277641 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Constructing, operating, etc.,
improvement without licence or contrary to a licence

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Except in accordance with a
licence issued under this Act, no person shall construct, operate or
maintain an international river improvement.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-20, s. 4
- 2009, c. 14, s. 91
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-4-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

- **[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  purpose of this section is to prevent the risk of environmental harm
  resulting from the permanent loss of water from Canadian ecosystems.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No licence may be issued for the construction,
  operation or maintenance of an international river improvement that
  links waters that are neither boundary waters nor transboundary waters
  to an international river if the purpose or effect of the improvement
  is to increase the annual flow of the international river at the
  international boundary.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 12, s. 14

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-277655 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This Act
  does not apply in respect of an international river improvement

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} constructed under the authority of an Act of
    Parliament;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} situated within boundary waters; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} constructed, operated or maintained solely for
    domestic, sanitary or irrigation purposes or other similar
    consumptive uses.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspections permitted

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), inspections may be carried
  out under this Act in respect of any international river improvement
  referred to in paragraph (1)(c) to verify that the improvement is
  being constructed or continues to be operated or maintained solely for
  domestic, sanitary or irrigation purposes or other similar consumptive
  uses.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-20, s. 7
- 2009, c. 14, s. 92
- 2017, c. 26, s. 15
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-22, s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When provincial laws to apply

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding anything in
this Act, an international river improvement is subject to the same laws
to which it would be subject if it were a river improvement within the
legislative jurisdiction of the legislature of the province in which it
is situated except in so far as the laws of that province are repugnant
to this Act or the regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-22, s. 9
:::

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-277674 .Part}

### [Enforcement Officers and Analysts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-277675 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate as an enforcement officer or analyst for the
  purpose of this Act or any provision of this Act any person or member
  of a class of persons who, in the Minister's opinion, is qualified to
  be so designated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, an enforcement officer
  has all the powers of a peace officer, but the Minister may specify
  limits on those powers when designating the enforcement officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limits

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may limit the powers that may be
  exercised by an enforcement officer or analyst under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Production of certificate

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must provide every enforcement officer
  and analyst with a certificate of designation that includes any limits
  under subsection (2) or (3). On entering any place, the enforcement
  officer or analyst must, if so requested, produce the certificate to
  the person in charge of the place.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-20, s. 10
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-10-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 10"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Enforcement officers and
analysts are not personally liable for anything they do or omit to do in
good faith under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of passage

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** While carrying out duties
or functions under this Act, enforcement officers and analysts, and any
persons accompanying them, may enter on and pass through or over private
property without being liable for doing so and without any person having
the right to object to that use of the property.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

### [Inspections]{.HTitleText2} {#h-277695 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to enter and inspect

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), for the
  purposes of this Act, an enforcement officer may, at any reasonable
  time, enter and inspect any place in which the officer believes, on
  reasonable grounds, there is anything to which this Act applies or any
  document relating to its administration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dwelling-place

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An enforcement officer may not enter a dwelling-place
  without the occupant's consent or a warrant issued under subsection
  (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant ---
  dwelling-places

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application, a
  justice may issue a warrant authorizing an enforcement officer named
  in the warrant to conduct an inspection of a dwelling-place, subject
  to any conditions specified in the warrant --- and authorizing any
  other person named in the warrant to accompany the enforcement officer
  and exercise any power specified in the warrant subject to any
  conditions specified in the warrant --- if the justice is satisfied by
  information on oath that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conditions for entry described in
    subsection (1) exist in relation to the dwelling-place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entry to the dwelling-place is necessary for any
    purpose relating to the administration of this Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} entry to the dwelling-place has been refused or
    there are reasonable grounds for believing that entry will be
    refused.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant ---
  non-dwellings

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} On [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application, a
  justice may issue a warrant authorizing an enforcement officer named
  in the warrant to conduct an inspection of a place other than a
  dwelling-place, subject to any conditions specified in the warrant ---
  and authorizing any other person named in the warrant to accompany the
  enforcement officer and exercise any power specified in the warrant
  subject to any conditions specified in the warrant --- if the justice
  is satisfied by information on oath that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conditions for entry described in
    subsection (1) exist in relation to that place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entry to the place is necessary for any purpose
    relating to the administration of this Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} entry to the place has been refused, the
    enforcement officer was not able to enter without the use of force
    or the place was abandoned; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (5), all reasonable attempts
    were made to notify the owner, operator or person in charge of the
    place.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waiving notice

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The justice may waive the requirement to give notice
  under paragraph (4)(d) if he or she is satisfied that attempts to give
  the notice would be unsuccessful because the owner, operator or person
  in charge is absent from the jurisdiction of the justice or that it is
  not in the public interest to give the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} In executing a warrant issued under subsection (3) or
  (4), an enforcement officer shall not use force unless its use has
  been specifically authorized in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stopping and detaining conveyances

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, an enforcement officer
  may, at any reasonable time, direct that any conveyance be stopped ---
  or be moved, by the route and in the manner that the officer may
  specify, to a place specified by the officer where an inspection can
  be carried out --- and the officer may, for a reasonable time, detain
  any conveyance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement officer's powers

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} In carrying out an inspection of a place under this
  section, an enforcement officer may, for the purposes of this Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine any thing relevant to the administration of
    this Act that is found in the place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} open and examine any receptacle or package found
    that the officer believes on reasonable grounds contains any thing
    referred to in paragraph (a);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} examine any books, records, electronic data or
    other documents that the officer believes on reasonable grounds
    contain any information relevant to the administration of this Act
    and make copies of them or take extracts from them;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} take samples of anything relevant to the
    administration of this Act; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} conduct any tests or take any measurements.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Analysts

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} An analyst may, for the purposes of this Act,
  accompany an enforcement officer who is carrying out an inspection of
  a place under this section, and the analyst may, when accompanying the
  enforcement officer, enter the place and exercise any of the powers
  described in subsection (8).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition of samples

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} An enforcement officer or analyst may dispose of a
  sample taken under paragraph 8(d) in any manner that the officer or
  analyst considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operation of computer systems and
  copying equipment

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} In carrying out an inspection, an enforcement
  officer may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any computer system or data
    processing system at the place being inspected to examine any data
    contained in, or available to, the system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reproduce any record, or cause it to be reproduced
    from the data, in the form of a printout or other intelligible
    output and remove the printout or other output for examination or
    copying; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any copying equipment at
    the place being inspected to make copies of any books, records,
    electronic data or other documents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of person in possession or control

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is in possession or control of a
  place being inspected under this section shall permit the enforcement
  officer to do anything referred to in subsection (11).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Production of documents and samples

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, for the purposes of this Act, by registered letter or by
  a demand served personally, require any person, within any reasonable
  time and in any reasonable manner that may be stipulated in the letter
  or demand,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to produce at a place specified by the Minister any
    sample taken or any document; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to conduct any tests or take any measurements or
    samples there.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person who is required to do anything under
  subsection (1) shall, despite any other law to the contrary, comply
  with the requirement.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

### [Search and Seizure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-277747 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Search and seizure without warrant

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of this
Act and the regulations, an enforcement officer may exercise the powers
of search and seizure provided in section 487 of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46) without a search warrant if the conditions for
obtaining a warrant exist but by reason of exigent circumstances it
would not be feasible to obtain it.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

### [Assistance to Enforcement Officers and Analysts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-277752 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The owner or the person
in charge of a place entered by an enforcement officer or analyst under
this Act, and every person found in the place, shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} give the enforcement officer or analyst all
  reasonable assistance to enable the officer or analyst to carry out
  their duties or functions under this Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the enforcement officer or analyst with any
  information with respect to the administration of this Act that the
  officer or analyst may reasonably require.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

### [Miscellaneous Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-277759 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents admissible in evidence

- **[[16.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document made, given or issued under this Act and appearing to be
  signed by an analyst is admissible in evidence and, in the absence of
  evidence to the contrary, is proof of the statements contained in the
  document without proof of the signature or official character of the
  person appearing to have signed the document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attendance of analyst

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party against whom the document is produced may,
  with leave of the court, require the attendance of the analyst who
  signed it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No document referred to in subsection (1) may be
  received in evidence unless the party intending to produce it has
  given to the party against whom it is intended to be produced
  reasonable notice of that intention together with a copy of the
  document.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Custody of things seized

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), if an
  enforcement officer seizes a thing under this Act or under a warrant
  issued under the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sections 489.1 and 490 of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46) apply; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the officer, or any person that the officer may
    designate, shall retain custody of the thing subject to any order
    made under section 490 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture if ownership not
  ascertainable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the lawful ownership of or entitlement to a seized
  thing cannot be ascertained within 30 days after the day on which it
  is seized, the thing, or any proceeds of its disposition, are
  forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition by Minister

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Any thing that has been
forfeited under this Act may be dealt with and disposed of as the
Minister may direct.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability for costs

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** If a thing is seized
under this Act or under a warrant issued under the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the person who owned the thing at the time it was
seized, the person who had charge or control of the thing immediately
before it was seized and the person who possessed it immediately before
it was seized are jointly and severally, or solidarily, liable for all
the costs of seizure, detention, maintenance and forfeiture, including
any destruction or disposal costs, incurred by Her Majesty in right of
Canada in relation to the thing in excess of any proceeds of its
disposition, if any.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

### [Compliance Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-277786 .Subheading}

Meaning of [[order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
sections 21 to 30, [[order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a compliance
order issued under section 21.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Whenever, during the course of an
  inspection or a search, an enforcement officer has reasonable grounds
  to believe that any provision of this Act or the regulations has been
  contravened by a person who is continuing the commission of the
  offence, or that any of those provisions are likely to be contravened,
  the enforcement officer may issue a compliance order directing any
  person who causes or contributes to the alleged contravention, or who
  is likely to do so, to take any of the measures referred to in
  subsection (2) that are reasonable in the circumstances in order to
  cease or refrain from committing the alleged contravention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specific measures

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The order may specify that the person to whom the
  order is directed take one or more of the following measures:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} refrain from doing anything in contravention of
    this Act or the regulations, or do anything to comply with this Act
    or the regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} stop or shut down any activity, work, undertaking
    or thing for a specified period;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} cease the operation of any activity or any part of
    a work, undertaking or thing until the enforcement officer is
    satisfied that the activity, work, undertaking or thing will be
    operated in accordance with this Act and the regulations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} move any conveyance to another location;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} unload or reload the contents of any conveyance;
    and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} take any other measure that the enforcement officer
    considers necessary to facilitate compliance with the order,
    including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} maintaining records on any relevant matter,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} reporting periodically to the officer, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} submitting to the enforcement officer any
      information, proposal or plan specified by the officer that sets
      out any action to be taken by the person with respect to the
      subject matter of the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 22, an order must be made in
  writing and must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person or persons to whom the order
    is directed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the provision of this Act or the regulations that
    is alleged to have been or that is likely to be contravened;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the relevant facts surrounding the alleged
    contravention;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the measures to be taken;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the time or the day when each measure is to begin
    or the period during which it is to be carried out;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (4), the duration of the
    order;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a statement that a request for a review may be made
    to the Chief Review Officer; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the period within which a request for a review may
    be made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of order

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An order may not be in force for a period of more
  than 180 days.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failing to file report

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a person who
  commits an offence by failing to file a report required by this Act or
  the regulations is deemed to be continuing the commission of the
  offence each day that the report is not filed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} An order is not a statutory instrument for the
  purposes of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
- 2017, c. 26, s. 63(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exigent circumstances

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  case of exigent circumstances, an order may be given orally on the
  condition that it is followed, within seven days, by a written order
  issued in accordance with section 21.

- Meaning of [[exigent circumstances]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, [[exigent
  circumstances]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes circumstances in which
  the delay that is necessary to issue a written order that meets the
  requirements of subsection 21(3) would result in danger to human life
  or to the environment.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of intent

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  in exigent circumstances, an enforcement officer shall, whenever
  practicable, before issuing an order,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide every person who will be subject to the
    order with an oral or written notice of the officer's intention to
    issue it; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} allow a reasonable opportunity in the circumstances
    for the person to make oral representations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice of intent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of intent to issue the order must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the purpose of the notice;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a reference to the statutory authority under which
    the order is to be issued; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the party notified may make oral
    representations to the enforcement officer within the period stated
    in the notice.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with order

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person to whom an order is directed shall, immediately on receipt of
  the order or a copy of it or on being directed by an enforcement
  officer in an order given orally under subsection 22(1), comply with
  the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No bar to proceedings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The issuance of or compliance with an order in
  respect of a person's alleged contravention of this Act or the
  regulations is not a bar to any proceedings against the person under
  this or any other Act in relation to the alleged contravention.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intervention by enforcement officer

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a

  specified in it, an enforcement officer may take the measures or cause
  them to be taken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to property

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An enforcement officer or other person authorized or
  required by an enforcement officer to take measures under
  subsection (1) may enter and have access to any place or property and
  may do any reasonable thing that may be necessary in the

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal liability

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any person, other than a person referred to in

  required by an enforcement officer under subsection (1) is not

  or omission in the course of providing assistance or advice or taking
  any measures under that subsection unless it is established that the

  in the circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recourse or indemnity

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-277924 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person commits an offence who contravenes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} section 4, subsection 24(1), section 31 or
    subsection 32(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any provision of the regulations designated by
    regulations made under paragraph 3(f); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an order made by a court under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty --- individuals

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every individual who commits an offence under
  subsection (1) is liable,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine of not less than
      \$15,000 and not more than \$1,000,000 or to imprisonment for a
      term of not more than five years, or to both, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a second or subsequent offence, to a fine of
      not less than \$30,000 and not more than \$2,000,000 or to
      imprisonment for a term of not more than five years, or to both;
      or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine of not less than
      \$5,000 and not more than \$300,000 or to imprisonment for a term
      of not more than six months, or to both, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a second or subsequent offence, to a fine of
      not less than \$10,000 and not more than \$600,000 or to
      imprisonment for a term of not more than six months, or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty --- other persons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person, other than an individual or a
  corporation referred to in subsection (4), who commits and offence
  under subsection (1) is liable,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine of not less than
      \$500,000 and not more than \$6,000,000, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a second or subsequent offence, to a fine of
      not less than \$1,000,000 and not more than \$12,000,000; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine of not less than
      \$100,000 and not more than \$4,000,000, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a second or subsequent offence, to a fine of
      not less than \$200,000 and not more than \$8,000,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty --- small revenue corporations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every corporation that commits an offence under
  subsection (1) and that the court determines under section 37 to be a
  small revenue corporation is liable,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine of not less than
      \$75,000 and not more than \$4,000,000, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a second or subsequent offence, to a fine of
      not less than \$150,000 and not more than \$8,000,000; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine of not less than
      \$25,000 and not more than \$2,000,000, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a second or subsequent offence, to a fine of
      not less than \$50,000 and not more than \$4,000,000.

- 2009, c. 14, s. 93

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fundamental purpose of sentencing

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** The fundamental purpose
of sentencing for offences under this Act is to contribute to respect
for this Act through the imposition of just sanctions that have as their
objectives

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to deter the offender and any other person from
  committing offences under this Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to denounce unlawful conduct that harms water
  resources; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to restore the environment harmed by the offence.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sentencing principles

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  addition to the principles and factors that the court is otherwise
  required to consider, including those set out in sections 718.1 to
  718.21 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the court shall
  consider the following principles when sentencing a person who is
  convicted of an offence under this Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the fine should be increased to
    account for every aggravating factor associated with the offence,
    including the aggravating factors set out in subsection (2); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the fine should reflect the gravity
    of each aggravating factor associated with the offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aggravating factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The aggravating factors are the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the offence caused damage or risk of damage to the
    environment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the offence caused damage or risk of damage to any
    unique, particularly important or vulnerable environment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the damage caused by the offence is extensive,
    persistent or irreparable;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the offender committed the offence intentionally or
    recklessly;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the offender failed to take reasonable steps to
    prevent the commission of the offence despite having the financial
    means to do so;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} by committing the offence or failing to take action
    to prevent its commission, the offender increased revenue or
    decreased costs or intended to increase revenue or decrease costs;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the offender committed the offence despite having
    been warned by an enforcement officer of the circumstances that
    subsequently became the subject of the offence;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the offender has a history of non-compliance with
    federal or provincial legislation that relates to water resource
    management; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} after the commission of the offence, the offender

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} attempted to conceal its commission,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} failed to take prompt action to prevent,
      mitigate or remediate its effects, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} failed to take prompt action to reduce the risk
      of committing similar offences in the future.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence of aggravating factor

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The absence of an aggravating factor set out in
  subsection (2) is not a mitigating factor.

- Meaning of [[damage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraphs (2)(a) to (c),
  [[damage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes loss of use value and non-use
  value.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the court is satisfied of the existence of one or
  more of the aggravating factors set out in subsection (2) but decides
  not to increase the amount of the fine because of that factor, the
  court shall give reasons for that decision.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of fines

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendations of court

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The court imposing the fine may recommend to the

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders of court

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person is convicted of an offence under this Act, in addition to any
  punishment imposed, the court may, having regard to the nature of the
  offence and the circumstances surrounding its commission, make an
  order containing one or more of the following prohibitions, directions
  or requirements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prohibiting the person from doing any act or
    engaging in any activity that may, in the opinion of the court,
    result in the continuation or repetition of the offence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to take any action that the
    court considers appropriate to remedy or avoid any damage to the
    environment that resulted or may result from the commission of the
    offence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to post a bond, provide surety
    or pay into court an amount of money that the court considers
    appropriate for the purpose of ensuring compliance with any
    prohibition, direction or requirement mentioned in this section;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to carry out environmental
    effects monitoring in the manner established by the Minister or
    directing the person to pay, in the manner specified by the court,
    an amount for the purposes of environmental effects monitoring;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to implement an environmental
    management system that meets a recognized Canadian or international
    standard specified by the court;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to have an environmental audit
    conducted by a person of a class and at the times specified by the
    Minister and directing the person to remedy any deficiencies
    revealed during the audit;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to pay to Her Majesty in right
    of Canada an amount of money that the court considers appropriate
    for the purpose of promoting sustainable water resource management;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to publish, in the manner
    specified by the court, the facts relating to the commission of the
    offence and the details of the punishment imposed, including any
    orders made under this subsection;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to notify, at the person's own
    cost and in the manner specified by the court, any person aggrieved
    or affected by the person's conduct of the facts relating to the
    commission of the offence and of the details of the punishment
    imposed, including any orders made under this subsection;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to submit to the Minister,
    when requested to do so by the Minister at any time within three
    years after the date of conviction, any information with respect to
    the person's activities that the court considers appropriate in the
    circumstances;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to compensate any person,
    monetarily or otherwise, in whole or in part, for the cost of any
    remedial or preventive action taken, caused to be taken or to be
    taken as a result of the act or omission that constituted the
    offence, including costs of assessing appropriate remedial or
    preventive action;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to perform community service,
    subject to any reasonable conditions that may be imposed in the
    order;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} requiring the person to surrender to the Minister
    any licence issued under the regulations to the person;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} prohibiting the person from applying for any new
    licence under the regulations during any period that the court
    considers appropriate;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to pay, in the manner
    prescribed by the court, an amount to environmental or other groups
    to assist in their work in or for a community near the place where
    the offence was committed;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to pay, in a manner specified
    by the court, an amount to enable research to be conducted into the
    protection, conservation or restoration of international rivers;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to pay, in the manner
    prescribed by the court, an amount to an educational institution
    including for scholarships for students enrolled in studies related
    to the environment; and

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} requiring the person to comply with any other
    conditions that the court considers appropriate for securing the
    offender's good conduct and for deterring the person and any other
    person from committing offences under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person fails to comply with an order made under
  paragraph (1)(h), the Minister may, in the manner that the court
  directed the person to do so, publish the facts relating to the
  commission of the offence and the details of the punishment imposed
  and recover the costs of publication from the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt due to Her Majesty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the court makes an order under paragraph (1)(g)
  or (k) directing a person to pay an amount to Her Majesty in right of
  Canada, or if the Minister incurs publication costs under subsection
  (2), the amount or the costs, as the case may be, constitute a debt
  due to Her Majesty in right of Canada and may be recovered in any
  court of competent jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the court makes an order under paragraph (1)(k)
  directing a person to pay an amount to another person, other than to
  Her Majesty in right of Canada, that other person may, by filing the
  order, enter as a judgment, in the superior court of the province in
  which the trial was held, the amount ordered to be paid, and that
  judgment is enforceable against the person who was directed to pay the
  amount in the same manner as if it were a judgment rendered against
  the offender in that court in civil proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation or suspension of licences

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the court makes an order under paragraph (1)(m),
  any licence to which the order relates is cancelled unless the court
  makes an order suspending it for any period that the court considers
  appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force and duration of order

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} An order made under subsection (1) comes into force
  on the day on which it is made or on any other day that the court may
  determine and shall not continue in force for more than three years
  after that day unless the court provides otherwise in the order.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person is convicted of an offence under this Act, the court may, in
  addition to any punishment imposed, order that any seized thing by
  means of or in relation to which the offence was committed, or any
  proceeds of its disposition, be forfeited to Her Majesty in right of
  Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return if no forfeiture ordered

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the court does not order the forfeiture, the
  seized thing or the proceeds of its disposition shall be returned or
  paid to its lawful owner or the person lawfully entitled to it.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention or sale

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** If a fine is imposed on a
person who is convicted of an offence, any seized thing, or any proceeds
of its disposition, may be retained until the fine is paid or the thing
may be sold in satisfaction of the fine and the proceeds applied, in
whole or in part, in payment of the fine.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation for loss of property

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person has been convicted of an offence under this Act, the court may,
  at the time sentence is imposed and on the application of the person
  aggrieved, order the offender to pay to the aggrieved person an amount
  by way of satisfaction or compensation for loss of or damage to
  property suffered by that person as a result of the commission of the
  offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the amount ordered to be paid under subsection (1)
  is not paid without delay, the aggrieved person may, by filing the
  order, enter as a judgment, in the superior court of the province in
  which the trial was held, the amount ordered to be paid, and that
  judgment is enforceable against the offender in the same manner as if
  it were a judgment rendered against the offender in that court in
  civil proceedings.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** No proceedings by way of
summary conviction in respect of an offence under this Act may be
instituted more than five years after the day on which the subject
matter of the proceedings arose, unless the prosecutor and the defendant
agree that they may be instituted after the five years.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of information about
contraventions

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of encouraging compliance with this Act and the
  regulations, the Minister shall maintain, in a registry accessible to
  the public, information about all convictions of corporations for
  offences under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Information in the registry is to be maintained for a
  minimum of five years.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-278125 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report --- operations under the Act

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** As soon as practicable
after the 31st day of December of each year, the Minister shall prepare
and cause a report of the operations under this Act for that year to be
tabled in each House of Parliament.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review --- sections 33 to 50

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall, 10 years after the day on which this section comes
  into force and every 10 years after that, undertake a review of
  sections 33 to 50.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, no later than one year after the
  day on which the review is undertaken, cause a report on the review to
  be tabled in each House of Parliament.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 93
:::
