## Integrated Cross-border Law Enforcement Operations Act {#integrated-cross-border-law-enforcement-operations-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2012, c. 19, s. 368
:::

Assented to 2012-06-29

An Act to implement a Framework Agreement on Integrated Cross-Border Law
Enforcement Operations between the Government of Canada and the
Government of the United States of America

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 368 of chapter 19 of the Statutes of Canada, 2012,
in force August 20, 2012, see SI/2012-68.\]
:::

::: Enacts

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270566 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Integrated Cross-border Law Enforcement Operations
Act](/eng/acts/I-14.7).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270569 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Framework Agreement on
    Integrated Cross-Border Maritime Law Enforcement Operations between
    the Government of Canada and the Government of the United States of
    America signed on May 26, 2009. ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[designated officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual
    appointed as a cross-border maritime law enforcement officer by

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Central Authority for Canada under subsection
      7(1) or 8(1); and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person designated as the Central Authority
      for the United States for the application of the
      Agreement. ([agent désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[integrated cross-border operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[integrated cross-border operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    deployment of a vessel crewed jointly by designated officers from
    Canada and the United States for cross-border law enforcement
    purposes in undisputed areas of the sea or internal waters along the
    international boundary between Canada and the United
    States. ([opération transfrontalière intégrée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270577 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to implement the Agreement, the objectives of which are to provide
additional means to prevent, detect and suppress criminal offences and
violations of the law in undisputed areas of the sea or internal waters
along the international boundary between Canada and the United States
and to facilitate the investigation and prosecution of such offences and
violations.

## [Principles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270580 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** It is recognized and
declared that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Canada and the United States have a common interest
  in the security of the undisputed areas of the sea or internal waters
  along the international boundary between Canada and the United States;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} integrated cross-border operations must

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} respect the sovereignty of Canada and the United
    States,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} be conducted in accordance with the rule of law,
    and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} be conducted as directed by a designated officer
    from the host country; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in Canada, integrated cross-border operations must be
  conducted in a manner respecting the rights and freedoms guaranteed by
  the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms.

## [Central Authority for Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270589 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
implementing the Agreement, the Central Authority for Canada is the
Commissioner of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police or his or her
delegate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direction and management

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Central Authority for
Canada has, in cooperation with the person designated as the Central
Authority for the United States for the purposes of implementing the
Agreement, responsibility for the direction and management of integrated
cross-border operations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of officers from Canada

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Central Authority for Canada may appoint as a cross-border maritime
  law enforcement officer an individual who is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a police officer appointed or employed under the
    law of a province; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a pilot, co-pilot, observer or other member of the
    crew of an aircraft that is operated by the Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police or a police service established under the law of a province
    and that is used to provide aerial support in an integrated
    cross-border operation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criterion for appointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An individual may be appointed under subsection (1)
  only if they have satisfactorily completed the required training,
  approved by the Central Authority for Canada, for appointment as a
  designated officer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of officers from United
States

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Central Authority for Canada may appoint as a cross-border maritime
  law enforcement officer an individual who is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a commissioned, warrant or petty officer of the
    United States Coast Guard;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a police officer or other law enforcement officer
    appointed or employed under the law of the United States or of a
    state of the United States; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a pilot, co-pilot, observer or other member of the
    crew of an aircraft that is operated by the United States Coast
    Guard or a police service, or other law enforcement agency of the
    United States or of a state of the United States, and that is used
    to provide aerial support in an integrated cross-border operation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria for appointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An individual may be appointed under subsection (1)
  only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they have been recommended for appointment by the
    person designated as the Central Authority for the United States for
    the application of the Agreement; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they have satisfactorily completed the required
    training, approved by the Central Authority for Canada, for
    appointment as a designated officer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension or revocation

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Central Authority for
Canada may suspend or revoke the appointment of any designated officer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificates

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Central Authority for Canada may issue a certificate to any designated
  officer stating that the person to whom it is issued is a designated
  officer for the purposes of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence of appointment or designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any document purporting to be a certificate referred
  to in subsection (1) is evidence in all courts and in all proceedings
  of the facts stated in it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendation for appointment

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Central Authority for
Canada may recommend, to the person designated as the Central Authority
for the United States for the purposes of implementing the Agreement,
that an individual described in paragraph 7(1)(a), (b) or (c) be
appointed as a cross-border maritime law enforcement officer in the
United States.

## [Powers of Designated Officers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270621 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers --- designated officer

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Every designated officer
has the same power to enforce an Act of Parliament as a member of the
Royal Canadian Mounted Police when

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} participating in an integrated cross-border
  operation; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} engaging in an activity incidental to such an
  operation, including travel for the purpose of participating in the
  operation and appearances in court arising from the operation.

## [Detention of Persons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270626 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons taken into custody

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  laws of Canada apply to any person detained or taken into custody
  within Canada in the course of an integrated cross-border operation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal from Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person referred to in subsection (1) may be
  removed from Canada, except in accordance with the laws of Canada.

## [Seizure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270632 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vessel, etc., seized in Canada

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  laws of Canada apply to any vessel or other thing seized within Canada
  in the course of an integrated cross-border operation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal from Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), no vessel or other thing
  referred to in subsection (1) may be removed from Canada, except in
  accordance with the laws of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A vessel or other thing referred to in subsection (1)
  may be removed from Canada in situations of operational or
  geographical necessity, including situations in which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before delivering the vessel or thing to the place
    where it is to be delivered in Canada, designated officers are
    required to participate in a continuing integrated cross-border
    operation or respond to an emergency in the waters of the United
    States;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} due to poor weather or mechanical difficulties with
    a vessel operated by designated officers, it is necessary to transit
    through the waters of the United States in order to reach the
    nearest port; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the navigable shipping channels between the
    location in Canada where the vessel or thing was seized and the
    place where the vessel or thing is to be delivered in Canada pass
    through the waters of the United States.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vessel, etc., seized in United States

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A vessel or other thing
seized in the United States in the course of an integrated cross-border
operation remains in the custody and control of the American designated
officer if it is brought into Canada in situations of operational or
geographical necessity, including situations in which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} before delivering the vessel or thing to the place
  where it is to be delivered in the United States, designated officers
  are required to participate in a continuing integrated cross-border
  operation or respond to an emergency in the waters of Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} due to poor weather or mechanical difficulties with a
  vessel operated by designated officers, it is necessary to transit
  through the waters of Canada in order to reach the nearest port; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the navigable shipping channels between the location
  in the United States where the vessel or thing was seized and the
  place where the vessel or thing is to be delivered in the United
  States pass through the waters of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of certain laws

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** No Act of Parliament
relating to the import or export of goods applies to the import or
export of a vessel or other thing by a designated officer in the
circumstances described in subsection 14(3) or section 15.
