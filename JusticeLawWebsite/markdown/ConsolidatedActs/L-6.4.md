## Latin American Heritage Month Act {#latin-american-heritage-month-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2018, c. 19
:::

Assented to 2018-06-21

An Act respecting Latin American Heritage Month
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the Parliament of Canada recognizes that members of the Latin
American community in Canada have made significant contributions to the
social, economic and political fabric of the nation;

Whereas the designation of a month as Latin American Heritage Month
would be a meaningful way to remember, celebrate and educate the public
about these contributions;

Whereas Latin American communities across Canada would be mobilized by a
Latin American Heritage Month to jointly celebrate, share and promote
their unique culture and traditions with all Canadians;

And whereas October is a significant month for the Latin American
community around the world;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344563 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Latin American Heritage Month Act](/eng/acts/L-6.4).

## [Latin American Heritage Month]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344566 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Latin American Heritage Month

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Throughout Canada, in each
and every year, the month of October is to be known as "Latin American
Heritage Month".
