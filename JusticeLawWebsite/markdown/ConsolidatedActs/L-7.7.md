## Library and Archives of Canada Act {#library-and-archives-of-canada-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2004, c. 11
:::

Assented to 2004-04-22

An Act to establish the Library and Archives of Canada, to amend the
Copyright Act and to amend certain Acts in consequence
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS it is necessary that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the documentary heritage of Canada be preserved for
  the benefit of present and future generations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Canada be served by an institution that is a source
  of enduring knowledge accessible to all, contributing to the cultural,
  social and economic advancement of Canada as a free and democratic
  society;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} that institution facilitate in Canada cooperation
  among the communities involved in the acquisition, preservation and
  diffusion of knowledge; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} that institution serve as the continuing memory of
  the government of Canada and its institutions;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345210 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Library and Archives of Canada Act](/eng/acts/L-7.7).

## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345213 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Act.

[[documentary heritage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[documentary heritage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means publications and
    records of interest to Canada. ([patrimoine
    documentaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[government institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[government institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in section 3 of the [Access to Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1) or
    in section 3 of the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21) or means an
    institution designated by the Governor in Council. ([institution
    fédérale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[government record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[government record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a record that is
    under the control of a government institution. ([document
    fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Librarian and Archivist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Librarian and Archivist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Librarian
    and Archivist of Canada appointed under subsection 5(1). ([Version
    anglaise seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada who is designated by the Governor in
    Council as the Minister for the purposes of this
    Act. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[ministerial record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ministerial record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a record of a
    member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada who holds the office
    of a minister and that pertains to that office, other than a record
    that is of a personal or political nature or that is a government
    record. ([document ministériel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[publication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[publication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any library matter that is
    made available in multiple copies or at multiple locations, whether
    without charge or otherwise, to the public generally or to
    qualifying members of the public by subscription or otherwise.
    Publications may be made available through any medium and may be in
    any form, including printed material, on-line items or
    recordings. ([publication]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any documentary material other
    than a publication, regardless of medium or
    form. ([document]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Establishment and Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345228 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby established
a branch of the federal public administration to be known as the Library
and Archives of Canada presided over by the Minister and under the
direction of the Librarian and Archivist.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2004, c. 11, ss. 4, 54(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Librarian and Archivist

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall appoint an officer, to be called the
  Librarian and Archivist of Canada, to hold office during pleasure and
  to have the rank and powers of a deputy head of a department.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting Librarian and Archivist

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Librarian and Archivist is absent or
  incapacitated or if the office of Librarian and Archivist is vacant,
  the Minister may appoint a person to act as Librarian and Archivist,
  but the term of such an appointment shall not exceed six months except
  with the approval of the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of Advisory Council

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may establish
an Advisory Council to advise the Librarian and Archivist with regard to
making the documentary heritage known to Canadians and to anyone with an
interest in Canada and facilitating access to it.

## [Objects and Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345240 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The objects of the Library
and Archives of Canada are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to acquire and preserve the documentary heritage;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to make that heritage known to Canadians and to
  anyone with an interest in Canada and to facilitate access to it;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to be the permanent repository of publications of the
  Government of Canada and of government and ministerial records that
  are of historical or archival value;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to facilitate the management of information by
  government institutions;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} to coordinate the library services of government
  institutions; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} to support the development of the library and
  archival communities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Librarian and Archivist

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Librarian and Archivist may do anything that is conducive to the
  attainment of the objects of the Library and Archives of Canada,
  including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} acquire publications and records or obtain the
    care, custody or control of them;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} take measures to catalogue, classify, identify,
    preserve and restore publications and records;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} compile and maintain information resources such as
    a national bibliography and a national union catalogue;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide information, consultation, research or
    lending services, as well as any other services for the purpose of
    facilitating access to the documentary heritage;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} establish programs and encourage or organize any
    activities, including exhibitions, publications and performances, to
    make known and interpret the documentary heritage;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} enter into agreements with other libraries,
    archives or institutions in and outside Canada;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} advise government institutions concerning the
    management of information produced or used by them and provide
    services for that purpose;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} provide leadership and direction for library
    services of government institutions;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} provide professional, technical and financial
    support to those involved in the preservation and promotion of the
    documentary heritage and in providing access to it; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} carry out such other functions as the Governor in
    Council may specify.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sampling from Internet

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In exercising the powers referred to in paragraph
  (1)(a) and for the purpose of preservation, the Librarian and
  Archivist may take, at the times and in the manner that he or she
  considers appropriate, a representative sample of the documentary
  material of interest to Canada that is accessible to the public
  without restriction through the Internet or any similar medium.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Destruction or disposal

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Librarian and Archivist may dispose of any publication or record under
  his or her control, including by destruction, if he or she considers
  that it is no longer necessary to retain it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any such disposition is subject to the terms and
  conditions under which the publication or record has been acquired or
  obtained.

## [Legal Deposit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345269 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deposit of publications

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, the
  publisher who makes a publication available in Canada shall, at the
  publisher's own expense, provide two copies of the publication to the
  Librarian and Archivist --- who shall acknowledge their receipt ---
  within

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in any case other than one referred to in paragraph
    (b), seven days after the day it is made available; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a publication that is in a class
    prescribed under paragraph (2)(d), seven days after receiving a
    written request from the Librarian and Archivist or any longer
    period specified in the request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may make regulations for the purposes of
  this section, including regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} defining "publisher";

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting any measures that must be taken to make
    the publications that use a medium other than paper and their
    contents accessible to the Librarian and Archivist;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the classes of publications in respect
    of which only one copy is required to be provided; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the classes of publications in respect
    of which the obligation under subsection (1) applies only on a
    written request from the Librarian and Archivist.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Publications provided to the Librarian and Archivist
  under this section belong to Her Majesty and form part of the
  collection of the Library and Archives of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Each version, edition or form of a
  publication

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, every version,
  edition or form of a publication shall be considered a distinct
  publication.

## [Obtaining Archival Quality Recordings for Preservation Purposes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345285 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing archival quality copy

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Librarian and Archivist determines that a recording that was made
  available to the public in Canada has historical or archival value, he
  or she may by a written request require any other person who is
  legally entitled to provide such a copy to provide to the Librarian
  and Archivist, in accordance with the terms specified, a copy of that
  recording in the form and quality that the Librarian and Archivist
  determines is suitable for archival purposes and specifies in the
  request.

- Definition of [[recording]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[recording]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means anything that requires a machine in order to use its content,
  whether sounds, images or other information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment for copy

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Librarian and Archivist shall reimburse the
  person, other than Her Majesty in right of Canada or one of Her
  agents, who provides a copy under subsection (1) for the actual cost
  of making that copy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Crown in right of a province

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} This section binds Her Majesty in right of a
  province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Copies provided to the Librarian and Archivist under
  this section belong to Her Majesty and form part of the collection of
  the Library and Archives of Canada.

## [Government and Ministerial Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345297 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Destruction and disposal

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  government or ministerial record, whether or not it is surplus
  property of a government institution, shall be disposed of, including
  by being destroyed, without the written consent of the Librarian and
  Archivist or of a person to whom the Librarian and Archivist has, in
  writing, delegated the power to give such consents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of access to records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite anything in any other Act of Parliament, the
  Librarian and Archivist has a right of access to any record to whose
  disposition he or she has been asked to consent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the Librarian and
  Archivist may have access to a record to which subsection 69(1) of the
  [Access to Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1) applies, only with the
  consent of the Clerk of the Privy Council and to a government record
  that contains information the disclosure of which is restricted by or
  pursuant to any provision set out in Schedule II to that Act, only
  with the consent of the head of the government institution in
  question.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to give access

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite anything in any other Act of Parliament, any
  officer or employee of a government institution may grant to the
  Librarian and Archivist access to any record to whose disposition the
  Librarian and Archivist has been asked to consent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security requirements

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Librarian and Archivist and every person acting
  on behalf or under the direction of the Librarian and Archivist shall,
  with respect to access to records, satisfy any security requirements
  applicable to, and take any oath of secrecy required to be taken by,
  persons who normally have access to those records.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of records

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  transfer to the care and control of the Librarian and Archivist of
  government or ministerial records that he or she considers to have
  historical or archival value shall be effected in accordance with any
  agreements for the transfer of records that may be made between the
  Librarian and Archivist and the government institution or person
  responsible for the records.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by regulation, prescribe
  terms and conditions governing the transfer of records under
  subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Government records at risk

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If government records referred to in subsection (1)
  are, in the opinion of the Librarian and Archivist, at risk of serious
  damage or destruction, the Librarian and Archivist may require their
  transfer in the manner and at the time that the Librarian and
  Archivist specifies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records of former government
  institutions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise directed by the Governor in
  Council, the Librarian and Archivist shall have the care and control
  of all records of a government institution whose functions have
  ceased.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Sections 12 and 13 do not
apply in respect of records that are library or museum materials kept by
a government institution for reference or exhibition purposes.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restricted access to Privy Council
confidences

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Librarian and
Archivist shall not provide access to confidences of the Queen's Privy
Council for Canada to which subsection 69(1) of the [Access to
Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1) applies without the consent of the Clerk
of the Privy Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public opinion research

**[[15.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.1 .sectionLabel}** Every department, as
defined in section 2 of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), shall, within six months after the completion of
any data collection done for the purposes of public opinion research
carried out under a contract at the request of the department and for
the exclusive use of Her Majesty in right of Canada, send to the
Librarian and Archivist the written report referred to in subsection
40(2) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

- 2006, c. 9, s. 180

## [Surplus Crown Assets Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345326 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surplus publications

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Despite the [Surplus
Crown Assets Act](/eng/acts/S-27), all publications that have become
surplus to the requirements of any government institution shall be
placed in the care or control of the Librarian and Archivist.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Surplus Crown Assets
Act](/eng/acts/S-27) not to apply

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The [Surplus Crown Assets
Act](/eng/acts/S-27) does not apply to any record or publication that is
in the care or control of the Librarian and Archivist.

## [Financial Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345331 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Account

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be an account in the accounts of Canada called the Library and
  Archives of Canada Account to which shall be credited all amounts
  received for the Library and Archives of Canada including by way of
  gift.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts payable out of Account

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any amounts required for the purposes of this Act may
  be paid out of the Library and Archives of Canada Account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of funds

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Amounts referred to in subsection (1) shall be used
  in accordance with any terms and conditions attached to them.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345339 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Production of certified copy

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), if the
  Librarian and Archivist is compelled to produce a record or
  publication that is under his or her control, the Librarian and
  Archivist may certify a copy of it and produce that copy, which is
  receivable in evidence in the same manner as the original without
  proof of the signature or official character of the person or persons
  appearing to have certified it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Production of original

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the court, tribunal or other entity under whose
  authority the Librarian and Archivist may be compelled to produce a
  record or publication is satisfied that it is necessary, taking into
  account the risks implicit in the production of the original and the
  importance of its preservation and of continued access to it, to order
  that the original of the record or publication be produced, the court,
  tribunal or other entity shall ensure that any measures that are
  required to protect and preserve it are taken and that it is returned
  to the care and control of the Librarian and Archivist as soon as it
  is no longer needed for the purposes of the matter in question.

## [Offences and Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345345 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who contravenes subsection 10(1) or the regulations or fails to
  comply with a request of the Librarian and Archivist under subsection
  11(1) is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to the fine referred
    to in subsection 787(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, to the fine referred
    to in paragraph 735(1)(b) of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No imprisonment in default of fine
  payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 787(2) of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46), a term of imprisonment may not be imposed for
  default of payment of a fine imposed under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of fines

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A fine imposed under subsection (1) is a debt payable
  to Her Majesty in right of Canada and may be recovered as such in any
  court of competent jurisdiction or by any manner provided for in any
  Act of Parliament.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345407 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No continuation in office

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  persons who were the National Archivist of Canada and the National
  Librarian immediately before the coming into force of section 55 cease
  to hold those offices on the coming into force of subsection 5(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of existing holdings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The records and publications that constituted the
  collections of the National Archives of Canada and the National
  Library immediately before the coming into force of section 55 are
  transferred to the Librarian and Archivist subject to any terms and
  conditions that were applicable to those records and publications.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of personnel

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who was, immediately before the coming
  into force of section 55, an employee employed in the National
  Archives of Canada or the National Library is an employee of the
  Library and Archives of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of funds

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} All amounts that were in the accounts of Canada
  called the National Archives of Canada Account and the National
  Library Special Operating Account immediately before the coming into
  force of section 55 are transferred to the Library and Archives of
  Canada Account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context requires otherwise, "National
  Archives of Canada" and "National Library" are replaced by "Library
  and Archives of Canada" in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any regulation, as defined in section 2 of the
    [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other instrument made

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the execution of a power conferred by or under
      an Act of Parliament, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by or under the authority of the Governor in
      Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context requires otherwise, "National
  Archivist of Canada" and "National Librarian" are replaced by
  "Librarian and Archivist of Canada" in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any regulation, as defined in section 2 of the
    [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other instrument made

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the execution of a power conferred by or under
      an Act of Parliament, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by or under the authority of the Governor in
      Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context requires otherwise, a reference in
  any contract or other document to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the "National Archives of Canada" or the "National
    Library" shall be read as a reference to the "Library and Archives
    of Canada"; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the "National Archivist of Canada" or the "National
    Librarian" shall be read as a reference to the "Librarian and
    Archivist of Canada".
