## Government Annuities Improvement Act {#government-annuities-improvement-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1974-75-76, c. 83
:::

Assented to 1975-12-20

An Act to increase the rate of return on Government Annuity contracts,
to increase their flexibility and to discontinue future sales thereof
::::

::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247907 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Government Annuities Improvement Act](/eng/acts/G-3.8).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247910 .Part}

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act, [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
  Privy Council for Canada as is designated by the Governor in Council
  to administer the [Government Annuities Act](/eng/acts/G-3.7).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Words and expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context otherwise indicates, words and
  expressions have the same meaning as in the [Government Annuities
  Act](/eng/acts/G-3.7).

## [Increased Rate of Return]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247916 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Increased interest for deferred annuities

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  determining the value of an annuity prior to the date on which it
  becomes payable, the rate or rates of interest to be used shall, for
  the period before April 1st, 1975, be the rate or rates of interest
  applicable to that annuity contract and, for the period commencing
  April 1st, 1975, shall, subject to subsection (2), be seven per cent
  per annum.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Governor in Council may vary interest
  rate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may by regulation vary, in
  respect of any annuity or category of annuities, the seven per cent
  rate of interest mentioned in subsection (1), but the rate of interest
  fixed by regulation may not, in respect of any annuity, be lower than
  the rate or rates of interest applicable to that annuity prior to
  April 1st, 1975.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of amount of annuity

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** At the date on which an
annuity becomes payable, if that date is on or after April 1st, 1975,
the amount of annuity payable shall be calculated by using the value of
that annuity determined in accordance with section 3 and the table or
tables of values prescribed under paragraph 13(b) of the [Government
Annuities Act](/eng/acts/G-3.7) applicable to that annuity contract.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annuity payments to be increased

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  amounts payable by way of an annuity on or after April 1st, 1975 shall
  be increased by the applicable percentage or percentages shown in the
  schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of schedule

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by regulation, amend the
  schedule in respect of any annuity or category of annuities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum annuity max exceed \$1,200 per
year

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection 8(1) of
  the [Government Annuities Act](/eng/acts/G-3.7), the total amount
  payable by way of an annuity or annuities to any annuitant or to joint
  annuitants may exceed twelve hundred dollars a year to the extent that
  such excess results from the application of section 3 or 5 or both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations may permit further increase
  in maximum

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make regulations for
  permitting the total amount payable by way of annuity or annuities to
  any annuitant or to joint annuitants to exceed the maximum permitted
  by subsection (1).

## [Increased Flexibility]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247934 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Variation and substitution

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, upon such
terms and conditions as are prescribed by regulation,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} vary an existing annuity contract with the consent of
  the purchaser; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} substitute a new annuity contract for an existing one
  where there is a change of purchaser.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When purchaser dies or goes out of
existence

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where an annuity contract
under the [Government Annuities Act](/eng/acts/G-3.7) does not permit
the annuitant to request changes in his annuity without the purchaser's
consent and the purchaser has died or gone out of existence, the
annuitant has the same right to request changes in his annuity as had
the purchaser.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commutation available in certain cases

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** When, under an annuity
contract under the [Government Annuities Act](/eng/acts/G-3.7), the
annuity is payable for a term of years certain or for the life of the
annuitant, whichever period is the longer, and the annuitant has died
before the expiration of that term of years certain, then,
notwithstanding subsection 12(2) of the [Government Annuities
Act](/eng/acts/G-3.7), the remaining instalments may, at the request of
the purchaser or his legal representative, be commuted into a lump sum
payment, the amount of which shall be calculated in accordance with the
regulations.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247943 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest to date of repayment in certain
cases

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The interest referred to
in subsection 12(1) of the [Government Annuities Act](/eng/acts/G-3.7)
shall accrue to the date of repayment in cases where the relevant death
occurs after the commencement of this Act, even if the annuity contract
stipulates that the interest will accrue only to the date of death.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rate of interest in certain cases

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding the
provisions of The Appropriation Act No. 4, 1950, Vote 695, and of
Appropriation Act No. 6, 1960, Vote 526, the interest mentioned in those
Votes shall, after March 31st, 1975, accrue at a rate determined from
time to time by the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transferability of benefits

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding
subsection 10(1) the [Government Annuities Act](/eng/acts/G-3.7), where
an annuity contract is a registered retirement savings plan within the
meaning of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) or is under a
registered pension fund or plan within the meaning of the [Income Tax
Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), any monies payable under that contract may, at
the request of the annuitant or, if the annuitant is dead, at the
request of the beneficiary, be paid to another registered retirement
savings plan within the meaning of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3)
or to another registered pension fund or plan within the meaning of the
[Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

## [Discontinuance of Sales of Annuities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247950 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No new contracts

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** After the commencement of
this Act, no further annuity contracts may be entered into under the
[Government Annuities Act](/eng/acts/G-3.7), except as provided by the
terms of an existing annuity contract under that Act or by paragraph
7(b) of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration deadline under group
contracts

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** No further registration
of employees under annuity contracts mentioned in subsection 6(3) of the
[Government Annuities Act](/eng/acts/G-3.7) may take place after March
31st, 1979.

## [Government Annuities Account]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247955 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of liability

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything in section
  15 of the [Government Annuities Act](/eng/acts/G-3.7), the liability
  outstanding at the end of each fiscal year in respect of annuity
  contracts shall be calculated on the basis of such rate or rates of
  interest and mortality tables, and in such manner, as is prescribed by
  regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Credits to Account

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If at the end of any fiscal year the liability
  calculated under subsection (1) exceeds the balance of the Government
  Annuities Account, there shall be credited to the Account and charged
  to the Consolidated Revenue Fund an amount equal to the excess of the
  liability over the balance of the Account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charges to Account

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If at the end of any fiscal year the liability
  calculated under subsection (1) is less than the balance of the
  Government Annuities Account, there shall be charged to the Account
  and credited to the Consolidated Revenue Fund an amount equal to the
  amount by which the balance of the Account exceeds the liability.

## [Audit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247963 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audit

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The accounts and
financial transactions arising under the [Government Annuities
Act](/eng/acts/G-3.7) and this Act shall be audited annually by the
Auditor General and a report of the audit shall be made to the Minister.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247966 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing terms and conditions for the purposes of
  section 7;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the manner in which a grant of letters
  probate, or similar document, of any court or authority outside Canada
  is to be proved for the purposes of the [Government Annuities
  Act](/eng/acts/G-3.7);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the manner in which lump sum payments
  mentioned in section 9 are to be calculated; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the manner in which the liability
  outstanding at the end of each fiscal year in respect of annuity
  contracts is to be calculated, and the rate or rates of interest and
  mortality tables to be used in that regard.

## [Report to Parliament]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247973 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report to Parliament

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  nine months after the end of each fiscal year, the Minister shall
  prepare a report on all business done under the [Government Annuities
  Act](/eng/acts/G-3.7) and this Act during that fiscal year and shall
  lay the report before Parliament within fifteen days after it bas been
  prepared or, if Parliament is not then sitting, within the first
  fifteen days next thereafter that Parliament is sitting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repeal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Section 16 of the [Government Annuities
  Act](/eng/acts/G-3.7) is repealed.
