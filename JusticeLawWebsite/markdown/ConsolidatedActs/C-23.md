## Canadian Security Intelligence Service Act {#canadian-security-intelligence-service-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. C-23
:::

An Act to establish the Canadian Security Intelligence Service
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
:::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the protection of Canada's national security and of the security
of Canadians is a fundamental responsibility of the Government of
Canada;

Whereas it is essential, to discharge that responsibility, for Canada to
have a civilian intelligence service;

Whereas it is important that the civilian intelligence service perform
its duties and functions in accordance with the rule of law and in a
manner that respects the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms;

And whereas the Government of Canada, by carrying out its national
security and information activities in a manner that respects rights and
freedoms, encourages the international community to do the same;

::: HistoricalNote
- 2019, c. 13, s. 92

### [Establishment of Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-76189 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of Service

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Canadian Security Intelligence Service is hereby established,
  consisting of the Director and employees of the Service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principal office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The principal office of the Service shall be in the
  National Capital Region described in the schedule to the [National
  Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other offices

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Director may, with the approval of the Minister,
  establish other offices of the Service elsewhere in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1984, c. 21, s. 3
:::

### [Director]{.HTitleText2} {#h-76199 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall appoint the Director of the Service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Director shall be appointed to hold office during
  pleasure for a term not exceeding five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), the Director is eligible,
  on the expiration of a first or any subsequent term of office, to be
  re-appointed for a further term not exceeding five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall hold office as Director for terms
  exceeding ten years in the aggregate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  Director, or if the office of Director is vacant, the Governor in
  Council may appoint another person to hold office instead of the
  Director for a term not exceeding six months, and that person shall,
  while holding that office, have all of the powers, duties and
  functions of the Director under this Act or any other Act of
  Parliament and be paid such salary or other remuneration and expenses
  as may be fixed by the Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1984, c. 21, s. 4

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  in Council and shall be paid reasonable travel and living expenses

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pension benefits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The provisions of the [Public Service Superannuation

### [Management of Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-76220 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role of Director

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Director, under the direction of the Minister, has the control and
  management of the Service and all matters connected therewith.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may issue directions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In providing the direction referred to in subsection
  (1), the Minister may issue to the Director written directions with
  respect to the Service and a copy of any such direction shall,
  forthwith after it is issued, be given to the Review Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions deemed not to be statutory
  instruments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Directions issued by the Minister under
  subsection (2) shall be deemed not to be statutory instruments for the
  purposes of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Periodic reports by Director

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Director shall, in relation to every 12-month
  period or any lesser period that is specified by the Minister, submit
  to the Minister, at any times that the Minister specifies, reports
  with respect to the Service's operational activities during that
  period, and shall cause the Review Agency to be given a copy of each
  such report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures to reduce threats to the
  security of Canada

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The reports shall include, among other things, the
  following information in respect of the Service's operational
  activities, during the period for which the report is made, to reduce
  threats to the security of Canada:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each of the paragraphs of the definition
    [[threats to the security of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section
    2, a general description of the measures that were taken during the
    period in respect of the threat within the meaning of that paragraph
    and the number of those measures;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of warrants issued under subsection
    21.1(3) during the period and the number of applications for
    warrants made under subsection 21.1(1) that were refused during the
    period; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for each threat to the security of Canada for which
    warrants have been issued under subsection 21.1(3) before or during
    the period, a general description of the measures that were taken
    under the warrants during the period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Justification

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The reports shall also include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a general description of the information and
    intelligence collection activities in the context of which employees
    designated under subsection 20.1(6) or (8) committed acts or
    omissions that would otherwise constitute offences;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a general description of those acts or omissions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the information referred to in paragraphs
    20.1(24)(a) to (e); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} information on the training received by employees
    designated under subsection 20.1(6) or (8) and by senior employees
    designated under subsection 20.1(7).

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a Canadian dataset, a designated

  section 11.08.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} During the evaluation period referred to in

#### [Threats to the Security of Canada]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1479408 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures to reduce threats to the
security of Canada

- **[[12.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  there are reasonable grounds to believe that a particular activity
  constitutes a threat to the security of Canada, the Service may take
  measures, within or outside Canada, to reduce the threat.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The measures shall be reasonable and proportional in
  the circumstances, having regard to the nature of the threat, the
  nature of the measures, the reasonable availability of other means to
  reduce the threat and the reasonably foreseeable effects on third
  parties, including on their right to privacy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternatives

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before taking measures under subsection (1), the
  Service shall consult, as appropriate, with other federal departments
  or agencies as to whether they are in a position to reduce the threat.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms

  [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} The Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms is part
  of the supreme law of Canada and all measures taken by the Service
  under subsection (1) shall comply with it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant --- Canadian Charter of Rights
  and Freedoms

  [(3.2)]{.lawlabel} The Service may take measures under subsection (1)
  that would limit a right or freedom guaranteed by the Canadian Charter
  of Rights and Freedoms only if a judge, on an application made under
  section 21.1, issues a warrant authorizing the taking of those
  measures.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition for issuance

  [(3.3)]{.lawlabel} The judge may issue the warrant referred to in
  subsection (3.2) only if the judge is satisfied that the measures, as
  authorized by the warrant, comply with the Canadian Charter of Rights
  and Freedoms.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant --- Canadian law

  [(3.4)]{.lawlabel} The Service may take measures under subsection (1)
  that would otherwise be contrary to Canadian law only if the measures
  have been authorized by a warrant issued under section 21.1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of Review Agency

  [(3.5)]{.lawlabel} The Service shall, after taking measures under
  subsection (1), notify the Review Agency of the measures as soon as
  the circumstances permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, nothing in subsection (1)
  confers on the Service any law enforcement power.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited conduct

- **[[12.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  taking measures to reduce a threat to the security of Canada, the
  Service shall not

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of section --- human sources

- **[[18.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of this section is to
  ensure that the identity of human sources is kept confidential in
  order to protect their life and security and to encourage individuals
  to provide information to the Service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition on disclosure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (3) and (8), no person shall,
  in a proceeding before a court, person or body with jurisdiction to
  compel the production of information, disclose the identity of a human
  source or any information from which the identity of a human source
  could be inferred.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- consent

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The identity of a human source or information from
  which the identity of a human source could be inferred may be
  disclosed in a proceeding referred to in subsection (2) if the human
  source and the Director consent to the disclosure of that information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to judge

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A party to a proceeding referred to in subsection
  (2), an [amicus curiae]{.otherLang lang="la"} who is appointed in
  respect of the proceeding or a person who is appointed to act as a
  special advocate if the proceeding is under the [Immigration and
  Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) may apply to a judge for one
  of the following orders if it is relevant to the proceeding:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an order declaring that an individual is not a
    human source or that information is not information from which the
    identity of a human source could be inferred; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the proceeding is a prosecution of an offence,
    an order declaring that the disclosure of the identity of a human
    source or information from which the identity of a human source
    could be inferred is essential to establish the accused's innocence
    and that it may be disclosed in the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents and service of application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The application and the applicant's affidavit
  deposing to the facts relied on in support of the application shall be
  filed in the Registry of the Federal Court. The applicant shall,
  without delay after the application and affidavit are filed, serve a
  copy of them on the Attorney General of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attorney General of Canada

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Once served, the Attorney General of Canada is deemed
  to be a party to the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The hearing of the application shall be held in
  private and in the absence of the applicant and their counsel, unless
  the judge orders otherwise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order --- disclosure to establish
  innocence

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If the judge grants an application made under

  considers appropriate subject to any conditions that the judge

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date of order

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} If the judge grants an application made under

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} The judge shall ensure the confidentiality of the

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identity of any human source and any
    information from which the identity of a human source could be
    inferred; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information and other evidence provided in respect
    of the application if, in the judge's opinion, its disclosure would
    be injurious to national security or endanger the safety of any
    person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality on appeal

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an appeal, subsection (10) applies,
  with any necessary modifications, to the court to which the appeal is

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[20.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in
  this section.

  [[designated employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee who
      is designated under subsection (6) or (8). ([employé
      désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated senior employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated senior employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      employee who is designated under subsection (7). ([employé
      supérieur désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principle --- justification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is in the public interest to ensure that employees
  effectively carry out the Service's information and intelligence
  collection duties and functions, including by engaging in covert
  activities, in accordance with the rule of law and, to that end, to
  expressly recognize in law a limited justification for designated
  employees acting in good faith and persons acting under their
  direction to commit acts or omissions that would otherwise constitute
  offences.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classes of acts or omissions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} At least once every year, the Minister shall, by
  order, determine the classes of acts or omissions that would otherwise
  constitute offences and that designated employees may be justified in
  committing or directing another person to commit if the Minister
  concludes that the commission of those acts or omissions is
  reasonable, having regard to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Service's information and intelligence
    collection duties and functions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any threats to the security of Canada that may be
    the object of information and intelligence collection activities or
    any objectives to be achieved by such activities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An order made under subsection (3) is not a
  [[statutory instrument]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} within the meaning of
  the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of Commissioner

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall notify the Commissioner of the
  Minister's determination under subsection (3) for the purposes of the
  Commissioner's review and approval under the [Intelligence
  Commissioner Act](/eng/acts/I-14.85).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of employees

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may personally, on the recommendation of
  the Director and for a period of not more than one year, designate,
  for the purpose of this section, employees who perform information and
  intelligence collection activities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of senior employees

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may personally, on the recommendation of
  the Director, designate, for the purpose of this section, senior
  employees who are responsible for information and intelligence
  collection activities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergency designation

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The Director or a designated senior employee may, for
  a period of not more than 48 hours, designate, for the purpose of this
  section, an employee who performs information and intelligence
  collection activities if the Director or the designated senior
  employee is of the opinion that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by reason of exigent circumstances, it is not
    feasible for the Minister to designate the employee under subsection
    (6); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the employee would be justified in committing or
    directing another person to commit an act or omission that would
    otherwise constitute an offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of Minister

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The Director or the designated senior employee, as
  the case may be, shall notify the Minister of the designation as soon
  as the circumstances permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} A designation under subsection (6) or (8) may be
  made subject to conditions, including conditions limiting

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its duration;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the information and intelligence
    collection activities in the context of which a designated employee
    may be justified in committing, or directing another person to
    commit, an act or omission that would otherwise constitute an
    offence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the act or omission that would otherwise constitute
    an offence and that a designated employee may be justified in
    committing or directing another person to commit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Justification for acts or omissions

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (15), a designated employee is
  justified in committing an act or omission that would otherwise
  constitute an offence if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the designated employee

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is engaged, in good faith, in an information and
      intelligence collection activity in relation to a threat to the
      security of Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} believes on reasonable grounds that the
      commission of the act or omission, as compared to the threat, is
      reasonable and proportional in the circumstances, having regard to
      such matters as the nature of the threat, the nature of the act or
      omission and the reasonable availability of other means for
      performing the information and intelligence collection activity;
      or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the designated employee

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is engaged, in good faith, in an information and
      intelligence collection activity under section 16, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} believes on reasonable grounds that the
      commission of the act or omission is reasonable and proportional
      in the circumstances, having regard to such matters as the nature
      of the act or omission, the nature of the objective to be achieved
      and the reasonable availability of other means to achieve the
      objective.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} The Director or a designated senior employee may, in
  writing, authorize designated employees, for a period of not more than
  one year, to direct the commission of acts or omissions that would
  otherwise constitute offences if the Director or the designated senior
  employee believes on reasonable grounds

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an activity described in
    subparagraph (11)(a)(i), that the commission of the acts or
    omissions, as compared to the threat to the security of Canada to
    which the activity relates, is reasonable and proportional in the
    circumstances, having regard to such matters as the nature of the
    threat, the nature of the acts or omissions and the reasonable
    availability of other means for performing the activity; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an activity described in
    subparagraph (11)(b)(i), that the commission of the acts or
    omissions is reasonable and proportional in the circumstances,
    having regard to such matters as the nature of the acts or
    omissions, the nature of the objective to be achieved and the
    reasonable availability of other means to achieve the objective.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(13)]{.lawlabel} The authorization shall specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the acts or omissions that would otherwise
    constitute offences;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the designated employees who are authorized to
    direct the commission of those acts or omissions; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the persons who may be directed to commit those
    acts or omissions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment or cancellation

  [(14)]{.lawlabel} The authorization may be amended or cancelled by the
  Director or a designated senior employee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Justification for directing

  [(15)]{.lawlabel} A designated employee is only justified in directing
  the commission of an act or omission that would otherwise constitute
  an offence if the conditions in paragraph (11)(a) or (b) are met and
  the designated employee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is personally authorized to direct the commission
    of the act or omission under subsection (12); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} believes on reasonable grounds that grounds for
    obtaining an authorization under that subsection exist but it is not
    feasible in the circumstances to obtain the authorization and that
    the act or omission is necessary to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} preserve the life or safety of any individual,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} prevent the compromise of the identity of an
      employee acting covertly, of a human source or of a person acting
      covertly under the direction of an employee, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} prevent the imminent loss or destruction of
      information or intelligence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person acting under direction of
  designated employee

  [(16)]{.lawlabel} A person, other than an employee, is justified in
  committing an act or omission that would otherwise constitute an
  offence if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are directed by a designated employee to
    commit the act or omission; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they believe on reasonable grounds that the person
    who directs them to commit the act or omission has the authority to
    give that direction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classes of acts or omissions

  [(17)]{.lawlabel} A designated employee is only justified in
  committing, or directing another person to commit, an act or omission
  that would otherwise constitute an offence if the act or omission
  falls within a class of acts or omissions the determination of which
  is approved by the Commissioner under the [Intelligence Commissioner
  Act](/eng/acts/I-14.85).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(18)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section justifies

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} causing, intentionally or by criminal negligence,
    death or bodily harm to an individual;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} wilfully attempting in any manner to obstruct,
    pervert or defeat the course of justice;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} violating the sexual integrity of an individual;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} subjecting an individual to torture or cruel,
    inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment, within the meaning of
    the Convention Against Torture;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} detaining an individual; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} causing the loss of, or any serious damage to, any
    property if doing so would endanger the safety of an individual.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial direction

  [(19)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section justifies the commission of
  an act or omission specified in a direction issued by the Minister for
  the purpose of this section, under subsection 6(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection, defences and immunities
  unaffected

  [(20)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section affects the protection,
  defences and immunities of employees and other persons recognized
  under the law of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to obtain warrant

  [(21)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section relieves the Director or an
  employee from the requirement to obtain a warrant in accordance with
  section 21.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms

  [(22)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section justifies the commission of
  an act or omission that would infringe a right or freedom guaranteed
  by the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report by designated employee

  [(23)]{.lawlabel} A designated employee who commits an act or omission
  in accordance with subsection (11) or who directs the commission of an
  act or omission in accordance with subsection (15) shall, as soon as
  the circumstances permit after doing so, submit a written report to
  the Director or a designated senior employee describing the act or
  omission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

  [(24)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, every year, publish or otherwise
  make available to the public a report in respect of the previous year
  that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of designations that were made under
    subsection (8);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of authorizations that were given under
    subsection (12);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number of times that designated employees
    directed the commission of acts or omissions in accordance with
    paragraph (15)(b);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the threats to the security of Canada
    that were the object of the information and intelligence collection
    activities in the context of which the designations referred to in
    paragraph (a) were made, the authorizations referred to in
    paragraph (b) were given or the acts or omissions referred to in
    paragraph (c) were directed to be committed; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the acts or omissions that were
    committed under the designations referred to in paragraph (a) or
    that were directed to be committed under the authorizations referred
    to in paragraph (b) or in accordance with paragraph (15)(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(25)]{.lawlabel} The report is not to contain any information the
  disclosure of which would

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} compromise or hinder an ongoing information and
    intelligence collection activity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} compromise the identity of an employee acting
    covertly, of a human source or of a person acting covertly under the
    direction of an employee;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} endanger the life or safety of any individual;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prejudice a legal proceeding; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be contrary to the public interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of Review Agency

  [(26)]{.lawlabel} The Service shall notify the Review Agency as soon
  as the circumstances permit after

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a designation is made under subsection (8);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an authorization is given under subsection (12); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a written report is submitted under subsection
    (23).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(27)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, designations, authorizations
  and directions referred to in this section are not statutory
  instruments within the meaning of the [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preservation order

- **[[20.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20.3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Director or any employee who is
  designated by the Director for the purpose may make an application to
  a judge for a preservation order under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Making of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite any other law but subject to the [Statistics
  Act](/eng/acts/S-19), the judge may order a person or entity to
  preserve any information, record or document --- regardless of medium
  or form --- or thing that is in their possession or control when they
  receive the order, if the judge is satisfied by information on oath in
  Form 1 of Schedule 2 that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there are reasonable grounds to suspect that the
    information, record, document or thing is in the person or entity's
    possession or control and will assist the Service to investigate,
    within or outside Canada, a threat to the security of Canada or to
    perform its duties and functions under section 16;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the order is necessary to prevent the loss or
    destruction or ensure the preservation of the information, record,
    document or thing; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Director or an employee who is designated by
    the Director for the purpose intends to apply or has applied for a
    production order under section 20.4 or a warrant under section 21 or
    22.21 to obtain the information, record, document or thing or, under
    section 23, to remove a thing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form 2

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The order is to be made in Form 2 of Schedule 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preservation outside Canada

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The order may be made in respect of information,
  records, documents or things located outside Canada, with any
  modifications that the circumstances require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The judge may include in the order any measure that
  they consider necessary in the public interest, including to ensure
  the confidentiality of the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expiry of order

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The order expires 90 days after the day on which it
  is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of Minister

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Director shall notify the Minister that an
  application for an order has been made under this section as soon as
  feasible after the application is made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for warrant --- measures to
reduce threats to security of Canada

- **[[21.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the Director or any employee who is designated by the Minister for the
  purpose believes on reasonable grounds that a warrant under this
  section is required to enable the Service to take measures referred to
  in subsection (1.1), within or outside Canada, to reduce a threat to
  the security of Canada, the Director or employee may, after having
  obtained the Minister's approval, make an application in accordance
  with subsection (2) to a judge for a warrant under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), the measures are
  the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} altering, removing, replacing, destroying,
    disrupting or degrading a communication or means of communication;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} altering, removing, replacing, destroying,
    degrading or providing --- or interfering with the use or delivery
    of --- any thing or part of a thing, including records, documents,
    goods, components and equipment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fabricating or disseminating any information,
    record or document;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} making or attempting to make, directly or
    indirectly, any financial transaction that involves or purports to
    involve currency or a monetary instrument;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} interrupting or redirecting, directly or
    indirectly, any financial transaction that involves currency or a
    monetary instrument;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} interfering with the movement of any person,
    excluding the detention of an individual; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} personating a person, other than a police officer,
    in order to take a measure referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to
    (f).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matters to be specified in application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application to a judge under subsection (1) shall
  be made in writing and be accompanied by the applicant's affidavit
  deposing to the following matters:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the facts relied on to justify the belief on
    reasonable grounds that a warrant under this section is required to
    enable the Service to take measures to reduce a threat to the
    security of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the measures proposed to be taken;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reasonableness and proportionality, in the
    circumstances, of the proposed measures, having regard to the nature
    of the threat, the nature of the measures, the reasonable
    availability of other means to reduce the threat and the reasonably
    foreseeable effects on third parties, including on their right to
    privacy;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the persons, if known, who are
    directly affected by the proposed measures;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the persons or classes of persons to whom the
    warrant is proposed to be directed;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a general description of the place where the
    warrant is proposed to be executed, if a general description of that
    place can be given;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the period, not exceeding 60 days or 120 days, as
    the case may be, for which the warrant is requested to be in force
    that is applicable by virtue of subsection (6); and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any previous application made under subsection (1)
    in relation to a person who is identified in the affidavit in
    accordance with paragraph (d), the date on which each such
    application was made, the name of the judge to whom it was made and
    the judge's decision on it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of warrant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite any other law but subject to the [Statistics
  Act](/eng/acts/S-19), if the judge to whom an application under
  subsection (1) is made is satisfied of the matters referred to in
  paragraphs (2)(a) and (c) that are set out in the affidavit
  accompanying the application, the judge may issue a warrant
  authorizing the persons to whom it is directed to take the measures
  specified in it and, for that purpose,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to enter any place or open or obtain access to any
    thing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to search for, remove or return, or examine, take
    extracts from or make copies of or record in any other manner the
    information, record, document or thing;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to install, maintain or remove any thing; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to do any other thing that is reasonably necessary
    to take those measures.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures taken outside Canada

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Without regard to any other law, including that of
  any foreign state, a judge may, in a warrant issued under subsection
  (3), authorize the measures specified in it to be taken outside
  Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matters to be specified in warrant

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} There shall be specified in a warrant issued under
  subsection (3)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the measures authorized to be taken;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the persons, if known, who are
    directly affected by the measures;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the persons or classes of persons to whom the
    warrant is directed;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a general description of the place where the
    warrant may be executed, if a general description of that place can
    be given;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the period for which the warrant is in force; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any terms and conditions that the judge considers
    advisable in the public interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum duration of warrant

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A warrant shall not be issued under subsection (3)
  for a period exceeding

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 60 days if the warrant is issued to enable the
    Service to take measures to reduce a threat to the security of
    Canada within the meaning of paragraph (d) of the definition
    [[threats to the security of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section
    2; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 120 days in any other case.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Renewal of warrant --- measures to reduce
threats to the security of Canada

- **[[22.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  application in writing to a judge for the renewal of a warrant issued
  under subsection 21.1(3) made by a person who is entitled, after
  having obtained the Minister's approval, to apply for such a warrant
  and who believes on reasonable grounds that the warrant continues to
  be required to enable the Service to take the measures specified in it
  to reduce a threat to the security of Canada, the judge may renew the
  warrant if the judge is satisfied by evidence on oath of the following
  matters:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the facts relied on to justify the belief on
    reasonable grounds that the warrant continues to be required to
    enable the Service to take the measures specified in it to reduce a
    threat to the security of Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the continued reasonableness and proportionality,
    in the circumstances, of the measures specified in the warrant,
    having regard to the nature of the threat, the nature of the
    measures, the reasonable availability of other means to reduce the
    threat and the reasonably foreseeable effects on third parties,
    including on their right to privacy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limits on renewal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A warrant issued under subsection 21.1(3) may be
  renewed only twice, with each renewal being for a period not exceeding
  the period for which it may be issued under subsection 21.1(6).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for warrant to obtain

- **[[22.21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the Director or any employee who
  is designated by the Minister for the purpose believes, on reasonable
  grounds, that a warrant under this section is required to enable the
  Service to obtain any information, record or document --- regardless
  of medium or form --- or thing that will assist the Service to

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application to a judge under subsection (1) shall

    enable the Service to obtain any information, record or document ---
    regardless of medium or form --- or thing that will assist the
    Service to investigate, within or outside Canada, a threat to the
    security of Canada or to perform its duties and functions under
    section 16;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the type of information, record, document or thing
    proposed to be obtained;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the powers referred to in paragraphs (3)(a) to (c)
    proposed to be exercised;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the person, if known, who has
    possession of the information, record, document or thing proposed to
    be obtained;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the persons or classes of persons to whom the
    warrant is proposed to be directed;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a general description of the place where the
    warrant is proposed to be executed, if a general description of that
    place can be given;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the period, not exceeding 120 days, for which the
    warrant is requested to be in force; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any previous application made under subsection (1)

    the judge's decision on it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of warrant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite any other law but subject to the [Statistics
  Act](/eng/acts/S-19), if the judge to whom an application under
  subsection (1) is made is satisfied of the matters referred to in
  paragraph (2)(a) set out in the affidavit accompanying the
  application, the judge may issue a warrant authorizing the persons to
  whom it is directed to obtain, through a single attempt, any
  information, record or document --- regardless of medium or form ---
  or thing and, for that purpose,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to enter any place or open or obtain access to any
    thing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to search for, remove or return, or examine, take
    extracts from or make copies of or record in any other manner the
    information, record, document or thing; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to install, maintain or remove any thing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activities outside Canada

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Without regard to any other law, including that of
  any foreign state, a judge may, in a warrant issued under subsection
  (3), authorize activities outside Canada to enable the Service to

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obtaining information, records or

  within Canada, any information, record or document --- regardless of
  medium or form --- that is located outside Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matters to be specified in warrant

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} There shall be specified in a warrant issued under
  subsection (3)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the type of information, record, document or thing

    (3)(a) to (c) authorized to be exercised for that purpose;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the person, if known, who has

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the persons or classes of persons to whom the

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a general description of the place where the

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the period for which the warrant is in force in

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any terms and conditions that the judge considers

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of warrant

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A warrant issued under subsection (3) ceases to have

  begins on the day on which the warrant is issued and the day on which

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification --- other warrants

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Parliamentary Review]{.HTitleText1} {#h-76568 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Five-year review

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** As soon as feasible after
the fifth anniversary of the day on which this section comes into force
and after each subsequent fifth anniversary, a review of this Act and of
its administration and operation is to be undertaken by a committee of
the Senate, of the House of Commons or of both Houses of Parliament that
may be designated or established for that purpose.
