[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Board of Directors]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board of Directors]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
    [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Board of Directors of the
    Corporation referred to in subsection
    6(1); ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

:   [[Chairman]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2006, c. 9, s.
    231\]]{.Repealed}

[[Chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chairperson of the
    Board, appointed pursuant to subsection 6(2);  ([Version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

:   [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Mortgage and
    Housing Corporation established by section
    3; ([Société]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of the Board of
    Directors; ([administrateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

:   [[Executive Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Executive
    Committee of the Board established by section 9; ([comité de

    Act](/eng/acts/N-11), the [National Housing Act](/eng/acts/N-11),
    chapter N-10 of the Revised Statutes of Canada, 1970, the National
    Housing Act, 1954, and the [National Housing Act](/eng/acts/N-11),

    Corporation, appointed pursuant to subsection
    7(1). ([président]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Constitution of the Corporation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-165175 .Part}

### [Establishment of the Corporation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165176 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation established

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby established
a corporation to be called the "Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation"
consisting of the Minister and those persons who compose the Board of
Directors.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-16, s. 3
- 1978-79, c. 16, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the
Corporation shall be in the city of Ottawa.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-16, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agent of Her Majesty

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as provided in section 13, the Corporation is for all purposes an
  agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation shall comply with any directions
  given to it from time to time by the Minister respecting the exercise
  or performance of its powers, duties and functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicable provisions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 89(2) to (6) and section 153 of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) apply, with such
  modifications as the circumstances require, to a direction given under
  subsection (2) as though it were a directive referred to in those
  provisions.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-16, s. 5
- 1984, c. 31, s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board of Directors

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board of Directors shall consist of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Chairperson;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the President;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister's Deputy Minister;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Deputy Minister of Finance; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} eight other directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (4) to (6) do not apply to the
  directors referred to in paragraphs (1)(c) and (d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternate director

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} A director referred to in paragraph (1)(c) or (d)
  may designate an alternate to attend in the director's absence at any
  meeting of the Board of Directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Chairperson of the Board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson shall be appointed by the Governor in
  Council to hold office during pleasure for such term as the Governor
  in Council considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson shall perform such duties and carry
  out such functions as may be assigned to that office by the by-laws of
  the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of directors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Each director, other than the Chairperson and the
  President, shall be appointed by the Minister, with the approval of
  the Governor in Council, to hold office during pleasure for a term not
  exceeding four years that will ensure, as far as possible, the
  expiration in any one year of the terms of office of not more than one
  half of the directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancy

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the office of a director becomes vacant during
  the term of the director appointed thereto, the Governor in Council
  shall appoint a director for the remainder of that term.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Substitute directors

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where a director is a member of the federal public
  administration, the Governor in Council may authorize another member
  of the federal public administration to act as director in the
  director's stead and that member of the federal public administration
  while so acting is deemed to be a director.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors' fees

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Any director appointed from outside the federal
  public administration is entitled to receive for attendance at
  directors' meetings and Executive Committee meetings the fees that may
  be fixed by the Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote

- 1999, c. 27, s. 25

- 2012, c. 19, s. 360

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligibility

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall be appointed or continue as President or as a director
  from outside the federal public administration if that person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a director, officer or employee of a lending
    institution;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not a Canadian citizen or otherwise a British
    subject ordinarily resident in Canada; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2012, c. 31, s. 315\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is employed in any capacity in the federal public
    administration or the public service of a province or holds any
    office or position for which a salary is payable out of public
    moneys, but nothing in this paragraph prohibits such a person from
    holding office while performing temporary services for the
    Government of Canada or of a province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shareholder in lending institution

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person appointed as President or as a director from
  outside the federal public administration who is a shareholder of a
  lending institution shall divest themselves of ownership of their
  shares of the lending institution within three months after the date
  of their appointment and afterwards shall not during their term of
  office have an interest or right either directly or indirectly as a
  shareholder in a lending institution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to comply

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person appointed as President or as a director from
  outside the federal public administration who fails to comply with
  subsection (2) ceases to hold office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply to an interest arising
  out of the lawful investment of all or part of a pension fund
  established under subsection 13(3).

::: HistoricalNote

- 2003, c. 22, ss. 116(E), 224(E)

### [Conduct of Business of Corporation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165249 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board to manage affairs of Corporation

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall manage the affairs of the Corporation and conduct its
  business and may for such purposes exercise all powers of the
  Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voting

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each director has one vote in the transaction of the
  business of the Board and if the number of votes is equally divided
  the Chairperson of the Board has an additional vote.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board may make by-laws with respect to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the calling of meetings of the Board and of the
    Executive Committee, the number of persons that shall constitute a
    quorum in each case, and the manner in which questions considered at
    such meetings shall be determined; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the conduct of the affairs of the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, all by-laws relating to the
  pension fund referred to in subsection 13(3) are deemed to be or to
  have been by-laws made with respect to the conduct of the affairs of
  the Corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-7, s. 10
- 2000, c. 12, s. 41
- 2006, c. 9, s. 233(E)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Executive Committee powers

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Executive Committee
may exercise the powers of the Board and shall submit at each meeting of
the Board minutes of its proceedings since the last preceding meeting of
the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President is the chief executive officer of the Corporation and has on
  behalf of the Board the direction and control of the business of the
  Corporation with authority to act in the conduct of the business of
  the Corporation in all matters that are not by this Act or by the
  by-laws of the Corporation specifically reserved to the Board, the
  Executive Committee or the Chairperson of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity of President

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  President, or if the office of President is vacant, the director or
  officer of the Corporation that the Board designates has and may
  exercise and perform all the powers and functions of the President,
  including those as a member of the Board and of the Executive
  Committee.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-7, s. 12
- 1999, c. 27, s. 28
- 2006, c. 9, s. 233(E)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers and employees

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation may on its own behalf employ such officers and employees
  for such purposes and on such terms and conditions as may be
  determined by the Executive Committee and such officers and employees
  are not officers or servants of Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oath of office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each officer or employee employed by the Corporation
  shall, before entering on his duties, take, before a justice of the
  peace or a commissioner for oaths, an oath of fidelity and secrecy in
  the form set out in the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pension fund

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board may establish a pension fund for the
  officers and employees of the Corporation and their dependants and may
  contribute to it out of the funds of the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized investments

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The pension fund established under subsection (3)
  shall be invested in securities in which the funds of a pension plan
  may be invested under the [Pension Benefits Standards Act,
  1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-7, s. 13
- R.S., 1985, c. 32 (2nd Supp.), s. 41
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Branches and agents

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation may
establish branches and employ agents.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-7, s. 14
- 1999, c. 27, s. 29

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President's authority

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The President may
authorize any officer, agent or employee of the Corporation to act in
the conduct of the business of the Corporation in all matters that are
not by this Act or by the by-laws of the Corporation specifically
reserved to the Board, the Executive Committee or the Chairperson of the
Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-7, s. 15
- 2006, c. 9, s. 233(E)

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Objects and Powers of the Corporation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-165298 .Part}

### [Business and Powers of the Corporation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165299 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of authority from Housing Acts

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 33 of
this Act and to section 100 of the [National Housing
Act](/eng/acts/N-11), the Corporation shall, on behalf of Her Majesty
and in the place of the Minister, have, exercise and perform all rights,
powers, duties, liabilities and functions of the Minister under the
Housing Acts or under any contract entered into under those Acts, except
the authority of the Minister under those Acts to pay money out of the
Consolidated Revenue Fund.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-7, s. 17
- 1999, c. 27, s. 31
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Functions vested in Corporation

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Wherever in any contract,
agreement, guarantee or mortgage made or entered into under The Dominion
Housing Act, 1935, The Home Improvement Loans Guarantee Act, 1937, The
National Housing Act, 1938, or under The National Housing Act, 1944
prior to January 1, 1946, it is provided that any right, power, duty,
liability or function is vested in or to be exercised or performed by
the Minister of Finance or by any officer of the Department of Finance
or by any person acting on behalf of the Minister of Finance, that
right, power, duty, liability or function shall be vested in and
exercised or performed by the Corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-16, s. 19
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation may make loans and payments

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation may, in
the place of the Minister, make, out of moneys received by it under this
Act or otherwise made available to it by Parliament, all loans or
payments that Her Majesty or the Minister may make or is liable to make
under or pursuant to the Housing Acts.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-16, s. 20
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Moneys owing to the Crown

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  moneys owing to Her Majesty under or pursuant to the Housing Acts
  shall be paid to the Corporation and payment thereof to the
  Corporation is payment to Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Moneys paid into C.R.F.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where any moneys owing to Her Majesty under or
  pursuant to the Housing Acts are paid to the Receiver General or to
  any other person on behalf of Her Majesty or are in any way paid into
  the Consolidated Revenue Fund, those moneys shall be paid to the
  Corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-16, s. 21
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loans to the Corporation

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  request of the Corporation, the Minister of Finance may, out of the
  Consolidated Revenue Fund, lend money to the Corporation on any terms
  and conditions that that Minister may fix.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Borrowing otherwise than from the Crown

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may borrow money otherwise than from
  the Crown and is hereby authorized to so borrow money in amounts such
  that the total indebtedness outstanding at any time in respect of such
  borrowings does not exceed the aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fifteen billion dollars, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any additional amounts authorized by Parliament for
    the purposes of this subsection under an appropriation Act or any
    other Act of Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuing obligations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may issue or reissue, sell or pledge
  debt securities or obligations, secured or unsecured, of the
  Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Apportioning costs of borrowing

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of determining the costs of its
  activities, the Corporation may apportion the costs related to its
  borrowings as it considers appropriate.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-7, s. 21
- 1992, c. 32, s. 1
- 1999, c. 27, s. 32
- 2007, c. 29, s. 90
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reimbursement for losses, payments and
expenditures

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall
reimburse the Corporation

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for losses sustained by it in respect of Her
  Majesty's share of loans made by Her Majesty jointly with any other
  person, or in respect of loans made by Her Majesty alone, under the
  Housing Acts, out of moneys appropriated under those Acts for the
  making of those loans by the Minister;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for payments made by it to discharge liabilities of
  Her Majesty under any contract, agreement or guarantee made or entered
  into under the Housing Acts, out of moneys appropriated for the
  payment by the Minister of such liabilities under those Acts; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for expenditures incurred by the Corporation under
  any contract not referred to in paragraph (a) or (b) entered into
  under the Housing Acts, out of moneys appropriated for the payment of
  those expenditures by the Minister under those Acts or otherwise
  appropriated by Parliament therefor.

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, R.S., 1985, c. 25 (4th Supp.), s.
  33\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-7, s. 23
- R.S., 1985, c. 25 (4th Supp.), s. 33

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Research and information

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation shall
conduct research into the business of lending money on the security of
mortgages and for that purpose shall compile information relating
thereto.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-16, s. 26
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement with lending institution

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation may enter
into an agreement with a lending institution under which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lending institution agrees to furnish the
  Corporation with information in respect of interest rates, losses on
  loans secured by mortgages, costs of construction and of land,
  comparative rentals, availability of money for loans on the security
  of mortgages in different parts of Canada, costs of operation of the
  business of lending money on the security of mortgages, methods of
  appraising real property and such other information as may assist the
  Corporation in research into the conduct of the business of lending
  money on the security of mortgages; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Corporation agrees to furnish the lending
  institution with information and material relating to the lending of
  money on the security of mortgages.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-16, s. 27
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers under agreement with lending
institution

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Corporation has entered into an agreement with a lending
  institution under section 26, the Corporation may out of its capital
  and reserve fund established under section 29 or out of such other
  moneys as may be appropriated by Parliament for that purpose

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} purchase all right or interest of the lending
    institution in a mortgage and take an assignment of the mortgage;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the lending institution is a trust or loan
    company,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} make loans to the company on such terms and
      conditions, including the rate of interest, as the Board may
      determine, on the security of an assignment of or an agreement to
      assign mortgages held by the company, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} buy the debentures or other evidences of
      indebtedness, guaranteed investment receipts or guaranteed
      investment certificates of the company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale of mortgages by Corporation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may sell to any person all right or
  interest of the Corporation in any mortgage held by it as a
  consequence of a transaction made pursuant to subsection (1) and
  assign the mortgage to that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acquisition and disposal of collateral
  security

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may acquire, hold and dispose of
  collateral security for the repayment of loans or the payment of
  debentures, other evidences of indebtedness, guaranteed investment
  receipts or guaranteed investment certificates, made or purchased by
  the Corporation under this section.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-16, s. 28
:::

### [Ancillary Powers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165368 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ancillary powers

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} manage, acquire, hold, lend, lease and dispose of
    real and personal property;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give guarantees with respect to any property
    disposed of by the Corporation;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} procure the incorporation, dissolution or
    amalgamation of a corporation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} enter into any kind of transaction for the
    financial management of the Corporation and make any investment,
    including transactions and investments that are insured or
    guaranteed by the Corporation and, without restricting the
    generality of the foregoing, enter into

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a currency or interest rate swap agreement,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a basis swap agreement,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a spot, future, forward or other foreign
      exchange agreement,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a cap, collar or floor transaction,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a financial swap,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a forward rate agreement,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} a sale and repurchase agreement, a purchase and
      resale agreement, a reverse purchase agreement or a reverse sale
      agreement,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} a spot, future, forward or other financial
      contract,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} an agreement to buy or sell or borrow or lend
      securities,

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} an agreement to issue securities on the basis of
      assets or investments,

    - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} a guarantee agreement,

    - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} any derivative, combination or option in
      respect of, or agreement similar to, an agreement, transaction or
      contract referred to in any of subparagraphs (i) to (xi), or

    - [(xiii)]{.lawlabel} any master agreement in respect of any
      agreement, transaction or contract referred to in any of
      subparagraphs (i) to (xii);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} borrow from the Minister and expend moneys
    appropriated by Parliament to be lent by the Minister to the
    Corporation or expend any moneys appropriated by Parliament to be
    expended by the Corporation, for the purposes for which those moneys
    are appropriated;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} exercise or perform any other powers, duties or
    functions conferred on or required to be performed by the
    Corporation under any other Act or order in council; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} do any act or thing incidental to the conduct of
    the business of the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Funds

  [(1.01)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the power of the
  Corporation referred to in paragraph (1)(c) may be exercised in
  relation to any fund referred to in any of the Housing Acts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vesting

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by order, transfer to
  the Corporation any land and interests in land the title to which is
  vested in Her Majesty, and the title thereto vests in the Corporation
  on the date fixed in the order or, where no date is so fixed, on the
  date of the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} There shall be deemed to be vested in the
  Corporation title to land and interests in land acquired pursuant to
  The Dominion Housing Act, 1935, The National Housing Act, 1938, The
  National Housing Act, 1944 or the Housing Acts and title to which,
  apart from this subsection, is vested in Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments in lieu of taxes

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may pay to a municipality or other
  taxing authority, in respect of land and interests in land title to
  which is vested in the Corporation, an amount equivalent to the taxes
  that would be payable in respect of the property were it not the
  property of Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Steps to safeguard

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may, to protect its security in
  respect of any indebtedness to it, make loans to the debtor and take
  such other measures and steps as may be required in accordance with
  normal mortgage practice to safeguard the interests of the
  Corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-7, s. 28
- R.S., 1985, c. 25 (4th Supp.), s. 34
- 1992, c. 32, s. 3
- 1999, c. 27, s. 33
:::

### [Cooperation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165404 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cooperation

- **[[28.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may cooperate with
  any person, group of persons, organization, municipality or department
  or agency of the government of Canada or a province in the exercise of
  any of the Corporation's powers and functions, and for that purpose
  may participate in joint ventures.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may enter into agreements with a
  party that is a person, group of persons, organization, municipality
  or department or agency of the government of Canada or a province

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for that party to have any of the powers, duties,
    functions, rights, obligations and liabilities of the Corporation;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the Corporation to have any of the powers,
    duties, functions, rights, obligations and liabilities of that
    party.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1999, c. 27, s. 34
:::

### [Reserve]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165414 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reserve fund

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation shall establish a reserve fund.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Profits credited to fund

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the profits of the
  Corporation in each fiscal year remaining after such provision as the
  Board thinks proper for bad and doubtful debts, depreciation in
  assets, anticipated future losses and all other matters whatever that
  in the opinion of the Board should be provided for in carrying out the
  purposes of the Corporation shall be credited to the reserve fund
  established under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amount of fund

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When the reserve fund established under
  subsection (1) has reached a total of an amount designated from time
  to time by the Governor in Council, any profits of the Corporation
  that would be credited to the reserve fund shall be paid to the
  Receiver General.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-7, s. 29
- 1992, c. 32, s. 4
:::

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Audit and General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-165424 .Part}

### [Audit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165425 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Auditor

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  auditor of the Corporation shall be appointed annually by the Governor
  in Council and may be removed at any time by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications of auditor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The auditor must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an accountant who is a member in good standing of
    an institute or association of accountants incorporated by or under
    an Act of the legislature of a province and who has for at least six
    years preceding the date of the appointment practised the profession
    of accountant; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a partnership of accountants, one of whom is
    designated by the partnership, with the approval of the Corporation,
    to conduct the audit on behalf of the partnership and who meets the
    requirements of paragraph (a).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, R.S., 1985, c. 1 (4th Supp.), s.
  4\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-7, s. 30
- R.S., 1985, c. 1 (4th Supp.), s. 4
- 1993, c. 44, s. 30
- 1999, c. 27, s. 35
:::

### [Fiscal Year of Corporation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165436 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fiscal year

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** The fiscal year of the
Corporation is the calendar year unless the Governor in Council
otherwise directs.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-16, s. 32
- 1984, c. 31, s. 14
:::

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165441 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insolvency or winding-up not applicable

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** No Act relating to the
insolvency or winding-up of any corporation applies to the Corporation
and in no case shall the affairs thereof be wound up unless Parliament
so provides.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-16, s. 36
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of Governor in Council on
recommendation of Minister

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding section
17, where under the Housing Acts any act, matter or thing to be done by
the Minister requires the approval of the Governor in Council or where
the Governor in Council may make any orders or regulations on the
recommendation of the Minister, the approval may be given or the orders
or regulations may be made on a recommendation of the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-16, s. 37
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance from government services

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The minister of any
department or the officer in charge of any other portion of the
Government of Canada, or any agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada,
may, on request of the Corporation and with the approval of the Governor
in Council, agree to assist the Corporation on such terms and conditions
as may be approved by the Governor in Council by providing such services
as the Executive Committee may deem advisable for carrying out the
business of the Corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-16, s. 38
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deposit accounts

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation may maintain in its own name one or more accounts with the
  Bank of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest may be paid

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Bank of Canada may pay interest on any money that
  the Corporation deposits with it.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2016, c. 12, s. 125
:::

## [Oath of Fidelity and Secrecy]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#oath-of-fidelity-and-secrecy .scheduleLabel}
:::

I, ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}, do solemnly swear
that I will faithfully, truly and to the best of my judgment, skill and
ability, execute and perform the duties required of me as a director
(officer or employee *as the case may be*) of the Canada Mortgage and
Housing Corporation and which properly relate to any office or position
in the Corporation held by me.

I further solemnly swear that I will not communicate or allow to be
communicated to any person not legally entitled thereto any information
relating to the affairs of the Corporation, nor will I allow any such
person to inspect or have access to any books or documents belonging to
or in the possession of the Corporation and relating to the business of
the Corporation.

- 1978-79, c. 16, s. 12
