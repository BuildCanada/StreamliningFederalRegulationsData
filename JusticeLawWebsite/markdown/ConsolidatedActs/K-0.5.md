## Kanesatake Interim Land Base Governance Act {#kanesatake-interim-land-base-governance-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2001, c. 8
:::

Assented to 2001-06-14

An Act to implement an agreement between the Mohawks of Kanesatake and
Her Majesty in right of Canada respecting governance of certain lands by
the Mohawks of Kanesatake and to amend an Act in consequence
::::

::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Kanesatake Interim Land Base Governance Act](/eng/acts/K-0.5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in this Act.

  [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the "Agreement with
      respect to Kanesatake Governance of the Interim Land Base" between
      the Mohawks of Kanesatake and Her Majesty in right of Canada,
      signed on December 21, 2000 and tabled in Parliament on March 27,
      2001. ([accord territorial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the council of the band,
      known as the Mohawk Council of
      Kanesatake. ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Kanesatake Mohawk interim land base]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Kanesatake Mohawk interim land base]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      the lands set out in the schedule to this Act. ([territoire
      provisoire de Kanesatake]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Kanesatake Mohawk law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Kanesatake Mohawk law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a law enacted
      in accordance with the provisions of this Act. ([lois des Mohawks
      de Kanesatake]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Kanesatake Mohawk neighbouring lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Kanesatake Mohawk neighbouring lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      the lands in the Kanesatake Mohawk interim land base that are
      located in the sectors set out in Schedule B to the Agreement, in
      the area commonly known as the "Village of Oka". ([terres mohawks
      avoisinantes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Mohawks of Kanesatake]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Mohawks of Kanesatake]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the band known
      as the Mohawks of Kanesatake. ([Mohawks de
      Kanesatake]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to Municipality

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A reference in this Act to the Municipality of Oka
  includes any successor municipality.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  purpose of this Act is to implement the Agreement and, in doing so,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to ensure that certain lands in the Kanesatake
    Mohawk interim land base constitute lands reserved for the Indians
    within the meaning of class 24 of section 91 of the [Constitution
    Act, 1867](/eng/Const/) but not a reserve within the meaning of the
    [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to provide a framework for the exercise of
    jurisdiction by the Mohawks of Kanesatake over the use and
    development of the lands in the Kanesatake Mohawk interim land base;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to establish principles to govern the harmonious
    use and development of Kanesatake Mohawk neighbouring lands and
    certain lands in the Municipality of Oka.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aboriginal and treaty rights

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Act does not address any aboriginal or treaty
  rights of the Mohawks of Kanesatake. Nothing in this Act is intended
  either to prejudice such rights or to represent a recognition of such
  rights by Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of and interests in lands

  [[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#K-0.5_en_1){#K-0.5_en_1-ID0EBDAA}[(3)]{.lawlabel} This
  Act does not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} address the constitutional status of any lands
    before this Act comes into effect; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} alter existing jurisdiction over the creation or
    transfer of rights or interests in relation to lands in the
    Kanesatake Mohawk interim land base.

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#K-0.5_en_1-ID0EBDAA){#K-0.5_en_1}\[Note:
      Act in force on assent June 14, 2001.\]
      :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lands reserved

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The lands in the Kanesatake
Mohawk interim land base, other than the lands known as Doncaster
Reserve No. 17, are set aside for the use and benefit of the Mohawks of
Kanesatake as lands reserved for the Indians within the meaning of class
24 of section 91 of the [Constitution Act, 1867](/eng/Const/) but not as
a reserve within the meaning of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal status and capacity

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Mohawks of Kanesatake
have the capacity of a natural person and, in particular, may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} acquire and hold property;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into contracts or agreements;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} borrow money;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} expend and invest money;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} be a party to legal proceedings; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} do any other thing that is conducive to the exercise
  of the rights, powers and privileges associated with that capacity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exercise of jurisdiction and powers

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Mohawks of Kanesatake
shall act through the Council in exercising the jurisdiction and powers
under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Mohawks of Kanesatake have jurisdiction to make laws in relation to
  the use and development of the lands in the Kanesatake Mohawk interim
  land base, including in relation to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the health and quality of life of residents;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the protection and management of wildlife and fish;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the observance of law and order and the prevention
    of disorderly conduct and nuisances;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the prevention of trespass, including entry onto,
    or occupation of, the lands without lawful authority;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} residency;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} fire safety and fire protection services;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the construction, maintenance, management and use
    of local works, including water supplies;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the construction or alteration of buildings,
    including inspection in connection with the construction or
    alteration;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} zoning;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} waste management and public sanitation; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} traffic regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This jurisdiction includes the power to create
  offences punishable on summary conviction and to provide for the
  following sanctions for those offences, namely, orders, fines,
  imprisonment and sanctions that are consistent with restorative
  justice objectives. However, a fine or period of imprisonment shall
  not exceed the limits established under subsection 787(1) of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, this jurisdiction does not
  include jurisdiction in relation to the subject-matters referred to in
  class 27 of section 91 of the [Constitution Act, 1867](/eng/Const/) or
  in relation to labour relations or working conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply to Kanesatake Mohawk laws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction --- Doncaster Reserve No. 17

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Jurisdiction shall not be exercised
  under section 7 with respect to the lands known as Doncaster Reserve
  No. 17 except in accordance with an agreement between the Mohawks of
  Kanesatake and Kahnawake, and exercise of the jurisdiction with
  respect to those lands shall be subject to Her Majesty in right of
  Canada agreeing to take any measures that may be necessary to
  implement that agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of the [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} To the extent that a Kanesatake Mohawk law enacted in
  accordance with subsection (1) makes provision for any matter for
  which provision is made by the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), the
  [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) does not apply to the Mohawks of
  Kanesatake or to members of the Mohawks of Kanesatake.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction --- land governance code

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  jurisdiction under section 7 is exercised, the Mohawks of Kanesatake
  shall adopt a land governance code that sets out the principles,
  processes and rules to which the exercise of jurisdiction is subject.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory elements of the code

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The code shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish processes for the preparation, enactment
    and publication of Kanesatake Mohawk laws;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish processes for the assessment and approval
    of proposed uses and development of lands in the Kanesatake Mohawk
    interim land base;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} establish principles and rules, including conflict
    of interest rules, to ensure the accountability of the Council to
    members of the Mohawks of Kanesatake;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide for rights of appeal and redress in
    relation to decisions and other measures taken by the Council under
    the authority of Kanesatake Mohawk laws; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} include a procedure to amend the code.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply to the land governance code.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Land use plan

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Mohawks of Kanesatake
shall adopt a plan that establishes general policies governing land use
for the Kanesatake Mohawk interim land base before any process
preliminary to the granting of an authorization is undertaken in
relation to the proposed use of any of the lands in the land base for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a commercial or industrial activity that results in
  or may result in an adverse impact on the environment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the storage or transportation of any dangerous
  materials; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} waste disposal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Environmental protection

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  federal laws contain no standards of environmental protection with
  respect to a matter, a Kanesatake Mohawk law, or an action or measure
  taken by the Council, with respect to that matter shall be consistent
  with the standards of environmental protection that apply generally in
  the province with respect to the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), environmental
  protection does not include environmental assessment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stricter standards

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in subsection (1) precludes the Mohawks of
  Kanesatake or the Council from setting or applying standards that are
  stricter than the provincial standards.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Kanesatake Mohawk neighbouring lands

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Until the Mohawks of
Kanesatake make a Kanesatake Mohawk law that allows a land use on, or
that establishes a land use standard that applies to, one or more of the
lots or land sites that constitute Kanesatake Mohawk neighbouring lands,
no person shall alter an existing land use, building or structure on
that lot or land site or those lots or land sites unless the alteration
complies with the land uses or the land use standards set out in
Schedule B to the Agreement for the applicable sector.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Harmonization arrangement --- Kanesatake
Mohawk neighbouring lands

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  the Mohawks of Kanesatake make any Kanesatake Mohawk law that allows a
  land use, or establishes a land use standard, that is significantly
  different from the uses or standards set out in Schedule B to the
  Agreement for the applicable sector, they shall enter into a
  harmonization arrangement with the Municipality of Oka.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of harmonization arrangement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The harmonization arrangement

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall establish the reciprocal obligations of the
    parties to harmonize, with respect to land use or land use
    standards, the Kanesatake Mohawk laws that apply to Kanesatake
    Mohawk neighbouring lands and the municipal by-laws that apply to
    the properties in the Municipality of Oka that adjoin Kanesatake
    Mohawk neighbouring lands;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall establish a dispute resolution process, which
    may include arbitration, to resolve any disagreement between the
    parties in relation to the arrangement; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} may determine criteria for compatibility between
    those Kanesatake Mohawk laws and municipal by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in relation to any of
  the sectors in Schedule B to the Agreement if the Municipality of Oka,
  before entering into a harmonization arrangement, amends a municipal
  by-law to allow a land use or to establish a land use standard in any
  sector that is significantly different from the uses or standards set
  out in that Schedule for that sector, without being legally obliged by
  a provincial law to amend the by-law for that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enactment despite harmonization
  arrangement

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Mohawks of Kanesatake may, in relation to any of
  the sectors in Schedule B to the Agreement, make Kanesatake Mohawk
  laws despite the terms of the harmonization arrangement if, without
  the agreement of the Mohawks of Kanesatake, the Municipality of Oka

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in breach of its obligations under the arrangement,
    amends or repeals a municipal by-law with respect to land use or
    land use standards without being legally obliged by a provincial law
    to amend or repeal the by-law; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} condones any use, activity or construction on a
    property in the Municipality of Oka that contravenes a municipal
    by-law with respect to land use or land use standards and that
    significantly prejudices any right or interest of an occupant of
    Kanesatake Mohawk neighbouring lands.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National building standards

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Any construction that
takes place on lands in the Kanesatake Mohawk interim land base shall
comply with the building standards contained in the National Building
Code of Canada 1995, issued by the Canadian Commission on Building and
Fire Codes of the National Research Council of Canada, as amended from
time to time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prosecution

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Mohawks of Kanesatake may prosecute an offence committed under a
  Kanesatake Mohawk law in a court of competent jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Contraventions Act](/eng/acts/C-38.7)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Mohawks of Kanesatake may adopt the procedure
  applicable under the [Contraventions Act](/eng/acts/C-38.7) for the
  prosecution of contraventions, subject to the necessary modifications.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to appoint justices of the peace

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To
  ensure the enforcement of Kanesatake Mohawk laws, the Mohawks of
  Kanesatake may appoint justices of the peace to adjudicate offences
  for contraventions of those laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement with Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Mohawks of Kanesatake shall not appoint any
  justices of the peace except in accordance with an agreement with Her
  Majesty in right of Canada that addresses the following matters:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the jurisdiction and qualifications of the justices
    of the peace;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the independence and impartiality, including the
    financial security and security of tenure, of the justices of the
    peace;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the supervision of the justices of the peace;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the right to appeal any decision of a justice of
    the peace; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the relationship between the justices of the peace
    and the existing justice system and its institutions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial laws that do not apply of
their own force and municipal by-laws

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  provincial law to which section 88 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)
  applies, or a municipal by-law, does not apply to the extent that it
  is inconsistent or conflicts with this Act or with a Kanesatake Mohawk
  law, or to the extent that it makes provision for any matter for which
  provision is made by or under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other provincial laws

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of an inconsistency or conflict between
  a Kanesatake Mohawk law and any other provincial law, the Kanesatake
  Mohawk law prevails to the extent of the inconsistency or conflict.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Federal laws

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the event of an inconsistency or conflict between
  a Kanesatake Mohawk law and a federal law, the federal law prevails to
  the extent of the inconsistency or conflict.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** In any proceeding, a copy
of a Kanesatake Mohawk law that appears to be certified as a true copy
by a duly authorized officer of the Council is, without proof of the
officer's signature or official character, evidence of its enactment on
the date specified in the law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment to schedule

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, by order, add to the schedule to this Act a
  reference to lands that the Mohawks of Kanesatake and Her Majesty in
  right of Canada agree should be added to the Kanesatake Mohawk interim
  land base.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of plan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Mohawks of Kanesatake shall revise the plan
  referred to in section 10 to include the lands added to the schedule
  to this Act before any process preliminary to the granting of an
  authorization is undertaken in relation to the proposed use of the
  added lands for any of the activities referred to in paragraphs 10(a)
  to (c).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing rights or interests

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#K-0.5_en_2){#K-0.5_en_2-ID0EBCA}[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20
.sectionLabel}** A right or interest in relation to lands in the
Kanesatake Mohawk interim land base that exists on the coming into force
of this Act continues in accordance with its terms and conditions.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#K-0.5_en_2-ID0EBCA){#K-0.5_en_2}\[Note: Act in
  force on assent June 14, 2001.\]
  :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Canada Lands Surveys Act](/eng/acts/L-6)

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 29(3) of the [Canada Lands Surveys
  Act](/eng/acts/L-6), in relation to lands in the Kanesatake Mohawk
  interim land base, the survey and plans must be satisfactory to the
  Council rather than to the Minister of Indigenous Services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in relation to
  boundaries between the Kanesatake Mohawk interim land base and other
  lands.
