## Translation Bureau Act {#translation-bureau-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. T-16
:::

An Act respecting the Translation Bureau
::::

::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451755 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Translation Bureau Act](/eng/acts/T-16).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-13, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451760 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of \"Minister\"

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's Privy
Council for Canada as is designated by the Governor in Council as the
Minister for the purposes of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-16, s. 2
- 1996, c. 16, s. 57
:::

## [Establishment of Translation Bureau]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451765 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bureau established

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby established
a bureau under the authority of the Minister called the Translation
Bureau.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-13, s. 3
:::

## [Duties and Functions of Bureau]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451770 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties and functions of Bureau

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Bureau shall collaborate with and act for all departments, boards,
  agencies and commissions established by Act of Parliament or appointed
  by order of the Governor in Council and collaborate with and act for
  both Houses of Parliament in all matters relating to the making and
  revising of translations from one language into another of documents,
  including correspondence, reports, proceedings, debates, bills and
  Acts, and to interpretation, sign-language interpretation and
  terminology.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Departments to collaborate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All departments, boards, agencies and commissions
  referred to in subsection (1) shall collaborate with the Bureau in
  carrying into effect this Act and the regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-16, s. 4
- R.S., 1985, c. 1 (4th Supp.), s. 34
:::

## [Personnel]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451778 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, 1996, c. 16,
s. 58\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers and employees

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Such officers, translators,
clerks or other employees as are necessary for the proper conduct of the
business of the Bureau may be appointed under the [Public Service
Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) to hold office during pleasure.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-16, s. 6
- 1996, c. 16, s. 59(E)
:::

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451784 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, with the
approval of the Governor in Council, make all regulations that the
Minister deems necessary or expedient for carrying this Act into effect.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-13, s. 6
:::
