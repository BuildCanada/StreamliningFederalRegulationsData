## Canadian Tourism Commission Act {#canadian-tourism-commission-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2000, c. 28
:::

Assented to 2000-10-20

An Act to establish the Canadian Tourism Commission
::::

:::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the Canadian tourism industry is vital to the social and
cultural identity and integrity of Canada;

WHEREAS the Canadian tourism industry makes an essential contribution to
the economic well-being of Canadians and to the economic objectives of
the Government of Canada;

WHEREAS the Canadian tourism industry consists of mainly small and
medium-sized businesses that are essential to Canada's goals for
entrepreneurial development and job creation;

AND WHEREAS it is desirable to strengthen Canada's commitment to
Canadian tourism by establishing a Tourism Commission that would work
with the governments of the provinces and the territories and the
Canadian tourism industry to promote the interests of that industry and
to market Canada as a desirable tourist destination;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Canadian Tourism Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-75175 .Part}

### [Establishment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-75176 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby established
a corporation to be known as the Canadian Tourism Commission.

### [Status]{.HTitleText2} {#h-75179 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agent of Her Majesty

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commission is, for the
purposes of this Act, an agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

### [Objects]{.HTitleText2} {#h-75182 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The objects of the
Commission are to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sustain a vibrant and profitable Canadian tourism
  industry;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} market Canada as a desirable tourist destination;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} support a cooperative relationship between the
  private sector and the governments of Canada, the provinces and the
  territories with respect to Canadian tourism; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide information about Canadian tourism to the
  private sector and to the governments of Canada, the provinces and the
  territories.

### [Powers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-75189 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of carrying out its objects, the Commission has the capacity
  and, subject to this Act, the rights, powers and privileges of a
  natural person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may not initiate or finance programs
  involving the acquisition or construction of real property, immovables
  or facilities related to tourism.

### [Board of Directors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-75195 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The affairs and business of
the Commission shall be managed by a Board of Directors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Constitution

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Board consists of not
more than 12 directors, including a Chairperson and a President.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Chairperson and term of
office

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Chairperson shall be
appointed by the Governor in Council to hold office during pleasure on a
part-time basis for a term of not more than five years.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of President and term of
office

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The President shall be
appointed by the Governor in Council to hold office during pleasure on a
full-time basis for a term of not more than five years.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of directors

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Up to
  nine directors shall be appointed by the Minister, with the approval
  of the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall establish a committee that shall
  provide advice to the Minister on the appointment of directors under
  subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The directors appointed under subsection (1) hold
  office during pleasure on a part-time basis for a term not exceeding
  four years.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A director is eligible to
be re-appointed to the Board in the same or another capacity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The President shall be
paid the remuneration that the Governor in Council may fix.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Chairperson and the
directors appointed under subsection 11(1) shall be paid the fees that
the Governor in Council may fix.

### [Chairperson]{.HTitleText2} {#h-75228 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Chairperson shall
determine the times when and places where the Board will meet and shall
preside at those meetings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence, etc., of Chairperson

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** If the Chairperson is
absent or incapacitated or if the office of Chairperson is vacant, the
Board may designate any director appointed under section 11 to exercise
the powers and perform the duties and functions of the Chairperson
during the absence, incapacity or vacancy, but no director may be so
designated for a period exceeding ninety days without the approval of
the Governor in Council.

### [President]{.HTitleText2} {#h-75233 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The President is the
chief executive officer of the Commission and has supervision over and
direction of the work of the Commission including the management of its
internal affairs and the hiring and termination of its staff.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence, etc., of President

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** If the President is
absent or incapacitated or if the office of President is vacant, the
Board may designate any person to exercise the powers and perform the
duties and functions of the President during the absence, incapacity or
vacancy, but no person may be so designated for a period exceeding
ninety days without the approval of the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Subject to any contrary
provision in any other Act of Parliament, the President may delegate to
any person any power, duty or function conferred on the President under
this Act or any other enactment.

### [Meetings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-75240 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum number of meetings

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall meet at
least twice a year.

### [By-laws]{.HTitleText2} {#h-75243 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall make
by-laws respecting the management and conduct of the affairs of the
Commission and the carrying out of the duties of the Board, including
by-laws establishing

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a code of ethics for the directors and employees of
  the Commission;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} committees of the Board, including an executive
  committee, a human resources committee, a committee for the purposes
  of section 11 and an audit committee; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a contracting policy for the Commission.

### [Head Office]{.HTitleText2} {#h-75249 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the
Commission shall be in the place in Canada that the Governor in Council
may, by order, designate.

### [Accident Compensation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-75252 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accident compensation

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The Chairperson, the
President, the directors appointed under subsection 11(1) and the
employees of the Commission are deemed to be employees for the purposes
of the [Government Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and to be
employed in the federal public administration for the purposes of any
regulations made under section 9 of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

### [Agreements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-75257 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may enter into an agreement with the government of any
  province or territory to carry out its objects.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation of corporations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the agreement provides for the incorporation of a
  corporation or the acquisition of shares in a corporation or all or
  substantially all of the assets of a corporation, the Commission may,
  with the approval of the Governor in Council, do any of those things,
  either by itself or jointly with any person or the government of a
  province or a territory, in order to carry out the provisions of the
  agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consistent activities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A corporation mentioned in subsection (2) may carry
  out only activities that are consistent with the objects of the
  Commission, taking into account the restriction set out in subsection
  6(2).

### [Staff]{.HTitleText2} {#h-75265 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may engage
any officers, employees and agents and any technical and professional
advisers that it considers necessary for the proper conduct of its
activities and may fix the terms and conditions of their engagement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff outside Canada

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** When the Commission,
outside Canada, engages persons referred to in section 27 to perform
duties outside Canada, it shall engage them and establish their terms
and conditions of employment or work, and the [Canada Labour
Code](/eng/acts/L-2) does not apply to those persons.

### [Former Commission]{.HTitleText2} {#h-75279 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission dissolved

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  former Commission is dissolved and its work is transferred from the
  Department of Industry to the new Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of section 40.1 of the [Public
  Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the transfer of the work
  of the former Commission is deemed to be a transfer or divestiture, by
  Her Majesty in right of Canada, of the administration of a service to
  a person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** The person who holds the
office of Chairperson of the former Commission immediately before the
commencement day continues in office as the Chairperson of the new
Commission for the remainder of the term for which the person was
appointed Chairperson.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The person who holds the
office of President of the former Commission immediately before the
commencement day continues in office as the President of the new
Commission for the remainder of the term for which the person was
appointed President.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other directors

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** Each person who is a
director of the former Commission immediately before the commencement
day continues as a director of the new Commission for the remainder of
the term for which the person was appointed as a director.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property transferred

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** All property of Her
Majesty in right of Canada that is under the administration and control
of the Minister and used for the purpose of carrying out the objects of
the former Commission is transferred to the new Commission to be held in
the name of the new Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations transferred

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** All obligations and
liabilities of Her Majesty in right of Canada incurred in respect of the
former Commission are transferred to the new Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** Every reference to the
former Commission in a deed, contract or other document shall, unless
the context otherwise requires, be read as a reference to the new
Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement of legal proceedings

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  action, suit or other legal proceeding in respect of an obligation or
  a liability incurred in carrying out the objects of the former
  Commission may be brought against the new Commission in any court that
  would have had jurisdiction if the action, suit or proceeding had been
  brought against Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of legal proceedings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal proceeding in respect
  of the former Commission that is pending in a court immediately before
  the commencement day may, on that day, be continued by or against the
  new Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian Tourism Commission Account

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** The amount outstanding on
the commencement day in the accounts of Canada in respect of the
carrying out of the objects of the former Commission shall be paid to
the new Commission in the manner most appropriate to give effect to the
purpose for which the moneys or property constituting or otherwise
giving rise to that amount were given, bequeathed or otherwise made
available to the former Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First corporate plan and budgets

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** Despite the period
prescribed for submitting a corporate plan, an operating budget and a
capital budget under the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11),
the new Commission shall, within six months after the commencement day,
submit to the Minister in accordance with that Act a corporate plan, an
operating budget and a capital budget for its first financial year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriations

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#C-23.3_en_3){#C-23.3_en_3-ID0EBCA}[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40
.sectionLabel}** Any amount appropriated, for the fiscal year in which
this section comes into force, by an appropriation Act based on the
Estimates for that year for defraying the charges and expenses of the
former Commission in carrying out its objects is an amount appropriated
for defraying the charges and expenses of the new Commission.

### [Human Resources and Labour Relations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-75308 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pending competitions and appointments

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** A competition being
conducted or an appointment being or about to be made under the [Public
Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) in respect of a position
within the Department of Industry, the duties and functions of which are
assigned to a position within the new Commission, may continue to be
conducted or made as if the new Commission were a department for the
purposes of that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility lists

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** An eligibility list made
under the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) in respect
of positions within the Department of Industry related to the carrying
out of the objects of the former Commission that is valid on the
commencement day continues to be valid for the period provided for by
subsection 17(2) of that Act, but that period may not be extended.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pending appeals

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appeal made under section 21 of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) by any person against an appointment to a
  position within the Department of Industry the duties and functions of
  which are assigned to a position within the new Commission, and not
  finally disposed of on the assignment, must be dealt with and disposed
  of in accordance with that Act as if the new Commission were a
  department for the purposes of that Act and the person continued to be
  an employee for the purposes of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other recourse

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any recourse commenced by an employee under the
  [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) that has not been
  finally dealt with on the employee's engagement by the new Commission
  must be dealt with and disposed of in accordance with that Act as if
  the new Commission were a department for the purposes of that Act and
  the person continued to be an employee for the purposes of that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employees on probation

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  employee who was considered to be on probation under section 28 of the
  [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) immediately before
  being engaged by the new Commission continues on probation with the
  new Commission until the end of the period established by the Public
  Service Commission by regulation for that employee or a class of
  persons of which that employee is a member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rejection

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 28(2) of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) applies to an employee of the new Commission
  who is on probation but the reference to deputy head in that
  subsection is to be read as a reference to the President.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pending grievances

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  grievance commenced by an employee under the [Public Service Staff
  Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-35) that has not been finally dealt with on
  the employee's engagement by the new Commission must be dealt with and
  disposed of in accordance with that Act as if the employee's
  employment in the Department of Industry had not been terminated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation of decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A final decision with respect to a grievance referred
  to in subsection (1) that provides for the reinstatement of or payment
  of money to a person must be implemented by the new Commission as soon
  as is practicable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligibility for benefits --- executive
group

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** An indeterminate employee
who was a member of the Executive Group in the Department of Industry
and whose employment is terminated under paragraph 11(2)(g.1) of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) is not eligible for
benefits under the Treasury Board Executive Employment Transition
Policy.
