An Act respecting the energy efficiency of energy-using products and the
use of alternative energy sources

of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-219031 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Energy Efficiency Act](/eng/acts/E-6.4).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-219034 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    business of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} manufacturing energy-using products in Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} importing energy-using products into Canada, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} selling or leasing energy-using products obtained
      directly or indirectly from a person engaged in a business
      described in paragraph (a) or (b) or an agent
      thereof; ([fournisseur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[energy efficiency standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[energy efficiency standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
    energy-using product, means the standard, if any, prescribed
    pursuant to section 20 for that product or for a class of
    energy-using products that includes that product; ([norme
    d'efficacité énergétique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[energy-using product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[energy-using product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prescribed
    product; ([matériel consommateur d'énergie]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated as an
    inspector pursuant to subsection
    9(1); ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[manufacture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[manufacture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the process of
    assembling or altering an energy-using product in order to complete
    that product for sale to the first retail purchaser or for lease to
    the first lessee; ([fabrication]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Natural
    Resources; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by
    regulation. ([Version anglaise seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

::: HistoricalNote
- 1992, c. 36, s. 2
- 1994, c. 41, s. 37
:::

Meaning of [[class]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
a reference in this Act to a [[class]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in relation
to energy-using products includes classes based on common
energy-consuming characteristics, the intended use of the products or
the conditions under which the products are normally used.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 8, s. 1
:::

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-219053 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Energy-using Products]{.HTitleText1} {#h-219056 .Part}

### [Trade in Energy-Using Products]{.HTitleText2} {#h-219057 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interprovincial trade and importation

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  dealer shall, for the purpose of sale or lease, ship an energy-using
  product from one province to another province, or import an
  energy-using product into Canada, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the product complies with the energy efficiency
    standard; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the product or its package is labelled in
    accordance with the regulations, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tampering with label

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall, before an energy-using product is
  sold to the first retail purchaser or leased to the first lessee,
  remove, deface, obscure or alter any label put on the product or its
  package in accordance with the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Savings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A dealer does not contravene paragraph (1)(b) if the
  requirements of that paragraph are met before the energy-using product
  leaves the possession of the dealer or the dealer's consignee.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1992, c. 36, s. 4
- 2009, c. 8, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be provided by dealers

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  dealer who ships or imports energy-using products as described in
  subsection 4(1) shall provide the Minister, in the prescribed form and
  manner and at the prescribed time, with prescribed information
  respecting those products, including their energy efficiency, their
  shipment or their importation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A dealer is not required to provide prescribed
  information in respect of the energy efficiency of any particular
  energy-using products if the Minister is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information has previously been provided under
    subsection (1); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information has previously been provided under
    subsection (1) in respect of the energy efficiency of comparable
    energy-using products that differ from those products only in a
    manner that does not relate to energy efficiency.

### [Test Products]{.HTitleText2} {#h-219078 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to supply test products

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may require any dealer who ships or imports energy-using
  products as described in subsection 4(1) to make available, at such
  place as the Minister may specify, such number of those products as
  the Minister considers to be reasonably necessary for examination and
  testing under this section, and the dealer shall forthwith comply with
  the request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Testing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may dismantle and examine any
  energy-using product made available pursuant to subsection (1) and may
  conduct such tests on it as the Minister considers to be reasonably
  necessary to determine the product's energy efficiency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not retain any energy-using
  product made available pursuant to subsection (1) longer than the
  Minister considers to be reasonably necessary to complete the
  examination and tests referred to in subsection (2), unless the dealer
  consents to the further retention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seizure

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (3), an inspector may
  seize and detain any energy-using product that is examined and tested
  under subsection (2) if the inspector believes on reasonable grounds
  that any provision of this Act or the regulations has been contravened
  and that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the contravention occurred by means of or in
    relation to the product; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the product will afford evidence in respect of the
    contravention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions applicable

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 11(2) and (3) and sections 13 to 18
  apply, with such modifications as the circumstances require, with
  respect to an energy-using product seized under subsection (4) as if
  it were a thing seized under subsection 11(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Outside testing

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may enter into an agreement or other
  arrangement with any person for the examination and testing of
  energy-using products under this section.

keep, at the dealer's place of business or other prescribed place in

### [Inspection and Seizure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-219103 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of inspectors

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate as an inspector for the purposes of this Act
  any person who, in the opinion of the Minister, is qualified to be so
  designated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall furnish every inspector with a
  certificate of designation as an inspector and, on exercising any
  power vested in the inspector by this Act, an inspector shall, if so
  required, produce the certificate to any person in authority who is
  affected by that exercise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspection

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of ensuring compliance with this Act and the regulations,
  an inspector may, subject to subsection (3), at any reasonable time
  enter and inspect any place in which the inspector believes on
  reasonable grounds there is an energy-using product for which an
  energy efficiency standard or a label has been prescribed and that is
  owned by or is on the premises of a dealer or a consignee of imported
  energy-using products or there is any document or record required by
  section 7 to be kept, and may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine any energy-using product, or any other
    thing relevant to the administration of this Act, that is found in
    that place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} open and examine any package or receptacle found in
    the place that the inspector believes on reasonable grounds contains
    an energy-using product;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} examine any document or record that the inspector
    believes on reasonable grounds contains any information relevant to
    the administration of this Act and make copies thereof or extracts
    therefrom; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} conduct any tests or take any measurements.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Computer systems

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In carrying out an inspection of a place under
  subsection (1), an inspector may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any computer system at the
    place to examine any information contained in or available to the
    system that the inspector believes on reasonable grounds is relevant
    to the administration of this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reproduce any record or cause it to be reproduced
    from the information in the form of a printout or other intelligible
    output; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} take a printout or other output for examination or
    copying.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Private dwelling-place

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An inspector may not enter a private dwelling-place
  without the consent of the occupant of the place except under the
  authority of a warrant issued under subsection (4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where on [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application
  a justice of the peace is satisfied by information on oath that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conditions for entry described in
    subsection (1) exist in relation to a private dwelling-place,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entry to the dwelling-place is necessary for any
    purpose relating to the administration of this Act, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} entry to the dwelling-place has been refused or
    there are reasonable grounds for believing that entry thereto will
    be refused,

  the justice of the peace may issue a warrant authorizing the inspector
  named therein to enter and inspect the dwelling-place, subject to such
  conditions as may be specified in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In executing a warrant issued under subsection (4),
  the inspector named in the warrant shall not use force unless the
  inspector is accompanied by a peace officer and the use of force has
  been specifically authorized in the warrant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seizure

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  in the course of an inspection under section 10, an inspector believes
  on reasonable grounds that any provision of this Act or the
  regulations has been contravened, the inspector may seize and detain
  any energy-using product or other thing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by means of or in relation to which the inspector
    believes on reasonable grounds the contravention occurred; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the inspector believes on reasonable grounds
    will afford evidence in respect of the contravention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage and removal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A thing seized by an inspector under subsection (1)
  may, in the inspector's discretion, be stored in the place where it
  was seized or be removed to any other place for storage and, where it
  is so removed at the request of the owner of the thing or the person
  who was in possession of it at the time of the seizure, the costs of
  the storage and removal shall be paid by that owner or person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interference with thing seized

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless authorized by an inspector, no person shall
  remove, alter or interfere with a thing seized and detained under
  subsection (1), but an inspector shall, at the request of the owner of
  the thing or the person who was in possession of it at the time of the
  seizure, allow that owner or person or any person authorized by that
  owner or person to examine the thing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to inspectors

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  owner or person in charge of a place entered by an inspector under
  section 10, and every person present in that place, shall give the
  inspector all reasonable assistance to enable the inspector to carry
  out the inspector's duties and functions under this Act or the
  regulations and shall furnish the inspector with such information with
  respect to the administration of this Act or the regulations as the
  inspector may reasonably require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction of inspectors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall wilfully obstruct or hinder, or
  knowingly make any false or misleading statement orally or in writing
  to, an inspector engaged in carrying out the inspector's duties or
  functions under this Act or the regulations.

### [Detention]{.HTitleText2} {#h-219145 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Detention

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  thing seized under subsection 11(1) shall not be detained after the
  expiration of one hundred and twenty days after the day of seizure
  unless, before that time, the thing has been forfeited under section
  15 or proceedings have been instituted in respect of the contravention
  in relation to which the thing was seized.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Detention until end of proceedings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where proceedings have been instituted as described
  in subsection (1), the thing may be detained until the proceedings are
  finally concluded or an order for the return of the thing is made
  under subsection 14(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for return

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 15, where
  proceedings have been instituted as described in subsection 13(1), the
  owner of the thing or the person who was in possession of it at the
  time of the seizure may apply to the court before which the
  proceedings are held for an order that the thing be returned.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order for return

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, on an application under subsection (1), the
  court is satisfied that sufficient evidence exists or may reasonably
  be obtained without detaining the thing, the court may order that it
  be returned to the applicant, subject to any condition the court
  thinks fit to ensure that it is preserved for any purpose for which it
  may subsequently be required.

### [Forfeiture]{.HTitleText2} {#h-219156 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture on consent

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an inspector has seized a thing under subsection 11(1) and the owner
  of the thing or the person who was in lawful possession of it at the
  time of the seizure consents in writing at the request of the
  inspector to the forfeiture of the thing, it is thereupon forfeited to
  Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal or destruction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may dispose of or destroy a thing
  forfeited under subsection (1) and, if the Minister so directs, the
  costs of the disposal or destruction shall be paid by the owner of the
  thing or the person who was in lawful possession of it at the time of
  the seizure.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture by order of court

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a person is convicted of an offence under this Act and a thing seized
  under subsection 11(1) is then being detained,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the court so directs, the thing is, on the
    conviction and in addition to any punishment imposed for the
    offence, forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the thing shall, on the expiration of the time for
    taking an appeal from the conviction or on the final conclusion of
    the proceedings, as the case may be, be returned to the person from
    whom it was seized or to any other person entitled to possession of
    it, on such conditions, if any, as may be imposed by order of the
    court and as, in the opinion of the court, are necessary to avoid
    the commission of any further offence under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal or destruction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may dispose of or destroy a thing
  forfeited under subsection (1) and the costs of the forfeiture and the
  disposal or destruction shall be paid by the offender.

### [Illegal Import]{.HTitleText2} {#h-219169 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice for removal

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an inspector believes on reasonable grounds that an energy-using
  product is being or has been imported into Canada in contravention of
  this Act or the regulations, the inspector may, whether or not the
  product is seized, require the importer to remove it from Canada by
  delivering personally to the importer a notice for its removal or by
  sending the notice by registered mail to the importer's business
  address in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an energy-using product is not removed from
  Canada within the period specified for its removal in a notice under
  subsection (1) or, where no period is specified, within ninety days
  after the notice was delivered or sent to the importer, the product
  shall, notwithstanding sections 13 and 14, be forfeited to Her Majesty
  in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal or destruction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may dispose of or destroy an
  energy-using product forfeited under subsection (2) and, if the
  Minister so directs, the costs of the disposal or destruction shall be
  paid by the importer of the product.

### [Third Parties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-219177 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection of persons claiming interest

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Sections 74 to 76 of the
[Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) apply, with such modifications as the
circumstances require, to a thing forfeited under section 15 or 16, or
an energy-using product forfeited under section 17, as though it were a
thing forfeited under subsection 72(1) of that Act.

### [Warranty]{.HTitleText2} {#h-219180 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure is not warranty

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A disclosure relating to
energy efficiency that is required to be made by a dealer under this Act
in respect of tests conducted under this Act does not create an express
or implied warranty by anyone, including Her Majesty in right of Canada,
that the energy efficiency established by those tests will be achieved
under conditions of actual use.

### [Regulations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-219183 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing as an energy-using product any
    manufactured product, or class of manufactured products, that is
    designed to operate using electricity, oil, natural gas or any other
    form or source of energy or that affects or controls energy
    consumption;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing energy efficiency standards for
    energy-using products or classes of energy-using products;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the labelling of energy-using products
    or their packages, or classes of energy-using products or their
    packages;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} providing for the testing of energy-using products
    to determine their energy efficiency;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} respecting the detention of things seized and
    detained under subsection 6(4) or 11(1); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} respecting the disposition or destruction of
    anything forfeited under section 15, 16 or 17.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under paragraph (1)(b) or (d)
  incorporating standards by reference may incorporate the standards as
  amended from time to time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[20.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in
  this section and in section 20.2:

  [[harmonize]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[harmonize]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, with respect to

      substantively. ([harmoniser]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[jurisdiction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[jurisdiction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means 

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the government of a province;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any agency or body that is established under an
        Act of the legislature of a province;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a government or court of a foreign state or of
        a subdivision of a foreign state, or any agency, body or
        institution of such a government; and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an international organization of states or any
        agency, body, court or institution of such an
        organization. ([instance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[requirement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[requirement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means energy efficiency
      standards, testing or information that must be provided by a
      dealer under section 5. ([exigence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by regulation --- with respect to
  energy-using products or classes of energy-using products that are
  specified in regulations made by the Governor in Council under
  paragraph 25(c) --- amend regulations made under paragraph 20(1)(b)
  or (d) or 25(b) for the purpose of maintaining harmonization between a
  requirement set out in those regulations and that of a jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrictions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In exercising the power under subsection (2), the
  Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} update an erroneous reference to a document
    incorporated by reference, as it is amended from time to time;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} modify an energy efficiency standard for
    energy-using products or classes of energy-using products;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide for modified or alternative testing of
    energy-using products to determine their energy efficiency; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prescribe information respecting energy-using
    products, including their energy efficiency, that must be provided
    by a dealer under section 5.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[technical standards
document]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[20.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[technical standards document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means a document that is published in both official languages by the
  Minister and that adapts, combines or reproduces, in whole or in part,
  documents that are produced by jurisdictions, standards development
  organizations or industry associations and that, for energy-using
  products or classes of energy-using products, set out requirements or
  guidance related to those requirements. The adaptations may include
  modifications to the content of the originating document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation of technical standards
  document

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Regulations made under paragraphs 20(1)(b) or (d) or
  25(b) may, for the purpose of harmonizing the requirements set out in
  those regulations with those of a jurisdiction to which those
  regulations or a technical standards document refers, incorporate by

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Promotion of Energy Efficiency and Alternative Energy Sources]{.HTitleText1} {#h-219224 .Part}

### [Powers of Minister]{.HTitleText2} {#h-219225 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, for the
purpose of promoting the efficient use of energy and the use of
alternative energy sources,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} conduct, or cooperate with persons conducting,
  research, development, tests, demonstrations and studies;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} publish information, research or test results;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} assist, cooperate with, consult and enter into
  agreements with any person, including any department or agency of the
  Government of Canada or of any province;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} make grants and contributions; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} undertake such other projects, programs and
  activities as in the Minister's opinion advance that purpose.

### [Statistics]{.HTitleText2} {#h-219233 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations requiring prescribed persons to file with the
Minister, in the prescribed form and manner, at the prescribed time and
for each prescribed reporting period, a report setting out prescribed
statistics and information respecting

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the value, quantity, type and use of energy,
  including alternative energy, purchased, consumed or sold by that
  person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the expenditures of that person on the research,
  development, acquisition and operation of energy-using equipment and
  related technology; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the sales of prescribed energy-using products or
  classes of energy-using products by that person, including the revenue
  from, and geographic distribution of, the sales.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section and section 24,

  [[authorized person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[authorized person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person engaged
      or employed, or formerly engaged or employed, by or on behalf of
      Her Majesty in right of Canada for any purpose relating to the
      administration or enforcement of this Act; ([personne
      autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person employed in, or
      occupying a position of responsibility in, the service of Her
      Majesty in right of Canada, and any person formerly so employed or
      occupying such a position. ([fonctionnaire]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privileged information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as provided in section 24, the statistics and
  information filed with the Minister pursuant to regulations made under
  section 22 are privileged and no official or authorized person shall
  knowingly

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} communicate or allow to be communicated to any
    person any statistics or information so filed (in this section and
    section 24 referred to as "privileged information"); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} allow any person to inspect or have access to any
    report, statement or other document containing any privileged
    information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidentiary privilege

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding any other Act or law, no official or
  authorized person shall be required, in connection with any legal
  proceedings,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to give evidence relating to any privileged
    information; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to produce any report, statement or other document
    containing any privileged information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception for proceedings under this Act

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 23(2) and (3) do not
  apply in respect of proceedings relating to the administration or
  enforcement of this Act or the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception for authorized persons

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An authorized person may communicate or allow to be
  communicated privileged information, or allow inspection of or access
  to any report, statement or other document containing any privileged
  information, to or by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person engaged or employed by or on behalf of
    Her Majesty in right of Canada for any purpose relating to the
    administration or enforcement of this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Chief Statistician of Canada for the purposes
    of the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person, with the consent of the person who
    filed the information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception for compilations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Compilations of privileged information may be
  published in any manner that does not identify or permit the
  identification of any person who filed the information, unless that
  person consents in writing to being identified.

### [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-219270 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who contravenes subsection 4(1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
    conviction and liable to a fine not exceeding fifty thousand
    dollars; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an indictable offence and liable to a
    fine not exceeding two hundred thousand dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes subsection 4(2), section
  5, subsection 6(1), section 7 or 8, subsection 11(3), section 12 or
  subsection 23(2) or any regulation made under this Act is guilty of an
  offence punishable on summary conviction and liable to a fine not
  exceeding ten thousand dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who, in purported compliance with this
  Act or the regulations, submits any report, statistic, information,
  document or record, makes any statement or answers any question
  knowing that the report, statistic, information, document, record,
  statement or answer is false or misleading, or misrepresents or fails
  to disclose a material fact, is guilty of an offence punishable on
  summary conviction and liable to a fine not exceeding ten thousand
  dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes any provision of this
  Act or the regulations, other than a provision referred to in
  subsection (1) or (2), is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
  conviction and liable to a fine not exceeding five thousand dollars.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offences

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Where an offence under
section 27 is committed on more than one day or is continued for more
than one day, it shall be deemed to be a separate offence for each day
on which the offence is committed or continued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers, etc., of corporations

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** Where a corporation
commits an offence under section 27, any officer, director or agent of
the corporation who directed, authorized, assented to, acquiesced in or
participated in the commission of the offence is a party to and guilty
of the offence and is liable on conviction to the punishment provided
for the offence, whether or not the corporation has been prosecuted or
convicted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** In a prosecution for a
contravention of subsection 4(1) by a dealer engaged in the business of
assembling or altering energy-using products, it is a defence for the
dealer if the contravention occurred as a result of work done previously
on an energy-using product by another dealer engaged in the manufacture
of the energy-using products.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** A prosecution by way of
summary conviction under this Act may not be instituted later than two
years after the time when the subject-matter of the prosecution arose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Venue

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** A prosecution under this
Act may be instituted, tried and determined by a court in any
territorial jurisdiction in which the accused is resident or carries on
business regardless of where the subject-matter of the prosecution
arose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court order

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a person is convicted of an offence under section 27, in addition to
  any other punishment that may be imposed, the court may, having regard
  to the nature of the offence and the circumstances surrounding its
  commission, make an order to any or all of the following effects:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prohibiting the person from doing any act or
    engaging in any activity that may result in the continuation or
    repetition of the offence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to publish, in any manner the
    court considers appropriate, the facts relating to the commission of
    the offence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to compensate the Minister, in
    whole or in part, for the cost of examining and testing any
    energy-using product in respect of which the offence was committed;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to submit to the Minister, on
    application by the Minister within three years after the date of the
    conviction, such information respecting the activities of that
    person as the court considers appropriate in the circumstances;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to post such bond, or pay into
    court such amount of money, as the court considers appropriate for
    ensuring compliance with any order made under this subsection; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} requiring the person to comply with such other
    reasonable conditions as the court considers appropriate for
    securing the person's good conduct and preventing the person from
    repeating the offence or committing other offences under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An order under subsection (1) comes into force on the
  day on which it is made or on such other day as the court may fix and
  continues in force for such period, not exceeding three years, as the
  court may fix.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a person fails to comply with an order made
  under paragraph (1)(b), the Minister may publish the facts and recover
  the costs of publication from that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt due to Her Majesty

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The costs referred to in paragraph (1)(c) and
  subsection (3) constitute a debt due to Her Majesty in right of Canada
  and may be recovered as such in any court of competent jurisdiction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Variation of order

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  court that has made an order under section 33 may, on application by
  the Attorney General of Canada or the person to whom the order
  applies, require that person to appear before it and may, after
  hearing that person and the Attorney General, make an order varying
  the original order in any manner the court considers appropriate,
  including an order

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} extending the period for which the original order
    is to remain in force for a period not exceeding one year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} decreasing the period for which the original order
    is to remain in force; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} relieving that person, either absolutely or
    conditionally or for such period as the court considers appropriate,
    from compliance with any term of the original order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an application to vary an order has been heard
  under subsection (1), no other application may be made under that
  subsection in respect of that order except with leave of the court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes an order made under section 33 or 34

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
  conviction and liable to a fine not exceeding the maximum fine to
  which a person is liable on summary conviction for the original
  offence; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an indictable offence and liable to a
  fine not exceeding the maximum fine to which a person is liable on
  conviction on indictment for the original offence.

### [Report to Parliament]{.HTitleText2} {#h-219319 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall, as soon as possible after the end of each fiscal year,
  prepare and cause to be laid before each House of Parliament a report
  on the administration and enforcement of this Act for that year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Comparison of energy efficiency
  standards

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Once every three years, in the report referred to in
  subsection (1), the Minister shall demonstrate the extent to which the
  energy efficiency standards prescribed under this Act are as stringent
  as comparable standards established by a province, the United Mexican
  States, the United States of America or a state of the United States
  of America.
