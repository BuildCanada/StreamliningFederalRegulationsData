## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169766 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

the [National Security and Intelligence Review Agency

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169769 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a department named in Schedule I
      to the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the
      Minister presiding over the department;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a division or branch of the
      federal public administration set out in column I of Schedule I.1
      to the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the
      Minister set out in column II of that schedule;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a corporation named in Schedule
      II to the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the
      Minister designated as the appropriate Minister by order of the
      Governor in Council made under that Act;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a [[parent Crown
      corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 83(1)
      of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the
      [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in that
      subsection; or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the Canadian Forces, the Minister
      of National Defence. ([ministre compétent]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, other than in subsection
    42(2), a department named in Schedule I to the [Financial
    Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), a division or branch of the
    federal public administration  ---  other than a review body and the
    office of the Intelligence Commissioner ---  set out in column I of
    Schedule I.‍1 to that Act, a corporation named in Schedule II to that
    Act, a [[parent Crown corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined
    in subsection 83(1) of that Act or the Canadian
    Forces. ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[deputy head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deputy head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, except in section 42,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a department named in Schedule I
      to the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the deputy
      minister of that department;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the Canadian Forces, the Chief of
      the Defence Staff;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the Royal Canadian Mounted
      Police, the Commissioner of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the Canadian Security
      Intelligence Service, the Director;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} with respect to any other portion of the federal
      public administration, the person designated by order of the
      Governor in Council to be the deputy head of that portion of the
      federal public administration for the purposes of this Act; and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} with respect to any inquiry established under the
      [Inquiries Act](/eng/acts/I-11), if there is a single
      commissioner, that commissioner or, if there is more than one
      commissioner, the commissioner designated by order of the Governor
      in Council to be the deputy head of that inquiry for the purposes
      of this Act. ([administrateur général]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Director of the Canadian
    Security Intelligence Service. ([directeur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Review Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Review Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the National Security
    and Intelligence Review Agency established under section 3. ([Office
    de surveillance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[review body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[review body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Civilian Review and Complaints Commission for
      the Royal Canadian Mounted Police established by subsection
      45.29(1) of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
      Act](/eng/acts/R-10); or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the National Security and Intelligence Committee
      of Parliamentarians. ([organisme de surveillance]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

## [Review Agency and Members]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169789 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review Agency established

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The National Security and
Intelligence Review Agency is established, consisting of a Chair and no
fewer than three and no more than six other members.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of members

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council is to appoint, on the recommendation of the Prime
  Minister, the members of the Review Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member may be appointed to the Review Agency only
  after consultation by the Prime Minister with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Leader of the Government in the Senate or
    Government Representative in the Senate and the Leader of the
    Opposition in the Senate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Leader or Facilitator of every recognized party
    or parliamentary group in the Senate;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Leader of the Opposition in the House of
    Commons; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the leader in the House of Commons of each party
    having at least twelve members in that House.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each member of the Review Agency must be appointed to
  hold office during good behaviour for a term not exceeding five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Each member of the Review Agency, on the expiry of
  their first term of office, is eligible to be reappointed for one
  further term.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of Chair

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council is to designate, on the
  recommendation of the Prime Minister, the Chair of the Review Agency
  from among the members of the Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of Vice-chair

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may designate, on the
  recommendation of the Prime Minister, the Vice-chair of the Review
  Agency from among the members of the Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Full- or part-time members

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Chair and Vice-chair may be designated to hold
  office on a full-time or part-time basis. Every member of the Review
  Agency who is not designated as the Chair or Vice-chair holds office
  on a part-time basis.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting Chair

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Chair is absent or incapacitated or if the office of Chair is vacant,
  the Vice-chair of the Review Agency is to act as the Chair; but if the
  Vice-chair is absent or incapacitated, or if that office is vacant,
  the Chair may designate another member of the Agency to act as the
  Chair or, if no such designation is made, the Agency must designate a
  member of the Agency to act as the Chair.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No member designated under subsection (1) may act as
  the Chair for more that 90 days without the approval of the Governor
  in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and expenses

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  member of the Review Agency who holds office on a part-time basis is
  entitled to be paid, for each day that the member exercises powers and
  performs duties and functions under this Act, the remuneration that is
  fixed by the Governor in Council and, in accordance with Treasury
  Board directives, reasonable travel and living expenses incurred in
  the exercise of their powers and the performance of their duties and
  functions while absent from their ordinary place of residence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Office held on full-time basis

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chair and Vice-chair, if they are designated to
  hold office on a full-time basis, are entitled to be paid the
  remuneration that is fixed by the Governor in Council and, in
  accordance with Treasury Board directives, reasonable travel and
  living expenses incurred in the exercise of their powers and the
  performance of their duties and functions while absent from their
  ordinary place of work.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acts and regulations applicable

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Each member of the Review
Agency is deemed to be an employee for the purposes of the [Government
Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and to be employed in the
federal public administration for the purposes of any regulations made
under section 9 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2). Each full-time
member of the Agency is also deemed to be employed in the public service
for the purposes of the [Public Service Superannuation
Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agency procedures

**[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Act,
the Review Agency may determine the procedure to be followed in the
exercise of its powers or the performance of any of its duties or
functions.

## [Mandate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169825 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review and investigation

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  mandate of the Review Agency is to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} review any activity carried out by the Canadian
    Security Intelligence Service or the Communications Security
    Establishment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} review any activity carried out by a department
    that relates to national security or intelligence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} review any matter that relates to national security
    or intelligence that a minister of the Crown refers to the Agency;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} investigate

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any complaint made under subsection 16(1), 17(1)
      or 18(3),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any complaint referred to the Agency under
      subsection 45.53(4.1) or 45.67(2.1) of the [Royal Canadian Mounted
      Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10),

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} reports made to the Agency under section 19 of
      the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29), and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} matters referred to the Agency under section 45
      of the [Canadian Human Rights Act](/eng/acts/H-6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of measures

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the course of its review of activities carried out
  by the Canadian Security Intelligence Service, the Review Agency must,
  each calendar year, review at least one aspect of the Service's
  performance in taking measures to reduce threats to the security of
  Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review --- ministerial direction

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} The Review Agency must review the implementation of
  significant aspects of every new or modified ministerial direction
  that is issued to any of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Security Intelligence Service;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Communications Security Establishment; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other department if the ministerial direction
    relates to national security or intelligence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Avoiding Complicity in Mistreatment by
  Foreign Entities Act](/eng/acts/A-18.8)

  [(2.2)]{.lawlabel} In the course of its review of activities carried
  out by departments, the Review Agency must, each calendar year, review
  the implementation of all directions issued under the [Avoiding
  Complicity in Mistreatment by Foreign Entities Act](/eng/acts/A-18.8).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Findings and recommendations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the course of its reviews, the Review Agency may
  make any finding or recommendation that it considers appropriate,
  including findings and recommendations relating to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a department's compliance with the law and any
    applicable ministerial directions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reasonableness and necessity of a department's
    exercise of its powers.

## [Access to Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169848 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of access --- reviews

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite
  any other Act of Parliament and subject to section 12, the Review
  Agency is entitled, in relation to its reviews, to have access in a
  timely manner to any information that is in the possession or under
  the control of any department.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protected information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Under subsection (1), the Review Agency is entitled
  to have access to information that is subject to any privilege under
  the law of evidence, solicitor-client privilege or the professional
  secrecy of advocates and notaries or to litigation privilege.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the disclosure to the Review
  Agency under this section of any information that is subject to
  solicitor-client privilege or the professional secrecy of advocates
  and notaries or to litigation privilege does not constitute a waiver
  of those privileges or that secrecy.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of access --- complaints

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Despite any other Act of
Parliament and any privilege under the law of evidence and subject to
section 12, the Review Agency is entitled to have access in a timely
manner to the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a complaint made under subsection
  16(1), any information that relates to the complaint and that is in
  the possession or under the control of the Canadian Security
  Intelligence Service or the Communications Security Establishment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a complaint made under subsection
  17(1), any information that relates to the complaint and that is in
  the possession or under the control of the Canadian Security
  Intelligence Service or the Communications Security Establishment;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a complaint made under subsection
  18(3), any information that relates to the complaint and that is in
  the possession or under the control of the deputy head concerned, the
  Canadian Security Intelligence Service or the Communications Security
  Establishment; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a complaint referred to it under
  subsection 45.53(4.1) or 45.67(2.1) of the [Royal Canadian Mounted
  Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10), any information that relates to the
  complaint and that is in the possession or under the control of the
  Civilian Review and Complaints Commission for the Royal Canadian
  Mounted Police established by subsection 45.29(1) of the [Royal
  Canadian Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10), the Royal Canadian
  Mounted Police, the Canadian Security Intelligence Service or the
  Communications Security Establishment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents and explanations

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Under
  sections 9 and 10, the Review Agency is entitled to receive from the
  deputy head or employees of the department concerned any documents and
  explanations that the Agency deems necessary for the exercise of its
  powers and the performance of its duties and functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision --- Review Agency

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of sections 9 and 10, the Review
  Agency is entitled to decide whether information relates to the review
  or complaint in question.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency or conflict

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the event of any inconsistency or conflict between
  sections 9 and 10 and any provision of an Act of Parliament other than
  this Act, section 9 or 10 prevails to the extent of the inconsistency
  or conflict.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Review Agency is not
entitled to have access to a confidence of the Queen's Privy Council for
Canada the disclosure of which could be refused under section 39 of the
[Canada Evidence Act](/eng/acts/C-5).

## [Complaints]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169890 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaints --- Canadian Security
Intelligence Service

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person may make a complaint to the Review Agency with respect to any
  activity carried out by the Canadian Security Intelligence Service and
  the Agency must, subject to subsection (2), investigate the complaint
  if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the complainant has made a complaint to the
    Director with respect to that activity and the complainant has not
    received a response within a period of time that the Agency
    considers reasonable or is dissatisfied with the response given; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Agency is satisfied that the complaint is not
    trivial, frivolous or vexatious or made in bad faith.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other redress available

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Review Agency must not investigate a complaint in
  respect of which the complainant is entitled to seek redress by means
  of a grievance procedure established under the [Canadian Security
  Intelligence Service Act](/eng/acts/C-23) or the [Federal Public
  Sector Labour Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-33.3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaints --- Communications Security
Establishment

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person may make a complaint to the Review Agency with respect to any
  activity carried out by the Communications Security Establishment and
  the Agency must, subject to subsection (2), investigate the complaint
  if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the complainant has made a complaint to the Chief
    of the Communications Security Establishment with respect to that
    activity and the complainant has not received a response within a
    period of time that the Agency considers reasonable or is
    dissatisfied with the response given; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Agency is satisfied that the complaint is not
    trivial, frivolous or vexatious or made in bad faith.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other redress available

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Review Agency must not investigate a complaint in
  respect of which the complainant is entitled to seek redress by means
  of a grievance procedure established under the [Communications
  Security Establishment Act](/eng/acts/C-35.3) or the [Federal Public
  Sector Labour Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-33.3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Denial of security clearance

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, by
  reason only of the denial of a security clearance required by the
  Government of Canada, a decision is made by a deputy head to deny
  employment to an individual or to dismiss, demote or transfer an
  individual or to deny a promotion or transfer to an individual, the
  deputy head must send, within 10 days after the decision is made, a
  notice informing the individual of the denial of the security
  clearance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Denial of security clearance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, by reason only of the denial of a security
  clearance required by the Government of Canada to be given in respect
  of an individual, a decision is made to deny the individual or any
  other person a contract to provide goods or services to the Government
  of Canada, the deputy head concerned must send, within 10 days after
  the decision is made, a notice informing the individual and, if
  applicable, the other person of the denial of the security clearance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt and investigation of complaints

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Review Agency must receive and investigate a
  complaint from

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any individual referred to in subsection (1) who
    has been denied a security clearance; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person who has been denied a contract to
    provide goods or services to the Government of Canada by reason only
    of the denial of a security clearance in respect of that person or
    any individual.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time within which complaint is to be
  made

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A complaint under subsection (3) must be made within
  30 days after receipt of the notice referred to in subsection (1)
  or (2) or within any longer period that the Review Agency allows.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaints --- Royal Canadian Mounted
Police

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Review Agency must
receive and investigate a complaint referred to it under subsection
45.53(4.1) or 45.67(2.1) of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
Act](/eng/acts/R-10) if the Agency is satisfied that the complaint is
not trivial, frivolous or vexatious or made in bad faith.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaints submitted on behalf of
complainants

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act
precludes the Review Agency from receiving and investigating complaints
referred to in subsections 16(1), 17(1) and 18(3) that are submitted by
a person authorized by the complainant to act on behalf of the
complainant, and a reference to a complainant in any other provision of
this Act includes a reference to a person so authorized.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written complaint

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A complaint under
subsection 16(1), 17(1) or 18(3) must be made to the Review Agency in
writing unless the Agency authorizes otherwise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement to complainant

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The Review Agency must,
as soon as feasible after receiving a complaint made under subsection
18(3), send to the complainant a statement summarizing the information
available to the Agency that will enable the complainant to be as fully
informed as possible of the circumstances giving rise to the denial of
the security clearance and must send a copy of the statement to the
Director and the deputy head concerned.

## [Informal Resolution]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169924 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informal resolution

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Review Agency may attempt to resolve the complaint informally.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement to informal resolution in
  writing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The resolution of a complaint through an informal
  process, as well as the agreement of the parties to that resolution,
  must be set out in writing and a copy is to be provided to the Review
  Agency.

## [Investigations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169930 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of intention to investigate

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Before commencing an
investigation referred to in subsection 18(3), the Review Agency must
inform the Director and, if applicable, the deputy head concerned of its
intention to carry out the investigation and of the substance of the
matter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigations in private

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  investigation by the Review Agency is to be conducted in private.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to make representations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the course of an investigation of a complaint, the
  complainant, the deputy head concerned and, if the complaint is made
  under subsection 18(3), the Director must be given an opportunity to
  make representations to the Review Agency, to present evidence and to
  be heard personally or by counsel, but no one is entitled as of right
  to be present during, to have access to or to comment on
  representations made to the Agency by any other person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian Human Rights Commission may
comment

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** In the course of an
investigation of a complaint, the Review Agency must, if appropriate,
ask the Canadian Human Rights Commission for its opinion or comments
with respect to the complaint.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Review Agency

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** The Review Agency has, in
relation to the investigation of any complaint, the power

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to summon and enforce the appearance of persons
  before the Agency and to compel them to give oral or written evidence
  on oath and to produce the documents and things that the Agency deems
  requisite to the full investigation and consideration of the complaint
  in the same manner and to the same extent as a superior court of
  record;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to administer oaths; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to receive and accept the evidence and other
  information, whether on oath or by affidavit or otherwise, that the
  Agency considers appropriate, whether or not that evidence or
  information is or would be admissible in a court of law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to suspend

**[[27.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27.1 .sectionLabel}** Despite any provision
of this Act, the Review Agency must suspend an investigation if, after
consultation with the appropriate department, the Agency is of the
opinion that continuing the investigation would compromise or seriously
hinder an ongoing criminal investigation or proceeding.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence in other proceedings

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Except in a prosecution
under section 132 or 136 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) in
respect of a statement made under this Act, evidence given by a person
in proceedings under this Act and evidence of the existence of the
proceedings are inadmissible against that person in a court or in any
other proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report of findings

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Review Agency must,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on completion of an investigation in relation to a
    complaint under subsection 16(1), provide the appropriate Minister
    and the Director with a report containing the findings of the
    investigation and any recommendations that the Agency considers
    appropriate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on completion of an investigation in relation to a
    complaint under subsection 17(1), provide the appropriate Minister
    and the Chief of the Communications Security Establishment with a
    report containing the findings of the investigation and any
    recommendations that the Agency considers appropriate; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on completion of an investigation in relation to a
    complaint referred to it under subsection 45.53(4.1) or 45.67(2.1)
    of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10), provide
    the appropriate Minister and the Commissioner of the Royal Canadian
    Mounted Police with a report containing the findings of the
    investigation and any recommendations that the Agency considers
    appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complainant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} After providing a report under any of paragraphs
  (1)(a) to (c), the Review Agency must report the findings of the
  investigation to the complainant and may report to the complainant any
  recommendations it thinks fit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report --- denial of security clearance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On completion of an investigation in relation to a
  complaint under subsection 18(3), the Review Agency must provide the
  Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness, the Director,
  the deputy head concerned and the complainant with a report containing
  any recommendations that the Agency considers appropriate, and those
  findings of the investigation that the Agency thinks fit to report to
  the complainant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review Agency member authorized to act
alone

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** A member of the Review
Agency may exercise any of the powers or perform any of the duties and
functions of the Agency under sections 16 to 29 in relation to
complaints.

## [Departmental Study]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169961 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power --- Review Agency

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of ensuring that a department's activities that relate to
  national security or intelligence are carried out in compliance with
  the law and any applicable ministerial directions, and are reasonable
  and necessary, the Review Agency may direct the department to conduct
  a study of those activities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A department that conducts a study under
  subsection (1) must provide the appropriate Minister with a report on
  the study and must give a copy of the report to the Review Agency at
  the same time.

## [Reports to Ministers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169967 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report --- Canadian Security
Intelligence Service

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Review Agency must, in relation to every calendar year, submit to the
  appropriate Minister a report with respect to the activities of the
  Canadian Security Intelligence Service during that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The annual report must include information relating
  to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the compliance of the Canadian Security
    Intelligence Service with the law and any applicable ministerial
    directions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reasonableness and necessity of the Canadian
    Security Intelligence Service's exercise of its powers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report --- Communications Security
Establishment

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Review Agency must, in relation to every calendar year, submit to the
  appropriate Minister a report with respect to the activities of the
  Communications Security Establishment during that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The annual report must include information relating
  to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the compliance of the Communications Security
    Establishment with the law and any applicable ministerial
    directions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reasonableness and necessity of the
    Communications Security Establishment's exercise of its powers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report of review

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The Review Agency may
submit to the appropriate Minister a report with respect to any matter
that relates to a department and that is the subject of a review by the
Agency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance report

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Review Agency must submit to the appropriate Minister a report with
  respect to any activity that is related to national security or
  intelligence and carried out by a department if, in the Agency's
  opinion, the activity may not be in compliance with the law. The
  Agency must give a copy of the report to the deputy head concerned.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report and comments to Attorney General
  of Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} As soon as feasible after the Minister receives a
  report under subsection (1), he or she must give a copy of the report
  to the Attorney General of Canada, together with any comment that he
  or she considers appropriate in the circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy to Review Agency

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must give a copy of anything given to
  the Attorney General of Canada under subsection (2) to the Review
  Agency at the same time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy to Intelligence Commissioner

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** The Review Agency must
give a copy of any report, or any part of a report, that it submits
under any of sections 32 to 35 to the Intelligence Commissioner if that
report or part relates to that Commissioner's powers, duties and
functions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual briefing --- Canadian Security
Intelligence Service

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At
  least once a calendar year the Chair of the Review Agency, or a person
  designated by the Chair, must meet with the appropriate Minister and
  brief him or her on the exercise of, or the performance by, the
  Canadian Security Intelligence Service of its powers, duties and
  functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual briefing --- Communications
  Security Establishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At least once a calendar year the Chair of the Review
  Agency, or a person designated by the Chair, must meet with the
  appropriate Minister and brief him or her on the exercise of, or the
  performance by, the Communications Security Establishment of its
  powers, duties and functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other briefings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chair of the Review Agency, or a person
  designated by the Chair, may meet with any appropriate Minister and
  brief him or her on a department's performance in carrying out any
  activity related to national security or intelligence.

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  year and the findings and recommendations it made during the calendar
  year in question.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling

  the report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Security of Canada Information
Disclosure Act](/eng/acts/S-6.9)

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Review Agency must, every calendar year, submit to the Minister of
  Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness a report respecting the
  disclosure of information under the [Security of Canada Information
  Disclosure Act](/eng/acts/S-6.9) during the previous calendar year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
  Preparedness must cause to be laid before each House of Parliament, on
  any of the first 15 days on which that House is sitting after a report
  is submitted under subsection (1), a copy of the report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special report

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Review Agency is of the opinion that it is in the public interest to
  report on any matter related to its mandate, it may submit a special
  report to the appropriate Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate Minister must cause to be laid before
  each House of Parliament, on any of the first 15 days on which that
  House is sitting after a report is submitted under subsection (1), a
  copy of the report.

## [Secretariat]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170016 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Secretariat established

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  National Security and Intelligence Review Agency Secretariat is
  established.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Secretariat is to assist the Review Agency in
  fulfilling its mandate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Executive director

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  is to be an executive director of the Secretariat, who is to be
  appointed by the Governor in Council to hold office during pleasure
  for a term of up to five years. The executive director is eligible to
  be reappointed on the expiry of a first or subsequent term of office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy head

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The executive director has the rank and all the
  powers of a deputy head of a department.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** If the executive director
is absent or incapacitated or the office of executive director is
vacant, the Governor in Council may appoint another person to act as
executive director.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and expenses

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  executive director is entitled to be paid the remuneration that is
  fixed by the Governor in Council and is entitled to be paid, in
  accordance with Treasury Board directives, reasonable travel and
  living expenses incurred in the performance of his or her duties or
  functions while absent from his or her ordinary place of work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The executive director is deemed to be employed in
  the public service for the purposes of the [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), to be an employee for the
  purposes of the [Government Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5)
  and to be employed in the federal public administration for the
  purposes of any regulations made under section 9 of the [Aeronautics
  Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personnel

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  executive director has exclusive authority to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint or lay off the Secretariat's employees,
    revoke their appointment or terminate their employment; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish standards, procedures and processes
    governing staffing, including the appointment or lay-off of
    employees, revocation of their appointment or termination of their
    employment otherwise than for cause.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of employer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in the [Federal Public Sector Labour
  Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-33.3) is to be construed so as to affect
  the right or authority of the executive director to deal with the
  matters referred to in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Political activities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Part 7 of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) applies to the executive director and the
  Secretariat's employees. For the purposes of that Part, the executive
  director is deemed to be a [[deputy head]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, and
  the Secretariat's employees are deemed to be employees, as defined in
  subsection 2(1) of that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of the executive director

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** In exercising his or her
authority under subsection 45(1), the executive director may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine the human resources requirements of the
  Secretariat and provide for the allocation and effective utilization
  of human resources in the Secretariat;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide for the classification of positions in and
  employees of the Secretariat;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} after consultation with the President of the Treasury
  Board, determine and regulate the pay to which Secretariat employees
  are entitled for services rendered, the hours of work and leave of
  those employees and any related matters;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} after consultation with the President of the Treasury
  Board, determine and regulate the payments that may be made to
  Secretariat employees by way of reimbursement for travel or other
  expenses and by way of allowances in respect of expenses and
  conditions arising out of their employment;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} determine the learning, training and development
  requirements of the Secretariat employees and fix the terms on which
  the learning, training and development may be carried out;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} provide for the awards that may be made to
  Secretariat employees for outstanding performance of their duties, for
  other meritorious achievement in relation to their duties or for
  inventions or practical suggestions for improvements;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} establish standards of discipline and set penalties,
  including termination of employment, suspension, demotion to a
  position at a lower maximum rate of pay and financial penalties;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} provide for the termination of employment, or the
  demotion to a position at a lower maximum rate of pay, of Secretariat
  employees for reasons other than breaches of discipline or misconduct;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} establish policies respecting the exercise of the
  powers granted by this section; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} provide for any other matters, including terms and
  conditions of employment not otherwise specifically provided for in
  this section, that the executive director considers necessary for
  effective human resources management in the Secretariat.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Negotiation of collective agreements

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** Before entering into
collective bargaining with the bargaining agent for a bargaining unit
composed of Secretariat employees, the executive director must have the
Secretariat's negotiating mandate approved by the President of the
Treasury Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Technical assistance

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** The executive director
may engage the services of persons having technical or specialized
knowledge to advise and assist the Review Agency in the exercise of its
powers and the performance of its duties and functions and, with the
approval of the Treasury Board, may fix and pay their remuneration and
expenses.

## [Security and Confidentiality]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170059 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oath or solemn affirmation

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** Each member of the Review
Agency must take the following oath or make the following solemn
affirmation:

I, ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}, swear (solemnly
affirm) that I will, to the best of my ability, discharge my
responsibilities as a member of the National Security and Intelligence
Review Agency and will not communicate or use without due authority any
information obtained in confidence by me in that capacity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with security requirements

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** Each member of the Review
Agency, the executive director, each employee of the Secretariat and any
person whose services the Secretariat engages under section 48 must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain the security clearance required by the
  Government of Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} follow established procedures or practices, including
  any requirement found in a Treasury Board policy, guideline or
  directive, for the secure handling, storage, transportation and
  transmission of information or documents.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure prohibited

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** A member or former member
of the Review Agency, the executive director or a former executive
director of the Secretariat or a person who is or was engaged by the
Secretariat may disclose information that they obtained, or to which
they had access, in the course of exercising their powers or performing
their duties and functions under this Act only for the purpose of
exercising their powers or performing their duties and functions under
this Act or as required by any other law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection of confidential information

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Review Agency must consult with the deputy heads concerned in
  preparing any of the following, in order to ensure that they do not
  contain information the disclosure of which would be injurious to
  national security, national defence or international relations or is
  information that is subject to solicitor-client privilege or the
  professional secrecy of advocates and notaries or to litigation
  privilege:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement under section 22 of this Act,
    subsection 45(6) of the [Canadian Human Rights Act](/eng/acts/H-6)
    or subsection 19(5) of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a report under subsection 29(2) or (3) or any of
    sections 38 to 40 of this Act, subsection 46(1) of the [Canadian
    Human Rights Act](/eng/acts/H-6) or subsection 19(6) of the
    [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional consultation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the consultation set out in
  subsection (1) and for the same purpose, the Review Agency must
  consult with the Director

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in preparing a statement under section 22 of this
    Act, subsection 45(6) of the [Canadian Human Rights
    Act](/eng/acts/H-6) or subsection 19(5) of the [Citizenship
    Act](/eng/acts/C-29);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in preparing a report under subsection 29(3) of
    this Act, subsection 46(1) of the [Canadian Human Rights
    Act](/eng/acts/H-6) or subsection 19(6) of the [Citizenship
    Act](/eng/acts/C-29); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the Agency considers it appropriate, in
    preparing any other report referred to in paragraph (1)(b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Independence of peace officer

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** The Review Agency must,
if appropriate, consult with the department concerned in preparing any
report referred to in sections 32 to 34 and 38 to 40 to ensure that the
report does not contain information relating to the alleged
contravention of any law of Canada or a province that, if it were to be
disclosed to any Minister, may be seen to jeopardize the independence of
a peace officer having jurisdiction to investigate the alleged
contravention.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170080 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers not limited

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act is to
be construed as limiting the power of any body or person that is
authorized under an Act of Parliament to conduct a review or
investigation in relation to any activity of a department.

## [Governor in Council Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170083 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designations

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in council
may, by order, designate

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a federal Minister as the Minister responsible for
  this Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person to act as deputy head of a portion of the
  federal public administration for the purposes of paragraph (e) of the
  definition [[deputy head]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a commissioner appointed under the [Inquiries
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11) to act as deputy head of an inquiry for the
  purposes of paragraph (f) of the definition [[deputy
  head]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 3

  - ###### Definitions {#definitions .MarginalNote}

    [3]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in sections 4 to 17.

    [[former Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[former Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
        Commissioner of the Communications Security Establishment
        appointed under subsection 273.63(1) of the [National Defence
        Act](/eng/acts/N-5), as it read immediately before the coming
        into force of section 68. ([ancien commissaire]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

    [[former Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[former Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Security
        Intelligence Review Committee established by subsection 34(1) of
        the [Canadian Security Intelligence Service
        Act](/eng/acts/C-23), as it read immediately before the coming
        into force of section 2. ([ancien comité]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

    [[new Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[new Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the National Security
        and Intelligence Review Agency. ([nouvel
        office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 4

  - ###### Chairman of former Committee {#chairman-of-former-committee .MarginalNote}

    - [4]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} The person who, immediately before
      the day on which section 2 comes into force, held office as
      Chairman of the former Committee ceases to be Chairman and is
      continued in office as a member of the new Agency for the
      remainder of the term for which he or she was appointed.

    - ###### Members of former Committee {#members-of-former-committee .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The members of the former Committee who,
      immediately before the day on which section 2 comes into force,
      held office are continued in office as members of the new Agency
      for the remainder of the term for which they were appointed.

    - ###### Chair of new Agency {#chair-of-new-agency .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} As soon as feasible after the day on which
      section 2 comes into force, the Governor in Council must
      designate, under subsection 4(5) of the [National Security and
      Intelligence Review Agency Act](/eng/acts/N-16.62), the Chair of
      the new Agency from among the members who continue in office under
      subsection (1) or (2) or who are appointed under subsection 4(1)
      of that Act.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 5

  - ###### Employment continued --- staff {#employment-continued-staff .MarginalNote}

    - [5]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Part is to be
      construed as affecting the status of any person who, immediately
      before the day on which section 2 comes into force, occupies a
      position in the former Committee, except that the person is to, as
      of that day, occupy their position in the Secretariat of the new
      Agency.

    - ###### Managerial or confidential position {#managerial-or-confidential-position .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a person's status includes
      whether or not they occupy a managerial or confidential position.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 6

  - ###### Powers, duties and functions {#powers-duties-and-functions .MarginalNote}

    [6]{.lawlabel} A person referred to in section 5 whom the former
    Committee has authorized to exercise certain powers or perform
    certain duties or functions relating to a matter before that former
    Committee, on the day on which section 2 comes into force, retains
    the authority to exercise those powers and perform those duties and
    functions.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 7

  - ###### Appropriations {#appropriations .MarginalNote}

    [7]{.lawlabel} Any money that is appropriated by an Act of
    Parliament, for the fiscal year in which section 2 comes into force,
    to defray any expenditure of the former Committee and that, on the
    day on which section 2 comes into force, is unexpended is deemed, on
    that day, to be an amount appropriated to defray any expenditure of
    the new Agency.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 8

  - ###### Rights and obligations {#rights-and-obligations .MarginalNote}

    [8]{.lawlabel} All rights and property of the former Committee and
    of Her Majesty in right of Canada that are under the administration
    and control of the former Committee and all obligations of the
    former Committee are transferred to the new Agency.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 9

  - ###### Contracts {#contracts .MarginalNote}

    [9]{.lawlabel} A contract respecting the provision of services or
    materiel to the former Committee entered into before the day on
    which section 2 comes into force is deemed to have been entered into
    by the executive director of the Secretariat of the new Agency.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 10

  - ###### Information --- former Committee {#information-former-committee .MarginalNote}

    [10]{.lawlabel} The former Committee must transfer to the new Agency
    all information under its control, including information that
    relates to a complaint before that former Committee, or to any
    review or investigation that is being undertaken by that Committee,
    immediately before the day on which section 2 comes into force.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 11

  - ###### Complaints --- former Committee {#complaints-former-committee .MarginalNote}

    - [11]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} The new Agency becomes seized of
      any complaint made to the former Committee before the day on which
      section 2 comes into force and that complaint is deemed to have
      been made to the new Agency.

    - ###### Reports and matters {#reports-and-matters .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The new Agency becomes seized of any report made
      under section 19 of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29), and any
      matter referred under section 45 of the [Canadian Human Rights
      Act](/eng/acts/H-6), to the former Committee before the day on
      which section 2 comes into force and that report or matter is
      deemed to have been made or referred, as the case may be, to the
      new Agency.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 12

  - ###### First report {#first-report .MarginalNote}

    - [12]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} The first report that the new
      Agency submits under each of subsections 32(1) and 38(1) of the
      [National Security and Intelligence Review Agency
      Act](/eng/acts/N-16.62) must include information in respect of any
      period for which the former Committee has not yet submitted a
      report under section 53 of the [Canadian Security Intelligence
      Service Act](/eng/acts/C-23), as it read immediately before the
      coming into force of section 2.

    - ###### First report --- Communications Security Establishment {#first-report-communications-security-establishment .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The first report that the new Agency submits
      under each of subsections 33(1) and 38(1) of the [National
      Security and Intelligence Review Agency Act](/eng/acts/N-16.62)
      must include information in respect of any period for which the
      former Commissioner has not yet submitted a report under
      subsection 273.63(3) of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5),
      as it read immediately before the coming into force of section 2.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 13

  - ###### Commencement of proceedings {#commencement-of-proceedings .MarginalNote}

    - [13]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal
      or administrative proceeding that could have been brought against
      the former Committee may be brought against the new Agency in any
      court that would have had jurisdiction if the action, suit or
      proceeding had been brought against the former Committee.

    - ###### Pending proceedings {#pending-proceedings .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal or administrative
      proceeding to which the former Committee is a party that is
      pending immediately before the day on which section 2 comes into
      force may be continued by or against the new Agency in the same
      manner and to the same extent as it could have been continued by
      or against the former Committee.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 14

  - ###### No compensation {#no-compensation .MarginalNote}

    [14]{.lawlabel} Despite the provisions of any contract, agreement or
    order, no person appointed to hold office as Chairman or as a member
    of the former Committee has any right to claim or receive any
    compensation, damages, indemnity or other form of relief from Her
    Majesty in right of Canada or from any employee or agent or
    mandatary of Her Majesty for ceasing to hold that office or for the
    abolition of that office by the operation of this Part.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 15

  - ###### Information --- former Commissioner {#information-former-commissioner .MarginalNote}

    [15]{.lawlabel} The former Commissioner must transfer to the new
    Agency all information under his or her control, including
    information that relates to a complaint before that former
    Commissioner, or to any review or investigation that is being
    undertaken by that former Commissioner, immediately before the day
    on which section 2 comes into force.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 16

  - ###### Complaints --- former Commissioner {#complaints-former-commissioner .MarginalNote}

    [16]{.lawlabel} The new Agency becomes seized of any complaint made
    to the former Commissioner before the day on which section 2 comes
    into force and that complaint is deemed to have been made to the new
    Agency.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 17

  - ###### Commencement of proceedings {#commencement-of-proceedings-1 .MarginalNote}

    - [17]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal
      or administrative proceeding, relating to a review or an
      investigation, that could have been brought against the former
      Commissioner may be brought against the new Agency in any court
      that would have had jurisdiction if the action, suit or proceeding
      had been brought against the former Commissioner.

    - ###### Pending proceedings {#pending-proceedings-1 .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal or administrative
      proceeding to which the former Commissioner is a party that
      relates to a review or an investigation and that is pending
      immediately before the day on which section 2 comes into force may
      be continued by or against the new Agency in the same manner and
      to the same extent as it could have been continued by or against
      the former Commissioner.

- --- 2019, c. 13, par. 82(1)(f), ss. (2)

  - ###### References {#references .MarginalNote}

    - [82]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} A reference to the former
      department in any of the following is deemed to be a reference to
      the new department:

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the [National Security and Intelligence Review
        Agency Act](/eng/acts/N-16.62); and

    - ###### Deputy head {#deputy-head .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designation of a person as deputy head of the
      former department in any order of the Governor in Council made
      under section 55 of the [National Security and Intelligence Review
      Agency Act](/eng/acts/N-16.62) is deemed to be a designation of
      the Chief of the new department as deputy head of that department.
