## An Act respecting the Hudson Bay Mining and Smelting Co., Limited {#an-act-respecting-the-hudson-bay-mining-and-smelting-co.-limited .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1947, c. 62
:::

Assented to 1947-07-17

An Act respecting the Hudson Bay Mining and Smelting Co., Limited
::::

:::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Works declared for the advantage of two
or more provinces

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** It is hereby declared that
the works and undertakings of Hudson Bay Mining and Smelting Co.,
Limited, in the Flin Flon mineral area on both sides of the boundary
between Manitoba and Saskatchewan, are works for the advantage of two or
more of the provinces.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicable safety and health legislation

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  provisions of the laws of Manitoba referred to in the schedule apply
  to the Hudson Bay Mining and Smelting Co., Limited and its employees
  in respect of employment on and in the works and undertakings referred
  to in section 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of schedule

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by regulation, amend the
  schedule

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by adding thereto a reference to any Act of the
    Legislature of Manitoba or any regulation of the Lieutenant Governor
    in Council of Manitoba relating to the protection of safety or
    health;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by adding thereto a reference to such an Act or
    regulation as amended to a specified date, or as amended from time
    to time; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by deleting any reference therefrom.

::: HistoricalNote
- S.C. 1987, c. 52, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and penalty

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who, in doing anything or failing to do anything, contravenes
  any provision of a law of Manitoba referred to in the schedule in
  respect of employment described in subsection 2(1) is guilty of an
  offence against this Act and is liable to the same punishment as is
  imposed by or under any Act of the Legislature of Manitoba for an
  offence against that provision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The prosecution of an offence in respect of a
  provision referred to in subsection (1) shall be commenced by the
  Attorney General of Manitoba or an agent thereof and conducted in
  accordance with the laws of Manitoba applicable to the prosecution of
  offences against that provision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition of fines

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The proceeds of any fine imposed in proceedings under
  this section, or of any recognizance forfeited in such proceedings,
  belong to Her Majesty in right of Manitoba and shall be paid to the
  Treasurer of Manitoba

::: HistoricalNote
- S.C. 1987, c. 52, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2)

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The provisions of the
[Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2) relating to occupational safety and
health do not apply to the Hudson Bay Mining and Smelting Co., Limited
or its employees in respect of employment described in subsection 2(1)

::: HistoricalNote
- S.C. 1987, c. 52, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Workers compensation not affected

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act affects
the operation of any Act of a province in relation to compensation for
injuries sustained or industrial diseases contracted by workers in the
course of their employment.

::: HistoricalNote
- S.C. 1987, c. 52, s. 1
:::

###### [PART I]{.HLabel}[Acts]{.HTitleText}

- **1** The Elevator Act, chapter E60 of the Continuing Consolidation of
  the Statutes of Manitoba, as re-enacted by The Re-enacted Statutes of
  Manitoba, 1987, Act, as amended from time to time.

- **2** The Fires Prevention Act, chapter F80 of the Continuing
  Consolidation of the Statutes of Manitoba, as re-enacted by The
  Re-enacted Statutes of Manitoba, 1987, Act, as amended from time to
  time.

- **3** The Gas and Oil Burner Act, chapter G30 of the Continuing
  Consolidation of the Statutes of Manitoba, as re-enacted by The
  Re-enacted Statutes of Manitoba, 1987, Act, as amended from time to
  time.

  Consolidation of the Statutes of Manitoba, as re-enacted by The
  Re-enacted Statutes of Manitoba, 1987, Act, as amended from time to
