## National Arts Centre Act {#national-arts-centre-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. N-3
:::

An Act to establish a corporation for the administration of the National
Arts Centre
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374033 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [National Arts Centre Act](/eng/acts/N-3).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-2, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374038 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Board of Trustees of the
    Corporation; ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Centre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Centre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the National Arts Centre
    located in the city of Ottawa; ([Centre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the National Arts Centre
    Corporation continued by section 3; ([Société]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2001, c. 34, s.
    54\]]{.Repealed}

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada as is designated by the Governor in Council
    as the Minister for the purposes of this
    Act; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[President]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[President]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the President of the Centre
    appointed under subsection 6(1); ([président]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[performing arts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[performing arts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the arts of the
    theatre and the concert hall, including the creating, staging and
    performing of drama, music and the dance. ([arts
    d'interprétation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-3, s. 2
- 2001, c. 34, s. 54
- 2006, c. 9, s. 280
:::

## [Corporation Established]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374050 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation continued

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The National Arts Centre
Corporation is continued as a corporation consisting of a Board of
Trustees composed of a Chairperson, a Vice-Chairperson, the mayors of
the cities of Ottawa and Gatineau and six other members to be appointed
as provided in subsection 4(2).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-3, s. 3
- 1995, c. 29, s. 51
- 2006, c. 9, s. 281
:::

## [Members of the Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374055 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Chairperson and
Vice-Chairperson

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson of the Board shall be appointed by
  the Governor in Council for such terms, not exceeding four years each,
  as are fixed by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of other members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each of the members of the Board, other than the
  Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson and the mayors referred to in section
  3, shall be appointed by the Governor in Council for a term not
  exceeding four years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility for re-appointment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who has served two consecutive terms as
  Chairperson of the Board, as Vice-Chairperson thereof or as a member
  appointed under subsection (2) is not, during the twelve months
  following the completion of the second term, eligible to be
  re-appointed to the Board in the capacity in which that person served.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancy in membership

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A vacancy in the membership of the Board does not
  impair the right of the remaining members to act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-3, s. 4
- 1995, c. 29, ss. 52, 53(E)
- 2006, c. 9, s. 282
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-4-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  member of the Board, other than a member who is in receipt of a salary
  fixed by the Governor in Council, shall be paid by the Corporation,
  for each day the member attends any meeting of the Board or of any
  committee of the Board, such remuneration as is fixed by by-law of the
  Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each member of the Board is entitled to be paid by
  the Corporation such travel and living expenses incurred by the member
  in connection with the performance of his duties as are fixed by
  by-law of the Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-2, s. 5
:::

## [President]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374074 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President of the Centre

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be a President of the Centre to be appointed by the Board to
  hold office for a term not exceeding five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person appointed as President is eligible for
  re-appointment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salary

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The President shall be paid by the Corporation such
  salary as is fixed by the Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-3, s. 6
- 2001, c. 34, s. 55
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President chief executive officer

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President is the chief executive officer of the Corporation and has
  supervision over and direction of the work and staff of the
  Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting President

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the President is absent or unable to perform the
  duties of the office of President or that office is vacant, the Board
  may authorize an officer of the Corporation to act as President.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-3, s. 7
- 2001, c. 34, ss. 55, 56(F)
:::

## [Officers and Employees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374091 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation may employ
such officers and employees and such technical and professional advisers
as the Corporation considers necessary for the proper conduct of its
activities, at such remuneration and on such other terms and conditions
as it deems fit.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-2, s. 8
:::

## [Objects and Powers of the Corporation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374096 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects of Corporation

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  objects of the Corporation are to operate and maintain the Centre, to
  develop the performing arts in the National Capital Region described
  in the schedule to the [National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4) and to
  assist the Canada Council for the Arts in the development of the
  performing arts elsewhere in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In furtherance of its objects, but without limiting
  the generality of subsection (1), the Corporation may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} arrange for and sponsor performing arts activities
    at the Centre;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} encourage and assist in the development of
    performing arts companies resident at the Centre;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} arrange for or sponsor radio and television
    broadcasts from the Centre and the showing of films in the Centre;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide accommodation at the Centre, on such terms
    and conditions as the Corporation may fix, for national and local
    organizations the objects of which include the development and
    encouragement of the performing arts in Canada; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} at the request of the Government of Canada or the
    Canada Council for the Arts, arrange for performances elsewhere in
    Canada by performing arts companies, whether resident or
    non-resident in Canada, and arrange for performances outside Canada
    by performing arts companies resident in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-3, s. 9
- 2001, c. 34, s. 16
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Corporation

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** In carrying out its
objects under this Act, the Corporation may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} acquire real, personal, movable and immovable
  property, including securities, and hold, manage or dispose of them as
  the Corporation may determine;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} lease as lessee real, personal, movable and
  immovable property;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} acquire by gift, bequest or devise real, personal,
  movable and immovable property and, despite anything in this Act,
  expend, administer or dispose of any such property, subject to the
  terms, if any, on which it was given, bequeathed or devised to the
  Corporation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} operate restaurants, lounges, parking facilities,
  shops and other facilities in the Centre, for the use of the public,
  and lease or otherwise make available, on such terms and conditions as
  the Corporation sees fit, any such facilities or space therefor;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} expend any moneys appropriated by Parliament for the
  purposes of the Corporation or received by the Corporation from its
  operation of the Centre, including any moneys received by it from
  leasing or otherwise making available any facilities or space referred
  to in paragraph (c); and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} generally, do and authorize such things as the
  Corporation may deem necessary for the attainment of the objects and
  the exercise of the powers of the Corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-3, s. 10
- 2001, c. 4, s. 101
- 2004, c. 25, s. 146(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Board may make
by-laws

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the regulation of its proceedings, including the
  establishment of special and standing committees of the Board, the
  delegation to those committees of any of its duties and the fixing of
  quorums for meetings of the Board or of those committees;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the establishment of advisory committees
  consisting of members of the Board and persons other than members
  thereof;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to the approval of the Minister, fixing the
  remuneration and travel and living expenses to be paid to members of
  the Board as provided in this Act; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} generally, for the conduct and management of its
  activities.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-2, s. 11
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374127 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the
Corporation shall be at the city of Ottawa.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-2, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Superannuation

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President and the officers and employees of the Corporation shall be
  deemed to be employed in the public service for the purposes of the
  [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and the
  Corporation shall be deemed to be a Public Service corporation for the
  purposes of section 37 of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the [Government Employees
  Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and any regulation made pursuant to
  section 9 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), the President and
  the officers and employees of the Corporation shall be deemed to be
  employees in the federal public administration.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-3, s. 13
- 2001, c. 34, s. 55
- 2003, c. 22, ss. 224(E), 225(E)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-13-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 13"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not agent of Her Majesty or part of
federal public administration

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation is not an
agent of Her Majesty and, except as provided in section 13, the
President and the officers and employees of the Corporation are not part
of the federal public administration.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-3, s. 14
- 2001, c. 34, s. 55
- 2003, c. 22, s. 224(E)
:::

Version](section-14-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 14"){.PITLink}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation deemed registered charity

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation shall,
for the purposes of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), be deemed to
be a registered charity within the meaning of that expression for the
purposes of that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-3, s. 15
- 1999, c. 31, s. 246(F)
:::

## [Audit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374147 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audit

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The accounts and
financial transactions of the Corporation shall be audited annually by
the Auditor General of Canada and a report of the audit shall be made to
the Chairperson of the Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-3, s. 16
- 1995, c. 29, s. 53(E)
:::

## [Report to Parliament]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374152 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson of the Board shall, within three months after the
  termination of each fiscal year, submit to the Minister a report of
  all proceedings under this Act for that fiscal year, including the
  financial statement of the Corporation and the report thereon of the
  Auditor General of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause the report submitted under
  subsection (1) to be laid before Parliament within fifteen days after
  the receipt thereof by the Minister or, if Parliament is not then
  sitting, on any of the first fifteen days next thereafter that either
  House of Parliament is sitting.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-3, s. 17
- 1995, c. 29, s. 53(E)
