## Borrowing Authority Act {#borrowing-authority-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2017, c. 20, s. 103
:::

Assented to 2017-06-22

An Act to provide the Minister of Finance with borrowing authority and
to provide for a maximum amount of certain borrowings

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 103 of chapter 20 of the Statutes of Canada, 2017,
in force November 23, 2017, see SI/2017-73.\]
:::
:::::

::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Borrowing Authority Act](/eng/acts/B-6.3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[agent corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agent corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 83(1) of the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11). ([société mandataire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Finance. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Borrowing authority

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister, with the
authorization of the Governor in Council under subsection 44(1) of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) and in accordance with
that Act, may borrow money on behalf of Her Majesty in right of Canada,
by way of the issue and sale of [[securities]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
defined in section 2 of that Act, or otherwise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amount that may be borrowed

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 3 and any
other Act of Parliament, but subject to section 6, the total of the
following amounts must not at any time exceed \$2,126,000,000,000:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total amount of money borrowed by the Minister
  under section 3, the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)
  and the borrowing authority Acts listed in the schedule,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total amount of money borrowed by way of the
  issue and sale of Canada Mortgage Bonds that are guaranteed by the
  Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation, except if they are purchased
  by the Minister and are not resold, other than for the purpose of
  providing a source of temporary liquidity, and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total amount of money borrowed by all agent
  corporations, by way of the issue and sale of their securities or
  otherwise, other than

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} amounts that they borrowed from Her Majesty in
    right of Canada, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} amounts that they are deemed under any other Act
    of Parliament to have borrowed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- amounts not counting
towards total

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The following amounts do
not count in the calculation of the amount referred to in paragraph
4(a):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} amounts borrowed by the Minister under an order made
  under paragraph 46.1(c) of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11), other than those borrowed under such an order
  made during the period beginning on March 23, 2021 and ending on May
  6, 2021; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} amounts borrowed by the Minister under an order made
  under paragraph 46.1(a) of that Act for the payment of any amount in
  respect of a debt that was originally incurred under an order made
  under paragraph 46.1(c) of that Act, other than in respect of a debt
  that was originally incurred under such an order made during the
  period beginning on March 23, 2021 and ending on May 6, 2021.

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2021, c. 7, s. 16\]]{.Repealed}

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2021, c. 7, s. 16\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- maximum amount exceeded

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may borrow an
amount under an order made under paragraph 46.1(a) or (b) of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) even if that borrowing
causes the maximum amount referred to in section 4 of this Act to be
exceeded.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's responsibility

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** In the exercise of the
Minister's powers and duties under subsection 14(1) of the [National
Housing Act](/eng/acts/N-11) in respect of guarantees of Canada Mortgage
Bonds by the Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation, and of his or her
powers and duties under subsections 127(2) and (3) of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) in respect of agent corporations,
the Minister must ensure that the maximum amount referred to in section
4 of this Act is not exceeded.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Parliament

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must cause to be tabled in each House of Parliament within
  three years after the day on which this section comes into force ---
  or, if the House is not sitting on the last day of that three-year
  period, during the next 30 days that it is sitting --- a report
  indicating

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total amount of borrowed money referred to in
    each of paragraphs 4(a) to (c);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total amount of money borrowed under orders
    made under each of paragraphs 46.1(a) to (c) of the [Financial
    Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11); and

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2021, c. 7, s. 18\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister's assessment of whether the maximum
    amount referred to in section 4 of this Act should be increased or
    decreased.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent triennial reports

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must cause to be tabled in each House of
  Parliament, on or before the May 31 that follows the end of the third
  fiscal year after the fiscal year during which a report was previously
  tabled under this section --- or, if the House is not sitting on that
  May 31, during the next 30 days that it is sitting --- a report
  indicating the matters set out in paragraphs (1)(a) to (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts not included

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), any amounts
  borrowed by the Minister during the period beginning on March 23, 2021
  and ending on May 6, 2021 under an order made under paragraph 46.1(c)
  of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) do not count in
  the calculation of the amounts referred to in paragraph (1)(b).
