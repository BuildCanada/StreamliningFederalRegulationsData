## Canadian Forces Superannuation Act {#canadian-forces-superannuation-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. C-17
:::

An Act respecting the superannuation of members of the Canadian Forces

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-70063 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canadian Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-9, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-70068 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[Canadian Forces Pension Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian Forces Pension Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      fund established under section 55.2; ([Caisse de retraite des
      Forces canadiennes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian Forces Superannuation Investment Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian Forces Superannuation Investment
      Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the fund established under
      section 55.1; ([Fonds de placement du compte de pension de
      retraite des Forces canadiennes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a child or stepchild of --- or
      an individual adopted either legally or in fact by --- a
      contributor, who at the time of the contributor's death was
      dependent on the contributor for
      support; ([enfant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[contributor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[contributor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is required
      by section 5 to contribute to the Canadian Forces Pension Fund,
      and includes, unless the context otherwise requires,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who has ceased to be required by this
        Act to contribute to the Superannuation Account or the Canadian
        Forces Pension Fund, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of sections 26 to 35 and 38 to
        40, a contributor under Part V of the former Act who has become
        entitled to a pension under that Part or has
        died; ([contributeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[disabled]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[disabled]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, as applied to any member of the
      regular force, has reference to any condition rendering him
      mentally or physically unfit to perform his duties as such
      member; ([invalide]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[former Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[former Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Defence Services
      Pension Act, chapter 63 of the Revised Statutes of Canada, 1952,
      as it read before March 1, 1960, and includes, unless the context
      otherwise requires, any other enactment of Parliament providing
      for the payment of pensions to members of the regular force based
      on length of service, other than this Act; ([ancienne
      loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[intermediate engagement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[intermediate engagement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      2003, c. 26, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[member of the regular force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[member of the regular force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      officer or non-commissioned member of the regular force; ([membre
      de la force régulière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[member of the reserve force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[member of the reserve force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      officer or non-commissioned member of the reserve force; ([membre
      de la force de réserve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
      Defence; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commissioned or
      subordinate officer of the regular
      force; ([officier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[pay]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[pay]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, as applied to the Canadian Forces,
      means pay at the rates prescribed or established under the
      [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) for the rank held by the
      person in respect of whom the expression is being applied,
      together with the allowances prescribed by the regulations made
      under this Act for that rank, and, as applied to the public
      service or the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, means the salary or
      pay and allowances, as the case may be, applicable in the case of
      that person, as determined under the [Public Service
      Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) or the [Royal Canadian Mounted
      Police Superannuation
      Act](/eng/acts/R-11); ([solde]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[provincial pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[provincial pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8); ([régime
      provincial de pensions]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[public service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[public service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning given that
      expression in subsection 3(1) of the [Public Service
      Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36); ([fonction
      publique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[rank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[rank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes
      appointment; ([grade]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[regular force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[regular force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the regular force of
      the Canadian Forces and includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the forces known before February 1, 1968 as the
        regular forces of the Canadian Forces, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the forces known before February 1, 1968 as the
        Royal Canadian Navy, the Canadian Army Active Force, the
        Permanent Active Militia, the Permanent Militia Corps, the
        permanent staff of the Militia, the Royal Canadian Air Force
        (Regular) and the Permanent Active Air Force; ([force
        régulière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[retirement age]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[retirement age]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2003, c. 26, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[salary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[salary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} as applied to a member of the
      Canadian Forces means the pay received by the member from
      employment as a member of the Canadian
      Forces; ([traitement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[short engagement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[short engagement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2003, c.
      26, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[Superannuation Account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Superannuation Account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian
      Forces Superannuation Account referred to in section 4; ([compte
      de pension de retraite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[supplementary benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[supplementary benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      supplementary benefit payable under Part III; ([prestation
      supplémentaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a contributor,
      means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who was married to the contributor at
        the time of the contributor's death, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person referred to in subsection
        29(1). ([survivant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1999, c. 34, s. 115\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References to R.C.M.P. Superannuation
  Act

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employment in forces pensionable

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Except as provided in the regulations, employment as

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of males and females

## [Application to Certain Reserve Force Members]{.HTitleText1} {#h-70112 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations respecting the manner in
  which and the extent to which any provisions of Parts I, II and III,
  or of any regulations made under those Parts, apply to members or
  former members, or classes of members or former members, of the
  reserve force that are prescribed in those regulations and adapting
  any of those provisions for the purposes of that application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reserve force members who were deemed
  re-enrolled in regular force

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, members of the reserve force
  who, immediately before the coming into force of this section, were
  deemed to be re-enrolled in the regular force under subsection 41(2)
  or (3) of this Act as it read immediately before its repeal are
  members, or classes of members, who may be prescribed by regulations
  made under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reserve force members who were
  participants under Part II

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, members of the reserve force
  who, immediately before the coming into force of this section, were
  participants within the meaning of paragraph (b) of the definition
  [[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 60(1) of this Act as
  it read immediately before its repeal are members, or classes of
  members, who may be prescribed by regulations made under
  subsection (1) for the purposes of the application and adaptation of
  any provisions of Part II.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2003, c. 26, s. 2
:::

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Superannuation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-70122 .Part}

### [Eligibility for Benefits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-70123 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this Act, an annuity or other benefit specified in this Act shall
  be paid to or in respect of every person who, being required to
  contribute to the Superannuation Account or the Canadian Forces
  Pension Fund in accordance with this Act, ceases to be a member of the
  regular force or dies, and that annuity or other benefit shall,
  subject to this Act, be based on the number of years of pensionable
  service to the credit of that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Superannuation Account

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Permanent Services Pension Account in the
  accounts of Canada, established pursuant to the former Act, is hereby
  continued under the name of the Canadian Forces Superannuation
  Account.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-17, s. 4
- 1999, c. 34, s. 116
:::

### [Contributions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-70131 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contribution rates --- 2013 and later

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member
  of the regular force, except a person described in subsection (6), is
  required to contribute to the Canadian Forces Pension Fund, in respect
  of every portion of the period beginning on January 1, 2013 by
  reservation from salary or otherwise, at the contribution rates
  determined by the Treasury Board in respect of that portion on the
  joint recommendation of the President of the Treasury Board and the
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contribution rates --- 35 years of
  service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who has to his or her credit, on or after
  January 1, 2013, a period of pensionable service --- or a period of
  pensionable service and other pensionable service --- totalling at
  least 35 years is not required to contribute under subsection (1) but
  is required to contribute, by reservation from salary or otherwise, to
  the Canadian Forces Pension Fund, in respect of the period beginning
  on the later of January 1, 2013 and the day on which the person has to
  his or her credit those 35 years, in addition to any other amount
  required under this Act, at the rates determined by the Treasury Board
  on the joint recommendation of the President of the Treasury Board and
  the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- determination of
  contribution rate

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In determining the contribution rates for the
  purposes of subsections (1) and (2), the rates must not exceed the
  rates paid under section 5 of the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36) by Group 1 contributors who are described in
  subsection 12(0.1) of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other pensionable service

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (2), [[other
  pensionable service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means years of service,
  other than service credited under a plan established in accordance
  with Part I.1, giving rise to a superannuation or pension benefit of a
  kind specified in the regulations that is payable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund, or out of any
    account in the accounts of Canada other than the Superannuation
    Account; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} out of the Public Service Pension Fund within the
    meaning of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36)
    or the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Pension Fund within the meaning
    of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contributions not required

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite anything in this Part, no person shall, in
  respect of any period of his or her service on or after December 15,
  1994, make a contribution under this Part in respect of any portion of
  his or her annual rate of pay that is in excess of the annual rate of
  pay that is fixed by or determined in the manner prescribed by the
  regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The exceptions are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member who, immediately before March 1, 1960, was
    a member of the regular force but not a contributor under Part V of
    the former Act and who has not elected under subsection 18(2) of the
    [Canadian Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17), chapter C-9 of
    the Revised Statutes of Canada, 1970, to become a contributor under
    this Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person on leave of absence from employment
    outside the regular force who, in respect of current service
    continues to contribute to or under any superannuation or pension
    fund or plan established for the benefit of employees of the
    employer from whose employment the member is absent.

### [Pensionable Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-70151 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pensionable service

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Act, the
following service may be counted by a contributor as pensionable service
for the purposes of this Act, namely,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} non-elective service, comprising,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a contributor who, immediately
    before March 1, 1960, was a contributor under Part V of the former
    Act, any period of service that he would have been entitled to count
    for the purposes of computing any pension or gratuity under that
    Part had he, at that time, retired from the regular force, except
    any such period for which he elected under that Part to pay, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any contributor,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} any period during which he or she was required by
      subsections 5(1) and (1.01), as they read on December 31, 2012, to
      contribute to the Superannuation Account or the Canadian Forces
      Pension Fund, and any period during which he or she is required by
      subsection 5(1) to contribute to the Canadian Forces Pension Fund,
      and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} any period of service that may be counted by him
      as pensionable service pursuant to section 41; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} elective service, comprising,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a contributor who, immediately
    before March 1, 1960, was a contributor under Part V of the former
    Act,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} any period of service for which he elected under
      that Part to pay, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} any period of service for which he might have
      elected, under the provisions of that Part in force immediately
      before March 1, 1960, to pay, if he elects, within the time
      prescribed by those provisions, to pay for that service, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any contributor,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} any period of service during which he was
      employed in the public service on a full-time basis and was in
      receipt of salary, if he elects, within one year of becoming a
      contributor under this Act, to pay for that service, and any
      period of service with any board, commission, corporation in, or
      portion of, the federal public administration that is added to
      Schedule I to the [Public Service Superannuation
      Act](/eng/acts/P-36) on or after March 1, 1960, during which he
      was employed on a full-time basis and was in receipt of salary, if
      he elects, within one year of such addition, to pay for that
      service,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} any period of service as a member of the Royal
      Canadian Mounted Police, if he elects, within one year of becoming
      a contributor under this Act, to pay for that service,

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} any period of service on active service during
      time of war in the naval, army or air forces of Her Majesty raised
      by Canada, if he elects, within one year of becoming a contributor
      under this Act, to pay for that service,

    - [(D)]{.lawlabel} any period of service in the Canadian Army
      Special Force established by Order in Council P.C. 3860 of August
      7, 1950 made under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), if
      he elects, within one year of becoming a contributor under this
      Act, to pay for that service,

    - [(E)]{.lawlabel} any period of full-time service during time of
      war between such dates as are fixed by the regulations in the
      naval, army or air forces of Her Majesty other than those raised
      by Canada, if he elects, within one year of becoming a contributor
      under this Act, to pay for that service,

    - [(F)]{.lawlabel} any period of full-time service during time of
      war or otherwise in the permanent naval, army or air forces of Her
      Majesty other than those raised by Canada, except any such service
      that may be counted by him under clause (E), if he elects, within
      one year of becoming a contributor under this Act, to pay for that
      service,

    - [(G)]{.lawlabel} any continuous period of full-time service of
      three months or more in the Canadian Forces or in the naval, army
      or air forces of Her Majesty raised by Canada, other than the
      regular force, if he elects, within one year of becoming a
      contributor under this Act, to pay for that service,

    - [(H)]{.lawlabel} one-fourth of any period of service in the
      Canadian Forces or in the naval, army or air forces of Her Majesty
      raised by Canada, other than the regular force, during which he
      was liable to be called out for periodic training or duty by the
      Governor in Council otherwise than during an emergency, except any
      such service that may be counted by him under clause (C) or (G),
      if he elects, within one year of becoming a contributor under this
      Act, to pay for that service,

    - [(I)]{.lawlabel} any period of service that may be counted by him
      as pensionable service pursuant to section 18 of the [Canadian
      Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17), chapter C-9 of the
      Revised Statutes of Canada, 1970, or section 42 or sections 43 to
      48 of this Act,

    - [(J)]{.lawlabel} any period of service in respect of which he was
      entitled to be paid a return of contributions or other lump sum
      payment under this Act or Part V of the former Act, if he elects,
      within one year of subsequently becoming a contributor under this
      Act, to pay for that service,

    - [(K)]{.lawlabel} any period of service described in this paragraph
      for which he might have elected, under this Act, Part V of the
      former Act, the Civil Service Superannuation Act, the [Public
      Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the [Royal Canadian
      Mounted Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11) or any order in
      council made under The Canadian Forces Act, 1950, as amended by
      the Canadian Forces Act, 1954, to pay, but for which he failed so
      to elect within the time prescribed therefor, if he elects, at any
      time before he ceases to be a member of the regular force, to pay
      for that service, and

    - [(L)]{.lawlabel} any period of service in respect of which the
      contributor makes an election under subsection 6.1(1), if the
      contributor elects, at any time before the contributor ceases to
      be a member of the regular force, to pay for that service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election for absence from duty

- **[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), where,
  under any regulations made under paragraph 50(c), a contributor is
  required to count as pensionable service for the purposes of this Act
  a period of service that exceeds three months, the contributor may,
  notwithstanding those regulations, elect, at the time and in the
  manner prescribed by the regulations made under paragraph 50.1(1)(b),
  not to count as pensionable service that portion of the period that is
  in excess of three months.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contributions not required

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding section 5, a contributor who makes an
  election under subsection (1) is not required to contribute to the
  Superannuation Account or the Canadian Forces Pension Fund under that
  section in respect of the portion of the period to which the election
  relates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A contributor is not entitled to make an election
  under subsection (1) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the period of service referred to in that
    subsection ended before the day on which that subsection comes into
    force; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the contributor has, before that day, made all of
    the contributions that are required to be made by the contributor to
    the Superannuation Account in respect of that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transitional

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A contributor who makes an election under
  subsection (1) in respect of a period of service that ended before the
  day on which that subsection comes into force and who has, before that
  day, made some but not all of the contributions that are required to
  be made by the contributor to the Superannuation Account in respect of
  that period shall, at the time the election is made, cease to be
  required to make any further contributions to the Superannuation
  Account in respect of that period and shall count as pensionable
  service for the purposes of this Act such portion of that period as is
  prescribed by the regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1992, c. 46, s. 35
- 1999, c. 34, s. 119

### [Elective Pensionable Service: Amount Required to be Paid]{.HTitleText2} {#h-70191 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount to be paid

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 9, a contributor who is entitled under this Act to count as
  pensionable service any period of elective service specified in
  paragraph 6(b), is required to pay, in respect thereof, as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any period specified in clause
    6(b)(i)(A), any amount that he would have been required to pay under
    Part V of the former Act had that Part continued in force;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any period specified in clause
    6(b)(i)(B), any amount that he would have been required to pay under
    the provisions of Part V of the former Act in force immediately

### [Elections]{.HTitleText2} {#h-70232 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of making elections

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  election made by a contributor under this Part shall be made by him
  while a member of the regular force and shall be evidenced in writing,
  in the form prescribed by the regulations, and witnessed, and the
  original thereof shall be forwarded to a person designated by the
  Minister for the purpose, within the time prescribed by this Act for
  the making of the election or, in the case of an election that may be
  made by the contributor at any time before he ceases to be a member of
  the regular force, within one month from the time of the making of the
  election.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Void elections

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An election under this Part is void in so far as it
  is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an election to pay for any period of service
    described in any of clauses 6(b)(ii)(A) to (H) that the elector is
    entitled to count for the purposes of any superannuation or pension
    benefit of a kind specified in the regulations, otherwise than under
    the provisions of this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an election to pay for any period of service
    described in clause 6(b)(ii)(K) or (L) or an election under
    subsection 18(2) of the [Canadian Forces Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-17), chapter C-9 of the Revised Statutes of Canada,
    1970, unless the elector has passed a medical examination, as
    prescribed by the regulations, within such time immediately before
    or after the making of the election as is prescribed by the
    regulations; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an election to pay for any continuous period of
    full-time service in the reserve force of one year by a person who
    became a contributor by virtue of subsection 41(3), unless the
    person has elected under subsection 41(4) to repay that part of the
    annuity or pension, as during that period, the person was entitled
    to receive under this Act or the former Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to elect for part of period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A contributor who is entitled under this Part to
  elect to pay for a period of service is entitled to elect to pay for
  part only of that period but only that part which is most recent in
  point of time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to amend or revoke

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An election under this Part may be amended by the
  elector, within the time prescribed by this Act for the making of the
  election, by increasing the period or periods of service for which he
  elects to pay, and is otherwise irrevocable except under such
  circumstances and on such terms and conditions, including payment by
  the elector to Her Majesty of such amount in respect of any benefit
  accruing to the elector during the subsistence of the election, as a
  consequence of his having so elected, as is prescribed by the
  regulations.

### [Annuities: How Computed]{.HTitleText2} {#h-70302 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Computation of annuities

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of any annuity to which a contributor may become entitled under
  this Act is an amount equal to the aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of years of pensionable service to the
      credit of the contributor occurring in the period preceding the
      day on which this subsection comes into force, not exceeding
      thirty-five, divided by fifty,

    multiplied by

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the average annual pay received by the
      contributor during any five-year period of pensionable service
      selected by or on behalf of the contributor, or during any period
      so selected consisting of consecutive periods of pensionable
      service totalling five years, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a contributor who has to the
      contributor's credit less than five years of pensionable service,
      the average annual pay received by the contributor during the
      period of pensionable service to the contributor's credit, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of years of pensionable service to the
      credit of the contributor occurring in the period on and after the
      day on which this subsection comes into force, not exceeding
      thirty-five years less the number of years of pensionable service
      to the credit of the contributor under subparagraph (a)(i),
      divided by fifty,

    multiplied by the lesser of

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the average annual pay received by the
      contributor during the period referred to in subparagraph (a)(ii)
      or (iii), as applicable, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the annual rate of pay that is fixed by the
      regulations made under paragraph 50(1)(g), or that may be
      determined in the manner prescribed by those regulations, and in
      force on the day on which the contributor most recently ceased to
      be a member of the regular force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction from annuity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), unless the Minister
  is satisfied that a contributor

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has not reached the age of sixty-five years, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has not become entitled to a disability pension
    payable under paragraph 44(1)(b) of the [Canada Pension
    Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or a provision of a provincial pension plan
    similar to the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8),

  there shall be deducted from the amount of any annuity to which that
  contributor is entitled under this Act an amount equal to the
  percentage, as set out in subsection (2.1), of

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the average annual pay received by the contributor
    during the period of pensionable service described in subsection (1)
    applicable to him or her, not exceeding his or her Average Maximum
    Pensionable Earnings,

  multiplied by

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number of years of pensionable service after
    1965 or after he or she has attained the age of eighteen years,
    whichever is the later, to the credit of the contributor, not
    exceeding thirty-five, divided by fifty.

### [Benefits Payable to Contributors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-70348 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immediate annuity

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  contributor who ceases to be a member of the regular force and who has
  to their credit two or more years of pensionable service is entitled
  to an immediate annuity if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they have completed not less than 25 years of
    Canadian Forces service as prescribed by regulations made under
    paragraph 50(1)(m);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they have reached 60 years of age;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} they have reached 55 years of age and have to their
    credit not less than 30 years of pensionable service;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} they are disabled and have to their credit not less
    than 10 years of pensionable service; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} they cease, otherwise than voluntarily, to be a
    member of the regular force because of a reduction in the maximum
    number of officers or non-commissioned members of the regular force
    authorized by the Governor in Council under section 15 of the
    [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) or they cease, otherwise than
    voluntarily, to be a member of the regular force in any
    circumstances specified by the Treasury Board, and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} they have reached 55 years of age and have to
      their credit not less than 10 years of pensionable service, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} they have to their credit not less than 20 years
      of pensionable service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(a), the Governor in Council may
  make regulations establishing, for officers, according to their rank,
  a number of years of Canadian Forces service greater than the minimum

  that number to be reduced to 25 years over a maximum period of five
  years from the coming into force of this section, in the case of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contributors who are members of the regular force
    on the coming into force of this section; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contributors who are entitled to an annuity on the
    coming into force of this section and who are subsequently

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual allowance

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  contributor who is entitled to a deferred annuity may opt, in
  accordance with the regulations, for an annual allowance in place of
  the deferred annuity. The allowance is payable to the contributor

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immediately, if they are 50 or more years of age
    when they exercise their option; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on their reaching 50 years of age, if they are less

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of allowance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the annual allowance is equal to the

  60.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a contributor is 50 years or more of age when they

  allowance is the greater of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount calculated under subsection (2), and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the deferred annuity reduced by the
    product obtained by multiplying five per cent of the amount of that
    annuity by the greater of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 55 minus the contributor's age in years at the
      time they exercise their option, to the nearest one-tenth of a
      year, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 30 minus the number of years of pensionable
      service to their credit, to the nearest one-tenth of a year.

### [Benefits Payable to Survivors, Children and Other Beneficiaries]{.HTitleText2} {#h-70428 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefits payable on death of retired
member

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  death of a contributor who, at the time of their death, was entitled
  under this Part to an annuity or an annual allowance, the survivor and
  children of the contributor are entitled to the following allowances,
  computed on the basis of the product obtained by multiplying the
  average annual pay received by the contributor during the period
  specified in subparagraph 15(1)(a)(ii) by the number of years of
  pensionable service to the contributor's credit, one one-hundredth of
  the product so obtained being referred to in this section as the
  "basic allowance":

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a survivor, an immediate annual
    allowance equal to the basic allowance; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of each child, an immediate annual
    allowance equal to one-fifth of the basic allowance or, if the
    contributor died without leaving a survivor, the survivor is dead or
    the survivor is not entitled to receive an allowance under this
    Part, other than an immediate annual allowance under section 25.1,
    two-fifths of the basic allowance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Total child allowance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The total amount of the allowances paid under
  paragraph (1)(b) shall not exceed four-fifths of the basic allowance
  or, if the contributor died without leaving a survivor, the survivor
  is dead or the survivor is not entitled to receive an allowance under
  this Part, other than an immediate annual allowance under section
  25.1, eight-fifths of the basic allowance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Apportionment of total among children

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, in computing the allowances to which the children
  of a contributor are entitled under subsections (1) and (2), it is
  determined that there are more than four children of the contributor
  entitled to an allowance, the total amount of the allowances shall be
  apportioned among the children in such shares as the Minister
  considers just and proper under the circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefits payable on death of serving
  member --- more than two years

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} On the death of a contributor who has to their credit
  two or more years of pensionable service and was a member of the
  regular force at the time of death, the survivor and children of the
  contributor are entitled to the annual allowances to which they would
  have been entitled under subsections (1), (2) and (3) had the
  contributor, immediately before death, become entitled under this Part
  to an annuity or an annual allowance.

- Definition of [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (1) to (4),
  [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a child of the contributor who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is less than eighteen years of age; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is eighteen or more years of age but less than
    twenty-five years of age, and is in full-time attendance at a school
    or university as defined in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefits payable on death of serving
  member --- less than two years

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} On the death of a contributor who has to their credit
  less than two years of pensionable service and was a member of the
  regular force at the time of death, the survivor and children of the
  contributor, in any case where the contributor died leaving a survivor
  or a child less than eighteen years of age, are entitled jointly to a
  death benefit equal to the greater of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a return of contributions, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to one month's pay for each year of
    pensionable service to the credit of the contributor, computed on
    the basis of the rate of pay authorized to be paid to them at the
    time of their death.

:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allowances paid to children

**[[85]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-85 .sectionLabel}** When a child is entitled
to an annual allowance or other amount under this Act, payment of it
shall, if the child is less than eighteen years of age, be made to the
person having custody and control of the child, or, if there is no
person having custody and control of the child, to the person whom the
Minister may direct.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2003, c. 26, s. 36
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reservation of unpaid instalments for
elective service

**[[86]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-86 .sectionLabel}** If a person who has
elected under this Act or Part V of the former Act to pay for any period
of service and has undertaken to pay for that period of service in
instalments ceases to be a member of the regular force or the reserve
force, as the case may be, before all the instalments have been paid,
the unpaid instalments may be reserved, in accordance with the
regulations, from any amount payable to them by Her Majesty in right of
Canada, including any periodic benefit payable to them under this Act,
until such time as all the instalments have been paid, or the person
dies, whichever occurs first.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2003, c. 26, s. 36
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of amounts due at time of death

**[[87]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-87 .sectionLabel}** When an amount payable by
a person into the Superannuation Account, the Canadian Forces Pension
Fund or a fund established under regulations made under section 59.1 by
reservation from salary or otherwise has become due, but remains unpaid
at the time of death, that amount, with interest at four per cent per
annum from the time when it became due, may be recovered, in accordance
with the regulations, from any allowance payable under this Act to the
survivor or children of that person, without prejudice to any other
recourse available to Her Majesty with respect to the recovery of it.
Any amount so recovered shall be credited to the Superannuation Account
or paid into the Canadian Forces Pension Fund or the fund established
under regulations made under section 59.1, as the case may be, and is
deemed to have been paid into the Superannuation Account, the Canadian
Forces Pension Fund or the fund established under regulations made under
section 59.1, as the case may be, by that person.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2003, c. 26, s. 36
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of amount paid in error

**[[88]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-88 .sectionLabel}** If any amount has been
paid in error under Part I, I.1 or III on account of any periodic
benefit, the Minister may retain by way of deduction from any subsequent
payment of that benefit, in accordance with the regulations, an amount
equal to the amount paid in error, without prejudice to any other
recourse available to Her Majesty with respect to the recovery of the
amount paid in error.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2003, c. 26, s. 36
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of debit balance in pay account
of former member

- **[[89]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-89 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  debit balance in the pay account of a former member of the regular
  force or of the reserve force, as the case may be, may be recovered
  from any benefit to which they are entitled under this Act or from any
  amount that becomes payable under this Act to their service estate,
  whether the debit balance existed at the time of their retirement or
  was ascertained after that time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of recovery

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Recovery of a debit balance pursuant to this section
  shall be effected in the manner and to the extent that may be
  prescribed by the regulations, but, in the case of any benefit to
  which a former member of the regular force or of the reserve force, as
  the case may be, is entitled under this Act, such recovery shall not
  be effected unless notice of the existence of the debit balance and
  the amount of it has been given to them, or has been forwarded by
  registered mail addressed to them at their latest known address.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2003, c. 26, s. 36
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Diversion of payments to satisfy
financial support order

- **[[90]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-90 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  any court in Canada of competent jurisdiction has made an order
  requiring a recipient to pay financial support, amounts payable under
  Part I, I.1 or III to that recipient are subject to being diverted to
  the person named in the order in accordance with Part II of the
  [Garnishment, Attachment and Pension Diversion Act](/eng/acts/G-2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where recipient unable to manage own
  affairs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, for any reason, a recipient is unable to manage
  their own affairs, or where the recipient is incapable of managing
  their own affairs and there is no person entitled by law to act as the

  designated by the Minister to receive payment on behalf of the
  recipient any amount that is payable to the recipient under Part I,

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment deemed to be to recipient

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of Parts I, I.1 and III, any payment
  made by the Receiver General pursuant to subsection (1) or (2) is
  deemed to be a payment to the recipient in respect of whom the payment
  was made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section,
  [[recipient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person to whom any amount is
  or is about to become payable under Part I, I.1 or III.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2003, c. 26, s. 36
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission of overpayments

**[[91]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91 .sectionLabel}** If a person has received
or obtained an overpayment and the Minister is satisfied that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the overpayment cannot be recovered within the
  reasonably foreseeable future,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the administrative costs of recovering the
  overpayment are likely to equal or exceed the amount to be recovered,
  or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} repayment of the overpayment would cause undue
  hardship to the person,

the Minister may, unless that person has been convicted of an offence
under the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) in connection with the
receiving or obtaining of the overpayment, remit all or any portion of
the overpayment.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2003, c. 26, s. 36
:::

election or exercise an option under this Act, the Minister may take any
remedial action that the Minister considers appropriate to permit that
person to make that election or exercise that option, as the case may
be, on any terms and conditions that the Minister may determine,
including as to the time for making the election or exercising the
option and any amount payable in respect of the election.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2003, c. 26, s. 36
