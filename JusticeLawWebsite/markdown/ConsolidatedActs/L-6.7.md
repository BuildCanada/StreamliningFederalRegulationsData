## Law Commission of Canada Act {#law-commission-of-canada-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1996, c. 9
:::

Assented to 1996-05-29

An Act respecting the Law Commission of Canada
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS, after extensive national consultations, the Government of
Canada has determined that it is desirable to establish a commission to
provide independent advice on improvements, modernization and reform of
the law of Canada, which advice would be based on the knowledge and
experience of a wide range of groups and individuals;

AND WHEREAS the Government adopted the recommendations arising from
those consultations that the commission should be guided by the
following principles, namely,

the commission's work should be open to and inclusive of all Canadians
and the results of that work should be accessible and understandable,

the commission should adopt a multidisciplinary approach to its work
that views the law and the legal system in a broad social and economic
context,

the commission should be responsive and accountable by cooperating and
forging partnerships with a wide range of interested groups and
individuals, including the academic community,

the commission should employ modern technology when appropriate and be
innovative in its research methods, its consultation processes, its
management practices and its communications in order to achieve
efficiency in its operations and effectiveness in its results, and

the commission should take account of cost-effectiveness and the impact
of the law on different groups and individuals in formulating its
recommendations;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344657 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Law Commission of Canada Act](/eng/acts/L-6.7).

## [Establishment of Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344660 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission established

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby established
a corporation, to be called the Law Commission of Canada.

## [Purpose, Powers and Duties of Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344663 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of the
Commission is to study and keep under systematic review, in a manner
that reflects the concepts and institutions of the common law and civil
law systems, the law of Canada and its effects with a view to providing
independent advice on improvements, modernization and reform that will
ensure a just legal system that meets the changing needs of Canadian
society and of individuals in that society, including

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the development of new approaches to, and new
  concepts of, law;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the development of measures to make the legal system
  more efficient, economical and accessible;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the stimulation of critical debate in, and the
  forging of productive networks among, academic and other communities
  in Canada in order to ensure cooperation and coordination; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the elimination of obsolete laws and anomalies in the
  law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** In furtherance of its
purpose, the Commission may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} undertake, promote, initiate and evaluate studies and
  research;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} support, publish, sell and otherwise disseminate
  studies, reports and other documents;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} sponsor or support conferences, seminars and other
  meetings;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} facilitate and support cooperative efforts among the
  Commission, governments, the academic community, the legal profession
  and other organizations and persons interested in the Commission's
  work;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} acquire any money, securities or other property by
  gift, bequest or otherwise and hold, expend, invest, administer or
  dispose of that property, subject to any terms on which it is given,
  bequeathed or otherwise made available to the Commission;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} generate revenues through the provision of goods or
  services;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} expend during a fiscal year any money provided by
  Parliament or received from other sources in that year through the
  conduct of its operations, subject to any terms on which the money was
  provided or received; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} do all such things as are conducive to the
  furtherance of its purpose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} consult with the Minister of Justice with respect
    to the annual program of studies that it proposes to undertake;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prepare such reports as the Minister, after
    consultation with the Commission and taking into consideration the
    workload and resources of the Commission, may require; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} submit to the Minister any report that it has
    initiated itself or on the request of the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's response

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Justice shall respond to the
  Commission with respect to any report that the Minister receives from
  the Commission under this section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accountable to Parliament

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Commission is
ultimately accountable, through the Minister of Justice, to Parliament
for the conduct of its affairs.

## [Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344692 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissioners

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission consists of a President and four other Commissioners, to be
  appointed by the Governor in Council on the recommendation of the
  Minister of Justice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In recommending persons for appointment as
  Commissioners, the Minister of Justice shall not restrict
  consideration to members of the legal community.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} As a group, the Commissioners should be broadly
  representative of the socio-economic and cultural diversity of Canada,
  represent various disciplines and reflect knowledge of the common law
  and civil law systems.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term and tenure of office

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioners hold office during pleasure for
  such term, not exceeding five years, as the Governor in Council may
  fix so as to ensure, as far as possible, the expiration of not more
  than two terms of office in any year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Full-time and part-time Commissioners

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The President holds office on a full-time basis and
  the other Commissioners hold office on a part-time basis.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  Commissioner is eligible for reappointment to the Commission in the
  same or another capacity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resignation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Commissioner who wishes to resign shall notify the
  Minister of Justice in writing to that effect, and the resignation
  becomes effective at the time the Minister receives the notice or at
  the time specified in the notice, whichever is the later.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vice-President

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioners shall
elect one of themselves as Vice-President of the Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Functions of President

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President is the chief executive officer of the Commission and
  presides at meetings of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the President is absent or unable to act or if the
  office of President is vacant, the Vice-President has all the powers,
  duties and functions of the President.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Residence of President

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The President must reside in the area of the head
  office of the Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration of President

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President shall be paid such remuneration as the Governor in Council
  may fix.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees of other Commissioners

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioners, other than the President, shall be
  paid such fees for their work for the Commission as the Governor in
  Council may fix.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A Commissioner is entitled to be paid reasonable
  travel and living expenses incurred by the Commissioner while absent
  from the Commissioner's ordinary place of residence in the course of
  performing duties under this Act.

## [Head Office and Meetings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344725 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the
Commission is in the National Capital Region described in the schedule
to the [National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4) or in such other place in
Canada as the Governor in Council may fix.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission shall meet at such times and places as the President may
  determine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the by-laws of the Commission, a
  Commissioner may participate in a meeting of the Commission or of a
  committee of the Commission by means of such telephone or other
  communication facilities as permit all persons participating in the
  meeting to communicate with each other, and a Commissioner so
  participating in a meeting is deemed for the purposes of this Act to
  be present at the meeting.

## [By-laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344733 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may make
by-laws respecting

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the constitution of committees of the Commission,
  including committees with members who are not Commissioners, the
  duties of the committees and the expenses to be paid to members who
  are not Commissioners;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quorum and procedure at meetings of the
  Commission or its committees; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} generally, the conduct and management of the work of
  the Commission.

## [Staff]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344739 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  executive director of the Commission, and such other officers and
  employees as are necessary for the proper conduct of the work of the
  Commission, shall be appointed in accordance with the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary assistance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of advising and assisting the
  Commission in the performance of its duties and functions, the
  Commission may, on a temporary basis, contract for the services of any
  person who has specialized knowledge of any matter relating to a
  specific project of the Commission and may fix and pay the
  remuneration and expenses of that person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Executive director

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Under the direction of
the President, the executive director has the day-to-day supervision
over the staff and work of the Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioners are
deemed to be employees for the purposes of the [Government Employees
Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and to be employed in the federal
public administration for the purposes of any regulations made pursuant
to section 9 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

## [Advisory Bodies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344751 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Law Commission of Canada Advisory Council

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  is hereby established a council, to be called the Law Commission of
  Canada Advisory Council, consisting of not less than twelve or more
  than twenty-four members appointed by the Commission to hold office
  during pleasure for a term not exceeding three years and the Deputy
  Minister of Justice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} In appointing persons as members of the Council,
  the Commission shall not restrict consideration to members of the
  legal community.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} As a group, the members of the Council should be
  broadly representative of the socio-economic and cultural diversity of
  Canada, represent various disciplines and reflect knowledge of the
  common law and civil law systems.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member of the Council is eligible for reappointment
  to the Council in the same or another capacity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Council shall elect one of
  themselves as Chairperson of the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A member of the Council serves without remuneration,
  but is entitled to be paid reasonable travel and living expenses
  incurred by the member while absent from the member's ordinary place
  of residence in the course of performing duties under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advisory function

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Council shall advise
the Commission on the Commission's strategic directions and long-term
program of studies and on the review of the Commission's performance and
may advise the Commission on any other matters relating to the purpose
of the Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Study panels

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of advising and assisting the Commission in any
  particular project, the Commission may establish a study panel
  presided over by a Commissioner and consisting of persons having
  specialized knowledge in, or particularly affected by, the matter to
  be studied.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person serving on a study panel serves without
  remuneration, but is entitled to be paid reasonable travel and living
  expenses incurred by the person while absent from the person's
  ordinary place of residence in the course of performing duties under
  this Act.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344772 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agent of Her Majesty

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission is for all its purposes an agent of Her Majesty in right of
  Canada and may exercise its powers only as an agent of Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contracts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may enter into contracts in the name
  of Her Majesty in right of Canada or in its own name.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Property acquired by the Commission is the property
  of Her Majesty in right of Canada and title to the property may be
  vested in the name of Her Majesty or in the name of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal proceedings

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Actions, suits or other legal proceedings in respect
  of any right or obligation acquired or incurred by the Commission,
  whether in the name of Her Majesty in right of Canada or in its own
  name, may be brought or taken by or against the Commission in the name
  of the Commission in any court that would have jurisdiction if the
  Commission were not an agent of Her Majesty.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audit

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The accounts and
financial transactions of the Commission shall be audited by the Auditor
General of Canada at such times as the Auditor General considers
appropriate, and a report of the audit shall be made to the Commission
and to the Minister of Justice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Within three months after
the end of each fiscal year, the President shall submit to the Minister
of Justice an annual report of the activities of the Commission in that
year, including the financial statements of the Commission and the
Auditor General's report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of reports

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Justice
shall cause a copy of any report of the Commission to be tabled in each
House of Parliament on any of the first fifteen days on which that House
is sitting after the Minister receives it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of response

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Justice
shall cause a copy of the Minister's response to any report of the
Commission to be tabled in each House of Parliament on any of the first
fifteen days on which that House is sitting after the Minister gives the
response to the Commission.
