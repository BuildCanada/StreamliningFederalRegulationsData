## First Nations Fiscal Management Act {#first-nations-fiscal-management-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2005, c. 9
:::

Assented to 2005-03-23

An Act to provide for powers of First Nations respecting taxation,
financial administration and the provision of services on reserve lands,
to facilitate First Nations' access to financing secured by local
revenues or other revenues, to establish a First Nations Tax Commission,
First Nations Financial Management Board, First Nations Finance
Authority and First Nations Infrastructure Institute and to make
consequential amendments to other Acts
::::

::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the Government of Canada is committed to responding to, to the
extent of its authority, Call to Action 44 in the Final Report of the
Truth and Reconciliation Commission of Canada and implementing the
United Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples,
including Articles 3 to 5, 20, 21 and 23 as they relate to the pursuit
of economic growth in Indigenous communities and to economic
reconciliation;

Whereas early contact Indigenous peoples had innovative economic and
trade systems supported by public infrastructure, tax systems, sharing
practices and the development of trading languages to enable commerce
across linguistically diverse regions;

Whereas Indigenous languages have words for taxes and sharing, including
the word "taksis" in the Chinook trading language;

Whereas the Government of Canada has adopted a policy recognizing the
inherent right of self-government as an Aboriginal right and providing
for the negotiation of self-government;

Whereas this Act is not intended to define the nature and scope of any
right of self-government or to prejudge the outcome of any
self-government negotiation;

Whereas First Nations governments led an initiative in 1988 to amend the
[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) in order to recognize their jurisdiction
over real property taxation;

Whereas First Nations leaders led an initiative that resulted in the
development of legislation that would enable First Nations to better
exercise their jurisdiction with respect to taxation, financial
administration and the provision of services on reserve lands and to
participate in pooled debenture borrowing;

Whereas economic development through the application of local revenues
and other revenues to support borrowing on capital markets for the
development of public infrastructure is available to other governments
in Canada;

Whereas local revenue systems on reserves should recognize both the
interests of on-reserve taxpayers and the rights of members of First
Nations communities;

And whereas First Nations and the Government of Canada recognize the
benefits of establishing Indigenous institutions as part of a
comprehensive fiscal framework;
:::

NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224847 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[borrowing member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[borrowing member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a First Nation that
      has been accepted as a borrowing member under subsection 76(2) and
      has not ceased to be a borrowing member under section 77. ([membre
      emprunteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[capital assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[capital assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes capital
      infrastructure. ([immobilisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as [[council
      of the band]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) of the
      [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([conseil de la première
      nation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a band named in the
      schedule. ([première nation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[First Nations Finance Authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[First Nations Finance Authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      corporation established under section 58. ([Administration
      financière des premières nations]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[First Nations Financial Management Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[First Nations Financial Management
      Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the board established under
      subsection 38(1). ([Conseil de gestion financière des premières
      nations]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[First Nations Gazette]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[First Nations Gazette]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      publication published under section 34. ([Gazette des premières
      nations]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[First Nations Infrastructure Institute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[First Nations Infrastructure
      Institute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the institute established
      under subsection 102(1). ([Institut des infrastructures des
      premières nations]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[First Nations Statistical Institute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[First Nations Statistical
      Institute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2012, c. 19, s.
      659\]]{.Repealed}

  [[First Nations Tax Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[First Nations Tax Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      commission established under subsection 17(1). ([Commission de la
      fiscalité des premières nations]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to reserve lands in
      Canada elsewhere than in Quebec, means any estate, right or
      interest of any nature in or to the lands, including any right to
      occupy, possess or use the lands, but does not include title to
      the lands that is held by her Majesty. ([intérêt]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[intermediate account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[intermediate account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an account
      established by a First Nation in which other revenues to be used
      for financing under this Act are deposited and out of which the
      First Nations Finance Authority is authorized to transfer such
      revenues to a secured revenues trust account. ([compte
      intermédiaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[local revenue law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[local revenue law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a law made under
      subsection 5(1). ([texte législatif sur les recettes
      locales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[local revenues]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[local revenues]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means moneys raised under a
      local revenue law and payments made to a First Nation in lieu of a
      tax imposed by a law made under paragraph 5(1)(a). ([recettes
      locales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
      Crown-Indigenous Relations. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[other revenues]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[other revenues]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} tax revenues and fees imposed or collected by a
        First Nation under a law or agreement, other than

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} local revenues, and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} revenues from taxes administered by His
          Majesty in right of Canada on the First Nation's behalf,
          including under the [First Nations Goods and Services Tax
          Act](/eng/acts/F-11.7), unless an agreement between them
          specifically permits the use of the revenues as security for a
          loan referred to in paragraph 74(b) and any other applicable
          conditions are satisfied;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} royalties payable to a First Nation under the
        [[Framework Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
        subsection 2(1) of the [Framework Agreement on First Nation Land
        Management Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46), or under the [First Nations
        Oil and Gas and Moneys Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.9);

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} royalties payable to His Majesty in right of
        Canada under the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) or the [Indian Oil
        and Gas Act](/eng/acts/I-7) on behalf of a First Nation that has
        assumed control of its moneys under the [First Nations Oil and
        Gas and Moneys Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.9);

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} revenues that are from leases, permits or other
        instruments or acts authorizing the use of reserve land issued
        under the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) and that a First Nation
        has assumed control of under the [First Nations Oil and Gas and
        Moneys Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.9);

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} revenues from leases, permits or other
        instruments or acts authorizing the use of reserve land issued
        under the [[Framework Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
        defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Framework Agreement on First
        Nation Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46);

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} revenues otherwise payable to a First Nation
        under any agreement with a person other than His Majesty in
        right of Canada --- with the exception of revenues collected by

        as security for a loan referred to in paragraph 74(b) and any
        other applicable conditions are satisfied;

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} revenues, other than local revenues, received
        by a First Nation from businesses wholly or partly owned by it,
        including dividends from shares owned by it;

      - [(h)]{.lawlabel} transfers from a provincial, regional,
        municipal or local government to a First Nation;

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} transfers from His Majesty in right of Canada
        to a First Nation if the agreement governing the transfer
        specifically permits the use of the transfer as security for a
        loan referred to in paragraph 74(b) and if any other applicable
        conditions are satisfied;

      - [(j)]{.lawlabel} interest earned by a First Nation on deposits,
        investments or loans, other than interest held by His Majesty in
        right of Canada on the First Nation's behalf; and

      - [(k)]{.lawlabel} revenues prescribed by regulation. ([autres
        recettes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[property taxation law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[property taxation law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a law made
      under paragraph 5(1)(a). ([texte législatif relatif à l'imposition
      foncière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[right]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[right]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to reserve lands in
      Quebec, means any right of any nature in or to the lands,
      including any right to occupy, possess or use the lands and any
      right of a lessee, but does not include title to the lands that is

  [[secured revenues trust account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[secured revenues trust account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an

  [[third-party management]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[third-party management]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extended meaning of "borrowing member"

  [(2.2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of section 61, [[borrowing
  member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} also means an Indigenous group, other
  than a band named in the schedule, that has been accepted as a
  borrowing member under a regulation made under section 141.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments to schedule

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} At the request of the council of a band, the Minister
  may, by order, amend the schedule in order to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} add or change the name of the band; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} delete the name of the band, as long as there are
    no amounts owing by the band to the First Nations Finance Authority
    that remain unpaid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make regulations
  prescribing anything that is to be prescribed under paragraph (k) of
  the definition [[other revenues]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, nothing in this Act shall be
  construed as requiring capital assets for the provision of local
  services on reserve lands to be located on reserve lands.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[First Nations Fiscal Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224882 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial administration laws

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The council of a First
Nation may not make a law under paragraph 5(1)(d) or 8.1(1)(a) until the
council has made a law respecting the financial administration of the
First Nation under paragraph 9(1)(a) and that law has been approved by
the First Nations Financial Management Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Local revenue laws

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to sections 4 and 6 and any regulations made under paragraph 36(1)(d),
  the council of a First Nation may make laws

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting taxation for local purposes of reserve
    lands and interests or rights in reserve lands, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the assessment of the value of those lands and
      interests or rights, the requisition of any information necessary
      to conduct the assessment and the inspection, in accordance with
      procedures prescribed by regulation, for assessment purposes of
      any reserve lands that are subject to taxation for local purposes,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a mechanism to establish tax rates and apply
      them to the assessed value of those lands and interests or rights,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} taxation for the provision of services in
      respect of reserve lands,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the taxation of business activities on reserve
      lands, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the imposition of development cost charges;

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} respecting the charging of fees for the provision
    of services or the use of facilities on reserve lands, or for a
    regulatory process, permit, licence or other authorization, in
    relation to water, sewers, waste management, animal control,
    recreation and transportation, as well as any other similar
    services;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} authorizing the expenditure of local revenues;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting procedures by which the interests of
    taxpayers may be represented to the council;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the borrowing of money from the First
    Nations Finance Authority that is secured by local revenues,
    including any authorization to enter into a particular borrowing
    agreement with that Authority;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} subject to any conditions and procedures prescribed
    by regulation, respecting the enforcement of laws made under
    paragraphs (a) and (a.1), including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the creation of liens or, in Quebec, prior claims
      or legal hypothecs on reserve lands and on interests or rights in
      reserve lands,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the imposition and recovery of interest and
      penalties on an amount payable pursuant to a law made under that
      paragraph, where the amount is not paid when it is due, and the
      rate of interest or the amount of the penalty, as the case may be,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (7), the seizure,
      forfeiture and assignment of interests or rights in reserve lands,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the seizure and sale of personal or movable
      property located on reserve lands, other than property located in
      a dwelling,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the discontinuance of services, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the recovery of costs that are incurred by the
      First Nation for the enforcement of those laws;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} delegating to any person or body any of the
    council's powers to make laws under any of paragraphs (a) to (e);
    and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} delegating to the First Nations Financial
    Management Board any of the council's other powers that are required
    to give effect to a co-management arrangement entered into under
    section 52 or to give effect to third-party management under section
    53.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval required

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A law made under subsection (1) does not have any
  force or effect until it is approved by the First Nations Tax
  Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A law made under subsection (1) comes into force on
  the later of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day of coming into force set out in the law,
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day after it is approved by the First Nations
    Tax Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeals

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A law made under subparagraph (1)(a)(i) shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an appeal procedure in respect of assessments,
    incorporating such procedures as are prescribed by regulation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fixed rates of remuneration and fixed terms of
    office for any persons designated to decide the appeals.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to competent court

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The First Nation may apply to a court of competent
  jurisdiction for an order directing the person or entity named in the
  application to comply with a local revenue law, such as by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} refraining from doing anything that, in the opinion
    of the court, constitutes or is directed toward the contravention of
    that law; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} doing anything that, in the opinion of the court,
    may prevent the contravention of that law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collection --- competent court

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The First Nation may commence a proceeding in a court
  of competent jurisdiction to collect an amount owing to the First
  Nation under a local revenue law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement --- [Framework Agreement on
  First Nation Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46)

  [(6.1)]{.lawlabel} If a First Nation has adopted a [[land
  code]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(2) of the
  [Framework Agreement on First Nation Land Management
  Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46) or the council of a First Nation has enacted a
  [[First Nation law]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection
  2(1) of that Act, the First Nation may use any enforcement measure ---
  other than a measure for the investigation or prosecution of an
  offence punishable on summary conviction referred to in paragraph
  19.1(a) of the [[Framework Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
  defined in subsection 2(1) of that Act --- that is provided for in
  that land code or First Nation law to enforce a local revenue law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assignment of interest or right

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Despite the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) or any
  instrument or act conferring an interest or right in reserve lands, if
  there are outstanding taxes payable under a law made under paragraph
  (1)(a) for more than two years, the First Nation may assign the
  interest or right in accordance with the conditions and procedures
  prescribed by regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} In any proceedings, judicial notice may be taken of a
  local revenue law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply in respect of local revenue laws or laws made under section
  9.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of proposed laws

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At least
  30 days --- or any longer period fixed by a standard made under
  subsection 35(1) --- before making a law under paragraph 5(1)(a),
  (a.1) or (c), including a law repealing or amending such a law, other
  than a law referred to in subsection 10(1), the council of a First
  Nation shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} publish a notice of the proposed law in the First
    Nations Gazette;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} post the notice in a public place on the reserve
    lands of the First Nation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} send the notice, by mail or electronic means, to
    the First Nations Tax Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The First Nations Tax Commission may exempt a First
  Nation from the requirements of subsection (1) in respect of an
  amendment of a law if the Commission considers that the amendment is
  not significant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice referred to in subsection (1) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} describe the proposed law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} state where a copy of the proposed law may be
    obtained;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} invite representations regarding the proposed law
    to be made, in writing, to the council within the period referred to
    in subsection (1); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the council is to review the proposed law at a
    public meeting, state the time and place of the meeting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Council to consider representations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Before making a law under paragraph 5(1)(a), (a.1) or
  (c), the council of a First Nation shall consider any representations
  that were made in accordance with paragraph (3)(c) or at a meeting
  referred to in paragraph (3)(d).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further representations

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** When the council of a First
Nation sends a law made under paragraph 5(1)(a), (a.1) or (c), other
than a law referred to in subsection 10(1), to the First Nations Tax
Commission for its approval, the council shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide a copy of the law to any persons who made
  representations under paragraph 6(3)(c); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} invite those persons to make written representations
  to the Commission within 30 days after the day on which they receive
  the copy of the law.

### [Purposes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-225127 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandate

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The purposes of the
Commission are to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} support and protect the integrity of First Nations
  local revenue systems and promote common approaches to those systems
  as part of the Canadian fiscal framework;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} support and promote the reconciliation, in First
  Nations local revenue systems, of the interests of taxpayers with the
  responsibilities of councils to govern the affairs of First Nations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} support and promote positive relationships between
  First Nations and taxpayers, including through the provision of
  support services for the resolution of disputes related to First
  Nations local revenue systems;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} assist First Nations in the exercise of their
  jurisdiction over local revenues;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} develop and deliver, and provide support for the
  development and delivery of, training and education services --- and
  conduct research --- respecting the implementation and administration
  of First Nations local revenue systems, respecting First Nations
  economic growth and respecting the evolution of local revenue systems;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} assist First Nations in growing their economies and
  increasing their local revenues;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} promote transparency of First Nations local revenue
  systems and understanding of those systems by members of First
  Nations, taxpayers and the public;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} conduct research and provide advice and information
  to the Government of Canada, including the Minister, regarding the
  future development and implementation of frameworks to support First
  Nations in exercising their jurisdiction over local revenues;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} conduct research, analyze information and provide
  advice to support the development, implementation and administration
  of First Nations local revenue systems;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} collaborate with First Nations, Indigenous
  institutions and organizations and all levels of government to
  strengthen First Nations economies and support the development of
  legal and administrative frameworks to promote the evolution of their
  jurisdiction over local revenues;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} support the negotiation, development and
  implementation of agreements related to First Nations local revenue
  systems;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} provide services to any Indigenous group named in the
  schedule to any regulation made under section 141; and

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} collect data, publish statistical information and
  conduct research and analysis on matters related to the purposes set
  out in the other paragraphs of this section.

::: HistoricalNote

### [Purposes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-225294 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandate

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** The purposes of the Board
are to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} assist First Nations and entities referred to in
  subsection 50.1(1) in developing the capacity to meet their financial
  management requirements;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} assist First Nations and entities referred to in
  subsection 50.1(1) in developing and implementing laws and by-laws
  respecting financial administration;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} assist First Nations and entities referred to in
  paragraphs 50.1(1)(a) to (c) in their dealings with different levels
  of governments respecting financial management, including matters of
  accountability and shared fiscal responsibility;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} assist First Nations and entities referred to in
  paragraphs 50.1(1)(a) to (c) in the development, implementation and
  improvement of financial relationships with financial institutions,
  business partners and different levels of governments, to enable the
  economic and social development of First Nations and of those
  entities;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} develop and support the application of general credit
  rating criteria to First Nations;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide review and audit services respecting First
  Nation financial management;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} provide assessment and certification services
  respecting First Nation financial management and financial
  performance;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} provide monitoring and reporting services respecting
  financial management systems and financial performance;

- [(g.1)]{.lawlabel} provide First Nations and entities referred to in
  subsection 50.1(1) with monitoring and reporting services respecting
  the implementation of laws and by-laws respecting financial
  administration and the compliance of those laws and by-laws with
  applicable standards;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} provide services respecting the co-management and
  third-party management of local revenues and other revenues;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} provide advice, policy research and review and
  evaluative services on the development of fiscal arrangements between
  different levels of governments and First Nations, as well as on the
  development of fiscal arrangements between different levels of
  governments and entities referred to in paragraphs 50.1(1)(a) to (c);

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} develop, implement, test and evaluate, as well as
  conduct research with respect to, proposals and pilot projects related
  to the purposes set out in the other paragraphs of this section;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} assist First Nations, entities referred to in
  subsection 50.1(1), other levels of government and public and private
  organizations in the development and implementation of fiscal and
  economic proposals that contribute to responding to the Truth and
  Reconciliation Commission of Canada's Calls to Action and to
  implementing the United Nations Declaration on the Rights of
  Indigenous Peoples; and

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} collect data, publish statistical information and
  conduct research and analysis on matters related to the purposes set
  out in the other paragraphs of this section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Imposed co-management --- local revenues

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may, on giving notice to the council of a First Nation, require
  the First Nation to enter into a co-management arrangement in respect
  of the First Nation's local revenues, including its local revenue
  account,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if, in the opinion of the Board, there is a serious
    risk that the First Nation will default on an obligation to the
    First Nations Finance Authority relating to a loan secured by local
    revenues; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Board has received a notice under paragraph
    33(3)(b) or subsection 86(4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Under the co-management arrangement, the Board may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} recommend amendments to a law of the First Nation
    made under any of paragraphs 5(1)(a) to (f) or subsection 9(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} recommend changes to the First Nation's
    expenditures or budgets with respect to its local revenues;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} recommend improvements to the First Nation's
    financial management system with respect to its local revenues;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} recommend changes to the delivery of programs and
    services paid for out of the First Nation's local revenues;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} order that expenditures of local revenues of the
    First Nation be approved by, or paid with cheques co-signed by, a
    manager appointed by the Board; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} with respect to local revenues, exercise any powers
    delegated to the Board under a law of the First Nation or under an
    agreement between the First Nation and the Board or the First Nation
    and the First Nations Finance Authority.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination by Board

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board may terminate the co-management arrangement
  on giving notice to the council of the First Nation that the Board is
  of the opinion that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is no longer a serious risk that the First
    Nation will default on an obligation to the First Nations Finance
    Authority relating to a loan secured by local revenues;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a First Nation that was in default
    of a payment obligation to the First Nations Finance Authority
    relating to a loan secured by local revenues, the First Nation has
    remedied the default;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the co-management arrangement is no longer
    required; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} third-party management of the First Nation's local
    revenues is required.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opinion final

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An opinion given by the Board under this section is
  final and conclusive and is not subject to appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall advise the First Nations Finance
  Authority and the First Nations Tax Commission of the commencement or
  termination of the co-management arrangement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Third-party management --- local revenues

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may, on giving notice to the council of a First Nation and to
  the Minister, assume management of the First Nation's local revenues,
  including its local revenue account,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if, in the opinion of the Board, the co-management
    arrangement entered into under section 52 has not been effective;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if, in the opinion of the Board, there is a serious
    risk that the First Nation will default on an obligation to the
    First Nations Finance Authority relating to a loan secured by local
    revenues; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the Board has received a notice under paragraph
    33(3)(b) or subsection 86(4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Board assumes third-party management of the
  local revenues of a First Nation, the Board has the exclusive
  authority to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (3), act in the place of the
    council of the First Nation to make laws under paragraphs 5(1)(a)
    to (f) and subsection 9(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} act in the place of the council of the First Nation
    to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} with respect to local revenues, exercise any
      powers and fulfil any obligations of the council under this Act,
      including under the regulations made under this Act, or under any
      laws made under paragraphs 5(1)(a) to (e) and 9(1)(a),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} manage the First Nation's local revenues,
      including the local revenue account,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} undertake any necessary borrowing for the
      purpose of remedying the situation for which third-party
      management was required, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} provide for the delivery of programs and
      services that are paid for out of the First Nation's local
      revenues, manage assets related to those programs and services and
      enter into or terminate agreements in respect of those programs,
      services and assets;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2018, c. 27, s. 399\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} assign interests or rights under subsection 5(7);
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} with respect to local revenues, exercise any powers
    or fulfil any obligations delegated to the Board under a law of the
    First Nation or an agreement between the First Nation and the Board
    or between the First Nation and the First Nations Finance Authority.

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2018, c. 27, s. 399\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation --- consent of council
  required

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall not make a law under paragraph
  5(1)(f) or 9(1)(b) that delegates a power to a person or body to whom
  a power was not delegated at the time the Board assumed third-party
  management of the local revenues of a First Nation, unless the council
  of the First Nation gives its consent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The council of the First Nation shall not, during the
  time that the board assumes third-party management of the First
  Nation's local revenues, repeal any law made under paragraph 5(1)(g).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review every six months

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board has assumed third-party management of
  a First Nation's local revenues, it shall review the need for
  third-party management at least once every six months and advise the
  First Nations Finance Authority, the First Nations Tax Commission and
  the council of the First Nation of the results of its review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board not agent or mandatary

  [(5.1)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Board is not an agent or
  mandatary of the First Nations Finance Authority or the First Nations
  Tax Commission while exercising its exclusive authority under
  subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination by Board

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Board may terminate third-party management of a
  First Nation's local revenues, on giving notice to the council of the
  First Nation, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is of the opinion that there is no longer a
    serious risk that the First Nation will default on an obligation to
    the First Nations Finance Authority relating to a loan secured by
    local revenues and the Authority consents to the termination in
    writing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a First Nation that was in default
    of an obligation to the First Nations Finance Authority relating to
    a loan secured by local revenues, it is of the opinion that the
    First Nation has remedied the default and the Authority consents to
    the termination in writing;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is of the opinion that the situation for which
    third-party management of the First Nation's local revenues was
    required has been remedied; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of third-party management that was
    assumed by the Board following receipt of a notice under subsection
    86(4), the First Nations Finance Authority has, in writing,
    requested the termination and stated its reasons for the request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opinion final

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} An opinion given by the Board under this section is
  final and conclusive and is not subject to appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall advise the First Nations Finance
  Authority and First Nations Tax Commission of the assumption or
  termination of third-party management of a First Nation's local
  revenues.

### [Establishment and Organization of Authority]{.HTitleText2} {#h-225469 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby
established a non-profit corporation without share capital, to be known
as the First Nations Finance Authority.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Membership

**[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** The members of the
Authority shall be its borrowing members and investing members.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not agent of Her Majesty

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Authority is not an agent of Her Majesty or a Crown corporation within

  its officers and employees are not part of the federal public
  administration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No guarantees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall give a guarantee on behalf of Her
  Majesty for the discharge of an obligation or liability of the

### [Purposes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-225572 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandate

**[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** The purposes of the
Authority are to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} secure for its borrowing members, through the use of
  property tax revenues,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} loans that have terms of one year or longer to
    finance or refinance capital assets for the provision of local
    services on reserve lands, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} loans that have terms of less than one year to
    meet cash-flow requirements for operating or capital purposes or to
    refinance a short-term debt incurred for capital purposes;

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2023, c. 16, s. 35\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} secure for its borrowing members, through the use of
  other revenues, loans for any purpose that promotes a First Nation's
  economic or social development, including loans for

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} capital assets that are to be wholly or partly
    owned by the First Nation, including capital assets for the
    provision of services, housing, plants, machinery, roads and
    buildings,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} rolling stock that is to be wholly or partly owned
    by the First Nation,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} land that is to be wholly or partly owned by the
    First Nation,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} shares or any other ownership interest in a
    corporation whose purpose includes the ownership, operation,
    management or sale of the products of power generating facilities,
    waste or wastewater treatment facilities or other public service
    utilities or facilities, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} short-term financing to meet cash flow requirements
    for capital purposes or to refinance a short-term debt incurred for
    capital purposes;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} secure the best possible credit terms for its
  borrowing members;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide investment services to First Nations and
  entities referred to in any of paragraphs 50.1(1)(a) to (e); and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide advice regarding the development of financing
  mechanisms for First Nations.

    paid before maturity, for a term not longer than the remainder of

    Management Board under subsection 50(3) and forwarded a copy of it
    to the Authority;

    established a secured revenues trust account that is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} managed by a third party approved by the

      may prevent or remedy the contravention of those laws,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} recover costs incurred by the First Nation in
      enforcing those laws and impose and recover interest and penalties
      with respect to those costs,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} create liens or, in Quebec, prior claims or
      legal hypothecs on reserve lands and on interests or rights in
      reserve lands, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} discontinue services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, laws made under subsection (1)
  apply only on the reserve lands of the First Nation that made the
  laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-compliance with measure

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person or entity is not complying with a measure
  referred to in subparagraph (1)(c)(i) or (ii), the First Nation may
  take appropriate corrective measures at the expense of the person or
  entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to competent court

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The First Nation may apply to a court of competent
  jurisdiction for an order directing a person or entity named in the
  application to comply with a law made under subsection (1), such as by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} refraining from doing anything that, in the opinion
    of the court, constitutes or is directed toward the contravention of
    that law; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} doing anything that, in the opinion of the court,
    may prevent the contravention of that law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement --- [Framework Agreement on
  First Nation Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46)

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a First Nation has adopted a [[land
  code]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(2) of the
  [Framework Agreement on First Nation Land Management
  Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46) or the council of a First Nation has enacted a
  [[First Nation law]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection
  2(1) of that Act, the First Nation may use any enforcement measure ---
  other than a measure for the investigation or prosecution of an
  offence punishable on summary conviction referred to in paragraph

  that land code or First Nation law to enforce a law made under
  subsection (1).

### [Purposes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1405610 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandate

**[[113.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-113.3 .sectionLabel}** The purposes of the
Institute are to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} assist First Nations and entities referred to in
  subsection 50.1(1) in planning, developing, procuring, owning,
  managing, operating and maintaining infrastructure, including by
  providing review, analysis, assessment, certification and monitoring
  services;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} assist First Nations in the exercise of their
  jurisdiction over the provision of [[services]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
  as defined in section 96, and over infrastructure;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide services or support the provision of services
  respecting asset management;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} develop and deliver, and provide support for the
  development and delivery of, training and education services --- and
  conduct research --- respecting infrastructure and social, cultural,
  environmental, economic and fiscal sustainability;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} support capacity development for infrastructure
  planning, development, procurement, management, operation,
  maintenance, funding and financing by First Nations and entities
  referred to in subsection 50.1(1);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} advance options to assist First Nations and entities
  referred to in subsection 50.1(1) in developing and implementing
  approaches to support the stable, effective and long-term funding and
  financing of infrastructure;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} collaborate with First Nations, Indigenous
  institutions and organizations and all levels of government to support
  the development of legal and administrative frameworks to improve the
  planning, development, procurement, management, operation and
  maintenance of infrastructure;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} provide policy research services, review and
  evaluative services and advice to support First Nations and entities
  referred to in subsection 50.1(1) in developing fiscal frameworks and
  revenue streams to support the development, management, operation and
  maintenance of infrastructure;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} conduct research and provide advice and information
  to the Government of Canada, including the Minister, on the
  development and implementation of frameworks to support the
  development of infrastructure that is socially, culturally,
  environmentally, economically and fiscally sustainable; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} collect data, publish statistical information and
  conduct research and analysis on matters related to the purposes set
  out in the other paragraphs of this section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial year

**[[116]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-116 .sectionLabel}** The financial year of

otherwise prescribed by regulation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenditure of revenues

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporate plans

- **[[118]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-118 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  institution shall, in accordance with any directions given by the
  Minister, establish a five-year corporate plan and a budget for each
  financial year and submit them to the Minister for approval.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scope and contents of corporate plan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The corporate plan of each institution shall
  encompass all of the businesses and activities of the institution and
  include a statement of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the objects or purposes of the institution;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the institution's objectives for the five-year
    period to which the plan relates and the strategy it intends to
    employ to achieve those objectives; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the institution's expected performance for that
    five-year period as compared to its objectives set out in the last
    corporate plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of budget

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The budget of each institution must include a
  statement of the institution's projected revenues and expenses for the
  financial year on account of capital and operations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of corporate plan

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The corporate plan of each institution shall be
  prepared in a form that clearly sets out information according to the
  major businesses or activities of the institution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on business or activity

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} No institution may carry on any business or activity
  in any financial year in a manner that is not consistent with its
  corporate plan for that year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Any amendment by an institution to its corporate plan
  or budget shall be submitted to the Minister for approval.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Books and systems

- **[[119]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-119 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep books of account and records in relation to

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The books, records and systems referred to in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the institution's assets are safeguarded and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its transactions are in accordance with this Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its financial, human and physical resources are
    managed economically and efficiently; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} its operations are carried out effectively.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An institution may cause internal audits to be

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual auditor's report

- **[[120]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-120 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any quantitative information required to be audited
    under subsection (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A report under subsection (1) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} include separate statements as to whether in the
    auditor's opinion

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the financial statements are presented fairly, in
      accordance with generally accepted accounting principles, applied
      on a basis consistent with that of the preceding year,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quantitative information is accurate in all
      material respects and, if applicable, was prepared on a basis
      consistent with that of the preceding year, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the transactions of the institution that have
      come to the auditor's notice in the course of their examination
      for the report were carried out in accordance with this Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} call attention to any other matter falling within
    the scope of the auditor's examination for the report that, in their
    opinion, should be brought to the attention of the institution or
    the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audit of quantitative information

  pursuant to paragraph (2)(a) be audited.

  statements to the Minister at least 30 days before the day of its
  annual meeting.

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} review, and advise the board of directors in

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} perform any other functions that are assigned to it

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calling meeting

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2012, c. 19, s. 667

  - ###### Definition {#definition .MarginalNote}

    [667]{.lawlabel} In sections 668 to 673,
    [[Institute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the First Nations
    Statistical Institute.

- --- 2012, c. 19, s. 668

  - ###### Appointments terminated {#appointments-terminated .MarginalNote}

    - [668]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Directors of the Institute,
      including the Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson, cease to hold
      office on the coming into force of this subsection.

    - ###### No compensation {#no-compensation .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite the provisions of any contract, agreement
      or order, no person appointed to hold office as a director of the
      Institute, including as the Chairperson or Vice-Chairperson, has
      any right to claim or receive any compensation, damages, indemnity
      or other form of relief from Her Majesty in right of Canada or
      from any employee or agent of Her Majesty for ceasing to hold that
      office or for the abolition of that office by the operation of
      this Division.

- --- 2012, c. 19, s. 669

  - ###### References {#references .MarginalNote}

    [669]{.lawlabel} Every reference to the Institute in a deed,
    contract or other document executed by the Institute in its own name
    is to be read, unless the context otherwise requires, as a reference
    to Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- --- 2012, c. 19, s. 670

  - ###### Minister authorized {#minister-authorized .MarginalNote}

    [670]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern
    Development is authorized to sell or otherwise dispose of all of the
    Institute's assets and to do everything necessary for or incidental
    to closing out the Institute's affairs.

- --- 2012, c. 19, s. 671

  - ###### Surplus {#surplus .MarginalNote}

    - [671]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any surplus that remains after
      the satisfaction of the Institute's debts and liabilities and the
      winding-up charges, costs and expenses belongs to Her Majesty in
      right of Canada.

    - ###### Unsatisfied debts and liabilities {#unsatisfied-debts-and-liabilities .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any of the Institute's debts and liabilities that
      remain unsatisfied on the day on which the Institute is dissolved
      become the debts and liabilities of Her Majesty in right of
      Canada.

    - ###### Exception {#exception .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), Her Majesty in right of
      Canada is not liable in respect of any claim against the Institute
      arising from its exercise of, or its failure to exercise, any of
      its powers or functions.

- --- 2012, c. 19, s. 672

  - ###### Commencement of legal proceedings {#commencement-of-legal-proceedings .MarginalNote}

    - [672]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal
      proceeding in respect of an obligation or liability incurred by
      the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development in closing
      out the Institute's affairs may be brought against Her Majesty in
      right of Canada in any court that would have had jurisdiction if
      the action, suit or other legal proceeding had been brought
      against the Institute.

    - ###### Continuation of legal proceedings {#continuation-of-legal-proceedings .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal proceeding to
      which the Institute is a party --- other than an action, suit or
      legal proceeding in respect of any claim against the Institute
      arising from its exercise of, or failure to exercise, any of its
      powers or functions --- that is pending in a court on the coming
      into force of this subsection may be continued by or against Her
      Majesty in right of Canada in the same manner and to the same
      extent as it could have been continued by or against the
      Institute.

- --- 2012, c. 19, s. 673

  - ###### Limit of liability {#limit-of-liability .MarginalNote}

    [673]{.lawlabel} No civil proceedings lie against a former director
    or employee of the Institute for anything done, or omitted to be
    done, in the exercise or purported exercise in good faith of any
    power, or in the performance or purported performance in good faith
    of any duty, of that person in accordance with the [First Nations
    Fiscal Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.67), as that Act read
    immediately before the coming into force of section 660.

- --- 2023, c. 16, s. 60

  - ###### Transition to five-year plan {#transition-to-five-year-plan .MarginalNote}

    [60]{.lawlabel} An [[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined
    in section 114 of the [First Nations Fiscal Management
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11.67), may, for the financial year following the
    day on which section 45 comes into force, establish a corporate plan
    in accordance with section 118 of that Act either as it read
    immediately before that day or as it reads on or after that day.

- --- 2023, c. 16, s. 61

  - ###### Part 6 of [First Nations Fiscal Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.67) {#part-6-of-first-nations-fiscal-management-act .MarginalNote}

    - [61]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 118(1), 120(4),
      121(1) and 128(1), section 129 and subsection 130(1) of the [First
      Nations Fiscal Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.67) do not apply to
      the Institute or to its present or former directors, officers,
      employees or agents or mandataries until the first day of the
      second financial year following the financial year in which all
      the directors have been appointed under section 104 of that Act.

    - ###### Institute's first annual meeting {#institutes-first-annual-meeting .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 131(1) of the [First Nations
      Fiscal Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.67), the board of directors
      of the Institute must call its first annual meeting within 18
      months after the first day of the second financial year following
      the financial year in which all the directors have been appointed
      under section 104 of that Act.

    - ###### Definition of [[Institute]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} {#definition-of-institute .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section,
      [[Institute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 101 of the [First Nations Fiscal Management
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11.67).
