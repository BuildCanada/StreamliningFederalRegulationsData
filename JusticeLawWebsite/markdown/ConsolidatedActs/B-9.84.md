## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[First Nations' Sales Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-35872 .Part}

### [General Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-35873 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in this Part.

  [[alcohol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[alcohol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means ethyl
      alcohol. ([alcool]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[alcoholic beverage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[alcoholic beverage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} beer, within the meaning assigned by section
        B.02.130 of the [Food and Drug
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), that contains
        more than 0.5% alcohol by volume;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ale, stout, porter or malt liquor, within the
        meaning assigned by section B.02.131 of the [Food and Drug
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), that contains
        more than 0.5% alcohol by volume;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} wine, within the meaning assigned by section 2
        of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1);

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any beverage that contains more than 0.5%
        alcohol by volume, that is obtained from the distillation of
        grains, fruits or other agricultural products or from the
        distillation of beer or wine; and

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other beverage that contains a combination
        of any beverage referred to in paragraphs (a) to (d) that is
        suitable for human consumption and that contains more than 0.5%
        alcohol by volume. ([boisson alcoolisée]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[council of a band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[council of a band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      the expression [[council of the band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Indian
      Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[direct]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[direct]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning, for the
      purpose of distinguishing between a direct and an indirect tax, as
      in class 2 of section 92 of the [Constitution Act,
      1867](/eng/Const/). ([directe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} diesel fuel, including any fuel oil that is
        suitable for use in internal combustion engines of the
        compression-ignition type, other than fuel oil that is intended
        for use and is actually used as heating oil;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} gasoline type fuels for use in internal
        combustion engines; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} propane gas. ([carburant]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
      Finance. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[tobacco product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[tobacco product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned
      by section 2 of the [Excise Act,
      2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1). ([produit du tabac]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expressions defined in other Acts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Part,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the expressions [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and
    [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have the meanings assigned by
    subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the expressions [[supply]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and
    [[taxable supply]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have the meanings assigned by
    subsection 123(1) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tax on specific supplies

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite section 87 of the [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5), the council of a band listed in the schedule may
  make a by-law imposing a direct tax in respect of taxable supplies of
  alcoholic beverages, fuel or tobacco products made on the reserves of
  the band at the rates at which tax is imposed under subsections 165(1)
  and (3) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) in respect of those
  supplies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collection of tax

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A direct tax imposed under a by-law made under
  subsection (1) shall be collected pursuant to an administration
  agreement entered into under subsection 26(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supply on a reserve

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a supply is made
  on a reserve only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} tax under section 165 of the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15) is not payable in respect of the supply because
    of the connection of the supply with the reserve and the application
    of section 87 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} tax under section 165 of the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15) would not be payable in respect of the supply
    for the reasons set out in paragraph (a) if the recipient of the
    supply were eligible for exemption from taxation under section 87 of
    the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of section 87 of the [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  greater certainty, except in respect of a tax imposed by a by-law made
  under subsection 24(1), nothing in that subsection affects the
  application of section 87 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indian moneys

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Moneys raised pursuant to a direct tax imposed under
  subsection 24(1) are not Indian moneys within the meaning of
  subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenditures

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Expenditures made out of moneys raised pursuant to a
  tax imposed under a by-law made under subsection 24(1) must be made
  under the authority of a resolution approved by a majority of
  councillors present at a meeting of the council of a band that is duly
  convened.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-law

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A by-law made under subsection 24(1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is valid only if approved by a majority of
    councillors present at a meeting of the council of a band that is
    duly convened;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not come into force before it has been
    received by the Minister and an administration agreement has been
    entered into under subsection 26(1);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} shall provide that the rates of tax under the
    by-law are those at which tax is imposed under subsections 165(1)
    and (3) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} may be made in respect of any matter arising out of
    or ancillary to the exercise of powers under this subsection; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} is not subject to the [Statutory Instruments
    Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of by-law

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A copy of a by-law made under this Part, if it is
  certified to be a true copy by the Minister or a person authorized by
  the Minister, is evidence that the by-law was duly made by the council
  of a band and received by the Minister, without proof of the signature
  or the authorization of the person, and no such by-law is invalid by
  reason of any defect in form.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The council of a band shall, on demand, provide a
  copy of any by-law made by the council under this Part and shall
  publish a copy of every such by-law in a newspaper that has general
  circulation in the place where the tax applies and in the First
  Nations Gazette, but no by-law shall be invalid by reason of a failure
  to publish it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement with Government of Canada

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  council of a band has made a by-law imposing a tax under this Part,
  the council may enter into an administration agreement within the
  meaning of subsection 2(1) of the [Federal-Provincial Fiscal
  Arrangements Act](/eng/acts/F-7.8) in respect of the by-law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules where agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the council of a band has entered into an
  administration agreement under subsection (1) in respect of a by-law,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Part IX of, and Schedules V, VI and VII to, the
    [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) apply for the purposes of the
    by-law as if the tax imposed under the by-law were imposed under
    section 165 of that Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15)
    applies for all other purposes as if the by-law were part of that
    Part and the tax imposed under the by-law were imposed under that
    Part;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the by-law applies as if Part IX of the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15) were part of the by-law and tax imposed under
    that Part were imposed under the by-law;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for greater certainty,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where a person does anything to satisfy a
      requirement of the by-law that would satisfy a corresponding
      requirement of Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) if
      the tax imposed under the by-law were imposed under section 165 of
      that Act, the requirement of the by-law is deemed to have been
      satisfied,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where a person does anything to exercise an
      authority under the by-law that would be a valid exercise of a
      corresponding authority under Part IX of the [Excise Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/E-15) if the tax imposed under the by-law were
      imposed under section 165 of that Act, the authority under the
      by-law is deemed to have been validly exercised, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} every person who is a registrant for the
      purposes of Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) is a
      registrant for the purposes of the by-law; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any proceeding that could be taken under any other
    Act of Parliament in respect of the tax imposed under section 165 of
    the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) may be taken in respect of the
    tax imposed under the by-law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tax under subsection 165(1) of the
[Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) not payable

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** No tax is payable under
subsection 165(1) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) in respect of
a supply if tax is payable in respect of the supply under a by-law made
under subsection 24(1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Where a person commits an
act or omission in respect of a by-law made under subsection 24(1) that
would be an offence under a provision of Part IX of the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15) if the act or omission were committed in relation
to that Part,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (b), the person is guilty of an
  offence punishable on summary conviction;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Attorney General of Canada may elect to prosecute
  the person by indictment where an offence under that provision of that
  Part may be prosecuted by indictment; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person is liable on conviction to the punishment
  provided for in that provision of that Part.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of schedule

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, amend the schedule by adding to it or deleting from it
the name of any band.
