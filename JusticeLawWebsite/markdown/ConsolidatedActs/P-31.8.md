of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

### [Enactment of Schedules]{.HTitleText2} {#h-402869 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Schedule I

**[[106]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-106 .sectionLabel}** The [Special Retirement
Arrangements Act](/eng/acts/S-15.2), as set out in Schedule I, is hereby
enacted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Schedule II

**[[107]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-107 .sectionLabel}** The [Pension Benefits
Division Act](/eng/acts/P-6.7), as set out in Schedule II, is hereby
enacted.

### [Transitional]{.HTitleText2} {#h-402874 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transitional

**[[108]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-108 .sectionLabel}** Payments made to the
Supplementary Retirement Benefits Account on or after April 1, 1991,
pursuant to Part III of the [Public Service Superannuation
Act](/eng/acts/P-36), Part III of the [Canadian Forces Superannuation
Act](/eng/acts/C-17), or Part III of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11), as those Parts read immediately
prior to the day on which this Act is assented to, are deemed to have
been made to the Superannuation Account, the Canadian Forces
Superannuation Account or the Royal Canadian Mounted Police
Superannuation Account, as the case may be.

  - ###### Retroactive coming into force {#retroactive-coming-into-force .MarginalNote}

    [184]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 109(1) of [An Act to amend
    certain Acts in relation to pensions and to enact the Special
    Retirement Arrangements Act and the Pension Benefits Division
    Act](/eng/acts/P-31.8), chapter 46 of the Statutes of Canada, 1992,
    (in this section referred to as the "amending Act") and Order in
    Council P.C. 1994-2097, made on December 14, 1994 and registered as
    SI/94-146,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subsections 2(4) and 6(2), sections 8, 11 and 18,
      subsection 33(2), sections 40 and 41, subsection 48(1) and
      sections 61, 68 and 70 of the amending Act are deemed to have come
      into force on December 15, 1994;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 42.1(1)(a) of the [Public Service
      Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), as enacted by section 22 of
      the amending Act, is deemed to have come into force on December
      15, 1994;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 50.1(1)(a) of the [Canadian Forces
      Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17), as enacted by section 49 of
      the amending Act, is deemed to have come into force on December
      15, 1994;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 26.1(1)(a) of the [Royal Canadian
      Mounted Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11), as enacted by
      section 77 of the amending Act, is deemed to have come into force
      on December 15, 1994; and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} section 106 of the amending Act is deemed to have
      come into force on December 14, 1994.
