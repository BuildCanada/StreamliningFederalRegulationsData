## Emergencies Act {#emergencies-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. 22 (4th
Supp.)
:::

An Act to authorize the taking of special temporary measures to ensure
safety and security during national emergencies and to amend other Acts
in consequence thereof

::: ReaderNote
\[1988, c. 29, assented to 21st July, 1988\]
:::
:::::

::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the safety and security of the individual, the protection of the
values of the body politic and the preservation of the sovereignty,
security and territorial integrity of the state are fundamental
obligations of government;

AND WHEREAS the fulfilment of those obligations in Canada may be
seriously threatened by a national emergency and, in order to ensure
safety and security during such an emergency, the Governor in Council
should be authorized, subject to the supervision of Parliament, to take
special temporary measures that may not be appropriate in normal times;

AND WHEREAS the Governor in Council, in taking such special temporary
measures, would be subject to the Canadian Charter of Rights and
Freedoms and the [Canadian Bill of Rights](/eng/acts/C-12.3) and must
have regard to the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights,
particularly with respect to those fundamental rights that are not to be
limited or abridged even in a national emergency;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-213805 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Emergencies Act](/eng/acts/E-4.5).

## [Application and Construction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-213808 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This Act
  is binding on Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Federal jurisdiction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, nothing in this Act derogates
  from the authority of the Government of Canada to deal with
  emergencies on any property, territory or area in respect of which the
  Parliament of Canada has jurisdiction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National emergency

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Act, a [[national emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} is an urgent and
critical situation of a temporary nature that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} seriously endangers the lives, health or safety of
  Canadians and is of such proportions or nature as to exceed the
  capacity or authority of a province to deal with it, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} seriously threatens the ability of the Government of
  Canada to preserve the sovereignty, security and territorial integrity
  of Canada

and that cannot be effectively dealt with under any other law of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act shall
be construed or applied so as to confer on the Governor in Council the
power to make orders or regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} altering the provisions of this Act; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} providing for the detention, imprisonment or
  internment of Canadian citizens or permanent residents within the
  meaning of subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) on the basis of race, national or ethnic origin,
  colour, religion, sex, age or mental or physical disability.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Public Welfare Emergency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-213825 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-213826 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

[[declaration of a public welfare emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[declaration of a public welfare
    emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a proclamation issued pursuant
    to subsection 6(1); ([déclaration de sinistre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[public welfare emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public welfare emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an emergency
    that is caused by a real or imminent

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fire, flood, drought, storm, earthquake or other
      natural phenomenon,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} disease in human beings, animals or plants, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} accident or pollution

    and that results or may result in a danger to life or property,
    social disruption or a breakdown in the flow of essential goods,
    services or resources, so serious as to be a national
    emergency. ([sinistre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Declaration of a Public Welfare Emergency]{.HTitleText2} {#h-213835 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration of a public welfare emergency

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When the
  Governor in Council believes, on reasonable grounds, that a public
  welfare emergency exists and necessitates the taking of special
  temporary measures for dealing with the emergency, the Governor in
  Council, after such consultation as is required by section 14, may, by
  proclamation, so declare.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A declaration of a public welfare emergency shall
  specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} concisely the state of affairs constituting the
    emergency;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the special temporary measures that the Governor in
    Council anticipates may be necessary for dealing with the emergency;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the direct effects of the emergency do not
    extend to the whole of Canada, the area of Canada to which the
    direct effects of the emergency extend.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  declaration of a public welfare emergency is effective on the day on
  which it is issued, but a motion for confirmation of the declaration
  shall be laid before each House of Parliament and be considered in
  accordance with section 58.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expiration of declaration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A declaration of a public welfare emergency expires
  at the end of ninety days unless the declaration is previously revoked
  or continued in accordance with this Act.

### [Orders and Regulations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-213849 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} While a
  declaration of a public welfare emergency is in effect, the Governor
  in Council may make such orders or regulations with respect to the
  following matters as the Governor in Council believes, on reasonable
  grounds, are necessary for dealing with the emergency:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the regulation or prohibition of travel to, from or
    within any specified area, where necessary for the protection of the
    health or safety of individuals;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the evacuation of persons and the removal of
    personal property from any specified area and the making of
    arrangements for the adequate care and protection of the persons and
    property;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the requisition, use or disposition of property;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the authorization of or direction to any person, or
    any person of a class of persons, to render essential services of a
    type that that person, or a person of that class, is competent to
    provide and the provision of reasonable compensation in respect of
    services so rendered;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the regulation of the distribution and availability
    of essential goods, services and resources;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the authorization and making of emergency payments;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the establishment of emergency shelters and
    hospitals;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the assessment of damage to any works or
    undertakings and the repair, replacement or restoration thereof;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the assessment of damage to the environment and the
    elimination or alleviation of the damage; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the imposition

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, of a fine not exceeding
      five hundred dollars or imprisonment not exceeding six months or
      both that fine and imprisonment, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on indictment, of a fine not exceeding five
      thousand dollars or imprisonment not exceeding five years or both
      that fine and imprisonment,

    for contravention of any order or regulation made under this
    section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a declaration of a public welfare emergency
  specifies that the direct effects of the emergency extend only to a
  specified area of Canada, the power under subsection (1) to make
  orders and regulations, and any powers, duties or functions conferred
  or imposed by or pursuant to any such order or regulation, may be
  exercised or performed only with respect to that area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The power under subsection (1) to make orders and
  regulations, and any powers, duties or functions conferred or imposed
  by or pursuant to any such order or regulation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall be exercised or performed

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in a manner that will not unduly impair the
      ability of any province to take measures, under an Act of the
      legislature of the province, for dealing with an emergency in the
      province, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} with the view of achieving, to the extent
      possible, concerted action with each province with respect to
      which the power, duty or function is exercised or performed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall not be exercised or performed for the purpose
    of terminating a strike or lock-out or imposing a settlement in a
    labour dispute.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control or direction of police force

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Nothing
  in a declaration of a public welfare emergency or in any order or
  regulation made pursuant thereto shall be construed or applied so as
  to derogate from, or to authorize the derogation from, the control or
  direction of the government of a province or a municipality over any
  police force over which it normally has control or direction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}R.C.M.P.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Royal Canadian Mounted Police is used or
  employed in a province or municipality pursuant to an arrangement
  under section 20 of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
  Act](/eng/acts/R-10), subsection (1) applies in respect of the Royal
  Canadian Mounted Police, subject to the terms and conditions of the
  arrangement.

### [Revocation, Continuation and Amendment of Declaration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-213879 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation by Parliament

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Parliament may revoke a
declaration of a public welfare emergency in accordance with section 58
or 59.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation by Governor in Council

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by proclamation, revoke a declaration of a public welfare emergency
either generally or with respect to any area of Canada effective on such
day as is specified in the proclamation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation by Governor in Council

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At any
  time before a declaration of a public welfare emergency would
  otherwise expire, the Governor in Council, after such consultation as
  is required by section 14, may, by proclamation, continue the
  declaration either generally or with respect to any area of Canada for
  such period, not exceeding ninety days, as is specified in the
  proclamation if the Governor in Council believes, on reasonable
  grounds, that the emergency will continue to exist or that the direct
  effects of the emergency will continue to extend to that area, as the
  case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of orders and regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before issuing a proclamation continuing a
  declaration of a public welfare emergency, the Governor in Council
  shall review all current orders and regulations made under section 8
  to determine if the Governor in Council believes, on reasonable
  grounds, that they continue to be necessary for dealing with the
  emergency and shall revoke or amend them to the extent that they do
  not so continue.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Multiple continuations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A declaration of a public welfare emergency may be
  continued more than once pursuant to subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A proclamation continuing a declaration of a public
  welfare emergency is effective on the day on which it is issued, but a
  motion for confirmation of the proclamation shall be laid before each
  House of Parliament and be considered in accordance with section 60.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment by Governor in Council

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Governor in Council

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has issued a declaration of a public welfare
    emergency specifying that the direct effects of the emergency extend
    only to a specified area of Canada, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} believes, on reasonable grounds, that the direct
    effects of the emergency have extended to any other area of Canada
    or to the rest of Canada,

  the Governor in Council, after such consultation as is required by
  section 14, may, by proclamation, amend the declaration to specify
  that other area as an area of Canada to which the direct effects of
  the emergency extend or to remove the existing specification, as the
  case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A proclamation amending a declaration of a public
  welfare emergency is effective on the day on which it is issued, but a
  motion for confirmation of the proclamation shall be laid before each
  House of Parliament and be considered in accordance with section 60.

### [Consultation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-213901 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), before
  the Governor in Council issues, continues or amends a declaration of a
  public welfare emergency, the lieutenant governor in council of each
  province in which the direct effects of the emergency occur shall be
  consulted with respect to the proposed action.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may not issue a declaration
  of a public welfare emergency where the direct effects of the
  emergency are confined to, or occur principally in, one province
  unless the lieutenant governor in council of the province has
  indicated to the Governor in Council that the emergency exceeds the
  capacity or authority of the province to deal with it.

### [Effect of Expiration or Revocation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-213907 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of expiration of declaration

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  pursuant to this Act, a declaration of a public welfare emergency
  expires either generally or with respect to any area of Canada, all
  orders and regulations made pursuant to the declaration or all orders
  and regulations so made, to the extent that they apply with respect to
  that area, as the case may be, expire on the day on which the
  declaration expires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of revocation of declaration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, pursuant to this Act, a declaration of a
  public welfare emergency is revoked either generally or with respect
  to any area of Canada, all orders and regulations made pursuant to the
  declaration or all orders and regulations so made, to the extent that
  they apply with respect to that area, as the case may be, are revoked
  effective on the revocation of the declaration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of revocation of continuation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where, pursuant to this Act, a proclamation
  continuing a declaration of a public welfare emergency either
  generally or with respect to any area of Canada is revoked after the
  time the declaration would, but for the proclamation, have otherwise
  expired either generally or with respect to that area,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the declaration and all orders and regulations made
    pursuant to the declaration, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the declaration and all orders and regulations made
    pursuant to the declaration to the extent that the declaration,
    orders and regulations apply with respect to that area,

  as the case may be, are revoked effective on the revocation of the
  proclamation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of revocation of amendment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where, pursuant to this Act, a proclamation amending
  a declaration of a public welfare emergency is revoked, all orders and
  regulations made pursuant to the amendment and all orders and
  regulations to the extent that they apply pursuant to the amendment
  are revoked effective on the revocation of the proclamation.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Public Order Emergency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-213920 .Part}

  opinion that the lieutenant governor in council of a province in which
  the effects of the emergency occur cannot, before the issue or
  amendment of a declaration of a public order emergency, be adequately
  consulted without unduly jeopardizing the effectiveness of the
  proposed action, the lieutenant governor in council of that province
  may be consulted with respect to the action after the declaration is
  issued or amended and before the motion for confirmation of the
  declaration or amendment is laid before either House of Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indication

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may not issue a declaration
  of a public order emergency where the effects of the emergency are
  confined to one province, unless the lieutenant governor in council of
  the province has indicated to the Governor in Council that the
  emergency exceeds the capacity or authority of the province to deal
  with it.

### [Effect of Expiration or Revocation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-213997 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of expiration of declaration

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  pursuant to this Act, a declaration of a public order emergency
  expires either generally or with respect to any area of Canada, all
  orders and regulations made pursuant to the declaration or all orders
  and regulations so made, to the extent that they apply with respect to
  that area, as the case may be, expire on the day on which the
  declaration expires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of revocation of declaration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, pursuant to this Act, a declaration of a
  public order emergency is revoked either generally or with respect to
  any area of Canada, all orders and regulations made pursuant to the
  declaration or all orders and regulations so made, to the extent that
  they apply with respect to that area, as the case may be, are revoked
  effective on the revocation of the declaration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of revocation of continuation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where, pursuant to this Act, a proclamation
  continuing a declaration of a public order emergency either generally
  or with respect to any area of Canada is revoked after the time the
  declaration would, but for the proclamation, have otherwise expired
  either generally or with respect to that area,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the declaration and all orders and regulations made
    pursuant to the declaration, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the declaration and all orders and regulations made
    pursuant to the declaration to the extent that the declaration,
    orders and regulations apply with respect to that area,

  as the case may be, are revoked effective on the revocation of the
  proclamation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of revocation of amendment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where, pursuant to this Act, a proclamation amending
  a declaration of a public order emergency is revoked, all orders and
  regulations made pursuant to the amendment and all orders and
  regulations to the extent that they apply pursuant to the amendment
  are revoked effective on the revocation of the proclamation.

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[International Emergency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-214010 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-214011 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

[[declaration of an international emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[declaration of an international

    to subsection 28(1); ([déclaration d'état de crise
    internationale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[international emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an emergency
    involving Canada and one or more other countries that arises from
    acts of intimidation or coercion or the real or imminent use of

    emergency. ([état de crise internationale]{.DefinedTermLink

  international emergency exists and necessitates the taking of special
  temporary measures for dealing with the emergency, the Governor in
  Council, after such consultation as is required by section 35, may, by
  proclamation, so declare.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A declaration of an international emergency shall
  specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} concisely the state of affairs constituting the
    emergency; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the special temporary measures that the Governor in
    Council anticipates may be necessary for dealing with the emergency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  declaration of an international emergency is effective on the day on
  which it is issued, but a motion for confirmation of the declaration
  shall be laid before each House of Parliament and be considered in
  accordance with section 58.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expiration of declaration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A declaration of an international emergency expires
  at the end of sixty days unless the declaration is previously revoked
  or continued in accordance with this Act.

### [Orders and Regulations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-214029 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} While
  a declaration of an international emergency is in effect, the Governor
  in Council may make such orders or regulations with respect to the
  following matters as the Governor in Council believes, on reasonable
  grounds, are necessary for dealing with the emergency:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the control or regulation of any specified industry
    or service, including the use of equipment, facilities and
    inventory;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the appropriation, control, forfeiture, use and
    disposition of property or services;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the authorization and conduct of inquiries in
    relation to defence contracts or defence supplies as defined in the
    [Defence Production Act](/eng/acts/D-1) or to hoarding,
    overcharging, black marketing or fraudulent operations in respect of
    scarce commodities, including the conferment of powers under the
    [Inquiries Act](/eng/acts/I-11) on any person authorized to conduct
    such an inquiry;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the authorization of the entry and search of any
    dwelling-house, premises, conveyance or place, and the search of any
    person found therein, for any thing that may be evidence relevant to
    any matter that is the subject of an inquiry referred to in
    paragraph (c), and the seizure and detention of any such thing;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the authorization of or direction to any person, or
    any person of a class of persons, to render essential services of a
    type that that person, or a person of that class, is competent to
    provide and the provision of reasonable compensation in respect of
    services so rendered;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the designation and securing of protected places;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the regulation or prohibition of travel outside
    Canada by Canadian citizens or permanent residents within the
    meaning of subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee
    Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) and of admission into Canada of
    other persons;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the removal from Canada of persons, other than

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Canadian citizens,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} permanent residents within the meaning of
      subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
      Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} protected persons within the meaning of
      subsection 95(2) of that Act who are not inadmissible under that
      Act on grounds of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} security, violating human or international
        rights, sanctions or serious criminality, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} criminality and who have not been convicted of
        any offence under any Act of Parliament for which a term of
        imprisonment of more than six months has been imposed, or five
        years or more may be imposed;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the control or regulation of the international
    aspects of specified financial activities within Canada;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the authorization of expenditures for dealing with
    an international emergency in excess of any limit set by an Act of
    Parliament and the setting of a limit on such expenditures;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the authorization of any minister of the Crown to
    discharge specified responsibilities respecting the international
    emergency or to take specified actions of a political, diplomatic or
    economic nature for dealing with the emergency; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the imposition

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, of a fine not exceeding
      five hundred dollars or imprisonment not exceeding six months or
      both that fine and imprisonment, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on indictment, of a fine not exceeding five
      thousand dollars or imprisonment not exceeding five years or both
      that fine and imprisonment,

    for contravention of any order or regulation made under this
    section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The power under subsection (1) to make orders and
  regulations, and any powers, duties or functions conferred or imposed
  by or pursuant to any such order or regulation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall be exercised or performed

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in a manner that will not unduly impair the
      ability of any province to take measures, under an Act of the
      legislature of the province, for dealing with an emergency in the
      province, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} with the view of achieving, to the extent
      possible, concerted action with each province with respect to
      which the power, duty or function is exercised or performed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall not be exercised or performed for the purpose
    of censoring, suppressing or controlling the publication or
    communication of any information regardless of its form or
    characteristics.

### [Compensation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-214159 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2) and the
  regulations made under section 49, the Minister shall award reasonable
  compensation to any person who suffers loss, injury or damage as a
  result of any thing done, or purported to be done, under any of Parts
  I to IV or any proclamation, order or regulation issued or made
  thereunder.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Release

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No compensation shall be paid to a person unless that
  person, in consideration of the compensation, signs, in a form
  provided by the Minister, a release of any right of action that the
  person may have against the Crown as a result of any thing done, or
  purported to be done, under any of Parts I to IV or any proclamation,
  order or regulation issued or made thereunder.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subrogation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Crown is subrogated to all rights of any person
  to whom compensation is paid to recover damages in respect of the
  loss, injury or damage for which the compensation is paid and may
  maintain an action in the name of that person or in the name of the
  Crown against any person against whom the action lies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of recovered sums

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any sum recovered by the Crown pursuant to an action
  under subsection (3) shall be applied

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} first, to payment of the costs actually incurred in
    the action and in levying execution, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} second, to reimbursement of the Crown for the
    compensation paid to the person whose rights were subrogated,

  and the balance, if any, shall be paid to that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Settlement

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} No settlement or release bars the rights of the Crown
  under subsection (3) unless the Minister has concurred therein.

  compensation must be made;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the criteria to be used in determining
  the eligibility of any person for compensation;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the methods and criteria to be used in
  assessing any loss, injury or damage for which compensation shall be
  paid;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the maximum amount of compensation that
  may be paid to any person either generally or with respect to any
  particular loss, injury or damage;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the terms and conditions for the payment
  of compensation;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} providing for the payment of compensation in a lump
  sum or in periodic payments;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} providing for [pro rata]{.otherLang lang="la"}

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} establishing priorities among persons applying for
  compensation on the basis of classes of persons or classes of loss,

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} respecting the giving of notices to persons affected
  by applications for compensation; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} generally, for carrying into effect the purposes and
  provisions of this Part.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Assessor may designate a Deputy Assessor to hear
  and determine any appeal under this Part and, where the Assessor does
  so, the references in sections 52 and 53 to the "Assessor" shall be
  construed as including references to the "Deputy Assessor".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who has applied for compensation and is not satisfied with the
  decision of the Minister thereon may appeal the decision to the
  Assessor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No appeal may be made under this section more than
  three months after the day on which the person applying for
  compensation receives notice of the Minister's decision thereon or
  such longer period as the Assessor may, either before or after the
  expiration of that period of three months, allow for special reasons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Assessor

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  hearing of an appeal under this Part, the Assessor may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} confirm the decision of the Minister;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notwithstanding the maximum amount, if any, of
    compensation that may be paid to the person appealing, vary the
    decision of the Minister; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} refer the matter back to the Minister for such
    further action as the Assessor may direct, including the calculation
    of compensation without regard to the maximum amount, if any, that
    may otherwise be paid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In any appeal under this Part, costs may be awarded
  to or against the Crown.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision final

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The decision of the Assessor on any appeal under this
  Part is final and conclusive and, except for judicial review under the
  [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7), is not subject to appeal to or
  review by any court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Assessor varies a decision of the Minister
  by awarding compensation or increasing the amount of compensation
  awarded by the Minister or, on a matter referred back for further
  action, the Minister increases the amount of compensation previously
  awarded, the Minister shall pay that compensation or increased
  compensation, as the case may be.

## [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Parliamentary Supervision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-214226 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-214227 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

[[declaration of emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[declaration of emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    proclamation issued pursuant to subsection 6(1), 17(1), 28(1) or
    38(1); ([déclaration de situation de crise]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Parliamentary Review Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Parliamentary Review Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    committee referred to in subsection 62(1); ([comité d'examen
    parlementaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sitting day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sitting day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a House of
    Parliament, means a day on which that House is sitting. ([jour de
    séance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Consideration of Declaration of Emergency]{.HTitleText2} {#h-214233 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling in Parliament when sitting

- **[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), a motion
  for confirmation of a declaration of emergency, signed by a minister
  of the Crown, together with an explanation of the reasons for issuing
  the declaration and a report on any consultation with the lieutenant
  governors in council of the provinces with respect to the declaration,
  shall be laid before each House of Parliament within seven sitting
  days after the declaration is issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summoning Parliament or House

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a declaration of emergency is issued during a
  prorogation of Parliament or when either House of Parliament stands
  adjourned, Parliament or that House, as the case may be, shall be
  summoned forthwith to sit within seven days after the declaration is
  issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summoning Parliament

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a declaration of emergency is issued at a time
  when the House of Commons is dissolved, Parliament shall be summoned
  to sit at the earliest opportunity after the declaration is issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling in Parliament after summoned

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where Parliament or a House of Parliament is summoned
  to sit in accordance with subsection (2) or (3), the motion,
  explanation and report described in subsection (1) shall be laid
  before each House of Parliament or that House of Parliament, as the
  case may be, on the first sitting day after Parliament or that House
  is summoned.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a motion is laid before a House of Parliament
  as provided in subsection (1) or (4), that House shall, on the sitting
  day next following the sitting day on which the motion was so laid,
  take up and consider the motion.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vote

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A motion taken up and considered in accordance with
  subsection (5) shall be debated without interruption and, at such time
  as the House is ready for the question, the Speaker shall forthwith,
  without further debate or amendment, put every question necessary for
  the disposition of the motion.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of declaration

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If a motion for confirmation of a declaration of
  emergency is negatived by either House of Parliament, the declaration,
  to the extent that it has not previously expired or been revoked, is
  revoked effective on the day of the negative vote and no further
  action under this section need be taken in the other House with
  respect to the motion.

### [Revocation of Declaration of Emergency]{.HTitleText2} {#h-214249 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motion for revocation

- **[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a motion, for the consideration of the Senate or the House of Commons,
  to the effect that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a declaration of emergency under Part I or II be
    revoked either generally or with respect to any area of Canada, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a declaration of emergency under Part III or IV be
    revoked,

  signed by not less than ten members of the Senate or twenty members of
  the House of Commons, as the case may be, is filed with the Speaker
  thereof, that House of Parliament shall take up and consider the
  motion within three sitting days after it is filed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vote

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A motion taken up and considered in accordance with
  subsection (1) shall be debated without interruption for not more than
  ten hours and, on the expiration of the tenth hour or at such earlier
  time as the House is ready for the question, the Speaker shall
  forthwith, without further debate or amendment, put every question
  necessary for the disposition of the motion.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of declaration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a motion debated in accordance with subsection (2)
  is adopted by the House, the declaration, to the extent that it has
  not previously expired or been revoked, is revoked in accordance with
  the motion, effective on the day specified in the motion, which day
  may not be earlier than the day of the vote adopting the motion.

### [Consideration of Continuation or Amendment of Declaration of Emergency]{.HTitleText2} {#h-214260 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motion for confirmation of proclamation
continuing a declaration

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  motion for confirmation of a proclamation continuing a declaration of
  emergency and of any orders and regulations named in the motion
  pursuant to subsection (3), signed by a minister of the Crown,
  together with an explanation of the reasons for issuing the
  proclamation, a report on any consultation with the lieutenant
  governors in council of the provinces with respect to the proclamation
  and a report on the review of orders and regulations conducted before
  the issuing of the proclamation, shall be laid before each House of
  Parliament within seven sitting days after the proclamation is issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motion for confirmation of proclamation
  amending a declaration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A motion for confirmation of a proclamation amending
  a declaration of emergency, signed by a minister of the Crown,
  together with an explanation of the reasons for issuing the
  proclamation and a report on any consultation with the lieutenant
  governors in council of the provinces with respect to the
  proclamation, shall be laid before each House of Parliament within
  seven sitting days after the proclamation is issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations named

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A motion for confirmation of a proclamation
  continuing a declaration of emergency shall name the orders and
  regulations in force on the issuing of the proclamation that the
  Governor in Council believed, on reasonable grounds, continued at that
  time to be necessary or, in the case of a proclamation issued pursuant
  to subsection 43(1), advisable, for dealing with the emergency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a motion is laid before a House of Parliament
  as provided in subsection (1) or (2), that House shall, on the sitting
  day next following the sitting day on which the motion was so laid,
  take up and consider the motion.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vote

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A motion taken up and considered in accordance with
  subsection (4) shall be debated without interruption and, at such time
  as the House is ready for the question, the Speaker shall forthwith,
  without further debate or amendment, put every question necessary for
  the disposition of the motion.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of proclamation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a motion for confirmation of a proclamation is
  negatived by either House of Parliament, the proclamation, to the
  extent that it has not previously expired or been revoked, is revoked
  effective on the day of the negative vote and no further action under
  this section need be taken in the other House with respect to the
  motion.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of orders or regulations

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If a motion for confirmation of a proclamation
  continuing a declaration of emergency is amended by either House of
  Parliament by the deletion therefrom of an order or regulation named
  in the motion pursuant to subsection (3), the order or regulation is
  revoked effective on the day on which the motion, as amended, is
  adopted.

### [Orders and Regulations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-214276 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling in Parliament

- **[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), every
  order or regulation made by the Governor in Council pursuant to this
  Act shall be laid before each House of Parliament within two sitting
  days after it is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to Committee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an order or regulation made pursuant to this
  Act is exempted from publication in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) by regulations made under the
  [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22), the order or regulation,
  in lieu of being laid before each House of Parliament as required by
  subsection (1), shall be referred to the Parliamentary Review
  Committee within two days after it is made or, if the Committee is not
  then designated or established, within the first two days after it is
  designated or established.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motion for revocation or amendment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a motion, for the consideration of the Senate
  or the House of Commons, to the effect that an order or regulation
  laid before it pursuant to subsection (1) be revoked or amended,
  signed by not less than ten members of the Senate or twenty members of
  the House of Commons, as the case may be, is filed with the Speaker
  thereof, that House of Parliament shall take up and consider the
  motion within three sitting days after it is filed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vote

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A motion taken up and considered in accordance with
  subsection (3) shall be debated without interruption and, at such time
  as the House is ready for the question, the Speaker shall forthwith,
  without further debate or amendment, put every question necessary for
  the disposition of the motion.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motion for concurrence

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a motion debated in accordance with subsection (4)
  is adopted by the House, a message shall forthwith be sent from that
  House informing the other House that the motion has been so adopted
  and requesting that the motion be concurred in by that other House.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where a request for concurrence in a motion is made
  pursuant to subsection (5), the House to which the request is made
  shall take up and consider the motion within three sitting days after
  the request is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vote on motion for concurrence

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A motion taken up and considered in accordance with
  subsection (6) shall be debated without interruption and, at such time
  as the House is ready for the question, the Speaker shall forthwith,
  without further debate or amendment, put every question necessary for
  the disposition of the motion.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation or amendment of order or
  regulation

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If a motion taken up and considered in accordance
  with subsection (6) is concurred in, the order or regulation is
  revoked or amended in accordance with the motion, effective on the day
  specified in the motion, which day may not be earlier than the day of
  the vote of concurrence.

### [Parliamentary Review Committee]{.HTitleText2} {#h-214294 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review by Parliamentary Review Committee

- **[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  exercise of powers and the performance of duties and functions
  pursuant to a declaration of emergency shall be reviewed by a
  committee of both Houses of Parliament designated or established for
  that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Membership

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Parliamentary Review Committee shall include at
  least one member of the House of Commons from each party that has a
  recognized membership of 12 or more persons in that House and at least
  the Leader of the Government in the Senate or Government
  Representative in the Senate, or his or her nominee, the Leader of the
  Opposition in the Senate, or his or her nominee, and the Leader or
  Facilitator who is referred to in any of paragraphs 62.4(1)(c) to (e)
  of the [Parliament of Canada Act](/eng/acts/P-1), or his or her
  nominee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oath of secrecy

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every member of the Parliamentary Review Committee
  and every person employed in the work of the Committee shall take the
  oath of secrecy set out in the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings in private

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every meeting of the Parliamentary Review Committee
  held to consider an order or regulation referred to it pursuant to
  subsection 61(2) shall be held in private.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation or amendment of order or
  regulation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If, within thirty days after an order or regulation
  is referred to the Parliamentary Review Committee pursuant to
  subsection 61(2), the Committee adopts a motion to the effect that the
  order or regulation be revoked or amended, the order or regulation is
  revoked or amended in accordance with the motion, effective on the day
  specified in the motion, which day may not be earlier than the day on
  which the motion is adopted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Parliament

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Parliamentary Review Committee shall report or
  cause to be reported the results of its review under subsection (1) to
  each House of Parliament at least once every sixty days while the
  declaration of emergency is in effect and, in any case,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within three sitting days after a motion for
    revocation of the declaration is filed under subsection 59(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within seven sitting days after a proclamation
    continuing the declaration is issued; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} within seven sitting days after the expiration of
    the declaration or the revocation of the declaration by the Governor
    in Council.

### [Inquiry]{.HTitleText2} {#h-214311 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inquiry

- **[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall, within sixty days after the expiration or
  revocation of a declaration of emergency, cause an inquiry to be held
  into the circumstances that led to the declaration being issued and
  the measures taken for dealing with the emergency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A report of an inquiry held pursuant to this section
  shall be laid before each House of Parliament within three hundred and
  sixty days after the expiration or revocation of the declaration of
  emergency.
