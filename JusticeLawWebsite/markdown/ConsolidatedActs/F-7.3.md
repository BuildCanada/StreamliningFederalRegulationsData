## Federal Framework for Suicide Prevention Act {#federal-framework-for-suicide-prevention-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2012, c. 30
:::

Assented to 2012-12-14

An Act respecting a Federal Framework for Suicide Prevention
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas suicide is a complex problem involving biological,
psychological, social and spiritual factors, and can be influenced by
societal attitudes and conditions;

Whereas Canadians want to reduce suicide and its impact in Canada, and
suicide prevention is everyone's responsibility;

Whereas suicide is preventable by knowledge, care and compassion;

Whereas concerted, collaborative action by committed communities,
governments, organizations and individuals across Canada will help
prevent deaths by suicide, and assist in educating and comforting those
who have been affected by suicidal behaviour;

Whereas suicide is a significant public health issue in Canada and the
grief and trauma associated with it produce long-term social costs and
devastating effects on surviving individuals and communities;

Whereas the Parliament of Canada affirmed its respect for life by
unanimously adopting Motion No. 388, in 2009, which called for
meaningful deterrents and punishment for those who encourage vulnerable
individuals to commit suicide;

And whereas a federal plan designed to disseminate information, promote
the use of research, share best practices and affect public attitudes
towards suicide and its prevention is in the interest of all Canadians;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-238675 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Federal Framework for Suicide Prevention Act](/eng/acts/F-7.3).

## [Federal Framework for Suicide Prevention]{.HTitleText1} {#h-238678 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Framework

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
must establish a federal framework for suicide prevention that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} recognizes that suicide, in addition to being a
  mental health issue, is a public health issue and that, as such, it is
  a health and safety priority; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designates the appropriate entity within the
  Government of Canada to assume responsibility for

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} providing guidelines to improve public awareness
    and knowledge about suicide,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} disseminating information about suicide, including
    information concerning its prevention,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} making publically available existing statistics
    about suicide and related risk factors,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} promoting collaboration and knowledge exchange
    across domains, sectors, regions and jurisdictions,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} defining best practices for the prevention of
    suicide, and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} promoting the use of research and evidence-based
    practices for the prevention of suicide.

## [Consultations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-238689 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultations

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Within 180 days after the
day on which this section comes into force, the Government of Canada
must enter into consultations with relevant non-governmental
organizations, relevant entities within the governments of the provinces
and territories and relevant federal departments, in order to share
information and align the elements of the framework described in section
2 with existing efforts that relate to suicide prevention.

## [Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-238692 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Within four years after the
coming into force of this Act and every two years thereafter, the entity
designated in accordance with paragraph 2(b) must report to Canadians on
its progress and activities related to the federal framework for suicide
prevention.
