## Canada Foundation for Sustainable Development Technology Act {#canada-foundation-for-sustainable-development-technology-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2001, c. 23
:::

Assented to 2001-06-14

An Act to establish a foundation to fund sustainable development
technology
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-136007 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada Foundation for Sustainable Development Technology
Act](/eng/acts/C-5.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-136010 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Act.

[[board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the board of directors of the
    Foundation. ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chairperson of the
    board appointed under paragraph
    9(2)(a). ([président]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the corporation described
    in the order made under subsection
    35(1). ([société]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is on the board
    and includes the Chairperson. ([administrateur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[eligible project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a project carried on,
    or to be carried on, primarily in Canada by an eligible recipient to
    develop and demonstrate new technologies to promote sustainable
    development, including technologies to address issues related to
    climate change and the quality of air, water and soil. ([travaux
    admissibles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible recipient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible recipient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is established in Canada and carries on or, in
      the opinion of the board, is capable of carrying on eligible
      projects;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} meets the criteria of eligibility established in
      any agreement entered into between Her Majesty in right of Canada
      and the Foundation for provision of funding by Her Majesty to the
      Foundation; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has legal capacity or is composed of
      organizations, each of which has legal capacity. ([bénéficiaire
      admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employee or agent of Her Majesty in right of a province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employee or agent of Her Majesty in right of a
    province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include an employee or agent
    of Her Majesty in right of a province whose duties and functions in
    that capacity are restricted to work in a university, college or
    other educational institution. ([fonctionnaire ou mandataire de Sa
    Majesté du chef d'une province]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Foundation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Foundation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Foundation for
    Sustainable Development Technology referred to in section
    3. ([Fondation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is a member of the
    Foundation. ([membre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada who is designated by the Governor in
    Council as the Minister for the purposes of this
    Act. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[not-for-profit organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[not-for-profit organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    corporation, society, association, university, research institute,
    organization or body no part of whose income is payable to or
    otherwise available for the personal benefit of any of its
    proprietors, members or shareholders. ([organisation sans but
    lucratif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[special resolution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[special resolution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a resolution of the
    members passed by a majority of not less than two thirds of the
    votes cast by the members who voted on the resolution at a meeting
    of members or signed by all the members entitled to vote on the
    resolution. ([résolution extraordinaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[sustainable development]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sustainable development]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means development
    that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability
    of future generations to meet their own needs. ([développement
    durable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 23, ss. 2, 36
- 2004, c. 22, s. 12

## [Continuation of the Corporation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-136031 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  corporation is continued as a corporation without share capital, to be
  called the Canada Foundation for Sustainable Development Technology,
  consisting of the Foundation's members and directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If under any Act of Parliament, any instrument made
  under an Act of Parliament or any contract, licence or other document,
  a power, duty or function is vested in or exercisable by the
  corporation, the power, duty or function is vested in or exercisable
  by the Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every reference to the corporation in any deed,
  contract, agreement or other document executed by the corporation
  shall, unless the context otherwise requires, be read as a reference
  to the Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and obligations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} All rights and property of the corporation and all
  obligations of the corporation are transferred to the Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement of legal proceedings

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal proceeding in respect
  of an obligation or liability incurred by the corporation may be
  brought against the Foundation in any court that would have had
  jurisdiction if the action, suit or other legal proceeding had been
  brought against the corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of legal proceedings

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal proceeding to which
  the corporation is party pending in any court immediately before the
  day on which this section comes into force may be continued by or
  against the Foundation in like manner and to the same extent as it
  could have been continued by or against the corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Every by-law of the corporation is, to the extent
  that it is not inconsistent with this Act, a by-law of the Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers and employees

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act affects the status of any person
  who, immediately before the day on which this section comes into
  force, was an officer or employee of the corporation except that, as
  of that day, the person is an officer or employee, as the case may be,
  of the Foundation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 23, ss. 3, 37
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foundation not agent of Her Majesty

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Foundation is not an
agent of Her Majesty.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects and purposes of Foundation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The objects and purposes of
the Foundation are to provide funding to eligible recipients for
eligible projects.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
carrying out its objects and purposes, the Foundation has the capacity
and, subject to this Act, the rights, powers and privileges of a natural
person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the
Foundation shall be in a place in Canada designated by the Governor in
Council.

## [Directors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-136093 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of board of directors of
corporation

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  board of directors of the corporation ceases to exist and there shall
  be a board of directors of the Foundation that shall supervise the
  management of the business and affairs of the Foundation and, subject
  to the by-laws of the Foundation, exercise all its powers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of directors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), the board consists of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Chairperson of the board appointed by the
    Governor in Council on the Minister's recommendation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} six persons appointed by the Governor in Council on
    the Minister's recommendation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} eight persons appointed by the members in
    accordance with subsection 13(5) and the by-laws of the Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility for directors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person is not eligible to be appointed as a
  director if the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a member of the Senate, the House of Commons or
    the legislature of a province;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is an employee or agent of Her Majesty in right of
    Canada or in right of a province;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not ordinarily reside in Canada; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is disqualified under subsection 105(1) of the
    [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial organization

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Chairperson is appointed before directors have
  been appointed under paragraph (2)(c), the Chairperson and any other
  directors that may have been appointed under paragraph (2)(b)
  constitute the board until directors are appointed under paragraph
  (2)(c) and may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} undertake the organization of the Foundation
    including the appointment of officers and employees;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make banking arrangements for the Foundation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} enact organizational by-laws for the Foundation;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} receive on behalf of the Foundation any moneys paid
    to the Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Until directors are appointed under paragraph (2)(c),
  the Foundation shall not provide any funding from the funds of the
  Foundation or enter into any agreements or arrangements, or review any
  applications, for or in respect of funding to be provided from the
  funds of the Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foundation not owned by Crown

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The operation of the Foundation under subsection (4)
  by the Chairperson and any directors appointed under paragraph (2)(b)
  shall not, despite subsection 83(2) of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11), result in the Foundation being considered, for
  the purposes of Part X of that Act or for any other purpose, to be
  wholly owned directly by Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms of office of directors

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  the Chairperson and the directors shall be appointed to hold office
  during good behaviour for terms not exceeding five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal from office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson and any director appointed under
  paragraph 9(2)(b) may be removed for cause by the Governor in Council.
  Any director appointed under paragraph 9(2)(c) may be removed for
  cause by a special resolution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation in office

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Except where they cease to be directors under
  subsection (6), directors shall continue to hold office until their
  successors are appointed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional terms of office

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A director is eligible to be reappointed for one or
  more terms not exceeding five years each.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of replacements

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person appointed to fill a vacancy in the office of
  a director who has ceased to hold office before the expiry of the
  director's term of office shall be appointed to hold office for the
  unexpired portion of that term.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ceasing to be director

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A director ceases to be a director when the director

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} resigns;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is appointed to the Senate;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is elected to the House of Commons or to the
    legislature of a province;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} becomes an employee or agent of Her Majesty in
    right of Canada or in right of a province;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} ceases to be ordinarily resident in Canada;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} becomes disqualified under subsection 105(1) of the
    [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44); or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} is removed from office under subsection (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Director representation and experience

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The appointment of
directors shall be made having regard to the following considerations:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the need to ensure, as far as possible, that at all
  times the board will be representative of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} persons engaged in the development and
    demonstration of technologies to promote sustainable development,
    including technologies to address issues related to climate change
    and the quality of air, water and soil,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the business community, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} not-for-profit organizations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the importance of having a board that is
  representative of various regions of Canada and includes men and women
  who are able to contribute to the achievement of the objects and
  purposes of the Foundation; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the need for a board that has sufficient knowledge of
  technologies that promote sustainable development.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and expenses for directors

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} From
  the funds of the Foundation, the directors

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} may be paid remuneration that is fixed by the
    Foundation's by-laws; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are entitled to be paid reasonable travel and
    living expenses incurred by them in the performance of their duties

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors not to profit

## [Staff]{.HTitleText1} {#h-136222 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  board may appoint any officers, employees and agents of the Foundation
  that it considers necessary to carry out the objects and purposes of
  the Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of offices

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the by-laws of the Foundation, the board
  may designate the offices of the Foundation and specify the duties and
  functions of each office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors and members not employees or
  agents

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Directors and members are not eligible to be
  employees or agents of the Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not part of public service of Canada

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The directors, members, officers, employees and
  agents of the Foundation are not, because of being directors, members,
  officers, employees or agents of the Foundation, part of the federal
  public administration.

- 2003, c. 22, s. 224(E)

## [Operations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-136234 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrative expenses

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** From its funds, the
Foundation may pay salaries and wages of its officers and employees,
rent for its accommodation, remuneration for its directors and agents,
reimbursement to the directors and members for reasonable travel and
living expenses incurred by them in the performance of their duties
under this Act while absent from their ordinary place of residence, and
other costs and expenses of carrying on the business of the Foundation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Funding for eligible projects

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} From
  its funds, the Foundation may provide funding to eligible recipients
  to be used by them solely for the purposes of eligible projects in
  accordance with any terms and conditions specified by the Foundation
  in respect of the funding, including terms and conditions as to
  repayment of the funding, intellectual property rights and the maximum
  amount and proportion of funding for eligible projects to be provided
  by the Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement with eligible recipient

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Foundation shall enter into an agreement with an
  eligible recipient respecting, among other things,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which the Foundation will make
    advances in respect of funding to the eligible recipient and when
    those advances will be made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any terms or conditions on which the funding will
    be provided, including those terms and conditions referred to in
    subsection (1);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the evaluation of the eligible recipient's
    performance in achieving the objectives of the eligible project and
    the evaluation of the results of the project, including the
    potential performance of the technology that is developed and
    demonstrated by the project; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the eligible recipient is composed of
    organizations, each of which has legal capacity, the requirement for
    those organizations to be jointly and severally or solidarily liable
    for the obligations of that eligible recipient.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foundation not to acquire an interest

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In providing funding to an eligible recipient in
  respect of an eligible project, the Foundation shall not acquire any
  interest, whether through the acquisition of share capital, a
  partnership interest or otherwise, in any research infrastructure
  acquired by the eligible recipient for the project.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Donations to Foundation

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the
  Foundation may accept conditional or unconditional donations of money.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of donations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All money donated to the Foundation, and any income
  arising from the investment of that money, shall be used by the
  Foundation in carrying out its objects and purposes in accordance with
  the terms and conditions of any agreement for provision of funding
  entered into between a donor and the Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditional donations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Foundation shall not accept a donation of money
  that is made on the condition that the Foundation use the money or any
  income arising from the investment of the money for any purpose that
  is not within the objects and purposes of the Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (3) does not apply if the conditions of a
  donation of money merely restrict or direct the manner of investing
  the money until it can be used to provide funding to eligible
  recipients for eligible projects.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investment policies

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The board shall establish
investment policies, standards and procedures that a reasonably prudent
person would apply in respect of a portfolio of investments to avoid
undue risk of loss and obtain a reasonable return, having regard to the
Foundation's obligations and anticipated obligations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investments

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any conditions of a
  donation restricting the investment of money donated until it can be
  used to provide funding to eligible recipients for eligible projects,
  the Foundation shall invest its funds, and reinvest any income from
  those funds, in accordance with the investment policies, standards and
  procedures established by the board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation of other corporations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Foundation shall not cause any corporation to be
  incorporated or participate in the incorporation of a corporation or
  become a partner in a partnership.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control of corporation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Except for the investment of its funds, the
  Foundation shall not carry on any business for gain or profit and
  shall not hold or acquire any interest in any corporation or
  enterprise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Borrowing prohibited

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Foundation shall not borrow money, issue any debt obligations or
  securities, give any guarantees to secure a debt or other obligation
  of another person or mortgage, pledge or otherwise encumber property
  of the Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Real property or immovables

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Foundation shall not purchase or accept a
  donation of real property or immovables.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation by board

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the board
  may delegate to the Chairperson, a committee of directors or an
  officer of the Foundation any of the powers or rights of the board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrictions on delegation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The board shall not delegate any power or right of
  the board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to enact, amend or repeal by-laws;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to authorize the provision of funding to eligible
    recipients for eligible projects;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to appoint directors to, or fill vacancies on, a
    committee of the board;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to appoint officers of the Foundation or fix their
    remuneration;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} to accept donations;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} to approve the annual financial statements or
    reports of the Foundation; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} to submit to the members any matter requiring the
    approval of the members.

## [Financial Matters and Audit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-136283 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operating and capital budgets

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  board shall cause an operating budget and a capital budget to be
  prepared for each fiscal year of the Foundation and shall submit those
  budgets to the members for consideration at the annual meeting of
  members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Books of account

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The board shall cause books of account and other
  records to be kept and shall establish financial and management
  controls, information systems and management practices that will
  ensure that the business and affairs of the Foundation are carried on,
  and the financial, human and physical resources of the Foundation are
  managed effectively, efficiently and economically.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information systems

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The books of account and other records of the
  Foundation shall be maintained in a way that will ensure that the
  assets of the Foundation are properly protected and controlled and
  that its business and affairs are carried on in compliance with this
  Act and, in particular, in such a way that they will show

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} descriptions and book values of all investments of
    the Foundation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the eligible recipients who have received, or are
    about to receive, funding from the Foundation in respect of eligible
    projects, the nature and extent of the projects and the amount of
    the funding.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Auditor

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  first meeting of the members, and in any subsequent fiscal year at the
  annual meeting, the members shall appoint an auditor for the
  Foundation for the fiscal year and fix, or authorize the board to fix,
  the auditor's remuneration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications of auditor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The auditor shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is a member in good standing of an institute or
      association of accountants incorporated by or under an Act of the
      legislature of a province,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has at least five years experience at a senior
      level in carrying out audits,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is ordinarily resident in Canada, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} is independent of the board, the directors, the
      members and the officers of the Foundation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a firm of accountants of which the member or
    employee jointly designated by the board and the firm to conduct the
    audit of the books and records of the Foundation on behalf of the
    firm meets the qualifications set out in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of auditor

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an auditor is not appointed at the annual general
  meeting in any fiscal year, the auditor for the preceding fiscal year
  continues in office until a successor is appointed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal of auditor

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The members may by a special resolution remove an
  auditor from office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ceasing to hold office

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An auditor ceases to hold office when the auditor

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} resigns;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is removed from office under subsection (4); or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} no longer meets the requirements under subsection
    (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacement

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The members may, at a meeting of the members, appoint
  an auditor to fill any vacancy in the office of the auditor but, if
  the members fail to fill the vacancy at that meeting or if no meeting
  of the members is convened without delay after the vacancy occurs, the
  board may appoint an auditor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unexpired term

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} An auditor appointed to fill a vacancy in the office
  holds office for the unexpired term of the predecessor in the office.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Auditor's report

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** The auditor shall, within
four months after the end of each fiscal year, complete the audit of the
books and records of the Foundation for the fiscal year and submit a
report of the audit to the members.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audit committee

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  board shall appoint an audit committee consisting of not fewer than
  three directors and fix the duties and functions of the committee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Internal audit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to any other duties and functions that it
  is required to perform, the audit committee shall cause internal
  audits to be conducted to ensure compliance by the officers and
  employees of the Foundation with management and information systems
  and controls established by the board.

## [Annual Meeting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-136325 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual meeting

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The board shall call an
annual meeting of members not later than six months after the end of
each fiscal year of the Foundation for the purpose of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} examining the audited financial statements and the
  report of the auditor on those statements for the preceding fiscal
  year;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} examining the annual report of the Foundation for the
  preceding fiscal year;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} examining the operating budget and the capital budget
  submitted by the board under subsection 25(1);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} considering and confirming, rejecting or amending
  by-laws made by the board or amendments to or the repeal of by-laws
  made by the board;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} appointing an auditor under subsection 26(1); and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} considering any other matter respecting the
  operations of the Foundation.

## [Annual Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-136334 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Foundation shall, within five months after the end of each fiscal
  year, prepare an annual report in both official languages of its
  activities during the preceding fiscal year and include in the report

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its financial statements for the year as approved
    by the board and the report of the auditor respecting those
    statements;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a detailed statement of its investment activities
    during the year, its investment portfolio as at the end of the year
    and its investment policies, standards and procedures;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a detailed statement of its funding activities;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement of its plans for fulfilling its objects
    and purposes for the next year; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an evaluation of the overall results achieved by
    the funding of eligible projects by the Foundation during the year
    in review, and since the inception of the Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration of report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before the annual report of the Foundation for a
  fiscal year is distributed to the public it shall be approved by the
  board and examined by the members at a meeting of the members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution of report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} After the annual report of the Foundation for a
  fiscal year is approved as required under subsection (2), the report
  shall be made public in accordance with the by-laws of the Foundation
  and a copy shall be sent to the Minister who shall cause a copy of the
  report to be laid before each House of Parliament on any of the first
  15 days on which that House is sitting after the Minister receives it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public meeting

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  it publishes its annual report for a fiscal year, the Foundation shall
  convene a public meeting at a city in Canada selected by the board to
  consider the report and other matters relating to the activities of
  the Foundation during the year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of meeting

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At least 30 days before the date of a meeting
  convened under subsection (1) to consider the Foundation's annual
  report for a fiscal year, the Foundation shall give notice of the time
  and place of the meeting in accordance with the by-laws of the
  Foundation.

## [Winding-up]{.HTitleText1} {#h-136352 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property to be divided

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Foundation is wound up or dissolved, its property remaining after its
  debts and obligations have been satisfied shall be liquidated and the
  moneys arising from the liquidation shall be distributed among all the
  eligible recipients that have received funding from the Foundation and
  that are, as of the day the distribution begins, still carrying on
  projects to develop and demonstrate new technologies to promote
  sustainable development, to be used by them for the purpose of those
  projects. Each of those eligible recipients shall receive an amount
  that is the same proportion of the moneys arising from the liquidation
  as the total funding received by that eligible recipient from the
  Foundation is of the total of all funding that has been provided by
  the Foundation to all of those eligible recipients.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repayment out of remaining property

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Minister may require the
  Foundation to repay out of the moneys arising from the liquidation to
  the Receiver General for credit to the Consolidated Revenue Fund any
  amount that is so repayable under the terms or conditions on which
  public moneys were provided to the Foundation.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-136360 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Official Languages
Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01) applies

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** The [Official Languages
Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01) applies to the Foundation as if it were a federal
institution.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory by-laws

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The Foundation shall
include in its by-laws provisions

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} entitling an eligible recipient that has made an
  application for funding from the Foundation to request the board to
  make a ruling as to the possible conflict of interest of a director in
  the consideration or disposal of the application;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} establishing procedures to be followed by the board
  in responding to the request and giving the ruling;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} determining the fiscal year of the Foundation;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} requiring the creation of advisory committees,
  including technical advisory committees, and their mandates; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fixing the remuneration for directors.
