## National Public Service Week: Serving Canadians Better Act {#national-public-service-week-serving-canadians-better-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1992, c. 15
:::

Assented to 1992-06-04

An Act respecting National Public Service Week: Serving Canadians Better
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
WHEREAS the people of Canada recognize the value of the services
rendered by federal public service employees;

AND WHEREAS the Government of Canada wishes to acknowledge the
contribution of federal public service employees to the federal
administration;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363376 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [National Public Service Week: Serving Canadians Better
Act](/eng/acts/N-14.4).

## [National Public Service Week]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363379 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National Public Service Week

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Throughout Canada, in each and every
  year, the third week of the month of June shall be known as the
  \"National Public Service Week\".

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of \"third week\"

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, [[third
  week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period ending the third Saturday
  of the month and beginning the previous Sunday.
