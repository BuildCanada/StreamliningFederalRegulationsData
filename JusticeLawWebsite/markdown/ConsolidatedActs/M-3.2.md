## Meat Inspection Act {#meat-inspection-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. 25 (1st
Supp.)
:::

[\[Repealed, 2012, c. 24, s. 77\] - 2019-01-15]{.Repealed}

An Act respecting the import and export of and interprovincial trade in
meat products, the registration of establishments, the inspection of
animals and meat products in registered establishments and the standards
for those establishments and for animals slaughtered and meat products
prepared in those establishments

::: ReaderNote
\[1985, c. 17, assented to 16th May, 1985\]
