### [Transitional]{.HTitleText2} {#h-252852 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything in this Act
  but subject to subsection (3), no person who, on the day on which
  subsection 11(1) or (2) of the [Hazardous Materials Information Review
  Act](/eng/acts/H-2.7), as enacted by this Act, comes into force, would
  but for this section be entitled to file a claim for exemption under
  that subsection in relation to a controlled product is entitled to
  file a claim for exemption in relation to that controlled product
  under that subsection unless, during the period beginning on the first
  day of the second month after the coming into force of this section
  and ending on the last day of the sixth month after the coming into
  force of this section, that person has, in accordance with this
  section, filed a notice of intent to file a claim for exemption in
  relation to that controlled product with the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of notice and
  accompanying fee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of intent referred to in subsection (1)
  shall be in such form and contain such information as the Minister
  may, by order, determine and shall be accompanied by a fee of five
  hundred dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by order, on the application of any
  person and on payment of a fee of five hundred dollars, exempt a
  controlled product specified in the order from the application of
  subsection (1) where the Minister is satisfied that reasonable grounds
  exist to allow the exemption.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee still payable

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The payment of a fee under subsection (2) or (3) does
  not relieve a person of the obligation to pay any fee required to be
  paid on the filing of a claim for exemption under section 11 of the
  [Hazardous Materials Information Review Act](/eng/acts/H-2.7), as
  enacted by this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In this section,

  [[controlled product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[controlled product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in the [Hazardous Products Act](/eng/acts/H-3), as amended by this
      Act; ([produit contrôlé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Consumer
      and Corporate Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Review by Parliament]{.HTitleText2} {#h-252871 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review by Parliament

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** On the expiration of two
years after the coming into force of section 12 of the [Hazardous
Products Act](/eng/acts/H-3), as enacted by this Act, that section shall
stand referred to such committee of the House of Commons, of the Senate
or of both Houses of Parliament as may be designated or established for
that purpose and the committee shall, as soon as practicable thereafter,
undertake a comprehensive review of the exemptions provided by that
section and shall, within one year after the review is undertaken or
within such further time as the House of Commons may authorize, submit a
report thereon to Parliament including any recommendations pertaining to
the continuation of any of those exemptions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

force on October 31, 1988 or on such earlier day or days as may be fixed
