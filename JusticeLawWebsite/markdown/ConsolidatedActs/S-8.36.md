## Self-Government Treaty Recognizing the Whitecap Dakota Nation / Wapaha Ska Dakota Oyate Act {#self-government-treaty-recognizing-the-whitecap-dakota-nation-wapaha-ska-dakota-oyate-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2023, c. 22
:::

Assented to 2023-06-22

An Act to give effect to the self-government treaty recognizing the
Whitecap Dakota Nation / Wapaha Ska Dakota Oyate and to make
consequential amendments to other Acts
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the Whitecap Dakota Nation and the Government of Canada
recognize distinctive historical relationships between certain Dakota
communities and the Crown based on, at various times, treaties or
alliances of peace and friendship;

Whereas the Whitecap Dakota Nation is an Indigenous people of Canada;

Whereas the Government of Canada recognizes that the inherent right of
self-government is a right of the Indigenous peoples of Canada
recognized and affirmed by section 35 of the [Constitution Act,
1982](/eng/Const/) and that the Whitecap Dakota Nation has that inherent
right;

Whereas the Whitecap Dakota Nation and the Government of Canada are
committed to achieving reconciliation through a renewed nation-to-nation
relationship based on respect, cooperation, partnership and the
recognition of the Whitecap Dakota Nation's rights;

Whereas the Whitecap Dakota Nation aspires to participate fully in the
economic, social, political and cultural aspects of Canadian society in
a way that preserves and enhances the collective identity of Whitecap
Dakota members as a self-sufficient and sustainable community now and
into the future;

Whereas the Whitecap Dakota First Nation members have approved the
treaty entitled "A Self-Government Treaty Recognizing the Whitecap
Dakota Nation / Wapaha Ska Dakota Oyate";

Whereas the Treaty was signed on May 2, 2023 on behalf of the Whitecap
Dakota First Nation and His Majesty in right of Canada;

Whereas the Government of Canada is committed to implementing the United
Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples and the Whitecap
Dakota Nation and the Government of Canada view the Treaty as
contributing to the implementation of that Declaration;

And whereas the Treaty requires the coming into force of an Act of
Parliament that gives effect to it;
:::

::: Enacts
Now therefore, His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows: 
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1412108 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Self-Government Treaty Recognizing the Whitecap Dakota Nation /
Wapaha Ska Dakota Oyate Act](/eng/acts/S-8.36).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1412111 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in 1.01
    of the Treaty. ([règle de droit fédérale]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Indigenous peoples of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indigenous peoples of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
    assigned by the definition [[aboriginal peoples of
    Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 35(2) of the
    [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/). ([peuples autochtones du
    Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Tax Treatment Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Tax Treatment Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the tax
    treatment agreement, as amended from time to time in accordance with
    its provisions, that is referred to in 11.16 of the Treaty. ([accord
    sur le traitement fiscal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Treaty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Treaty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the treaty entitled "A
    Self-Government Treaty Recognizing the Whitecap Dakota Nation /
    Wapaha Ska Dakota Oyate," signed on behalf of the Whitecap Dakota
    First Nation and His Majesty in right of Canada on May 2, 2023, as
    amended from time to time in accordance with its
    provisions. ([traité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Whitecap Dakota Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Whitecap Dakota Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
    meaning as in 1.01 of the Treaty. ([gouvernement des Dakotas de
    Whitecap]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Whitecap Dakota institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Whitecap Dakota institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
    assigned by the definition [[WDN Institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
    in 1.01 of the Treaty. ([institution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Whitecap Dakota law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Whitecap Dakota law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned
    by the definition [[WDN Law]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in 1.01 of the
    Treaty. ([loi de la nation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Whitecap Dakota member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Whitecap Dakota member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
    assigned by the definition [[WDN Member]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
    1.01 of the Treaty. ([membre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Whitecap Dakota Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Whitecap Dakota Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
    assigned by the definition [[WDN]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in 1.01 of
    the Treaty. ([Nation dakota de Whitecap]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Whitecap Dakota reserve lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Whitecap Dakota reserve lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the
    meaning assigned by the definition [[WDN Reserve
    Lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in 1.01 of the Treaty. ([terres de
    réserve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of Treaty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, the
Treaty is a treaty within the meaning of sections 25 and 35 of the
[Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

## [Treaty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1412126 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Treaty given effect

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Treaty is approved, given effect and declared valid and has the force
  of law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and obligations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a person or entity has the
  powers, rights, privileges and benefits conferred on the person or
  entity by the Treaty and must perform the duties, and is subject to
  the liabilities, imposed on the person or entity by the Treaty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Third parties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Treaty is binding on, and
  may be relied on by, all persons and entities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Treaty prevails

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  event of any conflict between the Treaty and this Act or any other
  federal law, the Treaty prevails to the extent of the conflict.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act prevails

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of any conflict between this Act and any
  other federal law, this Act prevails to the extent of the conflict.

## [Whitecap Dakota Nation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1412139 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity, rights, powers and privileges

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Whitecap Dakota Nation is a legal entity and has, subject to the
  Treaty, the capacity, rights, powers and privileges of a natural
  person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vesting of rights, interests, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the coming into effect of the Treaty, all rights,
  interests, assets and obligations of the Whitecap Dakota First Nation
  as a band, within the meaning of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), vest
  in the Whitecap Dakota Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Whitecap Dakota Government

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Whitecap Dakota Nation exercises the power and
  authority to make laws, carries out other government functions and
  otherwise exercises power or authority through the Whitecap Dakota
  Government.

## [Whitecap Dakota Laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1412147 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Third parties

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the Treaty,
Whitecap Dakota laws are binding on and may be relied on by all persons
and entities, the Whitecap Dakota Nation, the Whitecap Dakota Government
and Whitecap Dakota institutions.

## [Taxation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1412150 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tax Treatment Agreement

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** On the day on which the Tax
Treatment Agreement comes into effect by application of 11.16 of the
Treaty, that Agreement is approved, given effect and declared valid.
That Agreement has the force of law during the period in which it is in
effect.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Tax Treatment Agreement
does not form part of the Treaty and it is not a treaty or a land claims
agreement within the meaning of sections 25 and 35 of the [Constitution
Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

## [Application of Other Acts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1412155 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the Treaty,
the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) does not apply to the Whitecap Dakota
Nation, the Whitecap Dakota Government, Whitecap Dakota members or
Whitecap Dakota reserve lands.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The [Statutory
Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does not apply to Whitecap Dakota laws.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1412160 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice --- Treaty and Tax
Treatment Agreement

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice must be taken of the
  Treaty and the Tax Treatment Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Treaty and the Tax Treatment Agreement must be
  published by the King's Printer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the Treaty or the Tax Treatment Agreement
  published by the King's Printer is evidence of that Treaty or that
  Agreement, and of its contents, and a copy purporting to be published
  by the King's Printer is presumed to be so published unless the
  contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice --- Whitecap Dakota law

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice must be taken of any
  Whitecap Dakota law that is registered in the public registry referred
  to in 5.12 of the Treaty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of any Whitecap Dakota law purporting to be
  registered in the public registry referred to in 5.12 of the Treaty is
  evidence of that law and of its contents, unless the contrary is
  shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7)

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  decision-making body established under a Whitecap Dakota law,
  including a Whitecap Dakota institution, is not a [[federal board,
  commission or other tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saskatchewan Court of King's Bench

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Saskatchewan Court of King's Bench has
  jurisdiction to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make orders and issue injunctions and declaratory
    relief against a decision-making body referred to in subsection (1);
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} undertake judicial review of the decisions of that
    decision-making body, provided that all review mechanisms set out in
    Whitecap Dakota laws have been exhausted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  issue arises in any judicial or administrative proceeding in respect
  of the interpretation, validity or applicability of the Treaty, of
  this Act or of any Whitecap Dakota law, then the issue must not be
  decided until the party raising the issue has served notice

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the Attorney General of Canada and the Whitecap
    Dakota Government, in the case of a provision of the Treaty or this
    Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the Whitecap Dakota Government, in the case of a
    provision of a Whitecap Dakota law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content and timing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must be served at least 14 days before the
  day on which the issue is to be argued, unless the court or tribunal
  authorizes a shorter period, and must identify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the subject matter of the issue;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the particulars that are necessary to show the
    point to be argued; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the issue is to be argued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The recipient of a notice under subsection (1) may
  appear and participate in the proceeding as a party with the same
  rights as any other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsections (2) and (3) do not
  require that an oral hearing be held if one is not otherwise required.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retroactive effect

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsection 4(1),
Chapter 33 and Schedule B of the Treaty are deemed to have effect as of
August 22, 2022.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make any orders and regulations that the
  Governor in Council considers necessary for the purpose of carrying
  out any of the provisions of the Treaty, of the Tax Treatment
  Agreement or of any other agreements that are related to the
  implementation of the Treaty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[First Nations Fiscal Management
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11.67)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of enabling the Whitecap Dakota
  Nation to benefit from the provisions of the [First Nations Fiscal
  Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.67) or obtain the services of any body
  established under that Act, the Governor in Council may make any
  regulations that the Governor in Council considers necessary,
  including regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} adapting any provision of that Act or of any
    regulation made under that Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} restricting the application of any provision of
    that Act or of any regulation made under that Act.
