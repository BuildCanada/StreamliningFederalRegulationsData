## Agricultural and Rural Development Act (ARDA) {#agricultural-and-rural-development-act-arda .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. A-3
:::

An Act to provide for the rehabilitation and development of rural areas
in Canada
::::

:::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9590 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the Agricultural and Rural Development Act (*ARDA*).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. A-4, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9595 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of \"Minister\"

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's Privy
Council for Canada as is designated by the Governor in Council as the
Minister for the purposes of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. A-4, s. 2
:::

## [Federal-Provincial Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9600 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint projects

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, with the
approval of the Governor in Council, enter into an agreement with any
province providing for the undertaking jointly with the government of
the province, or any agency of such a government, of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} projects for the more efficient use and economic
  development of rural lands specified in the agreement;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} projects for

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the development and conservation of water supplies
    for agricultural or other rural purposes, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} soil improvement and the conservation of rural
    lands in the province or in any area of the province specified in
    the agreement; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} projects for the development of income and employment
  opportunities in rural areas specified in the agreement and the
  improvement of standards of living in those areas.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. A-4, ss. 3, 4, 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial projects

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, with the
approval of the Governor in Council, enter into an agreement with any
province providing for the payment to the province of contributions in
respect of the cost of any projects referred to in section 3 undertaken
by the government of the province or any agency of that government.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. A-4, ss. 3, 4, 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions to be included

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every agreement entered
into pursuant to this Act shall specify

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the authority that is to be responsible for the
  undertaking, operation and maintenance of any project or any part
  thereof to which the agreement relates;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the respective proportions of the cost of any project
  to which the agreement relates that are to be paid by the Minister and
  the province, or the contribution in respect of any such project that
  is to be paid by the Minister, and the times at which the amounts to
  be paid by the Minister or the province are to be paid;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the respective proportions of the revenues from any
  project to which the agreement relates that are to be paid to the
  Minister and the province;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the terms and conditions respecting the undertaking,
  operation and maintenance of any project to which the agreement
  relates; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the charges, if any, that are to be charged to
  persons to whom any of the benefits of the project to which the
  agreement relates are made available.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. A-4, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity of agreements

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No agreement entered into
pursuant to this Act and providing for the payment of any money by the
Minister shall have any force or effect until such time as money has
been appropriated by Parliament for the purpose of discharging any
commitment under that agreement.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. A-4, s. 8
:::

## [Research]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9627 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rural land use and water supplies

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may cause to be prepared and undertaken, directly or in
  cooperation with the government of any province or any agency of such
  a government, programs of research and investigation for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the more effective use and economic development of
    rural lands in the province; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the development and conservation of water supplies
    and for soil improvement and conservation in the province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rural income and employment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of assisting the development of
  income and employment opportunities in rural areas in Canada and the
  improvement of standards of living in those areas, the Minister may
  cause to be prepared and undertaken, with the government of any
  province or any agency of such a government or with any university,
  educational institution or person, programs of research and
  investigation, and may coordinate those programs with other similar
  programs being undertaken in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. A-4, ss. 3, 4, 5
:::

## [Services of Other Departments and Agencies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9637 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Income and employment projects and
research

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall, in
carrying out any project referred to in paragraph 3(c) or research
program referred to in subsection 7(2), make use, wherever possible, of
the services and facilities of other departments and agencies of the
Government of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. A-4, s. 4
:::

## [Advisory Committees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9642 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of committees

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, in order to carry out the purposes and provisions of
  this Act, establish such advisory committees as the Minister deems
  necessary and appoint the members of those committees.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and expenses of members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each member of a committee established under
  subsection (1) shall be paid such amount for each day the member
  attends any meeting of the committee as may be fixed by the Governor
  in Council and reasonable travel and living expenses while absent from
  his ordinary place of residence in the course of his duties.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. A-4, s. 7
:::

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9650 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by regulation, make provision for any matters concerning which he
deems regulations necessary or desirable to carry out the purposes and
provisions of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. A-4, s. 9
:::

## [Annual Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9655 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Parliament

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall, as
soon as possible after the termination of each fiscal year, submit a
report to Parliament respecting the operations for that year of the
agreements made under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. A-4, s. 10
:::
