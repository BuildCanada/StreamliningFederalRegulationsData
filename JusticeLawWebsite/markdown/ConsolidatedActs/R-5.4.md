## Remote Sensing Space Systems Act {#remote-sensing-space-systems-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2005, c. 45
:::

Assented to 2005-11-25

An Act governing the operation of remote sensing space systems
::::

:::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-426985 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Remote Sensing Space Systems Act](/eng/acts/R-5.4).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-426988 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[controlled activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[controlled activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, subject to subsection
    8(6), means any of the following activities in the operation of a
    remote sensing space system:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} formulating or giving a command to a remote
      sensing satellite of the system;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} receiving raw data from a remote sensing
      satellite of the system;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} storing, processing or distributing raw data from
      the system;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} establishing or using

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} cryptography in communications with a remote
        sensing satellite of the system, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} information assurance measures for the
        system. ([activité contrôlée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[information assurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[information assurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the protection
    of information and information systems by ensuring their
    availability, integrity, authentication, confidentiality and
    non-repudiation. ([assurance de l'information]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence issued under
    subsection 8(1). ([licence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensed system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensed system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a remote sensing space
    system for whose operation a licence has been issued. ([système
    agréé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is the holder of
    a licence. ([titulaire de licence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, subject to section 3, means the
    Minister of Foreign Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a partnership, a government,
    a government agency and an unincorporated
    organization. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by the
    regulations. ([Version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[raw data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[raw data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means sensor data from a remote
    sensing satellite, and any auxiliary data required to produce remote
    sensing products from the sensor data, that have not been
    transformed into a remote sensing product. ([données
    brutes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[remote sensing product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[remote sensing product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an image or
    data produced from raw data in any way that transforms the raw
    data. ([produit dérivé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[remote sensing satellite]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[remote sensing satellite]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a satellite
    that is capable of sensing the surface of the Earth through the use
    of electromagnetic waves. ([satellite de
    télédétection]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[remote sensing space system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[remote sensing space system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one or more remote sensing satellites and the
      mission control centre and other facilities used to operate the
      satellites; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the facilities used to receive, store, process or
      distribute raw data from the satellites, even after the satellites
      themselves are no longer in operation. ([système de télédétection
      spatiale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[system participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[system participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is
    designated in a licence condition referred to in paragraph
    8(5)(b). ([participant autorisé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transform]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transform]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to raw data, means,
    having regard to the regulations, to so process them that it is
    substantially impossible to reconstitute them from the resulting
    remote sensing product. ([traitement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Designation of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427013 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of Governor in Council

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
designate a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada to be the
Minister for the purposes of this Act.

## [Application of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427016 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Crown

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This Act
  binds Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order modifying application of Act to
  certain systems

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make an order with
  respect to a remote sensing space system operated by the Department of
  National Defence or the Canadian Space Agency providing that this Act
  and the regulations apply to that system only in the manner and to the
  extent provided for in the order. The order may adapt any of the
  provisions of this Act or the regulations for the purposes of that
  application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption by order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by order, exempt any person or
  remote sensing space system --- or any class of person, system or data
  --- from the application of any provisions of this Act or the
  regulations. The exemption order may be limited in scope or made
  subject to conditions. It may be made only if the Minister is
  satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the exemption is neither injurious to national
    security, to the defence of Canada, to the safety of Canadian Forces
    or to Canada's conduct of international relations nor inconsistent
    with Canada's international obligations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} adequate provision will be made for the protection
    of the environment, public health and the safety of persons and
    property; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the interests of the provinces are protected.

## [Operation of Remote Sensing Space Systems]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427027 .Part}

### [Requirement for Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-427028 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}System operator requires licence

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall operate a
remote sensing space system in any manner, directly or indirectly,
except under the authority of a licence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activities outside Canada

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Section 5 applies also to
the following persons with respect to their activities outside Canada:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Canadian citizens;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} permanent residents within the meaning of subsection
  2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} corporations that are incorporated or continued under
  the laws of Canada or a province; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} members of any prescribed class of persons having a
  substantial connection to Canada related to remote sensing space
  systems.

### [Applications, Licences and Related Matters]{.HTitleText2} {#h-427037 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications regarding licences

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An application to the
Minister to issue, amend or renew a licence must be made in the
prescribed form and manner, be supported by a proposed system disposal
plan, proposed guarantee arrangements referred to in paragraph 9(1)(b)
and any other prescribed information, documents and undertakings and be
accompanied by any prescribed application fee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance, amendment or renewal of
licences

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  application, the Minister may, having regard to national security, the
  defence of Canada, the safety of Canadian Forces, Canada's conduct of
  international relations, Canada's international obligations and any
  prescribed factors,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} issue a provisional approval of a licence
    application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} issue a licence; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} amend or renew a licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisional approval binding

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A provisional approval is binding on the Minister for
  so long as the material facts on which it was based remain
  substantially unchanged.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons for refusal of application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, without delay, provide reasons to
  the applicant for any refusal of an application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of licences

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every licence is subject to the conditions referred
  to in subsections (5) to (7), any prescribed conditions and the
  following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the licensee keep control of the licensed
    system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the licensee not permit any other person to
    carry on a controlled activity in the operation of the system except
    in accordance with the licence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that raw data and remote sensing products from the
    system about the territory of any country --- but not including data
    or products that have been enhanced or to which some value has been
    added --- be made available to the government of that country within
    a reasonable time, on reasonable terms and for so long as the data
    or products have not been disposed of, but subject to any licence
    conditions under subsection (6) or (7) applicable to their
    communication or provision;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that the licensee keep control of raw data and
    remote sensing products from the system until they are disposed of
    in accordance with this Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} that raw data from the system be communicated only
    to a government referred to in paragraph (c), the licensee, a system
    participant or a person to whom they may be communicated under
    subsection (6);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} that the licensee encourage a recipient of raw data
    or a remote sensing product who has entered into an agreement
    referred to in paragraph (6)(b) or (7)(b) to comply with the
    agreement; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} that any prescribed fees be paid when due.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions specified by Minister

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In a licence, the Minister may specify any other
  condition that the Minister considers appropriate, including any
  condition

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} relating to the use of cryptography or information
    assurance measures; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} designating any person as a system participant and
    authorizing the licensee to permit that person to carry on any
    controlled activity in the operation of the licensed system that the
    Minister specifies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions specified by Minister ---
  raw data

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} In a licence, the Minister may authorize the
  communication of raw data or classes of raw data from the licensed
  system to any persons or classes of persons other than the licensee or
  system participants on any conditions that the Minister considers
  appropriate. The conditions may include requirements that, in
  specified cases or circumstances, the communication of the raw data

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be subject to the Minister's prior approval; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be done only under a legally enforceable agreement,
    entered into in good faith, that includes measures respecting their
    security or their further communication.

  The receipt, communication, processing or storage of raw data by such
  persons is not a controlled activity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions specified by Minister ---
  remote sensing products

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In a licence, the Minister may restrict the provision
  of remote sensing products or classes of such products from the
  licensed system to persons or classes of persons other than the
  licensee or system participants on any conditions that the Minister
  considers appropriate. The conditions may include requirements that,
  in specified cases or circumstances, the provision of the remote
  sensing products

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be subject to the Minister's prior approval; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be done only under a legally enforceable agreement,
    entered into in good faith, that includes measures respecting their
    security or their further provision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} A licence is valid for the period that the Minister
  considers appropriate and specifies in it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-transferable

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} A licence is not transferable without the Minister's
  consent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of [Statutory
  Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply to a licence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}System disposal plan and arrangements

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may not issue a licence without having approved

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a system disposal plan for the licensed system
    satisfactory to the Minister that, among other things, provides for
    the protection of the environment, public health and the safety of
    persons and property; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} arrangements satisfactory to the Minister relating
    to the guarantee of the performance of the licensee's obligations
    under the system disposal plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations regarding plan and
  arrangements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensee and, in the case of a licence that has
  terminated, the former licensee, shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the following things are disposed of in
    accordance with the system disposal plan approved by the Minister:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} every system satellite,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the things used in connection with the
      cryptography and information assurance measures of the system,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any raw data and remote sensing products from
      the system that are under the control of the licensee or former
      licensee, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} anything else prescribed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} put into effect the guarantee arrangements approved
    by the Minister under paragraph (1)(b) and keep them in effect until
    the system disposal plan has been carried out.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of system disposal plan or
  arrangements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, if the Minister is satisfied that
  it is desirable to do so, having regard to national security, the
  defence of Canada, the safety of Canadian Forces, Canada's conduct of
  international relations, Canada's international obligations and the
  protection of the environment, public health and the safety of persons
  and property and any prescribed factors,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on application, amend a system disposal plan or the
    guarantee arrangements approved by the Minister under paragraph
    (1)(b); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the Minister's own initiative, on first giving
    notice to the licensee or former licensee that provides a reasonable
    opportunity to make representations, amend a system disposal plan.

### [Amendment, Suspension and Cancellation of Licences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-427095 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of conditions on Minister's
initiative

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may on the Minister's own initiative, if the Minister is
  satisfied that the amendment is desirable, having regard to national
  security, the defence of Canada, the safety of Canadian Forces,
  Canada's conduct of international relations, Canada's international
  obligations and any prescribed factors, amend a licence with respect
  to any condition contemplated by any of subsections 8(5) to (7).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment --- s. 8(5)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the amendment concerns a condition contemplated by
  subsection 8(5), the Minister must first give to the licensee notice
  of the proposed amendment and a reasonable opportunity to make
  representations regarding it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment --- s. 8(6) or (7)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An amendment concerning a condition contemplated by
  subsection 8(6) or (7) may be effective immediately on notice to the
  licensee, but the Minister shall give to the licensee an opportunity
  --- during a period of 15 days after the notice or any longer period
  that the Minister specifies --- to make representations regarding it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of licence

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may suspend a licence in whole or in part for a period of up
  to 90 days if the Minister is satisfied that the continued operation
  of a licensed system is likely to be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} injurious to national security, the defence of
    Canada, the safety of Canadian Forces or Canada's conduct of
    international relations; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inconsistent with Canada's international
    obligations.

  The suspension may be effective immediately on notice to the person,
  but the Minister shall, in the notice, set out the reasons for the
  suspension and give to the person an opportunity --- during a period
  of 15 days after the notice or any longer period that the Minister
  specifies --- to make representations regarding it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of suspension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the licence is suspended for less than 90 days,
  the Minister may extend the suspension for any further period or
  periods to a maximum total of 90 days if the Minister is satisfied
  that there continues to be a reason for the suspension under
  subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further automatic extension of
  suspension

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister gives notice of a proposed
  cancellation before the suspension expires, the suspension continues,
  unless the Minister earlier ends it, until the end of the period for
  making representations regarding the proposed cancellation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of licence

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may cancel a
licence if it is suspended and a reason for the suspension continues to
exist or if, at any time, the Minister is otherwise satisfied, having
regard to national security, the defence of Canada, the safety of
Canadian Forces, Canada's conduct of international relations, Canada's
international obligations and any prescribed factors, that the licence
ought to be cancelled. But the Minister shall first give to the licensee
notice of the reasons for the proposed cancellation and an opportunity
--- during a period of 45 days after the notice or any longer period
that the Minister specifies --- to make representations regarding it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures ordered on suspension or
termination of licence

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may make an order requiring a person whose licence is
  suspended or cancelled or has expired to take any measures related to
  the operation of the remote sensing space system that the Minister
  considers advisable, having regard to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} national security, the defence of Canada, the
    safety of Canadian Forces, Canada's conduct of international
    relations and Canada's international obligations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the system disposal plan, including its provisions
    for the protection of the environment, public health and the safety
    of persons and property.

  The order may be effective immediately on notice to the person, but
  the Minister shall give to the person an opportunity --- during a
  period of 15 days after the notice or any longer period that the
  Minister specifies --- to make representations regarding it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of [Statutory
  Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply to an order made under this section.

### [Interruptions of Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-427123 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's order

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may make an order requiring a licensee to interrupt or
  restrict, for the period specified in the order, any operation,
  including the provision of any service, of the licensed system if the
  Minister believes on reasonable grounds that the continuation of that
  operation would be injurious to Canada's conduct of international
  relations or inconsistent with Canada's international obligations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of Minister of National Defence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of National Defence may make an order
  requiring a licensee to interrupt or restrict, for the period
  specified in the order, any operation, including the provision of any
  service, of the licensed system if the Minister of National Defence
  believes on reasonable grounds that the continuation of that operation
  would be injurious to the defence of Canada or the safety of Canadian
  Forces.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of [Statutory
  Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply to an order made under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-disclosure direction

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the minister making an order under subsection (1)
  or (2) is satisfied that the substance of the order ought not to be
  disclosed for the same reasons as those on which the order is founded,
  that minister may include in the order a direction that no person
  shall disclose its substance to any other person except as required by
  law or as necessary to give it effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice and opportunity to make
  representations

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An order made under this section may take effect
  immediately on notice to the licensee, but the minister making the
  order shall give to the licensee an opportunity --- during a period of
  15 days after the notice or any longer period that the minister
  specifies --- to make representations regarding it.

### [Priority Access]{.HTitleText2} {#h-427135 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's order for priority access

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may make an order requiring a licensee to provide to Her
  Majesty in right of Canada any service through the licensed system
  that the Minister believes on reasonable grounds is desirable for the
  conduct of international relations or the performance of Canada's
  international obligations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of Minister of National Defence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of National Defence may make an order
  requiring a licensee to provide to Her Majesty in right of Canada any
  service through the licensed system that that minister believes on
  reasonable grounds is desirable for the defence of Canada or the
  safety of Canadian Forces.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of Minister of Public Safety and
  Emergency Preparedness

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
  Preparedness may make an order requiring a licensee to provide any
  service through the licensed system

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the Royal Canadian Mounted Police that that
    minister believes on reasonable grounds is desirable for the
    fulfilment of its members' responsibilities under subsection 6(1) of
    the [Security Offences Act](/eng/acts/S-7);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the Canadian Security Intelligence Service that
    that minister believes on reasonable grounds is desirable for the
    fulfilment of its duties and functions under the [Canadian Security
    Intelligence Service Act](/eng/acts/C-23); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to Her Majesty in right of Canada that that
    minister believes on reasonable grounds is desirable for critical
    infrastructure protection or emergency preparedness.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Details of orders

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An order made under this section must specify the
  period during which the service is to be provided and may specify how
  and with what priority it is to be provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of [Statutory
  Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply to an order made under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-disclosure direction

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the minister making an order is satisfied that the
  substance of the order ought not to be disclosed for the same reasons
  as those on which the order is founded, that minister may include in
  the order a direction that no person shall disclose its substance to
  any other person except as required by law or as necessary to give it
  effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice and opportunity to make
  representations

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} An order made under this section may take effect
  immediately on notice to the licensee, but the minister making the
  order shall give to the licensee an opportunity --- during a period of
  15 days after the notice or any longer period that the minister
  specifies --- to make representations regarding it.

### [Transfer of Remote Sensing Satellites]{.HTitleText2} {#h-427156 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition on transfer of control

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  licensee or former licensee shall permit a command to a remote sensing
  satellite of the remote sensing space system for which the licence was
  issued to be given from outside Canada or by any other person unless
  the licensee or former licensee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} can override the command from Canada; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has obtained the approval of the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors for approval

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In deciding whether to give an approval, the Minister
  shall have regard to national security, the defence of Canada, the
  safety of Canadian Forces, Canada's conduct of international
  relations, Canada's international obligations and any prescribed
  factors.

## [Inspection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427164 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of inspectors

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate as inspectors for the purposes of this Act
  persons or classes of persons that the Minister considers qualified.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every inspector is to be provided with a certificate
  attesting to their designation, and shall, at the request of any
  person appearing to be in charge of any place entered by the
  inspector, present the certificate to the person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of inspectors

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Inspectors may, in carrying out
  their duties and functions,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), enter and inspect, at
    any reasonable time, any place owned by or under the control of a
    licensee, a system participant or any other person who the inspector
    reasonably believes may be carrying on a controlled activity in the
    operation of a remote sensing space system, and in which the
    inspector believes on reasonable grounds there is any document,
    information or thing relevant to the administration or enforcement
    of this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} examine any document, information or thing at the
    place or remove it for examination or copying;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} inspect any equipment at the place related to the
    operation of a remote sensing space system and perform tests of it;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any data processing system
    at the place to examine any data contained in or available to the
    system;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} reproduce any record from those data or cause it to
    be reproduced from them in the form of a printout or other
    intelligible output and remove the printout or other output for
    examination or copying; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any copying equipment or
    means of communication located at the place.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant required to enter
  dwelling-place

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An inspector may not enter a dwelling-place except
  with the consent of the occupant or under the authority of a warrant
  issued under subsection (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application, a
  justice, as defined in section 2 of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46), may issue a warrant authorizing an inspector
  named in the warrant to enter a dwelling-place, subject to any
  conditions specified in the warrant, if the justice is satisfied by
  information on oath that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dwelling-place is a place described in
    paragraph (1)(a);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entry to the dwelling-place is necessary for any
    purpose relating to the administration or enforcement of this Act;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} entry has been refused, there are reasonable
    grounds for believing that entry will be refused or consent to entry
    cannot be obtained from the occupant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An inspector executing a warrant issued under
  subsection (3) shall not use force unless the inspector is accompanied
  by a peace officer and the use of force has been specifically
  authorized in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to inspector

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The owner or other person in charge of a place
  entered by an inspector under this section shall give the inspector
  all reasonable assistance in the power of that person and furnish the
  inspector with any information that the inspector reasonably requires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction and false information

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} No person shall, with respect to an inspector engaged
  in carrying out their duties and functions,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} wilfully obstruct or hinder the inspector; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} knowingly make or provide to the inspector any
    false or misleading statement or information.

## [Administrative Monetary Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427262 .Part}

### [Violations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-427263 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violations

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes a provision, requirement or condition designated under
subparagraph 20(1)(k)(i) commits a violation and is liable to an
administrative monetary penalty not exceeding the maximum prescribed or,
if no maximum has been prescribed, to a penalty not exceeding \$5,000,
in the case of an individual, and \$25,000, in any other case.

### [Enforcement Officers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-427266 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of enforcement officers

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate as enforcement officers for the purposes of
  this Act persons or classes of persons that the Minister considers
  qualified.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certification of designated persons

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Enforcement officers are to receive a certificate
  attesting to their designation and shall, on demand, present the
  certificate to any person from whom they request information in the
  course of their duties or functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of inspector

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every enforcement officer is, in carrying out their
  duties and functions, an inspector.

### [Notices of Violation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-427274 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance and service

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  enforcement officer believes on reasonable grounds that a person has
  committed a violation, the officer may issue, and shall cause to be
  served on the person, a notice of violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may establish the form and content of
  notices of violation, but each notice of violation must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name the person believed to have committed a
    violation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identify the violation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set out the penalty that the enforcement officer
    proposes to impose;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} inform the person that they may, within 30 days
    after the notice is served or within any longer period specified in
    the notice, either pay the penalty set out in the notice or make
    representations to the officer with respect to the alleged violation
    or proposed penalty --- including any representations about entering
    into a compliance agreement --- and set out the manner for doing so;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} inform the person that, if they do not pay the
    penalty or make representations in accordance with the notice, they
    will be considered to have committed the violation and the officer
    may impose a penalty in respect of it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria for penalty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amount of a proposed penalty is, in each case, to
  be determined taking into account the following matters:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the degree of intention or negligence on the part
    of the person who committed the violation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the harm done by the violation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person's history of prior violations or
    convictions under this Act during the five-year period immediately
    before the violation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any prescribed criteria; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other relevant matter.

### [Determination of Responsibility and Penalty]{.HTitleText2} {#h-427292 .Subheading}

#### [Payment of Penalty]{.HTitleText3} {#h-427293 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** If the person pays the
penalty proposed in the notice of violation, the person is considered to
have committed the violation and proceedings in respect of it are ended.

#### [Making Representations]{.HTitleText3} {#h-427296 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Making of representations

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  person alleged to have committed a violation makes representations,
  the enforcement officer shall either

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter into a compliance agreement with the person
    on behalf of the Minister; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} decide on a balance of probabilities whether the
    person committed the violation and, if so, impose the penalty
    proposed, a lesser penalty or no penalty, taking into account the
    matters mentioned in subsection 25(3).

  The enforcement officer shall cause notice of any decision made under
  paragraph (b) to be issued and served on the person together with
  notice of the person's right of appeal under subsection 29(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the course of the making of representations, an
  enforcement officer may, on behalf of the Minister, enter into a
  compliance agreement with the person alleged to have committed a
  violation on any terms that the officer considers satisfactory. The
  terms

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} must provide for payment by the person to the
    Receiver General of a specified amount not greater than the penalty
    proposed in the notice of violation if the person does not comply
    with the agreement; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may provide for the giving of reasonable security,
    in a form and an amount that the officer considers satisfactory, for
    the person's performance of the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement ends proceedings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Entry into a compliance agreement ends the violation
  proceedings and precludes any further violation or offence proceedings
  in relation to the act or omission in question.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If agreement not complied with

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may issue and serve a notice of default
  on a person who has entered into a compliance agreement but has not
  complied with it. On service of the notice, the person is liable to
  pay without delay the amount provided for in the agreement, failing
  which, the Minister may realize or enforce any security for the
  person's performance of the agreement.

#### [Responsibility]{.HTitleText3} {#h-427311 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to act

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** A person who neither pays
the penalty nor makes representations in accordance with the notice of
violation is considered to have committed the violation, and the
enforcement officer shall impose the penalty proposed and notify the
person of it.

### [Appeal to Minister]{.HTitleText2} {#h-427314 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of appeal

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person served with notice of a decision made under paragraph 27(1)(b)
  may, within 30 days after the notice is served, or within any longer
  period that the Minister allows, appeal the decision to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On an appeal, the Minister may confirm, set aside or
  vary the decision of the enforcement officer.

### [Rules about Violations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-427320 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vicarious liability --- acts of
employees, agents and mandataries

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** A person is liable for a
violation that is committed by the person's employee acting in the
course of the employment or the person's agent or mandatary acting
within the scope of their authority, whether or not the employee, agent
or mandatary who actually committed the violation is identified or
proceeded against.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Due diligence

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** Due diligence is a
defence in a proceeding in relation to a violation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing violation

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** A violation that is
committed or continued on more than one day constitutes a separate
violation for each day on which it is committed or continued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** Any proceedings in
respect of a violation may be instituted at any time within, but not
later than, two years after the time when the subject-matter of the
proceedings arose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violation or offence

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If it
  is possible to proceed with any act or omission as a violation and it
  is also possible to proceed with it as an offence, proceeding in one
  manner precludes proceeding in the other.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violations not offences

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a violation is not an offence
  and, accordingly, section 126 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)
  does not apply in respect of one.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Admissibility of documents

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** In the absence of
evidence to the contrary, a document that appears to be a notice issued
under subsection 25(1) or 27(1) or (4) or given under section 28 or a
certificate issued under subsection 37(1) is presumed to be authentic
and is proof of its contents in any proceeding in respect of a violation
or a prosecution for an offence.

### [Recovery of Penalties and Amounts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-427336 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debts to Her Majesty

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  penalty imposed under this Act and an amount referred to in subsection
  27(4) each constitute a debt due to Her Majesty in right of Canada and
  may be recovered in the Federal Court or any other court of competent
  jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No proceedings to recover such a debt may be
  commenced later than seven years after the debt became payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceeds payable to Receiver General

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each such debt is payable to the Receiver General.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may issue a certificate certifying the unpaid amount of any
  debt referred to in subsection 36(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration in Federal Court

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Registration in the Federal Court or in any other
  court of competent jurisdiction of a certificate issued under
  subsection (1) has the same effect as a judgment of that Court for a
  debt of the amount specified in the certificate and all related
  registration costs.

## [Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427349 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention --- section 5, 13 or 14 or
subsection 16(1)

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who contravenes section 5 or subsection 16(1) or an order made
  under section 13 or 14 is guilty of an offence and liable on summary
  conviction

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine not
    exceeding \$50,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 18
    months, or to both; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, to a fine not exceeding
    \$250,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention --- section 15 or
  subsection 18(5) or (6)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes subsection 18(5) or (6)
  or an order made under section 15 is guilty of an offence and liable
  on summary conviction

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine not
    exceeding \$25,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
    months, or to both; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, to a fine not exceeding
    \$125,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction --- contravention outside
Canada

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person is alleged to have committed an act or omission that is a
  contravention of section 5 because of the application of section 6,
  proceedings in relation to that contravention may, whether or not the
  person is in Canada, be commenced in any territorial division in
  Canada, and the person may be tried and punished in respect of that
  offence in the same manner as if it had been committed in that
  territorial division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appearance of accused at trial

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The provisions of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46), including their exceptions, relating to
  requirements that an accused appear at and be present during
  proceedings apply in respect of proceedings commenced in a territorial
  division under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence dealt with outside Canada

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person is alleged to have committed an act or
  omission referred to in subsection (1) and the person has been dealt
  with outside Canada for the contravention in a manner that, if the
  person had been dealt with in Canada for the contravention in that
  manner, would allow the person to plead [*autrefois
  acquit*]{.otherLang lang="fr"}, [*autrefois convict*]{.otherLang
  lang="fr"} or pardon, the person is deemed to have been so dealt with
  in Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of officers, directors, agents
or mandataries

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** If a corporation commits
an offence under this Act, any officer, director, agent or mandatary of
the corporation who directed, authorized, assented to, acquiesced in or
participated in the commission of the offence is a party to and guilty
of the offence and is liable to the punishment provided for the offence,
whether or not the corporation has been prosecuted or convicted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences by employees, agents or
mandataries

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** In a prosecution for an
offence under this Act, it is sufficient proof of the offence to
establish that it was committed by an employee, an agent or a mandatary
of the accused, whether or not the employee, agent or mandatary is
identified or has been prosecuted for the offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Due diligence

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Due
  diligence is a defence in a prosecution for an offence under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Section 126 of [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall be prosecuted under section 126 of
  the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) for a contravention of subsection
  9(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offence

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** A contravention of this
Act that is committed or continued on more than one day constitutes a
separate offence for each day on which it is committed or continued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** Any proceedings by way of
summary conviction in respect of an offence under this Act may be
instituted at any time within, but not later than, two years after the
time when the subject-matter of the proceedings arose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Injunctions

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** If a court of competent
jurisdiction --- including the Federal Court --- is satisfied, on
application by the Minister, that a contravention of section 5 is being
or is likely to be committed, the court may grant an injunction, subject
to any conditions that the court considers appropriate, ordering any
person to cease or refrain from any activity related to that
contravention, or ordering the person to take any measure that a
licensee could, under this Act, be required to take.
