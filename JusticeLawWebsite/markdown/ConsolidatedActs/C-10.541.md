## Canada--Ukraine Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act, 2023 {#canadaukraine-free-trade-agreement-implementation-act-2023 .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2024, c. 3
:::

Assented to 2024-03-19

An Act to implement the 2023 Free Trade Agreement between Canada and
Ukraine
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1448728 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada--Ukraine Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act,
2023](/eng/acts/C-10.541).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1448731 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Free Trade Agreement
    between Canada and Ukraine, done at Ottawa on September 22,
    2023. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Joint Commission
    continued under paragraph 1 of Article 27.1 of the
    Agreement. ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the whole or any portion
    of an Act of Parliament or a regulation, order or other instrument
    issued, made or established in the exercise of a power conferred by
    or under an Act of Parliament. ([texte législatif
    fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister for
    International Trade. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation consistent with Agreement

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, this
Act and any federal law that implements a provision of the Agreement or
that allows the Government of Canada to meet an obligation under the
Agreement is to be interpreted in a manner consistent with the
Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of Act and Agreement to
water

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act or the Agreement applies to natural surface or
ground water in liquid, gaseous or solid state.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act, by specific mention or omission, is to be construed
to affect in any manner the right of Parliament to enact legislation
that implements any provision of the Agreement or that allows the
Government of Canada to meet an obligation under the Agreement.

## [His Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1448744 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on His Majesty

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on His
Majesty in right of Canada.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1448747 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to implement the Agreement, the objectives of which, as elaborated more
specifically through its provisions, are to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish a free trade area in accordance with the
  Agreement;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} promote, through the elimination of barriers to trade
  in goods and services, the expansion of reciprocal trade and the
  strengthening of economic relations between Canada and Ukraine in
  order to create opportunities for economic development;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} promote conditions of fair competition affecting
  trade between Canada and Ukraine;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} ensure a predictable commercial framework for
  business planning and investment;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} promote high levels of environmental protection,
  including through effective enforcement of environmental laws,
  mutually supportive trade and environmental policies and practices,
  and strengthened environmental cooperation between Canada and Ukraine;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} promote sustainable development;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} protect, enhance and enforce basic workers' rights,
  strengthen cooperation on labour matters and build on the respective
  international commitments of Canada and Ukraine on labour matters;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} recognize that Indigenous Peoples in Canada and
  Ukraine have the right to economic development and participation in
  trade and to engage freely in all their traditional and other economic
  activities;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} support the growth and development of micro, small
  and medium-sized enterprises by enhancing their ability to participate
  in and benefit from the opportunities created by the Agreement;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} facilitate equal access to, and the ability to
  benefit from, the opportunities created by the Agreement for women and
  men and support the conditions for women's full participation in
  domestic, regional and international trade and investment;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} recognize the right of the Parties to the Agreement
  to preserve, develop and implement their cultural policies for the
  purpose of strengthening cultural diversity in accordance with the
  rights and obligations provided for in the Agreement;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} encourage enterprises operating within Canada or
  Ukraine or subject to their jurisdiction to respect internationally
  recognized corporate social responsibility and responsible business
  conduct standards and principles and to pursue best practices;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} promote transparency, good governance and the rule of
  law, while strengthening commitments to combat bribery and corruption
  in trade and investment; and

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} respect the values and principles of democracy and
  promote and protect human rights and fundamental freedoms.

## [Causes of Action]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1448764 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Causes of action under sections 9 to 15

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person has any cause of action and no proceedings of any kind are to
  be taken, without the consent of the Attorney General of Canada, to
  enforce or determine any right or obligation that is claimed or arises
  solely under or by virtue of sections 9 to 15 or an order made under
  those sections.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Causes of action under Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to Section D of Chapter 17 and Article 20.24
  of the Agreement, no person has any cause of action and no proceedings
  of any kind are to be taken, without the consent of the Attorney
  General of Canada, to enforce or determine any right or obligation
  that is claimed or arises solely under or by virtue of the Agreement.

## [Implementation of the Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1448770 .Part}

### [Approval]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1448771 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement approved

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement is approved.

### [Administrative and Institutional Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1448774 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian representative on Commission

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is the
principal representative of Canada on the Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of expenditures

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
is to pay its appropriate share of the aggregate of any expenditures
incurred by or on behalf of the Commission.

### [Panels, Committees, Subcommittees, Working Groups, Expert Groups and Other Bodies]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1448779 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2) and paragraph (3)(a),
    appoint representatives of Canada to any committee, subcommittee or
    working group referred to in paragraph 5 of Article 27.1 of the
    Agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} appoint a panellist in accordance with Article 28.8
    of the Agreement; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} propose candidates to serve as the chair of a
    panel, or select the chair, in accordance with Article 28.8 of the
    Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of Minister of the Environment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of the Environment may appoint
  representatives of Canada to the Committee on the Environment
  continued under paragraph 2 of Article 13.25 of the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister of Labour

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Labour may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint representatives of Canada to the Labour
    Council established under paragraph 1 of Article 14.10 of the
    Agreement; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} designate a National Administrative Office in
    accordance with paragraph 1 of Article 14.11 of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrative support

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is to
designate an agency, division or branch of the Government of Canada to
facilitate the operation of Chapter 28 of the Agreement and to provide
administrative assistance to panels established under that Chapter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of costs

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
is to pay the costs of or its appropriate share of the costs of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the remuneration and expenses payable to members of
  panels, committees, subcommittees, working groups, expert groups and
  other bodies, to independent experts and to the assistants of panel
  members; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the general expenses incurred by panels, committees,
  subcommittees, working groups, expert groups and other bodies.

### [Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1448798 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders --- Article 28.13 of Agreement

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, for the purpose of suspending benefits in
  accordance with Article 28.13 of the Agreement, by order, do any of
  the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend rights or privileges granted by Canada to
    Ukraine or to goods, service suppliers, investors or investments of
    investors of Ukraine under the Agreement or any federal law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} modify or suspend the application of any federal
    law, with respect to Ukraine or to goods, service suppliers,
    investors or investments of investors of Ukraine;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} extend the application of any federal law to
    Ukraine or to goods, service suppliers, investors or investments of
    investors of Ukraine;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} take any other measure that the Governor in Council
    considers necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless repealed, an order made under subsection (1)
  has effect for the period specified in the order.

### [Compliance with Principles and Guidelines --- Canadian Companies]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1448808 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principles and guidelines

- **[[15.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must ensure that
  Canadian companies operating in Ukraine comply with the principles and
  guidelines referred to in article 15.14 of the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaints process

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must establish a process for receiving
  and responding to complaints of non-compliance with those principles
  and guidelines.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On or before January 1st of each year starting in
  2025, the Minister must prepare a report that summarizes activities
  carried out in relation to the Minister's obligations under this
  section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of report

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must table a copy of the report in each
  House of Parliament on any of the first 30 days on which that House is
  sitting after the report is completed.
