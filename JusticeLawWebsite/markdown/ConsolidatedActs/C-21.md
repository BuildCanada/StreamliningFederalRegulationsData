## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Canadian Payments Association]{.HTitleText1} {#h-74080 .Part}

### [Establishment and Membership]{.HTitleText2} {#h-74081 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Association established

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  corporation is hereby established to be called the Canadian Payments
  Association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Association not an agent of Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Association is not an agent of Her Majesty.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 40, s. 56
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members of Association

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Association shall consist of the following members:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Bank of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} every bank;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} every authorized foreign bank;

  - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} every cooperative credit association, loan
    company or trust company that is designated as a bridge institution
    under the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3);
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other person who is entitled under this Part to
    be a member and who, on application to the Association for
    membership in the Association, establishes entitlement to be a
    member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entitled members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each of the following persons is entitled to be a
  member of the Association if they meet the requirements set out in the
  regulations and the by-laws:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a central, a trust company, a loan company and any
    other person, other than a local that is a member of a central or a
    cooperative credit association, that accepts deposits transferable
    by order;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2001, c. 9, s. 223\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of a province or an agent or
    mandatary of Her Majesty in right of a province, if Her Majesty in
    right of a province or the agent or mandatary accepts deposits
    transferable by order;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a life insurance company;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a securities dealer;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a cooperative credit association;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the trustee of a qualified trust; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a qualified corporation, on behalf of its money
    market mutual fund.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement of membership

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Membership in the Association commences

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a bank and an authorized foreign
    bank, on the day on which an order is made under the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) approving its commencement and carrying on of
    business; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person referred to in paragraph
    (1)(d), on the day on which the person's application for membership
    in the Association is approved by the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of membership rights

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A suspension, imposed in accordance with the by-laws,
  of any of a member's membership rights is not effective unless the
  Association has, in advance, sent the Minister a notice of the
  suspension.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of membership

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A member, other than a member referred to in
  paragraphs (1)(a) to (c), ceases being a member of the Association if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the member gives the Association notice of its
    intention to cease being a member at least ninety days prior to the
    effective date thereof or such longer notice as the by-laws may
    require; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the member satisfies its liabilities to the
    Association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of membership

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A member, other than a member referred to in
  paragraphs (1)(a) to (c), ceases being a member of the Association
  three days after the day on which the Board adopts a resolution, by a
  majority of not less than two-thirds of the votes cast by directors
  voting in respect of the resolution, declaring that, in the opinion of
  the Board, the member does not meet the requirements set out in the
  regulations and the by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of resolution

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A member to which a resolution of the Board described
  in subsection (6) is applicable is not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} eligible to vote at any meeting of members held in
    the three days following the day on which the resolution is adopted;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} eligible, despite any other provision of this Part,
    for readmission to the Association as a member until a resolution is
    adopted by the Board, by a majority of not less than two-thirds of
    the votes cast by directors voting in respect of the resolution,
    declaring that, in the opinion of the Board, the member or former
    member concerned meets the requirements set out in the regulations
    and the by-laws; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} relieved of the obligation to pay to the
    Association its liabilities to the Association on the day it ceases
    being a member.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to vote

- **[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  member shall have one vote on all matters to be decided by members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proxies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member entitled to vote at a meeting of members
  may, by means of a proxy, appoint a proxyholder or one or more
  alternate proxyholders to attend and act at the meeting in the manner
  and to the extent authorized by the proxy and within the authority
  conferred by the proxy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} At a meeting of members, the members present, in
  person or by proxy, constitute a quorum if, together, they represent
  the majority of all members.

### [Objects and Powers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-74135 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  objects of the Association are to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish and operate national systems for the
    clearing and settlement of payments and other arrangements for the
    making or exchange of payments;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} facilitate the interaction of its clearing and
    settlement systems and related arrangements with other systems or
    arrangements involved in the exchange, clearing or settlement of
    payments; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} facilitate the development of new payment methods
    and technologies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of Association

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In pursuing its objects, the Association shall
  promote the efficiency, safety and soundness of its clearing and
  settlement systems and take into account the interests of users.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-21, s. 5
- 2001, c. 9, s. 224
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Association may, in carrying out its objects,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} arrange the exchange of payment items at such
    places in Canada as the Association considers appropriate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require the payment of dues by members and
    establish the amount thereof;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} employ such officers and employees and engage the
    services of such advisers, agents and consultants as it considers
    necessary for the proper conduct of its business, and fix the terms
    and conditions of their employment or engagement and remuneration;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide or procure the provision of pension,
    insurance or other benefits for officers and employees of the
    Association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity and powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of carrying out its objects, the
  Association has the capacity, rights, powers and privileges of a
  natural person.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-21, s. 6
- 2001, c. 9, s. 225
:::

### [Board of Directors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-74162 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be a Board of Directors of the Association consisting of the
  following 13 directors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the President;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} three directors who are directors, officers or
    employees of members that, in the normal course of business,
    maintain a settlement account at the Bank of Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} two directors who are directors, officers or
    employees of members other than those described in paragraph (b);
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} seven directors who are independent of the
    Association and of its members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election of directors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The directors referred to in paragraphs (1)(b) to (d)
  are to be elected by the members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} At a meeting of the Board, the directors present
  constitute a quorum if at least seven directors are present and a
  majority of the directors present are directors referred to in
  paragraph (1)(d).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-21, s. 8
- 2001, c. 9, s. 226
- 2014, c. 39, s. 337

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligibility

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person is eligible to be a director if they are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a director, officer or employee of the Bank of
    Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} employed in any capacity in the federal public
    administration or the public service of a province or hold any
    office or position for which any salary or other remuneration is
    payable out of public moneys; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Senate or House of Commons or a
    member of a provincial legislature.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a director, officer or employee of a member is a
  director of the Association, no other director, officer or employee of
  that member, or of an affiliate of that member, is eligible to be a
  director of the Association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affiliates

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one corporation is affiliated with another
    corporation if one of them is the subsidiary of the other or both
    are subsidiaries of the same corporation or each of them is
    controlled by the same person; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if two corporations are affiliated with the same
    corporation at the same time, they are deemed to be affiliated with
    each other.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of control

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In paragraph (3)(a), [[control]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means control in any manner that results in control in fact, whether
  directly through the ownership of securities or indirectly through a
  trust, agreement or arrangement, the ownership of any corporation or
  otherwise.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Designated Payment Systems]{.HTitleText1} {#h-74511 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-74512 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Part.

[[designated payment system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated payment system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a payment
    system designated under subsection 37(1). ([système de paiement
    désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a party to an arrangement
    in respect of a payment system. ([participant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[payment system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payment system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a system or arrangement
    for the exchange of messages effecting, ordering, enabling or
    facilitating the making of payments or transfers of value. ([système
    de paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[rule]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[rule]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a rule, by whatever name called,
    that governs a designated payment system. It includes an amendment
    to or a repeal of a rule. ([règles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Designation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-74526 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation by Minister

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, if he or she considers that it is in the public interest
  to do so, designate a payment system that in the opinion of the
  Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is national or substantially national in its scope;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} plays a major role in supporting transactions in
    Canadian financial markets or the Canadian economy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following factors shall be considered in a
  determination of whether it is in the public interest to designate a
  payment system:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the level of financial safety provided by the
    payment system to the participants and users;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the efficiency and competitiveness of payment
    systems in Canada; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the best interests of the financial system in
    Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before a payment system is designated, the Minister
  shall consult the manager and the participants of the payment system
  and may consult interested parties, with respect to the effect of the
  designation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall notify the manager and the
  participants of a designated payment system in any manner that the
  Minister considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation not statutory instrument

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A designation is not a statutory instrument for the
  purposes of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22). However,
  it must be published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 9, s. 244
:::

### [Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#h-74545 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies of rules to be sent to Minister

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A copy

  the manager of the designated payment system or, if there is none, by
  the participants to the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a rule made before the designation
    of the payment system, within 30 days after the designation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a rule made after the designation,
    within 10 days after it is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date of rule

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A rule, other than a rule referred to in paragraph
  (1)(a), does not come into force before the thirtieth day after a copy
  of it is sent to the Minister under subsection (1), but the Minister
  may declare the rule to be in force at any time before that period
  expires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of time

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is of the opinion that an extension
  of the period mentioned in subsection (2) is necessary to permit
  adequate review of a rule, the Minister may within 10 days after its
  receipt, on written notice to the sender of the rule, extend that
  period by up to 30 days.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disallowance

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may disallow the whole or a part of a
  rule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may exempt a designated payment system
  from the application of subsection (2).

- 2001, c. 9, s. 244

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** Every person who, without
reasonable cause, contravenes any provision of this Act is guilty of an
offence and

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a natural person, liable on summary
  conviction to a fine not exceeding \$100,000 or to imprisonment for a
  term not exceeding twelve months, or to both; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other entity, liable on summary
  conviction to a fine not exceeding \$500,000.

- 2001, c. 9, s. 244
:::

Minister of State appointed under the [Ministries and Ministers of State
Act](/eng/acts/M-8) to assist the Minister.

      Chairperson.

      Canadian Payments Association immediately before the day
      subsection 9(2) of the [Canadian Payments Act](/eng/acts/C-21), as
      amended by subsection 219(2) of this Act, comes into force ceases
      to hold office at the close of the next annual meeting of the
      members of the Association that is held after the coming into
      force of that subsection. The new directors of the Board shall be
      elected at that meeting.

    into force of subsection 2(3) of that Act, as enacted by section 219
    of this Act, is deemed to have come into force on the day the rule

    - [358]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within 30 days after the day on

      to be held at which a new Board of Directors shall be established.

    - ###### Former directors cease to hold office {#former-directors-cease-to-hold-office .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The persons who hold office as directors of the
      Board of the Canadian Payments Association on the day on which
      section 337 comes into force cease to hold office at the close of
      the meeting of the members referred to in subsection (1).

    - ###### New Board of Directors {#new-board-of-directors .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections 8(2) and 9.1(1) of the
      [Canadian Payments Act](/eng/acts/C-21), the directors of the new
      Board of Directors, other than the President of the Association,
      shall be appointed under subsection (4) or elected under
      subsection (5) for the terms of office provided for under those
      subsections.

    - ###### Appointment of directors {#appointment-of-directors .MarginalNote}

      [(4)]{.lawlabel} At the commencement of the meeting of the members
      referred to in subsection (1), the Board of Directors shall
      appoint

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} two directors from among the directors in
        office who represent the classes described in subsection 9(3) of
        the [Canadian Payments Act](/eng/acts/C-21), as it read
        immediately before the coming into force of section 337, to act
        as directors described in paragraph 8(1)(b) or (c) of the
        [Canadian Payments Act](/eng/acts/C-21) for a term of one year;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one director from among the directors in office
        who represent the classes described in subsection 9(3) of the
        [Canadian Payments Act](/eng/acts/C-21), as it read immediately
        before the coming into force of section 337, to act as a
        director described in paragraph 8(1)(b) or (c) of the [Canadian
        Payments Act](/eng/acts/C-21) for a term of two years;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} two directors from among the directors in
        office who were appointed by the Minister under subsection
        9(1.1) of the [Canadian Payments Act](/eng/acts/C-21), as it
        read immediately before the coming into force of section 337, to
        act as directors described in paragraph 8(1)(d) of the [Canadian
        Payments Act](/eng/acts/C-21) for a term of one year;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} one director from among the directors in office
        who were appointed by the Minister under subsection 9(1.1) of
        the [Canadian Payments Act](/eng/acts/C-21), as it read
        immediately before the coming into force of section 337, to act
        as a director described in paragraph 8(1)(d) of the [Canadian
        Payments Act](/eng/acts/C-21) for a term of two years; and

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} one director from among those described in
        paragraph 8(1)(d) of the [Canadian Payments Act](/eng/acts/C-21)
        for a term of three years.

    - ###### Election of directors {#election-of-directors .MarginalNote}

      [(5)]{.lawlabel} Immediately after seven directors have been
      appointed under subsection (4), the members shall elect

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one director from among those described in
        paragraph 8(1)(b) or (c) of the [Canadian Payments
        Act](/eng/acts/C-21), for a term of two years;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one director from among those described in
        paragraph 8(1)(b) or (c) of the [Canadian Payments
        Act](/eng/acts/C-21), for a term of three years;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one director from among those described in
        paragraph 8(1)(d) of the [Canadian Payments
        Act](/eng/acts/C-21), for a term of two years; and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} two directors from among those described in
        paragraph 8(1)(d) of the [Canadian Payments
        Act](/eng/acts/C-21), for a term of three years.

    - ###### List of qualified candidates {#list-of-qualified-candidates .MarginalNote}

      [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the election of directors
      under paragraph (5)(a) or (b), the Board shall endeavour to
      identify candidates that are broadly representative of the
      diversity of the membership of the Association and, at least 15
      days before the meeting of the members referred to in subsection
      (1), the Board shall provide to members a list of qualified
      candidates.

    - ###### Chairperson {#chairperson .MarginalNote}

      [(7)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 15(1) of the [Canadian
      Payments Act](/eng/acts/C-21), as enacted by section 339 of this
      Act, the directors appointed under subsection (4) or elected under
      subsection (5) shall elect a Chairperson of the Board from among
      those who were appointed under paragraph (4)(d) or (e) or elected
      under (5)(c) or (d).

    - ###### Deeming {#deeming .MarginalNote}

      [(8)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the [Canadian Payments
      Act](/eng/acts/C-21), the directors appointed under subsection (4)
      or elected under subsection (5) are deemed to be directors who
      have been elected under subsection 8(2) of that Act. For greater
      certainty, they are eligible to be elected for only one further
      term.
