## Canada Pension Plan Investment Board Act {#canada-pension-plan-investment-board-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1997, c. 40
:::

Assented to 1997-12-18

An Act to establish the Canada Pension Plan Investment Board and to
amend the Canada Pension Plan and the Old Age Security Act and to make
consequential amendments to other Acts
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-165482 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada Pension Plan Investment Board Act](/eng/acts/C-8.3).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-165485 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Act.

[[appropriate provincial Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appropriate provincial Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect
    of a province, means the province's minister of the Crown who has
    primary responsibility for that province's finances. ([ministre
    provincial compétent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Pension Plan
    Investment Board established by section
    3. ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[by-law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[by-law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a by-law of the
    Board. ([Version anglaise seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Ontario, the Ontario Court
      (General Division);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Quebec, the Superior Court of
      the Province;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the Provinces of Nova Scotia and British
      Columbia, the Supreme Court of the Province;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the Provinces of New Brunswick, Manitoba,
      Saskatchewan and Alberta, the Court of Queen's Bench for the
      Province;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the Provinces of Prince Edward Island and
      Newfoundland, the trial division of the Supreme Court of the
      Province; and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the Supreme Court of Yukon, the Supreme Court of
      the Northwest Territories and the Nunavut Court of
      Justice. ([tribunal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an obligation

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that, before April 1, 1998, was held to the
        credit of the Canada Pension Plan Investment Fund, as
        established under subsection 109(1) of the [Canada Pension
        Plan](/eng/acts/C-8),

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that, as applied to Canada, is an obligation
        of the Government of Canada and, as applied to a province, is an
        obligation of the government of the province or an obligation of
        any agent of Her Majesty in right of the province that is
        guaranteed as to principal and interest by that government, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that complies with the conditions that were
        set out in section 111 of the [Canada Pension
        Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) as that section read immediately before
        April 1, 1998; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an obligation that

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on or after April 1, 1998, was purchased by the
        Minister of Finance under section 110 of the [Canada Pension
        Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or is purchased by the Board under section
        6.1, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is an obligation of the government of a
        province or an obligation of any agent of Her Majesty in right
        of a province that is guaranteed as to principal and interest by
        that government. ([titre désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a body corporate, a trust, a
    partnership, a fund, an unincorporated association or organization,
    Her Majesty in right of Canada or of a province or an agency of Her
    Majesty in right of Canada or of a province and the government of a
    foreign country or any political subdivision or agency of the
    government of a foreign country. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Finance. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[participating province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[participating province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a province
    other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a territory; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a province providing a comprehensive pension plan
      as defined in subsection 3(1) of the [Canada Pension
      Plan](/eng/acts/C-8). ([province participante]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[subsidiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[subsidiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation that is
    wholly owned by the Board directly or indirectly through any number
    of subsidiaries each of which is wholly owned directly or indirectly
    by the Board. ([filiale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Constitution of the Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-165514 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board established

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  established a corporation to be known as the Canada Pension Plan
  Investment Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not agent of Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board is not an agent of Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not part of federal public
  administration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Directors, officers, employees and agents of the
  Board are not part of the federal public administration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act not applicable to Board

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The [Canada Not-for-profit Corporations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-7.75) does not apply to the Board.

## [Capital and Shares]{.HTitleText1} {#h-165526 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capital

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  capital of the Board is \$100. The Minister shall pay the capital of
  the Board out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shares

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The capital is divided into 10 shares having a par
  value of \$10 each. The shares shall be issued to the Minister to be
  held on behalf of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The shares issued to the Minister shall be registered
  by the Board in the name of the Minister.

## [Objects and Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-165534 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The objects of the Board
are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to assist the Canada Pension Plan in meeting its
  obligations to contributors and beneficiaries under the [Canada
  Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to manage any amounts transferred to it under
  sections 108.1 and 108.3 of the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8),
  and its right, title or interest in any designated securities, in the
  best interests of the contributors and beneficiaries under that Act;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to invest its assets with a view to achieving a
  maximum rate of return, without undue risk of loss, having regard to
  the factors that may affect the funding of the Canada Pension Plan and
  the ability of the Canada Pension Plan to meet its financial
  obligations on any given business day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Board

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board has the capacity and, subject to this Act, the rights, powers
  and privileges of a natural person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No inconsistent business or activity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board and its subsidiaries shall not, directly or
  indirectly, carry on any business or activity or exercise any power
  that is inconsistent with the Board's objects, or that the Board is
  restricted by this Act from carrying on or exercising, and shall not,
  directly or indirectly, exercise any of its powers in a manner
  contrary to this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No invalidity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No act of the Board, including a transfer of
  property, is invalid by reason only that the Board was without the
  capacity or power to so act.

## [Designated Securities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-165549 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacement security

- **[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  the maturity of a designated security of a province that was issued
  before January 1, 1998, the Board shall purchase another security
  issued by that province if the Board is requested to do so, in
  writing, by the appropriate provincial Minister of that province at
  least 30 days before the date of maturity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principal amount

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The principal amount of the replacement security
  shall be not more than the principal outstanding under the maturing
  designated security.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term to maturity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The replacement security shall be for a term of 20
  years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The replacement security shall bear interest at a
  rate fixed by the Board, in accordance with any agreement entered into
  between the Board and the Minister. The rate shall be substantially
  the same as the interest rate that the province would be required to
  pay if it were to borrow the same amount for the same term through the
  issuance of a security on the public capital market.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Features of replacement security

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The replacement security shall be issued to or
  payable to the Board and shall be expressed to be not negotiable and
  not transferable or assignable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Redemption at request of province

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall redeem a designated security in whole
  or in part before maturity if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Board is requested to do so, in writing, by the
    appropriate provincial Minister of a province at least 30 days
    before the proposed redemption date; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the appropriate provincial Minister has agreed to
    pay on the proposed redemption date

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any payments of principal or interest due on or
      before the proposed redemption date but not yet paid,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} interest on the principal amount being redeemed
      accrued to the proposed redemption date, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to the present value of the
      remaining instalments of principal being redeemed and interest on
      that principal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of present value

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subparagraph (6)(b)(iii), the
  present value shall be calculated by discounting the instalments of
  principal being redeemed and interest on that principal using an
  interest rate fixed by the Board, in accordance with any agreement
  entered into between the Board and the Minister of Finance. In fixing
  that rate, the Board shall choose a rate that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the designated security to be redeemed was
    issued before January 1, 1998, is substantially the same as the rate
    that the Government of Canada would be required to pay if it were to
    borrow the principal amount being redeemed for a term equal to the
    remaining term of that designated security through the issuance of a
    security on the public capital market; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the designated security to be redeemed was
    issued on or after January 1, 1998, is substantially the same as the
    rate that the province would be required to pay if it were to borrow
    the principal amount being redeemed for a term equal to the
    remaining term of that designated security through the issuance of a
    security on the public capital market.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consolidation of securities

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} At the request of the provincial treasurer or other
  similar officer of a province, the Board may accept in the place of
  any series of designated securities of that province acquired during
  any consecutive period of not more than twelve months, on payment of
  any interest then accrued on the securities, another security of that
  province that is in an amount equal to the aggregate amount then
  outstanding of the designated securities of that series, and that
  bears interest at a rate determined by the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation guaranteed by the provincial
  government

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} Any security purchased by the Board under this
  section must be an obligation of the government of a province or an
  obligation of an agent of Her Majesty in right of a province that is
  guaranteed as to principal and interest by that government.

- 2003, c. 5, s. 14

## [Management]{.HTitleText1} {#h-165578 .Part}

### [Board of Directors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165579 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board of directors

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall be managed
by a board of directors of 12 directors, including the Chairperson.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principal duties

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this Act, the board of directors shall manage or supervise the
  management of the business and affairs of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specific duties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Without limiting the generality of subsection (1),
  the board of directors shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish written investment policies, standards
    and procedures in accordance with section 35;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish procedures for the identification of
    potential conflicts of interest and procedures to resolve those
    conflicts;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} establish a code of conduct for officers and
    employees of the Board; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} designate a committee of the board of directors to
    monitor application of the conflict of interest procedures and the
    code of conduct.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to delegate

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and the by-laws, the board of directors may delegate
  to the Chairperson, to a committee of the board of directors or to any
  officer of the Board any of the powers or duties of the board of
  directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limits on power

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The board of directors may not delegate the power to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} adopt, amend or repeal by-laws;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish the Board's investment policies,
    standards and procedures;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fill a vacancy in a committee of directors or in
    the office of auditor of the Board;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} appoint officers to the Board or fix their
    remuneration; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} approve the annual financial statements of the
    Board and any other financial statements issued by the Board.

### [Directors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165601 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of directors

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  director shall be appointed by the Governor in Council, on the
  recommendation of the Minister, to hold office during good behaviour
  for such term, not exceeding three years, as will ensure, as far as
  possible, the expiration in any one year of the terms of office of not
  more than one half of the directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committee to advise Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may establish a committee to advise the
  Minister on the appointment of directors. The committee shall consist
  of a representative designated by the Minister and a representative of
  each participating province designated by the appropriate provincial
  Minister for that province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation with participating
  provinces

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall consult with the appropriate
  provincial Ministers of the participating provinces before making any
  recommendation to the Governor in Council with respect to the
  appointment of directors and before making an appointment under
  subsection (8).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment factors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Before making a recommendation to the Governor in
  Council with respect to the appointment of directors and before making
  an appointment under subsection (8), the Minister shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have regard to the desirability of having on the
    board of directors

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} directors who are representative of the various
      regions of Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a sufficient number of directors with proven
      financial ability or relevant work experience to enable the Board
      to effectively achieve its objects; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} endeavour to ensure that no more than three of the
    12 directors reside outside Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A director is eligible for reappointment for one or
  more additional terms of office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may remove a director for
  cause.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation in office

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If no person is appointed to take office as a
  director on the expiration of the term of an incumbent director, the
  incumbent director continues in office until a successor is appointed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancy

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Where a person ceases to be a director during the
  term for which the person was appointed, the Minister shall appoint a
  qualified person to hold office as a director for the remainder of the
  term.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disqualified persons

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The following persons are disqualified from being
  directors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who is less than 18 years of age;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who is of unsound mind and has been so
    found by a court in Canada or elsewhere;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who has the status of a bankrupt;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a person who is not a natural person;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a person who is an agent or employee of Her Majesty
    in right of Canada or in right of a province;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a person who is a member of the Senate or House of
    Commons of Canada or a member of a provincial legislature; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a person who is an agent or employee of the
    government of a foreign country or any political subdivision of a
    foreign country.

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2013, c. 40, s. 277\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and benefits of directors

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} A director is entitled to receive from the Board
  such remuneration and benefits as may be fixed by the by-laws, which
  remuneration and benefits shall be fixed having regard to the
  remuneration and benefits received by persons having similar
  responsibilities and engaged in similar activities.

### [Chairperson]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165642 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall, on the recommendation of the Minister made
  after the Minister has consulted with the board of directors and the
  appropriate provincial Ministers of the participating provinces,
  designate one of the directors as Chairperson to hold office during
  good behaviour for such term as the Governor in Council deems
  appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may remove the Chairperson
  for cause.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presiding at meetings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson shall preside at all meetings of the
  board of directors and may exercise such powers and perform such
  duties and functions as are specified by the board of directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacement of Chairperson

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Chairperson is absent at any meeting of the
  board of directors, one of the directors present who is chosen to so
  act by the directors present shall preside and have all the powers,
  duties and functions of the Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration of Chairperson

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson is entitled to receive from the Board
  such remuneration and benefits as may be fixed by the by-laws, which
  remuneration and benefits shall be fixed having regard to the
  remuneration and benefits received by persons having similar
  responsibilities and engaged in similar activities.

### [Officers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165654 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of officers

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  board of directors may, subject to the by-laws, designate the offices
  of the Board, appoint officers of the Board and specify their duties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors not officers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A director is not eligible to be appointed an officer
  of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Two or more offices

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person may hold two or more offices of the Board.

### [Duty of Care]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165662 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of care

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  director and officer of the Board in exercising any of the powers of a
  director or an officer and in discharging any of the duties of a
  director or an officer shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} act honestly and in good faith with a view to the
    best interests of the Board; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} exercise the care, diligence and skill that a
    reasonably prudent person would exercise in comparable
    circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special knowledge or skill

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A director or officer of the Board who in fact
  possesses, or by reason of profession or business ought to possess, a
  particular level of knowledge or skill relevant to the director's or
  officer's powers or duties shall employ that particular level of
  knowledge or skill in the exercise of those powers or the discharge of
  those duties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reliance on statements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A director or an officer of the Board is deemed to
  comply with subsections (1) and (2) if they rely in good faith on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} financial statements of the Board represented by an
    officer of the Board, or represented in a written report of the
    Board's auditor, to be a fair reflection of the financial condition
    of the Board; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a report of an accountant, lawyer, notary or other
    professional person whose profession lends credibility to a
    statement made by the person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to comply

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  director, officer and employee of the Board shall comply with this Act
  and the by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No exculpation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No provision in any contract, in any resolution of
  the Board or in the by-laws relieves any director, officer or employee
  of the Board from the duty to act in accordance with this Act or
  relieves a director, officer or employee from liability for a breach
  of the Act.

### [Indemnification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165679 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnification

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  in respect of an action by or on behalf of the Board to procure a
  judgment in its favour, the Board may indemnify a director or officer
  of the Board, a former director or officer of the Board, or any person
  who acts or acted at the Board's request as a director or officer of
  an entity of which the Board is or was a shareholder or in which the
  Board has or had a financial interest, against all costs and expenses
  reasonably incurred by the person, including amounts paid in
  settlement or to satisfy a judgment, in respect of any civil, criminal
  or administrative action or proceeding to which the person is made a
  party by reason of being or having been such a person, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person acted honestly and in good faith with a
    view to the best interests of the Board or the entity; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a criminal or administrative action
    or proceeding enforced by a monetary penalty, the person had
    reasonable grounds for believing that the impugned conduct was
    lawful.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnification in derivative action

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may, with the approval of a court,
  indemnify a person referred to in subsection (1), in respect of an
  action by or on behalf of the Board or an entity referred to in that
  subsection to procure a judgment in its favour to which the person is
  made a party by reason of being or having been a director or an
  officer of the Board or entity, against all costs and expenses
  reasonably incurred by the person, including an amount paid in
  settlement or to satisfy a judgment, in respect of that action if the
  person fulfils the conditions set out in paragraphs (1)(a) and (b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to indemnity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding the other subsections of this
  section, a person referred to in subsection (1) is entitled to
  indemnity from the Board in respect of all costs and expenses,
  including an amount paid in settlement or to satisfy a judgment,
  reasonably incurred by the person in respect of the defence of any
  civil, criminal or administrative action or proceeding to which the
  person is made a party by reason of being or having been a director or
  an officer of the Board or of an entity referred to in that
  subsection, if the person seeking indemnity

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} was substantially successful on the merits in the
    defence of the action or proceeding; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fulfils the conditions set out in paragraphs (1)(a)
    and (b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal representatives

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board could indemnify a person under any of
  subsections (1) to (3), the Board may to the same extent indemnify the
  heirs or personal representatives of the person.

- Definition of [[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section,
  [[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an investigation that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pertains to the administration or enforcement of an
    Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is authorized by or pursuant to an Act of
    Parliament or of the legislature of a province; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is within a class of investigations prescribed in
    the regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors' and officers' insurance

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Board may purchase
and maintain insurance for the benefit of any person referred to in
subsection 16(1) and the personal representatives of that person against
any liability incurred by the person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the capacity of a director or officer of the
  Board, except where the liability relates to a failure to act honestly
  and in good faith with a view to the best interests of the Board; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the capacity of a director or officer of another
  entity where the person acts or acted in that capacity at the Board's
  request, except where the liability relates to a failure to act
  honestly and in good faith with a view to the best interests of the
  entity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to court for indemnification

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  court may order an indemnity under section 16 on the application of
  the Board or a person referred to in subsection 16(1) or (4) and may
  make any further order that it thinks fit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an application is made for an order under
  subsection (1), the court may order notice to be given to any
  interested person, and that person is entitled to appear and be heard
  in person or by counsel.

### [Meetings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165707 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resolution in lieu of meeting

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  resolution in writing, signed by all the directors entitled to vote on
  that resolution at a meeting of the board of directors or of one of
  its committees, is as valid as if it had been passed at a meeting of
  the board of directors or of one of its committees.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing resolution

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of every resolution referred to in
  subsection (1) shall be kept with the minutes of the proceedings of
  the board of directors or its committees.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation by telephone

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** A director may, subject
to the by-laws, participate in a meeting of the board of directors or of
one of its committees by means of any telephone or other communications
facilities that permit all persons participating in the meeting to
communicate with each other, and a director who participates in that way
is deemed for the purposes of this Act to be present at the meeting.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dissent

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  director who is present at a meeting of the board of directors or of
  one of its committees is deemed to have consented to any resolution
  passed or action taken at the meeting unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the director's dissent is entered in the minutes of
    the meeting or the director requests that the director's dissent be
    entered in the minutes of the meeting;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the director sends a written dissent to the
    secretary of the meeting before the meeting is adjourned; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the director sends a dissent by registered mail or
    delivers it to the head office of the Board immediately after the
    meeting is adjourned.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loss of right to dissent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A director who votes for or consents to a resolution
  is not entitled to dissent under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dissent of absent director

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A director who was not present at a meeting at which
  a resolution was passed or an action was taken is deemed to have
  consented to the resolution or the action unless, within seven days
  after becoming aware of the resolution or the action, the director

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} causes a dissent to be placed with the minutes of
    the meeting; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sends a dissent by registered mail or delivers it
    to the head office of the Board.

### [Conflicts of Interest]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165727 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of director's interest

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  director or officer of the Board shall disclose in writing to the
  Board or request to have entered in the minutes of a meeting of the
  board of directors or one of its committees the nature and extent of
  the director's or officer's interest

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as a party to a transaction or proposed transaction
    with the Board; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as a director or an officer of any entity that is a
    party to a transaction or proposed transaction with the Board or as
    a person who holds a material interest in any such entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of disclosure for director

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The disclosure must be made, in the case of a
  director,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the meeting at which a proposed transaction is
    first considered;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the director was not at the time of that meeting
    interested in a proposed transaction, at the first meeting after the
    director becomes so interested;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the director becomes interested after a
    transaction is made, at the first meeting after the director becomes
    so interested; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if a person who is interested in a transaction
    later becomes a director, at the first meeting after the person
    becomes a director.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of disclosure for officer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The disclosure must be made, in the case of an
  officer,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} without delay after the officer becomes aware that
    the transaction or proposed transaction is to be considered or has
    been considered at a meeting of the board of directors or one of its
    committees;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the officer becomes interested after a
    transaction is made, without delay after the officer becomes so
    interested; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if a person who is interested in a transaction
    later becomes an officer, without delay after the person becomes an
    officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of disclosure for director or
  officer

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a transaction or proposed transaction is one that,
  in the ordinary course of the Board's business, would not require
  approval by the board of directors, a director or officer shall
  disclose in writing to the Board or request to have entered in the
  minutes of a meeting of the board of directors or one of its
  committees the nature and extent of the interest of the director or
  officer without delay after the director or officer becomes aware of
  the transaction or proposed transaction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voting

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A director referred to in subsection (1) shall not
  vote on a resolution or participate in a discussion to approve the
  transaction mentioned in that subsection unless the transaction is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one relating primarily to the director's
    remuneration as a director of the Board or one of its subsidiaries;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one for indemnity or insurance under section 16 or
    17; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one with a subsidiary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing disclosure

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, a general notice to
  the board of directors or to one of its committees by a director or
  officer, declaring that the director or officer is a director or
  officer of, or has a material interest in, an entity and is to be
  regarded as interested in any transaction made with that entity, is a
  sufficient declaration of interest in relation to any transaction so
  made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Avoidance standards

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A transaction between the Board and one or more of
  its directors or officers, or between the Board and another entity of
  which a director or officer of the Board is a director or officer or
  in which a director or officer of the Board has a material interest,
  is neither void nor voidable by reason only of that relationship or by
  reason only that a director with an interest in the transaction is
  present at or is counted to determine the presence of a quorum at a
  meeting of the board of directors or on one its committees that
  authorized the transaction, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the director or officer disclosed the interest in
    accordance with subsection (2), (3), (4) or (6), as the case may be;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the transaction was approved by the directors; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transaction was reasonable and fair to the
    Board at the time it was approved.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to court

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Where a director or officer of the Board fails to
  disclose an interest in a transaction in accordance with this section,
  a court may, on the application of the Board, set aside the
  transaction on any terms that it thinks fit.

- Meaning of [[transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  includes a contract, a guarantee and an investment.

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165762 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No constructive notice

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** No person dealing with
the Board or with any person who has acquired rights from the Board is
deemed to have notice or knowledge of the contents of a document, other
than an Act of Parliament or any instrument required to be published in
the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) pursuant to the
[Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22), concerning the Board by
reason only that the document is available at the head office of the
Board or has been made public.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity of acts

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** An act of the Chairperson
or other director of the Board, or of the chief executive officer or
other officer of the Board, is not invalid by reason only of an
irregularity in that person's appointment or a defect in that person's
qualifications.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assertions

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The Board may not assert
against a person dealing with the Board or with any person who has
acquired rights from the Board

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that this Act or the by-laws have not been complied
  with, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that a document issued by any director, officer or
  agent of the Board having apparent authority to issue the document is
  not valid or genuine by reason only that the person who issued the
  document lacked actual authority to issue the document,

except where the person has knowledge that the facts asserted are true.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporate seal

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The Board may, but need
not, have a corporate seal, and an instrument or agreement executed on
behalf of the Board is not invalid merely because a corporate seal is
not affixed to it.

### [Audit Committee]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165798 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of audit committee

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** The audit committee shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} require the Board's management to implement and
  maintain appropriate internal control procedures;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} review, evaluate and approve those internal control
  procedures;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} review and approve the Board's annual financial
  statements and report to the board of directors before those
  statements are approved by the board of directors;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} meet with the Board's auditor to discuss the Board's
  annual financial statements and the auditor's report;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} review all investments and transactions that could
  adversely affect the return on the Board's investments that are
  brought to the committee's attention by the Board's auditor or
  officers; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} meet with the chief internal auditor of the Board, or
  with the person acting in any similar capacity, and with the Board's
  management, to discuss the effectiveness of the internal control
  procedures.

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board's auditor or any member of the audit committee may call a
  meeting of the committee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meeting of directors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The audit committee may call a meeting of the board
  of directors to consider any matter of concern to the committee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Auditor's right to attend meetings

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board's auditor is entitled to receive notice of and to attend
  meetings of the board of directors and meetings of the audit
  committee, at the Board's expense, and to be heard at those meetings
  on matters relating to the auditor's duties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requiring auditor's attendance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board's auditor shall attend meetings of the
  audit committee, if requested to do so by a member of the audit
  committee, at the Board's expense, and shall attend meetings of the
  board of directors, if requested to do so by a director.

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} approve the engagement of investment managers

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} require management to implement and maintain

    and

  referred to in paragraph (d).

## [Financial Management]{.HTitleText1} {#h-165833 .Part}

### [Financial Year]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165834 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial year

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** The financial year of the
Board is the period beginning on April 1 in one calendar year and ending
on March 31 in the next calendar year.

### [Financial Statements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165837 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Books and systems

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall, in respect of itself and each of its subsidiaries, cause

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} books of account and records to be kept;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} financial and management control and information
    systems and management practices to be maintained; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a record of the investments held during the
    financial year to be kept, showing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the book value of each investment,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the market value of each investment and such
      information as will permit the verification of that value, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} such information as will permit the
      determination of whether the requirements of this Act and the
      investment policies, standards and procedures have been met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner in which books, etc., to be kept

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The books, records, systems and practices required by
  subsection (1) shall be kept and maintained in such manner as will
  provide reasonable assurance that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Board's assets and those of its subsidiaries
    are safeguarded and controlled;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Board's transactions and those of its
    subsidiaries are in accordance with this Act and the by-laws, or the
    by-laws of the subsidiaries, as the case may be; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Board's financial, human and physical resources
    and those of its subsidiaries are managed economically and
    efficiently and that the Board's operations and those of its
    subsidiaries are carried out effectively.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Internal audit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall cause internal audits to be
  conducted, in respect of itself and each of its subsidiaries, to
  assess compliance with subsections (1) and (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual financial statements

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall cause financial statements to be
  prepared annually, in respect of itself and each of its subsidiaries,
  including, with respect to the financial year to which it relates,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a balance sheet as at the end of the financial
    year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement of income for the financial year;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement of change in net assets for the
    financial year; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement of investment portfolio.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of statements

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The annual financial statements shall show such
  information and particulars as in the opinion of the directors are
  necessary to present fairly, in accordance with generally accepted
  accounting principles, the primary source of which is the Handbook of
  the Chartered Professional Accountants of Canada, the Board's
  financial position as at the end of the financial year to which it
  relates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quarterly financial statements

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} During each financial year, the Board shall cause
  quarterly financial statements to be prepared for each three month
  period of the year. The quarterly statements shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} show the same information for the most recent three
    month period as is required to be set out in the Board's annual
    financial statements, except that a balance sheet is not required;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} show the same information in respect of the part of
    the year up to the date of the statements in relation to the
    corresponding period in the preceding financial year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval by board of directors

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The board of directors shall approve the annual
  financial statements of the Board and of each of its subsidiaries and
  that approval shall be evidenced by the signature of at least one
  director of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jointly and separately

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The financial statements required to be prepared
  under this section shall be prepared, as one set of statements,
  jointly and separately in relation to amounts managed by the Board for
  the purposes of the [[base Canada Pension Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
  and the [[additional Canada Pension Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
  defined in section 91 of the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual auditor's report

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  respect of itself and each of its subsidiaries, on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the annual financial statements referred to in

  or in a report prepared by the auditor or former auditor under section
  40, the auditor or former auditor shall without delay notify each
  director of the Board of the error or omission if the auditor or
  former auditor is of the opinion that the error or omission is
  material.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Correction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the directors receive a notification under
  subsection (2) of an error or omission in a financial statement or a
  report, the Board shall prepare a revised financial statement or the
  auditor or former auditor shall issue a correction to the report, as
  the case may be, and a copy of it shall be given to the Minister and
  the appropriate provincial Ministers.

### [Auditor]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165901 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of auditor

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  auditor of the Board shall be appointed annually by the board of
  directors, and may be removed at any time by the board of directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the expiration of the appointment of the auditor
  of the Board, the auditor is eligible for re-appointment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation in office

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), if an auditor of the
  Board is not appointed to take office on the expiration of the
  appointment of an incumbent auditor, the incumbent auditor continues
  in office until a successor is appointed.

Definition of [[firm of accountants]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[firm of accountants]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
  partnership, the members of which are accountants engaged in the
  practice of accounting, or a body corporate that is incorporated by or
  under an Act of the legislature of a province and engaged in the
  practice of accounting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualification of auditor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A natural person or firm of accountants is qualified
  to be the auditor of the Board if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a natural person, the person is an
    accountant who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is a member in good standing of an institute or
      association of accountants incorporated by or under an Act of the
      legislature of a province,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has at least five years experience at a senior
      level in performing audits of a financial institution,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is ordinarily resident in Canada, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} is independent of the Board, each of its
      subsidiaries, and the directors and officers of the Board and
      those of each of its subsidiaries; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a firm of accountants, the member or
    officer of the firm jointly designated by the firm and the Board to
    conduct the audit of the Board on behalf of the firm meets the
    qualifications described in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Independence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} independence is a question of fact; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person is deemed not to be independent if that
    person or any of that person's business partners

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is a business partner, director, officer or
      employee of the Board or of any of its subsidiaries, or a business
      partner of any director, officer or employee of the Board or of
      any of its subsidiaries, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has been a liquidator, trustee in bankruptcy,
      receiver or receiver and manager of any of the Board's
      subsidiaries within the two years before the natural person's or
      the firm of accountants' proposed appointment, as the case may be,
      as the Board's auditor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint designation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Within 15 days after appointing a firm of accountants
  as its auditor, the Board and the firm shall jointly designate a
  member or officer of the firm who has the qualifications described in
  paragraph (2)(a) to conduct the audit of the Board on behalf of the
  firm.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resignation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An auditor of the Board who becomes disqualified
  under this section shall resign without delay after becoming aware of
  the disqualification.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When resignation becomes effective

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A resignation of an auditor of the Board becomes
  effective at the time the Board receives a written resignation from
  the auditor or at the time specified in the resignation, whichever is
  later.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement of auditor

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board's auditor resigns or receives a
  notice or otherwise learns of a meeting of the board of directors at
  which another auditor is to be appointed in place of the auditor and
  the auditor objects to being replaced, the auditor shall submit to the
  Board a written statement giving the reasons for the resignation or
  the reasons why the auditor objects to being replaced.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement to be sent to Ministers

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board receives a written statement referred
  to in subsection (7) that relates to a resignation of its auditor as a
  result of a disagreement with the directors or officers of the Board
  or that relates to a replacement or proposed replacement of the
  auditor, the Board shall without delay send a copy of the statement to
  the Minister and to the appropriate provincial Ministers of the
  participating provinces.

### [Right to Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165936 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to information

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  request of the Board's auditor, the present or former directors,
  officers, employees or agents of the Board shall provide to the
  auditor all information and explanations, and all access to records,
  documents, books, accounts and vouchers of the Board and its
  subsidiaries, that the auditor considers necessary to prepare any
  report required by this Act and that they are reasonably able to
  furnish.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors' duties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the request of the Board's auditor, the directors
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtain from the present or former directors,
    officers, employees or agents of any of its subsidiaries all
    information and explanations that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the auditor considers necessary to enable the
      auditor to prepare any report required by this Act, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the present or former directors, officers,
      employees or agents are reasonably able to furnish; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} furnish the auditor with the information and
    explanations so obtained.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reliance on reports

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board's auditor may reasonably rely on any report
  of any other auditor of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No civil liability

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person who in good faith makes an oral or written
  communication under subsection (1) or (2) shall not be liable in any
  civil action arising from having made the communication.

### [Qualified Privilege]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165950 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualified privilege

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** Any oral or written
statement or report made under this Act by the Board's auditor or a
former auditor of the Board has qualified privilege.

### [Special Audit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165953 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special audit

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, if the Minister considers it necessary, appoint an
  auditor to conduct a special audit of the Board or any of its
  subsidiaries.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs of audit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The costs of a special audit are payable by the
  Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other sections to apply

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 43 to 45 apply in respect of a special
  auditor, with any modifications that the circumstances require.

### [Special Examination]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165961 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special examination

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall cause a special examination to be carried out at least
  once every six years in respect of the Board or any of its
  subsidiaries to determine if the systems and practices referred to in
  paragraph 39(1)(b) were, in the period under examination, maintained
  in a manner that provided reasonable assurance that they met the
  requirements of paragraphs 39(2)(a) and (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation required

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before causing a special examination to be carried
  out, the Minister shall consult with the appropriate provincial
  Ministers of the participating provinces.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Plan

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before an examiner commences a special examination,
  the examiner shall survey the systems and practices of the Board or of
  the subsidiary being examined, as the case may be, and submit a plan
  to the audit committee for the examination, including a statement of
  the criteria to be applied in the examination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resolution of disagreements

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Disagreements, if any, between the examiner and the
  audit committee with respect to a plan referred to in subsection (3)
  may be resolved by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reliance on internal audit

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An examiner shall rely on any internal audit
  conducted under subsection 39(3), to the extent that the examiner
  considers that reliance to be practicable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  examiner shall, on completion of the special examination, submit a
  report on the examiner's findings to the Minister and the appropriate
  provincial Ministers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report of an examiner shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating whether, in the examiner's
    opinion, with respect to the criteria established under subsection
    47(3), there is reasonable assurance that there are no significant
    deficiencies in the systems and practices examined; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the extent to which the examiner
    relied on internal audits.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Who conducts examination

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a special
  examination referred to in section 47 shall be carried out by the
  Board's auditor or, where the Minister has consulted the board of
  directors and is of the opinion that it is necessary, by an auditor
  appointed by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sections 43 to 45 apply

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 43 to 45 apply in respect of an examiner as
  though the references in that section to an auditor were references to
  an examiner.

  second and third quarters of the financial year, prepared in
  accordance with subsection 39(6), to the Minister and the appropriate
  provincial Ministers within 45 days after the end of the three-month
  period to which they relate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statements to be made public

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within seven days after the financial statements are
  sent as required under subsection (1), the Board shall make the
  statements available to the public.

### [Annual Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165994 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report required

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall as soon as possible, but in any case within 60 days, after
  the end of each financial year provide the Minister and the
  appropriate provincial Ministers with an annual report on the
  operations of the Board in that year and the Board shall make copies
  of the report available to the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling in Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} After receiving the annual report, the Minister shall
  cause it to be laid before each House of Parliament on any of the next
  15 days during which that House is sitting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The annual report shall contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the financial statements for the previous year
    prepared as required under section 39;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Board's auditor's report for that year prepared
    as required under section 40;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a certificate, signed by a director on behalf of
    the board of directors, stating that the investments of the Board
    held during that year were in accordance with this Act and the
    Board's investment policies, standards and procedures;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the Board's objectives for that year
    and a statement on the extent to which the Board met those
    objectives;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the Board's objectives for the next
    year and for the foreseeable future;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the Board's investment policies,
    standards and procedures; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} such other information as is required by the
    regulations.

## [Public Meetings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-166011 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public meetings

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall hold a public meeting once every two years in each
  participating province to discuss the Board's most recent annual
  report and to give interested persons an opportunity to comment on it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of meeting

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall publish a notice of each meeting at
  least 10 days before the date of the meeting in at least one newspaper
  of general circulation in the province where the meeting will take
  place. The notice shall indicate the date, time and place of the
  meeting and where copies of the Board's most recent annual report can
  be obtained.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors and officers to attend

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall require one or more directors or
  officers of the Board to be at the meeting to answer questions from
  the public and shall have copies of the Board's most recent annual
  report available for distribution at the meeting.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-166019 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} specifying which provisions of the [Pension
    Benefits Standards Act, 1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01) and any regulations
    made under that Act apply to the Board and its subsidiaries and
    adapting those provisions in the manner that the Governor in Council
    considers appropriate for the purpose of applying them to the Board
    and its subsidiaries;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the investments the Board and its
    subsidiaries may make; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that this Act provides is to
    be prescribed or is to be determined by regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under subsection (1) has no force
  or effect until the appropriate provincial Minister of each of at
  least two thirds of the participating provinces having in total not
  less than two thirds of the population of all of the participating
  provinces has approved the regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of proposed regulation

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (2), the approval of
  a proposed regulation published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) is deemed to be the approval of
  the regulation if the regulation is the same or substantially the same
  as the proposed regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of population

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), the population of
  a province at any time in a year in respect of which the determination
  of the province's population is relevant means the province's
  population on June 1 of that year, as estimated by the Chief
  Statistician of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of coming into force date

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the approvals necessary to give force and effect
  to a regulation are given only after the regulation is made, the
  Minister shall, as soon as feasible, cause to be published in the
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) the date that the
  regulation came into force.

## [Offence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-166036 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False statements

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  director, officer, employee, agent or auditor of the Board or of any
  of its subsidiaries who, in carrying out a duty under this Act or the
  by-laws, prepares, signs, approves or concurs in any statement, report
  or other document respecting the affairs of the Board or the
  subsidiary that contains any false or deceptive information is guilty
  of an offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who commits an offence under
  subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a natural person, to a fine not
    exceeding \$100,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12
    months, or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, to a fine not exceeding
    \$500,000.

## [Winding-up]{.HTitleText1} {#h-166044 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insolvency and winding-up

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** No Act relating to the
insolvency or winding-up of any corporation applies to the Board and in
no case shall the affairs of the Board be wound up unless Parliament so
provides.
