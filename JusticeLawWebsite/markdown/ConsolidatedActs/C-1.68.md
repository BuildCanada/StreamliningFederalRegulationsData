## Canada Consumer Product Safety Act {#canada-consumer-product-safety-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2010, c. 21
:::

Assented to 2010-12-15

An Act respecting the safety of consumer products
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the Parliament of Canada recognizes the objective of protecting
the public by addressing dangers to human health or safety that are
posed by consumer products;

Whereas the Parliament of Canada recognizes that the growing number of
consumer products that flow across the borders of an increasingly global
marketplace make the realization of that objective a challenge;

Whereas the Parliament of Canada recognizes that along with the
Government of Canada, individuals and suppliers of consumer products
have an important role to play in addressing dangers to human health or
safety that are posed by consumer products;

Whereas the Parliament of Canada wishes to foster cooperation within the
Government of Canada, between the governments in this country and with
foreign governments and international organizations, in particular by
sharing information, in order to effectively address those dangers;

Whereas the Parliament of Canada recognizes that, given the impact
activities with respect to consumer products may have on the
environment, there is a need to create a regulatory system regarding
consumer products that is complementary to the regulatory system
regarding the environment;

Whereas the Parliament of Canada recognizes that a lack of full
scientific certainty is not to be used as a reason for postponing
measures that prevent adverse effects on human health if those effects
could be serious or irreversible;

And whereas the Parliament of Canada recognizes that the application of
effective measures to encourage compliance with the federal regulatory
system for consumer products is key to addressing the dangers to human
health or safety posed by those products;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-44273 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada Consumer Product Safety Act](/eng/acts/C-1.68).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-44276 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[advertisement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[advertisement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a representation by
    any means for the purpose of promoting directly or indirectly the
    sale of a consumer product. ([publicité]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[analyst]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[analyst]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual designated as an
    analyst under section 29 or under section 28 of the [Food and Drugs
    Act](/eng/acts/F-27). ([analyste]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[article to which this Act or the regulations apply]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[article to which this Act or the regulations
    apply]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a consumer product;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} anything used in the manufacturing, importation,

[[confidential business information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[confidential business information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}  --- in
    respect of a person to whose business or affairs the information
    relates --- means business information

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is not publicly available;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which the person has taken measures
      that are reasonable in the circumstances to ensure that it remains
      not publicly available; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that has actual or potential economic value to
      the person or their competitors because it is not publicly
      available and its disclosure would result in a material financial
      loss to the person or a material financial gain to their
      competitors. ([renseignements commerciaux
      confidentiels]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[consumer product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[danger to human health or safety]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
    unreasonable hazard --- existing or potential --- that is posed by a
    consumer product during or as a result of its normal or foreseeable
    use and that may reasonably be expected to cause the death of an
    individual exposed to it or have an adverse effect on that
    individual's health --- including an injury --- whether or not the
    death or adverse effect occurs immediately after the exposure to the
    hazard, and includes any exposure to a consumer product that may
    reasonably be expected to have a chronic adverse effect on human
    health. ([danger pour la santé ou la sécurité
    humaines]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means anything on which information

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an aboriginal government as defined in subsection
      13(3) of the [Access to Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1);

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a government of a foreign state or of a

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an international organization of
      states. ([administration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[import]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[import]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to import into
    Canada. ([importer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual designated as

[[manufacture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[manufacture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes produce, formulate,

     ([fabrication]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Health. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual or an
    organization as defined in section 2 of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[personal information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[personal information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as

    personnels]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by
    regulation. ([Version anglaise seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[review officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[review officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual

    34. ([réviseur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes offer for sale, expose for
    sale or have in possession for sale --- or distribute to one or more
    persons, whether or not the distribution is made for consideration
    --- and includes lease, offer for lease, expose for lease or have in
    possession for lease. ([vente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[storing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[storing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include the storing of a

    use. ([entreposage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-44309 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to protect the public by addressing or preventing dangers to human
health or safety that are posed by consumer products in Canada,
including those that circulate within Canada and those that are
imported.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-44312 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consumer products

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This Act
  applies to consumer products with the exception of those listed in
  Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tobacco products

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Act does not apply to a [[tobacco
  product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 2 of the
  [Tobacco and Vaping Products Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5), except in respect
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ignition propensity of that product; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the devices and parts referred to in that
    definition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty --- devices and
  parts made of tobacco

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the devices and parts
  referred to in paragraph (2)(b) do not include those made in whole or
  in part of tobacco.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Natural health products

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, this Act does not apply to
  natural health products as defined in subsection 1(1) of the [Natural
  Health Products Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196) made under
  the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27).

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-44328 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consumer products in Schedule 2

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall
manufacture, import, advertise or sell a consumer product listed in
Schedule 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Products that do not meet regulatory
requirements

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No person shall
manufacture, import, advertise or sell a consumer product that does not
meet the requirements set out in the regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manufacturer and importer

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No manufacturer or importer
shall manufacture, import, advertise or sell a consumer product that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a danger to human health or safety;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is the subject of a recall order made under section
  31 or such an order that is reviewed under section 35 or is the
  subject of a voluntary recall in Canada because the product is a
  danger to human health or safety; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is the subject of a measure that the manufacturer or
  importer has not carried out but is required to carry out under an
  order made under section 32 or such an order that is reviewed under
  section 35.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advertising and selling

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No person shall advertise
or sell a consumer product that they know

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a danger to human health or safety;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is the subject of a recall order made under section
  31 or such an order that is reviewed under section 35 or is the
  subject of a voluntary recall in Canada because the product is a
  danger to human health or safety; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is the subject of a measure that has not been carried
  out but is required to be carried out under an order made under
  section 32 or such an order that is reviewed under section 35.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Misleading claims --- package or label

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No person shall package or
label a consumer product

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a manner --- including one that is false,
  misleading or deceptive --- that may reasonably be expected to create
  an erroneous impression regarding the fact that it is not a danger to
  human health or safety; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a manner that is false, misleading or deceptive
  regarding its certification related to its safety or its compliance
  with a safety standard or the regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Misleading claims --- advertise or sell

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** No person shall advertise
or sell a consumer product that they know is advertised, packaged or
labelled in a manner referred to in section 9.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False or misleading information

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** No person shall knowingly
provide the Minister with false or misleading information in relation to
a matter under this Act or the regulations.

## [Tests, Studies and Compilation of Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-44351 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tests, studies and information

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, by
written notice, order any person who manufactures or imports a consumer
product for commercial purposes to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} conduct tests or studies on the product in order to
  obtain the information that the Minister considers necessary to verify
  compliance or prevent non-compliance with this Act or the regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} compile any information that the Minister considers
  necessary to verify compliance or prevent non-compliance with this Act
  or the regulations; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide him or her with the documents that contain
  that information and the results of the tests or studies in the time
  and manner that the Minister specifies.

## [Preparing and Maintaining Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-44357 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who manufactures, imports, advertises, sells or tests a
  consumer product for commercial purposes shall prepare and maintain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} documents that indicate

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a retailer, the name and address
      of the person from whom they obtained the product and the location
      where and the period during which they sold the product, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other person, the name and
      address of the person from whom they obtained the product or to
      whom they sold it, or both, as applicable; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed documents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period for keeping documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person shall keep the documents until the expiry
  of six years after the end of the year to which they relate or for any
  other period that may be prescribed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Keeping and providing documents in
  Canada

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person shall keep the documents at their place of
  business in Canada or at any prescribed place and shall, on written
  request, provide the Minister with them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- outside Canada

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, subject to any terms and conditions
  that he or she may specify, exempt a person from the requirement to
  keep documents in Canada if the Minister considers it unnecessary or
  impractical for the person to keep them in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Importation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person who imports a consumer product for
  commercial purposes shall, no later than at the time of the product's
  importation, provide the Minister with those documents referred to in
  paragraph (1)(b) that are specified in the regulations.

## [Duties in the Event of an Incident]{.HTitleText1} {#h-44373 .Part}

Definition of [[incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, with respect to
  a consumer product,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an occurrence in Canada or elsewhere that resulted
    or may reasonably have been expected to result in an individual's
    death or in serious adverse effects on their health, including a
    serious injury;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a defect or characteristic that may reasonably be
    expected to result in an individual's death or in serious adverse
    effects on their health, including a serious injury;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} incorrect or insufficient information on a label or
    in instructions --- or the lack of a label or instructions --- that
    may reasonably be expected to result in an individual's death or in
    serious adverse effects on their health, including a serious injury;
    or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a recall or measure that is initiated for human
    health or safety reasons by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a foreign entity,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a provincial government,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a public body that is established under an Act
      of the legislature of a province,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an aboriginal government as defined in
      subsection 13(3) of the [Access to Information
      Act](/eng/acts/A-1), or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} an institution of an entity referred to in
      subparagraphs (ii) to (iv).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to provide information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who manufactures, imports or sells a
  consumer product for commercial purposes shall provide the Minister
  and, if applicable, the person from whom they received the consumer
  product with all the information in their control regarding any
  incident related to the product within two days after the day on which
  they become aware of the incident.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The manufacturer of the consumer product, or if the
  manufacturer carries on business outside Canada, the importer, shall
  provide the Minister with a written report --- containing information
  about the incident, the product involved in the incident, any products
  that they manufacture or import, as the case may be, that to their
  knowledge could be involved in a similar incident and any measures
  they propose be taken with respect to those products --- within 10
  days after the day on which they become aware of the incident or
  within the period that the Minister specifies by written notice.

## [Disclosure of Information by the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-44390 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal information

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may disclose personal information to a person or a government
  that carries out functions relating to the protection of human health
  or safety without the consent of the individual to whom the personal
  information relates if the disclosure is necessary to identify or
  address a serious danger to human health or safety.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21) not
  affected

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, nothing in this section
  affects the provisions of the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidential business information ---
agreement

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may disclose
confidential business information to a person or a government that
carries out functions relating to the protection of human health or
safety or the environment --- in relation to a consumer product ---
without the consent of the person to whose business or affairs the
information relates and without notifying that person if the person to
whom or government to which the information may be disclosed agrees in
writing to maintain the confidentiality of the information and to use it
only for the purpose of carrying out those functions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidential business information ---
serious and imminent danger

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, without the consent of the person to whose business or
  affairs the information relates and without notifying that person
  beforehand, disclose confidential business information about a
  consumer product that is a serious and imminent danger to human health
  or safety or the environment, if the disclosure of the information is
  essential to address the danger.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of information ---
  notification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister discloses confidential business
  information under subsection (1), he or she shall, not later than the
  next business day following the disclosure, notify the person to whose
  business or affairs the information relates.

- Definition of [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means a day other than a Saturday or a holiday.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
the Minister may disclose to the public information about a danger to
human health or safety that a consumer product poses.

## [Inspectors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-44407 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Number of inspectors

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall decide on the number of inspectors sufficient for the
  purpose of the administration and enforcement of this Act and the
  regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the administration and
  enforcement of the provisions of this Act and the regulations, the
  Minister may designate individuals or classes of individuals as
  inspectors to exercise powers or perform duties or functions in
  relation to any matter referred to in the designation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate to be produced

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An inspector shall be given a certificate in a form
  established by the Minister attesting to the inspector's designation
  and, on entering a place under subsection 21(1), the inspector shall,
  on request, produce the certificate to the person in charge of that
  place.

## [Inspection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-44419 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to enter place

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 22(1), an
  inspector may, for the purpose of verifying compliance or preventing
  non-compliance with this Act or the regulations, at any reasonable
  time enter a place, including a conveyance, in which they have
  reasonable grounds to believe that a consumer product is manufactured,
  imported, packaged, stored, advertised, sold, labelled, tested or
  transported, or a document relating to the administration of this Act
  or the regulations is located.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The inspector may, for the purpose referred to in
  subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine or test anything --- and take samples free
    of charge of an article to which this Act or the regulations apply
    --- that is found in the place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} open a receptacle or package that is found in the
    place;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} examine a document that is found in the place, make
    a copy of it or take an extract from it;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} seize and detain for any time that may be necessary

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an article to which this Act or the regulations
      apply that is found in the place, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the conveyance;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} order the owner or person having possession, care
    or control of an article to which this Act or the regulations apply
    that is found in the place --- or of the conveyance --- to move it
    or, for any time that may be necessary, not to move it or to
    restrict its movement;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used a computer or other device
    that is at the place to examine a document that is contained in or
    available to a computer system or reproduce it or cause it to be
    reproduced in the form of a printout or other intelligible output
    and remove the output for examination or copying;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used copying equipment that is
    at the place and remove the copies for examination;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} take photographs and make recordings and sketches;
    and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} order the owner or person in charge of the place or
    a person who manufactures, imports, packages, stores, advertises,
    sells, labels, tests or transports a consumer product at the place
    to establish their identity to the inspector's satisfaction or to
    stop or start the activity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conveyance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of entering the conveyance, an
  inspector may order the owner or person having possession, care or
  control of the conveyance to stop it or move it to a place where the
  inspector can enter it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entering private property

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An inspector who is carrying out their functions and
  any person accompanying them may enter on or pass through or over
  private property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance and information to be given
  to inspector

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The owner or person in charge of the place and every
  person found in the place shall give an inspector who is carrying out
  their functions all reasonable assistance and provide them with any
  information that they may reasonably require.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant or consent required to enter
dwelling-house

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  place mentioned in subsection 21(1) is a dwelling-house, an inspector
  may not enter it without the consent of the occupant except under the
  authority of a warrant issued under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A justice of the peace may, on [ex parte]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} application, issue a warrant authorizing, subject to the
  conditions specified in the warrant, the person who is named in it to
  enter a dwelling-house if the justice of the peace is satisfied by
  information on oath that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dwelling-house is a place described in
    subsection 21(1);

## [Procedures Following Seizure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-44454 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interference

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Except with the authority
of an inspector, no person shall remove, alter or interfere in any way
with anything seized under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage of things seized

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** An inspector who seizes a
thing under this Act may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on notice to and at the expense of its owner or the
  person having possession, care or control of it at the time of its
  seizure, store it or move it to another place; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} order its owner or the person having possession, care
  or control of it at the time of its seizure to store it or move it to
  another place at their expense.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Release of things seized

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** An inspector who seizes a
thing under this Act shall release it if they are satisfied that the
provisions of this Act and the regulations with respect to it have been
complied with.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture --- thing unclaimed

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  seized thing is, at Her election, forfeited to Her Majesty in right of
  Canada if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 60 days after the seizure, no person is
    identified, in accordance with the regulations, if any, as its owner
    or as the person who is entitled to possess it; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the owner or person who is entitled to possess it,
    within 60 days after the day on which they are notified that the
    inspector has released the seized thing, does not claim it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings instituted

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if proceedings are
  instituted in respect of an offence that relates to the thing that was
  seized.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A seized thing that is forfeited may be disposed of
  at the expense of its owner or the person who was entitled to possess
  it at the time of its seizure.

## [Removal or Forfeiture of Unlawful Imports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-44481 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unlawful imports

- **[[28.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector who has reasonable grounds to believe that an imported
  consumer product does not meet the requirements of the regulations or
  was imported in contravention of a provision of this Act or the
  regulations may decide whether to give the owner or importer, or the
  person having possession, care or control of the product, the
  opportunity to take a measure in respect of it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In making a decision under subsection (1), the
  inspector shall consider, among other factors

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the consumer product is a danger to human
    health or safety; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other prescribed factors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of inspector

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the inspector decides under subsection (1) not to
  give the owner, importer or the person having possession, care or
  control of the consumer product the opportunity to take a measure in
  respect of it, the inspector shall exercise, in respect of the
  product, any of the powers conferred by the provisions of this Act,
  other than this section, or of the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures that may be taken and notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} However, if the inspector decides under
  subsection (1) to give the owner, importer or person having
  possession, care or control of the consumer product the opportunity to
  take a measure in respect of it, the inspector, or any other inspector
  who is informed of the decision, shall decide whether the owner,
  importer or person having possession, care or control of it may remove
  it from Canada at their expense, consent to its forfeiture or take
  either of these measures, and shall notify or cause to be notified the
  owner, importer or person having possession, care or control of the
  product that they may take that measure within the period specified by
  the inspector or other inspector, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a person is notified under subsection (4) that
  they may consent to the forfeiture of the consumer product and the
  person consents to its forfeiture, the product is forfeited to Her
  Majesty in right of Canada and may be disposed of, as the Minister may
  direct, at the person's expense.

## [Orders for Recalls and Taking Measures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-44505 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recall

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister believes on reasonable grounds that a consumer product is a
  danger to human health or safety, he or she may order a person who
  manufactures, imports or sells the product for commercial purposes to
  recall it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The order shall be provided in the form of a written
  notice and must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the reasons for the recall; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the time and manner in which the recall is to be
    carried out.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taking measures

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may order a person who manufactures, imports, advertises or
  sells a consumer product to take any measure referred to in
  subsection (2) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that person does not comply with an order made
    under section 12 with respect to the product;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has made an order under section 31
    with respect to the product;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister believes on reasonable grounds that
    the product is the subject of a measure or recall undertaken
    voluntarily by the manufacturer or importer; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Minister believes on reasonable grounds that
    there is a contravention of this Act or the regulations in relation
    to the product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The measures include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} stopping the manufacturing, importation, packaging,
    storing, advertising, selling, labelling, testing or transportation
    of the consumer product or causing any of those activities to be
    stopped; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any measure that the Minister considers necessary
    to remedy a non-compliance with this Act or the regulations,
    including any measure that relates to the product that the Minister
    considers necessary in order for the product to meet the
    requirements of the regulations or to address or prevent a danger to
    human health or safety that the product poses.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The order shall be provided in the form of a written
  notice and must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the reasons for the measure; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the time and manner in which the measure is to be
    carried out.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recall or measures taken by Minister

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** If a person does not
comply with an order made under section 31 or 32 within the time
specified, the Minister may, on his or her own initiative and at that
person's expense, carry out the recall or measure required.

## [Review of Orders for Recalls and Taking Measures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-44530 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review officer

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
designate any individual or class of individuals that are qualified as
review officers for the purpose of reviewing orders under section 35.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for review

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any other provision of
  this section, an order that is made under section 31 or 32 shall be
  reviewed on the written request of the person who was ordered to
  recall a consumer product or to take another measure --- but only on
  grounds that involve questions of fact alone or questions of mixed law
  and fact --- by a review officer other than the individual who made
  the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of and time for making request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The written request must state the grounds for review
  and set out the evidence --- including evidence that was not
  considered by the individual who made the order --- that supports
  those grounds and the decision that is sought. It shall be provided to
  the Minister within seven days after the day on which the order was
  provided or, in the event of a serious and imminent danger to human
  health or safety, any shorter period that may be specified in the
  order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No authority to review

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The review is not to be done if the request does not
  comply with subsection (2) or is frivolous, vexatious or not made in
  good faith.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons for refusal

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The person who made the request shall, without delay,
  be notified in writing of the reasons for not doing the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review initiated by review officer

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A review officer --- other than the individual who
  made the order --- may review an order, whether or not a request is
  made under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order in effect

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} An order continues to apply during a review unless
  the review officer decides otherwise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Completion of review

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A review officer shall complete the review no later
  than 30 days after the day on which the request is provided to the
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of period for review

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The review officer may extend the review period by no
  more than 30 days if they are of the opinion that more time is
  required to complete the review. They may extend the review period
  more than once.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons for extension

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} If the review period is extended, the person who made
  the request shall, without delay, be notified in writing of the
  reasons for extending it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision on completion of review

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} On completion of a review, the review officer shall
  confirm, amend, terminate or cancel the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} The person who made the request or, if there is no
  request, the person who was ordered to recall the consumer product or
  to take another measure shall, without delay, be notified in writing
  of the reasons for the review officer's decision under subsection
  (10).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of amendment

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} An order that is amended is subject to review under
  this section.

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who contravenes a provision of this Act, other than section 8,
  10, 11 or 20, a provision of the regulations or an order made under
  this Act is guilty of an offence and is liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine of not more
    than \$5,000,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than two
    years or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, for a first offence, to a
    fine of not more than \$250,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not
    more than six months or to both and, for a subsequent offence, to a
    fine of not more than \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not
    more than 18 months or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence of due diligence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Due diligence is a defence in a prosecution for an
  offence under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence --- fault

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who contravenes section 8, 10, 11 or 20 or
  who knowingly or recklessly contravenes another provision of this Act,
  a provision of the regulations or an order made under this Act is
  guilty of an offence and is liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine in an amount
    that is at the discretion of the court or to imprisonment for a term
    of not more than five years or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, for a first offence, to a
    fine of not more than \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not
    more than 18 months or to both and, for a subsequent offence, to a
    fine of not more than \$1,000,000 or to imprisonment for a term of
    not more than two years or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sentencing considerations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A court that imposes a sentence shall take into
  account, in addition to any other principles that it is required to
  consider, the harm or risk of harm caused by the commission of the
  offence and the vulnerability of individuals who use the consumer
  product.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences by corporate officers, etc.

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** If a person other than an
individual commits an offence under this Act, any of the person's
directors, officers, agents or mandataries who directed, authorized,
assented to, acquiesced in or participated in the commission of the
offence is a party to the offence and is liable on conviction to the
punishment provided for by this Act, even if the person is not
prosecuted for the offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences by employees, agents or
mandataries

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** In a prosecution for an
offence under this Act, it is sufficient proof of the offence to
establish that it was committed by any employee, agent or mandatary of
the accused, even if the employee, agent or mandatary is not identified
or is not prosecuted for the offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offence

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** If an offence under this
Act is committed or continued on more than one day, it constitutes a
separate offence for each day on which it is committed or continued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Venue

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** A prosecution for an
offence under this Act may be instituted, heard and determined

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the place where the offence was committed or the
  subject-matter of the prosecution arose;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the accused was apprehended; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the accused happens to be or is carrying on
  business.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** Proceedings by way of
summary conviction in respect of an offence under this Act may be
instituted at any time within two years after the time the Minister
becomes aware of the acts or omissions that constitute the alleged
offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Admissibility of evidence

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  proceedings for an offence under this Act, a declaration, certificate,
  report or other document of the Minister or an inspector, analyst or
  review officer purporting to have been signed by that person is
  admissible in evidence without proof of the signature or official
  character of the person appearing to have signed it and, in the
  absence of evidence to the contrary, is proof of the matters asserted
  in it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies and extracts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In proceedings for an offence under this Act, a copy
  of or an extract from any document that is made by the Minister or an
  inspector, analyst or review officer that appears to have been
  certified under the signature of that person as a true copy or extract
  is admissible in evidence without proof of the signature or official
  character of the person appearing to have signed it and, in the
  absence of evidence to the contrary, has the same probative force as
  the original would have if it were proved in the ordinary way.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumed date of issue

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A document referred to in this section is, in the
  absence of evidence to the contrary, presumed to have been issued on
  the date that it bears.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No document referred to in this section may be
  received in evidence unless the party intending to produce it has
  provided reasonable notice of that intention on the party against whom
  it is intended to be produced together with a duplicate of the
  document.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Self-incrimination

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** The information and
results contained in the documents that a person provides under an order
made under section 12 may not be used or received to incriminate the
person in any proceeding against them in respect of an offence under
this Act.

## [Administrative Monetary Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-44707 .Part}

### [Violation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-44708 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission of violation

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes an order that is made under section 31 or 32 or reviewed
under section 35 commits a violation and is liable to the penalty
established in accordance with the regulations.

### [Powers of the Governor in Council and Minister]{.HTitleText2} {#h-44711 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fixing a penalty, or a range of penalties, in
    respect of each violation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} classifying each violation as a minor violation, a
    serious violation or a very serious violation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the circumstances under which, the
    criteria by which and the manner in which a penalty may be increased
    or reduced, including a reduction in the amount that is provided for
    in a compliance agreement; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the determination of a lesser amount
    that may be paid as complete satisfaction of a penalty if paid in
    the prescribed time and manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum penalties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The maximum penalty for a violation is \$5,000 in the
  case of a violation committed by a non-profit organization --- or by
  any other person for non-commercial purposes --- and \$25,000 in any
  other case.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notices of violation

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} designate individuals, or classes of individuals, who
  are authorized to issue notices of violation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish, in respect of each violation, a short-form
  description to be used in notices of violation.

### [Proceedings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-44725 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of notice of violation

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person designated under paragraph 51(a) believes on reasonable grounds
  that a person has committed a violation, the designated person may
  issue, and shall provide the person with, a notice of violation that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sets out the person's name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identifies the alleged violation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sets out the penalty for the violation that the
    person is liable to pay;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} sets out the particulars concerning the time and
    manner of payment; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} subject to the regulations, sets out a lesser
    amount that may be paid as complete satisfaction of the penalty if
    paid in the prescribed time and manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summary of rights

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of violation must clearly summarize, in
  plain language, the rights and obligations under this section and
  sections 53 to 66 of the person named in it, including the right to
  have the acts or omissions that constitute the alleged violation or
  the amount of the penalty reviewed and the procedure for requesting
  that review.

### [Penalties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-44736 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  person named in the notice pays, in the prescribed time and manner,
  the amount of the penalty or, subject to the regulations, the lesser
  amount set out in a notice of violation that may be paid in lieu of
  the penalty,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are deemed to have committed the violation in
    respect of which the amount is paid;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall accept that amount as complete
    satisfaction of the penalty; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proceedings commenced in respect of the
    violation under section 52 are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternatives to payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Instead of paying the penalty set out in a notice of
  violation or, if applicable, the lesser amount that may be paid in
  lieu of the penalty, the person named in the notice may, in the
  prescribed time and manner,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the penalty is \$5,000 or more, request to enter
    into a compliance agreement with the Minister that ensures the
    person's compliance with the order to which the violation relates;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} request a review by the Minister of the acts or
    omissions that constitute the alleged violation or the amount of the
    penalty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the person named in the notice of violation does
  not pay the penalty in the prescribed time and manner or, if
  applicable, the lesser amount that may be paid in lieu of the penalty,
  and does not exercise any right referred to in subsection (2) in the
  prescribed time and manner, they are deemed to have committed the
  violation identified in the notice.

### [Compliance Agreements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-44749 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance agreements

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  considering a request under paragraph 53(2)(a), the Minister may enter
  into a compliance agreement, as described in that paragraph, with the
  person making the request on any terms and conditions that are
  satisfactory to the Minister, which terms and conditions may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} include a provision for the giving of reasonable
    security, in a form and in an amount satisfactory to the Minister,
    as a guarantee that the person will comply with the compliance
    agreement; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide for the reduction, in whole or in part, of
    the penalty for the violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who enters into a compliance agreement with
  the Minister is, on doing so, deemed to have committed the violation
  in respect of which the compliance agreement was entered into.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If compliance agreement complied with

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is satisfied that a person who has
  entered into a compliance agreement has complied with it, the Minister
  shall cause a notice to that effect to be provided to the person, at
  which time

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proceedings commenced in respect of the
    violation under section 52 are ended; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any security given by the person under the
    compliance agreement shall be returned to the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If compliance agreement not complied
  with

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is of the opinion that a person who
  has entered into a compliance agreement has not complied with it, the
  Minister shall cause a notice of default to be provided to the person
  to the effect that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} instead of the penalty set out in the notice of
    violation in respect of which the compliance agreement was entered
    into, the person is liable to pay, in the prescribed time and
    manner, twice the amount of that penalty and, for greater certainty,
    subsection 50(2) does not apply in respect of that amount; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the security, if any, given by the person under the
    compliance agreement shall be forfeited to Her Majesty in right of
    Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of notice of default

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Once provided with the notice of default, the person
  may not deduct from the amount set out in the notice any amount they
  spent under the compliance agreement and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is liable to pay the amount set out in the notice;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the notice provides for the forfeiture of the
    security given under the compliance agreement, that security is
    forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada and the proceedings
    commenced in respect of the violation under section 52 are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of payment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a person pays the amount set out in a notice of
  default under subsection (4) in the prescribed time and manner,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall accept the amount as complete
    satisfaction of the amount owing; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proceedings commenced in respect of the
    violation under section 52 are ended.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to enter into compliance
agreement

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister refuses to enter into a compliance agreement pursuant to a
  request under paragraph 53(2)(a), the person who made the request is
  liable to pay the amount of the penalty in the prescribed time and
  manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person pays the amount referred to in subsection
  (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are deemed to have committed the violation in
    respect of which the payment is made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall accept the amount as complete
    satisfaction of the penalty; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proceedings commenced in respect of the
    violation under section 52 are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person does not pay the amount referred to in
  subsection (1) in the prescribed time and manner, they are deemed to
  have committed the violation identified in the notice of violation.

### [Review by the Minister]{.HTitleText2} {#h-44783 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review --- with respect to facts

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  completion of a review requested under paragraph 53(2)(b) with respect
  to the acts or omissions that constitute the alleged violation, the
  Minister shall determine whether the person requesting the review
  committed the violation. If the Minister determines that the person
  committed the violation but that the amount of the penalty was not
  established in accordance with the regulations, the Minister shall
  correct the amount and cause a notice of any decision under this
  subsection to be provided to the person who requested the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review --- with respect to penalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On completion of a review requested under paragraph
  53(2)(b), with respect to the amount of the penalty, if the Minister
  determines that the amount of the penalty was not established in
  accordance with the regulations, the Minister shall correct the amount
  and cause a notice of any decision under this subsection to be
  provided to the person who requested the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines under subsection (1) that
  a person committed a violation, the person is liable to pay the amount
  of the penalty confirmed or corrected in that decision in the
  prescribed time and manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of payment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a person pays the amount referred to in subsection
  (3),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall accept the amount as complete
    satisfaction of the penalty; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proceedings commenced in respect of the
    violation under section 52 are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written evidence and submissions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is to consider only written evidence and
  written submissions in determining whether a person committed a
  violation or whether a penalty was established in accordance with the
  regulations.

### [Enforcement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-44797 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debts to Her Majesty

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following amounts constitute debts due to Her Majesty in right of
  Canada that may be recovered as such in the Federal Court:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of a penalty, from the time the notice
    of violation setting out the penalty is provided;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} every amount set out in a compliance agreement
    entered into with the Minister under subsection 54(1), from the time
    the compliance agreement is entered into;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount set out in a notice of default referred
    to in subsection 54(4), from the time the notice is provided;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount of a penalty as set out in a decision of
    the Minister under subsection 56(1) or (2), from the time the notice
    under that subsection is provided; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any reasonable expenses incurred
    under section 64, from the date they are incurred.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No proceedings to recover a debt referred to in
  subsection (1) may be commenced later than five years after the debt
  became payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt final

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A debt referred to in subsection (1) is final and not
  subject to review or to be restrained, prohibited, removed, set aside
  or otherwise dealt with except to the extent and in the manner
  provided by sections 53 to 56.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of default

- **[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  debt referred to in subsection 57(1) in respect of which there is a
  default of payment, or the part of any such debt that has not been
  paid, may be certified by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judgments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On production to the Federal Court, the certificate
  shall be registered in that Court and, when registered, has the same
  force and effect, and all proceedings may be taken on the certificate,
  as if it were a judgment obtained in that Court for a debt of the
  amount specified in it and all reasonable costs and charges associated
  with the registration of the certificate.

### [Rules of Law About Violations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-44815 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain defences not available

- **[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person named in a notice of violation does not have a defence by
  reason that the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exercised due diligence to prevent the violation;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reasonably and honestly believed in the existence
    of facts that, if true, would exonerate the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Common law principles

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every rule and principle of the common law that
  renders any circumstance a justification or excuse in relation to a
  charge for an offence under this Act applies in respect of a violation
  to the extent that it is not inconsistent with this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Burden of proof

**[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** In every case where the
facts of a violation are reviewed by the Minister, he or she must
determine, on a balance of probabilities, whether the person named in
the notice of violation committed the violation identified in the
notice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violation by corporate officers, etc.

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** If a person other than an
individual commits a violation under this Act, any of the person's
directors, officers, agents or mandataries who directed, authorized,
assented to, acquiesced in or participated in the commission of the
violation is a party to and liable for the violation whether or not the
person who actually committed the violation is proceeded against in
accordance with this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vicarious liability --- acts of employees
and agents

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** A person is liable for a
violation that is committed by any employee, agent or mandatary of the
person acting in the course of the employee's employment or the scope of
the agent or mandatary's authority, whether or not the employee, agent
or mandatary who actually committed the violation is identified or
proceeded against in accordance with this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing violation

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** A violation that is
continued on more than one day constitutes a separate violation in
respect of each day on which it is continued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

**[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** Anything seized under
this Act that is the subject of a violation is, at Her election,
immediately forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada and may be
disposed of, at the expense of its owner or the person who was entitled
to possess it at the time of its seizure, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is deemed by this Act to have committed
  the violation; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister, on the basis of a review under this
  Act, has determined that the person has committed the violation.

### [Other Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-44835 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** In any proceeding for a
violation or for a prosecution for an offence, a notice of violation
purporting to be issued pursuant to this Act is admissible in evidence
without proof of the signature or official character of the person
appearing to have signed the notice of violation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

**[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** No proceedings in respect
of a violation may be commenced later than six months after the Minister
becomes aware of the acts or omissions that constitute the alleged
violation.

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-44840 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committee

- **[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall establish a committee to provide him or her with advice
  on matters in connection with the administration of this Act,
  including the labelling of consumer products.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advice available to public

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The committee shall make available to the public the
  advice it provided to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and travel and living
  expenses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Committee members may be paid remuneration up to the
  amount that is fixed by the Governor in Council and are entitled to
  the reasonable travel and living expenses that they incur while
  performing their duties away from their ordinary place of residence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
orders made under this Act, except under section 40, are not statutory
instruments within the meaning of the [Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How act or omission may be proceeded with

**[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** If an act or omission can
be proceeded with either as a violation or as an offence, proceeding in
one manner precludes proceeding in the other.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certification by Minister

**[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** A document appearing to
have been issued by the Minister, certifying the day on which the acts
or omissions that constitute the alleged offence or violation became
known to the Minister, is admissible in evidence without proof of the
signature or official character of the person appearing to have signed
the document and, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, is proof
that the Minister became aware of the acts or omissions on that day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of information about
contraventions

**[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may publish
information about any contravention of this Act or the regulations, or
any violation referred to in section 49, for the purpose of encouraging
compliance with this Act and the regulations.

      a warrant under the provision if the application is submitted, and

      - [(h)]{.lawlabel} subsection 22(4) of the [Canada Consumer

- --- 2022, c. 17, s. 78.1

  - ###### Impact of remote proceedings {#impact-of-remote-proceedings .MarginalNote}

    - [78.1]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Justice must,
      no later than three years after the day on which this Act receives
      royal assent, initiate one or more independent reviews on the use
      of remote proceedings in criminal justice matters that must
      include an assessment of whether remote proceedings

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enhance, preserve or adversely affect access to
        justice;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintain fundamental principles of the
        administration of justice; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} adequately address the rights and obligations
        of participants in the criminal justice system, including
        accused persons.

    - ###### Report {#report .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Justice must, no later than five
      years after the day on which a review is initiated, cause a report
      on the review --- including any findings or recommendations
      resulting from it --- to be laid before each House of Parliament.

- --- 2022, c. 17, s. 78.2

  - ###### Review by committee {#review-by-committee .MarginalNote}

    - [78.2]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the start of the fifth year
      after the day on which this Act receives royal assent, the
      provisions enacted or amended by this Act are to be referred to a
      committee of the Senate and a committee of the House of Commons
      that may be designated or established for the purpose of reviewing
      the provisions.

    - ###### Report {#report-1 .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The committees to which the provisions are
      referred are to review them and the use of remote proceedings in
      criminal justice matters and submit reports to the Houses of
      Parliament of which they are committees, including statements
      setting out any changes to the provisions that they recommend.
