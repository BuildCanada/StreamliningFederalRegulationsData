## Identification of Criminals Act {#identification-of-criminals-act .Title-of-Act}

## [Identification of Criminals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-257617 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fingerprints and photographs

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following persons may be fingerprinted or photographed or subjected to
  such other measurements, processes and operations having the object of
  identifying persons as are approved by order of the Governor in
  Council:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person who is in lawful custody charged with or
    convicted of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an indictable offence --- or an offence
      punishable on summary conviction if it is an offence that could
      also have been prosecuted by indictment --- other than an offence
      that is designated as a contravention under the [Contraventions
      Act](/eng/acts/C-38.7) in respect of which the Attorney General,
      within the meaning of that Act, has made an election under section
      50 of that Act, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an offence under the [Foreign Interference and
      Security of Information Act](/eng/acts/O-5);

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2022, c. 17, s. 62\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person who has been apprehended under the
    [Extradition Act](/eng/acts/E-23.01);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person who is required under subsection
    485.2(1), 500(3), 501(4) or 509(5) or section 515.01 of the
    [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) to appear for the purposes of this
    Act by an appearance notice, undertaking, summons or order because
    they are alleged to have committed an indictable offence --- or an
    offence punishable on summary conviction if it is an offence that
    could also have been prosecuted by indictment --- other than an
    offence that is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an offence that is designated as a contravention
      under the [Contraventions Act](/eng/acts/C-38.7) in respect of
      which the [[Attorney General]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, within the
      meaning of that Act, has made an election under section 50 of that
      Act, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an offence in respect of which proceedings were
      commenced by a peace officer under section 51 of the [Cannabis
      Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5); or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any person who is in lawful custody pursuant to
    section 83.3 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Such force may be used as is necessary to the
  effectual carrying out and application of the measurements, processes
  and operations described under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The results of the measurements, processes and
  operations to which a person has been subjected pursuant to
  subsection (1) may be published for the purpose of affording
  information to officers and others engaged in the execution or
  administration of the law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No liability for acting under Act

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No liability, civil or
criminal, for anything lawfully done under this Act shall be incurred by
any person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} having custody of a person described in subsection
  2(1);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} acting in the aid or under the direction of a person
  having such custody; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} concerned in the publication of results under
  subsection 2(3).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-1, s. 3
- 1992, c. 47, s. 75
:::

## [Destruction of Fingerprints and Photographs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-257642 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Destruction of fingerprints and
photographs

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where a person charged with
an offence that is designated as a contravention under the
[Contraventions Act](/eng/acts/C-38.7) is fingerprinted or photographed
and the Attorney General, within the meaning of that Act, makes an
election under section 50 of that Act, the fingerprints or photographs
shall be destroyed.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1992, c. 47, s. 76
- 1996, c. 7, s. 40
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Destruction of fingerprints and
photographs -- [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5)

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** If a person charged with an
offence referred to in any of paragraphs 51(2)(a) to (j) of the
[Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) is fingerprinted or photographed and
the Attorney General, within the meaning of that Act, makes an election
under section 58 of that Act, the fingerprints or photographs shall be
destroyed.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2018, c. 16, s. 167
:::

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2022, c. 17, s. 76

  - ###### Clarification --- immediate application {#clarification-immediate-application .MarginalNote}

    [76]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, but subject to sections 77
    and 78, the amendments made by this Act also apply with respect to
    proceedings that are ongoing on the day on which this Act comes into
    force.

- --- 2022, c. 17, s. 78.1

  - ###### Impact of remote proceedings {#impact-of-remote-proceedings .MarginalNote}

    - [78.1]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Justice must,
      no later than three years after the day on which this Act receives
      royal assent, initiate one or more independent reviews on the use
      of remote proceedings in criminal justice matters that must
      include an assessment of whether remote proceedings

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enhance, preserve or adversely affect access to
        justice;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintain fundamental principles of the
        administration of justice; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} adequately address the rights and obligations
        of participants in the criminal justice system, including
        accused persons.

    - ###### Report {#report .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Justice must, no later than five
      years after the day on which a review is initiated, cause a report
      on the review --- including any findings or recommendations
      resulting from it --- to be laid before each House of Parliament.

- --- 2022, c. 17, s. 78.2

  - ###### Review by committee {#review-by-committee .MarginalNote}

    - [78.2]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the start of the fifth year
      after the day on which this Act receives royal assent, the
      provisions enacted or amended by this Act are to be referred to a
      committee of the Senate and a committee of the House of Commons
      that may be designated or established for the purpose of reviewing
      the provisions.

    - ###### Report {#report-1 .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The committees to which the provisions are
      referred are to review them and the use of remote proceedings in
      criminal justice matters and submit reports to the Houses of
      Parliament of which they are committees, including statements
      setting out any changes to the provisions that they recommend.
