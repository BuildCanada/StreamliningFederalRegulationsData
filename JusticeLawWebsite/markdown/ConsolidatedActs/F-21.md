## Fisheries Development Act {#fisheries-development-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. F-21
:::

An Act to provide for the development of the commercial fisheries of
Canada
::::

::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233172 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Fisheries Development Act](/eng/acts/F-21).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-21, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233177 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[fishery products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fishery products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any fishery
    resources and any products derived from fishery
    resources; ([produits de la pêche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fishery resources]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fishery resources]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes fish, molluscs,
    crustaceans, marine mammals and marine plants; ([ressources de la
    pêche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Fisheries and
    Oceans. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-21, s. 2
- R.S., c. 14(2nd Supp.), s. 30
- 1978-79, c. 13, s. 33
:::

## [Fisheries Development Projects]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233185 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may undertake projects

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may undertake projects

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the more efficient exploitation of fishery
    resources and for the exploration for and development of new fishery
    resources and new fisheries;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the introduction and demonstration to fishermen
    of new types of fishing vessels and fishing equipment and of new
    fishing techniques; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the development of new fishery products and for
    the improvement of the handling, processing and distribution of
    fishery products.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Projects with a province

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may enter into an agreement with any
  province providing for the undertaking jointly with the government of
  the province or any agency thereof of any project that the Minister is
  authorized to undertake under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments to a province

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, with the approval of the Governor
  in Council, enter into an agreement with any province providing for
  the payment to the province of contributions in respect of the cost of
  any project that is undertaken by the government of the province or
  any agency thereof and that the Minister is authorized to undertake
  under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Projects with a person

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may enter into an agreement with any
  person for the joint undertaking of any project that the Minister is
  authorized to undertake under subsection (1) or for the payment to any
  person of contributions in respect of the cost of any such project
  undertaken by that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Economic studies

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of assisting the formulation and
  assessment of fisheries development projects, the Minister may
  undertake economic studies alone or jointly with the government of any
  province or agency thereof or with any university, educational
  institution or person, and may coordinate any such studies with
  similar studies undertaken in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-21, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Services, etc., of other departments

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall, in
carrying out any project or economic study under this Act, make use,
wherever possible, of the services and facilities of other departments
of the Government of Canada or of any agencies thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-21, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may make payments

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may make
payments to such persons or classes of persons, in such amounts and on
such terms and conditions as may be prescribed by the Governor in
Council,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the construction and equipment of commercial cold
  storages, or commercial bait freezing facilities equipped with
  mechanical refrigeration, that will be suitable for the preservation
  of fishery products;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the modification of commercial cold storages, or
  commercial bait freezing facilities equipped with mechanical
  refrigeration, to make the cold storages or bait freezing facilities
  suitable for the preservation of fishery products;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the construction, modification, conversion and
  equipment of fishing vessels; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for the construction and equipment of commercial
  ice-making and ice-storing facilities, or commercial fish-chilling
  facilities, that will contribute to improvements in productivity in
  commercial fishing operations.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-21, s. 5
- 1973-74, c. 25, s. 1
- 1974, c. 4, s. 1
:::

## [Terms and Conditions of Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233214 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions to be included in agreements

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every agreement entered
into pursuant to subsection 3(2) or (3) shall specify

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the respective proportions of the cost of any project
  to which the agreement relates that are to be paid by the Minister and
  the province, or the contribution in respect of any such project that
  is to be paid by the Minister, and the times at which the amounts to
  be paid by the Minister or the province are to be paid;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the authority that is to be responsible for the
  undertaking, operation and maintenance of any project or any part
  thereof to which the agreement relates;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the respective proportions of the revenues from any
  project to which the agreement relates that are to be paid to the
  Minister and the province; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the terms and conditions respecting the operation and
  maintenance of any project to which the agreement relates and the
  charges, if any, to be charged to persons to whom any of the benefits
  of the project are made available.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-21, s. 6
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233223 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advisory committees

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, in order to carry out the purposes and provisions of
  this Act, establish such advisory committees as he deems necessary and
  appoint the members thereof.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and expenses of members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each member of a committee established under
  subsection (1) is entitled to be paid such amount for each day he
  attends any meeting of the committee as may be fixed by the Governor
  in Council and is entitled to be paid reasonable travel and living
  expenses while absent from his ordinary place of residence in the
  course of his duties under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-21, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity of agreements

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No agreement entered into
pursuant to subsection 3(2), (3) or (4) providing for the payment of any
money by the Minister has any force or effect until such time as money
has been appropriated by Parliament for the purpose of discharging any
commitment under that agreement.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-21, s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing, for the purposes of section 5, the
  persons to whom payments may be made, the maximum amount of each
  payment and the terms and conditions on which the payments may be
  made;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing matters, including matters similar to
  those required to be specified in an agreement under subsection 3(2)
  or (3), to be provided for in agreements entered into pursuant to
  subsection 3(4); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to any other matter concerning which he
  deems regulations necessary or desirable to carry out the purposes and
  provisions of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-21, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall, as
soon as possible after the termination of each fiscal year, submit a
report to Parliament respecting the operations for that year under this
Act and under agreements made under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-21, s. 10
:::
