## Pesticide Residue Compensation Act {#pesticide-residue-compensation-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. P-10
:::

An Act to provide compensation to farmers whose agricultural products
are contaminated by pesticide residue
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-391423 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Pesticide Residue Compensation Act](/eng/acts/P-10).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-10, s. 1
- 2001, c. 4, s. 113(F)
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-391428 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[farmer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[farmer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a producer of primary
    agricultural products for sale; ([agriculteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated as an
    inspector pursuant to section 6; ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:    [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Health; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pesticide residue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pesticide residue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the residue of any
    pesticide or degradation product thereof. ([résidus de
    pesticide]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-10, s. 2
- 1994, c. 38, s. 25
- 2002, c. 28, s. 87
:::

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Pesticide Residue Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-391437 .Part}

### [Compensation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-391438 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for compensation

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this Act, the Minister may pay to a farmer, on such terms and
  conditions as are prescribed by the regulations, compensation for any
  loss suffered by the farmer as a result of the presence of pesticide
  residue in or on an agricultural product of that farmer, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an inspection of that product made under the [Food
    and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27) has disclosed the presence of
    pesticide residue in or on the product and, as a result, the sale of
    the product would be contrary to that Act or the regulations made
    under it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the pesticide used is a pest control product
    registered under the [Pest Control Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01)
    or is deemed by any other Act of Parliament to be registered under
    that Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the opinion of the Minister, the pesticide was
    used in accordance with recommendations made by the department of
    agriculture of any province and approved by the Minister, or in
    accordance with practices recommended, directed or concurred in by
    the Minister; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is satisfied that the presence of the
    pesticide residue in or on the product is not due to any fault of
    the farmer, the farmer's employee, agent or mandatary or of a
    previous owner of the land on which the product was grown, or that
    previous owner's employee, agent or mandatary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amount of compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The compensation that may be paid to a farmer
  pursuant to subsection (1) in respect of any loss shall not exceed the
  maximum prescribed by the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum loss eligible for compensation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No compensation shall be paid to a farmer pursuant to
  subsection (1) in respect of a loss that is less than the minimum
  amount of loss prescribed by the regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-10, s. 3
- 1996, c. 8, s. 32
- 2001, c. 4, s. 115
- 2002, c. 28, s. 88
:::

Version](section-3-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the procedures to be followed in claiming
  compensation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the maximum amount of compensation that
  may be paid to a farmer in respect of any loss;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the methods to be used in determining the
  eligibility of a farmer for compensation, the amount of loss suffered
  by a farmer and the amount of compensation to be paid in respect of
  any loss;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the minimum amount of loss in respect of
  which compensation may be paid under this Act;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the terms and conditions for the payment
  of compensation under this Act;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} excluding any agricultural product or any person or
  any class of products or persons from the operation of this Act, and
  prescribing the conditions of exclusion;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the courses of action that may be
  undertaken or the amounts that may be paid by the Minister to reduce
  any loss occasioned by pesticide residue; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} generally, for carrying out the purposes and
  provisions of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-11, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action by farmer

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  payment of compensation shall be made to a farmer under this Act in
  respect of a loss suffered by the farmer by reason of pesticide
  residue in or on an agricultural product until the farmer has taken
  any steps that the Minister considers necessary

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to reduce the loss suffered by the farmer by reason
    of that pesticide residue; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to pursue any legal action that the farmer may have
    against

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer of the pesticide causing the
      residue in or on the product, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} every person responsible for the presence of the
      pesticide residue in or on the product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal action by Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where he deems it necessary, the Minister may
  require, as a condition for the payment of any compensation to a
  farmer under this Act, the consent of that farmer for the Minister to
  pursue on his behalf any legal action against any manufacturer or
  person referred to in paragraph (1)(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of compensation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, in paying compensation, take into
  account any amounts realized by the farmer as a result of any steps
  taken pursuant to subsection (1) or as a result of any legal action
  taken on behalf of the farmer pursuant to subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification to Minister

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a farmer realizes any amount from the
  disposition or use of any product or property in respect of which
  compensation may be or has been paid pursuant to this Act, he shall
  forthwith notify the Minister of the amounts so received and, if he
  has been paid any compensation by the Minister, shall repay to the
  Minister such compensation payment or part thereof as the Minister may
  direct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of overpayment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where, for any reason, a farmer has been paid any
  compensation under this Act to which he is not entitled or
  compensation in an amount in excess of the compensation to which he is
  entitled, the amount thereof or the excess amount, as the case may be,
  may be recovered at any time as a debt due to Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action by Minister to reduce loss

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subject to regulations made under paragraph 4(g), the
  Minister may, with respect to any loss in respect of which
  compensation may be paid under this Act, undertake such course of
  action or pay such amount as he deems necessary to reduce that loss.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation not to affect other legal
  rights

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Except as provided by this Act, no compensation paid
  under this Act affects the right of an aggrieved person to any legal
  remedy to which that person may be entitled.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-10, s. 5
- 2001, c. 4, s. 116(E)
- 2004, c. 25, s. 167(F)
:::

### [Enforcement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-391485 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspectors

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may designate
any qualified person as an inspector for the purposes of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-11, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of inspectors

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (1.1), an inspector may at any reasonable time enter any
  place or premises in which the inspector believes on reasonable
  grounds there is any agricultural product, pesticide or thing that
  will enable the inspector to carry out any investigation that may be
  required by the Minister for the purposes of paragraph 3(1)(d), and
  may open any container or package found therein that the inspector has
  reason to believe will assist in the investigation, and take samples
  thereof.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant required to enter
  dwelling-house

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Where any place or premises referred to in
  subsection (1) is a dwelling-house, an inspector may not enter that
  dwelling-house without the consent of the occupant except under the
  authority of a warrant issued under subsection (1.2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} Where on [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  application a justice of the peace is satisfied by information on oath

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the conditions for entry described in
    subsection (1) exist in relation to a dwelling-house,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that entry to the dwelling-house is necessary for
    any purpose relating to the administration or enforcement of this
    Act, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that entry to the dwelling-house has been refused
    or that there are reasonable grounds for believing that entry
    thereto will be refused,

  the justice of the peace may issue a warrant under his hand
  authorizing the inspector named therein to enter that dwelling-house
  subject to such conditions as may be specified in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

  [(1.3)]{.lawlabel} In executing a warrant issued under subsection
  (1.2), the inspector named therein shall not use force unless the
  inspector is accompanied by a peace officer and the use of force has
  been specifically authorized in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspection and copying of documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An inspector may require any person in a place or
  premises entered pursuant to subsection (1) to produce for inspection
  or for the purpose of obtaining copies or extracts, any books,
  shipping bills, bills of lading, documents containing mixing
  instructions, or other documents or papers concerning any matter
  relevant to the administration of this Act or the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate to be produced

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall furnish every inspector with a
  certificate of his designation as an inspector and on entering any
  place or premises referred to in subsection (1) an inspector shall, if
  so required, produce the certificate to the person in charge thereof.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to inspectors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The owner or person in charge of any place or
  premises referred to in subsection (1) and every person found therein
  shall give an inspector all reasonable assistance to enable the
  inspector to carry out his duties and functions under this Act and the
  regulations and shall furnish the inspector with any information he
  may reasonably require with respect to the administration of this Act
  and the regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-10, s. 7
- R.S., 1985, c. 31 (1st Supp.), s. 17
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction of inspectors

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No person shall obstruct or
hinder an inspector in the carrying out of his duties or functions under
this Act or the regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-11, s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False statements

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No person shall knowingly
make a false or misleading statement either orally or in writing in any
claim or statement required or permitted by this Act or make any such
claim or statement that by reason of any non-disclosure of facts is
false or misleading.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-11, s. 8
:::

### [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-391519 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of Act

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every person who, or
whose employee, or whose agent or mandatary, contravenes any provision
of this Act is guilty of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an indictable offence and liable to imprisonment for
  a term not exceeding two years; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an offence punishable on summary conviction.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-10, s. 10
- 2004, c. 25, s. 168(E)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-10-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 10"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence by employee, or agent or
mandatary

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** In any prosecution for an
offence under this Act, it is sufficient proof of the offence to
establish that it was committed by an employee, or an agent or a
mandatary, of the accused, whether or not the employee, or agent or
mandatary, is identified or has been prosecuted for the offence, unless
the accused establishes that the offence was committed without the
accused's knowledge or consent and that the accused exercised all due
diligence to prevent its commission.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-10, s. 11
- 2004, c. 25, s. 169(E)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-11-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 11"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** No proceedings by way of
summary conviction in respect of an offence under this Act shall be
instituted more than one year after the time when the subject-matter of
the proceedings arose.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-11, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Venue

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A complaint or
information in respect of an offence under this Act may be heard, tried
or determined by a provincial court judge or a justice if the accused is
resident or carrying on business within the territorial jurisdiction of
the provincial court judge or justice, although the subject-matter of
the complaint or information did not arise in that territorial
jurisdiction.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-10, s. 13
- R.S., 1985, c. 27 (1st Supp.), s. 203
:::

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Compensation Appeals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-391538 .Part}

### [Assessor]{.HTitleText2} {#h-391539 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessor and Deputy Assessors

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, from among the judges of the Federal Court
  and the judges of the superior, district or county courts of the
  provinces, appoint an Assessor and the number of Deputy Assessors that
  the Governor in Council considers necessary to hear and determine
  appeals from compensation awards made under this Act or under any
  other Act to which this Part is made applicable and, subject to this
  Act, may prescribe their jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting assessor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may appoint from among the
  judges referred to in subsection (1) an acting assessor, who shall act
  in the place of the Assessor in the event of his absence or
  incapacity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy Assessor

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Assessor may designate a Deputy Assessor to hear
  and determine any appeal under this Part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In sections 16 and 17, the term "Assessor" includes a
  Deputy Assessor.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-10, s. 14
- 1990, c. 8, s. 60
- 2006, c. 11, s. 25
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-14-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 14"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeals

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appeal may be brought to the Assessor by a farmer referred to in
  subsection 3(1) where the compensation awarded to the farmer is less
  than the maximum compensation prescribed under this Act or where no
  compensation is awarded to the farmer, on the ground that the amount
  of compensation awarded or the failure to award compensation was
  unreasonable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for bringing appeals

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An appeal to the Assessor shall be brought within
  three months from the date the farmer receives notification of the
  decision or the compensation that is the subject of the appeal or
  within such longer period as the Assessor may in any case for special
  reasons allow.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-11, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Assessor

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  hearing of an appeal brought pursuant to this Part, the Assessor may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} confirm the decision of the Minister;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to any maximum amount of compensation
    prescribed pursuant to this Act and applicable to the loss in
    respect of which the appeal is brought, vary the decision of the
    Minister; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} refer the matter back to the Minister for such
    further action as the Assessor may direct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In any proceedings under this Part costs may be
  awarded to or against the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decisions final

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The decision of the Assessor on any appeal brought
  pursuant to this Part is final and conclusive and not subject to
  appeal to or review by any court.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-11, s. 13
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sittings and hearings

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Assessor may sit and hear appeals at any place or places, and shall
  arrange for sittings and hearings as may be required.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Assessor is entitled to be paid travel allowances
  in accordance with section 34 of the [Judges Act](/eng/acts/J-1).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-10, s. 17
- 1990, c. 8, s. 61
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make such rules respecting the conduct of appeals and the procedure
for the bringing of appeals as the Governor in Council deems necessary
to enable the Assessor to discharge the Assessor's duties under this
Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-10, s. 18
- 1990, c. 8, s. 62
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registrar

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may appoint a registrar of appeals and such other persons as the
Governor in Council considers necessary to carry out the purposes of
this Part.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-11, s. 16
:::
