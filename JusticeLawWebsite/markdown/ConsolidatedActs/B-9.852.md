## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[General --- Payments to Certain Entities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-36304 .Part}

### [Payments to a Trust]{.HTitleText2} {#h-36305 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments to trust --- immunization and
public health

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Finance may make direct payments, in an aggregate amount
  of not more than four hundred million dollars, to a trust established
  to provide the provinces with funding for the purposes of supporting a
  national immunization strategy and assisting in the enhancement of
  their public health capacities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial share

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount that may be provided to a province under
  this section is to be determined in accordance with the terms of the
  trust indenture establishing the trust referred to in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of C.R.F.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any amount payable under this section may be paid by
  the Minister of Finance out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund at the
  times and in the manner that the Minister of Finance considers
  appropriate.

### [Payments to Nova Scotia]{.HTitleText2} {#h-36313 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment for 2004-2005

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  fiscal year beginning on April 1, 2004, the Minister of Finance may
  make a cash payment to the Province of Nova Scotia in the amount of
  thirty-four million dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment for 2005-2006

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the fiscal year beginning on April 1, 2005, the
  Minister of Finance may make a cash payment to the Province of Nova
  Scotia in the amount of four million dollars.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2005, c. 30, s. 86\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment out of C.R.F.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any amount payable under subsection (1) or (2) may be
  paid by the Minister of Finance out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund
  at the time and in the manner that the Minister of Finance considers
  appropriate.

- Definition of [[former legislation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In subsections (1) to (3), [[former
  legislation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Federal-Provincial Fiscal
  Arrangements Act as it read on May 13, 2004.

### [Payment to Saskatchewan]{.HTitleText2} {#h-36326 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of \$120,000,000

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Finance may make a cash payment of one hundred and twenty
  million dollars to the Province of Saskatchewan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment out of C.R.F.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any amount payable under subsection (1) may be paid
  by the Minister of Finance out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund at the
  time and in the manner that the Minister of Finance considers
  appropriate.

### [Appropriations for Grants]{.HTitleText2} {#h-36332 .Subheading}

#### [Canada Foundation for Sustainable Development Technology]{.HTitleText3} {#h-36333 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}\$200,000,000 granted

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** From and out of the
Consolidated Revenue Fund there may, on the requisition of the Minister
of the Environment and the Minister of Natural Resources, be paid and
applied a sum not exceeding two hundred million dollars for payment to
the Canada Foundation for Sustainable Development Technology for its
use.

#### [Canada Health Infoway Inc.]{.HTitleText3} {#h-36336 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}\$100,000,000 granted

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** From and out of the
Consolidated Revenue Fund there may, on the requisition of the Minister
of Health, be paid and applied a sum not exceeding one hundred million
dollars for payment to the Canada Health Infoway Inc. for its use.
