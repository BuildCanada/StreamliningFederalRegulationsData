## Communications Security Establishment Act {#communications-security-establishment-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2019, c. 13, s. 76
:::

Assented to 2019-06-21

An Act to establish the Communications Security Establishment

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 76 of chapter 13 of the Statutes of Canada, 2019,
in force August 1, 2019, see SI/2019-70.\]
:::
:::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the protection of Canada's national security and of the security
of Canadians is a fundamental responsibility of the Government of
Canada;

Whereas it is essential, to discharge that responsibility, for Canada to
have a communications security establishment;

And whereas it is important that the communications security
establishment carry out its activities in accordance with the rule of
law and in a manner that respects the Canadian Charter of Rights and
Freedoms;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170321 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Communications Security Establishment Act](/eng/acts/C-35.3).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170324 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canadian citizen, a
    [[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) or a corporation incorporated or continued
    under the laws of Canada or a province. ([Canadien]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Chief]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chief]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chief of the Establishment
    appointed under section 8. ([chef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Intelligence
    Commissioner appointed under subsection 4(1) of the [Intelligence
    Commissioner
    Act](/eng/acts/I-14.85). ([commissaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person, group, trust,
    partnership or fund or an unincorporated association or organization
    and includes a state or a political subdivision or agency of a
    state. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Communications
    Security Establishment established under section
    5. ([Centre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[federal institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any of the
    following institutions of Parliament or the Government of Canada:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Senate;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the House of Commons;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Library of Parliament;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the office of the Senate Ethics Officer, the
      office of the Conflict of Interest and Ethics Commissioner, the
      Parliamentary Protective Service and the office of the
      Parliamentary Budget Officer;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any federal court;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any board, commission, council, other body or
      other office established to perform a governmental function by or
      under an Act of Parliament, or by or under the authority of the

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined
      in section 2 of the [Financial Administration
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

## [Principle]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170349 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principle

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** It is in the public
interest to ensure that the Establishment may effectively carry out its
mandate in accordance with the rule of law and, to that end, to
expressly recognize in law a justification for persons who are
authorized to carry out activities under this Act to, in the course of
carrying out those activities, commit acts or omissions that would
otherwise constitute offences.

## [Communications Security Establishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170355 .Part}

### [Establishment and Organization]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1170356 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment established

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Communications Security
Establishment is established.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister is responsible

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is responsible
for the Establishment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The head
  office of the Establishment is to be in the National Capital Region
  described in the schedule to the [National Capital
  Act](/eng/acts/N-4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other offices

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief may, with the approval of the Minister,
  establish other offices elsewhere in Canada.

### [Chief of the Communications Security Establishment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1170366 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council must appoint a Chief of the Communications
  Security Establishment to hold office during pleasure for a term not
  exceeding five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief is eligible to be reappointed at the end of
  a term of office for a further term not exceeding five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salary and expenses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chief is to be paid the salary that is fixed by
  the Governor in Council and is entitled to payments for reasonable
  travel and living expenses incurred in the exercise of his or her
  powers or the performance of his or her duties and functions while
  absent from his or her ordinary place of work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Chief is deemed to be an employee for the
  purposes of the [Government Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5)
  and to be employed in the federal public administration for the
  purposes of any regulations made under section 9 of the [Aeronautics
  Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence, incapacity or vacancy

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the Chief is absent or incapacitated or the office
  of Chief is vacant, the Minister may appoint another person to act as
  Chief, but must not appoint a person for a term of more than 90 days
  without the approval of the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chief's powers, duties and functions

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief, under the direction of the Minister, has the management and
  control of the Establishment and all matters relating to it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rank of deputy head

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief has the rank and all the powers of a deputy
  head of a department.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation by Chief

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chief may delegate to any person any power, duty
  or function conferred on the Chief under this Act, except the power to
  delegate under this subsection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment's powers, duties and
functions

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The powers, duties and
functions of the Establishment may be exercised or performed by any
person who is appointed to serve in the Establishment in a capacity
appropriate to the exercise of the power or the performance of the duty
or function.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions by Minister

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may issue written directions to the Chief respecting the
  performance of the Chief's duties and functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Directions issued under subsection (1) are not
  statutory instruments within the meaning of the Statutory Instruments
  Act.

### [Human Resources]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1170392 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personnel

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief has exclusive authority to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint or lay off the Establishment's employees,
    revoke their appointment or terminate their employment; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish standards, procedures and processes
    governing staffing, including governing the appointment of
    employees, lay-off of employees, revocation of their appointment or
    termination of their employment otherwise than for cause.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of employer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in the [Federal Public Sector Labour
  Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-33.3) is to be construed so as to affect
  the right or authority of the Chief to deal with the matters referred
  to in subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of the Chief

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** In exercising his or her
authority under subsection 12(1), the Chief may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine the human resources requirements of the
  Establishment and provide for the allocation and effective utilization
  of human resources in the Establishment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide for the classification of positions and of
  the Establishment's employees;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} after consultation with the President of the Treasury
  Board, determine and regulate the pay to which the Establishment's
  employees are entitled for services rendered, their hours of work and
  their leave and any related matters;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} after consultation with the President of the Treasury
  Board, determine and regulate the payments that may be made to the
  Establishment's employees by way of reimbursement for travel or other
  expenses and by way of allowances in respect of expenses and
  conditions arising out of their employment;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} determine the learning, training and development
  requirements of the Establishment's employees and fix the terms on
  which the learning, training and development may be carried out;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} provide for the awards that may be made to the
  Establishment's employees for outstanding performance of their duties,
  for other meritorious achievement in relation to their duties or for
  inventions or practical suggestions for improvements;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} establish standards of discipline and set penalties,
  including termination of employment, suspension, demotion to a
  position at a lower maximum rate of pay and financial penalties;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} provide for the termination of employment, or the
  demotion to a position at a lower maximum rate of pay, of the
  Establishment's employees for reasons other than breaches of
  discipline or misconduct;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} establish policies respecting the exercise of the
  powers granted by this section; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} provide for any other matters, including terms and
  conditions of employment not otherwise specifically provided for in
  this section, that the Chief considers necessary for effective human
  resources management in the Establishment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Negotiation of collective agreements

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Before entering into
collective bargaining with the bargaining agent for a bargaining unit
composed of Establishment employees, the Chief must have the
Establishment's negotiating mandate approved by the President of the
Treasury Board.

## [Mandate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170414 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandate

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Establishment is the national signals intelligence agency for foreign
  intelligence and the technical authority for cybersecurity and
  information assurance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aspects of the mandate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Establishment's mandate has five aspects: foreign
  intelligence, cybersecurity and information assurance, defensive cyber
  operations, active cyber operations and technical and operational
  assistance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foreign intelligence

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The foreign intelligence
aspect of the Establishment's mandate is to acquire, covertly or
otherwise, information from or through the global information
infrastructure, including by engaging or interacting with foreign
entities located outside Canada or by using any other method of
acquiring information, and to use, analyse and disseminate the
information for the purpose of providing foreign intelligence, in
accordance with the Government of Canada's intelligence priorities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cybersecurity and information assurance

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The cybersecurity and
information assurance aspect of the Establishment's mandate is to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide advice, guidance and services to help protect

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} federal institutions' electronic information and
    information infrastructures, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} electronic information and information
    infrastructures designated under subsection 21(1) as being of
    importance to the Government of Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} acquire, use and analyse information from the global
  information infrastructure or from other sources in order to provide
  such advice, guidance and services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defensive cyber operations

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The defensive cyber
operations aspect of the Establishment's mandate is to carry out
activities on or through the global information infrastructure to help
protect

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} federal institutions' electronic information and
  information infrastructures; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} electronic information and information
  infrastructures designated under subsection 21(1) as being of
  importance to the Government of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Active cyber operations

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The active cyber
operations aspect of the Establishment's mandate is to carry out
activities on or through the global information infrastructure to
degrade, disrupt, influence, respond to or interfere with the
capabilities, intentions or activities of a foreign individual, state,
organization or terrorist group as they relate to international affairs,
defence or security.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Technical and operational assistance

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The technical and
operational assistance aspect of the Establishment's mandate is to
provide technical and operational assistance to federal law enforcement
and security agencies, the Canadian Forces and the Department of
National Defence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by order, designate any electronic information, any
  information infrastructures or any class of electronic information or
  information infrastructures as electronic information or information
  infrastructures --- as the case may be --- of importance to the
  Government of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An order made under subsection (1) is not a statutory
  instrument within the meaning of the [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

## [Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170441 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No activities --- Canadians and persons
in Canada

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Activities carried out by the
  Establishment in furtherance of the foreign intelligence,
  cybersecurity and information assurance, defensive cyber operations or
  active cyber operations aspects of its mandate must not be directed at
  a Canadian or at any person in Canada and must not infringe the
  Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No activities --- global information
  infrastructure in Canada or without authorization

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Activities carried out by the Establishment in
  furtherance of the defensive cyber operations or active cyber
  operations aspects of its mandate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} must not be directed at any portion of the global
    information infrastructure that is in Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} must not be carried out except under an
    authorization issued under subsection 29(1) or 30(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of other Acts --- foreign
  intelligence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Activities carried out by the Establishment in
  furtherance of the foreign intelligence aspect of its mandate must not
  contravene any other Act of Parliament --- or involve the acquisition
  by the Establishment of information from or through the global
  information infrastructure that interferes with the reasonable
  expectation of privacy of a Canadian or a person in Canada --- unless
  they are carried out under an authorization issued under subsection
  26(1) or 40(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of other Acts ---
  cybersecurity and information assurance

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Activities carried out by the Establishment in
  furtherance of the cybersecurity and information assurance aspect of
  its mandate must not contravene any other Act of Parliament --- or
  involve the acquisition by the Establishment of information from the
  global information infrastructure that interferes with the reasonable
  expectation of privacy of a Canadian or a person in Canada --- unless
  they are carried out under an authorization issued under subsection
  27(1) or (2) or 40(1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment's activities

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections 22(1) and (2),
  the Establishment may carry out any of the following activities in
  furtherance of its mandate:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} acquiring, using, analysing, retaining or
    disclosing publicly available information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} acquiring, using, analysing, retaining or
    disclosing infrastructure information for the purpose of research
    and development, for the purpose of testing systems or conducting
    cybersecurity and information assurance activities on the
    infrastructure from which the information was acquired; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} testing or evaluating products, software and
    systems, including testing or evaluating them for vulnerabilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Investment Canada
  Act](/eng/acts/I-21.8)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 22(1), in furtherance of its
  mandate the Establishment may analyse information for the purpose of
  providing advice to the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
  Preparedness and to the Minister responsible for the administration of
  the [Investment Canada Act](/eng/acts/I-21.8) with regard to that
  latter Minister's powers and duties under Part IV.1 of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cybersecurity and information assurance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 22(1), the Establishment may carry
  out any of the following activities in furtherance of the
  cybersecurity and information assurance aspect of its mandate:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} carrying out activities on information
    infrastructures to identify or isolate malicious software, prevent
    malicious software from harming those information infrastructures or
    mitigate any harm that malicious software causes to them; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} analysing information in order to be able to
    provide advice on the integrity of supply chains and on the
    trustworthiness of telecommunications, equipment and services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information acquired incidentally

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Establishment may acquire information relating to
  a Canadian or a person in Canada incidentally in the course of
  carrying out activities under an authorization issued under subsection
  26(1), 27(1) or (2) or 40(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in this section.

  [[incidentally]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[incidentally]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to the
      acquisition of information, means that the information acquired
      was not itself deliberately sought and that the
      information-acquisition activity was not directed at the Canadian
      or person in Canada. ([incidemment]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[infrastructure information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[infrastructure information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      information relating to

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any functional component, physical or logical,
        of the global information infrastructure; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} events that occur during the interaction
        between two or more devices that provide services on a network
        --- not including end-point devices that are linked to
        individual users --- or between an individual and a machine, if
        the interaction is about only a functional component of the
        global information infrastructure.

      It does not include information that could be linked to an
      identifiable person. ([information sur
      l'infrastructure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures to protect privacy

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The Establishment must
ensure that measures are in place to protect the privacy of Canadians
and of persons in Canada in the use, analysis, retention and disclosure
of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} information related to them acquired in the course of
  the furtherance of the foreign intelligence and cybersecurity and
  information assurance aspects of the Establishment's mandate; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} publicly available information related to them
  acquired under paragraph 23(1)(a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Technical and operational assistance
activities

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Establishment provides assistance in furtherance of the technical and
  operational assistance aspect of its mandate, then the Establishment,
  in the course of providing the assistance, has the same authority to
  carry out any activity as would have the federal law enforcement or
  security agency, the Canadian Forces or the Department of National
  Defence, as the case may be, if it were carrying out the activity, and
  is subject to any limitations imposed by law on the agency, the
  Canadian Forces or that Department, including requirements with
  respect to any applicable warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemptions, protections and immunities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Establishment provides assistance in
  furtherance of the technical and operational assistance aspect of its
  mandate, then persons authorized to act on the Establishment's behalf
  benefit from the same exemptions, protections and immunities as would
  persons authorized to act on behalf of the federal law enforcement or
  security agency, the Canadian Forces or the Department of National
  Defence, as the case may be, if those persons were carrying out the
  activity.

## [Authorizations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170483 .Part}

### [Foreign Intelligence and Cybersecurity Authorizations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1170484 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foreign Intelligence Authorizations

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may issue a Foreign Intelligence Authorization to the
  Establishment that authorizes it, despite any other Act of Parliament
  or of any foreign state, to carry out, on or through the global
  information infrastructure, any activity specified in the
  authorization in the furtherance of the foreign intelligence aspect of
  its mandate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activities authorized

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Activities and classes of activities that a Foreign
  Intelligence Authorization may authorize the Establishment to carry
  out may include any of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} gaining access to a portion of the global
    information infrastructure;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} acquiring information on or through the global
    information infrastructure, including unselected information;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} installing, maintaining, copying, distributing,
    searching, modifying, disrupting, deleting or intercepting anything
    on or through the global information infrastructure;

### [Procedure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1170531 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications for authorizations

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may issue an authorization under subsection 26(1), 27(1) or
  (2), 29(1) or 30(1) only on the written application of the Chief.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must set out the facts that would
  allow the Minister to conclude that there are reasonable grounds to
  believe that the authorization is necessary and that the conditions
  for issuing it are met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written request of infrastructure owner
  or operator

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the application is for an authorization to be
  issued under subsection 27(2), the application must include the
  written request of the owner or operator of the information
  infrastructure to the Establishment to carry out the activity that
  would be authorized.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister of Foreign Affairs' request or
  consent

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the application is for an authorization to be
  issued under subsection 30(1), the application must include the
  request or consent referred to in subsection 30(2) if it is in
  writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for authorizations

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may issue an authorization under subsection 26(1), 27(1) or
  (2), 29(1) or 30(1) only if he or she concludes that there are
  reasonable grounds to believe that any activity that would be
  authorized by it is reasonable and proportionate, having regard to the
  nature of the objective to be achieved and the nature of the
  activities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for authorizations ---
  foreign intelligence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may issue an authorization under
  subsection 26(1) only if he or she concludes that there are reasonable
  grounds to believe --- in addition to the matters referred to in
  subsection (1) --- that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any information acquired under the authorization
    could not reasonably be acquired by other means and will be retained
    for no longer than is reasonably necessary;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any unselected information acquired under the
    authorization could not reasonably be acquired by other means, in
    the case of an authorization that authorizes the acquisition of
    unselected information; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the measures referred to in section 24 will ensure
    that information acquired under the authorization that is identified
    as relating to a Canadian or a person in Canada will be used,
    analysed or retained only if the information is essential to
    international affairs, defence or security.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for authorizations ---
  cybersecurity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may issue an authorization under
  subsection 27(1) or (2) only if he or she concludes that there are
  reasonable grounds to believe --- in addition to the matters referred
  to in subsection (1) --- that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any information acquired under the authorization
    will be retained for no longer than is reasonably necessary;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the consent of all persons whose information may be
    acquired could not reasonably be obtained, in the case of an
    authorization to be issued under subsection 27(1);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any information acquired under the authorization is
    necessary to identify, isolate, prevent or mitigate harm to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} federal institutions' electronic information or
      information infrastructures, in the case of an authorization to be
      issued under subsection 27(1), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} electronic information or information
      infrastructures designated under subsection 21(1) as being of
      importance to the Government of Canada, in the case of an
      authorization to be issued under subsection 27(2); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the measures referred to in section 24 will ensure
    that information acquired under the authorization that is identified
    as relating to a Canadian or a person in Canada will be used,
    analysed or retained only if the information is essential to
    identify, isolate, prevent or mitigate harm to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} federal institutions' electronic information or
      information infrastructures, in the case of an authorization to be
      issued under subsection 27(1), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} electronic information or information
      infrastructures designated under subsection 21(1) as being of
      importance to the Government of Canada, in the case of an
      authorization to be issued under subsection 27(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for authorizations ---
  defensive and active cyber operations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may issue an authorization under
  subsection 29(1) or 30(1) only if he or she concludes that there are
  reasonable grounds to believe --- in addition to the matters referred
  to in subsection (1) --- that the objective of the cyber operation
  could not reasonably be achieved by other means and that no
  information will be acquired under the authorization except in
  accordance with an authorization issued under subsection 26(1) or
  27(1) or (2) or 40(1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of authorizations

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** An authorization issued
under subsection 26(1), 27(1) or (2), 29(1) or 30(1) must specify

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the activities or classes of activities that it
  authorizes the Establishment to carry out;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the activities or classes of activities referred to
  in paragraph (a) that would otherwise be contrary to any other Act of
  Parliament;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the persons or classes of persons who are authorized
  to carry out the activities or classes of activities referred to in
  paragraph (a);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any terms, conditions or restrictions that the
  Minister considers advisable in the public interest, or advisable to
  ensure the reasonableness and proportionality of any activity
  authorized by the authorization;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an authorization issued under
  subsection 26(1) or 27(1) or (2), any other terms, conditions or
  restrictions that the Minister considers advisable to protect the
  privacy of Canadians and of persons in Canada, including conditions to
  limit the use, analysis and retention of, access to, and the form and
  manner of disclosure of, information related to them;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an authorization issued under
  subsection 26(1), whether the activities authorized include acquiring
  unselected information, and any terms, conditions or restrictions that
  the Minister considers advisable to limit the use, analysis and
  retention of, and access to, unselected information;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the authorization is issued;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the authorization expires; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} anything else reasonable in the circumstances and
  reasonably necessary in aid of any other activity, or class of
  activities, authorized by the authorization.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of validity of authorizations

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  authorization issued under subsection 26(1), 27(1) or (2), 29(1) or
  30(1) may be valid for a period not exceeding one year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension --- foreign intelligence or
  cybersecurity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may extend the period of validity of an
  authorization issued under subsection 26(1) or 27(1) or (2) by up to a
  period not exceeding one year from the day referred to in paragraph
  35(h).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No review by Commissioner

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister's decision to extend a period of
  validity is not subject to review by the Commissioner under the
  [Intelligence Commissioner Act](/eng/acts/I-14.85).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension --- authorization

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must, as soon as feasible, notify the
  Commissioner of any extension of an authorization.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, an activity that is specified
  in an amended authorization issued under subsection 26(1) or 27(1)
  or (2) in respect of which the Commissioner has provided the Minister

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activities under amended authorization
  --- cyber operations

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, an activity that is specified
  in an amended authorization issued under subsection 29(1) or 30(1) is

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170673 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition on disclosure

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited, in a proceeding before a court, person or body with
  jurisdiction to compel the production of information, to disclose the
  identity of a person or entity that has assisted or is assisting the
  Establishment on a confidential basis, or any information from which
  the identity of such a person or entity could be inferred.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply when

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information or identity is disclosed by the
    person or entity to their solicitor or, in Quebec, advocate in
    connection with a proceeding, if the information is relevant to that
    proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information or identity is disclosed to enable
    the Attorney General of Canada, a judge or a court hearing an appeal
    from, or a review of, an order of the judge to discharge their
    responsibilities under this section; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the information or identity is disclosed to the
    Commissioner or to the Review Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- consent

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The identity of a person or entity that has assisted
  or is assisting the Establishment on a confidential basis, or any
  information from which the identity of such a person or entity could
  be inferred, may be disclosed in a proceeding referred to in
  subsection (1) if the person or entity and the Chief consent to the
  disclosure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of other Acts

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Sections 38 to 38.16 of the [Canada Evidence
  Act](/eng/acts/C-5), or sections 83 and 87 of the [Immigration and
  Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), as the case may be, apply to
  a proceeding referred to in subsection (1), with any necessary
  modifications.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality --- information

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The judge must ensure the confidentiality of the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identity of any person or entity that has
    assisted or is assisting the Establishment on a confidential basis,
    and any information from which the identity of such a person or
    entity could be inferred; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information and other evidence provided in respect
    of an application under any provision referred to in subsection (4)
    if, in the judge's opinion, its disclosure would be injurious to
    international relations, national defence or national security or
    would endanger the safety of any person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality --- application

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If, in the judge's opinion, the disclosure of the
  fact that an application under any provision referred to in
  subsection (4) would result in the disclosure of an identity or
  information referred to in paragraph (5)(a), the judge must ensure the
  confidentiality of the application and all information related to it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order authorizing disclosure

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The judge may, by order, authorize disclosure that
  the judge considers appropriate, subject to any conditions that the
  judge specifies, of the identity or information referred to in
  subsection (1) if, in the judge's opinion,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person or entity is not a person or entity that
    has assisted or is assisting the Establishment on a confidential
    basis, or the information is not information from which the identity
    of such a person or entity could be inferred; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a proceeding that is a prosecution
    of an offence, the disclosure of the identity of a person or entity
    that has assisted or is assisting the Establishment on a
    confidential basis, or information from which the identity of such a
    person or entity could be inferred, is essential to establish the
    accused's innocence and may be disclosed in the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order confirming prohibition

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If the judge does not authorize disclosure under
  paragraph (7)(a) or (b), the judge must, by order, confirm the
  prohibition of disclosure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When determination takes effect

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} An order of the judge that authorizes disclosure does
  not take effect until the time provided or granted to appeal the order
  has expired or, if the order is appealed, the time provided or granted
  to appeal a judgment of an appeal court that confirms the order has
  expired and no further appeal from a judgment that confirms the order
  is available.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality on appeal

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an appeal, subsections (5) and (6)
  apply, with any necessary modifications, to the court to which the
  appeal is taken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
  the Chief Justice of the Federal Court or a judge of that Court
  designated by the Chief Justice to conduct hearings under any Act of
  Parliament for the protection of information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance or disclosure of information
--- no presumptions

**[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** The provision of
assistance or the disclosure of information by the Establishment under
this Act does not create a presumption

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the Establishment is conducting a joint
  investigation or decision-making process with the entity to which
  assistance is provided or information is disclosed and therefore has
  the same obligations, if any, as the entity to disclose or produce
  information for the purposes of a proceeding; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} there has been a waiver of any privilege, or of any
  requirement to obtain consent, for the purposes of any other
  disclosure of that information either in a proceeding or to an entity
  that is not a federal institution.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Access to Information
Act](/eng/acts/A-1)

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
[Access to Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1), if any
[[record]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 3 of that Act,
of any other [[government institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
defined in that section, or of any other organization is contained in or
carried on the Establishment's information infrastructure on behalf of
that institution or organization, the record is not under the
Establishment's control.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21)

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
[Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21), if any [[personal
information]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 3 of that
Act, of any other [[government institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
defined in that section, or of any other organization is contained in or
carried on the Establishment's information infrastructure on behalf of
that institution or organization, the personal information is not held
by the Establishment and is not under the Establishment's control.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual Report

**[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** The Establishment must,
within three months after the end of each fiscal year, publish an annual
report on its activities during that fiscal year.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170714 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, on the recommendation of the Minister, make regulations for
carrying out the purposes and provisions of this Act, including
regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the management and control of the
  Establishment, including security on and around the Establishment's
  premises, access to its premises, the search of persons on or around
  its premises and the search and seizure of items on or around its
  premises;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the measures referred to in section 24 to
  protect the privacy of Canadians and of persons in Canada; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} amending the definition of any term defined in
  section 2 or subsection 23(5) or 44(3) to respond, directly or
  indirectly, to any technological change.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 77

  - ###### Definitions {#definitions .MarginalNote}

    [77]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in sections 78 to
    82.

    [[former department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[former department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the portion of
        the federal public administration known as the Communications
        Security Establishment. ([ancien ministère]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

    [[new department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[new department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Communications
        Security Establishment as established by section 5 of the
        [Communications Security Establishment
        Act](/eng/acts/C-35.3). ([nouveau ministère]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 78

  - ###### Chief {#chief .MarginalNote}

    - [78]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Chief of the Communications
      Security Establishment holding office immediately before the
      coming into force of section 76 continues in office for the
      remainder of the term for which he or she was appointed.

    - ###### Employees {#employees .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in the [Communications Security
      Establishment Act](/eng/acts/C-35.3) is to be construed as
      affecting the status of an employee who, immediately before the
      coming into force of section 76, occupied a position in the former
      department, except that the employee, on the coming into force of
      that section, occupies that position in the new department.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 79

  - ###### Transfer of appropriations {#transfer-of-appropriations .MarginalNote}

    - [79]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any amount that is appropriated,
      for the fiscal year in which section 76 comes into force, by an
      appropriation Act based on the Estimates for that year for
      defraying the federal public administration's charges and expenses
      for the former department that is unexpended on the day on which
      that section comes into force is deemed, on that day, to be an
      amount appropriated for defraying the federal public
      administration's charges and expenses for the new department.

    - ###### Transfer of powers, duties and functions {#transfer-of-powers-duties-and-functions .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a power, duty or function is vested in or
      exercisable by the former department's Chief or an employee of the
      former department under any Act, order, rule, regulation or
      direction, or any contract, lease, licence or other document, that
      power, duty or function is vested in or is exercisable by the new
      department's Chief or an employee of the new department.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 80

  - ###### Ministerial authorizations {#ministerial-authorizations .MarginalNote}

    - [80]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} An authorization that was issued
      under subsection 273.65(1) or (3) of the [National Defence
      Act](/eng/acts/N-5) before the day on which section 76 comes into
      force and is valid on that day continues to be valid for the
      period specified in it or, if it was renewed before that day, for
      the period specified in the renewal.

    - ###### Repeal {#repeal .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may repeal an authorization referred
      to in subsection (1) at any time.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 81

  - ###### Arrangements {#arrangements .MarginalNote}

    [81]{.lawlabel} Any arrangement entered into by the former
    department before the day on which section 76 comes into force
    continues in accordance with its terms.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 82, as amended by 2024, c. 16, s. 59

  - ###### References {#references .MarginalNote}

    - [82]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} A reference to the former
      department in any of the following is deemed to be a reference to
      the new department:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Schedule I to the [Access to Information
        Act](/eng/acts/A-1) under the heading "Other Government

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Schedules I.1, V and VI to the [Financial

        Security of Information Act](/eng/acts/O-5);

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Schedule 3 to the [Security of Canada
        Information Disclosure Act](/eng/acts/S-6.9);

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the [National Security and Intelligence Review
        Agency Act](/eng/acts/N-16.62); and

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the [Intelligence Commissioner
        Act](/eng/acts/I-14.85).

    - ###### Other references {#other-references .MarginalNote}

      reference to the former department in any Act of Parliament, other
      than an Act referred to in subsection (1), or in any order,
      regulation or other instrument made under an Act of Parliament is
      deemed to be a reference to the new department.

    - ###### Deputy head {#deputy-head .MarginalNote}

      former department in any order of the Governor in Council made
      under section 55 of the [National Security and Intelligence Review
      Agency Act](/eng/acts/N-16.62) is deemed to be a designation of
      the Chief of the new department as deputy head of that department.
