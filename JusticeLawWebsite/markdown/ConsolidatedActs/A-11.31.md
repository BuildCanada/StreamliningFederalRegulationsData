## Anishinabek Nation Governance Agreement Act {#anishinabek-nation-governance-agreement-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2022, c. 9, s. 1
:::

Assented to 2022-06-23

An Act to give effect to the Anishinabek Nation Governance Agreement and
to make related and consequential amendments to other Acts

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 1 of chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2022, in
force October 1, 2022, see SI/2022-48.\]
:::
:::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the Government of Canada is committed to achieving
reconciliation with First Nations through renewed nation-to-nation
relationships based on recognition of rights, respect, cooperation and
partnership;

Whereas the Government of Canada recognizes that the inherent right of
self-government is a right of the Indigenous peoples of Canada
recognized and affirmed by section 35 of the [Constitution Act,
1982](/eng/Const/);

Whereas the Anishinabek Nation Governance Agreement specifies that the
First Nations and the Anishinabek Nation may enact laws in respect of
governance, as well as in respect of Anishinabek culture and languages;

Whereas the Agreement provides that the parties may enter into
additional self-government agreements in relation to other law-making
powers, and that those agreements would become part of the Agreement,
unless the parties agree otherwise;

Whereas the Government of Canada is committed to implementing the United
Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples and the parties
to the Agreement view it as contributing to the implementation of that
declaration;

And whereas ratification of the Agreement requires the coming into force
of an Act of Parliament that gives effect to it;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1365984 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Anishinabek Nation Governance Agreement Act](/eng/acts/A-11.31).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1365987 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Anishinabek Nation
    Governance Agreement, signed on April 6, 2022, including any
    amendments made to it. ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Anishinabek law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Anishinabek law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a law made by a First
    Nation or the Anishinabek Nation under section 6. ([texte législatif
    anishinabe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Anishinabek Nation Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Anishinabek Nation Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
    meaning as in section 1.1 of the Agreement. ([gouvernement de la
    Nation des Anishinabes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[citizen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[citizen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a citizen --- an
    "E'Dbendaagzijig" --- of a First Nation, within the meaning of
    section 1.1 of the Agreement, or of the Anishinabek Nation, under
    section 4.15 of the Agreement.  ([citoyen]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[constitution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[constitution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a constitution ratified
    by a First Nation or the Anishinabek Nation in accordance with the
    Agreement. ([constitution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, except in subsection
    14(1), a band that is named in the schedule. ([première
    nation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[First Nation Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[First Nation Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in section 1.1 of the Agreement. ([gouvernement de la première
    nation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Indigenous peoples of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indigenous peoples of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
    assigned by the definition [[aboriginal peoples of
    Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 35(2) of the
    [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/). ([peuples autochtones du
    Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not a treaty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement is not a
treaty within the meaning of section 35 of the [Constitution Act,
1982](/eng/Const/).

## [Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1366001 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement given effect

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement is approved, given effect and declared valid and has the
  force of law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and obligations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a person or body has the
  powers, rights, privileges and benefits conferred on the person or
  body by the Agreement and must perform the duties, and is subject to
  the liabilities, imposed on the person or body by the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement binding

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Agreement is binding on,
  and may be relied on by, all persons and bodies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement prevails

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  event of any inconsistency or conflict between the Agreement and the
  provisions of any Act of Parliament, or of any regulation made under
  an Act of Parliament, the Agreement prevails to the extent of the
  inconsistency or conflict.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act prevails

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of any inconsistency or conflict between
  the provisions of this Act and the provisions of any other Act of
  Parliament, or of any regulation made under any other Act of
  Parliament, the provisions of this Act prevail to the extent of the
  inconsistency or conflict.

## [Anishinabek Laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1366014 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to enact laws

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A First Nation or the
Anishinabek Nation, as the case may be, may, in accordance with the
applicable constitution and the Agreement, make laws respecting

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the selection of government representatives;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the determination of who is a citizen, as well as the
  rights, privileges and responsibilities associated with being a
  citizen;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the management and operation of government;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the preservation, promotion and development of
  culture and language; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other matters covered by any additional
  self-government agreements entered into by the First Nations, the
  Anishinabek Nation and the Government of Canada in accordance with
  Chapter 13 of the Agreement.

## [Application of Other Acts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1366022 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) to (4), the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) continues
  to apply in respect of every First Nation, its citizens and its
  reserve as of the day of coming into force of section 4.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) ---
  sections 8 to 14

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 8 to 14 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)
  cease to apply in respect of the First Nation and its members on the
  day on which a law referred to in paragraph 6(b) made by that First
  Nation comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) ---
  sections 74 to 79

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 74 to 79 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)
  cease to apply in respect of the First Nation and its members on the
  day on which a law referred to in paragraph 6(a) made by that First
  Nation comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) --- section
  80

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Section 80 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) ceases
  to apply in respect of First Nations and their members on the day on
  which section 4 comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) ---
  definitions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the continuing application of the
  [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) under this section, the definitions
  [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[council of the
  band]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[member of a
  band]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) of that Act have the
  meaning assigned to[[ First Nation,]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}[[First
  Nation Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
  [[citizen]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, respectively, in section 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[First Nations Elections
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.65)

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The [First Nations
Elections Act](/eng/acts/F-11.65) ceases to apply in respect of a First
Nation and its members on the day on which a law referred to in
paragraph 6(a) made by that First Nation comes into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An Anishinabek law and any
regulation or order made under it are not statutory instruments for the
purposes of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1366038 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice --- Agreement

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice must be taken of the
  Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Agreement must be published by the Queen's
  Printer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the Agreement published by the Queen's
  Printer is evidence of the Agreement and of its contents, and a copy
  purporting to be published by the Queen's Printer is presumed to be so
  published unless the contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice --- Anishinabek laws

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice must be taken of an
  Anishinabek law that is registered in the official registry of laws
  maintained by a First Nation or the Anishinabek Nation in accordance
  with the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of an Anishinabek law purporting to be
  registered in an official registry of laws referred to in
  subsection (1) is evidence of that law and of its contents, unless the
  contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  issue arises in any judicial or administrative proceeding in respect
  of the interpretation or validity of a provision of the Agreement,
  this Act or an Anishinabek law, then the issue must not be decided
  until the party raising the issue has served notice

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the Attorney General of Canada and the
    Anishinabek Nation Government, in the case of a provision of the
    Agreement or this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the Anishinabek Nation Government, in the case
    of a provision of a law of the Anishinabek Nation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the First Nation Government, in the case of a
    provision of a law of a First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content and timing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} describe the proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} state the subject matter of the issue;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state the day on which the issue is to be argued;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} give the particulars that are necessary to show the
    point to be argued; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be served at least 30 days before the day on which
    the issue is to be argued, unless the court or tribunal authorizes a
    shorter period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation in proceedings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In any proceeding, the recipient of a notice may
  appear and participate as a party with the same rights as any other
  party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsections (2) and (3) do not
  require that an oral hearing be held if one is not otherwise required.

## [Orders and Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1366068 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make any orders or regulations that the Governor in Council
considers necessary for the purpose of carrying out the Agreement or
other agreements related to the implementation of the Agreement.

## [Amendment of Schedule]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1366071 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Addition of name of First Nation

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, by order, add the name of any First Nation to
  the schedule if the Governor in Council is satisfied that the First
  Nation has, in a manner consistent with the Agreement, ratified the
  Agreement and has a constitution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment or removal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by order, amend the
  schedule to amend or remove the name of a First Nation if the Governor
  in Council is satisfied that consent to the amendment or removal was
  obtained in a manner consistent with the Agreement.
