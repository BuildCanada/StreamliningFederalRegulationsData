## Restoring Rail Service Act {#restoring-rail-service-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2012, c. 8
:::

Assented to 2012-05-31

An Act to provide for the continuation and resumption of rail service
operations
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427606 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Restoring Rail Service Act](/eng/acts/R-7.3).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427609 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[arbitrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[arbitrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an arbitrator appointed
      under subsection 8(1). ([arbitre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[collective agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[collective agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any collective
      agreement between the employer and the union that expired on
      December 31, 2011. ([convention collective]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is employed by
      the employer and bound by a collective
      agreement. ([employé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Pacific
      Railway. ([employeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
      Labour. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Teamsters Canada Rail
      Conference, representing the rail traffic controllers' bargaining
      unit or the running trades employees' bargaining
      unit. ([syndicat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Words and expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise provided, words and expressions used
  in this Act have the same meaning as in Part I of the [Canada Labour
  Code](/eng/acts/L-2).

## [Rail Service Operations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427621 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation or resumption of rail
service operations

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** On the coming into force of
this Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the employer must continue, or resume without delay,
  as the case may be, rail service operations; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} every employee must, when so required, continue, or
  resume without delay, as the case may be, the duties of their
  employment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibitions

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for the
employer and for any officer or representative of the employer to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in any manner impede any employee from complying with
  paragraph 3(b); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} discharge or in any other manner discipline, or
  authorize or direct the discharge or discipline of, any employee by
  reason of the employee having been on strike before the coming into
  force of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The union and each officer
and representative of the union must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} without delay on the coming into force of this Act,
  give notice to the employees that, by reason of that coming into
  force, rail service operations are to be continued or resumed, as the
  case may be, and that the employees, when so required, are to
  continue, or resume without delay, as the case may be, the duties of
  their employment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} take all reasonable steps to ensure that employees
  comply with paragraph 3(b); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} refrain from any conduct that may encourage employees
  not to comply with paragraph 3(b).

## [Extension of Collective Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427635 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The term
  of each collective agreement is extended to include the period
  beginning on January 1, 2012 and ending on the day on which a new
  collective agreement between the parties comes into effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collective agreement binding for
  extended term

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A collective agreement, as extended by subsection
  (1), is effective and binding on the parties to it for the period for
  which it is extended, despite anything in the collective agreement or
  in Part I of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2). However, that
  Part applies in respect of the collective agreement, as extended, as
  if that period were the term of the collective agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Strikes and lockouts prohibited

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Until the day on which a
collective agreement, as extended by subsection 6(1), expires, it is
prohibited

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the employer and for any officer or
  representative of the employer to declare or cause a lockout against
  the union in respect of the bargaining unit to which the collective
  agreement applies;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the union and for any officer or representative
  of the union to declare or authorize a strike against the employer in
  respect of that bargaining unit; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for an employee who is a member of that bargaining
  unit to participate in a strike against the employer.

## [Arbitration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427646 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arbitration

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must, after the coming into force of this Act, appoint an
  arbitrator in respect of each collective agreement and refer to the
  arbitrator all matters relating to the amendment or revision of the
  collective agreement that, at the time of the appointment, are in
  dispute between the parties to it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers and duties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An arbitrator has, with any modifications that the
  circumstances require, all the powers and duties of an arbitrator
  under paragraphs 60(1)(a) and (a.2) to (a.4) and section 61 of the
  [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit and report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An arbitrator must decide all matters referred to the
  arbitrator under this Act within 90 days after the day on which he or
  she is appointed, or any longer period that the Minister may allow,
  and make a report to the Minister on the arbitrator's decision in
  respect of those matters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of decision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The decision of an arbitrator respecting a collective
  agreement must be set out in a form that enables it to be incorporated
  into the collective agreement in accordance with section 9.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation in collective agreement

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Beginning on the day on
which an arbitrator reports to the Minister under subsection 8(3), the
collective agreement is deemed to be amended by the incorporation into
it of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any agreement between the parties to it arrived at
  before the appointment of the arbitrator;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any agreement resolving the matters in dispute
  between the parties to it arrived at after the appointment of the
  arbitrator and before the day on which the arbitrator reports to the
  Minister; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the decision of the arbitrator in respect of the
  matters that were referred to arbitration.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings prohibited

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** No order is to be made,
no process entered into and no proceeding taken in court

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to question the appointment of an arbitrator; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to review, prohibit or restrain any proceeding or
  decision of an arbitrator.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New collective agreements not precluded

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act
precludes the parties to a collective agreement from entering into a new
collective agreement at any time before the arbitrator reports to the
Minister under subsection 8(3) and, if they do so, the arbitrator's
duties under this Act cease as of the day on which the new collective
agreement is entered into.

## [New Collective Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427667 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New collective agreements

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite anything in Part I of the
  [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2), an arbitrator's decision
  constitutes a new collective agreement between the parties that is
  effective and binding on them beginning on the day on which it is
  made. However, that Part applies in respect of the new collective
  agreement as if it had been entered into under that Part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into effect of provisions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A new collective agreement may provide that any of
  its provisions are effective and binding on a day that is before or
  after the day on which the new collective agreement becomes effective
  and binding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act is to be construed so as to limit
  or restrict the rights of the parties to a new collective agreement to
  amend any of its provisions, other than a provision relating to its
  term, and to give effect to the amendment.

## [Costs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427675 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** All costs incurred by Her
Majesty in right of Canada relating to the appointment of an arbitrator
and the performance of an arbitrator's duties under this Act are debts
due to Her Majesty in right of Canada and may be recovered as such, in
equal parts from the employer and the union, in any court of competent
jurisdiction.

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427678 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Individuals

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual who contravenes any provision of this Act is guilty of an
  offence punishable on summary conviction and is liable, for each day
  or part of a day during which the offence continues, to a fine of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} not more than \$50,000 if the individual was acting
    in the capacity of an officer or representative of the employer or
    the union when the offence was committed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} not more than \$1,000 in any other case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employer or union

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the employer or the union contravenes any
  provision of this Act, it is guilty of an offence punishable on
  summary conviction and is liable, for each day or part of a day during
  which the offence continues, to a fine of not more than \$100,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No imprisonment

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsection 787(2)
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), no term of imprisonment is to be
imposed in default of payment of a fine that is imposed under section
14.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of fines

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** If a person is convicted
of an offence under section 14 and the fine that is imposed is not paid
when required, the prosecutor may, by filing the conviction, enter as a
judgment the amount of the fine and costs, if any, in a superior court
of the province in which the trial was held, and the judgment is
enforceable against the person in the same manner as if it were a
judgment rendered against the person in that court in civil proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Act, the union is deemed to be a person.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427692 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

hour after the time at which it is assented to.
