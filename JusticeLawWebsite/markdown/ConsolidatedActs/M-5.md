## Members of Parliament Retiring Allowances Act {#members-of-parliament-retiring-allowances-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. M-5
:::

An Act to provide retiring allowances on a contributory basis to persons
who have served as Members of Parliament
::::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-355705 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Members of Parliament Retiring Allowances Act](/eng/acts/M-5).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. M-10, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-355710 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[annual allowance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[annual allowance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an annual allowance
      payable to a member under section 62, 62.3 or 62.4 of the
      [Parliament of Canada Act](/eng/acts/P-1) or payable to a member
      under an appropriation Act as Deputy Chair or Assistant Deputy
      Chair of a committee. ([indemnité annuelle]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[average annual pensionable earnings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[average annual pensionable earnings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with
      respect to any member, means the average annual pensionable
      earnings received as a member during any five-year period of
      pensionable service selected by or on behalf of the member, or
      during any period so selected consisting of consecutive periods of
      pensionable service totalling five years. ([moyenne annuelle des
      gains ouvrant droit à pension]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[average annual sessional indemnity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[average annual sessional indemnity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with
      respect to any member, means the average annual sessional
      indemnity received as a member during any five year period of
      pensionable service selected by or on behalf of the member, or
      during any period so selected consisting of consecutive periods of
      pensionable service totalling five years. ([moyenne annuelle de
      l'indemnité de session]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[average maximum pensionable earnings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[average maximum pensionable earnings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with
      respect to any former member, means the average of the Year's
      Maximum Pensionable Earnings, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
      [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), for the earlier of the years
      referred to in paragraphs (a) and (b), and for each of the four
      years preceding that earlier year,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the year in which the former member ceased to
        be a member, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the year in which the former member becomes
        entitled to receive a retirement pension under the [Canada
        Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or under a similar provincial
        pension plan. ([moyenne des maximums des gains ouvrant droit à
        pension]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Chief Actuary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Chief Actuary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chief Actuary of
      the Office of the Superintendent of Financial
      Institutions. ([actuaire en chef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a child or stepchild of --- or
      an individual adopted either legally or in fact by --- a member or
      former member who

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is less than eighteen years of age, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is eighteen years of age or older but less than
        twenty-five years of age, and is in full-time attendance at a
        school or university within the meaning provided for that
        expression by the regulations, having been in such attendance
        substantially without interruption since the child reached
        eighteen years of age or the member or former member died,
        whichever occurred later. ([enfant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Compensation Arrangements Account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Compensation Arrangements Account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      the Members of Parliament Retirement Compensation Arrangements
      Account established by section 27. ([compte de
      convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[defined benefit limit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[defined benefit limit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a calendar year before 1995,
        \$1,722.22, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the 1995 calendar year and any
        subsequent calendar year, the amount prescribed. ([plafond des
        prestations déterminées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[earnings limit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[earnings limit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a member in
      respect of one or more sessions in any calendar year, means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for any calendar year before 2016, the amount
        in respect of which benefits may be accrued during that calendar
        year for the purposes of a registered pension plan within the
        meaning of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), determined by
        dividing the defined benefit limit in respect of that calendar
        year by 0.02, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for 2016 and subsequent calendar years, the
        amount determined by the following formula, rounded to the next
        highest multiple of \$100:

        ::: {#355731 .Paragraph}
        \[(A -- (B × C)) / 0.02\] + C

        where

        [A]{.dfn}
        :   is the the amount determined for the calendar year in
            accordance with the definition [[defined benefit
            limit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 8500(1) of the
            [Income Tax Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._945),

        [B]{.dfn}
        :   is the number fixed by the Chief Actuary under subsection
            (6), and

        [C]{.dfn}
        :   is the amount of the Year's Maximum Pensionable Earnings, as
            defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Canada Pension
            Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), for that calendar year. ([gains
            maximums]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})
        :::

  [[former Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[former Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means this Act as it read on
      December 31, 1991. ([version antérieure]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[joint and survivor benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[joint and survivor benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      2000, c. 12, s. 176\]]{.Repealed}

  [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of the Senate or
      House of Commons. ([parlementaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the President of the
      Treasury Board. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[pensionable earnings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[pensionable earnings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to any
      member in respect of any calendar year, means the aggregate of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} his or her sessional indemnity for the year,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any annual allowance payable to him or her in
        respect of the year, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any salary payable to the member in respect of
        the year. ([gains ouvrant droit à pension]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by
      regulation. ([Version anglaise
      seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

  [[reduction factor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[reduction factor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to any
      former member, means the number determined by the calculation
      referred to in the description of D in subsection
      37.3(2). ([facteur de réduction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Retiring Allowances Account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Retiring Allowances Account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      Members of Parliament Retiring Allowances Account continued by
      section 3. ([compte d'allocations]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[salary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[salary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a salary payable to a member
      pursuant to section 4 or 4.1 of the [Salaries Act](/eng/acts/S-3)
      or section 60, 61, 62.1 or 62.2 of the [Parliament of Canada
      Act](/eng/acts/P-1), or payable to a member pursuant to an
      appropriation Act as a minister of state or a minister without
      portfolio. ([traitement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[session]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[session]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a session of
      Parliament. ([session]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[sessional indemnity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[sessional indemnity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a period before October 8, 1970,
        the allowances payable to a member under section 55 and
        subsection 63(3) of the [Parliament of Canada
        Act](/eng/acts/P-1), as that subsection read immediately before
        the coming into force of this paragraph,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a period after October 7, 1970
        and before July 8, 1974,

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a member of the Senate, five
          sixths of the allowance payable to the member under section 55
          of the [Parliament of Canada Act](/eng/acts/P-1), and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a member of the House of
          Commons, the allowance payable to the member under section 55
          of the [Parliament of Canada Act](/eng/acts/P-1), and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a period after July 7, 1974, the
        allowance payable to a member under section 55 or 55.1 of the
        [Parliament of Canada Act](/eng/acts/P-1). ([indemnité de
        session]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a member or
      former member, means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who was married

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a member or former member, to
          the member or former member immediately before his or her
          death, and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a former member, to the
          former member immediately before the time when he or she
          ceased to be a member, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who establishes that the person was
        cohabiting in a relationship of a conjugal nature

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a member or former member,
          with the member or former member for at least one year
          immediately before his or her death, and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a former member, with the
          former member before he or she ceased to be a
          member. ([survivant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of average annual sessional
  indemnity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of calculating a member's average
  annual sessional indemnity, any period during which he or she is, by
  virtue of paragraph 12(1)(b) or (c) or 34(1)(b), as it read
  immediately before January 1, 2013, prohibited from contributing in
  respect of the sessional indemnity payable to him or her is deemed to
  be a period of pensionable service to the member's credit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of average annual sessional
  indemnity

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of calculating a member's average
  annual sessional indemnity, any period during which he or she is
  required to make contributions under subsection 12(2) or would be
  required to contribute under that subsection if he or she were under
  71 years of age is deemed to be a period of pensionable service to the
  member's credit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of average annual
  pensionable earnings

  [(2.2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of calculating a member's average
  annual pensionable earnings, any period during which he or she is
  required to make contributions under subsection 12(2.1) or would be
  required to contribute under that subsection if he or she were under
  71 years of age is deemed to be a period of pensionable service to the
  member's credit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Parts I to V to members
  of Senate

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of Parts I to V, except section 58,
  a [[member of the Senate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or a
  [[Senator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who was summoned to the
  Senate after June 1, 1965.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ceasing to be member

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person does not cease to be a member of the House
    of Commons by reason only of a dissolution of the House of Commons;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who, immediately before a dissolution of
    the House of Commons, was a member of that House ceases to be a
    member on the day on which the general election next following the
    dissolution is held, unless that person is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} summoned to the Senate before that general
      election, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} elected as a member at that general election.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When allowances payable

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, every allowance, other than a
  withdrawal allowance, payable to a person under this Act is an annuity
  that begins to be payable to the person immediately on that person
  becoming entitled to the allowance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to fix number

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Actuary may, taking into account the
  percentage fixed for the purposes of subsection 17.1(2), fix a number
  for the purpose of the description of B in paragraph (b) of the
  definition [[earnings limit]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection (1).

## [Election to Continue or Come Under Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-355782 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing under Act

- **[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member of the House of Commons in the thirty-fifth Parliament who is
  contributing under subsection 9(1) or (2), 11(1), 12(2), 31(1), (2) or
  (3), 33(1) or (2) or 34(2) or section 47 on the coming into force of
  this section may, within sixty days after the day on which this
  section comes into force, elect in accordance with subsection 56(2) to
  continue to contribute under all those provisions under which the
  member is contributing at the time of the election.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming under Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who, after the coming into force of this
  section, becomes a member of the House of Commons in the thirty-fifth
  Parliament may, within sixty days after the first day on which that
  House is sitting after that person becomes a member, elect in
  accordance with subsection 56(2) to contribute, from the day on which
  the person becomes a member, under such of subsections 9(1) and (2),
  12(2), 31(1), (2) and (3) and 34(2) and section 47 as are otherwise
  applicable to the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply to a person who was
  entitled to make an election under subsection (1) and did not do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Irrevocable

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An election under this section is irrevocable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed election

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member who, being entitled to make an election
    under subsection (1), dies before doing so is deemed to have elected
    immediately before the death to continue to contribute under all
    those provisions under which the member was contributing at that
    time; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who, being entitled to make an election
    under subsection (2), dies before doing so is deemed to have elected
    immediately before the death to contribute under such of the
    provisions of this Act as would otherwise be applicable to the
    person.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 30, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Act to members making
election

**[[2.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.2 .sectionLabel}** This Act continues to
apply to a member who makes an election under section 2.1.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 30, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Act to members not making
election

- **[[2.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section and sections
  2.4 and 2.5, this Act ceases to apply to a member who, being entitled
  to make an election under section 2.1, does not do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal allowance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} There shall be paid to a member referred to in
  subsection (1) a withdrawal allowance, in a lump sum, equal to the
  aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total amount of the contributions that the
    member has paid under this Act and Parts I, III and IV of the former
    Act, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the interest on those contributions that the member
    has paid under section 11 or paragraph 33(1)(c) or (2)(d) of this
    Act or under section 23 of the former Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reduction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The withdrawal allowance payable to a member who was
  a member for six or more years before October 25, 1993 shall be
  reduced by the amount of the contributions, and the interest thereon,
  that the member has paid in respect of pensionable service as a member
  before that date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Interest shall be paid on the amount of the
  withdrawal allowance and shall be calculated in accordance with
  subsection 63(2) as if a reference to paragraphs (2)(a) and (b) were
  included in paragraph 63(2)(a) and as if the references in that
  subsection to "the year in which the person ceased to be a member"
  were references to "the year in which the allowance became payable".

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 30, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Act to members not making
election who become members again

- **[[2.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Act recommences to apply to a member referred to in subsection 2.3(1)
  who ceases to be a member and subsequently becomes a member in the
  thirty-sixth or any subsequent Parliament.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1998, c. 23, s. 9\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 30, s. 2
- 1998, c. 23, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Act to members not making
election who were vested

- **[[2.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Section 11, subsection 12(3),
  sections 13, 16, 17, 19 to 26, 33, 35 to 37, 39 to 46 and 48 to 55,
  subsection 56(2) and sections 57 to 63 of this Act and section 23 of
  the former Act continue to apply, with such modifications as the
  circumstances require, to a member referred to in subsection 2.3(1)
  who was a member for six or more years before October 25, 1993.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of applying the provisions referred
  to in subsection (1), the member is deemed to have received no
  sessional indemnity for any period after October 24, 1993.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 30, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election to come under Act

- **[[2.6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member of the House of Commons who was entitled to make an election
  under section 2.1 and who did not do so may, within 90 days after the
  day this section comes into force, elect in accordance with subsection
  56(2) to have this Act recommence to apply to the member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) applies only in respect of members of
  the House of Commons who serve continuously in that capacity from the
  day this section comes into force until the day they make their
  election.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Irrevocable

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An election under subsection (1) is irrevocable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Act deemed to be
  continuous

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} This Act is deemed to apply to a member of the House
  of Commons who makes an election under subsection (1) as if it had
  always applied to the member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repayment of withdrawal allowance and
  payment of contributions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A member of the House of Commons who makes an
  election under subsection (1) shall, within 90 days after making the
  election, pay into the Consolidated Revenue Fund, in a lump sum, an
  amount equal to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the withdrawal allowance paid to the member under
    subsection 2.3(2);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any interest on the withdrawal allowance paid to
    the member under subsection 2.3(4); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the contributions that the member would have made
    had the Act not ceased to apply to the member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crediting to accounts

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The amount paid under subsection (5) shall be
  credited to the Retiring Allowances Account and the Compensation
  Arrangements Account in the appropriate amounts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed contributions

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The portion of the amount paid under subsection (5)
  in respect of the withdrawal allowance mentioned in paragraph (5)(a)
  and the contributions mentioned in paragraph (5)(c) are for all
  purposes deemed to be contributions made by the member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-payment cancels election

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} An election under subsection (1) is deemed not to
  have been made if the amount required to be paid under subsection (5)
  is not paid in the time provided, but subsection 57(2) applies if the
  member who made the election dies before the time expires.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1998, c. 23, s. 10
:::

## [Contribution Rates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-355850 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to fix rates

- **[[2.7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Actuary shall fix contribution rates for the purpose of the
  provisions of this Act that require contributions to be made at the
  applicable contribution rate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No distinguishing based on House
  membership

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Actuary shall not distinguish between
  members of the Senate and members of the House of Commons when fixing
  contribution rates for the purpose of any provision of this Act.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2015, c. 36, s. 94\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contribution rate for purpose of
  paragraph 31.1(1)(a) --- 2013 to 2015

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), but subject to subsection
  (9), the applicable contribution rate for the purpose of paragraph
  31.1(1)(a) for the calendar years 2013 to 2015 is 4%.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contribution rates for purpose of
  paragraph 31.1(1)(b) --- 2013 to 2015

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the applicable contribution
  rates for the purpose of paragraph 31.1(1)(b) for the calendar years
  2013 to 2015 are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for 2013, 4%;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for 2014, 5%; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for 2015, 6%.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contribution rates for purpose of
  paragraphs 31.1(1)(c) and (2)(a) and (b) --- 2013 to 2015

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), but subject to
  subsections (7) to (9), the applicable contribution rates for the
  purpose of paragraphs 31.1(1)(c) and (2)(a) and (b) for the calendar
  years 2013 to 2015 are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for 2013, 8%;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for 2014, 9%; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for 2015, 10%.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contribution rate for certain members
  --- 2013 to 2015

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the applicable contribution
  rate for the purpose of paragraph 31.1(1)(c) for the calendar years

### [Survivor Benefits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-356185 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Survivor benefits

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  death of a member or former member, there shall be paid

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the survivor, an allowance equal to three fifths
    of the basic retirement allowance, but, if two survivors are
    entitled to an allowance under this paragraph, the total amount of
    the allowances shall not exceed three fifths of the basic retirement
    allowance and that total amount shall be apportioned in accordance
    with subsection (1.1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to each child, an allowance equal to one tenth of
    the basic retirement allowance or, if the member or former member
    died leaving no one entitled to an allowance under paragraph (a),
    two tenths of the basic retirement allowance, but the total amount
    of the allowances shall not exceed three tenths of the basic
    retirement allowance or, if the member or former member died leaving
    no one entitled to an allowance under paragraph (a), eight tenths of
    the basic retirement allowance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Apportionment

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(a), the total
  amount shall be apportioned so that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the survivor referred to in paragraph (a) of the
    definition [[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1)
    receives an amount, if any, equal to the total amount less any
    amount determined under paragraph (b); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the survivor referred to in paragraph (b) of that
    definition receives an amount equal to that proportion of the total
    amount that the number of years that survivor cohabited with the
    member or former member while a member is of the number of years
    that the member or former member was a member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Years

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} In determining a number of years for the purposes
  of subsection (1.1), part of a year shall be counted as a full year if
  the part is six or more months and shall be ignored if it is less.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[basic retirement
  allowance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[basic retirement
  allowance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a former member, the aggregate of
    all the retirement allowances and additional retirement allowances
    that the former member was receiving under this Part at the time of
    death or would have been entitled to receive under this Part if the
    former member had reached sixty years of age immediately before
    death; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a member, the aggregate of all the
    retirement allowances and additional retirement allowances that the
    member would have been entitled to receive under this Part if the
    member had ceased to be a member and had reached sixty years of age
    immediately before death.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (2), if any amount was
  or would have been subtracted in the calculation of the amount of the
  member or former member's retirement allowance under section 17.1 or
  17.2, the member or former member is deemed to have received or to
  have been entitled to receive, as the case may be, a retirement
  allowance under that section calculated without that subtracted
  amount.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts credited to Account

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Survivor benefits

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  death of a member or former member, there shall be paid

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the survivor an allowance equal to three fifths
    of the basic compensation allowance, but, if more than one survivor
    is entitled to an allowance under this paragraph, the total amount
    of the allowances shall not exceed three fifths of the basic
    compensation allowance and that total amount shall be apportioned in
    accordance with subsection (1.1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to each child, an allowance equal to one tenth of
    the basic compensation allowance or, if the member or former member
    died leaving no one entitled to an allowance under paragraph (a),
    two tenths of the basic compensation allowance, but the total amount
    of the allowances shall not exceed three tenths of the basic
    compensation allowance or, if the member or former member died
    leaving no one entitled to an allowance under paragraph (a), eight
    tenths of the basic compensation allowance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Apportionment

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(a), the total
  amount shall be apportioned so that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the survivor referred to in paragraph (a) of the

    receives an amount, if any, equal to the total amount less any
    amount determined under paragraph (b); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the survivor referred to in paragraph (b) of that
    definition receives an amount equal to that proportion of the total
    amount that the number of years that survivor cohabited with the
    member or former member while a member is of the number of years
    that the member or former member was a member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Years

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} In determining a number of years for the purposes
  of subsection (1.1), a part of a year shall be counted as a full year

  allowance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

other benefit is suspended in respect of any period during which that
person is a member.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[59.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section,

  [[employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the position of an
      individual in the service of some other person, including Her
      Majesty, that entitles the individual to fixed or ascertainable
      salary, fees or other compensation; ([emploi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[federal position]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal position]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an office or employment the salary, fees or
        other compensation for which is paid in whole or in part out of
        the Consolidated Revenue Fund or out of monies appropriated by
        Parliament, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an office or employment in a departmental
        corporation or Crown corporation as defined respectively in
        sections 2 and 83 of the [Financial Administration
        Act](/eng/acts/F-11); ([emploi fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[federal service contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal service contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a contract
      for the provision of service the consideration for the performance
      of which is paid in whole or in part out of the Consolidated
      Revenue Fund or out of monies appropriated by Parliament or by a
      departmental corporation or Crown corporation as defined
      respectively in sections 2 and 83 of the [Financial Administration
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11), but does not include an agreement under
      which an individual is engaged as an employee; ([marché fédéral de
      services]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the position of an individual
      that entitles the individual to fixed or ascertainable salary,
      fees or other compensation, and includes a judicial or diplomatic
      office and the position of corporation
      director; ([charge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[remuneration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[remuneration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a federal position, the salary,
        fees or other compensation paid for or in respect of the
        carrying out of the duties and functions of the position, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a federal service contract, the
        consideration paid for the performance of the
        contract. ([rémunération]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, where a former
  member controls a partnership, corporation, association or other body
  that enters into a federal service contract,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the former member is deemed to have entered into
    the contract at the time the body did so and to be a party to the
    contract so long as the body is a party and the former member
    continues to control it; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the remuneration in respect of the contract is
    deemed to equal the amount of the salary, fees or other compensation
    paid to the former member for or in respect of the services provided
    by the former member under the contract.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every former member who, on or after July 13, 1995,
  commences to hold a federal position or enters into a federal service
  contract and who is receiving or commences to receive an allowance or
  other benefit under Part I, II, III or IV, other than a withdrawal
  allowance or an allowance under paragraph 20(1)(a), subsection 25(3),
  paragraph 40(1)(a) or subsection 45(3), 49(1) or 49.1(2), shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within sixty days after the later of the day on
    which the former member commenced to hold the position or entered
    into the contract and the day on which the former member commenced
    to receive the allowance or other benefit, notify the Minister in
    writing of the position or contract and the amount of the
    remuneration;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within sixty days after each anniversary of the
    later day referred to in paragraph (a) and while holding the
    position or being a party to the contract, notify the Minister in
    writing of the total amount of the remuneration received in the
    preceding year; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} within sixty days after ceasing to hold the
    position or to be a party to the contract or the contract is
    completed or otherwise terminated, notify the Minister in writing of
    the cessation or termination and the total amount of the
    remuneration received that was not previously reported under this
    section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A former member referred to in subsection (3) shall
  furnish the Minister in writing with such additional information
  respecting the federal position or federal service contract as the
  Minister may require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reduction

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a former member referred to in subsection (3)
  receives remuneration of \$5,000 or more in any year beginning on the
  day on which the former member commenced holding the federal position
  or entered into the federal service contract, or on any anniversary of
  that day, the aggregate of the allowances or other benefits referred
  to in that subsection payable to that former member in that year shall
  be reduced by one dollar for each dollar of the remuneration received
  in that year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the reduction under subsection (5) may
  be recovered in accordance with the regulations from any allowance or
  other benefit payable to or in respect of the former member under this
  Act or otherwise, without prejudice to any other recourse available to
  Her Majesty with respect to the recovery of the amount.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reductions ignored for certain purposes

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The amount of an allowance or other benefit payable
  under section 20, 25, 40, 45, 49, 49.1 or 51 to or in respect of a
  former member to whom this section applies or applied shall be
  determined as if no reduction were made under this section to the
  allowances or other benefits payable to the former member.

- 1995, c. 30, s. 20
- 2000, c. 12, s. 183

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefits not assignable, etc.

**[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the [Pension
Benefits Division Act](/eng/acts/P-6.7) and to Part II of the

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an allowance or other benefit under this Act is not
  capable of being assigned, charged, anticipated or given as security
  and any transaction that purports to assign, charge, anticipate or
  give as security any such allowance or other benefit is void;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an allowance or other benefit to which a person is
  entitled under this Act is not capable of being surrendered or
  commuted during the lifetime of that person and any transaction that
  purports to do so is void; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an allowance or other benefit under this Act is
  exempt from attachment, seizure and execution, either at law or in
  equity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum benefit

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** Where a member or former
member dies and there is no person to whom an allowance or other benefit
may be paid in respect of that member, or the persons to whom an
allowance or other benefit may be paid die or cease to be entitled
thereto, any amount by which the total amount of the contributions paid
by the member or former member under Parts I to III exceeds the
aggregate of all amounts paid to those persons and the member or former
member under those Parts or this Part as allowances or other benefits
and under Part IV as supplementary benefits shall be paid, as a death
benefit, to the estate of that member or former member or, if the amount
to be paid is less than \$1,000, shall be paid as authorized by the
Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1992, c. 46, s. 81
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption as to death

- **[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a member or former member or any person entitled to an allowance or
  other benefit under this Act has, either before or after December 31,
  1992, disappeared under circumstances that, in the opinion of the
  Minister, raise beyond a reasonable doubt a presumption that the
  person is dead, the Minister may, for the purposes of this Act,
  determine the date on which that person's death is presumed to have
  occurred, and thereupon that person shall be deemed for all purposes
  of this Act to have died on that date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of date

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} After having determined the date of a person's death
  pursuant to subsection (1), the Minister may, on the basis of new
  information or evidence that the date of death is different from that
  determined, determine a different date of death, in which case the
  person shall be deemed for all purposes of this Act to have died on
  that different date.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 1989, c. 6, s. 34

  - ###### Resumption of allowances to spouses {#resumption-of-allowances-to-spouses .MarginalNote}

    [34]{.lawlabel} Where, before the coming into force of this Act,
    payment of an allowance, annuity or pension to a spouse or surviving
    spouse was suspended or ceased, on remarriage of the spouse or
    surviving spouse, pursuant to subsection 25(2) of the [Public
    Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), section 27 of the
    [Canadian Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17), subsection
    30(2) of the [Defence Services Pension Continuation
    Act](/eng/acts/D-1.3), subsection 9(5) of the [Diplomatic Service
    (Special) Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/D-2), subsection 14(2),
    19(4) or 31(5) of the [Members of Parliament Retiring Allowances
    Act](/eng/acts/M-5), subsection 38.1(2) or 39(2) of the [Members of
    Parliament Retiring Allowances Act](/eng/acts/M-5), R.S.C. 1970, c.
    M-10 (as amended by R.S.C. 1970, c. 25 (1st Supp.), ss. 14 and 15),
    subsection 24(2) of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Pension
    Continuation Act](/eng/acts/R-10.6) or section 16 of the [Royal
    Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11), as that
    provision read from time to time before the coming into force of
    this Act, payment of the allowance, annuity or pension, as the case
    may be, to the spouse or surviving spouse shall, subject to that
    Act, be resumed on and with effect from the day on which this Act
    comes into force.
