## National Film Act {#national-film-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. N-8
:::

An Act respecting the National Film Board
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-382681 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [National Film Act](/eng/acts/N-8).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-7, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-382686 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the National Film Board
    established by section 4; ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Government Film
    Commissioner appointed under section
    16; ([commissaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any department or board of
    the Government of Canada and any agent of Her Majesty in right of
    Canada; ([département]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[film]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[film]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means motion pictures, still
    photographs, photographic displays, filmstrips and such other forms
    of visual presentation as consist primarily of photographs or
    photographic reproductions; ([film]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[film activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[film activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any activity in relation
    to the production, distribution, projection or exhibition of
    film; ([activité filmique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada as is designated by the Governor in Council
    as the Minister for the purposes of this
    Act. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-7, s. 2
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 17, s. 17
:::

## [Responsibility of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-382697 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibility of Minister

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Act and subject to its provisions, the Minister shall control and direct
the operations of the National Film Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-7, s. 3
:::

## [National Film Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-382702 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National Film Board

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a National Film Board, consisting of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Commissioner, who shall be Chairperson, and six
    other members, to be appointed by the Governor in Council; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the executive director of Telefilm Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure of office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each member of the Board, other than the Commissioner
  and the executive director of Telefilm Canada, holds office for three
  years, but may be removed for cause by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A retiring member of the Board is eligible for
  re-appointment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Casual vacancy

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} When, before the end of the term for which a member
  of the Board was appointed, the member ceases to be a member thereof,
  the Governor in Council shall appoint a person to be a member of the
  Board for the remainder of that term.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-8, s. 4
- 1995, c. 29, s. 57
- 2002, c. 17, s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person who has any
pecuniary interest, direct or indirect, individually or as a shareholder
or partner or otherwise, in commercial film activity is eligible for
appointment to the Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-7, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees and expenses

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A member of the Board,
other than the Commissioner, the executive director of Telefilm Canada
or a member of the Board who is a member of the federal public
administration, may be paid such fee for each meeting of the Board that
the member attends as may be fixed by by-law of the Board, and the
members of the Board are entitled to be paid actual travel and living
expenses necessarily incurred in connection with the business of the
Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-8, s. 6
- 1995, c. 29, s. 58
- 2002, c. 17, s. 14
- 2003, c. 22, s. 224(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall meet at the call of the Chairperson of the Board but, in
  any event, not more than three months shall elapse between meetings of
  the Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1995, c. 29, s. 59\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voting

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each member of the Board has one vote in the
  transaction of the business of the Board, and if the number of votes
  is equal the Chairperson has an additional vote.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister to get minutes

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson of the Board shall furnish the
  Minister with a copy of the minutes of each meeting of the Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-8, s. 7
- 1995, c. 29, s. 59
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Board, with the
approval of the Minister, may make by-laws with respect to the conduct
of the business of the Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-7, s. 7
:::

## [Purposes of the Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-382738 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purposes for which Board established

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Board is established to
initiate and promote the production and distribution of films in the
national interest and, in particular,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to produce and distribute and to promote the
  production and distribution of films designed to interpret Canada to
  Canadians and to other nations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to represent the Government of Canada in its
  relations with persons engaged in commercial motion picture film
  activity in connection with motion picture films for the Government or
  any department thereof;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to engage in research in film activity and to make
  available the results thereof to persons engaged in the production of
  films;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to advise the Governor in Council in connection with
  film activities; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} to discharge such other duties relating to film
  activity as the Governor in Council may direct the Board to undertake.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-7, s. 9
:::

## [Powers of Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-382748 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Board

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the direction and control
  of the Minister, the Board may, for the purposes for which it is
  established,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make, project, exhibit or distribute or cause to be
    made, projected, exhibited or distributed films in Canada or
    elsewhere on behalf of the Board or on behalf of other departments
    or persons;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine the manner in which moneys available to
    the Board for the production of a film may best be expended in the
    production thereof;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} acquire personal property and movable property in
    the name of the Board;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} enter into contracts in the name of the Board,
    including contracts for personal services;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} dispose of personal property and movable property
    held in the name of the Board or administered by the Board on behalf
    of Her Majesty, in processed form or otherwise, at the price and on
    the terms that the Board considers advisable;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} acquire in the name of the Board copyrights in any
    literary, musical or artistic works, plays, songs, recordings or
    films;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} acquire in the name of the Board and use any patent
    or patent rights, licences or concessions;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} make arrangements or agreements with any person or
    organization for the use of any rights, privileges or concessions;
    and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} do such other acts and things as are necessary for
    or incidental to the purposes for which the Board is established.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal proceedings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Actions, suits or other legal proceedings in respect
  of any right or obligation acquired or incurred by the Board on behalf
  of Her Majesty, whether in its name or in the name of Her Majesty, may
  be brought or taken by or against the Board in the name of the Board
  in any court that would have jurisdiction if the Board were a
  corporation that is not an agent of Her Majesty.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-8, s. 10
- 2001, c. 4, s. 107
:::

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, 1996, c. 16,
s. 44\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on entry into contracts

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding anything
in this Act, the Board shall not, unless the approval of the Treasury
Board has been obtained on the recommendation of the Minister, enter
into a contract involving an estimated expenditure in excess of such
amount as the Treasury Board may determine.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-7, s. 12
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 47, s. 32
- 1984, c. 40, s. 49
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Plan of organization

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may, with the approval of the Treasury Board obtained on the
  recommendation of the Minister, formulate a plan of organization for
  the establishment and classification of the continuing positions
  necessary for the proper functioning of the Board and the
  establishment of rates of compensation for each class of position,
  having regard to the rates of compensation and conditions of
  employment for comparable positions outside the federal public
  administration and in other branches of the federal public
  administration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of plan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may, with the approval of the Treasury
  Board obtained on the recommendation of the Minister, amend or vary a
  plan approved under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of employees

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the plan of organization approved under
  this section, the Board may appoint persons for a term or during
  pleasure to fill the positions established by the plan, prescribe
  their conditions of employment and provide for their promotion, salary
  and salary increases, but the provisions of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) relating to political activities
  and, where applicable, the condition of employment relating to payment
  of gratuity on death pursuant to the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11) apply to the persons appointed under this
  section.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2002, c. 17, s. 21\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-8, s. 13
- 2002, c. 17, s. 21
- 2003, c. 22, ss. 175(E), 240
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oath

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Each officer or employee
employed by the Board under section 13 shall, before entering on his
duties, take an oath of office and secrecy in the form set out in the
schedule.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-7, s. 13
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary employees

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Board may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} employ such persons in positions other than in
  continuing positions in the plan approved under section 13 as may be
  required from time to time for the operations of the Board; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine the remuneration and conditions of
  employment of those persons.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-7, s. 14
:::

## [Government Film Commissioner]{.HTitleText1} {#h-382790 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment and salary

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be a Government Film Commissioner who shall be appointed by the
  Governor in Council on the recommendation of the Board and paid such
  salary as the Governor in Council may determine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term and tenure of office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall be appointed to hold office
  for a period not exceeding five years but may be removed from office
  for cause by the Governor in Council on the recommendation of the
  Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On the expiration of the Commissioner's term of
  office, the Commissioner is eligible to be re-appointed as
  Commissioner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence, inability or vacancy in office
  of Commissioner

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Commissioner is absent or unable to carry out
  the Commissioner's duties for any reason or the office of Commissioner
  is vacant, the Board may, subject to the approval of the Minister,
  appoint an acting Commissioner.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-7, s. 15
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissioner is chief executive officer,
to administer operations and exercise powers, of Board

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner is the chief executive officer of the Board and is
  charged with the administration of the operations of the Board and
  may, subject to the by-laws of the Board, exercise all powers of the
  Board in the name of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority of officers and employees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the by-laws of the Board, the Commissioner
  may authorize officers or employees of the Board to act on behalf of
  and in the name of the Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-7, s. 16
:::

## [Financial Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-382809 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual budget

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall submit to the Minister an annual budget for each fiscal
  year showing the estimated revenues and expenditures of the Board for
  its operations during that fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accounts of Board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall establish and maintain an accounting
  system satisfactory to the Minister and all books of account, records
  and papers of the Board shall at all times be open to the inspection
  of the Minister or such persons as the Minister may designate.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-7, s. 17
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11)

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided in this Act, the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)
applies in respect of operations under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-7, s. 19
:::

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-382821 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As
  soon as is practicable after the end of each fiscal year, the Board
  shall submit to the Minister a report of the operations of the Board
  for the fiscal year in such form as the Minister may prescribe.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laying before Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall lay the annual report of the Board
  made under subsection (1) before Parliament on any of the first
  fourteen days on which either House of Parliament is sitting after the
  Minister receives it.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-7, s. 20
- 1977-78, c. 22, s. 17
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports to Minister

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall furnish
the Minister with such statements or reports in addition to those
required by this Act in respect of its business or operations, at such
times and in such manner, as the Minister may require.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-7, s. 21
:::
