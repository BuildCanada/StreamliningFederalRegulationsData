## Canada Council for the Arts Act {#canada-council-for-the-arts-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. C-2
:::

An Act for the establishment of a Canadian council for the encouragement
of the arts
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73261 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada Council for the Arts Act](/eng/acts/C-2).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-2, s. 1
- 2001, c. 34, s. 14(E)
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73266 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[arts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[arts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes architecture, the arts of the
    theatre, literature, music, painting, sculpture, the graphic arts
    and other similar creative and interpretative
    activities; ([arts]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the corporation established by
    section 3. ([Conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-2, s. 2
:::

## [Canada Council for the Arts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73273 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of Council

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby established
a corporation, to be known as the Canada Council for the Arts,
consisting of a Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson and not more than nine
other members, to be appointed by the Governor in Council as provided in
section 4.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-2, s. 3
- 1995, c. 29, s. 6
- 2001, c. 34, s. 15(E)
:::

## [Members of the Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73278 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson of the Council shall be appointed for
  such terms, not exceeding five years each, as are fixed by the
  Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each of the other members of the Council shall be
  appointed for a term of four years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility for re-appointment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who has served two consecutive terms as
  Chairperson of the Council, as Vice-Chairperson thereof or as a member
  appointed under subsection (2) is not, during the twelve months
  following the completion of the second term, eligible to be
  re-appointed to the Council in the same capacity.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-2, s. 4
- 1995, c. 29, s. 10(E)
- 2006, c. 9, s. 230
:::

## [Director]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73288 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Director

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be a Director of the Council to be appointed by the Governor in
  Council to hold office during pleasure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chief executive officer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Director is the chief executive officer of the
  Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-2, s. 5
- 1995, c. 29, s. 7
- 2009, c. 2, s. 376

## [Remuneration and Allowances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73296 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson and Director of the Council shall be
  paid such salary or other amount by way of remuneration, and the
  members of the Council appointed under subsection 4(2) shall be paid
  such allowance for each day while in attendance at meetings of the
  Council, as may be fixed by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Travel and living expenses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The members and Director of the Council are entitled
  to be paid reasonable travel and living expenses incurred by them
  while absent from their ordinary places of residence in the course of
  their duties.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-2, s. 6
- 1995, c. 29, s. 7
:::

## [Employees and Advisers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73304 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Council may appoint and
pay the remuneration and expenses of the employees and the technical and
professional advisers necessary for the proper conduct of the activities
of the Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-2, s. 7
:::

## [Objects, Powers and Duties of the Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73309 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects and powers

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  objects of the Council are to foster and promote the study and
  enjoyment of, and the production of works in, the arts and, in
  particular, but without limiting the generality of the foregoing, the
  Council may, in furtherance of its objects,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assist, cooperate with and enlist the aid of
    organizations the objects of which are similar to any of the objects
    of the Council;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide, through appropriate organizations or
    otherwise, for grants, scholarships or loans to persons in Canada
    for study or research in the arts in Canada or elsewhere or to
    persons in other countries for study or research in the arts in
    Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} make awards to persons in Canada for outstanding
    accomplishment in the arts;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} arrange for and sponsor exhibitions, performances
    and publications of works in the arts;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} make grants to universities and similar
    institutions of higher learning by way of capital assistance in
    respect of building construction projects;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} exchange with other countries or organizations or
    persons therein knowledge and information respecting the arts; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} arrange for representation and interpretation of
    Canadian arts in other countries.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties in relation to UNESCO

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may assign to the Council
  such functions and duties in relation to the United Nations
  Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization as the Governor in
  Council considers desirable.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-2, ss. 8, 9
- 1976-77, c. 24, s. 23
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Council may make
by-laws regulating its proceedings and generally for the conduct and
management of its activities, including the appointment of honorary
officers and advisory committees.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-2, s. 10
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings of Council

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Council shall meet at
least three times a year in the city of Ottawa on such days as are fixed
by the Council and at such other times and places as the Council deems
necessary.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-2, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Superannuation

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Director and
employees of the Council shall be deemed to be employed in the public
service for the purposes of the [Public Service Superannuation
Act](/eng/acts/P-36), and the Council shall be deemed to be a Public
Service corporation for the purposes of section 37 of that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-2, s. 11
- 1995, c. 29, s. 8
- 2003, c. 22, s. 225(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not agent of Her Majesty or part of
federal public administration

agent of Her Majesty and, except as provided in section 11, the members,
Director and employees of the Council are not part of the federal public

## [Financial Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73340 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Endowment Fund

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Finance
may, out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund, pay to the Council the sum of
fifty million dollars, which shall constitute an Endowment Fund for the
purposes of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-2, s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenditures

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Any expenditure made for
any of the purposes of this Act, except paragraph 8(1)(e), may be paid
out of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the return on investments made out of the Endowment
  Fund; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} money, securities or other property received by the
  Council by gift, bequest or otherwise, if available for that purpose.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-2, s. 16
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}University Capital Grants Fund

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Council shall establish a fund to be called the University Capital
  Grants Fund, to which shall be credited the sum of fifty million
  dollars, which shall be paid to the Council by the Minister of Finance
  out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capital assistance to universities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Grants made by the Council pursuant to paragraph
  8(1)(e) may be paid out of the University Capital Grants Fund but
  shall not exceed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any particular project, one-half of
    the total expenditures made in respect of the project; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any province, an amount that is in the same
    proportion to the aggregate of the amounts credited to the
    University Capital Grants Fund as the population of the province,
    according to the latest census, is to the aggregate population,
    according to that census, of those provinces in which there is a
    university or other similar institution of higher learning.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on investments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Investments out of money standing to the credit of
  the University Capital Grants Fund may be made only in bonds or other
  securities of or guaranteed by the Government of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-2, s. 17
:::

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, 1995, c. 29,
s. 9\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property and investments

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Council may, for the purposes of this Act, acquire, hold, manage and
  dispose of real, personal, movable and immovable property and, subject
  to this Act and on the advice of the Investment Committee, may invest
  in any manner it sees fit any money standing to the credit of the
  Endowment Fund or the University Capital Grants Fund or received by
  the Council by gift, bequest or otherwise, and may hold, manage and
  dispose of the investment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceeds of disposal of investments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The proceeds of the sale or other disposition of any
  investment made out of the Endowment Fund or the University Capital
  Grants Fund shall be credited to the fund out of which the investment
  was made.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-2, s. 17
- 2001, c. 4, s. 65
- 2004, c. 25, s. 104(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Donations

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Council may acquire
money, securities or other property by gift or bequest or otherwise and
may, notwithstanding anything in this Act, expend, administer or dispose
of any such money, securities or other property not forming part of the
Endowment Fund or the University Capital Grants Fund, subject to the
terms, if any, on which the money, securities or other property was
given, bequeathed or otherwise made available to the Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Council deemed registered charity

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Council shall, for
the purposes of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), be deemed to be a
registered charity within the meaning of that expression for the

## [Audit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73378 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audit

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The accounts and
financial transactions of the Council shall be audited annually by the
Auditor General of Canada and a report of the audit shall be made to the
Council and to the member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada
designated under subsection 21(1).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-2, s. 22
:::

## [Report to Parliament]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73383 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson of the Council shall, within three months after the
  termination of each fiscal year, submit to the member of the Queen's
  Privy Council for Canada designated by the Governor in Council for the
  purpose a report of all proceedings under this Act for that fiscal
  year, including the financial statements of the Council and the report
  thereon of the Auditor General of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The member designated under subsection (1) shall
  cause the report submitted under that subsection to be laid before
  Parliament within fifteen days after the receipt thereof by the member
  or, if Parliament is not then sitting, on any of the first fifteen
  days next thereafter that either House of Parliament is sitting and
  provision shall be made for a review thereof by Parliament.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-2, s. 21
- 1995, c. 29, s. 10(E)
