## [Federal Public Sector Labour Relations and Employment Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-406812 .Part}

### [Continuance and Composition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-406813 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuance of Board

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Public Service Labour Relations and Employment Board is continued
  under the name of the Federal Public Sector Labour Relations and
  Employment Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board's composition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board is composed of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Chairperson, who is to hold office on a full-time
    basis;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} not more than two Vice-chairpersons, who are to
    hold office on a full-time basis;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} not more than 12 other members who are to hold
    office on a full-time basis; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any part-time members that the Governor in Council
    considers necessary to carry out the Board's powers, duties and
    functions.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 40, s. 365 "4"
- 2017, c. 9, s. 38

### [Appointment of Members]{.HTitleText2} {#h-406825 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To be
  eligible to hold office as a member, a person must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be a Canadian citizen within the meaning of the
    [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29) or a permanent resident as defined
    in subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} not hold any other office or employment under the
    employer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} not be a member of or hold an office or employment
    under an employee organization, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
    [Federal Public Sector Labour Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-33.3), that
    is certified as a bargaining agent; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} not accept any office or employment, or carry on
    any activity, that is inconsistent with the person's duties or
    functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(b), a person is not ineligible
  to hold office as a member by reason only of holding office as a
  member of any board that may be constituted by the Commissioner in
  Council of the Northwest Territories or the Legislature of Yukon or
  the Legislature for Nunavut with powers, duties and functions similar
  to those of the Board.

- 2013, c. 40, s. 365 "5"

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointments of other members from list

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  member, other than the Chairperson or a Vice-chairperson, must be
  appointed from among eligible persons whose names are on a list
  prepared by the Chairperson after consultation with the employer and
  the bargaining agents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Knowledge of police organizations

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} In preparing the list, the Chairperson must take
  into account the need for the Board to have two members with knowledge
  of police organizations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson must set out on the list

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the names of all eligible persons who are
    recommended by the employer;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the names of all eligible persons who are
    recommended by the bargaining agents; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the names of any other eligible persons whom the
    Chairperson considers suitable for appointment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equal numbers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The appointment of members, other than the
  Chairperson and the Vice-chairpersons, is to be made so as to ensure
  that, to the extent possible, an equal number are appointed from among
  persons recommended by the employer and from among persons recommended
  by the bargaining agents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-representative Board

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite being recommended by the employer or the
  bargaining agents, a member does not represent either the employer or
  the employees and must act impartially in the exercise of their powers
  and the performance of their duties and functions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Residence of full-time members

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A full-time member must
reside in the National Capital Region as it is described in the schedule
to the [National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4) or within any distance of
it that the Governor in Council may determine.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of members

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  member is to be appointed by the Governor in Council, on the
  Minister's recommendation, to hold office during good behaviour and
  may be removed by the Governor in Council for cause.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A full-time member may be appointed for a term of
  office that is not more than five years and a part-time member may be
  appointed for a term of office that is not more than three years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A member is eligible for reappointment on the expiry
  of any term of office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Completion of duties and functions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person who ceases to be a member for any reason
  other than removal may, at the request of the Chairperson, within
  eight weeks after ceasing to be a member, carry out and complete any
  duties or functions that they would otherwise have had in connection
  with any matter that came before the Board while they were still a
  member and in respect of which there was any proceeding in which they
  participated as a member. For that purpose, the person is deemed to be
  a part-time member.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oath or solemn affirmation

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Before beginning their
duties or functions, a person who is appointed as a member of the Board
must take an oath or make a solemn affirmation in the following form
before a commissioner of oaths or other person having authority to
administer oaths or solemn affirmations:

I, ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}, do swear (or solemnly
affirm) that I will faithfully, truly and impartially, to the best of my
judgment, skill and ability, execute and perform the office of member
(or Chairperson or Vice-chairperson) of the Federal Public Sector Labour
Relations and Employment Board.

### [Remuneration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-406869 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every member and former
member referred to in subsection 8(4)

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is to be paid the remuneration that may be determined
  by the Governor in Council; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is entitled to be paid reasonable travel and other
  expenses incurred by them in the course of their duties while absent
  from, in the case of full-time members, their ordinary place of work
  and, in the case of part-time members, their ordinary place of
  residence.

### [Application of Other Acts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-406874 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of [Public Service
Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36)

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A full-time member is
deemed to be employed in the public service for the purposes of the
[Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of certain Acts

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A member is deemed to be
an employee for the purposes of the [Government Employees Compensation
Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and to be employed in the federal public
administration for the purposes of regulations made under section 9 of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Services and facilities

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** In exercising its powers
and performing its duties and functions, the Board may use any services
and facilities of departments, boards and agencies of the Government of
Canada that are appropriate for the Board's operation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Meetings of the Board are to be held
  at any date, time and place that the Chairperson considers appropriate
  for the conduct of the Board's business.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Off-site participation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A meeting of the Board may be held by any means of
  telecommunication that permits all persons who are participating to
  communicate adequately with each other. A person who is participating
  by such means is deemed to be present at the meeting.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Chairperson, one
Vice-chairperson and a majority of the other full-time members
constitute a quorum at a meeting of the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attendance of part-time members at
meetings

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A part-time member is not
entitled to attend a meeting of the Board, but may attend at the
Chairperson's invitation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision of majority

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A decision of a majority
of the Board's members who are present at a meeting of the Board is a
decision of the Board.

### [Board's Powers, Duties and Functions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-406897 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Board is to exercise
the powers and perform the duties and functions that are conferred or
imposed on it by this Act or any other Act of Parliament.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Board

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The Board has, in
relation to any matter before it, the power to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} summon and enforce the attendance of witnesses and
  compel them to give oral or written evidence on oath in the same
  manner as a superior court of record;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} order pre-hearing procedures, including pre-hearing
  conferences that are held in private, and determine the date, time and
  place of the hearings for those procedures;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} order that a pre-hearing conference or a hearing be
  conducted using any means of telecommunication that permits all
  persons who are participating to communicate adequately with each
  other;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} administer oaths and solemn affirmations;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} accept any evidence, whether admissible in a court of
  law or not; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} compel, at any stage of a proceeding, any person to
  produce the documents and things that may be relevant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Frivolous matters

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The Board may dismiss
summarily any matter that in its opinion is trivial, frivolous,
vexatious or was made in bad faith.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination without oral hearing

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The Board may decide any
matter before it without holding an oral hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General power to assist parties

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Board or a member of
the Board or an employee of the Administrative Tribunals Support Service
of Canada who is authorized by the Board may, if the parties agree,
assist the parties in resolving any issues in dispute at any stage of a
proceeding and by any means that the Board considers appropriate,
without prejudice to the Board's power to determine issues that have not
been settled.

  course of the exercise or performance or purported exercise or

### [Review and Enforcement of Orders and Decisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-406959 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No review by court

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  order or decision of the Board is final and is not to be questioned or
  reviewed in any court, except in accordance with the [Federal Courts
  Act](/eng/acts/F-7) on the grounds referred to in paragraph
  18.1(4)(a), (b) or (e) of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standing of Board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board has standing to appear in proceedings under
  subsection (1) for the purpose of making submissions regarding the
  standard of review to be used with respect to its orders or decisions
  and its jurisdiction, policies and procedures.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No review by [certiorari]{.otherLang
  lang="la"}, etc.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Except as permitted by subsection (1), no order,
  decision or proceeding of the Board made or carried on under or
  purporting to be made or carried on under any Act of Parliament may,
  on any ground, including the ground that the order, decision or
  proceeding is beyond the Board's jurisdiction to make or carry on or
  that, in the course of any proceeding, the Board for any reason
  exceeded or lost its jurisdiction,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be questioned, reviewed, prohibited or restrained;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be made the subject of any proceedings in or any
    process of any court, whether by way of injunction,
    [certiorari]{.otherLang lang="la"}, prohibition, [quo
    warranto]{.otherLang lang="la"} or otherwise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of Board's orders in Federal Court

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board must, on the written request of any person or organization
  affected by any order of the Board, file a certified copy of the
  order, exclusive of the reasons for it, in the Federal Court, unless,
  in the Board's opinion,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is no indication, or likelihood, of failure
    to comply with the order; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is another good reason why the filing of the
    order in the Federal Court would serve no useful purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of filing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An order of the Board becomes an order of the Federal
  Court when a certified copy of it is filed in that court, and it may
  subsequently be enforced as such.

### [Regulations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-406976 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** The Board may make
regulations respecting

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the practice and procedure for hearings and
  pre-hearing proceedings of the Board;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the use of any means of telecommunication in the
  conduct of its activities;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the hearing or determination of any application,
  complaint, question or dispute that may be made to, referred to or
  otherwise come before the Board;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the establishment of an expeditious procedure and
  matters that may be determined under that procedure;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the forms to be used in respect of any proceeding
  that may come before the Board;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which and the period during which
  evidence and information may be presented to the Board in connection
  with any proceeding that may come before it;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the time within which and the persons to whom
  notices, other than those referred to in subsections 130(1) and (2) of
  the Public Service Labour Relations Act, and other documents must be
  sent or given, and when the notices are deemed to have been sent,
  given or received; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} any other matters or things that are incidental or
  conducive to the exercise of the Board's powers and the performance of
  its duties and functions.

### [Panels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-406987 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Panels

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), matters
  of which the Board is seized are to be heard by a panel consisting of
  one member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Three-member panels

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Chairperson considers that the complexity of a
  matter requires it, he or she may assign the matter to a panel
  consisting of three members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson of three-member panel

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Chairperson is a member of a three-person
  panel, he or she is to be its chairperson; otherwise, he or she must
  designate a member of it to be its chairperson.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Member's death or incapacity

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  event of the death or incapacity of a member of a three-member panel,
  other than the death or incapacity of the chairperson of the panel,
  the chairperson of the panel may determine any matter that was before
  the panel and his or her decision is deemed to be the panel's
  decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson's death or incapacity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the death or incapacity of the
  chairperson of a panel, or of the member when the panel consists of
  one member, the Chairperson must establish a new panel to hear and
  determine the matter on any terms and conditions that the Chairperson
  may specify for the protection and preservation of the rights and
  interests of the parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, rights and privileges

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** A panel has all of the
Board's powers, rights and privileges with respect to any matter
assigned to the panel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Panel's decision

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  decision made by a majority of the members of a panel is the decision
  of the panel or, if no decision is supported by the majority, the
  decision of the chairperson of the panel is the decision of the panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board's decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A decision of a panel is a decision of the Board.

### [Witness Fees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-407007 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of witness fees

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** A person who is summoned
by the Board to attend as a witness at any of its proceedings is
entitled to receive fees and allowances for so attending that are equal
to those to which the person would be entitled if they were summoned to
attend before the Federal Court.

### [Annual Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-407010 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to prepare report

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As
  soon as feasible after the end of each fiscal year, the Board must
  prepare and submit to the Minister a report on its activities during
  the immediately preceding fiscal year, other than its activities under
  the [Parliamentary Employment and Staff Relations
  Act](/eng/acts/P-1.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling in Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must cause the report to be tabled in
  each House of Parliament within the first 15 days on which that House
  is sitting after the Minister receives it.
