## Corruption of Foreign Public Officials Act {#corruption-of-foreign-public-officials-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1998, c. 34
:::

Assented to 1998-12-10

An Act respecting the corruption of foreign public officials and the
implementation of the Convention on Combating Bribery of Foreign Public
Officials in International Business Transactions, and to make related
amendments to other Acts
::::

::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-112297 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Corruption of Foreign Public Officials Act](/eng/acts/C-45.2).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-112300 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Act.

[[business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any business, profession,
    trade, calling, manufacture or undertaking of any kind carried on in
    Canada or elsewhere. ([affaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[foreign public official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foreign public official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who holds a legislative, administrative
      or judicial position of a foreign state;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who performs public duties or functions
      for a foreign state, including a person employed by a board,
      commission, corporation or other body or authority that is
      established to perform a duty or function on behalf of the foreign
      state, or is performing such a duty or function; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an official or agent of a public international
      organization that is formed by two or more states or governments,
      or by two or more such public international organizations. ([agent
      public étranger]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[foreign state]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foreign state]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a country other than
    Canada, and includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any political subdivision of that country;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the government, and any department or branch, of
      that country or of a political subdivision of that country; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any agency of that country or of a political
      subdivision of that country. ([État étranger]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2013, c. 26, s.
    2\]]{.Repealed}

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person as defined in section
    2 of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([quiconque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- 1998, c. 34, s. 2
- 2013, c. 26, s. 2

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-112316 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bribing a foreign public official

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person commits an offence who, in order to obtain or retain an
  advantage in the course of business, directly or indirectly gives,
  offers or agrees to give or offer a loan, reward, advantage or benefit
  of any kind to a foreign public official or to any person for the
  benefit of a foreign public official

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as consideration for an act or omission by the
    official in connection with the performance of the official's duties
    or functions; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to induce the official to use his or her position
    to influence any acts or decisions of the foreign state or public
    international organization for which the official performs duties or
    functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes subsection (1) is guilty
  of an indictable offence and liable to imprisonment for a term of not
  more than 14 years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving provision

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person is guilty of an offence under
  subsection (1) if the loan, reward, advantage or benefit

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is permitted or required under the laws of the
    foreign state or public international organization for which the
    foreign public official performs duties or functions; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} was made to pay the reasonable expenses incurred in
    good faith by or on behalf of the foreign public official that are
    directly related to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the promotion, demonstration or explanation of
      the person's products and services, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the execution or performance of a contract
      between the person and the foreign state for which the official
      performs duties or functions.

- [(4) and (5)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2013, c. 26, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- 1998, c. 34, s. 3
- 2013, c. 26, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accounting

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person commits an offence who, for the purpose of bribing a foreign
  public official in order to obtain or retain an advantage in the
  course of business or for the purpose of hiding that bribery,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establishes or maintains accounts which do not
    appear in any of the books and records that they are required to
    keep in accordance with applicable accounting and auditing
    standards;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} makes transactions that are not recorded in those
    books and records or that are inadequately identified in them;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} records non-existent expenditures in those books
    and records;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} enters liabilities with incorrect identification of
    their object in those books and records;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} knowingly uses false documents; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} intentionally destroys accounting books and records
    earlier than permitted by law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes subsection (1) is guilty
  of an indictable offence and liable to imprisonment for a term of not
  more than 14 years.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1998, c. 34, s. 4
- 2001, c. 32, s. 58
- 2013, c. 26, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence committed outside Canada

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who commits an act or omission outside Canada that, if
  committed in Canada, would constitute an offence under section 3 or 4
  --- or a conspiracy to commit, an attempt to commit, being an
  accessory after the fact in relation to, or any counselling in
  relation to, an offence under that section --- is deemed to have
  committed that act or omission in Canada if the person is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian citizen;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a permanent resident as defined in subsection 2(1)
    of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5)
    who, after the commission of the act or omission, is present in
    Canada; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a public body, corporation, society, company, firm
    or partnership that is incorporated, formed or otherwise organized
    under the laws of Canada or a province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person is alleged to have committed an act or
  omission that is deemed to have been committed in Canada under
  subsection (1), proceedings for an offence in respect of that act or
  omission may, whether or not that person is in Canada, be commenced in
  any territorial division in Canada. The person may be tried and
  punished for that offence as if the offence had been committed in that
  territorial division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appearance of accused at trial

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the provisions of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) relating to the requirements that an
  accused appear at and be present during proceedings and the exceptions
  to those requirements apply to proceedings commenced in any
  territorial division under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person previously tried outside Canada

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a person is alleged to have committed an act or
  omission that is deemed to have been committed in Canada under
  subsection (1) and they have been tried and dealt with outside Canada
  for an offence in respect of the act or omission so that, if they had
  been tried and dealt with in Canada, they would be able to plead
  [autrefois acquit]{.otherLang lang="la"}, [autrefois
  convict]{.otherLang lang="la"} or pardon, they are deemed to have been
  so tried and dealt with in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception for foreign trials [in
  absentia]{.otherLang lang="la"}

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (4), a person may not plead
  [autrefois convict]{.otherLang lang="la"} to a count that charges an
  offence in respect of the act or omission if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person was not present and was not represented
    by counsel acting under the person's instructions at the trial
    outside Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person was not punished in accordance with the
    sentence imposed on conviction in respect of the act or omission.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1998, c. 34, s. 5
- 2001, c. 32, s. 58
- 2013, c. 26, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laying an information

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An information may be laid
under section 504 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) in respect of
an offence under this Act --- or a conspiracy to commit, an attempt to
commit, being an accessory after the fact in relation to, or any
counselling in relation to, an offence under this Act --- only by an
officer of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police or any person designated as
a peace officer under the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
Act](/eng/acts/R-10).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1998, c. 34, s. 6
- 2001, c. 32, s. 58
- 2013, c. 26, s. 4
:::

## [Annual Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-112373 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Within four months of the
end of each fiscal year, the Minister of Foreign Affairs, the Minister
for International Trade and the Minister of Justice and Attorney General
of Canada shall jointly prepare a report on the implementation of the
Convention on Combating Bribery of Foreign Public Officials in
International Business Transactions, and on the enforcement of this Act,
and the Minister of Foreign Affairs shall cause a copy of the report to
be laid before each House of Parliament on any of the first fifteen days
on which that House is sitting after the report is completed.
