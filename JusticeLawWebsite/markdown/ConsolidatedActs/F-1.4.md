## Family Orders and Agreements Enforcement Assistance Act {#family-orders-and-agreements-enforcement-assistance-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. 4 (2nd
Supp.)
:::

An Act respecting the release of information, garnishment of moneys
payable by Her Majesty in right of Canada and denial of licences in
relation to family orders

::: ReaderNote
\[1986, c. 5, assented to 13th February, 1986\]
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-221517 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Family Orders and Agreements Enforcement Assistance
Act](/eng/acts/F-1.4).

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Release of Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-221520 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-221521 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Part,

  [[access provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[access provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a provision of an
      order providing for access to a child; ([disposition prévoyant
      l'accès]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[access right]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[access right]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2019, c. 16, s.
      43\]]{.Repealed}

  [[central authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[central authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person or
      entity that is acting as a central authority for the purposes of a
      convention prescribed by the regulations and that is designated in
      an agreement entered into with a province under section
      3; ([autorité centrale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[contact provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[contact provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a provision in an
      order made under subsection 16.5(1) or (2) of the [Divorce
      Act](/eng/acts/D-3.4) or a provision of a similar nature in an
      order made under provincial law; ([disposition sur les
      contacts]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2019, c. 16, s.
      43\]]{.Repealed}

  [[custody provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[custody provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a provision of an
      order providing for custody of a child; ([disposition de
      garde]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person or
      entity that is responsible under the [Divorce
      Act](/eng/acts/D-3.4) or a provincial Act for processing
      inter-jurisdictional support applications and that is designated
      in an agreement with a province entered into under section
      3; ([autorité désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[family provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[family provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a support
      provision, a parenting provision, a contact provision, a custody
      provision or an access provision; ([disposition
      familiale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[information bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[information bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an information bank
      that is designated by the regulations; ([fichier]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[information bank director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[information bank director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
      who is designated by the regulations as the information bank
      director for a given information bank; ([directeur de
      fichier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
      Justice; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2019, c. 16, s.
      43\]]{.Repealed})

  [[parenting provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[parenting provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a provision in
      an order made under subsection 16.1(1) or (2) of the [Divorce
      Act](/eng/acts/D-3.4) or a provision of a similar nature in an
      order made under provincial law; ([disposition
      parentale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[provincial child support service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[provincial child support service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
      entity that is designated in an agreement with a province entered
      into under section 3, and that calculates or recalculates the
      amount of child support; ([service provincial des aliments pour
      enfants]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[provincial enforcement service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[provincial enforcement service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
      entity that is entitled under the laws of a province to enforce
      family provisions and that is designated in an agreement with the
      province entered into under section 3; ([autorité
      provinciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[provincial information bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[provincial information bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      2019, c. 16, s. 43\]]{.Repealed}

  [[support provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[support provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a provision of an
      order for maintenance, alimony or support. ([disposition
      alimentaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[order]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), paragraphs

  a contrary intention appears, [[order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
  order, judgment, decision or agreement --- whether interim or final
  --- that is enforceable in a province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of application --- establishment
or variation of support provision

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  application under section 7 in relation to the establishment or
  variation of a support provision must be accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an affidavit that sets out the reasons for the
    making of the application; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application in relation to the
    variation of a support provision, a copy of the order that contains
    the support provision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the application is made [ex parte]{.otherLang
  lang="la"}, the affidavit referred to in paragraph (1)(a) must also

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state that reasonable steps have been taken to
    locate the person in respect of whom the applicant is seeking to
    have a support provision established or varied and that the person
    has not been located; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out the particulars of those reasonable steps.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  application by individual

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the application is made [ex parte]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} by an individual,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application must also be accompanied by the
    results of a recent criminal record check in respect of the
    applicant and copies of the documents referred to in subparagraph
    (b)(ii), if any; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the affidavit referred to in paragraph (1)(a) must
    also

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} state that the sole purpose of the application is
      to obtain information for the establishment or variation of a
      support provision,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} state whether or not there is a court order,
      agreement, undertaking or recognizance or any other document that
      is of a similar nature that restricts the applicant's
      communication or contact with the person referred to in paragraph
      2(a), or with the child or children that is, are or may be the
      subject of the support provision, or there is a proceeding
      respecting such a restriction,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} state whether or not the applicant has caused
      or has attempted to cause physical harm to the person, child or
      children or has caused them to fear for their safety or security
      or that of another person, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} state whether or not the applicant has been
      charged with or found guilty of an offence against the person,
      child or children.

  the confidentiality of the information.

#### [Peace Officer]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1428529 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for release of information

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  peace officer who is investigating a child abduction under section 282
  or 283 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) may apply to the
  Minister, in the time and manner prescribed by the regulations, to
  have the information banks searched in order to release the
  information prescribed by the regulations to the peace officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supporting documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be accompanied by an affidavit
  submitted by the peace officer in accordance with subsection (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of affidavit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The affidavit must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state that the officer has reasonable grounds to
    believe that an offence referred to in section 282 or 283 of the
    [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) has been committed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} state that the information will be used in the
    investigation of the offence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} identify the person who is believed to have
    committed the offence and the child or children who is or are
    alleged to have been abducted; and

  :   [[garnishable moneys]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means moneys authorized

      regulations; ([sommes saisissables]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

      anglaise seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

      Canada; ([Sa Majesté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[judgment debtor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[judgment debtor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person named in a
      garnishee summons in respect of whom garnishable moneys are sought
      to be garnisheed under this Part; ([débiteur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

      Justice; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

      provinciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[provincial garnishment law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[provincial garnishment law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the law
      of a province relating to garnishment as it applies to the
      enforcement of orders; ([droit provincial en matière de
      saisie-arrêt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[support order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[support order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an order or judgment
      for maintenance, alimony or family financial support that is
      enforceable in any province; ([ordonnance
      alimentaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[support provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[support provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a provision in an
      agreement relating to the payment of maintenance or family
      financial support that is enforceable by a garnishee summons under
      provincial garnishment law. ([entente
      alimentaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Licence Denial Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-221850 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  debtor is in persistent arrears, a provincial enforcement service may
  apply to the Minister requesting that the following actions be taken:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that no new schedule licences be issued to the
    debtor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that all schedule licences held by the debtor be
    suspended; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that schedule licences held by the debtor not be
    renewed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application must be in the form approved by the
  Minister and must contain the prescribed information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application must contain a statement by an officer
  of the provincial enforcement service confirming the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the provincial enforcement service is
    satisfied that the debtor is in persistent arrears;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the provincial enforcement service has made
    reasonable attempts to enforce the support order before making the
    licence denial application; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that the provincial enforcement service has sent a
    notice to the debtor, at the debtor's last known address,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} stating that the provincial enforcement service
      has reasonable grounds to believe that the debtor is in persistent
      arrears,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} stating that the provincial enforcement service
      intends to make a licence denial application in relation to the
      debtor,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} informing the debtor of the consequences to the
      debtor of a licence denial application, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} advising the debtor that a licence denial
      application will not be made if the debtor enters into a payment
      plan that is acceptable to the provincial enforcement service or
      satisfies the provincial enforcement service that the debtor is
      unable to pay the amount in arrears and that the making of the
      application is not reasonable in the circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time for making application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An application may be made only after thirty days
  have expired after the notice referred to in subsection (3) was
  received by the debtor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed receipt

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A notice referred to in subsection (3) is deemed to
  have been received by a debtor ten days after it is sent to the
  debtor.

### [Request to Terminate Application of Part]{.HTitleText2} {#h-221900 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request to terminate application of Part

- **[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  provincial enforcement service shall immediately request that all
  actions taken under this Part in respect of a debtor be terminated
  where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the provincial enforcement service is satisfied
    that the debtor

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is no longer in arrears under all support orders
      against the debtor that are enforced by a licence denial
      application,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is complying, in respect of those support
      orders, with a payment plan that the provincial enforcement
      service considers reasonable, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is unable to pay the amount in arrears and that
      the application of this Part against the debtor is not reasonable
      in the circumstances; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the provincial enforcement service ceases to
    enforce those support orders against the debtor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request --- form and manner

  Minister in the prescribed manner and in the form approved by the

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

this Part and who fails to return the passport forthwith to a Passport

Order](/eng/regulations/SI-81-86), or who subsequently uses the passport
