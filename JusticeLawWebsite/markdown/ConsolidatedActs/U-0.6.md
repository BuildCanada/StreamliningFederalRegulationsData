## Unemployment Assistance Act {#unemployment-assistance-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"} 1970, c. U-1
:::

An Act to authorize contributions by Canada in respect of unemployment
assistance costs in the provinces
::::

::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-461853 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Unemployment Assistance Act](/eng/acts/U-0.6).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1956, c. 26, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-461858 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act

[[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement made under
    section 3; ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contributions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contributions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means contributions by Canada
    pursuant to an agreement; ([contributions]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Employment
    and Social Development; ([Ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unemployment assistance costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unemployment assistance costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    aggregate of the cost to the province, and the cost to
    municipalities in the province, of providing financial assistance to
    persons who are in need. ([frais
    d'assistance-chômage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. U-1
- 1999, c. 31, s. 221
- 2005, c. 34, s. 80
- 2013, c. 40, s. 238
:::

## [Agreements with Provinces]{.HTitleText1} {#h-461867 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements for contributions

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this Act, the Minister may, with the approval of the Governor in
  Council, enter into an agreement with any province to provide for the
  payment by Canada to the province of contributions in respect of
  unemployment assistance costs in the province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amount

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The contributions to a province shall not exceed
  fifty per cent of the unemployment assistance costs in the province in
  respect of which Canada has, under the agreement with the province,
  undertaken to contribute.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of agreements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 4 and 5, an agreement entered
  into under this Act shall be susbstantially in the form set out in the
  schedule.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1956, c. 26, s. 3
- 1957-58, c. 20, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of agreement

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  agreement shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} include provisions for defining, calculating and
    determining unemployment assistance costs in respect of which
    contributions may be made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide for the time, manner and form of making
    claims or applications for contributions, and for the conditions of
    payment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide for the carrying out by the province or by
    municipalities or other agencies in the province of any arrangements
    for administering assistance to persons who are in need, and for
    supplying to Canada all necessary information and documents with
    respect thereto;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide for the determination of differences
    arising under the agreement; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} fix the time of the agreement at not more than five
    years and thereafter until terminated by either party giving to the
    other party one year's notice in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of unemployment
  assistance costs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as provided in subsection (3), an agreement
  shall, for the purposes of this Act, exclude from unemployment
  assistance costs

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} payments made to or on behalf of persons who are
    inmates of any institution or class of institution maintained in
    whole or in part out of funds provided by Canada, a province, a
    municipality or a charitable organization;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} payments made to or on behalf of persons who are in
    receipt of financial assistance under any Act of the legislature of
    a province the cost of which is shared by Canada under an Act of the
    Parliament of Canada other than this Act, and payments made by way
    of supplemental allowance or cost-of-living bonus to those persons
    or to persons who are in receipt of financial assistance under any
    Act of the Parliament of Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} payments made to persons by way of mothers'
    allowance;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} payments made in respect of medical, hospital,
    nursing, dental and optical care, drugs and dressings, funeral
    expenses and travelling expenses; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the costs of administration of any legislation or
    arrangements for providing assistance to persons who are in need.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An agreement may include as unemployment assistance
  costs

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} payments made to or on behalf of persons who are
    inmates of homes for special care and who would not normally be
    cared for in general, acute, chronic or convalescent hospitals,
    tuberculosis sanatoria, mental institutions, institutions for
    incurables, orphanages or child welfare institutions;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} relief payments made to or on behalf of the persons
    referred to in paragraph (2)(b), in addition to the payments therein
    specified; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} travelling costs incurred for the benefit of
    persons to whom assistance is provided or their dependants.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[[Homes for special
  care]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section, the expression [[homes for special
  care]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means nursing homes, hostels for indigent
  transients, homes for the aged, poor houses, alms houses, and hostel
  facilities provided for the aged within housing projects constructed
  under the [National Housing Act](/eng/acts/N-11).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1956, c. 26, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date of agreements

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An agreement may provide
for contributions in respect of unemployment assistance costs that were
incurred within a period of one year prior to the execution thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1956, c. 26, s. 5
- 1957-58, c. 20, s. 2
:::

## [Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-461905 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of C.R.F.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Contributions shall be paid
out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund upon the certificate of the
Minister of Employment and Social Development; but all payments of
contributions are subject to the conditions specified in the agreement
and to the observance by the province of the provisions thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. U-1
- 1996, c. 11, s. 96
- 2005, c. 34, s. 80
- 2013, c. 40, s. 238

year.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 3)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-461921 .scheduleLabel}
:::

Memorandum of Agreement Respecting Unemployment Assistance made this
![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader} day of ![blank
line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader} 19![blank
line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}.

Between:

THE GOVERNMENT OF CANADA, hereinafter called "Canada",

[of the First Part]{.Smallcaps}

and

THE GOVERNMENT OF THE PROVINCE OF ![blank
line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}, hereinafter called ![blank
line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader},

[of the Second Part]{.Smallcaps}

Whereas Canada and ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader} are
desirous of entering into an agreement for the purpose of providing
assistance to unemployed persons who are in need, and for the sharing of
the costs thereof;

Now, Therefore, this agreement witnesseth that in consideration of the
premises and of the mutual convenants and agreements herein contained,
the parties hereto hereby convenant and agree each with the other as
follows:

- [1]{.lawlabel} In this agreement, unless the contrary intention
  appears,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the province
    of ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader};

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [[municipality]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    municipal corporation in the province and includes any city, town or
    local governmental body established under the authority of the law
    of the province and authorized to administer assistance to
    unemployed persons who are in need;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[a recipient of mother's
    allowance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a dependent child of a person receiving a
      mother's allowance if such child is within the age group for whom
      provision is made under the law of the province that provides for
      the payment of mother's allowances; and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the husband of a person receiving a mother's
      allowance if an allowance is being paid on his behalf under the
      law of the province that provides for the payment of mothers'
      allowances;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[population]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, except in
    subparagraph 12(a), the most recent estimate of the population of
    the province made by the Dominion Bureau of Statistics and published
    by the Queen's Printer at Ottawa prior to the month to which the
    reimbursement claim relates;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} [[homes for special
    care]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means nursing homes, hostels for indigent
    transients, homes for the aged, poor houses, alms houses, and hostel
    facilities provided for the aged within housing projects constructed
    under the provisions of the [National Housing Act](/eng/acts/N-11);
    and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} words in the singular include the plural and words
    in the plural include the singular.

- [2]{.lawlabel} ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}shall make
  all the necessary arrangements for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the receipt, by itself or by municipalities, of
    applications for assistance from unemployed persons in the province,
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the verification of the representations made by
    applicants

  and the province shall assume responsibility for the correctness of
  such representations.

- [3]{.lawlabel} ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader} shall make
  available to officials of Canada particulars of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conditions prescribed by ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader} or by municipalities under which
    assistance may be given to persons in need, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the rates of assistance payable.

- [4]{.lawlabel} Length of residence shall not be made a condition for
  the receipt of assistance if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has come from a province whose
    government has entered into an agreement similar to this respecting
    unemployment assistance, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} such agreement includes a like clause as herein
    contained in respect of length of residence not being a condition
    for receipt of assistance.

- [5]{.lawlabel} ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader} shall
  deliver to the Minister of National Health and Welfare, at Ottawa, a
  monthly statement, hereinafter referred to as a reimbursement claim,
  in such form as Canada may require, indicating, [inter
  alia]{.otherLang lang="la"},

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total number of persons who are unemployed and
    in need in the province, including their dependants, who have
    received assistance during the month to which the reimbursement
    claim relates, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total amounts paid to or on behalf of such
    persons during the month to which the reimbursement claim relates.

- [6]{.lawlabel} ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader} shall keep a
  record of the names and addresses of any persons and of the number of
  their dependants who have received assistance and the particulars of
  such assistance, which record shall be made available for examination
  on request by officials of Canada.

- [7]{.lawlabel} There shall be excluded from the reimbursement claim
  any person, together with any payment made to or on behalf of such
  person, who is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an inmate in any institution maintained in whole or
    in part out of funds appropriated by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Parliament of Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Legislature of the province,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a municipality, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a charitable organization;

    except that there may be included in the reimbursement claim
    payments made by the province or by a municipality for the upkeep of
    inmates in homes for special care and the number of persons in
    respect of whom such payments are made, provided that said imates
    are unemployed and in need and the payments claimed do not exceed
    what an individual might reasonably be expected to pay for
    accommodation of a comparable kind and quality in the same locality
    and provided further that said inmates are not such as would
    normally be cared for in general, acute, chronic or convalescent
    hospitals, tuberculosis sanatoria, mental institutions, institutions
    for incurables, orphanages or child welfare institutions;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person in receipt of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} unemployment benefit under the [Employment
      Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a pension under the [Old Age Security
      Act](/eng/acts/O-9),

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1974-75-76, c. 58, s.
      11\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an allowance under the Blind Persons Act,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} an allowance under the Disabled Persons Act, or

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a supplemental allowance or cost-of-living bonus
      provided under the law of the province to recipients of benefit
      under any of the aforementioned Acts; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a recipient of mother's allowance.

- [8]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subparagraph 7(b) there may be included
  in the reimbursement claim any additional relief payments made by the
  province or by a municipality to persons described in the said
  subparagraph and the number of persons to whom such payments are made
  if such persons are unemployed and in need.

- [9]{.lawlabel} There shall also be excluded from the reimbursement
  claim payments made in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} medical, hospital, nursing, dental and optical care
    and drugs and dressings;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} funeral expenses;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all travelling expenses except those, if Canada is
    not contributing thereto under some other arrangement, that are made
    for the purpose of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} returning a recipient of unemployment assistance
      and his dependants, if any, to his normal place of residence under
      an arrangement to which the municipality or the government of the
      province to which he is being returned has agreed in advance,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} enabling a recipient of unemployment assistance
      or dependent member of his family to obtain assured employment as
      certified by the National Employment Service, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} enabling a recipient of unemployment assistance
      or a dependent member of his family to obtain needed medical,
      hospital or nursing home care which cannot be provided at his
      normal place of residence; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the cost of administration.

- [10]{.lawlabel} There shall be deducted from the reimbursement claim
  an amount calculated by multiplying the average per person monthly
  cost of assistance by the decrease in the number of recipients of
  mothers' allowances.

- [11]{.lawlabel} The average per person monthly cost of assistance
  shall be calculated by dividing the total of the payments made during
  the month, as set forth in the reimbursement claim, by the total
  number of persons, including dependants, who received assistance
  during the said month as set forth in the reimbursement claim.

- [12]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph 10, the decrease in the
  number of recipients of mothers' allowances shall be calculated as
  follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the average monthly percentage of the population of
    the province who were recipients of mother's allowance during each
    twelve-month period from the first day of July 1945, to the
    thirtieth day of June prior to the month to which the reimbursement
    claim relates shall be determined, and in determining these
    percentages the latest estimate made by the Dominion Bureau of
    Statistics and published by the Queen's Printer at Ottawa of the
    population of the province on the first day of June or nearest date
    thereto in each twelve-month period shall be used;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the average monthly percentage determined for the
    twelve-month period ending the thirtieth day of June immediately
    preceding the month to which the reimbursement claim relates shall
    be subtracted from the highest percentage ascertained pursuant to
    subparagraph (a) of this paragraph in respect of any other
    twelve-month period;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} from the difference ascertained pursuant to
    subparagraph (b) of this paragraph there shall be subtracted .10 per
    cent;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the difference ascertained pursuant to
    subparagraph (c) of this paragraph shall be multiplied by the
    population; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the event that the calculation in
    subparagraph (c) of this paragraph results in a negative quantity,
    paragraph 10 shall have no application.

- [13]{.lawlabel} The reimbursement claim shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be submitted within six (6) months next following
    the last day of the month to which it relates, but no claim
    submitted by ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader} in respect
    of any month prior to the actual date on which this agreement is
    executed shall be disqualified solely on the ground that it was not
    submitted within the said six (6) months, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contain the following certificate signed by the
    provincial auditor:

    "I hereby certify that this claim for reimbursement has been
    prepared in accordance with the terms and conditions set forth in an
    agreement respecting unemployment assistance dated the ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader} day of ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader} 19![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}, and made between the Government
    of Canada and the Government of the province of ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}".

- [14]{.lawlabel} Canada shall, on receipt of a reimbursement claim that
  has been prepared in accordance with this agreement, pay fifty (50)
  per cent of the total claim.

- [15]{.lawlabel} In the event of any controversy arising between Canada
  and ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader} in respect of this
  agreement, either party may submit such controversy to the Exchequer
  Court of Canada for determination.

- [16]{.lawlabel} This agreement shall be deemed to have come into force
  and shall bind the parties from the ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader} day of ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}19![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}, to the ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}day of ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}19![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}, and thereafter until terminated by
  either party giving to the other party one year's notice in writing.

- [17]{.lawlabel} Any notice given pursuant to paragraph 16 may be
  communicated as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to Canada, by registered mail addressed to the
    Minister of National Health and Welfare, at Ottawa; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader} by
    registered mail addressed to the Premier of the province at ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}.

  In Witness Whereof the Honourable ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}, Minister of National Health and
  Welfare, has hereunto set his hand on behalf of the Government of
  Canada and the Honourable ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}
  Minister of ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader} has hereunto
  set his hand on behalf of the Government of the province of ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}
