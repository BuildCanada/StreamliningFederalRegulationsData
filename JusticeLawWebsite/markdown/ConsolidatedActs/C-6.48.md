## Canada--Korea Economic Growth and Prosperity Act {#canadakorea-economic-growth-and-prosperity-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2014, c. 28
:::

Assented to 2014-11-26

An Act to implement the Free Trade Agreement between Canada and the
Republic of Korea
::::

::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-147855 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada--Korea Economic Growth and Prosperity
Act](/eng/acts/C-6.48).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-147858 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Free Trade Agreement
    between Canada and the Republic of Korea, done at Ottawa on
    September 22, 2014. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Joint Commission
    established under Article 20.1 of the
    Agreement. ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the whole or any portion
    of an Act of Parliament or a regulation, order or other instrument
    issued, made or established in the exercise of a power conferred by
    or under an Act of Parliament. ([texte législatif
    fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister for
    International Trade. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation consistent with Agreement

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, this
Act and any federal law that implements a provision of the Agreement or
fulfils an obligation of the Government of Canada under the Agreement is
to be interpreted in a manner consistent with the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of Act or Agreement to
water

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act or the Agreement applies to natural surface or
ground water in liquid, gaseous or solid state.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act, by specific mention or omission, is to be construed
to affect in any manner the right of Parliament to enact legislation to
implement any provision of the Agreement or fulfil any of the
obligations of the Government of Canada under the Agreement.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-147871 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-147874 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to implement the Agreement, the objectives of which, as elaborated more
specifically through its provisions, are to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish a free trade area in accordance with the
  Agreement;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} promote, through the expansion of reciprocal trade,
  the harmonious development of the economic relations between Canada
  and the Republic of Korea in order to create opportunities for
  economic development;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} promote conditions of fair competition affecting
  trade between Canada and the Republic of Korea;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} substantially increase investment opportunities in
  Canada and the Republic of Korea;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} eliminate barriers to trade in goods and services in
  order to contribute to the harmonious development and expansion of
  world and regional trade;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} enhance and enforce environmental laws and
  regulations and strengthen cooperation between Canada and the Republic
  of Korea on environmental matters;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} protect, enhance and enforce basic workers' rights,
  strengthen cooperation on labour matters, and build on the respective
  international commitments of Canada and the Republic of Korea on
  labour matters; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} promote sustainable development.

## [Causes of Action]{.HTitleText1} {#h-147885 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Causes of action under sections 9 to 15

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person has any cause of action and no proceedings of any kind are to
  be taken, without the consent of the Attorney General of Canada, to
  enforce or determine any right or obligation that is claimed or arises
  solely under or by virtue of sections 9 to 15 or an order made under
  those sections.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Causes of action under the Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to Section B of Chapter 8 and Annex 18-E of
  the Agreement, no person has any cause of action and no proceedings of
  any kind are to be taken, without the consent of the Attorney General
  of Canada, to enforce or determine any right or obligation that is
  claimed or arises solely under or by virtue of the Agreement.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Implementation of the Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-147891 .Part}

### [Approval]{.HTitleText2} {#h-147892 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement approved

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement is approved.

### [Administrative and Institutional Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-147895 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian representative on Commission

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is the
principal representative of Canada on the Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of expenditures

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
is to pay its appropriate share of the aggregate of any expenditures
incurred by or on behalf of the Commission.

### [Panels, Committees, Subcommittees, Working Groups and Other Bodies]{.HTitleText2} {#h-147900 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint representatives of Canada to any committee,
    subcommittee, working group or other body referred to in paragraph 5
    of Article 20.1 of the Agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} appoint a panellist in accordance with paragraph 3
    of Article 21.7 of the Agreement; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} propose candidates to serve as the chair of a panel
    in accordance with that Article 21.7.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister of the Environment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of the Environment may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint representatives of Canada to the
    Environmental Affairs Council referred to in Article 17.11 of the
    Agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} select a panellist in accordance with paragraph 1
    of Annex 17-A of the Agreement; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} propose candidates to serve as the chair of a panel
    of experts, or select the chair, in accordance with paragraph 2 of
    that Annex.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister of Labour

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Labour may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} select a panellist in accordance with paragraph 1
    of Annex 18-D of the Agreement; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} propose candidates to serve as the chair of a
    Review Panel, or select the chair, in accordance with that
    paragraph.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrative support

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is to
designate an agency, division or branch of the Government of Canada to
facilitate the operation of Chapter 21 of the Agreement and to provide
administrative assistance to panels established under that Chapter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of costs

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
is to pay the costs of or its appropriate share of the costs of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the remuneration and expenses payable to members of
  panels, committees, subcommittees, working groups and other bodies, to
  independent experts and to the assistants of panel members; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the general expenses incurred by panels, committees,
  subcommittees, working groups and other bodies.

### [Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-147922 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders re Article 21.11 of Agreement

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, for the purpose of suspending benefits in
  accordance with Article 21.11 of the Agreement, by order, do any one
  or more of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend rights or privileges granted by Canada to
    the Republic of Korea or to goods of the Republic of Korea under the
    Agreement or any federal law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} modify or suspend the application of any federal
    law, with respect to the Republic of Korea or to goods of the
    Republic of Korea;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} extend the application of any federal law to the
    Republic of Korea or to goods of the Republic of Korea;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} take any other measure that the Governor in Council
    considers necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless repealed, an order made under subsection (1)
  has effect for the period specified in the order.
