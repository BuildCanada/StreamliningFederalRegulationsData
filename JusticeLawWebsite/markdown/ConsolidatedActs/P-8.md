## Pension Fund Societies Act {#pension-fund-societies-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. P-8
:::

An Act to empower the employees of incorporated companies to establish
pension fund societies
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-417830 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Pension Fund Societies Act](/eng/acts/P-8).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-9, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-417835 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Act.

[[parent corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[parent corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the corporation any
    of whose officers establish or take proceedings to establish a
    pension fund society under this Act. ([personne morale
    mère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[relative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[relative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an officer or
    employee, includes a relative of the survivor of the officer or
    employee. ([parent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an officer or
    employee, means their spouse or a person who was cohabiting with the
    officer or employee in a conjugal relationship for a period of at
    least one year immediately before the death of the officer or
    employee. ([survivant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-8, s. 2
- 2000, c. 12, s. 266
:::

## [Establishment of Pension Fund Societies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-417843 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The president,
vice-president, general manager, assistant general manager, or the
person acting as such, and the cashier, assistant cashier and inspector
of any corporation legally transacting business in Canada under any Act
of Parliament, or any two of those officers, with any other of the
superior officers, may establish a pension fund society in connection
with the administration of the corporation under the regulations and
subject to the supervision and control designated in this Act, and
thereupon they and the employees of the corporation who join the society
and those who replace them from time to time are and shall be designated
as the pension fund society of the corporation, and under that name are
a body corporate.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-9, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration to be signed

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any of
  the officers referred to in section 3 of any corporation who desire to
  establish a pension fund society under this Act may make and sign in
  duplicate a declaration to that effect according to the schedule
  setting out therein

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the names, residences and official positions of the
    officers;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the parent corporation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the exact name of the society; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the place within Canada that is to be the chief
    place of business of the society.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}To be filed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The declaration referred to in subsection (1) shall
  be filed in the office of the Minister of Industry and in the office
  of the registrar of deeds for the county or registration division
  within which the chief place of business of the society is to be
  situated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisional directors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The officers who make and sign the declaration
  referred to in subsection (1) shall be the provisional directors of
  the society and hold office until their successors are appointed or
  elected.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-8, s. 4
- 1992, c. 1, s. 145(F)
- 1995, c. 1, s. 62
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of incorporation

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notice
  of the incorporation of a pension fund society shall be given by
  publication in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) for
  four weeks and that notice shall set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the exact name adopted by the society;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the designation of the chief place of business of
    the society; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the secretary thereof on whom legal
    process may be served.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other notices

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notice of any change in the chief place of business
  of the society or of the secretary thereof shall be given by notice in
  the manner described in subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-9, s. 5
:::

## [Change of Name]{.HTitleText1} {#h-417871 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of name

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  corporate name of a pension fund society established under this Act
  may be changed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if at a special general meeting called for that
    purpose, or at any general meeting the notice for which specifically
    mentions that purpose among others, a by-law or a resolution to
    change the corporate name to another name specified in the by-law or
    resolution has been passed by a majority of at least two-thirds of
    the votes cast at the meeting by the contributories to the funds or
    members of the society, including the parent corporation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if, on application by the board of directors of the
    society to the Minister of Industry and the filing with him of a
    duly certified copy of the resolution so passed and of such other
    information as he may require, the Minister is satisfied

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the change of name is not proposed for any
      improper purpose,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that the new name proposed is not that of any
      other known corporation, association or body corporate or
      unincorporated, or liable to be confounded therewith, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that the proposed change is not otherwise
      objectionable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of Minister to alter proposed
  name

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the proposed new name of a pension fund society
  appears to the Minister of Industry to be objectionable, he may
  substitute therefor another name approved by him.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When the Minister of Industry has approved of a
  change of name of a pension fund society, he may direct that notice
  thereof be published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) for four consecutive weeks, at the
  society's expense, specifying

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the corporate name of the society, its chief place
    of business and the name of its secretary;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the new name as approved by the Minister of
    Industry; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date after the publication of notice on which
    the change of name will take effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of effect

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} On and after the date specified in the notice
  published under subsection (3), the new name of the pension fund
  society as approved by the Minister of Industry shall be the corporate
  name of the society.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-8, s. 6
- 1992, c. 1, s. 145(F)
- 1995, c. 1, s. 62
:::

## [First Meeting of a Pension Fund Society]{.HTitleText1} {#h-417891 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisional directors

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  provisional directors of a pension fund society have power to call the
  first meeting of the society, and at that meeting directors may be
  elected and by-laws may be passed under the provisions of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws to be filed with Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the passage of the by-laws referred to in
  subsection (1), a copy thereof and subsequent copies of other by-laws
  in amendment thereof, in addition thereto or in diminution thereof,
  shall be filed with the Minister of Industry within two weeks after
  the passage thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-8, s. 7
- 1992, c. 1, s. 145(F)
- 1995, c. 1, s. 62
:::

## [Board of Directors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-417899 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board of directors

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  affairs of a pension fund society shall be administered by a board of
  directors who shall be appointed or elected in such manner, in such
  number, with such qualifications and for such period as are determined
  by the by-laws of the society.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How elected

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the first meeting of a pension fund society to be
  held under this Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} five directors shall be elected, subject to
    addition to that number if sanctioned by the by-laws of the society;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} other officers may be appointed in such manner,
    with such remuneration and under such provisions concerning their
    powers and duties as are established by the by-laws of the society.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-9, s. 8
:::

## [Right to Vote]{.HTitleText1} {#h-417909 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to vote

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Each contributor to the
funds of a pension fund society, including the parent corporation, has
such right to vote at general meetings of the society, on such
occasions, subject to such restrictions and on such conditions as are
determined by the by-laws of the society.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-9, s. 9
:::

## [Fund]{.HTitleText1} {#h-417914 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to form a fund

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** After its incorporation
under this Act, every pension fund society has the power, by means of
voluntary contribution or otherwise as its by-laws provide, to form a
fund, and may invest, hold and administer the fund and from and out of
the fund may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide for the support and payment of pensions to
  officers and employees of the parent corporation incapacitated by age
  or infirmity; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the death of those officers or employees, pay
  annuities or gratuities to their survivors and minor children, or
  other relatives, in such manner as may be specified by the by-laws of
  the society.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-8, s. 10
- 2000, c. 12, s. 267
:::

## [By-laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-417921 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  incorporated pension fund society has all powers necessary for the
  purposes of this Act and may make by-laws defining and regulating in
  the premises, and prescribing the mode of enforcement of all the
  rights, powers and duties of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the society;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the individual members thereof;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the officers and employees of the parent
    corporation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the survivors and minor children, or other
    relatives, of those officers and employees; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the parent corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every incorporated pension fund society may make
  by-laws for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the formation and maintenance of the pension fund;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the management and distribution thereof generally;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} enforcing any penalty or forfeiture in the
    premises; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the government and ordering of all business and
    affairs of the society.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sanction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No by-law referred to in this section has any force
  or effect unless it has been sanctioned by the board of directors of
  the parent corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-8, s. 11
- 2000, c. 12, s. 268
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-417940 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** All the powers,
authority, rights, penalties and forfeitures whatever in the premises,
whether of the pension fund society, of the individual members thereof,
of the officers and employees thereof or of their survivors, minor
children and relatives, or of the parent corporation, shall be such as
are defined and limited by the by-laws of the society and may be
exercised and enforced in the manner prescribed by those by-laws.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-8, s. 12
- 2000, c. 12, s. 269
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of revenues

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** All the revenues of a
pension fund society, from whatever source derived, shall be devoted
exclusively to the maintenance of the society and the furtherance of its
objects.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-9, s. 13
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contribution by parent corporation

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The parent corporation
may and is hereby authorized to contribute annually or otherwise to the
funds of a pension fund society, by a vote of either its directors or
its shareholders.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-9, s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No assignment of interest of members

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The interest of any
member of a pension fund society in the funds of the society is not
transferable, and may not be charged by way of pledge, hypothecation or
movable hypothec without delivery, or be sold or assigned in any manner.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-8, s. 15
- 2001, c. 4, s. 112
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Returns

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Every pension fund
society established under this Act shall at all times, when required by
the Governor in Council or by either House of Parliament, make a full
return of its property and of its receipts and expenditures for such
period and with such details and other information as the Governor in
Council or either House of Parliament requires.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-9, s. 16
:::

## [Subsidiary Corporations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-417961 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsidiary corporations

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  pension fund society established under this Act may, at the request of
  the parent corporation evidenced by a resolution of its directors,
  admit to membership in the society, on such terms and conditions as
  the society may determine, any officers or employees of a subsidiary
  corporation of the parent corporation and may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide for the support and payment of pensions to
    the officers and employees incapacitated by age or infirmity; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the death of those officers or employees, pay
    annuities or gratuities to their survivors and minor children, or
    other relatives, in such manner as may be specified by the by-laws
    of the society.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All the provisions of this Act applicable to officers
  and employees of the parent corporation who are members of a pension
  fund society apply with such modifications as the circumstances
  require to officers and employees admitted to membership pursuant to
  this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to contribute and vote

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A subsidiary corporation any of the officers or
  employees of which have been admitted to membership in a pension fund
  society under this section may, and is hereby authorized to,
  contribute annually or otherwise to the funds of the society by a vote
  of either its directors or its shareholders, and as a contributor has
  such right to vote at general meetings of the society, on such
  occasions, subject to such restrictions and on such conditions as are
  determined by the by-laws of the society.

- Definition of [[subsidiary corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[subsidiary
  corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation legally
  transacting business in Canada, under any Act of Parliament, the
  majority of the shares of which that have under all circumstances full
  voting rights is owned or controlled directly or indirectly by or for
  the parent corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-8, s. 17
- 2000, c. 12, s. 270
:::
