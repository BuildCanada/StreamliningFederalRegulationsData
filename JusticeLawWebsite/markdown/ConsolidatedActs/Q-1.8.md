## Queen Elizabeth II Canadian Research Fund Act {#queen-elizabeth-ii-canadian-research-fund-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"} 1970, c. Q-1
:::

An Act respecting the establishment and administration of a fund to aid
in research on the diseases of children
::::

::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
MOST GRACIOUS SOVEREIGN,

###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS it has pleased Your Majesty to approve the suggestion that the
occasion of Your visit to Canada and Your personal interest in the
well-being of children should be marked by the establishment in Your
Name of a Fund to be devoted to the furthering of research into the
diseases of children and the prevention and cure of such diseases, to
which all who wish in this way to make a gift to Your Majesty may
contribute:
:::

::: Enacts
May it therefore please Your Majesty, that it may be enacted, and be it
enacted by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice
and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-419930 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Queen Elizabeth II Canadian Research Fund Act](/eng/acts/Q-1.8).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1959, c. 33, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-419935 .Part}

Definition of [[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,
[[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Medical Research Council
established by section 3 of the Medical Research Council Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 29, s. 69
:::

## [The Fund]{.HTitleText1} {#h-419940 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fund established

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby established
a fund, to be known as The Queen Elizabeth II Canadian Fund to Aid in
Research on the Diseases of Children (hereinafter called the Fund),
which shall consist of all money, securities and other property received
by the Council for the purposes of the Fund and shall include all income
derived from the Fund and all gifts, bequests, appropriations and other
contributions made to the Fund.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S.C. 1970, c. Q-1, s. 2
- 1995, c. 29, s. 70
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purposes of Fund

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purposes of the Fund
are to assist individuals or organizations to undertake or carry on
research into the diseases of children, and the causes, prevention and
treatment of such diseases.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1959, c. 33, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriation to the Fund

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby
appropriated for the Fund, out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund, the sum
of one million dollars.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1959, c. 33, s. 4
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, 1995, c. 29,
s. 71\]]{.Repealed}

## [Powers and Duties of the Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-419954 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Management and administration

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Council shall manage
and administer the Fund in accordance with and subject to this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S.C. 1970, c. Q-1, s. 6
- 1995, c. 29, s. 73(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Council may make such
payments out of the Fund for the purposes of the Fund as it considers
desirable.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S.C. 1970, c. Q-1, s. 7
- 1995, c. 29, s. 73(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investments

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Council may invest any
moneys of the Fund in bonds or other securities of the Government of
Canada and may sell any such bonds and securities.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S.C. 1970, c. Q-1, s. 8
- 1995, c. 29, s. 73(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advisory committees

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Council may appoint
from among its members or otherwise such advisory or other committees as
it considers necessary or advisable for the administration of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S.C. 1970, c. Q-1, s. 9
- 1995, c. 29, s. 73(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Council may, for the
purposes of this Act, acquire by purchase, lease, gift, bequest or
otherwise any real or personal property and may own, hold, sell, manage
or otherwise deal therewith in such manner as it may determine.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S.C. 1970, c. Q-1, s. 10
- 1995, c. 29, s. 73(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Council may make
by-laws for regulating its proceedings and generally for carrying out
the purposes and provisions of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S.C. 1970, c. Q-1, s. 11
- 1995, c. 29, s. 73(E)
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-419979 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Gifts to Her Majesty

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A gift or bequest to the
Fund or to the Council for the purposes of the Fund shall be deemed to
be a gift to Her Majesty.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S.C. 1970, c. Q-1, s. 12
- 1995, c. 29, s. 73(E)
:::

**[[13 to 15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13_to_15 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
1995, c. 29, s. 72\]]{.Repealed}
