## Government Employees Compensation Act {#government-employees-compensation-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. G-5
:::

An Act respecting compensation for Government employees
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-251655 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Government Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-8, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-251660 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[accident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[accident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a wilful and an
    intentional act, not being the act of the employee, and a fortuitous
    event occasioned by a physical or natural
    cause; ([accident]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an
    employee, means a person who was, immediately before the employee's
    death, cohabiting with the employee in a conjugal relationship,
    having so cohabited for a period of at least one year; ([conjoint de
    fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[compensation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[compensation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes medical and hospital
    expenses and any other benefits, expenses or allowances that are
    authorized by the law of the province where the employee is usually
    employed respecting compensation for workmen and the dependants of
    deceased workmen; ([indemnité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dependant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dependant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an employee,
    includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a common-law partner of the employee, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who was cohabiting with the employee
      immediately before the employee's death and is a parent of the
      employee's child; ([personne à charge]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person in the service of Her Majesty who is
      paid a direct wage or salary by or on behalf of Her Majesty,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any member, officer or employee of any
      department, corporation or other body that is established to
      perform a function or duty on the Government of Canada's behalf
      who is declared by the Minister with the approval of the Governor
      in Council to be an employee for the purposes of this Act,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person who, for the purpose of obtaining
      employment in any department, corporation or other body that is
      established to perform a function or duty on the Government of
      Canada's behalf, is taking a training course that is approved by
      the Minister for that person,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any person who is employed by any department,
      corporation or other body that is established to perform a
      function or duty on the Government of Canada's behalf, who is on
      leave of absence without pay and, for the purpose of increasing
      the skills used in the performance of their duties, is taking a
      training course that is approved by the Minister for that purpose,
      and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any officer or employee of the Senate, House of
      Commons, Library of Parliament, office of the Senate Ethics
      Officer, office of the Conflict of Interest and Ethics
      Commissioner, Parliamentary Protective Service or office of the
      Parliamentary Budget Officer; ([agents de l'État]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Her Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Her Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Her Majesty in right of
    Canada; ([Sa Majesté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[industrial disease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[industrial disease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any disease in
    respect of which compensation is payable under the law of the
    province where the employee is usually employed respecting
    compensation for workmen and the dependants of deceased
    workmen; ([maladie professionnelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Labour. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-251680 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons excluded

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This Act
  does not apply to any person who is a member of the regular force of
  the Canadian Forces or of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Act applies in respect of an accident occurring
  or a disease contracted within or outside Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-8, s. 2
:::

## [Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-251688 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons eligible for compensation

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this Act, compensation shall be paid to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an employee who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is caused personal injury by an accident arising
      out of and in the course of his employment, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is disabled by reason of an industrial disease
      due to the nature of the employment; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the dependants of an employee whose death results
    from such an accident or industrial disease.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rate of compensation and conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The employee or the dependants referred to in
  subsection (1) are, notwithstanding the nature or class of the
  employment, entitled to receive compensation at the same rate and
  under the same conditions as are provided under the law of the
  province where the employee is usually employed respecting
  compensation for workmen and the dependants of deceased workmen,
  employed by persons other than Her Majesty, who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are caused personal injuries in that province by
    accidents arising out of and in the course of their employment; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are disabled in that province by reason of
    industrial diseases due to the nature of their employment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of compensation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Compensation under subsection (1) shall be determined
  by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the same board, officers or authority as is or are
    established by the law of the province for determining compensation
    for workmen and dependants of deceased workmen employed by persons
    other than Her Majesty; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} such other board, officers or authority, or such
    court, as the Governor in Council may direct.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1996, c. 10, s. 229.3\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payable to persons determined by
  awarding authority

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any compensation awarded to an employee or the
  dependants of a deceased employee by a board, officer, authority or
  court, under the authority of this Act, shall be paid to the employee
  or dependants or to such person as the board, officer, authority or
  court may direct, and the board, officer, authority or court has the
  same jurisdiction to award costs as is conferred in cases between
  private parties by the law of the province where the employee is
  usually employed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation, etc., payable out of
  C.R.F.

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} There may be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue
  Fund,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any compensation or costs awarded under this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the board, officers, authority or court
    authorized by the law of any province or under this Act to determine
    compensation cases, such amount as an accountable advance in respect
    of compensation or costs that may be awarded under this Act as, in
    the opinion of the Treasury Board, is expedient;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in any province where the general expenses of
    maintaining the board, officers, authority or court are paid by the
    province or by contributions from employers, or by both, such
    portion of the contributions as, in the opinion of the Treasury
    Board, is fair and reasonable;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in any province where the board, officers or
    authority may make expenditures to aid in getting injured workmen
    back to work or removing any handicap resulting from their injuries,
    such portion of those expenditures as, in the opinion of the
    Treasury Board, is fair and reasonable; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} to the board, officers, authority or court, such
    amount as an accountable advance in respect of any expenses or
    expenditures that may be paid under paragraph (c) or (d) as, in the
    opinion of the Treasury Board, is expedient.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. G-5, s. 4
- 1996, c. 10, s. 229.3
- 2015, c. 3, s. 99(F)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Yukon and Northwest Territories

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an
  employee is usually employed in Yukon or the Northwest Territories,
  the employee shall for the purposes of this Act be deemed to be
  usually employed in the Province of Alberta.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nunavut

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an employee is usually employed in Nunavut, the
  employee shall for the purposes of this Act be deemed to be usually
  employed in the Province of Alberta.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. G-5, s. 5
- 1993, c. 28, s. 78
- 2002, c. 7, s. 174

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person employed outside Canada

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where an employee, other
than a person locally engaged outside Canada, is usually employed
outside Canada, the employee shall for the purposes of this Act be
deemed to be usually employed in the Province of Ontario.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-8, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contributions to workmen's compensation
fund in certain cases

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an
  employee locally engaged outside Canada is usually employed in a place
  where under the law respecting compensation to workmen and the
  dependants of deceased workmen payments are made to a fund out of
  which compensation is paid to workmen and the dependants of deceased
  workmen, there may, with the approval of the Treasury Board, be paid
  to that fund, out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund, such payments in
  respect of that employee as may be deemed necessary by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation to employee or dependants
  in special cases

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, with the approval of the Treasury
  Board, award compensation in such amount and in such manner as he
  deems fit to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an employee locally engaged outside Canada who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is caused personal injury by an accident arising
      out of and in the course of his employment, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is disabled by reason of any disease that is due
      to the nature of the employment and peculiar to or characteristic
      of the particular process, trade or occupation in which the
      employee was employed at the time the disease was contracted, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the dependants of such an employee whose death
    results from such an accident or disease,

  and who are not otherwise entitled to compensation under any law
  respecting compensation to workmen and the dependants of deceased
  workmen.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-8, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations re compensation for
disability or death

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations prescribing the conditions
  under which compensation is to be payable, the amount of compensation
  payable and the manner in which the compensation is to be determined,
  in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any employee who is disabled or whose death is
    caused by reason of any disease that is not an industrial disease
    but is due to the nature of his employment and peculiar to or
    characteristic of the particular process, trade or occupation in
    which the employee was employed at the time the disease was
    contracted; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any employee, other than an employee engaged
    locally outside Canada, who is disabled or whose death is caused by
    reason of any disease that results from the environmental conditions
    of any place outside Canada to which the employee was assigned.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Award according to regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Compensation shall be awarded to an employee, or the
  dependants of a deceased employee, referred to in subsection (1) in
  accordance with the regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-8, s. 7
:::

## [Claims Against Third Parties and Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-251748 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election of claims

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  accident happens to an employee in the course of their employment
  under any circumstances that entitle the employee or their dependants
  to an action against a third party, the employee or their dependants,
  if they are entitled to compensation under this Act, may claim
  compensation under it or may make a claim against the third party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election is final

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The election made by the employee or their dependants
  is final.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. G-5, s. 9
- 1999, c. 35, s. 12
- 2012, c. 19, s. 421
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application --- prescribed corporation or
other body

- **[[9.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (2) to (4) apply to
  employees who are employed by a corporation or other body that is
  prescribed under paragraph 13(1)(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entitlement to difference as
  compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a claim is made against the third party and less
  is recovered and collected, either on a settlement approved by the
  employer or under a judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction,
  than the amount of compensation to which the employee or their
  dependants who made the claim are entitled under this Act, the
  difference between the amount so recovered and collected and the
  amount of that compensation shall be paid as compensation to the
  employee or their dependants.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subrogation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the employee or their dependants elect to claim
  compensation under this Act, the employer shall be subrogated to the
  rights of the employee or their dependants and may, subject to the
  Agreements implemented by the [Civil International Space Station
  Agreement Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-31.3) and the [Civil Lunar
  Gateway Agreement Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-31.4), maintain an
  action, against the third party, in its own name or in the name of the
  employee or their dependants.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Portion of excess payable to employee

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If an action is brought under subsection (3) and the
  amount recovered and collected exceeds the amount of compensation to
  which the employee or their dependants are entitled under this Act,
  the employer may pay to the employee or their dependants any portion
  of the excess that remains after the employer recovers its costs, but
  if after that payment has been made the employee becomes entitled to
  an additional amount of compensation in respect of the same accident,
  the amount paid under this subsection may be deducted from the
  additional compensation.

- 2012, c. 19, s. 421

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application --- other employers

- **[[9.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (2) to (4) apply to
  employees who are not employed by a corporation or other body that is
  prescribed under paragraph 13(1)(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entitlement to difference as
  compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a claim is made against the third party and less
  is recovered and collected, either on a settlement approved by the
  Minister or under a judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction,
  than the amount of compensation to which the employee or their
  dependants who made the claim are entitled under this Act, the
  difference between the amount so recovered and collected and the
  amount of that compensation shall be paid as compensation to the
  employee or their dependants.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subrogation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the employee or their dependants elect to claim
  compensation under this Act, His Majesty shall be subrogated to the
  rights of the employee or their dependants and may, subject to the
  Agreements implemented by the [Civil International Space Station
  Agreement Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-31.3) and the [Civil Lunar
  Gateway Agreement Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-31.4), maintain an
  action in the name of the employee or their dependants or of His
  Majesty against the third party and any sum recovered shall be paid
  into the Consolidated Revenue Fund.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Portion of excess payable to employee

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If an action is brought under subsection (3) and the
  amount recovered and collected exceeds the amount of compensation to
  which the employee or their dependants are entitled under this Act,
  there may be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund to the employee
  or their dependants any portion of the excess that the Minister with
  the approval of the Treasury Board considers necessary, but if after
  that payment has been made the employee becomes entitled to an
  additional amount of compensation in respect of the same accident, the
  amount paid under this subsection may be deducted from the additional
  compensation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parent, etc., may elect

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** In the case of a child,
the parent, or a person who stands in the place of a parent, may make an
election under section 9 for that child.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. G-5, s. 10
- 2000, c. 12, s. 126
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of election

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Notice of an election
under section 9 shall be given within three months after the happening
of the accident, or, in the case of an accident resulting in death,
within three months after the death, or within such longer period as may
be allowed either before or after the expiration of the three months by
the board, officers, authority or court having power to determine the
right to and the amount of the compensation under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-8, s. 8
:::

## [No Other Claims Against Crown]{.HTitleText1} {#h-251786 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No claim against Her Majesty

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Where an accident happens
to an employee in the course of his employment under such circumstances
as entitle him or his dependants to compensation under this Act, neither
the employee nor any dependant of the employee has any claim against Her
Majesty, or any officer, servant or agent of Her Majesty, other than for
compensation under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-8, s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acts or omissions by corporation or other
body

**[[12.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.1 .sectionLabel}** No action lies
against Her Majesty for anything done or omitted to be done under this
Act by a corporation or other body referred to in subsection 9.1(1).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2012, c. 19, s. 422
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-251795 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the Governor in Council's
  approval, the Minister may make regulations for carrying out the
  purposes and provisions of this Act, including regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determining the place where an employee is usually
    employed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing corporations or other bodies for the
    purposes of section 9.1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extended application of section 9.2

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a corporation or other body is prescribed under
  paragraph (1)(b), section 9.2 continues to apply in cases where the
  notice of election referred to in section 11 is given before the day
  on which the regulation comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extended application of section 9.1

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a regulation is made under paragraph (1)(b) by
  virtue of which a corporation or other body ceases to be prescribed,
  section 9.1 continues to apply in cases where the notice of election
  referred to in section 11 is given before the day on which the
  regulation comes into force.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. G-5, s. 13
- 2012, c. 19, s. 423

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employer contribution

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may require
any corporation or other body, whose employees are subject to this Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to pay such percentage of payroll or such other rate
  or specific sum as the Minister may deem sufficient to pay the
  compensation during the current year in respect of injuries to those
  employees;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to pay such proportion of the expenses of
  administering this Act as the Minister may determine; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to maintain a reserve fund to pay the compensation
  payable in future years in respect of claims of those employees
  arising under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. G-5, s. 14
- 2012, c. 19, s. 424(E)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Death of employee at place other than
that of employment

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Where the death of an
employee results from an accident arising out of and in the course of
his employment at a place other than the place where the employee is
usually employed and the reasonable additional expenses incurred by
reason of the death occurring at that other place exceed the amount of
compensation to which the employee's dependants are entitled for those
expenses under this Act, there may be paid out of the Consolidated
Revenue Fund such sum as the Minister, with the approval of the Treasury
Board, deems necessary to pay any portion of the excess.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-8, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accident prevention activities and safety
program

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may promote
and encourage accident prevention activities and safety programs among
persons employed in the federal public administration.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. G-5, s. 16
- 2003, c. 22, s. 224(E)
