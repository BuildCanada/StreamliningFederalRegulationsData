## Boucherville Islands Bridge and Tunnel Act {#boucherville-islands-bridge-and-tunnel-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1963, c. 6
:::

Assented to 1963-07-31

An Act to authorize the Construction and Maintenance of a Bridge and
Tunnel across the St. Lawrence River at the Boucherville Islands, in the
Province of Quebec
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:---
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-30727 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Boucherville Islands Bridge and Tunnel Act](/eng/acts/B-6.4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction of bridge authorized

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Act, the
Province of Quebec (hereinafter referred to as \"the Province\") is
hereby authorized to construct and maintain a bridge, approaches and
other works ancillary thereto, for the use and passage of persons,
vehicles and goods, across and over the St. Lawrence River from a point
at or near the City of Jacques Cartier, Quebec, to a point on Charron
Island, one of the Boucherville Islands, Quebec.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction of tunnel authorized

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Act, the
Province is hereby authorized to construct and maintain a tunnel,
approaches and other works ancillary thereto, for the use and passage of
persons, vehicles and goods, across and under the St. Lawrence River
from a point at or near the City of Montreal, Quebec to a point on
Charron Island, one of the Boucherville Islands, Quebec.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Plans and drawings to be submitted

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  bridge and tunnel described in sections 2 and 3 respectively and the
  works ancillary thereto shall be constructed and maintained in
  accordance with and subject to such regulations for the safeguarding
  of navigation of the St. Lawrence River as the Governor in Council may
  prescribe, and, for that purpose, the Province shall, prior to the
  commencement of construction of the bridge, tunnel or ancillary works,
  submit to the Governor in Council for examination and approval plans
  and drawings thereof and a map of their proposed locations, indicating
  accurately all relevant soundings and showing the bed of the stream
  and the location of all other bridges in the area, and furnish to the
  Governor in Council such other information as is required for a full
  and satisfactory understanding of the project.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of plans and drawings prior to
  commencement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Construction of the bridge, tunnel or works ancillary
  thereto shall not be commenced until such time as the plans and
  drawings referred to in subsection (1) and the location of the bridge,
  tunnel and ancillary works have been approved by the Governor in
  Council, and no material change in such plans or drawings, or in the
  location of the bridge, tunnel or ancillary works shall be made after
  the commencement of construction thereof except with the prior
  approval of the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, in addition to any regulations authorized by
  section 4, make such regulations in relation to the bridge and tunnel
  described in sections 2 and 3 and the works ancillary thereto, as he
  deems necessary for navigation purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All persons affected by any regulation made under the
  authority of this Act shall comply therewith.
