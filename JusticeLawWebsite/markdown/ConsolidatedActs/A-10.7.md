## Alternative Fuels Act {#alternative-fuels-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1995, c. 20
:::

Assented to 1995-06-22

An Act to accelerate the use of alternative fuels for motor vehicles
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS Canada has a commitment to environmental reform and thus to
better control over the emission of greenhouse gases, notably carbon
dioxide, and of other air pollutants;

WHEREAS damage to the environment is caused by the emission of air
pollutants by internal combustion engines using conventional fuels;

WHEREAS the federal government is a major user of such engines;

AND WHEREAS government can lead the conversion to less harmful fuels by
progressively replacing its motor vehicles with others using alternative
fuels, thereby promoting the replacement of petroleum-based fuels for
transportation;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and the House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Alternative Fuels Act](/eng/acts/A-10.7).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[acquire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[acquire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes to lease for a period of
      twelve months or more, with or without an option to
      purchase; ([acquérir]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[alternative fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[alternative fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means fuel that is

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for use in motor vehicles to deliver direct
        propulsion,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} less damaging to the environment than
        conventional fuels, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribed by regulation,

      including, without limiting the generality of the foregoing,
      ethanol, methanol, propane gas, natural gas, hydrogen or
      electricity when used as a sole source of direct propulsion
      energy; ([carburant de remplacement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Crown corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Crown corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation
      named in Schedule III to the [Financial Administration
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11), unless excluded pursuant to subsection
      (2); ([société d'État]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[federal body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any body named in
      Schedule I, I.1 or II to the [Financial Administration
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11); ([organisme fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[motor vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[motor vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any motor vehicle of a
      class prescribed by regulation, including, without limiting the
      generality of the foregoing, an automobile, passenger van or light
      duty truck. ([véhicule automobile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excluding Crown corporations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Treasury Board may, by order, exclude any Crown
  corporation from the application of this Act, after consultation with
  the board of directors of the corporation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative fuels policy

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** It is the purpose of this
Act that, for the fiscal year commencing on April 1, 2004 and for every
fiscal year thereafter, where it is cost effective and operationally
feasible, seventy-five per cent of motor vehicles operated by all
federal bodies and Crown corporations will be motor vehicles operating
on alternative fuels, thereby promoting the replacement of
petroleum-based fuels for transportation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation of policy

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President of the Treasury Board shall take such measures as may be
  necessary to ensure that all federal bodies that acquire motor
  vehicles shall, where it is cost effective and operationally feasible
  to do so, in the aggregate, select, in percentages not less than those
  following, motor vehicles powered by engines that are capable of
  operating on alternative fuels:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fifty per cent, for the fiscal year commencing
    April 1, 1997;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sixty per cent, for the fiscal year commencing
    April 1, 1998;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} seventy-five per cent, for the fiscal year
    commencing April 1, 1999 and for every fiscal year thereafter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligatory use

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where it is cost effective and operationally feasible
  to do so, a federal body shall use an alternative fuel in the
  operation of any motor vehicle capable of operating on such a fuel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crown corporations

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  Crown corporation that acquires motor vehicles shall, where it is cost
  effective and operationally feasible to do so, select, in percentages
  not less than those following, vehicles powered by motors that are
  capable of operating on alternative fuels:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fifty per cent, for the fiscal year commencing
    April 1, 1997;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sixty per cent, for the fiscal year commencing
    April 1, 1998; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} seventy-five per cent, for the fiscal year
    commencing April 1, 1999 and for every fiscal year thereafter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligatory use

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where it is cost effective and operationally feasible
  to do so, a Crown corporation shall use an alternative fuel in the
  operation of any motor vehicle capable of operating on such a fuel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Treasury Board may, on
the recommendation of the President of the Treasury Board made after the
President has consulted with such representatives of industry and
environmental groups as the President considers appropriate, make
regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing any fuel for the purposes of the
  definition "alternative fuel";

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing any class of motor vehicle for the
  purposes of the definition "motor vehicle";

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the criteria to be used in determining
  cost effectiveness and operational feasibility; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} generally for carrying out the purpose or any of the
  provisions of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Treasury Board may take
such measures as it considers appropriate for giving effect to the
purpose or any provision of this Act.

s. 221\]]{.Repealed}
