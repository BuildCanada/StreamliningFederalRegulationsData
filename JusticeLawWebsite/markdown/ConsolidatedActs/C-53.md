## Customs and Excise Offshore Application Act {#customs-and-excise-offshore-application-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. C-53
:::

An Act to apply the customs and excise jurisdiction of Canada to the
continental shelf of Canada
::::

::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-142544 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Customs and Excise Offshore Application Act](/eng/acts/C-53).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1984, c. 17, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-142549 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[British ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[British ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 1998, c. 16, s.
      33\]]{.Repealed}

  [[Canadian lessee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian lessee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a lessee that is an
      individual, a corporation or a group referred to in the definition
      [[Canadian-owned]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and that carries on
      business in Canada; ([locataire canadien]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian-owned]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian-owned]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means wholly owned by any
      of the following individuals, corporations or groups carrying on
      business in Canada:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an individual who is a resident of Canada
        within the meaning of section 250 of the [Income Tax
        Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3),

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation incorporated in Canada under
        federal or provincial law, or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a group comprised of individuals or
        corporations or both at least one of the members of which is a
        person referred to in paragraph (a) or (b); ([appartenance
        canadienne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      the [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9); ([navire
      canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[continental shelf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[continental shelf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 1996, c.
      31, s. 76\]]{.Repealed}

  [[designated goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} artificial islands, ships, vessels,
        installations, structures or apparatus, including drilling rigs,
        drilling ships, production platforms, storage vessels, storage
        tanks, docks, caissons and pipelines, permanently or temporarily
        attached to or resting on the continental shelf of Canada for
        the exploration, development, production or transportation of
        the mineral or other non-living natural resources thereof,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ships, vessels, equipment, structures,
        apparatus or conveyances used for the construction, erection or
        servicing of any artificial island, ship, vessel, installation,
        structure or apparatus referred to in paragraph (a) or for the
        transportation of goods between any such thing and a point in
        Canada or between any such things, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} goods for use or consumption on any artificial
        island, ship, vessel, installation, structure, apparatus,
        equipment or conveyance referred to in paragraph (a) or
        (b); ([biens désignés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[federal customs laws]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal customs laws]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Acts of Parliament,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} regulations within the meaning of the
        [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22), and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} rules of law applicable in connection with
        those Acts or regulations,

      that relate to customs or excise, whether those Acts, regulations
      or rules come into force before or after June 30, 1983 and, for
      greater certainty but without restricting the generality of the
      foregoing, includes the following Acts, namely, the [Excise
      Act](/eng/acts/E-14), the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the
      [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), the [Importation
      of Intoxicating Liquors Act](/eng/acts/I-3), the [Special Import
      Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15), the [Customs
      Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011),
      the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1) and the [Select Luxury
      Items Tax Act](/eng/acts/S-8.35); ([législation douanière
      fédérale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[internal waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[internal waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 1996, c.
      31, s. 76\]]{.Repealed}

  [[lease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[lease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes
      charter; ([bail]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[renewal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[renewal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a lease, includes
      the extension of the lease and the exercise of any option to
      continue the lease; ([reconduction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[territorial sea]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[territorial sea]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 1996, c.
      31, s. 76\]]{.Repealed}

  [[warehouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[warehouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in the
      [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([entrepôt]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Designated goods shall be deemed to be Canadian-owned
  if they are held under a lease by any individual, corporation or group
  referred to in the definition [[Canadian-owned]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
  in subsection (1) that wholly owned the goods immediately before the
  lessor owned them.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1996, c. 31, s. 76\]]{.Repealed}

## [Customs Jurisdiction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-142580 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of federal customs laws to
designated goods

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to sections 5 to 8, federal customs laws apply in respect of goods on
  their arrival within the limits of the continental shelf of Canada for
  use as designated goods as if those goods were for use or consumption
  in Canada, and for those purposes references in federal customs laws
  to importation of goods into Canada shall be deemed to include
  bringing goods within the limits of the continental shelf of Canada
  for use as designated goods.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Relocation of goods for use as
  designated goods

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Goods in respect of which all duties and taxes are
  paid under federal customs laws and that are moved directly from one
  place to another within the limits of the continental shelf of Canada
  or from a place in Canada to a place within the limits of the
  continental shelf of Canada, for use as designated goods, shall be
  treated under federal customs laws as if they are moved from one place
  to another in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Relocation of designated goods

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Designated goods in respect of which all duties and
  taxes are paid under federal customs laws and that are moved directly
  from a place within the limits of the continental shelf of Canada to a
  place in Canada shall be treated under federal customs laws as if they
  are moved from one place to another in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-53, s. 3
- 1996, c. 31, s. 108
:::

## [Information and Documentation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-142590 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information and documentation

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President of the Canada Border Services Agency may require from any
  person, within any reasonable time that the President stipulates, the
  production of any book, record, writing or other document or any
  information that the President considers necessary for ascertaining
  whether any of sections 5 to 10 apply in any particular case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who fails to comply with a requirement
  of the President under subsection (1) is guilty of an offence
  punishable on summary conviction.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-53, s. 4
- 1999, c. 17, s. 129
- 2005, c. 38, s. 86
:::

## [Acquired Rights]{.HTitleText1} {#h-142598 .Part}

### [Goods For Use on Site]{.HTitleText2} {#h-142599 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Goods for use on site

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this section and section 8, section 3 does not apply in respect of
  goods for use as designated goods that, on June 30, 1983,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} were within the limits of the continental shelf of
    Canada,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} were ships stores or were in warehouse or storage
    in Canada, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} were imported for temporary use in Canada,

  when the goods are used or consumed within the limits of the
  continental shelf of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Leased goods

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 8, section 3 does not apply in
  respect of goods for use as designated goods that are under lease
  pursuant to a written contract, that were not Canadian-owned on June
  30, 1983 and that, on that date,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} were within the limits of the continental shelf of
    Canada,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} were ships stores or were in warehouse or storage
    in Canada, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} were imported for temporary use in Canada,

  throughout the term of the lease to the first Canadian lessee or any
  renewal thereof or, where the lease has no fixed term, for a period of
  twelve months after June 30, 1983, if the lease or any renewal thereof
  was entered into prior to January 6, 1983 and the goods are used or
  consumed within the limits of the continental shelf of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where exemption period expires

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On the expiration of the exemption period provided by
  subsection (2), section 3 applies in respect of the goods referred to
  in that subsection as if those goods were arriving within the limits
  of the continental shelf of Canada for use as designated goods.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-53, s. 5
- R.S., 1985, c. 1 (2nd Supp.), s. 213
- 1996, c. 31, s. 108
:::

### [Goods Under Prior Order]{.HTitleText2} {#h-142617 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Goods under prior order

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this section and section 8, section 3 does not apply in respect of
  goods for use as designated goods that are brought within the limits
  of the continental shelf of Canada after June 30, 1983 pursuant to a
  written contract entered into prior to January 6, 1983, when the goods
  are used or consumed within the limits of the continental shelf of
  Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Leased goods

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 8, section 3 does not apply in
  respect of goods for use as designated goods that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are under lease pursuant to a written contract
    entered into prior to January 6, 1983,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are not Canadian-owned, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} are brought within the limits of the continental
    shelf of Canada after June 30, 1983,

  throughout the term of the lease to the first Canadian lessee or any
  renewal thereof, excluding renewals entered into on or after January
  6, 1983, or, where the lease has no fixed term, for a period of twelve
  months after the day the goods are brought within the limits of the
  continental shelf of Canada, if the goods are used or consumed within
  the limits of the continental shelf of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply in respect of
  goods for use as designated goods that are brought within the limits
  of the continental shelf of Canada or that are under lease, pursuant
  to a written contract entered into prior to January 6, 1983, unless
  the goods are brought within the limits of the continental shelf of
  Canada within twelve months after the date that the persons acquiring
  the goods pursuant to the contract take possession of the goods.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) applies to any successor in title to
  any goods for use as designated goods that are referred to in that
  subsection when the goods are used or consumed within the limits of
  the continental shelf of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where exemption period expires

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} On the expiration of the exemption period provided by
  subsection (2), section 3 applies in respect of the goods referred to
  in that subsection as if those goods were arriving within the limits
  of the continental shelf of Canada for use as designated goods.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-53, s. 6
- 1996, c. 31, s. 108
:::

### [Special Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#h-142635 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Five year successive operation

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where, after June 30, 1983,
goods referred to in section 5 or 6, other than goods under lease that
are not Canadian-owned, are used exclusively for a period of five
successive years after June 30, 1983

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} within the limits of the continental shelf of Canada,
  or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in Canadian waters, including the inland waters
  within the meaning of section 2 of the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6),

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1996, c. 31, s. 77\]]{.Repealed}

those goods shall, after that five year period, be deemed to be duty and
tax paid goods under federal customs laws.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-53, s. 7
- 1996, c. 31, s. 77
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Goods removed and returned

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where goods referred to in
section 5 or 6 are removed from Canada or from a place within the limits
of the continental shelf of Canada to a place outside Canada and the
continental shelf of Canada, section 3 applies in respect of the goods
if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are returned to a place within the limits of the
  continental shelf of Canada having been advanced in value or having
  been improved in condition by any means or combined with any other
  goods outside Canada and the continental shelf of Canada; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} they are returned to a place within the limits of the
  continental shelf of Canada after five years following the day of
  their removal.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-53, s. 8
- 1996, c. 31, s. 108
:::

### [Ships]{.HTitleText2} {#h-142650 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian ships in coasting trade

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Canadian
  ships, the product of a country entitled to the benefits of the
  British Preferential Tariff or the product of the United Kingdom of
  Great Britain and Northern Ireland, the Channel Islands, the Isle of
  Man, the Republic of Ireland or Hong Kong, that, on June 30, 1983, are
  engaged in the coasting trade of Canada pursuant to Part X of the
  [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9) shall be deemed to be duty and
  tax paid ships under federal customs laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}British ships in coasting trade

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} British ships, other than Canadian ships, the product
  of a country entitled to the benefits of the British Preferential
  Tariff or the product of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and
  Northern Ireland, the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man, the Republic
  of Ireland or Hong Kong, that, on June 30, 1983, are under lease and
  are engaged in the coasting trade of Canada pursuant to Part X of the
  [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9) shall be deemed to be duty and
  tax paid ships under federal customs laws throughout the term of the
  lease to the first Canadian lessee or any renewal thereof if the lease
  or renewal was entered into prior to January 6, 1983.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1984, c. 17, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian ships under prior order

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Canadian ships, the product of a
  country entitled to the benefits of the British Preferential Tariff or
  the product of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern
  Ireland, the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man, the Republic of Ireland
  or Hong Kong, ordered pursuant to a written contract entered into
  prior to January 6, 1983 for the purpose of engaging in the coasting
  trade of Canada pursuant to Part X of the [Canada Shipping
  Act](/eng/acts/S-9), shall be deemed to be duty and tax paid ships
  under federal customs laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}British ships under prior lease

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} British ships, other than Canadian ships, the product
  of a country entitled to the benefits of the British Preferential
  Tariff or the product of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and
  Northern Ireland, the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man, the Republic
  of Ireland or Hong Kong, under lease pursuant to a written contract
  entered into prior to January 6, 1983 for the purpose of engaging in
  the coasting trade of Canada pursuant to Part X of the [Canada
  Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9), shall be deemed to be duty and tax paid
  ships under federal customs laws throughout the term of the lease to
  the first Canadian lessee or any renewal thereof, excluding renewals
  entered into on or after January 6, 1983.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply in respect of a
  ship ordered or under lease pursuant to a written contract entered
  into prior to January 6, 1983, unless the ship has been released under
  the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) within twelve months after the
  date that the persons acquiring the ship pursuant to the contract take
  possession of the ship.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-53, s. 10
- R.S., 1985, c. 1 (2nd Supp.), s. 213
