## National Flag of Canada Act {#national-flag-of-canada-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2012, c. 12
:::

Assented to 2012-06-28

An Act respecting the National Flag of Canada
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the Canadian flag is the symbol of the nation's unity;

Whereas the Canadian flag represents the principles of freedom,
democracy, courage, and justice upon which our great nation is based;

Whereas the Canadian flag represents all the citizens of Canada;

Whereas the Canadian flag represents pride in our great nation and
support for those who have sacrificed their lives for it;

And whereas it is in the national and public interest to encourage the
displaying of the National Flag;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-382663 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [National Flag of Canada Act](/eng/acts/N-8.6).

## [Displaying the National Flag]{.HTitleText1} {#h-382666 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Displaying flag

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  Canadians are encouraged to proudly display the National Flag of
  Canada in accordance with flag protocol.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allowing display

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is in control of an apartment
  building, a condominium building or building in divided co-ownership
  or another multiple-residence building or a gated community is
  encouraged to allow the National Flag of Canada to be displayed in
  accordance with flag protocol.
