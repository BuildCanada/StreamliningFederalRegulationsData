## International Development Research Centre Act {#international-development-research-centre-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. I-19
:::

An Act to establish the International Development Research Centre
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-273868 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [International Development Research Centre Act](/eng/acts/I-19).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 21(1st Supp.), s. 1
:::

## [Centre Established]{.HTitleText1} {#h-273887 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Centre established

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A corporation is
established, to be called the International Development Research Centre,
consisting of a Board of Governors that is composed of a Chairperson,
the President and not more than 12 other governors.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-19, s. 3
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1747
- 2012, c. 19, s. 427
:::

## [Objects and Powers of Centre]{.HTitleText1} {#h-273892 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporate objects

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  objects of the Centre are to initiate, encourage, support and conduct
  research into the problems of the developing regions of the world and
  into the means for applying and adapting scientific, technical and
  other knowledge to the economic and social advancement of those
  regions and, in carrying out those objects,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to enlist the talents of natural and social
    scientists and technologists in Canada and other countries;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to assist the developing regions to build up the
    research capabilities, the innovative skills and the institutions
    required to solve their problems;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to encourage generally the coordination of
    international development research; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to foster cooperation in research on development
    problems between the developed and developing regions for their
    mutual benefit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Centre, in furtherance of its objects, may
  exercise any or all of the following powers in Canada or elsewhere,
  namely, the power to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish, maintain and operate information and
    data centres and facilities for research and other activities;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} initiate and carry out research and technical
    development, including the establishment and operation of any pilot
    plant or project, to the point where the appropriate results of the
    research and development can be applied;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} support or assist research by governments, by
    international, public or private organizations and agencies, or by
    individuals;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} enter into contracts or agreements with
    governments, with international, public or private organizations and
    agencies, or with individuals;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} give recognition, by such means as the Centre deems
    appropriate, for outstanding contributions to international
    development by international, public or private organizations and
    agencies, or by individuals, and publish and otherwise disseminate
    scientific, technical or other information;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} sponsor or support conferences, seminars and other
    meetings;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} acquire, hold and alienate real property or any
    interest therein at pleasure;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} acquire by gift, bequest or otherwise, and hold,
    expend, invest, administer or dispose of, any money, securities or
    other property subject to the terms, if any, on which the money,
    securities or other property is given, bequeathed or otherwise made
    available to the Centre;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} expend, for the purposes of this Act, any money
    appropriated by Parliament for the work of the Centre or received by
    the Centre through the conduct of its operations; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} do such other things as are conducive to the
    carrying out of its objects and the exercise of its powers.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 21(1st Supp.), s. 4
:::

## [Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-273914 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Chairperson

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson of the Board shall be appointed by the Governor in Council
  to hold office during pleasure for a term not exceeding five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of President

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The first President of the Centre shall be appointed
  by the Governor in Council to hold office during pleasure for a term
  not exceeding five years and any subsequent President shall be
  appointed by the Governor in Council on the recommendation of the
  Board to hold office during pleasure for a term not exceeding five
  years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other governors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each of the governors, other than the Chairperson and
  the President, shall be appointed by the Governor in Council to hold
  office during pleasure for such term not exceeding four years as will
  ensure as far as possible the expiration in any one year of the terms
  of appointment of fewer than half of the governors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility for re-appointment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any retiring governor is eligible for re-appointment
  to the Board in the same or another capacity.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-19, s. 5
- 2010, c. 12, ss. 1748(E), 1753(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vice-Chairperson

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall elect one of the governors to be Vice-Chairperson of the
  Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence, etc., of Chairperson

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  Chairperson, or if the office of Chairperson is vacant, the
  Vice-Chairperson of the Board has all the duties and may perform all
  the functions of the Chairperson.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-19, s. 6
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1749(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President is the chief executive officer of the Centre and has
  supervision over and direction of the work and staff of the Centre.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting President

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  President, or if the office of President is vacant, the Board may
  authorize an officer of the Centre to act as President, but no person
  so authorized shall act as President for a period exceeding sixty days
  without the approval of the Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 21(1st Supp.), s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary substitute governor

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on any terms and conditions that the Governor
  in Council prescribes, appoint a temporary substitute governor if a
  governor, other than the Chairperson or President, is unable to
  perform the duties of his or her office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancy

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the office of a governor becomes vacant during
  the term of the governor appointed thereto, the Governor in Council
  may appoint a person to that office for the remainder of that term.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-19, s. 8
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1753(E)
- 2012, c. 19, s. 428(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salary and expenses

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The governors shall be paid
such remuneration and expenses as are fixed by the Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 21(1st Supp.), s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Majority of governors

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson, the Vice-chairperson and at least six other governors
  must be Canadian citizens.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At least eight of the governors must have experience
  in the field of international development or experience or training in
  the natural or social sciences or technology.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parliamentary governors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Two of the governors who are Canadian citizens, other
  than the Chairperson and the Vice-chairperson, may be appointed from
  among the members of the Senate or the House of Commons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salary and expenses

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A member appointed under subsection (3) shall not be
  paid remuneration but is eligible for expenses and, if he or she is a
  member of the House of Commons, is not, by reason of being the holder
  of the office or place in respect of which those expenses are payable,
  rendered incapable of being elected, or of sitting or voting, as a
  member of that House.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-19, s. 10
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1750
- 2012, c. 19, s. 429
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Executive committee

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be an executive committee of the Board consisting of the
  Chairperson, the President and at least three other governors annually
  elected from the Board by the governors in such a manner that a
  majority of the members of the committee are Canadian citizens.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of executive committee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The executive committee shall exercise such of the
  powers, and perform such of the functions, of the Centre as the Board
  may by by-law assign to it and shall submit at each meeting of the
  Board minutes of its proceedings since the last preceding meeting of
  the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall appoint one of the members of the
  executive committee to be the chairperson of the executive committee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The executive committee shall meet at least four
  times in each year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Three members of the executive committee, at least
  two of whom are Canadian citizens, constitute a quorum.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-19, s. 11
- 2010, c. 12, ss. 1751(E), 1753(E)
- 2012, c. 19, s. 430
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fellows of the Centre

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may appoint any person who, in the opinion of the governors, has
  made an outstanding contribution in the field of international
  development as a Fellow of the International Development Research
  Centre.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stipend for Fellows

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Centre may prescribe the period of time during
  which any person shall be named a Fellow pursuant to subsection (1)
  and the stipend, if any, to be paid to any such person.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 21(1st Supp.), s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advisory and other committees

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Board may appoint
advisory or other committees under such terms and conditions as the
Board may by by-law prescribe.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 21(1st Supp.), s. 13
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers and employees

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the by-laws,
the Board may appoint such officers, agents and employees as are
necessary for the proper conduct of the work of the Centre.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 21(1st Supp.), s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  head office of the Centre shall be at such place in Canada as may be
  designated by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of head office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may, by by-law approved by the Governor in
  Council, change the head office of the Centre to another place in
  Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 21(1st Supp.), s. 15
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings of Board

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall meet at least twice in each year, with at least one such
  meeting at the head office of the Centre, and at such other times and
  places as the Chairperson deems necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson to preside

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson shall preside at meetings of the
  Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Seven governors, at least four of whom are Canadian
  citizens, constitute a quorum of the Board. However, the proceedings
  of any meeting of the Board that is attended by more than seven
  governors are not valid unless a majority of those governors are
  Canadian citizens.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-19, s. 16
- 2010, c. 12, ss. 1752(E), 1753(E)
- 2012, c. 19, s. 431
:::

## [By-laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-274006 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Board may, with the
approval of the Governor in Council, make by-laws respecting

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the constitution of advisory or other committees
  appointed pursuant to section 13, and the salaries and expenses, if
  any, to be paid to the members of those committees;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the duties and conduct of officers, agents and
  employees of the Centre;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the conditions of employment and the remuneration of
  officers, agents and employees of the Centre;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the procedure in all business at meetings;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the assignment of any powers and functions of the
  Centre to the executive committee of the Board and the manner in which
  the powers and functions so assigned shall be exercised or performed;
  and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} generally the conduct and management of the affairs
  of the Centre.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 21(1st Supp.), s. 17
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not agent of Her Majesty

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Centre is not an agent of Her Majesty, and, except as provided in
  subsection (2), the governors and the officers, agents and employees
  of the Centre are not part of the public service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employees deemed employed in public
  service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The officers and employees of the Centre shall be
  deemed to be employed in the public service for the purposes of the
  [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and the Centre
  shall be deemed to be a public service corporation for the purposes of
  section 37 of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36) does not apply to the governors unless in the
  case of any such governor the Governor in Council otherwise directs.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-19, s. 18
- 2003, c. 22, s. 225(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of [Income Tax
Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3)

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Centre shall, for the

## [Financial]{.HTitleText1} {#h-274030 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}International Development Research Centre
Account

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Centre shall establish, under its management in a bank or in an
  authorized foreign bank within the meaning of section 2 of the [Bank
  Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) that is not subject to the restrictions and
  requirements referred to in subsection 524(2) of that Act in respect
  of its business in Canada, an account to be known as the International
  Development Research Centre Account, in this section called the
  "Account".

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Credits to Account

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} There shall be credited to the Account all amounts
  realized by the Centre under this Act in carrying out research or
  technical development or from providing any other services in Canada
  or elsewhere under any contract or agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charges to Account

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} There shall be charged to the Account all
  expenditures incurred by or for the Centre under this Act in carrying
  out the research and development activities or providing the services
  referred to in subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grant

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Finance shall, out of the special
  account for international development assistance in the accounts of
  Canada, pay to the Centre a grant of one million dollars to establish
  the Account.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-19, s. 20
- 1999, c. 28, s. 167
:::

## [Audit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-274042 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audit

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The accounts and
financial transactions of the Centre shall be audited annually by the
Auditor General of Canada and a report of the audit shall be made to the
Centre and to the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 21(1st Supp.), s. 21
- 1976-77, c. 34, s. 30(F)
:::

## [Report to Parliament]{.HTitleText1} {#h-274047 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson shall, within four months after the termination of each
  fiscal year, submit to the Minister a report relating to the
  activities of the Centre for that fiscal year, including the financial
  statements of the Centre and the report thereon of the Auditor General
  of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause the report submitted under
  subsection (1) to be laid before Parliament within fifteen days after
  the receipt thereof by the Minister or, if Parliament is not then
  sitting, on any of the first fifteen days next thereafter that either
  House of Parliament is sitting.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-19, s. 22
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1753(E)
:::
