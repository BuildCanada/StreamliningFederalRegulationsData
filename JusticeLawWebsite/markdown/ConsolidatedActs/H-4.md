## Historic Sites and Monuments Act {#historic-sites-and-monuments-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. H-4
:::

An Act to establish the Historic Sites and Monuments Board of Canada
::::

:::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-254780 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Historic Sites and Monuments Act](/eng/acts/H-4).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. H-6, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-254785 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Historic Sites and Monuments
    Board of Canada established by section
    4; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[historic place]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[historic place]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a site, building or
    other place of national historic interest or significance, and
    includes buildings or structures that are of national interest by
    reason of age or architectural design; ([lieu
    historique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister responsible for
    the Parks Canada Agency. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. H-4, s. 2
- 1995, c. 11, s. 23
- 2005, c. 2, s. 11
:::

## [Commemoration of Historic Sites]{.HTitleText1} {#h-254793 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by means of plaques or other signs or in any other
  suitable manner mark or otherwise commemorate historic places;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} make agreements with any persons for marking or
  commemorating historic places pursuant to this Act and for the care
  and preservation of any places so marked or commemorated;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} with the approval of the Governor in Council,
  establish historic museums;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} with the approval of the Treasury Board, acquire on
  behalf of Her Majesty in right of Canada any historic places, or lands
  for historic museums, or any interest therein, by purchase, lease or
  otherwise; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide for the administration, preservation and
  maintenance of any historic places acquired or historic museums
  established pursuant to this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. H-6, s. 3
:::

## [Historic Sites and Monuments Board of Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-254803 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board established

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  established a Board, to be called the Historic Sites and Monuments
  Board of Canada, consisting of 16 members as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Librarian and Archivist of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an officer of the Canadian Museum of History
    designated by the member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada who
    is designated by the Governor in Council under the [Museums
    Act](/eng/acts/M-13.4) as the Minister responsible for that Museum;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an officer of the Parks Canada Agency designated by
    the Minister; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} one representative for each province or territory,
    to be appointed by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility of appointed members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person is not eligible to be appointed or to
  continue as a representative for a province or a territory, unless
  that person resides in that province or territory.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure of office

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A member appointed by the Governor in Council holds
  office during pleasure for such period not exceeding five years as may
  be fixed by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A retiring member of the Board is eligible for
  re-appointment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairman

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall designate one of the members of the Board to
  be its Chairman.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall meet at least once in every calendar
  year at the call of the Chairman, but the time and place of each such
  meeting is subject to the approval of the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall hold such other meetings at such
  times and places as the Minister may require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Seven members of the Board constitute a quorum.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. H-4, s. 5
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1741
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Secretary

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate an officer or employee of the Parks Canada
  Agency established under the [Parks Canada Agency
  Act](/eng/acts/P-0.4) to be the Secretary of the Board, or appoint a
  Secretary of the Board at the remuneration and under the terms or
  conditions of employment that may be prescribed by the Governor in
  Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other staff

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, from among the persons employed in
  the Parks Canada Agency, provide the Board with any other employees or
  assistants that are necessary for the proper conduct of the business
  of the Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. H-4, s. 6
- 1995, c. 11, s. 25(E)
- 1998, c. 31, s. 51
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers and duties of Board

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Board may receive and
consider recommendations respecting the marking or commemoration of
historic places, the establishment of historic museums and the
administration, preservation and maintenance of historic places and
historic museums, and shall advise the Minister in carrying out his
powers under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. H-6, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Travel and living expenses

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  member of the Board appointed by the Governor in Council may be paid

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each day the member is necessarily absent from
    his ordinary place of residence for the purpose of attending at
    meetings or to other business of the Board, such remuneration as is
    fixed by the Governor in Council; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} actual travel expenses necessarily incurred in
    connection with the business of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Members of the Board other than those appointed by
  the Governor in Council are entitled to be paid actual travel and
  living expenses necessarily incurred in connection with the business
  of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clerical and stenographic assistance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} There may be paid for clerical and stenographic
  assistance

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sum of seventy-five dollars per year to the
    Chairman of the Board; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sum of thirty dollars per year to the other
    members of the Board appointed by the Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. H-6, s. 6
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-254854 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations for carrying the purposes and provisions of this Act
into effect.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. H-6, s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As
  soon as practicable after the end of each calendar year, the Board
  shall submit to the Minister a report of its proceedings for the
  calendar year in such form as the Minister may prescribe.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further reports

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the report required by subsection (1),
  the Board shall furnish the Minister with such other statements or
  reports in respect of its activities, at such time and in such manner,
  as the Minister may require.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. H-6, s. 9
:::
