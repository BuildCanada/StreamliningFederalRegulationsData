## Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act {#public-service-rearrangement-and-transfer-of-duties-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. P-34
:::

An Act to authorize rearrangements and transfers of duties in the
federal public administration
::::

::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-407760 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-34, s. 1
:::

## [Transfer of Functions and Amalgamation of Departments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-407765 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transferring functions and combining
departments

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} transfer any powers, duties or functions or the
  control or supervision of any portion of the federal public
  administration from one minister to another, or from one department
  in, or portion of, the federal public administration to another; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} amalgamate and combine any two or more departments
  under one minister and under one deputy minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-34, s. 2
- 2003, c. 22, s. 207
:::

## [Substituting of Minister or Department]{.HTitleText1} {#h-407772 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties and powers of minister and
department

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Where under this Act, or
under any other lawful authority, any power, duty or function, or the
control or supervision of any portion of the federal public
administration, is transferred from one minister to another, or from one
department in, or portion of, the federal public administration to
another, the minister, department, or portion to whom or which the
power, duty, function, control or supervision is transferred, and the
appropriate officers of that department or portion, shall, in relation
thereto, be substituted for and have and carry out the respective powers
and duties that formerly belonged to or were to be carried out by the
minister, department or portion and the respective officers of the
department or portion from whom or which the power, duty, function,
control or supervision is so transferred.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-34, s. 3
- 2003, c. 22, s. 208
:::

## [Death of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-407777 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Death of minister

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where a minister to whom
any powers, duties or functions have been transferred by name pursuant
to this Act dies or otherwise ceases to be a minister, those powers,
duties or functions may be transferred to another minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 14(2nd Supp.), s. 31
:::
