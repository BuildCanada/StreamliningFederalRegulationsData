## Public Service Pension Adjustment Act {#public-service-pension-adjustment-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"} 1970, c. P-33
:::

An Act respecting the adjustment of certain public service pensions
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-407472 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Public Service Pension Adjustment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.6).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1959, c. 32, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-407477 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act

[[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is in receipt of a
    pension by virtue of being the child of an employee and, unless the
    context otherwise requires, does not include an
    orphan; ([enfant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person in receipt of a
    pension who was

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} employed in the public service of Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a member of the forces, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Royal Canadian Mounted
      Police; ([employé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[forces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[forces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the regular forces of the
    Canadian Forces, and includes the forces known before the coming
    into force of Part II of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5)
    as the Royal Canadian Navy, the Canadian Army Active Force, the
    Permanent Active Militia, the Permanent Militia Corps, the permanent
    staff of the Militia, the Royal Canadian Air Force (Regular) and the
    Permanent Active Air Force; ([forces]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[orphan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[orphan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a child of an employee who is
    in receipt of a pension by virtue of being an
    orphan; ([orphelin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the basic pension, annual
    allowance or annuity

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} payable pursuant to an enactment or pension plan
      listed in Schedule I, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} calculated, without regard to any reduction or
      decrease made after the date the pension first became payable, on
      the basis of the length of service of the employee to or in
      respect of whom such pension, annual allowance or annuity is
      payable; ([pension]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[recipient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[recipient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee, widow, child or
    orphan; ([bénéficiaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[salary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[salary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes wages and pay and
    allowances; ([traitement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[widow]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[widow]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is in receipt of a
    pension by virtue of being the widow of an
    employee. ([veuve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- 1959, c. 32, s. 2
:::

## [Calculation of Adjustment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-407495 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of adjustment

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this Act, the annual rate of the pension payable to a recipient
  whose pension does not exceed the annual rate specified in column 2 of
  Schedule III for the class to which that recipient belongs is
  increased by an amount equal to the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount obtained by multiplying

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the annual rate of the pension payable to the
      recipient, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the annual rate specified in column 1 of
      Schedule III for the class to which the recipient belongs,

    whichever is the lesser, by the figure in columns 2 to 7 of Schedule
    II that is appropriate to the salary basis on which the pension of
    the recipient is calculated and is opposite the latest period of
    service of the employee, to or in respect of whom the pension is
    payable, as set out in column 1 of the said Schedule, that was used
    to calculate the pension; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to the amount remaining after
    subtracting the annual rate of the pension payable to that recipient
    from the annual rate specified in column 2 of Schedule III for the
    class to which the recipient belongs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pension deemed to be calculated on ten
  year average salary basis

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the pension of a recipient was calculated on
  the average annual salary received by the employee to or in respect of
  whom the pension is payable during the period of pensionable service
  to that employee's credit, but would, if that employee had ten years
  or more pensionable service, have been calculated on the basis of the
  average annual salary received by him during a ten year period, the
  pension of that recipient shall, for the purposes of this Act, be
  deemed to be calculated on a ten year average salary basis.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1959, c. 32, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person in receipt of more than one
pension

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where a recipient is in
receipt of more than one pension and the total annual rate of the
pensions payable to that recipient exceeds the annual rate specified in
column 2 of Schedule III for the class to which the recipient belongs,
no increase shall be made pursuant to this Act in any of the pensions
payable to that recipient.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1959, c. 32, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules applicable where person is in
receipt of two pensions

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), where a recipient is in receipt of two pensions and
  the total annual rate of both pensions does not exceed the annual rate
  specified in column 2 of Schedule III for the class to which the
  recipient belongs, each pension of that recipient shall be increased
  in accordance with the following rules:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the annual rate of the first pension of the
    recipient is equal to or greater than the annual rate specified in
    column 1 of Schedule III for the class to which the recipient
    belongs, the annual rate of the first pension of that recipient
    shall be increased in accordance with the formula set out in section
    3, and no increase shall be made in the annual rate of the second
    pension of the recipient;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the aggregate of the annual rate of the first
    and second pensions of the recipient is less than the annual rate
    specified in column 1 of Schedule III for the class to which the
    recipient belongs, the annual rate of each of the pensions of that
    recipient shall be increased in accordance with the formula set out
    in section 3; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the annual rate of the first pension of the
    recipient is less than the annual rate specified in column 1 of
    Schedule III for the class to which the recipient belongs, but the
    aggregate of the annual rate of the first and second pensions of the
    recipient exceeds the annual rate specified in column 1 of Schedule
    III for the class to which the recipient belongs, the annual rate of
    each of the pensions of that recipient shall be increased in
    accordance with the formula set out in section 3, and for the
    purposes of this paragraph the annual rate of the second pension
    shall be deemed to be an amount equal to the resultant obtained by
    subtracting from the annual rate specified in column 1 of Schedule
    III for the class to which the recipient belongs, the annual rate of
    the first pension of the recipient.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum increase

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No increase shall be made pursuant to this section in
  the pension payable to a recipient so that the aggregate of the annual
  rate of both pensions of that recipient and the increases thereto
  exceeds the annual rate specified in column 2 of Schedule III for the
  class to which the recipient belongs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section

  [[first pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[first pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the pension payable to
      a recipient and calculated in respect of a period of service that
      is earlier in time than the latest period of service in respect of
      which the other pension payable to that recipient is
      calculated; ([première pension]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[second pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[second pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pension payable to
      a recipient other than a first pension. ([seconde
      pension]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- 1959, c. 32, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person in receipt of two or more pensions

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where a recipient is in
receipt of more than two pensions and the total annual rate of all the
pensions payable to him does not exceed the annual rate specified in
column 2 of Schedule III for the class to which that recipient belongs,
each pension of that recipient shall be increased by such amount as the
Treasury Board determines, but the aggregate of the increases in the
pensions payable to that recipient shall not exceed an annual amount
equal to the lesser of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the maximum annual increase specified in column 3 of
  Schedule III for the class to which that recipient belongs; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount remaining after subtracting the aggregate
  of the annual rate of all the pensions payable to that recipient from
  the annual rate specified in column 2 of Schedule III for the class to
  which the recipient belongs.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1959, c. 32, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person in receipt of two or more pensions
as employee and widow

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything in this
  Act, where a recipient as an employee and as a widow of an employee is
  in receipt of two or more pensions

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the total rate of all the pensions payable to
    that recipient exceeds three thousand dollars, no increase shall be
    made in the annual rate of any of the pensions payable to that
    recipient; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the total rate of all the pensions payable to
    that recipient does not exceed three thousand dollars, each pension
    shall, subject to subsection (2), be increased in accordance with
    the formula set out in section 3.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum increase

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The aggregate of all increases made in the pensions
  of a recipient described in subsection (1) shall not exceed an annual
  amount equal to the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount remaining after subtracting from three
    thousand dollars the aggregate of the annual amount of the pensions
    payable to the recipient; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an amount determined by the Treasury Board but in
    no case exceeding six hundred and forty dollars.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1959, c. 32, s. 7
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-407543 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pensions payable under Civil Service
Superannuation Act

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  increase shall be made pursuant to this Act in the annual rate of the
  pension payable to a recipient under the Civil Service Superannuation
  Act only where the pension is payable in respect of service in the
  Civil Service to the credit of the employee to or in respect of whom
  the pension is payable under that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pensions payable under [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An increase shall be made pursuant to this Act in the
  annual rate of the pension payable to a recipient under the [Public
  Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) only where the pension is
  payable in respect of pensionable service to the credit of the
  employee to or in respect of whom the pension is payable under that
  Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1959, c. 32, s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement of payment of increase

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No increase shall be made
pursuant to this Act in the annual rate of the pension payable to a
recipient for any period before the 1st day of August 1959.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1959, c. 32, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pensions of recipients re-employed in
public services

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations specifying, notwithstanding
  anything in this Act, the extent to which and the circumstances under
  which any increase in a pension payable to a recipient who holds any
  office or position or performs any services the remuneration for which
  is payable out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund or by an agent of Her
  Majesty in right of Canada shall be reduced or suspended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding section 9 and subject to such
  conditions as the Treasury Board may prescribe, the amount of the
  increase determined in accordance with this Act in the annual rate of
  the pension payable to a person who was a recipient described in
  subsection (1) at any time between April 1st, 1959 and July 8th, 1959,
  is payable from April 1st, 1959.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1959, c. 32, s. 10
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Increases deemed paid under [Public
Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), etc.

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of section 15 of the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36) or a similar provision in any other
  superannuation Act that provides for the payment to the estate of a
  contributor under that Act of any amount by which the amount of a
  return of contributions exceeds the aggregate of all amounts paid
  under that Act to or in respect of that contributor, the amount of any
  increase to a pension paid to a recipient pursuant to this Act shall
  be deemed to have been paid to the recipient under the [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) or other superannuation Act, as
  the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section "other superannuation Act" means any
  Act under which a pension is paid that is increased pursuant to this
  Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1959, c. 32, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of payment of increases

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The amount by which the
pension of a recipient is increased pursuant to this Act shall be paid
at the same times, in the same manner, during or in respect of the same
periods and subject to the same terms and conditions, including
termination of payment, as the pension of the recipient.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1959, c. 32, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts payable out of C.R.F.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The amounts payable under
this Act shall be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1959, c. 32, s. 13
:::
