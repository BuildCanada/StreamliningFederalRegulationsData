## Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development Act {#department-of-foreign-affairs-trade-and-development-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2013, c. 33, s. 174
:::

Assented to 2013-06-26

An Act respecting the Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and
Development

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 174 of chapter 33 of the Statutes of Canada, 2013,
in force on assent June 26, 2013.\]
:::

::: Enacts

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233893 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development
Act](/eng/acts/F-27.5).

## [Continuation of the Department]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233896 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Department continued

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Department of Foreign Affairs and International Trade is continued
  under the name of the Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and
  Development over which the Minister of Foreign Affairs, appointed by
  commission under the Great Seal, is to preside.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Foreign Affairs, in this Act referred
  to as the "Minister", holds office during pleasure and has the
  management and direction of the Department in Canada and abroad.

## [Additional Ministers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233902 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister for International Trade

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A Minister for
International Trade is to be appointed by commission under the Great
Seal to hold office during pleasure and to assist the Minister in
carrying out his or her responsibilities relating to international
trade.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister for International Development

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A Minister for
International Development is to be appointed by commission under the
Great Seal to hold office during pleasure and to assist the Minister in
carrying out his or her responsibilities relating to international
development, poverty reduction and humanitarian assistance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of departmental services and
facilities

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A minister appointed under
section 3 or 4 is to act with the concurrence of the Minister in
carrying out his or her responsibilities and is to make use of the
services and facilities of the Department.

## [Committees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233909 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committees to advise and assist

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
establish advisory and other committees to advise or assist the Minister
or to exercise and perform any powers, duties and functions that the
Governor in Council specifies and may fix the remuneration and expenses
to be paid to the members of the committees so established.

## [Officers of the Department]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233912 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy head

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
appoint an officer called the Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs to hold
office during pleasure and to be the deputy head of the Department.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional deputy heads

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may appoint three Associate Deputy Ministers of
  Foreign Affairs, each of whom is to have the rank and status of a
  deputy head of a department and is, under the Deputy Minister of
  Foreign Affairs, to exercise and perform any powers, duties and
  functions, as a deputy of the Minister and otherwise, that the
  Minister specifies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy Ministers for International
  Trade and for International Development

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may designate one of the
  Associate Deputy Ministers appointed under subsection (1) to be Deputy
  Minister for International Trade and one to be Deputy Minister for
  International Development.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coordinator, International Economic
Relations

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
designate or appoint a person in the federal public administration as
the Coordinator, International Economic Relations who is to have the
rank and status of a deputy head of a department and is, subject to the
direction of the Governor in Council, to exercise and perform any
powers, duties and functions, as a deputy of the Minister and otherwise,
that the Minister specifies.

## [Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233922 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions of Minister

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  powers, duties and functions of the Minister extend to and include all
  matters over which Parliament has jurisdiction, not by law assigned to
  any other department, board or agency of the Government of Canada,
  relating to the conduct of the external affairs of Canada, including
  international trade and commerce and international development.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions of
  Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In exercising and performing his or her powers,
  duties and functions under this Act, the Minister is to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} conduct all diplomatic and consular relations on
    behalf of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} conduct all official communication between the
    Government of Canada and the government of any other country and
    between the Government of Canada and any international organization;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} conduct and manage international negotiations as
    they relate to Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} coordinate Canada's international economic
    relations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} foster the expansion of Canada's international
    trade and commerce;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} foster sustainable international development and
    poverty reduction in developing countries and provide humanitarian
    assistance during crises;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} coordinate the direction given by the Government of
    Canada to the heads of Canada's diplomatic and consular missions;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} have the management of Canada's diplomatic and
    consular missions;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} administer the foreign service of Canada;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} foster the development of international law and its
    application in Canada's external relations; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} carry out any other duties and functions that are
    by law assigned to him or her.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supply management

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} In exercising and performing the powers, duties and
  functions set out in subsection (2), the Minister must not make any
  commitment on behalf of the Government of Canada, by international
  trade treaty or agreement, that would have the effect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} increasing the [[tariff rate
    quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, within the meaning of subsection 2(1)
    of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), applicable to dairy
    products, poultry or eggs; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reducing the tariff applicable to those goods when
    they are imported in excess of the applicable tariff rate quota.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Programs

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may develop and carry out programs
  related to the Minister's powers, duties and functions for the
  promotion of Canada's interests abroad, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fostering of the expansion of Canada's
    international trade and commerce; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fostering of sustainable international
    development and of poverty reduction in developing countries and the
    provision of humanitarian assistance during crises.

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233943 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of the Minister and the
  Treasury Board, make regulations prescribing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} documents issued by the Minister of Citizenship and
    Immigration for travel purposes for which fees are payable; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the fees and the time and manner of
    their payment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cost recovery

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fees are to be prescribed with a view to the
  recovery of the costs incurred by Her Majesty in right of Canada in
  providing consular services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional to other fees

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The fees are to be paid in addition to any other fees
  payable under section 19 of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11) in respect of the same documents.

## [Agreements with Provinces]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233955 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, with
the approval of the Governor in Council, enter into agreements with the
government of any province or any agency of a province respecting the
carrying out of programs related to the Minister's powers, duties and
functions.

## [Duties of Additional Ministers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233958 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister for International Trade

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5, the
Minister for International Trade is to promote the expansion of Canada's
international trade and commerce by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} assisting Canadian exporters in their international
  marketing initiatives and promoting Canadian export sales;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} improving the access of Canadian produce, products
  and services to external markets through trade negotiations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} fostering trade relations with other countries; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} contributing to the improvement of world trading
  conditions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister for International Development

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5, the
Minister for International Development is to foster sustainable
international development and poverty reduction in developing countries
and provide humanitarian assistance during crises by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} undertaking activities related to international
  development and humanitarian assistance;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensuring the effectiveness of Canada's international
  development and humanitarian assistance activities;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} fostering relations with other countries and
  organizations engaged in international development or humanitarian
  assistance activities; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} ensuring Canada's contributions to international
  development and humanitarian assistance are in line with Canadian
  values and priorities.

## [Heads of Missions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233971 .Part}

Definition of [[head of mission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[head of mission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an ambassador, high commissioner or consul-general
    of Canada; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other person that is appointed to represent
    Canada in another country or a portion of another country or at an
    international organization or diplomatic conference and that is
    designated head of mission by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of head of mission

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise instructed by the Governor in
  Council, a head of mission is to have the management and direction of
  their mission and its activities and the supervision of the official
  activities of the various departments and agencies of the Government
  of Canada in the country or portion of the country or at the
  international organization to which they are appointed.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233979 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister for International Cooperation
and President of CIDA

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Any person who holds the
office of Minister for International Cooperation or of President of the
Canadian International Development Agency on the day on which this
section comes into force is deemed to hold the office of Minister for
International Development or Deputy Minister for International
Development, respectively, on and after that day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Positions

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act is to
be construed as affecting the status of an employee who, immediately
before the coming into force of this Act, occupied a position in the
Canadian International Development Agency except that the employee, on
the coming into force of this section, occupies their position in the
Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development under the authority
of the Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of appropriations

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Any amount appropriated
by an Act of Parliament for the fiscal year in which this section comes
into force to defray the charges and expenses of the Canadian
International Development Agency that is unexpended is deemed to have
been appropriated to defray the charges and expenses of the Department
of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of powers, duties and functions

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** If, under any Act of
Parliament, any instrument made under an Act of Parliament or any order,
contract, lease, licence or other document, any power, duty or function
is vested in or may be exercised or performed by the Minister for
International Cooperation or Minister of International Cooperation, the
President of the Canadian International Development Agency or any other
employee of that Agency, that power, duty or function is vested in or
may be exercised or performed by the Minister for International
Development, the Deputy Minister for International Development or the
appropriate officer of the Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and
Development, as the case may be.
