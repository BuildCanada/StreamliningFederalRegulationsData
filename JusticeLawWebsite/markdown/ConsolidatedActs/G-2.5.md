## Genetic Non-Discrimination Act {#genetic-non-discrimination-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2017, c. 3
:::

Assented to 2017-05-04

An Act to prohibit and prevent genetic discrimination
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247308 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Genetic Non-Discrimination Act](/eng/acts/G-2.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247311 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[disclose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[disclose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes to authorize
    disclosure. ([communiquer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[genetic test]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[genetic test]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a test that analyzes DNA,
    RNA or chromosomes for purposes such as the prediction of disease or
    vertical transmission risks, or monitoring, diagnosis or
    prognosis. ([test génétique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[health care practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[health care practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
    lawfully entitled under the law of a province to provide health
    services in the place in which the services are provided by that
    person. ([professionnel de la santé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247317 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Genetic test

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for any person to require an individual to undergo a
  genetic test as a condition of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} providing goods or services to that individual;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entering into or continuing a contract or agreement
    with that individual; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} offering or continuing specific terms or conditions
    in a contract or agreement with that individual.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to undergo genetic test

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for any person to refuse to engage
  in an activity described in any of paragraphs (1)(a) to (c) in respect
  of an individual on the grounds that the individual has refused to
  undergo a genetic test.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of results

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for any person to require an individual to disclose the
  results of a genetic test as a condition of engaging in an activity
  described in any of paragraphs 3(1)(a) to (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to disclose results

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for any person to refuse to engage
  in an activity described in any of paragraphs 3(1)(a) to (c) in
  respect of an individual on the grounds that the individual has
  refused to disclose the results of a genetic test.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written consent

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person who is engaged in an activity described in any of paragraphs
3(1)(a) to (c) in respect of an individual to collect, use or disclose
the results of a genetic test of the individual without the individual's
written consent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions: health care practitioners and
researchers

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Sections 3 to 5 do not
apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a physician, a pharmacist or any other health care
  practitioner in respect of an individual to whom they are providing
  health services; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who is conducting medical, pharmaceutical or
  scientific research in respect of an individual who is a participant
  in the research.

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247337 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of sections 3 to 5

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes any of sections 3 to 5 is guilty of an offence and is liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine not exceeding
  \$1,000,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years, or
  to both; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding
  \$300,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding twelve months,
  or to both.
