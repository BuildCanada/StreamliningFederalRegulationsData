## Motor Vehicle Safety Act {#motor-vehicle-safety-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1993, c. 16
:::

Assented to 1993-05-06

An Act to regulate the manufacture and importation of motor vehicles and
motor vehicle equipment to reduce the risk of death, injury and damage
to property and the environment
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-353310 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Motor Vehicle Safety Act](/eng/acts/M-10.01).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-353313 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} who is engaged in the business of manufacturing
      vehicles or equipment in Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} who is engaged in the business of selling to
      other persons, for the purpose of resale by those persons,
      vehicles or equipment obtained directly from a person described in
      paragraph (a) or the agent of such a person, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} who imports a vehicle or article of equipment
      into Canada for the purpose of
      sale; ([entreprise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any equipment set out in
    Schedule I that is designed for use in or on a
    vehicle; ([équipement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated as an
    automotive inspector pursuant to section
    14; ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[manufacture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[manufacture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a vehicle,
    includes any process of assembling or altering the vehicle prior to
    its sale to the first retail
    purchaser; ([fabrication]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou
    [construction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Transport; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[national safety mark]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[national safety mark]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prescribed
    expression, symbol or abbreviation or any combination of
    them; ([marque nationale de sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by regulations
    made under section 11; ([Version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes to offer for sale or lease,
    have in possession for sale or lease or deliver for sale or
    lease; ([vente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a standard that governs the
    design, manufacture, functioning or marking of vehicles or equipment
    for the purpose of reducing, directly or indirectly, the risk of
    death, injury or property damage from vehicle use, including a
    standard aimed at increasing the use of safety features by the
    public or facilitating the creation, recording or retrieval of
    information; ([norme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Transportation Appeal
    Tribunal of Canada established by subsection 2(1) of the
    [Transportation Appeal Tribunal of Canada
    Act](/eng/acts/T-18.5); ([Tribunal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any vehicle that is designed
    to be, or is capable of being, driven or drawn on roads by any means
    other than muscular power exclusively, but does not include any
    vehicle that is designed to run exclusively on
    rails. ([véhicule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [National Safety Marks]{.HTitleText1} {#h-353347 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National trademarks

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  national safety marks are hereby declared to be national trademarks
  and, except as provided in this Act, the exclusive property in and
  right to the use of those marks are vested in Her Majesty in right of
  Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of marks

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company authorized by the Minister, as provided for
  in the regulations, may apply a national safety mark to a vehicle or
  equipment, as provided for in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of address

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} The company shall provide the Minister with the
  address of the premises at which the national safety mark is to be
  applied.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall use a national safety mark except as
  authorized by this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confusing marks

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall use a mark other than a national
  safety mark in such a manner that it is likely to be mistaken for a
  national safety mark.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interprovincial shipments

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided by the regulations, no company shall ship from one province to
another, or deliver to any person for the purpose of being so shipped,
any vehicle or equipment of a prescribed class manufactured in Canada

section 3.

## [Vehicle and Equipment Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-353363 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance by companies

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  company shall apply a national safety mark to any vehicle or
  equipment, sell any vehicle or equipment to which a national safety
  mark has been applied, or import into Canada any vehicle or equipment
  of a prescribed class unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle or equipment conforms to the standards
    prescribed for vehicles or equipment of its class at the time the
    main assembly of the vehicle was completed or the equipment was
    manufactured;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} evidence of that conformity has been obtained and
    produced as provided for in the regulations or, if the regulations
    so provide, to the Minister's satisfaction;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1999, c. 33, s. 351\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prescribed information is marked on the vehicle or
    equipment as provided for in the regulations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} prescribed documentation or prescribed accessories
    accompany the vehicle or equipment as provided for in the
    regulations;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} prescribed information relating to the operation of
    the vehicle or equipment is disseminated as provided for in the
    regulations;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} records are maintained and furnished, as provided
    for in the regulations, in relation to the design, manufacture,
    testing and field performance of the vehicle or equipment, for the
    purpose of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} enabling an inspector to determine whether the
      vehicle or equipment conforms to all requirements applicable to
      it, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} facilitating the identification and analysis of
      defects referred to in subsection 10(1); and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in the case of equipment, the company maintains, as
    provided for in the regulations, a registration system by which any
    person who has purchased equipment manufactured, imported or sold by
    the company and who wishes to be identified may be so identified.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2014, c. 20, s. 216\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of compliance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise provided by the regulations, a
  company may apply a national safety mark to, or import into Canada, a
  vehicle that does not satisfy a requirement of subsection (1) if that
  requirement is satisfied before the vehicle leaves the possession or
  control of the company and before the vehicle is presented for
  registration under the laws of a province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certification by foreign agency

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the regulations so provide in relation to a
  prescribed standard that corresponds to a prescribed enactment of a
  foreign government, a vehicle shall be deemed to conform to the
  standard if a prescribed agency of that government has certified that
  the vehicle conforms to the enactment as applied by the agency, unless
  the Minister determines that the vehicle does not conform to that
  enactment as so applied.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance by all persons importing
vehicles

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No person shall import into
Canada a vehicle of a prescribed class unless the requirements of
paragraphs 5(1)(a), (b), (d) and (e) are satisfied in respect of the
vehicle.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions for certain importations

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections
  5 and 6 do not apply in respect of the importation of a vehicle or
  equipment if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the importation, the person importing the
    vehicle or equipment makes a declaration, as provided for in the
    regulations, that the vehicle or equipment

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} will be used in Canada solely for a prescribed
      purpose,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} will remain in Canada for a period of not more
      than one year or any other period that is specified by the
      Minister, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} meets or will meet any other prescribed
      requirement; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle or equipment is passing through Canada
    to another country or is exclusively for use by a visitor to Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Export or destruction

  [(1.01)]{.lawlabel} A person who imports a vehicle or equipment under
  paragraph (1)(a) shall, as provided for in the regulations, export or
  destroy the vehicle or equipment before the end of the period referred
  to in subparagraph (1)(a)(ii).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- donation

  [(1.02)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1.01), a person who imports a
  vehicle under paragraph (1)(a) may, with the Minister's approval,
  donate it as provided for in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- temporary importation

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Sections 5 and 6 do not apply to a resident of
  Canada who imports a vehicle that is licensed in the United States if
  the vehicle is rented in the United States from a vehicle rental
  business and is being imported into Canada for non-commercial
  purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Importer to remove vehicle from Canada

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} A resident of Canada who imports a vehicle under
  subsection (1.1) shall remove the vehicle from Canada within a period
  of 30 days, or any other prescribed period, beginning on the day on
  which the vehicle is imported.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rental business to remove vehicle from
  Canada

  [(1.3)]{.lawlabel} If, within the period referred to in subsection
  (1.2), the resident of Canada delivers the vehicle to a vehicle rental
  business in Canada with the consent of the business, then the vehicle
  rental business shall remove the vehicle from Canada before the end of
  that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection (1.2) does not apply

  [(1.4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, if a vehicle rental business
  is required under subsection (1.3) to remove the vehicle from Canada,
  subsection (1.2) does not apply to the resident of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(1.5)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting vehicles imported under subsection
    (1.1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the importation or removal of vehicles
    under subsections (1.1) to (1.4); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} defining, for the purposes of those subsections,
    any term used in them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vehicles from the United States or
  Mexico

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 5 and 6 do not apply to the importation of a
  vehicle that has been sold at the retail level in the United States or
  that is a prescribed vehicle from Mexico if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle meets any prescribed requirements; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person importing the vehicle makes a
    declaration, as provided for in the regulations, that the vehicle
    will be, before being presented for registration by a province and
    within the prescribed period,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} brought into compliance with any prescribed
      requirements, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} certified, as provided for in the regulations,
      as compliant with the requirements referred to in subparagraph (i)
      by the person designated in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Importation for components

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} Sections 5 and 6 do not apply to the importation of
  a vehicle that has been sold at the retail level in the United States
  or that is a prescribed vehicle from Mexico if the person importing
  the vehicle makes a declaration, as provided for in the regulations,
  that the vehicle

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} will not be presented for registration by a
    province;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} will be registered, as provided for in the
    regulations, by the person designated in the regulations; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} will be dismantled for its components.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change in standard since manufacture

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A vehicle that does not conform to a standard
  prescribed for vehicles of its class at the time of its main assembly
  may be imported notwithstanding section 5 or 6 if, at the time of its
  importation, the standard is no longer in effect and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle conforms to the corresponding standard
    prescribed for vehicles of its class at that time; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is no corresponding standard at that time.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2014, c. 20, s. 217\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declarations binding

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person who makes a declaration under this section
  shall comply with the declaration.

## [Vehicle Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-353430 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from standards

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  application by a company as provided for in the regulations, the
  Minister may, by order, grant an exemption for a specified period, in
  accordance with any conditions specified in the order, for any model
  of vehicle manufactured or imported by the company from conformity
  with any prescribed standard if the exemption from that standard
  would, in the opinion of the Minister, promote the development of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} new safety features that are equivalent to or
    superior to those that conform to prescribed standards; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} new kinds of vehicles, technologies, vehicle
    systems or components.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for granting exemption

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An exemption must only be granted for a model if the
  exemption would not substantially diminish the overall safety
  performance of the model.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each exemption order must, as soon as feasible, be
  published through the Internet or by any other means that the Minister
  considers appropriate.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2018, c. 2, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Renewal of exemption

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} On expiration of the period of an exemption, a new
  exemption may be granted in accordance with this section.

## [Notices of Defect and of Non-Compliance and Orders]{.HTitleText1} {#h-353445 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of defect

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company that applies a national safety mark to any vehicle or
  equipment, sells any vehicle or equipment to which a national safety
  mark has been applied or imports any vehicle or equipment of a class
  for which standards are prescribed shall, as provided for in the
  regulations, give notice of any defect in the design, manufacture or
  functioning of the vehicle or equipment that affects or is likely to
  affect the safety of any person to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister, on becoming aware of the defect; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the current owner and any other prescribed person,
    within the period provided for in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice already given

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company is not required to give notice of a defect

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for which notice has already been given by another
    company that manufactured, sold or imported the vehicle or
    equipment; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for which a notice of non-compliance has been given
    under section 10.1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent notice

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines that a defect has not
  been corrected in an adequate number of vehicles or equipment for
  which notice was given under subsection (1), the Minister may, by
  order, require the company to provide, in accordance with any
  conditions specified in the order, a subsequent notice to those
  persons who have not had the defect corrected. In making this
  determination, the Minister shall take into consideration, among other
  things,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the defect;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the safety risk arising from it; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total number of vehicles or equipment affected.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unknown owner

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is satisfied that the current owner
  of a vehicle or equipment cannot reasonably be determined by a
  company,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company shall give notice in any other manner
    that is acceptable to the Minister; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister may exempt the company from the
    requirement to give notice to the current owner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to order

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by order, require any company that
  applies a national safety mark to any vehicle or equipment, sells any
  vehicle or equipment to which a national safety mark has been applied
  or imports any vehicle or equipment of a class for which standards are
  prescribed to give notice of a defect in the manner specified in the
  order, if the Minister considers that it is in the interest of safety.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22)
  does not apply to an order made under subsection (2.1) or (4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Particulars to provincial authorities

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Forthwith on receiving any notice under subsection
  (1), the Minister shall forward full particulars of the notice to the
  minister or other officer responsible for motor vehicle administration
  in each province.

- [(6) and (7)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2014, c. 20, s.
  221\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of non-compliance

- **[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company that applies a national safety mark to any vehicle or
  equipment, sells any vehicle or equipment to which a national safety
  mark has been applied or imports any vehicle or equipment of a class
  for which standards are prescribed shall, as provided for in the
  regulations, give notice of any non-compliance of the vehicle or
  equipment with the regulations to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister, on becoming aware of the
    non-compliance; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the current owner and any other prescribed person,
    within the period provided for in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice already given

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company is not required to give notice of any
  non-compliance for which notice has already been given by another
  company that manufactured, sold or imported the vehicle or equipment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines that the non-compliance is
  inconsequential to safety, the company is not required to give notice
  under paragraph (1)(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines that a non-compliance has
  not been corrected in an adequate number of vehicles or equipment, the
  Minister may, by order, require a company to provide, in accordance
  with any conditions specified in the order, a subsequent notice to
  those persons who have not had the non-compliance corrected. In making
  this determination, the Minister shall take into consideration, among
  other things,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the non-compliance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the safety risk arising from it; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total number of vehicles or equipment affected.

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} An enforcement officer who enters a place under

  carry out the duties or functions of an inspector under subsection
  15(2).

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} An enforcement officer may, for a purpose related to
  determining whether a violation referred to in section 16.13 has been
  committed, order a person in writing to provide, on the date, at the
  time and place and in the manner specified by the enforcement officer,
  any document, information or electronic data specified by the
  enforcement officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to enforcement officer

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Any person who owns or has charge of a place entered
  by an enforcement officer under subsection (3) and every person
  present there shall answer all of the enforcement officer's reasonable
  questions related to the functions of the enforcement officer, provide
  access to all electronic data that the enforcement officer may
  reasonably require, provide all reasonable assistance in their power
  to enable the enforcement officer to carry out the enforcement
  officer's duties and furnish any information that the enforcement
  officer reasonably requires for the purposes of the administration of
  this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violation

- **[[16.13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16.13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes a
  provision designated under paragraph 16.1(a) commits a violation and
  is liable to a penalty of not more than the maximum amount prescribed
  under paragraph 16.1(b) or (d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of penalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of the penalty is to promote compliance
  with this Act and not to punish.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing violation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A violation that is committed or continued on more
  than one day constitutes a separate violation for each day on which it
  is committed or continued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a contravention of a provision designated under
  paragraph 16.1(a) may be proceeded with as a violation or as an
  offence, proceeding with it in one manner precludes proceeding with it
  in the other.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nature of violation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a violation is not an offence
  and, accordingly, section 126 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)
  does not apply in respect of a violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Due diligence available

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Due diligence is a defence in a proceeding in respect
  of a violation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for review

- **[[16.18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16.18
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person served with a notice of

  facts of the alleged contravention or the amount of the penalty
  reviewed shall, within 30 days after the notice of violation is served

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time and place for review

  a time and place for the review and shall notify the Minister and the
  person who filed the request of the time and place in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review procedure

  request with an opportunity that is consistent with procedural

  representations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Burden of proof

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister has the burden of establishing on a
  balance of probabilities that a person has committed a violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person not compelled to testify

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person who is alleged to have committed a violation
  is not required to give any evidence or testimony in the matter.

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-353686 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  corporation or company that contravenes this Act, the regulations or
  an order

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
    conviction and is liable to a fine of not more than \$200,000; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an indictable offence and is liable to
    a fine of not more than \$2 million.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every individual who contravenes this Act, the
  regulations or an order

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
    conviction and is liable to a fine of not more than \$4,000 or to
    imprisonment for a term of not more than six months, or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an indictable offence and is liable to
    a fine of not more than \$20,000 or to imprisonment for a term of
    not more than two years, or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offence

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} An offence that is committed or continued on more
  than one day constitutes a separate offence for each day on which it
  is committed or continued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence where more than one
  manufacturer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In a prosecution for a contravention of section 4 or
  subsection 5(1) by a company engaged in the business of assembling or
  altering vehicles, it is a defence for the company to establish that
  the contravention occurred as a result of work previously done on a
  vehicle by another person engaged in the manufacture of the vehicle.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Due diligence defence

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In a prosecution for a contravention of section 16, a
  person is not to be found guilty of an offence under subsection (1)
  or (2) if they establish that they exercised all due diligence to
  prevent the commission of the offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence by employee or agent

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In a
  prosecution under this Act, it is sufficient proof of an offence to
  establish that it was committed by an employee or agent of the accused
  whether or not the employee or agent is identified or has been
  prosecuted for the offence, unless the accused establishes that the
  offence was committed without the knowledge or consent of the accused
  and that the accused exercised all due diligence to prevent its
  commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A prosecution by way of summary conviction under this
  Act may not be instituted later than two years after the time when the
  subject-matter of the prosecution arose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Venue

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A prosecution under this Act may be instituted, tried
  and determined by a court in any territorial jurisdiction in which the
  accused carries on business, regardless of where the subject-matter of
  the prosecution arose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In a
  prosecution under this Act, evidence that a vehicle or equipment bore
  a name or mark purporting to be the name or mark of a company engaged
  in the business of manufacturing, importing or selling vehicles or
  equipment is, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, proof that
  the vehicle or equipment was manufactured, imported or sold, as the
  case may be, by that company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In a prosecution under this Act, evidence that a
  vehicle or equipment bearing a national safety mark was manufactured
  by a company is, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, proof
  that the national safety mark was affixed by the company.

## [Forfeiture]{.HTitleText1} {#h-353716 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture --- conviction for offence

- **[[19.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  a person is convicted of an offence under this Act, the court may
  order that a seized vehicle, equipment or component by means of or in
  relation to which the offence was committed be forfeited to Her
  Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A seized vehicle, equipment or component that is
  forfeited may be stored and disposed of at the expense of its owner or
  the person who was entitled to possess it at the time of its seizure.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2018, c. 2, s. 17
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture --- on consent

**[[19.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19.2 .sectionLabel}** If the owner of a
seized vehicle, equipment or component consents to its forfeiture, the
seized vehicle, equipment or component is forfeited to Her Majesty in
right of Canada and may be stored and disposed of at the owner's
expense.

## [Research, Analysis, Testing and Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-353728 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} conduct any research, studies, evaluations and
    analyses that the Minister considers necessary for the
    administration and enforcement of this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} undertake research and development programs for the
    study of the impact of vehicles, drivers of vehicles, and streets
    and highways on road safety, energy conservation and the environment
    and for the promotion of measures to control that impact;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} establish and operate facilities for the testing of
    vehicles, equipment and components, and acquire test equipment for
    that purpose;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} make the facilities referred to in paragraph (c)
    and all related materials, parts and services available to any
    person;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} collect any information related to vehicles or
    equipment that the Minister considers to be in the public interest;
    and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} publish or otherwise disseminate any information,
    other than personal information, relating to the activities of the
    Minister under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal information

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraphs (1)(a), (b) and (e),
  the Minister may collect personal information, as defined in section 3
  of the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21), including personal information
  from third parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees and prices to be charged

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by order, determine or establish
  the manner of determining a fee to be charged for any facility or
  service made available to any person under paragraph (1)(d), the price
  to be charged for any material or part referred to in that paragraph,
  and the terms of payment of fees and prices so charged.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of charges

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any amount charged pursuant to subsection (2) may be
  applied against the cost of making facilities, materials, parts and
  services available to persons under paragraph (1)(d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of charges

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any amount charged pursuant to subsection (2) may be
  recovered as a debt due to Her Majesty in the Federal Court.

    :::: section

    - ###### Compliance by companies of prescribed class {#compliance-by-companies-of-prescribed-class .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} No company of a prescribed class shall apply a
      national safety mark to any vehicle or equipment, sell any vehicle
      or equipment to which a national safety mark has been applied or
      import into Canada any vehicle or equipment of a prescribed class
      unless the company acquires and maintains, as provided for in the
      regulations and in relation to a vehicle or equipment that is
      substantially similar to that vehicle or equipment, records
      related to prescribed subject matters.

    - ###### Regulations {#regulations .MarginalNote}

      [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make regulations
      defining, for the purposes of subsection (2), any term used in it.
