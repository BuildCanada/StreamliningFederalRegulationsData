## Manganese-based Fuel Additives Act {#manganese-based-fuel-additives-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1997, c. 11
:::

Assented to 1997-04-25

An Act to regulate interprovincial trade in and the importation for
commercial purposes of certain manganese-based substances
::::

:::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348255 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Manganese-based Fuel Additives Act](/eng/acts/M-0.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348258 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[controlled substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[controlled substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a manganese-based
    substance that is mentioned in the schedule and includes any other
    substance that contains such a manganese-based
    substance; ([substance à usage contrôlé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada as may be designated by order of the
    Governor in Council as the Minister for the purposes of this
    Act; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unleaded gasoline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unleaded gasoline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means gasoline to which
    lead has not been added during the production process. ([essence
    sans plomb]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348264 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Interprovincial Trade and Importation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348267 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall engage in
interprovincial trade in or import for a commercial purpose a controlled
substance except under an authorization referred to in section 5.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization by Minister

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may authorize any person to engage in interprovincial trade
  in or to import for a commercial purpose a controlled substance if the
  Minister is satisfied

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the controlled substance is not unleaded
    gasoline; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the controlled substance will not be added to
    unleaded gasoline.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may attach to the authorization any
  condition respecting the controlled substance, the use that may be
  made of it, the term of the authorization and its renewal and any
  other condition that the Minister considers appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may require
the applicant for the authorization to post a bond or provide other
security, in a form and for an amount fixed by the Minister, to ensure

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the controlled substance is not unleaded
  gasoline and that the controlled substance will not be added to
  unleaded gasoline; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the applicant will comply with the conditions of
  the authorization.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may revoke the
authorization

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the application or with the consent of the holder
  of the authorization, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the Minister

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is no longer satisfied that the controlled
    substance is not unleaded gasoline,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is no longer satisfied that the controlled
    substance will not be added to unleaded gasoline, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is satisfied that the holder has not complied
    with the conditions of the authorization,

  and, where the Minister revokes the authorization under paragraph (b),
  the Minister may realise on the security provided by the holder.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations for carrying out the purposes and provisions of
sections 5 to 7, including regulations respecting applications for
authorizations referred to in section 5 and their consideration by the
Minister.

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348291 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interprovincial trade and importation

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who, under an authorization referred to in section 5, engages
  in interprovincial trade in or imports a controlled substance shall
  maintain a record respecting the controlled substance in accordance
  with this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A record that is required to be maintained by
  subsection (1) must contain the following information for each
  transaction under the authorization, which shall be entered within
  thirty days after the transaction and signed by the person who
  maintains the record:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the controlled substance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the place where and the person from whom it was
    acquired;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its quantity;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the use that is made or is to be made of it;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the place where and the person to whom it is
    disposed of; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the day on which it crossed the boundary of a
    province or was released in accordance with the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who is required by this section to maintain
  a record containing information for a transaction shall maintain the
  record containing that information in Canada for a period of five
  years after the end of the calendar year in which the information was
  entered in the record or, in the case of a corporation, after the end
  of the financial year of the corporation in which the information was
  entered in the record.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A person who is required
to maintain a record by section 9 shall, within sixty days of each
transaction under the authorization, file a report with the Minister, in
the form prescribed by the Minister, on the information contained in the
record.

## [Powers of the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348307 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice requiring information and samples

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of deciding whether to recommend to the Governor in
  Council that the name of a manganese-based substance be added to or
  deleted from the schedule, the Minister may publish in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) and in any other manner that the
  Minister considers appropriate a notice requiring any person described
  in the notice to provide the Minister with such information and
  samples referred to in subsection (2) as may be in the possession of
  that person or to which that person may reasonably be expected to have
  access.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice may require any information and samples that
  may assist the Minister to decide whether to recommend to the Governor
  in Council that the name of a manganese-based substance be added to or
  deleted from the schedule, including samples of the substance and
  information on the quantities, uses and composition of the substance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person to whom a notice is directed shall
  comply with the notice within such time or times as are specified in
  the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of time

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (3), the Minister may, on
  request in writing from any person to whom a notice is directed,
  extend the time or times within which the person shall comply with the
  notice.

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348317 .Part}

### [Inspectors and Analysts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-348318 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate as an inspector or analyst for the purposes of
  this Act any person who, in the opinion of the Minister, is qualified
  to be so designated, either personally or by reason of that person
  occupying a certain position.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspector to show certificate of
  designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An inspector shall be furnished with a certificate of
  designation and, on entering any place under this Act, shall produce
  the certificate on request of the person in charge of the place.

### [Provisions of the Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999]{.HTitleText2} {#h-348324 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of provisions of the
[Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Paragraphs 218(1)(a) and
(j), subsections 218(2) to (6) and (10) to (14) and sections 219, 220,
223, 224, 226 to 230 and 272 of the [Canadian Environmental Protection
Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) apply, with any modifications that the
circumstances require, in respect of this Act.

- 1999, c. 33, s. 348

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348329 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interprovincial trade and importation

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes section 4 is guilty of an offence and liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding
  \$300,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or
  to both; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine not exceeding
  \$1,000,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three years,
  or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False or misleading information

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
knowingly enters false or misleading information in a record required to
be maintained by this Act or provides the Minister with false or
misleading information, samples or results is guilty of an offence and
liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding
  \$300,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or
  to both; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine not exceeding
  \$1,000,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years, or
  to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other offences

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes any provision of this Act, other than section 4 or 15 or a
provision referred to in section 13, is guilty of an offence punishable
on summary conviction and liable to a fine not exceeding \$200,000 or to
imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of offence

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In any
  prosecution of an offence under this Act it is sufficient proof of the
  offence to establish that it was committed by an employee or agent of
  the accused, whether or not the employee or agent is identified or
  prosecuted for the offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall, by virtue of subsection (1), be
  found guilty of an offence under section 15 if the person establishes
  that the offence was committed without the person's knowledge or
  consent and that he or she exercised all due diligence to prevent its
  commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall be found guilty of an offence under
  this Act, other than an offence under section 15, if the person
  establishes that he or she exercised all due diligence to prevent its
  commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders of court

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an offender has been convicted of an offence under this Act, in
  addition to any other punishment that may be imposed under this Act,
  the court may, having regard to the nature of the offence and the
  circumstances surrounding its commission, make an order having any or
  all of the following effects:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prohibiting the offender from doing any act or
    engaging in any activity that may result in the continuation or
    repetition of the offence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} directing the offender to take such action as the
    court considers appropriate to remedy or avoid any harm that results
    or may result from the commission of the offence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} directing the offender to publish, in any manner
    that the court considers appropriate, the facts relating to the
    commission of the offence;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} directing the offender to notify, at the offender's
    own cost and in any manner that the court considers appropriate, any
    person aggrieved or affected by the offender's conduct of the facts
    relating to the commission of the offence;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} directing the offender to post such bond or pay
    such amount of money into court as will ensure compliance with any
    order made pursuant to this section;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} directing the offender to submit to the Minister,
    on application by the Minister made within three years after the
    date of conviction, such information with respect to the activities
    of the offender as the court considers appropriate and just in the
    circumstances;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} directing the offender to compensate the Minister,
    in whole or in part, for the cost of any remedial or preventative
    action taken by or caused to be taken on behalf of the Minister as a
    result of the commission of the offence; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} requiring the offender to comply with such other
    reasonable conditions as the court considers appropriate and just in
    the circumstances for securing the offender's good conduct and for
    preventing the offender from repeating the same offence or
    committing other offences.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an offender does not comply with an order made
  under paragraph (1)(c) directing the publication of the facts relating
  to the commission of the offence, the Minister may publish the facts
  in compliance with the order and recover the costs of publication from
  the offender.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt due to Her Majesty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the court makes an order under paragraph (1)(g)
  directing an offender to pay costs or the Minister incurs publication
  costs under subsection (2), the costs constitute a debt due to Her
  Majesty in right of Canada and may be recovered as such in any court
  of competent jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force and duration of order

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An order made under subsection (1) comes into force
  on the day on which it is made or on such other day as the court may
  determine but may not continue in force for more than three years
  after that day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of provisions of the
[Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Sections 39 to 42, 275,
276, 280, 285, 288 to 290, 292 to 294 and 311 of the [Canadian
Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) apply in respect
of this Act

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} as if each reference in any of those sections to
  section 291 of that Act were a reference to section 18 of this Act;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} with any other modifications that the circumstances
  require.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1997, c. 11, s. 19
- 1999, c. 33, s. 349
:::

## [Amendments to the Schedule]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348370 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders of the Governor in Council

may, by order, amend the schedule by adding or deleting the name of a
manganese-based substance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** This Act comes into force
