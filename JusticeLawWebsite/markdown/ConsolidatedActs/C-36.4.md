### [Tribunal Established]{.HTitleText2} {#h-90349 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tribunal

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a tribunal to be known as the Competition Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Constitution of Tribunal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal shall consist of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} not more than six members to be appointed from
    among the judges of the Federal Court by the Governor in Council on
    the recommendation of the Minister of Justice; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} not more than eight other members to be appointed
    by the Governor in Council on the recommendation of the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advisory council

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may establish an advisory
  council to advise the Minister with respect to appointments of lay
  members, which council is to be composed of not more than ten members
  who are knowledgeable in economics, industry, commerce or public
  affairs and may include, without restricting the generality of the
  foregoing, individuals chosen from business communities, the legal
  community, consumer groups and labour.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall consult with any advisory council
  established under subsection (3) before making a recommendation with
  respect to the appointment of a lay member.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 19 (2nd Supp.), s. 3
- 2002, c. 8, ss. 183, 198, c. 16, s. 16

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairman

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall designate one of the judicial members to be
  Chairman of the Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role of Chairman

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairman has supervision over and direction of
  the work of the Tribunal including, without restricting the generality
  of the foregoing, the allocation of the work of the members thereof.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the office of Chairman is vacant, or the
  Chairman is absent from Canada or is for any reason unable to act, the
  powers of the Chairman shall be exercised and the duties performed by
  the senior judicial member who is in Canada and is able and willing to
  act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure of judicial members

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  judicial member shall be appointed for a term not exceeding seven
  years and holds office so long as he remains a judge of the Federal
  Court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure of lay members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each lay member shall be appointed for a term not
  exceeding seven years and holds office during good behaviour but may
  be removed by the Governor in Council for cause.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A member of the Tribunal, on the expiration of a
  first or any subsequent term of office, is eligible to be re-appointed
  for a further term.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting after expiration of appointment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person may continue to act as a member of the
  Tribunal after the expiration of his term of appointment in respect of
  any matter in which he became engaged during the term of his
  appointment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary substitute members

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of a member
  of the Tribunal, the Governor in Council may appoint a temporary
  substitute member for such period of time as the Governor in Council
  prescribes.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration of lay members

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each lay
  member shall be paid such remuneration as may be fixed by the Governor
  in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses of members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each member of the Tribunal is entitled to be paid
  the travel allowances that a judge is entitled to be paid under the
  [Judges Act](/eng/acts/J-1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other benefits

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each lay member is deemed to be employed in the
  federal public administration for the purposes of the [Government
  Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and any regulations made
  under section 9 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 19 (2nd Supp.), s. 6
- 2003, c. 22, s. 224(E)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oath of office

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  member of the Tribunal shall, before entering on the duties of his
  office, take an oath that he will duly and faithfully, and to the best
  of his skill and knowledge, execute the powers and trusts reposed in
  him as a member of the Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How administered

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The oath referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  administered to the Chairman of the Tribunal before the Chief Justice
  of the Federal Court, and to the other members by the Chairman, or in
  his absence or incapacity, by any other member.

### [Jurisdiction and Powers of the Tribunal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-90395 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal has jurisdiction to hear and dispose of all applications made
  under Part VII.1 or VIII of the [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34) and
  any related matters, as well as any matter under Part IX of that Act
  that is the subject of a reference under subsection 124.2(2) of that
  Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal has, with respect to the attendance,
  swearing and examination of witnesses, the production and inspection
  of documents, the enforcement of its orders and other matters
  necessary or proper for the due exercise of its jurisdiction, all such
  powers, rights and privileges as are vested in a superior court of
  record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to penalize

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall be punished for contempt of the
  Tribunal unless a judicial member is of the opinion that the finding
  of contempt and the punishment are appropriate in the circumstances.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 19 (2nd Supp.), s. 8
- 1999, c. 2, s. 41
- 2002, c. 16, s. 16.1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

- **[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may award costs of proceedings before it in respect of
  reviewable matters under Parts VII.1 and VIII of the [Competition
  Act](/eng/acts/C-34) on a final or interim basis, in accordance with
  the provisions governing costs in the Federal Court Rules, 1998.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may direct by whom and to whom any costs
  are to be paid and by whom they are to be taxed and allowed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No award against the Crown

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite any other Act of Parliament, the Tribunal
  shall not award costs against His Majesty in right of Canada unless it
  is satisfied

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that an award is necessary to maintain confidence
    in the administration of justice; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the absence of an award would have a
    substantial adverse effect on the other party's ability to carry on
    business.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs adjudged to Her Majesty in right
  of Canada

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Costs adjudged to Her Majesty in right of Canada
  shall not be disallowed or reduced on taxation by reason only that
  counsel who earned the costs, or in respect of whose services the
  costs are charged, was a salaried officer of Her Majesty in right of
  Canada performing those services in the discharge of that counsel's
  duty and remunerated for those services by salary, or for that or any
  other reason was not entitled to recover any costs from Her Majesty in
  right of Canada in respect of the services so rendered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts to Receiver General

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any money or costs awarded to Her Majesty in right of
  Canada in a proceeding in respect of which this section applies shall
  be paid to the Receiver General.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court of record

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal is a court of record and shall have an official seal which
  shall be judicially noticed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All proceedings before the Tribunal shall be dealt
  with as informally and expeditiously as the circumstances and
  considerations of fairness permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interventions by persons affected

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any person may, with leave of the Tribunal, intervene
  in any proceedings before the Tribunal, other than proceedings under
  Part VII.1 of the [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34), to make
  representations relevant to those proceedings in respect of any matter
  that affects that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summary dispositions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} On a motion from a party to an application made under
  Part VII.1 or VIII of the [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34), a
  judicial member may hear and determine the application in a summary
  way, in accordance with any rules on summary dispositions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The judicial member may dismiss the application in
  whole or in part if the member finds that there is no genuine basis
  for it. The member may allow the application in whole or in part if
  satisfied that there is no genuine basis for the response to it.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 19 (2nd Supp.), s. 9

### [Organization of Work]{.HTitleText2} {#h-90431 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sittings of Tribunal

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 11, every
  application to the Tribunal shall be heard before not less than three
  or more than five members sitting together, at least one of whom is a
  judicial member and at least one of whom is a lay member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial member to preside at hearings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairman shall designate a judicial member to
  preside at any hearing or, if the Chairman is present at a hearing,
  may preside himself.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition where financial interest

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No member shall take part in any matter before the
  Tribunal in which the member has a direct or indirect financial
  interest.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing of applications

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairman of the Tribunal, sitting alone, or a judicial member
  designated by the Chairman, sitting alone, may hear and dispose of
  applications under subsection 100(1), section 103.1 or 103.3 or
  subsection 104(1) or 123.1(1) of the [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34)
  and any related matters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrative remedies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Applications for orders under Part VII.1 of the
  [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34) and any related matters shall be
  heard and disposed of by the Chairman of the Tribunal, sitting alone,
  or by a judicial member designated by the Chairman, sitting alone.

- 1999, c. 2, s. 43

### [Proceedings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-90446 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Questions of law, fact, mixed law and
fact

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In any
  proceedings before the Tribunal,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} questions of law shall be determined only by the
    judicial members sitting in those proceedings; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} questions of fact or mixed law and fact shall be
    determined by all the members sitting in those proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where there are differing opinions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In any proceedings before the Tribunal,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the event of a difference of opinion among the
    members determining any question, the opinion of the majority shall
    prevail; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the event of an equally divided opinion among
    the members determining any question, the presiding member may
    determine the question.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where member unable to take part in
  judgment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a member of the Tribunal is unable to take part
  in the giving of judgment in any proceedings or has died, the other
  members sitting in those proceedings may, whether or not they include
  a judicial member or a lay member, give judgment and, for that
  purpose, shall be deemed to constitute the Tribunal.

### [Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-90458 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), an appeal
  lies to the Federal Court of Appeal from any decision or order,
  whether final, interlocutory or interim, of the Tribunal as if it were
  a judgment of the Federal Court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Questions of fact

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An appeal on a question of fact lies under
  subsection (1) only with the leave of the Federal Court of Appeal.

- R.S., 1985, c. 19 (2nd Supp.), s. 13

### [Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#h-90470 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the approval of the
  Governor in Council, the Tribunal may make general rules that are not
  inconsistent with this Part or the [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for regulating the practice and procedure of the
    Tribunal; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for carrying out the work of the Tribunal and the
    management of its internal affairs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When effective

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No rule made under this section has effect until it
  has been published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of rules

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A copy of every rule made under this section shall be
  laid before Parliament on any of the first fifteen days after the
  making thereof that either House of Parliament is sitting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Five members of the Tribunal, at least three of whom
  are judicial members, constitute a quorum of the Tribunal for the
  purpose of making rules under this section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advance publication of rules and
amendments

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Where the Tribunal
proposes to make any rule under section 16, it

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall give notice of the proposal by publishing it in
  the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) and shall, in the
  notice, invite any interested person to make representations to it in
  writing with respect thereto within sixty days after the day of the
  publication; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} may, after the expiration of the sixty days referred
  to in paragraph (a) and subject to the approval of the Governor in
  Council, implement the proposal either as originally published or as
  revised in such manner as the Tribunal deems advisable having regard
  to any representations so made to it.

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-90509 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References to s. 114 of [Canada
Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8)

- **[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Wherever in any Act of Parliament a
  reference is made to section 114 of the [Canada Corporations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8), chapter C-32 of the Revised Statutes of Canada,
  1970, in respect of a company or corporation, sections 229 to 234, 236
  to 240 and 242 of the [Canada Business Corporations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-44) apply, with such modifications as the

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In construing the sections of the [Canada Business
  Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44) made applicable to a company or
  corporation under this section, in the case of a company, or
  corporation, incorporated without share capital, [[security
  holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, or [[registered holder or beneficial
  owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in relation to a security, means a member
  of the company or corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Directors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A Director or Deputy Director appointed under section
  260 of the [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44) may, for
  the purpose of giving effect to this section with respect to the
  application of sections 229 to 234, 236 to 240 and 242 of that Act,
  exercise the powers and perform the functions and duties of the
  Director under those sections.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2024, c. 15, s. 269

  - ###### Subsection 8.1(3) of the [Competition Tribunal Act](/eng/acts/C-36.4) {#subsection-8.13-of-the-competition-tribunal-act .MarginalNote}

    [269]{.lawlabel} Subsection 8.1(3) of the [Competition Tribunal
    Act](/eng/acts/C-36.4), as that subsection read before the day on
    which section 266 comes into force, continues to apply after that
    day in respect of any proceeding referred to in subsection 8.1(1) of
    that Act that commenced before that day.
