## First Nations Elections Act {#first-nations-elections-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2014, c. 5
:::

Assented to 2014-04-11

An Act respecting the election and term of office of chiefs and
councillors of certain First Nations and the composition of council of
those First Nations
::::

::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224423 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited
as the [First Nations Elections Act](/eng/acts/F-11.65).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224426 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in this Act.

[[council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by the
    definition [[council of the band]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Indian
    Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[deputy electoral officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deputy electoral officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person so
    appointed in accordance with the regulations. ([président d'élection
    adjoint]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[election]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[election]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the election of the chief and
    councillors of a participating First Nation, including by way of a
    by-election. ([élection]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[elector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[elector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is registered on
    a Band List, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Indian
    Act](/eng/acts/I-5), and

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to an election, is 18 years of age or
      older on the day of the election;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a petition to remove from office a
      chief or councillor, was 18 years of age or older on the day of
      the election of that chief or councillor;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a nomination referred to in
      section 9, is 18 years of age or older on the day of the
      nomination; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a vote on a proposed community
      election code referred to in paragraph 42(1)(b), is 18 years of
      age or older on the day of the vote. ([électeur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[electoral officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[electoral officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person so
    appointed in accordance with the regulations. ([président
    d'élection]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by the
    definition [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) of the
    [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([première nation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[mail-in ballot]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mail-in ballot]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a ballot that is sent
    to an elector by mail or otherwise provided to an elector other than
    at a polling station. ([bulletin de vote postal]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a participating First
    Nation, means a person whose name appears, or who is entitled to
    have their name appear, on the Band List maintained for that First
    Nation under section 8 of the [Indian
    Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([membre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Indigenous
    Services. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nomination meeting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nomination meeting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a meeting held for
    the purpose of nominating candidates for an election. ([assemblée de
    mise en candidature]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[participating First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[participating First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a First
    Nation that is named in the schedule. ([première nation
    participante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Indian
    Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([réserve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Adding to the Schedule]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224445 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by order, add the name of a First Nation to the schedule
  if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that First Nation's council has provided to the
    Minister a resolution requesting that the First Nation be added to
    the schedule;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is satisfied that a protracted
    leadership dispute has significantly compromised governance of that
    First Nation; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Governor in Council has set aside an election
    of the Chief and councillors of that First Nation under section 79
    of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) on a report of the Minister that
    there was corrupt practice in connection with that election.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of the order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The order must specify the date of the first election
  for the First Nation in respect of which the order is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of order on term of office

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The chief and councillors of a First Nation in
  respect of which an order is made who hold office on the day on which
  the order is made continue to hold office until the day of the first
  election and cease to hold office on that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 7(1) does not apply to the council that is
  in office on the day on which the order is made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of name

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-4 .sectionLabel}** In the event of a
change to the name of a participating First Nation, the Minister may, by
order, amend the schedule to reflect the change.

## [Election Dates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224460 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First election

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-5 .sectionLabel}** The date of a first
election must not be later than,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a First Nation whose name is added to
  the schedule under paragraph 3(1)(a),

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the day on which, but for the making of the
    Minister's order, the term of office of its chief and councillors
    would have expired, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if that First Nation's council has requested in
    their resolution the establishment of a common election date with
    five or more other First Nations whose councils have made a similar
    request, one year after the earliest day on which, but for the
    order, the term of office of the chief and councillors of any one of
    those First Nations would have expired; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a First Nation whose name is added to
  the schedule under paragraph 3(1)(b) or (c), six months after the day
  on which the order is made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent elections

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subsequent elections,
other than by-elections, must be held within the period of 30 days
before the day on which the term of office of the incumbent chief and
councillors expires.

## [Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224469 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  council of a participating First Nation is to consist of one chief
  and, for every 100 members of that First Nation, one councillor, but
  the number of councillors is not to be less than two or more than 12.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reduction --- number of councillors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the council may, by
  resolution, reduce the number of councillor positions but to not less
  than two. The reduction is applicable as of the next election that is
  not a by-election.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-8 .sectionLabel}** The resolution referred
to in subsection 7(2) is not subject to the [Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

## [Candidates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224477 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Only
  an elector of a participating First Nation is eligible to be nominated
  as a candidate for the position of chief or councillor of that First
  Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An elector is not to be nominated as a candidate for
  the position of chief and the position of councillor in the same
  election.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nomination

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An elector becomes a candidate only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their nomination is moved and seconded, in the
    manner prescribed by regulation, by other electors of the First
    Nation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they provide their consent to be a candidate; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fee imposed on them under section 11, if any,
    is remitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An elector must not nominate more than one candidate
  for each position to be filled.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-10 .sectionLabel}** A person must not, in
connection with an election, consent to be a candidate knowing that they
are not eligible to be a candidate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Candidacy fee

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-11 .sectionLabel}** Participating First
Nations may, if authorized to do so by regulation, impose a fee of up to
\$250 on each candidate in an election, to be refunded if the candidate
receives more than five per cent of the total votes cast.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-12 .sectionLabel}** A person must not, in
connection with an election,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by intimidation or duress, attempt to influence
  another person to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} nominate or refrain from nominating a particular
    candidate,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} accept or decline a nomination, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} withdraw as a candidate;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} act, or incite another person to act, in a disorderly
  manner, with the intention of disrupting the conduct of a nomination
  meeting; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} knowingly publish a false statement that a candidate
  is withdrawing or has withdrawn their candidacy.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order to leave

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  electoral officer or deputy electoral officer may order a person to
  leave a nomination meeting if the person is committing an offence
  under this Act that threatens the maintenance of order at the meeting,
  or if the officer believes on reasonable grounds that the person has
  done so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person to whom an order is given under
  subsection (1) must obey it without delay.

## [Ballots]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224507 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-14 .sectionLabel}** A person must not, in
connection with an election,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide a false name in order to obtain a ballot;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} possess a ballot that was not provided to them in
  accordance with the regulations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} purchase a mail-in ballot that was issued to another
  person;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} sell or give away a mail-in ballot; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} print or reproduce a ballot with the intention that
  the print or reproduction be used as a genuine ballot, unless that
  person is authorized to do so under the regulations.

## [Voting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224515 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elector entitled to vote

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), only an
  elector of a participating First Nation is entitled to vote in an
  election held by that First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An elector who is appointed as the electoral officer
  in respect of an election is not entitled to vote in that election.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- any person

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-16 .sectionLabel}** A person must not, in
connection with an election,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} vote or attempt to vote knowing that they are not
  entitled to vote;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} attempt to influence another person to vote knowing
  that the other person is not entitled to do so;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} knowingly use a forged ballot;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} put a ballot into a ballot box knowing that they are
  not authorized to do so under the regulations;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} by intimidation or duress, attempt to influence
  another person to vote or refrain from voting or to vote or refrain
  from voting for a particular candidate; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} offer money, goods, employment or other valuable
  consideration in an attempt to influence an elector to vote or refrain
  from voting or to vote or refrain from voting for a particular
  candidate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- elector

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-17 .sectionLabel}** An elector must not,
in connection with an election,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} intentionally vote more than once in respect of any
  given position of chief or councillor; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} accept or agree to accept money, goods, employment or
  other valuable consideration to vote or refrain from voting or to vote
  or refrain from voting for a particular candidate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Secrecy of voting

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-18 .sectionLabel}** Voting at an election
is to be conducted by secret ballot.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- elector

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-19 .sectionLabel}** An elector must not,
in connection with an election,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} show their ballot, when marked, to reveal the name of
  the candidate for whom the elector has voted, other than in accordance
  with the regulations; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the polling station, openly declare for whom the
  elector intends to vote or has voted.

## [Polling Stations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224539 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-20 .sectionLabel}** A person must not, in
connection with an election,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} post or display in, or on the exterior surface of, a
  polling station any campaign literature or other material that
  promotes or opposes the election of a particular candidate;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} within hearing distance of a polling station, orally
  promote or oppose the election of a candidate;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in a polling station, attempt to influence an elector
  to vote or refrain from voting or to vote or refrain from voting for a
  particular candidate; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} act, or incite another person to act, in a disorderly
  manner with the intention of disrupting the conduct of the vote in a
  polling station.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order to leave

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  electoral officer or deputy elector officer may order a person to
  leave a polling station if the person is committing an offence under
  this Act that threatens the maintenance of order at the polling
  station, or if the officer believes on reasonable grounds that a
  person has done so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person to whom an order is given under
  subsection (1) must obey it without delay.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-22 .sectionLabel}** A person must not, in
connection with an election, destroy, take, open or otherwise interfere
with a ballot box knowing that they are not authorized to do so under
the regulations.

## [Awarding of Positions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224553 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chief and councillor positions

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-23 .sectionLabel}** The chief and
councillor positions of a participating First Nation are awarded to the
candidates for those positions who receive the highest number of votes.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tied vote

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-24 .sectionLabel}** If it is not possible
to award a position under section 23 because there are two or more
candidates with the same number of votes, the electoral officer must
conduct a draw to break the tie.

## [By-Elections]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224558 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-elections

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-25 .sectionLabel}** If the chief or a
councillor of a participating First Nation ceases to hold office more
than three months before the day on which their term of office would
have expired under subsection 28(1) or section 29, the council of that
First Nation may direct that a by-election be held for that position, in
accordance with the regulations.

## [Obstruction of Elections]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224561 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-26 .sectionLabel}** A person must not
intentionally obstruct an electoral officer or deputy electoral officer
in the performance of their duties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-27 .sectionLabel}** A person must not, in
a manner that this Act does not otherwise prohibit, intentionally
obstruct the conduct of an election.

## [Term of Office]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224566 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-28
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2) and
  section 29, the chief and councillors of a participating First Nation
  hold office for four years commencing at the expiry of the term of
  office of the chief and councillors that they replace.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office ceases

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A chief or councillor of a participating First Nation
  ceases to hold office if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are convicted of an indictable offence and
    sentenced to a term of imprisonment of more than 30 consecutive
    days;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they are convicted of an offence under this Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} they die or resign from office;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a court sets aside their election under subsection
    35(1); or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} they are removed from office by means of a petition
    in accordance with the regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office after by-election

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-29 .sectionLabel}** A chief or councillor
who is elected in a by-election held under section 25 holds office
commencing on the date of their election for the remainder of the term
of office that they were elected to fill.

## [Contested Elections]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224579 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of contestation

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-30 .sectionLabel}** The validity of the
election of the chief or a councillor of a participating First Nation
may be contested only in accordance with sections 31 to 35.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contestation of election

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-31 .sectionLabel}** An elector of a
participating First Nation may, by application to a competent court,
contest the election of the chief or a councillor of that First Nation
on the ground that a contravention of a provision of this Act or the
regulations is likely to have affected the result.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-32 .sectionLabel}** An application must be
filed within 30 days after the day on which the results of the contested
election were announced.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Competent courts

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-33 .sectionLabel}** The following courts
are competent courts for the purpose of section 31:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Federal Court; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the superior court of a province in which one or more
  of the participating First Nation's reserves are located.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of application

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-34 .sectionLabel}** An application must be
served by the applicant on the electoral officer and all the candidates
who participated in the contested election.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court may set aside election

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-35
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After hearing the application, the
  court may, if the ground referred to in section 31 is established, set
  aside the contested election.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of court clerk

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the court sets aside an election, the clerk of the
  court must send a copy of the decision to the Minister.

## [Petition for Removal from Office]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224597 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-36 .sectionLabel}** A person must not

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide money, goods, employment or other valuable
  consideration to another person for the purpose of obtaining their
  signature on a petition for the removal from office of a chief or
  councillor of a participating First Nation; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} accept money, goods, employment or other valuable
  consideration in exchange for their signature on such a petition.

## [Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224602 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-37
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every person is guilty of an offence
  who contravenes paragraph 16(a) or (b) or 17(a), section 22 or
  paragraph 36(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences to which an additional penalty
  applies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person is guilty of an offence who contravenes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} section 10; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 12, paragraph 14(a), (c) or (e), 16(c), (e)
    or (f) or 20(d) or section 26 or 27.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences --- subsections 13(2) and
  21(2)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who intentionally contravenes subsection
  13(2) or 21(2) is guilty of an offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-38
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every person is guilty of an offence
  who contravenes paragraph 16(d), 17(b), 19(b) or 36(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Strict liability offence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An electoral officer who fails to perform their duty
  under section 24 or an electoral officer or deputy electoral officer
  who fails to perform any of their duties under the regulations is
  guilty of an offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Due diligence defence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person is not to be found guilty of an offence
  under subsection (2) if they establish that they exercised due
  diligence to prevent the commission of the offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences --- paragraphs 14(b) and (d),
  19(a) and 20(a) to (c)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every person who intentionally contravenes any of
  paragraphs 14(b) and (d), 19(a) and 20(a) to (c) is guilty of an
  offence.

## [Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224621 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dual procedure

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-39
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is guilty of an
  offence under section 37 is liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine of not more
    than \$5,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than five
    years, or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine of not more than
    \$2,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than six months,
    or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summary conviction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is guilty of an offence under
  section 38 is liable on summary conviction to a fine of not more than
  \$1,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than three months,
  or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional penalty

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-40 .sectionLabel}** Any person who is
convicted of an offence under paragraph 37(2)(a), or any candidate who
is convicted of an offence under paragraph 37(2)(b), in addition to any
other punishment for that offence prescribed by this Act, is not
eligible to be elected as chief or councillor of a participating First
Nation during the five years after the date of conviction.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224631 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-41 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in
Council may make regulations with respect to elections, including
regulations respecting

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the appointment, powers, duties and removal of
  electoral officers and deputy electoral officers;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the requirement that electoral officers be certified,
  the certification process and the grounds for withdrawing
  certification;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manner of identifying electors of a participating
  First Nation;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which candidates may be nominated;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the imposition, by participating First Nations, of a
  fee on each candidate in accordance with section 11;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which voting is to be carried out,
  including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} permitting the electoral officer to establish
    polling stations and advance polling stations,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} procedures for obtaining and using mail-in
    ballots, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the counting of votes;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the removal from office of a chief or councillor of a
  participating First Nation by means of a petition, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the percentage of electors of that First Nation who
    must sign that petition, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the period during which that petition is to be
    filed;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the holding of by-elections; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} anything else that by this Act is to be prescribed.

## [Removal from Schedule]{.HTitleText1} {#h-224648 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removing a participating First Nation
from the schedule

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  a participating First Nation's council has provided to the Minister a
  proposed community election code and a resolution requesting that the
  name of that First Nation be removed from the schedule, the Minister
  may, by order, remove the name from the schedule if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the code establishes a procedure for its amendment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the code and the request were approved by a
    majority of the votes cast in a secret vote in which a majority of
    the electors of that First Nation participated;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the code has been published by that First Nation on
    a website maintained by or for it or in the First Nations Gazette;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} there are no outstanding charges under this Act
    against any member of that First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date of community election
  code

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The community election code comes into force on the
  day on which the Minister's order is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Amendments to the community election code come into
  force on the day on which they are published by the First Nation on a
  website maintained by or for it or in the *First Nations Gazette*.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The community election code is not subject to the
  [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

- Meaning of [[community election code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[community election
  code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a written code that sets out rules
  regarding the election of the chief and councillors of a First Nation.
