## Merchant Navy Veterans Day Act {#merchant-navy-veterans-day-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2003, c. 17
:::

Assented to 2003-06-19

An Act to establish Merchant Navy Veterans Day
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the veterans of the Merchant Navy have served honourably and
courageously in the defence of Canada throughout the years;

AND WHEREAS the House of Commons wishes to set aside a day to
acknowledge the contribution of the veterans of the Merchant Navy to
ensuring the freedom and democracy enjoyed by Canadians;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-355690 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Merchant Navy Veterans Day Act](/eng/acts/M-5.8).

## [Merchant Navy Veterans Day]{.HTitleText1} {#h-355693 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Merchant Navy Veterans Day

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Throughout Canada, in each
and every year, the third day of September shall be known as "Merchant
Navy Veterans Day".
