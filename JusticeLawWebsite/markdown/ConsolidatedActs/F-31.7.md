## Fruit and Vegetable Customs Orders Validation Act {#fruit-and-vegetable-customs-orders-validation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1988, c. 5
:::

Assented to 1988-03-22

An Act to validate certain customs duty orders relating to fresh fruits
and vegetables
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Fruit and Vegetable Customs Orders Validation
Act](/eng/acts/F-31.7).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validation of orders

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding any other
Act, orders made in respect of imported fresh fruits and vegetables
pursuant to paragraph 15(1)(a) of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) between January 1, 1972 and January 10, 1985
are declared to have been validly made and any customs duties collected
on such fruits and vegetables pursuant to those orders are declared to
have been validly collected.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission of duties

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Customs duties imposed
between June 28, 1984 and January 10, 1985 on imported fresh fruits and
vegetables by orders made pursuant to paragraph 15(1)(a) of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and not collected are hereby remitted.
