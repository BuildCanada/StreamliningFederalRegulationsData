## Canadian Centre for Occupational Health and Safety Act {#canadian-centre-for-occupational-health-and-safety-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. C-13
:::

An Act to establish the Canadian Centre for Occupational Health and
Safety
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61676 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canadian Centre for Occupational Health and Safety
Act](/eng/acts/C-13).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 29, s. 1
:::

## [Purpose of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61681 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Promoting right to healthy and safe
working environment by creating Centre

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to promote the fundamental right of Canadians to a healthy and safe
working environment by creating a national institute concerned with the
study, encouragement and cooperative advancement of occupational health
and safety, in whose governing body the interests and concerns of
workers, trade unions, employers, federal, provincial and territorial
authorities, professional and scientific communities and the general
public will be represented.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 29, s. 2
- 1984, c. 40, s. 79(F)
:::

## [Constitution of the Centre]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61698 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment and government of Centre

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby established
a corporation, to be called the Canadian Centre for Occupational Health
and Safety, governed by a Council composed of the following persons, to
be appointed by the Governor in Council as provided in sections 7 and 8:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Chairman;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} thirteen other governors, one to be nominated by the
  lieutenant governor in council of each of the ten provinces, one to be
  nominated by the Commissioner of Yukon, one to be nominated by the
  Commissioner of the Northwest Territories and one to be nominated by
  the Commissioner of Nunavut;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} four other governors to be appointed after
  consultation with any organizations representative of workers that the
  Governor in Council considers appropriate; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} four other governors to be appointed after
  consultation with any organizations representative of employers that
  the Governor in Council considers appropriate.

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2010, c. 12, s. 1684\]]{.Repealed}

- R.S., 1985, c. C-13, s. 4

## [Objects, Activities and Powers of the Centre]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61708 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects of Centre

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The objects of the Centre
are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to promote health and safety in the workplace in
  Canada and the physical and mental health of working people in Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to facilitate

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} consultation and cooperation among federal,
    provincial and territorial jurisdictions, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} participation by labour and management

  in the establishment and maintenance of high standards of occupational
  health and safety appropriate to the Canadian situation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to assist in the development and maintenance of
  policies and programs aimed at the reduction or elimination of
  occupational hazards; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to serve as a national centre for statistics and
  other information relating to occupational health and safety.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 29, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activities and powers of Centre

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Centre may, in furtherance of its objects,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} promote, assist, initiate and evaluate research;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish and operate systems and facilities for
    collecting, recording, processing, analysing, evaluating and
    disseminating statistics and other information;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} publish and otherwise disseminate scientific,
    technological and other information;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide advice, information and service relating to
    existing or anticipated occupational health and safety problems to
    workers, trade unions, employers and government, to national,
    provincial and international organizations and to the public;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} support and facilitate the training of personnel in
    and for the field of occupational health and safety;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} sponsor and support public meetings, conferences
    and seminars;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} expend, for the purposes of this Act, any money
    appropriated by Parliament for the work of the Centre or received by
    the Centre through the conduct of its operations;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} give recognition to public or private organizations
    or individuals for outstanding contributions in the field of
    occupational health and safety; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} do such other things as are conducive to the
    carrying out of its objects.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Centre to consider briefs and other
  representations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Centre shall consider briefs and other written
  representations submitted to it on matters relating to occupational
  health and safety and may arrange public forums relating thereto.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Donations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Centre may acquire money, securities or other
  property by gift, bequest or otherwise and, notwithstanding anything
  in this Act, may expend, administer or dispose of the money,
  securities or other property subject to the terms, if any, on which
  the money, securities or other property is given, bequeathed or
  otherwise made available to the Centre.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ancillary powers

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of carrying out its activities under
  subsections (1) to (3), the Centre has the capacity, rights, powers
  and privileges of a natural person.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 29, s. 5
- 1984, c. 31, s. 14
:::

## [Members of the Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61740 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairman

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairman shall be appointed to hold office during good behaviour for a
  term not exceeding five years, but may be removed by the Governor in
  Council for cause.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of Chairman

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairman shall preside at meetings of the Council
  and may perform such other duties as are assigned to the Chairman by
  the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting Chairman

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Council may authorize one of the governors to act
  as Chairman if the Chairman is absent or incapacitated or the office
  of Chairman is vacant, but no person so authorized may act as Chairman
  for a period exceeding ninety days without the approval of the
  Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility of Chairman for
  re-appointment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person who has served two consecutive terms as
  Chairman is eligible to be re-appointed to the Council but is not,
  during the twelve months following the completion of the second term
  as Chairman, eligible to be re-appointed Chairman.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 29, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other governors

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each of
  the governors other than the Chairman shall be appointed to hold
  office during pleasure for such term not exceeding four years as will
  ensure as far as possible the expiration in any one year of the terms
  of appointment of fewer than half of the governors so appointed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary substitute governor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of a
  governor other than the Chairman, the Council may, on such terms and
  conditions as it may prescribe and after such consultation as is
  appropriate in the circumstances, appoint a temporary substitute
  governor, but no temporary substitute governor may serve for a period
  exceeding ninety days without the approval of the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility of governor for
  re-appointment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who has served two consecutive terms as a
  governor appointed under subsection (1) is not, during the twelve
  months following the completion of the second term, eligible to be
  re-appointed to the Council, except as Chairman.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 29, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancy

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A vacancy in the membership
of the Council does not impair the right of the remaining members to
act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 29, s. 8
:::

## [President]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61765 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of President

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President of the Centre shall be appointed by the Governor in Council
  after consultation with the Council, for a term not exceeding five
  years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure of President

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every President holds office during good behaviour,
  but may be removed by the Governor in Council for cause.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where appointee is a governor

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the person appointed President is a governor,
  he ceases to be a governor on assuming the office of President.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 29, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of President

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President is the chief executive officer of the Centre, has
  supervision over and direction of the work and staff of the Centre, is
  the chairman of the executive board and shall perform such duties as
  are assigned to the President by the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President to be full-time

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The President shall devote the whole of his time to
  the performance of the duties of the President under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 29, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting President

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Council may authorize
an officer of the Centre to act as President if the President is absent
or incapacitated or the office of President is vacant, but no person so
authorized may act as President for a period exceeding sixty days
without the approval of the Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 29, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility of President for
re-appointment

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The President is eligible
for re-appointment on the expiration of his term of office.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 29, s. 9
:::

## [Executive Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61790 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Executive board established

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  is hereby established an executive board of the Centre consisting of
  the President and at least six governors annually elected from the
  Council by the governors so that, at any time,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of members of the executive board who
    were appointed to the Council under paragraph 4(c) is equal to the
    number of members of the executive board who were appointed to the
    Council under paragraph 4(d); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of the number of members of the
    executive board appointed to the Council under paragraph 4(c) or (d)
    is not less than 50% of the total number of members of the executive
    board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of executive board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The executive board shall exercise such of the powers
  and perform such of the activities of the Centre as the Council may by
  by-law assign to it and shall submit at each meeting of the Council
  minutes of its proceedings since the last preceding meeting of the
  Council and a progress report on the work of the Centre.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairman of executive board

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The President is the chairman of the executive board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A majority of the members of the executive board, one
  of whom is the President, constitutes a quorum.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-13, s. 14
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1685
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advisory and other committees

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Centre may, in

## [Remuneration and Expenses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61817 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President shall be paid such salary and expenses as are fixed by the
  Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting President

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An acting President shall be paid such salary and
  expenses as are fixed by the Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 29, s. 13
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairman and other governors

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Chairman and the
other governors shall not be paid a salary, but may be paid

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} such allowance for each day spent in connection with
  the work of the Centre,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} such allowance for the performance, with the approval
  of the Council, of any duties over and above their ordinary duties,
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} such travel and living expenses while absent from
  their ordinary place of residence in connection with the work of the
  Centre

as are fixed by the Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 29, s. 14
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 47, ss. 5, 53(F)
- 1984, c. 40, s. 79(F)
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61833 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the
Centre shall be at such place in Canada as is designated by the Governor
in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 29, s. 15
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings of Council

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Council shall meet at least three times in each year, with at least
  one such meeting at the head office of the Centre, and at such other
  times and places in Canada as the executive board deems necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairman to preside

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairman shall preside at meetings of the
  Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Council meetings open to President

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The President is entitled to attend and participate
  in all meetings of the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum of Council

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A majority of governors constitutes a quorum of the
  Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 29, s. 16
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The Council may make
by-laws for the conduct and management of the affairs of the Centre and,
without restricting the generality of the foregoing, may make by-laws
respecting

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the appointment and operation of advisory or other
  committees referred to in section 16 and the allowances and expenses,
  if any, to be paid to members of those committees who are not members
  of the Council;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the employment or engagement and the remuneration,
  expenses and duties of officers, employees and agents of the Centre;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the procedure in all business at meetings of the
  Council and of the executive board;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the assignment of any powers and activities of the
  Centre to the executive board and the manner in which those powers and
  activities shall be exercised or performed; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which briefs and other written
  representations submitted to the Centre are to be considered.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 29, s. 17
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of governors, officers, employees
and agents

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as provided in subsections (2) and (3), the Chairman and President,
  the other governors and officers and the employees and agents of the
  Centre are not part of the federal public administration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Superannuation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The President, the other officers and the employees
  of the Centre shall be deemed to be employed in the public service for
  the purposes of the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The President, the other officers and the employees
  of the Centre shall be deemed to be employed in the federal public
  administration for the purposes of the [Government Employees
  Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and any regulations made under
  section 9 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) to governors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36) does not apply to the Chairman or the other
  governors unless, in the case of any of them, the Governor in Council
  directs that it shall apply.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985. c. C-13, s. 23
- 2003, c. 22, ss. 224(E), 225(E)
:::

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61874 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  four months after the end of each year, the Council shall submit to
  the Minister a report on the activities of the Centre for that year,
  including a summary of the disposition of briefs and other written
  representations considered by the Centre as required by subsection
  6(2) and the financial statements of the Centre.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause the report referred to in
  subsection (1) to be laid before Parliament not later than the tenth
  sitting day of Parliament after he receives it.

- Definition of [[sitting day of Parliament]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), [[sitting day of
  Parliament]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day on which either House of
  Parliament sits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to be sent to each province

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Without delay after the report is laid before
  Parliament under subsection (2), the Minister shall send a copy of it
  to the lieutenant governor of each province, the Commissioner of
  Yukon, the Commissioner of the Northwest Territories and the
  Commissioner of Nunavut.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-13, s. 26
- 1993, c. 28, s. 78
- 2002, c. 7, s. 121(E)
- 2012, c. 19, s. 171

Centre.
