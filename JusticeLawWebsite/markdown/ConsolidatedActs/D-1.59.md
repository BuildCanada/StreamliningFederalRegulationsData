## Department of Housing, Infrastructure and Communities Act {#department-of-housing-infrastructure-and-communities-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2024, c. 15, s. 323
:::

Assented to 2024-06-20

An Act to establish the Department of Housing, Infrastructure and
Communities

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 323 of chapter 15 of the Statutes of Canada, 2024,
in force on assent June 20, 2024.\]
:::
:::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas public infrastructure and housing are essential for communities
to be complete, inclusive and environmentally sustainable;

Whereas these types of communities foster a stronger national economy in
which the people of Canada can prosper and thrive;

Whereas advancing public infrastructure and housing outcomes is best
achieved through cooperation between governments as well as the
meaningful involvement of local communities;

Whereas effective support for infrastructure plays a key role in
improving housing outcomes;

And whereas promoting the use of innovative financial tools helps
attract investment from the private sector and institutional investors
in public infrastructure projects;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1477787 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Department of Housing, Infrastructure and Communities
Act](/eng/acts/D-1.59).

## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1477790 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,
[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the department established
under section 3.

## [Department of Housing, Infrastructure and Communities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1477793 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Department established

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A department of the
Government of Canada, called the Department of Housing, Infrastructure
and Communities, is established and is to be presided over by the
Minister of Infrastructure and Communities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy Minister

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
appoint a Deputy Minister of Housing, Infrastructure and Communities to
hold office during pleasure and to be the deputy head of the Department.

## [Minister of Infrastructure and Communities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1477798 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister of Infrastructure and
Communities

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of
Infrastructure and Communities, appointed by commission under the Great
Seal, holds office during pleasure and has the management and direction
of the Department.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Infrastructure and
  Communities extend to and include all matters relating to public
  infrastructure over which Parliament has jurisdiction and that are not
  by law assigned to any other department, board or agency of the
  Government of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Particulars

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Without restricting the generality of subsection (1),
  the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities' duties and functions
  include supporting and promoting infrastructure projects and
  initiatives that are in the public interest in order to foster the
  prosperity, inclusivity and environmental sustainability of
  communities.

## [Minister of Housing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1477806 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A Minister of Housing may
be appointed by commission under the Great Seal to hold office during
pleasure.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Housing extend to and
  include all matters relating to housing and the reduction and
  prevention of homelessness over which Parliament has jurisdiction and
  that are not by law assigned to any other Minister, department, board
  or agency of the Government of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Particulars

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Without restricting the generality of subsection (1),
  the Minister of Housing's duties and functions include advancing
  national housing priorities and reducing and preventing homelessness
  to foster the prosperity, inclusivity and environmental sustainability
  of communities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of departmental services and
facilities

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Housing is
to make use of the Department services and facilities and may authorize
employees of the Department to exercise any power or perform any duty or
function of the Minister of Housing.

## [Provisions Applicable to Both Ministers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1477816 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No Minister appointed

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** If no Minister is
appointed under section 7,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities is to
  exercise the powers and perform the duties and functions of the
  Minister of Housing; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} every reference to the Minister of Housing in any Act
  of Parliament or in any order, regulation or other instrument made
  under an Act of Parliament is to be read as a reference to the
  Minister of Infrastructure and Communities, unless the context
  otherwise requires.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General duties and powers

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of
Infrastructure and Communities or the Minister of Housing, as the case
may be, may, in exercising their powers and performing their duties and
functions,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish, recommend, coordinate and implement
  initiatives, programs and projects;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} make grants and contributions;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} collaborate or enter into agreements or other
  arrangements with other federal, provincial or territorial
  departments, boards and agencies, local governments, Indigenous bodies
  or any institution or person;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} undertake, coordinate and promote research
  activities; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} subject to the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19),
  collect, analyze, interpret, publish or distribute information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committees

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Infrastructure and Communities or the Minister of Housing,
  as the case may be, may establish advisory and other committees and
  provide for their membership, duties, functions and operation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Infrastructure and Communities or the
  Minister of Housing, as the case may be, may fix the remuneration that
  members of a committee are to be paid for the performance of their
  duties and functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Travel, living and other expenses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Members of a committee are entitled to be reimbursed,
  in accordance with Treasury Board directives, for the travel, living
  and other expenses incurred for the performance of their duties and
  functions while absent from their ordinary place of residence.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2024, c. 15, s. 324

  - ###### Deputy Minister {#deputy-minister .MarginalNote}

    - [324]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any person who, immediately
      before the day on which this section comes into force, holds the
      office of Deputy Head of Infrastructure and Communities, styled as
      Deputy Minister of Infrastructure and Communities, is deemed, as
      of that day, to have been appointed as the Deputy Minister
      referred to in section 4 of the [Department of Housing,
      Infrastructure and Communities Act](/eng/acts/D-1.59), as enacted
      by section 323.

    - ###### Persons who occupy a position {#persons-who-occupy-a-position .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in the [Department of Housing,
      Infrastructure and Communities Act](/eng/acts/D-1.59) is to be
      construed as affecting the status of any person who, immediately
      before the day on which this section comes into force, occupies or
      is assigned to a position in the Office of Infrastructure of
      Canada, except that, as of that day, the person occupies or is
      assigned to their position in the Department of Housing,
      Infrastructure and Communities.

- --- 2024, c. 15, s. 325

  - ###### Transfer of appropriations {#transfer-of-appropriations .MarginalNote}

    [325]{.lawlabel} Any amount that is appropriated by an Act of
    Parliament for the fiscal year in which this section comes into
    force to defray the expenditures of the public service of Canada
    within the Office of Infrastructure of Canada and that is unexpended
    on the day on which this section comes into force is deemed to be an
    amount appropriated to defray the expenditures of the public service
    of Canada within the Department of Housing, Infrastructure and
    Communities.

- --- 2024, c. 15, s. 326

  - ###### References {#references .MarginalNote}

    [326]{.lawlabel} On the day on which this section comes into force,
    every reference to the Office of Infrastructure of Canada in any
    agreement, contract, instrument or act or other document is to be
    read as a reference to the Department of Housing, Infrastructure and
    Communities, unless the context requires otherwise.
