## Mi'kmaq Education Act {#mikmaq-education-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1998, c. 24
:::

Assented to 1998-06-18

An Act respecting the powers of the Mi'kmaq of Nova Scotia in relation
to education
::::

::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358294 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Mi'kmaq Education Act](/eng/acts/M-7.6).

## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358297 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Act.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the agreement between Her
    Majesty the Queen in right of Canada and the Mi'kmaq Bands in Nova
    Scotia with respect to education, signed on February 14,
    1997. ([convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a band within the meaning of the
    [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[community]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[community]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a band that is named in the
    schedule. ([communauté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[constitution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[constitution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a constitution in
    relation to education adopted by the council of a community that is
    in conformity with the provisions of Schedule D to the
    Agreement. ([constitution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a band or community,
    means the council of the band or community as referred to in the
    [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a community, means a
    person whose name appears on the band list maintained for the
    community in accordance with the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) or who
    is entitled to have that name appear on the band
    list. ([membre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a reserve within the meaning
    of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([réserve]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal capacity

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of this Act, a community shall act through its council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Majority vote

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, paragraph 2(3)(b) of the
  [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) applies in respect of the powers of the
  council of a community under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to enable communities to exercise jurisdiction in relation to education,
as provided in the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Act

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Act applies to every
community for which a constitution is in effect.

## [Powers and Duties of Communities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358316 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legislative power

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  community may, to the extent provided by the Agreement, make laws
  applicable on the reserve of the community in relation to primary,
  elementary and secondary education.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legislative power --- post-secondary
  support

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A community may make laws in relation to the
  administration and expenditure of community funds in support of
  post-secondary education, as provided by the Agreement, for members of
  the community wherever resident.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Programs and services

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  community shall, to the extent provided by the Agreement, provide or
  make provision for primary, elementary and secondary educational
  programs and services for residents of its reserve.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transferability

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The educational programs and services so provided
  must be comparable to programs and services provided by other
  education systems in Canada, in order to permit the transfer of
  students to and from those systems without academic penalty to the
  same extent as students can transfer between those other education
  systems.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Community education boards

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A community education board
established by the laws of the community may carry out any powers
conferred on it by those laws or by resolution of the council acting
pursuant to section 7.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enactment of laws

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Laws of
  a community shall be enacted in the manner provided by its
  constitution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The council of a community shall publish a law not
  later than thirty days after its enactment in a manner that affords a
  reasonable opportunity to members of the community resident on the
  reserve to become aware of it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public access

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The council of a community shall make copies of the
  laws of the community and of its constitution available at its office
  for public inspection during normal business hours, and
  Mi'kmaw-Kina'matnewey shall make copies of the laws and constitutions
  of all communities available for public inspection at its office
  during normal business hours.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In any proceedings, evidence of a law of a community
  may be given by the production of a copy of the law, certified to be a
  true copy by a person authorized by the community, without proof of
  that person's signature or official character.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22) not applicable

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply in respect of the laws of a community.

## [Mi'kmaw-Kina'matnewey]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358340 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation established

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  corporation without share capital to be known as Mi'kmaw-Kina'matnewey
  is hereby established for the purpose of supporting the delivery of
  educational programs and services under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The chiefs of the communities are the members of the
  corporation and together constitute its board of directors, and the
  board may provide, by by-law or otherwise, for the management and
  conduct of the affairs of the corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of [Canada Not-for-profit
  Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-7.75)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, Part 19 of the [Canada
  Not-for-profit Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-7.75) applies to the
  corporation and, in the application of section 160 of that Act to the
  corporation, a reference to members shall be read as a reference to
  directors.

## [Indian Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358350 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) not
applicable

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Sections 114 to 122 of
the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) do not apply to a community or its
members.

## [Amendment of Schedule]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358353 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Addition of band to schedule

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, by order, add to the schedule the name of any
  band of Mi'kmaq in the province of Nova Scotia if the Governor in
  Council is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the council of the band has, in a manner consistent
    with the Agreement, authorized the Agreement to be signed on behalf
    of the band; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Agreement has been signed on behalf of the
    band.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deletion of community from schedule

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by order, delete the
  name of a community from the schedule if the Governor in Council is
  satisfied that the council of the community has, in a manner
  consistent with the Agreement, authorized the withdrawal of the
  community from the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date of amendment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the council of a band or community, during the
  twelve-month period ending on June 30 in any year, authorizes the
  signing of the Agreement or withdrawal from the Agreement, the
  amendment to the schedule adding the name of the band or deleting the
  name of the community shall be made effective on April 1 of the
  following year.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-358363 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order

- **[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#M-7.6_en_1){#M-7.6_en_1-ID0EBCA}[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), this Act
  comes into force, or shall be deemed to have come into force, on a day
  to be fixed by order of the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Section 10

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Section 10 comes into force on a day to be fixed by
  order of the Governor in Council following adoption by the chiefs of
  the bands named in the schedule of a resolution for the establishment
  of Mi'kmaw-Kina'matnewey.
