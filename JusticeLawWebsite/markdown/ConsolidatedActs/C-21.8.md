## Canadian Race Relations Foundation Act {#canadian-race-relations-foundation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1991, c. 8
:::

Assented to 1991-02-01

An Act to establish the Canadian Race Relations Foundation
::::

::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS Canada, as a party to the *International Convention on the
Elimination of All Forms of Racial Discrimination*, has resolved to
adopt all necessary measures for speedily eliminating racial
discrimination in all its forms and manifestations;

AND WHEREAS the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms provides that
every individual is equal before and under the law and has the right to
the equal protection and equal benefit of the law, without
discrimination;

AND WHEREAS the Constitution of Canada recognizes the importance of
preserving and enhancing the multicultural heritage of Canadians and
recognizes rights of the aboriginal peoples of Canada;

AND WHEREAS the [Canadian Multiculturalism Act](/eng/acts/C-18.7)
provides that it is the policy of the Government of Canada to promote
the full and equitable participation of individuals and communities of
all origins in the continuing evolution and shaping of all aspects of
Canadian society and to assist them in the elimination of any barrier to
such participation;

AND WHEREAS, in concluding the Japanese Canadian Redress Agreement with
the National Association of Japanese Canadians, the Government of Canada
has condemned the excesses of the past, reaffirmed the principles of
justice and equality for all in Canada and undertaken to establish a
race relations foundation;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73810 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canadian Race Relations Foundation Act](/eng/acts/C-21.8).

## [Establishment of Foundation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73822 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foundation established

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby established
a corporation, to be called the Canadian Race Relations Foundation.

## [Purpose of Foundation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73825 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of the
Foundation is to facilitate throughout Canada the development, sharing
and application of knowledge and expertise in order to contribute to the
elimination of racism and all forms of racial discrimination in Canadian
society by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} undertaking research and collecting data and
  developing a national information base in order to further
  understanding of the nature of racism and racial discrimination and to
  assist business, labour, voluntary, community and other organizations
  as well as public institutions, governments, researchers and the
  general public in eliminating racism and racial discrimination;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} acting as a clearing-house, providing information
  about race relations resources and establishing links with public,
  private and educational institutions and libraries;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} facilitating consultation, and the exchange of
  information, relating to race relations policies, programs and
  research;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} promoting effective race relations training and
  assisting in the development of professional standards;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} increasing public awareness of the importance of
  eliminating racism and racial discrimination;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} collaborating with business, labour, voluntary,
  community and other organizations, as well as public institutions and
  all levels of government, in instituting and supporting programs and
  activities; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} supporting and promoting the development of effective
  policies and programs for the elimination of racism and racial
  discrimination.

## [Powers and Capacity of Foundation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73835 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers and capacity of Foundation

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In order
  to carry out its purpose, the Foundation has the capacity and the
  rights, powers and privileges of a natural person and, without
  restricting the generality of the foregoing, the Foundation may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} initiate, finance and administer programs and
    activities related to its purpose;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} acquire any money, securities or other property by
    gift, bequest or otherwise and hold, expend, invest, administer or
    dispose of that property, subject to any terms on which it is given,
    bequeathed or otherwise made available to the Foundation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} expend any money provided by Parliament or any
    other sources for the activities of the Foundation, subject to any
    terms on which it is provided;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} undertake, support, publish and disseminate
    research studies, reports and other documents on its own initiative
    or at the request of the Minister;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} initiate, sponsor and support conferences, seminars
    and meetings;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} establish scholarly and professional links between
    the Foundation and universities, colleges and other organizations
    and persons interested in the Foundation's work; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} undertake any other activities that are conducive
    to the fulfillment of its purpose and the exercise of its powers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity in Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Foundation may carry on its activities throughout
  Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights preserved

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No act of the Foundation, including any transfer of
  property to or by the Foundation, is invalid by reason only that the
  act is contrary to this Act or the by-laws of the Foundation.

## [Board of Directors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73850 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board of Directors

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  activities of the Foundation shall be managed by a Board of Directors
  consisting of a Chairperson and not more than 11 other directors to be
  appointed by the Governor in Council, on the recommendation of the
  Minister, after the Minister, taking into account the multicultural
  character, linguistic duality and regional diversity of Canadian
  society, has consulted with any governments, institutions,
  organizations and individuals that the Minister considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criterium for appointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Persons appointed to the Board must have knowledge or
  experience that will assist the Foundation in fulfilling its purpose.

- 1991, c. 8, s. 6

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  director shall be appointed to hold office for a term not exceeding
  four years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A director is eligible for reappointment to the Board
  in the same or another capacity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resignation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A director who wishes to resign shall notify the
  Board in writing to that effect, and the resignation becomes effective
  when the Board receives the notice or at the time specified in the
  notice, whichever is the later.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1991, c. 8, s. 7
- 2006, c. 9, s. 243
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Functions of Chairperson

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson shall preside at meetings of the Board and shall perform
  such other duties as are assigned by the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election of Vice-Chairperson

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall elect one of the directors, other
  than the Chairperson, to be Vice-Chairperson of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity of Chairperson

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Chairperson is absent or unable to act or if
  the office of Chairperson is vacant, the Vice-Chairperson may act as
  Chairperson.

## [Executive Director]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73874 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Executive Director

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be an Executive Director of the Foundation, who shall be
  appointed by the Governor in Council, on the recommendation of the
  Minister, for a term not exceeding five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall consult the Board before making
  any recommendation with respect to the appointment of the Executive
  Director, other than the first Executive Director.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chief executive officer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Executive Director is the chief executive officer
  of the Foundation and has supervision over and direction of the work
  and staff of the Foundation and may engage such officers, employees
  and agents as are necessary for the proper conduct of the work of the
  Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity of Executive
  Director

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Executive Director is absent or unable to act
  or if the office of Executive Director is vacant, the Board may
  authorize a director or an officer or employee of the Foundation to
  act as Executive Director, but no person may so act for a period
  exceeding sixty days without the approval of the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Executive Director is eligible for
  re-appointment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Ex officio]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  member

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Executive Director is [ex officio]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} a member of the Board, but has no vote.

## [Remuneration and Expenses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73888 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees of directors

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson and other directors shall be paid such fees for their
  attendance at meetings of the Foundation and for the performance of
  other duties under this Act as the Governor in Council may fix.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses of directors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson and other directors are entitled to
  be paid such travel and living expenses incurred by them in the
  performance of duties under this Act as the Governor in Council may
  fix.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salary of Executive Director

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The salary and any other
remuneration to be paid to the Executive Director shall be fixed by the
Governor in Council.

## [Committees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73896 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committees

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Under such terms and
conditions as are fixed by by-law of the Board, the Board may appoint an
Executive Committee from among the directors and may appoint advisory
and other committees consisting, wholly or partly, of directors and
persons who are not directors.

## [Principal Office and Meetings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73899 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principal office

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The principal office of
the Foundation shall be located in the Municipality of Metropolitan
Toronto, Ontario.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to services

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Foundation shall take
reasonable measures to provide access to its services throughout Canada,
alone or in collaboration with other institutions and organizations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings of the Board

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall meet at such times and places as the Chairperson may
  determine, but it shall meet at least twice in each year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A majority of the directors in office constitutes a
  quorum at a meeting of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Deputy to the Minister, or a person designated by
  the Deputy, is entitled to receive notice of all meetings of the Board
  and of any committees thereof and to attend and take part in, but not
  to vote at, those meetings.

## [By-laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73911 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Board may make
by-laws respecting

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the duties of the officers, employees and agents of
  the Foundation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the remuneration and conditions of employment of the
  officers, employees and agents of the Foundation, other than the
  Executive Director;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the constitution of any committees appointed pursuant
  to section 12, the role and duties of the committees and the expenses,
  if any, to be paid to the members of those committees who are not
  directors;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the procedure at meetings of the Board and its
  committees;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the administration, management and control of the
  property of the Foundation; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the conduct and management of the work of the
  Foundation.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73920 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not an agent

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Foundation is not an agent of Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not part of federal public
  administration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson, directors, Executive Director,
  officers, employees and agents of the Foundation are not part of the
  federal public administration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Part X of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11), except for sections 89.8 to 89.92, subsection
  105(2) and sections 113.1, 131 to 148 and 154.01, does not apply to
  the Foundation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of care

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  exercising powers and performing duties under this Act, every director
  and every officer of the Foundation shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} act honestly and in good faith with a view to the
    best interests of the Foundation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} exercise the care, diligence and skill that a
    reasonably prudent person would exercise in comparable
    circumstances; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} comply with this Act and the by-laws of the
    Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reliance on statements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A director or officer is not liable for a breach of
  duty under subsection (1) if the director or officer relies in good
  faith on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} financial statements of the Foundation represented
    to the director or officer by an officer of the Foundation or in a
    written report of the auditor of the Foundation fairly to reflect
    the financial condition of the Foundation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a report of a lawyer, accountant, engineer,
    appraiser or other person whose position or profession lends
    credibility to a statement made by that person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of interest in contract

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  director or officer who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a party to a material contract or proposed
    material contract with the Foundation, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is a director or officer of, or has a material
    interest in, any person who is a party to a material contract or
    proposed material contract with the Foundation,

  shall disclose in writing to the Foundation the nature and extent of
  the interest of the director or officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure by-laws

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall make by-laws respecting

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the time when and the form and manner in which the
    disclosure required by subsection (1) shall be made; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the limitation on the participation of a director
    or officer who has made a disclosure as required by subsection (1)
    in any proceedings respecting the contract that is the subject of
    the disclosure.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to indemnify

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Foundation may indemnify a present or former director or officer of
  the Foundation or any other person who acts or acted at its request as
  a director or officer of another corporation of which the Foundation
  is or was a shareholder or creditor, and the person's heirs and legal
  representatives, against all costs, charges and expenses, including
  any amount paid to settle an action or satisfy a judgment, reasonably
  incurred by the person in respect of any civil, criminal or
  administrative action or proceeding to which the person is a party by
  reason of being or having been such a director or officer, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person acted honestly and in good faith with a
    view to the best interests of the Foundation or other corporation;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any criminal or administrative
    action or proceeding that is enforced by a monetary penalty, the
    person had reasonable grounds for believing that the person's
    conduct was lawful.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insurance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Foundation may purchase and maintain insurance
  for the benefit of a director or officer, and the director's or
  officer's heirs and legal representatives, against any liability,
  cost, charge and expense incurred by the director or officer as
  described in subsection (1).

## [Financial Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73957 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registered charity

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The Foundation shall be
deemed, for the purposes of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), to be
a registered charity within the meaning of that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1991, c. 8, s. 21
- 1999, c. 31, s. 246(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment to Foundation

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be paid to the Foundation out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund
  the sum of twenty-four million dollars, of which twelve million
  dollars shall be paid on behalf of the Japanese Canadian community in
  commemoration of members of that community who suffered injustices
  during and after World War II.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Endowment Fund

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The sum of twenty-four million dollars referred to in
  subsection (1) constitutes the capital of an Endowment Fund to be used
  only for investment and the earning of income, which income may be
  expended for the purpose of the Foundation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dissolution

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** If the Foundation is
dissolved,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the capital of the Endowment Fund and any unexpended
  interest thereon, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any of the Foundation's other property that remains
  after the payment of the Foundation's debts and liabilities, or after
  making adequate provision for their payment,

government on a proportional basis having regard to their total
contributions to the Foundation.

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73979 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  four months after the end of each financial year of the Foundation,
  the Chairperson shall submit to the Minister a report of the
  activities of the Foundation during that year, including the financial
  statements of the Foundation and the auditor's report thereon.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies for public scrutiny

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Foundation shall make copies of the report
  referred to in subsection (1) available for public scrutiny at the
  principal office of the Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling in Parliament

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause a copy of the report
  referred to in subsection (1) to be laid before each House of
  Parliament within the first fifteen days on which that House is
  sitting after the day on which the Minister has received the report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of Act

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As
  soon as possible after the fourth anniversary of the coming into force
  of this Act, the Minister, after consultation with the Board, shall
  evaluate and prepare a report on the Foundation's activities and
  organization, including a statement of any changes that the Minister
  recommends.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling in Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause a copy of the report
  referred to in subsection (1) to be laid before each House of
  Parliament within the first fifteen days on which that House is
  sitting after the report has been completed.
