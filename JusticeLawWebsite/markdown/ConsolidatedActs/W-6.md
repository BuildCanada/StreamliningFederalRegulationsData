## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468058 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Weights and Measures Act](/eng/acts/W-6).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 36, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468063 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who in the course of
    that person's business sells, consigns, imports, leases or lends
    devices; ([fournisseur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any weight, weighing machine,
    static measure or measuring machine and includes any equipment and
    accessories attached to or used in conjunction with the device that
    have or can have an effect on the accuracy of the
    device; ([instrument]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is designated
    under subsection 16.1(1) to verify compliance with this
    Act; ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[local standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[local standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any standard designated
    by the Minister under section 13; ([étalon local]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[measure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[measure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, when used as a verb, includes weigh
    and, when used as a noun, includes
    weight; ([mesure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} et
    [mesurer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[measuring machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[measuring machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any machine that
    measures length, area, volume or capacity, temperature or
    time; ([appareil de mesure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Industry; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by the
    regulations; ([Version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[reference standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reference standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a standard that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} represents or registers a unit of measurement
      referred to in Schedule I or II or that represents or registers a
      multiple or fraction of such a unit of measurement,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has been calibrated and certified by the National
      Research Council of Canada, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is or is to be used as a standard for the purpose
      of determining the accuracy of a local standard; ([étalon de
      référence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[static measure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[static measure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any measure that
    measures length, volume or capacity and does not have a moving or
    movable part that has or can have an effect on the accuracy of the
    measure; ([mesure matérialisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[trade]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[trade]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the selling, purchasing,
    exchanging, consigning, leasing or providing of any commodity,
    right, facility or service on the basis of measure and includes the
    business of providing facilities for
    measuring; ([commerce]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[trader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[trader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who trades in the
    course of business; ([commerçant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[weighing machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[weighing machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any machine that
    measures mass or weight. ([appareil de pesage]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Approval of Devices by the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468084 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of devices

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall, in accordance with the regulations, approve devices or
  classes, types or designs of devices for use in trade.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary approval

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, in any period during which a device
  or class, type or design of device is being evaluated for approval for
  use in trade under subsection (1), approve that device or class, type
  or design of device for use in trade on a temporary basis for such
  period and under such terms and conditions as the Minister may
  specify.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 36, s. 3
- 1976-77, c. 28, s. 46
:::

## [Units of Measurement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468092 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Basis for units of measurement

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  units of measurement used in Canada shall be determined on the basis
  of the International System of Units established by the General
  Conference of Weights and Measures.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Basic, supplementary and derived units

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The basic, supplementary and derived units of
  measurement for use in Canada and the symbols therefor are as set out
  and defined in Parts I, II and III of Schedule I, respectively.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Customary units

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the units of measurement otherwise
  referred to in this section, the customary units of measurement and
  the symbols therefor as set out and defined in Part IV of Schedule I
  may be used in Canada, which units of measurement are commonly used
  with the International System of Units.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Multiples and submultiples of units

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The prefixes for multiples and submultiples of the
  units of measurement referred to in subsection (2) and the symbols
  therefor are as set out and defined in Part V of Schedule I.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian units

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Canadian units of measurement are as set out and
  defined in Schedule II, and the symbols and abbreviations therefor are
  as added pursuant to subparagraph 6(1)(b)(ii).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 36, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seigneurial tenure

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding section 7,
the units of measurement set out and defined in Schedule III may be used
to describe land in the Province of Quebec that was originally granted
under seigneurial tenure.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 36, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments to Schedules I and II

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may by order

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} amend Schedule I by adding to or deleting from Part
    I, II, III, IV or V thereof any basic, supplementary, derived or
    customary unit of measurement or any prefix therefor, together with
    its symbol and definition; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} amend Schedule II

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by adding thereto or deleting therefrom any
      Canadian unit of measurement, together with its definition,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by adding thereto a symbol or an abbreviation
      for any Canadian unit of measurement, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} by deleting therefrom any symbol or
      abbreviation referred to in subparagraph (ii).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation of power to amend Schedule
  II

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), the Governor in
  Council may not amend Schedule II in such a manner that the ratio of
  any one unit of measurement to any other unit of measurement is
  altered.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 36, s. 6
- 1976-77, c. 55, s. 9
:::

## [Use of Units of Measurement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468122 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of units of measurement

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No person shall, in trade,
use or provide for the use of a unit of measurement unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that unit of measurement is set out and defined in
  Schedule I or II; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the use of that unit of measurement is authorized by
  the regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 36, s. 7
:::

## [Use of Devices]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468129 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of devices

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No trader shall use, or
have in their possession for use, in trade, any device unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that device or class, type or design of device has
  been approved for use in trade pursuant to section 3; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that device, in the case of a device other than a
  static measure, has been examined by an inspector who has certified
  that the device meets the requirements of this Act and the
  regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. W-6, s. 8
- 2011, c. 3, ss. 12, 29
:::

## [Marking of Commodities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468136 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marking commodities for sale

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  trader shall sell, offer for sale or have in their possession for sale
  on the basis of number or measure any commodity, unless its quantity
  is stated accurately within prescribed limits of error and in the
  manner prescribed in terms of number or units of measurement of
  length, area, volume or capacity, or mass or weight on the following
  items, as may be prescribed:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the commodity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the package containing the commodity; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a shipping bill, bill of lading or other document
    accompanying the commodity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply with respect to any
  commodity that has been packaged, on the basis of number or measure,
  or labelled, in terms of number or a unit of measurement, as required
  or authorized by or under any other Act of Parliament.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. W-6, s. 9
- 2011, c. 3, s. 13
:::

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468147 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the manner in which a person may apply
    to have any device or any class, type or design of device approved
    for use in trade;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing a date beyond which a class, type or
    design of device shall not be approved for use in trade unless it is
    capable of weighing or measuring in terms of units of measurement
    set out and defined in Schedule I;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} establishing temperature scales and prescribing the
    purposes for which those scales may be used in Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the manner in which inspectors shall
    carry out their functions under this Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the manner in which an inspector shall
    determine whether any lot, shipment, proposed shipment or
    identifiable quantity of any commodity meets the requirements of
    this Act and the regulations;

  - [(e.1)]{.lawlabel} prescribing a period, in relation to any category
    of trade or any class, type or design of device, for the purposes of
    subsection 15(1);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the circumstances in which an
    examination shall be required where an adjustment, alteration or
    repair is made to a device;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the circumstances and the manner in
    which a trader shall report the location of any device that is owned
    by the trader or held in the trader's possession for use in trade;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} requiring any person to report, and specifying the
    manner in which a report shall be made of, the removal of any
    marking or tag or the breaking of any seal placed on or attached to
    a device by an inspector or any person who has adjusted, altered or
    repaired that device and the nature of any adjustment, alteration or
    repair made to a device;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} establishing or providing for the establishment of
    specifications relating to design, composition, construction and
    performance to which any device or class, type or design of device
    shall conform before it may be approved for use in trade and
    specifications relating to the installation and use of any device or
    class, type or design of device;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} prescribing, in respect of any or all categories of
    trade and in respect of any or all class or classes of persons
    carrying on business therein, in any geographical areas of Canada, a
    date beyond which a class, type or design of device shall not be
    used in trade unless it is capable of weighing or measuring in terms
    of units of measurement set out and defined in Schedule I;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} respecting the detention of things seized and
    detained under paragraph 17(1)(c);

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} respecting the disposition of things forfeited
    under section 41;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} authorizing for any particular purpose the use of a
    unit of measurement that is not otherwise authorized under this Act;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} prescribing, in respect of any or all categories of
    trade in any geographical areas of Canada, the units or multiples or
    subdivisions of units of measurement that shall be used for
    offering, advertising or displaying commodities for retail trade in
    terms of price per unit of measurement;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} prescribing, in respect of any or all categories of
    trade and in respect of any or all class or classes of persons
    carrying on business therein, in any geographical areas of Canada, a
    date beyond which units of measurement set out and defined in
    Schedule II shall not be used in trade;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} respecting the form of any receipts, certificates,
    statements, tags, seals or documents that may be used under this
    Act;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the fees that shall be paid by any
    person for any examination or other service provided by an inspector
    under this Act;

  - [(q.1)]{.lawlabel} designating any class of examination or other
    service provided by an inspector under this Act as a class of
    examination or service to which any fees prescribed, or charges
    payable, under this Act do not apply;

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} determining the nature of any charges that a person
    may be required to pay in relation to any examination or other
    service provided by an inspector under this Act and the manner in
    which those charges shall be determined;

  - [(s)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the time when or the period of time
    within which, and the manner in which any fees or charges shall be
    paid;

  - [(t)]{.lawlabel} requiring any person who sells or offers for sale
    any liquid commodity by means of a coin-operated machine to state on
    the machine the owner's name and address, the identification number
    of the machine and the quantity of the commodity being sold or
    offered for sale in terms of a unit of measurement of volume or
    capacity together with the price demanded for that quantity and
    prescribing the form and manner in which such information shall be
    stated;

  - [(u)]{.lawlabel} exempting, conditionally or unconditionally, any
    device or class, type or design of device or any class or type of
    trade transaction from any or all of the provisions of this Act; and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} prescribing any matter or thing that by this Act
    may be prescribed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of proposed regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the Minister shall publish
  in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) a copy of each
  regulation that the Governor in Council proposes to make under
  paragraphs (1)(b), (j), (n) and (o) and a reasonable opportunity shall
  be afforded to interested persons to make representations with respect
  thereto.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is not required to publish a proposed
  regulation if it has been published pursuant to subsection (2) whether
  or not it has been amended as a result of representations made by
  interested persons as provided in that subsection.

## [Reference and Local Standards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468181 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference standards

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  reference standards for use in Canada are those described in Schedule
  IV.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additions to and deletions from
  Schedule IV

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may by order amend Schedule
  IV

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by adding thereto any standard that is to be used
    as a reference standard; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by deleting therefrom any reference standard.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 36, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calibration and certification of
standards

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  reference standards and other standards of measurement that are
  retained under the authority of the Minister for the purpose of
  determining the accuracy of local standards or of standards that are
  used by inspectors under the [Electricity and Gas Inspection
  Act](/eng/acts/E-4) shall, at the request of the Minister, be
  calibrated and certified by the National Research Council of Canada in
  terms of the units of measurement set out and defined in Schedule I or
  II.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The National Research Council of Canada may request
  the Minister to forward to the Council for calibration and
  certification any standards referred to in subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. W-6, s. 12
- R.S., 1985, c. 4 (1st Supp.), s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of local standards

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate as a local standard any standard that has been
  calibrated and certified in relation to a reference standard as
  accurate within prescribed tolerances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calibration and certification of local
  standards

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every local standard shall be calibrated and
  certified within such periods of time as may be prescribed.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 36, s. 13
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacement or restoration of standards

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** If a local standard that
is owned by Her Majesty in right of Canada or a reference standard is
lost, destroyed, defaced or damaged, the Minister shall take such action
as may be necessary to replace or restore the standard.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. W-6, s. 14
- 2001, c. 34, s. 82
:::

## [Administration and Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468225 .Part}

### [Designation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-468226 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to designate

- **[[16.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the
  administration and enforcement of this Act, the Minister may designate
  persons, or classes of persons, to exercise powers in relation to any
  matter referred to in the designation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Training and qualification

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall ensure that, for each particular
  sector, all persons designated under subsection (1) are trained and
  qualified in the same manner and that all examinations made by these
  persons are conducted consistently.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may designate only persons employed in
  the federal public administration to exercise powers under any of
  sections 17 to 18, subsection 19(3) or section 21 or subsection
  22.11(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension and revocation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may suspend or revoke a designation made
  under subsection (1).

### [Powers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-468238 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to enter and other powers

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector who has reasonable grounds to believe that an object to
  which this Act applies is located in or on a place, including a
  vehicle, or that an activity regulated by this Act is conducted in a
  place, including a vehicle, may, for the purpose of verifying
  compliance with this Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter the place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} examine the place or anything found in or on the
    place;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} seize and detain anything in or on the place;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} use any means of communication in the place or
    cause it to be used;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} use any computer system in the place, or cause it
    to be used, to examine data contained in or available to it;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} prepare a document, or cause one to be prepared,
    based on the data;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} use any copying equipment in the place, or cause it
    to be used;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} direct any person to put anything in or on the
    place into operation or to cease operating it; and

### [Proceedings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-468337 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of violation

- **[[22.11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An inspector may issue a notice of
  violation and cause it to be provided to a person if the inspector has
  reasonable grounds to believe that the person has committed a
  violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of violation must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name the person believed to have committed the
    violation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identify the acts or omissions that constitute the
    alleged violation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set out the penalty for the violation that the
    person is liable to pay;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} set out the particulars concerning the time and
    manner of payment; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} subject to the regulations, set out a lesser amount
    that may be paid as complete satisfaction of the penalty if it is
    paid within the prescribed time and in the prescribed manner that
    are set out in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summary of rights

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice of violation must clearly summarize, in
  plain language, the rights and obligations under this section and
  sections 22.12 to 22.25 of the person to whom it is provided,
  including the right to request a review of the acts or omissions that
  constitute the alleged violation or of the amount of the penalty and
  the procedure for requesting the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short-form descriptions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may establish a short-form description
  of each violation to be used in notices of violation.

### [Penalties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-468354 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of payment

- **[[22.12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the person who is named in a
  notice of violation pays, within the prescribed time and in the
  prescribed manner that are set out in the notice, the amount of the
  penalty --- or, if applicable, the lesser amount that may be paid as
  complete satisfaction of the penalty --- set out in the notice,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are deemed to have committed the violation to
    which the amount paid relates;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall accept that amount as complete
    satisfaction of the penalty; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proceedings commenced in respect of the
    violation under section 22.11 are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternatives to payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Instead of paying the penalty or, if applicable, the
  lesser amount that may be paid as complete satisfaction of the
  penalty, the person who is named in the notice may, within the
  prescribed time and in the prescribed manner that are set out in the
  notice,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} request to enter into a compliance agreement with
    the Minister that ensures the person's compliance with the provision
    to which the violation relates, if the penalty is \$1,000 or more;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} request a review by the Minister of the acts or
    omissions that constitute the alleged violation or the amount of the
    penalty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the person who is named in the notice does not pay
  the penalty, or, if applicable, the lesser amount that may be paid as
  complete satisfaction of the penalty, within the prescribed time and
  in the prescribed manner and does not exercise any right referred to
  in subsection (2) within the prescribed time and in the prescribed
  manner, the person is deemed to have committed the violation
  identified in the notice.

### [Compliance Agreements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-468369 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance agreements

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After considering a request made

  agreement, as described in that paragraph, with the person making the
  request on any conditions that are satisfactory to the Minister, which
  conditions may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} include a provision for the giving of reasonable
    security, in a form and in an amount that are satisfactory to the
    Minister, as a guarantee that the person will comply with the

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide for the reduction, in whole or in part, of

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who enters into a compliance agreement with
  the Minister is, on doing so, deemed to have committed the violation
  to which the compliance agreement relates, and the proceedings
  commenced in respect of the violation under section 22.11 are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If compliance agreement complied with

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is of the opinion that a person who
  has entered into a compliance agreement has complied with it, the
  Minister shall cause the person to be provided with a notice to that
  effect, at which time any security given by the person under that
  agreement shall be returned to the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If compliance agreement not complied
  with

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is of the opinion that a person who
  has entered into a compliance agreement has not complied with it, the
  Minister shall cause the person to be provided with a notice of
  default to the effect that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} instead of the penalty set out in the compliance
    agreement, the person is liable to pay, within the prescribed time
    and in the prescribed manner set out in the notice of default, twice
    the amount of the penalty set out in the notice of violation and,
    for greater certainty, subsection 22.1(3) does not apply in respect
    of that amount; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the security, if any, given by the person under the
    compliance agreement is forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of notice of default

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Once they have been provided with the notice of
  default, the person shall pay the amount set out in it or, if the
  notice provides for the forfeiture of the security given under the
  compliance agreement, that security is forfeited to Her Majesty in
  right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of payment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the person pays the amount set out in a notice of
  default under paragraph (4)(a) within the prescribed time and in the
  prescribed manner that are set out in the notice,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall accept the amount as complete
    satisfaction of the amount owing; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the compliance agreement entered into under
    subsection (2) terminates.

### [Rules of Law About Violations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-468441 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violations not offences

**[[22.18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.18 .sectionLabel}** For greater
certainty, a violation is not an offence and, accordingly, section 126
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) does not apply.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2011, c. 3, s. 19
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Due diligence defence

- **[[22.19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.19
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person shall not be found to be
  liable for a violation if they establish that they exercised due
  diligence to prevent the commission of the violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Common law principles

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every rule and principle of the common law that
  renders any circumstance a justification or an excuse in relation to a
  charge for an offence under this Act applies in respect of a violation
  to the extent that it is not inconsistent with this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2011, c. 3, s. 19
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Burden of proof

**[[22.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.2 .sectionLabel}** In every case where
the Minister is to determine whether a person who is named in a notice
of violation committed any violation identified in it, the Minister
shall do so on a balance of probabilities.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2011, c. 3, s. 19
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences by corporate officers, etc.

**[[22.21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.21 .sectionLabel}** If a person other
than an individual commits a violation under this Act, any of the
person's officers, directors, agents or mandataries who directs,
authorizes, assents to or acquiesces or participates in the commission
of the violation is a party to and liable for the violation whether or
not the person who actually committed it is proceeded against in
accordance with this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2011, c. 3, s. 19
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vicarious liability --- acts of
employees, agents and mandataries

**[[22.22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.22 .sectionLabel}** A person is liable
for a violation that is committed by their employee, agent or mandatary
who is acting in the course of the employee's employment or within the
scope of the agent's or mandatary's authority, whether or not the
employee, agent or mandatary who actually committed the violation is
identified or proceeded against in accordance with this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2011, c. 3, s. 19
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing violation

**[[22.23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.23 .sectionLabel}** A violation that is
continued on more than one day constitutes a separate violation in
respect of each day during which it is continued.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2011, c. 3, s. 19
:::

## [Seizure and Detention]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468645 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination and samples of seized things

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  inspector seizes and detains anything under paragraph 17(1)(c), they
  shall, at the request of the person from whom the thing is seized,
  allow that person or any person authorized by that person to examine
  the seized thing and, if possible, provide that person with a sample
  of it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Detention

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A seized thing is not to be detained

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} after the provisions of this Act or any regulations
    that are applicable to the seized thing have, in the inspector's
    opinion, been complied with; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} after the expiry of 60 days from the day of
    seizure, unless, before the expiry of that period,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the seized thing has been forfeited under section
      41,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a prosecution has been instituted in respect of
      an offence in relation to which the seized thing is evidence, in
      which case the seized thing may be detained until the prosecution
      is finally concluded, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} notice of an application for an order extending
      the period during which the seized thing may be detained has been
      served in accordance with section 40.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storing of seized things

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A seized thing may, at the option of the inspector,
  be kept or stored in the building or place where it was seized or may
  be removed to any other proper place by the inspector or at their
  direction.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the place where and the time when the application

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the seized thing in respect of which the

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the evidence on which the Minister intends to rely

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of extension granted

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, on the hearing of an application, the provincial

  detained, the provincial court judge shall order that it be detained

  proper and that on the expiry of that period the seized thing be
  restored to the person from whom it was seized or to any other person

  subparagraph 39(2)(b)(i) or (ii) applies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of extension refused

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, on the hearing of an application, the provincial

  be detained, the provincial court judge shall order that the thing be

  entitled to its possession

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the expiry of 60 days after the day of seizure,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} immediately, if, at the time of the hearing, the

## [Forfeiture and Return]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468677 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture on consent

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  owner or the person in lawful possession of a thing at the time it is
  seized under paragraph 17(1)(c) consents in writing to its forfeiture,
  it is forfeited to Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture by order of court

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person is convicted of an offence under this Act
  and a thing seized by means of or in relation to which the offence was
  committed is then being detained, it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is, on the conviction, in addition to any
    punishment imposed for the offence, forfeited to Her Majesty if the
    forfeiture is directed by the court; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall, on the expiry of the period for taking an
    appeal from the conviction or on the final conclusion of the
    proceedings, as the case may be, be restored to the person from whom
    it was seized or to any other person entitled to its lawful
    possession on any conditions, if any, relating to sale or
    advertising that are imposed by order of the court and that, in the
    opinion of the court, are necessary to avoid the commission of any
    further offence under this Act.

### For Measurement of Length {#for-measurement-of-length .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} The national standard for length in Canada realized,
  through the use of any device, in compliance with the definition
  [[[metre]{.otherLang lang="fr"}]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} adopted by the
  [26^e^ Conférence générale des poids et mesures]{.otherLang lang="fr"}
  as reported in the [Comptes rendus de la 26^e^ Conférence générale des
  poids et mesures]{.otherLang lang="fr"} published by the [Bureau
  international des poids et mesures]{.otherLang lang="fr"}, and
  determined by the National Research Council of Canada under
  subparagraph 5(1)(c)(iv) of the [National Research Council
  Act](/eng/acts/N-15).
