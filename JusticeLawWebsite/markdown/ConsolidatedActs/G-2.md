## Garnishment, Attachment and Pension Diversion Act {#garnishment-attachment-and-pension-diversion-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. G-2
:::

An Act to provide for the garnishment or attachment of Her Majesty in
right of Canada, the Senate, House of Commons, Library of Parliament,
Office of the Senate Ethics Officer, Office of the Conflict of Interest
and Ethics Commissioner, Parliamentary Protective Service and Office of
the Parliamentary Budget Officer and for the diversion of pension
benefits payable by Her Majesty in right of Canada under certain
enactments
::::

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Garnishment and Attachment Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247363 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-247364 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

[[debtor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[debtor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a garnishee summons,
    means the person whose salary or remuneration is sought to be
    garnisheed; ([débiteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[garnishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[garnishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes attachment; ([Version
    anglaise seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[Her Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Her Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Her Majesty in right of
    Canada; ([Sa Majesté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister or Ministers
    designated under section 48; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement relating to
    maintenance, alimony or support, an order, a judgment or a decision
    --- whether interim or final --- that is enforceable in a
    province; ([ordonnance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[parliamentary entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[parliamentary entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Senate;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the House of Commons;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Library of Parliament;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the office of the Senate Ethics Officer;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the office of the Conflict of Interest and Ethics
      Commissioner;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the Parliamentary Protective Service; or

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the office of the Parliamentary Budget
      Officer; ([entité parlementaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[provincial enforcement service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial enforcement service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
    meaning as in section 2 of the [Family Orders and Agreements
    Enforcement Assistance Act](/eng/acts/F-1.4); ([autorité
    provinciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[provincial garnishment law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial garnishment law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the law of
    general application of a province relating to garnishment that is in
    force at the time in question. ([droit provincial en matière de
    saisie-arrêt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [DIVISION I]{.HLabel2}[Departments and Certain Crown Corporations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-247379 .Subheading}

:   [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by

    [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Financial

[[garnishee summons]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    order and a document in respect of maintenance, alimony or support
    issued by a provincial enforcement service; ([bref de
    saisie-arrêt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pay period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pay period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of any
    particular person, the period beginning on the day after the day
    that is the regular pay-day of the person and ending on the day that
    is their next regular pay-day; ([période de paye]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by regulations
    made under this Division; ([prescrit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou
    [réglementaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[salary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[salary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a judge or associate judge to whom
      the [Judges Act](/eng/acts/J-1) applies, the salary payable under
      that Act, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other person,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the basic pay payable to that person for the
        performance of the regular duties of a position or office, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any amount payable as allowances, special
        remuneration, payment for overtime or other compensation or as a
        gratuity,

    excluding any amount deemed to be or to have been excluded from that
    person's salary pursuant to regulations made under paragraph
    12(b). ([traitement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service binds Her Majesty

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this Division, service on Her Majesty of a garnishee summons,
  together with a copy of the order against a debtor and an application
  in the prescribed form, binds Her Majesty 15 days after the day on
  which those documents are served.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When service is effective

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A garnishee summons served on Her Majesty is of no
  effect unless it is served on Her Majesty in the first 45 days
  following the first day on which it could have been validly served on
  Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Her Majesty no longer bound

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The garnishee summons ceases to bind Her Majesty
  after the periods and in the circumstances specified in the
  regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Moneys bound by service of garnishee
summons

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
garnishment proceedings permitted by this Division, service of a
garnishee summons binds Her Majesty in respect of the following money to
be paid by Her Majesty to the debtor named in the garnishee summons:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a salary,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the salary to be paid, no later than the last day
    of the second pay period following the pay period in which Her
    Majesty is bound by the garnishee summons, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the garnishee summons has continuing effect
    under the law of the province, the salary to be paid on the last day
    of each subsequent pay period; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of remuneration described in paragraph
  5(b),

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the remuneration payable, in respect of the
    department or Crown corporation named in the application referred to
    in section 6, on the fifteenth day following the day on which Her
    Majesty is bound by the garnishee summons, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} either

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} any remuneration becoming payable in respect of
      that department or Crown corporation in the thirty days following
      the fifteenth day after the day on which Her Majesty is bound by
      the garnishee summons that is owing on that fifteenth day or that
      becomes owing in the fourteen days following that fifteenth day,
      or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} where the garnishee summons has continuing effect
      under the law of the province, any remuneration becoming payable
      in respect of that department or Crown corporation subsequent to
      the fifteenth day after the day on which Her Majesty is bound by
      the garnishee summons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time period for Her Majesty's response to

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Her Majesty has the

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Methods of response

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  addition to any method of responding to a garnishee summons permitted
  by provincial garnishment law, Her Majesty may respond to a garnishee
  summons by any prescribed method.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where response is by registered mail

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where Her Majesty responds to a garnishee summons by
  registered mail, the receipt issued in accordance with regulations
  relating to registered mail made under the [Canada Post Corporation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-10) shall be received in evidence and is, unless the
  contrary is shown, proof that Her Majesty has responded to the
  garnishee summons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of payment into court

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A payment into court by Her Majesty is a good and
  sufficient discharge of Her Majesty's liability, to the extent of the
  payment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of payment to provincial
  enforcement service

  [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} If provincial garnishment law permits a payment to
  a provincial enforcement service of the province, a payment to the
  provincial enforcement service by Her Majesty is a good and sufficient
  discharge of Her Majesty's liability, to the extent of the payment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, in honouring a garnishee summons, Her Majesty,
  through error, pays to a debtor by way of salary or remuneration an
  amount in excess of the amount that Her Majesty should have paid to
  that debtor, the excess is a debt due to Her Majesty by that debtor
  and may be recovered from the debtor by way of deduction from, or
  set-off or compensation against, future moneys payable to the debtor
  as salary or remuneration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery from party who instituted
  proceedings

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If moneys are paid by Her Majesty to or for the
  benefit of a party who instituted garnishment proceedings permitted by
  this Division in excess of the amount that should be paid to or for
  the benefit of that party, the excess is a debt due to Her Majesty by
  that party and may be recovered from the party by way of deduction
  from, or set-off or compensation against, moneys payable to or for the
  benefit of that party under this Division.

### [Conditions for Diversion of Pension Benefits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-247611 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for diversion

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this Part and the
  regulations, a person named in a financial support order may make an
  application to the Minister for diversion of a pension benefit payable
  to a recipient if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the order is valid and subsisting; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person against whom the order has been made is
    a recipient.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application may be made, in accordance with the
  regulations, on behalf of a person by any other person or by a
  provincial enforcement service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of application

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} The application must contain the prescribed
  information and be accompanied by the prescribed documents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arrears of maintenance, alimony or
  support

  [(2.2)]{.lawlabel} A provincial enforcement service may, in respect of
  an application referred to in subsection (1), submit a document to the
  Minister that sets out a recipient's arrears of maintenance, alimony
  or support and any prescribed information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Diversion of pension benefits

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, not later than the first day of
  the fourth month after the month in which the Minister receives the
  duly completed application, divert an amount or amounts computed in
  accordance with sections 36 to 40 and the regulations from any net
  pension benefit that is payable to the recipient named in the
  application, and any amount so diverted shall be paid, subject to
  subsection (4), to the applicant, to any other person designated for
  that purpose in the financial support order or to a provincial
  enforcement service if the law of the province permits payment to it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where applicant under 18 years of age

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where, pursuant to this section, a diverted amount is
  to be paid to an applicant who is under eighteen years of age, payment
  thereof shall instead be made to the person having the custody and
  control of that applicant or, where there is no person having custody
  and control of that applicant, to such person as the Minister may
  direct.

### [Amount of Diversion]{.HTitleText2} {#h-247667 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules governing amount of diversion

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** Where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a financial support order provides only for periodic
  payments and the recipient's pension benefit consists only of periodic
  payments, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a financial support order provides only for a lump
  sum payment and the recipient's pension benefit consists only of a
  lump sum payment,

the amount to be diverted from the recipient's net pension benefit shall
be governed by the following rules:

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the recipient is domiciled in Canada and
  habitually resident in a province in which there is in force a law of
  general application permitting garnishment, attachment or diversion of
  pensions for the enforcement of financial support orders, the amount
  to be diverted shall be determined in accordance with the law of that
  province in force at the time of the diversion payment,

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the recipient is domiciled in Canada and
  habitually resident elsewhere than in a province described in
  paragraph (c), the amount to be diverted shall be the amount required
  to satisfy the financial support order, up to a maximum of 50% of the
  recipient's net pension benefit,

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the recipient is domiciled outside Canada and the
  applicant is habitually resident in a province described in paragraph
  (c), the amount to be diverted shall be determined in accordance with
  the law of that province in force at the time of the diversion
  payment,

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the recipient is domiciled outside Canada and the
  applicant is habitually resident in a province other than one
  described in paragraph (c), the amount to be diverted shall be the
  amount required to satisfy the financial support order, up to a
  maximum of 50% of the recipient's net pension benefit, or

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the recipient and the applicant are domiciled
  outside Canada and are habitually resident outside Canada, the amount
  to be diverted shall be the amount required to satisfy the financial
  support order, up to a maximum of 50% of the recipient's net pension
  benefit.
