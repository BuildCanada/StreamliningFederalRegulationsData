An Act respecting Holidays
::::

:::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-256758 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Holidays Act](/eng/acts/H-5).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. H-7, s. 1
:::

## [Canada Day]{.HTitleText1} {#h-256763 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canada Day

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} July 1,
  not being a Sunday, is a legal holiday and shall be kept and observed
  as such throughout Canada under the name of \"*Canada Day*\".

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When July 1 is a Sunday

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When July 1 is a Sunday, July 2 is a legal holiday
  and shall be kept and observed as such throughout Canada under the
  name of \"*Canada Day*\".

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. H-7, s. 2
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 124, s. 1
:::

## [Remembrance Day]{.HTitleText1} {#h-256771 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remembrance Day

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** November 11, being the day
in the year 1918 on which the Great War was triumphantly concluded by an
armistice, is a legal holiday and shall be kept and observed as such
throughout Canada under the name of "*Remembrance Day*".

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. H-5, s. 3
- 2018, c. 3, s. 1
:::

## [Victoria Day]{.HTitleText1} {#h-256776 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Victoria Day

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The first Monday
immediately preceding May 25 is a legal holiday and shall be kept and
observed as such throughout Canada under the name of \"*Victoria Day*\".

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. H-7, s. 4
