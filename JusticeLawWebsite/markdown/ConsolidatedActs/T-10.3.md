## The Financial Advisors Association of Canada Act {#the-financial-advisors-association-of-canada-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2003, c. 29
:::

Assented to 2003-11-07

An Act to amalgamate the Canadian Association of Insurance and Financial
Advisors and The Canadian Association of Financial Planners under the
name The Financial Advisors Association of Canada
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the Canadian Association of Insurance and Financial Advisors,
having its principal place of business in the City of Toronto, was
incorporated by an Act of the Parliament of Canada, being chapter 104 of
the Statutes of Canada, 1924, under the name of "The Life Underwriters'
Association of Canada";

AND WHEREAS, by Ministerial order issued on December 1, 1997 under
section 216 of the [Canada Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8), the name
of the Association was changed to the "Canadian Association of Insurance
and Financial Advisors";

AND WHEREAS The Canadian Association of Financial Planners, having its
principal place of business in the City of Toronto, was incorporated by
letters patent issued under Part II of the [Canada Corporations
Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8) on December 24, 1982, and the French form of its
name was changed by supplementary letters patent issued on June 20,
1986;

AND WHEREAS the two corporations have by their petition represented that
they wish to amalgamate and continue as one corporation under the name
of "The Financial Advisors Association of Canada";

AND WHEREAS there is no existing law of general application that would
enable the two corporations to amalgamate and continue as one
corporation;

AND WHEREAS the corporations have by their petition prayed that it be
enacted as hereinafter set forth and it is expedient to grant the prayer
of the petition;
:::

::: Enacts
[1924, c. 104; 1956-57, c. 46]{.HistoricalNote}

NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and the House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short Title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
[The Financial Advisors Association of Canada Act](/eng/acts/T-10.3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[amalgamating corporations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[amalgamating corporations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    corporations mentioned in subsection 3(1). ([associations
    fusionnantes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[by-laws]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[by-laws]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the by-laws of the
    Corporation. ([règlements administratifs]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amalgamated
    corporation named in subsection 3(2) as "The Financial Advisors
    Association of Canada". ([Association]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amalgamation

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Canadian Association of Insurance and Financial Advisors and The
  Canadian Association of Financial Planners are amalgamated and
  continued as one corporation without share capital.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Name

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The name of the amalgamated corporation is "The
  Financial Advisors Association of Canada".

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charter

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} This Act constitutes the charter of the Corporation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of amalgamation

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Without limiting the
generality of any other provision of this Act, upon amalgamation

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the property of each of the amalgamating corporations
  becomes the property of the Corporation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Corporation becomes liable for the obligations of
  each of the amalgamating corporations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a cause of action or claim against, or liability of,
  either of the amalgamating corporations that exists immediately prior
  to the amalgamation becomes a cause of action or claim against, or
  liability of, the Corporation;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a civil, criminal or administrative action or
  proceeding that has been taken or could have been taken by or against
  either of the amalgamating corporations immediately prior to the
  amalgamation may be continued or taken by or against the Corporation
  until the time limit that would have applied if the amalgamation had
  not taken place;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a conviction against, or a ruling, order or judgment
  in favour of or against, either of the amalgamating corporations may
  be enforced by or against the Corporation; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the claims, rights and privileges of the amalgamating
  corporations become the claims, rights and privileges of the
  Corporation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  objects of the Corporation are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to protect by all lawful means the interests of
    consumers by promoting the professionalism of its members;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to promote excellence among its members in the
    efficient practice of life insurance and personal financial planning
    by establishing best practices, promoting standards of practice, and
    encouraging basic and continuing education; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to improve public awareness and understanding of
    personal financial planning.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this Act, the Corporation has the capacity
  and the rights, powers and privileges of a natural person to carry out
  its objects.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} To carry out its objects, the Corporation has the
  power

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to distribute and sell journals, books and other
    literature relating to life insurance and personal financial
    planning;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to hold examinations on the principles and
    practices of life insurance and personal financial planning or on
    general educational attainments;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to grant certificates of proficiency to its
    members;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to authorize the use, by such of its members as it
    may designate, of the title and description "Chartered Life
    Underwriter of Canada";

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} to represent its members on matters of common
    interest before government and regulatory bodies and any other
    organizations or persons;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} to promote and encourage ethical behaviour by its
    members;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} to devote the funds of the Corporation to
    fulfilling its objects and promoting the welfare of its members in
    such manner as the Corporation may decide;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} to provide overall policy direction to the chapters
    and affiliated bodies of the Corporation;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to coordinate relations among the chapters and
    affiliated bodies of the Corporation;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} to provide such benefits to its members as are
    deemed by the board of directors to be in the best interests of the
    members;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} to award and administer professional designations;
    and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} to do all such other things as are incidental and
    ancillary to the furtherance of its objects or the exercise of its
    other powers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-profit corporation

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation is a non-profit corporation, carrying on its activities
  without pecuniary gain to its members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No personal benefit from property

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No income or other property, real or personal, of the
  Corporation is payable to, or otherwise available for, the personal
  benefit of any member of the Corporation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The head
  office of the Corporation shall be at such place in Canada as is
  determined by the Corporation in accordance with the by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial head office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless changed in accordance with the by-laws, the
  head office shall be in the City of Toronto.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation may make by-laws respecting the administration of its
  affairs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First by-laws

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The first by-laws shall be the by-laws of the
  Canadian Association of Insurance and Financial Advisors that were in
  force immediately before the coming into force of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  affairs of the Corporation shall be managed by a board of directors
  chosen in accordance with the by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First directors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The first directors of the Corporation shall be the
  following persons, who shall continue as directors until the first
  annual meeting of the Corporation or such earlier time as they resign
  or otherwise cease to act as directors in accordance with this Act and
  the by-laws: Ryan Beebe; James Cripps; Brian Davis; Robert
  Fleischacker; Cheryl Bauer Hyde; David MacFadyen; Brian Mallard; Gary
  McLeod; Lee Raine; Randall Reynolds; Kurt Rosentreter; and Timothy
  Squire.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  officers of the Corporation shall be chosen in accordance with the
  by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers of the board and corporate
  officers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The by-laws may provide for officers of the board,
  all of whom shall be directors of the Corporation, and corporate
  officers, who need not be directors of the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First officers of the board

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The first officers of the board of the Corporation
  shall be the following persons, who shall continue to act as the
  officers of the board of the Corporation until the first annual
  meeting of the Corporation or such earlier time as the directors of
  the Corporation appoint or elect their successors in accordance with
  this Act and the by-laws: Brian Mallard as Chair; Brian Davis as Vice
  Chair; Randall Reynolds as Treasurer; and James Cripps as Secretary.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Membership

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  persons who immediately before this Act comes into force were members
  of either amalgamating corporation are members of the Corporation
  until their membership terminates in accordance with the by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}One membership

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who is a member of both amalgamating
  corporations may accede to only one membership in the Corporation
  under subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No share capital

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation shall not
issue any share capital.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dissolution and winding-up

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Upon the dissolution and
winding-up of the Corporation, any assets remaining after payment of the
liabilities of the Corporation shall be distributed to one or more
organizations or corporations established under the laws of Canada or a
province for purposes other than profit and carrying on activities that
are substantially similar to those carried on by the Corporation, as may
be determined by the members of the Corporation at a meeting duly called
for that purpose.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** \[Repeal\]

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** This Act shall come into
force or be deemed to have come into force on the 12th day of June,
2003.
