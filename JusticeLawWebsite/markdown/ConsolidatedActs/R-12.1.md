## Royal Style and Titles Act, 2023 {#royal-style-and-titles-act-2023 .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2023, c. 26, s. 510
:::

Assented to 2023-06-22

An Act respecting the Royal Style and Titles, 2023

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 510 of chapter 26 of the Statutes of Canada, 2023,
in force on assent June 22, 2023.\]
:::
:::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas it is desirable that the Parliament of Canada should assent to
the issue of a Royal Proclamation establishing the Royal Style and
Titles for Canada;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Royal Style and Titles Act, 2023](/eng/acts/R-12.1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assent to Royal Style and Titles

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Parliament of Canada
assents to the issue by His Majesty of His Royal Proclamation under the
Great Seal of Canada establishing for Canada the following Royal Style
and Titles:

Charles the Third, by the Grace of God King of Canada and His other
Realms and Territories, Head of the Commonwealth.
