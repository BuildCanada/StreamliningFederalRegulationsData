## Farm Debt Mediation Act {#farm-debt-mediation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1997, c. 21
:::

Assented to 1997-04-25

An Act to provide for mediation between insolvent farmers and their
creditors, to amend the Agriculture and Agri-Food Administrative
Monetary Penalties Act and to repeal the Farm Debt Review Act
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232639 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Farm Debt Mediation Act](/eng/acts/F-2.27).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232642 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Act.

[[farmer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[farmer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person, cooperative,
    partnership or other association of persons that is engaged in
    farming for commercial purposes and that meets any prescribed
    criteria. ([agriculteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[farming]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[farming]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the production of field-grown crops, cultivated
      and uncultivated, and horticultural crops;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the raising of livestock, poultry and fur-bearing
      animals;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the production of eggs, milk, honey, maple syrup,
      tobacco, fibre, wood from woodlots and fodder crops; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the production or raising of any other prescribed
      thing or animal. ([exploitation d'une entreprise
      agricole]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Agriculture
    and Agri-Food. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by regulation.

[[secured creditor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[secured creditor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any creditor holding a mortgage, hypothec,
      pledge, charge, lien, privilege, priority claim or other security
      interest on or against the property of a farmer or any part
      thereof as security for a debt due or accruing due from the
      farmer;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person, cooperative, partnership or other
      association of persons

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} with which a farmer has entered into an
        agreement for sale, a lease with an option to purchase or a
        conditional sales contract relating to any property used or
        possessed by the farmer, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to which such an agreement or contract has
        been assigned; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any bank, or authorized foreign bank within the
      meaning of section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) to which
      security on the property of a farmer or any part of the property
      has been given under section 427 of that Act or under section 427
      as incorporated by section 555 of that Act, as the case may
      be. ([créancier garanti]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232661 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Administrators]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232664 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), administrators shall be appointed for the purposes
  of this Act in accordance with the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, in accordance with the regulations,
  if any, and on such terms and conditions as the Minister may specify,
  designate any person, other than an employee within the meaning of the
  [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), as an
  administrator for the purposes of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, the Minister may enter
  into an agreement with any individual or body in relation to the
  remuneration and travel and living expenses of administrators
  designated under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mediators, experts

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An administrator may enter into agreements

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the services of mediators, subject to the
    regulations, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the services of experts

  relating to applications made under section 5, and such agreements may
  include provision for remuneration and travel and living expenses.

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232677 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to administrator

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 6, a farmer may apply to an administrator for either

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a stay of proceedings against the farmer by all the
    farmer's creditors, a review of the farmer's financial affairs, and
    mediation between the farmer and all the farmer's creditors for the
    purpose of assisting them to reach a mutually acceptable
    arrangement; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a review of the farmer's financial affairs, and
    mediation between the farmer and all the farmer's secured creditors
    for the purpose of assisting them to reach a mutually acceptable
    arrangement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Names of creditors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application under subsection (1) must include the
  names and addresses of all the farmer's creditors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Farmer must be insolvent

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Only farmers

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} who are for any reason unable to meet their
  obligations as they generally become due,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} who have ceased paying their current obligations in
  the ordinary course of business as they generally become due, or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of whose property is not, at a fair
  valuation, sufficient, or if disposed of at a fairly conducted sale
  under legal process would not be sufficient, to enable payment of all
  their obligations, due and accruing due

are eligible to apply under section 5.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrator's duties on receiving
application

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of a duly completed application under section 5, the
  administrator shall forthwith

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} give notice of the application to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} each creditor listed in the application, in the
      case of an application made under paragraph 5(1)(a),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} each secured creditor listed in the application,
      in the case of an application made under paragraph 5(1)(b), and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Minister, if the Minister is the guarantor
      of a farmer's debt that is owed to a creditor or secured creditor
      listed in the application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under paragraph
    5(1)(a), issue a stay of proceedings for a period of 30 days,
    beginning at the time when the stay is issued, against the farmer by
    all of the farmer's creditors and give notice of the stay to each
    creditor listed in the application; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} determine whether the farmer is eligible under this
    Act to make that application, which determination may be based on,
    among other things, a preliminary review of the farmer's financial
    affairs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain decisions final

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an application under paragraph
  5(1)(b), a determination by the administrator under paragraph (1)(c)
  is final and is not subject to appeal.

## [Financial Review]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232709 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial review

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the administrator determines that the farmer is eligible to make the
  application, the administrator shall as soon as possible undertake, or
  cause an expert referred to in subsection 4(4) to undertake, a
  detailed review of the farmer's financial affairs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nature of review

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The review mentioned in subsection (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} must include the preparation of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an inventory of all the assets of the farmer, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} financial statements of the farmer's farming
      operation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may, in the case of an application made under
    paragraph 5(1)(b), include a recommendation that one or more
    creditors who are not secured creditors, and the Minister, if the
    Minister is the guarantor of a farmer's debt that is owed to one of
    those creditors, participate in the mediation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} may include the preparation of recovery plans for
    the purpose of reaching financial arrangements with creditors and
    the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preparation of recovery plans

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a farmer requests the administrator that the
  recovery plans referred to in paragraph (2)(c) be prepared by a person
  of the farmer's choice, the administrator may, in accordance with the
  regulations, if any, enter into an agreement for that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The results of a review under this section must take
  the form of a report prepared by or on behalf of the administrator.

- 1997, c. 21, s. 9
- 2015, c. 2, s. 143

## [Mediation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232726 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of mediator

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Forthwith after the report mentioned
  in subsection 9(4) has been prepared, the administrator shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with the regulations, appoint as a
    mediator any person who is unbiased and free from any conflict of
    interest relative to the application in question;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inform

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under
      paragraph 5(1)(a), the farmer, all of the creditors listed in the
      application and the Minister, if the Minister was given notice of
      the application by the administrator under subparagraph
      7(1)(a)(iii), of the mediator's appointment, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under
      paragraph 5(1)(b), the farmer, all of the secured creditors listed
      in the application, all of the creditors mentioned in a
      recommendation under paragraph 9(2)(b) and the Minister, if the
      Minister was given notice of the application by the administrator
      under subparagraph 7(1)(a)(iii) or is mentioned in the
      recommendation, of the mediator's appointment; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide a copy of the report mentioned in
    subsection 9(4) to the mediator and to the persons and entities that
    will be participating in the mediation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of mediator

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In accordance with the regulations, the mediator
  shall examine the report mentioned in subsection 9(4) and meet with
  the persons and entities referred to in subparagraph (1)(b)(i) or (ii)
  for the purpose of assisting them to reach a mutually acceptable
  arrangement, but shall not provide advice to the farmer or a creditor.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of mediation

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  case of an application under paragraph 5(1)(a), the mediation
  terminates

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when a termination of the stay of proceedings
    pursuant to subsection 14(2) takes effect pursuant to subsection
    14(4); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on a termination of the stay of proceedings by
    virtue of subsection 14(5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of mediation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an application under paragraph
  5(1)(b),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the administrator is of the opinion, based on
    information received from the mediator or from any other source,
    that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} either the farmer or the majority of the
      creditors referred to in subparagraph 10(1)(b)(ii)

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} refuse to participate in the mediation, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} refuse to continue to participate in good faith
        in the mediation, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the mediation will not result in an arrangement
      between the farmer and the majority of the creditors referred to
      in subparagraph 10(1)(b)(ii),

    the administrator may direct that the mediation be terminated and,
    where the administrator so directs, the mediation terminates; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the mediation terminates on the signing of an
    arrangement under section 19.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of termination

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the mediation terminates pursuant to
  subsection (1) or (2), the administrator shall so inform the farmer
  and all the creditors who were eligible to participate in the
  mediation.

## [Stay of Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232755 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of stay of proceedings

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding any other
law, during any period in which a stay of proceedings is in effect, no
creditor of the farmer

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall enforce any remedy against the property of the
  farmer; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall commence or continue any proceedings or any
  action, execution or other proceedings, judicial or extra-judicial,
  for the recovery of a debt, the realization of any security or the
  taking of any property of the farmer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of stay of proceedings

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the administrator considers an extension of the thirty day period
  referred to in paragraph 7(1)(b) to be essential to the formulation of
  an arrangement between a farmer and the farmer's creditors, the
  administrator may, subject to the regulations, extend that period for
  a maximum of three further periods of thirty days each.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interim extension of stay

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is an appeal under section 15 from a decision
    of the administrator not to extend a stay of proceedings, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the stay of proceedings expires before the appeal
    is decided,

  the administrator shall, on that expiration, extend the stay of
  proceedings until the appeal is decided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to creditors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The administrator shall give notice of any extension
  granted under subsection (1) or (2) to the farmer and to each creditor
  listed in the farmer's application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where appeal successful

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where, pursuant to an Appeal referred to in paragraph
  (2)(a), the Appeal Board reverses the administrator's decision, the
  resulting thirty day extension of the stay of proceedings starts at
  the expiration of the original stay of proceedings, or at the
  expiration of the previous thirty day extension thereof, as the case
  may be.

- 1997, c. 21, s. 13
- 2015, c. 2, s. 145

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligatory termination of stay of
proceedings

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  administrator determines, pursuant to paragraph 7(1)(c), that the
  farmer is not eligible to make the application, the administrator
  shall direct that the stay of proceedings be terminated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discretionary termination of stay of
  proceedings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the administrator is of the opinion, based on
  information received from the mediator or from any other source, that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} either the farmer or the majority of the creditors
    listed in the application

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} refuse to participate in the mediation, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} refuse to continue to participate in good faith
      in the mediation,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the mediation will not result in an arrangement
    between the farmer and the majority of the creditors listed in the
    application,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the farmer has contravened any directive issued to
    the farmer by the administrator pursuant to subsection 17(1), or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the farmer has, by any act or omission, jeopardized
    his or her assets or obstructed the guardian in the performance of
    the guardian's duties under subsection 17(2),

  the administrator may direct that the stay of proceedings be
  terminated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of termination

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the administrator directs that a stay of
  proceedings be terminated under subsection (1) or (2), the
  administrator shall inform the farmer, all of the creditors listed in
  the application and the Minister, if the Minister was given notice of
  the application by the administrator under subparagraph 7(1)(a)(iii).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When termination takes effect

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the administrator directs, pursuant to
  subsection (1) or (2), that a stay of proceedings be terminated, the
  termination takes effect

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the expiration of the time prescribed for making
    an appeal under section 15; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where an appeal is made under section 15, if and
    when the appeal is dismissed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Automatic termination of stay of
  proceedings

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A stay of proceedings terminates on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the signing of an arrangement under section 19; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the farmer's making an assignment under the
    [Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3).

## [Appeal Boards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232798 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal Boards

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, in accordance with the regulations, constitute one or
  more Appeal Boards and designate the members thereof, and may enter
  into agreements for the services of the members, which agreements may
  include provision for remuneration and travel and living expenses.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeals

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A farmer or a creditor may, in accordance with the
  regulations, appeal to an Appeal Board a decision of an administrator
  relating to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the eligibility of a farmer to make the application
    under paragraph 5(1)(a); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the extension or termination of a stay of
    proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Appeal Board shall deal with an appeal in
  accordance with the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stay not affected

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The making of an appeal does not affect a stay of
  proceedings that is in effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board's decision final

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A decision of an Appeal Board is final and is not
  subject to appeal.

## [Guardian of Farmer's Assets]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232812 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrator to appoint guardian

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the administrator issues a stay of proceedings under paragraph
  7(1)(b), the administrator shall forthwith appoint one of the
  following persons as guardian of the farmer's assets:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the farmer, where the farmer is qualified to be the
    guardian; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any other qualified person nominated by any
      secured creditor or secured creditors listed in the application,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any other qualified person chosen by the
      administrator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informing farmer and creditors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The administrator shall forthwith inform the farmer,
  and all the creditors listed in the application, of the appointment of
  the guardian.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses of guardian

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the administrator appoints a person referred to
  in subparagraph (1)(b)(i) as guardian, the expenses of the guardian
  shall be paid by the secured creditor or secured creditors who
  nominated that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses of guardian

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the administrator appoints a person referred to
  in subparagraph (1)(b)(ii) as guardian, the expenses of the guardian
  shall be paid by the administrator.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of guardian

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  administrator may issue directives to the guardian, and the guardian
  shall comply with any such directives.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of guardian

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The guardian shall, in addition to the obligation
  under subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prepare an inventory of all the assets of the
    farmer;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} verify periodically the presence and condition of
    those assets; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} advise the administrator of any act or omission
    that would jeopardize those assets.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of guardianship

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The appointment of a
guardian under section 16 terminates on the expiration or termination of
the stay of proceedings.

## [Arrangements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232836 .Part}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** If a farmer enters into
an arrangement with a creditor, or with the Minister, as a result of the
mediation, the administrator shall see to its signing by the parties.

## [New Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232841 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New applications under paragraph 5(1)(a)

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding any other provision
  of this Act, where a farmer makes an application under paragraph
  5(1)(a), that farmer, or any person who is related to that farmer
  within the meaning of the regulations, is not entitled to make a new
  application under that paragraph in respect of substantially the same
  farming operation for a period of two years after

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the first application was made,
    where the farmer did not enter into an arrangement with the
    creditors during the stay of proceedings, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the arrangement was signed, where
    the farmer entered into an arrangement with the creditors,

  unless, before making the new application, the person seeking to apply
  obtains the written consent of the administrator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New applications under paragraph
  5(1)(b)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding any other provision of this Act,
  where a farmer makes an application under paragraph 5(1)(b), that
  farmer, or any person who is related to that farmer within the meaning
  of the regulations, is not entitled to make a new application under
  that paragraph in respect of substantially the same farming operation
  for a period of two years after

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the first application was made,
    where the farmer did not enter into an arrangement with the
    creditors within the period prescribed for the mediation, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the arrangement was signed, where
    the farmer entered into an arrangement with the creditors,

  unless, before making the new application, the person seeking to apply
  obtains the written consent of the administrator.

## [Notice by Secured Creditors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232853 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice by secured creditors

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  secured creditor who intends to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enforce any remedy against the property of a
    farmer, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} commence any proceedings or any action, execution
    or other proceedings, judicial or extra-judicial, for the recovery
    of a debt, the realization of any security or the taking of any
    property of a farmer

  shall give the farmer written notice of the creditor's intention to do
  so, and in the notice shall advise the farmer of the right to make an
  application under section 5.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must be given to the farmer and to an
  administrator, in the form established by the Minister and in
  accordance with the regulations, at least 15 business days before the
  doing of any act described in paragraph (1)(a) or (b).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1997, c. 21, s. 21
- 2015, c. 2, s. 148
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232864 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention by creditor

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), any act
  done by a creditor in contravention of section 12 or 21 is null and
  void, and a farmer affected by such an act may seek appropriate
  remedies against the creditor in a court of competent jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Innocent parties protected

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} does not affect the title to property of a person
    who purchased the property in good faith from the creditor and who
    was not then related to the creditor within the meaning of the
    regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not confer on the farmer any remedy against a
    person described in paragraph (a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disputes

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act
prevents any party to an arrangement made under this Act from taking a
dispute arising therefrom to a court of competent jurisdiction for
disposition.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication of information

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as authorized by subsection (2), no person shall knowingly communicate
  or knowingly allow to be communicated to any person any information
  that is obtained under this Act from a farmer, from a farmer's
  creditor or from the Minister or knowingly allow any person to inspect
  or have access to that information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person engaged in the administration of this Act,
  or a mediator or expert referred to in subsection 4(4), may
  communicate or allow to be communicated, or allow inspection of or
  access to, the information referred to in subsection (1) to or by any
  other person engaged in the administration of this Act or any person
  otherwise legally entitled to the information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection of witness

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person engaged in the administration of this Act,
  or a mediator or expert referred to in subsection 4(4), is not
  compellable to answer questions concerning the information, or to
  produce records or other documents containing the information, as
  evidence in any proceedings not directly concerned with the
  enforcement or interpretation of this Act or the regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1997, c. 21, s. 24
- 2015, c. 2, s. 149

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal liability

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** A person engaged in the
administration of this Act, or a mediator or expert referred to in
subsection 4(4), is not personally liable for anything done or omitted
to be done in good faith in the performance of their duties under this
Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the designation of persons as
    administrators pursuant to subsection 4(2);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for permitting a farmer or creditor to request the
    administrator to replace the mediator or guardian with a different
    mediator or guardian;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the entering into of agreements under
    subsection 9(3);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the qualifications and appointment of
    mediators, and respecting the manner in which and the period within
    which mediators must perform their duties under subsection 10(2);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} respecting the time by which an administrator must
    decide whether or not to extend a stay of proceedings pursuant to
    subsection 13(1);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} respecting the number and constitution of Appeal
    Boards, the designation of the members thereof, and the manner in
    which and the period within which appeals under subsection 15(2)
    shall be made and dealt with;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} respecting the meaning of "related" for the
    purposes of section 20 and for the purposes of subsection 22(2);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that by this Act is to be
    prescribed; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} generally for carrying out the purposes and
    provisions of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forms and their content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may establish forms and other documents
  for carrying out the purposes and provisions of this Act and may
  determine the information to be included in such documents, and,
  without limiting the generality of the foregoing, may determine the
  manner of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} amending an application; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} providing information or notices required by this
    Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Any person who
contravenes a provision of this Act or the regulations is guilty of an
offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding fifty
thousand dollars or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding six months,
or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of Act

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  five years after the coming into force of this subsection, the
  Minister shall undertake a review of the operation of this Act and may
  for that purpose consult with representatives of any organizations
  that the Minister considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review by Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In conducting the review under subsection (1), the
  Minister shall review the operation of any program or service that is
  created after this section comes into force for the purpose of
  undertaking a detailed review of the financial affairs of a farmer in
  financial difficulty, at the farmer's request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Parliament

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} As soon as possible after completing the review
  referred to in subsection (1), the Minister shall cause a report of
  the results of the review to be laid before each House of Parliament.

- 1997, c. 21, s. 28
- 2015, c. 2, s. 152

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232920 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** In sections 33 to 35,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[old Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Farm Debt
  Review Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [new Act]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} means the [Farm
  Debt Mediation Act](/eng/acts/F-2.27).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications made under Farm Debt Review
Act

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 44 of the [Interpretation Act](/eng/acts/I-21),

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application made under section 16 of the old Act
  shall be dealt with as if it had been made under paragraph 5(1)(b) of
  the new Act, regardless of whether the farmer in question would be
  eligible to apply under paragraph 5(1)(b) of the new Act, but section
  8 of the new Act does not apply unless the farmer in fact meets the
  requirement of section 6 of the new Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an application made under section 20 of the old Act
  shall be dealt with as if it had been made under paragraph 5(1)(a) of
  the new Act, regardless of whether the farmer in question would be
  eligible to apply under paragraph 5(1)(a) of the new Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Two year rule

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 20(1) of the new Act
  applies, with such modifications as the circumstances require, in
  respect of farmers whose previous application was under section 20 of
  the old Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Two year rule

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 20(2) of the new Act does not apply in
  respect of farmers whose previous application was under section 16 of
  the old Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members of Farm Debt Review Boards

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** Chairmen and other
members of the Farm Debt Review Boards who hold office under section 4
of the old Act cease to hold office on the coming into force of the new
Act.
