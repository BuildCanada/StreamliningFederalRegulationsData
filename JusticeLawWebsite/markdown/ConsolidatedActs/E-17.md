## Explosives Act {#explosives-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. E-17
:::

An Act respecting the manufacture, testing, acquisition, possession,
sale, storage, transportation, importation and exportation of explosives
and the use of fireworks
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-202134 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Explosives Act](/eng/acts/E-17).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. E-15, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-202139 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[authorized explosive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorized explosive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any explosive
    that is declared to be an authorized explosive in accordance with
    the regulations; ([explosif autorisé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Marking of Plastic Explosives for the Purpose of Detection,
    concluded in Montreal on March 1, 1991, as amended from time to
    time; ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of Natural
    Resources; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[detection agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[detection agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the substances
    set out in the Table to Part 2 of the Technical Annex to the
    Convention; ([agent de détection]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[explosive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[explosive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any thing that is made,
    manufactured or used to produce an explosion or a detonation or
    pyrotechnic effect, and includes any thing prescribed to be an
    explosive by the regulations, but does not include gases, organic
    peroxides or any thing prescribed not to be an explosive by the
    regulations; ([explosif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[factory]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[factory]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any building, structure,
    premises or land in or on which the manufacture or any part of the
    process of manufacture of an explosive is carried on, the site on
    which the building, structure or premises are situated, and all
    other buildings, structures or premises within such a
    site; ([fabrique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[illicit manufacture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[illicit manufacture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any activity that
    is prohibited under paragraph 6(1)(a) or (e); ([fabrication
    illicite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[illicit trafficking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[illicit trafficking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any importation
    into Canada, exportation from Canada or transportation in transit
    through Canada of an explosive if

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the importation or exportation is not authorized
      by the country of origin or the country of destination, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the transportation in transit of the explosive
      through any country is not authorized by that country; ([trafic
      illicite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chief Inspector of
    Explosives, an inspector of explosives and a deputy inspector of
    explosives appointed under section 13, and any other person who is
    directed by the Minister to inspect an explosive, a restricted
    component, a vehicle, a licensed factory or a magazine, or to hold
    an inquiry in connection with any accident caused by an
    explosive; ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensed factory]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensed factory]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a factory in respect
    of which a licence issued under section 7 is in force; ([fabrique
    agréée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensed magazine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensed magazine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a magazine in
    respect of which a licence issued under section 7 is in
    force; ([poudrière agréée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[magazine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[magazine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any building, storehouse,
    structure or place in which any explosive is kept or stored, but
    does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a place where an explosive is kept or stored
      exclusively for use at or in a mine or quarry in a province in
      which provision is made by the law of that province for efficient
      inspection and control of explosives stored and used at or in
      mines and quarries,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vehicle in which an authorized explosive is
      being conveyed in accordance with this Act,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the structure or place in which is kept for
      private use, and not for sale, an authorized explosive to an
      amount not exceeding that authorized by regulation,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any store or warehouse in which are stored for
      sale authorized explosives to an amount not exceeding that
      authorized by regulation, or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any place at which the blending or assembling of
      the inexplosive component parts of an authorized explosive is
      allowed under section 8; ([poudrière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[military device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[military device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned to
    that expression by the regulations; ([engin
    militaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Natural
    Resources or such other Minister as the Governor in Council may
    designate; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the owner, manager or
    person in charge; ([exploitants]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[plastic explosive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[plastic explosive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an explosive that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is formulated with one or more high explosives
      that in their pure form have a vapour pressure less than 10^-4^ Pa
      at a temperature of 25^o^C,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is formulated with a binder material, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is, when mixed, malleable or flexible at normal
      room temperature; ([explosif plastique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[restricted component]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[restricted component]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any prescribed
    component of an explosive the acquisition, possession or sale of
    which is restricted by a regulation made under paragraph
    5(a.31); ([composant d'explosif limité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the portion of international
    transboundary transportation through the territory of a country that
    is neither the country of origin nor the country of
    destination; ([transit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unmarked plastic explosive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unmarked plastic explosive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a plastic
    explosive that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} does not contain a detection agent, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the time of manufacture, does not contain the
      required minimum concentration level of a detection agent as set
      out in the Table to Part 2 of the Technical Annex to the
      Convention; ([explosif plastique non marqué]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any truck, automobile or other
    conveyance for use on land but does not include any vehicle running
    only on rails to which Part III of the [Canada Transportation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4) applies. ([véhicules]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-202176 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Act

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Except as provided by the
regulations, this Act does not apply to or in respect of any explosives
under the direction or control of the Minister of National Defence.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. E-15, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3, this
Act is binding on Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. E-15, s. 3
:::

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-202186 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations generally for carrying the purposes or provisions of
this Act into effect, and in particular, but without limiting the
generality of the foregoing, may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for classifying explosives, and for prescribing the
  composition, quality and character of explosives;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} prescribing any thing that is to be included or not
  to be included in the definition "explosive" in section 2;

- [(a.2)]{.lawlabel} exempting any explosive or class of explosives from
  the application of this Act or the regulations or any provision of
  this Act or the regulations;

- [(a.3)]{.lawlabel} restricting to any person or body or class of
  persons or bodies the acquisition, possession, use or sale of any
  explosive or class of explosives;

- [(a.31)]{.lawlabel} prescribing any component of an explosive and
  restricting to any person or body or class of persons or bodies its
  acquisition, possession or sale;

- [(a.4)]{.lawlabel} prohibiting the acquisition, possession, use or
  sale of any explosive that, in the opinion of the Minister, is
  intrinsically unsafe, and identifying that explosive by reference to
  its common name or a description of the class of explosives to which
  it belongs;

- [(a.5)]{.lawlabel} respecting the transfer and possession of unmarked
  plastic explosives intended for use under paragraph 6.1(1)(a) or (b);

- [(a.6)]{.lawlabel} respecting the transport, transfer and possession
  of unmarked plastic explosives by a person referred to in paragraph
  6.1(3)(a) or (b) during the periods provided for in those paragraphs;

- [(a.7)]{.lawlabel} prescribing procedures for the destruction or
  disposal of unmarked plastic explosives referred to in subsection
  6.1(3);

- [(a.8)]{.lawlabel} defining the expression "military device";

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the duration of licences, permits and
  certificates issued pursuant to section 7 and of permits issued
  pursuant to section 9, the terms and conditions on which licences,
  permits and certificates shall be issued and the fees to be paid
  therefor, and providing for the cancellation and suspension thereof;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} not inconsistent with any other Act of Parliament or
  regulations made under any other Act of Parliament, for regulating the
  importation, exportation, packing, handling and transportation of
  explosives;

- [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} requiring that explosives be accompanied by safety
  instructions, and providing for the content of those instructions;

- [(c.2)]{.lawlabel} requiring that explosives and their packaging be
  marked for safety purposes, and respecting those markings;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for holding inquiries into any accident caused by
  explosives;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} for the taking of samples of explosives required for
  examination and testing, and for the establishing of testing stations,
  and of the tests and other examinations to which explosives shall be
  subjected;

- [(e.1)]{.lawlabel} providing for safety standards in respect of
  research relating to explosives and for safety standards for
  large-scale testing of explosives;

- [(e.2)]{.lawlabel} prescribing fees for declaring explosives to be
  authorized explosives and for testing explosives;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the procedure to be followed to have an
  explosive declared an authorized explosive, the nature of the
  investigation to be made to determine its suitability to be declared
  authorized and the circumstances in which an explosive may be declared
  an authorized explosive;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} relating to the construction, management and
  licensing of factories and magazines;

- [(g.1)]{.lawlabel} providing for safety standards in respect of
  factories and magazines;

- [(g.2)]{.lawlabel} providing for the training in safety procedures of
  employees at factories and magazines;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} for the safety of the public and of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} employees at any factory or magazine,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} persons engaged in the handling or packing of
    explosives or any class of explosives, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} persons engaged in the use of fireworks;

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1993, c. 32, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} not inconsistent with any other Act of Parliament or
  regulations made thereunder, respecting the safety of any person
  engaged in the transportation of explosives;

- [(i.1)]{.lawlabel} respecting security standards and security measures
  relating to explosives and restricted components;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} governing the establishment, location and maintenance
  of factories and magazines and the making, manufacture and storage of
  explosives;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} for the blending of the inexplosive components of an
  authorized explosive, and specifying the conditions under which the
  blending may be done;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} limiting the amount of authorized explosives that may
  be kept in places other than licensed factories and licensed
  magazines, and prescribing the manner in and conditions on which it
  shall be handled and stored in those places;

- [(l.1)]{.lawlabel} respecting record keeping and the exchange of
  information for the purposes of tracing, identifying and preventing
  the illicit manufacture and illicit trafficking of explosives;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} respecting the acquisition, possession and sale of
  explosives and restricted components; and

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the circumstances in which explosives
  shall for the purposes of this Act be deemed to be or not to be under
  the direction or control of the Minister of National Defence.

## [Prohibited Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-202226 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manufacture, use, etc.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Except as authorized under
this Act and subject to such exemptions as may be provided by
regulation, no person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} make or manufacture any explosive, either wholly or
  in part, except in a licensed factory;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sell any authorized explosive unless that person is
  the operator of a licensed factory or licensed magazine and is
  authorized to sell explosives;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} store any explosive in a magazine that is not a
  licensed magazine;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} have in his possession any explosive; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} carry on, except in a licensed factory, any of the
  following processes, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} dividing an explosive into its components, or
    otherwise breaking up or unmaking any explosive,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} making fit for use any damaged explosive, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} remaking, altering or repairing any explosive.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. E-17, s. 6
- 2004, c. 15, s. 38(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manufacture of unmarked plastic
explosives

- **[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall manufacture unmarked plastic explosives unless the
  explosives are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} manufactured in limited quantities solely for use
    in such of the following activities as are authorized, in writing,
    by the Chief Inspector of Explosives, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} research, development or testing of new or
      modified explosives,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} training in explosives detection, or in the
      development or testing of explosives detection equipment, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} forensic science activities; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} destined to be incorporated as an integral part of
    a military device in Canada within three years after the coming into
    force of this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transport and possession of unmarked
  plastic explosives

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (3) and (4), no person shall
  transport or possess unmarked plastic explosives unless the explosives
  are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} transported or possessed in limited quantities
    solely for use as authorized in the manner referred to in paragraph
    (1)(a); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} destined to be and are incorporated as an integral
    part of a military device in Canada within three years after the
    coming into force of this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unmarked plastic explosives manufactured or imported
  into Canada before the day on which this section comes into force may,
  in accordance with the regulations, be transported or possessed by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who performs military or police functions,
    during the period that begins on the coming into force of this
    section and ends fifteen years later; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other person, during the period that begins on
    the coming into force of this section and ends three years later.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Importation or exportation of unmarked
  plastic explosives

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall import or export unmarked plastic
  explosives unless the explosives are incorporated as an integral part
  of a military device.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 35, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Illicit trafficking, etc.

**[[6.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.2 .sectionLabel}** No person shall
knowingly

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} engage in illicit trafficking; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} acquire, possess, sell, offer for sale, transport or
  deliver any illicitly trafficked explosive.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2004, c. 15, s. 39
:::

## [Licences and Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-202265 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licences and permits

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may issue

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} licences for factories and magazines;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} permits for vehicles used for the transportation of
    explosives; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} certificates for carrying out, on an occasional and
    temporary basis, activities relating to the manufacture, testing or
    storage of explosives or the use of fireworks or the training of
    persons in the use of fireworks.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may make any licence, permit or
  certificate referred to in subsection (1) subject to any term or
  condition, in addition to those prescribed by the regulations, that
  the Minister considers necessary for the protection of any person or
  property, including, without limiting the generality of the foregoing,
  compliance with security or safety standards in respect of any factory
  or magazine or any class thereof that are supplementary to but not
  inconsistent with those provided for under paragraph 5(g.1).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. E-17, s. 7
- 1993, c. 32, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inexplosive component parts

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding anything in
this Act, the Governor in Council, on the recommendation of the
Minister, accompanied by a certificate from the Chief Inspector of
Explosives approving of the nature of the components and of the final
explosive product, may allow the inexplosive component parts of an
authorized explosive to be assembled and blended at or near the place of
use.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. E-15, s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permits

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may issue permits for the importation or exportation, or the
  transportation in transit through Canada, of explosives.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No import, export or transport without
  permit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as provided by the regulations, no person
  shall, without a permit issued under this section, import or export,
  or transport in transit through Canada, any explosive.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence of financial responsibility

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may require any person who engages or
  proposes to engage in the importation or exportation, or the
  transportation in transit through Canada, of any explosive and who
  does not reside in Canada or have a chief place of business or head
  office in Canada to provide evidence of financial responsibility in
  the form of insurance, or in the form of an indemnity bond or a
  suretyship, satisfactory to the Minister, or in any other form
  satisfactory to the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. E-17, s. 9
- 1993, c. 32, s. 5
- 2001, c. 4, s. 80(E)
- 2004, c. 15, s. 40
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence, permit or certificate issued pursuant to section 7 or a permit
issued pursuant to section 9 shall be made in such manner and be
accompanied by such information and other material as are prescribed by

- 1993, c. 32, s. 6

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form

**[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1 .sectionLabel}** Licences, permits and
certificates issued pursuant to section 7 and permits issued pursuant to
section 9 shall be in the form prescribed by the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1993, c. 32, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No alterations without a permit

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of a factory, magazine or vehicle for which a licence or
  permit has been issued under section 7 shall not,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a factory or magazine, relocate or
    make any alteration or addition to the factory or magazine or
    rebuild any part thereof, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vehicle, make any alteration to
    the vehicle

  until the operator has obtained written authority therefor from the
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergency situations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), an operator referred
  to in that subsection may undertake any of the activities referred to
  therein with the approval of the Minister granted by any means where,
  in the opinion of the Minister, extraordinary circumstances exist for
  doing so or it is necessary to do so for the protection of any person
  or property.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. E-17, s. 11
- 1993, c. 32, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of use where a special danger

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may require
the operator of any factory or magazine or the operator of any vehicle
in which an explosive is being conveyed to stop using, or to use only
under and subject to conditions specified by the Minister, any building,
structure or vehicle that, from its location or from the nature of the
processes carried on therein, constitutes, in the opinion of the
Minister, a special danger.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. E-15, s. 12
- 1974-75-76, c. 60, s. 6
:::

## [Inspectors and Chemists]{.HTitleText1} {#h-202311 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  may be appointed in the manner authorized by law a Chief Inspector of
  Explosives, together with such other inspectors and chemists as are
  necessary for the carrying out of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspectors without remuneration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may appoint, in addition to
  the inspectors appointed under subsection (1), such other inspectors
  and deputy inspectors, without remuneration, as the Governor in
  Council considers necessary for the carrying out of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. E-15, s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspection

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of ensuring compliance with this Act and the regulations,
  an inspector may, subject to subsection (5), at any reasonable time,
  enter and inspect any factory, magazine, vehicle or other place in
  which the inspector believes on reasonable grounds any explosive is
  being manufactured, tested, stored, sold or transported, any
  restricted component is being stored or sold, or any fireworks are
  being or are intended to be used, and the inspector may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} open or cause to be opened any thing found in that
    place that the inspector believes, on reasonable grounds, contains
    an explosive or a substance that may be used in the manufacture of
    an explosive;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inspect any thing found in that place that the
    inspector believes, on reasonable grounds, is an explosive or any
    thing that may be used in the manufacturing, packing or marking of
    an explosive;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} take samples of any thing found in that place that
    the inspector believes, on reasonable grounds, is an explosive or a
    substance that may be used in the manufacture of an explosive;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} require any person to produce for inspection or
    copying, in whole or in part, any document that the inspector
    believes, on reasonable grounds, contains any information relevant
    to the administration of this Act or the regulations; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} make copies of or take extracts from any document
    that the inspector believes, on reasonable grounds, contains any
    information relevant to the administration of this Act or the
    regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vehicle

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of carrying out an inspection under
  subsection (1), an inspector may stop a vehicle or direct that it be
  moved to a place where the inspection may be carried out.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operation of data processing and
  copying equipment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In carrying out an inspection at any place under this
  section, an inspector may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any data processing system
    at the place to examine any data contained in or available to the
    system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reproduce or cause to be reproduced any record from
    the data in the form of a print-out or other intelligible output and
    take the print-out or other output for examination or copying; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any copying equipment at
    the place to make copies of any record or other document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate to be produced

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An inspector, other than a peace officer, shall be
  furnished with a certificate of appointment and on entering any place
  described in subsection (1) shall, if so required, produce the
  certificate to the person in charge thereof.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant required to enter
  dwelling-house

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An inspector may not enter a dwelling-house except
  with the consent of the occupant of the dwelling-house or under the
  authority of a warrant issued pursuant to subsection (6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where on [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application
  a justice is satisfied by information on oath that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conditions for entry described in
    subsection (1) exist in relation to a dwelling-house,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entry to the dwelling-house is necessary for any
    purpose relating to the administration of this Act or the
    regulations, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} entry to the dwelling-house has been refused or
    there are reasonable grounds to believe that entry will be refused,

  the justice may issue a warrant authorizing the inspector named in the
  warrant to enter the dwelling-house, subject to any conditions that
  may be specified in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} An inspector who executes a warrant shall not use
  force unless the inspector is accompanied by a peace officer and the
  use of force is specifically authorized in the warrant.

## [Inquiries into Accidents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-202387 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may direct inquiry

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may direct an inquiry to be made, subject to the [Canadian
  Transportation Accident Investigation and Safety Board
  Act](/eng/acts/C-23.4), whenever any accidental explosion of any
  explosive has occurred, or when any accident has been caused by an
  explosive.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority of person appointed to
  inquire

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person authorized by the Minister to conduct an
  inquiry under subsection (1) has all the powers and authority of a
  commissioner appointed under Part I of the [Inquiries
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1989, c. 3, s. 42\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where provincial inquiry provided

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply where an accident has
  been caused by an explosion of an explosive occurring in any mine or
  quarry or metallurgical work in a province in which provision is made
  by the law of that province for a proper and thorough investigation
  and inquiry into the cause of such an accident.

## [Offences, Punishment and Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-202398 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fails to permit an inspector to enter any place or
    to inspect, examine or make inquiries in pursuance of the
    inspector's duties,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fails to comply with any order, direction or
    requirement of an inspector made in pursuance of this Act or any
    regulation, in respect of which no appeal has been taken under
    section 17,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fails to comply with any order, direction or
    requirement of an inspector made in pursuance of this Act or any
    regulation, as amended or confirmed by the Minister pursuant to
    section 17, or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in any manner whatever, obstructs an inspector in
    the execution of the inspector's duties under this Act,

  is guilty of an offence punishable on summary conviction and is liable
  to a fine not exceeding five thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a
  term not exceeding six months or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of Minister as evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In any prosecution under paragraph (1)(b) for failure
  to comply with an order, direction or requirement of an inspector, a
  certificate purporting to have been signed by or on behalf of the
  Minister stating that no appeal in respect of the order, direction or
  requirement has been taken under section 17 shall be received in
  evidence as proof, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, of that
  fact.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In any prosecution under paragraph (1)(c) for failure
  to comply with an order, direction or requirement of an inspector as
  amended or confirmed by the Minister, a certificate purporting to have
  been signed by or on behalf of the Minister stating

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the Minister has amended or confirmed the
    order, direction or requirement of the inspector, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the terms of the order, direction or requirement as
    amended or confirmed,

  shall be received in evidence as proof, in the absence of evidence to
  the contrary, of the matters set out in the certificate.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. E-17, s. 16
- 1993, c. 32, s. 9

after the day on which the order, direction or requirement was made,
submit the facts respecting the order, direction or requirement to the
Minister for the Minister's consideration and decision, and the Minister
may confirm, revoke or amend the order, direction or requirement.

conviction and is liable to a fine not exceeding five thousand dollars,
and may be forthwith removed therefrom by any peace officer or by any
person employed at the factory or magazine or the operator of the
vehicle, as the case may be.
