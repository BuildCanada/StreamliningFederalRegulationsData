## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-173570 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [DNA Identification Act](/eng/acts/D-3.8).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-173573 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Act.

[[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an authorization made
    under section 487.055 or 487.091 of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or section 196.24 of the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5). ([autorisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commissioner of the
    Royal Canadian Mounted Police. ([commissaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[designated offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a designated
    offence within the meaning of section 487.04 of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or section 196.11 of the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5). ([infraction désignée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[DNA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[DNA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means deoxyribonucleic
    acid. ([ADN]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[DNA profile]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[DNA profile]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the results of forensic
    DNA analysis of a bodily substance. ([profil d'identification
    génétique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[forensic DNA analysis]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[forensic DNA analysis]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a
    bodily substance, means forensic DNA analysis of the bodily
    substance. ([analyse génétique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[human remains]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[human remains]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any detached part of
    the body of a person who may still be alive. ([restes
    humains]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[investigating authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[investigating authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, as the case
    may be,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian law enforcement agency;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a coroner or medical examiner, or a person or
      organization with similar duties or functions, who is acting in
      the course of their duties under an Act of Parliament or of a
      provincial legislature; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a laboratory. ([autorité chargée de
      l'enquête]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an order made under section
    487.051 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or section 196.14 of
    the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5). ([ordonnance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[[Young Offenders Act](/eng/acts/Y-1)]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[[Young Offenders Act](/eng/acts/Y-1)]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    chapter Y-1 of the Revised Statutes of Canada, 1985. ([[Loi sur les
    jeunes contrevenants](/eng/acts/Y-1)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[young person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[young person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
    subsection 2(1) of the [Youth Criminal Justice Act](/eng/acts/Y-1.5)
    or subsection 2(1) of the [Young Offenders Act](/eng/acts/Y-1), as
    the case may be. ([adolescent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-173592 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to establish a national DNA data bank to help

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} law enforcement agencies identify persons alleged to
  have committed designated offences, including those committed before
  the coming into force of this Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} law enforcement agencies --- as well as coroners,
  medical examiners or persons and organizations with similar duties or
  functions --- find missing persons and identify human remains.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1998, c. 37, s. 3
- 2014, c. 39, s. 233

## [Principles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-173599 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principles

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** It is recognized and
declared that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the protection of society and the administration of
  justice are well served by the early detection, arrest and conviction
  of offenders, which can be facilitated by the use of DNA profiles;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} society is well served by locating missing persons
  and identifying human remains, which can be facilitated by the use of
  DNA profiles;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the DNA profiles, as well as samples of bodily
  substances from which the profiles are derived, may be used only in
  accordance with this Act, and not for any unauthorized purpose; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to protect the privacy of individuals with respect to
  personal information about themselves, safeguards must be placed on

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the use and communication of, and access to, DNA
    profiles and other information contained in the national DNA data
    bank, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the use of, and access to, bodily substances that
    are transmitted to the Commissioner for the purposes of this Act.

## [National DNA Data Bank]{.HTitleText1} {#h-173610 .Part}

### [Establishment and Contents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-173611 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness shall establish a
  national DNA data bank, to be maintained by the Commissioner,
  consisting of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for criminal identification purposes, a crime scene
    index, a convicted offenders index and a victims index;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of finding missing persons and
    identifying human remains, a missing persons index, a relatives of
    missing persons index and a human remains index; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes set out in paragraphs (a) and (b),
    a voluntary donors index.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissioner's duties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner's duties under this Act may be
  performed on behalf of the Commissioner by any person authorized by
  the Commissioner to perform those duties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crime scene index

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The crime scene index shall contain DNA profiles
  derived from bodily substances that are found

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at any place where a designated offence was
    committed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on or within the body of the victim of a designated
    offence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on anything worn or carried by the victim at the
    time when a designated offence was committed; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} on or within the body of any person or thing or at
    any place associated with the commission of a designated offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Convicted offenders index

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The convicted offenders index shall contain DNA
  profiles derived from bodily substances that are taken under orders
  and authorizations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Victims index

  [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} The victims index shall contain DNA profiles
  derived from bodily substances of a victim of a designated offence
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are voluntarily submitted by the victim for the
    purpose of having their DNA profile added to the index; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the victim is unidentified, deceased or unable
    to consent to submitting their bodily substances or their
    whereabouts are unknown, are obtained, as the case may be, from

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their personal effects,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any place associated with the commission of the
      designated offence, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the victim is deceased, their remains.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Missing persons index

  [(4.2)]{.lawlabel} The missing persons index shall contain DNA
  profiles derived from bodily substances of a missing person, including
  bodily substances obtained from their personal effects.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Relatives of missing persons index

  [(4.3)]{.lawlabel} The relatives of missing persons index shall
  contain DNA profiles derived from bodily substances of a person that
  are voluntarily submitted by them for the purpose of having their DNA
  profile added to the index, where their profile may assist in
  confirming the identity of a person whose DNA profile is contained in
  the missing persons index or human remains index.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Human remains index

  [(4.4)]{.lawlabel} The human remains index shall contain DNA profiles
  derived from human remains.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voluntary donors index

  [(4.5)]{.lawlabel} The voluntary donors index shall contain DNA
  profiles derived from the bodily substances of a person, other than a
  victim of a designated offence, that are voluntarily submitted by them
  for the purpose of having their DNA profile added to the index, where
  their profile may be relevant to an investigation of a designated
  offence or of a missing person or human remains.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other information

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the DNA profiles referred to in
  subsections (3) to (4.5), the DNA data bank shall contain, in relation
  to each of the profiles, information from which can be established

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a profile referred to in
    subsection (3) or any of subsections (4.1) to (4.4), the case number
    of the investigation associated with the bodily substance from which
    the profile was derived;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a profile referred to in any of
    subsections (4) to (4.5), the identity of the person from whose
    bodily substance the profile was derived, if that identity is known;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a profile referred to in subsection
    (4.3), the stated biological or other relationship of the person
    from whose bodily substance the profile was derived with the person
    whose identity is to be confirmed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of information transmitted

- **[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  index.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall retain the copy of the order

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defect in order or authorization

- **[[5.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the Commissioner is of the opinion that the offence referred to in the
  order or authorization is not a designated offence, the Commissioner
  shall retain any bodily substances collected under it and any
  information transmitted with it, and give notice of the apparent
  defect to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Attorney General of the province in which the
    order or authorization was made, if it was transmitted under section
    487.071 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Director of Military Prosecutions, if the order
    or authorization was transmitted under section 196.22 of the
    [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confirmation or correction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Attorney General or Director of Military
  Prosecutions, as the case may be, confirms in writing that the order
  or authorization is valid or sends a copy of a corrected order or
  authorization to the Commissioner, the Commissioner shall conduct a
  forensic DNA analysis of any bodily substances collected under it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Substantive defect

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Attorney General or the Director of Military
  Prosecutions, as the case may be, informs the Commissioner that the
  offence referred to in the order or authorization is not a designated
  offence, the Commissioner shall, without delay, destroy the bodily
  substances collected under the order or authorization and the
  information transmitted with it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Destruction of bodily substances

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall destroy any bodily substances
  and information retained under subsection (1) on the expiry of 180
  days after sending a notice under that subsection unless, before the
  expiry of that period, the Commissioner receives

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a confirmation that the order or authorization is
    valid;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corrected order or authorization;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a notice that the Attorney General or Director of
    Military Prosecutions requires an additional period of not greater
    than 90 days to review the order or authorization; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a notice that the issue of whether or not the order
    or authorization is defective is under review by a judge or in
    proceedings before a court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Precondition --- victims

- **[[5.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  DNA profile and related information shall be added to the victims
  index only if the Commissioner has reasonable grounds to suspect that
  the comparison of the profile conducted under subsection 5.5(1) will
  assist in the investigation of a designated offence with respect to
  which the profile was obtained.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preconditions --- missing persons and
  relatives

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A DNA profile and related information shall be added
  to the missing persons index or the relatives of missing persons index
  only if the Commissioner

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has reasonable grounds to suspect that the
    comparison of the profile conducted under section 5.5 will assist in
    the investigation of a missing person or human remains; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is satisfied that other investigative procedures
    have been tried and have failed or are unlikely to succeed, or that
    the urgency of the situation requires the comparison of the profile
    to others.

- 2014, c. 39, s. 238

**[[5.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.4 .sectionLabel}** A DNA profile and

index or the voluntary donors index, or to the victims index in the
circumstances described in paragraph 5(4.1)(a), only if the Commissioner
has received the written consent to that addition, provided in

### [Comparison of Profiles and Communication and Use of Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-173689 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Comparison of DNA profiles

- **[[5.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner shall compare each DNA profile that is added to the crime
  scene index, the convicted offenders index, the victims index, the
  missing person index, the voluntary donors index or the human remains
  index with the DNA profiles that are already contained in those
  indices.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Relatives of missing persons index

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall compare each DNA profile that
  is added to the relatives of missing persons index with the DNA
  profiles that are already contained in the missing persons index and
  the human remains index.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 238
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication --- match

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  comparison conducted under subsection 5.5(1) produces a match between
  DNA profiles, and none of the profiles that match is contained in the
  missing persons index or the human remains index, the Commissioner may
  communicate any information in relation to the profiles, to any
  laboratory or Canadian law enforcement agency that the Commissioner
  considers appropriate, for the purpose of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if at least one of the profiles is contained in the
    victims index, the investigation of a designated offence with
    respect to which that profile was obtained; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the investigation of any
    designated offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Missing persons and human remains
  indices

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a comparison conducted under subsection 5.5(1)
  produces a match between DNA profiles, and at least one of the
  profiles that match is contained in the missing persons index or the
  human remains index, the Commissioner may communicate any information
  in relation to the profiles, to any investigating authority that the
  Commissioner considers appropriate, for the purpose of the
  investigation of a missing person or human remains.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication --- similar profile

- **[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  comparison conducted under subsection 5.5(1) indicates that, in the
  Commissioner's opinion, two or more DNA profiles are similar, the
  Commissioner may communicate the similar DNA profiles, to any
  investigating authority that the Commissioner considers appropriate,
  for the purpose of determining whether the possibility of a match
  between the profiles can be excluded.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed match

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the investigating authority advises the
  Commissioner that the profiles are similar and that the possibility of
  a match between the DNA profiles has not been excluded, the
  Commissioner may communicate any information in relation to the
  profiles in accordance with subsection 6(1) or (2), as the case may
  be, as if there were a match between them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Relatives of missing persons index

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a comparison conducted under subsection 5.5(2)
  indicates that a DNA profile that is already contained in the missing
  persons index or human remains index could be the profile of a
  biological relative of someone whose DNA profile is added to the
  relatives of missing persons index, the Commissioner may communicate
  any information in relation to both DNA profiles, to any investigating
  authority that the Commissioner considers appropriate, for the purpose
  of the investigation of a missing person or human remains.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 238

profiles nor a result referred to in subsection 6.1(1) or (3), the
Commissioner may communicate that fact to any investigating authority
that the Commissioner considers appropriate.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 238

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication --- foreign law enforcement
agencies

- **[[6.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of a DNA profile from the government of a foreign state, an
  international organization established by the governments of states or
  an institution of such a government or international organization, the
  Commissioner may compare the profile with those already contained in
  the crime scene index, the convicted offenders index, the missing
  persons index and the human remains index to determine whether there
  is a match between profiles and may then communicate any of the
  following to the government, organization or institution, as the case
  may be:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if there is no match, that fact;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if there is a match, any information in relation to
    a matching DNA profile that is already contained in any of those
    indices;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if there is, in the Commissioner's opinion, a
    similar DNA profile already contained in an index, the similar DNA
    profile;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if, after receiving the similar DNA profile
    referred to in paragraph (c), the government, organization or
    institution advises the Commissioner that the possibility of a match
    between the similar profile with the DNA profile it provided has not
    been excluded, any information in relation to the similar DNA
    profile.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crime scene index

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may, on the request of a law
  enforcement agency in the course of the investigation of a designated
  offence, communicate a DNA profile contained in the crime scene index
  to the government of a foreign state, an international organization
  established by the governments of states or an institution of any such
  government or international organization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Missing persons or human remains index

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may, on the request of an
  investigating authority in the course of the investigation of a
  missing person or human remains, communicate a DNA profile contained
  in the missing persons index or the human remains index to the
  government of a foreign state, an international organization
  established by the governments of states or an institution of any such
  government or international organization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement or arrangement

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) to (3) apply only if the Government
  of Canada or one of its institutions has entered into an agreement or
  arrangement, in accordance with any regulations, with that government,
  international organization or institution, authorizing the
  communication solely for the purposes of, as the case may be, the
  investigation or prosecution of a criminal offence or the
  investigation of a missing person or human remains.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 238
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized users

**[[6.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.5 .sectionLabel}** Information as to
whether a person's DNA profile is contained in the convicted offenders
index may be communicated to an authorized user of the automated
criminal conviction records retrieval system maintained by the Royal
Canadian Mounted Police.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 238

**[[6.6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 6

- 2014, c. 39, s. 238

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Access to information
contained in the DNA data bank may be granted to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person or class of persons that the Commissioner
  considers appropriate for the purposes of the proper operation and
  maintenance of the DNA data bank; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the personnel of any laboratories that the
  Commissioner considers appropriate for training purposes.

### [Removal of Access to Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-173774 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal of access to information ---
crime scene index

- **[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Access to information in the crime
  scene index shall be removed from that index without delay if the
  information relates to a DNA profile derived from a bodily substance
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a victim of a designated offence that was the
    object of the relevant investigation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who has been eliminated as a suspect in
    the relevant investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other indices

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Access to information in relation to a DNA profile in
  the victims index, the missing persons index, the relatives of missing
  persons index, the human remains index or the voluntary donors index
  shall be removed from that index without delay if the Commissioner is
  advised that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person from whose bodily substances the profile
    was derived wishes to have access to the information removed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the comparison under this Act of the profile with
    other profiles will not assist in the investigation with respect to
    which the profile was obtained.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Periodic removal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} After each period prescribed by regulation, access to
  information in relation to a DNA profile in the victims index, the
  missing persons index, the relatives of missing persons index or the
  voluntary donors index shall be removed from that index without delay
  unless the Commissioner is advised before the end of the period, by
  any investigating authority that the Commissioner considers
  appropriate, that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the investigating authority has not been advised by
    the person from whose bodily substances the profile was derived that
    they wish to have access to the information removed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the comparison under this Act of the profile with
    other profiles may assist in the investigation with respect to which
    the profile was obtained.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent DNA profile

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Removal of access to information in relation to a DNA
  profile of a person from an index under this section does not prevent
  a DNA profile derived from the bodily substances of the same person,
  and any information in relation to that profile, from subsequently
  being added to any index in accordance with this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations --- requirements

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The removal of access to information under any of
  subsections (1) to (3) shall be done in accordance with the
  requirements set out in any regulations that apply with respect to
  that subsection.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1998, c. 37, s. 8.1
- 2014, c. 39, s. 240

### [Storage and Destruction of Bodily Substances]{.HTitleText2} {#h-173817 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage of bodily substances

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  bodily substances are transmitted to the Commissioner under section
  487.071 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or section 196.22 of
  the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), the Commissioner shall,
  subject to this section and section 10.1, safely and securely store,
  for the purpose of forensic DNA analysis, the portions of the samples
  of the bodily substances that the Commissioner considers appropriate
  and without delay destroy any remaining portions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change in technology

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Forensic DNA analysis of stored bodily substances may
  be performed if the Commissioner is of the opinion that the analysis
  is justified because significant technological advances have been made
  since the time when a DNA profile of the person who provided the
  bodily substances, or from whom they were taken, was last derived.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2005, c. 25, s. 20\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Access to stored bodily substances may be granted to
  any person or class of persons that the Commissioner considers
  appropriate for the purpose of preserving the bodily substances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of bodily substances

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} No person shall transmit stored bodily substances to
  any person or use stored bodily substances except for the purpose of
  forensic DNA analysis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Later destruction

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may at any time destroy any or all
  of the stored bodily substances if the Commissioner considers that
  they are no longer required for the purpose of forensic DNA analysis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory destruction in certain cases

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall destroy the stored bodily
  substances of a person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} without delay after every order or authorization
    for the collection of bodily substances from the person is finally
    set aside; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} without delay after the person is finally acquitted
    of every designated offence in connection with which an order was
    made or an authorization was granted.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2014, c. 39, s. 243\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When record suspension is in effect

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Despite anything in this section, stored bodily
  substances of a person in respect of whom a record suspension, as
  defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Criminal Records
  Act](/eng/acts/C-47), is in effect shall be kept separate and apart
  from other stored bodily substances, and no such bodily substance
  shall be used for forensic DNA analysis, nor shall the existence of
  such a bodily substance be communicated to any person.

- 2000, c. 10, s. 10
- 2005, c. 25, s. 20
- 2007, c. 22, s. 33
- 2012, c. 1, s. 148
