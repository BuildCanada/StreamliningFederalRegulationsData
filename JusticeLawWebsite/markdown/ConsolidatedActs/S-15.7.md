## Split Lake Cree First Nation Flooded Land Act {#split-lake-cree-first-nation-flooded-land-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1994, c. 42
:::

Assented to 1994-12-15

An Act respecting the Split Lake Cree First Nation and the settlement of
matters arising from an agreement relating to the flooding of land
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-437186 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Split Lake Cree First Nation Flooded Land Act](/eng/acts/S-15.7).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-437189 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the agreement concluded
    between

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Manitoba,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Manitoba Hydro-Electric Board, and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Split Lake Cree First Nation,

    pursuant to negotiations relating to the comprehensive
    implementation of the Flood Agreement and signed on June 24,
    1992; ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Flood Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Flood Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the agreement
    concerning the flooding of land concluded between

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Manitoba,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Manitoba Hydro-Electric Board, and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Northern Flood Committee, Inc.

    and signed on December 16, 1977. ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-437204 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indian moneys

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Amounts
  paid to the Split Lake Cree First Nation pursuant to the agreement,
  whether before or after the coming into force of this Act, are not
  Indian moneys within the meaning of subsection 2(1) of the [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of amounts held

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} As soon as practicable after the coming into force of
  this Act, all amounts collected, received or held by Her Majesty in
  right of Canada for the use or benefit of the Split Lake Cree First
  Nation pursuant to the agreement shall be transferred to the Split
  Lake Cree First Nation in accordance with the agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 35(4) of the [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 35(4) of the
[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) does not apply in respect of any amount
paid, whether before or after the coming into force of this Act, to the
Split Lake Cree First Nation pursuant to the agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Section 36 of the [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where land is held or
transferred pursuant to the agreement, section 36 of the [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5) does not apply in respect of that land if the holder
or transferee of the land, or any subsequent holder or transferee of the
land, is not Her Majesty in right of Canada.

## [Claims]{.HTitleText1} {#h-437214 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claims

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A claim provided for by
both the Flood Agreement and the agreement may be exercised by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Split Lake Cree First Nation,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the council of the Split Lake Cree First Nation, or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a body corporate or an unincorporated association to
  which the Flood Agreement applies

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that was incorporated or established by the council
    of the Split Lake Cree First Nation, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the shareholders or members of which are all or
    substantially all members of the Split Lake Cree First Nation,

only in accordance with the agreement.

## [Arbitration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-437223 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arbitration

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided in the agreement, the legislation of Manitoba relating to
arbitration applies in respect of any dispute between the parties to the
agreement that, under the terms of the agreement, is to be settled by
way of arbitration.
