Assented to 2003-11-07

An Act respecting employment in the public service

###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Recognizing that

the public service has contributed to the building of Canada, and will
continue to do so in the future while delivering services of highest
quality to the public;

Canada will continue to benefit from a public service that is based on
merit and non-partisanship and in which these values are independently
safeguarded;

Canada will also continue to gain from a public service that strives for
excellence, that is representative of Canada's diversity and that is
able to serve the public with integrity and in their official language
of choice;

the public service, whose members are drawn from across the country,
reflects a myriad of backgrounds, skills and professions that are a
unique resource for Canada;

authority to make appointments to and within the public service has been
vested in the Public Service Commission, which can delegate this
authority to deputy heads;

those to whom this appointment authority is delegated must exercise it
within a framework that ensures that they are accountable for its proper
use to the Commission, which in turn is accountable to Parliament;

delegation of staffing authority should be to as low a level as possible
within the public service, and should afford public service managers the
flexibility necessary to staff, to manage and to lead their personnel to
achieve results for Canadians; and

the Government of Canada is committed to an inclusive public service
that reflects the diversity of Canada's population, that embodies
linguistic duality and that is characterized by fair, transparent
employment practices, respect for employees, effective dialogue, and
recourse aimed at resolving appointment issues;

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-404019 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Federal Public Sector
      Labour Relations and Employment Board referred to in subsection
      4(1) of the [Federal Public Sector Labour Relations and Employment
      Board Act](/eng/acts/P-33.35). ([Commission des relations de
      travail et de l'emploi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Public Service
      Commission continued by subsection
      4(1). ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an organization named in Schedule I to the
        [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other organization that is designated by
        the Governor in Council as a department for the purposes of this
        Act; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any part of any organization that is designated
        by the Governor in Council as a department for the purposes of
        this Act. ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[deployment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[deployment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the transfer of a person
      from one position to another in accordance with Part
      3. ([mutation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[deputy head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[deputy head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to an organization named in
        Schedule I to the [Financial Administration
        Act](/eng/acts/F-11), its deputy minister;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to any organization or part of an
        organization that is designated as a department under this Act,
        the person that the Governor in Council designates as the deputy
        head for the purposes of this Act; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in relation to any organization named in
        Schedule IV or V to the [Financial Administration
        Act](/eng/acts/F-11) to which the Commission has the exclusive
        authority to make appointments, its chief executive officer or,
        if there is no chief executive officer, its statutory deputy
        head or, if there is neither, the person designated by the
        Governor in Council as its deputy head for the purposes of this
        Act. ([administrateur général]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person employed in that

  [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

        Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11); or

  [[equity-seeking group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[equity-seeking group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a group of

      prohibited grounds of discrimination within the meaning of the
      [Canadian Human Rights Act](/eng/acts/H-6). ([groupe en quête
      d'équité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[external appointment process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[external appointment process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      process for making one or more appointments in which persons may
      be considered whether or not they are employed in the public
      service. ([processus de nomination externe]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[internal appointment process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[internal appointment process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      process for making one or more appointments in which only persons
      employed in the public service may be considered. ([processus de
      nomination interne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, except in section 131, means any
      minister referred to in section 4.1 of the [Salaries

  [[organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any portion of the

      [Financial Administration
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11). ([administration]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[public service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[public service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the several positions

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the departments named in Schedule I to the
        [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the organizations named in Schedule IV to that
        Act; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the separate agencies named in Schedule V to
        that Act. ([fonction publique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[separate agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[separate agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an organization

      Act](/eng/acts/F-11). ([organisme distinct]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[statutory deputy head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[statutory deputy head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any officer

      or who has, or is deemed to have, the rank of a deputy
      head. ([administrateur général au titre de la
      loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2013, c. 40, s.
      403\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References to deputy head

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a reference to a deputy head in relation to an
    employee shall be construed as a reference to the deputy head of the
    department or other organization, as the case may be, in which the
    employee is employed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a reference to a deputy head in relation to an
    appointment shall be construed as a reference to the deputy head of
    the department or other organization, as the case may be, in which
    the appointment is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References to occupational groups

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A reference in this Act to an occupational group
  shall be construed as a reference to a group or subgroup of employees
  defined by the employer, and a reference to the executive group shall
  be construed as a reference to an occupational group or subgroup
  designated by the employer and consisting of management personnel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References to abuse of authority

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a reference in this Act to
  abuse of authority shall be construed as including bad faith and
  personal favouritism.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References to error, omission or
  improper conduct

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A reference in this Act to an error, an omission or
  improper conduct shall be construed as including an error, an omission
  or improper conduct that results from a bias or barrier that
  disadvantages persons who belong to any equity-seeking group.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Public Service Commission, Deputy Heads and Employer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-404061 .Part}

### [Commission]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404062 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission continued

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Public Service Commission is continued, consisting of a President and
  two or more other Commissioners.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In order to be eligible to hold office as a
  Commissioner, a person must be a Canadian citizen within the meaning
  of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29) or a permanent resident
  within the meaning of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Full-time or part-time

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The President shall serve on a full-time basis and
  the other Commissioners on a part-time basis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other employment or activities

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Commissioners shall not accept or hold any office or
  employment, or carry on any activity, that is inconsistent with their
  functions, and the President shall devote the whole of his or her time
  to the performance of the President's functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Commissioners

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The President and other Commissioners shall be
  appointed by the Governor in Council. The appointment of the President
  shall be made by commission under the Great Seal, after approval by
  resolution of the Senate and House of Commons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure and term of office

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A Commissioner holds office during good behaviour for
  a term of seven years, but may be removed by the Governor in Council
  at any time on address of the Senate and House of Commons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A Commissioner, on the expiration of a first or any
  subsequent term of office, is eligible to be re-appointed for a
  further term not exceeding seven years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oath or affirmation

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Before commencing his or her functions, a
  Commissioner shall take an oath or make a solemn affirmation in the
  following form before the Clerk of the Privy Council or the person
  designated by the Clerk:

  I, ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}, do swear (or
  solemnly affirm) that I will faithfully, truly and impartially, to the
  best of my judgment, skill and ability, execute and perform the office
  of (Commissioner or President, *as the case may be*) of the Public
  Service Commission. (*Add, in the case where an oath is taken,* "So
  help me God" (*or name of deity*).)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salaries

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioners shall be paid the remuneration determined by the
  Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioners are entitled to be paid reasonable
  travel and other expenses incurred by them in the course of their
  duties while absent from their ordinary place of residence or, in the
  case of the President, while absent from his or her ordinary place of
  work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The President is deemed to be employed in the public
  service for the purposes of the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of other Acts

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioners are deemed to be employed in the
  federal public administration for the purposes of the [Government
  Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and regulations made under
  section 9 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President is the chief executive officer of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Residence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The President shall reside in the National Capital
  Region as described in the schedule to the [National Capital
  Act](/eng/acts/N-4) or within the distance of it specified by the
  Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting President

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the President is absent or unable to act or if the
  office of President is vacant, the minister designated under section
  23 may authorize a Commissioner or other qualified person to act as
  President for a period not exceeding sixty days, and the Governor in
  Council may authorize a Commissioner or other qualified person to act
  as President for any longer period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  majority of the Commissioners constitutes a quorum of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancy

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A vacancy in the membership of the Commission does
  not impair the right of the remaining Commissioners to act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the
Commission shall be in the National Capital Region described in the
schedule to the [National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Human resources

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may appoint
the persons necessary for the proper conduct of its work in the manner
authorized by this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Experts and advisers

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may retain on a temporary basis the services of experts or
  other persons having technical or special knowledge to assist it in an
  advisory capacity and, subject to the approval of the Treasury Board,
  fix their remuneration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Persons whose services are retained under
  subsection (1) are not employed in the public service for the purposes
  of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

### [Mandate and Functions of Commission]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404111 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandate

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The mandate of the
Commission is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to appoint, or provide for the appointment of,
  persons to or from within the public service in accordance with this
  Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to conduct investigations and audits in accordance
  with this Act; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to administer the provisions of this Act relating to
  political activities of employees and deputy heads.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Functions assigned by Governor in Council

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Commission shall
perform any functions in relation to the public service that are
assigned to it by the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation to Commissioners and employees

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Any power or function of
the Commission under this Act, other than under section 20 or 22, may be
exercised or performed by any Commissioner or employee of the Commission
authorized by the Commission to do so and, if so exercised or performed,
is deemed to have been exercised or performed by the Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation by Commission

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Commission shall, on
request or if it considers consultation necessary or desirable, consult
with the employer or any employee organization certified as a bargaining
agent under the [Federal Public Sector Labour Relations
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.3) with respect to policies respecting the manner of
making and revoking appointments or with respect to the principles
governing lay-offs or priorities for appointment.

### [Delegation by Commission to Deputy Heads]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404125 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exercise of powers and functions by
deputy heads

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may authorize a deputy head to exercise or perform, in
  relation to his or her organization, in the manner and subject to any
  terms and conditions that the Commission directs, any of the powers
  and functions of the Commission under this Act, other than its powers
  under sections 17, 20 and 22, its power to investigate appointments
  under sections 66 to 69 and its powers under Part 7.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revision or rescission

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the Commission may revise
  or rescind an authorization granted under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of appointments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Commission authorizes a deputy head to make
  appointments pursuant to an internal appointment process, the
  authorization must include the power to revoke those appointments and
  to take corrective action whenever the deputy head, after
  investigation, is satisfied that an error, an omission or improper
  conduct affected the selection of a person for appointment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In authorizing a deputy head under subsection (3),
  the Commission is not required to include the authority to revoke
  appointments or to take corrective action in circumstances referred to
  in sections 68 and 69.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission jurisdiction

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may not revoke an appointment referred
  to in subsection (3) or take corrective action in relation to such an
  appointment except in circumstances referred to in sections 68 and 69.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment on revocation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where the appointment of a person is revoked by a
  deputy head acting pursuant to subsection (3), the Commission may
  appoint that person to another position if the Commission is satisfied
  that the person meets the essential qualifications referred to in
  paragraph 30(2)(a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with appointment policies

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** In exercising or
performing any of the Commission's powers and functions pursuant to
section 15, a deputy head is subject to any policies established by the
Commission under subsection 29(3).

### [Commission Audits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404141 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audits by Commission

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may conduct audits on any matter within its jurisdiction
  and on the exercise, by deputy heads, of their authority under
  subsection 30(2) and may make recommendations to deputy heads.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Biases and barriers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The power to conduct audits includes the power to
  determine whether there are biases or barriers that disadvantage
  persons belonging to any equity-seeking group.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Commission

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** In conducting an audit,
the Commission has all the powers of a commissioner under Part I of the
[Inquiries Act](/eng/acts/I-11).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons acting for Commission

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may direct that any audit under section 17 be conducted, in
  whole or in part, by a Commissioner or any other person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Commissioner

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In relation to a matter before a Commissioner under
  subsection (1), the Commissioner has the powers referred to in section
  18.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of other persons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In relation to a matter before a person other than a
  Commissioner under subsection (1), the person has the powers referred
  to in section 18 subject to any limitations specified by the
  Commission.

### [Exclusions from this Act]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404153 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of positions and persons

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Commission decides that it is neither practicable nor in the best
  interests of the public service to apply this Act or any of its
  provisions to any position or person or class of positions or persons,
  the Commission may, with the approval of the Governor in Council,
  exclude that position, person or class from the application of this
  Act or those provisions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation with employer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall consult the employer in respect
  of an exclusion from any provision of this Act whose application is
  not within the Commission's jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-application of provisions to persons
  or positions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may, with the approval of the Governor
  in Council, re-apply any of the provisions of this Act to any position
  or person, or class of positions or persons, excluded pursuant to
  subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations of Governor in Council

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, on the recommendation of the Commission, make regulations
prescribing how any position or person, or class of positions or
persons, excluded under section 20 from the application of this Act or
any of its provisions is to be dealt with.

### [Regulations of Commission]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404163 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General regulatory power

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may make any regulations that it considers necessary to
  give effect to the provisions of this Act relating to matters under
  its jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Without limiting the generality of subsection (1),
  the Commission may make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establishing for any person or class of persons a
    right to be appointed --- in priority to all persons other than
    those referred to in sections 39.1 and 40 and subsections 41(1)
    and (4) --- during the period specified by the Commission, to any
    position for which the Commission is satisfied that they meet the
    essential qualifications referred to in paragraph 30(2)(a);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determining the order of priority of the rights to
    appointment established by any regulations made under paragraph (a);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting appointments on an acting basis and the
    maximum period for which any such appointments or any class of such
    appointments may be made, and excluding any such appointments or
    class from the operation of any or all of the provisions of this

### [Basis of Appointment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404225 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment on basis of merit

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Appointments by the Commission to or
  from within the public service shall be made on the basis of merit and
  must be free from political influence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of merit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An appointment is made on the basis of merit when

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Commission is satisfied that the person to be
    appointed meets the essential qualifications for the work to be
    performed, as established by the deputy head, including official
    language proficiency; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Commission has regard to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any additional qualifications that the deputy
      head may consider to be an asset for the work to be performed, or
      for the organization, currently or in the future,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any current or future operational requirements
      of the organization that may be identified by the deputy head, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any current or future needs of the organization
      that may be identified by the deputy head.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Needs of public service

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The current and future needs of the organization
  referred to in subparagraph (2)(b)(iii) may include current and future
  needs of the public service, as identified by the employer, that the
  deputy head determines to be relevant to the organization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission is not required to consider more than
  one person in order for an appointment to be made on the basis of
  merit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualification standards

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  employer may establish qualification standards, in relation to
  education, knowledge, experience, occupational certification, language
  or other qualifications, that the employer considers necessary or
  desirable having regard to the nature of the work to be performed and
  the present and future needs of the public service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The qualifications referred to in paragraph 30(2)(a)
  and subparagraph 30(2)(b)(i) must meet or exceed any applicable
  qualification standards established by the employer under subsection
  (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identification of biases and barriers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When establishing or reviewing qualification
  standards, the employer shall conduct an evaluation to identify
  whether they include or create biases or barriers that disadvantage
  persons belonging to any equity-seeking group. If a bias or barrier is
  identified in the course of the evaluation, the employer shall make
  reasonable efforts to remove it or to mitigate its impact on those
  persons.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Deployments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-404408 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority of deputy heads to deploy

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except

  employees to or within the deputy head's organization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deployment from separate agencies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as provided in this or any other Act, a deputy
  head may deploy to the deputy head's organization persons who are
  employed in a separate agency to which the Commission does not have
  the exclusive authority to make appointments if the Commission has,
  after reviewing the staffing program of the separate agency at the
  agency's request, approved deployments from it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deployment within or between groups

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A deployment may be made within an occupational group
  or, unless excluded by regulations under paragraph 26(1)(a), between
  occupational groups.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Treasury Board directives and
  regulations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A deployment to or within an organization named in
  Schedule I or IV to the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)
  shall be made in the manner directed by the Treasury Board and in
  accordance with any regulations of the Treasury Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employment status preserved

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The deployment of a person may not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} constitute a promotion, within the meaning of
    regulations of the Treasury Board, in the case of an organization
    named in Schedule I or IV to the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11), or as determined by the separate agency, in
    the case of a separate agency to which the Commission has the
    exclusive authority to make appointments; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} change a person's period of employment from a
    specified term to indeterminate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent to deployment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} No person may be deployed without his or her consent
  unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} agreement to being deployed is a condition of
    employment of the person's current position; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the deputy head of the organization in which the
    person is employed finds, after investigation, that the person has
    harassed another person in the course of his or her employment and
    the deployment is made within the same organization.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Previous position

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** On deployment, a person
ceases to be the incumbent of the position to which he or she had
previously been appointed or deployed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deployment not an appointment

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  deployment is not an appointment within the meaning of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions to priority rights

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A deputy head may deploy a person without regard to
  any other person's right to be appointed under section 39.1 or
  subsection 41(1) or (4) or any regulations made under paragraph
  22(2)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date of deployment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The deployment of a person from within that part of
  the public service to which the Commission has exclusive authority to
  make appointments takes effect

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the date agreed to in writing by that person and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person's consent to the deployment is not

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indeterminate employment

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Act, any

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 59, an employee

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not an appointment or deployment

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rate of pay on appointment

**[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** The rate of pay on

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the notice period determined by the separate agency

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laying off of employees

- **[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of lay-off

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An employee ceases to be an employee when the

## [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Investigations and Complaints Relating to Appointments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-404509 .Part}

### [Investigation of Appointments by Commission]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404510 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}External appointments

**[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may
investigate any external appointment process and, if it is satisfied
that the appointment was not made or proposed to be made on the basis of
merit, or that there was an error, an omission or improper conduct that
affected the selection of the person appointed or proposed for
appointment, the Commission may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} revoke the appointment or not make the appointment,
  as the case may be; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} take any corrective action that it considers
  appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Internal appointments --- no delegation

- **[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may investigate an internal appointment process, other than
  one conducted by a deputy head acting under subsection 15(1), and, if
  it is satisfied that there was an error, an omission or improper
  conduct that affected the selection of the person appointed or
  proposed for appointment, the Commission may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} revoke the appointment or not make the appointment,
    as the case may be; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} take any corrective action that it considers
    appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Internal appointments --- delegation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may, at the request of the deputy
  head, investigate an internal appointment process that was conducted
  by a deputy head acting under subsection 15(1), and report its
  findings to the deputy head and the deputy head may, if satisfied that
  there was an error, an omission or improper conduct that affected the
  selection of the person appointed or proposed for appointment,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} revoke the appointment or not make the appointment,
    as the case may be; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} take any corrective action that he or she considers
    appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Political influence

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** If it has reason to
believe that an appointment or proposed appointment was not free from
political influence, the Commission may investigate the appointment
process and, if it is satisfied that the appointment or proposed
appointment was not free from political influence, the Commission may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} revoke the appointment or not make the appointment,
  as the case may be; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} take any corrective action that it considers
  appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fraud

**[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** If it has reason to
believe that fraud may have occurred in an appointment process, the
Commission may investigate the appointment process and, if it is
satisfied that fraud has occurred, the Commission may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} revoke the appointment or not make the appointment,
  as the case may be; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} take any corrective action that it considers
  appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Commission

- **[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  conducting any investigation under this Part, the Commission has all
  the powers of a commissioner under Part II of the [Inquiries
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informality

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An investigation shall be conducted by the Commission
  as informally and expeditiously as possible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons acting for Commission

- **[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may direct that any investigation under this Part be
  conducted, in whole or in part, by one or more Commissioners or other
  persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Commissioner

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Commissioner directed under subsection (1) has the
  powers referred to in section 70 in relation to the matter before the
  Commissioner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of other person

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any limitations specified by the
  Commission, a person directed under subsection (1), other than a
  Commissioner, has the powers referred to in section 70 in relation to
  the matter before the person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to make submissions

**[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** Where an investigation is
conducted under this Part in relation to a person's appointment or
proposed appointment, that person and the deputy head in relation to the
appointment --- or their representatives --- are entitled to make
submissions to the Commission, Commissioner or other person, whichever
is conducting the investigation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment following revocation

**[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** Where the appointment of
a person is revoked under any of sections 66 to 69, the Commission may
appoint that person to another position if the Commission is satisfied
that the person meets the essential qualifications referred to in
paragraph 30(2)(a).

### [Complaints to Board --- Revocation of Appointment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404548 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaint

**[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** A person whose

### [Purpose of Part]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404717 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[112]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-112 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this
Part is to recognize the right of employees to engage in political
activities while maintaining the principle of political impartiality in
the public service.

### [Employees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404720 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permitted activities

- **[[113]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-113 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  employee may engage in any political activity so long as it does not
  impair, or is not perceived as impairing, the employee's ability to
  perform his or her duties in a politically impartial manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of
  the Commission, make regulations specifying political activities that
  are deemed to impair the ability of an employee, or any class of
  employees, to perform their duties in a politically impartial manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In making regulations, the Governor in Council may
  take into consideration factors such as the nature of the political
  activity and the nature of the duties of an employee or class of
  employees and the level and visibility of their positions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seeking candidacy

- **[[114]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-114 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  employee may seek nomination as a candidate in a federal, provincial
  or territorial election before or during the election period only if
  the employee has requested and obtained permission from the Commission
  to do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Being a candidate before election
  period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An employee may, before the election period, be a
  candidate in a federal, provincial or territorial election only if the
  employee has requested and obtained permission from the Commission to
  do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Being a candidate during election
  period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An employee may, during the election period, be a
  candidate in a federal, provincial or territorial election only if the
  employee has requested and obtained a leave of absence without pay
  from the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Granting of permission

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may grant permission for the purpose
  of subsection (1) or (2) only if it is satisfied that the employee's
  ability to perform his or her duties in a politically impartial manner
  will not be impaired or perceived to be impaired.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Granting of leave

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may grant leave for the purpose of
  subsection (3) only if it is satisfied that being a candidate during
  the election period will not impair or be perceived as impairing the
  employee's ability to perform his or her duties in a politically
  impartial manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} In deciding whether seeking nomination as, or being,
  a candidate will impair or be perceived as impairing the employee's
  ability to perform his or her duties in a politically impartial
  manner, the Commission may take into consideration factors such as the
  nature of the election, the nature of the employee's duties and the
  level and visibility of the employee's position.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may make permission under
  subsection (4) conditional on the employee taking a leave of absence
  without pay for the period or any part of the period in which he or
  she seeks nomination as a candidate, or for the period or any part of
  the period in which he or she is a candidate before the election
  period, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of election

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} An employee ceases to be an employee on the day he or
  she is declared elected in a federal, provincial or territorial
  election.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Candidacy in municipal elections

- **[[115]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-115 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  employee may seek nomination as, or be, a candidate in a municipal
  election before or during the election period, only if the employee
  has requested and obtained permission from the Commission to do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Granting of permission

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may grant permission only if it is
  satisfied that seeking nomination as, or being, a candidate in the
  election will not impair or be perceived as impairing the employee's
  ability to perform his or her duties in a politically impartial
  manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In deciding whether seeking nomination as, or being,
  a candidate will impair or be perceived as impairing the employee's
  ability to perform his or her duties in a politically impartial
  manner, the Commission may take into consideration factors such as the
  nature of the election, the nature of the employee's duties and the
  level and visibility of the employee's position.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may make permission under this section
  conditional on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the employee taking a leave of absence without pay

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the period or any part of the period in which
      he or she seeks nomination as a candidate, or for the period or
      any part of the period in which he or she is a candidate before
      the election period, as the case may be, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for the period in which he or she is a candidate
      during the election period; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the employee taking a leave of absence without pay
    or ceasing to be an employee if he or she is declared elected.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

**[[116]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-116 .sectionLabel}** On granting an employee
permission under subsection 114(4), leave under subsection 114(5) or
permission under subsection 115(2), the Commission shall cause notice
that it has done so, together with the name of that employee, to be
published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

### [Deputy Heads]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404760 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Political activities

**[[117]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-117 .sectionLabel}** A deputy head shall not
engage in any political activity other than voting in an election.

### [Allegations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404763 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation and corrective action ---
employees

**[[118]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-118 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may
investigate any allegation, in accordance with the regulations, that an
employee has failed to comply with any of subsections 113(1), 114(1) to
(3) and 115(1) and, if it concludes that the allegation is
substantiated, may dismiss the employee or may take any corrective
action that it considers appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation and dismissal --- deputy
head

- **[[119]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-119 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may investigate any allegation, made to it by a person who
  is or has been a candidate in an election, that a deputy head has
  contravened section 117 and, if it concludes that the allegation is
  substantiated, the Commission shall report its conclusion to the
  Governor in Council and the Governor in Council may dismiss the deputy
  head.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of any
  deputy head whose removal from office is expressly provided for by
  this or any other Act, otherwise than by termination of his or her
  appointment at pleasure.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers under [Inquiries
Act](/eng/acts/I-11)

**[[120]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-120 .sectionLabel}** In conducting any
investigation under this Part, the Commission has all the powers of a
commissioner under Part II of the [Inquiries Act](/eng/acts/I-11).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons acting for Commission

- **[[121]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-121 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may direct that any investigation under this Part be
  conducted, in whole or in part, by one or more Commissioners or other
  persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Commissioner

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Commissioner directed under subsection (1) has the
  powers referred to in section 120 in relation to the matter before the
  Commissioner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of other person

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any limitations specified by the
  Commission, a person directed under subsection (1), other than a
  Commissioner, has the powers referred to in section 120 in relation to
  the matter before the person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to be heard

**[[122]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-122 .sectionLabel}** A person making an
allegation under section 118 or 119 and the employee or deputy head
against whom it is made --- or their representatives --- are entitled to
be heard by the Commission, Commissioner or other person, whichever is
conducting the investigation.

## [PART 8]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-404782 .Part}

### [Application of Act]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404783 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations of Governor in Council

- **[[123]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-123 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, notwithstanding any other Act, make
  regulations applying to any organization or any part of any
  organization all or any of the provisions of this Act that do not
  otherwise apply to it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of other Acts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under subsection (1) prevails over
  the provisions of any other Act or regulations made under any other
  Act respecting the same matter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of regulations

**[[124]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-124 .sectionLabel}** A regulation made by
the Commission, the Treasury Board or the Governor in Council under this
Act may be of general application or may apply to a specified person,
position, occupational group, organization or part of an organization,
or any class of them, or in respect of a specified process or any class
of process.

### [Head of Public Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404791 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment by Governor in Council

**[[125]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-125 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may appoint and fix the remuneration of the Clerk of the Privy Council
and Secretary to the Cabinet.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clerk of Privy Council

**[[126]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-126 .sectionLabel}** The Clerk of the Privy
Council and Secretary to the Cabinet is the head of the public service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report of head of the public service

**[[127]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-127 .sectionLabel}** The head of the public
service shall submit a report on the state of the public service in each
fiscal year to the Prime Minister, and the Prime Minister shall cause a
copy of the report to be laid before each House of Parliament on any of
the first fifteen days on which that House is sitting after the Prime
Minister receives it.

### [Deputy Ministers and Other Senior Officials]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404798 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment by Governor in Council

- **[[127.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-127.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may appoint
  persons to the following positions and fix their remuneration:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deputy minister, associate deputy minister and
    positions of equivalent ranks;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} deputy head, associate deputy head and positions of
    equivalent ranks; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} special adviser to a minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Part 7

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the provisions of Part 7
  applicable to deputy heads apply to persons appointed as such or as
  deputy ministers under subsection (1), and the provisions of that Part
  applicable to employees apply to other persons appointed under
  subsection (1).

- 2006, c. 9, s. 106

### [Ministerial Staff]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404809 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial staff

- **[[128]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-128 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  minister, or a person holding the recognized position of Leader of the
  Opposition in the House of Commons or Leader of the Opposition in the
  Senate, may appoint an executive assistant and other persons required
  in his or her office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of employment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who is employed in an office referred to in
  subsection (1) ceases to be so employed thirty days after the person
  holding a position referred to in subsection (1) ceases to hold that
  position.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[129]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-129 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations applying all or any of the provisions of this Act
to any of the positions of persons appointed by ministers under
subsection 128(1).

### [Public Officials]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404817 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointments by Governor in Council

**[[130]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-130 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may appoint and fix the remuneration of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Secretary to the Cabinet for Federal-Provincial
  Relations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Clerk of the Senate;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Clerk of the House of Commons; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Secretary to the Governor General.

### [Diplomatic Personnel]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404824 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Diplomatic appointments

**[[131]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-131 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act
shall be construed as affecting the right or authority of Her Majesty to
appoint ambassadors, ministers, high commissioners or consuls-general of
Canada to another country or to appoint other persons to represent
Canada in another country.

### [Block Transfers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404827 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of employees

- **[[132]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-132
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in an order made under the
  [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
  Act](/eng/acts/P-34) shall be construed as affecting the status of an
  employee who, immediately before the coming into force of the order,
  occupied a position in a portion of the core public administration the
  control or supervision of which has been transferred from one
  department or other portion of the core public administration to
  another, or in a department that has been amalgamated and combined,
  except that the employee shall, on the coming into force of the order,
  occupy that position in the department or other portion of the core
  public administration to which the control or supervision has been
  transferred or in the department as amalgamated and combined.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of other staff

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an order is made under the [Public Service
  Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), the
  Governor in Council may, by order made on the recommendation of the
  Treasury Board and where the Governor in Council is of the opinion
  that an employee or class of employees is carrying out powers, duties
  or functions that are in whole or in part in support of or related to
  the powers, duties and functions of employees referred to in
  subsection (1) and that it is in the best interests of the core public
  administration to do so, declare that the employee or class of
  employees shall, on the coming into force of the order, occupy their
  positions in the department or other portion of the core public
  administration where the employees referred to in subsection (1) are
  currently occupying their positions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Core public administration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the core public
  administration consists of the departments, as defined in subsection
  2(1), and the portions of the federal public administration named in
  Schedule IV to the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

### [Offence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404835 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fraud

**[[133]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-133 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
commits fraud in any appointment process is guilty of an offence
punishable on summary conviction.

### [Oaths and Affirmations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404838 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to administer

**[[134]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-134 .sectionLabel}** The Commission or a
deputy head may administer oaths and receive affidavits, declarations
and solemn affirmations in relation to matters within their respective
jurisdictions under this Act.

### [Access to Facilities and Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404841 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access by Commission

**[[135]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-135 .sectionLabel}** Deputy heads and
employees shall provide the Commission with any facilities, assistance,
information and access to their respective offices that the Commission
may require for the performance of its duties.

### [Five-year Review]{.HTitleText2} {#h-404844 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review

**[[136]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-136 .sectionLabel}** The minister designated
by the Governor in Council for the purposes of this section shall cause
a review of this Act and its administration and operation to be
conducted five years after this section comes into force, and that
minister shall cause a report of the review to be laid before each House
of Parliament on any of the first fifteen days on which that House is
sitting after the review is completed.

        Majesty or of any of the Allies of His Majesty, and who has left

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during World War II was on active service

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the naval, army or air forces of Canada,

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} has served at least three years in the Canadian
        Forces, has been honourably released within the meaning of

        period; ([ancien combattant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

      [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) ends on the
      earliest of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day that is five years after the day on
        which this Act comes into force,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the person is appointed to a
        position in the public service for an indeterminate period, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the person declines an
        appointment to a position in the public service for an
        indeterminate period without good and sufficient reason.
