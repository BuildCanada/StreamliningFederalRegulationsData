## An Act to incorporate the Jules and Paul-Émile Léger Foundation {#an-act-to-incorporate-the-jules-and-paul-émile-léger-foundation .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1980-81-82-83, c. 85
:::

Assented to 1981-12-18

An Act to incorporate the Jules and Paul-Émile Léger Foundation
::::

:::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS Jules Léger, Governor General of Canada from January 14, 1974 to
January 21, 1979, and his brother Paul-Émile Léger, Archbishop of
Montreal from May 17, 1950 to November 9, 1967, and Cardinal of the
Roman Catholic Church since January 12, 1953, have served Canada and
Canadians with distinction;

AND WHEREAS their devotion to the promotion of humanitarian goals and to
the alleviation of human suffering is worthy of special recognition;

AND WHEREAS it is appropriate to honour such devotion by the
establishment of a philanthropic Foundation bearing their name;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Foundation Established]{.HTitleText1} {#h-336572 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foundation established

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby established
a non-profit corporation to be known as the Jules and Paul-Émile Léger
Foundation, in this Act referred to as the "Foundation".

## [Objects]{.HTitleText1} {#h-336575 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The objects of the
Foundation are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to relieve sickness and hunger and to provide
  assistance to all in need including orphans, lepers, the aged, the
  handicapped and the ill-treated;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to support and establish hospitals, rehabilitation
  centres, refuges and other similar institutions;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to support persons and organizations with objects
  similar to its own;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to organize, promote and participate in fund raising
  drives for itself and for persons and organizations with objects
  similar to its own;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} to solicit and receive gifts, legacies and grants;
  and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} generally, to organize, promote, coordinate and
  support, both morally and financially, in Canada and elsewhere, all
  educational, religious, philanthropic and benevolent charities, causes
  and activities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Religious beliefs

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Foundation shall pursue
its objects without regard to the religious beliefs of those who may
benefit thereby.

## [Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-336586 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Borrowing powers

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Foundation may, in
furtherance of its objects,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} borrow money on the credit of the Foundation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} issue, reissue, sell or pledge debt obligations of
  the Foundation; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to the terms, if any, under which the
  property was acquired, create any security interest in, or
  hypothecate, all or any property of the Foundation, owned or
  subsequently acquired, to secure any obligation of the Foundation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 85, s. 4
- 2001, c. 4, s. 96
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investments

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Foundation may, in
furtherance of its objects, invest the funds of the Foundation in any
manner it deems suitable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Works or undertaking necessary for
objects

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Foundation may acquire,
establish and manage any charitable or benevolent work or undertaking
necessary for its objects.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incidental powers

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Foundation may do any
act or thing that is incidental to or may be conducive to the attainment
of its objects.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity of Foundation

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this Act, the Foundation has the capacity of a natural person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Foundation may carry on its activities throughout
  Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extraterritorial capacity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Foundation has the capacity to carry on its
  activities and affairs and to exercise its powers in any jurisdiction
  outside Canada to the extent that the laws of such jurisdiction
  permit.

## [Head Office]{.HTitleText1} {#h-336607 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The head
  office of the Foundation shall be in the City of Montreal or at such
  other place in Canada as the board of directors may determine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notice of every change of location of the head office
  of the Foundation shall be published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

## [Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-336613 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First members

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  first members of the Foundation are Robert Comtois, Victor Goldbloom,
  Fernand Legault, Robert Morin, Jean Roquet and Marcel Vincent, all of
  the City of Montreal, and John W. Pickersgill, Guy Roberge and Maxwell
  Yalden, all of the City of Ottawa.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First directors

  [[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#J-1.5_en_1){#J-1.5_en_1-ID0EBCBA}[(2)]{.lawlabel} The
  persons mentioned in subsection (1) shall be the first directors of
  the Foundation until other directors are elected in their stead at a
  general meeting of the members to be held within three months after
  the coming into force of this section.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#J-1.5_en_1-ID0EBCBA){#J-1.5_en_1}\[Note:
    Section 10 in force on assent December 18, 1981.\]
    :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility for election

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The first directors are eligible for election at the
  meeting referred to in subsection (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board of directors

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The affairs of the
Foundation shall be managed by a board of directors consisting of not
less than five and not more than twenty members of the Foundation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election of directors

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The directors of the
Foundation shall be elected at a general meeting of the members.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-election

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A director whose term of
office has expired is eligible for re-election.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The President and other
officers of the Foundation shall be elected by and from among the
directors as soon as is practicable after the meeting at which the
directors were elected.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Honorary presidents

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Paul-Émile Léger and
Gabrielle Léger shall be lifetime honorary co-presidents of the
Foundation and every subsidiary corporation incorporated pursuant to
subsection 17(1).

## [By-laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-336631 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to make by-laws

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  board of directors may make by-laws

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} providing for the election of directors and their
    term of office;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} providing for the appointment, the remuneration and
    expenses and the functions and duties of the officers and employees,
    and agents or mandataries, of the Foundation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} providing for the establishment and the operations
    of an executive committee and special committees;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} providing for different classes of members, the
    qualifications of members, the fees and dues payable by members and
    the rights and obligations of members;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} regulating the exercise of the powers of subsidiary
    corporations incorporated pursuant to subsection 17(1), the
    nomination of their members and the election of their directors; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} generally, for the conduct of the activities and
    affairs of the Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-law to be sanctioned

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No by-law of the Foundation is valid or shall be
  acted on until it is sanctioned at a general meeting of the members by
  at least two-thirds of those present and entitled to vote thereat.

- 2004, c. 25, s. 144

## [Subsidiary Corporations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-336645 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsidiary corporations

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  petition by the Foundation, the Minister of Consumer and Corporate
  Affairs may, subject to such conditions as may be set out in the
  petition, issue under his seal of office letters patent of
  incorporation constituting subsidiary corporations for the purpose of
  carrying out one or more of the Foundation's objects.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notice of the issuance of letters patent pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall be published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of petition

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  petition for a subsidiary corporation shall set out the proposed name
  of the corporation, its objects, the powers of the Foundation it is to
  possess and the place where its head office is to be situated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every petition for a subsidiary corporation shall be
  accompanied by a copy of any by-law of the Foundation regulating the
  exercise of the subsidiary corporation's powers, the nomination of its
  members and the election of its directors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supplementary letters patent

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  petition by a subsidiary corporation, and with the approval of the
  Foundation, the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs may issue
  under his seal of office supplementary letters patent to amend, vary
  or modify the objects or powers of the subsidiary corporation, but no
  supplementary letters patent issued pursuant to this subsection shall
  authorize the subsidiary corporation to pursue any object that the
  Foundation is not authorized to pursue.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notice of the issuance of supplementary letters
  patent pursuant to subsection (1) shall be published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Sections 3 and 21 of this
Act and Part 19 of the [Canada Not-for-profit Corporations
Act](/eng/acts/C-7.75) apply, with any modifications that the
circumstances require, to every subsidiary corporation incorporated
under subsection 17(1).

- 2009, c. 23, s. 303

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-336665 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Income revenues and profits

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Subject to any by-law of
the Foundation providing for the remuneration of officers and employees,
and agents or mandataries, of the Foundation, any profits or accretions
to the value of the property of the Foundation shall be used to further
the activities of the Foundation, and no part of the property or profits
of the Foundation may be distributed, directly or indirectly, to any
member of the Foundation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 85, s. 21
- 2004, c. 25, s. 145
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-21-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 21"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dissolution

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  petition therefor by the Foundation and in the manner specified in the
  petition, the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs may dissolve
  the Foundation or any subsidiary corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property of subsidiary corporation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the dissolution of a subsidiary
  corporation, any property of the subsidiary corporation that remains
  after the payment of its debts and liabilities or after the making of
  an adequate provision for the payment of its debts and liabilities
  shall be transferred to the Foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property of the Foundation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the dissolution of the Foundation,
  any property of the Foundation that remains after the payment of its
  debts and liabilities or after the making of an adequate provision for
  the payment of its debts and liabilities shall be transferred to the
  Procurer of the Roman Catholic Diocese of Montréal to be used for
  philanthropic purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notice of every dissolution pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall be published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).
