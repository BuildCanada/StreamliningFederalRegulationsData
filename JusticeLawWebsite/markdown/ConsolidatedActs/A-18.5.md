## Aviation Industry Indemnity Act {#aviation-industry-indemnity-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2014, c. 29, s. 2
:::

Assented to 2014-12-09

An Act respecting the indemnity of certain aviation industry
participants and beneficiaries for certain events

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 2 of chapter 29 of the Statutes of Canada, 2014, in
force on assent December 9, 2014.\]
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-7336 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Aviation Industry Indemnity Act](/eng/acts/A-18.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-7339 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 3(1) of the [Aeronautics
    Act](/eng/acts/A-2). ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[aviation industry participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aviation industry participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an air carrier, as defined in subsection 3(1) of
      the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), that is a Canadian, as
      defined in subsection 55(1) of the [Canada Transportation
      Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} NAV CANADA, a corporation incorporated on May 26,
      1995 under Part II of the [Canada Corporations
      Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8);

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an owner or operator of an airport;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a supplier of goods or services that directly
      support the operation of aircraft from an airport, including with
      respect to

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the preparation of an aircraft for departure or
        on its arrival, including maintenance and cleaning of the
        aircraft and the loading and unloading of passengers, baggage
        and cargo,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} freight forwarding,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} air navigation, or

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} airport security services; or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an entity that is prescribed by regulation or a
      member of a class of entity that is prescribed by
      regulation. ([participant de l'industrie
      aérienne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[event]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[event]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an act of unlawful interference with an aircraft,
      airport or air navigation facility, including an act of terrorism;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an act or omission in the course of armed
      conflict, war, invasion, hostilities, civil war, revolution,
      rebellion, insurrection, an application of martial law, a
      usurpation or attempted usurpation of power, a civil commotion or
      a riot; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an act or omission committed by NAV CANADA, or
      any of its officers, employees or agents or mandataries, in
      accordance with an instruction given under an agreement entered
      into between that corporation and Her Majesty in right of Canada
      respecting the provision of [[air navigation
      services]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of
      the [Civil Air Navigation Services Commercialization
      Act](/eng/acts/C-29.7), to the Department of National Defence or
      the Canadian Forces. ([événement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Transport. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Undertaking]{.HTitleText1} {#h-7357 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Undertaking by Minister

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, in writing, undertake to indemnify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any aviation industry participant or class of
    aviation industry participant against their loss or damage, or
    liability for loss or damage, that is caused by an event; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any beneficiary or class of beneficiary under an
    insurance policy held by an aviation industry participant against
    that beneficiary's, or class of beneficiary's, loss or damage that
    is caused by an event.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The undertaking to indemnify is limited to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} loss, damage or liability, or any portion of it,
    that is not insured or otherwise indemnified; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} loss, damage or liability that is not solely with
    respect to a loss of income.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may attach terms to an undertaking,
  including terms that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} specify the event or class of event that is covered
    by the undertaking or that is excluded from coverage;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specify the activity or class of activity in which
    an aviation industry participant engages that is covered by the
    undertaking or that is excluded from coverage;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} specify the class of loss, damage or liability that
    is covered by the undertaking or that is excluded from coverage;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} establish the maximum amount of indemnification, or
    the method to determine that amount, that may be paid out under the
    undertaking;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} require that an aviation industry participant
    obtain a specified minimum amount of insurance coverage for events;
    and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} require that an aviation industry participant or
    any beneficiary under an insurance policy held by an aviation
    industry participant enter, at the Minister's request, into an
    agreement with the Minister respecting the conduct or settlement of
    any proceedings to which the participant or beneficiary is a party
    that are related to loss, damage or liability covered by the
    undertaking.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Different terms

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In attaching terms to an undertaking, the Minister
  may distinguish among aviation industry participants or beneficiaries,
  including among aviation industry participants or beneficiaries of the
  same class, and may distinguish among classes of aviation industry
  participants or beneficiaries.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An undertaking may incorporate any material by
  reference, regardless of its source, as it exists on a particular
  date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No delegation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must personally exercise the powers that
  are set out in this section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does not apply to an
  undertaking.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must cause an undertaking, an amended
  undertaking or a notice of revocation of an undertaking to be
  published in Part I of the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/)
  within 23 days after the issuance, amendment or revocation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply to an undertaking, an
  amended undertaking or a revocation of an undertaking to indemnify NAV
  CANADA, or any beneficiary under an insurance policy held by NAV
  CANADA, that covers only events referred to in paragraph (c) of the
  definition [[event]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for information

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** At any time after the
Minister issues an undertaking, he or she may request that aviation
industry participants --- or beneficiaries under an insurance policy
held by an aviation industry participant --- that are covered by the
undertaking provide him or her with any information that he or she
specifies, including with respect to those aviation industry
participants' eligibility and their existing level of insurance coverage
for events covered by the undertaking.

## [Claim for Indemnification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-7386 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of potential claim

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In order
  to submit a claim for indemnification with respect to an event, an
  aviation industry participant or beneficiary under an insurance policy
  held by that participant must provide the Minister with a written
  notice of a potential claim within two years after the day on which
  the event that could give rise to a claim occurred.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be provided

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The aviation industry participant or beneficiary must
  provide the Minister with any additional information that the Minister
  considers necessary in the circumstances.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 29, s. 2 "6"
- [2019, c. 29, s. 285](#1156563-1170840){.wb-lbx

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claim and indemnification

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After an
  aviation industry participant or beneficiary under an insurance policy
  held by that participant submits a written claim for indemnification
  under an undertaking, the Minister must review the claim and --- if a
  notice was provided in accordance with section 6 and the Minister
  determines that the claim is eligible for indemnification under the
  undertaking as it read on the day on which the event that gave rise to
  the claim occurred --- indemnify the participant or beneficiary
  accordingly.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The aviation industry participant or beneficiary must
  provide the Minister with any additional information that the Minister
  considers necessary to determine if the claim is eligible for
  indemnification.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming  --- single event

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister considers that two or more events are
  directly connected by any factor or factors, including timing, cause
  or the parties involved, he or she may deem the events to be a single
  event.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reliance on court or tribunal

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, in determining if a claim is
  eligible for indemnification, rely on a final determination, not
  subject to any appeal, of a court or other tribunal, in or outside
  Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fault

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Minister is not required
  to indemnify an aviation industry participant or beneficiary if the
  Minister is of the opinion that the loss, damage or liability claimed
  is principally the fault of that aviation industry participant or
  beneficiary, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Minister's determination
  of whether the claim is eligible for indemnification includes a
  determination of the amount, if any, of the indemnification.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consolidated Revenue Fund

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Any amount payable under an
undertaking is to be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subrogation

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Her
  Majesty in right of Canada is subrogated, to the extent of a payment
  under an undertaking to an aviation industry participant or
  beneficiary under an insurance policy held by that participant, to all
  of the participant's or beneficiary's rights in respect of the loss,
  damage or liability for which the payment was made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Canada may maintain an
  action, in the aviation industry participant's or beneficiary's name
  or in the name of Her Majesty, against any person to enforce those
  rights.

## [Assessment and Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-7412 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must, at
least once every two years, assess whether it is feasible for aviation
industry participants to obtain insurance coverage, or other similar
coverage, for events referred to in paragraph (a) or (b) of the
definition [[event]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must prepare a report on his or her activities under this Act
  within

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 90 days after the day on which he or she issued,
    amended or revoked an undertaking; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} two years after the day on which a report was last
    tabled, if he or she has not, within that period, issued, amended or
    revoked an undertaking.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must only report on his or her
  activities with respect to an undertaking covering an event referred
  to in paragraph (c) of the definition [[event]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
  in section 2 if he or she issues, amends or revokes such an
  undertaking.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling in Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must cause each report to be tabled in
  each House of Parliament within the first 15 days on which the House
  is sitting after the report is prepared.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-7422 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Governor in Council

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, on the Minister's recommendation, make regulations for the purpose
of this Act, including regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing an entity or class of entity for the
  purpose of the definition [[aviation industry
  participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the circumstances in which an undertaking
  may cover only one aviation industry participant.
