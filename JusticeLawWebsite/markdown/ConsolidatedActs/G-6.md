## Government Property Traffic Act {#government-property-traffic-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. G-6
:::

An Act for the control of traffic on Government property
::::

::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-251831 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Government Property Traffic Act](/eng/acts/G-6).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-10, s. 1
:::

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-251836 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations for the control of traffic on
  any lands belonging to or occupied by Her Majesty in right of Canada
  and, in particular, but without restricting the generality of the
  foregoing, may make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} regulating the speed and parking of vehicles and
    prescribing routes of travel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting one-way traffic, obstruction of traffic
    and pedestrian traffic;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for directing traffic and erecting signs;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prohibiting traffic by such vehicles, at such
    times, in such places and in such circumstances, as may be
    prescribed in the regulations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} prohibiting unnecessary noise in the vicinity of
    buildings;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} authorizing officers to enforce the regulations;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} prescribing a fine not exceeding five hundred
    dollars or a term of imprisonment not exceeding six months, or both,
    to be imposed on summary conviction as punishment for the
    contravention of any regulation; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} providing for the voluntary payment of fines and
    for prohibiting persons who have contravened any regulation from
    driving a vehicle on the lands for any period not exceeding one
    year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classification of vehicles

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may classify vehicles
  according to dimensions, design, use, weight, kind or otherwise and
  may make regulations under subsection (1) with respect to any or all
  such class or classes.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. G-6, s. 2
- 1992, c. 47, s. 72.1
- 1996, c. 7, s. 38
:::

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-251852 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of owner

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  vehicle is operated or parked in contravention of any regulation, the
  owner of the vehicle is liable to the punishment prescribed by the
  regulations for the contravention unless, at the time of the
  contravention, the vehicle was not operated or parked, as the case may
  be, by the owner or by any other person with the owner's consent,
  express or implied.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No relief from liability for operating
  or parking

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section relieves a person who
  operates or parks a vehicle in contravention of a regulation from the
  punishment prescribed for the contravention.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-10, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** In any prosecution for a
contravention of a regulation, a certificate stating that Her Majesty in
right of Canada is the owner or occupant of the lands described therein
and purporting to be signed by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of Public Works and Government Services
  or the Deputy, Assistant Deputy or Acting Deputy of the Minister of
  Public Works and Government Services,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the minister having the administration of the lands
  or the deputy, assistant deputy or acting deputy of that minister, or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the officer or person having custody of the documents
  of title or other appropriate records,

is evidence that the lands belong to or are occupied by Her Majesty in
right of Canada, as the case may be, without proof of the signature or
official character of the person appearing to have signed the
certificate and without further proof.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. G-6, s. 4
- 1991, c. 50, s. 29
- 1999, c. 31, s. 126
:::
