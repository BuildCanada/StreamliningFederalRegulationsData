## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-440259 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Status of the Artist Act](/eng/acts/S-19.6).

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[General Principles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-440262 .Part}

### [Proclamation and Policy concerning the Status of the Artist]{.HTitleText2} {#h-440263 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proclamation

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
hereby recognizes

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the importance of the contribution of artists to the
  cultural, social, economic and political enrichment of Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the importance to Canadian society of conferring on
  artists a status that reflects their primary role in developing and
  enhancing Canada's artistic and cultural life, and in sustaining
  Canada's quality of life;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the role of the artist, in particular to express the
  diverse nature of the Canadian way of life and the individual and
  collective aspirations of Canadians;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} that artistic creativity is the engine for the growth
  and prosperity of dynamic cultural industries in Canada; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the importance to artists that they be compensated
  for the use of their works, including the public lending of them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Policy statement

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Canada's policy on the
professional status of the artist, as implemented by the Minister of
Canadian Heritage, is based on the following rights:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the right of artists and producers to freedom of
  association and expression;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the right of associations representing artists to be
  recognized in law and to promote the professional and socio-economic
  interests of their members; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the right of artists to have access to advisory
  forums in which they may express their views on their status and on
  any other questions concerning them.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Professional Relations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-440279 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-440280 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

[[artist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[artist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an independent contractor
    described in paragraph 6(2)(b); ([artiste]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[artists' association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[artists' association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any organization,
    or a branch or local thereof, that has among its objectives the
    management or promotion of the professional and socio-economic
    interests of artists who are members of the organization, and
    includes a federation of artists' associations; ([association
    d'artistes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Industrial Relations
    Board established by section 9 of the [Canada Labour
    Code](/eng/acts/L-2). ([Conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Labour; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the entering into, renewal or
      revision of a scale agreement or in respect of a difference in
      relation to the interpretation, application, administration or
      alleged breach thereof, the producer or the artists' association,
      and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a complaint made to the Board, the
      complainant or the person or organization that is the object of
      the complaint; ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pressure tactic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pressure tactic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a cessation of work or a refusal to work or to
      continue to work by artists or artists' associations in
      combination, in concert or in accordance with a common
      understanding, and a slowdown of work or other concerted activity
      by artists or artists' associations respecting the provision of
      their services, done to compel a producer to agree to terms or
      conditions of engagement, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the closing of a place of work, a suspension of
      production or a refusal to continue the engagement of one or more
      artists by a producer, done to compel artists, or to assist
      another producer to compel artists, to agree to terms or
      conditions of engagement; ([moyen de pression]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a government institution or
    broadcasting undertaking described in paragraph 6(2)(a), and
    includes an association of producers; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[scale agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[scale agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement in
    writing between a producer and an artists' association respecting
    minimum terms and conditions for the provision of artists' services
    and other related matters; ([accord-cadre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2012, c. 19, s.
    532\]]{.Repealed}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-440298 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Part is binding on Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Part applies

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the following organizations that engage one or
    more artists to provide an artistic production, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} government institutions listed in Schedule I to
      the [Access to Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1) or the schedule to
      the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21), or prescribed by regulation,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} broadcasting undertakings, regulated under the
      [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01), that are [[federal works,
      undertakings or businesses]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
      section 2 of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2), or that are
      corporations established to perform any function or duty on behalf
      of the Government of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to independent contractors determined to be
    professionals according to the criteria set out in paragraph 18(b),
    and who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are authors of artistic, dramatic, literary or
      musical works within the meaning of the [Copyright
      Act](/eng/acts/C-42), or directors responsible for the overall
      direction of audiovisual works,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} perform, sing, recite, direct or act, in any
      manner, in a musical, literary or dramatic work, or in a circus,
      variety, mime or puppet show, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} contribute to the creation of any production in
      the performing arts, music, dance and variety entertainment, film,
      radio and television, video, sound-recording, dubbing or the
      recording of commercials, arts and crafts, or visual arts, and
      fall within a professional category prescribed by regulation.

### [Purpose]{.HTitleText2} {#h-440311 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Part is
to establish a framework to govern professional relations between
artists and producers that guarantees their freedom of association,
recognizes the importance of their respective contributions to the
cultural life of Canada and ensures the protection of their rights.

### [Freedom of Association]{.HTitleText2} {#h-440314 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Freedom

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An artist is free to join
an artists' association and to participate in its formation, activities
and administration.

### [Exclusions and Presumptions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-440317 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Artist contracting through an
organization

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  artist is not excluded from the application of this Part simply by
  contracting through an organization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption for purposes of
  [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 4(1) of the
  [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} artists' associations certified under this Part
    that were formed for the purpose of providing appropriate protection
    for the professional interests of the artists they represent are
    deemed to be combinations of employees, in relation to those
    activities of the association that are directed to achieving that
    purpose; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contracts, agreements or arrangements between or
    among two or more producers, whether made directly between or among
    them or through a corporation or an association of producers,
    pertaining to bargaining in respect of remuneration and the terms
    and conditions of engagement of artists, are deemed to be contracts,
    agreements or arrangements.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employees excluded

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} This Part does not apply, in respect of work
  undertaken in the course of employment, to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} employees, within the meaning of the [Federal
    Public Sector Labour Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-33.3), including
    those determined to be employees by the Federal Public Sector Labour
    Relations and Employment Board, and members of a bargaining unit
    that is certified by that Board; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} employees, within the meaning of Part I of the
    [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2), including those determined to
    be employees by the Board, and members of a bargaining unit that is
    certified by the Board.

#### [Powers]{.HTitleText3} {#h-440338 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Board may make any
regulations that it considers conducive to the performance of its duties
under this Part, and in particular regulations providing for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the practice and procedure before the Board,
  including the assessment and awarding of costs;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the certification of artists' associations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the conduct of representation votes;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the period for submission by an artists' association
  of a new application for certification, if the Board previously
  refused to certify the association in respect of the same or
  substantially the same sector;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the period for submission of an application for
  revocation of the certification of an artists' association, if the
  Board previously refused an application for revocation in respect of
  the same sector;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the forms to be used in any proceeding that may come
  before the Board;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the periods in which and the circumstances under
  which the Board may exercise its powers under section 20;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the period and form in which evidence and information
  may be presented to the Board in connection with any proceeding before
  it;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the period for sending notices and other documents,
  the persons and associations to which they shall be sent, and the
  circumstances in which they are deemed to have been sent or received;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the criteria for deciding whether an artist is
  represented by an artists' association;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the circumstances in which the Board may receive
  evidence in order to establish whether any artists wish to be
  represented by a particular artists' association, and the
  circumstances in which that evidence may not be made public; and

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} the delegation to any person of the Board's powers
  and duties, other than the power to delegate or to make regulations,
  and that person's obligations with respect to those powers and duties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board's powers

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Board may, in
relation to any proceeding before it under this Part,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on application or of its own motion, summon and
  enforce the attendance of any person whose testimony is necessary, in
  the Board's opinion, and compel the person to give oral or written
  evidence on oath and to produce any documents or things that the Board
  considers necessary for the full investigation and consideration of
  any matter within its jurisdiction;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} administer oaths and solemn affirmations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} accept any evidence and information that it sees fit,
  on oath, by affidavit or otherwise, whether or not the evidence is
  admissible in a court of law;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} examine any evidence that is submitted to the Board
  respecting the membership of any artist in an artists' association
  that is seeking certification;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} examine documents pertaining to the constitution,
  articles of association or by-laws of an artists' association;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} make any examination of records and any inquiries
  that it considers necessary;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} require a producer or an artists' association to post
  in appropriate places and keep posted a notice concerning any matter
  relating to the proceeding that the Board considers necessary to bring
  to the attention of artists;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} order, at any time before the conclusion of the
  proceeding, that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a representation vote or an additional
    representation vote be taken among artists affected by the
    proceeding, whether or not a representation vote is provided for
    elsewhere in this Part, if the Board considers that the vote would
    assist it to decide any question that has arisen or is likely to
    arise in the proceeding, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the ballots cast in that representation vote be
    sealed in ballot boxes and counted only as directed by the Board;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} authorize any person to do anything that the Board
  may do under paragraphs (a) to (h), and to report to the Board on it;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} adjourn or postpone the proceeding;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} abridge or extend the time for instituting the
  proceeding or for doing any act, filing any document or presenting any
  evidence;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} amend or permit the amendment of any document filed;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} add any person to the proceeding at any stage
  thereof;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} set requirements for public notice in respect of any
  application made under this Part;

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} award costs; and

- [(p)]{.lawlabel} decide any question that arises in the proceeding,
  including whether

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a person is a producer or an artist,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an artist is a member of, or is represented by, an
    artists' association,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an organization constitutes an association of
    producers, an artists' association, or a federation of artists'
    associations,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a group of artists constitutes a sector suitable
    for bargaining,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a scale agreement has been entered into or is in
    force, and the dates that it comes into force and expires, and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} any person or organization is a party to or is
    bound by a scale agreement.

#### [Criteria for Application]{.HTitleText3} {#h-440383 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria for application by Board

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall take into
account

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in deciding any question under this Part, the
  applicable principles of labour law; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in determining whether an independent contractor is a
  professional for the purposes of paragraph 6(2)(b), whether the
  independent contractor

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is paid for the display or presentation of that
    independent contractor's work before an audience, and is recognized
    to be an artist by other artists,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is in the process of becoming an artist according
    to the practice of the artistic community, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is a member of an artists' association.

#### [Proceedings]{.HTitleText3} {#h-440393 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informal proceedings

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In any
  proceeding before it under this Part, the Board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall proceed as informally and expeditiously as
    the circumstances and considerations of fairness permit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not bound by legal or technical rules of
    evidence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} may receive and decide on any evidence adduced that
    the Board believes to be credible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In order to ensure that the purpose of this Part is
  achieved, the members of the Board may, in respect of any matter
  before it, consult with other members or with employees of the
  Administrative Tribunals Support Service of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to appear

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any interested person may intervene in a proceeding
  before the Board with its permission, and anyone appearing before the
  Board may be represented by counsel or an agent or mandatary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of facts

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board may take notice of facts that may be
  judicially noticed and, subject to subsection (5), of any other
  generally recognized facts and any information that is within its
  specialized knowledge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of intention

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall notify the parties and any intervenor
  in the proceeding before it of its intention to take notice of any
  facts or information, other than facts that may be judicially noticed,
  and afford them an opportunity to make representations in that regard.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report on evidence

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson of the Board may direct any member to
  receive evidence relating to a matter before the Board, to make a
  report on it to the Board, and to provide a copy of the report to all
  parties and any intervenor in the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conclusions

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} After granting all parties and intervenors an
  opportunity to make representations on any report made under
  subsection (6), the Board may make a determination on the basis of the
  report or hold any further hearings that it considers necessary in the
  circumstances.

#### [Review and Enforcement of Determinations and Orders]{.HTitleText3} {#h-440421 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination or order --- no review by
court

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this Part, every
  determination or order of the Board is final and shall not be
  questioned or reviewed in any court, except in accordance with the
  [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7) on the grounds referred to in
  paragraph 18.1(4)(a), (b) or (e) of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No review by [certiorari]{.otherLang
  lang="la"}, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as permitted by subsection (1), no
  determination, order or proceeding made or carried on, or purporting
  to be made or carried on, by the Board under this Part shall be
  questioned, reviewed, prohibited or restrained on any ground,
  including the ground that the Board did not have jurisdiction or
  exceeded or lost its jurisdiction, or be made the subject of any
  proceeding in or any process of any court on any such ground, whether
  by way of injunction, [certiorari]{.otherLang lang="la"}, prohibition,
  [quo warranto]{.otherLang lang="la"}, revision, evocation or
  otherwise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing in Federal Court

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  application in writing by any party or of its own motion, the Board
  shall file a copy of a determination or order, exclusive of the
  reasons for it, in the Federal Court unless, in the Board's opinion,
  there is no indication of failure or likelihood of failure to comply
  with it, or there is no useful purpose to be served by filing it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Board specifies in writing that it is filing a
  copy of a determination or order under subsection (1), the Federal
  Court shall accept it for filing and shall register it without further
  application or other proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of registration

### [Bargaining and Scale Agreements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-440534 .Subheading}

#### [Notice to Bargain]{.HTitleText3} {#h-440535 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to bargain to enter into a scale
agreement

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an artists' association is certified in respect of a sector, the
  association or a producer may issue a notice requiring the other party
  to begin bargaining for the purpose of entering into a scale
  agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to bargain to renew or revise a
  scale agreement or enter into a new scale agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a scale agreement is in force, either party
  may, in the three months immediately preceding the date that the
  agreement expires or within any longer period stipulated in the
  agreement, issue a notice to the other party to begin bargaining in
  order to renew or revise it or to enter into a new scale agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to bargain

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An association substituted as a party to a scale
  agreement pursuant to paragraph 28(5)(c) may, within six months after
  the date of its certification, issue a notice requiring the producer
  that is a party to the agreement to begin bargaining for the purpose
  of renewing or revising it or entering into a new scale agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revision during term

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a scale agreement provides for revision during
  its term, a party entitled to do so by the agreement may give notice
  to the other party to begin bargaining in order to revise any
  provision of the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any party that issues a notice to the other party to
  begin bargaining shall send a copy of the notice to the Minister
  without delay.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to bargain and not to change terms
and conditions

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** Where a notice to begin
bargaining has been issued under section 31,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the artists' association and the producer shall
  without delay, but in any case within twenty days after the notice was
  issued, unless they otherwise agree,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} meet, or send authorized representatives to meet,
    and begin to bargain in good faith, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} make every reasonable effort to enter into a scale
    agreement; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the producer shall not alter, without the consent of
  the artists' association, any term or condition of engagement,
  including the rates of remuneration, or any right or privilege of an
  artist or the association, that is contained in the scale agreement,
  until such time as pressure tactics are permitted under section 46.

#### [Duration and Effect of Scale Agreements]{.HTitleText3} {#h-440553 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of scale agreements

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the term set out in it, a scale agreement binds the parties to it and
  every artist in the sector engaged by the producer, and neither party
  may terminate the agreement without the Board's approval, except when
  a notice to bargain is issued under subsection 31(3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scale agreement to be filed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The parties to a scale agreement shall file a copy of
  the agreement with the Minister without delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Association of producers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A scale agreement entered into by an association of
  producers binds, even in the event that the association is dissolved,
  each producer that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a member of the association at the time the
    agreement is signed and did not give the parties notice of
    withdrawal before the agreement was signed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} not being a party to any other scale agreement in
    respect of the same sector, subsequently becomes a member of that
    association; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} withdraws from membership in the association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving more favourable benefits

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A scale agreement applies notwithstanding any
  inconsistency with a contract between an artist and a producer, but it
  shall not be applied so as to deprive an artist of a right or benefit
  under the contract that is more favourable to the artist than is
  provided for under the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall assess what is more favourable to the
  artist under subsection (4) in relation to each right or benefit and
  shall compare the elements of each right or benefit under the scale
  agreement with the elements of each under the contract.

#### [Content and Interpretation of Scale Agreements]{.HTitleText3} {#h-440576 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision for settlement without pressure
tactics

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  scale agreement must contain a provision for final settlement without
  pressure tactics, by arbitration or otherwise, of all differences
  between the parties or among artists bound by the agreement,
  concerning its interpretation, application, administration or alleged
  contravention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where arbitrator to be appointed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything in the scale agreement, a
  difference between the parties to an agreement that does not contain
  the provision for final settlement required by subsection (1) shall be
  submitted for final settlement

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to an arbitrator selected by the parties; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the parties are unable to agree on an
    arbitrator and either party makes a written request to the Minister
    to appoint one, to the arbitrator appointed by the Minister after
    any inquiry that the Minister considers necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission of difference to arbitration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything in the scale agreement, a
  difference between the parties to an agreement that contains a
  provision for final settlement by an arbitration board shall, if
  either party fails to name its nominee to the board, be submitted for
  final settlement to an arbitrator in accordance with subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request to Minister to appoint
  arbitrator or arbitration board chairperson

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a scale agreement contains a provision for
  final settlement without pressure tactics of differences described in
  subsection (1) by an arbitrator or arbitration board and the parties
  cannot agree on the selection of the arbitrator or arbitration board
  chairperson, either party or its nominee may, notwithstanding anything
  in the agreement, make a written request to the Minister to appoint
  the arbitrator or arbitration board chairperson, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment by Minister

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a request made under subsection (4),
  the Minister shall appoint an arbitrator or arbitration board
  chairperson, after any inquiry that the Minister considers necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} An arbitrator or arbitration board chairperson
  appointed pursuant to subsection (2), (3) or (5) is deemed to be
  appointed in accordance with the scale agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determinations not to be reviewed by
court

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  determination of an arbitrator or arbitration board is final and shall
  not be questioned or reviewed in any court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No review by [certiorari]{.otherLang
  lang="la"}, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No order shall be made or proceeding taken in any
  court, by way of injunction, [certiorari]{.otherLang lang="la"},
  prohibition, [quo warranto]{.otherLang lang="la"}, revision, evocation
  or otherwise, to question, review, prohibit or restrain an arbitrator
  or arbitration board in any proceedings under this Part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of arbitrator or arbitration
  board

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the [Federal Courts
  Act](/eng/acts/F-7), an arbitrator or an arbitration board appointed
  pursuant to a scale agreement or this Part is not a federal board,
  commission or other tribunal within the meaning of that Act.

### [Prohibitions and Remedies]{.HTitleText2} {#h-440644 .Subheading}

#### [Pressure Tactics]{.HTitleText3} {#h-440645 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When pressure tactics are permitted

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** No artist, artists'
association or producer shall participate in, authorize or apply
pressure tactics except during the period

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} beginning thirty days after a scale agreement binding
  the producer and the artists' association expires and ending on the
  day that a new agreement is entered into in respect of that sector; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} beginning six months after the date of certification
  of an artists' association and ending on the day that a scale
  agreement is entered into, where there is no scale agreement binding
  the producer and the artists' association in respect of that sector.

#### [Declarations respecting Pressure Tactics]{.HTitleText3} {#h-440650 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration --- association's pressure
tactics unlawful

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  producer alleges that an artists' association has authorized or
  applied pressure tactics, or that artists have participated, are
  participating or are likely to participate in pressure tactics, as a
  result of which an artist was, is or would be in contravention of this
  Part, the producer may apply to the Board for a declaration that the
  pressure tactics are unlawful.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration and prohibition of pressure
  tactics

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an application is made under subsection (1), the
  Board may, after affording the artists and the artists' association an
  opportunity to be heard, declare the pressure tactics to be unlawful
  and, if the producer so requests, make an order

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} requiring the association to cease or revoke its
    authorization of the pressure tactics;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} enjoining artists from participating in those
    pressure tactics and requiring them, where applicable, to resume the
    work for which they were engaged; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} requiring an artists' association or any officer or
    representative of an association of which any artist subject to an
    order made under paragraph (b) is a member, to give notice of the
    order to all artists in the sector who are members of the
    association.

#### [Unfair Practices]{.HTitleText3} {#h-440674 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibitions relating to producers

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** No producer or person
acting on behalf of a producer shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} refuse to engage an artist or to honour an artist's
  contract, or discriminate against an artist with respect to
  engagement, remuneration or any other term or condition of engagement,
  or intimidate, threaten or discipline an artist, because the artist

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is or proposes to become, or seeks to induce any
    other person to become, a member, officer or representative of an
    artists' association, or participates in the promotion, formation or
    administration of an artists' association,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has testified or participated in a proceeding
    under this Part, or may do so,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has made or is about to make a disclosure that
    may be required in a proceeding under this Part,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} has made an application or filed a complaint under
    this Part,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} has exercised any right under this Part or
    participated in pressure tactics that are not prohibited by it, or

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} has been expelled or suspended from membership in
    an artists' association for a reason other than a failure to pay the
    periodic dues, assessments and initiation fees uniformly required to
    be paid by all members of the association as a condition of
    acquiring or retaining membership;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} impose any condition in a contract of engagement that
  prevents or has the effect of preventing an artist from exercising
  rights under this Part;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} terminate an artist's contract, or impose a financial
  or other penalty or take disciplinary action against an artist,
  because the artist refuses to perform any of the duties of another
  artist who is participating in or is subject to pressure tactics that
  are not prohibited by this Part;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} seek by intimidation, threat of termination of a
  contract, imposition of a financial or other penalty, or by any other
  means, to compel a person to refrain from becoming or to cease to be a
  member, officer or representative of an artists' association, or to
  refrain from

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} testifying or participating in a proceeding under
    this Part,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} making a disclosure that may be required in a
    proceeding under this Part, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} making an application or filing a complaint under
    this Part;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} terminate the contract of, or impose any financial or
  other penalty on, an artist engaged by the producer, or take any
  disciplinary action because of the artist's refusal to perform an act
  that is prohibited by this Part; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} bargain for the purpose of entering into a scale
  agreement, or enter into a scale agreement, with an artists'
  association in respect of a sector, if the producer knows or, in the
  Board's opinion, ought to know that another artists' association is
  certified in respect of that sector.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibitions relating to artists'
associations

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** No certified artists'
association or person acting on behalf of such an association shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} bargain for the purpose of entering into a scale
  agreement, or enter into a scale agreement with a producer in respect
  of a sector, if the association or person knows or, in the Board's
  opinion, ought to know that another artists' association is certified
  in respect of that sector;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} bargain for the purposes of entering into a scale
  agreement, or enter into a scale agreement in respect of a sector,
  with a producer that the association knows or, in the Board's opinion,
  ought to know is represented by an association of producers that has
  filed its membership list under subsection 24(2);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} require a producer to terminate the contract of an
  artist engaged by the producer because the artist has been expelled or
  suspended from membership in the association for a reason other than a
  failure to pay the periodic dues, assessments and initiation fees
  uniformly required to be paid by all members of the association as a
  condition of acquiring or retaining membership;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} take disciplinary action against or impose any form
  of penalty on an artist by applying the standards of discipline of the
  association to that artist in a discriminatory manner;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} expel or suspend an artist from membership in the
  association, or take disciplinary action or impose any penalty against
  the artist, for refusal to perform an act that is contrary to this
  Part;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} discriminate unfairly against an artist with respect
  to becoming or continuing as a member of the association or being
  expelled from it, if the parties have included in a scale agreement a
  provision that requires membership in a specified artists' association
  as a condition of engagement, or that grants a preference in
  engagement to such members; or

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} intimidate, coerce or impose a financial or other
  penalty on an artist, because the artist

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has testified or participated in a proceeding under
    this Part, or may do so,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has made or is about to make a disclosure that may
    be required in a proceeding under this Part, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has made an application or filed a complaint
    under this Part.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intimidation or coercion prohibited

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** No person shall seek by
intimidation or coercion to compel any person or association to become
or refrain from becoming or to cease to be a member of an artists'
association or an association of producers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaints to Board

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person or organization may make a complaint in writing to the Board
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a producer, a person acting on behalf of a
    producer, an artists' association, a person acting on behalf of an
    artists' association, or an artist has contravened or failed to
    comply with section 32, 35, 50 or 51; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person has failed to comply with section 52.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time for making complaint

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The complaint shall be made to the Board within six
  months after the day on which the complainant knew or, in the Board's
  opinion, ought to have known of the action or circumstances giving
  rise to the complaint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inadmissible complaints

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall hear the complaint unless the Board
  is of the opinion that it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is moot, or is frivolous, vexatious or in bad
    faith; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not within the Board's jurisdiction, or could be
    referred by the complainant to an arbitrator or arbitration board,
    under a scale agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board's duty and power

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Board is of the opinion that the complaint
  must be heard, it may appoint a member who was never seized of the
  matter, or an employee of the Administrative Tribunals Support Service
  of Canada, to assist the parties to settle it and, if the matter is
  not settled within a period that the Board considers reasonable in the
  circumstances, or if the Board decides not to appoint a person to
  assist the parties to settle it, the Board shall hear and determine
  the complaint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Burden of proof

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A written complaint that a producer or any person
  acting on behalf of a producer failed to comply with section 50 is
  itself evidence that the failure actually occurred and, if any party
  to the complaint proceeding alleges that the failure did not occur,
  the burden of proof thereof is on that party.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board may make orders

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Board determines that a party to a complaint failed to comply with
  section 32, 35, 50, 51 or 52, the Board may order the party to comply
  with or to cease contravening that section and may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a failure to comply with paragraph
    32(b), order a producer to pay the artist compensation not exceeding
    the amount of remuneration that would, but for that failure, have
    been paid to the artist, in the Board's opinion, under the scale
    agreement or the artist's contract;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a failure to comply with section 35,
    require an artists' association to pursue the rights and remedies of
    any artist affected by that failure, or to assist the artist to
    pursue any rights and remedies that, in the Board's opinion, it was
    the duty of the association to pursue;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a failure to comply with paragraph
    50(a), (c) or (e), order a producer

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to engage or to continue to engage, if possible,
      an artist who has been dealt with in a manner prohibited by that
      paragraph,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to pay to any artist affected by that failure
      compensation not exceeding the amount of remuneration that would,
      but for that failure, have been paid to that artist, in the
      Board's opinion, under the scale agreement or the contract, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to rescind any disciplinary action taken
      against any artist affected by that failure, and pay the artist
      compensation not exceeding the amount, in the Board's opinion, of
      any financial or other penalty provided for in the scale agreement
      or the contract and imposed on the artist by the producer;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a failure to comply with paragraph
    50(d), order a producer to rescind any action taken against any
    artist affected by that failure, and pay the artist compensation not
    exceeding the amount, in the Board's opinion, of any financial or
    other penalty provided for in the scale agreement or the contract
    and imposed on the artist by the producer;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a failure to comply with paragraph
    51(d), order an artists' association to reinstate or admit the
    artist as a member; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a failure to comply with paragraph
    51(d), (e), (f) or (g), order an artists' association to rescind any
    disciplinary action taken against any artist affected by that
    failure, and pay the artist compensation not exceeding the amount,
    in the Board's opinion, of the artist's actual loss or of any
    financial or other penalty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other orders

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In order to ensure that the purpose of this Part is
  achieved, the Board may, in addition to or in lieu of any other order
  authorized under subsection (1), order a producer or an artists'
  association to do or refrain from doing anything that it is equitable
  to require of them, so as to counteract or remedy the contravention of
  or non-compliance with a provision referred to in that subsection.

#### [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText3} {#h-440754 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and penalty

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  every person who contravenes or fails to comply with any provision of
  this Part other than sections 32, 50 and 51 is guilty of an offence
  and liable to a fine not exceeding five thousand dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every artist, artists' association or producer that
  contravenes section 46 is guilty of an offence and liable to a fine

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} not exceeding two thousand dollars, in the case of
    an artist;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} not exceeding fifty thousand dollars, in the case
    of an officer, employee, director, agent or advisor of a certified
    artists' association or director, agent or advisor of a producer; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} not exceeding one hundred thousand dollars, in the
    case of a producer or a certified artists' association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further offences

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} being required to attend to give evidence pursuant
    to paragraph 17(a), fails to attend accordingly,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} being compelled to produce, pursuant to paragraph
    17(a), any document or thing in the person's possession or under the
    person's control, fails to produce the document or thing,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} refuses to be sworn or to affirm, as the case may
    be, after being required to be sworn or affirmed pursuant to
    paragraph 17(a), or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} refuses to answer any proper question put to them
    under paragraph 17(a) by the Board or one of its members or by an
    arbitrator or an arbitration board

  is guilty of an offence and liable to a fine not exceeding four
  hundred dollars.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2012, c. 19, s. 564

  - ###### Definitions {#definitions .MarginalNote}

    [564]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in sections 565 to
    570.

    [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Industrial
        Relations Board established by section 9 of the [Canada Labour
        Code](/eng/acts/L-2). ([Conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    [[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Artists and
        Producers Professional Relations Tribunal established by
        subsection 10(1) of the [Status of the Artist
        Act](/eng/acts/S-19.6), as that Act read immediately before the
        coming into force of this section. ([Tribunal]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

- --- 2012, c. 19, s. 565

  - ###### Appointments terminated {#appointments-terminated .MarginalNote}

    - [565]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Members of the Tribunal cease to
      hold office on the coming into force of this subsection.

    - ###### No compensation {#no-compensation .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite the provisions of any contract, agreement
      or order, no person appointed to hold office as a member of the
      Tribunal has any right to claim or receive any compensation,
      damages, indemnity or other form of relief from Her Majesty in
      right of Canada or from any employee or agent of Her Majesty for
      ceasing to hold that office or for the abolition of that office by
      the operation of this Division.

- --- 2012, c. 19, s. 566

  - ###### Continuation of proceedings {#continuation-of-proceedings .MarginalNote}

    [566]{.lawlabel} Every proceeding commenced under the [Status of the
    Artist Act](/eng/acts/S-19.6) before the coming into force of this
    section is to be taken up and continued under and in conformity with
    that Act, as it is amended by this Act.

- --- 2012, c. 19, s. 567

  - ###### Review of Tribunal's determination or order {#review-of-tribunals-determination-or-order .MarginalNote}

    [567]{.lawlabel} The Board may uphold, rescind or amend any
    determination or order made by the Tribunal.

- --- 2012, c. 19, s. 568

  - ###### Powers and duties {#powers-and-duties .MarginalNote}

    - [568]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any power or duty that is vested
      in or is exercisable by the Tribunal under a contract, lease,
      licence, deed, agreement or other document is vested in or is
      exercisable by the Board.

    - ###### References {#references .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every reference to the Tribunal in any contract,
      lease, licence, deed, agreement or other document executed by the
      Tribunal in its own name shall, unless the context otherwise
      requires, be read as a reference to the Board.

- --- 2012, c. 19, s. 569

  - ###### Commencement of legal proceedings {#commencement-of-legal-proceedings .MarginalNote}

    [569]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal proceeding in
    respect of an obligation or liability incurred by the Tribunal may
    be brought against the Board in any court that would have had
    jurisdiction if the action, suit or other legal proceeding had been
    brought against the Tribunal.

- --- 2012, c. 19, s. 570

  - ###### Continuation of legal proceedings {#continuation-of-legal-proceedings .MarginalNote}

    [570]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal proceeding to which
    the Tribunal is a party that is pending in any court immediately
    before the day on which this section comes into force may be
    continued by or against the Board in the same manner and to the same
    extent as it could have been continued by or against the Tribunal.
