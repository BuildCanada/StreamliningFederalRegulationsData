## Nordion and Theratronics Divestiture Authorization Act {#nordion-and-theratronics-divestiture-authorization-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1990, c. 4
:::

Assented to 1990-01-30

An Act to authorize the divestiture of Nordion International Inc. and
Theratronics International Limited
::::

:::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365533 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Nordion and Theratronics Divestiture Authorization
Act](/eng/acts/N-23.7).

## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365536 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
      Privy Council for Canada as may be designated by the Governor in
      Council for the purposes of this Act; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Nordion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Nordion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Nordion International Inc.,
      a corporation incorporated under the [Canada Business Corporations
      Act](/eng/acts/C-44) or any successor to that
      corporation; ([Nordion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Theratronics]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Theratronics]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Theratronics
      International Limited, a corporation incorporated under the
      [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44) or any
      successor to that corporation. ([Theratronics]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Same meaning

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless a contrary intention appears, words and
  expressions used in this Act have the same meaning as in the [Canada
  Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operation of [Canada Business
  Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the event of any inconsistency between this Act
  and the [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44), or
  anything issued, made or established under that Act, this Act prevails
  to the extent of the inconsistency.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1990, c. 4, s. 2
- 1994, c. 24, s. 34(F)
- 2014, c. 20, s. 206

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on the Crown

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Authorization of Divestiture]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365551 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal of shares

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Canada Development Investment Corporation is hereby authorized to sell
  or otherwise dispose of, on such terms and conditions as are approved
  by the Governor in Council, any or all shares of Nordion and
  Theratronics held by the Canada Development Investment Corporation,
  and may enter into any agreement or arrangement necessary or
  incidental thereto.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision not applicable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 189(3) to (9) of the [Canada Business
  Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44) and subsections 99(2) to (5) of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) do not apply in respect
  of any sale or other disposal of shares authorized by subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1990, c. 4, s. 4
- 1994, c. 24, s. 34(F)
:::

## [Nordion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365559 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Articles of amendment

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Nordion
  shall prepare articles of amendment in accordance with section 6 and
  submit them to the Minister for approval within four months after this
  section comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission to Director

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Forthwith after the Minister approves articles of
  amendment submitted pursuant to subsection (1), Nordion shall send the
  approved articles to the Director.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Articles of amendment sent to the Director pursuant
  to this section are, subject to this Act, deemed for all purposes to
  have been sent under section 177 of the [Canada Business Corporations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-44).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1990, c. 4, s. 5
- 1994, c. 24, s. 34(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory provisions in articles of
amendment

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  articles of amendment of Nordion shall contain provisions imposing
  constraints on the issue, transfer and ownership, including joint
  ownership, of voting shares of Nordion to prevent non-residents,
  together with the associates thereof, from holding, beneficially
  owning or controlling, directly or indirectly, otherwise than by way
  of security only, in the aggregate voting shares to which are attached
  more than twenty-five per cent of the votes that may ordinarily be
  cast to elect directors of Nordion.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement of constraint provisions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The regulations made pursuant to subsection 174(6) of
  the [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44) apply, with
  such modifications as the circumstances require, in respect of Nordion
  as if the constraints imposed pursuant to subsection (1) were a
  constraint referred to in paragraph 174(1)(a) of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No provision imposing constraints pursuant to
  subsection (1) and no regulation referred to in subsection (2) apply
  in respect of voting shares of Nordion that are held

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by one or more underwriters solely for the purpose
    of distributing the shares to the public;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by any person providing centralized facilities for
    the clearing of trades in securities and acting in relation to
    trading in the shares solely as an intermediary in the payment of
    funds or the delivery of the shares, or both; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by any person if the acquisition of those shares by
    that person

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is an investment that, under sections 21 to 23 of
      the [Investment Canada Act](/eng/acts/I-21.8), is, or is deemed,
      likely to be of net benefit to Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is not prohibited under Part IV.1 of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception  --- subsequent holders

  [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} No provision imposing constraints under
  subsection (1) and no regulation referred to in subsection (2) apply
  in respect of any voting shares of Nordion referred to in paragraph
  (3)(c) that are subsequently held by any other person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Associates

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, a person is an
  associate of a non-resident if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one is a corporation of which the other is an
    officer or director;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one is a corporation that is controlled by the
    other or by a group of persons of which the other is a member;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one is a partnership of which the other is a
    partner;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} one is a trust of which the other is a trustee;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} both are corporations controlled by the same
    person;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} both are members of a voting trust that relates to
    voting shares of Nordion;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} both are parties to an agreement or arrangement, a
    purpose of which is to require them to act in concert with respect
    to their interests, direct or indirect, in Nordion; or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} both are at the same time associates, within the
    meaning of any of paragraphs (a) to (g), of the same non-resident.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (4), for the purposes of
  this section,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where a resident who, but for this paragraph, would
    be an associate of a non-resident submits to Nordion a statutory
    declaration stating that no voting shares of Nordion held or to be
    held by the resident are or will be, to the resident's knowledge,
    held in the right of, for the use or benefit of or under the control
    of any non-resident of which, but for this paragraph, the resident
    would be an associate, that resident and that non-resident are not
    associates so long as the voting shares held by the resident are not
    held contrary to the statements made in the declaration;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} two corporations are not associates pursuant to
    paragraph (4)(h) by reason only that pursuant to paragraph (4)(a)
    each is an associate of the same individual; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where it appears from the central securities
    register of Nordion that any person holds, beneficially owns or
    controls voting shares to which are attached not more than the
    lesser of two one-hundredths of one per cent of the votes that may
    ordinarily be cast to elect directors of Nordion and two thousand
    such votes, that person is not an associate of anyone else and no
    one else is an associate of that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a body corporate is controlled by a person if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} securities of the body corporate to which are
      attached more than fifty per cent of the votes that may be cast to
      elect directors of the body corporate are held, otherwise than by
      way of security only, by or for the benefit of that person, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the votes attached to those securities are
      sufficient, if exercised, to elect a majority of the directors of
      the body corporate; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a partnership or unincorporated organization is
    controlled by a person if an ownership interest therein representing
    more than fifty per cent of the assets of the partnership or
    organization is held, otherwise than by way of security only, by or
    for the benefit of that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In this section,

  [[corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a body corporate,
      partnership and unincorporated
      organization; ([société]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[non-resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[non-resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an individual, other than a Canadian citizen,
        who is not ordinarily resident in Canada,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation incorporated, formed or otherwise
        organized outside Canada,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a foreign government or an agency thereof,

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a corporation controlled by non-residents as
        defined in any of paragraphs (a) to (c),

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a trust

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} established by a non-resident as defined in
          any of paragraphs (b) to (d), other than a trust for the
          administration of a pension fund for the benefit of
          individuals a majority of whom are residents, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in which non-residents as defined in any of
          paragraphs (a) to (d) have more than fifty per cent of the
          beneficial interest, or

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that is controlled by a trust
        described in paragraph (e); ([non-résident]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an individual,
      corporation, government or agency thereof, trustee, executor,
      administrator and other legal
      representative; ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual, corporation,
      government or agency thereof or trust that is not a
      non-resident; ([résident]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[voting share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[voting share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a share carrying voting
      rights under all circumstances or under some circumstances that
      have occurred and are continuing, and includes a security
      currently convertible into such a share and currently exercisable
      options and rights to acquire such a share or such a convertible
      security. ([action avec droit de vote]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- 1990, c. 4, s. 6
- 1994, c. 24, s. 34(F)
- 2014, c. 20, s. 207

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on amendment

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Nordion
  and its shareholders and directors shall not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} apply for continuance of Nordion in another
    jurisdiction; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make any articles or by-laws that are inconsistent
    with the provisions included in its articles of amendment pursuant
    to subsection 6(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if a person holds
  voting shares referred to in paragraph 6(3)(c) or subsection 6(3.1).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1990, c. 4, s. 7
- 2014, c. 20, s. 208

## [Theratronics]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365630 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions applicable to Theratronics

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Sections 5 to 7, with the
exception of paragraph 6(3)(c) and subsections 6(3.1) and 7(2), apply to
Theratronics as if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the references therein to "Nordion" were references
  to "Theratronics"; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reference in subsection 6(1) to "twenty-five per
  cent" were a reference to "forty-nine per cent".

::: HistoricalNote
- 1990, c. 4, s. 8
- 2014, c. 20, s. 209

## [Transitional and Consequential]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365637 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pension rights

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  manner and to the extent provided by the regulations made under
  subsection (3), the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and the regulations made under that Act apply to
  a person who meets all of the following criteria:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person was employed by Nordion or Theratronics
    and was a contributor under that Act immediately preceding the date
    on which shares of Nordion or Theratronics, as the case may be, are
    first sold or otherwise disposed of pursuant to subsection 4(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the President of the Treasury Board has not made a
    payment to Nordion or Theratronics pursuant to section 40 of that
    Act in respect of that person;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person has not received or opted to receive any
    annuity or other benefit under section 12 or 13 of that Act in
    respect of the pensionable service to that person's credit under
    that Act immediately preceding the date referred to in paragraph
    (a); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person elects, within the later of one year
    after the coming into force of this section and one year after the
    date on which shares of Nordion or Theratronics, as the case may be,
    are first sold or otherwise disposed of pursuant to subsection 4(1),
    and in such form and manner as the President of the Treasury Board
    directs, to have that Act and the regulations made under that Act
    apply to that person in the manner and to the extent provided by the
    regulations made under subsection (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election irrevocable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An election referred to in paragraph (1)(d) is
  irrevocable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, in relation to persons
  referred to in paragraph (1)(d), make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the manner in which and extent to which
    provisions of the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and the regulations made under that Act apply;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} adapting provisions of that Act and those
    regulations for the purposes of this section; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} generally for carrying out the purposes and
    provisions of this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retroactive application of regulations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Regulations made under subsection (3) may, if they so
  provide, be retroactive and have effect with respect to any period
  before they are made.

- 1990, c. 4, s. 9
- 1992, c. 46, s. 90

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of [Public Service
Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36)

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding
subsection 42(4) of the [Public Service Superannuation
Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the Governor in Council may, by order, delete the
name of Nordion or Theratronics from Part I of Schedule I to that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shares qualified

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
qualifying the shares of Nordion

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} as an authorized investment under paragraph 86(n) of
  the Canadian and British Insurance Companies Act, paragraph 61(1)(j)
  of the Loan Companies Act or paragraph 78(1)(j) of the Trust Companies
  Act,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} as a permitted investment under paragraph 1(s) of
  Schedule III to the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} as assets that may be vested in trust in Canada under
  paragraph 1(n) of Schedule II to the Canadian and British Insurance
  Companies Act or paragraph 1(n) of the schedule to the Foreign
  Insurance Companies Act,

Nordion is deemed to have satisfied the requirements of those paragraphs
with respect to each of the five years immediately preceding the date on
which shares of Nordion are first sold or otherwise disposed of pursuant
to subsection 4(1).
