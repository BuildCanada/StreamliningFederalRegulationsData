## National Security and Intelligence Committee of Parliamentarians Act {#national-security-and-intelligence-committee-of-parliamentarians-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2017, c. 15
:::

Assented to 2017-06-22

An Act to establish the National Security and Intelligence Committee of
Parliamentarians and to make consequential amendments to certain Acts
::::

::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363648 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [National Security and Intelligence Committee of Parliamentarians
Act](/eng/acts/N-16.6).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363651 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a department named in Schedule I
      to the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the
      Minister presiding over the department;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a division or branch of the
      federal public administration set out in column I of Schedule I.1
      to the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the
      Minister set out in column II of that Schedule;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a corporation named in Schedule
      II to the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the
      Minister designated as the appropriate Minister by order of the
      Governor in Council made under that Act;

    - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a [[parent Crown
      corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 83(1)
      of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the
      [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in that
      subsection; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the Canadian Forces, the Minister
      of National Defence. ([ministre compétent]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the National Security and
    Intelligence Committee of Parliamentarians established by section
    4. ([Comité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, except in subsection
    25(2), a department named in Schedule I to the [Financial
    Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), a division or branch of the
    federal public administration --- other than a review body or the
    office of the Intelligence Commissioner --- set out in column I of
    Schedule I.1 to that Act, a corporation named in Schedule II to that
    Act, a [[parent Crown corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined
    in subsection 83(1) of that Act or the Canadian
    Forces. ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[review body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[review body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Civilian Review and Complaints Commission for
      the Royal Canadian Mounted Police established by subsection
      45.29(1) of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
      Act](/eng/acts/R-10); or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the National Security and Intelligence Review
      Agency. ([organisme de surveillance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2019, c. 13, s. 49\]]{.Repealed}

[[Secretariat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Secretariat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Secretariat of the
    National Security and Intelligence Committee of Parliamentarians
    established by subsection 24(1). ([Secrétariat]{.DefinedTermLink

## [Designation of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363667 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of Governor in Council

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
designate a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada to be the
Minister for the purposes of this Act.

## [Committee and Members]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363670 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committee established

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  National Security and Intelligence Committee of Parliamentarians is
  established, consisting of a Chair and up to 10 other members, each of
  whom must be a member of either House of Parliament other than a
  minister of the Crown, a minister of state or a parliamentary
  secretary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Committee is to consist of not more than three
  members who are members of the Senate and not more than eight members
  who are members of the House of Commons. Not more than five Committee
  members who are members of the House of Commons may be members of the
  government party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not a committee of Parliament

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Committee is not a committee of either House of
  Parliament or of both Houses.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of members

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  members of the Committee are to be appointed by the Governor in
  Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, to hold office
  during pleasure until the dissolution of Parliament following their
  appointment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nomination deadline

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} After a general election, the members of the
  Committee are to be appointed within 60 days after the day on which
  Parliament is summoned to sit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member of the Senate may be appointed to the
  Committee only after the Prime Minister has consulted with the Leader
  of the Government in the Senate or Government Representative in the
  Senate, the Leader of the Opposition in the Senate and the Leader or
  Facilitator of every other recognized party or parliamentary group in
  the Senate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members of other parties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A member of the House of Commons who belongs to a
  party that is not the government party and that has a recognized
  membership of 12 or more persons in that House may be appointed to the
  Committee only after the Prime Minister has consulted with the leader
  of that party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ceasing to be member

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A member of the Committee ceases to be a member on
  being appointed a minister of the Crown, a minister of state or a
  parliamentary secretary or on ceasing to be a member of the Senate or
  the House of Commons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resignation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A member may resign by notifying the Prime Minister
  in writing of their intention to resign and the member ceases to be a
  member on the day on which the Prime Minister receives the
  notification or on the date specified in the notification, whichever
  is later.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of Chair

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council is to designate the Chair of the Committee from
  among the members of the Committee, on the recommendation of the Prime
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Chair is absent or incapacitated or the office
  of Chair is vacant, the Committee may designate one of its other
  members to act as Chair, but the member must not be so designated for
  more than 90 days without the Governor in Council's approval.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Each member of the
Committee is entitled to be paid, in accordance with Treasury Board
directives, reasonable travel and living expenses incurred by the member
in the exercise of their powers or the performance of their duties or
functions.

## [Mandate of Committee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363698 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of national security matters

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  mandate of the Committee is to review

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the legislative, regulatory, policy, administrative
    and financial framework for national security and intelligence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any activity carried out by a department that
    relates to national security or intelligence, unless the activity is
    an ongoing operation and the appropriate Minister determines that
    the review would be injurious to national security; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any matter relating to national security or
    intelligence that a minister of the Crown refers to the Committee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review injurious to national security

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the appropriate Minister determines that a review
  would be injurious to national security, he or she must inform the
  Committee of his or her determination and the reasons for it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review no longer injurious

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the appropriate Minister determines that the
  review would no longer be injurious to national security or if the
  appropriate Minister is informed that the activity is no longer
  ongoing, he or she must inform the Committee that the review may be
  conducted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cooperation

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Committee and each
review body are to take all reasonable steps to cooperate with each
other to avoid any unnecessary duplication of work by the Committee and
that review body in relation to the fulfilment of their respective
mandates.

## [Security and Confidentiality]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363711 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with security requirements

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Each member of the
Committee must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtain and maintain the necessary security clearance
  from the Government of Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} take the oath or solemn affirmation set out in the
  schedule; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} comply with the procedures and practices set out in
  the regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure prohibited

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a member
  or former member of the Committee, the executive director or a former
  executive director of the Secretariat or a person who is or was
  engaged by the Secretariat must not knowingly disclose any information
  that they obtained, or to which they had access, in the course of
  exercising their powers or performing their duties or functions under
  this Act and that a department is taking measures to protect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person referred to in subsection (1) may disclose
  information referred to in that subsection for the purpose of
  exercising their powers or performing their duties or functions under
  this Act or as required by any other law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parliamentary privilege

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite any other law, no member or
  former member of the Committee may claim immunity based on
  parliamentary privilege in a proceeding against them in relation to a
  contravention of subsection 11(1) or of a provision of the [Foreign
  Interference and Security of Information Act](/eng/acts/O-5) or in
  relation to any other proceeding arising from any disclosure of
  information that is prohibited under that subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A statement made by a member or former member of the
  Committee before either House of Parliament or a committee of the
  Senate, of the House of Commons or of both Houses of Parliament is
  admissible in evidence against them in a proceeding referred to in
  subsection (1).

## [Access to Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363727 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of access

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite any other Act of Parliament
  but subject to sections 14 and 16, the Committee is entitled to have
  access to any information that is under the control of a department
  and that is related to the fulfilment of the Committee's mandate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protected information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information includes information that is
  protected by litigation privilege or by solicitor-client privilege or
  the professional secrecy of advocates and notaries.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the disclosure to the
  Committee under this section of any information that is subject to
  solicitor-client privilege or the professional secrecy of advocates
  and notaries or to litigation privilege does not constitute a waiver
  of those privileges or that secrecy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency or conflict

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the event of any inconsistency or conflict between
  subsection (1) and any provision of an Act of Parliament other than
  this Act, subsection (1) prevails to the extent of the inconsistency
  or conflict.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Committee is not
entitled to have access to any of the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a [[confidence of the Queen's Privy Council for
  Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 39(2) of the
  [Canada Evidence Act](/eng/acts/C-5);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} information the disclosure of which is described in
  subsection 11(1) of the [Witness Protection Program
  Act](/eng/acts/W-11.2);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the identity of a person who was, is or is intended
  to be, has been approached to be, or has offered or agreed to be, a
  confidential source of information, intelligence or assistance to the
  Government of Canada, or the government of a province or of any state
  allied with Canada, or information from which the person's identity
  could be inferred;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} information relating directly to an ongoing
  investigation carried out by a law enforcement agency that may lead to
  a prosecution.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for information

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Committee is entitled to have access to information that is under the
  control of a department, the Committee may make a request to the
  appropriate Minister for that department that the information be
  provided to the Committee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Committee requests information that is in
  respect of an identifiable person or entity, that has been received or
  collected by the Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of
  Canada under the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist
  Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501) and that has been disclosed to a
  department under subsection 55(3), 55.1(1), 65(1), 65.01(1) or
  65.02(1) of that Act, the Committee may make the request only to the
  appropriate Minister for that department.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Department --- parent Crown corporation

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} If the requested information is under the control
  of a department that is a parent Crown corporation, the department
  must, at the request of the appropriate Minister, provide the
  information to that Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance

  [(2.2)]{.lawlabel} Compliance by a department with subsection (2.1) is
  deemed to be in the best interests of the department.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} After the appropriate Minister receives the request,
  he or she must provide or cause to be provided to the Committee, in a
  timely manner, the requested information to which it is entitled to
  have access.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information provided orally

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate Minister or officials of the
  department may appear before the Committee to provide the information
  orally.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal of information

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  appropriate Minister for a department may refuse to provide
  information to which the Committee would, but for this section,
  otherwise be entitled to have access and that is under the control of
  that department, but only if he or she is of the opinion that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information constitutes [[special operational
    information]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 8(1)
    of the [Foreign Interference and Security of Information
    Act](/eng/acts/O-5); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provision of the information would be injurious to
    national security.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the appropriate Minister refuses to provide
  information under subsection (1), he or she must inform the Committee
  of his or her decision and the reasons for the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review bodies informed of decision

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate Minister must provide the decision
  and reasons to the National Security and Intelligence Review Agency
  and, in the case of information under the control of the Royal
  Canadian Mounted Police, the Civilian Review and Complaints Commission
  for the Royal Canadian Mounted Police.

## [Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363766 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings --- all of Chair

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Committee is to meet
at the call of the Chair.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings held in private

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Meetings of the Committee
are to be held in private if any information that a department is taking
measures to protect is likely to be disclosed during the course of the
meeting or if the Chair considers it to be otherwise necessary.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voting

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Chair may vote at
meetings of the Committee only in the case of a tie.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the provisions
of this Act and the regulations, the Committee may determine the
procedure to be followed in the exercise of any of its powers or the
performance of any of its duties or functions, including in respect of
the appearance of persons before the Committee.

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363775 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  year the Committee must submit to the Prime Minister a report of the
  reviews it conducted during the preceding year. The report must
  contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Committee's findings;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its recommendations, if any;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any summary referred to in subsection (3); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number of times in the preceding year that an
    appropriate Minister

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} determined that a review referred to in paragraph
      8(1)(b) would be injurious to national security, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} decided to refuse to provide information under
      subsection 16(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, in the Committee's opinion, a special report on
  any matter related to its mandate is necessary, it may, at any time,
  submit such a report to the Prime Minister and the minister concerned.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summary of special report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Committee may prepare a summary of a special
  report and, on submitting the report, must notify the Prime Minister
  of its intention to do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special report not to be tabled

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (5) does not apply to a special report
  that is the subject of a notification under subsection (3) and that
  report is not to be laid before either House of Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direction to submit revised report

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If, after consulting the Chair of the Committee, the
  Prime Minister is of the opinion that information in an annual or
  special report is information the disclosure of which would be
  injurious to national security, national defence or international
  relations or is information that is protected by litigation privilege
  or by solicitor-client privilege or the professional secrecy of
  advocates and notaries, the Prime Minister may direct the Committee to
  submit to the Prime Minister a revised version of the annual or
  special report that does not contain that information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revised version of report

  [(5.1)]{.lawlabel} If the Committee is directed by the Prime Minister
  to submit a revised version, the revised version must be clearly
  identified as a revised version and must indicate the extent of, and
  the reasons for, the revisions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), the Prime Minister must
  cause to be laid before each House of Parliament, on any of the first
  30 days on which that House is sitting after a report is submitted
  under subsection (1) or (2), a copy of the report or, if the Committee
  was directed to submit a revised version, a copy of the revised
  version.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Referral

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} After it is tabled, the annual or special report
  stands referred to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Standing Committee on National Security and
    Defence of the Senate or, if there is not a Standing Committee on
    National Security and Defence, the appropriate committee of the
    Senate, as determined by its rules; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Standing Committee on Public Safety and
    National Security of the House of Commons or, if there is not a
    Standing Committee on Public Safety and National Security, the
    appropriate committee of the House of Commons, as determined by its
    rules.

## [Review Bodies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363801 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information to Committee

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite any provision of any other
  Act of Parliament --- including section 45.47 of the [Royal Canadian
  Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10) --- but subject to subsection (2),
  a review body may provide to the Committee information that is under
  its control and that is related to the fulfilment of the Committee's
  mandate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The review body must not provide to the Committee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} information that is referred to in section 14; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information that is the subject of a decision that
    has been provided to the review body under subsection 16(3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information to review bodies

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Committee may
provide,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the Civilian Review and Complaints Commission for
  the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, information that is obtained from
  --- or that is created by the Committee from information obtained from
  --- the Royal Canadian Mounted Police and that is related to the
  fulfilment of that review body's mandate; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the National Security and Intelligence Review
  Agency, information that is related to the fulfilment of that review
  body's mandate under paragraphs 8(1)(a) to (c) of the [National
  Security and Intelligence Review Agency Act](/eng/acts/N-16.62).

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2019, c. 13, s. 49\]]{.Repealed}

## [Secretariat]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363814 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Secretariat established

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Secretariat of the National Security and Intelligence Committee of
  Parliamentarians is established.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Secretariat is to assist the Committee in
  fulfilling its mandate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Office

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The head office of the Secretariat is to be in the
  National Capital Region as described in the schedule to the [National
  Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Executive director

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  is to be an executive director of the Secretariat, who is to be
  appointed by the Governor in Council to hold office during pleasure
  for a term of up to five years. The executive director is eligible to
  be reappointed on the expiry of a first or subsequent term of office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy head

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The executive director has the rank and all the
  powers of a deputy head of a department.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** If the executive director
is absent or incapacitated or the office of executive director is
vacant, the Minister designated under section 3 may designate another
person to act as executive director, but a person must not be so
designated for more than 90 days without the Governor in Council's
approval.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and expenses

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  executive director is to be paid the remuneration that is fixed by the
  Governor in Council and is entitled to be paid reasonable travel and
  living expenses incurred in the exercise of their powers or the
  performance of their duties or functions while absent from their
  ordinary place of work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The executive director is deemed to be a person
  employed in the public service for the purposes of the [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and to be employed in the federal
  public administration for the purposes of the [Government Employees
  Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and any regulations made under
  section 9 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chief executive officer

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The executive director is
the chief executive officer of the Secretariat and has the control and
management of the Secretariat and all matters connected with it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contracts, etc.

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The executive director
may enter into contracts, memoranda of understanding or other
arrangements, including contracts to engage the services of legal
counsel or other persons having professional, technical or specialized
knowledge to advise or assist the Committee or any of its members.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employees

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** The employees of the
Secretariat are to be appointed in accordance with the [Public Service
Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363840 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final decision

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  appropriate Minister's determination that a review referred to in
  paragraph 8(1)(b) would be injurious to national security or the
  appropriate Minister's decision to refuse to provide information under
  subsection 16(1) is final.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committee's response

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Committee is dissatisfied with the
  determination or the decision, the Committee is not to bring the
  matter before the courts, but it may note its dissatisfaction in a
  report referred to in section 21.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance of activity

**[[31.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31.1 .sectionLabel}** The Committee must
inform the appropriate Minister and the Attorney General of Canada of
any activity that is carried out by a department and is related to
national security or intelligence and that, in the Committee's opinion,
may not be in compliance with the law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses --- appearance before Committee

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the
regulations, a person is entitled to be paid reasonable travel and
living expenses incurred as a result of their appearance before the
Committee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the procedures and practices for the
  secure handling, storage, transportation, transmission and destruction
  of information or documents provided to or created by the Committee;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the procedure to be followed by the
  Committee in the exercise of any of its powers or the performance of
  any of its duties or functions;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the expenses referred to in section 32;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} generally for carrying out the purposes and
  provisions of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of Act after five years

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** Five years after the day
on which this Act comes into force, a comprehensive review of the
provisions and operation of the Act is to be undertaken by the committee
of the Senate, of the House of Commons or of both Houses of Parliament
that is designated or established by the Senate or the House of Commons,
or by both Houses of Parliament, as the case may be, for that purpose.
