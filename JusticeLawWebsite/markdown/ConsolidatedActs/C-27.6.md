## Chemical Weapons Convention Implementation Act {#chemical-weapons-convention-implementation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1995, c. 25
:::

Assented to 1995-07-13

An Act to implement the Convention on the Prohibition of the
Development, Production, Stockpiling and Use of Chemical Weapons and on
their Destruction
::::

::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80079 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Chemical Weapons Convention Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-27.6).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80082 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
      Prohibition of the Development, Production, Stockpiling and Use of
      Chemical Weapons and on their Destruction, signed at Paris, France
      on January 13, 1993, as amended from time to time pursuant to
      Article XV of the Convention; ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[international inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[international inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
      holds a certificate issued by the Minister under section
      12; ([inspecteur international]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
      Privy Council for Canada as may be designated by the Governor in
      Council to be the Minister for the purposes of this
      Act; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[National Authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[National Authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the portion of
      the federal public administration designated as the National
      Authority for Canada under subsection 3(1); ([autorité
      nationale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[place]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[place]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any means of
      transport; ([lieu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by the
      regulations; ([Version anglaise
      seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

  [[representative of the National Authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[representative of the National
      Authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a representative of the
      National Authority designated under subsection
      3(2). ([représentant de l'autorité nationale]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other words and expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context otherwise requires, all words and
  expressions used in this Act have the same meaning as in the
  Convention.

## [National Authority]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80099 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National Authority

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of implementing Canada's obligations under the Convention and
  of giving effect to paragraph 4 of Article VII of the Convention, the
  Minister may designate any portion of the federal public
  administration to be the National Authority for Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representatives

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of implementing Canada's obligations
  under the Convention, the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} may designate persons or classes of persons to act
    as representatives of the National Authority; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall furnish every representative of the National
    Authority with a certificate of designation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of certificate

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A certificate of designation must indicate the places
  or classes of places that the holder of the certificate is authorized
  to have access to for the purposes of this Act and any conditions
  applicable to the holder's activities under the certificate that the
  Minister considers advisable for those purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identification required

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every holder of a certificate of designation shall,
  on request of the person in charge of a place to be entered by the
  holder under this Act, show the certificate to that person.

- 1995, c. 25, s. 3
- 2003, c. 22, s. 224(E)

## [Purpose of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80113 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation of Convention

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to implement Canada's obligations under the Convention.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80116 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Chemical Weapons and Riot Control Agents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80119 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chemical weapons

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} develop, produce, otherwise acquire, stockpile or
  retain a chemical weapon, or transfer, directly or indirectly, a
  chemical weapon to anyone;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use a chemical weapon;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} engage in any military preparations to use a chemical
  weapon; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} assist, encourage or induce, in any way, anyone to
  engage in any activity prohibited to a State Party under the
  Convention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Riot control agents

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No person shall use a riot
control agent as a method of warfare.

## [Toxic Chemicals and Precursors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80128 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Schedule 1 toxic chemicals and precursors

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as authorized by or pursuant to any regulations made under paragraph
  18(a), no person shall produce, use, acquire or possess a toxic
  chemical or precursor listed in Schedule 1 of the Schedules of
  Chemicals set out in the Annex on Chemicals in the Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Export or import

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as authorized under the [Export and Import
  Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), no person shall export or import a toxic
  chemical or precursor listed in Schedule 1 of the Schedules of
  Chemicals set out in the Annex on Chemicals in the Convention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Schedule 2 toxic chemicals and precursors

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Except as authorized under
the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), no person shall
export or import a toxic chemical or precursor listed in Schedule 2 of
the Schedules of Chemicals set out in the Annex on Chemicals in the
Convention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Schedule 3 toxic chemicals and precursors

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Except as authorized
under the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), no person
shall export or import a toxic chemical or precursor listed in Schedule
3 of the Schedules of Chemicals set out in the Annex on Chemicals in the
Convention.

## [Information and Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80138 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information and documents

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every person who does
anything under an authorization referred to in section 8 or who
produces, processes, consumes, exports or imports a toxic chemical or
precursor listed in Schedule 2 of the Schedules of Chemicals set out in
the Annex on Chemicals in the Convention or who produces, exports or
imports a toxic chemical or precursor listed in Schedule 3 of the
Schedules of Chemicals set out in that Annex or who produces a discrete
organic chemical or who holds a riot control agent for riot control
purposes shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide the prescribed information, at the prescribed
  time and in the prescribed form, to the National Authority or to such
  other portion of the federal public administration as the Minister may
  by order authorize to collect the information; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} keep and maintain the prescribed documents in Canada,
  at the person's place of business or at such other place as may be
  designated by the Minister, in the prescribed manner and for the
  prescribed period and, on request by the Minister or the National
  Authority, provide the documents to the National Authority.

## [Inspections]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80145 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}International inspectors

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall issue to every person who is a member of an inspection
  team of the Technical Secretariat of the Organization for the
  Prohibition of Chemical Weapons that has been authorized under the
  Convention to conduct on behalf of the Organization inspections in
  Canada a certificate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} identifying the person by name and indicating the
    person's status and authority to conduct inspections in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specifying the privileges and immunities applicable
    to the person; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} setting out such other information and any
    conditions applicable to the person's inspection activities in
    Canada as the Minister considers advisable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identification required

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every international inspector shall, on request of
  the person in charge of a place to be inspected by the international
  inspector, show the certificate to that person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspections

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this Act, an
  international inspector may, at any reasonable time and consistent
  with the provisions of the Convention,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter any place in Canada

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which information has been provided
      under section 11,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is subject to an on-site challenge
      inspection referred to in paragraph 8 of Article IX of the
      Convention, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which an investigation under
      paragraph 9 of Article X of the Convention has been initiated;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inspect the place in a manner consistent with the
    provisions of the Convention and any facility agreement applicable
    to the place; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where appropriate, install, use and maintain in
    respect of any such place monitoring instruments, systems and seals
    in a manner consistent with the provisions of the Convention and any
    facility agreement applicable to the place.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspector may be accompanied

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} While carrying out a challenge inspection, an
  international inspector may be accompanied by an observer for the
  purposes of giving effect to paragraph 12 of Article IX of the
  Convention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National Authority representative powers
in relation to inspection

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  order to facilitate the conduct of an inspection under section 13, a
  representative of the National Authority may accompany an
  international inspector and may direct any person who is in control of
  the place being inspected to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide the international inspector with access to
    any area, container or thing in the place being inspected;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} permit the international inspector to examine any
    thing in the place being inspected;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} permit the international inspector to make copies
    of any information contained in the records, files, papers or
    electronic information systems kept or used in relation to the place
    being inspected and to remove the copies from the place;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} permit the international inspector to have
    photographs taken of any thing in the place being inspected and to
    remove the photographs from the place;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} permit the international inspector to interview any
    person in the place being inspected; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} take or permit the international inspector to take
    samples for analysis of any thing in the place being inspected and
    permit the international inspector to remove the samples for
    analysis outside the place.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direction to be complied with

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person to whom a direction is given under
  subsection (1) shall comply with the direction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False statements, obstruction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} While an international inspector is conducting an
  inspection under this Act, no person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} knowingly make any false or misleading statement to
    the inspector or to the representative of the National Authority
    accompanying the inspector in relation to the place or thing being
    inspected; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} wilfully obstruct the inspection, by act or
    omission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direction not statutory instrument

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A direction under subsection (1) is not a statutory
  instrument for the purposes of the [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant required where refusal of entry

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  representative of the National Authority or an international inspector
  may not enter a place referred to in section 13 without the consent of
  the person who is in control of the place except under the authority
  of a warrant issued under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, on [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  application, a justice is satisfied by information on oath that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a place referred to in section 13 meets the
    conditions for entry described in that section,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entry to the place is necessary for any purpose
    relating to the administration of this Act or the regulations, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} entry to the place has been refused or there are
    reasonable grounds to believe that entry will be refused,

  the justice may issue a warrant authorizing the representative of the
  National Authority and the international inspector named in it to
  enter the place for the purposes of the inspection referred to in
  section 13, subject to such conditions as may be specified in the
  warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When warrant not required

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A warrant authorizing entry into a place referred to
  in section 13 is not required if the conditions for obtaining the
  warrant exist but, by reason of exigent circumstances, it would not be
  practicable to obtain the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In executing a warrant issued under subsection (2), a
  representative of the National Authority or an international inspector
  shall not use force unless the representative or inspector is
  accompanied by a peace officer and the use of force is specifically
  authorized in the warrant.

## [Disclosure of Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80195 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice for disclosure of information

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may send a notice to any person who the Minister believes on
  reasonable grounds has information or documents relevant to the
  enforcement of this Act, requesting the person to provide the
  information or documents to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who receives a notice referred to in
  subsection (1) shall provide the requested information and documents
  to the Minister in the form and within the time specified in the
  notice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privileged information

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Information and documents obtained
  pursuant to this Act or the Convention are privileged.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Information and documents are not privileged to the
  extent that they are required to be disclosed or communicated for the
  purposes of an emergency involving public safety.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person in possession of privileged information or
  documents shall knowingly, without the written consent of the person
  from whom they were obtained, communicate them or allow them to be
  communicated to any person, or allow any person to have access to
  them, except

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of the enforcement of this Act or
    of giving effect to the Convention;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to an obligation of the Government of
    Canada under the Convention; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the information or documents are
    disclosed in accordance with the [Security of Canada Information
    Disclosure Act](/eng/acts/S-6.9).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence in legal proceedings

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding any other Act or law, no person shall
  be required, in connection with any legal proceedings, to produce any
  statement or other record containing privileged information or
  documents, or to give evidence relating to them, unless the
  proceedings relate to the enforcement of this Act.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80215 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations for carrying out and giving effect to the
provisions of the Convention and, without limiting the generality of the
foregoing, may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing conditions under which activities
  referred to in subsection 8(1) may be carried on, providing for the
  issue, suspension and cancellation of licences governing the carrying
  on of any such activity and prescribing the fees or the manner of
  calculating the fees to be paid in respect of any such licence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the procedures to be followed by
  representatives of the National Authority in exercising their
  functions under this Act; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that by this Act is to be
  prescribed.

## [Amendments to the Convention]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80221 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication required

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall, as
soon as practicable after any amendment to the Convention is made
pursuant to Article XV of the Convention, cause a copy of the amendment
to be published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80224 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes any provision of this Act is guilty of an offence and liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding
  \$5,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding eighteen months,
  or to both; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine not exceeding
  \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years, or
  to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)
provisions apply

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
the provisions of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) apply for the
purposes of enforcing this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence outside Canada

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Every individual who
commits, outside Canada, an act or omission that would, if committed in
Canada, be an offence under this Act, shall, if the individual is a
Canadian citizen or a permanent resident within the meaning of
subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), be deemed to have committed that act or omission
in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 25, s. 22
- 2001, c. 27, s. 227
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a person has been convicted of an offence under this Act, any thing
  seized by means of which or in respect of which the offence was
  committed is forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada and shall be
  disposed of as the Minister directs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture with consent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where any thing has been seized by means of which or
  in respect of which an offence has been committed under this Act and
  the owner of the thing, or the person in whose possession the thing
  was at the time of seizure, consents in writing to its forfeiture, the
  thing is forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada and shall be
  disposed of as the Minister directs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period for summary conviction
offences

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Proceedings by way of
summary conviction may be instituted at any time within, but not later
than, two years after the day on which the subject-matter of the
proceedings arose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offence

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Where an offence under
this Act is committed or continued on more than one day, the person who
committed the offence is liable to be convicted for a separate offence
for each day on which the offence is committed or continued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Venue

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** A complaint or an
information in respect of an offence under this Act may be dealt with by
any competent court of criminal jurisdiction if the accused is resident
or carrying on business within the territorial jurisdiction of that
court, even though the subject-matter of the complaint or information
did not arise in that territorial jurisdiction.
