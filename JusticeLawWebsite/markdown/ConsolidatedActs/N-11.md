## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-359276 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act, in
relation to financing for housing, is to promote housing affordability
and choice, to facilitate access to, and competition and efficiency in
the provision of, housing finance, to protect the availability of
adequate funding for housing at low cost, and generally to contribute to
the well-being of the housing sector in the national economy.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-11, s. 3
- 1992, c. 32, s. 6
- 1999, c. 27, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Avoiding or curing defaults

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation may make payments to approved lenders or to holders of
  insured loans, and may make loans or payments to borrowers or their
  assignees, for the purpose of avoiding or curing, in whole or in part,
  default under insured loans or facilitating variation of the terms of
  payment of insured loans, or for any other purposes that the
  Corporation considers appropriate to protect its interest as an
  insurer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subrogation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Corporation makes a payment to an approved
  lender or holder of an insured loan under subsection (1), the
  Corporation is subrogated, to the extent of the amount of the payment,
  to all the rights and interests of the lender or holder in respect of
  that amount, and may maintain an action in respect of those rights and
  interests in the name of the lender or holder or in the name of the
  Corporation. Any money recovered by the lender or holder must first be

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[21.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21.5 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in this Part.

[[affiliate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[affiliate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an affiliate as defined in
    section 2 of the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([groupe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[covered bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[covered bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, except in paragraph
    21.53(b), a debt obligation in relation to which the principal and
    interest owing are guaranteed to be paid from the loans or other
    assets held by a guarantor entity. ([obligation
    sécurisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[covered bond collateral]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[covered bond collateral]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the loans or
    other assets that secure the payment of principal, interest and any
    other amounts owing in relation to the covered bonds that are issued
    under a registered program. ([garantie d'obligations
    sécurisées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[derivatives agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[derivatives agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a financial
    agreement whose obligations are derived from, referenced to, or
    based on, one or more underlying reference items. ([contrat
    dérivé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[guarantor entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[guarantor entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity that is
    created and organized for the principal purpose of holding loans or
    other assets as the covered bond collateral for a registered program
    with the intention of legally isolating those loans or other assets
    from the registered issuer. ([societé garante]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[registered issuer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registered issuer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an institution that
    is registered in the registry under section 21.53. ([émetteur
    inscrit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registered program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registered program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a program that is
    registered in the registry under section 21.55. ([programme
    inscrit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the registry established
    under section 21.51. ([registre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Bankruptcy and Insolvency Protection]{.HTitleText2} {#h-359641 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Covered bonds

**[[21.63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21.63 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in any law
of Canada or a province relating to bankruptcy or insolvency, or any
order of a court made in relation to a reorganization, arrangement or
receivership involving bankruptcy or insolvency, prevents or prohibits
the following actions from being taken in accordance with the provisions
of contracts relating to covered bonds that are issued under a
registered program:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the making of any payments, including a payment to a
  registered issuer;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the netting or setting off or compensation of
  obligations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any dealing with covered bond collateral, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the sale or foreclosure or, in Quebec, the
    surrender of covered bond collateral, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the setting off or compensation of covered bond
    collateral or the application of the proceeds or value of covered
    bond collateral; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the termination of those contracts.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2012, c. 19, s. 356
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer not voidable and not subject to
remedies

**[[21.64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21.64 .sectionLabel}** Despite anything in
any law of Canada or a province relating to bankruptcy or insolvency, or
any order of a court made in relation to a reorganization, arrangement
or receivership involving bankruptcy or insolvency, the transfer of
loans or other assets to a guarantor entity --- to be held as covered
bond collateral --- by a registered issuer, any of its affiliates or any
prescribed entity

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is effective against every person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not voidable or, in Quebec, annullable;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is not subject to any other remedies available to
  creditors of the registered issuer; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} does not constitute a fraudulent conveyance, unjust
  preference or other reviewable transaction.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2012, c. 19, s. 356

**[[87]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-87 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,
[[charitable corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation
constituted exclusively for charitable purposes no part of the income of
which is payable to, or is otherwise available for the personal benefit
of, any proprietor, member or shareholder of the corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-11, s. 87
- 1999, c. 27, s. 16
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loans, etc., for student housing projects

- **[[88]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-88
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  Corporation may make loans and contributions to provinces or agencies
  thereof, municipalities or agencies thereof, hospitals, school boards,
  universities, colleges, cooperative associations or charitable
  corporations in respect of housing projects for students and their
  families, and may forgive amounts owing on those loans.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial approval

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Loans or contributions may be made and amounts owing
  on those loans may be forgiven under this section only with the
  approval of the government of the province where the corresponding
  rental housing project is, or will be, located.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply to loans and
  contributions to a province or the forgiving of amounts owing on those
  loans.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms and conditions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may determine the terms and
  conditions on which it makes a loan or contribution or forgives an
  amount under subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-11, s. 88
- 1999, c. 27, s. 16

## [PART XIII]{.HLabel1}[Community Services]{.HTitleText1} {#h-360011 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contributions for community services

- **[[91]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation may, under such terms and conditions as are determined in
  accordance with an agreement entered into with a province, pay to the
  province or any local government authority or body established by or
  under the law of the province contributions, in respect of each
  program period defined in the agreement, for the purpose of
  reimbursing expenditures incurred, or paying for the results obtained,
  in the provision, development, extension and improvement of such
  community services as are determined pursuant to the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation of contributions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Contributions pursuant to subsection (1) shall be
  allocated among local government authorities or bodies declared
  eligible for those contributions pursuant to and in the manner
  determined pursuant to the agreement referred to in that subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval by Governor in Council

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An agreement referred to in subsection (1) shall have
  no force or effect unless it is approved by order of the Governor in
  Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contracts with builders to encourage
construction

- **[[93]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-93 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation may enter into contracts with builders to encourage the
  construction of houses to be sold to prospective home owners.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions under which contracts made
  to encourage construction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A contract may be entered into under subsection (1)
  only with respect to houses the construction of which is assisted by
  loans made pursuant to this Act, and the contract shall provide that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the builder shall finance the construction of the
    houses under the provisions of this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the builder shall offer each of the houses for sale
    at a price not in excess of the price fixed in the contract; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Corporation shall, in consideration of the
    payment by the builder of such amount as the Governor in Council may
    prescribe, agree that any house built pursuant to the contract and
    remaining unsold will, within one year after the date of completion
    thereof, be purchased from the builder by the Corporation at a price
    fixed in the contract.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further conditions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A contract with a builder entered into under this
  section may provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that, during such period as the Corporation
    requires, the builder shall offer the houses for sale only to
    veterans or persons engaged in the production of defence supplies as
    defined in the [Defence Production Act](/eng/acts/D-1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that such other things shall be done as the
    Corporation deems necessary in order to carry out the intent of this
    section and to safeguard the interests of the Corporation.

- Definition of [[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of this section, "veteran" means a
  person who had been paid or is entitled to be paid a war service
  gratuity under the [War Service Grants Act](/eng/acts/W-2.5), chapter
  W-4 of the Revised Statutes of Canada, 1970, and includes a person who
  served on active service

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in any of the naval or army forces of Newfoundland
    or, having been recruited in Newfoundland, in any of the naval, army
    or air forces raised in Newfoundland by or on behalf of the United
    Kingdom; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any naval, army or air forces of His Majesty,
    other than those referred to in paragraph (a), in which the person
    enlisted when domiciled in Newfoundland.

  as are prescribed by regulation.

  subsection (1) is in addition to and not in derogation of any other
  condition required or permitted by this Act.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the payment of any applicable fees, the

  registering documents that may affect land or interests in land, cause
  any agreement respecting the operation of a housing project to which
  the Corporation is a party to be registered on the title of the
  housing project.

  subsection (3) constitutes notice of the agreement to the same extent
  as does the registration of any other instrument that affects land or

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Undertaking re contributions, etc.

- **[[97.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-97.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may require that any
  person who is to benefit from

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a loan from the Corporation with interest at a rate
    that is lower than the rate

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} otherwise available on the open market, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} specified by the Corporation to be the market
      rate,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a contribution from the Corporation, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} forgiveness of any amount by the Corporation

  provide the Corporation with an undertaking described in subsection
  (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Undertaking

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The undertaking referred to in subsection (1) is an
  undertaking that the person will comply with such operating, residency
  or other requirements as may be specified by the Corporation and that
  the person will, as the case may be,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pay to the Corporation an amount equal to the
    amount by which the interest the person would have paid in respect
    of the loan had the interest thereon been the higher rate referred
    to in subsection (1) exceeds the interest the person actually paid,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reimburse the Corporation all amounts contributed
    by the Corporation, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} pay to the Corporation all amounts forgiven by the
    Corporation,

  if the person fails to comply with any of those requirements.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} This section applies notwithstanding that the
  interest rate, contribution or forgiveness is subject to any other
  condition.
