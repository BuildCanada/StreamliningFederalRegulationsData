## An Act to repeal An Act to incorporate the Western Canada Telephone Company {#an-act-to-repeal-an-act-to-incorporate-the-western-canada-telephone-company .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2000, c. 29
:::

Assented to 2000-10-20

An Act to repeal An Act to incorporate the Western Canada Telephone
Company
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repeal

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** An Act to incorporate the
Western Canada Telephone Company, being chapter 66 of the Statutes of
Canada, 1916, is repealed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal existence continues

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** TELUS Communications (B.C.)
Inc., incorporated under the Act referred to in section 1, as amended,
and continued under the [Canada Business Corporations
Act](/eng/acts/C-44), continues to exist as a person in law despite the
repeal and is subject to the [Canada Business Corporations
Act](/eng/acts/C-44).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights not affected

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The rights of holders of
securities, debentures under trust deeds and beneficiaries of other
instruments issued by or in respect of TELUS Communications (B.C.) Inc.
are not affected by the repeal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#W-6.75_en_1){#W-6.75_en_1-ID0EBCA}[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
.sectionLabel}** This Act comes into force on a day to be fixed by order
of the Governor in Council.
