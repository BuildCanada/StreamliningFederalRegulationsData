## Security Screening Services Commercialization Act {#security-screening-services-commercialization-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2019, c. 29, s. 270
:::

Assented to 2019-06-21

An Act respecting the commercialization of security screening services

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 270 of chapter 29 of the Statutes of Canada, 2019,
sections 14, 16 to 24, 37 to 42 and 44 to 46 and subsection 47(1) not in
force.\]

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1168582 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Security Screening Services Commercialization
Act](/eng/acts/S-7.2).

## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1168585 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Transportation
      Agency. ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated screening authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated screening authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      body corporate designated under section 7. ([administration de
      contrôle désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
      Transport. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[screening contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[screening contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person or
      entity authorized under section 21 by the designated screening
      authority to conduct screening. ([fournisseur de services de
      contrôle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[screening officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[screening officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who
      is employed by the designated screening authority or a screening
      contractor to conduct screening. ([agent de
      contrôle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[state aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[state aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an aircraft, other
      than an aircraft operated for commercial purposes, that is owned
      and operated by the government of a country or the government of a
      colony, dependency, province, state, territory or municipality of
      a country. ([aéronef d'État]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transfer date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transfer date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the day specified as
      the transfer date in an agreement between the Canadian Air
      Transport Security Authority and the designated screening
      authority. ([date de cession]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Words and expressions --- [Aeronautics
  Act](/eng/acts/A-2)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context requires otherwise, words and
  expressions used in this Act have the same meaning as in the
  [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2) and aviation security regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publishing

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Any obligation under this
Act for the designated screening authority to publish information or a
document is satisfied if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information or document is published on its
  website; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information or document is made available on
  request at its head office.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2)

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act affects
the application of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Act does not apply in
respect of any aerodrome or aircraft operated by or under the authority
of the Minister of National Defence.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1168606 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her

## [Designated Screening Authority]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1168609 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate a body corporate incorporated under the [Canada
Not-for-profit Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-7.75) as the designated
screening authority for the purposes of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not an Agent of Her Majesty

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The designated screening
authority is not an agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The designated screening
authority must have its head office in Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Official Languages
Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01)

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The [Official Languages
Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01) applies to the designated screening authority as
if it were a federal institution.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canada's international obligations

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The designated screening
authority must take any measure that the Minister considers necessary to
permit Canada to meet its international obligations under bilateral and
multilateral agreements in respect of aeronautics.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of articles of incorporation

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  designated screening authority must not amend its articles of
  incorporation without the prior written approval of the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment or repeal of by-laws

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designated screening authority must not amend or
  repeal a by-law or any part of a by-law that requires the Minister's
  approval to amend or repeal without the prior written approval of the
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The designated screening authority must not make a
  by-law if that by-law would be inconsistent with a by-law or any part
  of a by-law that requires the Minister's approval to amend or repeal.
  The designated screening authority must not amend or repeal a by-law
  that does not require the Minister's approval to amend or repeal if
  the amendment or repeal would be inconsistent with a by-law or any
  part of a by-law that requires the Minister's approval to amend or
  repeal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial statements

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The designated screening
authority must, after the end of each financial year but before its next
annual meeting of members, publish its audited financial statements for
that financial year and provide the Minister with a copy.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establish, revise or terminate charges

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The designated screening
authority may establish, revise or terminate charges.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charging principles

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  designated screening authority must observe the following principles
  when it establishes, revises or terminates charges:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that charges must not be set at levels that, based
    on reasonable and prudent projections, would generate revenues
    exceeding the designated screening authority's current and future
    financial requirements related to security screening services made
    available or provided under section 18 at aerodromes designated by
    the regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that charges must be established, revised or
    terminated in accordance with an explicit methodology --- that
    includes any conditions affecting the charges --- that the
    designated screening authority has established and published;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that, unless authorized by the Minister, charges
    imposed under paragraph 24(1)(a) must be the same in respect of all
    passengers, except that the designated screening authority may
    impose different charges based on whether passengers are travelling
    from an aerodrome within Canada to an aerodrome within Canada, from
    an aerodrome within Canada to an aerodrome within the United States
    or from an aerodrome within Canada to an aerodrome outside of
    Canada, other than an aerodrome in the United States;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that charges may be used only to recover costs for
    security screening services made available or provided under section
    18 at aerodromes designated by the regulations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} that charges must be structured in a way that does
    not differentiate between Canadian air carriers and foreign air
    carriers, among Canadian air carriers or among foreign air carriers;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} that charges must be consistent with Canada's
    international obligations in respect of aeronautics;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} that charges should be structured in a way that
    prioritizes aviation security and the competitiveness of the air
    transportation system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Increase --- Consumer Price Index

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an annual increase, the percentage
    increase to the Consumer Price Index, rounded to the next 0.10%, for
    the previous year; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an increase in respect of a period,
    the aggregate of the annual percentage increases to the Consumer
    Price Index, rounded to the next 0.10%, for that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proposal submitted to Agency

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designated screening authority must submit a
  proposal to increase a charge in accordance with subsection (1) to the
  Agency for a determination of whether the proposed increase is in
  accordance with that subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In making a determination, the Agency may only
  consider whether the proposal is in accordance with subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agency determination

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Agency must make a determination within 30 days
  after the day on which it receives the proposal and must notify the
  designated screening authority in writing of its determination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The designated screening authority must not publish a
  notice of the proposal under section 29 until after it is notified of
  the Agency's determination. If the Agency does not make a
  determination within the 30-day period, the designated screening
  authority may publish the notice after the day on which that period
  expires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[Consumer Price
  Index]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[Consumer Price
  Index]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the annual average all-items
  Consumer Price Index for Canada (not seasonally adjusted) published by
  Statistics Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of proposal

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  must provide the Agency with a copy of the notice no later than the

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} describe the proposal, including the methodology,
    the proposed amount of the charge and the circumstances in which the
    charge would apply;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specify the date on which the charge or its
    termination would take effect;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set out the designated screening authority's
    reasons for establishing, revising or terminating the charge in
    relation to the charging principles;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a proposal to increase a charge in
    accordance with section 27, indicate whether the Agency determined
    that the proposal is in accordance with subsection 27(1);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a proposal referred to in section
    28, indicate that the Minister approved the proposal; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a proposal in respect of which a
    person may file a notice of objection under subsection 31(1) or (2),
    indicate that a notice of objection may be filed under one of those
    subsections.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The date on which a charge or its termination would
  take effect must not be before

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a proposal in respect of which a
    person may file a notice of objection under subsection 31(1) or (2),
    the 121st day after the day on which the notice is published; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other proposal, the 31st day
    after the day on which the notice is published.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to inform air carriers

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The designated screening authority must, no later
  than the day on which a notice of a proposal is published, inform air

  37.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designated screening authority may

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the 30th day before the day on which the charge

  Agency rejected a proposal, the designated screening authority must
  publish a notice indicating that the proposal has been rejected. If

  designated screening authority published a notice of the proposal
  under section 29 continues to be in effect and set out that amount.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proposal approved

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the Agency determines that the designated
  screening authority observed the charging principles, the Agency must
  approve the proposal and the charge takes effect on the date specified
  in the notice published under section 29.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination and reasons

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Agency must give its determination and the

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy to Minister

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Agency must provide the Minister with a copy of
  its determination and the reasons for it immediately after the
  determination is made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Closing out of affairs

- **[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Beginning on the transfer date, the
  Canadian Air Transport Security Authority is authorized to sell or
  otherwise dispose of all or substantially all of its assets and
  liabilities and do everything necessary for or incidental to closing
  out its affairs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the Canadian Air
  Transport Security Authority has the capacity, rights, powers and
  privileges of a natural person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's power

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Beginning on the transfer date, the Minister may
  require the Canadian Air Transport Security Authority to do anything
  that, in his or her opinion, is necessary to sell or otherwise dispose
  of all or substantially all of its assets and liabilities, satisfy its
  debts, manage its expenses or otherwise close out its affairs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Canadian Air Transport Security Authority must do
  what the Minister requires under subsection (3).

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** \[Amendments\]

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dissolution

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** The Canadian Air
Transport Security Authority is dissolved on a day to be fixed by order
of the Governor in Council.
