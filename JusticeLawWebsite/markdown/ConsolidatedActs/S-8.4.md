## [Short title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-445342 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Service Fees Act](/eng/acts/S-8.4).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-445345 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[federal entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a department named in Schedule I to the
        [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a division or branch of the federal public
        administration set out in column I of Schedule I.1 to that Act;
        or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a corporation named in Schedule II to that
        Act. ([entité fédérale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[fee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an amount --- called a fee,
      charge, levy or by any other name --- that, in relation to a
      federal entity, is fixed by the Governor in Council, the Treasury
      Board, a minister or the federal entity under a power conferred by
      an Act of Parliament or a capacity to contract and is payable for

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the provision of a service;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the provision of the use of a facility;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the conferral, by means of a licence, permit or
        other authorization, of a right or privilege;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the provision of a product; or

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the provision of a regulatory
        process. ([frais]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning on
      April 1 in one year and ending on March 31 in the next
      year. ([exercice]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[responsible authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[responsible authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, with respect
      to a fee fixed in relation to a federal entity, the [[appropriate
      Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the
      [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), with respect to
      that entity or a person designated under subsection
      2(2). ([autorité compétente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of responsible authority

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
  as defined in section 2 of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11), with respect to a federal entity may, in
  writing, designate the federal entity's chief executive officer or
  deputy head, whatever their title, as the responsible authority with
  respect to any fees fixed in relation to that entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of Act

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} This Act does not apply to a fee if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fee is fixed under the [Access to Information
    Act](/eng/acts/A-1) or the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fee is paid only by or on behalf of a minister
    or federal entity.

## [Performance Standards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-445363 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- sections 4 to 7

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Sections 4 to 7 do not
apply to a fee if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fee is fixed by contract;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the President of the Treasury Board approves that
  those sections do not apply and the person or body that fixes the fee
  does so by fixing a manner for determining the amount of the fee over
  which that person or body has no control, such as an auction or a
  method that is based on the market rate; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fee is referred to in paragraph (d) of the
  definition [[fee]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation --- responsible authority

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The responsible authority
with respect to a fee must ensure that a performance standard is
established in respect of the fee, in accordance with Treasury Board
policies or directives, if any.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Before a performance
standard established in respect of a fee that is fixed after this
section comes into force is amended, the responsible authority with
respect to the fee must consult any persons and organizations that the
responsible authority considers to be interested in the matter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessibility

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The responsible authority
with respect to a fee must ensure that the performance standard
established in respect of the fee, or any amended standard, is
accessible to the public.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remissions

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  responsible authority with respect to a fee considers that the
  performance standard in relation to the fee has not been met in a
  fiscal year, the responsible authority must remit, before July 1 of
  the following fiscal year, the portion of the fee that the responsible
  authority considers appropriate to any affected person who paid the
  fee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Policies or directives

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The consideration of whether the performance standard
  in relation to a fee has not been met and what is the appropriate
  portion of a fee to be remitted, as well as the remission, must be
  made in accordance with Treasury Board policies or directives.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A performance standard is
not a statutory instrument for the purposes of the [Statutory
Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

## [Consultation and Parliamentary Review]{.HTitleText1} {#h-445386 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- sections 10 to 15

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Sections 10 to 15 do not
apply to a fee if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fee is fixed by contract;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the President of the Treasury Board approves that
  those sections do not apply and the person or body that fixes the fee
  does so by fixing a manner for determining the amount of the fee over
  which that person or body has no control, such as an auction or a
  method that is based on the market rate;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fee is fixed by a
  [[regulation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of
  the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22), that is published in
  Part I of the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) before it is
  made; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an Act of Parliament other than this Act requires
  consultation before the fee is fixed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  requirements of sections 11 to 15 are to be met before a fee is fixed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application in case of adjustment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, those sections do not apply
  when a fee is adjusted annually by operation of section 17 or
  periodically by operation of an Act of Parliament other than this Act,
  or an instrument made under such an Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee proposal

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The responsible authority
with respect to a fee must develop a fee proposal that includes the
following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the fee or the manner for determining
  its amount;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the circumstances in which the fee will be payable;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the rationale for the fee;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the factors taken into account in determining the
  amount of the fee or the manner for determining its amount; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any performance standard that will apply in respect
  of the fee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The responsible authority
must consult interested persons and organizations on the fee proposal by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} making it accessible to the public;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} inviting interested persons and organizations to make
  representations on it, specifying a deadline for making those
  representations, providing information on the requirement of
  paragraph (c) and specifying the time limit for submitting a complaint
  under subsection 13(1); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} replying to any representations made by interested
  persons and organizations within 30 days after the deadline specified
  under paragraph (b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaints

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  interested person or organization may, within 10 days after the time
  limit set out in paragraph 12(c) expires, submit a complaint in
  writing to the responsible authority with respect to the responsible
  authority's reply to their representations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of panel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The responsible authority must, within 30 days after
  the time limit set out in subsection (1) expires, establish a panel to
  review all complaints submitted under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition of panel

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The panel is to be composed of the following members:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one person selected by the responsible authority;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one person selected by all the interested persons
    and organizations that submitted complaints; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one person selected by the persons selected under
    paragraphs (a) and (b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Selection by responsible authority

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a selection referred to in paragraph (3)(b) or (c)
  is not made within the time limit set out in subsection (2), the
  responsible authority must select a person to be a panel member as
  soon as feasible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The responsible authority is responsible for the
  costs of the panel, including the remuneration and expenses that are
  payable to the panel members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The panel must issue a report on the complaints
  within 90 days after the day on which the panel is established. Any
  recommendations set out in the report are not binding on the
  responsible authority.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of time limit

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The responsible authority may, at the request of the
  panel, extend the time limit set out in subsection (6) by a maximum of
  30 days.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of materials in Parliament

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The responsible authority
must cause the following materials to be tabled in both Houses of
Parliament:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fee proposal;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the consultations on the fee proposal;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if a review panel was established, its report and a
  summary of any actions taken by the responsible authority as a result
  of the report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parliamentary review

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  tabled materials stand permanently referred to the committee of each
  House of Parliament that is designated or established to review
  matters relating to the activities of the federal entity in question.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The committee may review the materials and may submit
  to the Senate or the House of Commons, as the case may be, a report
  that contains its recommendations with respect to the fee proposal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the committee does not submit a report that
  contains its recommendations within the first 20 sitting days after
  the day on which the materials are tabled, the committee is considered
  to have submitted a report to the Senate or the House of Commons, as
  the case may be, recommending the approval of the fee proposal.

## [Annual Adjustment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-445445 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- sections 17 and 18

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections 17 and 18 do not apply to a
  fee if the fee is fixed by contract.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval by President of Treasury Board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 17 and 18 do not apply to a fee if the
  President of the Treasury Board approves that those sections do not
  apply and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person or body that fixes the fee does so by
    fixing a manner for determining the amount of the fee over which
    that person or body has no control, such as an auction or a method
    that is based on the market rate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fee is fixed in a manner that takes inflation
    into account; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fee is adjusted periodically by operation of an
    Act of Parliament other than this Act or by operation of an
    instrument made under such an Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consumer Price Index

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A fee
  is adjusted in each fiscal year --- on the anniversary of a date that
  is selected by the responsible authority with respect to the fee
  before the first adjustment --- by the percentage change over 12
  months in the April All-items Consumer Price Index for Canada, as
  published by Statistics Canada under the [Statistics
  Act](/eng/acts/S-19), for the previous fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A fee is not adjusted by operation of subsection (1)
  in a fiscal year if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fee is fixed in that fiscal year before the
    adjustment date; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the percentage change described in subsection (1)
    for that fiscal year is less than 1%.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustment --- subsequent fiscal year

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If paragraph (2)(b) applies with respect to a fiscal
  year, the percentage change referred to in that paragraph is carried
  forward to the subsequent fiscal year. If the combined percentage
  change is less than 1%, the fee is not adjusted for that subsequent
  fiscal year and the combined percentage change is carried forward to
  the next subsequent fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustment of fee --- round down

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The responsible authority may, in accordance with
  Treasury Board policies or directives, round down a fee after an
  adjustment under subsection (1) in a given fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Anniversary date --- change

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the responsible authority may
  change the anniversary date selected in respect of a fee in accordance
  with Treasury Board policies or directives.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report --- responsible authority

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  each fiscal year, a responsible authority must, in accordance with
  Treasury Board policies or directives, if any, cause a report to be
  tabled before each House of Parliament that sets out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fees within the jurisdiction of the responsible
    authority that were payable in the previous fiscal year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the authorities under which those fees were fixed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the revenue received from those fees;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any costs incurred in relation to the things for
    which those fees were paid;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the degree of compliance with any performance
    standards established in respect of those fees;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any remissions made under section 7 in respect of
    those fees;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the fees within the jurisdiction of the responsible
    authority that will be adjusted by operation of section 17 in the
    fiscal year after the year in which the report is tabled along with
    the adjustment date and the adjusted amount or adjusted manner for
    determining the amount; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any other information required by the Treasury
    Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Referral to committee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report stands permanently referred to the
  committee of each House of Parliament that is designated or
  established to review matters relating to the activities of the
  federal entity in question.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report --- President of Treasury Board

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The President of the
Treasury Board must, no later than March 31 of a fiscal year in which
any report referred to in subsection 20(1) is tabled, make a report
accessible to the public that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} consolidates the information set out in the tabled
  reports;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} lists the amendments to the regulations made by the
  President of the Treasury Board under section 22 in that fiscal year;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} lists the approvals given by the President of the
  Treasury Board in that fiscal year under this Act.

## [Low-materiality Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-445478 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- sections 3 to 18

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, sections
  3 to 18 do not apply in respect of a low-materiality fee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Treasury Board may make regulations respecting
  low-materiality fees, including regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} listing the fees that are considered to be
    low-materiality fees or the fees that are not considered to be
    low-materiality fees;

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} setting out criteria for determining whether fees
    are low-materiality fees and when low-materiality fees cease to be
    low-materiality fees;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} setting out when sections 3 to 18 cease to apply in
    respect of low-materiality fees and when those sections apply to
    fees that have ceased to be low-materiality fees; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} establishing the factors that the President of the
    Treasury Board must take into account while exercising the power
    referred to in subsection (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amending regulations --- President of
  the Treasury Board

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The President of the Treasury Board may, by
  regulation, amend regulations made under paragraph (2)(a), taking into
  account any factors established under paragraph (2)(c).

      [[fee]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[responsible
      authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} have the same meaning as in
      section 2 of the [Service Fees Act](/eng/acts/S-8.4), as enacted

      performance standard is established in respect of the fee within

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 5 to 8 of the [Service Fees
      Act](/eng/acts/S-8.4) apply in respect of the fee beginning on the
      day on which the performance standard is established in respect of
      the fee.
