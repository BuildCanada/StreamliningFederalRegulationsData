## Nuclear Safety and Control Act {#nuclear-safety-and-control-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1997, c. 9
:::

Assented to 1997-03-20

An Act to establish the Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission and to make
consequential amendments to other Acts
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS it is essential in the national and international interests to
regulate the development, production and use of nuclear energy and the
production, possession and use of nuclear substances, prescribed
equipment and prescribed information;

AND WHEREAS it is essential in the national interest that consistent
national and international standards be applied to the development,
production and use of nuclear energy;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368751 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368754 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Act.

[[analyst]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[analyst]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated as an
    analyst under section 28. ([analyste]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Nuclear Safety
    Commission established by section 8. ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[designated officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated
    as a designated officer under section 37. ([fonctionnaire
    désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dosimetry service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dosimetry service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prescribed
    facility for the measurement and monitoring of doses of
    radiation. ([service de dosimétrie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated as an
    inspector under section 29. ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence issued under section
    24. ([licence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou
    [permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Natural
    Resources or such member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada as
    the Governor in Council may designate as the Minister for the
    purposes of this Act. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nuclear energy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear energy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any form of energy
    released in the course of nuclear fission or nuclear fusion or of
    any other nuclear transmutation. ([énergie
    nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nuclear energy worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear energy worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is
    required, in the course of the person's business or occupation in
    connection with a nuclear substance or nuclear facility, to perform
    duties in such circumstances that there is a reasonable probability
    that the person may receive a dose of radiation that is greater than
    the prescribed limit for the general public. ([travailleur du
    secteur nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following
    facilities, namely,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a nuclear fission or fusion reactor or
      subcritical nuclear assembly,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a particle accelerator,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a uranium or thorium mine or mill,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a plant for the processing, reprocessing or
      separation of an isotope of uranium, thorium or plutonium,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a plant for the manufacture of a product from
      uranium, thorium or plutonium,

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a plant for the processing or use, in a quantity
      greater than 10^15^ Bq per calendar year, of nuclear substances
      other than uranium, thorium or plutonium,

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a facility for the disposal of a nuclear
      substance generated at another nuclear facility,

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a vehicle that is equipped with a nuclear
      reactor, and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any other facility that is prescribed for the
      development, production or use of nuclear energy or the
      production, possession or use of a nuclear substance, prescribed
      equipment or prescribed information,

    and includes, where applicable, the land on which the facility is
    located, a building that forms part of, or equipment used in
    conjunction with, the facility and any system for the management,
    storage or disposal of a nuclear substance. ([installation
    nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nuclear substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deuterium, thorium, uranium or an element with an
      atomic number greater than 92;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a derivative or compound of deuterium, thorium,
      uranium or of an element with an atomic number greater than 92;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a radioactive nuclide;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a substance that is prescribed as being capable
      of releasing nuclear energy or as being required for the
      production or use of nuclear energy;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a radioactive by-product of the development,
      production or use of nuclear energy; and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a radioactive substance or radioactive thing that
      was used for the development or production, or in connection with
      the use, of nuclear energy. ([substance
      nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[penalty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[penalty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an administrative monetary
    penalty imposed under this Act for a
    violation. ([pénalité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by regulation of
    the Commission. ([réglementaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou
    [réglementé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[radiation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radiation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the emission by a nuclear
    substance, the production using a nuclear substance, or the
    production at a nuclear facility of, an atomic or subatomic particle
    or electromagnetic wave with sufficient energy for
    ionization. ([rayonnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned to that word
    by section 3 of the [Access to Information
    Act](/eng/acts/A-1). ([document]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any means of air, water or
    land transport, and includes railway equipment within the meaning
    assigned to that expression by subsection 4(1) of the [Railway
    Safety Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2). ([véhicule]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Purpose of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368791 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to provide for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the limitation, to a reasonable level and in a manner
  that is consistent with Canada's international obligations, of the
  risks to national security, the health and safety of persons and the
  environment that are associated with the development, production and
  use of nuclear energy and the production, possession and use of
  nuclear substances, prescribed equipment and prescribed information;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the implementation in Canada of measures to which
  Canada has agreed respecting international control of the development,
  production and use of nuclear energy, including the non-proliferation
  of nuclear weapons and nuclear explosive devices.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368796 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Her Majesty

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to any order made
pursuant to section 5, this Act is binding on Her Majesty in right of
Canada or a province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders excluding Department of National
Defence, etc.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, exclude the Department of National Defence or the
Canadian Forces from the application of this Act or any regulations made
pursuant to this Act, to the extent and under the conditions specified
in the order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act not applicable to certain naval
vessels

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Act does not apply to
a nuclear-powered or nuclear-capable naval vessel of a foreign state
that is invited into Canada by Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission may exclude certain substances

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may, in
accordance with the regulations, exempt any activity, person, class of
person or quantity of a nuclear substance, temporarily or permanently,
from the application of this Act or the regulations or any provision
thereof.

## [Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368805 .Part}

### [Establishment of Commission]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368806 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of Commission

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a body corporate to be known as the Canadian
  Nuclear Safety Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agent of Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission is for all its purposes an agent of
  Her Majesty and may exercise its powers only as an agent of Her
  Majesty.

### [Objects]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368812 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The objects of the
Commission are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to regulate the development, production and use of
  nuclear energy and the production, possession and use of nuclear
  substances, prescribed equipment and prescribed information in order
  to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} prevent unreasonable risk, to the environment and
    to the health and safety of persons, associated with that
    development, production, possession or use,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} prevent unreasonable risk to national security
    associated with that development, production, possession or use, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} achieve conformity with measures of control and
    international obligations to which Canada has agreed; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to disseminate objective scientific, technical and
  regulatory information to the public concerning the activities of the
  Commission and the effects, on the environment and on the health and
  safety of persons, of the development, production, possession and use
  referred to in paragraph (a).

### [Members]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368820 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Constitution

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission consists of not more than seven permanent members to be
  appointed by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), the Governor in
  Council may appoint temporary members of the Commission whenever, in
  the opinion of the Governor in Council, it is necessary to do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council shall designate one of the
  permanent members to hold office as President.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Full- or part-time members

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The President is a full-time member of the Commission
  and the other members may be appointed as full-time or part-time
  members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure and term of appointment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Each permanent member holds office during good
  behaviour for a term not exceeding five years and may be removed at
  any time by the Governor in Council for cause.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary members

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Each temporary member holds office during good
  behaviour for a term not exceeding three years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A member is eligible to be re-appointed to the
  Commission in the same or another capacity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in any activity, have
  any interest in a business or accept or engage in any office or
  employment that is inconsistent with the member's duties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of conflict of interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member who becomes aware that the member is in a
  conflict of interest contrary to subsection (1) shall, within one
  hundred and twenty days, terminate the conflict or resign from the
  Commission.

### [President]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368843 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of President

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President is the chief executive officer of the Commission and has
  supervision over and direction of the work of the members and officers
  and employees of the Commission, including the apportionment of work
  among the members and, where the Commission sits in a panel, the
  assignment of a member or members to the panel and of a member to
  preside over the panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence, etc. of President

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the President is absent or incapacitated or if the
  office of President is vacant, such other member as may be designated
  by the Commission has all the powers and functions of the President
  during the absence, incapacity or vacancy, but no person may so act
  for a period exceeding ninety days without the approval of the
  Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The President may delegate any of the powers
  delegated to the President pursuant to subsection 16(2) or 17(2) to
  any officer or employee of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations made pursuant to paragraph
  44(1)(d), the President shall make such reports to the Minister as the
  Minister may require concerning the general administration and
  management of the affairs of the Commission and such of these reports
  as the Minister may direct shall form part of the report referred to
  in section 72.

### [Remuneration and Expenses]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368853 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Each member and each
former member to whom subsection 23(2) applies shall be paid such
remuneration and allowances as are fixed by the Governor in Council and
is entitled to be paid reasonable travel and living expenses incurred in
the course of the member's or former member's duties under this Act
while absent from, in the case of a full-time member or former member,
their ordinary place of work and, in the case of a part-time member or
former member, their ordinary place of residence.

### [Meetings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368856 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may meet for the conduct of its affairs at such times and
  in such places as are established by by-law of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Telephone conferences

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member may, subject to the by-laws of the
  Commission, participate in a meeting of the Commission by means of a
  telephone or other communication device that permits all persons
  participating in the meeting to hear one another, and a member who
  participates in a meeting by those means is deemed, for the purposes
  of this Act, to be present at the meeting.

### [By-laws]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368862 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may make
by-laws respecting the management and conduct of its affairs and to meet
its objects and carry out its duties under this Act and may, without
limiting the generality of the foregoing, make by-laws

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the calling of meetings of the Commission;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting generally the carrying on of the work of
  the Commission, including establishing the number of members that
  constitutes a quorum of the Commission or of a panel of the
  Commission; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} determining the procedures to be followed in
  proceedings other than those for which rules are otherwise prescribed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation to President

### [Civil Liability]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368885 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  civil proceedings may be brought against any member or other person or
  authority acting on behalf or under the direction of the Commission
  for anything done, reported or said in good faith in the course of the
  exercise or performance or purported exercise or performance of any
  power, duty or function of the Commission under this Act or for any
  alleged neglect or default in the execution in good faith of any such
  power, duty or function.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No civil proceedings may be brought against any
  person or authority referred to in subsection 44(8) or (9) for
  anything done, reported or said in good faith in the course of the
  exercise or performance or purported exercise or performance of any
  power, duty or function of the Commission under this Act or for any
  alleged neglect or default in the execution in good faith of any such
  power, duty or function.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission not relieved

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section relieves the Commission of
  liability in respect of a tort or extracontractual civil liability to
  which the Commission would otherwise be subject.

### [Powers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368903 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court of record

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission is a court of record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witnesses and records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission has, with respect to the appearance,
  summoning and examination of witnesses, the production and inspection
  of records, the enforcement of its orders and other matters necessary
  or proper for the due exercise of its jurisdiction, all such powers,
  rights and privileges as are necessary to carry out the duties of the
  Commission or to enforce any order, including, without limiting the
  generality of the foregoing, the power to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} issue a summons requiring a person

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to appear at the time and place stated in the
      summons to give evidence concerning any matter relevant to any
      subject-matter before the Commission, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to produce, either before or at a hearing, such
      records and things as the Commission considers appropriate to the
      full investigation and consideration of matters within its
      jurisdiction; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} administer oaths and examine any person under oath.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informal and expeditious

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} All proceedings before the Commission shall be dealt
  with as informally and expeditiously as the circumstances and
  considerations of fairness permit, but, in any case, within the
  prescribed period of time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not bound by legal rules

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission is not bound by the legal rules of
  evidence and in particular may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} receive and accept such evidence and information on
    oath, by affidavit or otherwise, as in its discretion it considers
    appropriate; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} refuse to accept any evidence that the Commission
    does not consider relevant or trustworthy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control of subject-matter

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Before conducting a proceeding, the Commission may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} stay or dismiss an application where the applicant
    is not in compliance with a term or condition of a licence or of any
    order made under this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine the issues in respect of which it will
    receive evidence or hear argument; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} exclude from consideration any matter on which it
    has rendered a decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control of proceedings

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may take such measures as it considers
  necessary to maintain order during proceedings before it and in
  particular may limit the participation in the proceedings of, or eject
  from the proceedings, any person who disrupts the proceedings and,
  where the person is ejected, continue the proceedings in the person's
  absence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance of peace officers

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A peace officer shall provide such assistance as the
  Commission or a member of the Commission may request for the purpose
  of maintaining order during proceedings before the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement of orders of the Commission

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Any decision or order of the Commission may, for the
  purpose of enforcement, be made a rule, order or decree of the Federal
  Court or of a superior court of a province and may be enforced in like
  manner as a rule, order or decree of that court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure for enforcement

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} To make a decision or order of the Commission a rule,
  order or decree of the Federal Court or a superior court, the usual
  practice and procedure of the court in those matters may be followed,
  or the President may provide to the court a certified copy of the
  decision or order under the seal of the Commission and thereupon the
  decision or order becomes a rule, order or decree of the court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may, in order to attain its objects,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter into arrangements, including an arrangement
    to provide training, with any person, any department or agency of
    the Government of Canada or of a province, any regulatory agency or
    department of a foreign government or any international agency;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish and maintain programs to provide the
    Commission with scientific, technical and other advice and
    information;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} establish and maintain a participant funding
    program to facilitate the participation of the public in proceedings
    under this Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} establish, and fix the terms of reference of,
    advisory, standing and other committees;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} establish and maintain offices and laboratories;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} disseminate objective scientific, technical and
    regulatory information to the public concerning the activities of
    the Commission and the effects, on the environment or on the health
    or safety of persons, of the development, production or use of
    nuclear energy or the production, possession or use of a nuclear
    substance, prescribed equipment or prescribed information;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} provide, under an appropriate security
    classification, to any department or agency of a foreign government
    or international agency with which Canada or the Commission has
    entered into an arrangement for the provision of such information,
    information relating to the development, production or use of
    nuclear energy or the production, possession or use of a nuclear
    substance, prescribed equipment or prescribed information including,
    after obtaining such assurances as it considers necessary to protect
    any commercial interest, protected commercial information;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} charge any fees that may be prescribed for any
    information, product or service that it provides under this Act or
    for the participant funding program that it establishes and
    maintains under this Act;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} certify and decertify prescribed equipment for the
    purposes of this Act;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} certify and decertify persons referred to in
    paragraph 44(1)(k) as qualified to carry out their duties under this
    Act or the duties of their employment, as the case may be; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} authorize the return to work of persons whose dose
    of radiation has or may have exceeded the prescribed radiation dose
    limits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refund of fees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may, under the prescribed

  (1)(g).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenditure of revenue from fees

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may spend for its purposes the revenue
  from the fees it charges for licences or classes of licences issued
  under section 24 in the fiscal year in which the revenues are received
  or in the next fiscal year.

### [Decision-Making]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368960 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision-making by Commission

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President or the presiding member shall not vote at a meeting of the
  Commission or a panel of the Commission, as the case may be, except
  that the President or presiding member has and shall cast the deciding
  vote in case of an equal division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Member ceasing to hold office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who has ceased to be a member may, with the
  authorization of the President and for such period as the President
  may fix, take part in the disposition of any matter in which that
  person became engaged while holding office as a member, and a person
  so authorized shall, for that purpose, be deemed to be a member of the
  Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where member unable to act

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where any member has taken part in a matter and for
  any reason is unable to take part in the disposition of the matter,
  the remaining members who took part in the matter may, with the
  authorization of the President, make the disposition notwithstanding
  that the quorum may have been lost.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where one-member panel unable to act

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a panel consists of one member and the member
  is at any time unable to dispose of a matter that is before the panel,
  the President may authorize another member to consider and dispose of
  the matter.

### [Licences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-368970 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licences

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may establish classes of licences authorizing the licensee
  to carry on any activity described in any of paragraphs 26(a) to (f)
  that is specified in the licence for the period that is specified in
  the licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may issue, renew, suspend in whole or
  in part, amend, revoke or replace a licence, or authorize its
  transfer, on receipt of an application

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the prescribed form;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} containing the prescribed information and
    undertakings and accompanied by the prescribed documents; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} accompanied by the prescribed fee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refund of fees

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may, under the prescribed
  circumstances, refund all or part of any fee referred to in paragraph
  (2)(c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for issuance, etc.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No licence shall be issued, renewed, amended or
  replaced --- and no authorization to transfer one given --- unless, in
  the opinion of the Commission, the applicant or, in the case of an
  application for an authorization to transfer the licence, the
  transferee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is qualified to carry on the activity that the
    licence will authorize the licensee to carry on; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} will, in carrying on that activity, make adequate
    provision for the protection of the environment, the health and
    safety of persons and the maintenance of national security and
    measures required to implement international obligations to which
    Canada has agreed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms and conditions of licences

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A licence may contain any term or condition that the
  Commission considers necessary for the purposes of this Act, including
  a condition that the applicant provide a financial guarantee in a form
  that is acceptable to the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of proceeds of financial
  guarantee

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may authorize the application of the
  proceeds of any financial guarantee referred to in subsection (5) in
  such manner as it considers appropriate for the purposes of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refund

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall grant to any person who provided
  a financial guarantee under subsection (5) a refund of any of the
  proceeds of the guarantee that have not been spent and may give the
  person, in addition to the refund, interest at the prescribed rate in
  respect of each month or fraction of a month between the time the
  financial guarantee is provided and the time the refund is granted,
  calculated on the amount of the refund.

### [Analysts and Inspectors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-369011 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of analysts

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may
designate as an analyst for the purposes of this Act any person whom the
Commission considers qualified.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of inspectors

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may designate as an inspector for the purposes of this Act
  any person whom the Commission considers qualified and any person so
  designated shall be provided with a certificate in the prescribed form
  certifying the person's designation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The certificate must list any category of place or
  vehicle that the inspector has been designated to inspect, any
  condition that must be satisfied by the inspector in conducting an
  inspection and any restriction on the powers conferred on the
  inspector, and on entering or inspecting a place or vehicle the
  inspector shall, if requested, show the certificate to the person in
  charge of the place or vehicle.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspection

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  order to verify compliance with this Act, the regulations, an order or
  decision made under this Act or a condition of a licence, an inspector
  may, at any reasonable time and in accordance with the inspector's
  certificate, enter and inspect

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a nuclear facility;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a nuclear-powered vehicle or a vehicle that the
    inspector believes on reasonable grounds is transporting a nuclear
    reactor, nuclear substance, prescribed equipment or prescribed
    information; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a vehicle or place in which the inspector believes
    on reasonable grounds there is a nuclear substance, prescribed
    equipment, prescribed information or a record that is required by
    this Act, the regulations, an order or decision made under this Act,
    or a condition of a licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dwelling-houses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an inspection of a dwelling-house

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the inspector shall give reasonable notice to the
    licensee that an inspection is to be carried out;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the inspection shall be conducted between the hours
    of 7:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m., local time, where the licensee and the
    inspector cannot agree on a time for the inspection; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the inspection shall be limited to the parts of the
    dwelling-house in which the nuclear substance, prescribed equipment,
    prescribed information or record is kept.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special circumstances

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An inspector may, at any time, enter and inspect a
  vehicle or place in which the inspector believes on reasonable grounds
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is contamination by a nuclear substance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a nuclear substance is being used, handled, stored
    or transported in a manner that may cause an unreasonable risk to
    the environment or to the health or safety of persons; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a nuclear facility is being operated in a manner or
    is in a state that may cause an unreasonable risk to the environment
    or to the health or safety of persons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Search without warrant

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
ensuring compliance with this Act and the regulations, an inspector may
exercise the powers of search and seizure provided for in section 487 of
the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) without a warrant if the conditions
for obtaining a warrant exist but, by reason of exigent circumstances,
it would not be feasible to obtain a warrant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of inspectors

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The measures that an
inspector may take, in exercising authority under this Act, include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} using any equipment or causing any equipment to be
  used;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} taking any measurement;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} carrying out any test on a vehicle or in relation to
  anything in a vehicle or place that the inspector has been designated
  to inspect;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} examining any vehicle or place and making or causing
  to be made a record of anything in any vehicle or place that the
  inspector has been designated to inspect and removing anything from
  such a vehicle or place for a reasonable period for the purpose of
  making a record of it;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} opening or requesting the opening of any receptacle;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} taking and disposing of any sample;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} examining any records that are required to be kept or
  reports that are required to be made under this Act, or any books,
  records, electronic data or other documents that the inspector
  believes on reasonable grounds relate to such records or reports; or

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} questioning any person in charge of, found in or
  having a connection with, any vehicle or place that the inspector has
  entered, inspected or searched or from which any thing is seized by an
  inspector.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspector may be accompanied

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** While exercising any
authority under this Act, an inspector may be accompanied by any other
person chosen by the inspector.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal or return of seized property

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** Any thing seized by an
inspector in the course of exercising authority under this Act or under
a warrant issued under the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) shall be
disposed of or returned to the owner or person in charge of it

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the order of a court, after all proceedings before
  the court in respect of an offence under this Act or the regulations
  in relation to the thing are finally concluded; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the order of the Federal Court, at any time on
  application by the Commission or the owner or person in charge.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of an inspector

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector may order that a licensee take any measure that the
  inspector considers necessary to protect the environment or the health
  or safety of persons or to maintain national security or compliance
  with international obligations to which Canada has agreed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of an inspector

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an inspector enters or inspects any vehicle or
  place

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the circumstances described in paragraph
    30(3)(a), the inspector may order that any person evacuate, close,
    seal, label or take any measures that the inspector considers
    necessary to decontaminate, the place or vehicle;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the circumstances described in paragraph
    30(3)(b), the inspector may order that any person use, handle, store
    or transport the nuclear substance in a manner that will not cause
    an unreasonable risk to the environment or to the health or safety
    of persons;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in either of the circumstances described in
    paragraph 30(3)(c), the inspector may order that any person operate
    the nuclear facility in a manner or put it in a state that will not
    cause an unreasonable risk to the environment or to the health or
    safety of persons; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the circumstances described in section 31, the
    inspector may order that any person take any measure that the
    inspector considers necessary to protect the environment or the
    health or safety of persons or to maintain national security or
    compliance with international obligations to which Canada has
    agreed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review by Commission

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An inspector shall refer any order made under this
  section to the Commission for review and the Commission shall confirm,
  amend, revoke or replace the order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to inspectors

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** Every owner of a place or
vehicle that is entered, inspected or searched or from which any thing
is seized by an inspector, every person in charge of or found in such a
place and every person with control of or found in such a vehicle shall
give the inspector all reasonable assistance to enable the inspector to
carry out the inspector's duties and functions under this Act.

  paragraph (2)(f) to the Commission for review and the Commission shall
  confirm, amend, revoke or replace the order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability for costs of measures

  information or a nuclear facility, the person who is in possession of

  or the owner or person in charge of the nuclear facility at the time

  pay any costs that any other person incurs in complying with the
  order.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in subsection (1) shall be construed to
  restrict the owner's or person's right of recourse against or

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section shall be construed as
  limiting an operator's liability under the [Nuclear Liability and
  Compensation Act](/eng/acts/N-28.1).

### [Exceptional Powers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-369253 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of contamination, etc.

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** Every person who, on
reasonable grounds, believes that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a place or vehicle is contaminated, in excess of the
  prescribed limit, by a radioactive nuclear substance, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an event has occurred that is likely to result in the
  exposure of persons or the environment to a dose of radiation in
  excess of the prescribed limits,

shall immediately notify the Commission or an appropriate authority of
the location and circumstances of the contamination or event.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contaminated land

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Commission believes, on reasonable grounds, that there is
  contamination in excess of the prescribed limit by a radioactive
  nuclear substance at any place, the Commission may conduct a public
  hearing in accordance with the prescribed rules of procedure to
  determine whether such contamination has occurred.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of contamination

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, after conducting a hearing, the Commission is
  satisfied that there is contamination referred to in subsection (1),
  the Commission shall file a notice of contamination in the land
  registry office or other office where title to land is recorded for
  the area in which the place is located, or in any other prescribed
  public office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where, after conducting a hearing, the Commission is
  satisfied that there is contamination referred to in subsection (1),
  the Commission may, in addition to filing a notice under subsection
  (2), order that the owner or occupant of, or any other person who has
  the management and control of, the affected land or place take the
  prescribed measures to reduce the level of contamination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing re cancellation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Commission believes on reasonable grounds
  that there is no longer contamination referred to in subsection (1) at
  a place with respect to which a notice has been filed under subsection
  (2), the Commission shall conduct a public hearing in accordance with
  the prescribed rules of procedure to determine whether such
  contamination is no longer present.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of cancellation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where, after conducting a hearing under subsection
  (4), the Commission is satisfied that the contamination is no longer
  present, the Commission shall file a notice of cancellation wherever a
  notice of contamination in relation to the place was filed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of determination

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A notice of contamination or cancellation is to be
  filed following a hearing under this section and, before such filing,
  the Commission shall give notice in the prescribed manner to the owner
  or occupant of the affected land and any other prescribed person of
  the Commission's determination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of order

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall give notice in the prescribed
  manner of an order made under subsection (3) to any person named in or
  subject to the order.

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-369281 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** Every person commits an
offence who

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} alters, otherwise than pursuant to the regulations or
  a licence, or misuses any thing the purpose of which is to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} protect the environment or the health or safety of
    persons from any risk associated with the development, production or
    use of nuclear energy or the possession or use of a nuclear
    substance, prescribed equipment or prescribed information, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} maintain national security or implement
    international obligations to which Canada has agreed, at a nuclear
    facility or at a place where, or vehicle in which, a nuclear
    substance is located;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} discloses prescribed information, except pursuant to
  the regulations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} fails to comply with any condition of a licence;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} knowingly makes a false or misleading written or oral
  statement to the Commission, a designated officer or an inspector;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fails to comply with an order of the Commission, a
  designated officer or an inspector;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} fails to assist or give information to an inspector
  when requested to do so, or otherwise interferes with the performance
  of an inspector's duties;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} takes disciplinary action against a person who
  assists or gives information to an inspector, a designated officer or
  the Commission in the performance of the person's functions or duties
  under this Act;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} except in the prescribed manner and circumstances,
  terminates, or varies the terms and conditions of, employment of a
  nuclear energy worker who has received or is committed to a dose of
  radiation in excess of the prescribed limits;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} falsifies a record kept pursuant to this Act or the
  regulations or to a condition of a licence;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} fails to comply with an order made under any of
  sections 58 to 62; or

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} fails to comply with this Act or any regulation made
  pursuant to this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence re security at nuclear facility

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding the
occurrence of a legal strike or lockout, every person commits an offence
who

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} while in charge of a nuclear facility, fails to
  ensure that there is present in that facility at all times the staff
  required, under the licence for that facility, to maintain that
  facility in a safe condition; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not report for duty at a nuclear facility or
  who, while on duty at a nuclear facility, withdraws their services,
  except where the person is not required to report or is relieved in
  accordance with a procedure set out in the licence for that facility.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence to possess certain nuclear
substances, etc.

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** Every person commits an
offence who, except as authorized by this Act, possesses a nuclear
substance, prescribed equipment or prescribed information that is
capable of being used to produce a nuclear weapon or a nuclear explosive
device.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who contravenes section 36 is guilty of an offence punishable
  on summary conviction and liable to a fine not exceeding \$5,000 or to
  imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who commits an offence under section 50
  is guilty of an indictable offence and liable to imprisonment for a
  term not exceeding ten years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who commits an offence other than an
  offence in respect of which subsection (1) or (2) applies

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an indictable offence and liable to a
    fine not exceeding \$1,000,000 or to imprisonment for a term not
    exceeding five years or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
    conviction and liable to a fine not exceeding \$500,000 or to
    imprisonment for a term not exceeding eighteen months or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

**[[51.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51.1 .sectionLabel}** A person shall not be
found to have contravened any provision of this Act, other than section
50, if it is established that the person exercised all due diligence to
prevent its commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offence

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** Where an offence under
this Act is committed or continued on more than one day, it shall be
deemed to be a separate offence for each day on which it is committed or
continued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** Proceedings by way of
summary conviction in respect of an offence under this Act may be
instituted not later than two years after the time when the
subject-matter of the proceedings arose or the Commission became aware
of the subject-matter of the proceedings.

  paragraphs 60(1)(a) to (j).

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the offender contravenes an order made under
  subsection (1) or is convicted of any other offence under this Act
  that is committed after the order under subsection (1) was made, the
  prosecutor may apply to the court to impose any sentence that could
  have been imposed if the passing of sentence had not been suspended.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders of court

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an offender has been convicted of an offence under this Act, in
  addition to any other punishment that may be imposed under this Act,
  the court may make an order having any or all of the following
  effects:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prohibiting the offender from committing any act or
    engaging in any activity that may, in the opinion of the court,
    result in the continuation or repetition of the offence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} directing the offender to take any measure that the
    court considers appropriate to protect the environment or the health
    or safety of persons from harm resulting from the act or omission
    that constituted the offence, or to remedy such harm;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} directing the offender to publish, in the
    prescribed manner and at the offender's own expense, the facts
    relating to the conviction;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} directing the offender to notify, in the prescribed
    manner and at the offender's own expense, any person who is affected
    by the offender's conduct, of the facts relating to the conviction;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} directing the offender to post a bond or pay an
    amount of money into court that the court considers appropriate to
    ensure compliance with any order, prohibition, direction or
    requirement provided for in this subsection;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} directing the offender to submit to the Commission,
    on application to the court by the Commission made within three
    years after the date of conviction, such information with respect to
    the activities of the offender as the court considers appropriate in
    the circumstances;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} directing the offender to compensate the
    Commission, in whole or in part, for the cost of any remedial or
    preventive measure taken by or caused to be taken on behalf of the
    Commission as a result of the act or omission that constituted the
    offence;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} directing the offender to perform community
    service, subject to such reasonable conditions as may be imposed by
    the court on the recommendation of the Commission;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} directing the offender to pay an amount for the
    purposes of conducting research into the use and disposal of any
    nuclear substance, prescribed equipment or nuclear facility in
    respect of which the offence was committed; or

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} requiring the offender to comply with such other
    conditions as the court considers appropriate in the circumstances
    for securing the offender's good conduct and for preventing the
    offender from repeating the same offence or committing any other
    offence under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force and duration of order
  or condition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An order made under subsection (1), 58(1) or 59(1)
  shall come into force on the day on which the order is made or on such
  other day as the court may determine and shall not continue in force
  for more than three years after that day.

  of, or damage to, property, as a result of the commission of the

  as the court considers reasonable, an amount of compensation for the
  loss or damage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an amount that is ordered to be paid under

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Variation of sanctions

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes or

  liable to an administrative monetary penalty of an amount to be
  determined in accordance with the regulations.

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a person designated under

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of violation must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name the person that is believed to have committed

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out the relevant facts surrounding the
    violation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set out the amount of the penalty for the
    violation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} inform the person of their right to request a
    review with respect to the violation or the amount of the penalty,

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} inform the person of the manner of paying the

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} inform the person that, if they do not pay the

### [Rules About Violations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-369421 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain defences not available

- **[[65.06]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65.06
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person named in a notice of
  violation does not have a defence by reason that the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exercised due diligence to prevent the commission
    of the violation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reasonably and honestly believed in the existence
    of facts that, if true, would exonerate the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Common law principles

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every rule and principle of the common law that
  renders any circumstance a justification or excuse in relation to a
  charge for an offence under this Act applies in respect of a violation
  to the extent that it is not inconsistent with this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2012, c. 19, s. 130
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing violation

**[[65.07]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65.07 .sectionLabel}** A violation that is

violation for each day on which it is committed or continued.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2012, c. 19, s. 130
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violation or offence

- **[[65.08]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65.08
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Proceeding with any act or omission
  as a violation under this Act precludes proceeding with it as an
  offence under this Act, and proceeding with it as an offence under
  this Act precludes proceeding with it as a violation under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violations not offences

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a violation is not an offence
  and, accordingly, section 126 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)

  of the Board, may be brought against the Commission in any court that
  would have had jurisdiction if the action, suit or proceeding had been
  brought against the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of legal proceedings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal proceeding to which
  the Board is party pending in any court immediately before the
  commencement day may, on that day, be continued by or against the
  Commission in the like manner and to the same extent as it could have
  been continued by or against the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pending proceedings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Proceedings pending before the Board immediately
  before the commencement day shall be taken up and continued before the
  Commission under and in accordance with this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of staff

- **[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who, immediately before the commencement day, was an officer or
  employee of the Board becomes, on that day, an officer or employee, as
  the case may be, of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed appointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person to whom subsection (1) applies shall be
  deemed to have been appointed in accordance with subsection 16(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No severance pay

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, nothing in subsection (1)
  shall be construed as entitling any person to severance pay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of service

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person to whom subsection (1) applies shall be
  deemed, while an officer or employee of the Board, to have been
  employed in the Public Service for the purpose of determining the
  person's entitlement to leave or any other employment benefit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licences

**[[80]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80 .sectionLabel}** A licence that is issued
pursuant to regulations made under paragraph 9(b) of the Atomic Energy
Control Act and that is in force immediately before the commencement day
is deemed to have been issued under section 24 of this Act and to be in
force for the remainder of the period for which it was issued under the
Atomic Energy Control Act and any fees paid or payable under the AECB
Cost Recovery Fees Regulations, 1996 in respect of such a licence are
deemed to be paid or payable, as the case may be, under this Act.

- --- 2023, c. 32, s. 50

  - [50]{.lawlabel} The Act is amended by adding the following after
    section 27:

    :::: section
    ::: AmendedText
    #### [High-security Sites]{.HTitleText2} {#high-security-sites .SchedHeadL2}

    - ###### Security at site {#security-at-site .MarginalNote}

      - [27.1]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} A licensee who operates a
        high-security site is responsible for ensuring the security of
        the site in accordance with section 27.2 and the prescribed
        requirements.

      - ###### Nuclear security officers {#nuclear-security-officers .MarginalNote}

        [(2)]{.lawlabel} The licensee may, in accordance with the
        regulations,

        - [(a)]{.lawlabel} designate as a nuclear security officer any
          employee of the licensee who meets the prescribed
          requirements; and

        - [(b)]{.lawlabel} suspend or revoke the designation.

      - ###### Restriction {#restriction .MarginalNote}

        [(3)]{.lawlabel} An employee designated as a nuclear security
        officer may exercise the powers and perform the duties and
        functions of a nuclear security officer only if they are
        designated as a peace officer under subsection 27.3(1).

      - ###### Preservation and maintenance of public peace {#preservation-and-maintenance-of-public-peace .MarginalNote}

        [(4)]{.lawlabel} A nuclear security officer's duties include the
        preservation and maintenance of the public peace at the
        high-security site.

    - ###### On-site nuclear response force {#on-site-nuclear-response-force .MarginalNote}

      - [27.2]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} A licensee who operates a
        high-security site shall at all times maintain an on-site
        nuclear response force that is composed of nuclear security
        officers who are

        - [(a)]{.lawlabel} designated as peace officers under subsection
          27.3(1);

        - [(b)]{.lawlabel} trained in the handling and use of firearms,
          prohibited weapons and prohibited devices and qualified to
          handle and use them;

        - [(c)]{.lawlabel} posted to the high-security site on a
          permanent basis; and

        - [(d)]{.lawlabel} armed and equipped in accordance with the
          regulations.

      - ###### Additional training {#additional-training .MarginalNote}

        [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the licensee acquires any firearms,
        prohibited weapons or prohibited devices in accordance with an
        authorization granted under subsection 27.4(1), the licensee
        shall ensure that the members of the on-site nuclear response
        force are trained in the handling and use of those firearms,
        prohibited weapons or prohibited devices and that the members
        are qualified to handle and use them.

      - ###### Exemption {#exemption .MarginalNote}

        [(3)]{.lawlabel} The licensee is not required to comply with
        subsection (1) if the licensee

        - [(a)]{.lawlabel} makes on-site security arrangements with a
          local, provincial or federal police service or with the
          Canadian Forces that meet the prescribed requirements and are
          approved by the Commission; or

        - [(b)]{.lawlabel} takes other security measures that meet the
          prescribed requirements and are approved by the Commission.

    - ###### Peace officer designation {#peace-officer-designation .MarginalNote}

      - [27.3]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may, in
        accordance with the regulations,

        - [(a)]{.lawlabel} designate any nuclear security officer as a
          peace officer for a high-security site; and

        - [(b)]{.lawlabel} suspend or revoke the designation.

      - ###### Effect of designation {#effect-of-designation .MarginalNote}

        [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), a nuclear security
        officer who is designated as a peace officer is a peace officer
        within the meaning of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) for
        the purpose of performing their duties and functions at the
        high-security site for which they are designated and for the
        purpose of performing any prescribed off-site duties and
        functions that are ancillary to their duties and functions at
        the site.

      - ###### Limits on powers {#limits-on-powers .MarginalNote}

        [(3)]{.lawlabel} A nuclear security officer who is designated as
        a peace officer may exercise only the following powers as a
        peace officer and may do so only at the high-security site for
        which they are designated:

        - [(a)]{.lawlabel} verifying the identity of any individual;

        - [(b)]{.lawlabel} conducting searches of individuals and
          things;

        - [(c)]{.lawlabel} arresting without a warrant, in accordance
          with the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), any individual whom
          the nuclear security officer finds committing an offence under
          this Act, the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or the
          [Controlled Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8) that
          poses a risk to the safety or security of the site or any
          individual whom the nuclear security officer believes on
          reasonable grounds has committed or is about to commit such an
          offence at the site; and

        - [(d)]{.lawlabel} seizing any thing

          - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the nuclear security officer believes
            on reasonable grounds poses a risk to the safety or security
            of the site, or

          - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in relation to which the nuclear security
            officer believes on reasonable grounds that an offence
            referred to in paragraph (c) has been, is being or is about
            to be committed.

      - ###### Use of force {#use-of-force .MarginalNote}

        [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 25(1), (3) and (4) of the [Criminal
        Code](/eng/acts/C-46) apply to a nuclear security officer who is
        designated as a peace officer only when they are exercising
        their powers as a peace officer at the high-security site for
        which they are designated.

      - ###### Arrested individuals and seized things {#arrested-individuals-and-seized-things .MarginalNote}

        [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a nuclear security officer who is designated
        as a peace officer arrests an individual under paragraph (3)(c)
        or seizes a thing under paragraph (3)(d), the nuclear security
        officer shall arrange for the appropriate police service to take
        custody of the individual or thing as soon as feasible after the
        arrest or seizure.

      - ###### Complaints process {#complaints-process .MarginalNote}

        [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall, in accordance with the
        regulations, ensure that there is a process for handling
        complaints with respect to the conduct of nuclear security
        officers in the exercise of their powers or the performance of
        their duties and functions as peace officers.

    - ###### Firearms, prohibited weapons and prohibited devices {#firearms-prohibited-weapons-and-prohibited-devices .MarginalNote}

      - [27.4]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite Part III of the
        [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) and the [Firearms
        Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6), and subject to subsection (2), the
        Commission may grant an authorization, with or without
        conditions, to a licensee who operates a high-security site to
        acquire, possess, transfer and dispose of firearms, prohibited
        weapons and prohibited devices in order to carry out the
        responsibility referred to in subsection 27.1(1).

      - ###### Restriction {#restriction-1 .MarginalNote}

        [(2)]{.lawlabel} An authorization granted under subsection (1)
        may authorize a licensee to transfer firearms, prohibited
        weapons and prohibited devices only to a public service agency
        or to another licensee to whom an authorization has been granted
        under that subsection.

      - ###### Transfer to licensee {#transfer-to-licensee .MarginalNote}

        [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite Part III of the [Criminal
        Code](/eng/acts/C-46) and the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6),
        the Commission, a public service agency or any person in lawful
        possession of firearms, prohibited weapons or prohibited devices
        may transfer firearms, prohibited weapons and prohibited devices
        to a licensee to whom an authorization has been granted under
        subsection (1).

      - ###### Reporting to Registrar of Firearms {#reporting-to-registrar-of-firearms .MarginalNote}

        [(4)]{.lawlabel} A licensee to whom an authorization is granted
        under subsection (1) shall, as if the licensee were a public
        service agency, provide the Registrar of Firearms referred to in
        section 82 of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6) with the
        advisory information referred to in section 12 of the [Public
        Agents Firearms Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-203) and
        the reports referred to in sections 8 to 10, 11, 13, 14 and 16
        of those Regulations. However, any reference to "October 31,
        2008" in those Regulations is to be read as a reference to the
        day on which the authorization is granted to the licensee and
        any reference to "October 31, 2009" in those Regulations is to
        be read as a reference to the first anniversary of the day on
        which the authorization is granted.

      - ###### Reporting to Commission {#reporting-to-commission .MarginalNote}

        [(5)]{.lawlabel} A licensee to whom an authorization is granted
        under subsection (1) shall, in accordance with the regulations,
        report to the Commission with respect to the advisory
        information and reports that the licensee provides under the
        [Public Agents Firearms
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-203).

      - ###### Definition of [[public service agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} {#definition-of-public-service-agency .MarginalNote}

        [(6)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[public service
        agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
        1 of the [Public Agents Firearms
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-203).

    - ###### Delegation to President {#delegation-to-president .MarginalNote}

      [27.5]{.lawlabel} The Commission may delegate to the President the
      powers conferred on it under subsection 27.3(1) or section 27.4.
