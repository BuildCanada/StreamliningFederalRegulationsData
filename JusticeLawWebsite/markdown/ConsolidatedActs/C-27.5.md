## Centennial Flame Research Award Act {#centennial-flame-research-award-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1991, c. 17
:::

Assented to 1991-03-27

An Act respecting the establishment of the Centennial Flame Research
Award to publicize the contributions to Canadian public life of persons
with disabilities
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-79889 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Centennial Flame Research Award Act](/eng/acts/C-27.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-79892 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[award]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[award]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the award established by section
    3; ([bourse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means those committees of the
    Senate and the House of Commons established to deal with matters
    relating to the status of disabled persons, acting as administrators
    of the fund established under section 3; ([comité]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[disabled persons]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[disabled persons]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means persons who consider
    themselves disadvantaged by reason of any persistent physical,
    mental, psychiatric, learning or sensory impairment or who believe
    that a potential employer would likely consider them so
    disadvantaged; ([personne handicapée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning on
    April 1 in one year and ending on March 31 in the next
    year. ([exercice]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fund established

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a fund to be known as the Centennial Flame Research
  Award Fund into which shall be paid, in each fiscal year,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all money collected from the Centennial Flame
    monument on Parliament Hill in Ottawa; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any money otherwise received as a donation to the
    Centennial Flame Research Award Fund.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of fund

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Following an approved application for an award under
  this Act in the second or any subsequent fiscal year of the fund,
  there shall be paid from the fund an award for the purpose set out in
  section 4.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of award

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of the
Centennial Flame Research Award Fund is to provide awards of money to
enable disabled persons to conduct research and prepare reports on the
contributions of one or more disabled persons to the public life of
Canada or the activities of Parliament.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility of candidates

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  Canadian citizen who is a disabled person is eligible to receive the
  award and may apply to the Committee to that effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The award shall be granted annually.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Selection

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Committee shall receive the applications for the
  award and select one recipient who qualifies under subsection (1) and
  meets such other criteria as the Committee considers appropriate and
  the Speakers of the Senate and the House of Commons approve.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administration

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board of Internal Economy of the House of Commons shall supervise the
  collection of money for the Centennial Flame Research Award Fund and,
  subject to this Act and to such regulations as the Board may make in
  that behalf, may take any measures necessary for carrying out the
  purposes of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Award renewable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The award is renewable if the Speaker of the Senate
  and the Speaker of the House of Commons approve a recommendation of
  the Committee to that effect.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As soon
  as possible after the end of each fiscal year, the chairman of the
  Committee shall cause an annual report on the administration of this
  Act to be laid before both Houses of Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report of recipient

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The annual report shall include the report prepared
  by the award recipient in accordance with subsection (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of the report of the recipient

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The award recipient shall prepare a report describing
  the result of the research conducted by the recipient and presented in
  whatever medium or format that the recipient considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presence of recipient

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The report of the award recipient shall be laid
  before the Committee and the Committee may call the recipient as a
  witness for the occasion.
