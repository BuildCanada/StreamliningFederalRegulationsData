## Forgiveness of Debts (sub-Saharan Africa) Act {#forgiveness-of-debts-sub-saharan-africa-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1989, c. 25
:::

Assented to 1989-12-15

An Act relating to the forgiveness of debts incurred or assumed in
respect of certain official development assistance loans made by the
Government of Canada to the governments of certain countries in
sub-Saharan Africa
::::

::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Forgiveness of Debts (sub-Saharan Africa) Act](/eng/acts/F-30.4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forgiveness of debts

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The official development
assistance debts owing by the governments of certain countries in
sub-Saharan Africa to the Government of Canada under the loan agreements
entered into between those governments and referred to in the schedule
are hereby forgiven.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deletion from the accounts of Canada

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The amount outstanding as
recorded in the accounts of Canada in respect of the debts forgiven by
section 2 is hereby written off as a budgetary expenditure and deleted
from those accounts.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#F-30.4_en_1){#F-30.4_en_1-ID0EBCA}[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
.sectionLabel}** This Act shall come into force on a day to be fixed by
order of the Governor in Council.

## Republic of Cameroon {#republic-of-cameroon .SchedHeadL1}

- [1]{.lawlabel} Twelve loan agreements entered into between the
  Government of the Republic of Cameroon and the Government of Canada
  and dated May 17, 1974, October 2, 1974, August 21, 1975, January 29,
  1976, June 10, 1977, May 26, 1978, July 30, 1979, October 15, 1981,
  November 8, 1983, February 29, 1984, February 29, 1984 and October 4,
  1984, respectively.

## People's Republic of the Congo {#peoples-republic-of-the-congo .SchedHeadL1}

- [2]{.lawlabel} Two loan agreements entered into between the Government

  dated December 29, 1973 and November 30, 1974, respectively.
