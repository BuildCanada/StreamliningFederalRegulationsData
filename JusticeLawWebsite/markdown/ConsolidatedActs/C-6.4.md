## Canada-Israel Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act {#canada-israel-free-trade-agreement-implementation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1996, c. 33
:::

Assented to 1996-12-18

An Act to implement the Canada-Israel Free Trade Agreement
::::

::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-147264 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada-Israel Free Trade Agreement Implementation
Act](/eng/acts/C-6.4).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-147267 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in this Act.

  [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Free Trade Agreement
      entered into between the Government of Canada and the Government
      of the State of Israel and signed on July 31, 1996, as amended by
      the Canada -- Israel Free Trade Amending Protocol 2018 signed on
      May 28, 2018. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Joint Commission
      established under paragraph 1 of Article 18.1 of the
      Agreement. ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the whole or any portion
      of an Act of Parliament or a regulation, order or other instrument
      issued, made or established in the exercise of a power conferred
      by or under an Act of Parliament. ([texte législatif
      fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of any provision of
      this Act, means the member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada
      designated under section 9 as the Minister for the purposes of
      that provision. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Agreement shall be published in the Canada Treaty
  Series.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation consistent with Agreement

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, this
Act, any provision of an Act enacted or amended by Part II and any other
federal law that implements a provision of the Agreement or fulfils an
obligation of the Government of Canada under the Agreement shall be
interpreted in a manner consistent with the Agreement.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-147279 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to implement the Agreement, the objectives of which, as elaborated more
specifically through its provisions, are to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} strengthen the bilateral commercial relationship
  between Canada and the State of Israel;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} improve access to the Israeli market for Canadian
  businesses by reducing and eliminating tariffs, addressing non-tariff
  barriers, enhancing cooperation and increasing transparency in
  regulatory matters;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} ensure a high level of environmental protection
  through comprehensive and legally-binding commitments;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} build on the respective international commitments of
  Canada and the State of Israel on labour matters; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} promote gender equality and encourage women's
  economic empowerment and the use of voluntary corporate social
  responsibility standards and principles, as well as promote access for
  small and medium-sized enterprises to the opportunities created by the
  Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-147285 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Causes of action under Part I

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person has any cause of action and no proceedings of any kind shall be
  taken, without the consent of the Attorney General of Canada, to
  enforce or determine any right or obligation that is claimed or arises
  solely under or by virtue of Part I or an order or regulation made
  under Part I.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Causes of action under the Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person has any cause of action and no proceedings
  of any kind shall be taken, without the consent of the Attorney
  General of Canada, to enforce or determine any right or obligation
  that is claimed or arises solely under or by virtue of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of Agreement to water

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act or the Agreement applies to natural surface or
ground water in liquid, gaseous or solid state.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Implementation of Agreement Generally]{.HTitleText1} {#h-147296 .Part}

### [Approval of Agreement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-147297 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement approved

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement is hereby
approved.

### [Designation of Minister]{.HTitleText2} {#h-147300 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order designating Minister

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate any member of the Queen's Privy Council for
Canada to be the Minister for the purposes of any provision of this Act.

### [Administrative and Institutional Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-147303 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of representative on the
Commission

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister for
International Trade is the principal representative of Canada on the
Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of expenditures

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
shall pay its appropriate share of any expenditures incurred by or on
behalf of the Commission.

### [Panels, Committees, Subcommittees, Working Groups, Expert Groups and Other Bodies]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1200099 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister for International
Trade

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister for International Trade may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint representatives of Canada to any committee,
    subcommittee, working group or other body referred to in paragraph 6
    of Article 18.1 of the Agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} appoint a panellist in accordance with paragraph 2
    of Article 19.7 of the Agreement; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} propose candidates to serve as the chair of a
    panel, or select the chair, in accordance with that Article 19.7.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister of Labour

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Labour may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint representatives of Canada to any committee,
    working group or expert group referred to in sub-paragraph 4(a) of
    Article 12.7 of the Agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} appoint a panellist in accordance with paragraph 4
    of Annex 12.13.3 of the Agreement; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} propose candidates to serve as the chairperson of a
    Review Panel, or select the chairperson, in accordance with
    paragraphs 4 and 5 of that Annex.

### [Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-147316 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders --- Article 19.13 of Agreement

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, for the purpose of suspending benefits in
  accordance with Article 19.13 of the Agreement, by order, do any of
  the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend rights or privileges granted by Canada to
    the State of Israel or to goods of Israel under the Agreement or any
    federal law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} modify or suspend the application of any federal
    law, with respect to the State of Israel or to goods of Israel;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} extend the application of any federal law to the
    State of Israel or to goods of Israel;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} take any other measure that the Governor in Council
    considers necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless repealed, an order made under subsection (1)
  has effect for the period specified in the order.
