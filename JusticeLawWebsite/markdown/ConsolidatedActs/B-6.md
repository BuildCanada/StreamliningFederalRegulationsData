## Boards of Trade Act {#boards-of-trade-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. B-6
:::

An Act respecting the incorporation of boards of trade
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-30753 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Boards of Trade Act](/eng/acts/B-6).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-30758 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[board of trade]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[board of trade]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes chamber of commerce,
    and, for the purposes of the appointment of weighers of grain under
    this Act, means any board of trade or chamber of commerce
    incorporated under any Act of Parliament, of the legislature of the
    former Province of Canada or of the legislature of any
    province; ([chambre de commerce]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes "board of directors" and
    trustees of the governing body, however
    designated; ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[district]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[district]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any judicial district, or
    temporary judicial district, that is set apart or constituted as
    such by any Act of Parliament or of the legislature of any province
    or by any proclamation issued under or by virtue of any such Act,
    and any city, county, town or village, to any of which may be added
    one or more townships selected for that purpose, or any group of
    municipalities or divisions, within and for which a board of trade
    is established under this Act, and includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the Provinces of Saskatchewan, Alberta and
      Newfoundland and Labrador, any electoral district, as constituted
      for elections to the legislative assembly for any of those
      provinces, within and for which a board of trade is established,
      and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of British Columbia and in Yukon,
      any mining division, or any tract of country described as
      extending to certain specified distances and in certain specified
      directions from any stated point, within and for which a board of
      trade is established. ([district]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. B-6, s. 2
- 2002, c. 7, s. 86
- 2015, c. 3, s. 172

## [PART I]{.HLabel1} {#h-30768 .Part}

### [Incorporation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-30769 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons who may form a board of trade

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  number of persons, not fewer than thirty, who are merchants, traders,
  brokers, mechanics, manufacturers, managers of banks or insurance
  agents, carrying on business in, or resident in, a district that has a
  population of not less than two thousand five hundred, or in the
  Province of British Columbia or in Yukon not less than one thousand
  five hundred, may associate themselves together as a board of trade
  and appoint a secretary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of names restricted

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall, within any district in which there
  is a board of trade that is registered under this Act, use the words
  "Board of Trade" or "Chamber of Commerce" as part of the name under
  which that person is incorporated or doing business, or any other
  words so similar as to be liable to be confused therewith, unless that
  person is incorporated as a body corporate under this Act or under a
  special or general Act of Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any person who contravenes subsection (2) is guilty
  of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine of not more
  than five hundred dollars and costs and not less than one hundred
  dollars and costs or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
  months or to both.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. B-6, s. 3
- 2002, c. 7, s. 87
:::

Version](section-3-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure to change boundaries of
districts

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** On application by a board
of trade for any district under its corporate seal, signed by the
president and secretary of the board of trade and duly authorized by
by-law thereof, the Governor in Council may change the boundaries of
that district.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of formation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The persons associating
themselves together as a board of trade pursuant to subsection 3(1)
shall, under their hands and seals, make a certificate specifying the
name assumed by the board of trade and by which it is to be known, the
name of the district in which it is situated and its business is
transacted and the name of the person appointed secretary to the board
of trade.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statutory declaration

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the district referred to in section 5 is situated wholly or partly
  within a district for which there is an existing board of trade, the
  certificate shall be accompanied by a statutory declaration of two or
  more of the persons signing it with respect to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the facts in that regard;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the population of the district of the existing
    board of trade;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the population of the district of the proposed new
    board of trade;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the population of the district of the existing
    board of trade as diminished by the proposed change; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any facts or considerations that made the
    establishment of the new board of trade expedient.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recording certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In any case referred to in subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the existing board of trade shall be afforded an
    opportunity to show cause against the proposed change; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the certificate shall be recorded only with the
    sanction and authority of the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of subsection (2)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) applies to any application for
  incorporation under a special or general Act of Parliament, with the
  right to use the name "Board of Trade" or "Chamber of Commerce" or any
  other name so similar as to be liable to be confused therewith.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  certificate referred to in section 5 shall be acknowledged before a
  notary public, commissioner for taking affidavits or justice of the
  peace by the secretary of the board of trade, and shall be forwarded
  to the Minister of Industry who shall cause the certificate to be
  recorded in a register to be kept for that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the certificate, duly certified by the
  Minister of Industry, is evidence of the existence of the board of
  trade to which the certificate relates.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. B-6, s. 7
- 1992, c. 1, s. 145(F)
- 1995, c. 1, s. 62
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  persons named as incorporators in the certificate referred to in
  section 5, and such other persons as afterwards join them, are hereby
  authorized to carry into effect the objects for which the board of
  trade to which the certificate relates was constituted and to exercise
  the powers and privileges conferred by this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Body corporate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The persons referred to in subsection (1) and their
  associates, assigns and successors, by the name and style specified in
  the certificate, are a body corporate, with power to acquire, sell and
  convey any real property or immovable necessary for the objects of the
  board of trade.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. B-6, s. 8
- 2011, c. 21, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election of officers

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** When sections 3 to 7 have
been complied with, it is competent for a majority of the persons named
as incorporators in the certificate referred to in section 5 to hold a
meeting for the election of a president, vice-president and members of
the council and, without notice, to make and enact the by-laws, rules
and regulations authorized by this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal domicile

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The usual place of
meeting of a board of trade registered under this Act shall be held to
be the legal domicile thereof, at which service of any notice or process
may be made.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 10
:::

### [Officers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-30825 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers of board of trade

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The officers of every
board of trade shall be a president, vice-president and secretary, who,
together with at least eight other members, shall constitute a council
of the corporation, which shall be called "The Council of the Board of
Trade of (*adding the name of the district*)", and who shall have the
powers and perform the duties set out in this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election of president and members of
council

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  first quarterly meeting held in each year, the members of a
  corporation present, or a majority of them, shall elect, in the manner
  prescribed by the by-laws of the corporation, from among the members
  thereof, a president, vice-president and secretary, and at least eight
  other members, who, with the president, vice-president and secretary,
  shall form the council of the corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The persons elected shall hold their offices until
  others are elected in their place, at the first quarterly meeting of
  the next year or until they are removed from office or vacate it under
  the by-laws of the corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure of election

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Where an election
pursuant to section 12 does not take place at the first quarterly
meeting, the corporation is not thereby dissolved, but the election may
be held at any general meeting of the corporation, called in the manner
provided in section 17, and the members of the council in office shall
remain members until the election is held.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 13
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oath of office

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The president and
vice-president of the corporation shall, before entering on the duties
of their office, take and subscribe before the mayor of the city or town
constituting the district, or before any justice of the peace, an oath
in the following form:

I swear that I will faithfully and truly perform my duty as of the board
of trade, and that I will, in all matters connected with the discharge
of that duty, do all things, and only such things, as I truly and
conscientiously believe to be adapted to promote the objects for which
the board was constituted, according to the true intent and meaning of
the same. So help me God.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancies

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a member of the council of the corporation dies or resigns his office,
  or is absent for six months continuously from the meetings of the
  council, the council may, at any meeting thereof, elect a member of
  the corporation to be a member of the council in the place of the
  member who has died, resigned or is absent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Majority

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A new member of the council shall be elected by a
  majority of the members of the council present at any meeting thereof
  at which there is a quorum present.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A member elected pursuant to subsection (2) holds
  office until the next annual election.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 15
:::

### [Meetings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-30855 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Majority to have full power

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** At any annual or general
meeting of a corporation, whether for the purpose of electing members of
the council of the corporation or for any other purpose, a majority of
members present at the meeting are competent to do and perform all acts
that, either under this Act or under any by-law of the corporation, are
or shall be directed to be done at a meeting.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 16
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General quarterly meetings

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  members of a corporation shall hold general quarterly meetings in each
  year at any place within the district in respect of which the
  corporation was incorporated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to be published

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notice of general quarterly meetings of a
  corporation, naming the time and place, shall be given by the
  secretary of the council of the corporation for the time being, at
  least three days prior to the meetings, through one newspaper or
  otherwise, as is thought necessary by the council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 17
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special general meeting

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The council of a
corporation, or a majority of its members, may call a general meeting of
the corporation for any of the purposes of this Act by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a notice inserted in one or more newspapers published
  within the district in which the corporation is situated, one day
  prior to the meeting; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a circular letter to each member of the corporation,
  signed by the secretary of the corporation, and mailed one day prior
  to the meeting.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 18
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings of council

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  meetings of the council of a corporation shall be open to all members
  of the corporation who may attend those meetings, but not take part in
  any proceedings thereat.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minutes

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Minutes of the proceedings at all meetings, whether
  of the council or of the corporation, shall be entered, in books to be
  kept for that purpose, by the secretary of the corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presiding officer to sign

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The minutes of the proceedings of the council or of
  the corporation shall be signed by the president or vice-president of
  the corporation or the person who presides at the meeting at which the
  minutes were taken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Books open for inspection

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The books referred to in subsection (2) shall be open
  at all reasonable hours to any member of the corporation free of any
  charge.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 19
:::

### [Membership]{.HTitleText2} {#h-30884 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members of corporation

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person resident in a district in respect of which a board of trade was
  incorporated, who is or has been a merchant, broker, trader, mechanic,
  manufacturer, manager of a bank or insurance agent, is eligible to
  become a member of the corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proposal of candidate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At any general meeting of a corporation, any member
  of the corporation may propose any person referred to in
  subsection (1) as a candidate for membership in the corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Two-thirds majority required

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a proposal referred to in subsection (2) is
  carried by a majority of two-thirds of the members of the corporation
  then present, the person who is proposed for membership is from that
  time a member of the corporation and has all the rights and is subject
  to all the obligations of the other members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons other than traders

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any person who is not a merchant, broker, trader,
  mechanic, manufacturer, manager of a bank or insurance agent is
  eligible to become a member of a corporation, in the manner described
  in this section, if that person is recommended by the council of the
  corporation at any general meeting of the corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 20
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retirement of members

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Any member of a
corporation who intends to retire therefrom or to resign his membership
may do so, at any time, on giving to the secretary of the corporation
ten days notice in writing of his intention, and on discharging any
lawful liability that is standing on the books of the corporation
against him at the time of the notice.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 21
:::

### [By-laws]{.HTitleText2} {#h-30900 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws and regulations

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The majority of the
members of a corporation present at any general meeting of the
corporation may make by-laws and regulations for the government of the
corporation providing for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the admission and subscriptions of members;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the imposition of penalties;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the expulsion or retirement of members;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the management of its council, officers and affairs;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the guidance of the board of arbitrators referred to
  in this Act;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the fixing of the date and place of the regular
  meetings of its council;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the powers to be exercised by its council; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} all other matters concerning the government of the
  corporation not inconsistent with this Act or any other law of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 22
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}To be binding

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** By-laws made pursuant to
section 22 are binding on all members of the corporation, its officers
and employees, and all other persons lawfully under its control.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 23
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to be given

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** No by-law shall be made
by a corporation at any meeting of the corporation, without notice in
writing thereof having been given by one member and seconded by another
member at a previous meeting, and duly entered in the books of the
corporation as a minute of the corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 24
:::

### [Subscriptions and Dues]{.HTitleText2} {#h-30921 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subscriptions and penalties

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following sums, namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all subscriptions of members due to the
    corporation, under any by-law,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all penalties incurred under any by-law by any
    person bound thereby, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all other sums of money due to the corporation,

  shall be paid to the secretary thereof, and in default of payment are
  recoverable in an action brought in the name of the corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It shall only be necessary, in any action under
  subsection (1), to allege that the person is indebted to the
  corporation in the sum of money, the amount of the subscription in
  arrears, penalty incurred or other sum due, whereby an action has
  accrued to the corporation by virtue of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 25
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** On the trial or hearing
of any action under subsection 25(1), it is sufficient for the
corporation to prove that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the defendant, at the time the demand for payment was
  made, was or had been a member of the corporation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount claimed as the subscription, penalty or
  otherwise was standing unpaid on the books of the corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 26
:::

### [Powers of Council]{.HTitleText2} {#h-30939 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings of council

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  council of a corporation may hold meetings and adjourn them when
  necessary, and may, at those meetings, transact such business as is,
  by this Act or by the by-laws of the corporation, assigned to the
  council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How convened

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The meetings of the council shall be convened by the
  secretary of the corporation, at the instance of the president
  thereof, or on the request of any two members of the council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 27
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of council

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  council of a corporation has, in addition to the powers hereby
  expressly conferred on it, such powers as are assigned to it by any
  by-law of the corporation not inconsistent with this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The council shall not have or be given by any by-law
  the power of enacting or altering any by-law or admitting any member.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 28
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** Five or more members of
the council of a corporation constitute a quorum, and a majority of the
quorum may do all things within the powers of the council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 29
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Who to preside

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At all
  meetings of the council of a corporation, and all general meetings of
  the corporation, the president, or in his absence, the vice-president,
  or if both are absent, any member of the council then present who is
  chosen for the occasion, shall preside.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Casting vote

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In all cases of equality of votes on any division,
  the presiding officer at a meeting of the council has a casting vote.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 30
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws, rules and regulations

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  council of a corporation shall frame such by-laws, rules and
  regulations as appear to it best adapted to promote the welfare of the
  corporation and the purposes of this Act, and shall submit them for
  adoption at a general meeting of the corporation called for that
  purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Make by-laws concerning weighers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The council of a corporation may make by-laws that
  require that every weigher employed in connection with a grain
  elevator within the district for which the corporation is established
  be licensed and take an oath to faithfully, truly and impartially
  execute and perform the duties of weigher, and cause any person
  applying to be licensed as a weigher at grain elevators to be examined
  as to his capacity in such manner as the council deems proper.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The council may, if satisfied as to the fitness of an
  applicant referred to in subsection (1) to perform his duties, issue
  to the applicant a licence to act as a weigher.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 31
:::

### [Board of Arbitration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-30974 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board of Arbitration

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  time appointed pursuant to section 12 for the election of the council
  of a corporation, and in the same manner, the members of the
  corporation may elect from their number twelve persons who shall form
  a board, which shall be called the Board of Arbitration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any three persons elected under subsection (1) have
  power to arbitrate on, and make their award in, any commercial case or
  difference that is voluntarily referred to them by the parties
  concerned.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Three arbitrators to act

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When parties in a case referred to in subsection (2)
  agree to bind themselves to submit a matter in dispute between them to
  the decision of the Board of Arbitration, the submission is deemed to
  be made to any three members of the Board, who may, either by the
  special order of the Board or by virtue of any general rules adopted
  by the Board, or under any by-law of the corporation relating to the
  consideration of any cases so submitted, be appointed to hear,
  arbitrate and decide on the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision binding

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A decision made under subsection (3) is binding on
  the Board of Arbitration and the parties making the submission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A submission referred to in subsection (3) shall be
  in Form 1 of the schedule, or to the same effect.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. B-6, s. 32
- 2011, c. 21, s. 3
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-32-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 32"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members to be sworn

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  members of the Board of Arbitration shall, before they act as such,
  take and subscribe, before the president or vice-president of the
  corporation, an oath in Form 2 of the schedule, that they will
  faithfully, impartially and diligently perform their duties as members
  of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oath to be kept

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The oath referred to in subsection (1) shall be kept
  among the documents of the corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 33
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members of Board

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** Any member of the council
of a corporation may, at the same time, be a member of the Board of
Arbitration.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 34
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of arbitrators respecting oaths,
etc.

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  three members appointed to hear any case submitted for arbitration
  under section 32 or any two of them have full power to examine, under
  oath, which oath any one of the three members is hereby empowered to
  administer, any party or witness who, appearing before them, is so
  examined, and shall give their award thereupon in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decisions binding

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An award given under subsection (1) binds the parties
  according to the terms of the submission and the provisions of this
  Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 35
:::

### [Licensing of Weighers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-31006 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Weighers to be examined

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the council of a corporation has passed a by-law that requires that
  every weigher employed in connection with a grain elevator within the
  district for which the board of trade is established be licensed and
  take the oath set out in section 37, the board of trade may cause any
  person applying to be licensed as a weigher at grain elevators to be
  examined as to his capacity in such manner as the council deems
  proper.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The council may, if satisfied as to the fitness of an
  applicant referred to in subsection (1) to perform his duties, issue
  to the applicant a licence to act as a weigher.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who acts as a weigher at a grain
  elevator within a district is, unless he has been licensed under
  subsection (2) and has taken the oath of office set out in section 37,
  liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of ten dollars for each
  offence.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 36
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Weigher to take oath

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  weigher licensed under section 36 shall forthwith take and subscribe
  before a justice of the peace an oath of office in the following form
  or to the same effect:

  I, ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}, do solemnly swear
  (or affirm) that I will faithfully, truly and impartially, to the best
  of my skill and ability, execute and perform the duties of weigher. So
  help me God.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Custody of oath

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The oath taken under subsection (1) shall remain in
  the custody of the justice of the peace administering it, and any copy
  thereof certified by the justice of the peace is evidence that the
  oath has been taken and subscribed in accordance with this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 37
:::

### [Affiliation with Canadian Chamber of Commerce]{.HTitleText2} {#h-31024 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affiliation with Canadian Chamber of
Commerce

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  board of trade duly registered under this Act may become affiliated
  with the Canadian Chamber of Commerce on complying with all the terms
  and requirements of that organization, and may be represented at its
  annual meeting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election of delegates

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The delegates or representatives to the annual
  meeting of the Canadian Chamber of Commerce shall be elected at a
  general meeting or by the council of the board of trade desiring that
  representation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 38
:::

### [Change of Name]{.HTitleText2} {#h-31032 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of name

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** On application by a
corporation under its corporate seal, signed by its president and
secretary, the name of the corporation may be changed if the Governor in
Council is satisfied that the change is not desired for any improper
purpose and is not otherwise objectionable.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 39
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication in [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/)

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** A change of name of a
corporation is conclusively established by the insertion in the
[](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/)[Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/)
by the Minister of Industry of a notice of the change.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. B-6, s. 40
- 1992, c. 1, s. 145(F)
- 1995, c. 1, s. 62
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and liabilities

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** No rights or liabilities
of a corporation are affected by a change of its name, and all actions
commenced by or against the corporation prior to the change may be
proceeded with by or against it under its former name.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 41
:::

### [Annual Summary]{.HTitleText2} {#h-31045 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual summary

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  board of trade shall, on or before June 1 in every year, make a
  summary as of the March 31 preceding, specifying the following
  particulars:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the board of trade;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which the board of trade is
    incorporated, giving the date thereof;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the last general meeting of the
    members of the board of trade was held; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the names and addresses of the persons who at the
    date of the return compose the council of the board of trade.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}To be filed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The summary referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  completed and filed in duplicate in the Department of Industry on or
  before June 1 in every year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duplicates

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each of the duplicates referred to in subsection (2)
  shall be signed by the secretary of the board of trade.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Default

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a board of trade fails to comply with any
  requirements of this section, it is liable on summary conviction to a
  fine not exceeding twenty dollars for every day during which the
  failure continues.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every member of the council of a board of trade who
  knowingly or wilfully authorizes or permits a failure referred to in
  subsection (4) is liable to the same fine.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. B-6, s. 42
- 1992, c. 1, s. 145(F)
- 1995, c. 1, s. 63
:::

### [Dissolution]{.HTitleText2} {#h-31063 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dissolution of corporation

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** On its being made to
appear that a board of trade has become incapable of exercising or has
ceased to exercise its franchises, the Governor in Council may, subject
to such terms and conditions as he thinks just and proper, dissolve the
board as a corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 43
:::

## [PART II]{.HLabel1} {#h-31068 .Part}

### [Formation of Board]{.HTitleText2} {#h-31069 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Formation

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  number of persons, not fewer than thirty, who are directly or
  indirectly engaged or interested in trade, commerce or the economic
  and social welfare of any district, whether residents of the district
  or not, may associate themselves together as a board of trade for the
  purpose of promoting and improving trade and commerce and the
  economic, civic and social welfare of the district.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate, registration and
  incorporation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the provisions of sections 5 and 7 have been
  complied with, section 8 applies to an association formed under
  subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 44
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Memorandum of agreement

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** The persons referred to
in section 44 shall forward to the Minister of Industry, together with
the certificate of formation, a memorandum of agreement in duplicate,
which shall set out the by-laws or regulations of the proposed board of
trade and shall, more particularly, provide by-laws or regulations on
the following matters:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} conditions of membership, including societies or
  companies becoming members of the corporation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} mode of holding meetings, rights of voting and of
  making, repealing or amending by-laws or regulations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} appointment and removal of the directors, trustees,
  committee or officers, and their respective powers and remuneration;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} provision for audit of accounts and appointment of
  auditors;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} determination whether or how members may withdraw
  from the corporation; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} provision for custody of the seal of, and certifying
  of documents issued by, the corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. B-6, s. 45
- 1992, c. 1, s. 145(F)
- 1995, c. 1, s. 62
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  by-laws and regulations referred to in section 45 may be amended at a
  general meeting called for that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A variation or amendment of any by-law or regulation
  shall not be in force or acted on without the approval of the Minister
  of Industry.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. B-6, s. 46
- 1992, c. 1, s. 145(F)
- 1995, c. 1, s. 62
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing boards of trade

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** An existing board of
trade incorporated by or under any Act of Parliament, of the legislature
of the former Province of Canada or of the legislature of any province
may apply under this Part to establish the board of trade under this
Part.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 47
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** A board of trade
established under this Part has all the powers and authorities conferred
on a board of trade by Part I and is subject to all the provisions of
Part I except in so far as those provisions may be varied by this Part.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. B-8, s. 48
:::

Know all men, that the undersigned and the undersigned (*if there are
more parties, that is, more separate interests, mention them*) having a
difference as to the respective rights of those parties, as in the case
attached hereto, have agreed and bound themselves under a penalty of
dollars, to perform and abide by the award to be made by the Board of
Arbitration of the Board of Trade of in that case, under the penalty, to
be paid by the party refusing to perform or abide by the award to the
party ready and willing to perform or abide by it.

In witness whereof, the parties hereto have hereunto set their hands and
affixed their seals at the of on the day of ![blank

line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}.

cases in which I act as arbitrator, give a true and just award,
according to the best of my judgment and ability, without fear, favour
