## National Anthem Act {#national-anthem-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. N-2
:::

An Act respecting the national anthem of Canada
::::

::::::::::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [National Anthem Act](/eng/acts/N-2).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 5, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National anthem

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The words and music of the
song "O Canada", as set out in the schedule, are designated as the
national anthem of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 5, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public domain

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The words and music of the
national anthem of Canada are hereby declared to be in the public
domain.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 5, s. 3
:::
