## Public Sector Pension Investment Board Act {#public-sector-pension-investment-board-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1999, c. 34
:::

Assented to 1999-09-14

An Act to establish the Public Sector Pension Investment Board, to amend
the Public Service Superannuation Act, the Canadian Forces
Superannuation Act, the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation
Act, the Defence Services Pension Continuation Act, the Royal Canadian
Mounted Police Pension Continuation Act, the Members of Parliament
Retiring Allowances Act and the Canada Post Corporation Act and to make
a consequential amendment to another Act
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-402396 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Public Sector Pension Investment Board Act](/eng/acts/P-31.7).

section apply in this Act.

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Public Sector Pension

    3. ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Constitution of the Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-402412 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board established

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  established a body corporate to be known as the Public Sector Pension
  Investment Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not agent of Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board is not an agent of Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not part of federal public
  administration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Directors, officers, employees, and agents and
  mandataries, of the Board are not part of the federal public
  administration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The head office of the Board shall be in the National
  Capital Region as described in the schedule to the [National Capital
  Act](/eng/acts/N-4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Canada Not-for-profit Corporations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-7.75)

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The [Canada Not-for-profit Corporations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-7.75) does not apply to the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11)

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Part X of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11), except for sections 89.8 to 89.92, 113.1, 132 to
  147 and 154.01, does not apply to the Board. For the purposes of those
  sections, any reference to section 131 of that Act shall be read as a
  reference to section 35 of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports and special examination

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Any report or information in respect of the Board
  that is provided to the Minister under sections 132 to 147 of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) shall also be provided
  to the Minister of National Defence and the Minister of Public Safety
  and Emergency Preparedness. The Minister may only require a special
  examination under subsection 138(2) of that Act if the Minister has
  consulted with those Ministers.

## [Capital and Shares]{.HTitleText1} {#h-402430 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capital

- **[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  capital of the Board is \$100. The Minister shall pay the capital of
  the Board out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shares

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The capital is divided into 10 shares having a par
  value of \$10 each. The shares shall be issued to the Minister to be
  held on behalf of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The shares issued to the Minister shall be registered
  by the Board in the name of the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2006, c. 4, s. 217
:::

## [Objects and Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-402440 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  objects of the Board are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to manage amounts that are transferred to it under
    subsections 54(2) and 55.2(5) and section 59.4 of the [Canadian
    Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17), subsections 43(2) and
    44.2(5) of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36)
    and subsections 28(2) and 29.2(5) of the [Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11) in the best interests of
    the contributors and beneficiaries under those Acts; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to invest its assets with a view to achieving a
    maximum rate of return, without undue risk of loss, having regard to
    the funding, policies and requirements of the pension plans
    established under the Acts referred to in paragraph (a) and the
    ability of those plans to meet their financial obligations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs of operation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The costs associated with the operation of the Board
  shall be paid out of the funds.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall determine from which funds the
  costs shall be paid, but no amount shall be taken out of the Canadian
  Forces Pension Fund or the Canadian Forces Superannuation Investment
  Fund --- or, if regulations are made under section 59.1 of the
  [Canadian Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17), from the fund
  referred to in section 59.3 of that Act --- without consulting the
  Minister of National Defence, or from the Royal Canadian Mounted
  Police Pension Fund or the Royal Canadian Mounted Police
  Superannuation Investment Fund without consulting the Minister of
  Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Board

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board has the capacity and, subject to this Act, the rights, powers
  and privileges of a natural person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No inconsistent business or activity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board and its subsidiaries shall not, directly or
  indirectly, carry on any business or activity or exercise any power
  that is inconsistent with the Board's objects, or that the Board is
  restricted by this Act from carrying on or exercising, and shall not,
  directly or indirectly, exercise any of its powers in a manner
  contrary to this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No invalidity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No act of the Board, including a transfer of
  property, is invalid by reason only that the Board was without the
  capacity or power to so act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall consult the Board if changes to
  pension plan design or funding are proposed with respect to the
  pension plans created under the Acts referred to in paragraph 4(1)(a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investment management services

- **[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Without limiting the generality of
  subsection 5(1) and despite subsection 5(2), the Board may incorporate
  a subsidiary for the purpose of providing investment management
  services to the Canada Growth Fund Inc. in accordance with any terms
  agreed to by the subsidiary and the Canada Growth Fund Inc.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 4(2), the costs associated with
  the establishment and operation of the subsidiary and with the
  provision of investment management services are to be paid by the
  Canada Growth Fund Inc.

## [Payment into Consolidated Revenue Fund]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1481367 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of Board

- **[[5.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall, at the request of the Minister, pay into the Consolidated
  Revenue Fund

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any amount that is required for the payment of
    benefits under subsection 44.2(6) of the [Public Service
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), subsection 55.2(6) of the
    [Canadian Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17) or regulations
    made under section 59.1 of that Act, or subsection 29.2(6) of the
    [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any amount that is determined under paragraph
    44.4(2)(b) of the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36), paragraph 55.4(2)(b) of the [Canadian Forces
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17) or paragraph 29.4(2)(b) of the
    [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11);
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any amount that is required for the payment of the
    costs determined under section 44.5 of the [Public Service
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), section 55.5 of the [Canadian
    Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17) or section 29.5 of the
    [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11)
    or the costs for the administration of a fund established by
    regulations made under section 59.1 of the [Canadian Forces
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before requesting the payment of an amount referred
  to in paragraph (1)(a) or (c), the Minister shall consult

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of National Defence if the amount is
    in relation to the [Canadian Forces Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-17); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
    Preparedness if the amount is in relation to the [Royal Canadian
    Mounted Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11).

## [Management]{.HTitleText1} {#h-402461 .Part}

### [Board of Directors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-402462 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board of directors

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall be managed by a board of 13 directors, including the
  Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disqualified persons

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following persons are disqualified from being
  directors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who is less than 18 years of age;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who is of unsound mind and has been so
    found by a court in Canada or elsewhere;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who has the status of a bankrupt;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a person who is not a natural person;

  - [(d.1)]{.lawlabel} a person who is an employee of the Board;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a person who is an agent or employee of Her Majesty
    in right of Canada;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a person who is a member of the Senate or House of
    Commons of Canada or a member of a provincial legislature;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is entitled to or has been granted a pension
      benefit under the [Public Service Superannuation
      Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the [Canadian Forces Superannuation
      Act](/eng/acts/C-17) or the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
      Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is entitled to or has been granted a
      superannuation or pension benefit of a prescribed kind that is
      payable out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund and is chargeable to
      a Superannuation Account or another account in the accounts of
      Canada or is payable out of a fund, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is subject to a superannuation or pension fund
      or plan under which he or she may become entitled to a benefit
      referred to in subparagraph (i) or (ii);

  - [(g.1)]{.lawlabel} a person who is a member of an advisory committee
    established under section 41 of the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36), section 49.1 of the [Canadian Forces
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17) or section 25.1 of the [Royal
    Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a person who is an agent or employee of the
    government of a foreign country or any political subdivision of a
    foreign country; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a person who is not a resident of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principal duties

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this Act, the board of directors shall manage or supervise the
  management of the business and affairs of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specific duties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Without limiting the generality of subsection (1),
  the board of directors shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on an annual basis, establish written investment
    policies, standards and procedures for each fund that the Board
    manages;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} monitor the officers and employees of the Board to
    ensure compliance with the investment policies, standards and
    procedures established under paragraph (a);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prepare or have prepared financial statements for
    each fund on a quarterly and on an annual basis in accordance with
    this Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} establish procedures for the identification of real
    or potential conflicts of interest and procedures to resolve those
    conflicts;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} establish a code of conduct for officers and
    employees of the Board; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} designate a committee of the board of directors to
    monitor application of the conflict of interest procedures and the
    code of conduct.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to delegate

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and the by-laws, the board of directors may delegate
  to the Chairperson, to a committee of the board of directors or to any
  officer of the Board any of the powers or duties of the board of
  directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limits on power

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The board of directors may not delegate the power to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} adopt, amend or repeal by-laws;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish the Board's investment policies,
    standards and procedures;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fill a vacancy in a committee of directors;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} appoint officers to the Board or fix their
    remuneration; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} approve the annual financial statements of the
    Board and any other financial statements issued by the Board.

### [Directors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-402504 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of directors

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  director shall be appointed by the Governor in Council, on the
  recommendation of the Minister, to hold office during good behaviour
  for a term, not exceeding four years, that will ensure, as far as
  possible, the expiry in any one year of the terms of office of not
  more than one half of the directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendations from list of nominating
  committee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The recommendation of the Minister under
  subsection (1) shall be made from the list of qualified candidates
  proposed by the nominating committee established under section 10.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendations for certain directors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For two of the directors, the Minister's
  recommendation under subsection (1) shall be made from among the
  candidates who are included on the list in accordance with subsection
  10(6).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nominating committee

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall establish a nominating committee to establish a list of
  qualified candidates for proposed appointment as directors. The
  committee shall consist of eight members appointed as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an independent chairperson appointed by the
    Minister after consulting with the Minister of National Defence and
    the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness who, at the
    time of appointment,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is not entitled to nor has been granted a pension
      benefit under the [Public Service Superannuation
      Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the [Canadian Forces Superannuation
      Act](/eng/acts/C-17) or the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
      Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is neither entitled to nor has been granted a
      superannuation or pension benefit of a prescribed kind that is
      payable out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund and is chargeable to
      a Superannuation Account or another account in the accounts of
      Canada or is payable out of a fund, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is not subject to a superannuation or pension
      fund or plan under which he or she may become entitled to a
      benefit referred to in subparagraph (i) or (ii);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} two members appointed by the Minister, after the
    Minister has received recommendations from the advisory committee
    referred to in section 41 of the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36), one of whom must represent persons employed in
    the public service within the meaning of that Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one member appointed by the Minister who is in
    receipt of a pension under any of the [Canadian Forces
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17), the [Public Service
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and the [Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} two members appointed by the Minister of National
    Defence after that minister has received recommendations from the
    advisory committee referred to in section 49.1 of the [Canadian
    Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} two members appointed by the Minister of Public
    Safety and Emergency Preparedness after that minister has received
    recommendations from the advisory committee referred to in section
    25.1 of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/R-11).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The members of the nominating committee hold office
  for five years and are eligible for reappointment for one or more
  additional terms.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister who appoints a member may remove that
  member at any time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disqualified persons

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} When the nominating committee is establishing a list
  of suitable candidates for appointment as directors, it shall take
  into account that the persons referred to in subsection 6(2) are not
  eligible to be directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors for consideration

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} When the nominating committee is establishing a list
  of qualified candidates for proposed appointment as directors, it
  shall have regard to the desirability of having on the board of
  directors a sufficient number of directors with proven financial
  ability or relevant work experience such that the Board will be able
  to effectively achieve its objects.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inclusion of certain candidates

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} When including a candidate who the Minister may
  recommend under subsection 9(3) on a list of qualified candidates for
  proposed appointment as directors, the nominating committee shall
  consult the portion of the National Joint Council of the Public
  Service that represents employees and shall have regard to any factors
  for consideration provided by that portion of the National Joint
  Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment of directors

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  director is eligible for reappointment for one or more additional
  terms of office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may remove a director for
  cause.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation in office

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If no person is appointed to take office as a
  director on the expiry of the term of an incumbent director, the
  incumbent director continues in office until a successor is appointed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancy

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a person ceases to be a director during the term
  for which the person was appointed, the Minister shall consult the
  list established by the nominating committee and shall appoint a
  qualified person to hold office as a director for the remainder of the
  term.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration of directors

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A director is entitled to
receive from the Board the remuneration that may be fixed by the
by-laws, which remuneration shall be fixed having regard to the
remuneration received by persons having similar responsibilities and
engaged in similar activities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resignation

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  resignation of a director becomes effective at the time the Board
  receives a written resignation or at the time specified in the
  resignation, whichever is later.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of resignation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall send a copy of a director's
  resignation to the Clerk of the Privy Council within 15 days after
  receiving it.

### [Chairperson]{.HTitleText2} {#h-402549 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall, on the recommendation of the Minister after
  the Minister has consulted with the board of directors, the Minister
  of National Defence and the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
  Preparedness, designate one of the directors as Chairperson to hold
  office during good behaviour.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may remove the Chairperson
  for cause.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presiding at meetings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson shall preside at all meetings of the
  board of directors and may exercise the powers and perform the duties
  and functions that are specified by the board of directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacement of Chairperson

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Chairperson is absent at any meeting of the
  board of directors, one of the directors present who is chosen to so
  act by the directors present shall preside and have all the powers,
  duties and functions of the Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incapacity of Chairperson

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the Chairperson is incapable of performing his or
  her duties or there is a vacancy in the office of Chairperson, the
  Minister may designate another director to exercise the powers and
  perform the duties and functions of the Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration of Chairperson

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson is entitled to receive from the Board
  the remuneration that may be fixed by the by-laws, which remuneration
  shall be fixed having regard to the remuneration received by persons
  having similar responsibilities and engaged in similar activities.

### [Officers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-402565 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of officers

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  board of directors may, subject to the by-laws, designate the offices
  of the Board, appoint officers of the Board and specify their duties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors not officers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A director is not eligible to be appointed an officer
  of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Two or more offices

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person may hold two or more offices of the Board.

### [Standard]{.HTitleText2} {#h-402573 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  director and officer of the Board in exercising any of the powers of a
  director or an officer and in discharging any of the duties of a
  director or an officer shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} act honestly and in good faith with a view to the
    best interests of the Board; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} exercise the care, diligence and skill that a
    reasonably prudent person would exercise in comparable
    circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special knowledge or skill

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A director or officer of the Board who in fact
  possesses, or by reason of profession or business ought to possess, a
  particular level of knowledge or skill relevant to the director's or
  officer's powers or duties shall employ that particular level of
  knowledge or skill in the exercise of those powers or the discharge of
  those duties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reliance on statements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A director or an officer of the Board is deemed to
  comply with subsections (1) and (2) if he or she relies in good faith
  on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} financial statements of the Board represented by an
    officer of the Board, or represented in a written report of the
    Board's auditor, to be a fair reflection of the financial condition
    of the Board; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a report of an accountant, lawyer, notary or other
    professional person whose profession lends credibility to a
    statement made by the person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to comply

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  director, officer and employee of the Board shall comply with this Act
  and the by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No exculpation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No provision in any contract, in any resolution of
  the Board or in the by-laws relieves any director, officer or employee
  of the Board from the duty to act in accordance with this Act or
  relieves a director, officer or employee from liability for a breach
  of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors' and officers' insurance

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may purchase and maintain insurance for the benefit of a
  director or officer of the Board, a former director or officer of the
  Board, or any person who acts or acted at the Board's request as a
  director or officer of an entity of which the Board is or was a
  shareholder or in which the Board has or had a financial interest ---
  and the personal representatives of that person --- against any
  liability incurred by the person in that capacity unless the liability
  relates to a failure to act honestly and in good faith.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If no insurance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Board does not purchase and maintain insurance
  under subsection (1), the Board shall indemnify each person referred
  to in that subsection, out of the funds, against any liability
  incurred by the person in that capacity, so long as the person acted
  honestly and in good faith.

### [Decisions of Board of Directors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-402595 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decisions

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Apart from the meeting
required by section 49, the board of directors, or any committee of the
board of directors, need not have any meetings unless required to do so
by the by-laws. It shall make decisions by majority vote of a quorum of
members, either in person or otherwise, in accordance with the by-laws.

### [Conflicts of Interest]{.HTitleText2} {#h-402598 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of director's interest

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  director or officer of the Board shall disclose in writing to the
  Board or request to have entered in the minutes of a meeting of the
  board of directors or one of its committees the nature and extent of
  the director's or officer's interest, as prescribed,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as a party to a transaction or proposed transaction
    with the Board; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as a director or an officer of any entity that is a
    party to a transaction or proposed transaction with the Board or as
    a person who holds a material interest in any such entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of disclosure for director

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The disclosure must be made, in the case of a
  director,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the meeting at which a proposed transaction is
    first considered;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the director was not at the time of that meeting
    interested in a proposed transaction, at the first meeting after the
    director becomes so interested;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the director becomes interested after a
    transaction is made, at the first meeting after the director becomes
    so interested; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if a person who is interested in a transaction
    later becomes a director, at the first meeting after the person
    becomes a director.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of disclosure for officer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The disclosure must be made, in the case of an
  officer,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} without delay after the officer becomes aware that
    the transaction or proposed transaction is to be considered or has
    been considered at a meeting of the board of directors or one of its
    committees;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the officer becomes interested after the
    transaction is made, without delay after the officer becomes so
    interested; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if a person who is interested in the transaction
    later becomes an officer, without delay after the person becomes an
    officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of disclosure for director or
  officer

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a transaction or proposed transaction is one that,
  in the ordinary course of the Board's business, would not require
  approval by the board of directors, a director or officer shall
  disclose in writing to the Board or request to have entered in the
  minutes of a meeting of the board of directors or one of its
  committees the nature and extent of the interest of the director or
  officer without delay after the director or officer becomes aware of
  the transaction or proposed transaction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voting

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A director referred to in subsection (1) shall not
  vote on a resolution or participate in a discussion to approve the
  transaction mentioned in that subsection unless the transaction is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one relating primarily to the director's
    remuneration as a director of the Board or one of its subsidiaries;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one for insurance or indemnity under section 18; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one with a subsidiary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing disclosure

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, a general notice to
  the board of directors or to one of its committees by a director or
  officer, declaring that the director or officer is a director or
  officer of, or has a material interest in, an entity and is to be
  regarded as interested in any transaction made with that entity, is a
  sufficient declaration of interest in relation to any transaction so
  made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Avoidance standards

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A transaction between the Board and one or more of
  its directors or officers, or between the Board and another entity of
  which a director or officer of the Board is a director or officer or
  in which a director or officer of the Board has a material interest,
  is not null, void or voidable by reason only of that relationship or
  by reason only that a director with an interest in the transaction is
  present at or is counted to determine the presence of a quorum at a
  meeting of the board of directors or on one its committees that
  authorized the transaction, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the director or officer disclosed the interest in
    accordance with subsection (2), (3), (4) or (6);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the transaction was approved by the directors; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transaction was reasonable and fair to the
    Board at the time it was approved.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to court

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If a director or officer of the Board fails to
  disclose an interest in a transaction in accordance with this section,
  a court may, on the application of the Board, set aside the
  transaction on any terms that it thinks fit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of
  [[transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  includes a contract, a guarantee and an investment.

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-402633 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No constructive notice

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** No person dealing with
the Board or with any person who has acquired rights from the Board is
deemed to have notice or knowledge of the contents of a document, other
than an Act of Parliament or any instrument required to be published in
the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) pursuant to the
[Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22), concerning the Board by
reason only that the document is available at the head office of the
Board or has been made public.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity of acts

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** An act of the Chairperson
or other director of the Board, or of the chief executive officer or
other officer of the Board, is not invalid by reason only of an
irregularity in that person's appointment or a defect in that person's
qualifications.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assertions

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Board may not assert
against a person dealing with the Board --- or with a person who has
acquired rights from the Board --- other than one who has knowledge that
the facts asserted are true

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that this Act or the by-laws have not been complied
  with; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that a document issued by a director, officer or an
  agent or mandatary of the Board having apparent authority to issue the
  document is not valid or genuine by reason only that the person who
  issued the document lacked actual authority to issue the document.

## [By-laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-402642 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  board of directors may make by-laws that are consistent with this Act
  to govern the conduct and management of the Board's business and
  affairs, including by-laws

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the administration, management and control of
    the Board's property;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} governing the calling of meetings of the board of

    and the quorum and procedure in all matters relating to those
    meetings;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the functions, duties and remuneration
    of the directors, officers and employees of the Board; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the establishment of committees of the
    board of directors and the appointment of members to those
    committees.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A by-law is effective as soon as it is made or on a
  later date that may be stated in the by-law to be its effective date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy to ministers

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  board of directors shall provide a copy of every by-law and every
  amendment to or repeal of any by-law to the Minister, the Minister of
  National Defence and the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
  Preparedness within 14 days after its effective date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws available to the public

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of every by-law shall be kept at the head
  office of the Board. Anyone is entitled, during the usual business
  hours of the Board, to examine the by-laws and, on payment of a
  reasonable fee, to make copies of or take extracts from them.

### [Audit Committee]{.HTitleText2} {#h-402672 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of audit committee

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The audit committee shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} require the Board's management to implement and
  maintain appropriate internal control procedures;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} review, evaluate and approve those internal control
  procedures;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} review and approve the Board's annual financial
  statements and report to the board of directors before those
  statements are approved by the board of directors;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} meet with the Board's auditor to discuss the Board's
  annual financial statements and the auditor's report;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} review all investments and transactions that could
  adversely affect the return on the Board's investments that are
  brought to the committee's attention by the Board's auditor or
  officers;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} meet with the chief internal auditor of the Board, or
  with the person acting in a similar capacity, and with the Board's
  management, to discuss the effectiveness of the internal control
  procedures; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} perform the other duties that the board of directors
  assigns to it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audit committee may request consideration
of matters

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The board of directors
shall consider, on request of the audit committee, a matter of concern
to the committee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Auditor's right to attend meetings

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board's auditor is entitled to receive notice of and to attend
  meetings of the board of directors and meetings of the audit
  committee, at the Board's expense, and to be heard at those meetings
  on matters relating to the auditor's duties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights if no meeting

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the board of directors or the audit committee
  proposes to make a decision with respect to matters referred to in
  subsection (1) without holding a meeting, the auditor is entitled to
  notice of a proposed decision to be made by the board or the committee
  and the proposed decision shall not be made until the auditor has been
  given the opportunity to make submissions on the matter in writing, in
  accordance with the by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requiring auditor's attendance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board's auditor shall attend meetings of the
  audit committee, if requested to do so by a member of the audit
  committee --- and shall attend meetings of the board of directors, if
  requested to do so by a director --- at the Board's expense.

### [Investment Committee]{.HTitleText2} {#h-402691 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of investment committee

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** The investment committee
shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} perform the duties that are assigned to it by the
  board of directors;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} approve the engagement of investment managers
  empowered with discretionary authority to invest the assets of the
  Board;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} meet with the officers and employees of the Board to
  discuss the effectiveness of the Board's investment policies and the
  achievement of the Board's objects;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} require management to implement and maintain
  appropriate procedures to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} monitor the application of the Board's investment
    policies, standards and procedures, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the Board's agents and mandataries
    comply with this Act and the Board's investment policies, standards
    and procedures; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} review, evaluate and approve management's procedures
  referred to in paragraph (d).

## [Investments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-402701 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investment policies, standards and

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the

with the property of others.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- investment management
services

**[[32.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32.1 .sectionLabel}** The investment
policies, standards and procedures established under section 32 do not
apply in respect of a subsidiary incorporated for the purpose of
providing investment management services to the Canada Growth Fund Inc.

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** Every investment manager

## [Financial Management]{.HTitleText1} {#h-402706 .Part}

### [Financial Year]{.HTitleText2} {#h-402707 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial year

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The financial year of the
Board is the period beginning on April 1 in one calendar year and ending
on March 31 in the next calendar year.

### [Financial Statements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-402710 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Books and systems

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall, in respect of itself and each of its subsidiaries, cause

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} books of account and records to be kept for each
    fund;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} financial and management control and information
    systems and management practices to be maintained; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a record of the investments held by each fund
    during the financial year to be kept, showing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the book value of each investment,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the market value of each investment and the
      information that would permit the verification of that value, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the information that would permit the
      determination of whether the requirements of this Act and the
      investment policies, standards and procedures have been met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner in which books, etc., to be kept

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The books, records, systems and practices required by
  subsection (1) shall be kept and maintained in a manner that will
  provide reasonable assurance that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Board's assets and those of its subsidiaries
    are safeguarded and controlled;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Board's transactions and those of its
    subsidiaries are in accordance with this Act and the by-laws, or the
    by-laws of its subsidiaries; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Board's financial, human and physical resources
    and those of its subsidiaries are managed economically and
    efficiently and that the Board's operations and those of its
    subsidiaries are carried out effectively.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Internal audit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall cause internal audits to be
  conducted, in respect of itself and each of its subsidiaries, to
  assess compliance with subsections (1) and (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual financial statements

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall cause annual financial statements, in
  respect of itself and each of its subsidiaries, to be prepared in
  accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of statements

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The annual financial statements shall show the
  information and particulars that in the opinion of the directors are
  necessary to present fairly, in accordance with generally accepted
  accounting principles, the primary source of which is the Handbook of
  the Chartered Professional Accountants of Canada, the financial
  position for each fund as at the end of the financial year to which it
  relates and the results of the operations of the Board for that
  financial year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quarterly financial statements

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} During each financial year, the Board shall cause
  quarterly financial statements to be prepared for each three-month
  period of the year. The quarterly statements shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} show the same information for the most recent
    three-month period as is required to be set out in the Board's
    annual financial statements; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} show the same information in respect of the part of
    the year up to the date of the statements in relation to the
    corresponding period in the preceding financial year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval by board of directors

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The board of directors shall approve the annual
  financial statements and that approval shall be evidenced by the
  signature of at least one director of the Board.

## [Reporting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-402750 .Part}

### [Quarterly Statements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-402751 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statements to go to ministers

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall send
copies of the quarterly financial statements prepared in accordance with
subsection 35(6) to the Minister, the Minister of National Defence and
the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within 45 days
after the end of the three-month period to which they relate.

### [Annual Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-402756 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report required

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall as soon as possible, but in any case within 90 days after
  the end of each financial year, provide the Minister, the Minister of
  National Defence and the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
  Preparedness with an annual report on the operations of the Board in
  that year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report available to contributors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall make the report available to
  contributors under the [Canadian Forces Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-17), the Public Service Superannuation Act, and the
  [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11) as
  soon as is feasible after it is provided to the ministers under
  subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling in Parliament

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} After receiving the annual report, the Minister shall
  cause it to be laid before each House of Parliament on any of the next
  15 days during which that House is sitting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The annual report shall contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the financial statements for the financial year
    prepared as required under section 35;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the annual auditor's report referred to in section
    132 of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a certificate, signed by a director on behalf of
    the board of directors, stating that the investments of the Board
    held during the financial year were in accordance with this Act and
    the Board's investment policies, standards and procedures;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the Board's objectives for the
    financial year and a statement of the extent to which the Board met
    those objectives;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the Board's objectives for the next
    financial year and for the foreseeable future;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the corporate governance practices
    of the Board;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the Board's investment policies,
    standards and procedures established under paragraph 7(2)(a) and a
    comparison of its most recent investment policies with investments
    actually held by it;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the code of conduct for officers and
    employees of the Board established under paragraph 7(2)(e);

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a report of any special examination referred to in
    subsection 139(1) of the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11); and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} any prescribed information or other information
    that the Minister may require.

- 1999, c. 34, s. 48
- 2005, c. 10, s. 34, c. 30, s. 49

## [Meetings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-402778 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall meet once
a year with the members of the three advisory committees established
respectively under section 49.1 of the [Canadian Forces Superannuation
Act](/eng/acts/C-17), section 41 of the [Public Service Superannuation
Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and section 25.1 of the [Royal Canadian Mounted
Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11) in order to discuss the
Board's most recent annual report.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-402781 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the application to the Board and its
  subsidiaries of provisions of the [Pension Benefits Standards Act,
  1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01) and regulations made under that Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} adapting provisions of the [Pension Benefits
  Standards Act, 1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01) and regulations made under that
  Act in the manner that the Governor in Council considers appropriate
  for the purpose of applying them to the Board and its subsidiaries;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the limitations to which the Board is
  subject when it makes investments, so long as those limitations relate
  to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} borrowing and the use of derivatives,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the establishment of a percentage of the funds
    that must be held available for investment in Government of Canada
    bonds and the procedure used to determine that percentage, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the period during which the Board must, in
    acquiring securities other than debt obligations of Canadian
    corporations for investment, substantially replicate the composition
    of one or more widely-recognized broad market indexes of securities
    traded on a recognized stock exchange in Canada; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that this Act provides may be
  prescribed or may be determined by regulation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of regulations ---
investment management services

**[[50.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50.1 .sectionLabel}** Regulations made
under section 50 do not apply in respect of a subsidiary incorporated
for the purpose of providing investment management services to the
Canada Growth Fund Inc.

## [Offence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-402791 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False statements

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  director, officer, employee, agent, mandatary or auditor of the Board
  or of any of its subsidiaries who, in carrying out a duty under this
  Act or the by-laws, prepares, signs, approves or concurs in any
  statement, report or other document respecting the affairs of the
  Board or the subsidiary that contains any false or deceptive
  information is guilty of an offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who commits an offence under
  subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a natural person, to a fine not
    exceeding \$100,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12
    months, or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, to a fine not exceeding
    \$500,000.

## [Winding-up]{.HTitleText1} {#h-402799 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insolvency and winding-up

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** No Act relating to the
insolvency or winding-up of any corporation applies to the Board and in
no case shall the affairs of the Board be wound up unless Parliament so
provides.
