[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment to provinces --- fiscal year
2017--2018

- **[[195]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-195 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the fiscal year beginning on April 1, 2017, the Minister of Finance
  must pay to a province the amounts determined in accordance with
  subsections (2) and (3) for the purposes of assisting the province to
  provide home care services and mental health services if, no later
  than March 30, 2018, the Minister of Health notifies the Minister of
  Finance in writing that, in his or her opinion, the government of the
  province, before December 15, 2017, has accepted the federal proposal
  to strengthen health care for Canadians made on December 19, 2016.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount --- home care services

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount to be paid for the purposes of assisting a
  province to provide home care services is the amount determined in
  accordance with the formula

  ::: {#38702 .Subsection}
  A × (B/C)

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is \$200 million;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the population of the province, according to the official
      estimate of the population of the province on July 1, 2016
      published by Statistics Canada on September 28, 2016; and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the total of the population of all provinces, according to the
      official estimate of the population of all provinces on July 1,
      2016 published by Statistics Canada on September 28, 2016.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount --- mental health services

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amount to be paid for the purposes of assisting a
  province to provide mental health services is the amount determined in
  accordance with the formula

  ::: {#38709 .Subsection}
  A × (B/C)

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is \$100 million;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the population of the province, according to the official
      estimate of the population of the province on July 1, 2016
      published by Statistics Canada on September 28, 2016; and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the total of the population of all provinces, according to the
      official estimate of the population of all provinces on July 1,
      2016 published by Statistics Canada on September 28, 2016.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of C.R.F

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any amount to be paid under this section is to be
  paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund.
