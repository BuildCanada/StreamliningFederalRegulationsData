## Yukon First Nations Self-Government Act {#yukon-first-nations-self-government-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1994, c. 35
:::

Assented to 1994-07-07

An Act respecting self-government for first nations in Yukon
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS representatives of Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada, the
Government of the Yukon Territory and the Council for Yukon Indians
signed the Umbrella Final Agreement on May 29, 1993, the provisions of
which are intended to be incorporated into final agreements for the
settlement of land claims of first nations in Yukon;

WHEREAS the Champagne and Aishihik First Nations, the First Nation of
Nacho Nyak Dun, the Teslin Tlingit Council and the Vuntut Gwitchin First
Nation have each entered into a final agreement with Her Majesty and the
Government of the Yukon Territory, incorporating the provisions of the
Umbrella Final Agreement and including provisions specific to each first
nation;

WHEREAS those final agreements provide that Her Majesty and the
Government of Yukon are to enter into negotiations with those first
nations for self-government agreements appropriate to the circumstances
of each of them and in accordance with the Constitution of Canada;

WHEREAS self-government agreements have been concluded with those first
nations, and have been signed on behalf of each first nation, Her
Majesty and the Government of the Yukon Territory;

WHEREAS other first nations of Yukon may conclude self-government
agreements;

AND WHEREAS the Government of Canada has undertaken to recommend to
Parliament the enactment of legislation to bring self-government
agreements into effect;
:::

:::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474238 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Yukon First Nations Self-Government Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.6).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474241 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[citizen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    8; ([constitution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

:   [[final agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a land claims

    the first nation and incorporates the provisions of the Umbrella
    Final Agreement, and includes any amendments made to it from time to
    time in accordance with its provisions; ([accord
    définitif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[first nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[first nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a first nation named in
    column II of Schedule I; ([première nation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Crown-Indigenous Relations; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[predecessor band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[predecessor band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a first
    nation, means the band or bands, within the meaning of the [Indian
    Act](/eng/acts/I-5), named in column I of Schedule I opposite the

    Schedule; ([bande antérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[self-government agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[self-government agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement
    concluded by a first nation with Her Majesty the Queen in right of
    Canada and the Yukon Government respecting government by and for the
    first nation; ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[settlement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[settlement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means land identified in a
    first nation's final agreement as settlement land of the first
    nation; ([terres désignées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Umbrella Final Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    Final Agreement signed on May 29, 1993 by representatives of the

    provisions; ([accord-cadre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.01); ([loi territoriale]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Yukon Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Yukon Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commissioner of
    Yukon acting with the consent of the Executive Council of
    Yukon. ([gouvernement du Yukon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Purpose and Effect of this Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474262 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act and
orders of the Governor in Council made pursuant to this Act is to bring
into effect self-government agreements concluded with first nations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First agreements

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  self-government agreements of the Champagne and Aishihik First
  Nations, the First Nation of Nacho Nyak Dun, the Teslin Tlingit
  Council and the Vuntut Gwitchin First Nation, signed on May 29, 1993,
  are brought into effect on the day this Act comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a self-government agreement is concluded with a
  first nation after this Act comes into force, the Governor in Council
  may, by order, bring the agreement into effect and add the name of the
  first nation to Schedule II.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall publish in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) notice of the day on which a
  self-government agreement is brought into effect under subsection (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First nation succession

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When the
  self-government agreement of a first nation is brought into effect,
  the first nation, subject to sections 21 and 22, succeeds to the
  rights, titles, interests, obligations, assets and liabilities of its
  predecessor band and that band ceases to exist.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recognition of band

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event that the members of the first nation
  known as the Ta'an Kwach'an Council are recognized by the Governor in
  Council or a court of competent jurisdiction as a band within the
  meaning of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) before the first nation's
  self-government agreement is brought into effect, the band so
  recognized is deemed to be the predecessor band of that first nation
  for the purposes of this Act.

## [First Nation Constitutions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474277 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity of natural person

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A first nation named in
Schedule II is a legal entity having the capacity, rights, powers and
privileges of a natural person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First nation constitution

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  constitution of a first nation named in Schedule II shall, in a manner
  consistent with its self-government agreement, provide for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a citizenship code that includes the requirements
    for citizenship in the first nation and the procedure for
    determining whether a person is a citizen;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the governing bodies of the first nation and their
    composition, membership, powers, duties and procedures;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a system of reporting, by audits or otherwise,
    through which the governing bodies are financially accountable to
    citizens;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the recognition and protection of the rights and
    freedoms of citizens;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} challenging the validity of the laws of the first
    nation and quashing invalid laws; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the amendment of the constitution by the citizens.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Citizenship

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The citizenship code in the constitution of a first
  nation named in Schedule II shall entitle all persons who are enrolled
  in accordance with the eligibility and enrolment provisions of the
  first nation's final agreement to become citizens of the first nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registering of constitution

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The constitution of a first nation named in Schedule
  II and every amendment to it shall be entered in the register of laws
  referred to in section 10 forthwith after the first nation's
  self-government agreement is brought into effect or forthwith after
  the adoption of the amendment, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exercise of powers

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  powers of a first nation named in Schedule II shall be exercised in
  accordance with the first nation's constitution and, subject to
  subsection (2), by the bodies and persons specified in the
  constitution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation of powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any power of a first nation named in Schedule II,
  including a power to enact laws, may be delegated by a law of the
  first nation to any other first nation or to any other body or person
  to the extent that the delegation is consistent with the first
  nation's constitution and self-government agreement.

## [First Nation Laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474298 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Register of laws

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  first nation named in Schedule II shall maintain at its principal
  administrative office a register of laws, containing the constitution
  of the first nation and all laws enacted by the first nation, to which
  any person may have reasonable access during normal business hours.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Central registry

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} First nations may establish a central registry of
  their constitutions and laws, to which any person may have reasonable
  access during normal business hours.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration of laws

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On the enactment of a law by a first nation named in
  Schedule II, the original of the law signed and dated by the person
  presiding over the body that enacted it shall be deposited in the
  first nation's register of laws and a copy of the law certified by
  that person to be a true copy shall be provided to the central
  registry established under subsection (2), if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A law enacted by a first nation named in Schedule II
  comes into force at the beginning of the day following its enactment,
  or at such later time as is specified in the law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In any proceedings, evidence of a law enacted by a
  first nation named in Schedule II may be given by the production of a
  copy of the law, certified to be a true copy by a person authorized by
  the first nation, without proof of that person's signature or official
  character.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} In any proceedings, judicial notice may be taken of a
  law enacted by a first nation named in Schedule II that is held in a
  central registry established under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply in respect of laws enacted by a first nation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1994, c. 35, s. 10
- 2002, c. 17, s. 31(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legislative powers

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  first nation named in Schedule II has, to the extent provided by its
  self-government agreement,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the exclusive power to enact laws in relation to
    the matters enumerated in Part I of Schedule III;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the power to enact laws applicable in Yukon in
    relation to the matters enumerated in Part II of Schedule III; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the power to enact laws of a local or private
    nature applicable on the first nation's settlement land in relation
    to the matters enumerated in Part III of Schedule III.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} To the extent provided by the first nation's
  self-government agreement and subject to any limitations contained in
  it, a first nation named in Schedule II has the power to enact laws in
  relation to taxation as described in Part IV of Schedule III.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administration of justice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The power of a first nation under paragraph (1)(c) to
  enact laws in relation to the administration of justice is suspended
  until the earlier of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the effective date of an agreement respecting the
    administration of justice entered into between the parties to the
    first nation's self-government agreement, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the expiration of any interim period provided by
    the self-government agreement for the purpose of reaching an
    agreement respecting the administration of justice.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1994, c. 35, s. 11
- 2002, c. 7, s. 262

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers restricted on certain lands

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where

  certain of its powers to enact laws, those powers may not be exercised

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a first nation's self-government agreement so

  agree to the exercise by the first nation of any of the powers
  referred to in subsection (1), for which that Government or
  corporation has responsibility, in respect of portions of settlement
  land identified in the agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  power of a first nation to enact laws in relation to any matter
  enumerated in Part II or III of Schedule III includes the power to
  establish offences punishable on summary conviction by fine or
  imprisonment or both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fine that may be imposed on a person convicted of
  an offence under a law of a first nation may not exceed \$5,000 and
  the term of imprisonment that may be imposed for such an offence may
  not exceed six months, until an agreement respecting the
  administration of justice is in effect between the first nation, Her
  Majesty and the Yukon Government, or until the expiration of any
  interim period provided by the first nation's self-government
  agreement for the purpose of reaching such an agreement, whichever
  occurs earlier.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fines in relation to lands and
  environment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (2), until the earlier of
  the events referred to in that subsection, and to the extent provided
  by a first nation's self-government agreement, a fine not exceeding
  \$300,000 may be imposed on a person convicted of an offence under a
  law of the first nation enacted pursuant to paragraph 11(1)(c) in
  relation to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the use of settlement land and of natural resources
    on settlement land; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the control or prevention of pollution and the
    protection of the environment.

- 1994, c. 35, s. 13
- 2003, c. 7, s. 130

## [Administration of Justice]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474348 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administration of justice

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Until an agreement
respecting the administration of justice is in effect between a first
nation named in Schedule II, Her Majesty and the Yukon Government, or
until the expiration of any interim period provided by the first
nation's self-government agreement for the purpose of reaching such an
agreement, whichever occurs earlier,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the courts of Yukon have, subject to paragraph (b),
  jurisdiction in respect of laws enacted by the first nation according
  to the respective jurisdictions of those courts under territorial
  laws;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Territorial Court of Yukon has exclusive original
  jurisdiction in relation to the prosecution of offences under laws
  enacted by the first nation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} offences under those laws shall be prosecuted in
  accordance with the Summary Convictions Act (Yukon), as amended from
  time to time, as if they were offences under Yukon enactments, by
  prosecutors appointed by the Yukon Government; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} terms of imprisonment imposed for offences under
  those laws shall be served in a correctional facility in accordance
  with the Corrections Act (Yukon), as amended from time to time.

- 1994, c. 35, s. 14

## [Laws of General Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474364 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of general laws

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 17 to
19 and subsection 20(2), federal and territorial laws apply to a first
nation named in Schedule II, to its citizens and in respect of its
settlement land.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3)
  and section 22, the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) does not apply to a
  first nation named in Schedule II or to its citizens.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The provisions of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)
  respecting the registration of persons as Indians and entitlement to
  registration as such apply to citizens of a first nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Except as provided by the first nation's final
  agreement in relation to the application of section 87 of the [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5), the provisions of that Act, except sections 74 to
  80, apply to a first nation named in Schedule II and to its citizens

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any reserve, within the meaning of
    that Act, of a predecessor band of the first nation situated outside
    Yukon, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any rights, titles, interests,
    obligations, assets and liabilities of the predecessor band outside
    Yukon,

  as if the first nation were a band and as if citizens of the first
  nation registered or entitled to be registered as Indians were members
  of that band, within the meaning of that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1994, c. 35, s. 17
- 2002, c. 7, s. 266
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3)

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), a first nation
  named in Schedule II is deemed for a taxation year to be a public body
  performing a function of government in Canada as described in
  paragraph 149(1)(c) of that Act where, at all times during the year,
  the first nation satisfies the conditions set out in its
  self-government agreement relating to taxation of the first nation
  under that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No tax is payable under the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) by a corporation described as a subsidiary in
  the self-government agreement of a first nation named in Schedule II
  on the corporation's income, property or capital for a taxation year
  where, at all times during the year, the corporation satisfies the
  conditions for exemption from such tax set out in the self-government
  agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refund of Goods and Services Tax

- **[[18.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of National Revenue
  shall pay to a person, as defined in subsection 123(1) of the [Excise
  Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), a refund of the tax paid by the person under
  Part IX of that Act, to the extent and in the manner that the refund
  is provided for in a self-government agreement with a first nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The provisions of Part IX of the [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15) apply, with any modifications that the
  circumstances may require, in respect of any claim for a refund and
  any amount refunded under subsection (1) as though the refund were a
  rebate provided for under Division VI of that Part.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1999, c. 26, s. 35
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Yukon enactments

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To the
  extent that a Yukon enactment and a law enacted by a first nation make
  provision for the same matter, the Yukon enactment does not apply to
  the first nation, to its citizens or in respect of its settlement
  land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taxation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not affect the application of any
  Yukon enactment relating to taxation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Partial application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where, in the opinion of the Yukon Government,
  subsection (1) renders a Yukon enactment partially inapplicable and
  thereby unreasonably alters the character of the Yukon law, or makes
  it unduly difficult to administer the Yukon enactment in relation to a
  first nation named in Schedule II, its citizens or its settlement
  land, the Yukon Government may order that the Yukon enactment ceases
  to apply in whole or in part to the first nation, to its citizens or
  in respect of its settlement land.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergencies off settlement land

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  relation to any of the matters enumerated in Part II of Schedule III,
  where a situation that occurs outside the settlement land of a first
  nation named in Schedule II poses an emergency to any citizen of the
  first nation, the Yukon Government may, to the extent provided by the
  self-government agreement, exercise a power conferred by a territorial
  law in order to relieve the emergency, notwithstanding that a law
  enacted by the first nation applies in respect of the emergency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergencies on settlement land

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In relation to any of the matters enumerated in Part
  II of Schedule III, where a situation that occurs on the settlement
  land of a first nation named in Schedule II poses an emergency to any
  person who is not a citizen of the first nation, the first nation may,
  to the extent provided by its self-government agreement, exercise a
  power under a law enacted by the first nation in order to relieve the
  emergency, notwithstanding that a federal or territorial law applies
  in respect of the emergency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsections 19(1) and (3), in
  relation to any of the matters enumerated in Part III of Schedule III,
  where an emergency that occurs on settlement land of a first nation
  named in Schedule II has or is likely to have an effect outside the
  settlement land, territorial laws are applicable on the settlement
  land in respect of the emergency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Actions taken in good faith

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person acting pursuant to subsection (1) or (2) is
  not liable for any act done in good faith in the reasonable belief
  that the act was necessary to relieve an emergency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means an apprehended, imminent or actual danger to health, safety or
  the environment.

## [Disposition of Reserve Lands]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474409 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing reserve lands

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding sections 37 to 41 of
  the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), where the self-government agreement
  of a first nation named in Schedule II so provides, interests in lands
  previously held for the use and benefit of a predecessor band of the
  first nation shall be transferred to the first nation in accordance
  with the self-government agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lands subject to claims

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding sections 37 to 41 of the [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5), where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} lands are identified, in a final agreement that is
    in effect, as the subject of a claim against Her Majesty by the
    first nation, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a subsequent agreement between Her Majesty and the
    first nation declares those lands to have been previously held for
    the use and benefit of a predecessor band of the first nation,

  interests in those lands shall be transferred or recognized in
  accordance with the subsequent agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to transfer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may take such measures as are
  necessary to effect the transfer or recognition of any interest in
  lands referred to in subsection (1) or (2), or may authorize the
  taking of such measures.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of this Act

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided in the self-government
  agreement, this Act applies in respect of lands in which an interest
  is transferred or recognized under subsection (1) or (2) as if those
  lands were settlement land of the first nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of final agreement

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The final agreement of a first nation applies in
  respect of lands in which an interest is transferred or recognized
  under subsection (1) or (2) in the manner and to the extent provided
  in the self-government agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of the [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), after a
  self-government agreement referred to in subsection 21(1) or a
  subsequent agreement referred to in subsection 21(2) is brought into
  effect, lands referred to in the self-government agreement that were
  previously held by Her Majesty for the use and benefit of a
  predecessor band of the first nation, or lands declared in the
  subsequent agreement to have been so held, are not subject to the
  [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a first nation's self-government agreement
  provides that the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) will continue to apply
  in respect of lands referred to in subsection (1), that Act continues
  to apply in respect of those lands as if the first nation were a band
  and as if citizens of the first nation registered or entitled to be
  registered as Indians were members of that band, within the meaning of
  that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taxation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), section 87 of the
  [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) applies, for a period ending three years
  after the commencement of this Act, in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any interest of an Indian, a first nation or a
    band, as defined in that Act, in lands to which section 21 applies,
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any personal property of an Indian, a first nation
    or a band, as defined in that Act, situated on such lands,

  as if those lands were reserves of a band within the meaning of that
  Act and as if the first nation were such a band.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Constitution Act, 1867](/eng/Const/)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, lands in which an interest is
  transferred or recognized under section 21 remain lands reserved for
  the Indians within the meaning of class 24 of section 91 of the
  [Constitution Act, 1867](/eng/Const/).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnification

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A first nation shall indemnify Her Majesty for any
  liability arising from the transfer or recognition of lands under
  section 21 or arising from the first nation's management of those
  lands following their transfer or recognition.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[22.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in
  this section.

  [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 2
      of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
      2 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([Indien]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
      2 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([réserve]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[transition period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transition period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a first
      nation, means the period beginning on the day of the year on which
      the first nation's final agreement is brought into effect and
      ending on December 31 of that year. ([période de
      transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taxation exemption --- former reserves

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} During the transition period of a first nation,
  income of a band or an Indian, other than an Indian enrolled under a
  final agreement that came into effect before the calendar year that
  includes the transition period, is exempt from taxation under the
  [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), if the [situs]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} of the income is on land of the first nation that was a
  reserve throughout the portion of that calendar year before the
  transition period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taxation exemption --- Indians enrolled

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} During the transition period of a first nation,
  income of an Indian who is enrolled under the first nation's final
  agreement and is resident in the Yukon is exempt from taxation under
  the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), if the [situs]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} of the income is on a reserve.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474473 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws remain in force

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** Any by-law made under the
[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) by a predecessor band of a first nation, in
force immediately before the day the first nation's self-government
agreement is brought into effect, applies to the first nation and to its
citizens as if the by-law were a law enacted by the first nation, to the
extent that the by-law does not conflict with and is not inconsistent
with this Act or the constitution of the first nation and is not amended
or repealed by a law enacted by the first nation after that day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interim powers of councillors

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Unless the members of a
governing body of a first nation named in Schedule II have been selected
before the day the first nation's self-government agreement is brought
into effect, the persons who were the chief and councillors of the
predecessor band of the first nation immediately before that day shall
be the members of the governing body from that day until a governing
body is selected pursuant to the first nation's constitution.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Moneys held for bands by Her Majesty

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** Moneys held by Her
Majesty for the use and benefit of the predecessor band of a first
nation immediately before the first nation's self-government agreement
is brought into effect, including moneys received in respect of lands
referred to in subsection 17(3), shall be transferred from the
Consolidated Revenue Fund to the first nation as soon as practicable
after the first nation's self-government agreement is brought into
effect.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property of minors and mental
incompetents

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection 17(1),
  the Minister of Indigenous Services may continue to exercise any
  authority under the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) that the Minister of
  Indigenous Services has, immediately before a first nation's
  self-government agreement is brought into effect, in relation to the
  administration of the property of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a minor child of a citizen of the first nation who
    is registered or entitled to be registered as an Indian; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a citizen registered or entitled to be registered
    as an Indian who has been found to be mentally incompetent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer to trustee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The property of a person referred to in subsection
  (1), including moneys held in the Consolidated Revenue Fund, may be
  transferred in trust for that person or that person's estate on terms
  agreed to by the Minister of Indigenous Services and the first nation.
