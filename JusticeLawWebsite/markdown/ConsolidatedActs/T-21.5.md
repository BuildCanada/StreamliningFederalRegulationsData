## Tsawwassen First Nation Final Agreement Act {#tsawwassen-first-nation-final-agreement-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2008, c. 32
:::

Assented to 2008-06-26

An Act to give effect to the Tsawwassen First Nation Final Agreement and
to make consequential amendments to other Acts
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/) recognizes and affirms
the existing aboriginal and treaty rights of the aboriginal peoples of
Canada;

Whereas the Tsawwassen First Nation is part of the Coast Salish, an
aboriginal people of Canada;

Whereas the reconciliation between the prior presence of aboriginal
peoples and the assertion of sovereignty by the Crown is of significant
social and economic importance to Canadians;

Whereas Canadian courts have stated that this reconciliation is best
achieved through negotiation;

Whereas the Tsawwassen First Nation, the government of Canada and the
government of British Columbia have negotiated the Agreement to achieve
this reconciliation and to establish a new relationship among them;

And whereas the Agreement requires that legislation be enacted by the
Parliament of Canada in order for the Agreement to be ratified;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-459640 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Tsawwassen First Nation Final Agreement Act](/eng/acts/T-21.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-459643 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Tsawwassen First
      Nation Final Agreement, between the Tsawwassen First Nation, Her
      Majesty in right of Canada and Her Majesty in right of British
      Columbia, including any amendments made to
      it. ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Tax Treatment Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Tax Treatment Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      Tsawwassen Tax Treatment Agreement referred to in clause 22 of
      Chapter 20 of the Agreement, including any amendments made to
      it. ([accord sur le traitement fiscal]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions in the Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Act, [[Former Tsawwassen
  Reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Tsawwassen
  Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Tsawwassen First
  Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Tsawwassen
  Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Tsawwassen
  Lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Tsawwassen
  Law]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Tsawwassen
  Member]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Tsawwassen Public
  Institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[Tsawwassen
  Territory]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} have the same meanings as in Chapter
  1 of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of Agreement

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement is a treaty
and a land claims agreement within the meaning of sections 25 and 35 of
the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

## [Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-459653 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement given effect

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement is approved, given effect and declared valid and has the
  force of law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and obligations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, any person or body has the
  powers, rights, privileges and benefits conferred on the person or
  body by the Agreement and shall perform the duties, and is subject to
  the liabilities, imposed on the person or body by the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Third parties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Agreement is binding on,
  and may be relied on by, all persons and bodies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency with Agreement

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement prevails over this Act and any other federal law to the
  extent of any inconsistency between them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict with Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Act prevails over any other federal law to the
  extent of any conflict between them.

## [Appropriation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-459666 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of C.R.F.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** There shall be paid out of
the Consolidated Revenue Fund any sums that are required to meet the
monetary obligations of Her Majesty in right of Canada under Chapters 4
and 18 of the Agreement.

## [Lands]{.HTitleText1} {#h-459669 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee simple estate

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** On the effective date of
the Agreement, the Tsawwassen First Nation owns the estate in fee
simple, as set out in Chapter 4 of the Agreement, in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Tsawwassen Lands referred to in clause 1 of Chapter 4
  of the Agreement; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Other Tsawwassen Lands referred to in subclause 18.a
  of that chapter.

## [Taxation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-459674 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tax Treatment Agreement given effect

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Tax Treatment Agreement
is approved, given effect and declared valid and has the force of law
during the period that it is in effect.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not a treaty

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Tax Treatment Agreement
does not form part of the Agreement and is not a treaty or a land claims
agreement within the meaning of sections 25 and 35 of the [Constitution
Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

## [Fisheries]{.HTitleText1} {#h-459679 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister of Fisheries and
Oceans

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 7 of the
[Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14), the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans
may, on behalf of Her Majesty in right of Canada, enter into and
implement the Tsawwassen First Nation Harvest Agreement referred to in
clause 102 of Chapter 9 of the Agreement, including any amendments made
to it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not a treaty

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** That Harvest Agreement
does not form part of the Agreement and is not a treaty or a land claims
agreement within the meaning of sections 25 and 35 of the [Constitution
Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

## [Application of Other Acts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-459684 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the provisions
of Chapter 3 of the Agreement that deal with the continuing application
of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), and clauses 16 to 21 of Chapter 20
of the Agreement, the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) does not apply to the
Tsawwassen First Nation, Tsawwassen Members, the Tsawwassen Government
or Tsawwassen Public Institutions as of the effective date of the
Agreement, except for the purpose of determining whether an individual
is an Indian.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Framework Agreement on First Nation Land
Management Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46)

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The [Framework Agreement
on First Nation Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46), the [[Framework
Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of that
Act and the Tsawwassen land code --- adopted under subsection 6(1) of
the [First Nations Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.8), as it read
before the coming into force of the [Framework Agreement on First Nation
Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46) --- do not apply in respect of
the Tsawwassen First Nation, Tsawwassen Members, Tsawwassen Lands, the
Tsawwassen Government or Tsawwassen Public Institutions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Tsawwassen Laws and other
instruments made under the Agreement are not statutory instruments for
the purposes of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

## [Application of Laws of British Columbia]{.HTitleText1} {#h-459694 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** To the extent that a law
of British Columbia does not apply of its own force to the Tsawwassen
First Nation, Tsawwassen Members, Tsawwassen Lands, the Tsawwassen
Government, Tsawwassen Public Institutions or Tsawwassen Corporations
because of the exclusive legislative jurisdiction of Parliament set out
in Class 24 of section 91 of the [Constitution Act, 1867](/eng/Const/),
that law applies to it or to them by virtue of this section, in
accordance with the Agreement and subject to this Act and any other Act
of Parliament.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-459697 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice of Agreements

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice shall be taken of
  the Agreement and the Tax Treatment Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of Agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Agreement and the Tax Treatment Agreement shall
  be published by the Queen's Printer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the Agreement or the Tax Treatment
  Agreement published by the Queen's Printer is evidence of that
  agreement and of its contents, and a copy purporting to be published
  by the Queen's Printer is deemed to be so published, unless the
  contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice of Tsawwassen Laws

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice shall be taken of
  Tsawwassen Laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence of Tsawwassen laws

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of a Tsawwassen Law purporting to be deposited
  in a public registry of laws referred to in subclause 16.a of Chapter
  16 of the Agreement is evidence of that law and of its contents,
  unless the contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make any orders and regulations that are necessary for the purpose
of carrying out any of the provisions of the Agreement or of the Tax
Treatment Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chapters 21 and 24 of Agreement

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsection 4(1),
Chapters 21 and 24 of the Agreement are deemed to have effect as of
December 8, 2006.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of issues arising

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  issue arises in any judicial or administrative proceeding in respect
  of the interpretation or validity of the Agreement, or the validity or
  applicability of this Act, the British Columbia [Tsawwassen First
  Nation Final Agreement Act](/eng/acts/T-21.5) or any Tsawwassen Law,
  then the issue shall not be decided until the party raising the issue
  has served notice on the Attorney General of Canada, the Attorney
  General of British Columbia and the Tsawwassen First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} describe the judicial or administrative proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specify what the issue arises in respect of;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state the day on which the issue is to be argued;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} give particulars necessary to show the point to be
    argued; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be served at least 14 days before the day of
    argument, unless the court or tribunal authorizes a shorter period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation in proceedings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In any judicial or administrative proceeding to which
  subsection (1) applies, the Attorney General of Canada, the Attorney
  General of British Columbia and the Tsawwassen First Nation may appear
  and participate in the proceeding as parties with the same rights as
  any other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsections (2) and (3) do not
  require that an oral hearing be held if one is not otherwise required.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-459728 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing interests --- [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite section 12, if an interest
  in land in the Former Tsawwassen Reserve was granted or approved under
  the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) and exists on the effective date of
  the Agreement, the interest continues in effect in accordance with its
  terms and conditions unless a replacement interest is issued in
  accordance with Chapter 4 of the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of rights and obligations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the effective date of the Agreement, the rights
  and obligations of Her Majesty in right of Canada as grantor in
  respect of such an interest are transferred to the Tsawwassen First
  Nation, which assumes those rights and obligations in accordance with
  the interest's terms and conditions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing interests --- [First Nations
Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.8)

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** If an interest in land in
the Former Tsawwassen Reserve was granted or approved under the [First
Nations Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.8), as it read before the
coming into force of the [Framework Agreement on First Nation Land
Management Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46), and exists on the effective date of
the Agreement, the interest continues in effect in accordance with its
terms and conditions unless a replacement interest is issued in
accordance with Chapter 4 of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Her Majesty not liable

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  greater certainty, Her Majesty in right of Canada is not liable in
  respect of anything done or omitted to be done after the effective
  date of the Agreement by the Tsawwassen First Nation or any person or
  body authorized by it to act

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the exercise of the Tsawwassen First Nation's
    rights and obligations referred to in subsection 21(2) in respect of
    an interest in land referred to in subsection 21(1); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the exercise of powers, duties and functions in
    respect of such an interest that arise from Tsawwassen Laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnification of Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tsawwassen First Nation shall indemnify Her
  Majesty in right of Canada for any loss suffered by Her Majesty in
  right of Canada as a result of an act or omission referred to in
  subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnification of Tsawwassen First
Nation

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** For as long as the
[Framework Agreement on First Nation Land Management
Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46) is in force, Her Majesty in right of Canada
shall, as of the effective date of the Agreement, indemnify the
Tsawwassen First Nation in respect of lands in the Former Tsawwassen
Reserve in the same manner and under the same conditions as would be the
case if the [First Nations Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.8), as it
read before the coming into force of the [Framework Agreement on First
Nation Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46), continued to apply to
those lands.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents in land registries

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** As of the effective date
of the Agreement, registrations or records affecting Tsawwassen Lands
that are registered or recorded in a land registry under the [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5) or the [First Nations Land Management
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.8), as it read before the coming into force of the
[Framework Agreement on First Nation Land Management
Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46), have no effect.
