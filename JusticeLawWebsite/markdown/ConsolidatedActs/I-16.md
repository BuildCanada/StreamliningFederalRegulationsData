## International Boundary Commission Act {#international-boundary-commission-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. I-16
:::

An Act respecting the International Boundary Commission
::::

:::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270724 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [International Boundary Commission Act](/eng/acts/I-16).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-19, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270729 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[boundary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[boundary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the international boundary
    between Canada and the United States as determined and marked by the
    Commission; ([frontière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[boundary monument]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[boundary monument]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a buoy, post,
    tablet, cairn or other object or structure placed, erected or
    maintained by the Commission to mark the boundary and includes a
    reference monument, triangulation station or other marker or
    structure placed, erected or maintained by the Commission to assist
    in determining the boundary; ([borne frontière]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Boundary
    Commission established pursuant to the treaty of
    1908; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Surveyor General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Surveyor General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Canada Lands Surveys
    Act](/eng/acts/L-6); ([arpenteur général]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[treaty of 1908]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[treaty of 1908]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the treaty between His
    Majesty King Edward VII and the United States respecting the
    demarcation of the international boundary between the United States
    and Canada signed at Washington on April 11, 1908; ([traité de
    1908]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any ditch, earthwork, building or
    structure of any description or any lines of telephone, telegraph or
    power, including posts, piers or abutments for sustaining or
    protecting the wires or cables of those
    lines. ([ouvrage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-16, s. 2
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1743
:::

## [Powers of the Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270740 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission's powers

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
maintaining an effective boundary line between Canada and the United
States, the Commission and its members, officers, employees and agents
may at any time

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter on and pass over the land of any person in
  order to gain access to the boundary or to survey the boundary;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} erect and maintain boundary monuments on the land of
  any person; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} clear from the land of any person such trees and
  underbrush as the Commission deems necessary to maintain a vista ten
  feet in width from the boundary.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-19, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal of unauthorized works

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any work
  or any addition to a work that is, after July 6, 1960, constructed or
  placed within ten feet of the boundary without the permission of the
  Commission may be removed and destroyed by the Commission or its
  members, officers, employees or agents, and the materials contained in
  the work or addition may be sold, given away or otherwise disposed of.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs recoverable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The costs of and incidental to any removal,
  destruction or disposition of a work or addition pursuant to
  subsection (1), after deducting therefrom any sum that may be realized
  by the sale or other disposition, are recoverable with costs by Her
  Majesty from the owner as a debt due to Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of "owner"

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  includes the person authorizing or otherwise responsible for the
  construction or placement of any work or any addition to a work
  referred to in subsection (1), and the actual or reputed owner or
  person in possession or claiming ownership thereof for the time being,
  and all or any of those persons jointly and severally.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-19, s. 4
:::

## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270757 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction of works

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Except with the permission
of the Commission, no person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} construct or place within ten feet of the boundary
  any work or any addition to a work; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} enlarge any work that was on July 6, 1960 within ten
  feet of the boundary.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-19, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defacement of boundary monuments

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Except with the permission
of the Commission, no person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pull down, deface, alter or remove a boundary
  monument erected or maintained by the Commission; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have a boundary monument or any portion thereof in
  his possession or custody.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-19, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction of Commission

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No person shall obstruct or
hinder a member of the Commission or an officer, employee or agent of
the Commission in the carrying out of his duties or functions under this
Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-19, s. 6
:::

## [Offence and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270774 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of Act

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Every person who, or whose
employee or agent, has contravened any provision of this Act is guilty
of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding
five hundred dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
months or to both.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-19, s. 8
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270779 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian Commissioner

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Surveyor General is the
Canadian member of the Commission.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-16, s. 9
- 1993, c. 34, s. 86
- 2001, c. 4, s. 162
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1744
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claims in liability against Canadian
Commissioner

**[[9.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 3 of the [Crown Liability and Proceedings Act](/eng/acts/C-50),
the Canadian member of the Commission while acting within the scope of
that member's duties or employment is deemed to be a servant of the
Crown.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1744
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on
Her Majesty.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-19, s. 10
