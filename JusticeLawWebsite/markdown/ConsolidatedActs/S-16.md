## Standards Council of Canada Act {#standards-council-of-canada-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. S-16
:::

An Act to establish the Standards Council of Canada
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-439829 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Standards Council of Canada Act](/eng/acts/S-16).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 41(1st Supp.), s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-439834 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Standards Council of
    Canada established by section 3; ([Conseil]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada as is designated by the Governor in Council
    as the Minister for the purposes of this
    Act. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 41(1st Supp.), s. 2
:::

## [Council Established]{.HTitleText1} {#h-439841 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Council established

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A corporation is hereby
established, to be known as the Standards Council of Canada, consisting
of the following members:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson of the
  Provincial-Territorial Advisory Committee established under subsection
  20(1);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Chairperson of the Standards Development
  Organizations Advisory Committee established under subsection 21(1);
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} not more than 10 other persons to represent the
  private sector, including non-governmental organizations.

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2010, c. 12, s. 1769\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-16, s. 3
- 1996, c. 24, s. 1
- 2003, c. 22, s. 224(E)
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1769
:::

## [Mandate and Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-439850 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandate

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  mandate of the Council is to promote efficient and effective voluntary
  standardization in Canada, where standardization is not expressly
  provided for by law and, in particular, to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} promote the participation of Canadians in voluntary
    standards activities,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} promote public-private sector cooperation in
    relation to voluntary standardization in Canada,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} coordinate and oversee the efforts of the persons
    and organizations involved in the National Standards System,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} foster quality, performance and technological
    innovation in Canadian goods and services through standards-related
    activities, and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} develop standards-related strategies and long-term
    objectives,

  in order to advance the national economy, support sustainable
  development, benefit the health, safety and welfare of workers and the
  public, assist and protect consumers, facilitate domestic and
  international trade and further international cooperation in relation
  to standardization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Council, in carrying out its mandate, may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} promote cooperation among organizations concerned
    with voluntary standardization in Canada in order to coordinate
    standardization activities and develop common standards and codes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} promote cooperation between organizations concerned
    with voluntary standardization in Canada and departments and
    agencies of government at all levels in Canada with a view to
    achieving compatibility and maximum common usage of standards and
    codes;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} establish or recommend criteria and procedures
    relating to the preparation, approval, acceptance and designation of
    voluntary standards in Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} accredit, in accordance with criteria and
    procedures adopted by the Council, organizations in Canada or in a
    country designated by an order made under subsection (4) that are
    engaged in conformity assessment, and maintain a record of those
    accredited organizations and of their marks of conformity;

  - [(d.1)]{.lawlabel} accredit, in accordance with criteria and
    procedures adopted by the Council, organizations in Canada that are
    engaged in standards development, and maintain a record of those
    accredited organizations and of their marks that relate to
    standardization;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} approve standards submitted by organizations
    accredited by the Council as national standards where appropriate,
    and maintain an index of approved standards;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} provide for the identification and evaluation of
    the need for new standards, revisions to existing standards and
    additional conformity assessment services, and arrange for that need
    to be satisfied

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by obtaining the cooperation of organizations
      accredited by the Council, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where that need cannot be satisfied in the
      manner described in subparagraph (i), by promoting the
      establishment or utilization of new or other organizations for
      that purpose;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} establish and register under the [Trademarks
    Act](/eng/acts/T-13) its own marks and authorize and regulate their
    use, subject to that Act and any agreement or arrangement between
    the Council and any organization accredited by it respecting the use
    of the marks in relation to standards developed by that
    organization;

  - [(g.1)]{.lawlabel} provide advice and assistance to the Government
    of Canada in the negotiation of standards-related aspects of
    international trade agreements;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} unless otherwise provided for by any other Act of
    Parliament or by treaty,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} represent Canada as the Canadian member of the
      International Organization for Standardization, the International
      Electrotechnical Commission and any other similar international
      organization, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} ensure effective Canadian participation in the
      activities of those organizations;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} promote, in cooperation with Canadian organizations
    engaged in voluntary standards development and conformity
    assessment, arrangements with organizations similarly engaged in
    other countries for the exchange of information and for cooperation
    in those activities, or enter into such arrangements on its own
    behalf;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} provide financial assistance to Canadians and to
    Canadian organizations concerned with voluntary standardization to
    assist them in meeting national and international requirements;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} collect and distribute, by electronic or any other
    means, information on standards and standardization activities in
    and outside Canada and translate that information;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} promote the use of standards approved by the
    Council; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} make recommendations to the Minister on
    standards-related matters, including voluntary standards that are
    appropriate for incorporation by reference in any law, which
    recommendations may be included in the annual report of the Council.

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1996, c. 24, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of existing services and facilities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In carrying out its mandate and exercising its
  powers, the Council shall, to the greatest extent practicable, make
  use of the services and facilities of existing organizations in Canada
  engaged in standards development and conformity assessment and
  consider all alternatives before providing any new service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} The definitions in this subsection apply in this
  section.

  [[conformity assessment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[conformity assessment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any activity
      concerned with determining, directly or indirectly, that relevant
      requirements are fulfilled. ([évaluation de la
      conformité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[National Standards System]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[National Standards System]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the system
      for voluntary standards development, promotion and implementation
      in Canada. ([Système national de normes]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by order, designate any
  country for the purposes of paragraph (2)(d).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-16, s. 4
- 1988, c. 65, s. 145

- [2014, c. 20, s. 366(E)](#1034903-1145751){.wb-lbx

  role="button"}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further powers

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Council, in carrying
out its mandate and exercising its powers under section 4, may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} expend, for the purposes of this Act, any money
  appropriated by Parliament for the work of the Council or received by
  the Council through the conduct of its operations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} acquire and hold real property or immovables or any
  interest or right in them and dispose of that real property or those
  immovables or interest or right at pleasure;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} acquire any money, securities or other property by
  gift, bequest or otherwise, and expend, administer or dispose of any
  such money, securities or other property, subject to the terms, if
  any, on which the acquisition is made;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} publish and sell or otherwise distribute information
  on standardization; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} do such other things as are incidental or conducive
  to the fulfilment of the mandate and the exercise of the powers of the
  Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-16, s. 5
- 1996, c. 24, s. 4
- 2011, c. 21, s. 156

## [Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-439901 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of members

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  member of the Council, other than the persons referred to in
  paragraphs 3(a) and (b), shall be appointed by the Governor in
  Council, on the recommendation of the Minister, to hold office during
  pleasure for a term not exceeding four years in such a manner that
  will ensure, as far as possible, the expiration in any one year of the
  terms of office of not more than one half of the members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Council referred to in paragraph
  3(c) must be representative of a broad spectrum of interests in the
  private sector and have the knowledge or experience necessary to
  assist the Council in the fulfilment of its mandate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No right to vote

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The member of the Council referred to in paragraph
  3(b) is a non-voting member of the Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-16, s. 6
- R.S., 1985, c. 1 (4th Supp.), s. 33
- 1996, c. 24, s. 5
- 2006, c. 9, s. 299
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1770
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of Chairperson and
Vice-Chairperson

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  Chairperson of the Council and a Vice-Chairperson of the Council shall
  each be designated by the Governor in Council from among the members
  of the Council to hold office during pleasure for such term as the
  Governor in Council considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of Chairperson

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson shall preside at meetings of the
  Council and shall perform such other duties as are assigned to the
  Chairperson by the Council or the by-laws of the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting Chairperson

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Chairperson of the Council is absent or unable
  to act or the office of Chairperson is vacant, the Vice-Chairperson of
  the Council shall act as Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting Chairperson

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If both the Chairperson of the Council and the
  Vice-Chairperson of the Council are absent or unable to act, or if
  both those offices are vacant, a member designated by the members of
  the Council shall act as Chairperson.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-16, s. 7
- R.S., 1985, c. 1 (4th Supp.), s. 44(E)
- 1996, c. 24, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A retiring Chairperson,
Vice-Chairperson or other member of the Council is eligible for
re-appointment to the Council in the same or another capacity.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-16, s. 8
- 1996, c. 24, s. 7(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration of Chairperson

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson of the Council shall be paid such remuneration as may be
  fixed by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Travel and living expenses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Council, other than the
  Chairperson of the Council, shall serve without remuneration but each
  member is entitled to be paid reasonable travel and other expenses
  while absent from the member's ordinary place of residence in the
  course of the member's duties under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration of members for additional
  duties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (2), a member of the
  Council other than the Chairperson of the Council may, for any period
  during which the member performs with the approval of the Council any
  duties on behalf of the Council in addition to the member's ordinary
  duties as a member, be paid such remuneration as may be fixed by the
  Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the [Government Employees
  Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and any regulations made pursuant to
  section 9 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), the members of the
  Council are deemed to be employed in the federal public
  administration.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-16, s. 9
- 1996, c. 24, s. 8
- 2003, c. 22, s. 224(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the
Council shall be in the National Capital Region described in the
schedule to the [National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 41(1st Supp.), s. 10

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Council shall meet at
least once a year and may meet at such other times as it considers
appropriate.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-16, s. 11
- 1996, c. 24, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Council may make by-laws for the regulation of its proceedings and
  generally for the conduct of its activities, including by-laws
  establishing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ad hoc, standing and other committees of the
    Council;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of paragraphs 4(2)(d) and (d.1),
    criteria and procedures for the accreditation of organizations and
    for the revocation of such accreditations; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a membership scheme to allow broader participation
    by the public in Council activities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advisory committee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any by-law made pursuant to subsection (1)
  establishing an advisory committee of the Council may provide for the
  membership thereon of persons other than members of the Council, in
  addition to members of the Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-16, s. 13
- 1996, c. 24, s. 10
:::

## [Staff]{.HTitleText1} {#h-439956 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Executive Director

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  Executive Director of the Council shall be appointed by the Governor
  in Council to hold office during pleasure for such term as the
  Governor in Council considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direction of work and staff

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The executive director is the chief executive officer
  of the Council and, subject to subsection (3), has supervision over
  and direction of the work and staff of the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff appointment and duties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Council may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint such other officers and employees as are
    necessary for the proper conduct of the work of the Council; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribe the duties of the executive director and
    the other officers and employees of the Council appointed pursuant
    to this subsection and the terms and conditions of their employment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salary and expenses of executive
  director

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The executive director of the Council shall be paid
  such remuneration and expenses as are fixed by the Governor in
  Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salary and expenses of staff

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The officers and employees of the Council appointed
  pursuant to subsection (3) shall be paid such remuneration and
  expenses as are fixed by the Council with the approval of the Treasury
  Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-16, s. 14
- R.S., 1985, c. 1 (4th Supp.), s. 44(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advisers

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Council may engage
for temporary periods or for specific projects persons having a
technical or specialized knowledge of any matter relating to the work of
the Council to advise and assist the Council in the performance of its
duties under this Act and may, with the approval of the Treasury Board,
fix and pay their remuneration and expenses.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 41(1st Supp.), s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not agent of Her Majesty

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Council is not an
agent of Her Majesty and, except as provided in section 17, the members
and the executive director and other officers and employees of the
Council are not part of the federal public administration.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-16, s. 16
- 2003, c. 22, s. 224(E)
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1771
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of [Public Service
Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36)

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  executive director and the other officers and employees of the Council

  in the public service for the purposes of the [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), and the Council shall be deemed
  to be a Public Service corporation for the purposes of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members of the Council

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36) does not apply to the members of the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the [Government Employees
  Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and any regulations made pursuant to
  section 9 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), the persons
  referred to in subsection (1) are deemed to be employed in the federal
  public administration.

the purposes of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), be deemed to be a
registered charity within the meaning of that expression for the
purposes of that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-16, s. 18
- 1999, c. 31, s. 246(F)
:::

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Auditor General of
Canada is the auditor of the Council.

- R.S., c. 41(1st Supp.), s. 19

## [Provincial-territorial Advisory Committee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-439998 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committee established

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  is hereby established a committee, to be known as the
  Provincial-Territorial Advisory Committee, consisting of a
  representative of each province and territory appointed by that
  province or territory.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson of the
  Provincial-Territorial Advisory Committee shall be appointed from
  among the members of the committee by the members of the committee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandate

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The mandate of the Provincial-Territorial Advisory
  Committee is to advise and make recommendations to the Council on
  matters related to voluntary standardization and to promote
  cooperation and communication between the provinces, the territories
  and the Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1996, c. 24, s. 12
:::

## [Standards Development Organizations Advisory Committee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-440008 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committee established

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  is hereby established a committee, to be known as the Standards
  Development Organizations Advisory Committee, consisting of a
  representative of each organization accredited by the Council under
  paragraph 4(2)(d.1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson of the Standards Development
  Organizations Advisory Committee shall be appointed from among the
  members of the committee by the members of the committee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandate

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The mandate of the Standards Development
  Organizations Advisory Committee is to advise and make recommendations
  to the Council on matters related to voluntary standards development
  and to promote cooperation and communication between the Council and
  the organizations represented on the committee.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1996, c. 24, s. 12
:::

## [Meetings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-440018 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic meetings

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** A meeting of the Council
or a committee of the Council or a committee referred to in subsection
20(1) or 21(1) may be held by means of such telephonic, electronic or
other communications facilities as permit all persons participating in
the meeting to communicate adequately with each other during the
meeting.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1996, c. 24, s. 12

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 1996, c. 24, s. 13

  - ###### Members cease to hold office {#members-cease-to-hold-office .MarginalNote}

    - [13]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection 105(4)
      of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), every
      person who was a member of the Standards Council of Canada
      immediately before the day section 1 of this Act comes into force,
      except the President, shall cease to hold office on that day.

    - ###### President {#president .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The President of the Standards Council of Canada
      holding office immediately before the day section 6 of this Act
      comes into force shall, as of that day, be the Chairperson of the
      Standards Council of Canada and shall hold office as a member of
      the Standards Council of Canada, and as Chairperson, until the
      expiration of the remainder of that person's term of office.
