## Inquiries Act {#inquiries-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. I-11
:::

An Act respecting public and departmental inquiries
::::

:::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270165 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Inquiries Act](/eng/acts/I-11).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-13, s. 1
:::

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Public Inquiries]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270170 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inquiry

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, whenever the Governor in Council deems it expedient, cause inquiry
to be made into and concerning any matter connected with the good
government of Canada or the conduct of any part of the public business
thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-13, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of commissioners

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Where an inquiry as
described in section 2 is not regulated by any special law, the Governor
in Council may, by a commission, appoint persons as commissioners by
whom the inquiry shall be conducted.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-13, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of commissioners concerning
evidence

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The commissioners have the
power of summoning before them any witnesses, and of requiring them to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} give evidence, orally or in writing, and on oath or,
  if they are persons entitled to affirm in civil matters on solemn
  affirmation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} produce such documents and things as the
  commissioners deem requisite to the full investigation of the matters
  into which they are appointed to examine.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-13, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem, enforcement

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The commissioners have the
same power to enforce the attendance of witnesses and to compel them to
give evidence as is vested in any court of record in civil cases.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-13, s. 5
:::

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Departmental Investigations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270189 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of commissioners

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The minister presiding over
any department in the federal public administration may appoint, under
the authority of the Governor in Council, a commissioner or
commissioners to investigate and report on the state and management of
the business, or any part of the business, of the department, either in
the inside or outside service thereof, and the conduct of any person in
that service, so far as the same relates to the official duties of the
person.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-11, s. 6
- 2003, c. 22, s. 174
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-6-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 6"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of commissioners

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of an
investigation under section 6, the commissioners

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} may enter into and remain within any public office or
  institution, and shall have access to every part thereof;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} may examine all papers, documents, vouchers, records
  and books of every kind belonging to the public office or institution;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} may summon before them any person and require the
  person to give evidence, orally or in writing, and on oath or, if the
  person is entitled to affirm in civil matters on solemn affirmation;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} may administer the oath or affirmation under
  paragraph (c).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-13, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subpoena or summons

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  commissioners may, under their hands, issue a subpoena or other
  request or summons, requiring and commanding any person therein named

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to appear at the time and place mentioned therein;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to testify to all matters within his knowledge
    relative to the subject-matter of an investigation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to bring and produce any document, book or paper
    that the person has in his possession or under his control relative
    to the subject-matter of the investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person may be summoned from any part of Canada by
  virtue of a subpoena, request or summons issued under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Reasonable travel expenses shall be paid at the time
  of service of a subpoena, request or summons to any person summoned
  under subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-13, s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence taken by commission

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In lieu
  of requiring the attendance of a person whose evidence is desired, the
  commissioners may, if they deem it advisable, issue a commission or
  other authority to any officer or person named therein, authorizing
  the officer or person to take the evidence and report it to the
  commissioners.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers for that purpose

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An officer or person authorized under subsection (1)
  shall, before entering on any investigation, be sworn before a justice
  of the peace faithfully to execute the duty entrusted to the officer
  or person by the commission, and, with regard to the taking of
  evidence, has the powers set out in subsection 8(1) and such other
  powers as a commissioner would have had if the evidence had been taken
  before a commissioner.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-13, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witnesses failing to attend, etc.

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} being required to attend in the manner provided in
    this Part, fails, without valid excuse, to attend accordingly,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} being commanded to produce any document, book or
    paper, in his possession or under his control, fails to produce the
    same,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} refuses to be sworn or to affirm, or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} refuses to answer any proper question put to him by
    a commissioner, or other officer or person referred to in section 9,

  is liable, on summary conviction before any provincial court judge, or
  judge of a superior or county court, having jurisdiction in the county
  or district in which that person resides, or in which the place is
  situated at which the person was required to attend, to a fine not
  exceeding four hundred dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Justice of the peace

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Part, a judge of a superior
  or county court referred to in subsection (1) shall be a justice of
  the peace.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-11, s. 10
- R.S., 1985, c. 27 (1st Supp.), s. 203
:::

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270233 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employment of counsel, experts and
assistants

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  commissioners, whether appointed under Part I or under Part II, may,
  if authorized by the commission issued in the case, engage the
  services of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} such accountants, engineers, technical advisers or
    other experts, clerks, reporters and assistants as they deem
    necessary or advisable; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} counsel to aid and assist the commissioners in an
    inquiry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Experts may take evidence and report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The commissioners may authorize and depute any
  accountants, engineers, technical advisers or other experts, the
  services of whom are engaged under subsection (1), or any other
  qualified persons, to inquire into any matter within the scope of the
  commission as may be directed by the commissioners.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The persons deputed under subsection (2), when
  authorized by order in council, have the same powers as the
  commissioners have to take evidence, issue subpoenas, enforce the
  attendance of witnesses, compel them to give evidence, and otherwise
  conduct the inquiry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The persons deputed under subsection (2) shall report
  the evidence and their findings, if any, thereon to the commissioners.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-13, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parties may employ counsel

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The commissioners may
allow any person whose conduct is being investigated under this Act, and
shall allow any person against whom any charge is made in the course of
an investigation, to be represented by counsel.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-13, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to persons charged

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** No report shall be made
against any person until reasonable notice has been given to the person
of the charge of misconduct alleged against him and the person has been
allowed full opportunity to be heard in person or by counsel.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-13, s. 13
:::

## [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[International Commissions and Tribunals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270255 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to confer powers on

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, whenever the Governor in Council deems it
  expedient, confer on an international commission or tribunal all or
  any of the powers conferred on commissioners under Part I.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exercise of powers in Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The powers conferred on an international commission
  or tribunal pursuant to subsection (1) may be exercised by the
  commission or tribunal in Canada, subject to such limitations and
  restrictions as the Governor in Council may impose, in respect of all
  matters that are within the jurisdiction of the commission or
  tribunal.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-13, s. 14
:::

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 1992, c. 20, s. 230

  - ###### Correctional Investigator {#correctional-investigator .MarginalNote}

    [230]{.lawlabel} The person holding office as Correctional
    Investigator under the [Inquiries Act](/eng/acts/I-11) immediately
    before the coming into force of this section continues in office as
    Correctional Investigator and shall be deemed to have been appointed
    under Part III of this Act for a term of one year beginning on the
    coming into force of this section.

- --- 1992, c. 20, s. 231

  - ###### Staff of Correctional Investigator {#staff-of-correctional-investigator .MarginalNote}

    - [231]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person whose services were
      engaged by the Correctional Investigator on a full-time basis
      pursuant to the [Inquiries Act](/eng/acts/I-11) during any period
      immediately before the coming into force of this section shall be
      deemed to have been appointed in accordance with the [Public
      Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) on the coming into
      force of this section, unless the person otherwise elects in
      writing within ninety days after the coming into force of this
      section.

    - ###### Probation under [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) {#probation-under-public-service-employment-act .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1) of this section
      and section 28 of the [Public Service Employment
      Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), a person who is deemed by subsection (1)
      of this section to have been appointed in accordance with the
      [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not subject to probation under that Act if
        the person's services were engaged on a full-time basis by the
        Correctional Investigator during a period of at least one year
        immediately before the coming into force of this section; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is subject to probation under that Act for a
        period equal to one year minus the period during which the
        person's services were engaged on a full-time basis by the
        Correctional Investigator immediately before the coming into
        force of this section, where the latter period is less than one
        year.
