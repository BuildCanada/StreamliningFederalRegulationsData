## Thunder Bay Grain Handling Operations Act {#thunder-bay-grain-handling-operations-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1991, c. 31
:::

Assented to 1991-10-11

An Act to provide for the resumption and continuance of grain handling
operations at Thunder Bay, Ontario
::::

::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448497 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Thunder Bay Grain Handling Operations Act](/eng/acts/T-10.6).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448500 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Words and expressions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise provided,
words and expressions used in this Act have the same meaning as in
Part I of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448503 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[collective agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[collective agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the collective
    agreement entered into between an employer and the union that
    expired on January 31, 1991; ([convention
    collective]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is employed by
    an employer and is bound by the collective
    agreement; ([employé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any employer named in the
    schedule; ([employeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mediator-arbitrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mediator-arbitrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    mediator-arbitrator appointed pursuant to subsection
    8(1); ([médiateur-arbitre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the *Transportation ---
    Communications International Union* and its
    Lodge 650. ([syndicat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Grain Handling Operations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448511 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resumption of grain handling operations

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#T-10.6_en_1){#T-10.6_en_1-ID0EBDA}[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
.sectionLabel}** On the coming into force of this Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} each employer shall forthwith resume grain handling
  operations at Thunder Bay, Ontario; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} each employee shall, when so required, forthwith
  resume the duties of that employee's employment.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#T-10.6_en_1-ID0EBDA){#T-10.6_en_1}\[Note: Act
    in force on the day immediately after the day on which this Act is
    assented to (assent date being October 11, 1991), but not before the
    twelfth hour after the time at which this Act is assented to, *see*
    section 14.\]
    :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of employers

- **[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#T-10.6_en_2){#T-10.6_en_2-ID0EBCA}[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No employer or officer or
  representative of an employer shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in any manner impede any employee from complying
    with paragraph 4(b); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} discharge or in any other manner discipline, or
    authorize or direct the discharge or discipline of, any employee by
    reason of that employee's having been on strike before the coming
    into force of this Act.

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#T-10.6_en_2-ID0EBCA){#T-10.6_en_2}\[Note:
      Act in force on the day immediately after the day on which this
      Act is assented to (assent date being October 11, 1991), but not
      before the twelfth hour after the time at which this Act is
      assented to, *see* section 14.\]
      :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of union

  [[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#T-10.6_en_3){#T-10.6_en_3-ID0EBEAA}[(2)]{.lawlabel} The
  union and each officer and representative of the union shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} forthwith on the coming into force of this Act,
    give notice to the employees that, by reason of that coming into
    force, grain handling operations are forthwith to be resumed at
    Thunder Bay, Ontario and the employees, when so required, are
    forthwith to resume the duties of their employment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} take all reasonable steps to ensure that employees
    comply with paragraph 4(b); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} refrain from any conduct that may encourage
    employees not to comply with paragraph 4(b).

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#T-10.6_en_3-ID0EBEAA){#T-10.6_en_3}\[Note:
      Act in force on the day immediately after the day on which this
      Act is assented to (assent date being October 11, 1991), but not
      before the twelfth hour after the time at which this Act is
      assented to, *see* section 14.\]
      :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of collective agreement

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The term
  of the collective agreement is extended to include the period
  beginning on February 1, 1991 and ending on a date to be fixed by the
  mediator-arbitrator, which date shall not be earlier than January 31,
  1993 or later than January 31, 1994.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collective agreement binding for
  extended term

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The collective agreement, as amended by or pursuant
  to this Act, is effective and binding on the parties thereto for the
  period for which the agreement is extended by subsection (1)
  notwithstanding anything in Part I of the [Canada Labour
  Code](/eng/acts/L-2) or in the agreement, and Part I of that Act
  applies in respect of the agreement, as so amended, as if that period
  were the term of the agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Strikes and lockouts prohibited

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** During the term of the
collective agreement, as extended by subsection 6(1),

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} no employer shall declare or cause a lockout against
  the union;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} no officer or representative of the union shall
  declare or authorize a strike against an employer; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} no employee shall participate in a strike against an
  employer.

## [Mediator-Arbitrator]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448536 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of mediator-arbitrator

- **[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#T-10.6_en_4){#T-10.6_en_4-ID0EBEA}[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, after the coming
  into force of this Act, appoint a mediator-arbitrator and refer to the
  mediator-arbitrator all matters relating to the amendment or revision
  of the collective agreement that, at the time of the appointment,
  remain in dispute between the union and the employer.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#T-10.6_en_4-ID0EBEA){#T-10.6_en_4}\[Note: Act
    in force on the day immediately after the day on which this Act is
    assented to (assent date being October 11, 1991), but not before the
    twelfth hour after the time at which this Act is assented to, *see*
    section 14.\]
    :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The mediator-arbitrator shall, within ninety days
  after the mediator-arbitrator's appointment or such longer period as
  the Minister may allow,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} endeavour to mediate all the matters referred to in
    subsection (1) and to bring about agreement between the union and
    the employer on those matters;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the mediator-arbitrator is unable to bring about
    agreement in respect of any such matter, hear the union and the
    employer on the matter, arbitrate the matter and render a decision
    in respect thereof; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} report to the Minister on the resolution of all
    such matters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The mediator-arbitrator has, with such modifications
  as the circumstances require,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of the mediation referred to in
    paragraph (2)(a), all the powers of a conciliation commissioner
    under section 84 of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of the arbitration referred to in
    paragraph (2)(b), all the powers and duties of an arbitrator under
    sections 60 and 61 of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of decision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The decision of the mediator-arbitrator in respect of
  any matter arbitrated by the mediator-arbitrator shall be set out in
  such form as will enable the decision to be incorporated into the
  collective agreement in accordance with section 9.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation in collective agreement

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** When the
mediator-arbitrator reports to the Minister pursuant to subsection 8(2),
the collective agreement shall be deemed to be amended by the
incorporation therein of any amendments agreed to by the union and the
employer pursuant to the mediation and any decision of the
mediator-arbitrator in respect of a matter arbitrated by the
mediator-arbitrator, and the agreement, as so amended, constitutes a new
collective agreement that shall be deemed to have effect on and after
February 1, 1991.

## [Amendment of Collective Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448553 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parties may amend collective agreement

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act shall
be construed so as to limit or restrict the rights of the parties to the
collective agreement to agree to amend any provision of the agreement as
amended by or pursuant to this Act, other than a provision relating to
the term of the agreement, and to give effect thereto.

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448556 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence by individuals

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual who contravenes any provision of this Act is guilty of an
  offence punishable on summary conviction and is liable, for each day
  or part of a day during which the offence continues, to a fine

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} of not more than \$50,000, where the individual was
    acting in the capacity of an officer or representative of an
    employer or the union when the offence was committed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} of not more than \$1,000, in any other case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence by employer or union

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an employer or the union contravenes any
  provision of this Act, it is guilty of an offence punishable on
  summary conviction and is liable, for each day or part of a day during
  which the offence continues, to a fine of not more than \$100,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
enforcement proceedings under this Act, the employer and the union are
deemed to be persons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act shall be construed so as to restrict a person from
raising a defence of due diligence in a prosecution for an offence under
this Act.
