institutions of Canada and the Republic of Poland and the payment of any
benefits purporting to have been made under that Act, are deemed to be
lawful.

## [PART 18]{.HLabel1}[Atomic Energy of Canada Limited]{.HTitleText1} {#h-335072 .Part}

### [Reorganization and Divestiture]{.HTitleText2} {#h-335073 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2137]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2137
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in
  this Part.

  [[AECL]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[AECL]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Atomic Energy of Canada
      Limited. ([EACL]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to an entity, the securities of
        another entity held by, on behalf of or in trust for the entity;
        and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} intangible property. ([actifs]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Natural
      Resources. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a corporation, a share of any
        class or series of shares or a debt obligation of the
        corporation, and includes any conversion or exchange privilege,
        option or other right to acquire a share or debt obligation of
        the corporation; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to any other entity, any ownership
        interest in or debt obligation of the entity and includes any
        conversion or exchange privilege, option or other right to
        acquire an ownership interest or debt obligation of the
        entity. ([titre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Part [[corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
  [[share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[wholly-owned
  subsidiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have the same meaning as in
  subsection 83(1) of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless a contrary intention appears, words and
  expressions used in this Part have the same meaning as in the [Canada
  Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the event of any inconsistency between this Part
  and the [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44) or anything
  issued, made or established under that Act, this Part prevails to the
  extent of the inconsistency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operation of [Competition
  Act](/eng/acts/C-34)

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in, or done under the authority of, this Part
  affects the operation of the [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34) in
  respect of the acquisition of any interest in an entity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[2138]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2138 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this
Part is to authorize a number of measures for the reorganization and
divestiture of all or any part of AECL's business.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to sell shares, etc.

- **[[2139]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2139
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, with the approval
  of the Governor in Council and on any terms that the Governor in
  Council considers appropriate,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} despite section 11 of the [Nuclear Energy
    Act](/eng/acts/A-16), sell or otherwise dispose of some or all of
    the securities of AECL;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} procure an addition to, or other material change
    in, the objects or purposes for which AECL is incorporated or the
    restrictions on the businesses or activities that it may carry on,
    as set out in its articles;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} procure the amalgamation of AECL; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} procure the dissolution of AECL.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may do anything or cause anything to be
  done that is necessary for, or incidental to, a measure approved under
  subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority with respect to entities

- **[[2140]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2140
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, with the approval
  of the Governor in Council and on any terms that the Governor in
  Council considers appropriate,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} procure the incorporation of a corporation,
    securities of which, on incorporation, would be held by, on behalf
    of or in trust for, Her Majesty in right of Canada and sell or
    otherwise dispose of some or all of the securities of the
    corporation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} procure the formation of any other entity,
    securities of which, on formation, would be held by, on behalf of or
    in trust for, Her Majesty in right of Canada and sell or otherwise
    dispose of some or all of the securities of the entity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} acquire securities of a corporation that, on
    acquisition, would be held by, on behalf of or in trust for, Her
    Majesty in right of Canada and sell or otherwise dispose of some or
    all of the securities of the corporation; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} acquire securities of any other entity that, on
    acquisition, would be held by, on behalf of or in trust for, Her
    Majesty in right of Canada and sell or otherwise dispose of some or
    all of the securities of the entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may do anything or cause anything to be
  done that is necessary for, or incidental to, a measure approved under
  subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Part X of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by order, declare that
  any of the provisions of Part X of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11) do not apply to a corporation referred to in
  subsection (1)(a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority

- **[[2141]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2141
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} AECL, a corporation referred to in
  paragraph 2140(1)(a), any other entity referred to in paragraph
  2140(1)(b) or any one of their wholly-owned subsidiaries or
  wholly-owned entities may, with the approval of the Governor in
  Council and on any terms that the Governor in Council considers
  appropriate,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sell or otherwise dispose of some or all of its
    assets;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sell or otherwise dispose of some or all of its
    liabilities;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} issue securities and sell or otherwise dispose of
    some or all of those securities;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} reorganize its capital structure;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} acquire assets of a corporation or any other
    entity;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} procure an addition to, or other material change
    in, the objects or purposes for which it is incorporated or formed
    or the restrictions on the businesses or activities that it may
    carry on, as set out in its articles or constituting documents;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} procure the incorporation of a corporation,
    securities of which, on incorporation, would be held by, on behalf
    of or in trust for, the entity that procures the incorporation;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} procure the formation of any other entity,
    securities of which, on formation, would be held by, on behalf of or
    in trust for, the entity that procures the formation;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} acquire securities of a corporation or any other
    entity that, on acquisition, would be held by, on behalf of or in
    trust for, the entity that acquires the securities;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} sell or otherwise dispose of some or all of the
    securities of a corporation or any other entity that are held by, on
    behalf of or in trust for, the entity that sells or otherwise
    disposes of the securities;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} procure its amalgamation or the amalgamation of any
    of its wholly-owned subsidiaries;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} procure its dissolution or the dissolution of any
    of its wholly-owned subsidiaries; or

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} do anything that is necessary for, or incidental
    to, a measure approved under paragraphs (a) to (l).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of
  the Minister and on any terms that the Governor in Council considers
  appropriate, direct AECL, a corporation referred to in paragraph
  2140(1)(a) or any other entity referred to in paragraph 2140(1)(b) to
  take, or cause any of its wholly-owned subsidiaries or wholly-owned
  entities to take, a measure referred to in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may not issue a directive to
  AECL, a corporation referred to in paragraph 2140(1)(a) or any other
  entity referred to in paragraph 2140(1)(b)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} after any of its securities are sold or otherwise
    disposed of; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to any of its wholly-owned
    subsidiaries or wholly-owned entities, after the wholly-owned
    subsidiaries or wholly-owned entities' securities are sold or
    otherwise disposed of.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with directive

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The directors of AECL or of the corporation, or
  persons acting in a similar capacity with respect to the entity, must
  comply with a directive issued by the Governor in Council. That
  compliance is in the best interests of AECL, the corporation or the
  entity, as the case may be, to whom the directive is issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of implementation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} As soon as is feasible after implementing a directive
  and completing any actions that are required to be taken in connection
  with that implementation, AECL, the corporation or the entity must
  notify the Minister that it has done so.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of [Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[2142]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2142 .sectionLabel}** The [Statutory
Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does not apply to a directive.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling in Parliament

- **[[2143]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2143
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is to cause a copy of a
  directive to be laid before each House of Parliament on any of the
  first 15 days on which that House is sitting after the day on which
  the directive is issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- detrimental information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, if the Minister is of the opinion that
  publishing information contained in the directive would be detrimental
  to Canada's interests or the commercial interests of AECL or the
  corporation or other entity to whom the directive is issued, as the
  case may be, the Minister is to cause a copy of the directive to be
  laid before each House of Parliament on any of the first 15 days on
  which that House is sitting after the day on which he or she is
  notified that the directive has been implemented.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must consult the board of directors of
  AECL or the corporation, or the person or group of persons acting in a
  similar capacity for the entity, before forming an opinion whether
  publishing information contained in the directive would be
  detrimental.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of proceeds of disposition

**[[2144]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2144 .sectionLabel}** AECL or a corporation
or other entity referred to in subsection 2141(1) must pay the proceeds
from the sale or other disposition of any assets, securities or
liabilities under that subsection to the Receiver General.
