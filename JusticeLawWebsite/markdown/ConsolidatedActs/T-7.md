## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-461406 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-6, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-461411 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Crown]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Crown]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Her Majesty in right of
    Canada; ([Couronne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[grant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[grant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means letters patent under the Great
    Seal, a notification and any other instrument by which territorial
    lands may be granted in fee simple or for an equivalent
    estate; ([concession]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[judge of the Court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[judge of the Court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to any
    matter arising in the Yukon Territory, a judge of the Supreme Court
    of the Yukon Territory, in relation to any matter arising in the
    Northwest Territories, a judge of the Supreme Court of the Northwest
    Territories, and, in relation to any matter arising in Nunavut, a
    judge of the Nunavut Court of Justice; ([juge]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes mines, minerals, easements,
    servitudes and all other interests in real
    property; ([terre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Northern
    Affairs or, in respect of any matter related to the Northern
    Pipeline referred to in the [Northern Pipeline Act](/eng/acts/N-26),
    the member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada designated as the
    Minister for the purposes of that Act; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[notification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[notification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a direction in a form
    prescribed by the Governor in Council pursuant to paragraph 23(k)
    and issued pursuant to subsection
    9(2); ([notification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[penalty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[penalty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an administrative monetary
    penalty imposed under this Act for a
    violation; ([pénalité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit issued under this
    Act; ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[territorial lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[territorial lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means lands, or any
    interest in lands, in the Northwest Territories or Nunavut that are
    vested in the Crown or of which the Government of Canada has power
    to dispose. ([terres territoriales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[timber]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[timber]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2002, c. 7, s.
    239\]]{.Repealed}

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-461426 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), this Act applies only in respect of territorial
  lands under the administration of the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nunavut

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 9 and 12 to 16 and paragraph 23(k) apply to
  territorial lands under the administration and control of the
  Commissioner of Nunavut.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of certain Acts

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act shall be construed as limiting
  the operation of the [Dominion Water Power Act](/eng/acts/W-4) or the
  [Canada National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01).

## [Land Management Zones]{.HTitleText1} {#h-461437 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Land management zones

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 6, the
Governor in Council may, where the Governor in Council deems it
necessary for the protection of the ecological balance or physical
characteristics of any area in the Northwest Territories or Nunavut, set
apart and appropriate any territorial lands in that area as a land
management zone.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-7, s. 4
- 1993, c. 28, s. 78
- 2002, c. 7, s. 241
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-4-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations concerning zones

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 6, the
Governor in Council may make regulations respecting

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the protection, control and use of the surface of
  land in a land management zone set apart and appropriated under
  section 4; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the issue of permits for the use of the surface of
  land in a land management zone, the terms and conditions of those
  permits and the fees therefor.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 48(1st Supp.), s. 24
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
exercise the powers mentioned in sections 4 and 5 only after
consultation with the Legislative Assembly of the Northwest Territories
or Nunavut, as the case may be, or --- if it considers that consultation
to be impracticable --- after consultation with each of the members of
the relevant Legislative Assembly with whom consultation can be
effected.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-7, s. 6
- 1993, c. 28, s. 78
- 2002, c. 7, s. 242
- 2014, c. 2, s. 30
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principal offences

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person is guilty of an offence who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contravenes any regulation made under section 5; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fails to comply with any term or condition of a
    permit issued under such a regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Every person who commits an offence under
  subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine not exceeding
    \$100,000; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a second or subsequent offence, to a fine not
    exceeding \$200,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offences

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An offence under subsection (1) that is committed or
  continued on more than one day constitutes a separate offence for each
  day on which it is committed or continued.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-7, s. 7
- 2014, c. 2, s. 75

## [Sale or Lease of Territorial Lands]{.HTitleText1} {#h-461465 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorizing sale, lease, etc.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Act, the
Governor in Council may authorize the sale, lease or other disposition
of territorial lands and may make regulations authorizing the Minister
to sell, lease or otherwise dispose of territorial lands subject to such
limitations and conditions as the Governor in Council may prescribe.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-6, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  section, the expressions [[certificate of
  title]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
  have the meanings assigned by any law of the Legislature of the
  Northwest Territories or for Nunavut, as the case may be, in respect
  of title to real property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issue of notification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notification may be issued to a registrar directing
  the registrar to issue a certificate of title to a person named
  therein in respect of territorial lands described therein that are
  within the registration district administered by the registrar.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Execution of notification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notification pursuant to subsection (2) shall be
  signed and issued,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of territorial lands described in
    subsection 3(1), by the Minister, the Deputy Minister or any other
    officer of the Department authorized in writing for that purpose by
    the Minister; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of territorial lands described in
    subsection 3(2), by the Commissioner of Nunavut.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of issue of notification

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The issue of a notification pursuant to
  subsection (2) has the same force and effect as a grant of territorial
  land made by letters patent under the Great Seal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification to set out nature of grant

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A notification shall set out the nature of the estate
  thereby granted and any easements, rights or other interests excepted
  or reserved therefrom.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-7, s. 9
- 1993, c. 28, s. 78, c. 41, s. 14
- 2002, c. 7, s. 243
- 2014, c. 2, s. 31
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** No territorial lands
suitable for muskrat farming shall be sold.

::: HistoricalNote

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation on sale

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Not
  more than one hundred and sixty acres of territorial lands may be sold
  to any one person without the approval of the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation on lease

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), not more than six hundred
  and forty acres of territorial lands may be leased to any one person
  without the approval of the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other limitation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where territorial lands are hay lands or lands
  suitable for grazing or muskrat farming, not more than six thousand
  four hundred acres may be leased to any one person without the
  approval of the Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-6, s. 7
:::

## [Mining Rights]{.HTitleText1} {#h-461498 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Leasing of mining rights

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations for the leasing of mining rights in, under or on
territorial lands and the payment of royalties therefor, but such
regulations shall provide for the protection of and compensation to the
holders of surface rights.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-6, s. 8
:::

## [Reservation from Grants]{.HTitleText1} {#h-461503 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reservation

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise ordered
by the Governor in Council, a strip of land one hundred feet in width,
measured from ordinary high water mark or from the boundary line, as the
case may be, shall be deemed to be reserved to the Crown out of every
grant of territorial lands where the land extends

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the sea or an inlet thereof;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the shore of any navigable water or an inlet
  thereof; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to the boundary line between Yukon and Alaska, or
  between Yukon and the Northwest Territories, or between the Northwest
  Territories and Nunavut or between Yukon, the Northwest Territories or
  Nunavut and the Province of Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Alberta or British
  Columbia.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-7, s. 13
- 1993, c. 28, s. 78
- 1998, c. 15, s. 40
- 2002, c. 7, s. 244(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reservation of bed of body of water

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Unless the grant contains
a provision to the contrary, the bed, below ordinary high water mark, of
a body of water shall be deemed to be reserved to the Crown out of every
grant of territorial lands where the lands border a body of water.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-6, s. 10
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other reservations

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** There shall be deemed to
be reserved to the Crown out of every grant of territorial lands

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all mines and minerals whether solid, liquid or
  gaseous that may be found to exist in, under or on those lands,
  together with the right to work the mines and minerals and for this
  purpose to enter on, use and occupy the lands or so much thereof and
  to such extent as may be necessary for the working and extraction of
  the minerals; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all rights of fishery and fishing and occupation in
  connection therewith on or around or adjacent to those lands.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-6, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grant does not convey water rights

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Unless the grant or other
document establishing a grant, lease or other disposition of territorial
lands expressly states the contrary, no grant, lease or other
disposition of territorial lands conveys any exclusive right, privilege,
property or interest with respect to any lake, river, stream or other
body of water, within, bordering or passing through the lands.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-6, s. 12
:::

## [Slides, Streams and Lake Fronts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-461527 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Works for the movement of timber

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Unless the grant or other
document establishing a grant, lease or other disposition of territorial
lands expressly states the contrary, no grant, lease or other
disposition of territorial lands

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} conveys any right, title or interest in or to any
  slide, dam, pier, boom or other work constructed, for the purpose of
  facilitating the movement of timber, prior to the date of the grant,
  lease or other disposition of land;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} affects the unimpeded right to use or repair the
  works referred to in paragraph (a); or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} affects the unimpeded right to use, for the purpose
  of facilitating the movement of timber, all streams, lakes, bodies of
  water, portage roads or trails past rapids, falls or other natural
  obstacles or connecting bodies of water, or any land that has to be
  used in connection therewith.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-6, s. 15
:::

## [Trespass on Territorial Lands]{.HTitleText1} {#h-461535 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summons to vacate or show cause

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  under this Act the right of any person to use, possess or occupy
  territorial lands has been forfeited or where, in the opinion of the
  Minister, a person is wrongfully or without lawful authority using,
  possessing or occupying territorial lands and that person continues to
  use, possess or occupy or fails to deliver up possession of the lands,
  an officer of the Department of Indian Affairs and Northern
  Development authorized by the Minister for that purpose may apply to a
  judge of the Court for a summons directed to that person calling on
  that person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to forthwith vacate or abandon and cease using,
    possessing or occupying the lands; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within thirty days after service of the summons on
    that person to show cause why an order or warrant should not be made
    for the removal of that person from the lands.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant for removal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a summons has been served under subsection (1)
  and within thirty days from the service thereof the person named in
  the summons has not removed from, vacated or ceased using, possessing
  or occupying the lands or has not shown cause why he should not do so,
  a judge of the Court may make an order or warrant for that person's
  summary removal from the lands.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons executing warrant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A warrant made under subsection (2) shall be executed
  by a sheriff, bailiff, constable or other person to whom it is
  delivered for that purpose and he has all the powers, rights,
  immunities and privileges enjoyed by a sheriff, constable or other
  peace officer in the execution of his duty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Execution of warrant

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person to whom an order or warrant made under
  subsection (2) is addressed shall forthwith remove the person named
  therein from the lands and all members of that person's family,
  employees, servants, labourers, tenants or other persons employed by
  or living with that person or that person's tenants.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of summons or warrant

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Service of a summons or warrant under this section
  shall be made by leaving a copy with an adult person found on the
  lands and by posting up another copy in a conspicuous place on the
  lands or, where no adult person is found on the lands, by posting up
  copies in two conspicuous places thereon.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-6, s. 16
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences and punishment --- trespassing

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who remains on territorial lands, returns to them or assumes
  any possession or occupancy of them after having been ordered to
  vacate them under section 20 or after having been removed from them
  under that section is guilty of an offence and liable on summary
  conviction

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine not exceeding
    \$100,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or
    to both; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a second or subsequent offence, to a fine not
    exceeding \$200,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
    months, or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offences

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An offence under subsection (1) that is committed or
  continued on more than one day constitutes a separate offence for each
  day on which it is committed or continued.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-7, s. 21
- 2014, c. 2, s. 76
:::

## [Powers of the Governor in Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-461564 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Governor in Council

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on setting out the reasons for withdrawal in the
  order, order the withdrawal of any tract or tracts of territorial
  lands from disposal under this Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} set apart and appropriate territorial lands for the
  sites of places of public worship, burial grounds, schools, market
  places, jails, court houses, town halls, public parks or gardens,
  hospitals, harbours, landings, bridge sites, airports, landing fields,
  railway stations, town-sites, historic sites or for other public
  purposes and, at any time before the issue of a grant, alter or revoke
  those appropriations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} order that grants or leases for a nominal
  consideration be made of the lands appropriated under paragraph (b)
  and that there be expressed in any grant or lease the trusts and uses
  to which the territorial lands granted or leased thereby are subject;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} set apart and appropriate such areas or lands as may
  be necessary

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to enable the Government of Canada to fulfil its
    obligations under treaties with the Indians and to make free grants
    or leases for that purpose, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for any other purpose that the Governor in Council
    may consider to be conducive to the welfare of the Indians;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} set apart and appropriate territorial lands for use
  as game preserves, game sanctuaries, bird sanctuaries, public shooting
  grounds, public resorts or for any other similar public purpose;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} authorize the acquisition by any railway, power
  company or pipeline company, on and subject to such terms and
  conditions as may be deemed proper, of a right-of-way for a road bed,
  for transmission lines or for pipelines through territorial lands
  together with such other territorial lands as may be deemed necessary
  for stations, station grounds, workshops, buildings, yards, pumps,
  tanks, reservoirs or other appurtenances in connection therewith;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} divide territorial lands into mining districts and
  land districts;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} make regulations or orders with respect to any
  question affecting territorial lands under which persons designated in
  the regulations or orders may inquire into such a question and may,
  for the purposes of the inquiry, summon and bring before them any
  person whose attendance they consider necessary to the inquiry,
  examine that person under oath, compel the production of documents and
  do all things necessary to provide a full and proper inquiry;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} prescribe a tariff of fees for copies of maps, plans,
  field notes, documents, papers and other records pertaining to
  territorial lands, and for the preparation of documents evidencing a
  sale, lease or other disposition of territorial lands and for the
  registration of any documents pertaining to territorial lands;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} make regulations respecting the protection, control
  and use of the surface of territorial lands;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} prescribe the form of the notification that may be
  issued pursuant to section 9 and the fee for the issue thereof; and

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} make such orders and regulations as are deemed
  necessary to carry out the purposes and provisions of this Act.

- 1992, c. 1, s. 144(F)

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-461590 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of receipt

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** A receipt for payment
made on the filing of an application to purchase or lease land does not
entitle the person making the payment to take, occupy or use the land
described in the receipt.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-6, s. 20
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Execution of documents

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Any lease, agreement,
licence, permit or notice of cancellation issued or made pursuant to
this Act and any consent to any assignment of any such lease, agreement,
licence or permit may be executed on behalf of the Crown by the
Minister, the Deputy Minister or by any other officer of the Department
authorized in writing for the purpose by the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-6, s. 21
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Whenever interest is
payable under or by virtue of this Act or for or on account of any
claim, matter or thing arising under any provision of this Act, the rate
of interest shall be five per cent per annum, whether that interest is
payable under the terms of any sealed or unsealed instrument or not.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-6, s. 22
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forms

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
prescribe forms of leases, agreements of sale, licences and other
documents required for use under this Act, but not including instruments
issued under the Great Seal.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-6, s. 23
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Government employees

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  by or under the authority of an order of the Governor in Council, no
  officer or employee of or under the Government of Canada shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} directly or indirectly, in his own name or in the
    name of any other person, purchase or acquire any territorial land
    or any interest therein; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be interested as shareholder or otherwise in any
    corporation that purchases, acquires or holds any territorial land
    or any interest therein.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of Governor in Council

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An order of the Governor in Council made under
  subsection (1) that relates to any interest as shareholder or
  otherwise in a corporation may apply

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to a particular interest in a particular
    corporation described in the order or to interests in a class or
    classes of corporations described therein; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the order so specifies, to a particular
    interest or to interests acquired before the making of the order.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-6, s. 24
- 1974-75-76, c. 52, s. 1
:::

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every

  which no other punishment is provided is guilty of an offence
  punishable on summary conviction.

## [Administration and Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-461631 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate persons or classes of persons as enforcement
  officers for the purposes of the administration and enforcement of
  this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An enforcement officer is to receive a certificate
  attesting to their designation and must, on request, present the
  certificate to any person appearing to be in charge of any place that
  the enforcement officer enters under subsection 33(1).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to enter

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  enforcement officer may, for a purpose related to verifying compliance
  with this Act, enter any place in which the enforcement officer has
  reasonable grounds to believe there is any document, information or
  other thing to which this Act applies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers on entry

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The enforcement officer may, for that purpose,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine any document, information or other thing
    that is in the place and open or cause to be opened any container or
    other thing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} test or cause to be tested anything that is in the
    place;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} take samples of anything that is in the place;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} use, or cause to be used, any computer system in
    the place to examine any information contained in or available to
    the system;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} reproduce, or cause to be reproduced, any
    information in the form of a printout, or other intelligible output,
    and remove the printout, or other output, for examination or
    copying;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} use, or cause to be used, any copying equipment or
    means of telecommunication at the place;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} take photographs and make recordings or sketches;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} order the owner or person in charge of the place or
    any person at the place to establish their identity to the
    enforcement officer's satisfaction or to stop or start an activity;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} order the owner or person having possession, care
    or control of anything that is in the place to not move it, or to
    restrict its movement, for as long as, in the enforcement officer's
    opinion, is necessary;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} direct any person to put any machinery, vehicle or
    equipment that is in the place into operation or to cease operating
    it; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} prohibit or limit access to all or part of the
    place.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to assist

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The owner or person in charge of the place and every
  person in the place must give all assistance that is reasonably
  required to enable the enforcement officer to perform their functions
  under this section and must provide any documents or information, and
  access to any data, that are reasonably required for that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement officer may be accompanied

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The enforcement officer may be accompanied by any
  other person that they believe is necessary to help them perform their
  functions under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dwelling-place

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An enforcement officer must not enter any place
  designed to be used and being used as a permanent or temporary private
  dwelling-place.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Private property

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** An enforcement officer
and any person accompanying them may enter private property, other than
a dwelling-place, and pass through it in order to gain entry to any
place referred to in subsection 33(1).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False or misleading information

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person must not knowingly make a false or misleading statement or
  provide false or misleading information, in connection with any matter
  under this Act, to an enforcement officer who is performing their
  functions under section 33.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction or hindrance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person must not obstruct or hinder an enforcement
  officer who is performing their functions under section 33.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

## [Administrative Monetary Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-461674 .Part}

### [Regulations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-461675 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, with the approval of the Governor in Council, make
  regulations for the purposes of sections 37 to 55, including
  regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} designating as a violation that may be proceeded
    with in accordance with this Act

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the contravention of any specified provision of
      this Act or of its regulations,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the contravention of any order, direction or
      decision --- or of any order, direction or decision of a specified
      class --- made under this Act, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the failure to comply with a term or condition
      of any permit or licence --- or of any permit or licence of a
      specified class --- issued under this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the determination of or the method of
    determining the amount payable as the penalty, which may be
    different for individuals and other persons, for each violation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} establishing the form and content of notices of
    violations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the service of documents required or
    authorized under this Act, including the manner and proof of service
    and the circumstances under which documents are considered to be
    served; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} respecting reviews by the Minister in respect of a
    notice of violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amount of penalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount that may be determined under any
  regulations made under paragraph (1)(b) as the penalty for each
  violation must not be more than \$25,000, in the case of an
  individual, and \$100,000 in the case of any other person.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

### [Violations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-461691 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Who may issue notices

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** Persons who are
designated as enforcement officers under section 32 are authorized to
issue notices of violation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission of violation

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who contravenes or fails to comply with a provision, order,
  direction, decision, term or condition designated under paragraph
  36(1)(a) commits a violation and is liable to a penalty in the amount
  that is determined in accordance with the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of penalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of the penalty is to promote compliance
  with this Act and not to punish.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of directors, officers, etc.

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** If a corporation commits
a violation, any director, officer or agent of the corporation who
directed, authorized, assented to, acquiesced in or participated in the
commission of the violation is a party to the violation and is liable to
a penalty in the amount that is determined in accordance with the
regulations, whether or not the corporation has been proceeded against
in accordance with this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of violation

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** In any proceedings under
this Act against a person in relation to a violation, it is sufficient
proof of the violation to establish that it was committed by an employee
or agent of the person, whether or not the employee or agent is
identified or proceeded against in accordance with this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance and service of notice of
violation

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  enforcement officer believes on reasonable grounds that a person has
  committed a violation, the enforcement officer may issue a notice of
  violation and cause it to be served on the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of violation must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name the person that is believed to have committed
    the violation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out the relevant facts surrounding the
    violation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set out the amount of the penalty;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} inform the person of their right to request a
    review with respect to the violation or the amount of the penalty,
    and of the period within which that right must be exercised;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} inform the person of the time and manner of paying
    the penalty; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} inform the person that, if they do not pay the
    penalty or exercise their right referred to in paragraph (d), they
    are considered to have committed the violation and are liable to the
    penalty.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

### [Rules About Violations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-461724 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain defences not available

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person named in a notice of violation does not have a defence by
  reason that the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exercised due diligence to prevent the commission
    of the violation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reasonably and honestly believed in the existence
    of facts that, if true, would exonerate the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Common law principles

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every rule and principle of the common law that
  renders any circumstance a justification or excuse in relation to a
  charge for an offence under this Act applies in respect of a violation
  to the extent that it is not inconsistent with this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing violation

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** A violation that is
committed or continued on more than one day constitutes a separate
violation for each day on which it is committed or continued.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violation or offence

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Proceeding with any act or omission
  as a violation under this Act precludes proceeding with it as an
  offence under this Act, and proceeding with it as an offence under
  this Act precludes proceeding with it as a violation under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violations not offences

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a violation is not an offence
  and, accordingly, section 126 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)
  does not apply in respect of a violation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** No notice of violation is
to be issued more than two years after the day on which the Minister
becomes aware of the acts or omissions that constitute the alleged
violation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

### [Reviews]{.HTitleText2} {#h-461749 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to request review

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** A person who is served
with a notice of violation may --- within 30 days after the day on which
it is served or within any longer period that is prescribed by the
regulations --- make a request to the Minister for a review of the
amount of the penalty or the facts of the violation, or both.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Correction or cancellation of notice of
violation

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** At any time before a
request for a review in respect of a notice of violation is received by
the Minister, an enforcement officer may cancel the notice of violation
or correct an error in it.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** On receipt of a request
for a review in respect of a notice of violation, the Minister shall
conduct the review.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Object of review

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall determine, as the case may be, whether the amount of
  the penalty was determined in accordance with the regulations or
  whether the person committed the violation, or both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall render a determination in writing
  and cause the person who requested the review to be served with a copy
  of the determination and the reasons for it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Correction of penalty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines that the amount of the
  penalty was not determined in accordance with the regulations, the
  Minister shall correct it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibility

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines that the person who
  requested the review committed the violation, that person is liable to
  the penalty as set out in the determination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination final

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A determination made under this section is final and
  binding and, except for judicial review under the [Federal Courts
  Act](/eng/acts/F-7), is not subject to appeal or to review by any
  court.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Burden of proof

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** If the facts of a
violation are reviewed, the enforcement officer who issued the notice of
violation shall establish, on a balance of probabilities, that the
person named in it committed the violation identified in it.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

### [Responsibility]{.HTitleText2} {#h-461779 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** If a person pays the
penalty set out in a notice of violation, the person is considered to
have committed the violation and proceedings in respect of it are ended.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to act

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** A person that neither
pays the penalty within the period set out in the notice of violation
--- nor requests a review within the period referred to in section 46
--- is considered to have committed the violation and is liable to the
penalty.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

### [Recovery of Penalties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-461788 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debts to Her Majesty

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  penalty constitutes a debt due to Her Majesty in right of Canada and
  may be recovered in any court of competent jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No proceedings to recover the debt are to be
  instituted more than five years after the day on which the debt
  becomes payable.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may issue a certificate of non-payment certifying the unpaid
  amount of any debt referred to in subsection 53(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Registration in any court of competent jurisdiction
  of a certificate of non-payment has the same effect as a judgment of
  that court for a debt of the amount specified in the certificate and
  all related registration costs.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-461803 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authenticity of documents

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** In the absence of
evidence to the contrary, a document that appears to be a notice issued
under subsection 41(1) is presumed to be authentic and is proof of its
contents in any proceeding in respect of a violation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 2, s. 77
:::
