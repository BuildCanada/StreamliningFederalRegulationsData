## Civil Air Navigation Services Commercialization Act {#civil-air-navigation-services-commercialization-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1996, c. 20
:::

Assented to 1996-06-20

An Act respecting the commercialization of civil air navigation services
::::

::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80847 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Civil Air Navigation Services Commercialization
Act](/eng/acts/C-29.7).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80850 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in this Act.

  [[aeronautical communication services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[aeronautical communication services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      aeronautical broadcasting services, aeronautical fixed services
      and aeronautical mobile services as referred to in Annex 10 to the
      Chicago Convention. ([services de communication
      aéronautique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[aeronautical information services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[aeronautical information services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      services necessary to meet those requirements of Annexes 4 and 15
      to the Chicago Convention that relate to aeronautical
      information. ([services d'information
      aéronautique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[aeronautical radio navigation services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[aeronautical radio navigation
      services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means aeronautical radio navigation
      services as referred to in Annex 10 to the Chicago Convention, and
      includes the provision of electronic radio navigation guidance
      information enabling aircraft operating under instrument flight
      rules to navigate during the departure, *en route*, approach and
      terminal phases of flight. ([services de radionavigation
      aéronautique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Transportation
      Agency continued by subsection 7(1) of the [Canada Transportation
      Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4). ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[air navigation services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[air navigation services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} aeronautical communication services,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} aeronautical information services,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} aeronautical radio navigation services,

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} air traffic control services,

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} aviation weather services,

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} emergency assistance services, and

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} flight information services,

      in respect of Canadian airspace or any other airspace in respect
      of which Canada has responsibility for the provision of air
      traffic control services. ([services de navigation
      aérienne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[air traffic control services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[air traffic control services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      services, other than flight information services, provided for the
      purpose of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} preventing collisions between

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} aircraft,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} aircraft and obstructions, and

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} aircraft and vehicles on the manoeuvring
          area; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} expediting and maintaining an orderly flow of
        air traffic. ([services de contrôle de la circulation
        aérienne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport air traffic control services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport air traffic control services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      air traffic control services, other than *en route* air traffic
      control services, provided to aircraft on the manoeuvring area and
      in the airspace in the vicinity of an aerodrome, and includes the
      issuance of air traffic control clearances for aircraft to taxi,
      take off or land. ([services de contrôle de la circulation
      aérienne en zone aéroportuaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[apron]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[apron]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the part of an aerodrome that
      is intended to accommodate the loading and unloading of passengers
      and cargo, the refuelling, servicing, maintenance and parking of
      aircraft and any movement of aircraft, vehicles and pedestrians
      necessary for those purposes. ([aire de trafic]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[aviation weather services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[aviation weather services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      specification of the location and frequency of weather
      observations and forecasts for aviation purposes in accordance
      with the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2) and regulations made
      under that Act, the procurement of those observations and
      forecasts and the dissemination of weather information for
      aviation purposes, including the dissemination by weather
      briefings. ([services de météorologie
      aéronautique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Chicago Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Chicago Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on
      International Civil Aviation signed at Chicago on December 7,
      1944, as amended from time to time. ([Convention de
      Chicago]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[civil air navigation services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[civil air navigation services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means air
      navigation services other than when provided pursuant to the
      Minister of National Defence's authority under the [National
      Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) or the [Aeronautics
      Act](/eng/acts/A-2). ([services de navigation aérienne
      civile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means NAV CANADA, a
      corporation incorporated on May 26, 1995 under Part II of the
      [Canada Corporations
      Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8). ([société]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated civil air navigation services asset]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated civil air navigation services
      asset]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an asset designated pursuant to
      subsection (3). ([biens désignés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person whose
      name appears on a list established pursuant to subsection
      (4). ([employé désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated northern or remote services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated northern or remote
      services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} civil air navigation services designated
        pursuant to subsection (5); and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the services that result from a termination or
        reduction of designated northern or remote services in
        accordance with sections 18 to 22, including any civil air
        navigation services introduced in connection with the
        termination or reduction. ([services aux régions nordiques ou
        éloignées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[emergency assistance services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[emergency assistance services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      services provided for the purpose of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assisting aircraft in a state of emergency,
        including aircraft in the uncertainty, alert and distress
        phases;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} assisting aircraft involved in a hijacking; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} alerting rescue co-ordination agencies of
        missing or overdue aircraft. ([services
        d'urgence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[en route air traffic control services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[en route air traffic control
      services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means air traffic control services
      provided to aircraft operating between aerodromes, including
      aircraft flying through Canadian airspace or any other airspace in
      respect of which Canada has responsibility for the provision of
      air traffic control services without landing or taking off in
      Canada, and includes the issuance of air traffic control
      clearances for aircraft to proceed during the departure, *en
      route* and approach phases of flight. ([services de contrôle de la
      circulation des aéronefs en vol]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Executive Employment Transition Policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Executive Employment Transition
      Policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Executive Employment
      Transition Policy approved by the Treasury Board on August 24,
      1992, as amended from time to time. ([Politique de transition dans
      la carrière pour les cadres de direction]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[flight information services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[flight information services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dissemination of aviation weather
        information and aeronautical information for departure,
        destination and alternate aerodromes along a proposed route of
        flight;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the dissemination of aviation weather
        information and aeronautical information to aircraft in flight;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the acceptance, processing and activation of
        flight plans, amendments to flight plans and cancellations of
        flight plans;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the acceptance and processing of flight
        notifications;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the exchange of flight plan information with
        domestic or foreign governments or agencies or foreign air
        traffic services units; and

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the provision of known information concerning
        ground and air traffic. ([services d'information de
        vol]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[flight notification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[flight notification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a flight
      notification within the meaning of regulations made under the
      [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2). ([avis de vol]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[flight plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[flight plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a flight plan within the
      meaning of regulations made under the [Aeronautics
      Act](/eng/acts/A-2). ([plan de vol]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[manoeuvring area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[manoeuvring area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the part of an
      aerodrome that is intended to be used for the taking off and
      landing of aircraft and for the movement of aircraft associated
      with take-off and landing, but does not include aprons. ([aire de
      manoeuvre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
      Transport. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[public service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[public service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Federal Public Sector Labour Relations
      Act](/eng/acts/P-33.3). ([fonction publique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[state aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[state aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an aircraft, other
      than an aircraft operated for commercial purposes, that is owned
      and operated by the government of a country or the government of a
      colony, dependency, province, state, territory or municipality of
      a country. ([aéronef d'État]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transfer date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transfer date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the date provided for
      as the transfer date in an agreement between Her Majesty in right
      of Canada and the Corporation. ([date de cession]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[user]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[user]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an aircraft
      operator. ([usager]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Work Force Adjustment Directive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Work Force Adjustment Directive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      Work Force Adjustment Directive issued on the recommendation of
      the National Joint Council of the Public Service and with the
      approval of the Treasury Board, that came into force on December
      15, 1991, as amended from time to time. ([Directive sur le
      réaménagement des effectifs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Same meaning as [Aeronautics
  Act](/eng/acts/A-2)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless a contrary intention appears, words and
  expressions used in this Act have the same meaning as in subsection
  3(1) of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of assets

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may designate any asset that is under
  the management and control of the Minister and used in connection with
  the provision of civil air navigation services as a civil air
  navigation services asset.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of designated employees

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may establish a list setting out the
  name of every person employed in the public service whom the Minister
  designates for the purposes of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of northern or remote
  services

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, before the transfer date, designate
  as designated northern or remote services any civil air navigation
  services that are provided at or in respect of such northern or remote
  locations as are specified by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Civil air navigation services

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, the introduction,
  increase, termination or reduction of civil air navigation services
  means the introduction, increase, termination or reduction of those
  services, or of any particular service included in the definition
  [[air navigation services]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection (1),
  in respect of a particular location or airspace.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated northern or remote services

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, the termination or
  reduction of designated northern or remote services means the
  termination or reduction of those services, or of any particular civil
  air navigation services making up those services, in respect of a
  particular location or airspace.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister of National Defence

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, a person acting under
  the authority of the Minister of National Defence includes a person
  who is doing anything for a defence purpose pursuant to a contract or
  other arrangement with the Department of National Defence or the
  Canadian Forces.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80922 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80925 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aircraft

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act applies in respect
of every aircraft in Canadian airspace or any other airspace in respect
of which Canada has responsibility for the provision of air traffic
control services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2)

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act affects
the application of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority of Minister of National Defence

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act
derogates from the authority conferred on the Minister of National
Defence by the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5).

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Commercialization of Civil Air Navigation Services]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80932 .Part}

### [Authority to Transfer]{.HTitleText2} {#h-80933 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to transfer

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may sell,
lease or otherwise transfer the right, title and interest of Her Majesty
in right of Canada in designated civil air navigation services assets to
the Corporation.

### [Not Agent of Her Majesty]{.HTitleText2} {#h-80936 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not agent of Her Majesty

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For all purposes the
Corporation is not an agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada and, when
providing civil air navigation services, is not doing so on behalf of
Her Majesty in right of Canada.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Provision of Civil Air Navigation Services]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80939 .Part}

### [Provision of Services]{.HTitleText2} {#h-80940 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to provide services

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Act, the
Corporation shall, on and after the transfer date, provide all users
with the civil air navigation services that the Department of Transport
provided immediately before the transfer date and shall do so to the
same extent as the services were provided by the Department of
Transport.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) to (4),
  no person, other than the Corporation, shall, on or after the transfer
  date, provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} aeronautical information services,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} air traffic control services, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} specified flight information services,

  in respect of Canadian airspace or any other airspace in respect of
  which Canada has responsibility for the provision of air traffic
  control services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person may do anything mentioned in subsection (1)
  if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is acting under the authority of the
    Minister of National Defence; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person is doing so with the written consent of
    the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- airport air traffic
  control services

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a person was providing airport air traffic
  control services at an aerodrome immediately before March 14, 1996,
  the person may provide airport air traffic control services at that
  aerodrome on and after the transfer date until the person is no longer
  providing the services at the aerodrome.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- aeronautical information
  services

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person may provide aeronautical information
  services if the person is disseminating aeronautical information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that was initially disseminated by the Corporation;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is urgently required for reasons of aviation
    safety or the safety of the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of [[specified flight
  information services]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, [[specified flight
  information services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the acceptance, processing and activation of flight
    plans, amendments to flight plans and cancellations of flight plans;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exchange of flight plan information with
    domestic or foreign governments or agencies or foreign air traffic
    services units; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the provision of known information concerning
    ground and air traffic in respect of an aerodrome, where the
    Corporation is providing that information in respect of that
    aerodrome.

### [Designation as Authority for Chicago Convention]{.HTitleText2} {#h-80965 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of Corporation

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation is hereby
designated as the authority in Canada responsible for providing

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} aeronautical information services for the purposes of
  Annexes 4 and 15 to the Chicago Convention; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} air traffic control services for the purposes of
  Annex 11 to the Chicago Convention.

### [Aeronautical Radio Navigation Services]{.HTitleText2} {#h-80970 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Technical and operational standards

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  order to ensure that aeronautical radio navigation services are
  provided in accordance with technical and operational standards that
  are consistent and that persons who provide those services have
  adequate liability insurance in respect of those services, the
  Corporation may propose technical and operational standards, or the
  minimum amount of that insurance, to the Minister for inclusion in
  regulations made under the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendation of Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, after receiving a proposal under subsection (1),
  the Minister is satisfied that the standards, or the minimum amount of
  liability insurance, as the case may be,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are adequate with respect to aviation safety and
    the safety of the public,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} do not impose an undue obligation on providers of
    aeronautical radio navigation services, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} are reasonable, taking into account practices in
    other countries,

  the Minister shall, after having consulted with interested persons,
  recommend to the Governor in Council that the standards, or the
  minimum amount of liability insurance, as the case may be, be
  incorporated by reference, or specified as the minimum amount of
  liability insurance, as the case may be, in regulations made under the
  [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of standards

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation shall publish all of its technical
  and operational standards that are incorporated by reference in
  regulations made under the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

### [Planning and Management of Airspace]{.HTitleText2} {#h-80982 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation may plan and manage airspace

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the Governor
in Council's right under the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2) to make
regulations respecting the classification and use of airspace and the
control and use of aerial routes, the Corporation has the right to plan
and manage Canadian airspace and any other airspace in respect of which
Canada has responsibility for the provision of air traffic control
services, other than airspace under the control of a person acting under
the authority of the Minister of National Defence.

### [Changing Services and Closing Facilities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-80985 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation may make changes

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation may, in
accordance with this Act but subject to the provisions of the
[Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2) and of any regulations made under that
Act that relate to aviation safety or the safety of the public,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} introduce or increase civil air navigation services;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} terminate or reduce civil air navigation services;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} close or relocate facilities used by it in connection
  with civil air navigation services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of changes

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Corporation proposes to do anything mentioned in section 14 and,
  in the opinion of the Board of Directors of the Corporation acting
  reasonably and in good faith, the proposal is likely to affect a
  significant group of users in a material way, the Corporation shall
  give notice of the proposal in accordance with this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out the particulars of the proposal; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specify that persons interested in making
    representations in writing to the Corporation about the proposal may
    do so by writing to the address set out in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How notice is to be given

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation shall send, by mail or by electronic
  means, a copy of the notice to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} representative organizations of users whose members
    will, in the opinion of the Corporation, be affected by the
    proposal, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} every user and other person who has, at least 10
    days before, notified the Corporation of their desire to receive
    notices or announcements under this Act,

  and, after having done so, the Corporation shall post an electronic
  version of the notice in a location that is generally accessible to
  persons who have access to what is commonly referred to as the
  Internet.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When proposal can be implemented

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Where a notice is
required to be given under section 15 in respect of anything mentioned
in section 14, the Corporation may only do that thing after 60 days have
expired since the day the notice was posted in accordance with
subsection 15(3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order under s. 4.91(1) of the
[Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2)

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Sections 15 and 16 do not
apply if the Corporation is increasing civil air navigation services in
order to comply with an order made under subsection 4.91(1) of the
[Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

### [Designated Northern or Remote Services]{.HTitleText2} {#h-81007 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of termination or reduction

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Corporation proposes to terminate or reduce designated northern or
  remote services and, in the opinion of the Board of Directors of the
  Corporation acting reasonably and in good faith, the proposal is
  likely to affect a significant group of users or residents in a
  material way, the Corporation shall give notice of the proposal in
  accordance with this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out the particulars of the proposal, including
    whether civil air navigation services are going to be introduced as
    a result of the termination or reduction; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specify that persons interested in making
    representations in writing to the Corporation about the proposal may
    do so by writing to the address set out in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How notice is to be given

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} send, by mail or by electronic means, a copy of the

### [Charging Principles]{.HTitleText2} {#h-81125 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charging principles

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation shall observe the following principles when establishing a
  new charge for air navigation services or revising an existing charge:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} charges must be in accordance with a methodology
    established and published by the Corporation that is explicit and
    that also includes the terms and conditions affecting charges;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} charges must not be structured in such a way that a
    user would be encouraged to engage in practices that diminish safety
    for the purpose of avoiding a charge;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} charges for the same services must not
    differentiate between domestic and international flights of air
    carriers;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} charges for the same services must not
    differentiate among Canadian air carriers or among foreign air
    carriers;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} charges must differentiate between the provision of
    services in relation to the landing and take-off of aircraft and the
    provision of services in relation to aircraft in flight, and must
    reflect a reasonable allocation of the costs of providing the
    services in those circumstances;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} charges in respect of recreational and private
    aircraft must not be unreasonable or undue;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} charges for designated northern or remote services
    and for services directed to be provided under subsection 24(1) must
    not be higher than charges for similar services utilized to a
    similar extent elsewhere in Canada;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} charges must be consistent with the international
    obligations of the Government of Canada; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} charges must not be set at a level that, based on
    reasonable and prudent projections, would generate revenues
    exceeding the Corporation's current and future financial
    requirements in relation to the provision of civil air navigation
    services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of the services

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The charging methodology may recognize that the value
  of the services differs among users.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of principle

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Corporation's charging methodology
  recognizes the value of the services and aircraft weight is used as a
  measure of the value of the services, the principle referred to in
  paragraph (1)(a) is deemed not to have been observed if aircraft
  weight is taken into account either directly proportionally or greater
  than directly proportionally.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of
  [[weight]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (3),
  [[weight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an aircraft, means the
  maximum permissible take-off weight specified in the aircraft's
  certificate of airworthiness or in a document referred to in that
  certificate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial requirements

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(i), the financial
  requirements of the Corporation in relation to the provision of civil
  air navigation services include, without duplication, the
  Corporation's

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} costs incurred before the transfer date,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} operations and maintenance costs,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} management and administration costs,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} debt servicing requirements and financial
    requirements arising out of contractual agreements relating to the
    borrowing of money,

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} depreciation costs on capital assets,

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} financial requirements necessary for the
    Corporation to maintain an appropriate credit rating,

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} tax liability,

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} reasonable reserves for future expenditures and
    contingencies, and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} other costs determined in accordance with
    accounting principles recommended by the Chartered Professional
    Accountants of Canada or its successor,

  to the extent that they relate to the provision of those services,
  less the amount determined in accordance with subsection (6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction amount

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The amount to be deducted for the purpose of
  subsection (5) is the aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all grants, contributions and subsidies of a
    monetary nature received by the Corporation,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all transition period payments pursuant to section
    98,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all interest income and investment income earned by
    the Corporation, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all profits earned by the Corporation, other than
    in respect of the provision of civil air navigation services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flat-fee

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (3), a category of users
  may be charged on a flat-fee basis so long as the charge is otherwise
  consistent with the charging principles set out in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of paragraph (1)(c)

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(c) does not apply in respect of charges
  for *en route* air traffic control services for the period during
  which an air transportation tax is levied under the [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grounds

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** An appeal may only be
made on one or more of the following grounds:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that one or more of the charging principles set out
  in section 35 have not been observed in establishing the charge;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the notice requirements in section 36 have not
  been complied with;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} that the announcement requirements in section 37 have
  not been complied with; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where subsection 37(2) applies, that, based on
  reasonable and prudent projections, the total annual revenue to be
  generated by the announced charge is greater than the total annual
  revenue to be generated by the charge as proposed in the notice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Who may appeal

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** An appeal may be made by
any user, group of users or representative organization of users.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time for making appeal

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  an appeal must be made within 30 days after the day the announcement
  required by section 37 in respect of the charge that is the subject of
  the appeal was filed with the Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where no notice has been given under section 36 but
  an announcement has been made under section 37 in respect of a charge,
  an appeal in respect of the charge must be made within 180 days after
  the announcement was filed with the Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No announcement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where no announcement has been made under section 37
  in respect of a charge, an appeal in respect of the charge may be made
  at any time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of appeal

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** An appeal does not
prevent the charge that is the subject of the appeal from becoming
effective or prevent the Corporation from imposing the charge pending
the outcome of the appeal, and the Agency may not make an order that
prevents the charge from becoming effective or the Corporation from
imposing the charge.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of appeal

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** An appeal must be made in
the form and manner specified by the Agency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time for deciding appeal

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** The Agency shall decide
an appeal as expeditiously as possible, but no later than 60 days after
it is made, unless the Agency is of the opinion that there are special
circumstances involved in the determination of the appeal, in which case
the Agency has a further 30 days to decide the appeal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of decision

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** At the conclusion of an
appeal, the Agency shall provide the parties to the appeal with written
reasons for its decision.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preponderance of evidence

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** The Agency shall only
decide to allow an appeal based on the failure of the Corporation to
observe a charging principle if it is satisfied on a preponderance of
the evidence that the Corporation failed to observe that charging
principle.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order --- charging principles not
observed or no notice or announcement

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Agency's decision is that one or more of the charging principles
  set out in section 35 have not been observed in establishing a new
  charge or revising an existing charge or that no notice under section
  36 was given or no announcement under section 37 was made in respect
  of a new or revised charge, the Agency shall, as the case may be,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} order the Corporation to cancel the new charge that
    is the subject of the appeal and refund the amount of the cancelled
    charge to each user who paid it; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} order the Corporation to cancel the revised charge
    that is the subject of the appeal, re-establish the previous charge
    and refund to each user who paid the cancelled charge the amount, if
    any, collected in excess of the re-established charge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order --- notice or announcement
  requirements not met

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Agency's decision is that the requirements
  in section 36 or 37 have not been fully complied with in respect of a
  new or revised charge, the Agency may make such order, including an
  order requiring refunds, as it considers appropriate in the
  circumstances, having regard to the seriousness of the non-compliance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order --- charge different

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Agency's decision is that, based on
  reasonable and prudent projections, the total annual revenue to be
  generated by a charge set out in an announcement required under
  section 37 is greater than the total annual revenue to be generated by
  the charge as proposed in the notice required under section 36, the
  Agency shall, as the case may be,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} order the Corporation to cancel the new charge that
    is the subject of the appeal and refund the amount of the cancelled
    charge to each user who paid it; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} order the Corporation to cancel the revised charge
    that is the subject of the appeal, re-establish the previous charge
    and refund to each user who paid the cancelled charge the amount, if
    any, collected in excess of the re-established charge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of refund

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Corporation is ordered to refund amounts,
  it may do so by way of credit or payment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crediting amounts

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  time a user who is entitled to a refund under an order of the Agency
  is charged by the Corporation for air navigation services, the
  Corporation shall credit the user an amount not less than 10% of the
  charge, until the entire amount owing to the user is refunded.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Two year maximum

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation shall refund the entire amount owing
  to each user within two years after the date of the order of the
  Agency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision final

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** A decision of the Agency
pursuant to an appeal is final and binding and, notwithstanding any
other Act of Parliament, no appeal lies from the decision.

### [Seizure and Detention of Aircraft]{.HTitleText2} {#h-81308 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seizure and detention of aircraft

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  addition to any other remedy available for the collection of an unpaid
  and overdue charge imposed by the Corporation for air navigation
  services, and whether or not a judgment for the collection of the
  charge has been obtained, the Corporation may apply to the superior
  court of the province in which any aircraft owned or operated by the
  person liable to pay the charge is situated for an order, issued on
  such terms as the court considers appropriate, authorizing the
  Corporation to seize and detain any such aircraft until the charge is
  paid or a bond or other security for the unpaid and overdue amount in
  a form satisfactory to the Corporation is deposited with the
  Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application may be [ex
  parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for an order referred to in
  subsection (1) may be made [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} if the
  Corporation has reason to believe that the person liable to pay the
  charge is about to leave Canada or take from Canada any aircraft owned
  or operated by the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Release

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation shall release from detention an
  aircraft seized under this section if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount in respect of which the seizure was made
    is paid;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a bond or other security in a form satisfactory to
    the Corporation for the amount in respect of which the seizure was
    made is deposited with the Corporation; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an order of a court directs the Corporation to do
    so.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exempt aircraft

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  order under section 56 does not apply if the aircraft is exempt from
  seizure under the laws of the province in which the court that issued
  the order is situated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}State aircraft are exempt

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} State aircraft are exempt from seizure and detention
  under an order issued under section 56.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Her Majesty no longer responsible

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** Subject to any agreement
entered into between Her Majesty in right of Canada and the Corporation
before the transfer date, Her Majesty in right of Canada ceases on the
transfer date to be responsible for any obligation of Her Majesty in
right of Canada as represented by the Treasury Board arising out of or
under any collective agreement or arbitral award continued under section
62 or mentioned in section 63 or any terms and conditions of employment
applicable to a designated employee immediately before the transfer
date.

### [Bargaining Agents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-81427 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bargaining agents

- **[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  employee organization that immediately before the transfer date was
  certified under the [Public Service Staff Relations
  Act](/eng/acts/P-35) as the bargaining agent for a bargaining unit
  consisting of or including designated employees referred to in section
  58 is deemed to have been certified as the bargaining agent for the
  employees of the Corporation in that bargaining unit under Part I of
  the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2) on the transfer date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding any provision of Part I of the
  [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no application for certification as the bargaining
    agent for any employees of the Corporation represented by a
    bargaining agent deemed to have been certified under subsection (1)
    may be made by a trade union, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no bargaining unit or part of a bargaining unit
    consisting of employees of the Corporation represented by a
    bargaining agent deemed to have been certified under subsection (1)
    may be reviewed, rescinded, amended, altered or varied, otherwise
    than

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to include in the unit any employees who are not
      represented by a bargaining agent, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to merge bargaining units that are represented
      by the same bargaining agent,

  before the beginning of the last three months of the first collective
  agreement entered into after the transfer date that applies to those
  employees and that has resulted from a notice to bargain collectively
  given by or to the Corporation after that date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First collective agreement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, no collective agreement
  resulting from a notice to bargain collectively deemed under
  subsection 63(6) to have been given and no collective agreement or
  arbitral award referred to in subsection 63(9) is a first collective
  agreement within the meaning and for the purposes of subsection (2).

### [Severance Pay]{.HTitleText2} {#h-81440 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Severance pay

**[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding section
67, a designated employee referred to in section 58 is entitled to
severance pay in accordance with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any collective agreement or arbitral award that is
  binding on the designated employee immediately before the transfer
  date, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any terms and conditions of employment applicable to
  the designated employee immediately before the transfer date,

on the day the designated employee ceases to be employed in the Public
Service pursuant to this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recognition of service

**[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding section
67, when a designated employee is entitled to severance pay from the
Corporation pursuant to a collective agreement, an arbitral award or
terms and conditions of employment, the period for which the designated
employee is entitled to severance pay is deemed not to include any
period of employment for which the designated employee is entitled to
severance pay under section 70.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed lay-off

**[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** Designated employees are
deemed to be laid off from the Public Service on the day they cease to
be employed in the Public Service pursuant to this Act for the sole
purpose of entitlement to severance pay from Her Majesty in right of
Canada as represented by the Treasury Board.

## [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Services to Humanitarian or Emergency Flights]{.HTitleText1} {#h-81450 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in this section and sections 74
  to 84 and 90 to 92.

  [[collective agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[collective agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a collective
      agreement between the Corporation and a trade union, and includes
      a collective agreement or arbitral award continued under section
      62 or mentioned in section 63. ([convention
      collective]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[emergency flight support employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[emergency flight support employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      employee identified in accordance with the terms of an emergency
      support agreement. ([employé affecté aux vols
      d'urgence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[emergency support agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[emergency support agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      agreement between the Corporation and a trade union in respect of
      a bargaining unit represented by the trade union that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} identifies, in respect of the bargaining unit,
        the positions and the number of employees and their level of
        certification that are necessary to enable the Corporation to
        provide civil air navigation services in respect of humanitarian
        or emergency flights during a work stoppage;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specifies the manner in which employees in the
        bargaining unit shall be identified as emergency flight support
        employees and how they are to be informed of that designation;
        and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} requires emergency flight support employees who
        are members of that bargaining unit to perform duties in support
        of the provision of civil air navigation services in respect of
        humanitarian or emergency flights during a work
        stoppage. ([accord sur les services d'urgence]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[humanitarian or emergency flight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[humanitarian or emergency flight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an air ambulance flight;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an aerial fire-fighting flight;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a search and rescue flight;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a flight in support of police or military
        activities;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a flight to or from a location in Canada
        requiring designated northern or remote services;

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a flight incidental to any declared
        international, national, provincial or local emergency; or

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any other flight, or class of flights, declared
        by the Minister to be a humanitarian or emergency flight. ([vols
        d'urgence ou à vocation humanitaire]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[trade union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[trade union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bargaining agent for a
      bargaining unit of employees of the
      Corporation. ([syndicat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[work stoppage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[work stoppage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a strike or
      lockout. ([arrêt de travail]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Words and expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless a contrary intention appears, words and
  expressions in this Part have the same meaning as in the [Canada
  Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to provide services

**[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** In the event of a work
stoppage the Corporation shall continue to provide civil air navigation
services necessary for humanitarian or emergency flights.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parties to enter into agreement

**[[75]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-75 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation and every
trade union shall, in respect of each bargaining unit represented by the
trade union, enter into an emergency support agreement and, immediately
on its execution, the Corporation shall file a copy of the agreement
with the Minister of Labour.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Selection of mediator-arbitrator by
parties

- **[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  no emergency support agreement with respect to a bargaining unit is in
  force 90 days before the expiration of the collective agreement
  applicable to that unit, the Corporation and the trade union shall
  select a mediator-arbitrator within 15 days.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Selection of mediator-arbitrator by
  Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Corporation and a trade union are unable to
  agree to the selection of a mediator-arbitrator in the 15-day period,
  they shall so notify the Minister of Labour in writing, who shall,
  after such inquiry as that Minister considers necessary, appoint a
  mediator-arbitrator and inform them of the appointment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of mediator-arbitrator

- **[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  60 days after the mediator-arbitrator's selection or appointment, the
  mediator-arbitrator shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of concluding an emergency support
    agreement between the Corporation and the trade union,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} endeavour to mediate the matters in dispute and
      to bring about an emergency support agreement, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if unable to bring about an emergency support
      agreement, hear the Corporation and the trade union on the matters
      in dispute, arbitrate the matters and render a decision in the
      form of an emergency support agreement; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} file a copy of any decision under subparagraph
    (a)(ii) with the Minister of Labour.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Late decision not invalid

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The failure of a mediator-arbitrator to make a
  decision within the 60-day period does not affect the jurisdiction of
  the mediator-arbitrator to continue with and complete the arbitration
  proceedings, and any decision made by the mediator-arbitrator after
  the expiration of those 60 days is not for that reason invalid.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of mediator-arbitrator

**[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78 .sectionLabel}** A mediator-arbitrator
has, with such modifications as the circumstances require,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of the mediation referred to in
  subparagraph 77(1)(a)(i), all the powers of a conciliation
  commissioner under section 84 of the [Canada Labour
  Code](/eng/acts/L-2); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of the arbitration referred to in
  subparagraph 77(1)(a)(ii), all the powers and duties of an arbitrator
  under sections 60 and 61 of that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees and expenses of mediator-arbitrator

**[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** The fees and expenses of
a mediator-arbitrator shall be paid equally by the Corporation and the
trade union.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed agreement

**[[80]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80 .sectionLabel}** As of the day a
mediator-arbitrator makes a decision under subparagraph 77(1)(a)(ii),
the emergency support agreement that results from that decision is
deemed to have been entered into by the Corporation and the trade union
in respect of the bargaining unit to which the agreement applies, and
that agreement is binding on the Corporation, on the trade union and on
the employees in the bargaining unit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings prohibited

**[[81]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-81 .sectionLabel}** No order shall be made,
no process shall be entered into and no proceeding shall be taken in any
court

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to question the appointment of a mediator-arbitrator;
  or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to review, prohibit or restrain any proceeding or
  decision of a mediator-arbitrator.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revision of emergency support agreement

**[[82]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-82 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation and a
trade union may, at any time, revise any provision of an emergency
support agreement, but neither party may terminate the agreement during
the period beginning 180 days before the expiration of the collective
agreement that applies to the bargaining unit to which the emergency
support agreement relates and ending on the day on which a new
collective agreement that applies to that bargaining unit comes into
force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of termination

**[[83]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-83 .sectionLabel}** Where a party to an
emergency support agreement terminates the agreement, the party shall
immediately give written notice to the other party and to the Minister
of Labour.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms of employment

**[[84]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-84 .sectionLabel}** Unless the parties to an
emergency support agreement agree otherwise,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the terms and conditions of employment, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the rights, duties and privileges of the Corporation,
  of the employees in the bargaining unit to which the agreement applies
  and of the trade union representing those employees,

that were in effect immediately before the right to strike or lockout
was acquired continue to apply with respect to emergency flight support
employees who work during a work stoppage.

## [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Control and Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-81511 .Part}

### [Sending Notices]{.HTitleText2} {#h-81512 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sending notices

- **[[85]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-85 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  failure to send a notice or an announcement to any representative
  organization of users or to one or more users or persons that have
  notified the Corporation of their desire to receive notices or
  announcements under this Act does not constitute a failure to send the
  notice or announcement if the failure was unintentional.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Posting notices

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The failure to post an electronic version of a notice
  or announcement in a location that is generally accessible to persons
  who have access to what is commonly referred to as the Internet does
  not constitute a failure to post the notice or announcement if the
  failure was due to technical difficulties.

### [Amendment of Letters Patent and By-laws]{.HTitleText2} {#h-81518 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of letters patent

- **[[86]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-86 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  letters patent of the Corporation may not be amended without the prior
  written approval of the Minister, and any such amendment without that
  approval is invalid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment or repeal of by-laws

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A by-law of the Corporation that requires the
  Minister's approval to be amended or repealed may not be amended or
  repealed without the prior written approval of the Minister, and any
  such amendment or repeal without that approval is invalid.

### [Enforcement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-81524 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance or restraining order ---
letters patent and by-laws

- **[[87]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-87 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Corporation or any of its directors or officers fails to comply with
  the letters patent or the by-laws of the Corporation, any present or
  former member, director or officer of the Corporation or any other
  person who, in the discretion of the court, is a proper person to make
  an application under this section, may apply to the superior court of
  any province for an order directing the Corporation or any of its
  directors or officers to comply with, or restraining the Corporation
  or any of its directors or officers from acting in breach of, the
  letters patent or the by-laws, and on such application the court may
  so order and make any further order it thinks fit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance or restraining order ---
  contravention by Corporation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Corporation contravenes section 9 or 16,
  subsection 19(1), section 22 or subsection 23(1), (2), (4) or (5), any
  person may apply to the superior court of any province for an order
  directing the Corporation to comply with, or restraining the
  Corporation from acting in breach of, that provision, and on such
  application the court may so order and make any further order it
  thinks fit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summary application to court

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application may be made in a summary manner by
  petition, originating notice of motion or otherwise as the rules of
  the court provide and is subject to any order respecting notice to
  interested parties or costs, or any other order the court thinks fit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An order under subsection (1) may be appealed to the
  court to which appeals from the court making the order normally lie.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of s. 10(1)

**[[88]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-88 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes subsection 10(1) is guilty of an offence punishable on
summary conviction and liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine of not more
  than \$5,000, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, to a fine of not more
  than \$25,000

for each day or part of a day the offence continues.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to comply with a direction

**[[89]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-89 .sectionLabel}** Where the Corporation
fails to comply with a direction made under section 24, it is guilty of
an offence punishable on summary conviction and liable to a fine of not
more than \$25,000 for each day or part of a day the offence continues.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to provide services to
humanitarian or emergency flight

- **[[90]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-90 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Corporation contravenes section 74, it is guilty of an offence
  punishable on summary conviction and liable to a fine of not more than
  \$100,000 for each day or part of a day the offence continues.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In a prosecution for a contravention of section 74,
  it is a defence for the Corporation to establish that, in attempting
  to comply with that section, it was obstructed or hindered from doing
  so by its employees or a trade union representing its employees.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to work

**[[91]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91 .sectionLabel}** Any emergency flight
support employee who, during a work stoppage, refuses to perform any
duties required to be performed by that employee under an emergency
support agreement is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
conviction and liable to a fine of not more than \$5,000 for each day or
part of a day the employee refuses to perform those duties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hindering Corporation

- **[[92]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-92 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  officer or representative of the Corporation, any employee of the
  Corporation, any trade union that represents employees of the
  Corporation and any officer, representative or employee of such a
  trade union who obstructs or hinders the provision of civil air
  navigation services to a humanitarian or emergency flight by the
  Corporation, is guilty of an offence punishable on summary conviction
  and liable to a fine in accordance with subsection (3) for each day or
  part of a day the offence continues.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hindering Minister of National Defence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Corporation or any officer or
  representative of the Corporation, any employee of the Corporation,
  any trade union that represents employees of the Corporation or any
  officer, representative or employee of such a trade union obstructs or
  hinders a person acting under the authority of the Minister of
  National Defence from having access to facilities of the Corporation
  to which the person normally has access for the purposes of the
  person's normal duties, the Corporation or that person is guilty of an
  offence punishable on summary conviction and liable to a fine in
  accordance with subsection (3) for each day or part of a day the
  offence continues.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fine

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (1) and (2), the fine
  for each day or part of a day the offence continues is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$100,000, in the case of the Corporation or a
    trade union;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$50,000, in the case of a person acting in the
    capacity of an officer or representative of the Corporation or of a
    trade union when the offence was committed; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$5,000, in any other case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Trade unions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, a trade union is
  deemed to be a person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No imprisonment

**[[93]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-93 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding
subsection 787(2) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), a term of
imprisonment may not be imposed in default of payment of a fine that is
imposed under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of fines

**[[94]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-94 .sectionLabel}** Where a person is
convicted of an offence under this Act and the fine that is imposed is
not paid when required, the prosecutor may, by filing the conviction,
enter as a judgment the amount of the fine and costs, if any, in a
superior court of the province in which the trial was held, and the
judgment is enforceable against the person in the same manner as if it
were a judgment rendered against the person in that court in civil
proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate as evidence

**[[95]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-95 .sectionLabel}** If in any proceedings
under this Act a question arises as to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether an asset is a designated civil air navigation
  services asset,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether civil air navigation services were provided
  by the Department of Transport before the transfer date, or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any charge that was imposed immediately
  before the transfer date for the provision of civil air navigation
  services by the Minister,

a certificate purporting to have been issued by or under the authority
of the Minister stating any fact relevant to the question shall be
received and is admissible in evidence in those proceedings without
proof of the signature or authority of the person appearing to have
signed it and, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, is proof of
the fact so stated.
