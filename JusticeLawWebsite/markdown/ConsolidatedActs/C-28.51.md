to repeal the Family Allowances Act

## [Children's Special Allowances Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80633 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Children's special allowances

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  [Children's Special Allowances Act](/eng/acts/C-28.5) is enacted as
  set out in the schedule.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) shall come into force on January 1,
  1993.

## [Transitional]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80638 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transitional

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A department, agency or
institution that has applied for a special allowance in respect of a
child pursuant to the Family Allowances Act shall be deemed to have
applied for the special allowance in respect of the child pursuant to
the [Children's Special Allowances Act](/eng/acts/C-28.5) from and after
the day on which the [Children's Special Allowances
Act](/eng/acts/C-28.5) comes into force, and if the special allowance is
payable with respect to a period during which the Family Allowances Act
was in force, the amount of the special allowance with respect to that
period shall, notwithstanding section 8 of the [Children's Special
Allowances Act](/eng/acts/C-28.5), be the amount that would have been
payable under the Family Allowances Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transitional

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** A department, agency or
institution that is entitled to apply for a special allowance in respect
of a child pursuant to the Family Allowances Act shall be deemed to be
entitled to apply for the special allowance in respect of the child
pursuant to the [Children's Special Allowances Act](/eng/acts/C-28.5)
from and after the day on which the [Children's Special Allowances
Act](/eng/acts/C-28.5) comes into force, and if the special allowance is
payable with respect to a period during which the Family Allowances Act
was in force, the amount of the special allowance with respect to that
period shall, notwithstanding section 8 of the [Children's Special
Allowances Act](/eng/acts/C-28.5), be the amount that would have been
payable under the Family Allowances Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transitional

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** A department, agency or
institution to which a special allowance was payable in respect of a
child pursuant to the Family Allowances Act on the day on which that Act
is repealed shall be deemed to be a department, agency or institution
for which the payment of a special allowance has been approved in
respect of the child pursuant to the [Children's Special Allowances
Act](/eng/acts/C-28.5) on the day on which that Act comes into force.

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Sections 24 to 26 shall
come into force on January 1, 1993.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80648 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repeal of R.S., c. F-1

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Family Allowances Act is repealed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment after 1993

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No payment of an amount referred to in subsection
  3(1) of the Family Allowances Act shall be made on the basis of a
  request for payment thereof made after 1993.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) shall come into force on
  January 1, 1993.
