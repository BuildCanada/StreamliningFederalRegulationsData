## Forgiveness of Certain Official Development Assistance Debts Act {#forgiveness-of-certain-official-development-assistance-debts-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1987, c. 27
:::

Assented to 1987-06-30

An Act relating to the forgiveness of debts incurred or assumed in
respect of certain official development assistance loans made by the
Government of Canada to the Governments of Togo and of the Islamic
Republic of Mauritania and also to the former East African Community
::::

::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Forgiveness of Certain Official Development Assistance Debts
Act](/eng/acts/F-30.2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debts owing by Togo

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The official development
assistance debts owing by the Government of Togo to the Government of
Canada under the loan agreements entered into between those governments
and referred to in Part I of the schedule are hereby forgiven.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt owing by Mauritania

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The official development
assistance debt owing by the Government of the Islamic Republic of
Mauritania to the Government of Canada under the loan agreement entered
into between those governments and referred to in Part II of the
schedule is hereby forgiven.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debts owing in relation to loans made to
the East African Community

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The debts assumed by the
governments of the United Republic of Tanzania, of the Republic of
Uganda and of Kenya in accordance with agreements entered into between
those governments and the Government of Canada and referred to in Part
IV of the schedule, being agreements for the apportionment of
liabilities that arose, before the dissolution of the former East
African Community, under the loan agreements referred to in Part III of
the schedule, are hereby forgiven.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deletion from the accounts of Canada

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The amount outstanding as
recorded in the accounts of Canada in respect of the debts forgiven by
sections 2 to 4 is hereby written off as a budgetary expenditure and
deleted from those accounts.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#F-30.2_en_1){#F-30.2_en_1-ID0EBCA}[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6
.sectionLabel}** This Act shall come into force on a day to be fixed by
proclamation.

## Part I {#part-i .SchedHeadL1}

- [1]{.lawlabel} Loan agreement entered into between the Government of
  Togo and the Government of Canada and dated August 19, 1969, as
  modified by an agreement entered into between those parties and dated
  September 9, 1972.

- [2]{.lawlabel} Loan agreement entered into between the Government of
  Canada and the Government of Togo and dated March 7, 1977, as modified
  by amending agreement no. 1 entered into between those parties and
  dated November 6, 1980.

## Part II {#part-ii .SchedHeadL1}

- [1]{.lawlabel} Loan agreement entered into between the Government of
  Canada and the Government of the Islamic Republic of Mauritania and
  dated December 10, 1975.

## Part III {#part-iii .SchedHeadL1}

- [1]{.lawlabel} Loan agreement entered into between the East African
  Community and the Government of Canada and dated October 28, 1970, as
  modified by an agreement entered into between those parties and dated
  March 15, 1971.

- [2]{.lawlabel} Loan agreement entered into between the East African
  Community and the Government of Canada and dated December 18, 1972, as
  modified by an agreement entered into between those parties and dated
  August 5, 1974.

## Part IV {#part-iv .SchedHeadL1}

- [1]{.lawlabel} Agreement entered into between the Government of the
  United Republic of Tanzania and the Government of Canada and dated
  March 28, 1984.

- [2]{.lawlabel} Agreement entered into between the Government of the
  Republic of Uganda and the Government of Canada and dated March 28,
  1984.

- [3]{.lawlabel} Agreement entered into between the Government of Kenya
  and the Government of Canada and dated March 28, 1984.
