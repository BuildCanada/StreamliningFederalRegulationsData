An Act to promote the efficiency and adaptability of the Canadian

Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission Act, the
Competition Act, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic
Documents Act and the Telecommunications Act

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-176920 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[commercial activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[commercial activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any particular
      transaction, act or conduct or any regular course of conduct that
      is of a commercial character, whether or not the person who
      carries it out does so in the expectation of profit, other than
      any transaction, act or conduct that is carried out for the
      purposes of law enforcement, public safety, the protection of
      Canada, the conduct of international affairs or the defence of
      Canada. ([activité commerciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian
      Radio-television and Telecommunications
      Commission. ([Conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Commissioner of Competition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Commissioner of Competition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      Commissioner of Competition appointed under subsection 7(1) of the
      [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34). ([commissaire de la
      concurrence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[computer program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[computer program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in subsection 342.1(2) of the Criminal Code. ([programme
      d'ordinateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[computer system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[computer system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 342.1(2) of the [Criminal
      Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([ordinateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[court of competent jurisdiction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[court of competent jurisdiction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      Federal Court or a superior court of a province. ([tribunal
      compétent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means signs, signals, symbols or
      concepts that are being prepared or have been prepared in a form
      suitable for use in a computer system. ([données]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 487.011 of the [Criminal
      Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([document]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[electronic address]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[electronic address]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an address used
      in connection with the transmission of an electronic message to

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an electronic mail account;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an instant messaging account;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a telephone account; or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any similar account. ([adresse
        électronique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[electronic message]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[electronic message]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a message sent by
      any means of telecommunication, including a text, sound, voice or
      image message. ([message électronique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual, partnership,
      corporation, organization, association, trustee, administrator,
      executor, liquidator of a succession, receiver or legal
      representative. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Privacy Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Privacy Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Privacy
      Commissioner appointed under section 53 of the [Privacy
      Act](/eng/acts/P-21). ([Commissaire à la protection de la vie
      privée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[telecommunications facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[telecommunications facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
      facility, apparatus or other thing that is used for
      telecommunications or for any operation directly connected with
      telecommunications. ([installation de
      télécommunication]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[telecommunications service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[telecommunications service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      service, or a feature of a service, that is provided by means of
      telecommunications facilities, whether the telecommunications
      service provider owns, leases or has any other interest in or
      right respecting the telecommunications facilities and any related
      equipment used to provide the service. ([service de
      télécommunication]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[telecommunications service provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[telecommunications service provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      a person who, independently or as part of a group or association,
      provides telecommunications
      services. ([télécommunicateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transmission data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transmission data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means data that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} relates to the telecommunications functions of
        dialling, routing, addressing or signalling;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} either is transmitted to identify, activate or
        configure an apparatus or device, including a computer program,
        in order to establish or maintain a communication, or is
        generated during the creation, transmission or reception of a
        communication and identifies or purports to identify the type,
        direction, date, time, duration, size, origin, destination or
        termination of the communication; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not reveal the substance, meaning or
        purpose of the communication. ([données de
        transmission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of commercial electronic
  message

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, a commercial electronic
  message is an electronic message that, having regard to the content of
  the message, the hyperlinks in the message to content on a website or
  other database, or the contact information contained in the message,
  it would be reasonable to conclude has as its purpose, or one of its
  purposes, to encourage participation in a commercial activity,
  including an electronic message that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} offers to purchase, sell, barter or lease a
    product, goods, a service, land or an interest or right in land;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} offers to provide a business, investment or gaming
    opportunity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} advertises or promotes anything referred to in
    paragraph (a) or (b); or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} promotes a person, including the public image of a
    person, as being a person who does anything referred to in any of
    paragraphs (a) to (c), or who intends to do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other commercial electronic message

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An electronic message that contains a request for
  consent to send a message described in subsection (2) is also
  considered to be a commercial electronic message.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An electronic message described in subsection (2)
  or (3) that is sent for the purposes of law enforcement, public
  safety, the protection of Canada, the conduct of international affairs
  or the defence of Canada is not considered to be a commercial
  electronic message.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person to whom a message is sent

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, a reference to the
  person to whom an electronic message is sent means the holder of the
  account associated with the electronic address to which the message is
  sent, as well as any person who it is reasonable to believe is or
  might be authorized by the account holder to use the electronic
  address.

## [Conflict of Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-176959 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Precedence of this Act

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In the event of a conflict
between a provision of this Act and a provision of Part 1 of the
[Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents
Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6), the provision of this Act operates despite the
provision of that Part, to the extent of the conflict.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-176962 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of Act

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to promote the efficiency and adaptability of the Canadian economy by
regulating commercial conduct that discourages the use of electronic
means to carry out commercial activities, because that conduct

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} impairs the availability, reliability, efficiency and
  optimal use of electronic means to carry out commercial activities;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} imposes additional costs on businesses and consumers;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} compromises privacy and the security of confidential
  information; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} undermines the confidence of Canadians in the use of
  electronic means of communication to carry out their commercial
  activities in Canada and abroad.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-176969 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act binding on certain agents

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on any
corporation that is expressly declared by or under any Act of Parliament
or of the legislature of a province to be an agent of Her Majesty, when
the corporation is acting as such in the course of any commercial
activity.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-176972 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Broadcasting excluded

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Act does not apply in
respect of [[broadcasting]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} by a [[broadcasting
undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, other than an [[online
undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms are defined in
subsection 2(1) of the [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01).

## [Requirements and Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-176975 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unsolicited electronic messages

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited to send or cause or permit to be sent to an electronic
  address a commercial electronic message unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person to whom the message is sent has
    consented to receiving it, whether the consent is express or
    implied; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the message complies with subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of message

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The message must be in a form that conforms to the
  prescribed requirements and must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out prescribed information that identifies the
    person who sent the message and the person --- if different --- on
    whose behalf it is sent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out information enabling the person to whom the
    message is sent to readily contact one of the persons referred to in
    paragraph (a); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set out an unsubscribe mechanism in accordance with
    subsection 11(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of validity of contact
  information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person who sends the commercial electronic
  message and the person --- if different --- on whose behalf the
  commercial electronic message is sent must ensure that the contact
  information referred to in paragraph (2)(b) is valid for a minimum of
  60 days after the message has been sent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an electronic message is considered to have been
    sent once its transmission has been initiated; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is immaterial whether the electronic address to
    which an electronic message is sent exists or whether an electronic
    message reaches its intended destination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply to a commercial
  electronic message

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is sent by or on behalf of an individual to
    another individual with whom they have a personal or family
    relationship, as defined in the regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is sent to a person who is engaged in a
    commercial activity and consists solely of an inquiry or application
    related to that activity; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that is of a class, or is sent in circumstances,
    specified in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(a) does not apply to a commercial
  electronic message that solely

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provides a quote or estimate for the supply of a
    product, goods, a service, land or an interest or right in land, if
    the quote or estimate was requested by the person to whom the
    message is sent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} facilitates, completes or confirms a commercial
    transaction that the person to whom the message is sent previously
    agreed to enter into with the person who sent the message or the
    person --- if different --- on whose behalf it is sent;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provides warranty information, product recall
    information or safety or security information about a product, goods
    or a service that the person to whom the message is sent uses, has
    used or has purchased;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provides notification of factual information about

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the ongoing use or ongoing purchase by the person
      to whom the message is sent of a product, goods or a service
      offered under a subscription, membership, account, loan or similar
      relationship by the person who sent the message or the person ---
      if different --- on whose behalf it is sent, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the ongoing subscription, membership, account,
      loan or similar relationship of the person to whom the message is
      sent;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} provides information directly related to an
    employment relationship or related benefit plan in which the person
    to whom the message is sent is currently involved, is currently
    participating or is currently enrolled;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} delivers a product, goods or a service, including
    product updates or upgrades, that the person to whom the message is
    sent is entitled to receive under the terms of a transaction that
    they have previously entered into with the person who sent the
    message or the person --- if different --- on whose behalf it is
    sent; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} communicates for a purpose specified in the
    regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply to a telecommunications
  service provider merely because the service provider provides a
  telecommunications service that enables the transmission of the
  message.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(7.1)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply to a commercial
  electronic message that is sent or caused or permitted to be sent by
  an [[online undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01), if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person to whom the message is sent has
    expressly or implicitly consented to the transmission of a
    [[program]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in that subsection,
    from that online undertaking to an electronic address; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the message is or forms part of that program or is
    sent in the course of the transmission of that program to the
    electronic address to which that program is transmitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply to a commercial
  electronic message

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is, in whole or in part, an interactive
    two-way voice communication between individuals;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is sent by means of a facsimile to a telephone
    account; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that is a voice recording sent to a telephone
    account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Altering transmission data

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited, in the course of a commercial activity, to alter or cause
  to be altered the transmission data in an electronic message so that
  the message is delivered to a destination other than or in addition to
  that specified by the sender, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the alteration is made with the express consent of
    the sender or the person to whom the message is sent, and the person
    altering or causing to be altered the data complies with subsection
    11(4); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the alteration is made in accordance with a court
    order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if the alteration is
  made by a telecommunications service provider for the purposes of
  network management.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Installation of computer program

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not, in the course of a commercial activity, install or cause to
  be installed a computer program on any other person's computer system
  or, having so installed or caused to be installed a computer program,
  cause an electronic message to be sent from that computer system,
  unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person has obtained the express consent of the
    owner or an authorized user of the computer system and complies with
    subsection 11(5); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person is acting in accordance with a court
    order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person contravenes subsection (1) only if the
  computer system is located in Canada at the relevant time or if the
  person either is in Canada at the relevant time or is acting under the
  direction of a person who is in Canada at the time when they give the
  directions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of sections 6 to 8

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited to aid,
induce, procure or cause to be procured the doing of any act contrary to
any of sections 6 to 8.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Express consent --- sections 6 to 8

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who seeks express consent for the doing of an act described in
  any of sections 6 to 8 must, when requesting consent, set out clearly
  and simply the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the purpose or purposes for which the consent is
    being sought;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribed information that identifies the person
    seeking consent and, if the person is seeking consent on behalf of
    another person, prescribed information that identifies that other
    person; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other prescribed information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(b), for the purposes of section
  6, if a person is seeking express consent on behalf of a person whose
  identity is not known,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the only information that is required to be
    provided under that paragraph is prescribed information that
    identifies the person seeking consent; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person seeking consent must comply with the
    regulations in respect of the use that may be made of the consent
    and the conditions on which the consent may be used.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional requirement --- section 8

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who seeks express consent for the doing of
  any act described in section 8 must, when requesting consent, also, in
  addition to setting out any other prescribed information, clearly and
  simply describe, in general terms, the function and purpose of the
  computer program that is to be installed if the consent is given.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional requirements associated with
  certain functions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the requirements set out in
  subsections (1) and (3), if the computer program that is to be
  installed performs one or more of the functions described in
  subsection (5), the person who seeks express consent must, when
  requesting consent, clearly and prominently, and separately and apart
  from the licence agreement,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} describe the program's material elements that
    perform the function or functions, including the nature and purpose
    of those elements and their reasonably foreseeable impact on the
    operation of the computer system; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} bring those elements to the attention of the person
    from whom consent is being sought in the prescribed manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Description of functions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A function referred to in subsection (4) is any of
  the following functions that the person who seeks express consent
  knows and intends will cause the computer system to operate in a
  manner that is contrary to the reasonable expectations of the owner or
  an authorized user of the computer system:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} collecting personal information stored on the
    computer system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} interfering with the owner's or an authorized
    user's control of the computer system;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} changing or interfering with settings, preferences
    or commands already installed or stored on the computer system
    without the knowledge of the owner or an authorized user of the
    computer system;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} changing or interfering with data that is stored on
    the computer system in a manner that obstructs, interrupts or
    interferes with lawful access to or use of that data by the owner or
    an authorized user of the computer system;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} causing the computer system to communicate with
    another computer system, or other device, without the authorization
    of the owner or an authorized user of the computer system;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} installing a computer program that may be activated
    by a third party without the knowledge of the owner or an authorized
    user of the computer system; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} performing any other function specified in the
    regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (4) does not apply in respect of a
  computer program that performs a function described in subsection (5)
  if that function only collects, uses or communicates transmission data
  or performs an operation specified in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Updates and upgrades

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (3) do not apply in respect of
  the installation of an update or upgrade to a computer program the
  installation or use of which was expressly consented to in accordance
  with subsections (1) and (3) if the person who gave the consent is
  entitled to receive the update or upgrade under the terms of the
  express consent and the update or upgrade is installed in accordance
  with those terms.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person considered to expressly consent

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} A person is considered to expressly consent to the
  installation of a computer program if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the program is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a cookie,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} HTML code,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} Java Scripts,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an operating system,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any other program that is executable only through
      the use of another computer program whose installation or use the
      person has previously expressly consented to, or

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} any other program specified in the regulations;
      and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person's conduct is such that it is reasonable
    to believe that they consent to the program's installation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implied consent --- section 6

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of section 6, except subsection
  6(7.1), consent is implied only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person who sends the message, the person who
    causes it to be sent or the person who permits it to be sent has an
    existing business relationship or an existing non-business
    relationship with the person to whom it is sent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person to whom the message is sent has
    conspicuously published, or has caused to be conspicuously
    published, the electronic address to which the message is sent, the
    publication is not accompanied by a statement that the person does
    not wish to receive unsolicited commercial electronic messages at
    the electronic address and the message is relevant to the person's
    business, role, functions or duties in a business or official
    capacity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person to whom the message is sent has
    disclosed, to the person who sends the message, the person who
    causes it to be sent or the person who permits it to be sent, the
    electronic address to which the message is sent without indicating a
    wish not to receive unsolicited commercial electronic messages at
    the electronic address, and the message is relevant to the person's
    business, role, functions or duties in a business or official
    capacity; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the message is sent in the circumstances set out in
    the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[existing business
  relationship]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (9), [[existing business
  relationship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a business relationship
  between the person to whom the message is sent and any of the other
  persons referred to in that subsection --- that is, any person who
  sent or caused or permitted to be sent the message --- arising from

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the purchase or lease of a product, goods, a
    service, land or an interest or right in land, within the two-year
    period immediately before the day on which the message was sent, by
    the person to whom the message is sent from any of those other
    persons;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the acceptance by the person to whom the message is
    sent, within the period referred to in paragraph (a), of a business,
    investment or gaming opportunity offered by any of those other
    persons;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the bartering of anything mentioned in
    paragraph (a) between the person to whom the message is sent and any
    of those other persons within the period referred to in that
    paragraph;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a written contract entered into between the person
    to whom the message is sent and any of those other persons in
    respect of a matter not referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to (c),
    if the contract is currently in existence or expired within the
    period referred to in paragraph (a); or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an inquiry or application, within the six-month
    period immediately before the day on which the message was sent,
    made by the person to whom the message is sent to any of those other
    persons, in respect of anything mentioned in any of paragraphs (a)
    to (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (10), the following
  organizations are considered to be businesses:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a cooperative as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
    [Canada Cooperatives Act](/eng/acts/C-1.7);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a cooperative corporation as defined in section 2
    of the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any similar organization incorporated under an Act
    of Parliament or the legislature of a province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} If a person has an existing business relationship
  with another person in accordance with subsection (10), and the
  business is sold, the person who purchases the business is considered
  to have, in respect of that business, an existing business
  relationship with that other person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[existing non-business
  relationship]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(13)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (9), [[existing non-business
  relationship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a non-business relationship
  between the person to whom the message is sent and any of the other
  persons referred to in that subsection --- that is, any person who
  sent or caused or permitted to be sent the message --- arising from

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a donation or gift made by the person to whom the
    message is sent to any of those other persons within the two-year
    period immediately before the day on which the message was sent,
    where that other person is a registered charity as defined in
    subsection 248(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), a
    political party or organization, or a person who is a candidate ---
    as defined in an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a
    province --- for publicly elected office;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} volunteer work performed by the person to whom the
    message is sent for any of those other persons, or attendance at a
    meeting organized by that other person, within the two-year period
    immediately before the day on which the message was sent, where that
    other person is a registered charity as defined in subsection 248(1)
    of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), a political party or
    organization or a person who is a candidate --- as defined in an Act
    of Parliament or of the legislature of a province --- for publicly
    elected office; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} membership, as defined in the regulations, by the
    person to whom the message is sent, in any of those other persons,
    within the two-year period immediately before the day on which the
    message was sent, where that other person is a club, association or
    voluntary organization, as defined in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(14)]{.lawlabel} Where a period is specified in subsection (10)
  or (13) in relation to the purchase or lease of a product, goods, a
  service, land or an interest or right in land, or in relation to a
  donation, gift or membership,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a purchase, lease, donation or gift,
    if it involves an ongoing use or ongoing purchase under a
    subscription, account, loan or similar relationship, the period is
    considered to begin on the day that the subscription, account, loan
    or other relationship terminates; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a membership, the period is
    considered to begin on the day that the membership terminates.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unsubscribe mechanism --- section 6

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enable the person to whom the commercial electronic

  referred to in paragraph (1)(b) is valid for a minimum of 60 days

  and the person --- if different --- on whose behalf the message was
  sent must ensure that effect is given to an indication sent in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a period of one year after any computer program

    without cost to the person who gave consent, assist that person in
    removing or disabling the computer program as soon as feasible.

### [Preservation Demand]{.HTitleText2} {#h-177129 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preservation demand

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is designated for the purpose of this section may cause a
  demand to be served on a telecommunications service provider requiring
  it to preserve transmission data that is in, or comes into, its
  possession or control.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expiry and revocation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), the demand expires 21 days
  after the day on which it is served unless, before its expiry, a
  notice extending the demand for an additional period of 21 days is
  served on the telecommunications service provider. A demand may not be
  extended more than once and a notice revoking the demand may be served
  on the telecommunications service provider at any time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of demand

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who is designated for the purpose of this
  section may make or extend a demand only for the purpose of one or
  more of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} verifying compliance with this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determining whether any of sections 6 to 9 has been
    contravened; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} assisting an investigation or proceeding in respect
    of a contravention of the laws of a foreign state that address
    conduct that is substantially similar to conduct prohibited under
    any of sections 6 to 9.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions to prevent disclosure

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The designated person causing a demand to be served
  may impose conditions in the demand to prevent the disclosure of some
  or all of its contents or its existence if they have reasonable
  grounds to believe that the disclosure would jeopardize the conduct of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an investigation under this Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an investigation or proceeding in respect of a
    contravention of the laws of a foreign state that address conduct
    that is substantially similar to conduct prohibited under any of
    sections 6 to 9.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expiry and revocation of conditions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A condition imposed to prevent disclosure expires six
  months after the day on which the demand is served on the
  telecommunications service provider unless, before its expiry, a
  notice extending the condition for an additional period of six months
  is served on the telecommunications service provider. A condition may
  not be extended more than once and a notice revoking the condition may
  be served on the telecommunications service provider at any time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preservation and destruction of
  transmission data

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A telecommunications service provider that is served
  with a demand must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsections 16(2) and (3), preserve the
    data until the demand expires or is revoked; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} destroy the data that would not be retained in the
    normal course of business and any document that is prepared for the
    purpose of preserving data under this section as soon as the demand
    expires or is revoked, unless a notice requiring the production of a
    document based on that data has been served on it under section 17.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for review

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  five business days after the day on which a demand is served, a
  telecommunications service provider may apply in writing to the
  Commission either for a review of the demand on the grounds that
  preservation of some or all of the data would place an undue burden on
  it or for a review of the conditions imposed to prevent disclosure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Commission

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} After considering any representations made by the
  telecommunications service provider and by the person designated for
  the purposes of section 15, the Commission may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} allow the application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} deny the application; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} vary, in any manner that the Commission considers
    reasonable in the circumstances,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the requirement to preserve transmission data, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any condition imposed in the demand.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No obligation to preserve new data

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a telecommunications service provider applies for
  review on the grounds that preservation of some or all of the data
  would place an undue burden on it and the Commission does not make a
  decision in respect of that matter within five business days after the
  day on which the application was made, the telecommunications service
  provider is not required to preserve any data to which the application
  relates that comes into the telecommunications service provider's
  possession or control after the expiry of the five days.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission must cause a copy of its decision to
  be served on the telecommunications service provider together with a
  notice of their right to appeal.

### [Notice to Produce]{.HTitleText2} {#h-177164 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice for production

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is designated for the purpose of this section may cause a
  notice to be served on a person requiring them to produce a copy of a
  document that is in their possession or control, or to prepare a
  document based on data, information or documents that are in their
  possession or control and to produce that document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designated person may issue the notice only for
  the purpose of one or more of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} verifying compliance with this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determining whether any of sections 6 to 9 has been
    contravened; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} assisting an investigation or proceeding in respect

    conduct that is substantially similar to conduct prohibited under
    any of sections 6 to 9.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Particulars of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice must require the document to be produced
  to a person named in the notice within the time, at the place and in
  the form specified in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The designated person may impose conditions in the
  notice to prevent the disclosure of some or all of its contents or its
  existence if they have reasonable grounds to believe that the
  disclosure would jeopardize the conduct of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an investigation under this Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an investigation or proceeding in respect of a

    that is substantially similar to conduct prohibited under any of
    sections 6 to 9.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expiry and revocation of conditions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A condition imposed to prevent disclosure expires six
  months after the day on which the notice is served on the person
  unless, before its expiry, a notice extending the condition for an
  additional period of six months is served on them. A condition may not
  be extended more than once and a notice revoking the condition may be
  served on the person at any time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of documents not required

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Documents and copies of documents that are produced
  under this section need not be returned to the person who produced
  them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for review

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At any
  time before they are required to produce a document, a person may
  apply in writing to the Commission either for a review of the notice
  on the grounds that the requirement to prepare or produce a document
  is unreasonable in the circumstances or that the production would
  disclose privileged information or for a review of the conditions
  imposed to prevent disclosure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No obligation to produce

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person applies for review on the grounds that
  the requirement to prepare or produce a document is unreasonable in
  the circumstances, they are not required to prepare or produce the
  document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Commission

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} After considering any representations made by the
  applicant and by the person designated for the purposes of section 17,
  the Commission may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} allow the application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} deny the application; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} vary, in any manner that the Commission considers
    reasonable in the circumstances,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the requirement to produce, or prepare and
      produce, a document, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any condition imposed in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Particulars

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Commission decides to confirm the requirement
  to produce a document, it must specify in its decision that the
  document be produced to a person named in the decision within the

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Commission must cause a copy of its decision to
  be served on the person together with a notice of their right to
  appeal.

### [Warrants]{.HTitleText2} {#h-177199 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On an
  [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application, a justice of the peace
  may issue a warrant authorizing a person who is designated for the
  purpose of this section and who is named in the warrant to enter a
  place if the justice is satisfied by information on oath that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} entry to the place is necessary to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} verify compliance with this Act,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} determine whether any of sections 6 to 9 has
      been contravened, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} assist an investigation or proceeding in
      respect of a contravention of the laws of a foreign state that
      address conduct that is substantially similar to conduct
      prohibited under any of sections 6 to 9; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the place is a dwelling-house, entry has been
    refused or there are reasonable grounds to believe that entry will
    be refused or that consent to entry cannot be obtained from the
    occupant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions in warrant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The warrant may contain any conditions that the
  justice considers appropriate, including conditions to protect
  privileged information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers in execution of warrant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the conditions specified in the warrant,
  in executing the warrant, the designated person may, for any purpose
  set out in subparagraphs (1)(a)(i) to (iii),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine anything that is found in the place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} use any means of communication found in the place
    or cause it to be used;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} use any computer system found in the place --- or
    cause it to be used --- to examine data contained in, or available
    to, the system;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prepare a document --- or cause it to be prepared
    --- based on the data;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} use any copying equipment --- or cause it to be
    used --- to make copies of documents;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} remove anything found in the place for examination
    or copying; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} prohibit or limit access to all or part of the
    place.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to designated persons

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The owner of the place, the person in charge of the
  place and every person found in the place must give all assistance
  that is reasonably required to enable the designated person to execute
  the warrant and must provide any documents, data and information ---
  including information establishing their identity --- that are
  reasonably required for that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Execution of warrant

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A warrant must be executed between six o'clock in the
  morning and nine o'clock in the evening unless the justice authorizes
  its execution at another time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entry onto private property

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of gaining entry to the place
  referred to in the warrant issued under subsection (1), the designated
  person may enter private property and pass through it, and is not
  liable for doing so. For greater certainty, no person has the right to
  object to that use of the property and no warrant is required for
  entry onto the property, other than for entry to a dwelling-house.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons accompanying designated person

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A person may, at the designated person's request,
  accompany the designated person to assist the designated person to
  gain entry to the place referred to in the warrant and is not liable
  for doing so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} In executing a warrant, a designated person must not
  use force unless they are accompanied by a peace officer and the use
  of force has been specifically authorized in the warrant.

### [Violations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-177229 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violations

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who contravenes any of sections 6 to 9 commits a violation for
  which they are liable to an administrative monetary penalty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of penalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of a penalty is to promote compliance
  with this Act and not to punish.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors for penalty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The following factors must be taken into account when
  determining the amount of a penalty:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the penalty;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the nature and scope of the violation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person's history with respect to any previous
    violation under this Act, any previous conduct that is reviewable
    under section 74.011 of the [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34) and
    any previous contravention of section 5 of the [Personal Information
    Protection and Electronic Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6) that
    relates to a collection or use described in subsection 7.1(2) or (3)
    of that Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person's history with respect to any previous
    undertaking entered into under subsection 21(1) and any previous
    consent agreement signed under subsection 74.12(1) or 74.131(1) of
    the [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34) that relates to acts or
    omissions that constitute conduct that is reviewable under section
    74.011 of that Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any financial benefit that the person obtained from
    the commission of the violation;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the person's ability to pay the penalty;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} whether the person has voluntarily paid
    compensation to a person affected by the violation;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the factors established by the regulations; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any other relevant factor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum penalties

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The maximum penalty for a violation is \$1,000,000 in
  the case of an individual, and \$10,000,000 in the case of any other
  person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} designating provisions whose contravention
    constitutes a separate violation in respect of each day during which
    it continues; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establishing factors for the purposes of paragraph
    (3)(h).

### [Notices of Violation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-177267 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of violation

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is designated for the purpose of this section may issue a
  notice of violation and cause it to be served on a person if they
  believe on reasonable grounds that the person has committed a
  violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of violation must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name the person believed to have committed the
    violation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identify every act or omission for which the notice
    is served and every provision at issue;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set out the administrative monetary penalty that
    the person is liable to pay and the time and manner of payment;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} inform the person that they may make
    representations to the Commission within 30 days after the day on
    which the notice is served or any longer period set out in the
    notice, and set out the manner for making the representations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} inform the person that, if they do not pay the
    penalty or make representations in accordance with the notice, they
    will be deemed to have committed the violation and that the penalty
    set out in the notice will be imposed; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} inform the person that if they are found or are
    deemed to have committed a violation they may be made the subject of
    an order requiring them to do what this Act requires them to do, or
    forbidding them to do what this Act prohibits them from doing, and
    that the order can be enforced as an order of a court of competent
    jurisdiction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  proceeding in respect of a violation may be commenced later than three
  years after the day on which the subject matter of the proceeding
  became known to a person designated under section 14.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document that appears to have been issued by the
  secretary to the Commission, certifying the day on which the subject
  matter of any proceeding became known to a person designated under
  section 14, is admissible in evidence without proof of the signature
  or official character of the person who appears to have signed the
  document and is proof of the matter asserted in it in the absence of
  evidence to the contrary.

### [Determination of Responsibility]{.HTitleText2} {#h-177284 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Options

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is served with a notice of violation must, in accordance
  with the notice, pay the penalty set out in the notice or make
  representations with respect to the amount of the penalty or the acts
  or omissions that constitute the alleged violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed violation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person is deemed to have committed the violation if
  they either pay the penalty in accordance with the notice of violation
  or do not pay the penalty and do not make representations, in
  accordance with the notice of violation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representations

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person makes representations in accordance with the notice, the
  Commission must decide, on a balance of probabilities, whether the
  person committed the violation and, if so, may impose the penalty set
  out in the notice of violation, may reduce or waive the penalty, or
  may suspend payment of the penalty subject to any conditions that the
  Commission considers necessary to ensure compliance with this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission must cause a copy of its decision to
  be served on the person together with a notice of their right to
  appeal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restraining orders

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person is deemed under subsection 24(2) to have committed a violation
  or has been found under subsection 25(1) to have committed a
  violation, the Commission may make an order directing the person to
  cease contravening the provision to which the violation relates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission must cause a copy of its order to be
  served on the person together with a notice of their right to appeal.

### [Rules about Violations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-177323 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, a

[Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) does not apply.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors, officers, etc., of

in or participated in the commission of the violation, whether or not
the corporation is proceeded against.

violation that is committed by their employee acting within the scope of
their employment or their agent or mandatary acting within the scope of
their authority, whether or not the employee, agent or mandatary is
identified or proceeded against.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person must not be found to be liable for a violation if they
  establish that they exercised due diligence to prevent the commission
  of the violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Common law principles

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every rule and principle of the common law that makes
  any circumstance a justification or excuse in relation to a charge for
  an offence applies in respect of a violation to the extent that it is
  not inconsistent with this Act.

### [General Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-177335 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Questions of law and fact

  judgment of a court is admissible in proceedings of the Commission.

  the question is in issue.

to the attendance and examination of witnesses and the production and
examination of documents or things.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Panels of Commission

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  matter on behalf of the Commission in a proceeding under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presiding member

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a panel consists of more than one member, the
  Chairperson must designate one of the members to preside over the
  panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

  a decision of the panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting after expiry of appointment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A member whose appointment expires may, with the
  approval of the Chairperson, conclude any proceeding that the member
  has begun.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of panel

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A panel may exercise the powers and perform the

  any matter before the panel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules of procedure

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  representations to the Commission, and the conduct of proceedings held

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of demands and notices

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may establish the form and content of
  demands under section 15, notices to prepare or produce documents
  under section 17 and notices of violation under section 22.

  person.

### [Rules about Contraventions and Reviewable Conduct]{.HTitleText2} {#h-177457 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors and officers of corporations

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** An officer, director,
agent or mandatary of a corporation that commits a contravention of any
of sections 6 to 9 of this Act or of section 5 of the [Personal
Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6)
that relates to a collection or use described in subsection 7.1(2) or
(3) of that Act, or that engages in conduct that is reviewable under
section 74.011 of the [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34), is liable for
the contravention or reviewable conduct, as the case may be, if they
directed, authorized, assented to, acquiesced in or participated in the
commission of that contravention, or engaged in that conduct, whether or
not the corporation is proceeded against.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vicarious liability

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** A person is liable for a
contravention of any of sections 6 to 9 of this Act or of section 5 of
the [Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents
Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6) that relates to a collection or use described in
subsection 7.1(2) or (3) of that Act, or for conduct that is reviewable
under section 74.011 of the [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34), that is
committed or engaged in, as the case may be, by their employee acting
within the scope of their employment or their agent or mandatary acting
within the scope of their authority, whether or not the employee, agent
or mandatary is identified or proceeded against.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person must not be found to have committed a contravention of any of
  sections 6 to 9 of this Act or of section 5 of the [Personal
  Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6)
  that relates to a collection or use described in subsection 7.1(2)
  or (3) of that Act, or to have engaged in conduct that is reviewable
  under section 74.011 of the [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34), if they
  establish that they exercised due diligence to prevent the
  contravention or conduct, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Common law principles

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every rule and principle of the common law that makes
  any circumstance a justification or excuse in relation to a charge for
  an offence applies in respect of a contravention of any of sections 6
  to 9 of this Act or of section 5 of the [Personal Information
  Protection and Electronic Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6) that relates
  to a collection or use described in subsection 7.1(2) or (3) of that
  Act, or in respect of conduct that is reviewable under section 74.011
  of the [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34), to the extent that it is not
  inconsistent with this Act or the [Personal Information Protection and
  Electronic Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6) or the [Competition
  Act](/eng/acts/C-34), as the case may be.

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** If more than one person
is determined by the court under subsection 51(1) to have contravened
any of the provisions referred to in an application under subsection
47(1) or to have engaged in conduct referred to in an application under

[Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34), those persons are jointly and
severally, or solidarily, liable for the payment of the amounts ordered
to be paid under subsection 51(1) in respect of the contravention or
reviewable conduct.

## [Consultation and Disclosure of Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-177469 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure by an organization

**[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsection 7(3)
of the [Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents
Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6), any organization to which Part 1 of that Act
applies may on its own initiative disclose to the Commission, the
Commissioner of Competition or the Privacy Commissioner any information
in its possession that it believes relates to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a contravention of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any of sections 6 to 9,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} section 52.01 of the [Competition
    Act](/eng/acts/C-34) or, in respect of conduct carried out by
    electronic means, section 52, 52.1, 53, 55 or 55.1 of that Act,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} section 5 of the [Personal Information Protection
    and Electronic Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6), which contravention
    relates to a collection or use described in subsection 7.1(2) or (3)
    of that Act, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any prohibition or requirement of the Commission,
    in respect of conduct carried out by electronic means, under section
    41 of the [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} conduct that is reviewable under section 74.011 of
  the [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34) or, in respect of conduct
  carried out by electronic means, under section 74.01, 74.02, 74.04,
  74.05 or 74.06 of that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** The Commission, the
Commissioner of Competition and the Privacy Commissioner must consult
with each other to the extent that they consider appropriate to ensure
the effective regulation, under this Act, the [Competition
Act](/eng/acts/C-34), the [Personal Information Protection and
Electronic Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6) and the [Telecommunications
Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4), of commercial conduct that discourages the use of
electronic means to carry out commercial activities, and to coordinate
their activities under those Acts as they relate to the regulation of
that type of conduct.

    Commission believes that the information relates to the performance
    or exercise of the Commissioner of Competition's duties or powers
    related to section 52.01 or 74.011 of the [Competition

    electronic means, to section 52, 52.1, 53, 55, 55.1, 74.01, 74.02,

  Competition

      [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4).

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An agreement or arrangement referred to in
  subsection (1) must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} restrict the use of the information to purposes

    contravention of the laws of the foreign state that address conduct
    referred to in any of subparagraphs (1)(a)(i) to (iv); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} stipulate that the information be treated in a
    confidential manner and not be further disclosed without the express
    consent of the person responsible for disclosing the information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction --- arrangements

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The only information that may be disclosed under
  subsection (1) is information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtained in the course of assisting an
    investigation or proceeding in respect of a contravention of the

    similar to conduct referred to in any of subparagraphs (1)(a)(i) to
    (iv); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} obtained in the course of the activities described
    in any of subparagraphs (1)(b)(i) to (iv).

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-177563 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing business or non-business
relationships

**[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** A person's consent to
receiving commercial electronic messages from another person is implied
until the person gives notification that they no longer consent to
receiving such messages from that other person or until three years
after the day on which section 6 comes into force, whichever is earlier,
if, when that section comes into force,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} those persons have an existing business relationship
  or an existing non-business relationship, as defined in subsection
  10(10) or (13), respectively, without regard to the period mentioned
  in that subsection; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the relationship includes the communication between
  them of commercial electronic messages.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Software updates and upgrades

**[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** If a computer program was
installed on a person's computer system before section 8 comes into
force, the person's consent to the installation of an update or upgrade
to the program is implied until the person gives notification that they
no longer consent to receiving such an installation or until three years
after the day on which section 8 comes into force, whichever is earlier.
