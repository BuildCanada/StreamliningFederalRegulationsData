## Lieutenant Governors Superannuation Act {#lieutenant-governors-superannuation-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. L-8
:::

An Act to provide for the payment of superannuation benefits to
Lieutenant Governors
::::

:::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345479 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Lieutenant Governors Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/L-8).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 73, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345484 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[contributor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contributor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Lieutenant Governor who
    is required by subsection 4(1) to contribute to the Consolidated
    Revenue Fund and includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Lieutenant Governor who has, pursuant to
      subsection 4(2), ceased to be required so to contribute, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a Lieutenant Governor who has ceased to hold
      office as the lieutenant governor of a province and has become
      entitled to an immediate pension or a deferred pension under this
      Act; ([contributeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[deferred pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deferred pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pension that
    becomes payable to a person at the time he or she reaches sixty
    years of age; ([pension différée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[disabled]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[disabled]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to a contributor,
    means afflicted with a permanent infirmity that renders the
    contributor incapable of performing the duties and functions of his
    office or incapable of pursuing regularly any substantially gainful
    occupation commensurate with his
    qualifications; ([invalide]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[immediate pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[immediate pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pension that
    becomes payable to a person immediately on his becoming entitled
    thereto; ([pension immédiate]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Lieutenant Governor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Lieutenant Governor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who holds
    office as the lieutenant governor of a province on or after December
    2, 1975; ([lieutenant-gouverneur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who was married

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to a Lieutenant Governor or former Lieutenant
        Governor immediately before the death of the Lieutenant Governor
        or former Lieutenant Governor, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a former Lieutenant Governor,
        to him or her immediately before the time when he or she ceased
        to be a Lieutenant Governor; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who establishes that the person was
      cohabiting in a relationship of a conjugal nature

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} with a Lieutenant Governor or former Lieutenant
        Governor for at least one year immediately before the death of
        the Lieutenant Governor or former Lieutenant Governor, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a former Lieutenant Governor,
        with him or her immediately before he or she ceased to be a
        Lieutenant Governor. ([survivant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. L-8, s. 2
- 2000, c. 12, s. 170
- 2002, c. 17, s. 17
:::

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Superannuation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345503 .Part}

### [Benefits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-345504 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pension to Lieutenant Governor

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  contributor who has contributed under this Act in respect of five
  years of service as the lieutenant governor of a province is, subject
  to this Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} entitled on ceasing to hold office as the
    lieutenant governor of a province,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if he or she has reached the age of sixty years
      or is disabled, to an immediate pension calculated in accordance
      with subsection (2), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if he or she has not reached the age of sixty
      years, subject to subsection (3), to

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a deferred pension, calculated in accordance
        with subsection (2), or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a return of the total contributions made by him
        or her under this Part, together with interest, if any,
        calculated pursuant to subsection (5),

      at his or her option; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entitled, if he or she becomes disabled not having
    reached sixty years of age but having become entitled to a deferred
    pension, to an immediate pension calculated in accordance with
    subsection (2), in which case he or she ceases to be entitled to a
    deferred pension referred to in clause (a)(ii)(A).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of pension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this Act, the pension to which a
  contributor is entitled under this section is three-tenths of the
  average salary received by him during the last five years of his
  service as the lieutenant governor of a province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Option deemed to be exercised

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where under subsection (1) a contributor is entitled
  to a deferred pension or a return of contributions at his option and
  he fails to exercise the option within six months from the day on
  which he ceases to hold office as the lieutenant governor of a
  province, he shall be deemed to have exercised the option in favour of
  a deferred pension.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of contributions to Lieutenant
  Governor on ceasing to hold office

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every contributor who, on ceasing to hold office as
  the lieutenant governor of a province, is not entitled to a pension
  under subsection (1) is entitled, on ceasing to hold that office, to a
  return of the total contributions made by him under this Part,
  together with interest, if any, calculated pursuant to subsection (5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest on payment and amount of
  contributions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} When, at any time after December 31, 1975, a
  contributor or his or her survivor, estate or succession becomes
  entitled, pursuant to subsection (1) or (4) or section 8 or 9, to be
  paid any amount of the contributions made by the contributor under
  this Part, the President of the Treasury Board shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine the total amount of contributions that
    have been made under this Part by the contributor during each year,
    in this subsection called a "contribution year", in which
    contributions were made by the contributor; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} calculate interest at the rate of four per cent
    compounded annually on the aggregate amount in respect of each
    contribution year from December 31 of that year to December 31 of
    the year immediately preceding the year in which the contributor
    ceased to hold office as the lieutenant governor of a province.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. L-8, s. 3
- 2000, c. 12, s. 171
- 2002, c. 17, s. 18
:::

### [Contributions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-345527 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lieutenant Governor's contribution

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  Lieutenant Governor shall, by reservation from his salary, contribute
  to the Consolidated Revenue Fund six per cent of his salary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contributions not required

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything in this Part, no contributor
  shall contribute as required by subsection (1) after he has
  contributed under this Act in respect of service as the lieutenant
  governor of a province totalling five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), the amount contributed by a Lieutenant Governor
  pursuant to subsection (1) shall be deemed to be contributed to or
  under a registered pension plan.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. L-8, s. 4
- 1999, c. 31, s. 242
:::

### [Election not to Contribute]{.HTitleText2} {#h-345537 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election not to contribute under section
4

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  Lieutenant Governor may, in writing, elect, within six months after
  his appointment as the lieutenant governor of a province, not to
  contribute pursuant to section 4 and on so electing he shall,
  notwithstanding that section, not be required to contribute pursuant
  to that section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of contributions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Lieutenant Governor who has made an election under
  this section and who has made any contributions pursuant to section 4
  or 13 is entitled to a return of the total amount of those
  contributions, without interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election irrevocable

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An election made under this section is irrevocable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When sections 3, 4, 7, 8 and 8.1 do not
  apply

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Sections 3 and 4 do not apply to a Lieutenant
  Governor who has made an election under this section and sections 7, 8
  and 8.1 do not apply to the survivor of a Lieutenant Governor who has
  made an election under this section.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. L-8, s. 5
- 2000, c. 12, s. 172
:::

### [Diversion of Payments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-345549 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Diversion to satisfy financial support
order

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When any
  court in Canada of competent jurisdiction has made an order requiring
  a former Lieutenant Governor to pay financial support, amounts payable
  to the former Lieutenant Governor under this Part are subject to being
  diverted to the person named in the order in accordance with Part II
  of the [Garnishment, Attachment and Pension Diversion
  Act](/eng/acts/G-2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment deemed to be to pension
  recipient

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Part, any payment made
  pursuant to subsection (1) shall be deemed to have been made to the
  former lieutenant governor in respect of whom the payment was made.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. L-8, s. 6
- 2000, c. 12, s. 173
:::

### [Surviving Spouse]{.HTitleText2} {#h-345557 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pension of surviving spouse

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  contributor who has ceased to hold office as the lieutenant governor
  of a province but who is entitled to be paid an immediate pension or a
  deferred pension under section 3 dies, his surviving spouse, if that
  person was his spouse at the time he ceased to hold office as the
  lieutenant governor of a province, shall be paid a pension equal to
  one-half of the immediate pension or deferred pension to which the
  contributor was entitled under that section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a contributor who has, pursuant to subsection
  4(2), ceased to be required to contribute pursuant to subsection 4(1)
  dies while holding office as the lieutenant governor of a province,
  his surviving spouse shall be paid a pension equal to one-half of the
  immediate pension or deferred pension to which the contributor would
  have become entitled under section 3 had he, immediately prior to his
  death, ceased, for any reason other than death, to hold office as the
  lieutenant governor of the province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement of pension to surviving
  spouse

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A pension that is payable under this section to the
  surviving spouse of a contributor commences to be payable immediately
  after the death of the contributor.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 73, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of contributions to surviving
spouse

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where a contributor dies
while holding office as the lieutenant governor of a province and his
surviving spouse is not entitled to a pension under section 7, his
surviving spouse shall be paid the total amount of the contributions
made by the contributor under this Part, together with interest, if any,
calculated pursuant to subsection 3(5).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 73, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election for former Lieutenant Governor

- **[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the person to whom a former Lieutenant Governor is married or with
  whom he or she is cohabiting in a relationship of a conjugal nature,
  having so cohabited for a period of at least one year, would not be
  entitled to a pension under section 7 in the event of the former
  Lieutenant Governor's death, the former Lieutenant Governor may make
  an election, in accordance with the regulations, to reduce the amount
  of the pension to which he or she is entitled in order that the person
  could become entitled to a pension under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person referred to in subsection (1) is entitled to
  a pension in an amount determined in accordance with the election and
  the regulations if the former Lieutenant Governor dies and the
  election is not revoked or deemed to have been revoked, and the person
  was married to the former Lieutenant Governor at the time of his or
  her death, or was cohabiting with the former Lieutenant Governor in a
  relationship of a conjugal nature for a period of at least one year
  immediately before his or her death.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No entitlement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who is entitled to receive a pension under
  section 7 after the former Lieutenant Governor's death is not entitled
  to a pension under subsection (2) in respect of that former Lieutenant
  Governor.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 12, s. 174
- 2004, c. 16, s. 18
:::

### [Death Benefit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-345580 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Residual amounts

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Where, on the death of a
contributor, there is no surviving spouse to whom a pension or return of
contributions under this Act may be paid, or where a person who is
entitled to a pension under this Act as the surviving spouse of a
contributor dies, any amount by which the total amount of the
contributions made by the contributor under this Part, together with
interest, if any, calculated pursuant to subsection 3(5), exceeds the
total amount paid to the contributor and his surviving spouse under this
Part shall be paid, as a death benefit, to his estate or, if less than
one thousand dollars, as the President of the Treasury Board may direct.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 73, s. 10
:::

### [Payments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-345585 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of C.R.F.

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  pensions and returns of contributions payable under this Act shall be
  paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of pension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a pension becomes payable under this Act, it
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be paid in equal monthly instalments in arrears;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (c), be paid [pro
    rata]{.otherLang lang="la"} for any period of less than a month; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} continue, subject to this Act, during the lifetime
    of the recipient and thereafter until the end of the month in which
    the recipient dies.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 73, s. 8
:::

### [Regulations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-345596 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the terms on which a contributor may pay
  for any period of service in instalments and the method of determining

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amounts to be reserved from any amount payable
    to a contributor by Her Majesty in right of Canada, including any
    pension payable to him under this Act, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amounts to be recovered from any pension
    payable to the survivor of a contributor under this Act

  in respect of unpaid instalments;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the medical examination to be required to
  determine whether a contributor is disabled;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing forms for the purposes of this Act; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the election that may be made under
  section 8.1, including regulations respecting

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the time, manner and circumstances in which an
    election may be made, revoked or deemed to have been revoked;

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the reduction to be made in the amount of a former
    Lieutenant Governor's pension when an election is made;

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the pension to be paid under
    subsection 8.1(2); and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any other matter that the Governor in Council
    considers necessary for carrying out the purposes and provisions of
    section 8.1.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. L-8, s. 11
- 2000, c. 12, s. 175
:::

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Supplementary Retirement Benefits Contributions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-345612 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of "Supplementary Retirement
Benefits Account"

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,
[[Supplementary Retirement Benefits Account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
the Account established in the accounts of Canada pursuant to the
[Supplementary Retirement Benefits Act](/eng/acts/S-24).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 73, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contributions

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Every contributor, except
a contributor who has ceased to hold office as the lieutenant governor
of a province, is required to contribute to the Supplementary Retirement
Benefits Account, by reservation from his salary,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prior to 1977, an amount equal to one-half of one per
  cent of his salary; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} commencing with the month of January, 1977, an amount
  equal to one per cent of his salary.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 73, s. 13
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3)

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
[Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), the amount contributed by a
contributor pursuant to section 13 shall be deemed to be contributed to
or under a registered pension plan.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. L-8, s. 14
- 1999, c. 31, s. 243
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount to be credited to S.R.B. Account

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Where any amount is paid
into the Supplementary Retirement Benefits Account pursuant to section
13, an amount equal to the amount so paid shall be credited to that
Account.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 73, s. 15
:::
