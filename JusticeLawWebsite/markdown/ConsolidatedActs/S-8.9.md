## Shared Services Canada Act {#shared-services-canada-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2012, c. 19, s. 711
:::

Assented to 2012-06-29

An Act to establish Shared Services Canada

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 711 of chapter 19 of the Statutes of Canada, 2012,
in force on assent June 29, 2012.\]
:::
:::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Recognizing that

the Government of Canada wishes to stand­ardize and consolidate, within a
single shared services entity, certain administrative services that
support government institutions; and

doing so will enable those services to be provided more effectively and
will support the efficient use of public money;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-446389 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Shared Services Canada Act](/eng/acts/S-8.9).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-446392 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Crown corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Crown corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 83(1) of the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11). ([société d'État]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11). ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[President]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[President]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the President of Shared
    Services Canada appointed under subsection
    10(1). ([président]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Designation of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-446398 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of Governor in Council

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
designate a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada to be the
Minister for the purposes of this Act.

## [Establishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-446401 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A department is
established, called Shared Services Canada, for the purpose of assisting
the Minister in providing the services specified under section 6.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister presides over
Shared Services Canada and has the management and direction of it.

## [Powers, Duties and Functions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-446406 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Governor in Council

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
specify

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the services that the Minister must provide through
  Shared Services Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the services that the Minister may provide through
  Shared Services Canada;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the departments that must obtain a service that is
  specified under paragraph (a) exclusively from the Minister through
  Shared Services Canada and that are not permitted to meet their
  requirement for that service internally;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the departments and Crown corporations to which the
  Minister is permitted to provide services through Shared Services
  Canada;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the departments and Crown corporations to which the
  Minister is not permitted to provide services through Shared Services
  Canada; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} terms and conditions respecting the provision of the
  specified services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, for the
purpose of providing services under this Act, exercise any of the
powers, or perform any of the duties or functions, that are set out in
paragraph 6(a), (b), (c) or (g) of the [Department of Public Works and
Government Services Act](/eng/acts/P-38.2) in respect of departments,
Crown corporations, persons, organizations and governments to which
those services are provided.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation --- appropriate Minister

- **[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of providing services under this Act to a department
  through Shared Services Canada, the Minister may delegate any of his
  or her powers under section 7 in respect of that department, for any
  period and under any terms and conditions that he or she considers
  suitable, to that department's appropriate Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation --- chief executive

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of providing services under this Act,
  through Shared Services Canada, to a department over which the
  Minister does not preside but for which he or she is the appropriate
  Minister, the Minister may delegate any of his or her powers under
  section 7 in respect of that department, for any period and under any
  terms and conditions that he or she considers suitable, to that
  department's chief executive.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subdelegation --- chief executive

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate Minister for a department may,
  subject to and in accordance with the delegation under subsection (1),
  subdelegate to the chief executive of that department, for any period
  and under any terms and conditions that the appropriate Minister
  considers suitable, the power that was delegated to him or her under
  that subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subdelegation --- subordinate

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The chief executive of a department may, subject to
  and in accordance with the delegation under subsection (2) or the
  subdelegation under subsection (3), subdelegate to any person under
  his or her jurisdiction, for any period and under any terms and
  conditions that the chief executive considers suitable, the power that
  was delegated or subdelegated, as the case may be, to him or her under
  that subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in this section.

  [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
      as in section 2 of the [Financial Administration
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11). ([ministre compétent]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[chief executive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[chief executive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a department named in Part I of
        Schedule VI to the [Financial Administration
        Act](/eng/acts/F-11), its deputy minister;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a department named in Part II
        or III of that Schedule, the person occupying the position set
        out opposite that name; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a department that is not named
        in that Schedule, the chief executive officer, the deputy head
        or the person who occupies any other similar position, however
        called, in that department. ([administrateur
        principal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- 2017, c. 20, s. 113
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Governor in Council's approval

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may --- with
the Governor in Council's approval, given on a general or a specific
basis --- provide the services that are specified under paragraph 6(a)
or (b) through Shared Services Canada to any person, organization or
government, including a foreign government.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charging for services

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, subject
to any regulations that the Treasury Board makes for the purposes of
this section, charge for the services that are provided under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization

- **[[9.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite the Governor in Council
  having specified, under paragraph 6(c), that a department must obtain
  a service that is specified under paragraph 6(a) exclusively from the
  Minister through Shared Services Canada and is not permitted to meet
  its requirement for that service internally, the Minister may, if the
  Minister considers that exceptional circumstances justify it, and
  under any terms and conditions that the Minister specifies,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} authorize that department to obtain part of that
    service other than from the Minister through Shared Services Canada,
    including by meeting its requirement for that part of the service
    internally; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} authorize that department to obtain all of that
    service in respect of one or more portions of the department --- but
    not all of the department --- other than from the Minister through
    Shared Services Canada, including by meeting its requirement for
    that service internally.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power exercised personally

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must personally exercise the power that
  is set out in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Instructions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A department that is authorized, under subsection
  (1), to obtain all or part of a service other than from the Minister
  through Shared Services Canada, including by meeting its requirement
  for that service or that part of the service internally, must do so in
  accordance with any instructions that the Minister provides.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2017, c. 20, s. 114
:::

## [Organization and Head Office]{.HTitleText1} {#h-446452 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council is to appoint a President of Shared Services
  Canada to hold office during pleasure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Executive Vice-president

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may appoint an Executive
  Vice-president of Shared Services Canada to hold office during
  pleasure.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President is the deputy head of Shared Services Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Executive Vice-president

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Executive Vice-president is to act as President
  if that office is vacant or if the President is absent or
  incapacitated.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The President and the
Executive Vice-president are to be paid the remuneration that is fixed
by the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The head office of Shared
Services Canada is to be in the National Capital Region that is
described in the schedule to the [National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4).

## [Human Resources]{.HTitleText1} {#h-446467 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of employees

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The employees that are
necessary for the conduct of Shared Services Canada's work are to be
appointed in accordance with the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-446470 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Access to Information
Act](/eng/acts/A-1)

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
for the purposes of the [Access to Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1), the
records of other government institutions as defined in that Act or of
other organizations that are, on behalf of those institutions or
organizations, contained in or carried on Shared Services Canada's
information technology systems are not under the control of Shared
Services Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21)

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
for the purposes of the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21), personal
information that is collected by other government institutions as
defined in that Act or by other organizations and that is, on behalf of
those institutions or organizations, contained in or carried on Shared
Services Canada's information technology systems is not under the
control of Shared Services Canada.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-446475 .Part}

### [Definitions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-446476 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in sections 18 to 20.

[[former department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[former department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the portion of the
    federal public administration known as Shared Services
    Canada. ([ancien ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[new department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[new department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Shared Services Canada
    as established by section 4. ([nouveau ministère]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

### [Former Department]{.HTitleText2} {#h-446481 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  person occupying the position of President of the former department on
  the day on which this section comes into force becomes President of
  the new department on that day and is deemed to have been appointed
  under subsection 10(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act is to be construed as affecting
  the status of an employee who, immediately before the coming into
  force of this section, occupied a position in the former department,
  except that the employee is, on the coming into force of this section,
  to occupy that position in the new department.

- Definition of [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (2), [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  has the same meaning as in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of appropriations

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  amount that is appropriated, for the fiscal year in which this section
  comes into force, by an appropriation Act based on the Estimates for
  that year for defraying the federal public administration's charges
  and expenses for the former department that is unexpended on the day
  on which this section comes into force is deemed, on that day, to be
  an amount appropriated for defraying the federal public
  administration's charges and expenses for the new department.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of powers, duties and
  functions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a power, duty or function is vested in or
  exercisable by the former department's President or an employee of the
  former department under any Act, order, rule or regulation, or any
  contract, lease, licence or other document, that power, duty or
  function is vested in or is exercisable by the new department's
  President or an employee of the new department.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  reference to the former department in any of the following is deemed
  to be a reference to the new department:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Schedule I to the [Access to Information
    Act](/eng/acts/A-1) under the heading "OTHER GOVERNMENT
    INSTITUTIONS";

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the schedule to the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any order of the Governor in Council made under
    subsection 3.2(2) of the [Access to Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any order of the Governor in Council made under
    subsection 3.1(2) of the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any order of the Governor in Council made under the
    definition [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1)
    of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} Schedules I.1, IV and VI to the [Financial
    Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy head

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designation of a person as deputy head of the
  former department in any of the following is deemed to be a
  designation of the President of the new department as deputy head of
  that department:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any order of the Governor in Council made under
    paragraph 29(e) of the [Canadian Security Intelligence Service
    Act](/eng/acts/C-23); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any order of the Governor in Council made under the
    definition [[deputy head]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1)
    of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).
