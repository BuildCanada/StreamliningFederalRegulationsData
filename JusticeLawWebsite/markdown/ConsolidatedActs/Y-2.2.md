## Yukon Environmental and Socio-economic Assessment Act {#yukon-environmental-and-socio-economic-assessment-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2003, c. 7
:::

Assented to 2003-05-13

An Act to establish a process for assessing the environmental and
socio-economic effects of certain activities in Yukon
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-472875 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Yukon Environmental and Socio-economic Assessment
Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.2).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-472878 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[assessment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[assessment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an evaluation by a
      designated office, a screening by the executive committee or a
      review by a panel of the Board. ([évaluation]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence, permit or
      other form of approval that is issued or given by

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Governor in Council, a government agency,
        an independent regulatory agency or a municipal government, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a first nation under its final agreement or a
        first nation law,

      but does not include an access order issued under the [Yukon
      Surface Rights Board Act](/eng/acts/Y-4.3) or a consent given by a
      first nation for access to settlement land in circumstances where
      an access order could be issued under that
      Act. ([autorisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Yukon Environmental and
      Socio-economic Assessment Board established by section
      8. ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[category B settlement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[category B settlement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means land that
      is, or is to be treated as, category B settlement land, as
      referred to in the definition [[settlement
      land]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}. ([terres désignées de catégorie
      B]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Council for Yukon
      Indians or any successor to it or, in the absence of a successor,
      the first nations named in the schedule to the [Yukon First
      Nations Land Claims Settlement
      Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.3). ([Conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[decision body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[decision body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a project,
      means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a first nation, if the project is to be located
        wholly or partly on its settlement land and

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the first nation has the power under the
          [Yukon First Nations Self-Government Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.6) or
          under its final agreement to issue an authorization that is
          required for the project to be undertaken,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the first nation is a proponent of the
          project, has the power to grant an interest in land that is
          required for the project to be undertaken or has received an
          application for financial assistance for the project, or

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} no decision document is required for the
          project from any federal agency or the territorial minister;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the territorial minister, if any territorial
        agency, municipal government or territorial independent
        regulatory agency

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has the power to issue an authorization that
          is required for the project to be undertaken,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a project to be located
          wholly or partly on non-settlement land, is a proponent of the
          project, has the power to grant an interest in land that is
          required for the project to be undertaken or has received an
          application for financial assistance for the project, or

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is responsible for the administration of
          mines and minerals in category B or fee simple settlement land
          or Tetlit Gwich'in Yukon land, where the project involves a
          right to work those mines and minerals;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any federal agency that

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has the power to issue an authorization that
          is required for the project to be undertaken,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a project to be located
          wholly or partly on non-settlement land, is a proponent of the
          project, has the power to grant an interest in land that is
          required for the project to be undertaken or has received an
          application for financial assistance for the project, or

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is responsible for the administration of
          mines and minerals in category B or fee simple settlement land
          or Tetlit Gwich'in Yukon land, where the project involves a
          right to work those mines and minerals;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the federal minister, if the project is to be
        located wholly or partly on non-settlement land, no other
        federal agency is a decision body under paragraph (c) and

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the territorial minister is not a decision
          body, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the territorial minister is a decision body
          and

          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the Governor in Council has the power to
            issue an authorization that is required for the project to
            be undertaken, or

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a federal independent regulatory agency is
            a proponent of the project, has the power to issue such an
            authorization or has received an application for financial
            assistance for the project; or

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the federal minister, if the project is to be
        located wholly on settlement land and

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Governor in Council or a federal
          independent regulatory agency has the power to issue an
          authorization that is required for the project to be
          undertaken, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a federal independent regulatory agency has
          received an application for financial assistance for the
          project. ([décisionnaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[decision document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[decision document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a decision
      document issued by a decision body under section 75, 76 or
      77. ([décision écrite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an office
      maintained under subsection 22(1). ([bureau
      désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[effects monitoring]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[effects monitoring]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the monitoring of
      environmental and socio-economic effects, or of the effectiveness
      of mitigative measures. ([mesures de contrôle]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[environment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[environment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the components of the
      Earth and includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} air, land and water;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all layers of the atmosphere;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all organic and inorganic matter and living
        organisms; and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the interacting natural systems that include
        the components referred to in paragraphs (a) to
        (c). ([environnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[executive committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[executive committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the executive
      committee of the Board established by section 8. ([comité de
      direction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[existing project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[existing project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an activity that
      has been undertaken or completed and that, if proposed to be
      undertaken, would be subject to assessment under section
      47. ([ouvrage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[federal agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a minister of the
      federal government or a person or body carrying out a function of
      government under a federal law other than the [Yukon
      Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.01), the [Yukon First Nations Self-Government
      Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.6) or the [Yukon First Nations Land Claims
      Settlement Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.3), but does not include the
      Governor in Council, an independent regulatory agency or the Yukon
      Surface Rights Board. ([autorité fédérale]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[federal decision body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal decision body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a
      project, means a decision body referred to in any of
      paragraphs (c) to (e) of the definition [[decision
      body]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}. ([décisionnaire
      fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[federal independent regulatory agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal independent regulatory
      agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any body named in Part 1 of the
      schedule. ([organisme administratif autonome
      fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[federal minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
      Northern Affairs, unless another member of the Queen's Privy
      Council for Canada is designated by the Governor in Council as the
      federal minister for the purposes of this Act. ([ministre
      fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[fee simple settlement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fee simple settlement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means land that
      is, or is to be treated as, fee simple settlement land, as
      referred to in the definition [[settlement
      land]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}. ([terres désignées en fief
      simple]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[final agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[final agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a final agreement
      within the meaning of the [Yukon First Nations Land Claims
      Settlement Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.3) or the agreement contained in
      Appendix C to the Gwich'in Agreement. ([accord
      définitif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[financial assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[financial assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a payment, loan
      or loan guarantee, but does not include

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a refund, remission, reduction or deferral, or
        any other form of relief from the payment, of a tax, duty or
        fee, other than relief provided by law to permit the undertaking
        of an activity specifically named in the law;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} assistance for anything done preliminary to a
        project, such as a feasibility study, that does not have effects
        on the environment; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} assistance for an environmental or
        socio-economic study undertaken in relation to the assessment of
        a project, except a study that itself comprises a
        project. ([aide financière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[first nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[first nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Yukon First Nation,
      within the meaning of the Umbrella Final Agreement, and includes

  :   [[first nation law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a law enacted by a
      first nation in accordance with the [Yukon First Nations

      agreement. ([texte législatif d'une première
      nation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  :   [[government agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a federal agency

  [[joint panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[joint panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a joint panel

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Where, in relation to any
matter, a reference is made in this Act to consultation, the duty to
consult shall be exercised

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by providing, to the party to be consulted,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} notice of the matter in sufficient form and detail
    to allow the party to prepare its views on the matter,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a reasonable period for the party to prepare its
    views, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an opportunity to present its views to the party
    having the duty to consult; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by considering, fully and fairly, any views so
  presented.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final agreement prevails

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** In the event of an
inconsistency or conflict between a final agreement and this Act, the
agreement prevails to the extent of the inconsistency or conflict.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-472985 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of Act

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This Act
  gives effect to provisions of the Umbrella Final Agreement respecting
  assessment of environmental and socio-economic effects.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purposes of Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The purposes of this Act are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to provide a comprehensive, neutrally conducted
    assessment process applicable in Yukon;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to require that, before projects are undertaken,
    their environmental and socio-economic effects be considered;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to protect and maintain environmental quality and
    heritage resources;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to protect and promote the well-being of Yukon
    Indian persons and their societies and Yukon residents generally, as
    well as the interests of other Canadians;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} to ensure that projects are undertaken in
    accordance with principles that foster beneficial socio-economic
    change without undermining the ecological and social systems on
    which communities and their residents, and societies in general,
    depend;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} to recognize and, to the extent practicable,
    enhance the traditional economy of Yukon Indian persons and their
    special relationship with the wilderness environment;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} to guarantee opportunities for the participation of
    Yukon Indian persons --- and to make use of their knowledge and
    experience --- in the assessment process;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} to provide opportunities for public participation
    in the assessment process;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to ensure that the assessment process is conducted
    in a timely, efficient and effective manner that avoids duplication;
    and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} to provide certainty to the extent practicable with
    respect to assessment procedures, including information
    requirements, time limits and costs to participants.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The [Impact Assessment
Act](/eng/acts/I-2.75) does not apply in Yukon.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Yukon Environmental and Socio-economic Assessment Board and Designated Offices]{.HTitleText1} {#h-473008 .Part}

### [Yukon Environmental and Socio-economic Assessment Board]{.HTitleText2} {#h-473009 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board established

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Yukon Environmental and Socio-economic Assessment Board is hereby
  established, consisting of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an executive committee of three persons;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} four other members; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an even number of additional members that may be
    fixed from time to time by the federal minister following
    consultation with the territorial minister and the first nations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment by federal minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The federal minister shall appoint the members of the
  Board after seeking the views of the Minister of the Environment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Executive committee

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} One member of the executive committee referred to in
  paragraph (1)(a) shall be appointed on the nomination of the Council,
  another on the nomination of the territorial minister and the third,
  being the Chairperson of the Board, following consultation by the
  federal minister with the other two members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other members

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Of the four members of the Board referred to in
  paragraph (1)(b), two shall be appointed on the nomination of the
  Council and one on the nomination of the territorial minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional members

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If additional members of the Board are to be
  appointed pursuant to paragraph (1)(c), one-half shall be appointed on
  the nomination of the Council and one-half following consultation by
  the federal minister with the territorial minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation of first nations

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Council shall consult the first nations before
  nominating a person to the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Residence of Chairperson

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson of the Board must be a resident of Yukon.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Residency of majorities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A majority of the members appointed on the nomination
  of the Council, as well as a majority of the other members excluding
  the Chairperson, must be residents of Yukon.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  a member of the Board shall be appointed to hold office for a term of
  three years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of first members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The first members appointed under subsections 8(3)
  and (4) on the nomination of the Council or the territorial minister
  shall be appointed for the term, not exceeding three years, specified
  by the Council or territorial minister when making the nomination, and
  the other first members shall be appointed for the term, not exceeding
  three years, specified by the federal minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancy

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the event of a vacancy occurring during a member's
  term of office, the federal minister may, subject to the requirements
  of sections 8 and 9 applicable to the appointment of that member,
  appoint another person to fill the vacancy for the remainder of the
  term.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting after expiry of term

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a member is a member of the executive committee or
  of a panel of the Board and, while that committee or panel is
  conducting a screening or review of a project, their term expires
  before a decision document is issued by each decision body to whom the
  committee or panel has made a recommendation in respect of that
  project, they may --- in accordance with the by-laws of the Board or,
  in the absence of an applicable by-law, the direction of the
  Chairperson --- continue to perform their functions in relation to
  that screening or review until those decision documents are issued.
  For the purpose of the appointment of a replacement, the member's
  office is considered to be vacant as soon as their term expires.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member of the Board holds office during good behaviour but may be
  removed by the federal minister for cause or on any other ground set
  out in the by-laws of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loss of resident status

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The federal minister shall remove a member from
  office, in conformity with the regulations, if the federal minister
  determines that, because of a change in that member's place of
  residence, the requirements of section 9 are no longer met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation by federal minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before a member nominated by the Council or the
  territorial minister is removed from office, the federal minister
  shall consult the Council or the territorial minister, as the case may
  be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting after removal of member

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a member is a member of the executive committee or
  of a panel of the Board and, while that committee or panel is
  conducting a screening or review of a project, they are removed from
  office under subsection (2) before a decision document is issued by
  each decision body to whom the committee or panel has made a
  recommendation in respect of that project, they may --- in accordance
  with the by-laws of the Board or, in the absence of an applicable
  by-law, the direction of the Chairperson --- continue to perform their
  functions in relation to that screening or review until those decision
  documents are issued. For the purpose of the appointment of a
  replacement, the member's office is considered to be vacant as soon as
  they are removed from office.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Chairperson, a member
of the executive committee or any other member of the Board is eligible
to be reappointed to the Board in the same or a different capacity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member of the Board may not participate in any business of the Board,
  the executive committee or a panel of the Board, if doing so would
  place the member in a material conflict of interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Yukon Indian persons

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member is not in a material conflict of interest
  solely by virtue of being a Yukon Indian person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  member of the Board shall be paid the fees or other remuneration that
  is fixed by the federal minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each member is entitled to be reimbursed for any
  travel and living expenses incurred by the member in the course of
  performing duties under this Act, while absent from the member's
  ordinary place of residence, that are consistent with directives of
  the Treasury Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefits

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Board and its employees are deemed
  to be employees for the purposes of the [Government Employees
  Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and to be employed in the federal
  public administration for the purposes of any regulations made under
  section 9 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff and advisers

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Board may, in
accordance with the approved budget of the Board, employ any officers
and employees and engage the services of any agents, advisers and
consultants that are necessary for the proper conduct of the business of
the Board and of designated offices, fix the terms and conditions of
their employment or engagement and pay their remuneration.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnification

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The members and employees
of the Board shall be indemnified by the Board against all damages
awarded against them, any settlement paid by them with the approval of
the federal minister and all expenses reasonably incurred by them, in
respect of any claim arising out of their functions as members or
employees, if those functions were performed honestly and in good faith
with a view to the best interests of the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Main office

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The main office of the
Board shall be at Whitehorse or at any other place in Yukon that may be
designated by the Governor in Council.

### [Assessment Districts and Designated Offices]{.HTitleText2} {#h-473087 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment districts

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Following consultation by the
  federal minister with the territorial minister and the first nations,
  and in accordance with any agreement that may be concluded by those
  ministers with the first nations, the federal minister shall, by
  order, establish six contiguous assessment districts that together
  constitute the whole of Yukon.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change in number of districts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board recommends, on the basis of
  operational requirements, a change in the number of contiguous
  assessment districts that constitute Yukon, the federal minister
  shall, by order, change the number of assessment districts in
  accordance with the Board's recommendation, following consultation
  with the territorial minister and the first nations, or else provide
  written reasons to the Board for rejecting the recommendation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alteration of boundaries

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may, by order, alter the boundary between adjacent assessment
  districts, and shall do so where the federal minister changes the
  number of assessment districts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before altering the boundaries of assessment
  districts, the Board shall consult the designated offices for those
  assessment districts as well as the federal minister, the territorial
  minister, the Council and any first nation whose territory falls
  wholly or partly within those districts, and shall seek the views of
  residents and municipal governments of the communities located in
  those districts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 3, 5 and 11 of the [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22) do not apply in respect of an order altering
  boundaries, but the Board shall publish a notice of the order in the
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), in a periodical that, in
  the Board's opinion, has a large circulation in Yukon and, if any part
  of the affected assessment districts falls within the territory of the
  Tetlit Gwich'in, in a periodical distributed in the Gwich'in
  Settlement Area referred to in the Gwich'in Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated offices in named communities

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  federal minister shall name a community in each assessment district as
  the site of an office to be maintained by the Board and known as the
  designated office for that assessment district.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of designated community

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before any change is made in the community named as
  the site of a designated office for an assessment district, the
  federal minister shall consult the territorial minister, the Council
  and any first nation whose territory falls wholly or partly within
  that district, and shall seek the views of residents and municipal
  governments of the communities located in that district.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The federal minister shall publish a notice of the
  selection of a community, or of any change in the selection, in the
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), in a periodical that, in
  the federal minister's opinion, has a large circulation in Yukon and,
  if any part of the affected assessment district falls within the
  territory of the Tetlit Gwich'in, in a periodical distributed in the
  Gwich'in Settlement Area referred to in the Gwich'in Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  staff of each designated office shall be composed of employees of the
  Board assigned to that office by the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers in relation to evaluations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall authorize one or more members of the
  staff to exercise the powers of the designated office relating to
  evaluations, and those persons may delegate any of those powers to
  another member of the staff.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member of the staff of a designated office may not participate in any
  business of that office if doing so would place the member in a
  material conflict of interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Yukon Indian persons

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member of the staff is not in a material conflict
  of interest solely by virtue of being a Yukon Indian person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contracts and facilities

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall, at the
request of a designated office but in accordance with the approved
budget of the Board, procure services required by that office and make
property and facilities available to it.

### [Budgets and Reporting]{.HTitleText2} {#h-473119 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Budgets of designated offices

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Each designated office
shall annually, after consultation with any first nation whose territory
falls wholly or partly within its assessment district, prepare and
submit a budget for the ensuing fiscal year to the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Budget of Board

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall submit annually to the federal minister its budget for the
  ensuing fiscal year, which shall incorporate the budgets of the
  designated offices as submitted by them or as varied by the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of budget

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The federal minister may approve the budget as
  submitted by the Board, or with any variations that the federal
  minister may make after seeking the views of the Board, the
  territorial minister and the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Funding for languages and training

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall consider including in its annual
  budget funding in order to enable its members and employees to carry
  out their functions in their traditional languages and in order to
  provide its members and employees with training, including
  cross-cultural orientation and education, for the purpose of improving
  their ability to carry out their duties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accounts

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall maintain books of account and related records in
  accordance with accounting principles recommended by the Chartered
  Professional Accountants of Canada or its successor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consolidated financial statements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall, within the time after the end of
  each fiscal year that the federal minister specifies, prepare
  consolidated financial statements in respect of that fiscal year in
  accordance with the accounting principles referred to in
  subsection (1) and shall include in them any information or statements
  that are required in their support.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The accounts, financial statements and financial
  transactions of the Board shall be audited annually by the auditor of
  the Board and, where the federal minister requests, by the Auditor
  General of Canada. The auditor and, where applicable, the Auditor
  General of Canada shall, as soon as practicable, make a report of the
  audit to the Board and the federal minister.

### [Rules and By-Laws]{.HTitleText2} {#h-473140 .Subheading}

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the determination of the scope of a project;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of section 46, the participation

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the periods, for each step from the submission or

  panels

    purposes of subsection 50(3);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the conduct of screenings of projects by the

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reconsideration of recommendations referred

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the membership of panels of the Board and the
    establishment of their terms of reference; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} cooperation by the executive committee and panels

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the conduct of studies and research under section
    112.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Categories of projects, etc.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Rules made under this section may provide for
  different types of screenings or reviews for different categories of
  projects, existing projects or plans, or activities outside Yukon, as
  the case may be.

### [Assessments --- General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-473231 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-duplication and certainty

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** The Board, the designated
offices, the executive committee and panels of the Board shall avoid
duplication in the assessment process and shall provide certainty, to
the extent practicable, to persons participating in the assessment
process with respect to assessment procedures, including information
requirements, time limits and costs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timeliness of assessments

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** The designated offices,
the executive committee and panels of the Board shall conduct
assessments of projects, existing projects and plans in a timely and
expeditious manner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matters to be considered

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  conducting an assessment of a project or existing project, a
  designated office, the executive committee or a panel of the Board
  shall take the following matters into consideration:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the project or existing project;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all stages of the project or existing project;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the significance of any environmental or
    socio-economic effects of the project or existing project that have
    occurred or might occur in or outside Yukon, including the effects
    of malfunctions or accidents;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the significance of any adverse cumulative
    environmental or socio-economic effects that have occurred or might
    occur in connection with the project or existing project in
    combination with the effects of other projects for which proposals
    have been submitted under subsection 50(1) or any activities that
    have been carried out, are being carried out or are likely to be
    carried out in or outside Yukon;

  - [(d.1)]{.lawlabel} any studies or research undertaken under
    subsection 112(1) that are relevant to the project or existing
    project;

  - [(d.2)]{.lawlabel} the need for effects monitoring;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} alternatives to the project or existing project, or
    alternative ways of undertaking or operating it, that would avoid or
    minimize any significant adverse environmental or socio-economic
    effects;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} mitigative measures and measures to compensate for
    any significant adverse environmental or socio-economic effects;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the need to protect the rights of Yukon Indian
    persons under final agreements, the special relationship between
    Yukon Indian persons and the wilderness environment of Yukon, and
    the cultures, traditions, health and lifestyles of Yukon Indian
    persons and other residents of Yukon;

  - [(g.1)]{.lawlabel} the interests of first nations;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the interests of residents of Yukon and of Canadian
    residents outside Yukon;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any matter that a decision body has asked it to
    take into consideration; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} any matter specified by the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional matters to be considered

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the matters referred to in subsection
  (1), the executive committee or a panel of the Board shall take into
  consideration the capacity of any renewable resources that are likely
  to be significantly affected by the project or existing project to
  meet present and future needs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Potential activities of third parties

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} If the proponent is a government agency or first
  nation and the project or existing project concerns planning
  activities related to timber harvesting, the designated office,
  executive committee or panel of the Board shall take into
  consideration any potential activities of third parties that, if
  proposed to be undertaken, would be subject to an assessment under
  section 47 or 48.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration of standards

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a project or existing project to be assessed
  belongs to a class, or is to be located in a geographic area, for
  which standard mitigative measures have been developed, whether
  developed by the designated office or the executive committee, those
  measures shall be taken into consideration by the designated office,
  the executive committee or a panel of the Board when assessing the
  project or existing project.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Optional matters

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A designated office, the executive committee or a
  panel of the Board may also take into consideration any matter that it
  considers relevant in the assessment of a project or existing project.

    that the project be allowed to proceed without a review, if it
    determines that the project will not have significant adverse
    environmental or socio-economic effects in or outside Yukon;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} recommend to those decision bodies that the project
    be allowed to proceed without a review, subject to specified terms
    and conditions, if it determines that the project will have, or is
    likely to have, significant adverse environmental or socio-economic
    effects in or outside Yukon that can be mitigated by those terms and
    conditions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} recommend to those decision bodies that the project
    not be allowed to proceed and not be subject to a review, if it
    determines that the project will have, or is likely to have,
    significant adverse environmental or socio-economic effects in or
    outside Yukon that cannot be mitigated; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} require a review of the project, if, after taking
    into account any mitigative measures included in the project
    proposal, it cannot determine whether the project will have, or is
    likely to have, significant adverse environmental or socio-economic
    effects.

- [(1.1) to (1.3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2017, c. 34, s.
  4\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review required --- other cases

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Irrespective of any determination made under
  subsection (1), the executive committee shall require a review of the
  project if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it determines, after taking into account any
    mitigative measures included in the project proposal, that the
    project might contribute significantly to cumulative adverse
    environmental or socio-economic effects in Yukon or that the project
    is causing or is likely to cause significant public concern in
    Yukon; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it determines that the project involves technology
    that is controversial in Yukon or the effects of which are unknown.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A recommendation under paragraph (1)(a), (b) or (c)
  shall be made in writing with reasons and a copy provided to the
  proponent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of reasons for review

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the executive committee requires a review under
  paragraph (1)(d) or subsection (2), it shall provide written reasons
  for doing so to the proponent and any first nation consulted under
  subsection 50(3) as well as to any government agency, independent
  regulatory agency or first nation that has notified the executive
  committee of its interest in the project or in projects of that kind.

### [Request for Review]{.HTitleText2} {#h-473425 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of project

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  request for a review of a project may be made to the executive
  committee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by either the federal minister or the Minister of
    the Environment, if there is a federal decision body for the
    project;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by the territorial minister, if the territorial
    minister is a decision body for the project; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by a first nation with the consent of the federal
    minister and, if the territorial minister is a decision body for the
    project, with the consent of the territorial minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request for a review must be made jointly by the
  territorial minister and either the federal minister or the Minister
  of the Environment if the decision bodies for the project include both
  the territorial minister and a federal decision body.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A request for a review may not be made if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the executive committee has already required a
    review of the project under paragraph 58(1)(d) or subsection 58(2);
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a recommendation has already been made in respect
    of the project by a designated office or the executive committee and
    all decision bodies to which the recommendation was made have issued
    decision documents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of review

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A request shall specify whether a review is to be a
  public review or some other form of review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pending assessments

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a request is made for a review, any assessment
  of the project by a designated office or the executive committee or
  any consideration by a decision body of a recommendation made in
  respect of the project, as the case may be, shall be discontinued.

### [Panels of the Board]{.HTitleText2} {#h-473464 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of panel

- **[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a request is made under section 60 for a form of

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of the Environment does not direct the

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2015, c. 19, s. 20\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to enter into agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The executive committee shall establish a panel of

  entered into under paragraph 61(1)(b) for the establishment of a joint
  panel, no agreement is concluded under section 67.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination by executive committee

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before a panel of the Board is established, the
  executive committee shall determine whether the project is likely to
  have significant adverse environmental or socio-economic effects
  primarily on settlement land or on non-settlement land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of members

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The executive committee shall select the members of a
  panel of the Board for the review of a project from among the members
  of the Board, and shall designate one of them to be chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition of panels

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A panel of the Board shall be constituted as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the executive committee concludes under
    subsection (3) that effects are likely to occur primarily on
    settlement land, two thirds of the members of the panel must be
    members nominated to the Board by the Council and one third must be
    members, excluding the Chairperson of the Board, who are not so
    nominated;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the executive committee concludes under
    subsection (3) that effects are likely to occur primarily on
    non-settlement land, one third of the members of the panel must be
    members nominated to the Board by the Council and two thirds must be
    members, excluding the Chairperson of the Board, who are not so
    nominated; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, of the members of the panel
    other than its chairperson, one half must be members nominated to
    the Board by the Council and one half must be members who are not so
    nominated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attendance

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every member of a panel of the Board must be present
  at each meeting or hearing of the panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancies

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity, or a
  vacancy in the office, of a member of a panel of the Board, the
  executive committee shall, in a manner consistent with the proportions
  set out in subsection (5),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} direct some or all of the remaining members of the
    panel to resume the review;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} appoint another member to the panel and direct the
    panel to resume or recommence the review; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} appoint a new panel and direct the panel to
    recommence the review.

### [Agreements for Joint Panels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-473512 .Subheading}

- **[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister of the Environment agrees to a request made by the executive
  committee under paragraph 61(1)(b), the executive committee may, with
  the approval of the federal minister, enter into an agreement with the
  Minister of the Environment for the establishment of a joint panel to
  conduct a review of the project.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint panel agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In circumstances referred to in subsection 65(1), the
  executive committee may, with the approval of the federal minister,
  enter into an agreement for the purpose referred to in subsection (1)
  with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of the Environment; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any authority having power to examine the
    environmental or socio-economic effects of the project, or of an
    activity that is to be undertaken partly outside Yukon and of which
    the project forms part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of agreement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An agreement for the establishment of a joint panel
  must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide for the composition of the joint panel, the
    manner of appointment of its members and the selection of a
    chairperson;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contain the terms of reference of the joint panel
    and indicate how they may be modified;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} require the joint panel to take into consideration
    the matters set out in subsections 42(1) to (3) in its review and
    permit it to take into consideration any other matter that it
    considers relevant;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prescribe rules to be followed in the conduct of
    the review, including rules about the information to be provided by
    the proponent, the scheduling of the review, the quorum, the mode of
    participation of interested persons and the integration of
    scientific information, traditional knowledge and other information;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} name a person or body that consents to indemnify
    the members of the joint panel in accordance with subsection (5)
    and, if it is the Board, indicate that the federal minister concurs;
    and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} require that, on completion of the review, the
    joint panel submit a report to the decision bodies for the project
    and publish the report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The executive committee shall publish, in a
  periodical that, in its opinion, has a large circulation in Yukon,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a notice of the conclusion of an agreement for the
    establishment of a joint panel and the manner in which copies of the
    agreement may be obtained by the public; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a notice of any amendments made to the agreement
    and the manner in which copies of the amendments and the reasons for
    them may be obtained by the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnification of panel members

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The members of a joint panel shall be indemnified by
  the person or body referred to in paragraph (3)(e) for all damages
  awarded against them, for any settlement paid by them with the
  approval of the federal minister and that person or body and for all
  expenses reasonably incurred by them, in respect of any claim arising
  out of their functions as members that were carried out honestly and
  in good faith with a view to the best interests of the joint panel.

### [Reviews of Projects by Panels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-473543 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct of panel review

- **[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to its terms of reference
  and any specification referred to in subsection 60(4), a panel of the
  Board or a joint panel established to review a project may determine
  any matter that it considers appropriate for the conduct of the review
  and shall determine

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a schedule for the review;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information to be provided by the proponent;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manner of participation by first nations,
    residents of communities, the federal and territorial governments
    and interested persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination by panel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before commencing public hearings, a panel of the
  Board or a joint panel shall determine whether the project will be
  located, or might have significant adverse environmental or
  socio-economic effects, on the settlement land of a first nation or on
  non-settlement land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Location of hearings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A review of a project by a panel of the Board or a
  joint panel may include public hearings in any location chosen by the
  panel and, except in the case of a review requested under subsection
  60(4) that is not a public review, shall include public hearings

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a community within the territory of each first
    nation, other than the Tetlit Gwich'in, whose settlement land is
    identified under subsection (2) --- unless the panel and the first
    nation agree otherwise;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a community within the Gwich'in settlement area
    referred to in the Gwich'in Agreement, if Tetlit Gwich'in Yukon land
    is settlement land identified under subsection (2) --- unless the
    panel and the Gwich'in Tribal Council agree otherwise; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the community in Canada closest to the project,
    if non-settlement land is identified under subsection (2) --- unless
    another location in Canada is agreed on for that purpose by the
    panel, the proponent, the decision bodies for the project and any
    first nation whose settlement land is identified under subsection
    (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Combined public hearings

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A public hearing held by a panel of the Board or a
  joint panel for the purposes of the review of a project may be
  combined with a public hearing held by any other body in relation to
  the project

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with the approval of the executive committee, in
    the case of a panel of the Board; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with an agreement made under section
    67, in the case of a joint panel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial powers of panels

- **[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  panel of the Board or a joint panel has, for the purposes of a review
  of a project, the powers, rights and privileges of a superior court
  with respect to the attendance and examination of witnesses and the
  production and inspection of documents and other evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement by court process

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A summons issued or an order made by a panel of the
  Board or a joint panel under subsection (1) may be made a summons or
  order of a superior court by filing a certified copy of it with the
  registrar of the court and, when so made, is enforceable in the same
  manner as a summons or order of that court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement of panel review

- **[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  panel of the Board or a joint panel established to review a project
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} consider whether the applicable rules have been
    complied with and notify the proponent accordingly; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} commence the review as soon as possible after it
    notifies the proponent that, in its opinion, the applicable rules
    have been complied with.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A panel of the Board or a joint panel may seek any
  information or views that it believes relevant to its review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before making a recommendation under this section, a
  panel of the Board or a joint panel shall seek views about the
  project, and information that it believes relevant to the review, from
  any first nation consulted under subsection 50(3) or identified under
  subsection 66(3) or 68(1) and from any government agency, independent
  regulatory agency or first nation that has notified the executive
  committee of its interest in the project or in projects of that kind.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendations of panel

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} At the conclusion of its review, a panel of the Board
  or a joint panel shall recommend to the decision bodies for the
  project that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the project be allowed to proceed, if it determines
    that the project will have no significant adverse environmental or
    socio-economic effects in or outside Yukon;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the project be allowed to proceed, subject to
    specified terms and conditions, if it determines that the project
    will have, or is likely to have, significant adverse environmental
    or socio-economic effects in or outside Yukon that can be mitigated
    by those terms and conditions; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the project not be allowed to proceed, if it
    determines that the project will have, or is likely to have,
    significant adverse environmental or socio-economic effects in or
    outside Yukon that cannot be mitigated.

  6\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other recipient of recommendation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A recommendation of a panel of the Board or a joint
  panel shall be made in writing with reasons and a copy provided to the

### [Implementation of Decision Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-473649 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Federal agencies

- **[[82]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-82 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  federal agency that is a decision body for a project shall not
  undertake the project, require that it be undertaken or take any
  action that would enable it to be undertaken until it has issued a
  decision document under section 75, 76 or 77 allowing the project to
  be undertaken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementing decision document

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding the limitations in any other federal
  law, a federal agency undertaking a project, requiring it to be
  undertaken or taking any action that enables it to be undertaken shall
  implement a decision document issued by it in respect of the project.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Territorial agencies and municipal
governments

- **[[83]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-83 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the territorial minister is a decision body for a project, no
  territorial agency or municipal government shall undertake the
  project, require that it be undertaken or take any action that would
  enable it to be undertaken until the territorial minister has issued a
  decision document under section 75, 76 or 77 allowing the project to
  be undertaken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementing decision document

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} To the extent of its authority under the [Yukon
  Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.01), territorial laws or municipal by-laws, every
  territorial agency and every municipal government undertaking a
  project, requiring it to be undertaken or taking any action that
  enables it to be undertaken shall implement a decision document issued
  by the territorial minister in respect of the project.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First nations

- **[[84]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-84 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  first nation that is a decision body for a project shall not undertake
  the project, require that it be undertaken or take any action that
  would enable it to be undertaken until it has issued a decision
  document under section 75, 76 or 77 allowing the project to be
  undertaken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementing decision document

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} To the extent of its authority under the [Yukon First
  Nations Self-Government Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.6), first nation laws or
  its final agreement, a first nation undertaking a project, requiring
  it to be undertaken or taking any action that enables it to be
  undertaken shall implement a decision document issued by it in respect
  of the project.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision document relating to mines

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (2), if a project involves
  a right to work mines and minerals in category B or fee simple
  settlement land or Tetlit Gwich'in Yukon land, a first nation taking
  any action that enables the project to be undertaken shall implement

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a decision document issued in respect of the
    project by the territorial minister to the extent that it is
    inconsistent with the first nation's decision document, where the
    Commissioner of Yukon has the administration and control of those
    mines and minerals; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a decision document issued in respect of the
    project by a federal agency to the extent that it is inconsistent
    with the first nation's decision document, where the federal agency
    has the administration of those mines and minerals.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Water licences

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} set terms of such rights that conflict with such a

### [Existing Projects]{.HTitleText2} {#h-473752 .Subheading}

Definition of [[administrative authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[94]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-94 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  sections 95 to 101, [[administrative authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
  in relation to a review of an existing project, means a government
  agency, an independent regulatory agency, a municipal government or a
  first nation that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is the operator of the existing project,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has the power to assume the operation of or to shut
    down the existing project, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has the power to amend, suspend or revoke an
    authorization that was issued, or to modify, suspend or withdraw an
    interest in land that was granted, to enable the existing project to
    be undertaken or completed,

  and includes the Governor in Council, if the Governor in Council has a
  power referred to in paragraph (b) or (c).

- Definition of [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (1) and sections 95 to 101,
  [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an existing project,
  means the person or body responsible for its operation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for panel review

- **[[95]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-95 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  executive committee shall establish a panel of the Board to conduct a
  review of an existing project, of a proposed abandonment,
  decommissioning or temporary shutdown of an existing project, or of a
  proposed significant change to an existing project other than a change
  that is subject to assessment under any other provision of this Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the request of the federal minister, if there is
    an administrative authority for the existing project that is a
    federal agency or federal independent regulatory authority;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the request of the territorial minister, if
    there is an administrative authority for the existing project that
    is established by or under the [Yukon Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.01); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at the request of a first nation and with the
    consent of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the federal minister, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the territorial minister, where a territorial
      agency, a municipal government or a territorial independent
      regulatory agency is an administrative authority for the existing
      project.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request for a review must be made jointly by the
  federal and territorial ministers if there are administrative
  authorities described in paragraphs (1)(a) and (b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent of first nation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a first nation is an administrative authority for
  an existing project, a request for a review by the federal or
  territorial minister may only be made with the first nation's consent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of review

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A request for a review shall specify whether the
  review is to be a public review or some other form of review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discontinuance

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If any minister or first nation that requested a
  review withdraws the request, the panel conducting the review shall
  discontinue it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of members

- **[[96]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-96 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  executive committee shall select the members of a panel of the Board,
  including its chairperson, from among the members of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination by executive committee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before the executive committee establishes a panel of
  the Board, it shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the request is for the review of the existing
    project, determine whether the existing project is having or might
    subsequently have significant adverse environmental or
    socio-economic effects primarily on settlement land of a first
    nation or on non-settlement land; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the request is for the review of a proposed
    abandonment, decommissioning, temporary shutdown or significant
    change, determine whether it might have significant adverse
    environmental or socio-economic effects primarily on settlement land
    of a first nation or on non-settlement land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition of panels

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A panel of the Board shall be constituted as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the executive committee concludes under
    subsection (2) that effects are occurring or might occur primarily
    on settlement land, two thirds of the members of the panel must be
    members nominated to the Board by the Council and one third must be
    members, excluding the Chairperson of the Board, who are not so
    nominated;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the executive committee concludes under
    subsection (2) that effects are occurring or might occur primarily
    on non-settlement land, one third of the members of the panel must
    be members nominated to the Board by the Council and two thirds must
    be members, excluding the Chairperson of the Board, who are not so
    nominated; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, of the members of the panel
    other than its chairperson, one half must be members nominated to
    the Board by the Council and one half must be members who are not so
    nominated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attendance

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every member of a panel of the Board must be present
  at each meeting or hearing of the panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancies

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity, or a
  vacancy in the office, of a member of a panel of the Board, the
  executive committee shall, in a manner consistent with the proportions
  set out in subsection (3),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} direct some or all of the remaining members of the
    panel to resume the review;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} appoint another member to the panel and direct the
    panel to resume or recommence the review; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} appoint a new panel and direct it to recommence the
    review.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms of reference

- **[[97]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-97
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any specification as to
  the form of review referred to in subsection 95(4), the executive
  committee shall set the terms of reference of the panel of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The executive committee shall publish, in a
  periodical that in its opinion has a large circulation in Yukon, a
  notice of the establishment of a panel of the Board and of the manner
  in which the public may obtain copies of the panel's terms of
  reference.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Modification of terms of reference

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a panel's terms of reference are modified, the
  executive committee shall publish in a periodical referred to in
  subsection (2) a notice of the manner in which the public may obtain
  copies of the modification and of the reasons for it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution of terms of reference

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The executive committee shall provide copies of the
  terms of reference, and of any modification made to them with the
  reasons for the modification, to the operator of the existing project,
  to any minister or first nation that requested or consented to the
  review, to any first nation identified under subsection 96(2) and to
  any government agency, independent regulatory agency or first nation
  that has notified the executive committee of its interest in the
  existing project or in existing projects of that kind.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement of review

- **[[98]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-98 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  panel of the Board established under section 95 shall commence its
  review as soon as possible after it notifies the operator and the
  administrative authority that, in its opinion, the applicable rules
  have been complied with.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to require additional information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A panel of the Board may, before or after commencing
  its review, require the operator to provide any supplementary
  information that it considers necessary for the review.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial powers of panels

- **[[99]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-99 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  panel of the Board has, for the purposes of its review, the powers,
  rights and privileges of a superior court with respect to the
  attendance and examination of witnesses and the production and
  inspection of documents and other evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement by court process

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A summons issued or order made by a panel of the
  Board under subsection (1) may be made a summons or order of a
  superior court by filing a certified copy of it with the registrar of
  the court and, when so made, is enforceable in the same manner as a
  summons or order of that court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preliminary determination

- **[[100]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-100 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  panel of the Board established pursuant to a request that specified a
  public review shall determine whether the existing project is located
  on the settlement land of a first nation or on non-settlement land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination by panel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A panel of the Board referred to in subsection (1)
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the request is for the review of the existing
    project, determine whether the existing project is having, or might
    subsequently have, significant adverse environmental or
    socio-economic effects on settlement land of a first nation or on
    non-settlement land; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the request is for the review of a proposed
    abandonment, decommissioning, temporary shutdown or significant
    change, determine whether it might have significant adverse
    environmental or socio-economic effects on settlement land of a
    first nation or on non-settlement land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Location of hearings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A review by a panel of the Board established under
  section 95 may include public hearings in any location chosen by the
  panel and, where the request under that section is for a public
  review, shall include public hearings in the following locations:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a community within the territory of each first
    nation, other than the Tetlit Gwich'in, whose settlement land is
    identified under subsection (1) or (2) --- unless the panel and the
    first nation agree otherwise;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a community within the Gwich'in settlement area
    referred to in the Gwich'in Agreement, if Tetlit Gwich'in Yukon land
    is settlement land identified under subsection (1) or (2) --- unless
    the panel and the Gwich'in Tribal Council agree otherwise; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the community in Canada closest to the existing
    project, if non-settlement land is identified under subsection (1)
    or (2) --- unless another location in Canada is agreed on for that
    purpose by the panel, the operator, the decision bodies for the
    project and any first nation whose settlement land is identified
    under subsection (1) or (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendations of panel

- **[[101]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-101 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  panel of the Board established under section 95 shall make a written
  report of its review to the minister or first nation that requested
  the review, in which the panel may make any recommendation based on
  the review that it considers to be appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the report of a panel of the Board shall be
  provided to the operator of the existing project, every administrative
  authority in respect of the existing project and any minister that
  consented to the request for the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration of recommendations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The minister or first nation that requested a review
  shall give full and fair consideration to any recommendations made by
  a panel of the Board and shall provide a written response to the Board
  describing any action that they plan to take in response to the
  report.

### [Plans]{.HTitleText2} {#h-473835 .Subheading}

Definition of [[originator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[102]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-102 .sectionLabel}** In sections 103 to 109,
[[originator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the government agency, first
nation or other body by or for which a plan is prepared.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for panel review

- **[[103]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-103 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the executive committee considers that the implementation of a plan
  might have significant adverse environmental or socio-economic effects
  in Yukon, it may establish a panel of the Board to conduct a review of
  the plan

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the request of the federal minister, if a
    federal agency is the originator;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the request of the territorial minister, if a
    territorial agency or municipal government is the originator; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at the request of a first nation, and either at the
    first nation's expense or with the consent of the federal minister
    and, where a territorial agency or municipal government is the
    originator, the consent of the territorial minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request for a review shall be made jointly by the
  territorial minister and the federal minister if both a territorial
  agency or municipal government and a federal agency are originators of
  the plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent of municipal government

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a municipal government is an originator of a plan,
  a request for a review, or consent to a review, by the territorial
  minister may only be made or given with the agreement of the municipal
  government.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of review

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A request for a review shall specify whether the
  review is to be a public review or some other form of review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discontinuance

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a minister or first nation that requests a review
  of a plan withdraws the request, the review shall be discontinued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria for review

- **[[104]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-104 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  determining whether to establish a panel of the Board, the executive
  committee shall consider

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the matters referred to in subsection
    108(3) were considered in the preparation of the plan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether there is a need for the plan to be reviewed
    by an independent body such as a panel of the Board and whether
    there is a more appropriate body by which, or process under which,
    the plan could be reviewed; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the originator of the plan agrees to the
    review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of review

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The executive committee shall notify the originator
  of its determination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms of reference

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any specification as to the form of review
  referred to in subsection 103(4), the executive committee shall
  establish the terms of reference of a panel of the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of plans specified in regulations

- **[[105]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-105 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  executive committee may establish a panel of the Board to conduct a
  review of any plan of a type specified in the regulations and whose
  implementation it considers might have significant adverse
  environmental or socio-economic effects in Yukon.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of review

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The executive committee shall notify the originator
  of the establishment of a panel of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scope and terms of reference

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The executive committee shall specify the scope of
  the review of the plan, the panel's terms of reference and a schedule
  for the review.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of notice

- **[[106]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-106 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  executive committee shall publish, in a periodical that in its opinion
  has a large circulation in Yukon, a notice of the establishment of a
  panel of the Board under section 103 or 105 and of the manner in which
  the public may obtain copies of the panel's terms of reference.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Modification of terms of reference

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a panel's terms of reference are modified, the
  executive committee shall publish, in a periodical referred to in
  subsection (1), a notice of the manner in which the public may obtain
  copies of the modification and of the reasons for it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution of terms of reference

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The executive committee shall provide copies of the
  terms of reference, and of any modification made to them with the
  reasons for the modification, to the originator of the plan, to the
  minister or first nation that requested or consented to the review, if
  any, and to any government agency, independent regulatory agency or
  first nation that has notified the executive committee of its interest
  in the plan or in plans of that kind.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of members

- **[[107]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-107 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  executive committee shall select the members of a panel of the Board,
  including its chairperson, from among the members of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attendance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every member of a panel of the Board must be present
  at each meeting or hearing of the panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancies

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity, or a
  vacancy in the office, of a member of a panel of the Board, the
  executive committee shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} direct some or all of the remaining members of the
    panel to resume the review;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} appoint another member to the panel and direct the
    panel to resume or recommence the review; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} establish a new panel and direct the panel to
    recommence the review.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement of review

- **[[108]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-108 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  panel of the Board established to review a plan shall commence the
  review as soon as possible after it notifies the originator that, in
  its opinion, the applicable rules have been complied with.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to require additional information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A panel of the Board may, before or after commencing
  a review of a plan, require the originator of the plan to provide any
  supplementary information that it considers necessary for the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matters to be considered

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In conducting a review of a plan, a panel of the
  Board shall take the following matters into consideration:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the significance of any environmental or
    socio-economic effects in or outside Yukon that might occur from the
    implementation of the plan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the significance of any adverse cumulative
    environmental or socio-economic effects that might occur from the
    implementation of the plan in combination with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} projects for which proposals have been submitted
      under subsection 50(1), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} existing or proposed activities, or the
      implementation of other plans, in or outside Yukon, that are known
      to the panel from information provided to it or obtained by it
      under this Part;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} alternatives to the plan that would avoid or
    minimize any significant adverse environmental or socio-economic
    effects;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} mitigative measures and measures to compensate for
    any significant adverse effects;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the need to protect the rights of Yukon Indian
    persons under final agreements, the special relationship between
    Yukon Indian persons and the wilderness environment of Yukon and the
    cultures, traditions, health and lifestyles of Yukon Indian persons
    and other residents of Yukon;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the interests of residents of Yukon and Canadian
    residents outside Yukon;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the capacity of any renewable resources that are
    likely to be significantly affected by the implementation of the
    plan to meet present and future needs; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the need for effects monitoring.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other matters

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A panel of the Board conducting a review of a plan
  may take into consideration any other matter that it considers
  relevant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendations of panel

- **[[109]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-109 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  the completion of its review of a plan, a panel of the Board shall
  provide the originator with written recommendations, which may include
  a recommendation that the plan be implemented, with or without
  modifications, or that it not be implemented.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A panel shall provide a copy of its recommendations
  to any minister, municipal government or first nation that requested
  or consented to the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration to be given

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The originator of a plan shall give full and fair
  consideration to the recommendations provided to it by a panel and
  shall provide the Board with its reasons in writing for declining any
  of the recommendations.

### [Audits and Effects Monitoring]{.HTitleText2} {#h-473912 .Subheading}

- **[[110]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-110 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When

  to proceed, with or without terms and conditions, it may recommend
  that a project audit or that effects monitoring be conducted in
  respect of the project.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Results

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A decision body that accepts a recommendation for a

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} After reviewing the results of any project audit or

  subsections 95(1) to (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report of results

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The executive committee shall conduct a project audit
  or effects monitoring of an existing project where so requested, and
  shall make a report of the results to the minister or first nation
  that made or consented to the request, and may include recommendations
  in the report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration of report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The federal minister, the territorial minister or a

  recommendations of the executive committee.

### [Regulations and Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-473996 .Subheading}

**[[122]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-122 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council

  Order.

  that Minister had agreed to a request made by the executive committee

  Assessment Act](/eng/acts/C-15.2)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding section 6, the [Canadian
  Environmental Assessment Act](/eng/acts/C-15.2) continues to apply ---

  in respect of a proposal for a project for which a screening or

  a review panel pursuant to subsection 29(1) of that Act, that Act
  ceases to apply and section 63 of this Act applies as if the Minister
  of the Environment had agreed to a request made by the executive
  committee under paragraph 61(1)(b).

    applicant to submit a proposal under section 50.

  that the territorial agency or first nation has initiated as proponent

  subject to this Act by virtue of subsection 124(2) or 125(2).
