## Payment Card Networks Act {#payment-card-networks-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2010, c. 12, s. 1834
:::

Assented to 2010-07-12

An Act respecting payment card networks

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 1834 of chapter 12 of the Statutes of Canada, 2010;
Act, other than sections 6 and 7, in force on assent July 12, 2010.\]
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-411653 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Payment Card Networks Act](/eng/acts/P-4.3).

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-411656 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to regulate national payment card networks and the commercial practices
of payment card network operators.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-411659 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[acquirer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[acquirer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity that enables
    merchants to accept payments by payment card by providing merchants
    with access to a payment card network for the transmission or
    processing of those payments. It does not include that entity's
    agent or mandatary. ([acquéreur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation, trust or
    partnership, or an unincorporated association or
    organization. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[issuer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[issuer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity or provincial Crown
    corporation that issues payment cards. ([émetteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Finance. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[payment card]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payment card]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a credit or debit card
    --- or any other prescribed device --- used to access a credit or
    debit account on terms specified by the issuer. It does not include
    a credit card issued for use only with the merchants identified on
    the card. ([carte de paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[payment card network]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payment card network]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an electronic
    payment system --- other than a prescribed payment system --- used
    to accept, transmit or process transactions made by payment card for
    money, goods or services and to transfer information and funds among
    issuers, acquirers, merchants and payment card users. ([réseau de
    cartes de paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[payment card network operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payment card network operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    entity that operates or manages a payment card network, including by
    establishing standards and procedures for the acceptance,
    transmission or processing of payment transactions and by
    facilitating the electronic transfer of information and
    funds. ([exploitant de réseau de cartes de
    paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-411669 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act applies to payment
card network operators.

## [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-411672 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supervision

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Financial Consumer Agency of Canada, established under section 3 of
  the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1), is
  responsible for supervising payment card network operators to
  determine whether they are in compliance with the provisions of this
  Act and the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination and inquiry

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner of the Financial Consumer Agency of
  Canada, appointed under section 4 of the [Financial Consumer Agency of
  Canada Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1), must, from time to time but at least
  once in each year, make or cause to be made any examination and
  inquiry that the Commissioner considers necessary to determine whether
  the provisions of this Act and the regulations are being complied with
  and, after the conclusion of each examination and inquiry, must report
  on it to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of Commissioner on inquiry

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the Commissioner
  has all the powers of a person appointed as a commissioner under Part
  II of the [Inquiries Act](/eng/acts/I-11) for the purpose of obtaining
  evidence under oath, and may delegate those powers to any person
  acting under the Commissioner's direction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to records

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the Commissioner or
  a person acting under the Commissioner's direction

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has a right of access to any records, including
    electronic records, of a payment card network operator; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may require the directors or officers of a payment
    card network operator to provide information and explanations, to
    the extent that they are reasonably able to do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A payment card network operator must provide the
  Commissioner with any information that the Commissioner may require
  for the purposes of this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidential information

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (7), information regarding the
  business or affairs of a payment card network operator, or regarding
  persons dealing with one, that is obtained by the Commissioner or by a
  person acting under the Commissioner's direction, in the course of the
  exercise or performance of powers, duties and functions under this
  section or under subsection 5(1.1) or (2.1) of the [Financial Consumer
  Agency of Canada Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1), and any information prepared
  from that information, is confidential and must be treated
  accordingly.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure permitted

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If the Commissioner is satisfied that the information
  will be treated as confidential by the person to whom it is disclosed,
  the Commissioner may disclose it to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Deputy Minister of Finance, or any officer of
    the Department of Finance authorized in writing by the Deputy
    Minister of Finance, for the purpose of policy analysis related to
    the regulation of payment card network operators; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Governor of the Bank of Canada, or any officer
    of the Bank of Canada authorized in writing by the Governor of the
    Bank of Canada, for the purpose of carrying out the Bank of Canada's
    objects under the [Retail Payment Activities Act](/eng/acts/R-7.36)
    or, in relation to a provision of that Act that is not in force, for
    the purpose of planning to carry out those objects.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance agreement

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may enter into an agreement, called
  a "compliance agreement", with a payment card network operator for the
  purpose of implementing any measure designed to further compliance by
  it with the provisions of this Act and the regulations.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-411692 .Part}

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, on the recommendation of the Minister, make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting payment card networks;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} specifying the types of rates that a payment card
  network operator must disclose and the manner in which the disclosure
  must be made;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the time and manner in which a payment
  card network operator must give notice of any new rates or any changes
  in its rates or fee schedules, as well as to whom the notice must be
  given;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} prescribing conditions regarding the issuance of
  payment cards that a payment card network operator must include in any
  agreement entered into with an issuer;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} prescribing conditions that a payment card network
  operator must include in any agreement entered into with an acquirer;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that by this Act is to be
  prescribed; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} generally for carrying out the purposes and
  provisions of this Act.
::::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-411702 .Part}

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcing conditions

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A payment card network
operator that is a party to an agreement containing any of the
conditions required by regulations made under paragraph 6(d) or (e) must
take reasonable measures to enforce those conditions.
::::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, by order,
exempt a payment card network operator from any of the provisions of
this Act or the regulations.
