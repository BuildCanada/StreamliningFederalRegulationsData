## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-384841 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[Attorney General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Attorney General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Attorney
      General of Canada and includes his or her lawful
      deputy; ([procureur général]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[communicate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[communicate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes to make
      available; ([communiquer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes part of a
      document; ([document]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[foreign economic entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[foreign economic entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a foreign state or a group of foreign states,
        or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity that is controlled, in law or in
        fact, or is substantially owned, by a foreign state or a group
        of foreign states; ([entité économique
        étrangère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[foreign entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[foreign entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a foreign power,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a group or association of foreign powers, or of
        one or more foreign powers and one or more terrorist groups, or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person acting at the direction of, for the
        benefit of or in association with a foreign power or a group or
        association referred to in paragraph (b); ([entité
        étrangère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[foreign power]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[foreign power]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the government of a foreign state,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity exercising or purporting to exercise
        the functions of a government in relation to a territory outside
        Canada regardless of whether Canada recognizes the territory as
        a state or the authority of that entity over the territory, or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a political faction or party operating within a
        foreign state whose stated purpose is to assume the role of
        government of a foreign state; ([puissance
        étrangère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[foreign state]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[foreign state]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a state other than Canada,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a province, state or other political
        subdivision of a state other than Canada, or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a colony, dependency, possession, protectorate,
        condominium, trust territory or any territory falling under the
        jurisdiction of a state other than Canada; ([État
        étranger]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[model]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[model]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes design, pattern and
      specimen; ([modèle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[munitions of war]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[munitions of war]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means arms, ammunition,
      implements or munitions of war, military stores or any articles
      deemed capable of being converted thereinto or made useful in the
      production thereof; ([munitions de guerre]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[offence under this Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[offence under this Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any act,
      omission or other thing that is punishable under this
      Act; ([infraction à la présente loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[office under Her Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[office under Her Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any
      office or employment in or under any department or branch of the
      government of Canada or of any province, and any office or
      employment in, on or under any board, commission, corporation or
      other body that is an agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada or
      any province; ([fonction relevant de Sa Majesté]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[prohibited place]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prohibited place]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any work of defence belonging to or occupied or
        used by or on behalf of Her Majesty, including arsenals, armed
        forces establishments or stations, factories, dockyards, mines,
        minefields, camps, ships, aircraft, telegraph, telephone,
        wireless or signal stations or offices, and places used for the
        purpose of building, repairing, making or storing any munitions
        of war or any sketches, plans, models or documents relating
        thereto, or for the purpose of getting any metals, oil or
        minerals of use in time of war,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any place not belonging to Her Majesty where
        any munitions of war or any sketches, plans, models or documents
        relating thereto are being made, repaired, obtained or stored
        under contract with, or with any person on behalf of, Her
        Majesty or otherwise on behalf of Her Majesty, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any place that is for the time being declared
        by order of the Governor in Council to be a prohibited place on
        the ground that information with respect thereto or damage
        thereto would be useful to a foreign power; ([endroit
        prohibé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[senior police officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[senior police officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2001, c.
      41, s. 26\]]{.Repealed}

  [[sketch]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[sketch]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any mode of representing
      any place or thing; ([croquis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[terrorist activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[terrorist activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in subsection 83.01(1) of the [Criminal
      Code](/eng/acts/C-46); ([activité terroriste]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[terrorist group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[terrorist group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 83.01(1) of the [Criminal
      Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([groupe terroriste]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Act, any reference to Her Majesty means Her
  Majesty in right of Canada or any province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communicating or receiving

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} expressions referring to communicating or receiving
    include any communicating or receiving, whether in whole or in part,
    and whether the sketch, plan, model, article, note, document or
    information itself or the substance, effect or description thereof
    only is communicated or received;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} expressions referring to obtaining or retaining any
    sketch, plan, model, article, note or document include the copying
    of, or causing to be copied, the whole or any part of any sketch,
    plan, model, article, note or document; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} expressions referring to the communication of any
    sketch, plan, model, article, note or document include the transfer
    or transmission of the sketch, plan, model, article, note or
    document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Facilitation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsection 83.01(2) of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) applies for the purposes of the
  definitions [[terrorist activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
  [[terrorist group]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. O-5, s. 2
- 2001, c. 41, s. 26
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prejudice to the safety or interest of
the State

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of this Act, a purpose is prejudicial to the safety or
  interests of the State if a person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} commits, in Canada, an offence against the laws of
    Canada or a province that is punishable by a maximum term of
    imprisonment of two years or more in order to advance a political,
    religious or ideological purpose, objective or cause or to benefit a
    foreign entity or terrorist group;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} commits, inside or outside Canada, a terrorist
    activity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} causes or aggravates an urgent and critical
    situation in Canada that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} endangers the lives, health or safety of
      Canadians, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} threatens the ability of the Government of
      Canada to preserve the sovereignty, security or territorial
      integrity of Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} interferes with a service, facility, system or
    computer program, whether public or private, or its operation, in a
    manner that has significant adverse impact on the health, safety,
    security or economic or financial well-being of the people of Canada
    or the functioning of any government in Canada;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} endangers, outside Canada, any person by reason of
    that person's relationship with Canada or a province or the fact
    that the person is doing business with or on behalf of the
    Government of Canada or of a province;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} damages property outside Canada because a person or
    entity with an interest in the property or occupying the property
    has a relationship with Canada or a province or is doing business
    with or on behalf of the Government of Canada or of a province;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} impairs or threatens the military capability of the
    Canadian Forces, or any part of the Canadian Forces;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} interferes with the design, development or
    production of any weapon or defence equipment of, or intended for,
    the Canadian Forces, including any hardware, software or system that
    is part of or associated with any such weapon or defence equipment;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} impairs or threatens the capabilities of the
    Government of Canada in relation to security and intelligence;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} adversely affects the stability of the Canadian
    economy, the financial system or any financial market in Canada
    without reasonable economic or financial justification;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} impairs or threatens the capability of a government
    in Canada, or of the Bank of Canada, to protect against, or respond
    to, economic or financial threats or instability;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} impairs or threatens the capability of the
    Government of Canada to conduct diplomatic or consular relations, or
    conduct and manage international negotiations;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} contrary to a treaty to which Canada is a party,
    develops or uses anything that is intended or has the capability to
    cause death or serious bodily injury to a significant number of
    people by means of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} toxic or poisonous chemicals or their precursors,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a microbial or other biological agent, or a
      toxin, including a disease organism,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} radiation or radioactivity, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an explosion; or

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} does or omits to do anything that is directed
    towards or in preparation of the undertaking of an activity
    mentioned in any of paragraphs (a) to (m).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Harm to Canadian interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, harm is caused to
  Canadian interests if a foreign entity or terrorist group does
  anything referred to in any of paragraphs (1)(a) to (n).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. O-5, s. 3
- 2001, c. 41, s. 27
:::

## [Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-384913 .Part}

### [Miscellaneous Offences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-384914 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Wrongful communication, etc., of
information

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person is guilty of an offence under this Act who, having in his
  possession or control any secret official code word, password, sketch,
  plan, model, article, note, document or information that relates to or
  is used in a prohibited place or anything in a prohibited place, or
  that has been made or obtained in contravention of this Act, or that
  has been entrusted in confidence to him by any person holding office
  under Her Majesty, or that he has obtained or to which he has had
  access while subject to the Code of Service Discipline within the
  meaning of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) or owing to his
  position as a person who holds or has held office under Her Majesty,
  or as a person who holds or has held a contract made on behalf of Her
  Majesty, or a contract the performance of which in whole or in part is
  carried out in a prohibited place, or as a person who is or has been
  employed under a person who holds or has held such an office or
  contract,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} communicates the code word, password, sketch, plan,
    model, article, note, document or information to any person, other
    than a person to whom he is authorized to communicate with, or a
    person to whom it is in the interest of the State his duty to
    communicate it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} uses the information in his possession for the
    benefit of any foreign power or in any other manner prejudicial to
    the safety or interests of the State;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} retains the sketch, plan, model, article, note, or
    document in his possession or control when he has no right to retain
    it or when it is contrary to his duty to retain it or fails to
    comply with all directions issued by lawful authority with regard to
    the return or disposal thereof; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} fails to take reasonable care of, or so conducts
    himself as to endanger the safety of, the secret official code word,
    password, sketch, plan, model, article, note, document or
    information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication of sketch, plan, model,
  etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person is guilty of an offence under this Act
  who, having in his possession or control any sketch, plan, model,
  article, note, document or information that relates to munitions of
  war, communicates it, directly or indirectly, to any foreign power, or
  in any other manner prejudicial to the safety or interests of the
  State.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receiving code word, sketch, etc.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who receives any secret official code
  word, password, sketch, plan, model, article, note, document or
  information, knowing, or having reasonable ground to believe, at the
  time he receives it, that the code word, password, sketch, plan,
  model, article, note, document or information is communicated to him
  in contravention of this Act, is guilty of an offence under this Act,
  unless he proves that the communication to him of the code word,
  password, sketch, plan, model, article, note, document or information
  was contrary to his desire.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retaining or allowing possession of
  document, etc.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every person is guilty of an offence under this Act
  who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} retains for any purpose prejudicial to the safety
    or interests of the State any official document, whether or not
    completed or issued for use, when he has no right to retain it, or
    when it is contrary to his duty to retain it, or fails to comply
    with any directions issued by any Government department or any
    person authorized by any Government department with regard to the
    return or disposal thereof; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} allows any other person to have possession of any
    official document issued for his use alone, or communicates any
    secret official code word or password so issued, or, without lawful
    authority or excuse, has in his possession any official document or
    secret official code word or password issued for the use of a person
    other than himself, or on obtaining possession of any official
    document by finding or otherwise, neglects or fails to restore it to
    the person or authority by whom or for whose use it was issued, or
    to a police constable.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. O-3, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unauthorized use of uniforms;
falsification of reports, forgery, personation and false documents

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person is guilty of an offence under this Act who, for the purpose of
  gaining admission, or of assisting any other person to gain admission,
  to a prohibited place, or for any other purpose prejudicial to the
  safety or interests of the State,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} uses or wears, without lawful authority, any
    military, police or other official uniform or any uniform so nearly
    resembling such a uniform as to be calculated to deceive, or falsely
    represents himself to be a person who is or has been entitled to use
    or wear any such uniform;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} orally or in writing in any declaration or
    application, or in any document signed by him or on his behalf,
    knowingly makes or connives at the making of any false statement or
    omission;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} forges, alters or tampers with any passport or any
    military, police or official pass, permit, certificate, licence or
    other document of a similar character, in this section referred to
    as an official document, or uses or has in his possession any such
    forged, altered or irregular official document;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} personates or falsely represents himself to be a
    person holding, or to be in the employment of a person holding,
    office under Her Majesty, or to be or not to be a person to whom an
    official document or secret official code word or password has been
    duly issued or communicated, or with intent to obtain an official
    document, secret official code word or password, whether for himself
    or any other person, knowingly makes any false statement; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} uses, or has in his possession or under his
    control, without the authority of the Government department or the
    authority concerned, any die, seal or stamp of or belonging to, or
    used, made or provided by, any Government department, or by any
    diplomatic or military authority appointed by or acting under the
    authority of Her Majesty, or any die, seal or stamp so nearly
    resembling any such die, seal or stamp as to be calculated to
    deceive, or counterfeits any such die, seal or stamp, or uses, or
    has in his possession or under his control, any such counterfeited
    die, seal or stamp.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unlawful dealing with dies, seals, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who, without lawful authority or excuse,
  manufactures or sells, or has in his possession for sale, any die,
  seal or stamp referred to in subsection (1) is guilty of an offence
  under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. O-3, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approaching, entering, etc., a prohibited
place

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every person commits an
offence who, for any purpose prejudicial to the safety or interests of
the State, approaches, inspects, passes over, is in the neighbourhood of
or enters a prohibited place at the direction of, for the benefit of or
in association with a foreign entity or a terrorist group.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. O-5, s. 6
- 2001, c. 41, s. 29
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interference

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every person commits an
offence who, in the vicinity of a prohibited place, obstructs, knowingly
misleads or otherwise interferes with or impedes a peace officer or a
member of Her Majesty's forces engaged on guard, sentry, patrol or other
similar duty in relation to the prohibited place.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. O-5, s. 7
- 2001, c. 41, s. 29
:::

### [Special Operational Information and Persons Permanently Bound to Secrecy]{.HTitleText2} {#h-384952 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this section and sections 9 to 15.

  [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a department named in
      Schedule I to the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11),
      a division or branch of the federal public administration set out
      in column I of Schedule I.1 to that Act and a corporation named in
      Schedule II to that Act. ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[government contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[government contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
      has entered into a contract or arrangement with Her Majesty in
      right of Canada, a department, board or agency of the Government
      of Canada or a Crown corporation as defined in subsection 83(1) of
      the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), and includes
      an employee of the person, a subcontractor of the person and an
      employee of the subcontractor. ([partie à un contrat
      administratif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[person permanently bound to secrecy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[person permanently bound to secrecy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a current or former member or employee of a
        department, division, branch or office of the federal public
        administration, or any of its parts, set out in Schedule 1;

      - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} a current or former member of the National
        Security and Intelligence Review Agency;

      - [(a.2)]{.lawlabel} a current or former member of the National
        Security and Intelligence Committee of Parliamentarians;

      - [(a.3)]{.lawlabel} a current or former officer or
        non-commissioned member of a Canadian Forces unit or other
        element, or any of its parts, set out in Schedule 2 or a current
        or former officer or non-commissioned member who occupies or
        occupied a position in the Canadian Forces set out in that
        Schedule;

      - [(a.4)]{.lawlabel} a person who is or was appointed, attached,
        assigned or seconded to a Canadian Forces unit or other element,
        or any of its parts, set out in Schedule 2 or to a position in
        the Canadian Forces set out in that Schedule; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who has been personally served with a
        notice issued under subsection 10(1) in respect of the person or
        who has been informed, in accordance with regulations made under
        subsection 11(2), of the issuance of such a notice in respect of
        the person. ([personne astreinte au secret à
        perpétuité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[special operational information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[special operational information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      information that the Government of Canada is taking measures to
      safeguard that reveals, or from which may be inferred,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identity of a person, agency, group, body
        or entity that was, is or is intended to be, has been approached
        to be, or has offered or agreed to be, a confidential source of
        information, intelligence or assistance to the Government of
        Canada;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the nature or content of plans of the
        Government of Canada for military operations in respect of a
        potential, imminent or present armed conflict;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the means that the Government of Canada used,
        uses or intends to use, or is capable of using, to covertly
        collect or obtain, or to decipher, assess, analyse, process,
        handle, report, communicate or otherwise deal with information
        or intelligence, including any vulnerabilities or limitations of
        those means;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether a place, person, agency, group, body or
        entity was, is or is intended to be the object of a covert
        investigation, or a covert collection of information or
        intelligence, by the Government of Canada;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the identity of any person who is, has been or
        is intended to be covertly engaged in an information- or
        intelligence-collection activity or program of the Government of
        Canada that is covert in nature;

      - [(e.1)]{.lawlabel} the military vulnerabilities or advantages of
        the Canadian Forces, including the operational or technical
        vulnerabilities and advantages of any ally or adversary;

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the means that the Government of Canada used,
        uses or intends to use, or is capable of using, to protect or
        exploit any information or intelligence referred to in any of
        paragraphs (a) to (e.1), including, but not limited to,
        encryption and cryptographic systems, and any vulnerabilities or
        limitations of those means; or

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} information or intelligence similar in nature
        to information or intelligence referred to in any of
        paragraphs (a) to (f) that is in relation to, or received from,
        a foreign entity or terrorist group. ([renseignements
        opérationnels spéciaux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy head

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections 10(1) and 15(5), the
  deputy head is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for an individual employed in or attached or
    seconded to a department, the deputy head of the department;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for an officer or a non-commissioned member of the
    Canadian Forces, the Chief of the Defence Staff;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a person who is a member of the exempt staff of
    a Minister responsible for a department, the deputy head of the
    department;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for a government contractor in relation to a
    contract with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Department of Public Works and Government
      Services, the deputy head of that department or any other deputy
      head authorized for the purpose by the Minister of Public Works

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public interest defence

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person is guilty of an offence under section 13 or 14 if the person
  establishes that he or she acted in the public interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting in the public interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), a person acts in the
  public interest if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person acts for the purpose of disclosing an
    offence under an Act of Parliament that he or she reasonably
    believes has been, is being or is about to be committed by another
    person in the purported performance of that person's duties and
    functions for, or on behalf of, the Government of Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the public interest in the disclosure outweighs the
    public interest in non-disclosure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paragraph (2)(a) to be considered first

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In determining whether a person acts in the public
  interest, a judge or court shall determine whether the condition in
  paragraph (2)(a) is satisfied before considering paragraph (2)(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to be considered

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In deciding whether the public interest in the
  disclosure outweighs the public interest in non-disclosure, a judge or
  court must consider

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the extent of the disclosure is no more
    than is reasonably necessary to disclose the alleged offence or
    prevent the commission or continuation of the alleged offence, as
    the case may be;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the seriousness of the alleged offence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the person resorted to other reasonably
    accessible alternatives before making the disclosure and, in doing
    so, whether the person complied with any relevant guidelines,
    policies or laws that applied to the person;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether the person had reasonable grounds to
    believe that the disclosure would be in the public interest;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the public interest intended to be served by the
    disclosure;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the extent of the harm or risk of harm created by
    the disclosure; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the existence of exigent circumstances justifying
    the disclosure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior disclosure to authorities
  necessary

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A judge or court may decide whether the public
  interest in the disclosure outweighs the public interest in
  non-disclosure only if the person has complied with the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person has, before communicating or confirming
    the information, brought his or her concern to, and provided all
    relevant information in his or her possession to, his or her deputy
    head or, if not reasonably practical in the circumstances, the
    Deputy Attorney General of Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person has, if he or she has not received a
    response from the deputy head or the Deputy Attorney General of
    Canada, as the case may be, within a reasonable time, brought his or
    her concern to and provided all relevant information in the person's
    possession to the National Security and Intelligence Review Agency,
    if the person's concern relates to an alleged offence that has been,
    is being or is about to be committed by another person in the
    purported performance of that person's duties and functions of
    service for, or on behalf of, the Government of Canada and he or she
    has not received a response from that Agency within a reasonable
    time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exigent circumstances

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (5) does not apply if the communication or
  confirmation of the information was necessary to avoid grievous bodily
  harm or death.

### [Communications with Foreign Entities or Terrorist Groups]{.HTitleText2} {#h-385062 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communicating safeguarded information

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person commits an offence who, without lawful authority, communicates
  to a foreign entity or to a terrorist group information that the
  Government of Canada or of a province is taking measures to safeguard
  if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person believes, or is reckless as to whether,
    the information is information that the Government of Canada or of a
    province is taking measures to safeguard; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person intends, by communicating the
    information, to increase the capacity of a foreign entity or a
    terrorist group to harm Canadian interests or is reckless as to
    whether the communication of the information is likely to increase
    the capacity of a foreign entity or a terrorist group to harm
    Canadian interests.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communicating safeguarded information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person commits an offence who, intentionally
  and without lawful authority, communicates to a foreign entity or to a
  terrorist group information that the Government of Canada or of a
  province is taking measures to safeguard if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person believes, or is reckless as to whether,
    the information is information that the Government of Canada or of a
    province is taking measures to safeguard; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} harm to Canadian interests results.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who commits an offence under
  subsection (1) or (2) is guilty of an indictable offence and is liable
  to imprisonment for life.

### [Economic Espionage]{.HTitleText2} {#h-385090 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of trade secret for the benefit of
foreign economic entity

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person commits an offence who, at the direction of, for the benefit of
  or in association with a foreign economic entity, fraudulently and
  without colour of right and to the detriment of Canada's economic
  interests, international relations or national defence or national
  security

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} communicates a trade secret to another person,
    group or organization; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} obtains, retains, alters or destroys a trade
    secret.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who commits an offence under
  subsection (1) is guilty of an indictable offence and is liable to
  imprisonment for a term of not more than 10 years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person is not guilty of an offence under
  subsection (1) if the trade secret was

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtained by independent development or by reason
    only of reverse engineering; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} acquired in the course of the person's work and is
    of such a character that its acquisition amounts to no more than an
    enhancement of that person's personal knowledge, skill or expertise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of [[trade
  secret]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of this section, [[trade
  secret]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any information, including a
  formula, pattern, compilation, program, method, technique, process,
  negotiation position or strategy or any information contained or
  embodied in a product, device or mechanism that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is or may be used in a trade or business;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not generally known in that trade or business;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has economic value from not being generally known;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is the subject of efforts that are reasonable under
    the circumstances to maintain its secrecy.

### [Foreign-influenced or Terrorist-influenced Intimidation, Threats or Violence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-385110 .Subheading}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person commits an offence who, at the direction of, for the benefit of

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} a [[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

        Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5),

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} a person who owes allegiance to His Majesty in
        right of Canada, or

        performs their functions in a Canadian mission outside Canada,

      partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the
      [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), and the child, relative or
      partner is in or outside Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who commits an offence under

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other sentence, other than one of life

### [Political Interference for a Foreign Entity]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1487828 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Influencing political or governmental
process

- **[[20.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20.4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every person commits an indictable
  offence who, at the direction of, or in association with, a foreign
  entity, engages in surreptitious or deceptive conduct with the intent
  to influence a political or governmental process, educational
  governance, the performance of a duty in relation to such a process or
  such governance or the exercise of a democratic right in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who commits an offence under
  subsection (1) is liable to imprisonment for life.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sentences to be served consecutively

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A sentence, other than one of life imprisonment,
  imposed on a person for an offence under subsection (1) is to be
  served consecutively to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any other sentence imposed on the person, other
    than one of life imprisonment, for an offence arising out of the
    same event or series of events; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other sentence, other than one of life
    imprisonment, to which the person is subject at the time the
    sentence is imposed on the person for an offence under subsection
    (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in this section.

  [[educational governance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[educational governance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      governance of a school board or primary or secondary school,
      college, university or other institution of higher learning or
      training institution in Canada. ([gouvernance
      scolaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[political or governmental process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[political or governmental process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any proceeding of a legislative body;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the development of a legislative proposal;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the development or amendment of any policy or
        program;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the making of a decision by a public office
        holder or government body, including the awarding of a contract;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the holding of an election or referendum; and

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the nomination of a candidate or the
        development of an electoral platform by a political
        party. ([processus politique ou gouvernemental]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[public office holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[public office holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the
      following individuals:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any officer or employee of His Majesty in right
        of Canada and includes

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Senate or the House of
          Commons and any person on the staff of such a member,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a person who is appointed to any office or
          body by or with the approval of the Governor in Council or a
          minister of the Crown, other than a judge receiving a salary
          under the [Judges Act](/eng/acts/J-1) or the lieutenant
          governor of a province,

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an officer, director or employee of any
          [[federal board, commission or other
          tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection
          2(1) of the [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7),

        - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Canadian Forces, and

        - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Royal Canadian Mounted
          Police;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} members of the legislature of a province or
        persons on the staff of such members;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} employees of the government of a province;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} members of a council or other statutory body
        charged with the administration of the civil or municipal
        affairs of a city, town, municipality or district, persons on
        the staff of such members or officers or employees of a city,
        town, municipality or district;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} members of the council of a
        [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of
        the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), or of the council of an Indian
        band established by an Act of Parliament, persons on their staff
        or employees of such a council;

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} members of an aboriginal government or
        institution that exercises jurisdiction or authority under a
        self-government agreement, or under self-government provisions
        contained in a land claims agreement, given effect by or under
        an Act of Parliament, persons on the staff of those members or
        employees of that government or institution;

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} an officer or employee of an entity that
        represents the interests of First Nations, the Inuit or the
        Métis. ([titulaire d'une charge publique]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} This section applies to any of the following
  political or governmental processes in Canada:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} federal political or governmental processes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provincial or territorial political or governmental
    processes;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} municipal political or governmental processes;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the political or governmental processes of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a council, government or other entity that is
      authorized to act on behalf of an Indigenous group, community or
      people that holds rights recognized and affirmed by section 35 of
      the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any other entity that represents the interests
      of First Nations, the Inuit or the Métis.

::: HistoricalNote
- [2024, c. 16, s. 53](#1460875-1487870){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1460875-1487870.html"
  role="button"}
:::

### [Harbouring or Concealing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-385122 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Concealing person who carried out offence

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who, for the purpose of enabling or facilitating an offence
  under this Act, knowingly harbours or conceals a person whom they know
  to be a person who has committed an offence under this Act, is guilty
  of an indictable offence and liable to imprisonment

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a term of not more than 14 years, if the person
    who is harboured or concealed committed an offence under this Act
    for which that person is liable to imprisonment for life; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a term of not more than 10 years, if the person
    who is harboured or concealed committed an offence under this Act
    for which that person is liable to any other punishment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Concealing person who is likely to
  carry out offence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who, for the purpose of enabling or
  facilitating an offence under this Act, knowingly harbours or conceals
  any person whom he or she knows to be a person who is likely to carry
  out an offence under this Act, is guilty of an indictable offence and
  liable to imprisonment for a term of not more than 10 years.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 41, s. 29
- 2013, c. 9, s. 29
:::

### [Preparatory Acts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-385132 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preparatory acts

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person commits an offence who, for the purpose of committing an
  offence under this Act, other than under subsection 13(1) or 18(1),
  does anything that is directed towards or done in preparation of the
  commission of the offence, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} entering Canada at the direction of or for the
    benefit of a foreign entity, a terrorist group or a foreign economic
    entity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} obtaining, retaining or gaining access to any
    information;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} knowingly communicating to a foreign entity, a
    terrorist group or a foreign economic entity the person's
    willingness to commit the offence;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} at the direction of, for the benefit of or in
    association with a foreign entity, a terrorist group or a foreign
    economic entity, asking a person to commit the offence; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} possessing any device, apparatus or software useful
    for concealing the content of information or for surreptitiously
    communicating, obtaining or retaining information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who commits an offence under
  subsection (1) is guilty of an indictable offence and is liable to
  imprisonment for a term of not more than five years.

### [Conspiracy, Attempts, Etc.]{.HTitleText2} {#h-385145 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conspiracy, attempts, etc.

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Every person commits an
offence who conspires or attempts to commit, is an accessory after the
fact in relation to or counsels in relation to an offence under this Act
and is liable to the same punishment and to be proceeded against in the
same manner as if he or she had committed the offence.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-385150 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attorney General's consent

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** No prosecution shall be
commenced for an offence against this Act without the consent of the
Attorney General.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 41, s. 29
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** An offence against this
Act may be tried, in any place in Canada, regardless of where in Canada
the offence was committed.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 41, s. 29
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extraterritorial application

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who commits an act or omission outside Canada that would be an
  offence against this Act if it were committed in Canada is deemed to
  have committed it in Canada if the person is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian citizen;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who owes allegiance to Her Majesty in
    right of Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who is locally engaged and who performs
    his or her functions in a Canadian mission outside Canada; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a person who, after the time the offence is alleged
    to have been committed, is present in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person is deemed to have committed an act or
  omission in Canada, proceedings in respect of the offence may, whether
  or not the person is in Canada, be commenced in any territorial
  division in Canada, and the person may be tried and punished in
  respect of the offence in the same manner as if the offence had been
  committed in that territorial division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appearance of accused at trial

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the provisions of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) relating to requirements that a person
  appear at and be present during proceedings and the exceptions to
  those requirements apply in respect of proceedings commenced in a
  territorial division under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person previously tried outside Canada

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a person is alleged to have committed an act or
  omission that is an offence by virtue of this section and the person
  has been tried and dealt with outside Canada in respect of the offence
  in a manner such that, if the person had been tried and dealt with in
  Canada, the person would be able to plead *[autrefois
  acquit]{.otherLang lang="fr"}*, *[autrefois]{.otherLang lang="fr"}
  convict* or pardon, the person shall be deemed to have been so tried
  and dealt with in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 41, s. 29
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Unless this Act provides
otherwise, a person who commits an offence under this Act is guilty of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an indictable offence and liable to imprisonment for
  a term of not more than 14 years; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an offence punishable on summary conviction and
  liable to imprisonment for a term of not more than 12 months or to a
  fine of not more than \$2,000, or to both.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 41, s. 29
:::
