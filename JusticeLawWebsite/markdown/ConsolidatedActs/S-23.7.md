## Supplementary Fiscal Equalization Payments 1982-87 Act {#supplementary-fiscal-equalization-payments-1982-87-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1985, c. 29
:::

Assented to 1985-06-28

An Act to provide for the making of supplementary fiscal equalization
payments to certain provinces for the period April 1, 1982 to March 31,
1987
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-442429 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Supplementary Fiscal Equalization Payments 1982-87
Act](/eng/acts/S-23.7).

## [Supplementary Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-442432 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments authorized

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In addition to the fiscal
equalization payment amounts payable for the period April 1, 1982 to
March 31, 1987 to each province entitled to a fiscal equalization
payment under Part I of the Federal-Provincial Fiscal Arrangements and
Federal Post-Secondary Education and Health Contributions Act, 1977, the
Minister of Finance shall pay to each such province as a supplementary
fiscal equalization payment, the following amounts:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the fiscal year commencing on April 1,
  1985,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} one hundred and ten million dollars to the Province
    of Quebec,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} fifty million dollars to the Province of Manitoba,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} twenty million dollars to the Province of Nova
    Scotia,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} twenty million dollars to the Province of New
    Brunswick,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} fifteen million dollars to the Province of
    Newfoundland, and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} five million dollars to the Province of Prince
    Edward Island; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the fiscal year commencing on April 1,
  1986, sixty-five million dollars to the Province of Manitoba.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of C.R.F.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The amounts authorized to
be paid by section 2 shall be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the fiscal year commencing on April 1,
  1985, within thirty days after the coming into force of this Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the fiscal year commencing on April 1,
  1986, within thirty days after the commencement of that year.
