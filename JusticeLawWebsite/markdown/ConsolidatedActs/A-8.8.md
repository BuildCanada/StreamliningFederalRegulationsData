## Agriculture and Agri-Food Administrative Monetary Penalties Act {#agriculture-and-agri-food-administrative-monetary-penalties-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1995, c. 40
:::

Assented to 1995-12-05

An Act to establish a system of administrative monetary penalties for
the enforcement of the Farm Debt Mediation Act, the Feeds Act, the
Fertilizers Act, the Health of Animals Act, the Pest Control Products
Act, the Plant Protection Act, the Safe Food for Canadians Act and the
Seeds Act
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9748 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

Act](/eng/acts/A-8.8).

## [Purpose of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9765 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of Act

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to establish, as an alternative to the existing penal system and as a
supplement to existing enforcement measures, a fair and efficient
administrative monetary penalty system for the enforcement of the
agri-food Acts.

## [Powers of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9768 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} designating as a violation that may be proceeded
    with in accordance with this Act

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the contravention of any specified provision of
      an agri-food Act or of a regulation made under an agri-food Act,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the contravention of any specified order, or
      class of orders, made by the Minister under the [Plant Protection
      Act](/eng/acts/P-14.8), or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the refusal or neglect to perform any specified
      duty, or class of duties, imposed by or under the [Plant
      Protection Act](/eng/acts/P-14.8), the [Health of Animals
      Act](/eng/acts/H-3.3), the [Pest Control Products
      Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01) or the [Safe Food for Canadians
      Act](/eng/acts/S-1.1);

    if the contravention, or the failure or neglect to perform the duty,
    as the case may be, is an offence under an agri-food Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} classifying each violation as a minor violation, a
    serious violation or a very serious violation;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} establishing, in respect of each violation, a
    short-form description to be used in notices of violation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fixing a penalty, or a range of penalties, in
    respect of each violation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the circumstances under which, the
    criteria by which and the manner in which a penalty may be increased
    or reduced, including the reduction of a penalty pursuant to a
    compliance agreement under subsection 10(1);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} respecting the determination of a lesser amount
    that may be paid in complete satisfaction of a penalty if paid
    within the prescribed time and manner;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} respecting the circumstances under which reviews
    under this Act by the Tribunal shall be oral or in writing;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} respecting the service of documents required or
    authorized to be served under this Act including, without
    restricting the generality of the foregoing, the manner of serving
    such documents, the proof of their service and the circumstances
    under which such documents shall be deemed to have been served;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that by this Act is to be
    prescribed; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} generally, for carrying out the purposes and
    provisions of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum penalties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The maximum penalty for a violation is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a violation that is committed by an
    individual otherwise than in the course of a business and that is
    not committed to obtain a financial benefit, \$2,000; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, \$5,000 for a minor violation,
    \$15,000 for a serious violation and \$25,000 for a very serious
    violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Without restricting the generality of paragraph
  (1)(d), in making regulations respecting the criteria for increasing
  or reducing the amount of the penalty for a violation, the Minister
  shall include the following in any such criteria:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the degree of intention or negligence on the part
    of the person who committed the violation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the harm done by the violation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the history of the person who committed the
    violation of prior violations or convictions under agri-food Acts
    within the five year period immediately before the violation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How act or omission may be proceeded with

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where any act or omission
can be proceeded with as a violation or as an offence, the Minister may
commence proceedings in respect of that act or omission as a violation
or recommend that it be proceeded with as an offence, but proceeding
with it as a violation precludes proceeding with it as an offence, and
proceeding with it as an offence precludes proceeding with it as a
violation.

violation

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may designate
persons, or classes of persons, who are authorized to issue notices of
violation.

## [Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9803 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission of violation

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contravenes any provision of an agri-food Act or of
    a regulation made under an agri-food Act,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contravenes any order made by the Minister under
    the [Plant Protection Act](/eng/acts/P-14.8), or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} refuses or neglects to perform any duty imposed by
    or under the [Plant Protection Act](/eng/acts/P-14.8), the [Health
    of Animals Act](/eng/acts/H-3.3), the [Pest Control Products
    Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01) or the [Safe Food for Canadians
    Act](/eng/acts/S-1.1)

  the contravention of which, or the refusal or neglect of which, is
  designated to be a violation by a regulation made under paragraph
  4(1)(a) commits a violation and is liable to a warning or to a penalty
  in accordance with this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of notice of violation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person designated under section 6 has reasonable
  grounds to believe that a person has committed a violation, the
  designated person may issue, and shall cause to be served on the
  person, a notice of violation that names the person, identifies the
  violation and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contains a warning that the person has committed a
    violation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sets out

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the penalty, established in accordance with the
      regulations, for the violation that the person is liable to pay,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} particulars concerning the time for paying and
      the manner of paying the penalty, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} subject to the regulations, a lesser amount
      that may be paid in complete satisfaction of the penalty if paid
      within the time and manner specified in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summary of rights

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice of violation must clearly summarize, in
  plain language, the rights and obligations under this Act of the
  person on whom it is served, including the right to have the facts of
  the violation reviewed by the Minister or the Tribunal, and the
  procedure for requesting such a review.

## [Notices of Violation with Warning]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9822 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notices with warning --- request for
review

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  notice of violation contains a warning, the person named in the notice
  may, in the prescribed time and manner, request a review of the facts
  of the violation by the Minister or the Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a person who is served with a notice of
  violation that contains a warning does not request a review under
  subsection (1) in the prescribed time and manner, the person is deemed
  to have committed the violation identified in the notice of violation.

## [Notices of Violation with Penalty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9828 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notices with penalty --- payment

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  notice of violation sets out a penalty and the person named in the
  notice pays, in the prescribed time and manner, the amount of the
  penalty or, subject to the regulations, the lesser amount set out in
  the notice that may be paid in lieu of the penalty,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is deemed to have committed the
    violation in respect of which the amount is paid;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall accept that amount as and in
    complete satisfaction of the penalty; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proceedings commenced in respect of the
    violation under section 7 are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternatives to payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Instead of paying the penalty set out in a notice of
  violation or, where applicable, the lesser amount that may be paid in
  lieu of the penalty, the person named in the notice may, in the
  prescribed time and manner,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the penalty is \$2,000 or more, request to enter
    into a compliance agreement with the Minister that ensures the
    person's compliance with the agri-food Act or regulation to which
    the violation relates;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} request a review by the Minister of the facts of
    the violation; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} request a review by the Tribunal of the facts of
    the violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a person who is served with a notice of
  violation that sets out a penalty does not pay the penalty in the
  prescribed time and manner or, where applicable, the lesser amount
  that may be paid in lieu of the penalty, and does not exercise any
  right referred to in subsection (2) in the prescribed time and manner,
  the person is deemed to have committed the violation identified in the
  notice.

## [Compliance Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9842 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance agreements

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  considering a request under paragraph 9(2)(a), the Minister may enter
  into a compliance agreement, as described in that paragraph, with the
  person making the request on such terms and conditions as are
  satisfactory to the Minister, which terms may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} include a provision for the giving of reasonable
    security, in a form and in an amount satisfactory to the Minister,
    as a guarantee that the person will comply with the compliance
    agreement; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide for the reduction, in whole or in part, of
    the penalty for the violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who makes a request under paragraph 9(2)(a)
  and who enters into a compliance agreement with the Minister shall, on
  entering into the compliance agreement, be deemed to have committed
  the violation in respect of which the compliance agreement was entered
  into.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where compliance agreement complied
  with

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister is satisfied that a person who has
  entered into a compliance agreement has complied with the agreement,
  the Minister shall cause a notice to that effect to be served on the
  person and, on the service of that notice,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proceedings commenced in respect of the
    violation under section 7 are ended; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any security given under the compliance agreement
    by the person shall be returned to the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where compliance agreement not complied
  with

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister is of the opinion that a person
  who has entered into a compliance agreement has not complied with the
  agreement, the Minister shall cause a notice of default to be served
  on the person to the effect that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} instead of the penalty set out in the notice of
    violation in respect of which the compliance agreement was entered
    into, the person is liable to pay twice the amount of that penalty
    and, for greater certainty, subsection 4(2) does not apply in
    respect of that amount; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the security, if any, given under the compliance
    agreement by the person shall be forfeited to Her Majesty in right
    of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of notice of default

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} On the service of a notice under subsection (4), the
  person served has no right of set-off against any amount spent by the
  person under the compliance agreement and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person served is liable to pay the amount set
    out in the notice; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the notice of default provides for the
    forfeiture of the security given under the compliance agreement,
    that security is forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada and the
    proceedings commenced in respect of the violation under section 7
    are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of payment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where a person pays the amount set out in a notice of
  default under subsection (4) in the prescribed time and manner,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall accept the amount as and in
    complete satisfaction of the amount owing; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proceedings commenced in respect of the
    violation under section 7 are ended.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to enter into compliance
agreement

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Minister refuses to enter into a compliance agreement pursuant to
  a request under paragraph 9(2)(a), the person who made the request
  may, in the prescribed time and manner,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pay the amount of the penalty; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} request a review by the Tribunal of the facts of
    the violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a person pays the amount referred to in
  paragraph (1)(a),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is deemed to have committed the
    violation in respect of which the payment is made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall accept the amount as and in
    complete satisfaction of the penalty; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proceedings commenced in respect of the
    violation under section 7 are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person does not, in the prescribed time and
  manner, either pay the amount referred to in paragraph (1)(a) or
  request a review under paragraph (1)(b), the person is deemed to have
  committed the violation identified in the notice of violation.

## [Review by Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9880 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review under section 8

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  concluding a review requested under section 8, the Minister shall
  determine whether or not the person committed the violation, and the
  Minister shall cause a notice of any decision under this subsection to
  be served on the person who requested the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to review

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister decides under subsection (1) that
  a person has committed a violation, the person may, in the prescribed
  time and manner, request a review of the Minister's decision by the
  Tribunal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review under paragraph 9(2)(b)

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  concluding a review requested under paragraph 9(2)(b), the Minister
  shall determine whether or not the person requesting the review
  committed a violation and, where the Minister decides that the person
  committed a violation but considers that the amount of the penalty for
  the violation was not established in accordance with the regulations,
  the Minister shall correct the amount of the penalty for the
  violation, and the Minister shall cause a notice of any decision under
  this subsection to be served on the person who requested the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment or right to review

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister decides under subsection (1) that
  a person has committed a violation, the person may, in the prescribed
  time and manner,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pay the amount of the penalty set out in the notice
    referred to in subsection (1), in which case

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall accept the amount as and in
      complete satisfaction of the penalty, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the proceedings commenced in respect of the
      violation under section 7 are ended; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} request a review of the Minister's decision by the
    Tribunal.

## [Review by Tribunal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9895 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review by Tribunal

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  concluding a review requested under this Act, the Tribunal shall, by
  order, as the case may be,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} confirm, vary or set aside any decision of the
    Minister under section 12 or 13, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine whether or not the person requesting the
    review committed a violation and, where the Tribunal decides that
    the person committed a violation but considers that the amount of
    the penalty for the violation, if any, was not established in
    accordance with the regulations, the Tribunal shall correct the
    amount of the penalty,

  and the Tribunal shall cause a notice of any order made under this
  subsection to be served on the person who requested the review, and on
  the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Tribunal decides under subsection (1) that
  a person has committed a violation, the person is liable for the
  amount of the penalty as set out in the order of the Tribunal and, on
  the payment of that amount in the time and manner specified in the
  order,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall accept the amount as and in
    complete satisfaction of the penalty; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proceedings commenced in respect of the
    violation under section 7 are ended.

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9906 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debts to Her Majesty

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following amounts constitute debts due to Her Majesty in right of
  Canada that may be recovered as such in the Federal Court:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of a penalty, from the time the notice
    of violation setting out the penalty is served;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} every amount undertaken to be paid pursuant to a
    compliance agreement entered into with the Minister under subsection
    10(1), from the time the compliance agreement is entered into;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount set out in a notice of default referred
    to in subsection 10(4), from the time the notice is served;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount of a penalty as set out in a decision of
    the Minister under subsection 13(1), from the time the notice under
    that subsection is served;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the amount of a penalty as set out in an order of
    the Tribunal under subsection 14(1), from the expiration of the time
    specified in the order for the payment of that amount; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any reasonable expenses incurred
    pursuant to section 22, from the date they are incurred.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No proceedings to recover a debt referred to in
  subsection (1) may be commenced later than five years after the debt
  became payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt final

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A debt referred to in subsection (1) is final and not
  subject to review or to be restrained, prohibited, removed, set aside
  or otherwise dealt with except to the extent and in the manner
  provided by sections 9 to 14.

## [Rules of Law About Violations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9927 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violations not offences

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, a
violation is not an offence and, accordingly, section 126 of the
[Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) does not apply.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain defences not available

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person named in a notice of violation does not have a defence by
  reason that the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exercised due diligence to prevent the violation;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reasonably and honestly believed in the existence
    of facts that, if true, would exonerate the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Common law principles

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every rule and principle of the common law that
  renders any circumstance a justification or excuse in relation to a
  charge for an offence under an agri-food Act applies in respect of a
  violation to the extent that it is not inconsistent with this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Burden of proof

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** In every case where the
facts of a violation are reviewed by the Minister or by the Tribunal,
the Minister must establish, on a balance of probabilities, that the
person named in the notice of violation committed the violation
identified in the notice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vicarious liability --- licence holders,
etc.

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder of a licence, certificate, letter of accreditation, permit,
  notice or other document issued under an agri-food Act is liable for a
  violation that is committed in respect of any matter relating to any
  activity or requirement under that document, whether or not the person
  who actually committed the violation is identified or proceeded
  against in accordance with this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vicarious liability --- acts of
  employees and agents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person is liable for a violation that is committed
  by any employee or agent of the person acting in the course of the
  employee's employment or the scope of the agent's authority, whether
  or not the employee or agent who actually committed the violation is
  identified or proceeded against in accordance with this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing violation

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A violation that is
continued on more than one day constitutes a separate violation in
respect of each day during which it is continued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person is deemed by this Act to have committed a
  violation, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister, pursuant to a review under this Act,
  has decided that a person has committed a violation and no request to
  review the Minister's decision has been made to the Tribunal in the
  prescribed time and manner,

anything seized and detained under an agri-food Act in relation to the
violation is, at the election of Her Majesty in right of Canada,
immediately forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada and may be
disposed of, at the expense of the person from whom it was seized, in
accordance with the regulations made under the applicable agri-food Act
unless the Minister directs otherwise.

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-9951 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notations removed

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  notation of a violation shall, on application by the person who
  committed the violation, be removed from any records that may be kept
  by the Minister respecting that person after the expiration of five
  years from

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the notice of violation contained a warning,
    the date the notice was served, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the payment of any debt referred
    to in subsection 15(1),

  unless the removal from the record would not in the opinion of the
  Minister be in the public interest or another notation of a violation
  has been recorded by the Minister in respect of that person after that
  date and has not been removed in accordance with this subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to notify

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause a notice of removal to be
  served on the person in respect of whom a notation is removed pursuant
  to subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of documents

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Every document required
or authorized to be served under this Act shall be served in accordance
with the regulations, either personally or in such other manner as may
be authorized in the regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** In any proceeding for a
violation or for a prosecution for an offence, a notice of violation
purporting to be issued pursuant to this Act is admissible in evidence
without proof of the signature or official character of the person
appearing to have signed the notice of violation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** No proceedings in respect
of a violation may be commenced later than

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} six months after the day on which the subject matter
  of the proceedings arises, in the case of a minor violation; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} two years after the day on which the subject matter
  of the proceedings arises, in the case of a serious violation or a
  very serious violation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court of record

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal is a court of record with an official seal that must be
  judicially noticed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination of witnesses, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the powers conferred by subsection
  (1), the Tribunal has, with respect to the appearance, swearing and
  examination of witnesses, the production and inspection of documents
  and other things, the enforcement of its orders and other matters
  necessary or proper for the due exercise of its jurisdiction, all the
  powers, rights and privileges that are vested in a superior court of
  record and, without limiting the generality of the foregoing, it may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} issue a summons requiring a person

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to appear at the time and place stated in the
      summons to testify to all matters within the person's knowledge
      relative to any subject matter before the Tribunal, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to bring and produce any document, book or paper
      in the person's possession or under the person's control relative
      to that subject matter;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} administer oaths and examine any person on oath;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} during a hearing, receive any evidence that it
    considers relevant and trustworthy.
