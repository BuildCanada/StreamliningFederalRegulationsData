## An Act respecting the provision of funding for diagnostic and medical equipment {#an-act-respecting-the-provision-of-funding-for-diagnostic-and-medical-equipment .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2005, c. 11, s. 7
:::

Assented to 2005-03-23

An Act respecting the provision of funding for diagnostic and medical
equipment

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 7 of chapter 11 of the Statutes of Canada, 2005, in
force on assent March 23, 2005.\]
:::
:::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment --- equipment and services

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of giving effect to the 2004 10-Year Plan to Strengthen Health
  Care, the Minister of Finance may, for the fiscal year beginning on
  April 1, 2004, make direct payments, in an aggregate of \$500 million,
  to the provinces for the purposes of acquiring diagnostic and medical
  equipment and related specialized staff training in order to improve
  access to and reduce wait times for publicly funded diagnostic and
  treatment services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial share

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount payable to a province for the fiscal year
  shall be determined by multiplying the amount referred to in
  subsection (1) by the quotient obtained by dividing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the population of the province for the fiscal year

  by

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total population of all provinces for the
    fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of population

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, the population of a
  province for the fiscal year shall be determined by the Chief
  Statistician of Canada on the basis of Statistics Canada's official
  estimate of the population of that province on June 1, 2004.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of C.R.F.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any amount payable under this Act may be paid by the
  Minister out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund at the times and in the
  manner that the Minister considers appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communiqués

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty and
for the purposes of this Act, the 2004 10-Year Plan to Strengthen Health
Care includes the communiqués released in respect of the First
Ministers' Meeting on the Future of Health Care that was held from
September 13 to 15, 2004.
