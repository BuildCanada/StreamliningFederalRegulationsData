## Canada--Yukon Oil and Gas Accord Implementation Act {#canadayukon-oil-and-gas-accord-implementation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1998, c. 5
:::

Assented to 1998-05-12

An Act respecting an accord between the Governments of Canada and the
Yukon Territory relating to the administration and control of and
legislative jurisdiction in respect of oil and gas
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the Government of Canada and the Government of the Yukon
Territory, on May 28, 1993, entered into the Canada--Yukon Oil and Gas
Accord, by which the Government of Canada undertook to recommend to
Parliament the enactment of legislation for the purposes of the Accord;

NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Transitional and Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-60510 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-60511 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this section apply in sections 20 to 28.

  [[existing federal interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[existing federal interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an interest, within the meaning of section 2 of
        the [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5), in oil
        and gas in the Yukon Territory or the adjoining area in effect
        on the transfer date; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a production licence issued on or after the
        transfer date by virtue of section 23. ([titres fédéraux
        existants]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transfer date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transfer date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the effective date of
      the first order of the Governor in Council made pursuant to
      section 47.1 of the [Yukon Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.01), as amended by
      this Act, after this section comes into force that transfers the
      administration and control of oil and gas to the Commissioner of
      the Yukon Territory. ([date de transfert]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The expressions [[adjoining
  area]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[gas]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
  [[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[oil and gas
  interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[territorial oil and gas
  minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[Yukon oil and gas
  laws]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in sections 20 to 28 have the same
  meaning as in section 2 of the [Yukon Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.01), as it
  read immediately before the coming into force of this subsection.

- 1998, c. 5, s. 19
- 2002, c. 7, s. 116

### [Transitional]{.HTitleText2} {#h-60523 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of existing interests

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  existing federal interest remains in effect on and after the transfer
  date until it expires or is cancelled, until it is surrendered by the
  holder of the interest or until otherwise agreed to by the holder of
  the interest and the territorial oil and gas minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Yukon laws

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On and after the transfer date and subject to
  subsection (3), Yukon oil and gas laws apply in respect of every
  existing federal interest, except that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} rights under an existing federal interest, within
    the meaning of subsection (4), may not be diminished; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the term of an existing federal interest may not be
    reduced.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation or suspension

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An existing federal interest may be cancelled or
  rights under it suspended, in accordance with Yukon oil and gas laws,
  if the interest could have been cancelled or the rights suspended in
  like circumstances before the transfer date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), rights under an
  existing federal interest are the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an exploration licence issued under
    the [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the rights described in paragraphs 22(a) and (b)
      of that Act, in respect of the lands described in the licence, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in lieu of the right described in paragraph
      22(c) of that Act, the exclusive right to obtain, pursuant to
      Yukon oil and gas laws, production rights in respect of any part
      of the lands described in the licence in which oil or gas is
      determined, pursuant to those laws, to be commercially producible;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a significant discovery licence
    issued under the [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the rights described in paragraphs 29(a) and (b)
      of that Act, in respect of the lands described in the licence
      other than any part of the lands for which a determination has
      been made under Yukon oil and gas laws to the effect that there is
      no potential for sustained production of oil or gas, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in lieu of the right described in paragraph
      29(c) of that Act, the exclusive right to obtain, pursuant to
      Yukon oil and gas laws, production rights in respect of any part
      of those lands in which oil or gas is determined, pursuant to
      those laws, to be commercially producible;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a production licence issued under
    the [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the rights described in subsection 37(1) of that
      Act, in respect of the lands described in the licence, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a right to the extension of the term of the
      licence, as provided by subsection 41(3) of that Act; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a lease issued under the [Canada Oil
    and Gas Land Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1518),
    C.R.C., c. 1518,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the rights described in section 58 of those
      Regulations, as that section read on the transfer date, in respect
      of the lands described in the lease, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a right to the reissuance of the lease, as
      provided by section 63 of those Regulations, as that section read
      on the transfer date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confirmation of interests by Yukon laws

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Yukon oil and gas laws must include provisions
  corresponding to the provisions of this section for as long as any
  existing federal interest remains in effect.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exercise of access rights

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Where Yukon oil and gas
laws confer a right of access to lands for purposes of exploration for
or production or transportation of oil or gas, and provide for the
resolution of disputes between persons exercising that right and
persons, other than the Governments of Canada and Yukon, having rights
or interests in the surface of those lands, those laws shall provide for
such resolution to be by means of access orders of the Yukon Surface
Rights Board made in accordance with the [Yukon Surface Rights Board
Act](/eng/acts/Y-4.3).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1998, c. 5, s. 21
- 2002, c. 7, s. 117(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7)

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Where any lands have been
withdrawn from disposition pursuant to the [Territorial Lands
Act](/eng/acts/T-7) before the transfer date for any purposes and under
any conditions, no oil and gas interest may be granted pursuant to Yukon
oil and gas laws in relation to those lands, for those purposes and
under those conditions, for as long as those lands remain withdrawn from
disposition.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pending applications under [Canada
Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5)

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an application for a declaration of commercial discovery or a
  production licence under the [Canada Petroleum Resources
  Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5) in relation to lands in the Yukon Territory or
  the adjoining area was made but not disposed of before the transfer
  date, the application shall be disposed of in accordance with that Act
  as if it continued to apply in respect of those lands after the
  transfer date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications made after transfer date

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a declaration of commercial discovery is made
  before the transfer date under the [Canada Petroleum Resources
  Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5) and is in effect on the transfer date, or is
  made on or after the transfer date by virtue of subsection (1), in
  relation to any lands that immediately before that date were subject
  to an existing federal interest,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the owner of that interest may apply for a
    production licence in relation to those lands in accordance with
    that Act at any time within 30 days after the transfer date or after
    the making of the declaration, whichever is later, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an application so made shall be disposed of in
    accordance with that Act,

  as if that Act continued to apply in respect of those lands after the
  transfer date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pending applications under [Yukon Surface
Rights Board Act](/eng/acts/Y-4.3)

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Where an application was
made but not disposed of before the transfer date under section 65 of
the [Yukon Surface Rights Board Act](/eng/acts/Y-4.3) in respect of a
mineral right with a right of access under subsection 5.01(1) of the
[Canada Oil and Gas Operations Act](/eng/acts/O-7), the application
shall be disposed of as if section 65 of the [Yukon Surface Rights Board
Act](/eng/acts/Y-4.3) continued to apply in respect of the right of
access after the transfer date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnification by Yukon

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Yukon Government shall indemnify the Government of Canada against any
  claim, action or other proceeding for damages brought against the
  Government of Canada, or any of its employees or agents, arising out
  of any acts or omissions of the Yukon Government in respect of the
  operation of Yukon oil and gas laws on and after the transfer date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnification by Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Government of Canada shall indemnify the Yukon
  Government against any claim, action or other proceeding for damages
  brought against the Yukon Government, or any of its employees or
  agents, after the transfer date in respect of the operation of the
  [Canada Oil and Gas Operations Act](/eng/acts/O-7), the [Canada
  Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5) or Part II.1 of the
  [National Energy Board Act](/eng/acts/N-7) before the transfer date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A Government is not entitled to be indemnified under
  this section by the other Government if it has settled the claim,
  action or proceeding without the approval of the other Government.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1998, c. 5, s. 25
- 2002, c. 7, s. 118(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of transfer date

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Indian
Affairs and Northern Development shall cause notice of the transfer date
to be published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force of Yukon oil and gas
laws

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Yukon oil and gas laws
may be made at any time after this Act has been assented to,
notwithstanding the application of the [Canada Petroleum Resources
Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5) and the [Canada Oil and Gas Operations
Act](/eng/acts/O-7) in the Yukon Territory until the transfer date, but
those laws have no effect before the transfer date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laws of the Legislature

**[[27.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27.1 .sectionLabel}** Every reference to
"Yukon oil and gas laws" in sections 20, 21, 22 and 25 shall be read so
as to include any laws of the Legislature of Yukon in respect of oil and
gas.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2002, c. 7, s. 119
:::
