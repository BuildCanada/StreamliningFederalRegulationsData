## Pioneer Trust Payment Continuation Act {#pioneer-trust-payment-continuation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1985, c. 15
:::

Assented to 1985-05-16

An Act to provide for the continuation of payments under certain
guaranteed income averaging certificates issued by the Pioneer Trust
Company
::::

::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-392945 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Pioneer Trust Payment Continuation Act](/eng/acts/P-14.2).

## [Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-392948 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements and arrangements generally

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of State (Finance), or any officer of the Office of the
  Superintendent of Financial Institutions designated by the Minister,
  may, on behalf of Her Majesty, enter into such agreements or
  arrangements as may be necessary for or incidental to the continuation
  of payments under and in accordance with the guaranteed income
  averaging certificates issued by the Pioneer Trust Company for a term
  of more than five years and outstanding on February 15, 1985.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister or a designated officer enters
  into an agreement or arrangement under subsection (1), the Minister or
  officer may do such things and perform such acts as may be necessary
  for or incidental to carrying out the agreement or arrangement.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1987, c. 23, s. 42
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement with Saskatchewan

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of State
(Finance) may, on behalf of Her Majesty, enter into an agreement with
the government of Saskatchewan to provide for the sharing of any costs
incurred under this Act.

## [Appropriation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-392958 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriation

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of State (Finance) may, from time to time, pay out of the
  Consolidated Revenue Fund such amounts as are required for the
  purposes of section 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The aggregate of the amounts paid out of the
  Consolidated Revenue Fund under subsection (1) shall not exceed the
  aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} five million dollars, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amounts paid into the Fund that are received
    pursuant to agreements entered into under section 2.
