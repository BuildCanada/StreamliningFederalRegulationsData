## Cultural Property Export and Import Act {#cultural-property-export-and-import-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. C-51
:::

An Act respecting the export from Canada of cultural property and the
import into Canada of cultural property illegally exported from foreign
states

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Cultural Property Export and Import Act](/eng/acts/C-51).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-138698 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Control List]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Control List]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Cultural
    Property Export Control List established under section
    4; ([nomenclature]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[expert examiner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[expert examiner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person or
    institution designated as an expert examiner under section
    6; ([expert-vérificateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[export permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[export permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit to export
    issued by a permit officer under this
    Act; ([licence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[general permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[general permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit to export
    issued by the Minister under section 17; ([licence
    générale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an institution that is
    publicly owned and is operated solely for the benefit of the public,
    that is established for educational or cultural purposes and that
    conserves objects and exhibits them or otherwise makes them
    available to the public; ([établissement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada as is designated by the Governor in Council
    as the Minister for the purposes of this
    Act; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[permit officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permit officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated as
    a permit officer under section 5; ([agent]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[public authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Her Majesty in right
    of Canada or a province, an agent of Her Majesty in either such
    right, a municipality in Canada, a municipal or public body
    performing a function of government in Canada or a corporation
    performing a function or duty on behalf of Her Majesty in right of
    Canada or a province; ([administration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[resident of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[resident of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in the case of a
    natural person, a person who ordinarily resides in Canada and, in
    the case of a corporation, a corporation that has its head office in
    Canada or maintains one or more establishments in Canada to which
    employees of the corporation employed in connection with the
    business of the corporation ordinarily report for
    work; ([résident]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Review Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Review Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Cultural
    Property Export Review Board established by section
    18. ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Canadian Cultural Property Export Control List]{.HTitleText1} {#h-138718 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of Control List

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister made after
  consultation with the Minister of Foreign Affairs, may by order
  establish a Canadian Cultural Property Export Control List.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inclusions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the Governor in Council
  may include in the Control List, regardless of their places of origin,
  any objects or classes of objects hereinafter described in this
  subsection, the export of which the Governor in Council deems it
  necessary to control in order to preserve the national heritage in
  Canada:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} objects of any value that are of archaeological,
    prehistorical, historical, artistic or scientific interest and that
    have been recovered from the soil of Canada, the territorial sea of
    Canada or the inland or other internal waters of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} objects that were made by, or objects referred to
    in paragraph (d) that relate to, the aboriginal peoples of Canada
    and that have a fair market value in Canada of more than five
    hundred dollars;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} objects of decorative art, hereinafter described in
    this paragraph, that were made in the territory that is now Canada
    and are more than one hundred years old:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} glassware, ceramics, textiles, woodenware and
      works in base metals that have a fair market value in Canada of
      more than five hundred dollars, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} furniture, sculptured works in wood, works in
      precious metals and other objects of decorative art that have a
      fair market value in Canada of more than two thousand dollars;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} books, records, documents, photographic positives
    and negatives, sound recordings, and collections of any of those
    objects that have a fair market value in Canada of more than five
    hundred dollars;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} drawings, engravings, original prints and
    water-colours that have a fair market value in Canada of more than
    one thousand dollars; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other objects that have a fair market value in
    Canada of more than three thousand dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No object shall be included in the Control List if
  that object is less than fifty years old or was made by a natural
  person who is still living.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming provision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, an object within a
  class of objects included in the Control List is deemed to be an
  object included in the Control List.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-51, s. 4
- 1995, c. 5, s. 25
- 2001, c. 34, s. 37(F)
:::

## [Expert Examiners]{.HTitleText1} {#h-138743 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of expert examiners

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate any resident of Canada or any institution in
  Canada as an expert examiner for the purposes of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An expert examiner that is not an agent of Her
  Majesty in right of Canada or a province or is not an employee of, or
  an employee of an agent of, Her Majesty in right of Canada or a
  province shall be paid such remuneration for services performed under
  this Act as may be approved by the Treasury Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An expert examiner or, where an expert examiner is an
  institution, the person acting for the institution is entitled, within
  such limits as may be established by the Treasury Board, to be paid
  reasonable travel and living expenses incurred while absent from his
  ordinary place of residence in connection with services performed
  under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 5
:::

## [Export Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-138753 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immediate issue of export permit

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A permit officer who
receives from a resident of Canada an application for an export permit
shall issue the permit forthwith if the person applying for the permit
establishes to the satisfaction of the permit officer that the object in
respect of which the application is made

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} was imported into Canada within the thirty-five years
  immediately preceding the date of the application and was not exported
  from Canada under a permit issued under this Act prior to that
  importation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} was lent to an institution or public authority in
  Canada by a person who was not a resident of Canada at the time the
  loan was made; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is to be removed from Canada for a purpose prescribed
  by regulation for a period of time not exceeding such period of time
  as may be prescribed by regulation for the purposes of this paragraph.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination by permit officer

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A permit
  officer who receives from a resident of Canada an application for an
  export permit in respect of an object shall, where he does not issue
  an export permit under section 7, and where he is not aware of any
  notice of refusal sent in respect of the object under subsection 13(1)
  during the two years immediately preceding the date of the
  application, determine whether the object is included in the Control
  List.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Export permit where object not included
  in Control List

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a permit officer determines that an object in
  respect of which an application for an export permit is made is not
  included in the Control List, the permit officer shall forthwith issue
  an export permit in respect of the object.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to expert examiner

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a permit officer determines that an object in
  respect of which an application for an export permit is made is or
  might be included in the Control List, the permit officer shall
  forthwith refer the application to an expert examiner for
  consideration.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination by expert examiner

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Where an application for an
export permit is referred to an expert examiner pursuant to subsection
8(3), the expert examiner shall forthwith determine whether the object
in respect of which the application is made is included in the Control
List.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where object not included in Control List

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Where an expert examiner
determines that an object that is the subject of an application for an
export permit that has been referred to him is not included in the
Control List, the expert examiner shall forthwith in writing advise the
permit officer who referred the application to issue an export permit in
respect of the object and shall forthwith send a copy of that advice to
the Review Board and the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where object included in Control List

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an expert examiner determines that an object that is the subject of an
  application for an export permit that has been referred to him is
  included in the Control List, the expert examiner shall forthwith
  further determine

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether that object is of outstanding significance
    by reason of its close association with Canadian history or national
    life, its aesthetic qualities, or its value in the study of the arts
    or sciences; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the object is of such a degree of national
    importance that its loss to Canada would significantly diminish the
    national heritage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Export permit to be issued

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an expert examiner determines that an object
  that is the subject of an application for an export permit that has
  been referred to him is not of outstanding significance under
  paragraph (1)(a) or does not meet the degree of national importance
  referred to in paragraph (1)(b), the expert examiner shall forthwith
  in writing advise the permit officer who referred the application to
  issue an export permit in respect of the object and shall forthwith
  send a copy of that advice to the Review Board and the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Export permit not to be issued

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an expert examiner determines that an object
  that is the subject of an application for an export permit that has
  been referred to him is of outstanding significance under paragraph
  (1)(a) and meets the degree of national importance referred to in
  paragraph (1)(b), the expert examiner shall forthwith in writing
  advise the permit officer who referred the application not to issue an
  export permit in respect of the object and shall provide the permit
  officer with the reasons therefor.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 8
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 47, s. 53(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issue of export permit

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 14
and 16, a permit officer shall issue an export permit forthwith where
the permit officer is advised by an expert examiner or directed by the
Review Board to do so.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of refusal

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a permit officer is advised by an expert examiner pursuant to
  subsection 11(3) not to issue an export permit, the permit officer
  shall send a written notice of refusal to the applicant, which notice
  shall include the reasons given by the expert examiner for the
  refusal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy to Review Board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A permit officer who sends a notice of refusal under
  subsection (1) shall forthwith send a copy thereof to the Review
  Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 10
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deposit of copy

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** No export permit shall,
unless it is issued under section 7, be issued under this Act for an
object within a class of objects prescribed under paragraph 39(d), where
the object is included in the Control List, until a copy of that object
has been deposited by the person applying for the permit in such
institution as the Minister may direct.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alteration of permits by Minister and
notice

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may amend,
suspend, cancel or reinstate any export permit, other than an export
permit issued on the direction of the Review Board, and where an export
permit is amended, suspended, cancelled or reinstated, the Minister
shall forthwith send a written notice to that effect to the person who
applied for the permit.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No export permit for two years

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** No export permit shall,
unless it is issued under section 7 or on the direction of the Review
Board pursuant to section 29 or 30, be issued under this Act in respect
of an object, where the object is included in the Control List, during a
period of two years from the date on which a notice of refusal was sent
in respect of that object under subsection 13(1).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 13
:::

## [General Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-138812 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General permits to export

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may issue to any resident of Canada who applies therefor a
  general permit to export any objects included in the Control List
  subject to such terms and conditions as the Minister may require and
  may at any time amend, suspend, cancel or reinstate any such permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Open general permits to export

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, with the concurrence of the
  Minister of Foreign Affairs, issue generally to all persons a general
  permit to export objects within any class of objects that is included
  in the Control List and that is specified in the permit subject to
  such terms and conditions as the Minister may require and may, with
  the concurrence of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, at any time amend,
  suspend, cancel or reinstate any such permit.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-51, s. 17
- 1995, c. 5, s. 25
:::

## [Review Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-138820 .Part}

### [Review Board Established]{.HTitleText2} {#h-138821 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review Board established

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  is hereby established a board to be known as the Canadian Cultural
  Property Export Review Board, consisting of a Chairperson and not more
  than nine other members appointed by the Governor in Council on the
  recommendation of the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson and one other member shall be chosen
  generally from among residents of Canada, and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} up to four other members shall be chosen from among
    residents of Canada who are or have been officers, members or
    employees of art galleries, museums, archives, libraries or other
    collecting institutions in Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} up to four other members shall be chosen from among
    residents of Canada who are or have been dealers in or collectors of
    art, antiques or other objects that form part of the national
    heritage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting Chairperson

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Review Board may authorize one of its members to
  act as Chairperson in the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  Chairperson or if the office of Chairperson is vacant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Three members, at least one of whom is a person
  described in paragraph (2)(a) and one of whom is a person described in
  paragraph (2)(b), constitute a quorum of the Review Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-51, s. 18
- 1995, c. 29, ss. 21, 22(E)
- 2001, c. 34, s. 38
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  member of the Review Board who is not an employee of, or an employee
  of an agent of, Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province shall be
  paid such salary or other amount by way of remuneration as may be
  fixed by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each member of the Review Board is entitled, within
  such limits as may be established by the Treasury Board, to be paid
  reasonable travel and living expenses incurred while absent from his
  ordinary place of residence in connection with the work of the Review
  Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 16
:::

### [Duties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-138842 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The Review Board shall,
on request,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to section 29, review applications for
  export permits;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to section 30, make determinations
  respecting fair cash offers to purchase; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to section 32, make determinations for the
  purposes of subparagraph 39(1)(a)(i.1), paragraph 110.1(1)(c), the
  definition [[total cultural gifts]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
  subsection 118.1(1) and subsection 118.1(10) of the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-51, s. 20
- 1991, c. 49, s. 216
:::

### [Rules and Procedure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-138867 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The Review Board may make
rules not inconsistent with this Act for the conduct of its proceedings
and the performance of its duties and functions under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 21
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review Board may receive information

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The Review Board may
receive any information presented to it orally or in writing that it
considers to be relevant to any matter before it and in so doing it is
not bound by any legal or technical rules of evidence.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 22
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information given to applicant

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The Review Board shall
make the substance of any information received by it in respect of a
matter before it known to the person who applied for an export permit in
respect of the object to which the matter relates, or to the person,
institution or public authority that applied for a determination under
subsection 32(1), as the case may be, and, before the Review Board
decides the matter, it shall give that person, institution or public
authority an opportunity to make representations in respect of that
information.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 22
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion from hearing

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** The Review Board may
exclude any person not directly interested in a matter being heard
before it from the hearing unless, where the matter is in respect of an
object in respect of which an application for an export permit has been
made, the applicant for the permit requests that the hearing be held in
public, in which case it shall be so held.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 22
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review Board shall dispose of matters
informally and expeditiously

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The Review Board shall
dispose of any matter before it as informally and expeditiously as, in
its opinion, the circumstances and considerations of fairness will
permit.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 22
:::

### [Review of Applications for Export Permits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-138888 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for review by Review Board

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who receives a notice of refusal under section 13 or a notice
  under section 15 may, within thirty days after the date on which the
  notice was sent, by notice in writing given to the Review Board,
  request a review of his application for an export permit by the Review
  Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review to be held within four months

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Review Board shall review an application for an
  export permit and, unless the circumstances of a particular case
  require otherwise, render its decision within four months after the
  date a request is received under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of the Review Board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is included in the Control List;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is of outstanding significance for one or more of

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} meets the degree of national importance referred to
    in paragraph 11(1)(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Object that does not meet criteria

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Review Board determines that an object
  fails to meet one or more of the criteria set out in subsection (3),
  it shall direct a permit officer to issue an export permit forthwith
  in respect of the object.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Object that meets criteria

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the Review Board determines that an object
  meets all of the criteria set out in subsection (3), it shall,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it is of the opinion that a fair offer to
    purchase the object might be made by an institution or public
    authority in Canada within six months after the date of its
    determination, establish a delay period of not less than two months
    and not more than six months during which the Review Board will not
    direct that an export permit be issued in respect of the object; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, direct a permit officer to issue
    an export permit forthwith in respect of the object.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of delay period

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where the Review Board establishes a delay period

## [Convention for the Protection of Cultural Property in the Event of Armed Conflict and Its Protocols]{.HTitleText1} {#h-139015 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[36.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in
  this section.

  [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention for the
      Protection of Cultural Property in the Event of Armed Conflict,
      done at The Hague on May 14, 1954. Article 1 of the Convention is
      set out in the schedule. ([convention]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[First Protocol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[First Protocol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the first protocol,
      done at The Hague on May 14, 1954, to the Convention. ([premier
      protocole]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Second Protocol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Second Protocol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the second protocol,
      done at The Hague on March 26, 1999, to the Convention.
       ([deuxième protocole]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[State Party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[State Party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a state that is a party
      to the Convention and the First or Second Protocol. ([État
      partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Export or removal of cultural property

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall knowingly export or otherwise remove
  cultural property as defined in subparagraph (a) of Article 1 of the
  Convention from an occupied territory of a State Party to the Second
  Protocol, unless the export or removal conforms with the applicable
  laws of that territory or is necessary for the property's protection
  or preservation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence outside Canada deemed in Canada

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite anything in this Act or any other Act, a
  person who commits an act or omission outside Canada that if committed
  in Canada would constitute an offence under subsection (2), or a
  conspiracy or an attempt to commit such an offence, or being an
  accessory after the fact or counselling in relation to such an
  offence, is deemed to have committed that act or omission in Canada if
  the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a Canadian citizen;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not a citizen of any state and ordinarily
    resides in Canada; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is a permanent resident within the meaning of
    subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) and is, after the commission of the act or
    omission, present in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action for recovery of cultural
  property

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the government of a State Party submits a request
  in writing to the Minister for the recovery and return of any cultural
  property that has been exported from an occupied territory of that
  State Party and that is in Canada in the possession of or under the
  control of any person, institution or public authority, the Attorney
  General of Canada may institute an action in the Federal Court or in a
  superior court of a province for the recovery of the property by the
  State Party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Notice of the commencement of an action under this
  section on behalf of a State Party shall be served by the Attorney
  General of Canada on such persons and given in such manner as is
  provided by the rules of the court in which the action is taken, or,
  if the rules do not so provide, served on such persons and given in
  such manner as is directed by a judge of the court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order for recovery of cultural property

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The court in which an action has been taken under
  this section may, after affording all persons that it considers to
  have an interest in the action a reasonable opportunity to be heard,
  make an order for the recovery of the property in respect of which the
  action has been taken or any other order sufficient to ensure the
  return of the property to the State Party, if the court is satisfied
  that the property was exported in contravention of the applicable laws
  of the occupied territory of the State Party or was imported into
  Canada for its protection or preservation and that the amount fixed
  under subsection (7), if any, has been paid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The court in which an action has been taken under

  purchased or the title to the property was acquired that the property

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} had been exported in contravention of the

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} had been imported into Canada for its protection or

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safe-keeping

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The court may, at any time in the course of an action

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permit to export

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, on receipt of a copy of a court

## [Foreign Cultural Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-139048 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section,

  [[cultural property agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cultural property agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation
      to a foreign State, means an agreement between Canada and the
      foreign State or an international agreement to which Canada and
      the foreign State are both parties, relating to the prevention of
      illicit international traffic in cultural
      property; ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[foreign cultural property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[foreign cultural property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to
      a reciprocating State, means any object that is specifically
      designated by that State as being of importance for archaeology,
      prehistory, history, literature, art or science; ([biens culturels
      étrangers]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[reciprocating State]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[reciprocating State]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a foreign State
      that is a party to a cultural property agreement. ([État
      contractant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Illegal imports

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} From and after the coming into force of a cultural
  property agreement in Canada and a reciprocating State, it is illegal
  to import into Canada any foreign cultural property that has been
  illegally exported from that reciprocating State.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action for recovery of foreign cultural
  property

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the government of a reciprocating State submits
  a request in writing to the Minister for the recovery and return of
  any foreign cultural property that has been imported into Canada
  illegally by virtue of subsection (2) and that is in Canada in the
  possession of or under the control of any person, institution or
  public authority, the Attorney General of Canada may institute an
  action in the Federal Court or in a superior court of a province for
  the recovery of the property by the reciprocating State.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notice of the commencement of an action under this
  section shall be served by the Attorney General of Canada on such
  persons and given in such manner as is provided by the rules of the
  court in which the action is taken, or, where the rules do not so
  provide, served on such persons and given in such manner as is
  directed by a judge of the court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order for recovery of designated
  property

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A court in which an action has been taken under this
  section on behalf of a reciprocating State may, after affording all
  persons that it considers to have an interest in the action a
  reasonable opportunity to be heard, make an order for the recovery of
  the property in respect of which the action has been taken or any
  other order sufficient to ensure the return of the property to the
  reciprocating State, where the court is satisfied that the property
  has been illegally imported into Canada by virtue of subsection (2)
  and that the amount fixed under subsection (6), if any, has been paid
  to or for the benefit of the person, institution or public authority
  referred to in that subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where any person, institution or public authority
  establishes to the satisfaction of the court in which an action under
  this section is being considered that the person, institution or
  public authority

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a [bona fide]{.otherLang lang="la"} purchaser
    for value of the property in respect of which the action has been
    taken and had no knowledge at the time the property was purchased by
    him or it that the property had been illegally exported from the
    reciprocating State on whose behalf the action has been taken, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a valid title to the property in respect of
    which the action has been taken and had no knowledge at the time
    such title was acquired that the property had been illegally
    exported from the reciprocating State on whose behalf the action has
    been taken,

  the court may fix such amount to be paid as compensation by the
  reciprocating State to that person, institution or public authority as
  the court considers just in the circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safe-keeping

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The court may, at any time in the course of an action
  under this section, order that the property in respect of which the
  action has been taken be turned over to the Minister for safe-keeping
  and conservation pending final disposition of the action.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permit to export

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, on receipt of a copy of an order
  of a court made under subsection (5), issue a permit authorizing any
  person authorized by the reciprocating State on behalf of which the
  action was taken to export the property in respect of which the order
  was made to that State.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitations inapplicable

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} Section 39 of the [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7)
  does not apply in respect of any action taken under this section.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-51, s. 37
- 2002, c. 8, s. 182

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-139090 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Export or attempt to export

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** No person shall export or
attempt to export from Canada any object included in the Control List
except under the authority of and in accordance with a permit issued
under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 34
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No transfer of permits

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** No person who is
authorized under a permit issued under this Act to export an object from
Canada shall transfer the permit to or allow it to be used by a person
who is not so authorized.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 35
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False information

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** No person shall wilfully
furnish any false or misleading information or knowingly make any
misrepresentation

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in an application for a permit under this Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of procuring the issue of a permit
  under this Act; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in connection with the use of a permit issued under
  this Act or the disposition of any object to which such permit
  relates.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 36
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Import or attempt to import foreign
cultural property

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** No person shall import or
attempt to import into Canada any property that it is illegal to import
into Canada under subsection 37(2).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 37
:::

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall export or attempt to export from Canada any property in
  respect of which an action has been instituted under subsection 37(3)
  while the action is being considered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall export or attempt to export from
  Canada any property in respect of which an order has been made under
  subsection 37(5) except under the authority of and in accordance with
  a permit issued by the Minister under subsection 37(8).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 50, s. 38
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences and punishment

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every

  sections 40 to 44 is guilty of an offence and liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding five

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment to a fine not exceeding

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A prosecution under paragraph (1)(a) may be
  instituted at any time within but not later than three years after the

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsection 36.1(1))]{.OriginatingRef}[Article 1 of the Convention for the Protection of Cultural Property in the Event of Armed Conflict Done at the Hague on May 14, 1954]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-139164 .scheduleLabel}
:::

### ARTICLE 1 Definition of Cultural Property {#article-1-definition-of-cultural-property .SchedHeadL2}

For the purposes of the present Convention, the term [[cultural
property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} shall cover, irrespective of origin or
ownership:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} movable or immovable property of great importance to
  the cultural heritage of every people, such as monuments of
  architecture, art or history, whether religious or secular;
  archaeological sites; groups of buildings which, as a whole, are of
  historical or artistic interest; works of art; manuscripts, books and
  other objects of artistic, historical or archaeological interest; as
  well as scientific collections and important collections of books or
  archives or of reproductions of the property defined above;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} buildings whose main and effective purpose is to
  preserve or exhibit the movable cultural property defined in
  subparagraph (a) such as museums, large libraries and depositories of
  archives, and refuges intended to shelter, in the event of armed
  conflict, the movable cultural property defined in subparagraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} centres containing a large amount of cultural
  property as defined in subparagraphs (a) and (b), to be known as
  "centres containing monuments".

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 40, s. 6
