## Air Canada Public Participation Act {#air-canada-public-participation-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. 35 (4th
Supp.)
:::

An Act to provide for the continuance of Air Canada under the Canada
Business Corporations Act and for the issuance and sale of shares
thereof to the public

::: ReaderNote
\[1988, c. 44, assented to 18th August, 1988\]
:::
:::::

:::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1247 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Air Canada Public Participation Act](/eng/acts/A-10.1).

## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1250 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Air Canada, a
      corporation continued by the Air Canada
      Act; ([Société]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the President of the
      Queen's Privy Council or such other member of the Queen's Privy
      Council for Canada as may be designated by the Governor in Council
      as the Minister for the purposes of this
      Act. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Same meaning

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless a contrary intention appears, words and
  expressions used in this Act have the same meaning as in the [Canada
  Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operation of [Canada Business
  Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the event of any inconsistency between this Act
  and the [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44), or
  anything issued, made or established under that Act, this Act prevails
  to the extent of the inconsistency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operation of [Competition
  Act](/eng/acts/C-34)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in, or done under the authority of, this Act
  affects the operation of the [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34) in
  respect of the acquisition of any interest in the Corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (4th Supp.), s. 2
- 1994, c. 24, s. 34(F)
- 1996, c. 10, s. 206
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on the Crown

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Transfer of Shares to Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1266 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of Air Canada shares

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection 13(3) of
  the Air Canada Act, the shares of the Corporation held by the Minister
  of Transport in trust for Her Majesty in right of Canada are hereby
  transferred to the Minister, who is hereby authorized to acquire the
  shares.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration and holding of shares

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The shares transferred to the Minister pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall be registered in the books of the Corporation in
  the name of the Minister and shall be held by the Minister in trust
  for Her Majesty in right of Canada.

## [Continuance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1272 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission of draft application

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation shall submit an application for a certificate of
  continuance of the Corporation under section 187 of the [Canada
  Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44) to the Minister for
  approval.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission to Director

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Forthwith after the Minister approves an application
  submitted pursuant to subsection (1), the Corporation shall submit the
  approved application to the Director.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application submitted to the Director pursuant to
  this section is, subject to this Act, deemed for all purposes to have
  been made under subsection 187(1) of the [Canada Business Corporations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-44).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (4th Supp.), s. 5
- 1994, c. 24, s. 34(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory provisions in articles of
continuance

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  articles of continuance of the Corporation shall contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2001, c. 35, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(b) and (c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2018, c. 10, s.
    90\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provisions requiring the Corporation to carry out
    or cause to be carried out aircraft maintenance activities,
    including maintenance of any type relating to airframes, engines,
    components, equipment or parts, in Ontario, Quebec and Manitoba; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} provisions specifying that the head office of the
    Corporation is to be situated in the Montreal Urban Community.

- [(2) and (3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2018, c. 10, s. 90\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintenance activities

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of carrying out or causing to be
  carried out the aircraft maintenance activities referred to in
  paragraph (1)(d) in Ontario, Quebec and Manitoba, the Corporation may,
  while not eliminating those activities in any of those provinces,
  change the type or volume of any or all of those activities in each of
  those provinces, as well as the level of employment in any or all of
  those activities.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2001, c. 35, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has
  the same meaning as in subsection 3(1) of the [Aeronautics
  Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

- [(6.1) and (7)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2018, c. 10, s.
  90\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (4th Supp.), s. 6
- 1993, c. 34, s. 3
- 1994, c. 24, s. 34(F), c. 47, s. 220
- 2000, c. 15, s. 17
- 2001, c. 35, s. 1
- 2016, c. 8, s. 1
- 2018, c. 10, s. 90
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on amendment

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation and its
shareholders and directors shall not

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} apply for continuance of the Corporation in another
  jurisdiction; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} make any articles or by-laws that are inconsistent
  with the provisions included in its articles of continuance pursuant
  to subsection 6(1).

## [Share Transactions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1302 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dealing with shares, etc., by Minister

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister is hereby authorized to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} acquire, hold, dispose of and otherwise deal with
    shares or debt obligations of, or any security interest in, the
    Corporation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into any agreement or arrangement necessary
    or incidental to any activity referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issue and disposal of shares by
  Corporation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation is hereby authorized to issue and
  sell or otherwise dispose of shares of the Corporation.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1310 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Name

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding subsection
10(1) of the [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44), the
Corporation may continue to use and be legally designated by the name
"Air Canada" on and after the day on which it becomes a corporation to
which that Act applies.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (4th Supp.), s. 9
- 1994, c. 24, s. 34(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Official Languages
Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01)

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  [Official Languages Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01) applies to the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty re subsidiaries

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), if air services, including
  incidental services, are provided or made available by a subsidiary of
  the Corporation, the Corporation has the duty to ensure that any of
  the subsidiary's customers can communicate with the subsidiary in
  respect of those services, and obtain those services from the
  subsidiary, in either official language in any case where those
  services, if provided by the Corporation, would be required under Part
  IV of the [Official Languages Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01) to be provided in
  either official language.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsidiary body corporate

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, a body corporate is
  a subsidiary of the Corporation if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is controlled by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Corporation,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Corporation and one or more bodies corporate
      each of which is controlled by the Corporation, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} two or more bodies corporate each of which is
      controlled by the Corporation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is a subsidiary of a body corporate that is a
    subsidiary of the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (3), a body corporate
  is controlled by another body corporate if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} securities of the body corporate to which are
    attached more than 50% of the votes that may be cast to elect
    directors of the body corporate are held, other than by way of
    security only, by or for the benefit of the other body corporate;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the votes attached to those securities are
    sufficient, if exercised, to elect a majority of the directors of
    the body corporate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of subsection (2)

  [[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#A-10.1_en_25){#A-10.1_en_25-ID0EBDGA}[(5)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2)
  applies

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of air services, including incidental
    services, provided or made available by a subsidiary of the
    Corporation at a facility or office in Manitoba, British Columbia,
    Saskatchewan, Alberta, the Yukon Territory, the Northwest
    Territories or Nunavut or on a route wholly within those provinces,
    one year after that subsection comes into force if it had been a
    subsidiary of the Corporation on that coming into force; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a person that becomes a subsidiary of
    the Corporation only after that subsection comes into force, or in
    respect of Canadian Airlines International Ltd. or Canadian Regional
    Airlines Ltd. if that airline becomes a subsidiary of the
    Corporation before that subsection comes into force, three years
    after the person or airline becomes a subsidiary.

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#A-10.1_en_25-ID0EBDGA){#A-10.1_en_25}\[Note:
      Subsection 10(2) in force July 5, 2000, *see* SI/2000-59.\]
      :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by order made on the
  recommendation of the Minister of Transport, increase the three years
  referred to in paragraph (5)(b) to a maximum of four years in respect
  of a route served, or an office or facility from which service is
  provided, by a subsidiary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of replacements

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If Canadian Airlines International Ltd., Canadian
  Regional Airlines Ltd. or a subsidiary of the Corporation replaces the
  Corporation or one of its subsidiaries in providing an air service,
  including incidental services, that the Corporation or the subsidiary
  provided on or after December 21, 1999, the Corporation has the duty
  to ensure that any of the customers of the person who replaces the
  Corporation or the subsidiary can communicate with that person in
  respect of those services, and obtain those services from that person,
  in either official language in any case where those services, if
  provided by the Corporation or the subsidiary, would be required under
  Part IV of the [Official Languages Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01) or under
  subsection (2) to be provided in either official language.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsections (2) and (7) do not
  affect any duty that the Corporation may have under section 25 of the
  [Official Languages Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed duty

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of Parts VIII, IX and X of the
  [Official Languages Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01), the duties referred to in
  subsections (2) and (7) are deemed to be duties under Part IV of that
  Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} The definitions in this subsection apply in this
  section.

  [[air service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[air service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 55(1) of the [Canada Transportation
      Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4). ([service aérien]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[customer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[customer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a subsidiary
      referred to in subsection (2) or (7), a passenger, shipper or
      consignee using or intending to use an air service, including
      incidental services, provided or made available by that
      subsidiary. ([client]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[incidental services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[incidental services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} include, in respect of
      a subsidiary referred to in subsection (2) or (7),

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ticketing and reservation services;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information, including notices and
        announcements, that it publishes or causes to be published to
        inform its customers in respect of its routes or tariffs;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} services provided or made available to
        customers at an airport, including the control of passengers
        embarking and disembarking aircraft, announcements directed at
        customers and counter services; and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} services related to baggage or freight claims
        and client relations. ([services connexes]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[route]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[route]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a subsidiary of
      the Corporation, a route on which the subsidiary provides a
      two-way air service between the starting and finishing points of
      that service by a single conveyance, with or without intermediate
      stops. ([trajet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
      6 of the [Canada Transportation
      Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4). ([expéditeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (4th Supp.), s. 10
- 2000, c. 15, s. 18
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed approval

- **[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The proposed acquisition described
  in a letter dated December 21, 1999 from 853350 Alberta Ltd. and Air
  Canada to the Minister of Transport is deemed to be a transaction that
  has been approved by the Governor in Council under subsection 53.2(7)
  of the [Canada Transportation Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4) on the day on
  which that subsection comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed terms and conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The undertakings provided by 853350 Alberta Ltd. and
  Air Canada to the Minister of Transport in the letter referred to in
  subsection (1) are deemed to be terms and conditions specified in an
  approval by the Governor in Council under subsection 53.2(7) of the
  [Canada Transportation Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4) that relate to national
  transportation concerns, and the undertakings provided by 853350
  Alberta Ltd. and Air Canada to the Commissioner of Competition that
  are set out in Annex A to a letter from the Commissioner dated
  December 21, 1999 in respect of the acquisition referred to in that
  subsection are deemed to be terms and conditions of an approval under
  subsection 53.2(7) of the [Canada Transportation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4) that relate to potential prevention or
  lessening of competition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed affiliates

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of sections 45 and 61 of the
  [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34), Canadian Airlines Corporation,
  Canadian Airlines International Ltd. and Canadian Regional Airlines
  Ltd. are deemed to be affiliates of Air Canada in respect of any thing
  they do after December 21, 1999 and before the earlier of the coming
  into force of this subsection and the day on which the undertakings
  referred to in subsection (2) cease to have effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If undertakings cease to have effect

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by order, declare that
  853350 Alberta Ltd. and Air Canada are not subject to the terms and
  conditions referred to in subsection (2) if the undertakings cease to
  have effect and are not revived in the circumstances described in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the paragraph entitled "Effective Date" in the
    letter referred to in subsection (1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} undertaking #15, set out in Annex A referred to in
    subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of deemed approval

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the Governor in Council makes an order under
  subsection (4), the deemed approval under subsection (1) is revoked
  and any certification under paragraph 94(c) of the [Competition
  Act](/eng/acts/C-34) in respect of the acquisition referred to in that
  paragraph ceases to have effect.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 15, s. 19
- 2007, c. 19, s. 59

## [Transitional, Consequential, Repeal and Coming into Force Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1371 .Part}

### [Transitional]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1372 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation in office

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  members of the Board of Directors of the Corporation who were
  appointed pursuant to the Air Canada Act and held office immediately
  prior to the day on which the Corporation becomes a corporation to
  which the [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44) applies
  continue to hold office according to the terms of their appointment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors cease to hold office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Board of Directors of the
  Corporation cease to hold office at the close of the first annual
  meeting of shareholders of the Corporation held after the issuance
  date, which meeting shall be held not later than six months following
  the end of the Corporation's financial year in which that date falls.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No right to compensation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person has any right to claim or receive any
  compensation, damages, indemnity or other form of relief from Her
  Majesty in right of Canada or any servant or agent thereof for ceasing
  to hold office pursuant to, or for the abolition of that office by,
  this Act.

- Definition of [[issuance date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section and section 12, [[issuance
  date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the date on which shares of the
  Corporation are first issued after the coming into force of those
  sections to any person, other than the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (4th Supp.), s. 11
- 1994, c. 24, s. 34(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualification of shares

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of qualifying the shares of the Corporation

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as an authorized investment under paragraph 86(n)
    of the Canadian and British Insurance Companies Act, paragraph
    61(1)(j) of the Loan Companies Act or paragraph 78(1)(j) of the
    Trust Companies Act,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as a permitted investment under paragraph 1(n) of
    Schedule III to the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
    1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} as assets that may be vested in trust in Canada
    under paragraph 1(n) of Schedule II to the Canadian and British
    Insurance Companies Act or paragraph 1(n) of the schedule to the
    Foreign Insurance Companies Act,

  the Corporation is deemed to have satisfied the requirements of those
  paragraphs with respect to each of the five years immediately
  preceding the issuance date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualification of debt obligations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of qualifying the bonds, debentures
  or other evidences of indebtedness of the Corporation

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as an authorized investment under subparagraph
    86(k)(i) of the Canadian and British Insurance Companies Act,
    paragraph 61(1)(g) of the Loan Companies Act or paragraph 78(1)(g)
    of the Trust Companies Act,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as a permitted investment under paragraph 1(m) of
    Schedule III to the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
    1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} as assets that may be vested in trust in Canada
    under subparagraph 1(k)(i) of Schedule II to the Canadian and
    British Insurance Companies Act or subparagraph 1(k)(i) of the
    schedule to the Foreign Insurance Companies Act,

  the Corporation is deemed to have satisfied the requirements of the
  paragraphs referred to in subsection (1) with respect to each of the
  five years immediately preceding the issuance date.

### [Repeal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1399 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Repeal of R.S., c.
A-10]{.HistoricalNote}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Air Canada Act is repealed on the day on which the Corporation becomes
  a corporation to which the [Canada Business Corporations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-44) applies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Director to give notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Director is not required to comply with
  subsection 187(6) of the [Canada Business Corporations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-44) in respect of the Corporation, but the Director
  shall, on issuing the certificate of continuance of the Corporation,
  cause a notice to be published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) setting out the date on which the
  certificate was issued and on which the Air Canada Act was repealed.

- 1994, c. 24, s. 34(F)
