An Act respecting animal pedigree associations

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-2837 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Animal Pedigree Act](/eng/acts/A-11.2).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-2840 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[animal pedigree association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[animal pedigree association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
    [[association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an association
    incorporated under this Act and includes an association amalgamated
    pursuant to section 25 and an association subject to the provisions
    of this Act by virtue of section 68; ([association responsable de la
    généalogie des animaux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou
    [association]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Board of Directors of the
    Corporation; ([Conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[certificate of identification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certificate of identification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    certificate of identification issued under the authority of this Act
    for an animal of an evolving breed; ([certificat
    d'identification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[certificate of registration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certificate of registration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    certificate of registration issued under the authority of this Act
    for an animal of a distinct breed; ([certificat
    d'enregistrement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Livestock
    Records Corporation established by section
    35; ([Société]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[embryo certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[embryo certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an embryo
    certificate issued under the authority of this Act; ([certificat
    d'embryon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[evolving breed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[evolving breed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a group of animals in
    the process of evolving into a new breed; ([race en voie de
    constitution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[foundation stock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foundation stock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a distinct
    breed, means such animals as are recognized by the Minister as
    constituting the breed's original stock; ([premiers
    éléments]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[identification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[identification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the recognition that an
    animal is an animal of an evolving
    breed; ([identification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[individual identification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[individual identification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an
    animal, means the distinguishing of the animal by

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the marking or tagging of the animal, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the noting, by any means, including the taking of
      photographs, of some or all of the animal's physical
      characteristics; ([identification particulière]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Agriculture
    and Agri-Food; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pedigree]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pedigree]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an animal, means
    genealogical information showing the ancestral line of descent of
    the animal; ([dossier généalogique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by the
    regulations; ([prescrit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[purebred]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[purebred]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an animal of a
    distinct breed, means an animal that is a purebred of the breed as
    determined by the by-laws of the association authorized to register
    animals of that breed; ([animal de race pure]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[registration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the recognition that an
    animal is an animal of a distinct
    breed; ([enregistrement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[semen certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[semen certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a semen certificate
    issued under the authority of this Act. ([certificat de
    semence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Purposes of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-2863 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purposes of Act

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purposes of this Act
are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to promote breed improvement, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to protect persons who raise and purchase animals

by providing for the establishment of animal pedigree associations that
are authorized to register and identify animals that, in the opinion of
the Minister, have significant value.

## [Purpose of Associations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-2869 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principal purpose

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The principal purpose of
animal pedigree associations shall be the registration and
identification of animals and the keeping of animal pedigrees.

## [Scope of Associations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-2872 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scope of associations

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  association may be incorporated under this Act in respect of a
  distinct breed, an evolving breed or one or more distinct breeds and
  one or more evolving breeds.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit of one association per breed or
  evolving breed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Only one association may be incorporated in respect
  of each distinct breed and each evolving breed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Breeds must be of same species

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an association is incorporated in respect of
  one or more distinct breeds and one or more evolving breeds, the
  distinct breeds and evolving breeds must all be of the same species.

## [Incorporating an Association]{.HTitleText1} {#h-2880 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  association may be incorporated under this Act only if the Minister is
  satisfied

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the animals of each distinct breed and
    evolving breed in respect of which the association is sought to be
    incorporated have significant value;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the persons submitting the articles of
    incorporation in respect of the association represent the breeders
    throughout Canada of the animals of each distinct breed and evolving
    breed in respect of which the association is sought to be
    incorporated; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that the keeping of pedigrees and other records in
    respect of the animals of each distinct breed and evolving breed in
    respect of which the association is sought to be incorporated would
    be beneficial to the breeders thereof and to the public-at-large.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scientific genetic principles

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An association may be incorporated in respect of a
  distinct breed only if the Minister is satisfied that the breed is a
  breed determined in accordance with scientific genetic principles.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special requirement with respect to
  evolving breeds

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An association may be incorporated in respect of an
  evolving breed only if the Minister is satisfied that the requirements
  referred to in subsection (1) exist and that the creation, with
  genetic stability, of the new breed into which the animals of the
  evolving breed are intended to evolve is possible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to incorporate

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this Act, where five or more qualified persons desire to
  incorporate an association, they may make an application for that
  purpose by submitting articles of incorporation to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications of applicants

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person is qualified to apply to form an association
  if the person is eighteen years of age or more and is a Canadian
  citizen or a permanent resident within the meaning of subsection 2(1)
  of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 8 (4th Supp.), s. 7
- 2001, c. 27, s. 204
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of articles of incorporation

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Articles of incorporation
must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be in the prescribed form;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out, in respect of the proposed association,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the association,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the names and addresses of the persons submitting
    the articles of incorporation,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the names and addresses of the first directors
    and officers of the association,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the name of each distinct breed and evolving breed
    in respect of which the association is sought to be incorporated,
    and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} where the application is in respect of an evolving
    breed, the physical description and genetic make-up of the new breed
    into which the animals of the evolving breed are intended to evolve;
    and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be submitted in triplicate in the prescribed manner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of incorporation

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Minister is satisfied that the articles of incorporation of an
  association should be approved, the Minister shall issue a certificate
  of incorporation in respect thereof.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Endorsement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister issues a certificate of
  incorporation, the Minister shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cause to be endorsed on each copy of the articles
    of incorporation a statement attesting that a certificate of
    incorporation has been issued in respect thereof;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cause two copies of the endorsed articles of
    incorporation to be filed in the Department of Agriculture and
    Agri-Food;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} cause the remaining copy of the endorsed articles
    of incorporation to be returned to the association; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} cause a notice of the issuance of the certificate
    of incorporation to be published in the [Canada
    Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of certificate

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An association comes into existence on the date shown
  in its certificate of incorporation and the first directors and
  officers of the association are the directors and officers set out in
  the association's articles of incorporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 8 (4th Supp.), s. 9
- 1994, c. 38, s. 26
:::

## [Body Corporate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-2921 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Association is body corporate

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every association is a
body corporate.

## [Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-2924 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An association may do any
act or thing necessary for, or incidental or conducive to, the carrying
out of its purpose and business and affairs related thereto.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ownership and borrowing powers

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Without restricting the
generality of section 11, an association may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} acquire, hold and dispose of real, personal, movable
  and immovable property necessary for the carrying out of its business
  and affairs;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} borrow money on the credit of the association and
  draw, make, accept, endorse and execute promissory notes, bills of
  exchange and other negotiable instruments necessary for carrying out
  its business and affairs; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} mortgage or hypothecate, or create any security
  interest in, all or any property of the association to secure any
  obligation of the association.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 8 (4th Supp.), s. 12
- 2001, c. 4, s. 56
:::

## [Use of Funds]{.HTitleText1} {#h-2934 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of funds

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  association may use its funds for any purpose calculated to advance
  the purpose of the association, including, without restricting the
  generality of the foregoing, using those funds for developing and
  promoting breed improvement programs, making grants to exhibitions and
  providing services to the breeders of the animals in respect of which
  the association has been incorporated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Profits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any by-laws providing for the remuneration
  of its directors, officers and employees, and its agents or
  mandataries, all profits or accretions of value to the property of an
  association shall be used in furtherance of the purpose of the
  association, and no part of the property or profits of the association
  may be distributed, directly or indirectly, to any member of the
  association.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1985, c. 8 (4th Supp.), s. 13
- 2004, c. 25, s. 2

## [Liability]{.HTitleText1} {#h-2942 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limited liability

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  financial liability of a member of an association to the creditors of
  the association is limited to the amount of any fees owing to the
  association by the member and to the amount due from the member in
  respect of any services provided to the member by the association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal liability

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No director, officer or employee of an association,
  or other person acting on behalf of an association, is personally
  liable for any act done in good faith in the exercise of any of that
  person's powers or the performance of that person's duties and
  functions or for any default or neglect in good faith in the exercise
  of any of that person's powers or the performance of that person's
  duties and functions.

## [By-laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-2948 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory by-laws

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  association incorporated under this Act shall, within one year after
  coming into existence, make by-laws

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establishing requirements for membership in the
    association;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the payment of dues by the members of
    the association and establishing the method of determining the
    amount thereof;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the election, and the filling of
    vacancies, of directors and officers of the association and their
    powers, duties and functions;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the time and place for the holding of
    general, annual and special meetings of the association, the calling
    of such meetings, the quorum for such meetings and the procedure for
    all business at such meetings;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} respecting the consultation of the members of the
    association for the purpose of changing the association's articles
    of incorporation, amalgamating with one or more other associations
    or dissolving the association;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} respecting the making, amendment and repeal of the
    association's by-laws;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} respecting the place within Canada where the head
    office of the association is, and the places within Canada where the
    branch offices, if any, of the association are, to be situated;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} fixing the fiscal year of the association;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} respecting the keeping of the association's
    articles of incorporation and the association's by-laws, business
    records and books of accounts;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} respecting the annual audit of the financial
    affairs of the association and the preparation of audited annual
    financial reports and statements of assets and liabilities;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} establishing rules respecting the eligibility for
    the registration or identification, as the case may be, of animals
    by the association;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} establishing the procedure to be followed in
    applications for the registration or identification, as the case may
    be, of animals by the association;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} respecting the issuance of certificates of
    registration or certificates of identification, as the case may be,
    by the association and the amendment, transfer and cancellation of
    such certificates;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} respecting the individual identification of animals
    registered or identified, as the case may be, by the association,
    the supervision of all practices in relation thereto and the manner
    in which unsatisfactory practices in relation thereto are to be
    dealt with;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} respecting the keeping of pedigrees and breeding
    records by the association and its members and the inspection by the
    association of pedigrees and breeding records kept by its members;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} respecting the recording of transfers of ownership
    of animals registered or identified, as the case may be, by the
    association;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} respecting the payment of fees for certificates of
    registration and certificates of identification, as the case may be,
    for transfers of ownership and for all other services provided by
    the association, and establishing the method of determining the
    amount of those fees; and

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} for the administration and management of the
    business and affairs of the association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws generally

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), an association may
  make any by-law necessary for the conduct of its business and affairs,
  including, without restricting the generality of the foregoing,
  by-laws

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the promotion and establishment of breed
    improvement programs;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the inspection of animals as a
    prerequisite to their registration or identification, as the case
    may be, by the association;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting performance standards that animals must
    meet as a prerequisite to their registration or identification, as
    the case may be, by the association; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the resignation, suspension and
    expulsion of any member of the association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory by-laws

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an association keeps records of semen and
  embryos pursuant to section 33 and issues semen certificates and
  embryo certificates in respect thereof, the association shall do so
  only in accordance with its by-laws, and, for that purpose, the
  association shall make by-laws

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the recording and the individual
    identification of semen and embryos, the supervision of all
    practices in relation thereto and the manner in which unsatisfactory
    practices in relation thereto are to be dealt with;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the issuance of semen certificates and
    embryo certificates, and the amendment, transfer and cancellation of
    those certificates; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the recording of transfers of ownership
    of semen and embryos by the association.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When by-law has effect

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  by-law of an association and no amendment or repeal of any by-law of
  an association has any effect until it is approved by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for approval

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application to the Minister for approval of a
  by-law or an amendment or repeal of a by-law must be accompanied by
  three copies of the proposed by-law, amendment or repeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may require evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the making, amendment or repeal of any by-law
  of an association is, by reason of another by-law of the association,
  subject to any formality or requirement, the Minister may, before
  approving any by-law or any amendment or repeal of a by-law, require
  evidence by affidavit or statutory declaration that the formality or
  requirement in relation thereto has been complied with.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of by-law approval

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister approves a by-law or an amendment
  or repeal of a by-law, the Minister shall issue a certificate of
  by-law approval, or a certificate of by-law repeal, as the case may
  be, in respect thereof.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of by-laws

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The by-laws of an
association bind every member of the association.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Member's right to inspect by-laws

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The by-laws of an
association shall, at all reasonable times, be made available to the
members of the association for the purposes of inspection and making
copies thereof.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No fine or penalty

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  by-law of an association may impose any fine or monetary penalty of
  any kind and no fine or monetary penalty of any kind may be imposed by
  an association in respect of any non-compliance with its by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees not fines

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a fee referred to
  in paragraph 15(1)(q) is not a fine or a monetary penalty.

## [Amendment of Articles of Incorporation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-2999 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of articles of incorporation

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  association that desires to amend its articles of incorporation for
  one or more of the following purposes, namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to change the association's name,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to add the name of a distinct breed or evolving
    breed in respect of which the association is to be authorized to
    register or identify the animals thereof or to delete the name of
    any distinct breed or evolving breed that the association is
    authorized to register or identify the animals thereof, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the association is incorporated in respect of
    an evolving breed, to transform the association into an association
    incorporated in respect of a distinct breed,

  may make an application for that purpose by submitting articles of
  amendment to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of articles of amendment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Articles of amendment submitted pursuant to
  subsection (1) must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be in the prescribed form;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out the proposed amendment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} contain a statutory declaration attesting that the
    members of the association have been consulted in writing in the
    prescribed manner in relation to the proposed amendment, that
    twenty-five per cent or more of the members have responded in
    writing to the consultation and that at least two thirds of the
    members who responded have approved the proposed amendment; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be submitted in triplicate in the prescribed
    manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof required

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the amendment proposed in articles of amendment
  is the addition of the name of a distinct breed or evolving breed in
  accordance with paragraph (1)(b), the articles of amendment must be
  accompanied by proof that the association represents the breeders
  throughout Canada of the animals of the distinct breed or evolving
  breed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deletion for certain purposes

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an association is authorized to register or identify animals of more
  than one distinct breed or evolving breed, the association or one or
  more members of the association may submit articles of amendment to
  delete the name of the distinct breed or evolving breed from the
  association's articles of incorporation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to allow for the making of an application for
    incorporation in respect of one of the distinct breeds or evolving
    breeds; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to enable another association to amend its articles
    of incorporation to add the name of one of the distinct breeds or
    evolving breeds.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Articles of amendment submitted pursuant to
  subsection (1) must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be in prescribed form;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out the proposed amendment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} contain a statutory declaration attesting that the
    members of the association who are breeders of the distinct breed or
    evolving breed to which the proposed amendment relates have been
    consulted in writing in the prescribed manner in relation to the
    proposed amendment and that twenty-five per cent or more of those
    members have responded in writing to the consultation and that at
    least two thirds of the members who responded have approved the
    proposed amendment; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be submitted in the prescribed manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may determine entitlement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where there is any dispute as to who is entitled to
  be consulted in relation to articles of amendment submitted pursuant
  to subsection (1), any member of the association may request that the
  Minister make a determination thereof and any determination of the
  Minister is final and binding on all concerned.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other documents must accompany

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall
consider articles of amendment submitted pursuant to section 21 only if
the articles of amendment are accompanied by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application for incorporation in respect of the
  distinct breed or evolving breed to which the articles of amendment
  relate, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} articles of amendment to add the name of the distinct
  breed or evolving breed to which the articles of amendment relate to
  another association's articles of incorporation

and the Minister may approve the articles of amendment only if the
Minister is satisfied that the application for incorporation or the

## [Amalgamations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-3058 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amalgamation of associations

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Two or
  more associations that desire to amalgamate and continue as one
  association may make an application for that purpose by submitting
  articles of amalgamation to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of articles of amalgamation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Articles of amalgamation must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out the names of the associations that desire
    to amalgamate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be in the prescribed form;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set out, in respect of the proposed amalgamated
    association,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the association,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of every distinct breed and evolving
      breed in respect of which the association is deemed to be
      incorporated, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the association's proposed by-laws in respect
      of the matters referred to in paragraphs 15(1)(a) to (q);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} contain a statutory declaration attesting that the
    members of each of the associations that desire to amalgamate have
    been consulted in writing in the prescribed manner in relation to
    the proposed amalgamation and that, with respect to each
    association, twenty-five per cent or more of the members have
    responded in writing to the consultation and that at least two
    thirds of the members who responded have approved the proposed
    amalgamation; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be submitted in triplicate in the prescribed
    manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attached declarations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Articles of amalgamation submitted to the Minister
  must be accompanied by a sworn statutory declaration by a director of
  each amalgamating association stating that the director believes on
  reasonable grounds

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the value of the assets of the director's
    association exceeds the value of the association's liabilities;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that no creditor of the director's association will
    be prejudiced by the amalgamation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that adequate notice of the proposed amalgamation
    has been given to all known creditors of the director's association
    and that no creditor objects to the amalgamation for other than
    frivolous or vexatious grounds.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adequate notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (3)(c), adequate notice
  of a proposed amalgamation is given if a notice stating that an
  association intends to amalgamate with one or more specified
  associations and that creditors of the association may object to the
  amalgamation by filing an objection in writing with the association
  within thirty days after the date of the notice is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sent to each known creditor of the association
    whose claim against the association exceeds one thousand dollars;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} published once in a newspaper published or
    distributed in the place where the head office of the association is
    situated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of amalgamation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister is satisfied that articles of
  amalgamation should be approved, the Minister shall issue a
  certificate of amalgamation in respect thereof.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Endorsement

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister issues a certificate of
  amalgamation, the Minister shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cause to be endorsed on each copy of the articles
    of amalgamation a statement attesting that a certificate of
    amalgamation has been issued in respect thereof;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cause two copies of the endorsed articles of
    amalgamation to be filed in the Department of Agriculture and
    Agri-Food;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} cause the remaining copy of the endorsed articles
    of amalgamation to be returned to the amalgamated association; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} cause a notice of the issuance of the certificate
    of amalgamation to be published in the [Canada
    Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of certificate

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} On the date shown in a certificate of amalgamation

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amalgamation of the amalgamating associations
    becomes effective and the amalgamated association comes into
    existence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the property of each amalgamating association
    becomes the property of the amalgamated association;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amalgamated association is liable for the
    obligations of each amalgamating association;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} every existing cause of action, claim or liability
    to prosecution of or against an amalgamating association is
    unaffected;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} every civil, criminal or administrative action or
    proceeding pending by or against an amalgamating association may be
    prosecuted by or against the amalgamated association; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a conviction against, or ruling, order or judgment
    in favour of or against, an amalgamating association may be enforced
    by or against the amalgamated association.

## [Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-3101 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** The registration of
animals of a distinct breed shall be in accordance with the by-laws of
the association incorporated in respect of that distinct breed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility for registration

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The by-laws of an
association relating to the registration of animals of a distinct breed
by the association may only provide for the registration of animals
whose inheritance traces back to the foundation stock of the distinct
breed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificates of registration

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  association that registers an animal may issue a certificate of
  registration to evidence the registration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate is proof

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless cancelled, and in the absence of any proof to
  the contrary, a certificate of registration in respect of an animal
  named therein is proof that the animal is of the breed referred to in
  the certificate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and contents of certificates of
  registration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A certificate of registration issued by an
  association shall be in such form and contain such information as the
  association requires by its by-laws, but every certificate of
  registration must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the association;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the animal's name and particulars of the animal's
    individual identification;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the animal's date of birth, sex and registration
    number;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the names and registration numbers of the animal's
    parents or, if the name and registration number of one of the
    parents are unknown, a statement to that effect; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name of the animal's owner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the information referred to in
  subsection (3), a certificate of registration issued by an association
  in respect of an animal of a distinct breed for which purebred is
  defined in the association's by-laws must also set out the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the animal is a purebred, a statement to that
    effect; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the animal is other than a purebred, its
    percentage of purebred inheritance.

## [Purebred Status]{.HTitleText1} {#h-3122 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purebred status

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  determination of what is a purebred of a distinct breed shall be in
  accordance with the by-laws of the association incorporated in respect
  of that distinct breed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum inheritance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No association may, by its by-laws, determine that an
  animal is a purebred of a distinct breed if the animal has less than
  seven-eighths of its inheritance from the foundation stock of the
  animal's breed or from animals previously registered as purebreds by
  the association.

## [Recognition of New Breeds]{.HTitleText1} {#h-3153 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for recognition of new breed

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an association incorporated in respect of an evolving breed determines
  that animals it has identified have evolved to the point of
  constituting the new breed into which they were intended to evolve,
  the association may request that the Minister examine the animals with
  a view to recognizing the new breed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Articles of amendment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any association making a request under subsection (1)
  may, at the time of making the request or at any time thereafter, file
  with the Minister articles of amendment to transform the association
  into an association incorporated in respect of the new breed to which
  the request relates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recognition

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister is satisfied that the animals that
  are the subject of a request under subsection (1) have evolved into
  the new breed and that there are sufficient numbers of such animals to
  constitute the foundation stock of the new breed, the Minister may
  recognize the new breed and, if articles of amendment referred to in
  subsection (2) have been filed, issue a certificate of amendment in
  respect thereof.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister must be satisfied

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In determining whether animals have evolved into a
  new breed the Minister must be satisfied that the animals bear a
  physical resemblance to and have the same genetic make-up as the breed
  into which the animals were intended to evolve, and that the animals
  have been reproducing with genetic stability.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Association may continue to identify
  animals

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding that a certificate of amendment has
  been issued to an association in respect of articles of amendment
  referred to in subsection (2), the Minister may, for such period as
  the Minister may specify, authorize the association to continue to
  identify animals for the purposes of adding, subject to the Minister's
  approval, to the new breed's foundation stock.

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  association shall cease to be a member of the Corporation if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the association gives the Corporation notice of its
    intention to cease being a member at least ninety days prior to the
    effective date thereof or such longer notice as the by-laws of the
    Corporation may require; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the association has no outstanding debt owing to
    the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records to be returned

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an association ceases to be a member of the
  Corporation, the Corporation shall, on request therefor, provide the
  association with all of the Corporation's records relating to that
  association.

### [Annual Meeting]{.HTitleText2} {#h-3271 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual meeting

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** The members of the
Corporation shall meet at least once a year.

### [Liability]{.HTitleText2} {#h-3274 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal liability

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** No member of the Board,
General Manager of the Corporation or officer or employee of the
Corporation, or other person acting on behalf of the Corporation, is
personally liable for any act done in good faith in the exercise of any
of that person's powers or the performance of that person's duties and
functions or for any default or neglect in good faith in the exercise of
any of that person's powers or the performance of that person's duties
and functions.

### [General Stud and Herd Book]{.HTitleText2} {#h-3277 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General Stud and Herd Book

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} authorize the Corporation to exercise the powers
    referred to in section 37 in relation to such distinct breeds and
    evolving breeds as the Minister, applying scientific genetic
    principles, may designate and for which there is no association;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} authorize the Corporation to establish a General
    Stud and Herd Book for the keeping of pedigree records of the
    distinct breeds and evolving breeds referred to in paragraph (a);
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} establish rules of eligibility for registration and
    identification of animals in the General Stud and Herd Book.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records to be handed over

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the incorporation of an association in respect of
  a distinct breed or evolving breed referred to in subsection (1), the
  Corporation shall hand over to the association all records that have
  been kept in relation thereto.

## [Administration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-3286 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspections

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, at any time, undertake such inspections and examinations
  of the business and affairs of any association, or of the Corporation,
  as the Minister deems appropriate, including, without restricting the
  generality of the foregoing, inspections into and examinations of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which the registration or
    identification, as the case may be, of animals is carried out by any
    association or by the Corporation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which the individual identification
    of animals is carried out and supervised by any association or by
    the Corporation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the private breeding records of any member of an
    association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to require information and
  documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the course of an inspection or examination
  pursuant to subsection (1), the Minister may require any person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to furnish any information that, in the opinion of
    the Minister, the person may be able to furnish in relation to the
    matter being inspected or examined; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to produce, for examination by the Minister, any
    document, paper or thing that, in the opinion of the Minister,
    relates to the matter being inspected or examined and that may be in
    the possession or under the control of that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of document, etc.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any document, paper or thing produced by any person
  pursuant to this section shall be returned to that person by the
  Minister within ten days after a request is made to the Minister by
  that person, but nothing in this section precludes the Minister from
  again requiring its production in accordance with this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to make copies

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may make copies of any document, paper
  or thing produced pursuant to this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to enter

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the Minister may,
  at any reasonable time, enter any premises occupied by any association
  or by the Corporation and carry out therein an inspection or
  examination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant required to enter
  dwelling-house

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where the premises referred to in subsection (5) are
  a dwelling-house, the Minister or any person acting on the Minister's
  behalf may not enter that dwelling-house without the consent of the
  occupant except under the authority of a warrant issued under
  subsection (7).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Where on [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application
  a justice of the peace is satisfied by information on oath

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that entry to the dwelling-house is necessary for
    any purpose related to the carrying out of an inspection or
    examination, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that entry to the dwelling-house has been refused
    or that there are reasonable grounds for believing that entry
    thereto will be refused,

  the justice may issue a warrant under the justice's hand authorizing
  the Minister or a person acting on the Minister's behalf and named
  therein to enter that dwelling-house subject to such conditions as may
  be specified in the warrant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inquiries

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may appoint
a person to hold an inquiry into the manner in which any association is
or has been conducting its business, and every person so appointed has,
for the purposes of the inquiry, all the powers of a commissioner under
Part I of the [Inquiries Act](/eng/acts/I-11).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** On the conclusion of an
inspection or examination pursuant to section 52 or an inquiry under
section 53, the Minister may take, or may, by order, direct any
association or the Corporation to take, such action as the Minister
considers necessary.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions to associations

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** On being satisfied that
an association has for any period failed to conduct its business and
affairs in accordance with the provisions of its by-laws or this Act, or
has failed for a period of twelve months to carry on its business and
affairs, the Minister may, by order, make such direction to the
association as seems proper to the Minister in the interest of the
association's purpose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to comply

**[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** Where a direction of the
Minister under section 54 or 55 contains a time limit and an association
fails to carry out the direction within that time limit, the Minister
may take over the property and carry on the business and affairs of the
association for such time as the Minister considers appropriate.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-3318 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may make
regulations for carrying out the purposes and provisions of this Act
and, without limiting the generality of the foregoing, may make
regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that by this Act is to be
  prescribed; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the dissolution of associations under this
  Act.

## [Dissolution]{.HTitleText1} {#h-3323 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may order dissolution

- **[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by order, declare the corporate powers of any
  association to be terminated in any of the following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the association has failed to make by-laws in
    accordance with section 15 within one year after coming into
    existence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the Minister is satisfied that the
    association has failed for any period to conduct its business and
    affairs in accordance with the provisions of its by-laws or this
    Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the Minister is satisfied that the
    association has failed for a period of twelve months to carry on
    business;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the association has failed to carry out any
    direction of the Minister under section 54 or 55 in the time, if
    any, specified in the direction; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} on petition therefor by the association supported
    by a resolution to that effect passed by at least two thirds of the
    members of the association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dissolution in accordance with
  regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister declares the corporate powers of
  an association to be terminated, the affairs of the association shall
  be wound up and the association shall be dissolved in accordance with
  regulations made pursuant to paragraph 57(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution of assets

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an association is dissolved, any property of
  the association that remains after the payment of the association's
  debts and liabilities shall be transferred to such other association
  with a similar purpose or to such charity as the Minister may specify
  in writing.

## [Miscellaneous]{.HTitleText1} {#h-3336 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration on behalf of an association

- **[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  association may, with the consent of the Minister, authorize any other
  association to register or identify, on the association's behalf, the
  animals of any distinct breed or evolving breed in respect of which
  the association is incorporated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an association is authorized to register or
  identify animals on behalf of another association, the association
  shall do so in accordance with the other association's by-laws
  relating to registration and identification.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ability to contract

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in subsection (1) shall preclude any
  association from entering into any other contract or arrangement for
  the normal conduct of its business and affairs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of meetings and annual report

**[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** Every association shall
send to the Minister

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the same time and in the same manner as it sends
  to its members, notices of meetings setting out proposed amendments to
  its by-laws; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} immediately after each annual meeting, a copy of the
  annual report, including an audited financial statement, together with
  a list of the directors and officers of the association and, where the
  association is a member of the Corporation, the name or names of the
  association's voting representatives.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration and other rights

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding anything
in the by-laws of an association, no person shall be denied the right to
have registered, to have identified or to transfer the ownership of any
animal unless that person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is, at the time the right is denied, in arrears of
  any fees owing to the association; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} has contravened

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a by-law of the association relating to

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the eligibility for registration or
      identification, as the case may be, of animals by the association,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the individual identification of animals, or

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the keeping of private breeding records,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any provision of this Act or the regulations, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any provision of the [Health of Animals
    Act](/eng/acts/H-3.3) or the regulations thereunder relating to the
    identification, within the meaning of that Act, or testing of
    animals.

- R.S., 1985, c. 8 (4th Supp.), s. 61
- 1994, c. 38, s. 13

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indication of Minister's approval

- **[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Minister, on the completion of an inspection or examination
  pursuant to paragraph 52(1)(a), is satisfied that animals are being
  registered or identified, as the case may be, by an association in a
  manner that is acceptable to the Minister, the Minister may authorize
  the association to show on its certificates that the association's
  system of registration or identification, as the case may be, has been
  approved by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An approval referred to in subsection (1) must be in
  a form satisfactory to the Minister and the Minister may at any time
  and for any reason order an association to cease using the approval on
  its certificates.

## [Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-3365 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No person shall keep records

- **[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as authorized by this Act, where an association is authorized by this
  Act to register or identify animals of a distinct breed or evolving
  breed, no person shall keep pedigrees in respect of animals of that
  distinct breed or evolving breed or issue any document purporting to
  evidence that a particular animal is of that distinct breed or
  evolving breed or so closely resembling a certificate of registration,
  or certificate of identification, as the case may be, that it would
  likely be mistaken for such a certificate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No person shall issue document

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall issue in respect of any animal any
  document of any kind likely to deceive the public into believing that
  the document is a certificate of registration or certificate of
  identification in respect of the animal or that the animal is
  registered or identified under the authority of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibitions

**[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** No person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} knowingly sign or present, or cause or procure to be
  signed or presented, to a recording officer of any association or of
  the Corporation any declaration or application in relation to the
  registration, identification or transfer of ownership of any animal,
  semen or embryo that contains any material false statement or
  representation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} knowingly represent that a certificate of
  registration or certificate of identification applies to an animal
  other than the one in respect of which it was issued;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} knowingly represent that a semen certificate or
  embryo certificate applies to any semen or embryo other than the semen
  or embryo in respect of which it was issued;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} falsify or alter any certificate of registration,
  certificate of identification, semen certificate or embryo
  certificate;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} without an express statement that the animal's
  registration or identification is from a jurisdiction other than
  Canada, offer to sell, contract to sell or sell, as registered or
  identified, or as eligible to be registered or identified, within or
  outside the meaning of this Act, any animal that is not registered or
  identified, or eligible to be registered or identified;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} offer to sell, contract to sell or sell, as recorded,
  or as eligible to be recorded, in the books of any association, or of
  the Corporation, any semen or embryo that is not recorded or eligible
  to be recorded in those books;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} knowingly offer to sell, contract to sell or sell any
  animal in a manner that is likely to create an erroneous impression
  that the animal is registered or eligible to be registered;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} offer to sell, contract to sell or sell, as a
  purebred of a breed, any animal that is not registered or eligible to
  be registered as a purebred by the association authorized to register
  animals of that breed or by the Corporation;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} without an express statement that the animal's
  registration, identification or status as a purebred is from a
  jurisdiction other than Canada, offer to sell, contract to sell or
  sell, as registered or identified, or as a purebred, any animal for
  which there is no individual identification in accordance with the
  by-laws of the association that has registered or identified the
  animal; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} without an express statement that the animal's
  registration, identification or status as a purebred is from a
  jurisdiction other than Canada and that the animal will not be
  registered or identified in Canada by the person, sell, as registered
  or identified, or as eligible to be registered or identified, or as a
  purebred, any animal without providing to the buyer thereof within six
  months after the sale the animal's duly transferred certificate of
  registration or certificate of identification.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unlawful use of names

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** No person shall, without
lawful authority, use the name of the Corporation or the name of any
association or any name so clearly resembling the name of the
Corporation or the name of an association in a manner that is likely to
deceive the public.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

- **[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who contravenes any provision of this Act or the regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
    conviction and liable to a fine not exceeding twenty-five thousand
    dollars; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an indictable offence and liable to a
    fine not exceeding fifty thousand dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value to be considered

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In determining the fine in relation to an offence
  under any of sections 63 to 65, the judge making the determination
  shall take into account the value, or purported value, of the animal,
  semen or embryo to which the offence relates.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time for complaint

**[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** The provisions of the
[Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) prescribing a time limit for making a
complaint or laying an information in respect of offences punishable on
summary conviction do not apply to proceedings in respect of an offence
under this Act.

## [Existing Associations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-3394 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  provisions of this Act apply to every association to which the
  Livestock Pedigree Act applied immediately before the coming into
  force of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed articles of association

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application filed with the Department of
  Agriculture of every association referred to in subsection (1) is
  deemed to constitute the association's articles of incorporation for
  the purposes of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed authority

**[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 70 to
73, every association referred to in section 68 is deemed to be
authorized to register or identify animals of the distinct breeds
referred to in its articles of incorporation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination of existing associations

**[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** Forthwith after the
coming into force of this Act the Minister shall examine the articles of
incorporation of every association referred to in section 68 in order to
determine whether each such association may properly continue in
existence by reason of sections 3 to 5.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Situation of animal not covered by Act

- **[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  in the course of the examination referred to in section 70, the
  Minister determines that the animals referred to in an association's
  articles of incorporation are not of a distinct breed or of an
  evolving breed, the Minister shall, by order, direct the association
  to cease issuing certificates of registration in respect of those
  animals.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of dissolution

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the articles of incorporation of an association
  that is the subject of an order under subsection (1) refer only to the
  animals referred to in the order, the Minister shall, by order, direct
  the association to be dissolved in accordance with regulations made
  pursuant to paragraph 57(b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Situation of animal not of distinct breed

- **[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  in the course of the examination referred to in section 70, the
  Minister determines that the animals referred to in an association's
  articles of incorporation are not of a distinct breed but are of an
  evolving breed, the Minister shall, by order, direct the association
  to cease issuing certificates of registration in respect of those
  animals.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister issues an order under subsection
  (1), the Minister shall forthwith cause the association that is the
  subject of the order to be notified by registered mail that the
  Minister intends to dissolve the association unless the association
  files, within 60 days after the date of the mailing of the notice,
  articles of amendment to transform the association into an association
  incorporated in respect of the evolving breed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Situation of more than one association

- **[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  in the course of the examination referred to in section 70, the
  Minister determines that the articles of incorporation of more than
  one association refer to the same distinct breed, the Minister, taking
  into account the purposes of this Act and any other consideration the
  Minister deems appropriate, shall, by order, designate one of the
  associations as the association authorized to register the animals of
  the breed and direct the other association, or associations, as the
  case may be, to cease issuing certificates of registration in respect
  of animals of that breed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of dissolution

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the articles of incorporation of an association
  that is the subject of an order under subsection (1) directing it to
  cease issuing certificates of registration in respect of animals of a
  distinct breed refer only to that distinct breed, the Minister shall
  by order direct that the association be dissolved in accordance with
  regulations made pursuant to paragraph 57(b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of names

**[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** As soon as practical
after the examination referred to in section 70 the Minister shall cause
to be published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) the
name of every association referred to in section 68 that, in the opinion
of the Minister, may properly continue in existence and the name of each
distinct breed and evolving breed in respect of which each such
association is authorized to register and identify animals.

## [Transitional]{.HTitleText1} {#h-3421 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

- **[[75]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-75 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  by-laws of an association referred to in section 68 that are in force
  immediately before the coming into force of this Act remain in force
  until repealed but the provisions of this Act dealing with the
  amendment or repeal of by-laws apply thereto.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity of certificates of
  registration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in subsection 29(3) affects the validity of
  any certificate of registration issued by an association referred to
  in section 68 before the coming into force of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of existing purebreds not
  affected

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in subsection 30(2) affects the status of any
  animal registered as purebred by an association referred to in section
  68 before the coming into force of this Act.
