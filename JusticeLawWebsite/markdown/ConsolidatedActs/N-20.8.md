## Department of Natural Resources Act {#department-of-natural-resources-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1994, c. 41
:::

Assented to 1994-12-15

An Act to establish the Department of Natural Resources and to amend
related Acts
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364120 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Department of Natural Resources Act](/eng/acts/N-20.8).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364123 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Natural
    Resources; ([Version anglaise seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[natural resources]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[natural resources]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means mines, minerals and
    other non-renewable resources, energy, including energy developed
    from water, and forest resources; ([ressources
    naturelles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[remote sensing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[remote sensing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the reception and
    processing of earth observation data acquired by airborne or
    space-borne systems; ([télédétection]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sustainable development]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sustainable development]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means development
    that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability
    of future generations to meet their own needs; ([développement
    durable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[technical surveys]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[technical surveys]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Resources and Technical Surveys
    Act](/eng/acts/R-7). ([levés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Establishment of the Department]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364131 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Department established

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a department of the Government of Canada called the
  Department of Natural Resources over which the Minister of Natural
  Resources, appointed by commission under the Great Seal, shall
  preside.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister holds office during pleasure and has the
  management and direction of the Department.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy head

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
appoint an officer called the Deputy Minister of Natural Resources to
hold office during pleasure and to be the deputy head of the Department.

## [Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364139 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's powers, duties and functions

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The powers, duties and
functions of the Minister extend to and include all matters over which
Parliament has jurisdiction, not by law assigned to any other
department, board or agency of the Government of Canada, relating to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} natural resources;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} explosives; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} technical surveys relating to any matter other than a
  matter to which the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of
  the Environment and the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans extend by
  law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General duties

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** In exercising the powers
and performing the duties and functions assigned to the Minister by
section 5, the Minister shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have regard to the sustainable development of
  Canada's natural resources and the integrated management thereof;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} coordinate, promote, recommend and implement policies
  with respect to the matters referred to in that section, and programs
  and practices established pursuant to those policies;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} assist in the development and promotion of Canadian
  scientific and technological capabilities;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} participate in the development and application of
  codes and standards for technical surveys and natural resources
  products and for the management and use of natural resources;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} seek to enhance the responsible development and use
  of Canada's natural resources and the competitiveness of Canada's
  natural resources products;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} participate in the enhancement and promotion of
  market access for Canada's natural resources products and technical
  surveys industries, both domestically and internationally;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} promote the development and use of remote sensing
  technology;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} promote cooperation with the governments of the
  provinces and with non-governmental organizations in Canada, and
  participate in the promotion of cooperation with the governments of
  other countries and with international organizations; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} gather, compile, analyse, coordinate and disseminate
  information respecting scientific, technological, economic,
  industrial, managerial, marketing and related activities and
  developments affecting Canada's natural resources.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364156 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report required by Governor in Council

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations requiring the Minister to
  prepare a report respecting any matter related to the Minister's
  powers, duties and functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause a copy of any report
  required by the Governor in Council to be laid before each House of
  Parliament as soon as practicable after the report is prepared.

## [Transitional]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364162 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Positions

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Nothing
  in this Act shall be construed as affecting the status of an employee
  who, immediately before the coming into force of this Act, occupied a
  position in the Department of Energy, Mines and Resources or in the
  Department of Forestry, except that the employee shall, on the coming
  into force of this Act, occupy their position in the Department of
  Natural Resources under the authority of the Deputy Minister of
  Natural Resources.

- Definition of [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has
  the same meaning as in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of appropriations

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Any amount appropriated,
for the fiscal year in which this Act comes into force, by an
appropriation Act based on the Estimates for that year for defraying the
charges and expenses of the public service of Canada within the
Department of Energy, Mines and Resources or the Department of Forestry,
and that, on the day on which this Act comes into force, is unexpended,
is deemed, on that day, to be an amount appropriated for defraying the
charges and expenses of the Department of Natural Resources.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of powers, duties and functions

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Wherever under any Act,
order, rule or regulation, or any contract, lease, licence or other
document, any power, duty or function is vested in or exercisable by the
Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources, the Minister of Forestry, the
Deputy Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources, the Deputy Minister of
Forestry or any officer of the Department of Energy, Mines and Resources
or the Department of Forestry, the power, duty or function is vested in
and shall be exercised by the Minister of Natural Resources, the Deputy
Minister of Natural Resources or the appropriate officer of the
Department of Natural Resources, as the case may be, unless the Governor
in Council by order designates another Minister, Deputy Minister or
officer of the public service of Canada to exercise that power, duty or
function.
