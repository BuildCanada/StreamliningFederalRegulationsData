## Nova Scotia and Newfoundland and Labrador Additional Fiscal Equalization Offset Payments Act {#nova-scotia-and-newfoundland-and-labrador-additional-fiscal-equalization-offset-payments-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2005, c. 30, s. 85
:::

Assented to 2005-06-29

An Act respecting additional fiscal equalization offset payments for
Nova Scotia and Newfoundland and Labrador relating to revenues from
offshore petroleum resources

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 85 of chapter 30 of the Statutes of Canada, 2005,
in force on assent June 29, 2005.\]

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-367687 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Nova Scotia and Newfoundland and Labrador Additional Fiscal
Equalization Offset Payments Act](/eng/acts/N-27.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-367690 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Canada--New­foundland and Labrador Arrangement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canada--New­foundland and Labrador
    Arrangement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Arrangement between the
    Government of Canada and the Government of Newfoundland and Labrador
    on Offshore Revenues signed on February 14, 2005. ([Entente
    Canada -- Terre-Neuve-et- Labrador]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canada--Nova Scotia Arrangement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canada--Nova Scotia Arrangement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Arrangement between the Government of Canada and the Government of
    Nova Scotia on Offshore Revenues signed on February 14,
    2005. ([Entente Canada -- Nouvelle-Écosse]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Finance. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-367696 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of the Act

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to implement the Canada--Newfoundland and Labrador Arrangement and the
Canada--Nova Scotia Arrangement.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Additional Fiscal Equalization Offset Payments for Nova Scotia]{.HTitleText1} {#h-367699 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Part.

[[additional fiscal equalization offset payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[additional fiscal equalization offset
    payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the payment that may be made
    under section 6, 7 or 12. ([paiement de péréquation compensatoire
    supplémentaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fiscal equalization offset payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fiscal equalization offset
    payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2015, c. 4, s.
    110\]]{.Repealed}

[[fiscal equalization payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fiscal equalization payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of section 8, the fiscal
      equalization payment that would be received by the Province for a
      fiscal year if the amount of that payment were determined in
      accord-ance with section 3.2 of the [Federal-Provincial Fiscal
      Arrangements Act](/eng/acts/F-7.8), without regard to section 3.4
      of that Act; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of sections 10 to 12, the fiscal
      equalization payment that would be received by the Province for a
      fiscal year under Part I of the [Federal-Provincial Fiscal
      Arrangements Act](/eng/acts/F-7.8) if the Province's total per
      capita fiscal capacity were the amount determined by the formula

      ::: {#367707 .Paragraph}
      A + B + (C / F)

      where

      [A,]{.dfn}
      :   B, C and F have the same meaning as in the definition [[total
          per capita fiscal capacity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection
          3.5(1) of that Act. ([paiement de
          péréquation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})
      :::

[[offshore revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[offshore revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in respect of any fiscal
    year means the aggregate of the following amounts paid to the
    Province for that fiscal year:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} amounts paid under paragraph 219(2)(b) of the
      [Canada--Nova Scotia Offshore Petroleum Resources Accord
      Implementation and Offshore Renewable Energy Management
      Act](/eng/acts/C-7.8);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} amounts paid with respect to the portion of tax
      under subsection 165(2) and sections 218.1, 220.05, 220.06 and
      220.08 of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) that is
      attributable to offshore activity in the Nova Scotia offshore
      area, within the meaning of subsection 123(1) of that Act; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} amounts paid with respect to tax that would be
      payable under Part III of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3)
      (Nova Scotia), R.S.N.S. 1989, c. 217, as amended from time to
      time, on taxable capital of a corporation employed in the offshore
      area within the meaning of section 2 of the [Canada--Nova Scotia
      Offshore Petroleum Resources Accord Implementation and Offshore
      Renewable Energy Management Act](/eng/acts/C-7.8) if that area
      were in the land portion of the Province, unless those amounts
      have been included in paragraph (a). ([recettes
      extracôtières]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[petroleum]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[petroleum]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
    2 of the [Canada--Nova Scotia Offshore Petroleum Resources Accord
    Implementation and Offshore Renewable Energy Management
    Act](/eng/acts/C-7.8). ([hydro­carbures]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Province of Nova
    Scotia. ([Version anglaise seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of \$830 million

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall make a
payment to the Province in the amount of \$830 million, to allow the
Province to reduce its outstanding debt.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional fiscal equalization offset

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  2004-2005 fiscal year, the Minister shall make an additional fiscal
  equalization offset payment to the Province in the amount of \$30.5
  million.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional fiscal equalization offset
  payment of \$26.6 million

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the 2005-2006 fiscal year, the Minister shall
  make an additional fiscal equalization offset payment to the Province
  in the amount of \$26.6 million.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional fiscal equalization offset
payments for the 2006-2012 period

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For each fiscal year
between April 1, 2006 and March 31, 2012, the Minister shall make an
additional fiscal equalization offset payment to the Province calculated
in accordance with section 8.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of payments

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The additional fiscal
equalization offset payment that shall be made to the Province for a
fiscal year corresponds to the amount determined by the Minister in
accordance with the formula

::: {#367729 .Section}
A -- B

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the fiscal equalization payment that may be made to the Province
    for the fiscal year under the equalization formula in effect at that
    time, calculated as if the Province did not have any offshore
    revenue or petroleum production; and

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the fiscal equalization payment that may be made to the Province
    for that fiscal year under the equalization formula in effect at
    that time.
:::

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If for

  additional fiscal equalization offset payment it received for the
  previous fiscal year.

  March 31, 2020 the Province does not receive an additional fiscal
  equalization offset payment and did not receive an additional fiscal
  equalization offset payment for the previous fiscal year, but received
  an additional fiscal equalization offset payment for the fiscal year
  two years before that fiscal year, the Minister shall, for that fiscal
  year, pay to the Province a transitional payment equal to one third of
  the additional fiscal equalization offset payment it received for the
  fiscal year two years before that fiscal year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of the Canada--Nova Scotia
Arrangement

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** On or before March 31,
2019, the Minister, on behalf of the Government of Canada, and the
minister designated by the Province for the purpose will review the
Canada--Nova Scotia Arrangement in accordance with clause 8 of that
Arrangement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discussions

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
shall enter into discussions with the Province if requested to do so by
the Province under the terms of clause 9 of the Canada--Nova Scotia
Arrangement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A determination, for any
fiscal year, of the additional fiscal equalization offset payment for
the Province and for any other calculation that is necessary for the
purposes of this Part shall be made by the Minister at the same time as
the final computation of the amount, if any, of the fiscal equalization
payment that is payable to the Province is made for that fiscal year
under Part I of the [Federal-Provincial Fiscal Arrangements
Act](/eng/acts/F-7.8).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of per capita debt
  servicing charge

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(b), "per capita debt
  servicing charges" as of March 31, 2012, means the amount determined
  by the Minister in accordance with the formula

  ::: {#367810 .Subsection}
  (A - B - C + D) / E

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the total of a province's debt servicing charges for the
      2011-2012 fiscal year, based on figures published in the fully
      consolidated audited financial statements presented on a full
      accrual basis of accounting in the province's Public Accounts for
      the 2011-2012 fiscal year, with any necessary adjustments, as
      required, to include all of the debt charges related to unfunded
      pension liabilities, post-employment benefits and debt charges of
      government organizations and entities whose inclusion would be
      consistent with the accounting principles of full consolidation;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the portion of A, if any, related to borrowings made by a
      province on behalf of self-supporting government business
      enterprises that are not dependent on transfers, grants or other
      direct funding from the province to fund their day-to-day
      operations or debt repayments;

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the portion of A, if any, related to borrowings made by a
      province on behalf of municipalities that are not in default on
      their interest payment obligations to the province;

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the total of a province's debt servicing charges for the
      2011-2012 fiscal year related to borrowings made by the province
      on behalf of municipalities that are in default on their interest
      payment obligations to the province, if these are not already
      included in A; and

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is the total population of a province on July 1, 2011 based on the
      latest Statistics Canada official estimates available as soon as
      all provinces have released their Public Accounts for the
      2011-2012 fiscal year.
  :::

Canada--Newfoundland and Labrador Arrangement, in accordance with clause
8 of that Arrangement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discussions

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
shall enter into discussions with the Province if requested to do so by
the Province under the terms of clause 9 of the Canada--Newfoundland and
Labrador Arrangement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** A determination, for any
fiscal year, of the additional fiscal equalization offset payment for
the Province and for any other calculation that is necessary to be made
for the purposes of this Part shall be made by the Minister at the same
time as the final computation of the amount, if any, of the fiscal
equalization payment that is payable to the Province is made for that
fiscal year under Part I of the [Federal-Provincial Fiscal Arrangements
Act](/eng/acts/F-7.8).

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-367830 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriation

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The amounts authorized to
be paid under this Act shall be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue
Fund by the Minister at the time and in the manner, subject to section
33, that the Minister considers appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations, on the recommendation of the
  Minister,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the time and manner of making any
    payments under this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the determination of the amount referred
    to in subsection 12(2);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the determination of any matter that
    under this Act is to be determined by the Minister; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that is required or authorized
    to be prescribed by this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of the minister designated by
  the Province

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may recommend the making of a regulation
  under paragraph (1)(b) only after receipt by the Minister of the
  approval of the proposed regulation from the minister designated by
  the Province for that purpose.
