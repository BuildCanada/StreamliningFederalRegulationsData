## Canadian Dairy Commission Act {#canadian-dairy-commission-act .Title-of-Act}

:::

An Act respecting the Canadian Dairy Commission

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-67726 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canadian Dairy Commission Act](/eng/acts/C-15).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-7, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-67731 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a body that is constituted under
    the laws of a province for the purpose of regulating the production
    for marketing, or the marketing, in intraprovincial trade of any
    dairy product; ([office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Dairy
    Commission continued by section 3; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[cream]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cream]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means cream derived from
    milk; ([crème]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dairy product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dairy product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means milk, cream, butter,
    cheese, condensed milk, evaporated milk, milk powder, dry milk,
    ice-cream, malted milk or any other product manufactured wholly or
    mainly from milk and includes sherbet; ([produits
    laitiers]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[market]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[market]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to market in interprovincial or
    export trade; ([commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[milk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[milk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means milk from
    cows; ([lait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Agriculture
    and Agri-Food; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[place]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[place]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any vehicle, vessel, railway
    car or aircraft; ([lieu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[regulated product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regulated product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a dairy product the
    marketing of which is regulated or prohibited by regulations made
    under this Act. ([produits réglementés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- 1994, c. 38, s. 25

## [Constitution of the Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-67745 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission continued

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Canadian Dairy Commission is continued as a corporation consisting of
  a Chairperson, a Chief Executive Officer and one other member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding section 105 of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), each member of the Commission
  shall be appointed by the Governor in Council to hold office during
  pleasure for such term as the Governor in Council considers
  appropriate.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2009, c. 2, s. 379\]]{.Repealed}

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2006, c. 9, s. 240\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and expenses

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Each member of the Commission shall be paid such
  salary or other remuneration, and may be paid such travel and living
  expenses incurred in connection with the performance of his duties, as
  are fixed by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retirement age

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A member of the Commission ceases to hold office on
  reaching the age of seventy years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary substitute member

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If any member of the Commission is absent or unable
  to act, the Governor in Council may appoint a temporary substitute
  member for such term and on such conditions as the Governor in Council
  prescribes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The head office of the Commission shall be in the
  city of Ottawa, but meetings of the Commission may be held at such
  other places as the Commission may decide.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-15, s. 3
- R.S., 1985, c. 1 (4th Supp.), s. 44(E)
- 2006, c. 9, s. 240
- 2009, c. 2, s. 379
:::

## [Consultative Committee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-67767 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultative Committee

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall appoint a Consultative Committee consisting of a
  chairperson and eight other members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure of members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each member of the Consultative Committee shall be
  appointed for a term not exceeding three years, except that of those
  members first appointed three shall be appointed for a term of two
  years, three shall be appointed for a term of three years and three
  shall be appointed for a term of four years.

::: HistoricalNote

- 2006, c. 9, s. 241(E)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers and employees

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may appoint such officers and employees as are necessary
  for the proper conduct of the work of the Commission and prescribe
  their duties and, subject to the approval of the Treasury Board, the
  conditions of their employment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salaries and expenses of staff

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The officers and employees of the Commission
  appointed as provided in subsection (1) shall be paid such salaries
  and expenses as are fixed by the Commission with the approval of the
  Treasury Board.

## [Objects of the Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-67790 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects of Commission

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The objects of the
Commission are to provide efficient producers of milk and cream with the
opportunity of obtaining a fair return for their labour and investment
and to provide consumers of dairy products with a continuous and
adequate supply of dairy products of high quality.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-7, s. 8
:::

## [Powers of the Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-67795 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} purchase any dairy product and sell, or otherwise
    dispose of, any dairy product purchased by it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} package, process, store, ship, insure, import or
    export any dairy product purchased by the Commission;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} make payments for the benefit of producers of milk
    and cream for the purpose of providing protection for the income of
    those producers from the sale of those products, which payments may
    be made on the basis of volume, quality or on any other basis that
    the Commission deems appropriate;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} make investigations into any matter relating to the
    production, processing or marketing of any dairy product, including
    the cost of producing, processing or marketing that product;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} undertake and assist in the promotion of the use of
    dairy products, the improvement of the quality and variety of and
    the publication of information in relation to those products;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} establish and operate a pool or pools in respect of
    the marketing of milk or cream, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} distributing money to producers of milk or cream
      received from the marketing of any quantity of milk or cream, or
      any component, class, variety or grade of milk or cream from the
      pool or pools, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} deducting from the money distributed under
      subparagraph (i) any necessary and proper expenses of operating
      the pool or pools;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} establish the price, or minimum or maximum price,
    paid or to be paid to the Commission, or to producers of milk or
    cream, the basis on which that payment is to be made and the terms
    and manner of payment that is to be made in respect of the marketing
    of any quantity of milk or cream, or any component, class, variety
    or grade of milk or cream;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} collect the price paid or to be paid to the
    Commission, or to any producer in respect of the marketing of any
    quantity of milk or cream, or any component, class, variety or grade
    of milk or cream, or recover that price in a court of competent
    jurisdiction;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} subject to an agreement entered into under section
    9.1, establish and operate a program in respect of the quantities
    and prices of milk or cream, or of any component, class, variety or
    grade of milk or cream, necessary for the competitive international
    trade in, and the promotion and facilitation of the marketing of,
    dairy products, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} distributing money for the purpose of the
      equalization of returns to producers in respect of that milk or
      cream, or that component, class, variety or grade, from which
      those dairy products are made, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} deducting from the money distributed under
      subparagraph (i) any necessary and proper expenses of operating
      the program; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} do all acts and things necessary or incidental to
    the exercise of any of its powers or the carrying out of any of its
    functions under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inquiries

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of carrying out any investigation
  under paragraph (1)(d), the Commission has all the powers of a
  commissioner appointed under Part I of the [Inquiries
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules of procedure

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may make the rules it deems necessary
  for the regulation of its proceedings, for the fixing of a quorum for
  any of its meetings and generally for the conduct of its activities
  under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-15, s. 9
- 1994, c. 26, s. 19
- 1995, c. 23, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation to Boards, etc.

**[[9.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.1 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may,
with the approval of the Governor in Council, enter into an agreement
with a province or a Board, providing for the coordinated marketing of
dairy products, including the granting of authority for the performance

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by the Board of any powers of the Commission set out
  in paragraphs 9(1)(f) to (i);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by the Commission of any similar powers that the
  Board is authorized by the laws of a province to exercise; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} by the Commission of any similar powers that it is
  authorized to exercise by the lieutenant governor in council of a
  province.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 23, s. 3
:::

## [Duties of the Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-67826 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission to submit program to Minister

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  year, following determination by the Governor in Council pursuant to
  the [Farm Income Protection Act](/eng/acts/F-3.3) of the total amount
  to be paid by the Minister to the Commission for the purpose of
  providing protection for the income of producers of milk and cream
  from the sale of those products, the Commission shall submit to the
  Minister an outline of the program by which it proposes to carry out
  its functions under this Act for the following fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of carrying out functions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall carry out its functions under
  this Act in a manner that will achieve its objects and meet its
  obligations from the moneys available to it under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-15, s. 10
- 1994, c. 26, s. 20
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  exercising its powers under this Act or the regulations in relation to
  the importation or exportation of any dairy product, the Commission
  shall comply with any directions from time to time given to it by the
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 89(2) to (6) and section 153 of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) apply, with such
  modifications as the circumstances require, to a direction given under
  subsection (1) as though it were a directive referred to in those
  provisions.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-7, s. 11
- 1984, c. 31, s. 14
:::

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-67841 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations regulating the marketing of
  any dairy product, including regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} providing for the marketing of any dairy product on
    a quota basis;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} designating the agencies through which any
    regulated product shall be marketed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} providing for the issue of licences to persons
    engaged in the production or processing of a regulated product for
    market, prescribing the fees for those licences and providing for
    their cancellation or suspension;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prohibiting persons from engaging in the marketing
    of any dairy product, or any class, variety or grade of such a
    product, in whole or in part, except under the authority of a
    licence;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the books and records to be kept by
    persons engaged in the production or processing of a regulated
    product for market and the information to be furnished by those
    persons;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} authorizing the Commission to fix, impose and
    collect levies or charges from persons engaged in the marketing of
    any dairy product or the production or processing of a regulated
    product for market and for those purposes to classify those persons
    into groups, fix the levies or charges payable by the members of the
    different groups and to use those levies or charges for the purpose
    of carrying out its functions under this Act;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} providing for the seizure and disposal of any
    regulated product marketed in contravention of any regulation made
    under this section; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} generally, for carrying out the purposes and
    provisions of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulation may be general or specific

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under subsection (1) may be general
  or restricted to a specific dairy product, area, or group or class of
  persons.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-7, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations requiring the registration of producers of milk and
cream as a condition of the making of any payment under paragraph
9(1)(c) for the benefit of those producers and prescribing the books and
records to be kept and the information to be furnished to the Commission
by or on behalf of those producers.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-7, s. 13
:::

## [Financial]{.HTitleText1} {#h-67861 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses paid out of appropriations

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** All expenditures for
salaries, travel expenses and expenses of administration shall be paid
out of moneys appropriated by Parliament for the purpose, other than

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} expenditures that in the opinion of the Minister are
  directly attributable to action taken by the Commission to provide
  protection for the income of producers of any dairy product from the
  sale of that product; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} necessary and proper expenses incurred by the
  Commission in exercising any of its powers set out in paragraphs
  9(1)(f) to (i).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian Dairy Commission Account

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be established in the accounts of Canada a special account to be
  known as the Canadian Dairy Commission Account, in this section called
  the "Account".

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Credits to Account

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} There shall be credited to the Account

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all moneys received by the Commission from its
    operations described in paragraphs 9(1)(a) and (b);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all licence fees, levies and charges paid to the
    Commission;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all loans made to the Commission by the Minister of
    Finance pursuant to section 16; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all amounts paid to the Commission by the Minister
    under the [Farm Income Protection Act](/eng/acts/F-3.3) for the
    purpose of providing protection for the income of producers of any
    dairy product from the sale of that product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charges to Account

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} There shall be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue
  Fund and charged to the Account

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all expenditures under this Act, except those to be
    paid pursuant to section 14;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all amounts paid to the Minister of Finance in
    repayment of loans made to the Commission pursuant to section 16 or
    as interest on those loans; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any excess licence fees, levies and charges paid to
    the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit on payments

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No payment shall be made out of the Consolidated
  Revenue Fund under this section in excess of the amount of the balance
  to the credit of the Account.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-15, s. 15

- 1995, c. 23, s. 5

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-67900 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspectors

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may appoint or designate any person as an inspector for the
  purposes of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate to be produced

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall furnish every inspector with a
  certificate of appointment or designation and on entering any place
  under subsection 18(1) an inspector shall, if so required, produce the
  certificate to the person in charge of that place.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-7, ss. 18, 19
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of inspectors

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (1.1), an
  inspector may at any reasonable time

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter any place in which the inspector believes on
    reasonable grounds there is any regulated product; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require any person to produce for inspection, or
    for the purpose of obtaining copies thereof or extracts therefrom,
    any books, records or documents relating to that product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant required to enter
  dwelling-house

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Where any place referred to in paragraph (1)(a) is
  a dwelling-house, an inspector may not enter that dwelling-house
  without the consent of the occupant except under the authority of a
  warrant issued under subsection (1.2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} Where on [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  application a justice of the peace is satisfied by information on oath

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the conditions for entry described in
    paragraph (1)(a) exist in relation to a dwelling-house,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that entry to the dwelling-house is necessary for
    any purpose relating to the administration or enforcement of this
    Act, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that entry to the dwelling-house has been refused
    or that there are reasonable grounds for believing that entry
    thereto will be refused,

  the justice of the peace may issue a warrant under his hand
  authorizing the inspector named therein to enter that dwelling-house
  subject to such conditions as may be specified in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

  [(1.3)]{.lawlabel} In executing a warrant issued under subsection
  (1.2), the inspector named therein shall not use force unless the
  inspector is accompanied by a peace officer and the use of force has
  been specifically authorized in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to inspectors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner or persons in charge of any place referred
  to in subsection (1) and every person found in that place shall give
  the inspector all reasonable assistance to enable the inspector to
  carry out his duties and functions under this Act and shall furnish
  the inspector with any information he may reasonably require with
  respect to any regulated product found in that place.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-15, s. 18
- R.S., 1985, c. 31 (1st Supp.), s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction and false statements

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** No person shall obstruct
or hinder, or make any false or misleading statement either orally or in
writing to, an inspector engaged in carrying out his duties or functions
under this Act or any regulation made under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-7, s. 20
:::

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-67931 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of Act or regulations

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who, or whose employee or agent, contravenes or fails to comply
  with any provision of this Act or any regulation made under this Act
  is guilty of an offence and liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding five
    hundred dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
    months or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment to a fine not exceeding
    two thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one
    year or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence by employee or agent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In a prosecution for an offence under this section it
  is sufficient proof of the offence to establish that it was committed
  by an employee or agent of the accused, whether or not the employee or
  agent is identified.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where it is established in any prosecution for an
  offence under this section that the offence was committed by an
  employee or agent of the accused, it is a defence to the accused that
  he exercised all due diligence to prevent the commission of the
  offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Injunction

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may, with the approval of the Attorney
  General of Canada, seek injunctive relief in any court of competent
  jurisdiction, where the Commission believes on reasonable grounds that
  a person or their employee or agent has failed to comply with any
  provision of this Act or any regulation.

- 1995, c. 23, s. 8

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-67945 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inclusion of dairy product on Import
Control List

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may include on the Import Control List established under the [Export and
Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19) any dairy product the import of
which the Governor in Council deems it necessary to control for the
purpose of implementing any action taken under this Act to support the
price of that dairy product or that has the effect of supporting the
price of that dairy product.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-7, s. 17
:::
