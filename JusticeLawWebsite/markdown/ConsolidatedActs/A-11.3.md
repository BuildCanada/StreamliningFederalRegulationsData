## Anishinabek Nation Education Agreement Act {#anishinabek-nation-education-agreement-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2017, c. 32
:::

Assented to 2017-12-14

An Act to give effect to the Anishinabek Nation Education Agreement and
to make consequential amendments to other Acts
::::

::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-3447 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Anishinabek Nation Education Agreement Act](/eng/acts/A-11.3).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-3450 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Anishinabek Nation
    Education Agreement signed on August 16, 2017, including any
    amendments made to it. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[constitution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[constitution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a constitution ratified
    by a participating First Nation in conformity with the
    Agreement. ([constitution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[education]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[education]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means education programs and
    services of a nature generally provided to students from junior
    kindergarten to the end of secondary school in
    Ontario. ([éducatifs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[First Nation law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[First Nation law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a law made under
    section 7. ([texte législatif autochtone]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[participating First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[participating First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a band that
    is named in the schedule. ([première nation
    participante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[student]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[student]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
    1.1 of the Agreement. ([élève]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement prevails

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  event of an inconsistency or conflict between the Agreement and the
  provisions of any Act of Parliament, or of any regulation made under
  an Act of Parliament, the Agreement prevails to the extent of the
  conflict or inconsistency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act prevails

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), in the event of an
  inconsistency or conflict between the provisions of this Act and the
  provisions of any other Act of Parliament, or of any regulation made
  under any other Act of Parliament, the provisions of this Act prevail
  to the extent of the inconsistency or conflict.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Anishinabek Nation Governance
  Agreement Act](/eng/acts/A-11.31)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The provisions of the [Anishinabek Nation Governance
  Agreement Act](/eng/acts/A-11.31) prevail over the provisions of this
  Act, and of any regulation made under this Act, to the extent of any
  inconsistency or conflict between them.

## [Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-3465 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Act

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act applies to every
participating First Nation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement given effect

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement is approved, given effect and declared valid and has the
  force of law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Agreement is binding on,
  and may be relied on by, all persons and bodies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not a treaty

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement is not a
treaty within the meaning of section 35 of the [Constitution Act,
1982](/eng/Const/).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First Nation laws

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A participating First
Nation may, to the extent provided by the Agreement, make laws
respecting education that are applicable on its
[[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Education

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  participating First Nation must provide or make provision for
  education for students to the extent provided by the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transferability

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The education provided must, if practicable, be
  comparable to education provided by the public school system of
  Ontario, in order to facilitate the transfer of students without
  academic penalty to and from that school system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Kinoomaadziwin Education Body

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  corporation without share capital to be known as Kinoomaadziwin
  Education Body is established to support the delivery of education
  under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The powers, duties, functions, role and composition
  of the Kinoomaadziwin Education Body are to be determined in
  accordance with the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regional Education Council

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  participating First Nation may, with one or more participating First
  Nations, establish a Regional Education Council, in accordance with
  the Agreement, to support the coordination and delivery of education.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The powers, duties, functions, role and composition
  of the Regional Education Council are to be determined, in accordance
  with the Agreement, by the participating First Nations that establish
  it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Local Education Authority

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  participating First Nation may establish a Local Education Authority,
  in accordance with the Agreement, to support it in the exercise of its
  powers, including the power to make First Nation laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The powers, duties, functions, role and composition
  of the Local Education Authority are to be determined, in accordance
  with the Agreement, by the participating First Nation that establishes
  it.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-3497 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** On the coming into force
of a First Nation law, subsection 114(1) and sections 115 to 117 of the
[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) cease to apply to the participating First
Nation that made it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice  ---  First Nation laws

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice must be taken of a
  First Nation law that is registered in the official registry of laws
  maintained by a participating First Nation in accordance with the
  Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In any proceedings, evidence of a First Nation law
  may be given by the production of a copy of the law, certified to be a
  true copy by a person authorized by the participating First Nation,
  without proof of that person's signature or official character.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A First Nation law is not
a statutory instrument for the purposes of the [Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

## [Regulations and Orders]{.HTitleText1} {#h-3507 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations and orders

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make any regulations or orders that he or she considers necessary
for the purpose of carrying out the provisions of this Act, the
Agreement or other agreements related to the implementation of the
Agreement.

## [Amendment of Schedule]{.HTitleText1} {#h-3510 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Addition of name of participating First
Nation

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, by order, add the name of any First Nation to
  the schedule if he or she is satisfied that the First Nation has, in a
  manner consistent with the Agreement, ratified the Agreement and has a
  constitution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment or removal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by order, amend the
  schedule to amend or remove the name of a participating First Nation
  if he or she is satisfied that consent to the amendment or removal was
  obtained in a manner consistent with the Agreement.
