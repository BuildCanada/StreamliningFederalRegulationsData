## Northern Canada Power Commission (Share Issuance and Sale Authorization) Act {#northern-canada-power-commission-share-issuance-and-sale-authorization-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1988, c. 12
:::

Assented to 1988-04-27

An Act to enable the Northern Canada Power Commission to issue shares,
to authorize the sale of those shares to the Government of the Northwest
Territories, to repeal the Northern Canada Power Commission Act and to
provide for related matters
::::

::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365851 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Northern Canada Power Commission (Share Issuance and Sale
Authorization) Act](/eng/acts/N-24.2).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365854 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement entered into
    between the Minister and the Government of the Northwest Territories
    pursuant to section 7; ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Northern Canada Power
    Commission, a corporation without share capital established by the
    Northern Canada Power Commission Act and continued by
    section 3; ([Société]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Northern
    Affairs; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[territorial lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[territorial lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means lands in the
    Northwest Territories that are vested in Her Majesty in right of
    Canada or of which the Government of Canada has power to dispose,
    whether or not the lands have been withdrawn from disposal under the
    [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7) pursuant to paragraph 19(a)
    of that Act. ([terres territoriales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Capital Structure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365861 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation with share capital

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation is continued as a corporation with share capital.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Division of shares

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The share capital of the Corporation comprises such
  number of shares of such classes as are specified in the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conversion of debt to shares

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Of the
  debt of the Corporation to Her Majesty in the amount of ninety-six
  million, one hundred and twenty-eight thousand, eight hundred and
  thirty-eight dollars and forty-three cents, the Corporation shall
  convert that portion amounting to forty-three million, one hundred and
  twenty-eight thousand, eight hundred and thirty-eight dollars and
  forty-three cents into shares of equivalent value in the manner
  provided by the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of shares

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation shall issue the shares referred to in
  subsection (1) to the Minister, who shall hold them in trust for Her
  Majesty.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of promissory note

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation shall, on
terms and conditions specified in the Agreement, convert fifty-three
million dollars of the debt referred to in subsection 4(1) into a
promissory note payable to the Receiver General.

## [Crown Property and Liabilities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365874 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of assets

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  property, rights and interests of Her Majesty held in the name of the
  Corporation and used in relation to its operations in the Northwest
  Territories are transferred to the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of liabilities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any outstanding liabilities and obligations of Her
  Majesty incurred by the Corporation in relation to its operations in
  the Northwest Territories are transferred to the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to dispose of land

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding sections 4, 7, 9 and 10 and
  paragraph 19(f) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), the
  Minister may, on terms and conditions specified in the Agreement, sell
  or lease, or grant a licence or easement in respect of, any
  territorial lands that are under the control, management and
  administration of the Minister.

## [Sale to Territorial Government]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365882 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale of shares

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, with the
approval of the Governor in Council, enter into an agreement for the
sale of the shares referred to in section 4 and the assignment of the
promissory note referred to in section 5 to the Government of the
Northwest Territories.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directives by Minister

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may direct the
Corporation forthwith to execute the Agreement and any document related
thereto, to promptly pass, approve, amend or ratify any by-laws,
resolutions, authorities or similar instruments and to do such other
things as are necessary for or incidental to the implementation of this
Act or the Agreement.

## [Accounts of Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365887 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Write-off

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection 18(1) of
  the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the amount by
  which the value of the shares issued pursuant to section 4 and the
  promissory note referred to in section 5, as recorded in the accounts
  of Canada, exceeds the proceeds of their sale and assignment,
  respectively, pursuant to section 7, is hereby written off as a
  budgetary expenditure and deleted from the accounts of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report in Public Accounts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount written off by subsection (1) in a fiscal
  year shall be reported in the form determined by the Treasury Board,
  in accordance with subsection 18(6) of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11), in the Public Accounts for that year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accounts of Corporation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} This section does not require the Corporation to make
  any adjustments in its accounts to the value of the shares referred to
  in section 4 and the promissory note referred to in section 5.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365895 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation continued in N.W.T.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation is
continued as a corporation governed by the laws of the Northwest
Territories as if it had been incorporated under those laws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employment continuous

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
the employment of employees of the Corporation employed before and after
this section comes into force shall be deemed to be continuous.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from fees

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, with
the approval of the Governor in Council, make an order exempting the
Corporation from the requirement to pay fees for the right to use waters
or deposit waste under a licence under the [Northwest Territories Waters
Act](/eng/acts/N-27.3) or Part 1 of the [Nunavut Waters and Nunavut
Surface Rights Tribunal Act](/eng/acts/N-28.8).
