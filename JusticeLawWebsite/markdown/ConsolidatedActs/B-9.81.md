[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1997, c. 26

An Act to implement certain provisions of the budget tabled in
Parliament on February 18, 1997

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-35228 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Budget Implementation Act, 1997](/eng/acts/B-9.81).

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Canada Foundation for Innovation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-35231 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-35232 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Part.

[[auditor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[auditor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the auditor for the foundation
    appointed under section 26. ([vérificateur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the board of directors of the
    foundation. ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chairperson of the
    board appointed under subsection 9(2). ([président]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is on the board
    and includes the Chairperson. ([administrateur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[eligible project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a project carried on, or to be carried on, by an
      eligible recipient for the modernization, acquisition,
      development, operation or maintenance of research infrastructure
      by the recipient in Canada; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the purchasing by an eligible recipient of access
      to a world-leading research facility located outside Canada or to
      a major international collaborative research project, including
      the purchasing of such access by means of a contribution toward a
      portion of capital costs. ([travaux admissibles]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[eligible recipient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible recipient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a hospital, university or post-secondary college
      or educational institution that is situated in Canada and that
      carries on, or in the opinion of a majority of the directors is
      capable of carrying on, meaningful research; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a non-profit organization or a trust the
      activities of which are primarily carried on in Canada and that
      carries on, or in the opinion of a majority of the directors is
      capable of carrying on, meaningful research. ([bénéficiaire
      admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employee or agent of Her Majesty in right of a province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employee or agent of Her Majesty in right of a
    province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include an employee or agent
    of Her Majesty in right of a province whose duties and functions as
    such an employee or agent are restricted to work in a university, a
    college or other educational institution or a hospital. ([employé ou
    mandataire de Sa Majesté du chef d'une province]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the fiscal year of the
    foundation as determined in accordance with its
    by-laws. ([exercice]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[foundation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foundation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Foundation for
    Innovation established by section 3. ([fondation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is a member of the
    foundation. ([membre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Industry. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-profit organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-profit organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation,
    society, association, organization or body no part of whose income
    is payable to or otherwise available for the personal benefit of any
    of its proprietors, members or shareholders. ([organisation sans but
    lucratif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[research]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[research]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the attempt by careful
    scientific or technical enquiry, experimentation, study,
    observation, analysis and recording to discover new facts, knowledge
    and information, to develop new interpretations of facts, knowledge
    or information, or to discover new means of applying existing
    knowledge, relating to

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a science;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} health;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} engineering; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the environment. ([recherche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[research infrastructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[research infrastructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means equipment,
    specimens, scientific collections, computer software, information
    databases, communications linkages and other intangible property
    used or to be used primarily for carrying on research, including
    housing and installations essential for the use and servicing of
    those things. ([infrastructure de recherche]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[special resolution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[special resolution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of the members means a
    resolution passed by a majority of not less than two thirds of the
    votes cast by the members who voted on the resolution at a meeting
    of the members or signed by all the members entitled to vote on the
    resolution. ([résolution extraordinaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

- 1997, c. 26, s. 2

### [Establishment of Foundation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-35260 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foundation incorporated

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A corporation without share
capital is established to be called the Canada Foundation for Innovation
consisting of the members of the foundation and the directors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foundation not agent of Her Majesty

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The foundation is not an
agent of Her Majesty.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects and purposes of foundation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The objects and purposes of
the foundation are to make grants to eligible recipients for eligible
projects to increase Canada's capability of carrying on high quality
research.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1997, c. 26, s. 5
- 2001, c. 11, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
carrying out its objects and purposes, the foundation has the capacity
and, subject to this Part, the rights, powers and privileges of a
natural person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the
foundation shall be in the National Capital Region described in the
schedule to the [National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4).

    instrument);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} subsections 103(1) to (4) (powers of directors to
    make and amend by-laws, members' approval of by-laws and effective

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} subsection 105(1) (qualifications of directors);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} subsection 108(2) (resignation of director);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} subsections 114(1) and (2) (place of directors'

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} section 116 (validity of acts of directors and

### [Directors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-35306 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board of directors

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be a board of directors of the foundation which shall supervise
  the management of the business and affairs of the foundation and,
  subject to the by-laws of the foundation, exercise all its powers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of directors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the board consists of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Chairperson of the board, who is resident in
    Canada, appointed by the Governor in Council on the recommendation
    of the Minister;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} five persons, who are resident in Canada, appointed
    by the Governor in Council on the recommendation of the Minister;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} seven persons, who are resident in Canada and none
    of whom are employees or agents of Her Majesty in right of Canada or
    in right of a province or members of the Senate, the House of
    Commons or the legislature of a province, appointed by the members
    in accordance with the by-laws of the foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial organization

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Chairperson is appointed before directors
  are appointed under paragraph (2)(c), the Chairperson and such other
  directors, if any, as may have been appointed under paragraph (2)(b)
  constitute the board until directors are appointed under paragraph
  (2)(c) and may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} undertake the organization of the foundation
    including the appointment of officers and employees;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make banking arrangements for the foundation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} enact organizational by-laws for the foundation;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} receive on behalf of the foundation any moneys paid
    to the foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation on initial organization

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Until directors are appointed under paragraph (2)(c),
  the board shall not make any grants from or out of the funds of the
  foundation or enter into any agreements or arrangements, or review any
  applications, for or in respect of grants to be made from or out of
  the funds of the foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foundation not owned by Crown

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The operation of the foundation under subsection (3)
  by the Chairperson and directors, if any, appointed under paragraph
  (2)(b) shall not, notwithstanding subsection 83(2) of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), result in the foundation being
  considered, for the purposes of Part X of that Act or for any other
  purpose, to be wholly owned directly by Her Majesty in right of
  Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1997, c. 26, s. 9
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1658
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms of office of Governor in Council
appointees

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson and the directors appointed under paragraph 9(2)(b) shall
  be appointed to hold office during pleasure for terms of three years
  but, except where they cease to be directors under subsection (4),
  they shall continue to hold office until their successors are
  appointed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms of office of other directors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The directors appointed under paragraph 9(2)(c) by
  the members shall hold office for terms of three years, but they may
  be removed from office by a special resolution of the members and,
  except where they cease to be directors under subsection (4), they
  shall continue to hold office until their successors are appointed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional terms of office

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A director is eligible to be reappointed for one or
  more additional terms of office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ceasing to be director

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A director ceases to be a director when

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the director

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} dies,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} resigns,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} ceases to be resident in Canada, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} becomes disqualified under subsection 105(1) of
      the [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a director appointed by the Governor
    in Council, the appointment is terminated by the Governor in Council
    before the expiry of the term of the appointment; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a director appointed by the members,
    the director

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is removed from office before the expiry of the
      term of the appointment by a special resolution of the members,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is appointed to the Senate,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is elected to the House of Commons or the
      legislature of a province, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} becomes an employee or agent of Her Majesty in
      right of Canada or in right of a province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Director representation

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The appointment of
directors shall be made

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to ensure, as far as possible, that at all times
  approximately one half of the directors will be representative of
  persons engaged in research and one half representative of the
  business community or non-profit organizations; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} having regard to the desirability of having directors
  drawn from various regions of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1997, c. 26, s. 11
- 2001, c. 34, s. 7(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration for directors

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} From
  and out of the funds of the foundation, the directors may be paid
  remuneration for acting as directors in amounts determined by the
  board not exceeding maximum amounts prescribed by regulation and they
  may be reimbursed for any reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred
  by them in performing their duties or attending meetings of the board
  or of members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors not to profit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as provided in subsection (1), no director
  shall profit or gain any income or acquire any property from the
  foundation or its activities.

### [Members]{.HTitleText2} {#h-35358 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Membership

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be fifteen members of the foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Upon this Act receiving royal assent, the Governor in
  Council, on the recommendation of the Minister, shall without delay
  appoint six persons as members of the foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First meeting

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} As soon as possible after the appointment of the six
  members under subsection (2), the Minister shall make arrangements for
  a first meeting of those members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointing balance of first members

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} At the first meeting of the six members appointed
  under subsection (2), or at a meeting held as soon after that meeting
  as possible, those members shall appoint nine further members of the
  foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of successor members

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The appointment of a person as a member to succeed a
  person whose term as a member expires shall be made by the members at
  a general meeting of members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filling vacancies

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The appointment of a person as a member to fill a
  vacancy in the membership caused by a person ceasing to be a member
  before the member's term as a member expires shall be made by the
  members at a general meeting of members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility for members

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A person is not eligible to be appointed a member if
  the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a member of the Senate, the House of Commons or
    the legislature of a province;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is an employee or agent of Her Majesty in right of
    Canada or in right of a province;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is a director;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} does not reside in Canada; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} does not meet the qualifications set out in
    subsection 105(1) of the [Canada Business Corporations
    Act](/eng/acts/C-44).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms of members

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  members shall be appointed to hold office for terms of five years, but
  they may be removed from office by a special resolution of the members
  and, except where they cease to be members under subsection (5), they
  shall continue to hold office until their successors are appointed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms of first members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Of the members appointed under subsections 13(2) and
  (4), one-third shall be appointed for terms of five years, one-third
  shall be appointed for four years and one-third shall be appointed for
  three years but, except where they cease to be members under
  subsection (5), they shall continue to hold office until their
  successors are appointed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional terms of office

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A member is eligible to be reappointed for one or
  more terms of office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacements

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person appointed to fill a vacancy in the office of
  a member who has ceased to hold the office before the expiry of the
  member's term of office shall be appointed to hold office for the
  unexpired portion of that term.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ceasing to be member

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A member ceases to be a member when the member

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} resigns;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is appointed to the Senate;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is elected to the House of Commons or to the
    legislature of a province;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} is appointed a director;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} becomes an employee or agent of Her Majesty in
    right of Canada or in right of a province;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} no longer meets the qualifications set out in
    subsection 105(1) of the [Canada Business Corporations
    Act](/eng/acts/C-44); or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} is removed from office by a special resolution of
    the members.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Member representation

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The appointment of
members shall be made

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to ensure, as far as possible, that at all times
  approximately one half of the members will be representative of
  persons engaged in research and one half representative of the
  business community or non-profit organizations; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} having regard to the desirability of having members
  drawn from various regions of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No remuneration for members

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  members are not entitled to be paid any remuneration for acting as
  members but they may be reimbursed for any reasonable out-of-pocket
  expenses incurred by them in performing their duties or attending
  meetings of the members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members not to profit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as provided in subsection (1), no member shall
  profit or gain any income or acquire any property from the foundation
  or its activities.

### [Staff]{.HTitleText2} {#h-35407 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  board may appoint such officers, employees and agents of the
  foundation as they consider necessary to carry out the objects and
  purposes of the foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of offices

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the by-laws of the foundation, the board
  may designate the offices of the foundation and specify the duties and
  functions of each office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors not employees or agents

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Directors and members are not eligible to be employed
  as employees or agents of the foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not part of public service of Canada

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The directors, members, officers, employees and
  agents of the foundation are not, because of being a director, member,
  officer, employee or agent of the foundation, part of the federal
  public administration.

### [Operations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-35419 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrative expenses

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** From and out of its
funds, the foundation may pay salaries and wages of its officers and
employees, rent for its accommodation, remuneration for its directors
and agents, reimbursement to the directors and members for their
reasonable out-of-pocket expenses, and other costs and expenses of
carrying on the business of the foundation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance for eligible projects

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} From
  and out of its funds, the foundation may make grants to eligible
  recipients to be used by them solely for the purposes of eligible
  projects in accordance with any terms and conditions specified by the
  foundation in respect of the grants.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limits on financial assistance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In making grants to an eligible recipient in respect
  of an eligible project, the foundation shall not acquire any interest,
  whether through the acquisition of share capital, a partnership
  interest or otherwise, in any research infrastructure acquired by the
  recipient for the project.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements relating to assistance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of assisting an eligible recipient in
  completing an eligible project, the foundation may enter into
  agreements with the recipient respecting, among other things, the
  manner in which the foundation will make advances in respect of a
  grant to the recipient, when those advances will be made and any terms
  or conditions on which the grant will be made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Donations to foundation

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the
  foundation may accept donations of money.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of donations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All money donated to the foundation, and any income
  arising from the investment of the money, shall be used by the
  foundation in carrying out its objects and purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditional donations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The foundation shall not accept a donation of money
  that is made on the condition that the foundation use the money or any
  income arising from the investment of the money for any purpose that
  is not within the objects and purposes of the foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (3) does not apply where the conditions of
  a donation of money merely restrict or direct the manner of investing
  the money until it can be used to make grants to eligible recipients
  for eligible projects.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investment policies

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The board shall establish
investment policies, standards and procedures that a reasonably prudent
person would apply in respect of a portfolio of investments to avoid
undue risk of loss and obtain a reasonable return, having regard to the
foundation's obligations and anticipated obligations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investments

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any conditions of a
  donation restricting the investment of the money donated until it can
  be used to make grants to eligible recipients for eligible projects,
  the foundation shall invest its funds, and reinvest any income from
  those funds, in accordance with the investment policies, standards and
  procedures established by the board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The foundation shall not invest its funds in, or
  acquire any right in any property of, an eligible recipient that has
  received, is about to receive or has applied for a grant from the
  foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation of other corporations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The foundation shall not cause any corporation to be
  incorporated or participate in the incorporation of a corporation or
  become a partner in a partnership.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control of corporation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The foundation shall not carry on any business for
  gain or profit otherwise than in the investment of its funds, and
  shall not hold or acquire a controlling interest in any corporation or
  enterprise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Borrowing prohibited

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  foundation shall not borrow money, issue any debt obligations or
  securities, give any guarantees to secure a debt or other obligation
  of another person or mortgage, pledge or otherwise encumber property
  of the foundation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Real property

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The foundation shall not purchase or accept a
  donation of real property.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation by directors

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the board
  may delegate to the Chairperson, a committee of directors or an
  officer of the foundation any of the powers of the board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrictions on delegation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The board shall not delegate any power or right of
  the board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to enact, amend or repeal by-laws;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to authorize the making of grants to eligible
    recipients;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to appoint directors to, or fill vacancies on, a
    committee of the board;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to appoint officers of the foundation or fix their
    remuneration;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} to accept donations;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} to approve an annual financial statement or a
    report of the foundation; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} to submit to the members any matter requiring the
    approval of the members.

### [Financial Matters and Audits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-35466 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Books of account

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  board shall cause books of account and other records to be kept and
  shall establish financial and management controls, information systems
  and management practices that will ensure that the business and
  affairs of the foundation are carried on, and the financial, human and
  physical resources of the foundation are managed, effectively,
  efficiently and economically.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information systems

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The books of account and other records of the
  foundation shall be maintained in such a way that they will show that
  the assets of the foundation are properly protected and controlled and
  that its business and affairs are carried on in compliance with this
  Part and the regulations, and in particular in such a way that they
  will show

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} descriptions and book values of all investments of
    the foundation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the eligible recipients who have received, or are
    about to receive, grants from the foundation in respect of eligible
    projects, the nature and extent of the projects and the amount of
    the grants.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Auditor

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  members, at their first meeting in each fiscal year, shall appoint an
  auditor for the foundation for the fiscal year and fix, or authorize
  the board to fix, the auditor's remuneration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications of auditor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The auditor shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is a member in good standing of an institute or
      association of accountants incorporated by or under an Act of the
      legislature of a province,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has at least five years experience at a senior
      level in carrying out audits,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is ordinarily resident in Canada, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} is independent of the board, the directors and
      the officers of the foundation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a firm of accountants at least one of the members
    or employees of which who are jointly designated by the board and
    the firm to conduct on behalf of the firm the audit of the books and
    records of the foundation meet the qualifications set out in
    paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of auditor

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an auditor is not appointed at the first meeting
  of the members in a fiscal year, the auditor for the preceding fiscal
  year continues in office until a successor is appointed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal of auditor

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The members may by a special resolution of the
  members remove an auditor from office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ceasing to hold office

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An auditor ceases to hold office when the auditor

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} resigns; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is removed from office under subsection (4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacement

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The members, at a meeting of the members, may appoint
  an auditor to fill any vacancy in the office of the auditor, but if
  the members fail to fill the vacancy at a meeting, or if no meeting of
  the members is convened without delay after the vacancy occurs, the
  board may appoint an auditor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unexpired term

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} An auditor appointed to fill a vacancy in the office
  holds office for the unexpired term of the predecessor in the office.

  established by the board.

### [Reports and Consideration of Reports]{.HTitleText2} {#h-35510 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual reports

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  foundation shall, within three months after the end of each fiscal
  year, prepare an annual report in both official languages of its
  activities during the year and include in the report

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its financial statement for the year as approved by
    the board;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its balance sheet as at the end of the year
    including a detailed statement of its investment portfolio as at the
    end of the year; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the report of the auditor for the year in respect
    of the audit of the books and records of the foundation for the
    year, the auditor's certificates respecting the financial statement
    and balance sheet and any other reports of the auditor respecting
    the financial circumstances of the foundation in the year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration of report by members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before the annual report of the foundation for a
  fiscal year is distributed to the public, it shall be approved by the
  board and by the members at a meeting of the members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution of report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} After the annual report of the foundation for a
  fiscal year is approved as required under subsection (2), the report
  shall be made public in accordance with the by-laws of the foundation
  and a copy shall be sent to the Minister, who shall cause a copy of
  the report to be laid before each House of Parliament on any of the
  first fifteen days on which that House is sitting after the Minister
  receives it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public meeting

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  it publishes its annual report for a fiscal year, the foundation shall
  convene a public meeting at a city in Canada selected by the board to
  consider the report and other matters relating to its activities
  during the year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of meeting

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At least thirty days before the date of a meeting
  convened under subsection (1) to consider the foundation's annual
  report for a fiscal year, the foundation shall give notice of the time
  and place of the meeting in accordance with the by-laws of the
  foundation.

### [Winding Up]{.HTitleText2} {#h-35526 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property to be divided

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  foundation is wound up or dissolved, all its property remaining after
  all its debts and obligations have been satisfied shall be liquidated
  and the moneys arising from that liquidation shall be distributed
  among all the eligible recipients that have received grants from the
  foundation and that are, as of the day the distribution begins, still
  carrying on research, to be used by them for the purpose of that
  research, in such a way that each such recipient receives an amount
  that is the same proportion of those moneys that the total grants
  received by that recipient from the foundation is of the total of all
  grants that have been made by the foundation to all such eligible
  recipients.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repayment out of remaining property

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Minister may require the
  foundation to repay out of the moneys arising from the liquidation to
  the Receiver General for credit to the Consolidated Revenue Fund any
  amount that is so repayable under the terms or conditions on which
  public moneys were provided to the foundation.

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding section 87 of the

  the by-law.

  [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, except with respect to a tax

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Moneys raised pursuant to a tax referred to in

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Expenditures made out of moneys raised pursuant to a

  Tribes present at a meeting of the council duly convened.

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A by-law made under subsection (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall be valid only if approved by a majority of
    the councillors of the Cowichan Tribes present at a meeting of the
    council duly convened;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall not come into force before it is approved by
    the Minister;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} shall, except where an agreement has been entered
    into pursuant to subsection 39(1),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} provide the method of calculating the tax,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} set out the rate at which the tax is imposed,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} provide for the time and manner in which the
      tax that is required to be collected by the vendor of the tobacco
      is to be remitted to the council, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} provide for the form of, and the information to
      be contained in, the books and records to be kept pursuant to
      subsection 38(1);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} shall, where an agreement has been entered into
    pursuant to subsection 39(1), provide that the rate of tax is the
    rate provided for in the Tobacco Tax Act, R.S.B.C. 1979, c. 404, as
    amended from time to time;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} may apply to one or more kinds of tobacco and may
    provide for different rates for different kinds of tobacco;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} may be made with respect to any matter arising out
    of or ancillary to the exercise of powers under this section; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} is not subject to the [Statutory Instruments
    Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

## [PART XI]{.HLabel1}[Payment to the Canada Foundation for Innovation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-35656 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}\$800,000,000 granted

**[[94]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-94 .sectionLabel}** From and out of the
Consolidated Revenue Fund there may, on the requisition of the Minister
of Industry, be paid and applied a sum not exceeding eight hundred
million dollars, plus interest thereon compounded monthly based on the
average daily balance outstanding for the month and calculated from the
day on which this Act received first reading in the House of Commons to
the day on which this Act is assented to at an annual rate, in each
month in which the interest is calculated, that is equivalent to ninety
per cent of the simple arithmetic mean of the annual rate of yield on
the three month treasury bills that were issued and sold in the
immediately preceding month, for payment to the Canada Foundation for
Innovation for its use.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}\$1,250,000,000 granted

**[[95]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-95 .sectionLabel}** From and out of the
Consolidated Revenue Fund there may, on the requisition of the Minister
of Finance, be paid and applied a sum of one billion, two hundred and
fifty million dollars for the fiscal year beginning on April 1, 2000,
for payment to the Canada Foundation for Innovation for its use.
