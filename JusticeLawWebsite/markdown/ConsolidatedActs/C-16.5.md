## Canadian Food Inspection Agency Act {#canadian-food-inspection-agency-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1997, c. 6
:::

Assented to 1997-03-20

An Act to establish the Canadian Food Inspection Agency and to repeal
and amend other Acts as a consequence
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the Government of Canada wishes to enhance the effectiveness and
efficiency of federal inspection and related services for food and
animal and plant health by consolidating them;

WHEREAS the consolidation of those services under a single food
inspection agency will contribute to consumer protection and facilitate
a more uniform and consistent approach to safety and quality standards
and risk-based inspection systems;

WHEREAS the Government of Canada wishes to have that food inspection
agency deliver those services in a cost effective manner;

WHEREAS the Government of Canada wishes to promote trade and commerce;

AND WHEREAS the Government of Canada wishes to pursue a greater degree
of collaboration and consultation between federal departments and with
other orders of government in this area;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-67970 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canadian Food Inspection Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-16.5).

## [Establishment of the Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-67978 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby established
a body corporate called the Canadian Food Inspection Agency, which may
exercise powers only as an agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister responsible

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister is responsible for and has the overall direction of the
  Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation by Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may delegate to any person any power,
  duty or function conferred on the Minister under this Act or any Act
  or provision that the Agency enforces or administers by virtue of
  section 11, except the power to make regulations and the power to
  delegate under this subsection.

## [Organization and Head Office]{.HTitleText1} {#h-67986 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Agency executives

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
shall appoint a President and an Executive Vice-president of the Agency
to hold office during pleasure for a term not exceeding five years,
which term may be renewed for one or more further terms.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President's powers

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President is chief executive officer of the Agency and has supervision
  over and direction of its work and staff. The President has the rank
  and all the powers of a deputy head of a Department.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Executive Vice-president's powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Executive Vice-president shall exercise such
  powers and perform such duties and functions as the President may
  assign and shall act as President if that office is vacant or if the
  President is absent or incapacitated.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation by President

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The President may delegate
to any person any power, duty or function conferred on the President
under this Act or any other enactment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The President and Executive
Vice-president shall be paid such remuneration as is fixed by the
Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the
Agency shall be in the National Capital Region, as described in the
schedule to the [National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4).

## [Advisory Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-68000 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of members

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall appoint an advisory board of not more than twelve
  members to hold office during pleasure for a term not exceeding three
  years, which term may be renewed for one or more further terms.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role of advisory board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The board shall advise the Minister on any matter
  within the responsibilities of the Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may appoint any person with relevant
  knowledge or experience to the advisory board, including persons from
  the agriculture, fisheries, food processing, food distribution and
  public health sectors, consumer groups or provincial or municipal
  governments.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall appoint one of the members as
  Chairperson of the advisory board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees for services

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Each member of the advisory board shall be paid such
  fees for his or her services as are fixed by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Travel and living expenses

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Each member of the advisory board shall be reimbursed
  for reasonable travel and living expenses incurred by the member in
  the course of performing duties under this Act while absent from his
  or her ordinary place of residence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson may determine the times and places at
  which the advisory board will meet.

## [Responsibilities of the Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-68016 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administration and enforcement

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agency is responsible for the administration and enforcement of the
  [Agriculture and Agri-Food Administrative Monetary Penalties
  Act](/eng/acts/A-8.8), [Feeds Act](/eng/acts/F-9), [Fertilizers
  Act](/eng/acts/F-10), [Health of Animals Act](/eng/acts/H-3.3), [Plant
  Breeders' Rights Act](/eng/acts/P-14.6), [Plant Protection
  Act](/eng/acts/P-14.8), [Safe Food for Canadians Act](/eng/acts/S-1.1)
  and [Seeds Act](/eng/acts/S-8).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2012, c. 24, s. 103\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Agency is responsible for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the enforcement of the [Food and Drugs
    Act](/eng/acts/F-27) as it relates to food, as defined in section 2
    of that Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the administration of the provisions of the [Food
    and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27) as they relate to food, as defined in
    section 2 of that Act, except those provisions that relate to public
    health, safety or nutrition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role of Minister of Health

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Health is responsible for
  establishing policies and standards relating to the safety and
  nutritional quality of food sold in Canada and assessing the
  effectiveness of the Agency's activities related to food safety.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role of Canada Border Services Agency

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Canada Border Services Agency is responsible for
  the enforcement of the program legislation referred to in
  paragraph (b) of the definition [[program
  legislation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the [Canada Border
  Services Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-1.4) as that program legislation
  relates to the delivery of passenger and initial import inspection
  services performed at airports and other Canadian border points other
  than import service centres.

## [Recalls]{.HTitleText1} {#h-68060 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recall order

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Minister believes on reasonable grounds that a product regulated
  under an Act or provision that the Agency enforces or administers by
  virtue of section 11 poses a risk to public, animal or plant health,
  the Minister may, by notice served on any person selling, marketing or
  distributing the product, order that the product be recalled or sent
  to a place designated by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of recall order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person who contravenes a recall order referred to
  in subsection (1) is guilty of an offence and liable on summary
  conviction to a fine not exceeding \$50,000 or to a term of
  imprisonment not exceeding six months or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a recall order is not a
  statutory instrument for the purposes of the [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22), but no person shall be convicted of an offence
  under subsection (2) unless the person was notified of the order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[19.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19.1 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in
Council may make regulations respecting the recall of products regulated
under an Act or provision that the Agency enforces or administers by
virtue of section 11.

## [Review of Certain Measures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1109946 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review officer

**[[19.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19.2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
designate as review officers any individual or class of individuals that
are qualified to conduct reviews under section 19.3.

::: HistoricalNote

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for review

- **[[19.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19.3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  the request of a prescribed person in relation to a prescribed
  decision made under an Act or provision that the Agency enforces or
  administers by virtue of section 11, a review officer must conduct a
  review of the decision in the prescribed manner subject to the
  regulations and the other provisions of this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request must be made in the prescribed manner,
  contain the prescribed information and be made in the prescribed time
  after the making of the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligibility

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The review officer must be a person other than the
  one who made the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The review is restricted to questions of fact alone
  or questions of mixed law and fact.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No authority to review

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The review is not to be conducted if the request does
  not comply with subsection (2) or is frivolous, vexatious or not made
  in good faith.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons for refusal

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If subsection (5) applies, the person who made the
  request is to be, without delay, notified in writing of the reasons
  for the review not being conducted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision not stayed

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The conduct of the review does not stay the decision
  unless the review officer decides otherwise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision on completion of review

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} On completion of the review, the review officer must
  confirm, vary or cancel the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The person who made the request must, without delay,
  be notified in writing of the reasons for the review officer's
  decision under subsection (8).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make regulations
  prescribing anything that by this section is to be prescribed.

## [Corporate Business Plan and Annual Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-68075 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporate business plan

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As
  soon as possible after the Agency is established and at least once
  every five years after that, the Agency must submit a corporate
  business plan to the Minister for approval and the Minister must table
  a copy of the plan in each House of Parliament on any of the first
  fifteen days on which that House is sitting after the Minister
  approves the plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of corporate business plan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The corporate business plan must include a statement
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Agency's objectives for the period to which the
    plan relates and for each year in that period;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strategies that the Agency intends to use to
    achieve its objectives, including operational, financial and human
    resource strategies;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Agency's expected performance over that period;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Agency's operating and capital budgets for each
    year of that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Updating of corporate business plan

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Agency may update its corporate business plan in
  its annual report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President must, before September 30 of each year following the
  Agency's first full year of operations, submit an annual report on the
  operations of the Agency for the preceding year to the Minister and
  the Minister must table a copy of the report in each House of
  Parliament on any of the first fifteen days on which that House is
  sitting after the Minister receives the report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The annual report must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the financial statements of the Agency;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information about the Agency's performance with
    respect to the objectives established in the corporate business
    plan; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other information that the Minister or the
    Treasury Board may require to be included in it.

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24

  Minister may fix the fees to be paid for a service or the use of a
  facility provided by the Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount not to exceed cost

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Fees fixed under subsection (1) may not exceed the
  cost to Her Majesty in right of Canada of providing the service or the
  use of the facility.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees for products, rights and privileges

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the

  privileges provided by the Agency under the [Safe Food for Canadians

make regulations for the purposes of sections 24 to 26.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collection of fees

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The Agency may enter into

respecting the collection of fees fixed under this Act or any other Act
that the Agency enforces or administers by virtue of subsection 11(1)
and, notwithstanding subsections 17(1) and (4) of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), authorizing that person, government
or authority to withhold amounts from those fees.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-payment of fees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may withdraw or withhold a service, use
  of a facility, a product or conferral of a right or privilege within
  the responsibilities of the Agency, from any person who fails to pay
  the fee fixed for it if, in the Minister's opinion, it is consistent
  with public health and safety.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenditure

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** In carrying out its
responsibilities, the Agency may spend money that Parliament
appropriates to it and revenues received by it through the conduct of
its operations, including

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} payments for the sale, exchange, lease, loan,
  transfer or other disposition of personal or movable property;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} payments for the sale, lease or other disposition
  or transfer of real or immovable property;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} fees for the provision of a service or use of a
  facility or for a product, right or privilege; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} refunds of expenditures made in the previous fiscal
  year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Books and records

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** The Agency must keep
books of account and records prepared according to generally accepted
accounting principles.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-68131 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriation Act

**[[32.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32.1 .sectionLabel}** The provisions made
by any appropriation Act for the fiscal year in which this section comes
into force or a subsequent fiscal year, based on the Estimates for that
year, to defray the charges and expenses of the public service of Canada
within the Departments of Agriculture and Agri-Food, Fisheries and
Oceans and Health in relation to any matter for which the Agency is
responsible by virtue of section 11 are deemed to be an amount
appropriated for defraying the charges and expenses of the Canadian Food
Inspection Agency in such amount as the Treasury Board may, on the
recommendations of the Ministers of Agriculture and Agri-Food, Fisheries
and Oceans and Health, determine.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agency employees continued

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  coming into force of subsection 13(1), employees deployed to the
  Agency, or appointed to it by the Public Service Commission, are
  deemed to have been appointed by the President and continue to be
  employed by the Agency with the same tenure of office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pending competitions and appointments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the coming into force of subsection 13(1), a
  competition being conducted or an appointment being made under the
  [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) shall continue to
  be conducted or made as if that section had not come into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility lists

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** An eligibility list made
under the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) that is
valid on the coming into force of subsection 13(1) of this Act continues
to be valid for the period provided for by subsection 17(2) of that Act,
but that period may not be extended.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pending appeals

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appeal commenced under section 21 of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) and not finally disposed of on the coming into
  force of subsection 13(1) of this Act shall be dealt with and disposed
  of in accordance with that Act as if that subsection had not come into
  force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other recourse

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any recourse commenced under the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) that has not been finally dealt
  with on the coming into force of subsection 13(1) of this Act shall be
  dealt with and disposed of in accordance with that Act as if that
  subsection had not come into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designations continued

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** Every inspector, analyst,
grader, veterinary inspector or other officer appointed or designated
under an Act referred to in section 11 to enforce or administer any Act
or provision that the Agency enforces or administers by virtue of that
section is deemed to be designated by the President under subsection
13(3), according to the terms of the original designation or
appointment.
