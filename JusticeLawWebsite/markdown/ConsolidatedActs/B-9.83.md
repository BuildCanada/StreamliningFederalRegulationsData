## Budget Implementation Act, 1999 {#budget-implementation-act-1999 .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1999, c. 26
:::

Assented to 1999-06-17

An Act to implement certain provisions of the budget tabled in
Parliament on February 16, 1999
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
