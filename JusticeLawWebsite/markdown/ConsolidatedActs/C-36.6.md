## Condominium Ordinance Validation Act {#condominium-ordinance-validation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1985, c. 46
:::

Assented to 1985-10-29

An Act to validate certain ordinances of the Yukon Territory and the
Northwest Territories
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Condominium Ordinance Validation Act](/eng/acts/C-36.6).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Yukon Territory *Condominium Ordinance*
validated

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#C-36.6_en_1){#C-36.6_en_1-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** The *Condominium Ordinance* of the Yukon Territory,
chapter C-12 of the Revised Ordinances of the Yukon Territory, 1971, and
all amendments thereto made before the coming into force of this Act
shall be deemed for all purposes to have been validly made under the
[Yukon Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.01) as if the Ordinance as so amended were not
in conflict with the Land Titles Act, and everything done, whether
before or after the coming into force of this Act, under the Ordinance
as so amended shall be given such effect as it may otherwise have as if
the Ordinance as so amended were not in conflict with the Land Titles
Act.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#C-36.6_en_1-ID0EBCA){#C-36.6_en_1}\[Note: Act
  in force on assent October 29, 1985.\]
  :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Northwest Territories *Condominium
Ordinance* validated

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#C-36.6_en_2){#C-36.6_en_2-ID0EBCA}[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
.sectionLabel}** The *Condominium Ordinance* of the Northwest
Territories, chapter C-10 of the Revised Ordinances of the Northwest
Territories, 1974, and all amendments thereto made before the coming
into force of this Act shall be deemed for all purposes to have been
validly made under the [Northwest Territories Act](/eng/acts/N-27.05) as
if the Ordinance as so amended were not in conflict with the Land Titles
Act, and everything done, whether before or after the coming into force
of this Act, under the Ordinance as so amended shall be given such
effect as it may otherwise have as if the Ordinance as so amended were
not in conflict with the Land Titles Act.
