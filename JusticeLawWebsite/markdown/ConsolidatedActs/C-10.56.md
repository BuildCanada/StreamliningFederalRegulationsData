## Canada--United Kingdom Trade Continuity Agreement Implementation Act {#canadaunited-kingdom-trade-continuity-agreement-implementation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2021, c. 1
:::

Assented to 2021-03-17

An Act to implement the Agreement on Trade Continuity between Canada and
the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland
::::

::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1288229 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada--United Kingdom Trade Continuity Agreement Implementation
Act](/eng/acts/C-10.56).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1288232 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this section and in sections 3 to 15.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement on Trade
    Continuity between Canada and the United Kingdom of Great Britain
    and Northern Ireland, done at Ottawa, on December 9,
    2020. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canada--U.K. Joint Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canada--U.K. Joint Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Canada--U.K. Joint Committee established under the Agreement and
    whose powers, functions and duties are set out in Chapter Twenty-Six
    of CETA. ([Comité mixte Canada--R.-U.]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[CETA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CETA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Comprehensive Economic and
    Trade Agreement between Canada and the European Union and its Member
    States, done at Brussels on October 30,
    2016. ([AÉCG]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the whole or any portion
    of an Act of Parliament or a regulation, order or other instrument
    issued, made or established in the exercise of a power conferred by
    or under an Act of Parliament. ([texte législatif
    fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister for
    International Trade. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation consistent with Agreement

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, this
Act and any federal law that implements a provision of the Agreement or
fulfils an obligation of the Government of Canada under the Agreement is
to be interpreted in a manner consistent with the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References to CETA provisions

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A reference in this Act to
a provision of CETA is a reference to that provision as incorporated by
reference in the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of Act and Agreement to
water

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act or the Agreement, except Chapters Twenty-Two and
Twenty-Four of CETA, applies to natural surface or ground water in
liquid, gaseous or solid state.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act, by specific mention or omission, is to be construed
to affect in any manner the right of Parliament to enact legislation to
implement any provision of the Agreement or fulfil any of the
obligations of the Government of Canada under the Agreement.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1288248 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1288251 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to implement the Agreement, the objectives of which, as elaborated more
specifically through its provisions, are to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish a free trade area in accordance with the
  Agreement;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} promote, through the expansion of reciprocal trade,
  the harmonious development of the economic relations between Canada
  and the United Kingdom in order to create opportunities for economic
  development;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} promote conditions of fair competition affecting
  trade between Canada and the United Kingdom;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} substantially increase investment opportunities in
  Canada and the United Kingdom, while preserving the right of each
  party to the Agreement to regulate to achieve legitimate policy goals;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} eliminate barriers to trade in goods and services in
  order to contribute to the harmonious development and expansion of
  world and regional trade;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} provide adequate and effective protection and
  enforcement of intellectual property rights in the territory where the
  Agreement applies;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} protect, enhance and enforce basic workers' rights,
  strengthen cooperation on labour matters and build on the respective
  international commitments of Canada and the United Kingdom on labour
  matters;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} enhance and enforce environmental laws and
  regulations and strengthen cooperation between Canada and the United
  Kingdom on environmental matters; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} promote sustainable development.

## [Causes of Action]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1288263 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Causes of action under sections 10 to 15

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person has any cause of action and no proceedings of any kind are to
  be taken, without the consent of the Attorney General of Canada, to
  enforce or determine any right or obligation that is claimed or arises
  solely under or by virtue of sections 10 to 15 or an order made under
  those sections.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Causes of action under Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person has any cause of action and no proceedings
  of any kind are to be taken, without the consent of the Attorney
  General of Canada, to enforce or determine any right or obligation
  that is claimed or arises solely under or by virtue of the Agreement.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Implementation of the Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1288271 .Part}

### [Approval and Representation on the Canada--U.K. Joint Committee]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1288272 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement approved

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement is
approved.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian representative on Canada--U.K.
Joint Committee

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is the
principal representative of Canada on the Canada--U.K. Joint Committee.

### [Tribunals, Arbitration Panels and Panels of Experts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1288277 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may

  - ::: hiddenMsg
    The following provision is not in force.
    :::

    ::: nif
    [(a)]{.lawlabel} propose the names of individuals to serve as
    members of the tribunals established under Section F of Chapter
    Eight of CETA; and
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} propose the names of individuals to be included in
    the sub-lists referred to in paragraph 1 of Article 29.8 of CETA.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of Minister of Finance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Finance may propose the names of
  individuals to be included in the sub-lists referred to in paragraph 3
  of Article 13.20 of CETA.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister of Labour

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Labour may propose the names of
  individuals to be included in the list established under paragraph 6
  of Article 23.10 of CETA and propose, for inclusion in that list, the
  names of individuals to serve as the chairperson of a Panel of Experts
  established under that Article.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister of the Environment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of the Environment may propose the names
  of individuals to be included in the list established under paragraph
  6 of Article 24.15 of CETA and propose, for inclusion in that list,
  the names of individuals to serve as the chairperson of a Panel of
  Experts established under that Article.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operation of Chapter Twenty-Nine

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is to
designate an agency, division or branch of the Government of Canada to
facilitate the operation of Chapter Twenty-Nine of CETA.

### [Expenses]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1288291 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of expenses

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
is to pay its appropriate share of the aggregate of

- ::: hiddenMsg
  The following provision is not in force.
  :::

  ::: nif
  [(a)]{.lawlabel} the expenses incurred by tribunals established under
  the Agreement and the remuneration and expenses payable to members of
  those tribunals;
  :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the expenses incurred by arbitration panels and
  Panels of Experts established under the Agreement and the remuneration
  and expenses payable to arbitrators, panellists on those Panels of
  Experts and mediators; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the expenses incurred by the Canada--U.K. Joint
  Committee and the specialized committees, bilateral dialogues, working
  groups and other bodies established under the Agreement and the
  remuneration and expenses payable to representatives on the
  Canada--U.K. Joint Committee and those specialized committees and to
  members of those bilateral dialogues, working groups and other bodies.

### [Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1288297 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders --- Article 29.14 of CETA

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, for the purpose of suspending obligations in
  accordance with Article 29.14 of CETA, by order, do any one or more of
  the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend rights or privileges granted by Canada to
    the United Kingdom or to goods, service suppliers, investors or
    investments of investors of the United Kingdom under the Agreement
    or any federal law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} modify or suspend the application of any federal
    law with respect to the United Kingdom or to goods, service
    suppliers, investors or investments of investors of the United
    Kingdom;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} extend the application of any federal law to the
    United Kingdom or to goods, service suppliers, investors or
    investments of investors of the United Kingdom;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} take any other measure that the Governor in Council
    considers necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless repealed, an order made under subsection (1)
  has effect for the period specified in the order.
