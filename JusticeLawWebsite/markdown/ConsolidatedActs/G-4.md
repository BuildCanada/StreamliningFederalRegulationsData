## Government Corporations Operation Act {#government-corporations-operation-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. G-4
:::

An Act respecting the operation of Government corporations
::::

::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-251555 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Government Corporations Operation Act](/eng/acts/G-4).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-7, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-251560 .Part}

Definition of [[corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,
[[corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a company incorporated under
Part I of the [Canada Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8), chapter C-32
of the Revised Statutes of Canada, 1970, or a corporation incorporated
under the [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44), all the
issued shares of which are owned by or held in trust for Her Majesty in
right of Canada except, in the case of a company incorporated under Part
I of the [Canada Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8), shares necessary to
qualify other persons as directors.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. G-4, s. 2
- 1994, c. 24, s. 34(F)
:::

## [Status, Powers and Personnel of Corporations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-251565 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agent of Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every corporation is for
all its purposes an agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-7, s. 3
- 1984, c. 31, s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employment of officers and employees

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  corporation may, notwithstanding any statute or law, employ such
  officers or employees as it deems necessary to conduct its operations
  and determine the conditions of employment and the remuneration of
  those officers or employees.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation pays remuneration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The remuneration determined under subsection (1)
  shall be paid by the corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every corporation has the control and supervision of
  the officers and employees employed by the corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-7, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pension or superannuation

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) does not apply to
  officers and employees employed by a corporation but each corporation
  may, with the approval of the Governor in Council, establish and
  support a pension fund, a group insurance plan or other pension or
  superannuation arrangements for the benefit of officers and employees
  employed by the corporation and their dependants, including their
  spouse, common-law partner and children, any other relative of the
  officer or employee, and any child or other relative of the spouse or
  common-law partner of the officer or employee. A corporation may, with
  the approval of the Governor in Council, continue any such fund, plan
  or arrangement, established by the corporation, that existed on July
  26, 1946.

- Definition of [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (1), [[common-law
  partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an individual, means a
  person who is cohabiting with the individual in a conjugal
  relationship, having so cohabited for a period of at least one year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Superannuation rights preserved

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1) or any other statute
  or law, a person who, immediately prior to that person's employment by
  a corporation, was a contributor under the [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) continues while employed by the
  corporation to be a contributor under that Act, and for the purposes
  of that Act that person's service with the corporation shall be
  counted as pensionable service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment in public service

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person referred to in subsection (2) is retired
  from employment with a corporation for any reason other than
  misconduct, that person is eligible for re-appointment in the public
  service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of ss. (2) and (3)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (2) and (3) do not apply in respect of
  any person who becomes employed by a corporation on or after January
  1, 1954 to whom, but for this subsection, subsections (2) and (3)
  would apply, and every such person is deemed, for the purposes of the
  [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), to have ceased to
  be employed in the public service, for a reason other than disability
  or misconduct, as of the date of that person's employment by the
  corporation and to have ceased to be a contributor under the [Public
  Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) at that date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefits continued

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any person who at the time of that person's
  employment with a corporation held a position in the public service or
  was an employee within the meaning of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) continues to retain and is eligible for all
  the benefits, except salary as an employee in the public service, that
  that person would have been eligible to receive had that person
  remained an employee in the public service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of [Government Employees
  Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5)

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The [Government Employees Compensation
  Act](/eng/acts/G-5) applies to officers and employees of a corporation
  and for the purposes of that Act those officers and employees shall be
  deemed to be employees in the federal public administration.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. G-4, s. 5
- 2000, c. 12, s. 124
- 2003, c. 22, ss. 224(E), 225(E)
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-251596 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to corporation by
proclamation

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Act applies to a
corporation only from the date of the issue of a proclamation by the
Governor in Council declaring this Act to be applicable to that
corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-7, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of application

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may issue a proclamation declaring that this Act
  shall cease to apply to a corporation in respect of which a
  proclamation has been issued pursuant to section 6.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No prejudice to superannuation rights
  or benefits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the effective date of a proclamation issued
  pursuant to subsection (1), this Act ceases to apply to the
  corporation named therein and the corporation ceases to be an agent of
  Her Majesty but without prejudice to any rights acquired by any person
  pursuant to subsection 5(2), (3) or (5).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-7, s. 7
:::
