## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Miscellaneous]{.HTitleText1} {#h-176717 .Part}

### [Payments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-176718 .Subheading}

#### [Multilateral Debt Relief]{.HTitleText3} {#h-176719 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum payment of \$2,500,000,000

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of Canada's contribution to the Multilateral Debt Relief
  Initiative, or for making contributions towards multilateral debt
  relief, there may, in respect of fiscal years in the period that
  begins on April 1, 2009 and ends on March 31, 2054, be paid out of the
  Consolidated Revenue Fund, on the requisition of the Minister of
  Finance, sums, not exceeding in the aggregate \$200 million in each
  fiscal year, to international organizations in order to make debt
  payments on behalf of eligible countries. No more than a total of
  \$2.5 billion may be paid out under this subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Finance may enter into arrangements
  or agreements with international organizations for the purpose of the
  payments referred to in subsection (1).

#### [Payment to Nova Scotia --- Offshore Petroleum Resources]{.HTitleText3} {#h-176725 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of \$174,500,000

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Before April 1, 2010,
there may be paid to Nova Scotia out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund,
on the requisition of the Minister of Natural Resources, \$174,500,000.
