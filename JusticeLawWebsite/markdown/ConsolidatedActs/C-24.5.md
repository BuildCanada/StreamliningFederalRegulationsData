## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-76969 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to protect public health and public safety and, in particular, to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} protect the health of young persons by restricting
  their access to cannabis;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} protect young persons and others from inducements to
  use cannabis;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide for the licit production of cannabis to
  reduce illicit activities in relation to cannabis;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} deter illicit activities in relation to cannabis
  through appropriate sanctions and enforcement measures;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} reduce the burden on the criminal justice system in
  relation to cannabis;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} provide access to a quality-controlled supply of
  cannabis; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} enhance public awareness of the health risks
  associated with cannabis use.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Prohibitions, Obligations and Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-76979 .Part}

### [DIVISION 1]{.HLabel2}[Criminal Activities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-76980 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Possession

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  authorized under this Act, it is prohibited

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for an individual who is 18 years of age or older
    to possess, in a public place, cannabis of one or more classes of
    cannabis the total amount of which, as determined in accordance with
    Schedule 3, is equivalent to more than 30 g of dried cannabis;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for an individual who is 18 years of age or older
    to possess any cannabis that they know is illicit cannabis;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a young person to possess cannabis of one or
    more classes of cannabis the total amount of which, as determined in
    accordance with Schedule 3, is equivalent to more than 5 g of dried
    cannabis;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for an individual to possess, in a public place,
    one or more cannabis plants that are budding or flowering;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} for an individual to possess more than four
    cannabis plants that are not budding or flowering; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} for an organization to possess cannabis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 51, every person that contravenes
  subsection (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an indictable offence and is liable

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual who is 18 years of
      age or older, to imprisonment for a term of not more than five
      years less a day,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a young person, to a youth
      sentence under the [Youth Criminal Justice Act](/eng/acts/Y-1.5),
      or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an organization, to a fine in an
      amount that is in the discretion of the court; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
    conviction and is liable

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual who is 18 years of
      age or older, to a fine of not more than \$5,000 or imprisonment
      for a term of not more than six months, or to both,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a young person, to a youth
      sentence under the [Youth Criminal Justice Act](/eng/acts/Y-1.5),
      or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an organization, to a fine of
      not more than \$100,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[medical
emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of this section, [[medical
  emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a physiological event induced by
  the introduction of a psychoactive substance into the body of a person
  that results in a life-threatening situation and in respect of which
  there are reasonable grounds to believe that the person requires
  emergency medical or law enforcement assistance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- medical emergency

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person who seeks emergency medical or law
  enforcement assistance because that person, or another person, is
  suffering from a medical emergency is to be charged or convicted of an
  offence under subsection 8(1) if the evidence in support of that
  offence was obtained or discovered as a result of that person having
  sought assist­ance or having remained at the scene.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- persons at the scene

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The exemption under subsection (2) also applies to
  any person, including the person suffering from the medical emergency,
  who is at the scene on the arrival of the emergency medical or law
  enforcement assistance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- evidence

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person who seeks emergency medical or law
  enforcement assistance because that person, or another person, is
  suffering from a medical emergency, or who is at the scene on the
  arrival of the assistance, is to be charged with an offence concerning
  a violation of any condition of a pre-trial release or probation order
  relating to an offence under subsection 8(1) if the evidence in
  support of that offence was obtained or discovered as a result of that
  person having sought assistance or having remained at the scene.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any condition of a person's pre-trial release,
  probation order, conditional sentence or parole relating to an offence
  under subsection 8(1) that may be violated as a result of the person
  seeking emergency medical or law enforcement assistance for their, or
  another person's, medical emergency, or as a result of having been at
  the scene on the arrival of the assistance, is deemed not to be
  violated.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  authorized under this Act, it is prohibited

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for an individual who is 18 years of age or older

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to distribute cannabis of one or more classes of
      cannabis the total amount of which is equivalent, as determined in
      accordance with Schedule 3, to more than 30 g of dried cannabis,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to distribute cannabis to an individual who is
      under 18 years of age,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to distribute cannabis to an organization, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} to distribute cannabis that they know is illicit
      cannabis;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a young person

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to distribute cannabis of one or more classes of
      cannabis the total amount of which is equivalent, as determined in
      accordance with Schedule 3, to more than 5 g of dried cannabis,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to distribute cannabis to an organization;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for an individual

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to distribute one or more cannabis plants that
      are budding or flowering, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to distribute more than four cannabis plants
      that are not budding or flowering; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for an organization to distribute cannabis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Possession for purpose of distributing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless authorized under this Act, it is prohibited to
  possess cannabis for the purpose of distributing it contrary to
  subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence --- subparagraph (1)(a)(ii)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} It is not a defence to a charge arising out of the
  contravention of subparagraph (1)(a)(ii) that the accused believed
  that the individual referred to in that subparagraph was 18 years of
  age or older, unless the accused took reasonable steps to ascertain
  the individual's age.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence --- subsection (2)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} It is not a defence to a charge arising out of the
  contravention of subsection (2) of possessing cannabis for the purpose
  of distributing it to an individual referred to in subparagraph
  (1)(a)(ii) that the accused believed that the individual was 18 years
  of age or older, unless the accused took reasonable steps to ascertain
  the individual's age.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 51, every person that contravenes
  subsection (1) or (2)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an indictable offence and is liable

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual who is 18 years of
      age or older, to imprisonment for a term of not more than 14
      years,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a young person, to a youth
      sentence under the [Youth Criminal Justice Act](/eng/acts/Y-1.5),
      or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an organization, to a fine in an
      amount that is in the discretion of the court; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
    conviction and is liable

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual who is 18 years of
      age or older who contravenes any of subparagraphs (1)(a)(i), (iii)
      and (iv) and (c)(i) and (ii) --- or subsection (2) other than by
      possessing cannabis for the purpose of distributing it contrary to
      subparagraph (1)(a)(ii) --- to a fine of not more than \$5,000 or
      imprisonment for a term of not more than six months, or to both,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual who is 18 years of
      age or older who contravenes subparagraph (1)(a)(ii) --- or
      subsection (2) if the possession was for the purpose of
      distribution contrary to subparagraph (1)(a)(ii) --- to a fine of
      not more than \$15,000 or imprisonment for a term of not more than
      18 months, or to both,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a young person, to a youth
      sentence under the [Youth Criminal Justice Act](/eng/acts/Y-1.5),
      or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an organization, to a fine of not
      more than \$100,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Selling

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  authorized under this Act, it is prohibited to sell cannabis, or any
  substance represented or held out to be cannabis, to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an individual who is 18 years of age or older;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an individual who is under 18 years of age; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an organization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Possession for purpose of selling

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless authorized under this Act, it is prohibited to
  possess cannabis for the purpose of selling it contrary to any of
  paragraphs (1)(a) to (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence --- paragraph (1)(b)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} It is not a defence to a charge arising out of the
  contravention of paragraph (1)(b) that the accused believed that the
  individual referred to in that paragraph was 18 years of age or older,
  unless the accused took reasonable steps to ascertain the individual's
  age.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence --- subsection (2)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} It is not a defence to a charge arising out of the
  contravention of subsection (2) of possessing cannabis for the purpose
  of selling it contrary to paragraph (1)(b) that the accused believed
  that the individual referred to in that paragraph was 18 years of age
  or older, unless the accused took reasonable steps to ascertain the
  individual's age.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 51, every person that contravenes
  any of paragraphs (1)(a) to (c) or subsection (2)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an indictable offence and is liable to
    imprisonment for a term of not more than 14 years; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
    conviction and is liable

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual who contravenes
      paragraph (1)(a) or (c) --- or subsection (2) other than by
      possessing cannabis for the purpose of selling it contrary to
      paragraph (1)(b) --- to a fine of not more than \$5,000 or
      imprisonment for a term of not more than six months, or to both,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual who contravenes
      paragraph (1)(b) --- or subsection (2) if the possession was for
      the purpose of selling contrary to paragraph (1)(b) --- to a fine
      of not more than \$15,000 or imprisonment for a term of not more
      than 18 months, or to both, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an organization, to a fine of
      not more than \$100,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Importing and exporting

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  authorized under this Act, the importation or exportation of cannabis
  is prohibited.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Possession for purpose of exporting

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless authorized under this Act, it is prohibited to
  possess cannabis for the purpose of exporting it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person that contravenes subsection (1) or (2)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an indictable offence and is liable to
    imprisonment for a term of not more than 14 years; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
    conviction and is liable

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine of not
      more than \$5,000 or imprisonment for a term of not more than six
      months, or to both, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an organization, to a fine of not
      more than \$300,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Production

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  authorized under this Act, it is prohibited

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to obtain or offer to obtain cannabis by any method
    or process, including by manufacturing, by synthesis or by using any
    means of altering the chemical or physical properties of cannabis;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to alter or offer to alter the chemical or physical
    properties of cannabis by the use of an organic solvent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized alteration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An individual may alter the chemical or physical
  properties of any cannabis that they are not prohibited by this Act
  from possessing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[organic
  solvent]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In paragraph (1)(b), [[organic
  solvent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any organic compound that is
  explosive or highly or extremely flammable, including petroleum
  naphtha and compressed liquid hydrocarbons such as butane, isobutane,
  propane and propylene.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cultivation, propagation and harvesting
  --- 18 years of age or older

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Unless authorized under this Act, it is prohibited
  for an individual who is 18 years of age or older to cultivate,
  propagate or harvest, or to offer to cultivate, propagate or harvest,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a cannabis plant that is from a seed or plant
    material that they know is illicit cannabis; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} more than four cannabis plants at any one time in
    their dwelling-house.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cultivation, propagation and harvesting
  --- dwelling-house limit

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Unless authorized under this Act, if two or more
  individuals who are 18 years of age or older are ordinarily resident
  in the same dwelling-house, it is prohibited for any of those
  individuals to cultivate, propagate or harvest any cannabis plants if
  doing so results in there being more than four such plants being
  cultivated, propagated or harvested at any one time in the
  dwelling-house.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cultivation, propagation and harvesting
  --- 18 years of age or older --- without authorization

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Unless authorized under this Act, it is prohibited
  for an individual who is 18 years of age or older

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to cultivate, propagate or harvest any cannabis
    plant at a place that is not their dwelling-house or to offer to do
    so; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to cultivate, propagate or harvest any living
    thing, other than a cannabis plant, from which cannabis may be
    extracted or otherwise obtained, or to offer to do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cultivation, propagation and harvesting
  --- young persons and organizations

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Unless authorized under this Act, it is prohibited
  for a young person or an organization to cultivate, propagate or
  harvest any cannabis plant or any other living thing from which
  cannabis may be extracted or otherwise obtained, or to offer to do any
  of those things.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[dwelling-house]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section,
  [[dwelling-house]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an individual,
  means the dwelling-house where the individual is ordinarily resident
  and includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any land that is subjacent to it and the
    immediately contiguous land that is attributable to it, including a
    yard, garden or any similar land; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any building or structure on any land referred to
    in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 51, every individual who is 18
  years of age or older who contravenes any of subsections (1), (4), (5)
  and (6) or any organization that contravenes subsection (1) or (7)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an indictable offence and is liable to
    a term of imprisonment of not more than 14 years; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
    conviction and is liable

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine of not
      more than \$5,000 or imprisonment for a term of not more than six
      months, or to both, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an organization, to a fine of not
      more than \$100,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment --- young person

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} Every young person who contravenes subsection (1)
  or (7) is guilty of an indictable offence, or an offence punishable on
  summary conviction, and is liable to a youth sentence under the [Youth
  Criminal Justice Act](/eng/acts/Y-1.5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Possession, etc., for use in production
or distribution of illicit cannabis

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited to possess, produce, sell, distribute or import anything
  with the intention that it will be used to produce, sell or distribute
  illicit cannabis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person that contravenes subsection (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an indictable offence and is liable to
    imprisonment for a term of not more than seven years; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
    conviction and is liable

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine of not
      more than \$5,000 or imprisonment for a term of not more than six
      months, or to both, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an organization, to a fine of not
      more than \$100,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of young person

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited to use the services of, or to involve, a young person in
  the commission of an offence under subsection 9(1) or (2), 10(1) or
  (2), 11(1) or (2), 12(1), (4), (5), (6) or (7) or 13(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person that contravenes subsection (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an indictable offence and is liable to
    imprisonment for a term of not more than 14 years; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
    conviction and is liable

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine of not
      more than \$15,000 or imprisonment for a term of not more than 18
      months, or to both, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an organization, to a fine of not
      more than \$100,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sentencing

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Without restricting the generality
  of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the fundamental purpose of any
  sentence for an offence under this Division is to contribute to the
  respect for the law and the maintenance of a just, peaceful and safe
  society while encouraging rehabilitation, and treatment in appropriate
  circumstances, of offenders and acknowledging the harm done to victims
  and to the community.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to take into consideration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an individual is convicted of a designated
  offence, the court imposing sentence on the individual must consider
  any relevant aggravating factors, including that the individual

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the commission of the offence,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} carried, used or threatened to use a weapon,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} used or threatened to use violence, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} sold or distributed cannabis or possessed it
      for the purpose of sale or distribution, in or near a school, on
      or near school grounds or in or near any other public place
      usually frequented by young persons; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} was previously convicted of a [[designated
    offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of
    this Act, or a [[designated substance
    offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of
    the [Controlled Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, in the case of an individual who is convicted of
  a designated offence, the court is satisfied of the existence of one
  or more of the aggravating factors enumerated in paragraphs (2)(a) and
  (b), but decides not to sentence the individual to imprisonment, the
  court must give reasons for that decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Drug treatment court program

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A court sentencing an individual who is convicted of
  an offence under this Division may delay sentencing to enable the
  individual

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to participate in a drug treatment court program
    approved by the Attorney General; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to attend a treatment program under subsection
    720(2) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

### [DIVISION 2]{.HLabel2}[Other Prohibitions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-77140 .Subheading}

#### [SUBDIVISION A]{.HLabel3}[Promotion]{.HTitleText3} {#h-77141 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the
regulations, this Subdivision does not apply

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to a literary, dramatic, musical, cinematographic,
  scientific, educational or artistic work, production or performance
  that uses or depicts cannabis, a cannabis accessory or a service
  related to cannabis, or a brand element of any of those things,
  whatever the mode or form of its expression, if no consideration is
  given, directly or indirectly, for that use or depiction in the work,
  production or performance;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to a report, commentary or opinion in respect of
  cannabis, a cannabis accessory or a service related to cannabis or a
  brand element of any of those things, if no consideration is given,
  directly or indirectly, for the reference to the cannabis, cannabis
  accessory, service or brand element in that report, commentary or
  opinion;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to a promotion, by a person that is authorized to
  produce, sell or distribute cannabis, that is directed at any person
  that is authorized to produce, sell or distribute cannabis, but not,
  either directly or indirectly, at consumers; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to a promotion, by a person that sells or distributes
  cannabis accessories or that provides a service related to cannabis,
  that is directed at any person that sells or distributes cannabis
  accessories, at any person that is authorized to produce, sell or
  distribute cannabis, but not, either directly or indirectly, at
  consumers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Promotion

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  authorized under this Act, it is prohibited to promote cannabis or a
  cannabis accessory or any service related to cannabis, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by communicating information about its price or
    distribution;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by doing so in a manner that there are reasonable
    grounds to believe could be appealing to young persons;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by means of a testimonial or endorsement, however
    displayed or communicated;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by means of the depiction of a person, character or
    animal, whether real or fictional; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} by presenting it or any of its brand elements in a
    manner that associates it or the brand element with, or evokes a
    positive or negative emotion about or image of, a way of life such
    as one that includes glamour, recreation, excitement, vitality, risk
    or daring.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- informational promotion
  --- cannabis

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, a person that is
  authorized to produce, sell or distribute cannabis may promote
  cannabis by means of informational promotion or brand-preference
  promotion if the promotion is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a communication that is addressed and sent to an
    individual who is 18 years of age or older and is identified by
    name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a place where young persons are not permitted by
    law;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} communicated by means of a telecommunication, where
    the person responsible for the content of the promotion has taken
    reasonable steps to ensure that the promotion cannot be accessed by
    a young person;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in a prescribed place; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} done in a prescribed manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- informational promotion
  --- cannabis accessories and services

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, a person may promote a
  cannabis accessory or a service related to cannabis by means of
  informational promotion or brand-preference promotion if the promotion
  is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a communication that is addressed and sent to an
    individual who is 18 years of age or older and is identified by
    name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a place where young persons are not permitted by
    law;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} communicated by means of a telecommunication, where
    the person responsible for the content of the promotion has taken
    reasonable steps to ensure that the promotion cannot be accessed by
    a young person;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in a prescribed place; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} done in a prescribed manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- point of sale ---
  cannabis

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, a person that is
  authorized to sell cannabis may promote it at the point of sale if the
  promotion indicates only its availability, its price or its
  availability and price.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- point of sale ---
  cannabis accessory and services

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, a person that sells a
  cannabis accessory or provides a service related to cannabis may
  promote it at the point of sale if the promotion indicates only its
  availability, its price or its availability and price.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- brand element on other
  things

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, a person may promote
  cannabis, a cannabis accessory or a service related to cannabis by
  displaying a brand element of cannabis, of a cannabis accessory or of
  a service related to cannabis on a thing that is not cannabis or a
  cannabis accessory, other than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a thing that is associated with young persons;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a thing that there are reasonable grounds to
    believe could be appealing to young persons; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a thing that is associated with a way of life such
    as one that includes glamour, recreation, excitement, vitality, risk
    or daring.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False promotion --- cannabis

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited to promote cannabis in a manner that is false, misleading
  or deceptive or that is likely to create an erroneous impression about
  its characteristics, value, quantity, composition, strength,
  concentration, potency, purity, quality, merit, safety, health effects
  or health risks.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False promotion --- cannabis accessory

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited to promote a cannabis accessory in a
  manner that is false, misleading or deceptive or that is likely to
  create an erroneous impression about its design, construction,
  performance, intended use, characteristics, value, composition, merit,
  safety, health effects or health risks.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of certain terms, etc.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited to use
any term, expression, logo, symbol or illustration specified in
regulations made under paragraph 139(1)(z.1) in the promotion of
cannabis, a cannabis accessory or a service related to cannabis.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Promotion using foreign media

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited to
promote, in a way that is prohibited by this Part, cannabis, a cannabis
accessory, a service related to cannabis or a brand element of any of
those things in a publication that is published outside Canada, a
broadcast that originates outside Canada or any other communication that
originates outside Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sponsorship

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited to
display, refer to or otherwise use any of the following, directly or
indirectly in a promotion that is used in the sponsorship of a person,
entity, event, activity or facility:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a brand element of cannabis, of a cannabis accessory
  or of a service related to cannabis; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of a person that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} produces, sells or distributes cannabis,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} sells or distributes a cannabis accessory, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} provides a service related to cannabis.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Name of facility

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited to
display on a facility, as part of the name of the facility or otherwise,
if the facility is used for a sports or cultural event or activity,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a brand element of cannabis, a cannabis accessory or
  a service related to cannabis; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of a person that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} produces, sells or distributes cannabis,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} sells or distributes a cannabis accessory, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} provides a service related to cannabis.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication, etc. of prohibited
promotions

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited to publish, broadcast or otherwise disseminate, on behalf
  of another person, with or without consideration, any promotion that
  is prohibited by any of sections 17 to 22.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the distribution for sale of an
    imported publication;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of
    [[broadcasting]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection
    2(1) of the [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01), by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a [[distribution
      undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in that subsection
      2(1), that is lawful under that Act, other than the broadcasting
      of a promotion that is inserted by the undertaking, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an [[online undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
      as defined in that subsection 2(1), that is lawful under that Act,
      in respect of the retransmission of programs over the Internet,
      other than the broadcasting of a promotion that is inserted by the
      undertaking; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a person that disseminates a
    promotion if they did not know, at the time of the dissemination,
    that it includes a promotion that is prohibited under any of
    sections 17 to 22.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inducements

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless

#### [SUBDIVISION B]{.HLabel3}[Packaging and Labelling]{.HTitleText3} {#h-77220 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with regulations

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for a
person that is authorized to sell cannabis to sell cannabis that has not
been packaged or labelled in accordance with the regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited packaging and labelling ---

  health effects or health risks of the cannabis.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited packaging and labelling ---

  health risks of the cannabis accessory.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of certain terms, etc.

#### [SUBDIVISION C]{.HLabel3}[Display]{.HTitleText3} {#h-77239 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Display of cannabis

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** Unless authorized under
this Act, it is prohibited for a person that is authorized to sell
cannabis to display it, or any package or label of cannabis, in a manner
that may result in the cannabis, package or label being seen by a young
person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Display of cannabis accessory

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Unless authorized under
this Act, it is prohibited for a person that sells a cannabis accessory
to display it, or any package or label of a cannabis accessory, in a
manner that may result in the cannabis accessory, package or label being
seen by a young person.

#### [SUBDIVISION D]{.HLabel3}[Selling and Distributing]{.HTitleText3} {#h-77244 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal to young persons

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** Unless authorized under
this Act, it is prohibited to sell cannabis or a cannabis accessory that
has an appearance, shape or other sensory attribute or a function that
there are reasonable grounds to believe could be appealing to young
persons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Selling cannabis accessory to young
person

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  authorized under this Act, it is prohibited to sell a cannabis
  accessory to a young person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is not a defence to a charge under subsection (1)
  that the accused believed that the young person referred to in that
  subsection was at least 18 years of age, unless the accused took
  reasonable steps to ascertain the individual's age.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited sales

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** Unless authorized under
this Act, it is prohibited for a person that is authorized to sell
cannabis to sell cannabis of any class that is not referred to in
Schedule 4.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited substances

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  authorized under this Act, it is prohibited to sell any mixture of
  substances that contains cannabis and any substance that is referred
  to in column 1 of Schedule 5.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of subsection (1)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a mixture
  of substances that contains a substance that is referred to in column
  1 of Schedule 5 and any cannabis of a class of cannabis that is
  referred to in column 2 of that Schedule in respect of that substance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Selling or distributing recalled cannabis

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited to sell
or distribute cannabis that is the subject of a recall order made under
section 76.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Self-service display

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** Unless authorized under
this Act, it is prohibited to sell or distribute cannabis or a cannabis
accessory by means of a display that allows for self-service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dispensing device

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** Unless authorized under
this Act, it is prohibited to sell or distribute cannabis or a cannabis
accessory by means of a dispensing device.

#### [SUBDIVISION E]{.HLabel3}[Other Prohibitions]{.HTitleText3} {#h-77265 .Subheading}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False statements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited to knowingly make any false or

  engaged in the exercise of powers or the performance of duties or
  functions under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interference

  seized, detained or taken under section 86.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False or misleading statement

### [DIVISION 3]{.HLabel2}[Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-77275 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with conditions

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** The holder of a licence
or permit issued under this Act must comply with its conditions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** If a licence or permit
issued under this Act is suspended in respect of any or all activities,
its holder must cease conducting, for the duration of the suspension,
the activities to which the suspension relates.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public disclosure

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** Every person that is
authorized under this Act to produce, sell or distribute cannabis must
make available to the public, in the prescribed form and manner and
within the prescribed time, information about cannabis that is required
by the regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Promotion-related information ---
cannabis

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person that is authorized under this Act to produce, sell or
  distribute cannabis must provide to the Minister, in the prescribed
  form and manner and within the prescribed time, information that is
  required by the regulations about any promotion of cannabis that they
  conduct, including a promotion referred to in paragraph 16(c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Promotion-related information ---
  cannabis accessories and services

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person that sells or distributes a cannabis
  accessory, or that provides a service related to cannabis, must
  provide to the Minister, in the prescribed form and manner and within
  the prescribed time, information that is required by the regulations
  about any promotion of cannabis accessories or their service related
  to cannabis, as the case may be, that they conduct, including a
  promotion referred to in paragraph 16(d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inducement information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person that sells cannabis or cannabis
  accessories must provide to the Minister, in the prescribed form and
  manner and within the prescribed time, information that is required by
  the regulations about any thing, including cannabis or a cannabis
  accessory, or service referred to in any of paragraphs 24(1)(a) to (c)
  that they provide or offer to provide.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, subject to the regulations, request
  that a person that has provided information under any of
  subsections (1) to (3) provide additional information relating to the
  information they provided under that subsection, and that person must
  provide the requested information in the form and manner and within
  the time specified by the Minister.

### [DIVISION 4]{.HLabel2}[Miscellaneous]{.HTitleText2} {#h-77291 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 51,
every person that contravenes a provision of this Act for which no
punishment is otherwise provided by this Act, or that contravenes a
provision of a regulation, an order made under any of sections 73 to 76,
an order amended under section 79 or an order made under section 82,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an indictable offence and is liable to a
  fine of not more than \$5,000,000 or imprisonment for a term of not
  more than three years, or to both; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
  conviction and is liable, for a first offence, to a fine of not more
  than \$250,000 or imprisonment for a term of not more than six months,
  or to both, and, for any subsequent offence, to a fine of not more
  than \$500,000 or imprisonment for a term of not more than 18 months,
  or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** No summary conviction
proceedings in respect of an offence under section 44 may be commenced
after the expiry of one year after the day on which the subject-matter
of the proceedings arose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences by corporate officers, etc.

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** If a person other than an
individual commits an offence under section 44, any of the person's
directors, officers or agents or mandataries who directed, authorized,
assented to, acquiesced in or participated in the commission of the
offence is a party to the offence and is liable on conviction to the
punishment provided for by this Act, even if the person is not
prosecuted for the offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offence

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** If an offence under
section 44 is committed or continued on more than one day, it
constitutes a separate offence for each day on which it is committed or
continued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employees or agents or mandataries

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** In a prosecution for an
offence under section 44, it is sufficient proof of the offence to
establish that it was committed by any employee or agent or mandatary of
the accused, even if the employee or agent or mandatary is not
identified or is not prosecuted for the offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Venue

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** Proceedings in respect of
an offence in relation to a contravention of any provision of this Act
or of the regulations, or of an order made under any of sections 73 to
76, an order amended under section 79 or an order made under section 82,
may be held in the place where the offence was committed or where the
subject-matter of the proceedings arose or in any place where the
accused is apprehended or happens to be located.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to exception, exemption, etc.

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  exception, exemption, excuse or qualification prescribed by law is
  required to be set out or negatived, as the case may be, in an
  information or indictment for an offence under this Act or under
  section 463, 464 or 465 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) in
  respect of such an offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rebuttal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In any prosecution for an offence under this Act, the
  prosecutor is not required, except by way of rebuttal, to prove that a
  certificate, licence, permit, authorization, exemption or
  qualification does not operate in favour of the accused, whether or
  not it is referred to in the information or indictment.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Ticketable Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-77311 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  addition to the procedures set out in the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46), proceedings referred to in subsection (2)
  against an individual who is 18 years of age or older or an
  organization may be commenced by a peace officer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} completing a ticket that consists of a summons
    portion and an information portion;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of proceedings against an individual,
    delivering the summons portion of the ticket to the accused;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of proceedings against an organization,
    sending the summons portion of the ticket, or delivering it, to the
    organization in accordance with the regulations; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} filing the information portion of the ticket with a
    court of criminal jurisdiction before or as soon as feasible after
    the summons portion has been delivered or sent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The proceedings for the purposes of subsection (1)
  are the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} proceedings in respect of an offence arising out of
    the contravention of paragraph 8(1)(a) or (b) or any of
    subparagraphs 9(1)(a)(i), (iii) and (iv) in respect of cannabis of
    one or more classes of cannabis the total amount of which, as
    determined in accordance with Schedule 3, is equivalent to 50 g or
    less of dried cannabis;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} proceedings against an individual who is 18 years
    of age or older in respect of an offence arising out of the
    contravention of paragraph 8(1)(e) or subparagraph 9(1)(c)(ii) in
    respect of five or six cannabis plants;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} proceedings in respect of an offence arising out of
    the contravention of subsection 9(2) in respect of cannabis of one
    or more classes of cannabis the total amount of which is, as
    determined in accordance with Schedule 3, equivalent to 50 g or less
    of dried cannabis, if its possession was for the purpose of
    distributing it contrary to any of subparagraphs 9(1)(a)(i), (iii)
    and (iv);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} proceedings against an individual in respect of an
    offence arising out of the contravention of paragraph 10(1)(a)
    or (c) in respect of cannabis of one or more classes of cannabis the
    total amount of which is, as determined in accordance with Schedule
    3, equivalent to 50 g or less of dried cannabis;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} proceedings against an individual in respect of an
    offence arising out of the contravention of subsection 10(2) in
    respect of cannabis of one or more classes of cannabis the total
    amount of which is, as determined in accordance with Schedule 3,
    equivalent to 50 g or less of dried cannabis, if its possession was
    for the purpose of selling it contrary to paragraph 10(1)(a) or (c);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} proceedings against an individual in respect of an
    offence arising out of the contravention of paragraph 12(1)(a) in
    respect of cannabis of one or more classes of cannabis the total
    amount of which is, as determined in accordance with Schedule 3,
    equivalent to 50 g or less of dried cannabis;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} proceedings in respect of an offence arising out of
    the contravention of paragraph 12(4)(b) in respect of five or six
    cannabis plants;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} proceedings in respect of an offence arising out of
    the contravention of subsection 12(5) in respect of one or two
    cannabis plants; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} proceedings in respect of an offence arising out of
    the contravention of section 44 in respect of a contravention of a
    provision that is specified in regulations made under paragraph
    139(1)(z.6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of ticket

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The summons and information portions of the ticket
  must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the offence and the time and place
    of its alleged commission;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement, signed by the peace officer who
    completes the ticket, that the peace officer has reasonable grounds
    to believe that the accused committed the offence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to the amount, determined under
    subsection (4), to be paid for the offence;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which and period within which the
    amount is to be paid;

  - [(d.1)]{.lawlabel} a lesser amount than the amount determined under
    subsection (4) that may be paid for the offence if it is paid within
    a specified period that is shorter than the period referred to in
    paragraph (d);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement that if the accused pays the amount
    within the period referred to in paragraph (d) or (d.1),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a finding of guilt will be entered in the
      judicial record of the accused and the accused will be deemed to
      have received an absolute discharge and not to have been convicted
      of the offence,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the judicial record of the accused in respect of
      the offence will not be used for any purpose that would identify
      the accused as a person dealt with under this Act, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if cannabis has been seized in relation to the
      offence, the cannabis will be forfeited to Her Majesty;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statement that if the accused wishes to plead not
    guilty, the accused must appear in the court, at the place, day and
    time set out in the ticket;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a statement that if the accused pleads not guilty,
    he or she will be given an opportunity to indicate in which official
    language he or she wishes to be tried; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a statement that if the accused does not enter a
    plea and does not pay the amount within the period referred to in
    paragraph (d) or (d.1)

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a conviction will be entered in the judicial
      record of the accused, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if cannabis has been seized in relation to the
      offence, the cannabis will be forfeited to Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (3)(c), the amount is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for an offence referred to in any of paragraphs
    (2)(a) to (h), \$200 plus a victim surcharge, calculated in
    accordance with subsection 737(2) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46), and any applicable administrative fees; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for an offence in respect of a contravention of a
    provision that is specified in regulations made under paragraph
    139(1)(z.6), the amount specified in those regulations in respect of
    that offence plus a victim surcharge, calculated in accordance with
    subsection 737(2) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), and any
    applicable administrative fees.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consequences of payment

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** Payment of the amount set
out in the ticket by the accused within the period referred to in
paragraph 51(3)(d) or (d.1) constitutes a plea of guilty to the offence
described in the ticket and, following the payment,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a finding of guilt is to be entered in the judicial
  record of the accused and the accused is deemed to have received an
  absolute discharge and not to have been convicted of the offence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the judicial record of the accused in relation to the
  offence must not be used for any purpose that would identify the
  accused as a person dealt with under this Act; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if cannabis has been seized in relation to the
  offence, the cannabis is forfeited to Her Majesty.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consequences of being convicted

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  accused pleads not guilty and the accused is convicted of the offence
  described in the ticket, the accused is liable to a fine of not more
  than \$200, in the case of an offence referred to in any of paragraphs
  51(2)(a) to (h) or, in the case of an offence in respect of a
  contravention of a provision specified in regulations made under
  paragraph 139(1)(z.6), to a fine of not more than the amount specified
  in those regulations in respect of that offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of section 731

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} If the accused is convicted of the offence, no
  order is to be made under section 731 of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46) in respect of that conviction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the accused is convicted of the offence and the
  accused pays the amount owing in respect of the conviction, the
  judicial record of the accused in relation to the offence must not be
  used for any purpose that would identify the accused as a person dealt
  with under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consequences of failing to pay fine

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  accused fails to pay the amount set out in the ticket within the
  period referred to in paragraph 51(3)(d) or (d.1), the accused is
  liable for that amount and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a conviction is to be entered in the judicial
    record of the accused;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the conviction is deemed to have been pronounced by
    a court;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if cannabis has been seized in relation to the
    offence, the cannabis is forfeited to Her Majesty;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the accused has 60 days after the day of the
    conviction to pay the amount set out in the ticket; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the amount set out in the ticket, other than the
    amount in relation to the applicable fees, is deemed to be the fine
    imposed by the court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of payment or imprisonment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, after being convicted, the accused pays the
  amount set out in the ticket or, if the accused is an individual, the
  accused has served, in full, any period of imprisonment imposed as a
  result of a default in payment of the amount of the fine imposed by
  the court, the judicial record of the accused in relation to the
  offence must not be used for any purpose that would identify the
  accused as a person dealt with under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Imprisonment

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** Only an individual who is
unwilling though able to pay a fine or the amount of a victim surcharge
imposed in respect of a conviction referred to in subsection 53(1) or a
fine imposed in respect of a conviction referred to in section 54 may be
imprisoned in default of its payment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licences, permits, etc.

**[[55.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55.1 .sectionLabel}** If the amount to be
paid under this Part is owed to Her Majesty in right of Canada, the
person responsible, by or under an Act or ordinance of the legislature
of a territory, for issuing or renewing a licence, permit or other
similar instrument in relation to the offender may refuse to issue or
renew or may suspend the licence, permit or other instrument until the
fine or fee is paid in full, proof of which lies on the offender.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of laying information

**[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** No information under the
[Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) may be laid in respect of an offence for
which a summons portion of a ticket is delivered or sent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46)

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** Except where otherwise
provided by this Part, Part XXVII of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)
applies to proceedings commenced under this Part.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election of Attorney General

- **[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  proceeding in respect of an offence referred to in any of paragraphs
  51(2)(a) to (j) is commenced by the laying of an information, the

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the election is made, the clerk of the court must
  provide the accused with a notice that sets out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to the amount, determined under
    paragraph 51(4)(a) or (b), as the case may be, to be paid for the

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which and period within which the
    amount is to be paid;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} a lesser amount than the amount determined under
    paragraph 51(4)(a) or (b), as the case may be, that may be paid for
    the offence if it is paid within a specified period that is shorter
    than the period referred to in paragraph (b);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement that if the accused pays the amount
    within the period referred to in paragraph (b) or (b.1),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a finding of guilt will be entered in the
      judicial record of the accused and the accused will be deemed to
      have received an absolute discharge and not to have been convicted

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the judicial record of the accused in respect of
      the offence will not be used for any purpose that would identify
      the accused as a person dealt with under this Act, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if cannabis has been seized in relation to the

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement that if the accused wishes to plead not
    guilty or fails to pay the amount within the period referred to in
    paragraph (b) or (b.1), the accused must appear in the court at the
    place, day and time set out in the notice; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement that if the accused pleads not guilty,

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect on conditions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} All conditions imposed on the accused in an
  appearance notice, promise to appear, undertaking or recognizance
  issued, given or entered into in accordance with Part XVI or XXVII of

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed ticket

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The document and the information laid in respect of
  the offence are deemed to be a ticket delivered or sent under section
  51.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriation by Parliament

  making of the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification --- other powers

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section in any way limits the power
  of the Minister to otherwise determine the most efficient manner in
  which applications are considered.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue, renew and amend

- **[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to orders made under
  subsection 61(1), the regulations and subsection (2), the Minister
  may, on application, issue, renew or amend licences and permits that
  authorize the importation, exportation, production, testing,
  packaging, labelling, sending, delivery, transportation, sale,
  possession or disposal of cannabis or any class of cannabis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- importation and
  exportation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Licences and permits authorizing the importation or
  exportation of cannabis may be issued only in respect of cannabis for
  medical or scientific purposes or in respect of industrial hemp.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application for a licence or permit, or for its
  renewal or amendment, must be filed with the Minister in the form and
  manner specified by the Minister and set out the information required
  by the Minister, including financial information and any information
  required by the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (3), financial
  information in respect of an organization includes information about
  its shareholders or members and who controls it, directly or
  indirectly.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, on receiving an application,
  require the submission of any additional information, including
  financial information, that pertains to the information contained in
  the application and that is necessary for the Minister to consider the
  application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to consider

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may refuse to consider an application if
  any information required to be provided under any of subsections (3)
  to (5) is not provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grounds for refusal

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may refuse to issue, renew or amend a
  licence or permit if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the issuance, the renewal or the amendment is
    likely to create a risk to public health or public safety, including
    the risk of cannabis being diverted to an illicit market or
    activity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there are reasonable grounds to believe that false
    or misleading information or false or falsified documents were
    submitted in, or in support of, the application;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has contravened in the past 10 years
    a provision of this Act, the [Controlled Drugs and Substances
    Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8) or the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27)
    or of any regulation made under this Act or any of those Acts;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} there are reasonable grounds to believe that the
    applicant has contravened in the past 10 years

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an order made under this Act, the [Controlled
      Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8) or the [Food and Drugs
      Act](/eng/acts/F-27), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a condition of another licence or permit issued
      to the applicant under this Act or any of those Acts;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a young person,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an individual who is not ordinarily resident in
      Canada, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an organization that was incorporated, formed
      or otherwise organized outside Canada;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a security clearance in respect of the application
    has been refused or cancelled;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is of the opinion that it is in the
    public interest to do so; or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any prescribed grounds for refusal exist.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of refusal

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister refuses to issue, renew or amend a
  licence or permit, he or she must send the applicant a notice in
  writing that sets out the reasons for the refusal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions --- regulations

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} Every licence or permit is subject to the conditions
  set out in regulations made under paragraph 139(1)(g).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions --- Minister

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, the Minister may make a
  licence or permit subject to any conditions that he or she considers
  appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment on own initiative

- **[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, in accordance with the regulations, on his or her own
  initiative, amend a licence or permit if he or she is of the opinion
  that the amendment is necessary to protect public health or public
  safety, including to prevent cannabis from being diverted to an
  illicit market or activity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of proposed amendment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister proposes to amend a licence or permit
  on his or her own initiative, he or she must, in accordance with the
  regulations, send its holder a notice in writing that sets out the
  reasons for the proposed amendment and give the holder an opportunity
  to be heard.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension

- **[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, the
  Minister may suspend a licence or permit without prior notice to its
  holder in respect of any or all authorized activities in relation to
  any cannabis specified by the Minister if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the suspension is necessary to protect public health or public
    safety, including to prevent cannabis from being diverted to an
    illicit market or activity; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any prescribed circumstance exists.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of suspension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a licence or permit is suspended under subsection
  (1), the suspension takes effect as soon as the Minister provides the
  holder with a notice in writing of the suspension. The notice must
  also set out the reasons for the suspension.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opportunity to be heard

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The holder may, within 10 days after receipt of the
  notice under subsection (2), provide the Minister with reasons why the
  holder believes the suspension is unfounded.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reinstatement

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must, by notice to the holder, reinstate
  a licence or permit in respect of any or all activities or cannabis
  affected by the suspension if the reasons for the suspension no longer
  exist or the holder demonstrates to the Minister that the suspension
  was unfounded.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the
regulations, the Minister may revoke a licence or permit if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} there are reasonable grounds to believe that it was
  issued on the basis of false or misleading information or false or
  falsified documents submitted in, or in support of, the application;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder has, since its issuance, contravened a
  provision of this Act, the [Controlled Drugs and Substances
  Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8) or the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27) or
  of any regulation made under this Act or any of those Acts;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} there are reasonable grounds to believe that the
  holder has, since its issuance, contravened

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an order made under this Act, the [Controlled Drugs
    and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8) or the [Food and Drugs
    Act](/eng/acts/F-27), or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a condition of another licence or permit issued to
    the holder under this Act or any of those Acts;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} information received from a peace officer, a
  competent authority or an international organization of states or any
  of its institutions raises reasonable grounds to believe that its
  holder has been involved in the diversion of cannabis, or of any
  [[controlled substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or
  [[precursor]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as those terms are defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the [Controlled Drugs and Substances
  Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8), to an illicit market or activity;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the holder is an individual who has, since its
  issuance, ceased to be ordinarily resident in Canada;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} since the issuance of the licence or permit, a
  security clearance in respect of the licence or permit has been
  cancelled;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is of the opinion that it is in the
  public interest to revoke it; or

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} any prescribed circumstance exists.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of proposed revocation

**[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** If the Minister proposes
to revoke a licence or permit, he or she must, in accordance with the
regulations, send its holder a notice in writing that sets out the
reasons for the proposed revocation and give the holder an opportunity
to be heard.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security clearances

- **[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, the
  Minister may grant or refuse to grant a security clearance or suspend
  or cancel a security clearance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security clearance required by Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may specify, by name or position, any
  person --- other than a person specified in the regulations --- who
  must hold a security clearance if the Minister is of the opinion that
  the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} performs, has performed or is about to perform
    activities related to a licence or permit that is issued under this
    Part or that is the subject of an application under this Part; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has, has had or is about to have custody,
    management or control of the place where activities related to a
    licence or permit that is issued under this Part or that is the
    subject of an application under this Part, are being or will be
    performed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister specifies that a person must hold a
  security clearance under subsection (2), the Minister must provide the
  applicant for, or the holder of, the licence or permit related to that
  person with a notice to that effect in writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of application

- **[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by order, fix a date for the termination of applications
  of a class of applications for a licence or permit under section 62
  and, if he or she does so, every application of that class is

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees returned

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any fees paid in respect of an application that is

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No recourse or indemnity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person has a right of recourse or indemnity
  against Her Majesty in connection with an application that is
  terminated under subsection (1).

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[General Authorizations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-77505 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincially authorized selling

- **[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may possess, sell or distribute cannabis if the person is
  authorized to sell cannabis under a provincial Act that contains the
  legislative measures referred to in subsection (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) applies only if the provincial Act and
  the legislative measures are in force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legislative measures

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the legislative
  measures in a provincial Act that authorizes the selling of cannabis
  are the following in respect of persons authorized to sell cannabis:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they may sell only cannabis that has been produced
    by a person that is authorized under this Act to produce cannabis
    for commercial purposes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they may not sell cannabis to young persons;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} they are required to keep appropriate records
    respecting their activities in relation to cannabis that they
    possess for commercial purposes; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} they are required to take adequate measures to
    reduce the risk of cannabis that they possess for commercial
    purposes being diverted to an illicit market or activity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administration and enforcement activities
--- federal Acts

- **[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  the regulations provide otherwise, every individual who obtains
  cannabis in the course of activities performed in connection with the
  administration or enforcement of this Act or any other Act of
  Parliament is authorized to do anything that is prohibited by any
  provision of Division 1 of Part 1 if they do so in a manner that is
  consistent with the activities they are authorized to perform.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administration and enforcement
  activities --- provincial Acts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every individual who obtains cannabis in the course
  of activities performed in connection with the administration or
  enforcement of any provincial Act that authorizes the selling of
  cannabis is authorized to do anything that is prohibited by any
  provision of Division 1 of Part 1 if they do so in a manner that is
  consistent with the activities they are authorized to perform.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employees --- this Act

- **[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  the regulations provide otherwise, every employee of a person that is
  authorized under this Act to possess, sell, distribute or produce
  cannabis may do anything that is prohibited by any provision of
  Division 1 of Part 1 if they do so as part of their employment duties
  and functions and in a manner that is consistent with the conditions
  that apply to their employer's authorization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agents and mandataries --- this Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the regulations provide otherwise, every
  person who is acting as the agent or mandatary of a person that is
  authorized under this Act to possess, sell, distribute or produce
  cannabis may do anything that is prohibited by any provision of
  Division 1 of Part 1 if they do so as part of their role as agent or
  mandatary and in a manner that is consistent with the conditions that
  apply to their principal's or mandator's authorization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contractor --- this Act

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless the regulations provide otherwise, every
  person who is acting under a contract with a person that is authorized
  under this Act to possess, sell, distribute or produce cannabis ---
  other than an employee or an agent or mandatary of the authorized
  person --- may do anything that is prohibited by any provision of
  Division 1 of Part 1 if they do so in the performance of their
  contract and in a manner that is consistent with the conditions that
  apply to the authorized person's authorization.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employees --- provincial Acts

- **[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  employee of a person that is authorized under a provincial Act to sell
  cannabis may do anything that is prohibited by section 8, 9 or 10 if
  they do so as part of their employment duties and functions and in a
  manner that is consistent with the conditions that apply to their
  employer's authorization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agents and mandataries --- provincial
  Acts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is acting as the agent or mandatary
  of a person that is authorized under a provincial Act to sell cannabis
  may do anything that is prohibited by section 8, 9 or 10 if they do so
  as part of their role as agent or mandatary and in a manner that is
  consistent with the conditions that apply to their principal's or
  mandator's authorization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contractor --- provincial Acts

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is acting under a contract with a
  person that is authorized under a provincial Act to sell cannabis ---
  other than an employee or an agent or mandatary of the authorized
  person --- may do anything that is prohibited by section 8, 9 or 10 if
  they do so in the performance of their contract and in a manner that
  is consistent with the conditions that apply to the authorized
  person's authorization.

## [PART 6]{.HLabel1}[Cannabis Tracking System]{.HTitleText1} {#h-77600 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment and maintenance

**[[81]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-81 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, using
the information collected under section 82 and any other information to
which the Minister has access, establish and maintain a national
cannabis tracking system to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} enable the tracking of cannabis;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prevent cannabis from being diverted to an illicit
  market or activity; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prevent illicit cannabis from being a source of
  supply of cannabis in the legal market.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order requiring information

- **[[82]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-82 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of section 81, the Minister may, by order, require a class
  of persons that are authorized to import, export, produce, test,
  package, label, send, deliver, transport, sell, or dispose of cannabis
  to provide the Minister with information respecting their activities
  in relation to cannabis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The order must specify the information to be provided
  and the time within and the form and manner in which it is to be
  provided and may specify the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which and the place where the
    records, reports, electronic data or other documents containing the
    information --- and any information on which the information is
    based --- are to be retained;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the period for which the records, reports,
    electronic data or other documents referred to in paragraph (a) are
    to be retained, which must not be more than three years from the
    date the information is provided to the Minister; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which calculations, measurements and
    other data on which the information is based are to be documented.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An order under subsection (1) may not require the
  provision of any [[personal information]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
  defined in section 3 of the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21), in respect
  of a consumer that purchases cannabis at the retail level.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of time

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by order, on written request from a
  person that is subject to an order made under subsection (1), extend
  the time within which the person must comply with the order made under
  subsection (1). If the order under this subsection is granted, the
  original time in the order made under subsection (1) is deemed to be
  replaced, in relation to that person, by the new time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If an order is made under subsection (4) after a
  person has failed to provide information in the time provided for in
  the order made under subsection (1), the person is deemed not to have
  failed to provide the information within the required time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} An order made under subsection (4) is not a
  [[statutory instrument]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} within the meaning of
  subsection 2(1) of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of information

**[[83]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-83 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may disclose
any information contained in the national cannabis tracking system as
follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} he or she may disclose it to the government of a
  province or a public body established under a provincial Act if the
  disclosure is for a purpose related to verifying compliance or
  preventing non-compliance with the provisions of a provincial Act that
  contains the legislative measures referred to in subsection 69(3);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} he or she may disclose it to any federal Minister if
  the disclosure is for a purpose related to verifying compliance or
  preventing non-compliance with the provisions of any Act of
  Parliament, other than this Act, that applies directly or indirectly
  to cannabis or any activity in relation to cannabis;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} he or she may disclose it if he or she has reasonable
  grounds to believe that the disclosure is necessary to protect public
  health or public safety, including to prevent cannabis from being
  diverted to an illicit market or activity;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} he or she may disclose it if the disclosure is
  necessary to enable Canada to fulfil its international obligations;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} he or she may disclose it to any prescribed person;
  or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} he or she may disclose it in the prescribed
  circumstances.

## [PART 7]{.HLabel1}[Inspections]{.HTitleText1} {#h-77630 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of inspectors

- **[[84]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-84 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of the administration and enforcement of this Act, the
  Minister may designate any individuals or class of individuals as
  inspectors to exercise powers or perform duties or functions in
  relation to any matter referred to in the designation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each inspector must be provided with a certificate of
  designation in a form established by the Minister and, when entering
  any place under subsection 86(1), must, on request, produce the
  certificate to the person in charge of the place.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of documents, information or
samples

- **[[85]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-85 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector may, for a purpose related to verifying compliance or
  preventing non-compliance with the provisions of this Act or of the
  regulations, order a person that is authorized under this Act to
  conduct any activity in relation to cannabis to provide, on the date,
  at the time and place and in the manner specified by the inspector,
  any document, information or sample specified by the inspector.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to provide

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person that is ordered by an inspector to provide a
  document, information or a sample must do so on the date, at the time
  and place and in the manner specified by the inspector.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to enter

- **[[86]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-86
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (7), an
  inspector may, for a purpose related to verifying compliance or
  preventing non-compliance with the provisions of this Act or of the
  regulations, enter any place, including a conveyance, in which they
  believe on reasonable grounds

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an activity that may be regulated under this Act is
    being conducted;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any record, report, electronic data or other
    document relating to the administration of this Act or the
    regulations is located;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any record, report, electronic data or other
    document relating to the promotion of cannabis, a cannabis accessory
    or a service related to cannabis is located;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an activity could be conducted under a licence,
    permit, authorization or exemption that is under consideration by
    the Minister; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an activity was being conducted under a licence,
    permit, authorization or exemption before the expiry or revocation
    of the licence, permit, authorization or exemption, in which case
    the inspector may enter the place only within 45 days after the day
    on which it expired or was revoked.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The inspector may in the place entered under
  subsection (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} open and examine any receptacle or package found in
    the place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} examine anything found in the place that is used or
    may be capable of being used for the production, preservation,
    packaging, labelling or storage of cannabis;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} examine any record, report, electronic data or
    other document, or any label or promotional material, found in the
    place with respect to cannabis, other than the records of the
    medical condition of individuals, and make copies of them or take
    extracts from them;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any computer system at the
    place to examine any electronic data referred to in paragraph (c);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} reproduce any document from any electronic data
    referred to in paragraph (c), or cause it to be reproduced, in the
    form of a printout or other output;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} take the record, report or other document, or the
    label or promotional material, referred to in paragraph (c) or the
    printout or other output referred to in paragraph (e) for
    examination or copying;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any copying equipment at
    the place to make copies of any document;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} take photographs and make recordings and sketches;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} examine any substance found in the place and take,
    for the purpose of analysis, any samples of it;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} seize and detain in accordance with this Part,
    cannabis or any other thing found in the place that the inspector
    believes on reasonable grounds is something in relation to which the
    Act was contravened or is something the seizure and detention of
    which is necessary to prevent non-compliance with the provisions of
    this Act or of the regulations;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} order the owner or person having possession of
    cannabis or any other thing to which the provisions of this Act or
    of the regulations apply that is found in that place to move it or,
    for any time that may be necessary, not to move it or to restrict
    its movement;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} order the owner or person having possession of any
    conveyance that is found in the place and that the inspector
    believes on reasonable grounds contains cannabis to stop the
    conveyance, to move it or, for any time that may be necessary, not
    to move it or to restrict its movement;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} order any person in that place to establish their
    identity to the inspector's satisfaction; and

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} order a person that, at that place, conducts an
    activity to which the provisions of this Act or of the regulations
    apply to stop or start the activity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of telecommunication

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the inspector is
  considered to have entered a place when accessing it remotely by a
  means of telecommunication.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- access by means of
  telecommunication

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An inspector who enters remotely, by a means of
  telecommunication, a place that is not accessible to the public must
  do so with the knowledge of the owner or person in charge of the place
  and must be remotely in the place for no longer than for the period
  necessary for the purpose referred to in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Individual accompanying inspector

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The inspector may be accompanied by any other
  individual that the inspector believes is necessary to help them
  exercise their powers or perform their duties or functions under this
  section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entering private property

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The inspector and any individual accompanying them
  may enter and pass through private property, other than a
  dwelling-house on that property, in order to gain entry to a place
  referred to in subsection (1). For greater certainty, they are not
  liable for doing so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant required to enter
  dwelling-house

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a dwelling-house, an inspector may
  enter it only with the consent of an occupant or under the authority
  of a warrant issued under subsection (8).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} A justice may, on [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  application, issue a warrant authorizing the inspector named in it to
  enter a place and exercise any of the powers mentioned in paragraphs
  (2)(a) to (n), subject to any conditions that are specified in the
  warrant, if the justice is satisfied by information on oath that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the place is a dwelling-house but otherwise meets
    the conditions for entry described in subsection (1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entry to the dwelling-house is necessary for the
    purpose of verifying compliance or preventing non-compliance with
    the provisions of this Act or of the regulations; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} entry to the dwelling-house has been refused or
    there are reasonable grounds to believe that entry will be refused.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} In executing a warrant issued under subsection (8),
  an inspector must not use force unless they are accompanied by a peace
  officer and the use of force is specifically authorized in the
  warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of telecommunication

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} An application for a warrant under subsection (8)
  may be submitted, and the warrant may be issued, by a means of
  telecommunication and section 487.1 of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46) applies for those purposes with any necessary
  modifications.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to inspector

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} The owner or other person in charge of a place
  entered by an inspector under subsection (1) and every individual
  found there must give the inspector all reasonable assistance in their
  power and provide the inspector with any information that the
  inspector may reasonably require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage and notice

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} An inspector who seizes a thing under this section
  may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on notice to and at the expense of its owner or the
    person having possession of it at the time of its seizure, store it
    or move it to another place; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} order its owner or the person having possession of
    it at the time of its seizure to, at their expense, store it or move
    it to another place.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return by inspector

  [(13)]{.lawlabel} If an inspector determines that to verify compliance
  or prevent non-compliance with the provisions of this Act or of the
  regulations it is no longer necessary to detain anything seized by the
  inspector under this section, the inspector must notify in writing the
  owner or other person in charge of the place where the seizure
  occurred of that determination and, on being issued a receipt for it,
  must return the thing to that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return or disposition by Minister

  [(14)]{.lawlabel} If a period of 120 days has elapsed after the date
  of a seizure under this section and the thing has not been returned,
  disposed of or otherwise dealt with in accordance with subsection (13)
  or any of sections 103 to 107, it must be returned, disposed of or
  otherwise dealt with in accordance with the regulations or, if there
  are no applicable regulations, in the manner that the Minister
  directs.

## [PART 8]{.HLabel1}[Search Warrant]{.HTitleText1} {#h-77694 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information for search warrant

- **[[87]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-87 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  justice who, on [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application, is
  satisfied by information on oath that there are reasonable grounds to
  believe that any of the following is in a place may, at any time,
  issue a warrant authorizing a peace officer, at any time, to search
  the place for it and to seize it:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cannabis in respect of which this Act has been
    contravened;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} anything in which cannabis in respect of which this
    Act has been contravened is contained or concealed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} offence-related property; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} anything that will afford evidence in respect of an
    offence under this Act or an offence, in whole or in part, in
    relation to a contravention of this Act, under section 354 or 462.31
    of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of section 487.1 of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the information
  may be submitted by telephone or other means of telecommunication in
  accordance with section 487.1 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46),
  with any necessary modifications.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Execution of warrant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A warrant issued under subsection (1) may be executed
  at any place in Canada. Any peace officer who executes the warrant
  must have authority to act as a peace officer in the place where it is
  executed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of peace officer executing warrant

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Section 487.093 of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46), other than paragraph 487.093(1)(c), applies
  with respect to a warrant issued under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Search of person and seizure

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a peace officer who executes a warrant issued
  under subsection (1) has reasonable grounds to believe that any
  individual found in the place referred to in the warrant has on them
  any cannabis, property or thing referred to in the warrant, the peace
  officer may search the individual for it and seize it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seizure of other things

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A peace officer who executes a warrant issued under
  subsection (1) may seize, in addition to any cannabis, property or
  thing referred to in the warrant,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any cannabis in respect of which the peace officer
    believes on reasonable grounds that this Act has been contravened;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} anything that the peace officer believes on
    reasonable grounds contains or conceals cannabis;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} anything that the peace officer believes on
    reasonable grounds is offence-related property; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} anything that the peace officer believes on
    reasonable grounds will afford evidence in respect of an offence
    under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where warrant not necessary

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A peace officer may exercise any of the powers
  described in subsection (1), (5) or (6) without a warrant if the
  conditions for obtaining a warrant exist but by reason of exigent
  circumstances it would be impracticable to obtain one.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seizure of additional things

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} A peace officer who executes a warrant issued under
  subsection (1) or exercises powers under subsection (5) or (7) may
  seize, in addition to any cannabis, property or thing referred to in
  the warrant and in subsection (6), anything that the peace officer
  believes on reasonable grounds has been obtained by or used in the
  commission of an offence or that will afford evidence in respect of an
  offence.

## [PART 9]{.HLabel1}[Disposition of Seized Things]{.HTitleText1} {#h-77724 .Part}

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, every

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number of the file or police report related to

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any other prescribed information.

  the report to be filed with the justice who issued the warrant or

  not issued, a justice who would have had jurisdiction to issue a

### [DIVISION 1]{.HLabel2}[Non-chemical Offence-related Property]{.HTitleText2} {#h-77750 .Subheading}

#### [Restraint Orders]{.HTitleText3} {#h-77751 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for restraint order

- **[[91]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Attorney General may make an application in accordance with this
  section for a restraint order under this section in respect of any
  non-chemical offence-related property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application may be made [ex parte]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} and it must be made in writing to a judge and be
  accompanied by an affidavit sworn on the information and belief of the
  Attorney General or any other person deposing to the following
  matters:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the offence to which the property relates;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person that is believed to be in possession of
    the property; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restraint order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The judge to whom the application is made may, if
  satisfied that there are reasonable grounds to believe that the
  property is non-chemical offence-related property, make a restraint
  order prohibiting any person from disposing of or otherwise dealing
  with any interest in or right to the property specified in the order,
  other than in the manner that is specified in the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property outside Canada

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A restraint order may be issued under this section in
  respect of property situated outside Canada, with any modifications
  that the circumstances require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The restraint order may be subject to any reasonable
  conditions that the judge thinks fit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order in writing

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The restraint order must be in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the restraint order must be served on the
  person to which the order is addressed in the manner that the judge
  making it directs or in accordance with the rules of the court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the restraint order must be registered
  against any property in accordance with the laws of the province in
  which the property is situated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order continues in force

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The restraint order remains in effect until

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an order is made under subsection 97(3) or 98(3) of
    this Act or subsection 490(9) or (11) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46) in relation to the property; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an order of forfeiture of the property is made
    under subsection 94(1) or 95(2) of this Act or section 490 of the
    [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} Any person on which a restraint order is served in
  accordance with this section and that, while the order is in force,
  fails to comply with the order is guilty of an indictable offence or
  an offence punishable on summary conviction.

#### [Management Orders]{.HTitleText3} {#h-77783 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Management order

- **[[93]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-93 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  application of the Attorney General or of any other person with the
  written consent of the Attorney General, a justice in the case of
  non-chemical offence-related property seized under section 87 of this
  Act, the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or a power of seizure at
  common law, or a judge in the case of property restrained under
  section 91, may, if he or she is of the opinion that the circumstances
  so require,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint a person to take control of and to manage
    or otherwise deal with all or part of the property in accordance
    with the directions of the judge or justice; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require any person having possession of that
    property to give possession of the property to the person appointed
    under paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Minister of Public Works
  and Government Services

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Attorney General of Canada so requests, a
  judge or justice appointing a person under subsection (1) must appoint
  the Minister of Public Works and Government Services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to manage

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The power to manage or otherwise deal with property
  under subsection (1) includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the power to make an interlocutory sale of
    perishable or rapidly depreciating property;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the power to destroy, in accordance with
    subsections (4) to (7), property that has little or no value; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the power to have property --- other than real
    property or immovables or a conveyance --- forfeited to Her Majesty
    in accordance with subsection (8).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for destruction order

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Before a person that is appointed to manage property
  destroys property that has little or no value, they must apply to a
  court for a destruction order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Before making a destruction order, a court must
  require notice in accordance with subsection (6) to be given to, and
  may hear, any person that, in the opinion of the court, appears to
  have a valid interest in or right to the property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of giving notice

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The notice must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be given in the manner that the court directs or
    that may be specified in the rules of the court; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specify the effective period of the notice that the
    court considers reasonable or that may be set out in the rules of
    the court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Destruction order

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A court must order that the property be destroyed if
  it is satisfied that the property has little or no financial or other
  value.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture order

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} On application by a person that is appointed to
  manage the property, a court must order that the property --- other
  than real property or immovables or a conveyance --- be forfeited to
  Her Majesty to be disposed of or otherwise dealt with in accordance
  with the law if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a notice is given or published in the manner that
    the court directs or that may be specified in the rules of the
    court;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the notice specifies a period of 60 days during
    which a person may make an application to the court asserting their
    interest in or right to the property; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} during that period, no one makes such an
    application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, if property that is the
  subject of a management order is sold, the management order applies to
  the net proceeds of the sale.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to vary conditions

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} The Attorney General may at any time apply to the
  judge or justice to cancel or vary any condition to which a management
  order is subject but may not apply to vary an appointment made under
  subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When management order ceases to have
  effect

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} A management order ceases to have effect when the
  property that is the subject of the management order is returned in
  accordance with the law, destroyed or forfeited to Her Majesty.

#### [Forfeiture]{.HTitleText3} {#h-77817 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture order

- **[[94]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-94
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 96 to 98, if a
  person is convicted or discharged under section 730 of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46) of a designated offence and, on application of
  the Attorney General, the court is satisfied, on a balance of
  probabilities, that non-chemical offence-related property is related
  to the commission of the offence, the court must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the prosecution of the offence was commenced at
    the instance of the government of a province and conducted by or on
    behalf of that government, order that the property be forfeited to
    Her Majesty in right of that province and disposed of or otherwise
    dealt with in accordance with the law by the Attorney General or
    Solicitor General of that province; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, order that the property be
    forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada and disposed of or
    otherwise dealt with in accordance with the law by the member of the
    Queen's Privy Council for Canada who is designated by the Governor
    in Council for the purposes of this paragraph.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property related to other offences

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 96 to 98, if the evidence does
  not establish to the satisfaction of the court that property in
  respect of which an order of forfeiture would otherwise be made under
  subsection (1) is related to the commission of the designated offence
  of which a person is convicted or discharged, but the court is
  satisfied, beyond a reasonable doubt, that the property is
  non-chemical offence-related property, the court may make an order of
  forfeiture under subsection (1) in relation to that property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property outside Canada

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An order may be issued under this section in respect
  of property situated outside Canada, with any modifications that the
  circumstances require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person that has been convicted or discharged of a
  designated offence, or the Attorney General, may appeal to the court
  of appeal from an order or a failure to make an order under
  subsection (1) as if the appeal were an appeal against the sentence
  imposed on the person in respect of the offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for *in rem* forfeiture

- **[[95]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-95 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  information has been laid in respect of a designated offence, the
  Attorney General may make an application to a judge for an order of
  forfeiture under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of forfeiture

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 96 to 98, the judge to whom the
  application is made must order the property to be forfeited and
  disposed of in accordance with subsection (4)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the judge is satisfied beyond a reasonable doubt
    that any property is non-chemical offence-related property;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the judge is satisfied that proceedings were
    commenced in respect of a designated offence to which the property
    referred to in paragraph (a) is related; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the judge is satisfied that the accused charged
    with the designated offence has died or absconded.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accused deemed absconded

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), an accused is
  deemed to have absconded in connection with a designated offence if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an information has been laid alleging the
    commission of the offence by the accused;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a warrant for the arrest of the accused has been
    issued in relation to that information; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} reasonable attempts to arrest the accused under the
    warrant have been unsuccessful during a period of six months
    beginning on the day on which the warrant was issued.

  An accused who is deemed under this subsection to have absconded in
  connection with a designated offence is deemed to have done so on the
  last day of the six-month period referred to in paragraph (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Who may dispose of forfeited property

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), the judge must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the proceedings referred to in paragraph (2)(b)
    were commenced at the instance of the government of a province,
    order that the property be forfeited to Her Majesty in right of that
    province and disposed of or otherwise dealt with in accordance with
    the law by the Attorney General or Solicitor General of that
    province; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, order that the property be
    forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada and disposed of or
    otherwise dealt with in accordance with the law by the member of the
    Queen's Privy Council for Canada who is designated by the Governor
    in Council for the purposes of this paragraph.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property outside Canada

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An order may be issued under this section in respect
  of property situated outside Canada, with any modifications that the
  circumstances require.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to set aside transfers

**[[96]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-96 .sectionLabel}** A court may, before
ordering that property be forfeited under subsection 94(1) or 95(2), set
aside any transfer of the property that occurred after the seizure of
the property, or the making of a restraint order in respect of the
property, unless the transfer was for valuable consideration to a person
acting in good faith.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

- **[[97]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-97 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  making an order under subsection 94(1) or 95(2) in relation to any
  property, a court must require a notice in accordance with
  subsection (2) to be given to, and may hear, any person that, in the

  the property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of giving notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be given in the manner that the court directs or
    that may be specified in the rules of the court;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specify the period that the court considers
    reasonable or that may be set out in the rules of the court during
    which a person may make an application to the court asserting their
    interest in or right to the property; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set out the designated offence charged and a
    description of the property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of restoration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a court is satisfied that any person, other than
  one referred to in paragraph (a) or (b), is the owner of, or is
  entitled to possession of, any property or any part of any property
  that would otherwise be forfeited under an order made under subsection
  94(1) or 95(2) and that the person appears innocent of any complicity
  in a designated offence or of any collusion in relation to such an
  offence, the court may order that the property or part be returned to
  that person:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person that was charged with a designated
    offence; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person that acquired title to, ownership of or a
    right of possession of the property from a person referred to in
    paragraph (a) under circumstances that give rise to a reasonable
    inference that the title or right was transferred for the purpose of
    avoiding the forfeiture of the property.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

- **[[98]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-98 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If all
  or part of the property that would otherwise be forfeited under
  subsection 94(1) or 95(2) is a dwelling-house, before making an order

  subsection (2) to be given to, and may hear, any individual who

  under this Act in relation to which the property would be forfeited.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of giving notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be given in the manner that the court directs or
    that may be specified in the rules of the court;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specify the period that the court considers
    reasonable or that may be set out in the rules of the court during
    which a member of the immediate family who resides in the
    dwelling-house may make themselves known to the court; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set out the offence charged and a description of
    the property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-forfeiture of real property or

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to an order made under subsection 97(3), if a

  under subsection 94(1) or 95(2) in respect of real property or

  and the criminal record, if any, of the person charged with or
  convicted or discharged under section 730 of the [Criminal

  forfeiture of the property or part of the property and may revoke any
  restraint order made in respect of that property or part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors in relation to dwelling-house

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If all or part of the property that would otherwise
  be forfeited under subsection 94(1) or 95(2) is a dwelling-house, when
  making a decision under subsection (3), the court must also consider

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the impact of an order of forfeiture on any member
    of the immediate family of the person charged with or convicted or
    discharged of the offence, if the dwelling-house was the family
    member's principal residence at the time the charge was laid and
    continues to be the family member's principal residence; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the family member appears innocent of any
    complicity in the offence or of any collusion in relation to the
    offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of claimants

- **[[99]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-99 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If any

  subsection 94(1) or 95(2), any person that claims an interest in or

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of property forfeited under an order

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of property forfeited under an order
    made under subsection 95(2), a person that was charged with the

    or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person that acquired title to, ownership of or a
    right of possession of the property from a person referred to in
    paragraph (a) or (b) under circumstances that give rise to a
    reasonable inference that the title or right was transferred from
    that person for the purpose of avoiding the forfeiture of the
    property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fixing day for hearing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The judge to whom the application is made must fix a
  day that is not less than 30 days after the date of the filing of the
  application for the hearing of the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The applicant must serve a notice of the application
  and of the hearing of it on the Attorney General at least 15 days
  before the day fixed for the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order declaring interest or right not
  affected by forfeiture

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The judge to whom the application is made may make an
  order declaring that the interest or right of the applicant is not
  affected by the forfeiture and declaring the nature and the extent or
  value of the interest or right if the judge is satisfied that the
  applicant

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not a person referred to in paragraph
    (1)(a), (b) or (c) and appears innocent of any complicity in any
    designated offence that resulted in the forfeiture of the property
    or of any collusion in relation to such an offence; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} exercised all reasonable care to be satisfied that
    the property was not likely to have been used in connection with the
    commission of an unlawful act by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the person that was permitted by the applicant to
      obtain possession of the property or from which the applicant
      obtained possession,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the mortgagor or hypothecary debtor, if the
      applicant is a mortgagee or hypothecary creditor,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the person that is subject to the charge, if
      the applicant is a chargee,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the person that is subject to the prior claim,
      if the applicant is the holder of a prior claim,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the person that is subject to the lien, if the
      applicant is a lienholder, or

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the giver of a security interest, if the
      applicant is the creditor in relation to the security interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal --- subsection (4)

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An applicant or the Attorney General may appeal to
  the court of appeal from an order made under subsection (4), and the
  provisions of Part XXI of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) with

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of property

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must, on application made to the

  under subsection (4), and if the periods with respect to the taking of

  taken under subsection (5) has been determined, direct that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the property, or the part of it to which the
    interest or right of the applicant relates, be returned to the
    applicant; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to the value of the interest or
    right of the applicant, as declared in the order, be paid to the
    applicant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of order pending appeal

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any appeal taken from an order of forfeiture or
  restoration in respect of the property.

The property must not be disposed of or otherwise dealt with until the
expiry of the 30th day after the day an order is made under any of those
provisions.

### [DIVISION 2]{.HLabel2}[Cannabis and Chemical Property]{.HTitleText2} {#h-77910 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return

- **[[102]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-102 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  peace officer, inspector or prescribed person that seizes, finds or
  otherwise acquires cannabis or chemical offence-related property in
  the course of the administration or enforcement of this Act or any
  other Act of Parliament --- or that seizes, finds or otherwise
  acquires any chemical or thing that is referred to in paragraph (b)
  or (c) of the definition[[ chemical property]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
  in subsection 2(1) in the course of the administration or enforcement
  of this Act --- may return it to the person that is its owner or that
  is entitled to its possession if the peace officer, inspector or
  prescribed person is satisfied

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that there is no dispute as to who owns it or is
    entitled to its possession; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that its continued detention is not required for
    the purposes of a preliminary inquiry, trial or other proceeding
    under this Act or any other Act of Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When the cannabis or property is returned, the peace
  officer, inspector or prescribed person must obtain a receipt for it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a seizure made under section 87 of
  this Act, the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or a power of seizure at
  common law, the peace officer making the seizure must make a report
  about the return to the justice who issued the warrant or another
  justice for the same territorial division or, if a warrant was not
  issued, a justice who would have had jurisdiction to issue a warrant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for order to return

- **[[103]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-103 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  cannabis or chemical property has been seized, found or otherwise
  acquired by a peace officer, inspector or prescribed person, any
  person may, within 60 days after the date of the seizure, finding or
  acquisition, on prior notification being given to the Attorney General
  in the prescribed manner, apply, by notice in writing to a justice in
  the jurisdiction in which it is being detained, for an order to return
  it to the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order to return

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, on the hearing of the application, a justice is
  satisfied that the applicant is the owner or is entitled to possession
  of the cannabis or the property and the Attorney General does not
  indicate that it or any part of it may be required for the purposes of
  a preliminary inquiry, trial or other proceeding under this or any
  other Act of Parliament, the justice must, subject to subsection (5),
  order that it, or the part that is not required, as the case may be,
  be returned as soon as feasible to the applicant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order to return at specified time

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, on the hearing of the application, a justice is
  satisfied that the applicant is the owner or is entitled to possession
  of the cannabis or property but the Attorney General indicates that it
  or any part of it may be required for the purposes of a preliminary
  inquiry, trial or other proceeding under this Act or any other Act of
  Parliament, the justice must, subject to subsection (5), order that
  it, or the part that is required, as the case may be, be returned to
  the applicant

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the expiry of 180 days after the day on which
    the application was made, if no proceeding in relation to the
    cannabis or property has been commenced before that time; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the final conclusion of the proceeding or any
    other proceeding in relation to the cannabis or property if the
    applicant is not found guilty in those proceedings of an offence
    committed in relation to it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of forfeiture

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, on the hearing of the application, a justice is
  not satisfied that the applicant is the owner or is entitled to
  possession of the cannabis or property, and it or a part of it is not
  required for the purposes of a preliminary inquiry, trial or other
  proceeding under this Act or any other Act of Parliament, the justice
  must order that it, or the part that is not required, as the case may
  be, be forfeited to Her Majesty to be disposed of or otherwise dealt
  with in accordance with the regulations or, if there are no applicable
  regulations, in the manner that the Minister directs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of compensation in lieu

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If, on the hearing of the application, a justice is
  satisfied that the applicant is the owner or is entitled to possession
  of the cannabis or property, but it was disposed of or otherwise dealt
  with under section 105, the justice must make an order that an amount
  equal to its value be paid to the applicant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture if no application

**[[104]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-104 .sectionLabel}** If no application for
the return of the cannabis or the chemical property has been made under
subsection 103(1) within 60 days after the date of its seizure, finding
or acquisition by a peace officer, inspector or prescribed person and it
or any part of it is not required for the purposes of a preliminary
inquiry, trial or other proceeding under this Act or any other Act of
Parliament, the cannabis or the property, or the part that is not
required, as the case may be, is forfeited to Her Majesty and may be
disposed of or otherwise dealt with in accordance with the regulations
or, if there are no applicable regulations, in the manner that the
Minister directs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expedited disposition

**[[105]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-105 .sectionLabel}** If all or any part of
any cannabis or chemical property whose storage or handling poses a risk
to health or safety is not required for the purposes of a preliminary
inquiry, trial or other proceeding under this Act or any other Act of
Parliament, the Minister or a peace officer or prescribed person may
dispose of or otherwise deal with it in accordance with the regulations
or, if there are no applicable regulations, in the manner that the
Minister directs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Destruction of plants

**[[106]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-106 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, on
prior notification being given to the Attorney General, cause to be
destroyed any cannabis plant that is being produced contrary to the
provisions of this Act or of the regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition following proceedings

**[[107]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-107 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 103,
if, in a preliminary inquiry, trial or other proceeding under this Act
or any other Act of Parliament, the court before which the proceedings
have been brought is satisfied that any cannabis or chemical property
that is the subject of proceedings before the court is no longer
required by that court or any other court, the court

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} must order that it be returned to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the person from which it was seized if the court is
    satisfied that the person came into its possession lawfully and
    continued to deal with it lawfully, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the person that is its owner or that is entitled
    to its possession, if that person is known and if the court is
    satisfied that its possession by the person from which it was seized
    was unlawful; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} may order that it be forfeited to Her Majesty to be
  disposed of or otherwise dealt with in accordance with the regulations
  or, if there are no applicable regulations, in the manner that the
  Minister directs, if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the court is not satisfied that it should be
    returned under subparagraph (a)(i) or (ii), or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} its possession by the person from which it was
    seized was unlawful and the person that is its owner or that is
    entitled to its possession is not known.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal with consent

**[[108]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-108 .sectionLabel}** If cannabis or chemical
property has been seized, found or otherwise acquired by a peace
officer, inspector or prescribed person and it or a part of it is not
required for the purposes of a preliminary inquiry, trial or other
proceeding under this Act or any other Act of Parliament, its owner may
consent to its disposal and, if the consent is given, the cannabis or
property, or the part that is not required, as the case may be, is
forfeited to Her Majesty and may be disposed of or otherwise dealt with
in accordance with the regulations or, if there are no applicable
regulations, in the manner that the Minister directs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition report

- **[[109]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-109
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, every
  peace officer, inspector or prescribed person that disposes of or
  otherwise deals with cannabis or chemical property under this Act
  must, within 30 days after doing so, cause a report to be sent to the
  Minister setting out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the cannabis or property;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of it that was disposed of or otherwise
    dealt with;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which it was disposed of or otherwise
    dealt with;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date on which it was disposed of or otherwise
    dealt with;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name of the police force, agency or entity to
    which the peace officer, inspector or prescribed person belongs;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number of the file or police report related to
    its disposition or the other dealing with it; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any other prescribed information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), otherwise dealing
  with cannabis or chemical property by a peace officer includes using
  it to conduct an investigation or for training purposes.

## [PART 10]{.HLabel1}[Administrative Monetary Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-77961 .Part}

### [Powers of Minister]{.HTitleText2} {#h-77962 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

### [Violation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-77965 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission

- **[[111]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-111
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every person that contravenes a
  provision of this Act --- except provisions of Division 1 of Part 1
  --- a provision of the regulations, or an order made under any of
  sections 73 to 76, an order amended under section 79 or an order made
  under section 82, commits a violation and is liable to a penalty of
  not more than \$1,000,000 or, if the violation is classified by the
  regulations as a minor violation, a serious violation or a very
  serious violation, to a penalty of not more than the maximum amount
  fixed by the regulations in relation to that classification.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of penalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of a penalty is to promote compliance
  with this Act.

### [Proceedings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-77971 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of notice of violation

- **[[112]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-112 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  an individual who is designated under section 110 believes on
  reasonable grounds that a person has committed a violation, the
  designated individual may issue, and must provide the person with, a
  notice of violation that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sets out the person's name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identifies the alleged violation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sets out the penalty for the violation that the
    person is liable to pay; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} sets out the particulars concerning the time and
    manner of payment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summary of rights

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of violation must summarize, in plain
  language, the rights and obligations of the named person under this
  section and sections 113 to 125, including the right to have the acts
  or omissions that constitute the alleged violation or the amount of
  the penalty reviewed and the procedure for requesting that review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The penalty for a violation is to be determined by
  taking into account

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person's history of compliance or
    non-compliance with the provisions of this Act or of the
    regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the nature and scope of the violation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the person made reasonable efforts to
    mitigate or reverse the effects of the violation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether the person derived any competitive or
    economic benefit from the violation; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other prescribed criteria.

### [Penalties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-77988 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

- **[[113]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-113 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the person named in the notice of violation pays, in the time and
  manner specified in the notice, the amount of the penalty set out in
  the notice,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are deemed to have committed the violation in
    respect of which the amount is paid;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister must accept that amount as complete
    satisfaction of the penalty in respect of the violation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proceedings commenced in respect of the
    violation are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternatives

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Instead of paying the amount of the penalty set out
  in the notice, the person named in the notice may, in the time and
  manner specified in the notice,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the amount of the penalty set out in the notice
    is \$5,000 or more, request to enter into a compliance agreement
    with the Minister that ensures the person's compliance with the
    provision or the order to which the violation relates; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} request a review by the Minister of the acts or
    omissions that constitute the alleged violation or of the amount of
    the penalty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the person named in the notice does not pay the
  penalty set out in the notice in the time and manner specified in the
  notice and does not exercise any right referred to in subsection (2)
  in the specified time and manner, they are deemed to have committed
  the violation identified in the notice.

### [Compliance Agreements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-78001 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entering into compliance agreements

- **[[114]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-114
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After considering a request made
  under paragraph 113(2)(a), the Minister may enter into a compliance
  agreement, as described in that paragraph, with the person making the
  request on any terms and conditions that are satisfactory to the
  Minister. The terms and conditions may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} include a provision for the deposit of reasonable
    security, in a form and amount satisfactory to the Minister, as a
    guarantee that the person will comply with the compliance agreement;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide for the reduction, in whole or in part, of
    the penalty for the violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person that enters into a compliance agreement with
  the Minister is, on doing so, deemed to have committed the violation
  in respect of which the compliance agreement was entered into.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of compliance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is satisfied that a person that has
  entered into a compliance agreement has complied with it, the Minister
  must cause a notice to that effect to be provided to the person, at
  which time

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proceedings commenced in respect of the
    violation are ended; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any security given by the person under the
    compliance agreement must be returned to the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of default

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is of the opinion that a person that
  has entered into a compliance agreement has not complied with it, the
  Minister must cause a notice of default to be provided to the person
  to the effect that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} instead of being liable to pay the amount of the
    penalty set out in the notice of violation in respect of which the
    compliance agreement was entered into, the person is liable to pay,
    in the time and manner specified in the notice of violation, twice
    the amount of that penalty, and, for greater certainty, subsections
    111(1) and 139(4) do not apply in respect of that amount; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the security, if any, given by the person under the
    compliance agreement is forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of notice of default

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Once provided with the notice of default, the person
  may not deduct from the amount set out in the notice of default any
  amount they spent under the compliance agreement and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is liable to pay the amount set out in
    the notice of default in the time and manner specified in the notice
    of violation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the notice provides for the forfeiture of the
    security given under the compliance agreement, that security is
    forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada and the proceedings
    commenced in respect of the violation are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of payment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a person pays the amount set out in the notice of
  default in the time and manner specified in the notice of violation,
  the Minister must accept the amount as complete satisfaction of the
  amount owing in respect of the violation and the proceedings commenced
  in respect of the violation are ended.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to enter into compliance
agreement

- **[[115]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-115 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the Minister refuses to enter into a compliance agreement requested
  under paragraph 113(2)(a), the person that made the request is liable
  to pay, in the time and manner specified in the notice of violation,
  the amount of the penalty set out in the notice of violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person pays the amount referred to in subsection
  (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are deemed to have committed the violation in
    respect of which the amount is paid;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister must accept the amount as complete
    satisfaction of the penalty in respect of the violation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proceedings commenced in respect of the
    violation are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person does not pay the amount referred to in
  subsection (1) in the specified time and manner, they are deemed to
  have committed the violation identified in the notice of violation.

### [Review by Minister]{.HTitleText2} {#h-78033 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review --- with respect to facts

- **[[116]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-116 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  completion of a review requested under paragraph 113(2)(b) with
  respect to the acts or omissions that constitute the alleged
  violation, the Minister must determine whether the person that
  requested the review committed the violation. If the Minister
  determines that the person committed the violation but that the amount
  of the penalty was not established in accordance with the provisions
  of this Act and of the regulations, the Minister must correct the
  amount.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violation not committed --- effect

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines under subsection (1) that
  the person that requested the review did not commit the violation, the
  proceedings commenced in respect of it are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review --- with respect to penalty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On completion of a review requested under paragraph
  113(2)(b) with respect to the amount of the penalty, the Minister must
  determine whether the amount of the penalty was established in
  accordance with the provisions of this Act and of the regulations and,
  if not, the Minister must correct the amount.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must cause a notice of any decision made
  under subsection (1) or (3) to be provided to the person that
  requested the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The person that requested the review is liable to
  pay, in the time and manner specified in the notice of violation, the
  amount of the penalty that is confirmed or corrected in the Minister's
  decision made under subsection (1) or (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of payment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a person pays the amount referred to in subsection
  (5), the Minister must accept the amount as complete satisfaction of
  the penalty in respect of the violation and the proceedings commenced
  in respect of the violation are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written evidence and submissions

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is to consider only written evidence and
  written submissions in determining whether a person committed a
  violation or whether the amount of a penalty was established in
  accordance with the provisions of this Act and of the regulations.

### [Enforcement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-78049 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debts to Her Majesty

- **[[117]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-117 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following amounts constitute debts due to Her Majesty in right of
  Canada that may be recovered in the Federal Court:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of a penalty, from the time the notice
    of violation setting out the amount of the penalty is provided;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} every amount set out in a compliance agreement
    entered into with the Minister under subsection 114(1), from the
    time the compliance agreement is entered into;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount set out in a notice of default referred
    to in subsection 114(4), from the time the notice is provided; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount of a penalty as set out in a decision of
    the Minister made under subsection 116(1) or (3), from the time the
    notice of that decision is provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No proceedings to recover a debt referred to in
  subsection (1) may be commenced after the expiry of five years after
  the day on which the debt became payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt final

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A debt referred to in subsection (1) is final and not
  subject to review or to be restrained, prohibited, removed, set aside
  or otherwise dealt with except to the extent and in the manner
  provided by sections 113 to 116.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of default

- **[[118]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-118 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  debt referred to in subsection 117(1) in respect of which there is a
  default of payment, or the part of any such debt that has not been
  paid, may be certified by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judgment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On production to the Federal Court, a certificate
  made under subsection (1) must be registered in that Court and, when
  registered, has the same force and effect, and all proceedings may be
  taken on the certificate, as if it were a judgment obtained in that
  Court for a debt of the amount specified in it and all reasonable
  costs and charges associated with the registration of the certificate.

### [Rules About Violations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-78066 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain defences not available

- **[[119]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-119 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person named in a notice of violation does not have a defence by
  reason that the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exercised due diligence to prevent the violation;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reasonably and honestly believed in the existence
    of facts that, if true, would exonerate the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Common law principles

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every rule and principle of the common law that
  renders any circumstance a justification or excuse in relation to a
  charge for an offence under this Act applies in respect of a violation
  to the extent that it is not inconsistent with this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Burden of proof

**[[120]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-120 .sectionLabel}** In every case when the
facts of a violation are reviewed by the Minister, he or she must
determine, on a balance of probabilities, whether the person named in
the notice of violation committed the violation identified in the
notice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violation by corporate officers, etc.

**[[121]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-121 .sectionLabel}** If a person other than
an individual commits a violation, any of the person's directors,
officers or agents or mandataries who directed, authorized, assented to,
acquiesced in or participated in the commission of the violation is a
party to and liable for the violation whether or not the person that
actually committed the violation is proceeded against under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employees or agents or mandataries

**[[122]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-122 .sectionLabel}** A person is liable for
a violation that is committed by any employee or agent or mandatary of
the person acting in the course of the employee's employment or the
scope of the agent's or mandatary's authority, whether or not the
employee or agent or mandatary who actually committed the violation is
identified or proceeded against under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing violation

**[[123]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-123 .sectionLabel}** A violation that is
committed or continued on more than one day constitutes a separate
violation in respect of each day on which it is committed or continued.

### [Other Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-78082 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

**[[124]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-124 .sectionLabel}** In any proceeding in
respect of a violation or a prosecution for an offence, a notice of
violation purporting to be issued under this Act is admissible in
evidence without proof of the signature or official character of the
individual purporting to have signed the notice of violation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

**[[125]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-125 .sectionLabel}** No proceedings in
respect of a violation may be commenced after the expiry of six months
after the day on which the Minister becomes aware of the acts or
omissions that constitute the alleged violation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How act or omission may be proceeded with

**[[126]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-126 .sectionLabel}** If an act or omission
can be proceeded with either as a violation or as an offence, proceeding
in one manner precludes proceeding in the other.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certification by Minister

**[[127]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-127 .sectionLabel}** A document purporting
to have been issued by the Minister, certifying the day on which the
acts or omissions that constitute the alleged violation became known to
the Minister, is admissible in evidence without proof of the signature
or official character of the person appearing to have signed the
document and, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, is proof that
the Minister became aware of the acts or omissions on that day.

    - [77]{.lawlabel} [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each of the following provisions,
      as it read immediately before the day on which this Act comes into
      force, continues to apply with respect to an application made for
      a warrant under the provision if the application is submitted, and

        Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5);

- --- 2022, c. 17, s. 78.1

  - ###### Impact of remote proceedings {#impact-of-remote-proceedings .MarginalNote}

    - [78.1]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Justice must,
      no later than three years after the day on which this Act receives
      royal assent, initiate one or more independent reviews on the use
      of remote proceedings in criminal justice matters that must
      include an assessment of whether remote proceedings

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enhance, preserve or adversely affect access to
        justice;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintain fundamental principles of the
        administration of justice; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} adequately address the rights and obligations
        of participants in the criminal justice system, including
        accused persons.

    - ###### Report {#report .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Justice must, no later than five
      years after the day on which a review is initiated, cause a report
      on the review --- including any findings or recommendations
      resulting from it --- to be laid before each House of Parliament.

- --- 2022, c. 17, s. 78.2

  - ###### Review by committee {#review-by-committee .MarginalNote}

    - [78.2]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the start of the fifth year
      after the day on which this Act receives royal assent, the
      provisions enacted or amended by this Act are to be referred to a
      committee of the Senate and a committee of the House of Commons
      that may be designated or established for the purpose of reviewing
      the provisions.

    - ###### Report {#report-1 .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The committees to which the provisions are
      referred are to review them and the use of remote proceedings in
      criminal justice matters and submit reports to the Houses of
      Parliament of which they are committees, including statements
      setting out any changes to the provisions that they recommend.
