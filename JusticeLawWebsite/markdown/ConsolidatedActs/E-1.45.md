## [PART 6]{.HLabel1}[Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1292569 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment --- requisition of Minister of
Economic Development and Official Languages

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  may be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund for the fiscal year
  ending on March 31, 2021, on the requisition of the Minister of
  Economic Development and Official Languages, a sum not exceeding
  \$206,700,000 to the regional development agencies for the Regional
  Relief and Recovery Fund.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[regional development
  agencies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), [[regional
  development agencies]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Department of Western Economic Diversification;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Atlantic Canada Opportunities Agency;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Northern Economic Development Agency;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Economic Development Agency of Canada for the
    Regions of Quebec;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Federal Economic Development Agency for
    Southern Ontario; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the Federal Economic Development Initiative for
    Northern Ontario.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments --- requisition of Minister of
Health

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), there may
  be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund for the fiscal year
  ending on March 31, 2021, on the requisition of the Minister of
  Health, all money required to do anything in relation to the
  initiatives specified in the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The total of all payments made under subsection (1),
  in respect of an initiative specified in column 1 of the schedule,
  must not exceed the limit specified for that initiative in column 2 of
  the schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment --- requisition of Minister of
Employment and Social Development

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  may be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund until March 31, 2021,
  on the requisition of the Minister of Employment and Social
  Development, a sum not exceeding \$500,000,000 for the purpose of
  making income support payments under section 4 of the [Canada
  Emergency Response Benefit Act](/eng/acts/C-3.7).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the payments that may be made
  out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund under subsection (1) must not
  include the costs of administering or enforcing the [Canada Emergency
  Response Benefit Act](/eng/acts/C-3.7).
