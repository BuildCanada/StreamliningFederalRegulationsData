## Tla'amin Final Agreement Act {#tlaamin-final-agreement-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2014, c. 11
:::

Assented to 2014-06-19

An Act to give effect to the Tla'amin Final Agreement and to make
consequential amendments to other Acts
::::

:::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/) recognizes and affirms
the existing Aboriginal and treaty rights of the Aboriginal peoples of
Canada;

Whereas the reconciliation between the prior presence of Aboriginal
peoples and the assertion of sovereignty by the Crown is of significant
social and economic importance to Canadians;

Whereas Canadian courts have stated that this reconciliation is best
achieved through negotiation;

Whereas the Tla'amin Nation is an Aboriginal people of Canada;

Whereas the Tla'amin Nation, the Government of Canada and the Government
of British Columbia have negotiated the Agreement to achieve this
reconciliation and to establish a new relationship among them;

And whereas the Agreement requires that legislation be enacted by the
Parliament of Canada in order for the Agreement to be ratified;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448803 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Tla'amin Final Agreement Act](/eng/acts/T-11.2).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448806 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Tla'amin Final
      Agreement, between the Tla'amin Nation, Her Majesty in right of
      Canada and Her Majesty in right of British Columbia, including any
      amendments made to it. ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Tax Treatment Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Tax Treatment Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the tax
      treatment agreement referred to in paragraph 22 of Chapter 21 of
      the Agreement, including any amendments made to it. ([accord sur
      le traitement fiscal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions in Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Act, [[Former Sliammon Indian
  Reserves]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Other Tla'amin
  Lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Sliammon Indian
  Band]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Tla'amin
  Citizen]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Tla'amin
  Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Tla'amin
  Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Tla'amin
  Lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Tla'amin Law]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
  [[Tla'amin Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[Tla'amin Public
  Institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} have the same meanings as in
  Chapter 1 of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of Agreement

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement is a treaty
and a land claims agreement within the meaning of sections 25 and 35 of
the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

## [Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448816 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement given effect

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement is approved, given effect and declared valid and has the
  force of law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and obligations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, any person or body referred to
  in the Agreement has the powers, rights, privileges and benefits
  conferred on the person or body by the Agreement and must perform the
  duties, and is subject to the liabilities, imposed on the person or
  body by the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Third parties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Agreement is binding on,
  and may be relied on by, all persons and bodies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement prevails

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement prevails over this Act and any other federal law to the
  extent of any inconsistency between them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act prevails

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Act prevails over any other federal law to the
  extent of any conflict between them.

## [Appropriation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448829 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of C.R.F.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** There must be paid out of
the Consolidated Revenue Fund any sums that are required to meet the
monetary obligations of Her Majesty in right of Canada under Chapters 18
and 19 of the Agreement.

## [Lands]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448832 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee simple estate

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** On the effective date of
the Agreement, the Tla'amin Nation owns the estate in fee simple, as set
out in Chapter 3 of the Agreement, in the Tla'amin Lands, except for the
lands described in Part 1 of Appendix C-3 of the Agreement, and in the
Other Tla'amin Lands.

## [Taxation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448835 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tax Treatment Agreement given effect

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Tax Treatment Agreement
is approved, given effect and declared valid and has the force of law
during the period that it is in effect.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Tax Treatment Agreement
does not form part of the Agreement and is not a treaty or a land claims
agreement within the meaning of sections 25 and 35 of the [Constitution
Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

## [Application of Other Acts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448840 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the provisions
of Chapter 17 of the Agreement that deal with the continuing application
of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) and to paragraphs 16 to 21 of Chapter
21 of the Agreement, the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) does not apply to
the Tla'amin Nation, Tla'amin Citizens, the Tla'amin Government,
Tla'amin Public Institutions, Tla'amin Lands or Other Tla'amin Lands as
of the effective date of the Agreement, except for the purpose of
determining whether an individual is an Indian.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnification

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** For as long as the
[Framework Agreement on First Nation Land Management
Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46) is in force, Her Majesty in right of Canada or
the Tla'amin Nation, as the case may be, must, as of the effective date
of the Agreement, indemnify the other in respect of anything done or
omitted to be done in relation to the Former Sliammon Indian Reserves,
in the same manner and under the same conditions as would be applicable
if the [First Nations Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.8), as it read
before the coming into force of the [Framework Agreement on First Nation
Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46), continued to apply to the
Former Sliammon Indian Reserves.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Tla'amin Laws and other
instruments made under the Agreement are not statutory instruments for
the purposes of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

## [Application of Laws of British Columbia]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448854 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** To the extent that a law
of British Columbia does not apply of its own force to the Tla'amin
Nation, Tla'amin Citizens, the Tla'amin Government, Tla'amin Public
Institutions, Tla'amin Corporations, Tla'amin Lands or Other Tla'amin
Lands, because of the exclusive legislative authority of Parliament set
out in Class 24 of section 91 of the [Constitution Act,
1867](/eng/Const/), that law of British Columbia applies to it or to
them by virtue of this section, in accordance with the Agreement and
subject to this Act and any other Act of Parliament.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448857 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice of Agreements

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice must be taken of the
  Agreement and the Tax Treatment Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of Agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Agreement and the Tax Treatment Agreement must be
  published by the Queen's Printer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the Agreement or the Tax Treatment
  Agreement published by the Queen's Printer is evidence of that
  agreement and of its contents, and a copy purporting to be published
  by the Queen's Printer is presumed to be so published unless the
  contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice of Tla'amin Laws

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice must be taken of
  Tla'amin Laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence of Tla'amin Laws

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of a Tla'amin Law purporting to be deposited
  in the public registry referred to in subparagraph 19.a of Chapter 15
  of the Agreement is evidence of that law and of its contents, unless
  the contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of issues arising

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  issue arises in any judicial or administrative proceeding in respect
  of the interpretation or validity of the Agreement, or the validity or
  applicability of this Act, the British Columbia [Tla'amin Final
  Agreement Act](/eng/acts/T-11.2) or any Tla'amin Law, then the issue
  must not be decided until the party raising the issue has served
  notice on the Attorney General of Canada, the Attorney General of
  British Columbia and the Tla'amin Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content and timing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} describe the proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specify the subject matter of the issue;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state the day on which the issue is to be argued;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} give the particulars that are necessary to show the
    point to be argued; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be served at least 14 days before the day of
    argument, unless the court or tribunal authorizes a shorter period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation in proceedings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Attorney General of Canada, the Attorney General
  of British Columbia and the Tla'amin Nation may appear and participate
  in any proceeding in respect of which subsection (1) applies as
  parties with the same rights as any other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsections (2) and (3) do not
  require that an oral hearing be held if one is not otherwise required.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chapters 22 and 23 of Agreement

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsection 4(1),
Chapters 22 and 23 of the Agreement are deemed to have effect as of
April 1, 2009.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make any orders and regulations that are necessary for the purpose
of carrying out any of the provisions of the Agreement or of the Tax
Treatment Agreement.
