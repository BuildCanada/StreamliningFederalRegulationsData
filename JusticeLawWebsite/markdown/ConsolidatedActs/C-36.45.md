## Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership Implementation Act {#comprehensive-and-progressive-agreement-for-trans-pacific-partnership-implementation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2018, c. 23
:::

Assented to 2018-10-25

An Act to implement the Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for
Trans-Pacific Partnership between Canada, Australia, Brunei, Chile,
Japan, Malaysia, Mexico, New Zealand, Peru, Singapore and Vietnam
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-90547 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific
Partnership Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-36.45).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-90550 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Comprehensive and
    Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership between Canada,
    Australia, Brunei, Chile, Japan, Malaysia, Mexico, New Zealand,
    Peru, Singapore and Vietnam, done at Santiago on March 8,
    2018. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commission established
    under the Agreement and whose powers, functions and duties are set
    out in Chapter 27 of the TPP. ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the whole or any portion
    of an Act of Parliament or a regulation, order or other instrument
    issued, made or established in the exercise of a power conferred by
    or under an Act of Parliament. ([texte législatif
    fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister for
    International Trade. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[TPP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[TPP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Trans-Pacific Partnership
    Agreement as incorporated by reference into the Agreement by Article
    1 of the Agreement. ([PTP]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation consistent with Agreement

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, this
Act and any federal law that implements a provision of the Agreement or
fulfils an obligation of the Government of Canada under the Agreement is
to be interpreted in a manner consistent with the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of Act and Agreement to
water

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act or the Agreement applies to natural surface or
ground water in liquid, gaseous or solid state.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act, by specific mention or omission, is to be construed
to affect in any manner the right of Parliament to enact legislation to
implement any provision of the Agreement or fulfil any of the
obligations of the Government of Canada under the Agreement.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-90564 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-90567 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to implement the Agreement, the objectives of which, as elaborated more
specifically through its provisions, are to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish a free trade area in accordance with the
  Agreement;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} promote, through the expansion of reciprocal trade,
  the harmonious development of the economic relations between Canada
  and other parties to the Agreement in order to create opportunities
  for economic development;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} promote conditions of fair competition affecting
  trade between Canada and other parties to the Agreement;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} substantially increase investment opportunities
  between Canada and other parties to the Agreement, while preserving
  the right of each of the parties to regulate to achieve legitimate
  policy goals;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} eliminate barriers to trade in goods and services in
  order to contribute to the harmonious development and expansion of
  world and regional trade, and seek to ensure that the benefits and
  opportunities created by the Agreement are widely shared;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} support the growth and development of micro, small
  and medium-sized enterprises by enhancing their ability to participate
  in and benefit from the opportunities created by trade;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} enhance and enforce environmental laws and
  regulations and strengthen cooperation between Canada and other
  parties to the Agreement on environmental matters;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} protect, enhance and enforce basic workers' rights,
  strengthen cooperation on labour matters, and build on the respective
  international commitments of Canada and other parties to the Agreement
  on labour matters; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} recognise the importance of promoting corporate
  social responsibility, cultural identity and diversity, gender
  equality, the rights of Indigenous peoples, inclusive trade and
  sustainable development.

## [Causes of Action]{.HTitleText1} {#h-90579 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Causes of action under sections 9 to 13

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person has any cause of action and no proceedings of any kind are to
  be taken, without the consent of the Attorney General of Canada, to
  enforce or determine any right or obligation that is claimed or arises
  solely under or by virtue of sections 9 to 13 or an order made under
  those sections.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Causes of action under Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person has any cause of action and no proceedings
  of any kind are to be taken, without the consent of the Attorney
  General of Canada, to enforce or determine any right or obligation
  that is claimed or arises solely under or by virtue of the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply with respect to causes
  of action arising out of, and proceedings taken under, Section B of
  Chapter 9 or Article 11.22 of the TPP.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Implementation of the Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-90587 .Part}

### [Approval and Representation on the Commission]{.HTitleText2} {#h-90588 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement is approved.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian representative on Commission

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is the
principal representative of Canada on the Commission.

### [Panels and Labour Council]{.HTitleText2} {#h-90593 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint a panellist in accordance with Article 28.9
    of the TPP or establish a list of individuals who may serve as
    panellists in accordance with paragraph 9 of Article 28.11 of the
    TPP;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} propose the names of individuals for the roster
    referred to in Article 28.11 of the TPP; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} appoint an arbitrator in accordance with Section B
    of Chapter 9 of the TPP.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister of Labour

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Labour may act as Canada's senior
  governmental representative on the Labour Council referred to in
  Article 19.12 of the TPP or may designate that representative.

### [Expenses]{.HTitleText2} {#h-90602 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of expenditures

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
is to pay its appropriate share of the aggregate of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any expenditures incurred by or on behalf of the
  Commission,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the general expenses incurred by the committees,
  working groups and other bodies established under the Agreement and
  the remuneration and expenses payable to representatives on the
  Commission and those committees and to members of those working groups
  and other bodies, and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the expenses incurred by panels and arbitration
  tribunals established under the Agreement and the remuneration and
  expenses payable to the panellists on those panels, to arbitrators and
  to any experts retained by those panels or arbitration tribunals.

### [Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-90608 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders --- Article 28.20 of TPP

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, for the purpose of suspending benefits in
  accordance with Article 28.20 of the TPP, by order, do any of the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend rights or privileges granted by Canada to
    another party to the Agreement or to goods, service suppliers,
    investors or investments of investors of that party under the
    Agreement or any federal law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} modify or suspend the application of any federal
    law, with respect to a party to the Agreement other than Canada or
    to goods, service suppliers, investors or investments of investors
    of that party;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} extend the application of any federal law to a
    party to the Agreement other than Canada or to goods, service
    suppliers, investors or investments of investors of that party; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} take any other measure that the Governor in Council
    considers necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless repealed, an order made under subsection (1)
  has effect for the period specified in the order.
