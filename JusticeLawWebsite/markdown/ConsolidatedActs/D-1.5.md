## Déline Final Self-Government Agreement Act {#déline-final-self-government-agreement-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2015, c. 24
:::

Assented to 2015-06-18

An Act to give effect to the Déline Final Self-Government Agreement and
to make consequential and related amendments to other Acts
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/) recognizes and affirms
the existing Aboriginal and treaty rights of the Aboriginal peoples of
Canada;

Whereas the Sahtu Dene and Metis Comprehensive Land Claim Agreement,
signed on September 6, 1993, provides for the negotiation of
self-government agreements among the Sahtu Dene and Metis, the
Government of Canada and the Government of the Northwest Territories;

Whereas the Sahtu Dene and Metis Comprehensive Land Claim Agreement
provides that self-government negotiations will address the desire of
the Sahtu Dene and Metis to have self-government exercised as close to
the community level as is reasonably possible;

Whereas the Sahtu Dene and Metis of Déline, as represented by the Déline
First Nation Band and the Déline Land Corporation, the Government of
Canada and the Government of the Northwest Territories have negotiated
the Déline Final Self-Government Agreement in accordance with chapter 5
and Appendix B of the Sahtu Dene and Metis Comprehensive Land Claim
Agreement;

Whereas the Sahtu Dene and Metis of Déline approved the Déline Final
Self-Government Agreement by a vote held on March 10, 11 and 12, 2014;

And whereas the Déline Final Self-Government Agreement requires that
legislation be enacted by the Parliament of Canada in order for that
Agreement to be ratified;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-171499 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Déline Final Self-Government Agreement Act](/eng/acts/D-1.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-171502 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Déline Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Déline Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Déline Final
    Self-Government Agreement among the Déline First Nation Band, the
    Déline Land Corporation, the Government of Canada and the Government
    of the Northwest Territories, signed on February 18, 2015, including
    any amendments made to it. ([accord de Deline]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Déline First Nation Band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Déline First Nation Band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
    meaning as in chapter 1 of the Déline Agreement. ([bande de la
    Première Nation de Deline]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Déline Got'ine Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Déline Got'ine Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    government established in accordance with chapter 3 of the Déline
    Agreement. ([gouvernement Gotine de Deline]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Déline Land Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Déline Land Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in chapter 1 of the Déline Agreement. ([société foncière de
    Deline]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Déline law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Déline law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by the
    definition [[DGG Law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in chapter 1 of the
    Déline Agreement. ([loi de Deline]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Déline Tax Treatment Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Déline Tax Treatment Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the tax
    treatment agreement referred to in 22.3.1 of the Déline Agreement,
    including any amendments made to it. ([accord sur le traitement
    fiscal de Deline]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Sahtu Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Sahtu Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement as
    defined in section 2 of the [Sahtu Dene and Metis Land Claim
    Settlement Act](/eng/acts/S-1.5). ([accord du
    Sahtu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of Déline Agreement

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Déline Agreement is a
treaty within the meaning of sections 25 and 35 of the [Constitution
Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

## [Déline Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-171514 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Déline Agreement given effect

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Déline Agreement is approved, given effect and declared valid and has
  the force of law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and obligations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a person or entity has the
  powers, rights, privileges and benefits conferred on the person or
  entity by the Déline Agreement and must perform the duties, and is
  subject to the liabilities, imposed on the person or entity by the
  Déline Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Third parties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Déline Agreement is
  binding on, and may be relied on by, all persons and entities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Déline Agreement prevails

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 6, the Déline Agreement prevails over this Act and any
  other federal law to the extent of any inconsistency between them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act prevails

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 7, this Act prevails over any
  other federal law to the extent of any conflict between them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency with Déline Agreement

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The [Sahtu Dene and Metis
Land Claim Settlement Act](/eng/acts/S-1.5) and the Sahtu Agreement
prevail over the Déline Agreement to the extent of any inconsistency
between that Act and the Déline Agreement or between the Sahtu Agreement
and the Déline Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency with this Act

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The [Sahtu Dene and Metis
Land Claim Settlement Act](/eng/acts/S-1.5) and the Sahtu Agreement
prevail over this Act to the extent of any inconsistency between that
Act and this Act or between the Sahtu Agreement and this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency with Déline law

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The [Sahtu Dene and Metis
Land Claim Settlement Act](/eng/acts/S-1.5) and the Sahtu Agreement
prevail over Déline law to the extent of any inconsistency between that
Act and Déline law or between the Sahtu Agreement and Déline law.

## [Déline Got'Ine Government]{.HTitleText1} {#h-171533 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity of natural person

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Déline Got'ine
Government is a legal entity and has the capacity, rights, powers and
privileges of a natural person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Force of law

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Déline law that is made
in accordance with the Déline Agreement has the force of law.

## [Application of Other Acts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-171538 .Part}

Meaning of [[Déline First Nation Citizen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[Déline First Nation Citizen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has
  the meaning assigned by the definition [[DFN
  Citizen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in chapter 1 of the Déline Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to 2.8 of the Déline Agreement, the [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5) does not apply to the Déline Got'ine Government or
  to Déline First Nation Citizens as of the day on which section 4 comes
  into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A Déline law is not a
statutory instrument for the purposes of the [Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

## [Taxation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-171546 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Déline Tax Treatment Agreement

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Déline Tax Treatment
Agreement is approved, given effect and declared valid and has the force
of law during the period that it is in effect.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Déline Tax Treatment
Agreement does not form part of the Déline Agreement and it is not a
treaty or a land claims agreement within the meaning of sections 25 and
35 of the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-171551 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice --- Agreements

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice must be taken of the
  Déline Agreement and the Déline Tax Treatment Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Déline Agreement and the Déline Tax Treatment
  Agreement must be published by the Queen's Printer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the Déline Agreement or the Déline Tax
  Treatment Agreement published by the Queen's Printer is evidence of
  that agreement and of its contents, and a copy purporting to be
  published by the Queen's Printer is presumed to be so published unless
  the contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice --- Déline law

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice must be taken of any
  Déline law that is registered in the public registry referred to in
  3.7 of the Déline Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of any Déline law purporting to be deposited
  in the public registry referred to in 3.7 of the Déline Agreement is
  evidence of that law and of its contents, unless the contrary is
  shown.

Meaning of [[institutions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[institutions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
  assigned by the definition [[Institutions of the
  DGG]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in chapter 1 of the Déline Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Déline Got'ine Government and its institutions
  are not federal boards, commissions or other tribunals as defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction  --- Supreme Court of
  Northwest Territories

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Supreme Court of the Northwest Territories has
  exclusive original jurisdiction to hear and determine applications for
  judicial review of the decisions of the Déline Got'ine Government or
  its institutions, except if an agreement made in accordance with
  22.2.2 of the Déline Agreement provides otherwise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of issues arising

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  issue arises in any judicial or administrative proceeding in respect
  of the interpretation, validity or applicability of the Déline
  Agreement, of this Act, of the Act of the legislature of the Northwest
  Territories that gives effect to the Déline Agreement or of any Déline
  law, then the issue must not be decided until the party raising the
  issue has served notice on the Attorney General of Canada, the
  Attorney General of the Northwest Territories and the Déline Got'ine
  Government.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content and timing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} describe the proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} state the subject matter of the issue;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state the day on which the issue is to be argued;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} give the particulars that are necessary to show the
    point to be argued; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be served at least 14 days before the day on which
    the issue is to be argued, unless the court or tribunal authorizes a
    shorter period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation in proceedings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Attorney General of Canada, the Attorney General
  of the Northwest Territories and the Déline Got'ine Government may
  appear and participate in any proceeding in respect of which
  subsection (1) applies as parties with the same rights as any other
  party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsections (2) and (3) do not
  require that an oral hearing be held if one is not otherwise required.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retroactive effect

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsection 4(1),
2.5.1 and 4.6 of the Déline Agreement, chapter 31 of that Agreement and
2.1, 2.2, 3.3, 5.1 and 5.2 of schedule C to that Agreement are deemed to
have effect as of October 1, 2013.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make any orders and regulations that are necessary for the purpose
of carrying out any of the provisions of the Déline Agreement or of any
other agreements that are related to the implementation of the Déline
Agreement.
