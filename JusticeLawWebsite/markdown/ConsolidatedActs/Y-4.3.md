## Yukon Surface Rights Board Act {#yukon-surface-rights-board-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1994, c. 43
:::

Assented to 1994-12-15

An Act to establish a board having jurisdiction concerning disputes
respecting surface rights in respect of land in the Yukon Territory and
to amend other Acts in relation thereto

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474871 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-474872 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Yukon Surface Rights Board
    established by section 8; ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[category A settlement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[category A settlement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in
    relation to a final agreement, land that has been

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} identified or declared pursuant to the final
      agreement, or designated pursuant to section 63, as category A
      settlement land, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identified in a self-government agreement as land
      to which the final agreement is to apply as if it were category A
      settlement land,

    and that has not ceased to be settlement land in accordance with the
    final agreement; ([terre désignée de catégorie A]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[category B settlement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[category B settlement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in
    relation to a final agreement, land that has been

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} identified or declared pursuant to the final
      agreement, or designated pursuant to section 63, as category B
      settlement land, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identified in a self-government agreement as land
      to which the final agreement is to apply as if it were category B
      settlement land,

    and that has not ceased to be settlement land in accordance with the
    final agreement; ([terre désignée de catégorie B]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[cost of construction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cost of construction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
    hydro-electric or water storage facility, means the aggregate of the
    following costs:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the costs of constructing civil structures,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the costs of reservoir and site clearing,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the costs of constructing access routes,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} electrical and mechanical costs,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} design costs, including the cost of
      socio-economic and environmental studies required for any
      application to construct or operate the facility,

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} engineering and construction management costs,
      and

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a hydro-electric facility, the
      costs of constructing transmission lines to a grid; ([coûts de
      construction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Council for Yukon Indians]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Council for Yukon Indians]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any
    successor to the Council for Yukon Indians and, in the absence of
    any successor, the first nations referred to in the definition
    [[Yukon first nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, whether or not their
    land claims agreement has been added to Part I of Schedule
    I; ([Conseil des Indiens du Yukon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Crown land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Crown land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means land vested in Her Majesty
    in right of Canada, whether the administration and control thereof
    is appropriated to the Commissioner of the Yukon Territory or not,
    but does not include settlement land; ([terre
    domaniale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[existing mineral right]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[existing mineral right]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mineral
    right, other than a right to locate a claim or an unrecorded right
    to explore for minerals, other than oil and gas, existing at the
    date the affected land became settlement land, and includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a renewal or replacement of such a mineral right
      after that date,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a licence, permit or other right in respect of
      oil or gas granted after that date as of right to a person holding
      such a mineral right, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a licence, permit or other right in respect of
      mines or minerals granted after that date pursuant to the [Yukon
      Quartz Mining Act](/eng/acts/Y-4) or the [Yukon Placer Mining
      Act](/eng/acts/Y-3) to a person holding such a mineral
      right; ([droit minier existant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fee simple settlement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fee simple settlement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in
    relation to a final agreement, land that has been

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} identified or declared pursuant to the final
      agreement, or designated pursuant to section 63, as fee simple
      settlement land, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identified in a self-government agreement as land
      to which the final agreement is to apply as if it were fee simple
      settlement land,

    and that has not ceased to be settlement land in accordance with the
    final agreement; ([terre désignée en fief simple]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[final agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[final agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement referred
    to in Part I of Schedule I, and includes any amendments made to it
    from time to time in accordance with its provisions; ([accord
    définitif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} parts of fish,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shellfish, crustaceans, marine animals, marine
      plants and parts of them, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the eggs, spawn, larvae, spat and juvenile stages
      of fish, shellfish, crustaceans and marine
      animals; ([poisson]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou [ressources
      halieutiques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[gas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means natural gas and includes all
    substances, other than oil, that are produced in association with
    natural gas; ([gaz]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Government of Canada or
    the Government of the Yukon Territory, or both, depending on which
    has responsibility for the matter in
    question; ([gouvernement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[harvesting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[harvesting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means gathering, hunting,
    trapping or fishing in accordance with a final agreement or the
    Transboundary Agreement; ([exploitation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[mineral right]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mineral right]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence, permit or
    other right to explore for, locate, develop, produce or transport
    minerals, other than specified substances, and to enter on land for
    those purposes; ([droit minier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[minerals]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minerals]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means precious and base metals and
    other non-living, naturally occurring substances, whether solid,
    liquid or gaseous, and includes coal, oil, gas and specified
    substances; ([minéraux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mines]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mines]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means mines, opened and
    unopened; ([mines]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Northern
    Affairs; ([ministre fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[navigable water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[navigable water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a stream, river, lake,
    sea or other body of water, used or capable of being used by the
    public for navigation by boats, kayaks, canoes, rafts or other small
    craft, or log booms on a continuous or seasonal basis, and includes
    any parts of those bodies of water interrupted by occasional natural

    lang="fr"})

[[new mineral right]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[new mineral right]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any mineral right,
    other than an existing mineral right; ([droit minier
    nouveau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-settlement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means crude oil, regardless of gravity,

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a natural person or an

[[self-government agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[settlement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[Territorial Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[traditional territory]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[Transboundary Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

      nation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Yukon Indian person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Yukon Indian person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person enrolled

### [Tetlit Gwich'in Yukon Land]{.HTitleText2} {#h-474944 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to Tetlit Gwich'in Yukon land

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this section, this Act applies within the primary use area
  described in Annex A of the Transboundary Agreement as if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the references to settlement land, category B
    settlement land, fee simple settlement land and undeveloped
    settlement land were references to Tetlit Gwich'in Yukon land
    described in Annex B of the Transboundary Agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the references to traditional territory were
    references to the primary use area;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the references to a Yukon first nation, other than
    the reference in the definition [[Council for Yukon
    Indians]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 and in paragraph
    (4)(b), were references to the Gwich'in Tribal Council; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the references to a Yukon Indian person were
    references to a Tetlit Gwich'in as defined in the Transboundary
    Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions not applicable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 31(1), sections 58 and 63 and paragraphs
  1(g) and 2(1)(f) of Schedule II do not apply in respect of Tetlit
  Gwich'in Yukon land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing of applications

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application in respect of Tetlit Gwich'in Yukon
  land shall be heard in Fort McPherson, Northwest Territories, unless
  the parties agree otherwise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation for expropriation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In making an order under section 55 in respect of the
  expropriation of Tetlit Gwich'in Yukon land for the construction,
  maintenance and operation of a hydro-electric or water storage
  facility, the Board shall not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} take into consideration the cultural or other
    special value to the Gwich'in Tribal Council of the land or any land
    to be provided as compensation for the expropriation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fix compensation for improvements to the Tetlit
    Gwich'in Yukon land in an amount that, together with all other
    compensation for improvements provided to Tetlit Gwich'in and Yukon
    first nations in respect of the facility whether pursuant to such an
    order or otherwise, would exceed three per cent of the cost of
    construction of the facility.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of land

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where land within the primary use area described in
  Annex A of the Transboundary Agreement is to be transferred, pursuant
  to an order of the Board or as a result of negotiation, to the
  Gwich'in Tribal Council as compensation for the expropriation of
  Tetlit Gwich'in Yukon land, the land shall, on transfer, become Tetlit
  Gwich'in Yukon land.

### [Inconsistencies]{.HTitleText2} {#h-474962 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency between final agreement and
Act

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  there is any inconsistency or conflict between a final agreement and
  this Act, the agreement prevails to the extent of the inconsistency or
  conflict.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency between Transboundary
  Agreement and Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where there is any inconsistency or conflict between
  the Transboundary Agreement and this Act, the Agreement prevails to
  the extent of the inconsistency or conflict.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency between order and Act or
document

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  there is any inconsistency or conflict between an order of the Board
  respecting access and a requirement imposed by or under any Act, or a
  licence, permit or other document made or issued under such an Act,
  that regulates the activity for which the access is obtained, the Act
  or document prevails to the extent of the inconsistency or conflict.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision under [Yukon Environmental and
  Socio-economic Assessment Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.2)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following prevail over an order of the Board
  respecting access, to the extent of any inconsistency or conflict
  between them:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a decision document issued under section 75, 76 or
    77 of the [Yukon Environmental and Socio-economic Assessment
    Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.2) by a federal agency that the federal agency is
    required to implement under subsection 82(2) of that Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a decision document that is issued under those
    sections by the territorial minister, to the extent that a
    territorial agency or municipal government is required to implement
    it under subsection 83(2) of that Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a decision document that is issued under those
    sections by a first nation, to the extent that the first nation is
    required to implement it under subsections 84(2) and (3) of that
    Act.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Yukon Surface Rights Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474984 .Part}

### [Establishment and Organization of Board]{.HTitleText2} {#h-474985 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a board, to be called the Yukon Surface Rights
  Board, consisting of a Chairperson and not less than two or more than
  ten other members to be appointed by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Odd number

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall make such appointments as are
  necessary to ensure that an odd number of members holds office at any
  time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Chairperson

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson shall be appointed on the recommendation of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of other members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Half of the members, other than the Chairperson,
  shall be appointed on the nomination of the Council for Yukon Indians.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Residency qualification

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  majority of the members appointed on the nomination of the Council for
  Yukon Indians and a majority of the other members must be resident in
  the Yukon Territory.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of ceasing to be resident

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines that a member ceases to be
  resident in the Yukon Territory and that the condition imposed by
  subsection (1) is not satisfied, the appointment of the member is
  terminated as of the date the member receives written notification
  from the Minister that the determination has been made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting after termination of appointment

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} If the appointment of a member is terminated under
  subsection (2) before they have made a decision in a matter for which
  a hearing is held, they may, with the consent of the parties to the
  hearing, continue to perform their duties and functions as a member
  only in relation to that matter until the hearing is concluded and a
  decision is made. For the purpose of the appointment of a replacement,
  their office is deemed to be vacant as soon as their appointment is
  terminated under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No disqualification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person is disqualified from being appointed or
  continuing as a member of the Board or of a panel of the Board by
  reason only of being a Yukon Indian person or having an interest in
  land in the Yukon Territory.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a member
  shall be appointed to hold office for a term of three years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of first members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The first members appointed on the nomination of the
  Council for Yukon Indians shall be appointed for such term, not
  exceeding three years, as the Council may specify and the other first
  members shall be appointed for such term, not exceeding three years,
  as the Minister may determine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting after expiry of term

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} If the term of a member expires before they have
  made a decision in a matter for which a hearing is held, they may, in
  accordance with the Board's by-laws or, in the absence of an
  applicable provision in the by-laws, the direction of the Chairperson,
  continue to perform their duties and functions as a member only in
  relation to that matter until the hearing is concluded and a decision
  is made. For the purpose of the appointment of a replacement, their
  office is deemed to be vacant as soon as their term expires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A member holds office during good behaviour, but may
  be removed by the Minister for cause or for a ground set out in the
  by-laws of the Board made pursuant to paragraph 18(a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancy

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If the office of a member
becomes vacant during the term of the member, the Minister may, on the
conditions under section 9 on which that member was appointed, appoint a
person to that office for the remainder of the term.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A member is eligible to

### [Applications and Hearings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-475103 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informal and expeditious

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** An application before the
Board shall be dealt with as informally and expeditiously as the
circumstances and considerations of fairness permit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General powers of Board

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The Board has, with
respect to the attendance and examination of witnesses, the production
and inspection of documents and all other matters necessary or proper in
relation to applications before the Board, all the powers, rights and
privileges of a superior court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parties to a hearing

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The following are parties
to a hearing before the Board:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant and any other person who may apply for
  an order in respect of which the hearing is held;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where settlement land is in issue, the Yukon first
  nation and any affected interest holder;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for an order under
  section 47, the Minister and Territorial Minister;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for an order under
  section 55 where Ministers are notified under paragraph 57(2)(a), the
  Ministers;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for an order under
  section 60, the Government for whose benefit the reservation was made;
  and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application under section 65 in
  relation to a mineral right granted under the [Yukon Placer Mining
  Act](/eng/acts/Y-3) or the [Yukon Quartz Mining Act](/eng/acts/Y-4),
  the mining recorder.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing in absence of party

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** The Board may not hear an
application in the absence of any party unless that party consents to
the holding of the hearing in their absence or notice of the hearing was
given to that party in accordance with the rules of the Board or, in the
absence of rules respecting the giving of such notice, in a manner
satisfactory to the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Location of hearing --- settlement land

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application in respect of settlement land shall be heard in the
  traditional territory of the Yukon first nation whose settlement land
  is involved, unless the parties agree otherwise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Location of hearing --- non-settlement
  land

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application in respect of non-settlement land
  shall be heard in the community in Canada closest to the land, whether
  within or outside the Yukon Territory, unless the parties agree
  otherwise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Panels

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application to the Board shall be heard and decided by a panel
  consisting of three members of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nomination by Council

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At least one member of any panel hearing an
  application in respect of settlement land must be a member nominated
  by the Council for Yukon Indians.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Single member

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsections (1) and (2), an
  application may, with the consent of the parties, be heard and decided
  by a panel consisting of one member of the Board, whether or not
  nominated by the Council for Yukon Indians.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assignment of members

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Members shall be assigned to panels
  in accordance with the by-laws of the Board or, in the absence of
  by-laws respecting the assignment of members to panels, by the
  Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member shall not be assigned to, or continue on, a
  panel if doing so would place the member in a material conflict of
  interest.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions of panel

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  panel has all of the powers, and shall perform all of the duties and
  functions, of the Board in relation to an application before the
  panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of order of panel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An order made by a panel is an order of the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of hearing

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a member of a panel dies, resigns or for any other reason is absent
  during all or part of a hearing, the remaining member or members may,
  with the consent of the parties, continue the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member of a panel who is not present during the
  entire hearing of an application may not participate in the
  disposition of the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information made available

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** Before disposing of an
application, the Board shall make any information that it intends to use
in the disposition available to the parties and provide them a
reasonable opportunity to respond to the information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References to Supreme Court

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** The Board may, at any
stage of its proceedings, refer any question or issue of law or of
jurisdiction, other than a question or issue that has been referred to
arbitration pursuant to a final agreement or the Transboundary
Agreement, to the Supreme Court of the Yukon Territory.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Settlement Lands]{.HTitleText1} {#h-475179 .Part}

### [Rights of Access Subject to Consent or Order]{.HTitleText2} {#h-475180 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access order

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), on
  application by a person who has a right of access described in section
  1 of Schedule II, the Board shall make an access order establishing
  terms and conditions for the exercise of the right.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall not make an access order in respect
  of a right of access described in any of paragraphs 1(a) to (d), (f),
  (g), (i) and (k) of Schedule II unless the applicant satisfies the
  Board that the applicant reasonably requires access and that access
  across Crown land is not practicable and reasonable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms and conditions

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may include in an access order made under subsection 42(1) in
  respect of a right of access

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} terms and conditions respecting any of the
    following matters, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the times when the right may be exercised,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the giving of notice,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} limitations on the location in which the right
      may be exercised and on routes of access,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} limitations on the number of persons exercising
      the right,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} limitations on the activities that may be carried
      on and on the equipment that may be used,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} subject to any regulations made under paragraph
      78(c), the giving of security in the form of a letter of credit,
      guarantee or indemnity bond or insurance or any other form
      satisfactory to the Board and the purposes for which the security
      is given,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the payment of the entry fee, in the amount
      established by the rules of the Board, to the Yukon first nation
      and, in the case of an interim access order, to any affected
      interest holder designated by the Board,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the payment of compensation for the exercise
      of the right and any damages resulting from that exercise to the
      Yukon first nation and, in the case of an interim access order, to
      any affected interest holder designated by the Board,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} abandonment and restoration work, and

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} the right of a Yukon first nation to verify, by
      inspection or otherwise, whether the other terms and conditions
      have been complied with; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other terms and conditions that the Board
    considers appropriate for reducing any conflict between the exercise
    of that right and the use and peaceful enjoyment of the settlement
    land by the Yukon first nation and, in the case of an interim access
    order, any affected interest holder designated by the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of route

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When making an access order that designates a route
  of access, the Board shall designate a route that is least harmful to
  the interests of the Yukon first nation while being reasonably
  suitable to the needs of the applicant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parameter of entry fee

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board may, in respect of an access order, provide
  for the payment of only one entry fee to each Yukon first nation whose
  settlement land is the subject of the order, whether or not the order
  is amended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of entry fee where interim
  access order

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Board provides for the payment of an entry fee
  under an interim access order,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the entry fee shall be allocated by the Board among
    the Yukon first nation and any affected interest holders designated
    by the Board; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no additional entry fee is payable on the issuance
    of an order finalizing the interim access order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction for Government and public
  utility access

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Board may not provide for the payment of the
  entry fee or compensation, other than compensation for significant
  damage, in respect of the exercise of a right of access described in
  paragraph 1(c) or (d) of Schedule II.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[significant
  damage]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (5), [[significant
  damage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include necessary alteration of
  settlement land or watercourses required to maintain transportation
  corridors referred to in paragraph 1(c) of Schedule II.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation factors

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In determining the amount of compensation, the Board
  may consider such factors as it considers appropriate and, without
  limiting the generality of the foregoing, shall consider

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the market value of the settlement land;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any loss of, or interference with, the use of the
    land and any loss of opportunity in connection therewith;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any impact on the fish and wildlife, and their
    habitat, on the land;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in all cases, any impact on fish and wildlife
    harvesting on the land and, in the case of Tetlit Gwich'in Yukon
    land described in Annex B of the Transboundary Agreement, on the
    gathering of plants on the land;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any nuisance, including that caused by noise, or
    inconvenience or any damage to the land that may be caused by the
    applicant in exercising the right of access;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any cultural or other special value of the land to
    the Yukon first nation;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any expenses associated with the implementation of
    the order, including monitoring and inspection expenses;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any impact on other settlement land of the Yukon
    first nation; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any compensation paid or payable to the same person
    by or pursuant to another authority for the exercise of the right of
    access or for any damages resulting from that exercise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation factors

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} decrease the amount of compensation on account of
    any reversionary interest remaining in the Yukon first nation or of
    any entry fee payable; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} increase the amount of compensation on account of
    any aboriginal claim, right, title or interest or of the value of
    mines and minerals on or under category B settlement land or fee
    simple settlement land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The Board may require compensation to be paid by one
  lump sum payment or by periodic payments of equal or different amounts
  and may require the payment of interest, at a rate to be determined in
  accordance with the regulations, on compensation payments made after
  the day on which they are required to be made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction for military manoeuvres

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding
subsection 43(1), terms and conditions that the Board may include in an
access order made under subsection 42(1) in respect of the exercise of a
right of access described in paragraph 1(e) of Schedule II shall be
restricted to contact persons, areas, timing, environmental protection,
protection of wildlife and habitat, land use rent and compensation for
damage caused to the settlement land and any improvements and personal
property on the land.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interim access order

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Pending the final determination of
  an application for an access order, the Board may make an interim
  access order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final access hearing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall, within thirty days after making an
  interim access order, commence a hearing to finally determine the
  matter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment before exercise of right

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** Subject to subsection
43(5) and section 44, no person may exercise the right of access under
an interim access order made under subsection 45(1) until the entry fee
and any compensation provided for by the interim access order is paid,
as allocated by the Board, to the Yukon first nation and any affected
interest holder designated by the Board.

### [Additional Terms for Right of Access Not Subject to Consent]{.HTitleText2} {#h-475239 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order for additional terms and conditions

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** On application made by a
Yukon first nation that does not reach an agreement with the Minister in
respect of terms and conditions for the exercise by any person, on the
settlement land of the Yukon first nation, of a right of access
described in section 2 of Schedule II that are additional to any

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} make an order establishing terms and conditions that
  specify seasons, times, locations, methods of access or manner of
  access; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} dismiss the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrictions

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  the Yukon first nation and the Minister agree otherwise, terms and
  conditions may not be established pursuant to an order made under
  section 47 for a purpose other than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} protecting the environment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} protecting fish or wildlife or their habitat;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} reducing conflicts between the exercise of that
    right and the traditional or cultural uses of settlement land by the
    Yukon first nation or a Yukon Indian person; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} protecting the use and peaceful enjoyment of land
    used for communities or residences.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrictions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No additional term or condition established pursuant
  to an order made under section 47 may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} restrict law enforcement or any inspection
    authorized by law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} impose a fee or charge for the exercise of that
    right; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} unreasonably restrict the exercise of that right.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of order

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall publish
each order made under section 47 in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) and in such other manner as, in its
opinion, will best bring it to the attention of those persons affected
by it.

### [Disputes respecting Rights of Access Not Subject to Consent]{.HTitleText2} {#h-475258 .Subheading}

right of access described in paragraph 2(1)(b) or (c) of Schedule II or
of a term or condition established by the Yukon first nation with the
agreement of the Minister, or by an order made under section 47, in

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disputes over waterfront right-of-way

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the camp or structure substantially alters, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is a reasonable alternate area for the

### [Specified Substances Disputes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-475268 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[specified substances
right]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[specified substances right]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
  the right of a Yukon first nation to take and use a specified
  substance on its settlement land, without payment of a royalty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflicts between specified substances
  right and mineral right

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a conflict between the exercise by a
  Yukon first nation of its specified substances right and the exercise
  of a mineral right by its holder, the Board shall, on application by
  the Yukon first nation or that holder, make an order specifying terms
  and conditions for exercising either right or both rights so as to
  reduce interference between them as far as practicable, but, to the
  extent that interference with the exercise of the specified substances
  right cannot be avoided, the Board shall give priority to the exercise
  of the mineral right.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall include in an order under
  subsection (2) that gives priority to the exercise of a new mineral
  right a provision requiring the person who has that right to pay
  compensation to the Yukon first nation for any interference with, or
  loss of opportunity for, the exercise of the specified substances
  right, and, in determining the compensation for loss of opportunity,
  the Board shall take into account the production costs incurred by the
  person who has the new mineral right.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  determining the amount of compensation, the Board may consider such

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any loss of, or interference with, the use of the

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any expenses associated with the implementation of

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any impact on other settlement land of the Yukon

    mines and minerals on or under category B settlement land or fee

  is available.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board may require compensation in the form of
  money to be paid by one lump sum payment or by periodic payments of
  equal or different amounts and may require the payment of interest, at
  a rate to be determined in accordance with the regulations, on
  compensation payments made after the day on which they are required to
  be made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Land compensation

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  Yukon first nation requests land as all or part of the compensation,
  the Board shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine whether the authority holds the land
    identified by the Yukon first nation and, if so, whether the land is
    within the traditional territory of the Yukon first nation and is
    available;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine the value of the land that is available;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} order the authority to transfer to the Yukon first
    nation the amount of the available land that is necessary to provide
    the compensation in land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crown land

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the authority is not the Government and the Board
  determines that sufficient land is not available for the purposes of
  subsection (1), the Board shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the Minister and Territorial Minister to
    that effect;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine whether either Government holds land
    contiguous to the settlement land concerned and, if so, whether the
    land is within the traditional territory of the Yukon first nation
    and is available;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} determine the value of the land that is available;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} order the Government to transfer to the Yukon first
    nation the amount of the available land that, together with any land
    transferred pursuant to an order under paragraph (1)(c), is
    necessary to provide the compensation in land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Balance of compensation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the land transferred pursuant to orders under
  paragraphs (1)(c) and (2)(d) is not sufficient to provide the
  compensation in land, the Board shall order the authority to provide
  the balance of the compensation in any other form or combination of
  forms.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In determining the value of land that is to be
  transferred to the Yukon first nation, the Board may consider such

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the market value of the land;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the value of fish and wildlife harvesting and of
    the gathering of plants on the land to the Yukon first nation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any potential effect of the transfer of the land on
    settlement land of the Yukon first nation; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any cultural or other special value of the land to
    the Yukon first nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When land not available

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, land is not
  available if it is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} land that is subject to a lease or an agreement for
    sale, unless the Government and the holder of the interest consent
    to the land being available;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} land that is determined by the Board to be occupied
    or used, or required for future use, by the authority, any
    department or agency of a Government or any municipal government,
    unless the authority, department, agency or government consents to
    the land being available;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} land that is within thirty metres of the boundary
    line between the Yukon Territory and Alaska, British Columbia or the
    Northwest Territories;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} land the provision of which to a Yukon first nation
    would, in the determination of the Board, unreasonably limit the
    expansion of any community or the access by any person to navigable
    waters or highways;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a highway or highway right-of-way; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other land that is determined by the Board not
    to be available.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** The costs relating to an
application to or a hearing before the Board that are incurred by the
parties are in the discretion of the Board and the Board may, by order,
award such costs on or before the final disposition of the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons for decisions

**[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall give
written reasons for every decision that it makes in relation to an
application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies

**[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** As soon as practicable
after making a decision in relation to an application, the Board shall
give copies of the decision and the reasons for it to the parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of orders

**[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** A document purporting to
be an order of the Board, or to be certified by the Chairperson of the
Board or any other person authorized by the by-laws as a true copy of
such an order, is evidence of the making of the order and of its
contents, without proof of the signature or official character of the
person appearing to have signed the order or certified the copy.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order binding on successor

**[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** An order of the Board is
binding on and the rights thereunder extend to any person who
subsequently acquires the ownership of or other interest or right in the
land to which the order relates and, in the case of an access order, the
right of access and the right for which the right of access was
acquired.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement of orders

**[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** An order of the Board may
be made an order of the Supreme Court of the Yukon Territory by filing a
certified copy of the order with the registrar of the Court and, when so
made, the order is enforceable in the same manner as an order of that
Court.

### [Review of Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-475449 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Findings of fact

**[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 75 to
77, a determination of the Board on any question of fact within its
jurisdiction is final and binding.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review by Board

- **[[75]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-75
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the Board
  may, on application made by any person who was a party to the hearing
  held in respect of the order or any successor to such a party referred
  to in section 72, review any of its orders, including an order made
  under this section, where it appears that there has been a material
  change in the facts or circumstances relating to the order and shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where it determines that there has been a material
    change in the facts or circumstances relating to the order that
    would justify the amendment applied for,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the effects on the settlement land or on the
      Yukon first nation that owns the land that would be caused as a
      result of the amendment are significantly detrimental, revoke that
      order and make a new order accordingly, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, amend the order accordingly;
      or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, dismiss the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may not review under this section an order
  made under section 55, 60, 63 or 65.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for judicial review

- **[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding section 18 of the
  [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7), the Federal Court has concurrent
  original jurisdiction in respect of relief referred to in paragraph
  18(1)(b) of that Act, and the Attorney General of Canada, the
  Territorial Minister or anyone directly affected by the matter in
  respect of which relief is sought may make an application for judicial
  review to the Supreme Court of the Yukon Territory for any relief that
  the applicant could otherwise obtain in respect of the Board by way of
  an application for an order of or in the nature of
  [mandamus]{.otherLang lang="la"}, prohibition or
  [certiorari]{.otherLang lang="la"} or by way of an action for a
  declaration or an injunction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for judicial review in respect of a
  decision or order of the Board must be made within thirty days after
  the day on which the decision or order was first communicated to the
  office of the Deputy Attorney General of Canada, the Territorial
  Minister or the party directly affected by the order, or within such
  further period as a judge of the Supreme Court may, either before or
  after the end of those thirty days, fix.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Court

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On an application for judicial review, the Supreme
  Court may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} order the Board to do any act or thing that it has
    unlawfully failed or refused to do or has unreasonably delayed in
    doing; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prohibit, restrain, declare invalid or unlawful,
    quash, set aside or set aside and refer back for determination in
    accordance with such directions as it considers appropriate, any
    decision, order, act or proceeding of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grounds for review

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Supreme Court may grant relief if it is satisfied
  that the Board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} acted without jurisdiction, acted beyond its
    jurisdiction or refused to exercise its jurisdiction;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} failed to observe a principle of natural justice,
    procedural fairness or other procedure that it was required by law
    to observe;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} erred in law in making a decision or order, whether
    or not the error appears on the face of the record;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} based its decision or order on an erroneous finding
    of fact that it made in a perverse or capricious manner or without
    regard for the material before it;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} acted, or failed to act, by reason of fraud or
    perjured evidence; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} acted in any other way that was contrary to law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Technical irregularity

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Supreme Court may refuse any application for
  judicial review founded solely on a defect in form or other technical
  irregularity if it finds that no substantial wrong or miscarriage of
  justice has occurred and, if the technical irregularity was in a
  decision or order, it may make an order validating the decision or
  order, effective from such time and on such terms as it considers
  appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interim orders

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} On an application for judicial review, the Supreme
  Court may make such interim orders as it considers appropriate pending
  final disposition of the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Constitutional questions

  to be a party to the proceedings for the purposes of any appeal in
  respect of the question.

### Rights of Access Subject to Consent or Order {#rights-of-access-subject-to-consent-or-order .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} The rights subject to the consent of the Yukon first
  nation concerned are the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the right of any person to use a waterfront
    right-of-way on settlement land for commercial recreational
    purposes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the right of any person, other than a person having
    a right described in paragraph 2(1)(b), to enter, cross and make
    necessary stops on undeveloped settlement land in order to reach
    adjacent land for commercial or non-commercial purposes;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the right of a Government or its agents or
    contractors to enter, cross and stay on undeveloped settlement land
    for a period of more than one hundred and twenty consecutive days,
    and to use natural resources incidental to that access, in order to
    deliver, manage or maintain Government programs or projects,
    including, without limiting the generality of the foregoing, the
    right to make necessary alterations of land or watercourses by
    earthmoving equipment for routine or emergency maintenance of
    transportation corridors;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the right of any person authorized by law to
    provide utilities for public purposes, including electricity,
    telecommunications and municipal services, to enter, cross and stay
    on undeveloped settlement land for a period of more than one hundred
    and twenty consecutive days in order to carry out site
    investigations, assessments, surveys and studies in relation to
    proposed services;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the right of the Department of National Defence to
    conduct military manoeuvres on undeveloped settlement land;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the right of any person

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} having a right, under and in accordance with laws
      of general application, to use water incidental to the exercise of
      a right or interest of that person in settlement land, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} holding a licence under the [Yukon Waters
        Act](/eng/acts/Y-4.6) or the [Dominion Water Power
        Act](/eng/acts/W-4) for water on or flowing through settlement
        land, which licence existed when the land became settlement
        land,

    to use that settlement land in order to exercise that right or
    licence;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the right of the holder of a commercial timber
    permit or a timber harvesting agreement to cross and make necessary
    stops on settlement land in order to reach adjacent land or to reach
    settlement land subject to that permit or agreement;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the right of any person having an existing mineral
    right on settlement land, other than a person having a right
    described in paragraph 2(1)(g), to cross and make necessary stops on
    settlement land for the purpose of exercising that existing mineral
    right;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the right of any person having an existing mineral
    right on non-settlement land, other than a person having a right
    described in paragraph 2(1)(g), to cross and make necessary stops on
    settlement land for the purpose of exercising that existing mineral
    right;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the right of any person having a new mineral right
    on category B settlement land or fee simple settlement land, other
    than a person having a right described in paragraph 2(1)(h) or (i),
    to use, cross and make necessary stops on settlement land for the
    purpose of exercising that new mineral right;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the right of any person having a new mineral right
    on non-settlement land, other than a person having a right described
    in paragraph 2(1)(h), to cross and make necessary stops on
    settlement land for the purpose of exercising that new mineral
    right; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the right of a Government, in relation to a
    right-of-way on a parcel of settlement land that is identified in
    Appendix A to a final agreement as a right-of-way to which a
    Specified Access Right applies, to significantly alter that parcel
    of settlement land for the purpose of maintaining the right-of-way.

### Rights of Access Not Subject to Consent {#rights-of-access-not-subject-to-consent .SchedHeadL2}

- [2 (1)]{.lawlabel} The rights not subject to the consent of the Yukon
  first nation concerned are the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the right of any person to use a waterfront
    right-of-way on settlement land for travel or non-commercial
    recreation, including camping and sport fishing, and to use standing
    dead or deadfall firewood incidental to that use;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the right of any person to enter, cross and make
    necessary stops on undeveloped settlement land in order to reach
    adjacent non-settlement land for commercial or non-commercial
    purposes;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the right of any person to enter, cross and stay on
    undeveloped settlement land for a reasonable period for
    non-commercial recreational purposes;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} except where a final agreement requires, in
    relation to a parcel of settlement land, the consent of the Yukon
    first nation concerned, the right of a Government or its agents or
    contractors to enter, cross and stay on undeveloped settlement land
    for a period of not more than one hundred and twenty consecutive
    days, and to use natural resources incidental to that access, in
    order to deliver, manage or maintain Government programs or
    projects, including, without limiting the generality of the
    foregoing, the right to make necessary alterations of land or
    watercourses by earthmoving equipment for routine or emergency
    maintenance of transportation corridors;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the right of any person authorized by law to
    provide utilities for public purposes, including electricity,
    telecommunications and municipal services, to enter, cross and stay
    on undeveloped settlement land for a period of not more than one
    hundred and twenty consecutive days in order to carry out site
    investigations, assessments, surveys and studies in relation to
    proposed services, if there has been prior consultation with the
    Yukon first nation;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the right of the holder of a trapline on settlement
    land, other than a Yukon Indian person, to construct and occupy on
    settlement land such cabins as are necessary for the reasonable use
    and enjoyment of the trapline and to cut necessary trails on the
    trapline;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the right of any person having an existing mineral
    right on settlement land or non-settlement land to cross and make
    necessary stops on settlement land for the purpose of exercising
    that existing mineral right;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the right of any person having a new mineral right
    on category B settlement land or fee simple settlement land or on
    non-settlement land to cross and make necessary stops on settlement
    land for the purpose of exercising that new mineral right; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the right of any person having a new mineral right
    on category B settlement land or fee simple settlement land to use
    the land in a manner that does not require the use of heavy
    equipment or methods more disruptive or damaging to the land than
    hand labour methods, for the purpose of exercising that new mineral
    right.

  <!-- -->

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} A right described in paragraph (1)(a) or (c) is
    subject to the conditions that there will be no

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} significant damage to settlement land or to
      improvements on settlement land;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} mischief committed on settlement land; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} significant interference with the use and
      peaceful enjoyment of settlement land by the Yukon first nation.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} A right described in paragraph (1)(b) is subject to
    the conditions set out in subsection (2) and to the condition that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the access is of a casual and insignificant
      nature; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the route used is generally recognized and was
      being used on a regular basis, whether year round or
      intermittently,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} prior to public notification of the selection
        of the settlement land for the final agreement of the Yukon
        first nation concerned, or prior to June 8, 1992 in the case of
        Tetlit Gwich'in Yukon lands, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the land became settlement land after
        the date that agreement takes effect, on the date the land
        became settlement land,

      and the exercise of the right does not result in a significant
      alteration of that route.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} A right described in paragraph (1)(d) or (e) is
    subject to the conditions that there will be no

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} mischief committed on settlement land; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} unnecessary interference with the use and
      peaceful enjoyment of settlement land by the Yukon first nation.

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} A right described in paragraph (1)(g) or (h) is
    subject to the conditions set out in subsections (2) and (3) and to
    the condition that there will be no permanent structure erected on
    settlement land.

  - [(6)]{.lawlabel} A right described in paragraph (1)(i) is subject to
    the conditions that there will be no

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} unnecessary damage to settlement land or
      significant damage to improvements on settlement land;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} mischief committed on settlement land; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} unnecessary interference with the use and
      peaceful enjoyment of settlement land by the Yukon first nation.
