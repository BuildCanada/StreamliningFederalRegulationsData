## [Establishment and Constitution of Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-74785 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission established

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  established a commission, to be known as the Canadian Radio-television
  and Telecommunications Commission, consisting of not more than 13
  members, to be appointed by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member shall be appointed to hold office during
  good behaviour for a term not exceeding five years but may be removed
  at any time by the Governor in Council for cause.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 5, a member is eligible for
  re-appointment.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-22, s. 3
- 1991, c. 11, s. 76
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1701
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of members

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A member shall devote the
whole of his or her time to the performance of his or her duties under
this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-22, s. 4
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1702
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-4-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disqualifications

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  is not eligible to be appointed or to continue as a member of the
  Commission if the person is not a Canadian citizen ordinarily resident
  in Canada or if, directly or indirectly, as owner, shareholder,
  director, officer, partner or otherwise, the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is engaged in a telecommunications undertaking; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has any pecuniary or proprietary interest in

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a telecommunications undertaking, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the manufacture or distribution of
      telecommunication apparatus, except where the distribution is
      incidental to the general merchandising of goods by wholesale or
      retail.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal of interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member in whom any interest prohibited by
  subsection (1) vests by will or succession for the member's own
  benefit shall, within three months thereafter, absolutely dispose of
  that interest.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 49, s. 5
:::

## [Chairperson and Vice-chairpersons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-74810 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson and Vice-Chairpersons

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall designate one of the members to be
  Chairperson of the Commission and two of the members to be
  Vice-Chairpersons of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson chief executive officer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson is the chief executive officer of the
  Commission, has supervision over and direction of the work and staff
  of the Commission and shall preside at meetings of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson's absence, incapacity or
  office vacant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  Chairperson or if the office of Chairperson is vacant, the Commission
  may authorize one of the Vice-Chairpersons to exercise the powers and
  to perform the duties and functions of the Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting Chairperson

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may authorize one or more of its
  members to act as Chairperson if the Chairperson and both
  Vice-Chairpersons are absent or unable to act or if the office of
  Chairperson and each office of Vice-Chairperson are vacant.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-22, s. 6
- 2001, c. 34, s. 31(E)
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1703

## [Remuneration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-74822 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salaries and fees

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  member shall be paid a salary to be fixed by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each member is entitled to be paid such travel and
  living expenses incurred in the performance of his duties as are fixed
  by by-law of the Commission.

::: HistoricalNote

## [Staff]{.HTitleText1} {#h-74830 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The officers and employees
necessary for the proper conduct of the Commission's business shall be
appointed in accordance with the [Public Service Employment

## [Superannuation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-74835 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members' superannuation

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  members of the Commission are deemed to be persons employed in the
  public service for the purposes of the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of any regulations made under
  section 9 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), the members of the
  Commission are deemed to be persons employed in the federal public
  administration.

::: HistoricalNote

## [Offices, Meetings and Residence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-74843 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  head office of the Commission shall be in the National Capital Region
  described in the schedule to the [National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4)
  or at such other place within Canada as may be designated by the
  Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regional offices

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may direct the Commission
  to establish an office of the Commission in any region of Canada and
  the Commission shall comply with any such direction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall meet at least six times in each
  year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A majority of the members in office constitute a
  quorum of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Telephone conferences

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A member may, subject to the by-laws of the
  Commission, participate in a meeting of the Commission or a committee
  of the Commission by means of such telephone or other communication
  facilities as permit all persons participating in the meeting to hear
  each other, and a member who participates in such a meeting by those
  means is deemed for the purposes of this Act to be present at the
  meeting.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-22, s. 10
- 1991, c. 11, s. 78
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1706

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Residence of members

- **[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  members of the Commission shall reside in the National Capital Region
  as described in the schedule to the [National Capital
  Act](/eng/acts/N-4) or within any distance of it that may be
  determined by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Residence of members --- regional
  office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a regional office of the Commission is established
  under subsection 10(1.1), a member of the Commission who is designated
  for that region by the Governor in Council shall reside in that region
  and within any distance of that regional office that may be determined
  by the Governor in Council.

## [By-laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-74864 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws of Commission

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may make by-laws

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the calling of meetings of the
    Commission;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the conduct of business at meetings of
    the Commission, the establishment of special and standing committees
    of the Commission, the delegation of duties to those committees and
    the fixing of quorums for meetings thereof; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fixing the travel and living expenses to be paid to
    members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-law subject to Minister's approval

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No by-law made under paragraph (1)(c) has any effect
  unless it has been approved by the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote

- 2001, c. 34, s. 31(E)
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1708

## [Objects, Powers, Duties and Functions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-74875 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}In relation to broadcasting

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  objects and powers of the Commission in relation to broadcasting are
  as set out in the [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Digital news

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The Commission exercises the powers and performs
  the duties and functions conferred on it under the [Online News
  Act](/eng/acts/O-9.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Telecommunications

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The full-time members of the Commission and the
  Chairperson shall exercise the powers and perform the duties vested in
  the Commission and the Chairperson, respectively, by the
  [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4) or any special Act, as
  defined in subsection 2(1) of that Act, or by [An Act to promote the
  efficiency and adaptability of the Canadian economy by regulating
  certain activities that discourage reliance on electronic means of
  carrying out commercial activities, and to amend the Canadian
  Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission Act, the
  Competition Act, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic
  Documents Act and the Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/E-1.6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Commission may make by-laws

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the establishment of special and
    standing committees of the members, the delegation of the powers,
    duties and functions of the members to those committees and the
    fixing of quorums for meetings of those committees; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} providing that any act or thing done by those
    committees in the exercise of the powers or the performance of the
    duties and functions delegated to it are deemed to be an act or
    thing done by the members.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-22, s. 12

- 2010, c. 12, s. 1709, c. 23, s. 69

  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1413299-1413724.html"
  role="button"}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission shall, within three months after the end of each fiscal
  year, submit to the Minister a report, in such form as the Minister
  may direct, on the activities of the Commission for that fiscal year,
  and the Minister shall cause a copy of the report to be laid before
  each House of Parliament on any of the first fifteen days on which
  that House is sitting after the Minister receives it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Online News Act](/eng/acts/O-9.3)

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The report must include the contents of the annual
  auditor's report prepared under section 86 of the [Online News
  Act](/eng/acts/O-9.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must include information about the
  following in respect of the fiscal year, including their number:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} inquiries conducted under subsection 12(1) of the
    [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01) in relation to the
    identification, prevention and removal of barriers;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inquiries conducted under that subsection in
    relation to sections 42 to 44 of the [Accessible Canada
    Act](/eng/acts/A-0.6);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} orders made under subsection 12(2) of the
    [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01) in relation to the
    identification, prevention and removal of barriers;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} orders made under that subsection in relation to
    sections 42 to 44 of the [Accessible Canada Act](/eng/acts/A-0.6);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} notices of violation issued under section 34.8 of
    the [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01) in relation to
    contraventions of a regulation or order made under Part II of that
    Act in relation to the identification, prevention and removal of
    barriers; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} notices of violation issued under section 34.8 of
    the [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01) in relation to
    contraventions of any of subsections 42(1) to (4) and (7), 43(1)
    to (3) and 44(1) to (3) and (6) of the [Accessible Canada
    Act](/eng/acts/A-0.6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Telecommunications
  Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The report must include information about the
  following in respect of the fiscal year, including their number:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} inspections conducted under section 71 of the
    [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4) in relation to compliance
    with decisions made under that Act in relation to the
    identification, prevention and removal of barriers;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inspections conducted under that section in
    relation to compliance with sections 51 to 53 of the [Accessible
    Canada Act](/eng/acts/A-0.6);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} orders made under section 51 of the
    [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4) in relation to the
    identification, prevention and removal of barriers;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} orders made under that section in relation to
    sections 51 to 53 of the [Accessible Canada Act](/eng/acts/A-0.6);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} notices of violation issued under section 72.005 of
    the [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4) in relation to
    contraventions of decisions made under that Act in relation to the
    identification, prevention and removal of barriers;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} notices of violation issued under that section in
    relation to contraventions of any of subsections 51(1) to (4) and
    (7), 52(1) to (3) and 53(1) to (3) and (6) of the [Accessible Canada
    Act](/eng/acts/A-0.6);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} inquiries conducted under subsection 48(1) of the
    [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4) in relation to the
    identification, prevention and removal of barriers; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} inquiries conducted under subsection 48(1.1) of
    that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Observations and prescribed information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The report must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} observations about whether the information referred
    to in subsections (2) and (3) discloses any systemic or emerging
    issues related to the identification and removal of barriers, and
    the prevention of new barriers; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any information respecting the identification,
    prevention and removal of barriers that is prescribed by regulations
    made under subsection (5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make regulations for the
  purposes of paragraph (4)(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[barrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[barrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has
  the same meaning as in section 2 of the [Accessible Canada
  Act](/eng/acts/A-0.6).
