## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451802 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Transportation Appeal Tribunal of Canada Act](/eng/acts/T-18.5).

## [Transportation Appeal Tribunal of Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451805 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a tribunal to be known as the Transportation Appeal
  Tribunal of Canada ("the Tribunal").

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction generally

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal has jurisdiction in respect of reviews
  and appeals as expressly provided for under the [Aeronautics
  Act](/eng/acts/A-2), the [Pilotage Act](/eng/acts/P-14), the [Railway
  Safety Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2), the [Marine Transportation Security
  Act](/eng/acts/M-0.8), the [Canada Shipping Act,
  2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15), the [Wrecked, Abandoned or Hazardous Vessels
  Act](/eng/acts/W-12.3) and any other federal Act regarding
  transportation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction in respect of other Acts

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal also has jurisdiction in respect of
  reviews and appeals in connection with administrative monetary
  penalties provided for under sections 177 to 181 of the [Canada
  Transportation Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4), sections 43 to 55 of the
  [International Bridges and Tunnels Act](/eng/acts/I-17.05), sections
  129.01 to 129.19 of the [Canada Marine Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7), sections
  16.1 to 16.25 of the [Motor Vehicle Safety Act](/eng/acts/M-10.01),
  sections 39.1 to 39.26 of the [Canadian Navigable Waters
  Act](/eng/acts/N-22) and sections 130.01 to 130.19 of the [Marine
  Liability Act](/eng/acts/M-0.7).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall appoint as members of the Tribunal persons
  who, in the opinion of the Governor in Council, collectively have
  expertise in the transportation sectors in respect of which the
  federal government has jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Full- or part-time members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Members may be appointed as full-time or part-time
  members.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
shall designate one member as Chairperson of the Tribunal and one member
as Vice-Chairperson. The Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson must be
full-time members.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of Chairperson

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson has supervision over, and direction of, the work of the
  Tribunal, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the apportionment of work among members and the
    assignment of members to hear matters brought before the Tribunal
    and, when the Tribunal sits in panels, the assignment of members to
    panels and to preside over panels; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} generally, the conduct of the work of the Tribunal
    and the management of its internal affairs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence of Chairperson

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  Chairperson or if the office of Chairperson is vacant, the
  Vice-Chairperson shall act as Chairperson during the continuance of
  that absence or incapacity or until a new Chairperson is designated.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member
  shall be appointed to hold office during good behaviour for a term not
  exceeding seven years and may be removed for cause by the Governor in
  Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member is eligible to be reappointed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition after member ceases to hold
  office

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} At the request of the Chairperson, a former member,
  within eight weeks after ceasing to be a member, may make or take part
  in a determination or decision on a matter that they heard as a
  member. For that purpose, the former member is deemed to be a member.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Members
  shall receive the remuneration that is fixed by the Governor in
  Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each member is entitled to be paid reasonable travel
  and living expenses incurred while absent in the course of their
  duties from, in the case of a full-time member, their ordinary place
  of work and, in the case of a part-time member, their ordinary place
  of residence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Members are deemed to be employed in the federal
  public administration for the purposes of the [Government Employees
  Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and any regulations made under
  section 9 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistent interests --- full-time
members

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Full-time members shall not accept
  or hold any office, membership, employment or interest, or engage in
  any business activity, that is inconsistent with the proper
  performance of their duties and functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Divesting of interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an interest that is prohibited under
  subsection (1) vests, by whatever means, in a full-time member, the
  member shall disclose the interest to the Chairperson without delay
  and, within three months after the interest vests, either divest
  himself or herself of the interest or resign as a member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of full-time members

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Full-time members shall devote the whole of their
  time to the performance of their duties and functions under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistent interests --- part-time
  members

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a part-time member who is assigned to hear or is
  hearing any matter before the Tribunal, either alone or as a member of
  a panel, holds any pecuniary or other interest that could be
  inconsistent with the proper performance of their duties and functions
  in relation to the matter, the member shall disclose the interest to
  the Chairperson without delay and is ineligible to hear, or to
  continue to hear, the matter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principal office

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The principal office of the
Tribunal shall be in the National Capital Region described in the
schedule to the [National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sittings

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal shall sit at
those times and places in Canada that the Chairperson considers
necessary for the proper performance of its functions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearings on review

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A review shall be heard
by a member, sitting alone, who has expertise in the transportation
sector to which the review relates. However, a review that concerns a
matter of a medical nature shall be heard by a member with medical
expertise, whether or not that member has expertise in the
transportation sector to which the review relates.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearings on appeal

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), an appeal
  to the Tribunal shall be heard by an appeal panel consisting of three
  members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Size of panel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson may, if he or she considers it
  appropriate, direct that an appeal be heard by an appeal panel
  consisting of more than three members or, with the consent of the
  parties to the appeal, of one member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition of panel

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A member who conducts a review may not sit on an
  appeal panel that is established to hear an appeal from his or her
  determination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications of members

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} With the exception of the Chairperson and
  Vice-Chairperson, who may sit on any appeal panel, an appeal shall be
  heard by an appeal panel consisting of members who have expertise in
  the transportation sector to which the appeal relates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Medical matters

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (4), in an appeal that concerns a
  matter of a medical nature, at least one member of the appeal panel
  shall have medical expertise, whether or not that member has expertise
  in the transportation sector to which the appeal relates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision of panel

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A decision of a majority of the members of an appeal
  panel is a decision of the panel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nature of appeal

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** An appeal shall be on the
merits based on the record of the proceedings before the member from
whose determination the appeal is taken, but the appeal panel shall
allow oral argument and, if it considers it necessary for the purposes
of the appeal, shall hear evidence not previously available.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nature of hearings

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  Tribunal is not bound by any legal or technical rules of evidence in
  conducting any matter that comes before it, and all such matters shall
  be dealt with by it as informally and expeditiously as the
  circumstances and considerations of fairness and natural justice
  permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal shall not receive or accept as evidence
  anything that would be inadmissible in a court by reason of any
  privilege under the law of evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appearance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party to a proceeding before the Tribunal may
  appear in person or be represented by another person, including legal
  counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Private hearings

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Hearings shall be held in public. However, the
  Tribunal may hold all or any part of a hearing in private if it is of
  the opinion that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a public hearing would not be in the public
    interest;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} medical information about a person may be disclosed
    and the desirability of ensuring that, in the interests of that
    person, the information is not publicly disclosed outweighs the
    desirability of adhering to the principle that hearings be open to
    the public; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} confidential business information may be disclosed
    and the desirability of ensuring that the information is not
    publicly disclosed outweighs the desirability of adhering to the
    principle that hearings be open to the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standard of proof

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In any proceeding before the Tribunal, a party that
  has the burden of proof discharges it by proof on the balance of
  probabilities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Tribunal

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal, and each of
its members, has all the powers of a commissioner under Part I of the
[Inquiries Act](/eng/acts/I-11).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A member who conducts a
review shall provide a determination, and an appeal panel shall provide
a decision, with reasons, in writing to all parties to a proceeding.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules of Tribunal

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may, with
the approval of the Governor in Council, make rules that are not
inconsistent with this Act or any Act referred to in section 2 to govern
the management of its affairs and the practice and procedure in
connection with matters brought before it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may award any costs, and may require the reimbursement of any
  expenses incurred in connection with a hearing, that it considers
  reasonable if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is seized of the matter for reasons that are
    frivolous or vexatious;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a party that files a request for a review or an
    appeal and does not appear at the hearing does not establish that
    there was sufficient reason to justify their absence; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a party that is granted an adjournment of the
    hearing requested the adjournment without adequate notice to the
    Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Costs awarded to the Minister of Transport, and
  expenses of that Minister or the Tribunal that are subject to
  reimbursement, under subsection (1) are a debt due to Her Majesty in
  right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Costs or expenses under subsection (1) that have not
  been paid may be certified by the Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration of certificate

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} On production to the Federal Court, a certificate
  shall be registered. When it is registered, a certificate has the same
  force and effect as if it were a judgment obtained in the Federal
  Court for a debt of the amount specified in it and all reasonable
  costs and charges attendant on its registration, recoverable in that
  Court or in any other court of competent jurisdiction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings to be recorded

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Proceedings before the
Tribunal shall be recorded, and the record shall show all evidence taken
and all determinations, decisions and findings made in respect of the
proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision on appeal final

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A decision of an appeal
panel of the Tribunal is final and binding on the parties to the appeal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal shall, not
later than June 30 in each fiscal year, submit to Parliament, through
the member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada who is designated by
the Governor in Council as the Minister for the purposes of this
section, a report of its activities during the preceding fiscal year,
and that Minister shall cause the report to be laid before each House of
Parliament on any of the first 15 days on which that House is sitting
after the Minister receives it.
