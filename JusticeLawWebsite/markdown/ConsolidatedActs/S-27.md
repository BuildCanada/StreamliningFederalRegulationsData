## Surplus Crown Assets Act {#surplus-crown-assets-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. S-27
:::

An Act respecting surplus Crown assets
::::

::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-443841 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Surplus Crown Assets Act](/eng/acts/S-27).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. S-20, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-443846 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[accepted surplus Crown assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[accepted surplus Crown assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of
    a department or federal body, means surplus Crown assets identified
    in a notice sent to the department or federal body under subsection
    4(2) that have not been deleted from the notice with the authority
    of the Minister or disposed of pursuant to this Act; ([biens
    désignés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, R.S., 1985, c. 22 (1st
    Supp.), s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, R.S., 1985, c. 22
    (1st Supp.), s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as the
    definition of that term, other than paragraph (c) thereof, in
    section 2 of the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11); ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[federal body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a board, commission,
    corporation or other body that is an agent of Her Majesty or is
    ultimately accountable to Parliament for the conduct of its affairs
    including, notwithstanding Part X of the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11), a Crown corporation as defined in subsection
    83(1) of that Act, but does not include a department; ([organisme
    fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[government department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[government department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 1992, c.
    54, s. 82\]]{.Repealed}

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Public Works
    and Government Services; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[surplus Crown assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[surplus Crown assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
    department or federal body, means property of Her Majesty in its
    custody or under its control that the department or federal body has
    determined is surplus to its requirements. ([biens de surplus de la
    Couronne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-27, s. 2
- R.S., 1985, c. 22 (1st Supp.), s. 1, c. 35 (4th Supp.), s. 13
- 1992, c. 54, s. 82
- 1996, c. 16, s. 60
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** This Act does not apply
in respect of real property or immovables as defined in the [Federal
Real Property and Federal Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4) or licences
in respect thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1991, c. 50, s. 42
- 2001, c. 4, s. 170
:::

## [Surplus Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-443863 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Department dealing with surplus Crown
assets

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  department that has surplus Crown assets may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} request the Minister to dispose of or deal with the
    assets under this Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to such terms and conditions as the
    Treasury Board may prescribe, sell, exchange, transfer to another
    department, lease, lend or otherwise dispose of or deal with the
    assets, either gratuitously or for consideration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Federal body dealing with surplus Crown
  assets

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A federal body that has surplus Crown assets may
  request the Minister to dispose of or deal with the assets under this
  Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms and conditions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Terms and conditions prescribed pursuant to paragraph
  (1)(b) may be made applicable to any particular department or surplus
  Crown assets, any class thereof or departments or surplus Crown assets
  generally.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on departments

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No department shall dispose of any surplus Crown
  assets otherwise than in accordance with this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-27, s. 3
- 1989, c. 27, s. 24
- 1991, c. 50, s. 43
- 1992, c. 54, s. 83
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may refuse a request made under section 3 if the Minister
  considers it appropriate to do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of acceptance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, pursuant to a request made under section 3,
  the Minister accepts surplus Crown assets for disposal or dealing with
  under this Act, the Minister shall notify the department or federal
  body of the assets accepted.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-27, s. 4
- R.S., 1985, c. 22 (1st Supp.), s. 2
- 1992, c. 54, s. 83
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibility continues

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A department or federal
body continues to be responsible for accepted surplus Crown assets until
it surrenders the custody or control thereof pursuant to a direction of
the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-27, s. 5
- R.S., 1985, c. 22 (1st Supp.), s. 3, c. 35 (4th Supp.), s. 13
- 1992, c. 54, s. 83
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial powers

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** With specific or general
authority from the Governor in Council, the Minister may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sell, exchange, lease, lend or otherwise dispose of
  or deal with accepted surplus Crown assets either gratuitously or for
  consideration and on such terms and subject to such conditions as the
  Minister may consider desirable;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} hold, manage, operate, finish, assemble, store,
  transport, repair, maintain and service accepted surplus Crown assets;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} restore to its original condition any property that
  has been made available to Her Majesty and settle any claim in
  connection therewith;

- [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1992, c. 54, s. 84\]]{.Repealed}

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} convert accepted surplus Crown assets to basic
  materials;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} transfer accepted surplus Crown assets from one
  department to another;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} make such orders and issue such directions as the
  Minister may deem necessary or expedient to provide for the safety and
  preservation of accepted surplus Crown assets;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} direct any person to furnish, within such time as the
  Minister may specify, such information with regard to accepted surplus
  Crown assets as the Minister may specify;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} engage or make use of the services of any person in
  carrying out any of the purposes of this Act;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} delete from a notice given under section 4 any assets
  identified therein; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} do any other thing the Governor in Council may
  consider to be incidental to, or necessary or expedient for, carrying
  out the objects of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-27, s. 6
- R.S., 1985, c. 22 (1st Supp.), s. 4
- 1992, c. 54, s. 84
:::

## [Crown Assets Disposal Corporation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-443903 .Part}

**[[7. to 12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7._to_12 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
R.S., 1985, c. 22 (1st Supp.), s. 5\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceeds of sale by Minister

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3) and
  subsections 99(2) to (5) of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11), where the Minister sells any accepted surplus
  Crown assets of a federal body, the Minister shall remit to that body
  an amount equal to the net proceeds of the sale not later than the
  last day of the month following the month in which the proceeds were
  received.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), where the Minister sells
  any accepted surplus Crown assets of a department, other than property
  forfeited pursuant to an Act of Parliament, an amount equal to the net
  proceeds of the sale may, subject to such terms and conditions as the
  Treasury Board may prescribe, be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue
  Fund for the purposes of that department.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrative expenses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may retain out of the net proceeds of a
  sale referred to in subsection (1) or (2) such percentage of the net
  proceeds of sales of accepted surplus Crown assets in the period in
  which the sale is made as the Treasury Board may fix for the purpose
  of meeting the administrative or other expenses incurred with respect
  to those sales.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-27, s. 13
- R.S., 1985, c. 22 (1st Supp.), s. 6
- 1992, c. 54, s. 85
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceeds of sale by department

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Where a department sells
any surplus Crown assets, other than property forfeited pursuant to an
Act of Parliament, an amount equal to the proceeds of the sale may,
subject to such terms and conditions as the Treasury Board may
prescribe, be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund for the purposes
of that department.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-27, s. 14
- R.S., 1985, c. 22 (1st Supp.), s. 7
- 1992, c. 54, s. 85
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms and conditions

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Subject to such terms and
conditions as the Treasury Board may prescribe, the authority under
subsection 13(2) or section 14 to pay an amount does not lapse at the
end of the fiscal year in which the authority arose.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-27, s. 15
- R.S., 1985, c. 22 (1st Supp.), s. 7
- 1992, c. 54, s. 85
:::

**[[16. to 18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16._to_18
.sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, R.S., 1985, c. 22 (1st Supp.), s.
7\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Execution of deeds, contracts, etc.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Minister or any
person generally or specifically so authorized by the Minister may
execute, on behalf of Her Majesty, any bill of sale, contract or other
document transferring the ownership of, or otherwise dealing with or
relating to the disposition of, surplus Crown assets, and when any such
document has been so executed it is valid and binding on Her Majesty.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-27, s. 19
- R.S., 1985, c. 22 (1st Supp.), s. 8
- 1991, c. 50, s. 44
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

**[[19.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19.1 .sectionLabel}** The Minister through
which a department reports to Parliament or any person so authorized by
that Minister may execute, on behalf of Her Majesty, any bill of sale,
contract or other document transferring the ownership of, or otherwise
dealing with or relating to the disposal of or dealing with, surplus
Crown assets pursuant to paragraph 3(1)(b), and when any such document
has been so executed it is valid and binding on Her Majesty.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1992, c. 54, s. 86
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Governor in Council

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by order, confer on the Minister additional powers
  and duties with respect to the disposal of accepted surplus Crown
  assets; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} make or issue such orders, rules and regulations as
  may be deemed necessary or desirable to assist the Minister to perform
  the duties conferred or imposed on him by or pursuant to this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-27, s. 20
- R.S., 1985, c. 22 (1st Supp.), s. 9
- 1992, c. 54, s. 87
:::
