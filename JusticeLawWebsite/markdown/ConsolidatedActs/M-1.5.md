## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Commercialization of the Canadian Wheat Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-353203 .Part}

### [Continuance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-353204 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Part and in Parts 4 and 5.

  [[board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the board of directors of the
      Corporation as defined in subection 2(1) of the [Canadian Wheat
      Board (Interim Operations)
      Act](/eng/acts/C-24.2). ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Wheat Board
      continued by subsection 4(1) of the [Canadian Wheat Board (Interim
      Operations) Act](/eng/acts/C-24.2). ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Agriculture
      and Agri-Food. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Words and expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless it is otherwise provided, words and
  expressions used in this Part or in Part 4 or 5 have the same meaning
  as in the [Canadian Wheat Board (Interim Operations)
  Act](/eng/acts/C-24.2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission to Minister

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation must submit an application for continuance under one of
  the following Acts for the Minister's approval:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the [Canada Business Corporations
    Act](/eng/acts/C-44);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the [Canada Cooperatives Act](/eng/acts/C-1.7); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the [Canada Not-for-profit Corporations
    Act](/eng/acts/C-7.75).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be submitted to the Minister
  within four years, or any shorter period specified by the Minister,
  after the day on which this Part comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission to applicable authorities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Once the application has been approved by the
  Minister, the Corporation must submit the application to the
  applicable authorities under the relevant Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application not invalid

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The application is not invalid solely because the
  Corporation is incorporated by an Act of Parliament.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation is not
entitled to apply for continuance in another jurisdiction.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Dissolution of the Canadian Wheat Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-353233 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-353234 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Part

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies only if
the Corporation is not continued under Part 3 within five years, or any
shorter period specified by the Governor in Council, after the day on
which that Part comes into force.

### [Final Pool Period]{.HTitleText2} {#h-353237 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final pool period

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must, by order, in respect of each grain, designate one or
  more pool periods set by the Corporation under section 27 of the
  [Canadian Wheat Board (Interim Operations) Act](/eng/acts/C-24.2) as a
  final pool period for the purposes of winding up the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation must make all payments due under
  subsection 29(3) of the [Canadian Wheat Board (Interim Operations)
  Act](/eng/acts/C-24.2) in respect of the final pool period before
  winding up the Corporation under section 48.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exercising powers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} After the end of the final pool period, the
  Corporation may exercise its powers under the [Canadian Wheat Board
  (Interim Operations) Act](/eng/acts/C-24.2) only for the purpose of
  administering that pool period and winding up the Corporation.

### [Distribution of Assets]{.HTitleText2} {#h-353245 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution of property

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  property of the Corporation is to be applied in satisfaction of its
  debts and liabilities, and the charges, costs and expenses incurred in
  winding-up its affairs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the contingency fund
  established under subsection 18(1) of the [Canadian Wheat Board
  (Interim Operations) Act](/eng/acts/C-24.2) forms part of the property
  of the Corporation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline for claims

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may fix a date by which all claims against the Corporation
  are to be submitted to the Corporation and must publish a notice of
  that date in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) at least
  60 days before that date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to submit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any claim against the Corporation that is not
  submitted in accordance with subsection (1) is void.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Winding-up expenses

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** All charges, costs and
expenses properly incurred in the winding-up of the Corporation,
including the remuneration of any liquidator appointed under section 52,
are payable out of the assets of the Corporation, in priority to all
other claims.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surplus

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  surplus that remains after the satisfaction of the debts and
  liabilities of the Corporation and the winding-up charges, costs and
  expenses belongs to Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unsatisfied debts and liabilities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any debts and liabilities of the Corporation that
  remain unsatisfied on the day on which the Corporation is dissolved
  become the debts and liabilities of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

### [Appointment of Liquidator]{.HTitleText2} {#h-353263 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** If the Minister considers
it necessary to fully carry out the purpose of this Part, the Minister
may appoint a liquidator, who is to hold office during pleasure, to
administer the final pool period and wind up the Corporation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** On appointment of a
liquidator,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the president, chairperson and other directors cease
  to hold their respective offices and the liquidator may exercise all
  of the powers and perform all the duties and functions of the
  Corporation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the by-laws made under section 9 of the [Canadian
  Wheat Board (Interim Operations) Act](/eng/acts/C-24.2) are deemed to
  be revoked.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to honour

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** The liquidator may refuse
to honour any gratuitous contract, or conveyance or contract without
consideration or with a merely nominal consideration, respecting either
real or personal property or immovables or movables, made by the
Corporation before the appointment of the liquidator.

### [Dissolution Date]{.HTitleText2} {#h-353272 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dissolution

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation is
dissolved on a day fixed by order of the Governor in Council.
