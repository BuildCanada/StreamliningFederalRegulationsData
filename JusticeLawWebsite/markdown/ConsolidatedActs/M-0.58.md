## Marine Atlantic Inc. Acquisition Authorization Act {#marine-atlantic-inc.-acquisition-authorization-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1986, c. 36
:::

Assented to 1986-06-27

An Act to authorize the acquisition of Marine Atlantic Inc. and to
provide for other matters in relation thereto
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348738 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short Title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Marine Atlantic Inc. Acquisition Authorization
Act](/eng/acts/M-0.58).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348741 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Transport; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[National Company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[National Company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian National
    Railway Company, a company continued by the Canadian National
    Railways Act. ([Société nationale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Change of Name]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348746 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of name

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The name
  of CN Marine Inc., a corporation incorporated under the [Canada
  Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44), is hereby changed to
  Marine Atlantic Inc. and the articles of the corporation are amended
  accordingly.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A reference in any contract, document, instrument,
  regulation, proclamation or order in council to CN Marine Inc. shall
  be deemed, unless the context otherwise requires, to be a reference to
  Marine Atlantic Inc.

## [Transfer of Shares]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348752 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization to acquire shares of Marine
Altantic Inc.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister is hereby authorized to acquire all the common shares of
  Marine Atlantic Inc. held by the National Company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shares held in trust for Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Shares acquired by the Minister pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall be held by the Minister in trust for Her Majesty
  in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization to transfer shares of
Marine Atlantic Inc.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  National Company is hereby authorized to transfer, and on the
  direction of the Minister shall transfer, to the Minister all the
  common shares of Marine Atlantic Inc. held by the National Company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision not applicable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 101(1) of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11) does not apply in respect of a transfer of shares
  pursuant to subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization to transfer shares of
National Company

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  consideration of the transfer of shares, property and works to the
  Minister pursuant to this Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is hereby authorized to transfer to
    the National Company 523,902 common shares of the National Company,
    being all the common shares of the National Company held by the
    Minister; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of Finance is hereby authorized to
    transfer to the National Company 131,214 of the common shares of the
    National Company held by the Minister of Finance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acquisition and cancellation of shares
  of National Company

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The National Company is hereby empowered to acquire
  the shares transferred to it pursuant to subsection (1) and shall
  forthwith cancel the shares on the day on which it acquires them.

## [Transfer of Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348770 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of property to Her Majesty

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  direction of the Minister, the National Company shall transfer to Her
  Majesty in right of Canada the property and works listed in Part II of
  Schedule B to Order in Council P.C. 1979-1449 of May 9, 1979.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Management vested in Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The management, administration and control of the
  property and works transferred to Her Majesty pursuant to
  subsection (1) is hereby vested in the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of property to Marine Atlantic
Inc.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister, on such terms
and conditions as the Governor in Council may prescribe, may sell, lease
or otherwise dispose of to Marine Atlantic Inc., or by agreement in
writing permit Marine Atlantic Inc. to use,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any real or personal property or interest therein, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any power, right or privilege over or with respect to
  any real or personal property or interest therein

that is vested in or owned, controlled or occupied by Her Majesty in
right of Canada and over which the Minister has the management,
administration or control.

## [Amendment of Articles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348781 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on business of Marine
Atlantic Inc.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister and Marine
Atlantic Inc. are hereby authorized to take, and shall within three
months after the coming into force of this section take, such steps as
are necessary to amend the articles of Marine Atlantic Inc. to restrict
the business that it may carry on to the acquisition, establishment,
management and operation of a marine transportation service, a marine
maintenance, repair and refit service, a marine construction business
and any service or business related thereto.
