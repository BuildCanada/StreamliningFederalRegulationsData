## Marine War Risks Act {#marine-war-risks-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"} 1970, c. W-3
:::

An Act respecting marine war risks insurance and reinsurance agreements
::::

:::::::::::::::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Marine War Risks Act](/eng/acts/M-0.56).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. W-3, s. 1
- 2014, c. 29, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act

[[Account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Marine War Risks Insurance
    Account established under section 5; ([compte]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2014, c. 29, s.
    5\]]{.Repealed}

[[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who is a Canadian citizen,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a company or corporation created or incorporated
      under the laws of Canada or of a province thereof or that is
      licensed under any such laws to carry on business in Canada or a
      province thereof, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Government of Canada or the government of a
      province or municipality of Canada or a department, branch or
      agency of any of them; ([Canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cargo]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cargo]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any goods, securities, currency,
    articles or things defined as cargo by the Governor in
    Council; ([cargaison]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Transport; ([Ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a ship, boat or other type of
    water-craft that is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} registered in Canada under the Maritime Code, or
      under the Merchant Shipping Acts before the 1st day of August
      1936,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} registered in the United Kingdom and owned,
      chartered or otherwise controlled by a Canadian,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} registered in any country designated by the
      Governor in Council, whether or not it is owned, chartered or
      otherwise controlled by a Canadian, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} owned, chartered or otherwise controlled by a
      Canadian and employed, in or from Canada, in water transportation
      of goods or persons or in the fishing trade and industry,

    and includes the machinery, tackle, furniture, goods and stores
    thereof but does not include cargo; ([navire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[war risks]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[war risks]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the risks of loss or damage
    arising from hostilities, rebellion, revolution, civil war, piracy,
    action taken to repel an imagined attack or from civil strife
    consequent on the happening of any of them.  ([risques de
    guerre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. W-3, s. 2
- 2014, c. 29, s. 5

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insurance and reinsurance agreements

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister, for the
purpose of securing that ships are not laid up and that commerce is not
interrupted by reason of lack of insurance facilities, may enter into an
agreement, in the form and containing the terms and conditions that may
be prescribed by the regulations or otherwise approved by the Governor
in Council, with any person or association of persons for the insurance
or reinsurance by the Minister against any or all war risks of vessels
or cargo.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. W-3, s. 3
- 2014, c. 29, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations for carrying the purposes and provisions of this Act
into effect and, without restricting the generality of the foregoing,
may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the nature of insurances and reinsurances
  to be made under this Act and the form, terms and conditions of
  agreements and other matters relating thereto;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} providing for the definition of cargo, including the
  description, either by class or individual items and either for
  general or specific purposes, of the goods, securities, currency,
  articles or things that are cargo, the place and time at which they
  become or cease to be cargo and any other matter related to the manner
  of determining cargo; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the designation of countries of registry
  for the purposes of paragraph (c) of the definition
  [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. W-3, s. 4
- 2014, c. 29, s. 7

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marine War Risks Insurance Account

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be established in the accounts of Canada an account to be known
  as the Marine War Risks Insurance Account to which shall be credited

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} amounts equal to the premiums and other moneys
    received for or arising out of agreements for insurance or
    reinsurance entered into under this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all sums that are allocated to the Account from
    appropriations by Parliament; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} amounts directed to be credited to the Account by
    the Minister under section 6.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All payments of losses, costs, return of premiums or
  other moneys payable by the Minister under or arising out of
  agreements for insurance or reinsurance entered into under this Act
  and any other costs or expenses incurred in the administration of this
  Act shall be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charges

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} All moneys paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund
  under subsection (2) and all amounts directed to be charged to the
  Account by the Minister under section 6 shall be charged to the
  Account.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. W-3, s. 5
- 2014, c. 29, s. 8

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary credits and charges

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where the Minister is of
opinion that the aggregate amount to the credit of the Account is or
will be less than the aggregate amount required to pay the amounts
charged or to be charged to the Account, he may from time to time direct
that amounts be credited to the Account and at such times as he deems
advisable direct that the amounts so previously credited be charged to
the Account.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 328, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audit

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An audit
  of the Account and of the transactions in connection therewith shall,
  at such times and in such manner as he thinks proper, be made by the
  Auditor General of Canada, with a view to ascertaining whether or not
  such transactions have been carried out in accordance with this Act
  and whether or not the records of the Account clearly show the state
  of the Account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Auditor General shall, in such detail as he
  thinks proper having regard to the public interest and the security of
  Canada, submit to Parliament, within three months after the completion
  of each audit or, if Parliament is not then in session, within thirty
  days after the commencement of the next ensuing session, a report of
  his findings on the audit and his recommendations, if any, arising
  therefrom.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. W-3, s. 7
- 1976-77, c. 34, s. 30, item 34(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of agreements

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall lay
copies of reinsurance agreements before Parliament within thirty days
after they have been made or, if Parliament is not then in session,
within thirty days after the commencement of the next ensuing session.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. 328, s. 8
