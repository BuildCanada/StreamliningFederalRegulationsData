## Roosevelt Campobello International Park Commission Act {#roosevelt-campobello-international-park-commission-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1964-65, c. 19
:::

Assented to 1964-06-30

An Act respecting the Commission established to administer the Roosevelt
Campobello International Park
::::

::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-428146 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Roosevelt Campobello International Park Commission
Act](/eng/acts/R-8.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-428149 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

:   - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      Agreement between the Government of the United States of America
      and the Government of Canada relating to the establishment of the
      Roosevelt Campobello International Park Commission set out in the
      Schedule hereto; ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

:   - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      Roosevelt Campobello International Park Commission established
      pursuant to the Agreement; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

:   - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      Minister of Foreign Affairs; and ([Ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

:   - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[Park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Roosevelt
      Campobello International Park at Campobello, New
      Brunswick. ([parc]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 5, s. 25
:::

## [Powers and Exemptions of the Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-428162 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Commission

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commission has in
Canada the legal powers and capacities of a body corporate, including
those set out in section 30 of the [Interpretation Act](/eng/acts/I-21).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property of Commission exempt from
attachment, etc.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** All property of the
Commission in Canada is exempt from attachment, seizure or execution
under any writ or order of any court, or of a judge of any court,
established by Parliament.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission exempt from customs or excise
duties

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No duty or tax payable
under any Act of Parliament relating to customs or excise is payable on
any property imported into Canada by the Commission for use in
connection with the Park.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission charitable organization

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Commission shall be
deemed to be a charitable organization in Canada

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} as described in paragraph (e) of subsection (1) of
  section 62 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), for the purposes
  of that Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} as described in subparagraph (i) of paragraph (d) of
  subsection (1) of section 7 of the Estate Tax Act, for the purposes of
  that Act.

## [Appointment of Alternate Members]{.HTitleText1} {#h-428173 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Governor in council appointment

**[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
shall appoint no more than two of the alternate members to the
Commission.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1765
:::

## [Report to Parliament]{.HTitleText1} {#h-428178 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Parliament

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Commission shall,
within three months after the termination of each year, submit to the
Minister a report of the affairs of the Commission for that year,
including the financial statement of the Commission and the report of
its auditors thereon, and the Minister shall cause the report to be laid
before Parliament within fifteen days after the receipt thereof or, if
Parliament is not then sitting, on any of the first fifteen days next
thereafter that Parliament is sitting.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[]{.scheduleTitleText}  [Agreement Between the Government of Canada and the Government of the United States of America Relating to the Establishment of the Roosevelt Campobello International Park]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-428185 .scheduleLabel}
:::

### The Governments of Canada and the United States of America {#the-governments-of-canada-and-the-united-states-of-america .SchedHeadL2}

Noting the generous offer by the Hammer family of the summer home of
President Franklin Delano Roosevelt on Campobello Island, New Brunswick,
Canada, with the intention that it be opened to the general public as a
memorial to President Roosevelt, and the acceptance in principle of this
offer by Prime Minister Lester B. Pearson and President John F. Kennedy
at Hyannis Port in May 1963; and

Recognizing the many intimate associations of President Roosevelt with
the summer home on Campobello Island; and

Desiring to take advantage of this unique opportunity to symbolize the
close and neighbourly relations between the peoples of Canada and the
United States of America by the utilization of the gift to establish a
Canadian-United States memorial park;

Agree as follows:

### Article 1 {#article-1 .SchedHeadL2}

There shall be established a joint Canadian-United States commission, to
be called the "Roosevelt Campobello International Park Commission",
which shall have as its functions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to accept title from the Hammer family to the former
  Roosevelt estate comprising the Roosevelt home and other grounds on
  Campobello Island;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to take the necessary measures to restore the
  Roosevelt home as closely as possible to its condition when it was
  occupied by President Roosevelt;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to administer as a memorial the "Roosevelt Campobello
  International Park" comprising the Roosevelt estate and such other
  lands as may be acquired.

### Article 2 {#article-2 .SchedHeadL2}

The Commission shall have juridical personality and all powers and
capacity necessary or appropriate for the purpose of performing its
functions under this Agreement including, but not by way of limitation,
the following powers and capacity:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to acquire and dispose of personal and real property,
  excepting the power to dispose of the Roosevelt home and the tract of
  land on which it is located;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to enter into contracts;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to sue or be sued in either Canada or the United
  States;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to appoint a staff, including an Executive Secretary
  who shall act as secretary at meetings of the Commission, and to fix
  the terms and conditions of their employment and remuneration;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} to delegate to the Executive Secretary or other
  officials such authority respecting the employment and direction of
  staff and the other responsibilities of the Commission as it deems
  desirable and appropriate;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} to adopt such rules of procedure as it deems
  desirable to enable it to perform the functions set forth in this
  Agreement;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} to charge admission fees for entrance to the Park
  should the Commission consider such fees desirable; however, such fees
  shall be set at a level which will make the facilities readily
  available to visitors;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} to grant concessions if deemed desirable;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} to accept donations, bequests or devises intended for
  furthering the functions of the Commission and to use such donations,
  bequests or devises as may be provided in the terms thereof.

### Article 3 {#article-3 .SchedHeadL2}

The Commission shall consist of six members, of whom three shall be
appointed by the Government of Canada and three appointed by the
Government of the United States. One of the Canadian members shall be
nominated by the Government of New Brunswick and one of the United
States members shall be nominated by the Government of Maine. Alternates
may be appointed for each member of the Commission in the same manner as
the members. The Commission shall elect a chairman and a vice-chairman
from among its members, each of whom shall hold office for a term of two
years, in such a manner that members of the same nationality shall never
simultaneously serve as chairman and vice-chairman. The chairmanship
shall alternate between members of Canadian nationality and United
States nationality every two years. A quorum shall consist of at least
four members of the Commission or their alternates, including always two
from Canada and two from the United States. The affirmative vote of at
least two Canadian and two United States members or their respective
alternates shall be required for any decision to be taken by the
Commission.

### Article 4 {#article-4 .SchedHeadL2}

The Commission may employ both Canadian and United States citizens.
Their employment shall be subject to the relevant Canadian labour and
other laws, and the Government of Canada agrees to take such measures as
may be necessary to permit United States citizens to accept employment
with the Commission on a similar basis to Canadian citizens.

### Article 5 {#article-5 .SchedHeadL2}

The Commission shall maintain insurance in reasonable amounts,
including, but not limited to, liability and property insurance.

### Article 6 {#article-6 .SchedHeadL2}

The Commission shall hold at least one meeting every calendar year and
shall submit an annual report to the Canadian and United States
Governments on or before March 31 of each year, including a general
statement of the operations for the previous year and an audited
statement of the financial operations of the Commission. The Commission
shall permit inspection of its records by the accounting agencies of
both Governments.

### Article 7 {#article-7 .SchedHeadL2}

All property belonging to the Commission shall be exempt from
attachment, execution, or other processes for satisfaction of claims,
debts or judgments.

### Article 8 {#article-8 .SchedHeadL2}

The Commission shall not be subject to Federal, State, Provincial or
local taxation in Canada or the United States on any real or personal
property held by it or on any gift, bequest or devise to it of any
personal or real property, or on its income, whether from Governmental
appropriations, admission fees, concessions or donations. All personal
property imported or introduced into Canada by the Commission for use in
connection with the Park shall be free from customs duties. Further
consideration shall be given to granting exemption from other taxes the
imposition of which would be inconsistent with the functioning of the
Commission.

### Article 9 {#article-9 .SchedHeadL2}

Arrangements may be made with the competent agencies of Canada and the
United States for rendering, without reimbursement, such services as the
Commission may request for the orderly development, maintenance and
operation of the Park.

### Article 10 {#article-10 .SchedHeadL2}

The Commission shall take appropriate measures to emphasize the
international nature of the Park.

### Article 11 {#article-11 .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} The Governments of Canada and the United States shall
  share equally the costs of developing the Roosevelt Campobello
  International Park and the annual cost of operating and maintaining
  the Park.

- [2]{.lawlabel} Any revenues derived from admission fees or concession
  operations of the Commission shall be transmitted in equal shares to
  the two Governments within 60 days of the end of the Commission's
  fiscal year. Other funds received by the Commission may be used to
  further the purposes of the Commission, in accordance with the
  provisions of this Agreement.

- [3]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall submit annually to the Canadian
  and United States Governments a budget covering total anticipated
  expenditures to be financed from all sources, and shall conduct its
  operations in accordance with the budget as approved by the two
  Governments.

- [4]{.lawlabel} The Commissioners shall receive no remuneration from
  the Commission; however, they may be paid reasonable per diem and
  travel expenses by the Commission.

### Article 12 {#article-12 .SchedHeadL2}

This Agreement requires implementation by legislation in each country;
it shall come into effect after the enactment of such legislation on a
date to be fixed by an exchange of notes between the two Governments.

Done in duplicate at Washington, this 22nd day of January, 1964.

FOR THE GOVERNMENT OF CANADA:

(Sgd.) [Lester B. Pearson]{.Smallcaps}

FOR THE GOVERNMENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA:

(Sgd.) [Lyndon B. Johnson]{.Smallcaps}

1/22/64

Washington, D.C.
