## Indian Reserves of Nova Scotia Act {#indian-reserves-of-nova-scotia-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1959, c. 50
:::

Assented to 1959-07-18

An Act to confirm an Agreement between the Government of Canada and the
Government of the Province of Nova Scotia respecting Indian Reserves
::::

::::::: section
::: Enacts
[Her]{.Smallcaps} Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement ratified and confirmed

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement between the
Government of Canada and the Government of the Province of Nova Scotia,
set out in the Schedule, is ratified and confirmed, and it shall take
effect according to its terms.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[]{.scheduleTitleText}  [Memorandum of Agreement made this 14th Day of April, 1959]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-333158 .scheduleLabel}
:::

[between]{.Smallcaps}

THE GOVERNMENT OF CANADA, hereinafter referred to as "Canada",

of the first part,

[and]{.Smallcaps}

THE GOVERNMENT OF THE PROVINCE OF NOVA SCOTIA, hereinafter referred to
as "Nova Scotia",

of the second part.

[Whereas]{.Smallcaps} since the enactment of the British North America
Act, 1867, certain lands in the Province of Nova Scotia set aside for
Indians have been surrendered to the Crown by the Indians entitled
thereto;

[And Whereas]{.Smallcaps} from time to time Letters Patent have been
issued under the Great Seal of Canada purporting to convey said lands to
various persons;

[And Whereas]{.Smallcaps} two decisions of the Judicial Committee of the
Privy Council relating to Indian lands in the Provinces of Ontario and
Quebec lead to the conclusion that said lands could only have been
lawfully conveyed by authority of Nova Scotia with the result that the
grantees of said lands hold defective titles and are thereby occasioned
hardship and inconvenience;

[Now this Agreement witnesseth]{.Smallcaps} that the parties hereto, in
order to settle all outstanding problems relating to Indian reserves in
the Province of Nova Scotia and to enable Canada to deal effectively in
future with lands forming part of said reserves, have mutually agreed
subject to the approval of the Parliament of Canada and the Legislature
of the Province of Nova Scotia as follows:

- [1]{.lawlabel} In this agreement, unless the context otherwise
  requires,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[Province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Province
    of Nova Scotia;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [[reserve lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means those
    reserves in the Province referred to in the appendix to this
    agreement;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[patented lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means those
    tracts of land in the Province in respect of which Canada accepted
    surrenders of their rights and interests therein from the Indians
    entitled to the use and occupation thereof and in respect of which
    grants were made by Letters Patent issued under the Gread Seal of
    Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[minerals]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes salt,
    oil, natural gas, infusorial earth, ochres or paints, the base of
    which is found in the soil, fire clays, carbonate of lime, sulphate
    of lime, gypsum, coal, bituminous shale, albertite and uranium, but
    not sand, gravel and marl;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} [[[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), Revised Statutes of Canada
    1952, cap. 149, as amended from time to time and includes any
    re-enactment, revision or consolidation thereof;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} [[surrender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    surrender for sale of reserve lands or a portion thereof pursuant to
    the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) but does not include a surrender of
    rights and interests in reserve lands for purposes other than sale;
    and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} [[public highways]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means every
    road and bridge in reserve lands, constructed for public use by and
    at the expense of the Province or any municipality in the Province
    and in existence at the coming into force of this agreement.

- [2]{.lawlabel} All grants of patented lands are hereby confirmed
  except insofar as such grants purport to transfer to the grantees any
  minerals and said minerals are hereby acknowledged to be the property
  of the Province.

- [3]{.lawlabel} Nova Scotia hereby transfers to Canada all rights and
  interests of the Province in reserve lands except lands lying under
  public highways and minerals.

- [4 (1)]{.lawlabel} In the event that a band of Indians in the Province
  becomes extinct, Canada shall revest in the Province all the rights
  and interests transferred to it under this agreement in the reserve
  lands occupied by such band prior to its becoming extinct.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subparagraph (1) a band does not
    become extinct by enfranchisement.

- [5]{.lawlabel} The mining regulations made from time to time under the
  [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) apply to the prospecting for, mining of or
  other dealing in all minerals in unsurrendered reserve lands and all
  minerals reserved in the grants referred to in paragraph 2, and any
  payment made pursuant to such regulations whether by way of rent,
  royalty, or otherwise, shall be paid to the Receiver General of Canada
  for the use and benefit of the Indian band or Indians from whose
  reserve lands such monies are so derived.

- [6 (1)]{.lawlabel} Canada shall forthwith notify Nova Scotia of any
  surrender and Nova Scotia may within thirty days of receiving such
  notification elect to purchase the surrendered lands at a price to be
  agreed upon.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} If Nova Scotia fails to elect within such
    thirty-day period, Canada may dispose of the surrendered lands
    without further reference to Nova Scotia.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a surrender is made under the condition that
    the surrendered lands be sold to a named or designated person at a
    certain price or for a certain consideration, Nova Scotia shall
    exercise its election subject to that price or consideration.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subparagraph (3) of this paragraph,
    should Canada and Nova Scotia be unable, within thirty days of the
    date of an election to purchase being made, to reach agreement on
    the price to be paid by Nova Scotia for any surrendered lands, the
    matter shall be referred to arbitrators as follows:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Canada and Nova Scotia shall each appoint one
      arbitrator, and the two arbitrators so appointed shall appoint a
      third arbitrator;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the decision of the arbitrators as to the price
      to be paid by Nova Scotia for the surrendered lands shall be final
      and conclusive; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the costs of arbitration shall be borne equally
      by Canada and Nova Scotia.

[In Witness Whereof]{.Smallcaps} the Honourable Ellen L. Fairclough,
Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, has hereunto set her hand on
behalf of the Government of Canada and the Honourable R. Clifford Levy,
Minister of Lands and Forests, has hereunto set his hand on behalf of
the Government of the Province of Nova Scotia.
