students who are registered as Indians under the [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5) and for Inuit students.

### [DIVISION 13]{.HLabel2}[Ridley Terminals Inc.]{.HTitleText2} {#h-174455 .Subheading}

#### [Reorganization and Divestiture]{.HTitleText3} {#h-174456 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[200]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-200 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Division.

  [[assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to an entity, the securities of any
        other entity held by, on behalf of or in trust for the entity;
        and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} incorporeal
        property. ([actifs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
      Transport. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a corporation, a share of any
        class or series of shares or a debt obligation of the
        corporation, and includes any conversion or exchange privilege,
        option or other right to acquire a share or debt obligation of
        the corporation; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to any other entity, any ownership
        interest in or debt obligation of the entity, and includes any
        conversion or exchange privilege, option or other right to
        acquire an ownership interest or debt obligation of the
        entity. ([titre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Division,
  [[corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and
  [[wholly-owned subsidiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have the same meaning
  as in subsection 83(1) of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless a contrary intention appears, words and
  expressions used in this Division have the same meaning as in the
  [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the event of any inconsistency between this
  Division and the [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44) or
  anything issued, made or established under that Act, this Division
  prevails to the extent of the inconsistency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operation of [Competition
  Act](/eng/acts/C-34)

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in, or done under the authority of, this
  Division affects the operation of the [Competition
  Act](/eng/acts/C-34) in respect of the acquisition of any interest or
  right in an entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Section 53.1 of [Canada Transportation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4)

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Section 53.1 of the [Canada Transportation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4) does not apply to a transaction proposed to be
  taken under this Division.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[201]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-201 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this
Division is to authorize measures for the reorganization and divestiture
of all or any part of Ridley Terminals Inc.'s business, which will allow
the Government of Canada to pursue its objective of obtaining the best
value for the business from a buyer who will operate the business on a
long-term and sustainable basis and with open access to its services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to sell shares, etc.

**[[202]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-202 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, with
the approval of the Governor in Council and on any terms that the
Governor in Council considers appropriate, take any of the following
measures:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sell or otherwise dispose of some or all of the
  securities of Ridley Terminals Inc.;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} procure an addition to, or other material change in,
  the objects or purposes for which Ridley Terminals Inc. is
  incorporated or the restrictions on the businesses or activities that
  it may carry on, as set out in its articles;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} procure the amalgamation of Ridley Terminals Inc.;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} procure the dissolution of Ridley Terminals Inc.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority with respect to entities

- **[[203]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-203 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, with the approval of the Governor in Council and on any
  terms that the Governor in Council considers appropriate, take any of
  the following measures:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} procure the incorporation of a corporation,
    securities of which, on incorporation, would be held by, on behalf
    of or in trust for Her Majesty in right of Canada and sell or
    otherwise dispose of some or all of the securities of the
    corporation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} procure the formation of any other entity,
    securities of which, on formation, would be held by, on behalf of or
    in trust for Her Majesty in right of Canada and sell or otherwise
    dispose of some or all of the securities of the entity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} acquire securities of a corporation that, on
    acquisition, would be held by, on behalf of or in trust for Her
    Majesty in right of Canada and sell or otherwise dispose of some or
    all of the securities of the corporation; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} acquire securities of any other entity that, on
    acquisition, would be held by, on behalf of or in trust for Her
    Majesty in right of Canada and sell or otherwise dispose of some or
    all of the securities of the entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Part X of [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by order, declare that
  any provision of Part X of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11) does not apply to a corporation referred to in
  paragraph (1)(a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional powers

**[[204]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-204 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may do
anything or cause anything to be done that is necessary for, or
incidental to, a measure taken under section 202 or subsection 203(1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority

- **[[205]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-205
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Ridley Terminals Inc., any
  corporation referred to in paragraph 203(1)(a) or other entity
  referred to in paragraph 203(1)(b) or any wholly-owned subsidiary or
  wholly-owned entity of Ridley Terminals Inc., the corporation or the
  other entity may, with the approval of the Governor in Council and on
  any terms that the Governor in Council considers appropriate, take any
  of the following measures:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sell or otherwise dispose of some or all of its
    assets;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sell or otherwise dispose of some or all of its
    liabilities;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} issue securities and sell or otherwise dispose of
    some or all of those securities;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} reorganize its capital structure;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} acquire assets of a corporation or of any other
    entity;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} procure an addition to, or other material change
    in, the objects or purposes for which it is incorporated or formed
    or the restrictions on the businesses or activities that it may
    carry on, as set out in its articles or constituting documents;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} procure the incorporation of a corporation,
    securities of which, on incorporation, would be held by, on behalf
    of or in trust for it;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} procure the formation of any other entity,
    securities of which, on formation, would be held by, on behalf of or
    in trust for it;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} acquire securities of a corporation or any other
    entity that, on acquisition, would be held by, on behalf of or in
    trust for it;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} sell or otherwise dispose of some or all of the
    securities of a corporation or any other entity that are held by, on
    behalf of or in trust for it;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} procure its amalgamation or the amalgamation of any
    of its wholly-owned subsidiaries;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} procure its dissolution or the dissolution of any
    of its wholly-owned subsidiaries; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} do anything that is necessary for, or incidental
    to, a measure taken under any of paragraphs (a) to (l).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of
  the Minister and on any terms that the Governor in Council considers
  appropriate, direct Ridley Terminals Inc. or any corporation referred
  to in paragraph 203(1)(a) or other entity referred to in paragraph
  203(1)(b) to take, or cause any wholly-owned subsidiary or
  wholly-owned entity of Ridley Terminals Inc., the corporation or the
  other entity to take, a measure referred to in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council is not authorized to issue a
  directive to Ridley Terminals Inc. or any corporation referred to in
  paragraph 203(1)(a) or other entity referred to in paragraph 203(1)(b)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} after some or all of its securities are sold or
    otherwise disposed of; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to any of its wholly-owned
    subsidiaries or wholly-owned entities, after some or all of the
    wholly-owned subsidiaries or wholly-owned entities' securities are
    sold or otherwise disposed of.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with directive

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The directors of Ridley Terminals Inc. or of the
  corporation, or persons acting in a similar capacity with respect to
  the other entity, must comply with a directive issued by the Governor
  in Council. Compliance with that issued directive is in the best
  interests of Ridley Terminals Inc., the corporation or the other
  entity, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of implementation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} As soon as feasible after implementing a directive
  and completing any actions that are required to be taken in connection
  with that implementation, Ridley Terminals Inc., the corporation or
  the other entity, as the case may be, must notify the Minister that it
  has done so.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of [Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[206]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-206 .sectionLabel}** The [Statutory
Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does not apply to a directive.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling in Parliament

- **[[207]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-207 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister is to cause a copy of a directive to be laid before each
  House of Parliament on any of the first 15 days on which that House is
  sitting after the day on which the directive is issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- detrimental information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, if the Minister is of the opinion that
  publishing information contained in the directive would be detrimental
  to the commercial interests of Canada, of Ridley Terminals Inc., of
  any corporation referred to in paragraph 203(1)(a) or other entity
  referred to in paragraph 203(1)(b) or of a wholly-owned subsidiary or
  wholly-owned entity of Ridley Terminals Inc., the corporation or the
  other entity, the Minister is to cause a copy of the directive to be
  laid before each House of Parliament on any of the first 15 days on
  which that House is sitting after the day on which he or she is
  notified that the directive has been implemented.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before forming his or her opinion on whether
  publishing information contained in the directive would be
  detrimental, the Minister must consult the board of directors of
  Ridley Terminals Inc. or of the corporation, or the person or group of
  persons acting in a similar capacity with respect to the other entity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No liability

**[[208]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-208 .sectionLabel}** No action or other
proceeding, including any action or proceeding in restitution, or for
damages of any kind, that is based on or is in relation to any agreement
in relation to Ridley Terminals Inc. that existed on or before the
coming into force of this section lies or may be instituted by anyone
against Her Majesty in right of Canada or any minister or any employee
or agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada, or any person engaged to
provide advice or services to Her Majesty in right of Canada in relation
to such an agreement, for anything done or omitted to be done or for
anything purported to have been done or omitted to be done, in the
exercise of their powers or the performance of their duties or
functions, under this Division.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of money from disposition

**[[209]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-209 .sectionLabel}** The money from a sale
or other disposition made under paragraph 202(a), subsection 203(1) or
paragraph 205(1)(a), (b), (c) or (j), minus an amount equal to the
amount paid or payable by Her Majesty in right of Canada, or any agent
of Her Majesty in right of Canada, under an agreement relating to the
disposition, including the management of that disposition, is public
money for the purposes of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11) and is to be paid to the Receiver General.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11)

**[[210]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-210 .sectionLabel}** Sections 89, 90 and 91
and subsection 99(2) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11) do not apply to any measure referred to in sections
202 to 205.
