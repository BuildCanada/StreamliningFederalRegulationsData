## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-414146 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Pension Benefits Division Act](/eng/acts/P-6.7).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-414149 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:     [[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement referred to
    in subparagraph 4(2)(b )(ii); ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an application made under
    subsection 4(1);  ([demande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:     [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
    establishes that the person is cohabiting with a member of a pension
    plan in a relationship of a conjugal nature, having so cohabited for
    a period of at least one year; ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[court order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[court order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an order referred to in
    paragraph 4(2)(a) or subparagraph 4(2)(b)(i);
     ([ordonnance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a pension plan, means
    a person who is or may become entitled to a pension benefit under
    that pension plan by reason of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person ceasing or having ceased to be
      employed, to hold an office, to be a member of the Senate or House
      of Commons or to serve in the Canadian Forces or the Royal
      Canadian Mounted Police, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other prescribed
      circumstances; ([participant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, for the purposes of applications
    and other matters pertaining to a particular pension plan under this
    Act, means such member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada as is
    designated by the Governor in Council to act as the Minister for the
    purposes of those applications and matters;
     ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pension benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pension benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any pension,
    allowance, annuity, return of contributions or other benefit or
    amount payable under a pension plan, and includes any benefit or
    amount payable under the [Public Service Pension Adjustment
    Act](/eng/acts/P-33.6), chapter P-33 of the Revised Statutes of
    Canada, 1970, or the [Supplementary Retirement Benefits
    Act](/eng/acts/S-24) in respect of a pension, allowance or annuity
    payable under that pension plan;  ([prestation de
    retraite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a superannuation or pension plan provided by

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the [Public Service Superannuation
        Act](/eng/acts/P-36),

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the [Canadian Forces Superannuation
        Act](/eng/acts/C-17),

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the [Defence Services Pension Continuation
        Act](/eng/acts/D-1.3), chapter D-3 of the Revised Statutes of
        Canada, 1970,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
        Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11),

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Pension
        Continuation Act](/eng/acts/R-10.6), chapter R-10 of the Revised
        Statutes of Canada, 1970,

      - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the [Diplomatic Service (Special)
        Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/D-2),

      - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the [Lieutenant Governors Superannuation
        Act](/eng/acts/L-8), or

      - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the [Governor General's Act](/eng/acts/G-9),

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a pension plan or retirement compensation
      arrangement provided by the [Members of Parliament Retiring
      Allowances Act](/eng/acts/M-5), or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a special pension plan or retirement compensation

      Act](/eng/acts/S-15.2); ([régime]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application for Division]{.HTitleText1} {#h-414180 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member
  of a pension plan or a spouse, former spouse or former common-law
  partner of a member may, in the circumstances described in subsection
  (2), apply to the Minister to divide the member's pension benefits
  between the member and the spouse, former spouse or former common-law
  partner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Circumstances in which application may
  be made

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The circumstances in which an application may be made
  are:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where a court in Canada of competent jurisdiction,
    in proceedings in relation to divorce, annulment of marriage or
    separation, makes an order that provides for the pension benefits to
    be divided between the member and the spouse, former spouse or
    former common-law partner; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the member and the spouse, former spouse or
    former common-law partner are living separate and apart, having
    lived separate and apart for a period of at least one year and,
    either before or after they commenced to live separate and apart,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a court in Canada of competent jurisdiction makes
      an order that provides for the pension benefits to be divided
      between them, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the member and the spouse, former spouse or
      former common-law partner have entered into a written agreement
      that provides for the pension benefits to be divided between them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of period of separation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (2)(b),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the member and the spouse, former spouse or former
    common-law partner are deemed to have lived separate and apart for
    any period during which they lived apart and either of them had the
    intention to live separate and apart from the other; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a period during which the member and the spouse,
    former spouse or former common-law partner have lived separate and
    apart shall not be considered to have been interrupted or terminated
    by reason only

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the member or the spouse, former spouse or
      former common-law partner has become incapable of forming or
      having an intention to continue to live separate and apart or of
      continuing to live separate and apart of the member's or the
      spouse's, former spouse's or former common-law partner's own
      volition, if it appears to the Minister that the separation would
      probably have continued if the member or the spouse, former spouse
      or former common-law partner had not become so incapable, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that the member and the spouse, former spouse or
      former common-law partner have resumed cohabitation during a
      period of, or periods totalling, not more than ninety days with
      reconciliation as its primary purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of application, etc.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An application must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be made in writing and contain the prescribed
    information; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by a certified true copy of the
    court order or agreement and such other documents as are prescribed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interested party may object

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  interested party who objects to the division of the pension benefits
  on any of the grounds described in subsection (2) may submit a notice
  of objection in writing to the Minister within sixty days after the
  day on which notice of the receipt of the application is deemed under
  subsection 5(3) to be received by that party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grounds for objection

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The grounds for objection are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the court order or agreement has been varied

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the terms of the court order or agreement have
    been or are being satisfied by other means; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that proceedings have been commenced in a court in
    Canada of competent jurisdiction to appeal or review the court order
    or challenge the terms of the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documentary evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An interested party who submits a notice of objection
  shall include with that notice documentary evidence to establish the
  grounds for objection.

## [Division]{.HTitleText1} {#h-414227 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of division

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), the Minister shall, as soon as is
  practicable after the Minister is satisfied that an application meets
  the requirements of this Act, approve the division of the pension
  benefits for which the application is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If notice of objection is submitted

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an interested party submits a notice of objection
  to the Minister in accordance with section 6, the Minister shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the grounds for objection are the grounds
    referred to in paragraph 6(2)(a) or (b), defer any decision on the
    application until such time as the Minister is able to ascertain to
    the Minister's satisfaction whether those grounds have been
    established; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the grounds for objection are the grounds
    referred to in paragraph 6(2)(c), defer any decision on the
    application until the final disposition of the proceedings on which
    those grounds are based.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister shall refuse approval

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall refuse to approve the division of
  the pension benefits if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application is withdrawn in accordance with the
    regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where an interested party submits a notice of
    objection to the Minister in accordance with section 6 and the
    grounds for objection are the grounds referred to in paragraph
    6(2)(a) or (b), the Minister is satisfied that those grounds have
    been established and that they provide sufficient reason to refuse
    the division;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where an interested party submits a notice of
    objection to the Minister in accordance with section 6 and the
    grounds for objection are the grounds referred to in paragraph
    6(2)(c), the court order or agreement is of no force or effect as a
    result of the proceedings on which those grounds are based;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the period subject to division cannot be determined
    under subsection 8(2) or (3); or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is satisfied, based on evidence
    submitted to the Minister, that it would not be just to approve the
    division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (3), the Minister may
  approve the division of the pension benefits on the basis of an order
  of a court issued pursuant to any proceedings referred to in paragraph
  6(2)(c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transitional

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may approve the division of the pension
  benefits notwithstanding that the court order or agreement on which
  the application is based was made or entered into before the day on
  which subsection 4(1) comes into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Division

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  division of pension benefits shall be effected by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (4), transferring an amount
    representing fifty per cent of the value of the pension benefits
    that have accrued to the member of the pension plan during the
    period subject to division, as determined in accordance with the
    regulations, to the spouse, former spouse or former common-law
    partner, if that pension plan is a retirement compensation
    arrangement, or, in any other case, to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a pension plan selected by the spouse, former
      spouse or former common-law partner that is registered under the
      [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), if that pension plan so
      permits,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a retirement savings plan or fund for the
      spouse, former spouse or former common-law partner that is of the
      prescribed kind, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a financial institution authorized to sell
      immediate or deferred life annuities of the prescribed kind, for
      the purchase from that financial institution of such an annuity
      for the spouse, former spouse or former common-law partner; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} adjusting, in accordance with the regulations, the
    pension benefits that have accrued to the member of the pension plan
    under that pension plan, notwithstanding the provisions of that
    pension plan or the Act under which it is established or by which it
    is provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of period subject to
  division

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1) but subject to
  subsection (3), the period subject to division is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the period specified by the court order or
    agreement as the period during which the member of the pension plan
    and the spouse, former spouse or former common-law partner
    cohabited; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the court order or agreement does not specify
    a period as described in paragraph (a), such period as may be
    determined by the Minister, on the basis of evidence submitted by
    either of the interested parties or by both, as being the period
    during which the member of the pension plan and the spouse, former
    spouse or former common-law partner cohabited.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), where the
  application is based on a court order and the order provides that
  pension benefits that have accrued to the member of the pension plan
  during a period specified in the order are to be divided, the period
  specified in the order is the period subject to division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lump sum payment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the court order or agreement provides, or the
  interested parties agree, that the terms of the court order or
  agreement shall be satisfied by the payment of a lump sum amount and
  that lump sum amount, together with such interest as may be required
  by the regulations, is less than the amount that would otherwise be
  transferred in accordance with paragraph (1)(a), that lump sum amount,
  together with that interest, shall be transferred in accordance with
  that paragraph in lieu of the greater amount.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Death

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An amount that cannot be transferred in accordance
  with paragraph (1)(a) by reason only of the death of the spouse,
  former spouse or former common-law partner shall be paid to their
  estate or succession.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date of adjustment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The adjustment of pension benefits required by
  paragraph (1)(b) shall be effective as of the date determined in
  accordance with the regulations, which date may be before the date on
  which the adjustment is actually made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of division

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall send a notice of the division of
  the pension benefits in the prescribed manner to each interested
  party.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further divisions precluded

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Where a division of pension
benefits that have accrued to a member of a pension plan during any
period is effected under section 8, no further divisions may be made
under that section in respect of that period.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-414274 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts transferred in error

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** If the amount transferred
in respect of a spouse, former spouse or former common-law partner or
paid to their estate or succession under section 8 exceeds the amount
that the spouse, former spouse or former common-law partner was entitled
to have transferred or the estate or succession was entitled to be paid,
the amount in excess constitutes a debt due to Her Majesty in right of
Canada by that spouse, former spouse, former common-law partner, estate
or succession.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1992, c. 46, Sch. II, s. 10
- 2000, c. 12, s. 246
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts paid before adjustment

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Where an adjustment is
made under paragraph 8(1)(b) and an amount is or has been paid to a
member of a pension plan that exceeds the amount to which that member is
or would have been entitled under that pension plan after the effective
date of that adjustment, the amount in excess constitutes a debt due to
Her Majesty in right of Canada by that member and may be recovered at
any time by set-off against any pension benefit that is payable to that
member under any pension plan, without prejudice to any other recourse
available to Her Majesty in right of Canada with respect to the recovery
thereof.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Void transactions

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Amounts that a spouse, former spouse
  or former common-law partner is or may become entitled to have
  transferred under section 8 are not capable of being assigned,
  charged, anticipated or given as security, and any transaction that
  purports to assign, charge, anticipate or give as security any such
  amount is void.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from attachment, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Amounts that a spouse, former spouse or former
  common-law partner is or may become entitled to have transferred under
  section 8 are exempt from attachment, seizure and execution, either at
  law or in equity.

::: HistoricalNote

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding any other provision
  of this Act or the provisions of any pension plan or Act under which a
  pension plan is established or by which a pension plan is provided,

  partner or former common-law partner of that member to make an

  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), Part II of the [Canadian Forces
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17), the [Public Service Pension

## [Offence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-414299 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
knowingly makes a false or misleading representation to the Minister in
any application or other proceeding under this Act is guilty of an
offence punishable on summary conviction.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-414302 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, on the recommendation of the President of the Treasury Board, make
regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the circumstances in which a person may
  make an application under subsection 4(1) on behalf of another person
  or may act on behalf of another person in prosecuting an application
  that has been made by that other person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the circumstances in which the personal
  representative of a deceased member of a pension plan or of a deceased
  spouse, former spouse or former common-law partner may make an
  application under subsection 4(1) or may proceed with an application
  that was made by the member, spouse, former spouse or former
  common-law partner;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where regulations are made under paragraph (a) or
  (b), respecting the manner in which and the extent to which any
  provision of this Act applies to a person referred to in that
  paragraph or in the circumstances prescribed by those regulations, and
  adapting any provision of this Act to those persons or circumstances;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the withdrawal of applications;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of paragraph 8(1)(a), for
  determining, on the basis of generally accepted actuarial principles,
  the value of the pension benefits that have accrued to a member of a
  pension plan during the period subject to division;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of paragraph 8(1)(b), respecting the
  adjustment of the pension benefits that have accrued to a member of a
  pension plan under that pension plan;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsection 8(4), requiring
  interest to be paid on lump sum amounts and prescribing the rate of
  interest or the manner of determining the rate of interest;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsection 8(6), for determining
  the effective date of the adjustment of pension benefits;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} respecting the manner in which and the extent to
  which any provision of a pension plan or an Act under which a pension
  plan is established or by which a pension plan is provided, as amended
  from time to time, applies, notwithstanding the provisions of that
  pension plan or Act, to any member, spouse, former spouse, common-law
  partner, former common-law partner or other person in any case where a
  division of pension benefits is effected under section 8, and adapting
  any provision of that pension plan or Act to those persons;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} respecting the charging of amounts payable under this
  Act to the Consolidated Revenue Fund, to a fund as defined in section
  2 of the Public Sector Investment Board Act or to any account or
  accounts in the accounts of Canada;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} respecting the provision of information to a spouse,
  former spouse, common-law partner or former common-law partner of a
  member of a pension plan under subsection 13(2);

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} prescribing any matter or thing that by this Act is
  to be or may be prescribed; and

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} generally as the Governor in Council may consider
  necessary for carrying out the purposes and provisions of this Act.
