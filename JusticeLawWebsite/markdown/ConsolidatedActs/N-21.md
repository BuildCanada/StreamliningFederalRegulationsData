## Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council Act {#natural-sciences-and-engineering-research-council-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. N-21
:::

An Act to establish the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research
Council
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364274 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council
Act](/eng/acts/N-21).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1976-77, c. 24, s. 24
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364279 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Natural Sciences and
    Engineering Research Council established by section
    3; ([Conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada as is designated by the Governor in Council
    as the Minister for the purposes of this
    Act; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[President]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[President]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the President of the Council
    appointed under subsection 5(1); ([président]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Vice-President]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Vice-President]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Vice-President of
    the Council elected under subsection
    6(1). ([vice-président]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- 1976-77, c. 24, s. 25
:::

## [Constitution of the Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364288 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment and membership of Council

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** There is established a
corporation, to be called the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research
Council, consisting of a president and not more than 18 other members to
be appointed by the Governor in Council as provided in section 5.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-21, s. 3
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1761
:::

## [Functions and Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364293 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Functions of Council

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  functions of the Council are to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} promote and assist research in the natural sciences
    and engineering, other than the health sciences; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} advise the Minister in respect of such matters
    relating to such research as the Minister may refer to the Council
    for its consideration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Council

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Council, in carrying out its functions under
  subsection (1), may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} expend, for the purposes of this Act, any money
    appropriated by Parliament for the work of the Council or received
    by the Council through the conduct of its operations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} publish and sell or otherwise distribute such
    scholarly, scientific and technical information relating to the work
    of the Council as the Council considers necessary.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1976-77, c. 24, s. 27
:::

## [Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364305 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of President

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President of the Council shall be appointed to hold office for such
  term, not exceeding five years, as the Governor in Council may
  determine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each of the members of the Council, other than the
  President, shall be appointed to hold office for such term, not
  exceeding three years, as will ensure as far as possible the
  expiration in any one year of the terms of appointment of fewer than
  half of the members so appointed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility for re-appointment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A retiring President or other member is eligible for
  re-appointment to the Council in the same or another capacity.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1976-77, c. 24, s. 28
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vice-President

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Council may, with the approval of the Governor in Council, elect one
  of its members to be Vice-President of the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Vice-President

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  President or if the office of President is vacant, the Vice-President
  has all the powers of the President.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1976-77, c. 24, s. 29
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salary and expenses of President

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President shall be paid such salary and expenses as are fixed by the
  Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and expenses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Council, other than the President,
  shall serve without remuneration but each of those members is entitled
  to be paid reasonable travel and other expenses incurred by the member
  while absent from his ordinary place of residence in the course of his
  duties under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration of members for additional
  duties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (2), a member of the
  Council, other than the President, may, for any period during which
  the member performs with the approval of the Council any duties on
  behalf of the Council in addition to the ordinary duties of the member
  as such, be paid such remuneration therefor as may be authorized by
  the Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1976-77, c. 24, s. 30
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the
Council shall be at such place in Canada as may be designated by the
Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1976-77, c. 24, s. 31
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Council may meet at
such times and at such places in Canada as it considers necessary but it
shall meet at least twice a year.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1976-77, c. 24, s. 32
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority of President

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The President is the
chief executive officer of the Council and has supervision over and
direction of the work and staff of the Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1976-77, c. 24, s. 33
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Executive committee

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  is hereby established an executive committee of the Council consisting
  of the President, the Vice-President and at least six other members
  selected by the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of executive committee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The executive committee of the Council may exercise
  such of the powers and perform such of the functions of the Council as
  the Council may by by-law assign to it and shall submit at each
  meeting of the Council minutes of its proceedings since the last
  preceding meeting of the Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1976-77, c. 24, s. 34
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the approval of the
  Governor in Council, the Council may make by-laws for the regulation
  of its proceedings and generally for the conduct of its activities,
  including the establishment of special, standing and other committees
  of the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advisory committees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any by-law made pursuant to subsection (1)
  establishing an advisory committee of the Council may provide for the
  members of the committee to include persons other than members of the
  Council, in addition to members of the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration of advisory committee
  members

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The members of an advisory committee who are not
  members of the Council may be paid for their services such
  remuneration and expenses as are fixed by the Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1976-77, c. 24, s. 35
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of staff

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Council may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint such officers and employees as are
    necessary for the proper conduct of the work of the Council; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribe the duties of those officers and
    employees and the conditions of their employment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salaries and expenses of staff

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The officers and employees of the Council appointed
  as provided in subsection (1) shall be paid such salaries and expenses
  as are fixed by the Council with the approval of the Governor in
  Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary appointments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Council may authorize the President or any other
  officer of the Council to appoint persons to perform duties of a
  temporary nature for a period not exceeding six months.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1976-77, c. 24, s. 36
- 1978-79, c. 13, s. 29
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Council agent of Her Majesty

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Council is for all its purposes an agent of Her Majesty and it may
  exercise its powers only as an agent of Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contracts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Council may on behalf of Her Majesty enter into
  contracts in the name of Her Majesty or in its own name.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any property acquired by the Council is the property
  of Her Majesty and title thereto may be vested in the name of Her
  Majesty or in the name of the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Actions, suits or other legal proceedings in respect
  of any right or obligation acquired or incurred by the Council on
  behalf of Her Majesty, whether in its name or in the name of Her
  Majesty, may be brought or taken by or against the Council in the name
  of the Council in any court that would have jurisdiction if the
  Council were not an agent of Her Majesty.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1976-77, c. 24, s. 37
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of [Public Service
Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36)

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The [Public Service
Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) does not apply to members of the
Council unless, in the case of any such member, the Governor in Council
otherwise directs.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1976-77, c. 24, s. 38
:::

## [Donations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364385 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Donations to Council

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Council may acquire
money, securities or other property by gift, bequest or otherwise and,
notwithstanding anything in this Act, may expend, administer or dispose
of the money, securities or other property subject to the terms, if any,
on which the money, securities or other property is given, bequeathed or
otherwise made available to the Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-21, s. 16
- 2001, c. 4, s. 110(F)
- 2004, c. 25, s. 166(F)
:::

## [Report to Parliament]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364391 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President shall, within four months after the end of each fiscal year,
  submit to the Minister a report on the activities of the Council for
  that fiscal year, including the financial statements of the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause the report submitted under
  subsection (1) to be laid before Parliament within fifteen days after
  the receipt thereof by the Minister or, if Parliament is not then
  sitting, on any of the first fifteen days next thereafter that either
  House of Parliament is sitting.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-21, s. 18
- 2012, c. 19, s. 175
