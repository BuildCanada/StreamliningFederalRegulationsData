## Charities Registration (Security Information) Act {#charities-registration-security-information-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2001, c. 41, s. 113
:::

Assented to 2001-12-24

An Act respecting the registration of charities having regard to
security and criminal intelligence information

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 113 of chapter 41 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001,
in force December 24, 2001, see SI/2002-16.\]
:::
:::::

::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-79939 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Charities Registration (Security Information)
Act](/eng/acts/C-27.55).

## [Purpose and Principles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-79942 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  purpose of this Act is to demonstrate Canada's commitment to
  participating in concerted international efforts to deny support to
  those who engage in terrorist activities, to protect the integrity of
  the registration system for charities under the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) and to maintain the confidence of Canadian
  taxpayers that the benefits of charitable registration are made
  available only to organizations that operate exclusively for
  charitable purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principles

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Act shall be carried out in recognition of, and
  in accordance with, the following principles:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintaining the confidence of taxpayers may require
    reliance on information that, if disclosed, would injure national
    security or endanger the safety of persons; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the process for relying on the information referred
    to in paragraph (a) in determining eligibility to become or remain a
    registered charity must be as fair and transparent as possible
    having regard to national security and the safety of persons.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-79950 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation, an
    organization or a trust that applies to the Minister of National
    Revenue to become a registered charity.
     ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means security or criminal
    intelligence information and information that is obtained in
    confidence from a source in Canada, from the government of a foreign
    state, from an international organization of states or from an
    institution of such a government or
    organization. ([renseignements]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chief Justice of the Federal
    Court or a judge of that Court designated by the Chief
    Justice. ([juge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Public Safety
    and Emergency Preparedness. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registered charity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registered charity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registered
    charity as defined in subsection 248(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).  ([organisme de bienfaisance
    enregistré]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 41, ss. 113 "3", 125, 142
- 2005, c. 10, s. 34

## [Certificate Based on Intelligence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-79960 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signature by Ministers

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister and the Minister of National Revenue may sign a certificate
  stating that it is their opinion, based on information, that there are
  reasonable grounds to believe

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that an applicant or registered charity has made,
    makes or will make available any resources, directly or indirectly,
    to an entity that is a listed entity as defined in subsection
    83.01(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that an applicant or registered charity made
    available any resources, directly or indirectly, to an entity as
    defined in subsection 83.01(1) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46) and the entity was at that time, and continues
    to be, engaged in terrorist activities as defined in that subsection
    or activities in support of them; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that an applicant or registered charity makes or
    will make available any resources, directly or indirectly, to an
    entity as defined in subsection 83.01(1) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46) and the entity engages or will engage in
    terrorist activities as defined in that subsection or activities in
    support of them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A certificate is not a statutory instrument for the
  purposes of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 41, ss. 113 "4", 125
:::

## [Judicial Consideration of Certificate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-79971 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As soon
  as the Minister and the Minister of National Revenue have signed a
  certificate, the Minister, or a person authorized by the Minister,
  shall cause the applicant or registered charity to be served,
  personally or by registered letter sent to its last known address,
  with a copy of the certificate and a notice informing it that the
  certificate will be referred to the Federal Court not earlier than
  seven days after service and that, if the certificate is determined to
  be reasonable, the applicant will be ineligible to become a registered
  charity or the registration of the registered charity will be revoked,
  as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The certificate and any matters arising out of it are
  not subject to review or to be restrained, prohibited, removed, set
  aside or otherwise dealt with, except in accordance with this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-publication or confidentiality
  order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (2), the applicant or
  registered charity may apply to a judge for an order

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} directing that the identity of the applicant or
    registered charity not be published or broadcast in any way except
    in accordance with this Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that any documents to be filed with the Federal
    Court in connection with the reference be treated as confidential.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No appeal

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An order on an application referred to in
  subsection (3) is not subject to appeal or review by any court at the
  instance of a party to the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing in Federal Court

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Seven days after service under subsection (1), or as
  soon afterwards as is practicable, the Minister or a person authorized
  by the Minister shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} file a copy of the certificate in the Federal Court
    for it to make a determination under section 7; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cause the applicant or registered charity to be
    served, personally or by registered letter sent to its last known
    address, with a notice informing it of the filing of the
    certificate.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 41, ss. 113 "5", 125
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial consideration

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following provisions govern the determination:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any information or evidence the disclosure of which
    would be injurious to national security shall be returned to the
    Ministers and shall not be considered by the judge in determining
    whether the certificate is reasonable if either

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the judge determines that the information or
      evidence is not relevant or, if it is relevant, that it should be
      part of a summary of the information or evidence provided to the
      applicant or the registered charity, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the matter is withdrawn;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the judge shall provide the applicant or registered
    charity with an opportunity to be heard; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the judge may receive into evidence anything that,
    in the opinion of the judge, is reliable and appropriate, even if it
    is inadmissible in a court of law, and may base the decision on that
    evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, sections 38.2 to 38.45 of the
  [Canada Evidence Act](/eng/acts/C-5) apply to the determination.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 41, ss. 113 "6", 125
- [2024, c. 16, s. 94](#1461091-1486847){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1461091-1486847.html"
  role="button"}
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-6-20030101.html "Link to previous version of section 6"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination whether certificate is
reasonable

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  judge shall determine whether the certificate is reasonable on the
  basis of the information and evidence available.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate quashed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The judge shall quash a certificate if the judge is
  of the opinion that it is not reasonable.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 41, ss. 113 "7", 125
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of determination

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  certificate that is determined to be reasonable under subsection 7(1)
  is conclusive proof that, in the case of an applicant, it is
  ineligible to become a registered charity or, in the case of a
  registered charity, that it does not comply with the requirements to
  continue to be a registered charity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No appeal or review

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The determination of the judge is final and is not
  subject to appeal or judicial review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, without delay after a certificate
  is determined to be reasonable, cause the certificate to be published
  in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 41, ss. 113 "8", 125
:::

## [Review of Certificate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80022 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial review

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant or former registered charity in relation to which a
  certificate was determined to be reasonable under subsection 7(1) and
  that believes that there has been a material change in circumstances
  since the determination made under that subsection may apply in
  writing to the Minister for a review of the certificate by the
  Minister and the Minister of National Revenue.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister of National Revenue

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, without delay, notify the
  Minister of National Revenue of an application for review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Material to be considered

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of a review, the Ministers may
  consider any submission made by the applicant or former registered
  charity that applied for the review and any information that is made
  available to the Ministers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time for decision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Ministers shall make their decision on an
  application for review within 120 days after receipt of the
  application by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision on review

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Ministers may decide that, since the time the
  certificate was determined to be reasonable,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there has not been a material change in
    circumstances, in which case the Ministers shall deny the
    application; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there has been a material change in circumstances,
    in which case the Ministers shall determine whether there are
    reasonable grounds as provided in subsection 4(1) and, accordingly,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} continue the certificate in effect, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} cancel the certificate as of the date of the
      decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Automatic cancellation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If no decision is made within a period of 120 days
  after receipt of the application, the certificate is cancelled on the
  expiration of that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to applicant or charity

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} As soon as a decision is made or the certificate is
  cancelled under subsection (6), the Minister or a person authorized by
  the Minister shall cause the applicant or former registered charity
  that applied for the review to be served, personally or by registered
  letter sent to its last known address, with notice of the decision or
  cancellation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 41, ss. 113 "10", 125
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for review

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant or former registered charity that applied for a review under
  subsection 10(1) may, after giving written notice to the Minister who
  in turn shall notify the Minister of National Revenue, apply to the
  Federal Court for a review of a decision made under paragraph 10(5)(a)
  or subparagraph 10(5)(b)(i).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review by Court

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Court shall carry out the review in accordance
  with section 6, with any adaptations that may be required.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Referral to Ministers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Court quashes a decision of the Ministers made
  under paragraph 10(5)(a), it shall refer the application to the
  Ministers for a decision under paragraph 10(5)(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of certificate

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Court quashes a decision of the Ministers made
  under subparagraph 10(5)(b)(i), the certificate is cancelled as of the
  date the decision is quashed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No appeal

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The determination of the Court is not subject to
  appeal or judicial review.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of spent certificate

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall, in a
manner that mentions the original publication of the certificate, cause
to be published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/)
notice of the cancellation of a certificate by reason of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a decision made under subparagraph 10(5)(b)(ii);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the operation of subsection 10(6); or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a determination of the Federal Court referred to in
  subsection 11(4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of a certificate

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Unless it is cancelled
earlier, a certificate is effective for a period of seven years
beginning on the day it is first determined to be reasonable under
subsection 7(1).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 41, ss. 113 "13", 125
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make any regulations that the Governor in Council considers
necessary for carrying out the purposes and provisions of this Act.
