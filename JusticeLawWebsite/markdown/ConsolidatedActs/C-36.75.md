## Consumer-Driven Banking Act {#consumer-driven-banking-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2024, c. 17, s. 198
:::

Assented to 2024-06-20

An Act to establish a consumer-driven banking framework

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 198 of chapter 17 of the Statutes of Canada, 2024,
sections 4 to 7, 12 and 13 not in force.\]
:::
:::::

::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1477666 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Consumer-Driven Banking Act](/eng/acts/C-36.75).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1477669 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Financial Consumer Agency
    of Canada established under section 3 of the [Financial Consumer
    Agency of Canada Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1). ([Agence]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[derived data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[derived data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means data about a consumer,
    product or service that has been enhanced by a participating entity
    to significantly increase its usefulness or commercial
    value. ([données dérivées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation, trust,
    partnership, fund or an unincorporated association or
    organization. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Finance. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Senior Deputy Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Senior Deputy Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Senior
    Deputy Commissioner for Consumer-Driven Banking appointed under
    subsection 7.2(1) of the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1). ([commissaire adjoint
    principal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1477677 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to establish a framework within which consumers, including small
businesses, can direct that their data be shared among participating
entities of their choice and to ensure that the sharing of data among
participating entities is safe and secure.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit --- editing data

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Data that is shared between
participating entities in accordance with this Act is to be shared in a
manner that does not enable the participating entity that receives the
data to edit the data on servers that are used by the participating
entity that provides the data.

with respect to the sharing of information about a consumer with an

## [Technical Standards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1477698 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of body

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by order, designate a body to be the technical standards
  body that is responsible for establishing the technical standards that
  are to be used for the sharing of data by participating entities in
  accordance with this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principles

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In designating the technical standards body, the
  Minister must take into account the following principles:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the need to ensure the safe, secure and efficient
    sharing of data among participating entities;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fairness, accessibility, transparency and good
    governance;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other principle that the Minister considers
    relevant; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other principle provided for in the
    regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication in [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must publish the order in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must review
the designation every three years.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by order, revoke the designation, including in the
  following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Senior Deputy Commissioner advises the Minister
    to do so;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is of the opinion that the designation
    is no longer consistent with the principles referred to in
    subsection 8(2);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is of the opinion that the designation
    poses a risk to national security; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is of the opinion that the designation
    poses a risk to the integrity or security of the financial system in
    Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication in [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must publish the order in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The [Statutory
Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does not apply to an order made under
subsection 8(1) or 10(1).

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1477727 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claiming to be participating entity

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** An individual or entity,
other than a participating entity, must not

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} use the term "participating entity" or a variation,
  abbreviation or equivalent of that term, or any words, name or
  designation --- in any language --- in a manner that leads to a
  reasonable belief that the individual or entity is a participating
  entity for the purposes of this Act; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} represent themselves, in any way or by any means, to
  be a participating entity for the purposes of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False or misleading information

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** An individual or entity
must not knowingly provide false or misleading information in relation
to their participation under this Act.

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1477734 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Every individual or
entity who contravenes section 14 or 15 is guilty of an offence and is
liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine of not more
    than \$1,000,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than five
    years, or to both, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an entity, to a fine of not more
    than \$5,000,000; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine of not more
    than \$100,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than one
    year, or to both, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an entity, to a fine of not more
    than \$500,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order to comply

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  individual or entity is convicted of an offence under this Act, the
  court may, in addition to any punishment that it may otherwise impose,
  order the individual or entity to comply with the provisions of this
  Act or the regulations in respect of which the individual or entity
  was convicted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional fine

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an individual or entity is convicted of an offence
  under this Act, the court may, if it is satisfied that as a result of
  the commission of the offence the convicted individual or entity
  acquired any monetary benefits or that monetary benefits accrued to
  the convicted individual or entity or the individual's spouse,
  common-law partner or other dependant, order the convicted individual
  or entity to pay, despite the maximum amount of any fine that may
  otherwise be imposed under this Act, an additional fine in an amount
  equal to three times the court's estimation of the amount of those
  monetary benefits.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Party to offence

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** If an entity commits an
offence under this Act, any director, any officer, any agent or
mandatary or any principal officer of the entity who directed,
authorized, assented to, acquiesced in or participated in the commission
of the offence is a party to and guilty of the offence and is liable on
summary conviction or on conviction on indictment to the punishment
provided for an individual in respect of the offence, whether or not the
entity has been prosecuted or convicted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Proceedings by way of summary
  conviction in respect of an offence under this Act may be commenced at
  any time within, but not later than, two years after the day on which
  the subject matter of the proceedings became known to the Senior
  Deputy Commissioner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of Senior Deputy
  Commissioner

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document appearing to have been issued by the
  Senior Deputy Commissioner, certifying the day on which the subject
  matter of any proceedings became known to the Senior Deputy
  Commissioner, is admissible in evidence without proof of the signature
  or official character of the person appearing to have signed it and
  is, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, proof of the matter
  asserted in it.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1477755 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, on the recommendation of the Minister, make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} providing for data for the purposes of subsection
  4(1);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} providing for products and services for the purposes
  of paragraph 4(1)(e);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} providing for principles for the purposes of
  paragraph 8(2)(d); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the annual report referred to in section
  12, including the information that is to be included in the report and
  the form and manner in which, and the time within which, it is to be
  submitted.
