## Canada--European Union Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement Implementation Act {#canadaeuropean-union-comprehensive-economic-and-trade-agreement-implementation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2017, c. 6
:::

Assented to 2017-05-16

An Act to implement the Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement
between Canada and the European Union and its Member States and to
provide for certain other measures
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-96263 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada--European Union Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement
Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-4.8).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-96266 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this section and in sections 3 to 14.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Comprehensive Economic
    and Trade Agreement between Canada and the European Union and its
    Member States, done at Brussels on October 30,
    2016. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[CETA Joint Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CETA Joint Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the CETA Joint
    Committee established under Article 26.1 of the Agreement. ([Comité
    mixte de l'AÉCG]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the whole or any portion
    of an Act of Parliament or a regulation, order or other instrument
    issued, made or established in the exercise of a power conferred by
    or under an Act of Parliament. ([texte législatif
    fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister for
    International Trade. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation consistent with Agreement

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, this
Act and any federal law that implements a provision of the Agreement or
fulfils an obligation of the Government of Canada under the Agreement is
to be interpreted in a manner consistent with the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of Act and Agreement to
water

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act or the Agreement, except Chapters Twenty-Two and
Twenty-Four of the Agreement, applies to natural surface or ground water
in liquid, gaseous or solid state.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act, by specific mention or omission, is to be construed
to affect in any manner the right of Parliament to enact legislation to
implement any provision of the Agreement or fulfil any of the
obligations of the Government of Canada under the Agreement.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-96279 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-96282 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to implement the Agreement, the objectives of which, as elaborated more
specifically through its provisions, are to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish a free trade area in accordance with the
  Agreement;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} promote, through the expansion of reciprocal trade,
  the harmonious development of the economic relations between Canada
  and the European Union in order to create opportunities for economic
  development;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} promote conditions of fair competition affecting
  trade between Canada and the European Union;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} substantially increase investment opportunities in
  Canada and the European Union, while preserving the right of each of
  the parties to the Agreement to regulate to achieve legitimate policy
  goals;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} eliminate barriers to trade in goods and services in
  order to contribute to the harmonious development and expansion of
  world and regional trade;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} provide adequate and effective protection and
  enforcement of intellectual property rights in the territory where the
  Agreement applies;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} protect, enhance and enforce basic workers' rights,
  strengthen cooperation on labour matters, and build on the respective
  international commitments of Canada and the European Union on labour
  matters;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} enhance and enforce environmental laws and
  regulations and strengthen cooperation between Canada and the European
  Union on environmental matters; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} promote sustainable development.

  those specialized committees and to members of those bilateral
  dialogues, working groups and other bodies.

### [Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-96328 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders re Article 29.14 of Agreement

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, for the purpose of suspending obligations in
  accordance with Article 29.14 of the Agreement, by order, do any one
  or more of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend rights or privileges granted by Canada to
    the European Union and its member states or to goods, service
    suppliers, investors or investments of investors of the European
    Union and its member states under the Agreement or any federal law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} modify or suspend the application of any federal
    law with respect to the European Union and its member states or to
    goods, service suppliers, investors or investments of investors of
    the European Union and its member states;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} extend the application of any federal law to the
    European Union and its member states or to goods, service suppliers,
    investors or investments of investors of the European Union and its
    member states;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} take any other measure that the Governor in Council
    considers necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless repealed, an order made under subsection (1)
  has effect for the period specified in the order.
