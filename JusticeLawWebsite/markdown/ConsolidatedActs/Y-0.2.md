## Yale First Nation Final Agreement Act {#yale-first-nation-final-agreement-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2013, c. 25
:::

Assented to 2013-06-19

An Act to give effect to the Yale First Nation Final Agreement and to
make consequential amendments to other Acts
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/) recognizes and affirms
the existing Aboriginal and treaty rights of the Aboriginal peoples of
Canada;

Whereas the reconciliation between the prior presence of Aboriginal
peoples and the assertion of sovereignty by the Crown is of significant
social and economic importance to Canadians;

Whereas Canadian courts have stated that this reconciliation is best
achieved through negotiation;

Whereas the Yale First Nation, the Government of Canada and the
Government of British Columbia have negotiated the Agreement to achieve
this reconciliation and to establish a new relationship among them;

And whereas the Agreement requires that legislation be enacted by the
Parliament of Canada in order for the Agreement to be ratified;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-469948 .Part}

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Yale First Nation
      Final Agreement, between the Yale First Nation, Her Majesty in
      right of Canada and Her Majesty in right of British Columbia,
      including any amendments made to it. ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Tax Treatment Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Tax Treatment Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the tax
      treatment agreement referred to in 21.6.1 of the Agreement,
      including any amendments made to it. ([accord sur le traitement
      fiscal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions in Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Act, [[Yale First
  Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Yale First Nation
  corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Yale First Nation
  Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Yale First Nation
  land]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Yale First Nation
  law]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[Yale First Nation
  member]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[Yale First Nation public
  institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} have the same meanings as in
  Chapter 1 of the Agreement.
::::

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of Agreement

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement is a treaty
and a land claims agreement within the meaning of sections 25 and 35 of
the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).
::::

## [Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-469958 .Part}

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement given effect

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement is approved, given effect and declared valid and has the
  force of law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and obligations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, any person or body has the
  powers, rights, privileges and benefits conferred on the person or
  body by the Agreement and must perform the duties, and is subject to
  the liabilities, imposed on the person or body by the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Third parties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Agreement is binding on,
  and may be relied on by, all persons and bodies.
::::

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency with Agreement

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement prevails over this Act and any other federal law to the
  extent of any inconsistency between them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict with Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Act prevails over any other federal law to the
  extent of any conflict between them.
::::

## [Application of Other Acts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-469987 .Part}

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the provisions
of Chapter 22 of the Agreement that deal with the continuing application
of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) and to 21.5.1 to 21.5.6 of the
Agreement, the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) does not apply to the Yale
First Nation, Yale First Nation members, the Yale First Nation
Government, Yale First Nation public institutions or Yale First Nation
corporations as of the effective date of the Agreement, except for the
purpose of determining whether an individual is an Indian.
::::

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Yale First Nation laws
and other instruments made under the Agreement are not statutory
instruments for the purposes of the [Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22).
::::

## [Application of Laws of British Columbia]{.HTitleText1} {#h-469992 .Part}

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** To the extent that a law
of British Columbia does not apply of its own force to the Yale First
Nation, Yale First Nation members, Yale First Nation land, the Yale
First Nation Government, Yale First Nation public institutions or Yale
First Nation corporations, because of the exclusive legislative
authority of Parliament set out in Class 24 of section 91 of the
[Constitution Act, 1867](/eng/Const/), that law of British Columbia
applies to it or to them by virtue of this section, in accordance with
the Agreement and subject to this Act and any other Act of Parliament.
::::

  by the Queen's Printer is deemed to be so published, unless the
  contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chapters 25 and 26 of Agreement

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsection 4(1),
Chapters 25 and 26 of the Agreement are deemed to have effect as of
February 5, 2010.

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of issues arising

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  issue arises in any judicial or administrative proceeding in respect
  of the interpretation or validity of the Agreement, or the validity or
  applicability of this Act, the British Columbia [Yale First Nation
  Final Agreement Act](/eng/acts/Y-0.2) or any Yale First Nation law,
  then the issue must not be decided until the party raising the issue
  has served notice on the Attorney General of Canada, the Attorney
  General of British Columbia and the Yale First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content and timing of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} describe the proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specify what the issue arises in respect of;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state the day on which the issue is to be argued;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} give particulars necessary to show the point to be
    argued; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be served at least 14 days before the day of
    argument, unless the court or tribunal authorizes a shorter period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation in proceedings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In any proceeding in respect of which subsection (1)
  applies, the Attorney General of Canada, the Attorney General of
  British Columbia and the Yale First Nation may appear and participate
  in the proceeding as parties with the same rights as any other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsections (2) and (3) do not
  require that an oral hearing be held if one is not otherwise required.
::::
