## Yukon First Nations Land Claims Settlement Act {#yukon-first-nations-land-claims-settlement-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1994, c. 34
:::

Assented to 1994-07-07

An Act to approve, give effect to and declare valid land claims
agreements entered into between Her Majesty the Queen in right of
Canada, the Government of Yukon and certain first nations in Yukon, to
provide for approving, giving effect to and declaring valid other land
claims agreements entered into after this Act comes into force, and to
make consequential amendments to other Acts
::::

::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS representatives of Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada, the
Government of the Yukon Territory and the Council for Yukon Indians
signed the Umbrella Final Agreement on May 29, 1993, the provisions of
which are intended to be incorporated into final agreements for the
settlement of land claims of first nations in the Yukon Territory;

WHEREAS the Champagne and Aishihik First Nations, the First Nation of
Nacho Nyak Dun, the Teslin Tlingit Council and the Vuntut Gwitchin First
Nation have each entered into a final agreement with Her Majesty and the
Government of the Yukon Territory, incorporating the provisions of the
Umbrella Final Agreement and including provisions specific to each first
nation;

WHEREAS agreements may be entered into with respect to aboriginal claims
to lands in British Columbia and the Northwest Territories by persons
enrolled under final agreements as well as aboriginal claims to lands in
Yukon by certain people outside that territory;

AND WHEREAS the Government of Canada has undertaken to recommend to
Parliament the enactment of legislation for approving, giving effect to
and declaring valid final agreements and transboundary agreements;
:::

:::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474088 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Yukon First Nations Land Claims Settlement Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.3).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474091 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[final agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[final agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a land claims
    agreement for a first nation that includes provisions specific to
    the first nation and incorporates the provisions of the Umbrella
    Final Agreement, and includes any amendments made to it from time to
    time in accordance with its provisions; ([accord
    définitif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[first nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[first nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a first nation named in
    the schedule; ([première nation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[settlement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[settlement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means land identified in a
    first nation's final agreement as settlement land of the first
    nation; ([terres désignées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transboundary agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transboundary agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    transboundary agreement as defined in final agreements, and includes
    any amendments made to it from time to time in accordance with its
    provisions; ([accord transfrontalier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Umbrella Final Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Umbrella Final Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Umbrella
    Final Agreement signed on May 29, 1993 by representatives of the
    Council for Yukon Indians, Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada
    and the Government of the Yukon Territory, and includes any
    amendments made to it from time to time in accordance with its
    provisions. ([accord-cadre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474099 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Land Claims Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474102 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Four final agreements given effect

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Each of the following final
agreements entered into between Her Majesty the Queen in right of
Canada, the Government of the Yukon Territory and the respective first
nation, signed on May 29, 1993, is hereby approved, given effect and
declared valid:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Champagne and Aishihik First Nations Final
  Agreement;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the First Nation of Nacho Nyak Dun Final Agreement;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Teslin Tlingit Council Final Agreement; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Vuntut Gwitchin First Nation Final Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Future agreements

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, by order, approve, give effect to and declare
  valid any final agreement or transboundary agreement entered into
  after this Act comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An order approving a final agreement or transboundary
  agreement shall be laid before the House of Commons on any of the
  first thirty days on which that House is sitting after the order is
  made.

## [Effect of Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474114 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/)

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A final
  agreement or transboundary agreement that is in effect is a land
  claims agreement within the meaning of section 35 of the [Constitution
  Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Third parties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, such an agreement is binding
  on all persons and bodies that are not parties to it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Title to lands

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, a
first nation for which a final agreement is in effect has the rights,
title, obligations and liabilities in respect of settlement land
provided for in the final agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and duties

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, any
person or body has the powers, rights, privileges and benefits conferred
on the person or body by a final agreement or transboundary agreement
that is in effect and shall perform the duties and is subject to the
liabilities imposed on the person or body by the agreement.

## [Boards, Commissions and Councils]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474124 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bodies constituted

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of carrying out their objectives, the Yukon Land Use Planning
  Council, the Yukon Heritage Resources Board, the Yukon Geographical
  Place Names Board, the Fish and Wildlife Management Board and its
  salmon subcommittee, and the Dispute Resolution Board, established
  under final agreements, each have the capacity, rights, powers and
  privileges of a natural person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Renewable resources councils

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of carrying out its objectives, a
  renewable resources council established under a final agreement that
  is in effect has the capacity, rights, powers and privileges of a
  natural person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Kluane National Park Management Board

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of carrying out its objectives, the
  Kluane National Park Management Board established under the Champagne
  and Aishihik First Nations Final Agreement has the capacity, rights,
  powers and privileges of a natural person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other bodies

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of carrying out its objectives, any
  body established under a final agreement or transboundary agreement
  entered into after this Act comes into force has, to the extent
  provided by the agreement, the capacity, rights, powers and privileges
  of a natural person from the day the agreement is given effect.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of Enrollment Commission

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Enrollment Commission is deemed to have had,
  since it was established, the jurisdiction, power and authority
  provided by final agreements, other than the powers referred to in

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to compel evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Enrollment Commission has the powers provided in
  the Public Inquiries Act (Yukon), as amended from time to time, to
  direct and compel the production of documents and the attendance of
  witnesses, other than a minister of the Crown in right of Canada or a
  member of the Executive Council, as defined in the [Interpretation
  Act](/eng/acts/I-21) (Yukon), as amended from time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An order or decision of the Enrollment Commission
  made before or after this Act comes into force may be filed in the
  Supreme Court of Yukon, and when so filed may be enforced as an order
  of that Court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enrollment committees

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An enrollment committee established by a first nation
  has the powers required to carry out its responsibilities as referred
  to in final agreements.

## [Application of Laws and Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474148 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** When a final agreement is
given effect, the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) ceases to apply in respect
of any reserve, within the meaning of that Act, that is identified in
the agreement as settlement land.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laws of general application

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  federal and territorial laws, including the [Yukon First Nations
  Self-Government Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.6), apply to a first nation for
  which a final agreement is in effect, to persons enrolled under such
  an agreement and in respect of settlement land of the first nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of agreements with laws

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of a conflict or inconsistency between a
  final agreement or transboundary agreement that is in effect and any
  federal or territorial law, including this Act, the agreement prevails
  to the extent of the conflict or inconsistency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of Act with other Acts

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the event of a conflict or inconsistency between
  this Act and any other enactment, this Act prevails to the extent of
  the conflict or inconsistency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict within final agreement

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the event of a conflict or inconsistency between
  provisions of the Umbrella Final Agreement incorporated in a final
  agreement that is in effect and provisions of the final agreement that
  are specific to the first nation, the provisions of the Umbrella Final
  Agreement prevail to the extent of the conflict or inconsistency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict with I.F.A.

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In the event of a conflict or inconsistency between
  the Inuvialuit Final Agreement, as it read on March 31, 1993, and a
  final agreement or transboundary agreement that is in effect, the
  Inuvialuit Final Agreement prevails to the extent of the conflict or
  inconsistency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation of this Act

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where there is doubt as to the meaning of any
  provision of this Act, a final agreement or transboundary agreement
  that is in effect may be examined as an aid to interpretation.

## [Appropriation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474164 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of C.R.F.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** There shall be paid out
of the Consolidated Revenue Fund the sums that are required to meet the
monetary obligations of Canada under chapter 19 of each final agreement
that is given effect by section 4, and under the corresponding
provisions of each final agreement that is given effect under section 5.

## [Deposit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474167 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements and amendments

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Indian
Affairs and Northern Development shall cause a certified copy of each
final agreement and transboundary agreement that is given effect by or
under this Act, and of any amendments made to such an agreement, to be
deposited in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Library and Archives of Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the library of the Department of Indian Affairs and
  Northern Development situated in the National Capital Region;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} such regional offices of the Government of Canada
  situated in Yukon as the Minister considers advisable; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} such other places as the Minister considers
  advisable.

## [Consultation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474176 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Consultations referred to
in clause 20.6.3 of a final agreement in connection with measures
necessary for the purpose of giving effect to clause 20.6.1 or 20.6.2 of
the agreement shall be carried out in the manner provided by the
agreement.

## [Powers of Governor in Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-474179 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make such orders and regulations as are necessary for the purpose of
carrying out any provision of a final agreement or transboundary
agreement that is in effect.
