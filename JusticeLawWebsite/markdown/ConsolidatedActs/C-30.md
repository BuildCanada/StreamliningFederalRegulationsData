## Canada-United Kingdom Civil and Commercial Judgments Convention Act {#canada-united-kingdom-civil-and-commercial-judgments-convention-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. C-30
:::

An Act to implement a convention between Canada and the United Kingdom
of Great Britain and Northern Ireland providing for the reciprocal
recognition and enforcement of judgments in civil and commercial matters
::::

:::::::::::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada-United Kingdom Civil and Commercial Judgments Convention
Act](/eng/acts/C-30).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1984, c. 32, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Convention approved

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Convention entered into
between the Government of Canada and the Government of the United
Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland, set out in the schedule,
is approved and declared to have the force of law in Canada during such
period as, by its terms, the Convention is in force.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1984, c. 32, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistent laws

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In the event of any
inconsistency between the provisions of this Act, or the Convention, and
the provisions of any other law, the provisions of this Act and the
Convention prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1984, c. 32, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Justice may
make such regulations as are necessary for the purpose of carrying out
the Convention or for giving effect to any of the provisions thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1984, c. 32, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Promulgation of dates

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Notice of the day the
Convention comes into force and of the day it ceases to be effective
shall be given by proclamation of the Governor in Council published in
the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1984, c. 32, s. 5
:::

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Convention Between Canada and the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland Providing for the Reciprocal Recognition and Enforcement of Judgments in Civil and Commercial Matters]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-85021 .scheduleLabel}
:::

The Government of Canada and the Government of Great Britain and
Northern Ireland,

Desiring to provide on the basis of reciprocity for the recognition and
enforcement of judgments in civil and commercial matters,

Have agreed as follows:

## **PART I** Definitions {#part-i-definitions .SchedHeadL1}

### Article I {#article-i .SchedHeadL2}

In this Convention

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any
  proceeding by way of discharging or setting aside a judgment or an
  application for a new trial or a stay of execution;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [[the 1968 Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
  Convention of 27th September 1968 on Jurisdiction and the Enforcement
  of Judgments in Civil and Commercial Matters as amended;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[court of a Contracting State]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the United Kingdom, any court of the
    United Kingdom or of any territory to which this Convention extends
    pursuant to Article XIII;

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in relation to Canada, the Federal Court of Canada
    or any court of a province or territory to which this Convention
    extends pursuant to Article XII

  and the expressions [[court of the United
  Kingdom]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[court of
  Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} shall be construed accordingly;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[judgment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any decision,
  however described (judgment, order and the like), given by a court in
  a civil or commercial matter, and includes an award in proceedings on
  an arbitration if the award has become enforceable in the territory of
  origin in the same manner as a judgment given by a court in that
  territory;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} [[judgment creditor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
  person in whose favour the judgment was given, and includes his
  executors, administrators, successors and assigns;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} [[judgment debtor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
  person against whom the judgment was given and includes any person
  against whom the judgment is enforceable under the law of the
  territory of origin;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} [[original court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in relation to
  any judgment means the court by which the judgment was given;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} [[registering court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
  court to which an application for the registration of a judgment is
  made;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} [[territory of origin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
  territory for which the original court was exercising jurisdiction.

## **PART II** Scope of the Convention {#part-ii-scope-of-the-convention .SchedHeadL1}

### Article II {#article-ii .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} Subject to the provisions of this Article, this
  Convention shall apply to any judgment given by a court of a
  Contracting State after the Convention enters into force and, for the
  purposes of Article IX, to any judgment given by a court of a third
  State which is party to the 1968 Convention.

- [2]{.lawlabel} This Convention shall not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} orders for the periodic payment of maintenance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the recovery of taxes, duties or charges of a like
    nature or the recovery of a fine or penalty;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} judgments given on appeal from decisions of
    tribunals other than courts;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} judgments which determine

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the status or legal capacity of natural persons;

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} custody or guardianship of infants;

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} matrimonial matters;

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} succession to or the administration of the
      estates of deceased persons;

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} bankruptcy, insolvency or the winding up of
      companies or other legal persons;

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the management of the affairs of a person not
      capable of managing his own affairs.

- [3]{.lawlabel} Part III of this Convention shall apply only to a
  judgment whereby a sum of money is made payable.

- [4]{.lawlabel} This Convention is without prejudice to any other
  remedy available to a judgment creditor for the recognition and
  enforcement in one Contracting State of a judgment given by a court of
  the other Contracting State.

## **PART III** *Enforcement of Judgments* {#part-iii-enforcement-of-judgments .SchedHeadL1}

### Article III {#article-iii .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} Where a judgment has been given by a court of one
  Contracting State, the judgment creditor may apply in accordance with
  Article VI to a court of the other Contracting State at any time
  within a period of six years after the date of the judgment (or, where
  there have been proceedings by way of appeal against the judgment,
  after the date of the last judgment given in those proceedings) to
  have the judgment registered, and on any such application the
  registering court shall, subject to such simple and rapid procedures
  as each Contracting State may prescribe and to the other provisions of
  this Convention, order the judgment to be registered.

- [2]{.lawlabel} In addition to the sum of money payable under the
  judgment of the original court including interest accrued to the date
  of registration, the judgment shall be registered for the reasonable
  costs of and incidental to registration, if any, including the costs
  of obtaining a certified copy of the judgment from the original court.

- [3]{.lawlabel} If, on an application for the registration of a
  judgment, it appears to the registering court that the judgment is in
  respect of different matters and that some, but not all, of the
  provisions of the judgment are such that if those provisions had been
  contained in separate judgments those judgments could properly have
  been registered, the judgment may be registered in respect of the
  provisions aforesaid but not in respect of any other provisions
  contained therein.

- [4]{.lawlabel} Subject to the other provisions of this Convention

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a registered judgment shall, for the purposes of
    enforcement, be of the same force and effect,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} proceedings may be taken on it, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the registering court shall have the same control
    over its enforcement,

    as if it had been a judgment originally given in the registering
    court with effect from the date of registration.

### Article IV {#article-iv .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} Registration of a judgment shall be refused or set
  aside if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the judgment has been satisfied;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the judgment is not enforceable in the territory of
    origin;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the original court is not regarded by the
    registering court as having jurisdiction;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the judgment was obtained by fraud;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} enforcement of the judgment would be contrary to
    public policy in the territory of the registering court;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the judgment is a judgment of a country or
    territory other than the territory of origin which has been
    registered in the original court or has become enforceable in the
    territory of origin in the same manner as a judgment of that court;
    or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the view of the registering court the judgment
    debtor either is entitled to immunity from the jurisdiction of that
    court or was entitled to immunity in the original court and did not
    submit to its jurisdiction.

- [2]{.lawlabel} The law of the registering court may provide that
  registration of a judgment may or shall be set aside if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the judgment debtor, being the defendant in the
    original proceedings, either was not served with the process of the
    original court or did not receive notice of those proceedings in
    sufficient time to enable him to defend the proceedings and, in
    either case, did not appear;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} another judgment has been given by a court having
    jurisdiction in the matter in dispute prior to the date of judgment
    in the original court; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the judgment is not final or an appeal is pending
    or the judgment debtor is entitled to appeal or to apply for leave
    to appeal against the judgment in the territory of origin.

- [3]{.lawlabel} If at the date of the application for registration the
  judgment of the original court has been partly satisfied, the judgment
  shall be registered only in respect of the balance remaining payable
  at that date.

- [4]{.lawlabel} A judgment shall not be enforced so long as, in
  accordance with the provisions of this Convention and the law of the
  registering court, it is competent for any party to make an
  application to have the registration of the judgment set aside or,
  where such an application is made, until the application has been
  finally determined.

### Article V {#article-v .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of Article IV(1)(c) the original court
  shall be regarded as having jurisdiction if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the judgment debtor, being a defendant in the
    original court, submitted to the jurisdiction of that court by
    voluntarily appearing in the proceedings;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the judgment debtor was plaintiff in, or
    counterclaimed in, the proceedings in the original court;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the judgment debtor, being a defendant in the
    original court, had before the commencement of the proceedings
    agreed, in respect of the subject matter of the proceedings, to
    submit to the jurisdiction of that court or of the courts of the
    territory of origin;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the judgment debtor, being a defendant in the
    original court, was at the time when the proceedings were instituted
    habitually resident in, or being a body corporate had its principal
    place of business in, the territory of origin;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the judgment debtor, being a defendant in the
    original court, had an office or place of business in the territory
    of origin and the proceedings were in respect of a transaction
    effected through or at that office or place; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the jurisdiction of the original court is otherwise
    recognised by the registering court.

- [2]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything in sub-paragraphs (d), (e)
  and (f) of paragraph (1), the original court shall not be regarded as
  having jurisdiction if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the subject matter of the proceedings was immovable
    property outside the territory of origin; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the bringing of the proceedings in the original
    court was contrary to an agreement under which the dispute in
    question was to be settled otherwise than by proceedings in the
    courts of the territory of origin.

## **PART IV** Procedures {#part-iv-procedures .SchedHeadL1}

### Article VI {#article-vi .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} Any application for the registration in the United
  Kingdom of a judgment of a court of Canada shall be made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in England and Wales, to the High Court of Justice;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in Scotland, to the Court of Session;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in Northern Ireland, to the High Court of Justice.

- [2]{.lawlabel} Any application for the registration in Canada of a
  judgment of a court of the United Kingdom shall be made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a judgment relating to a matter
    within the competence of the Federal Court of Canada, to the Federal
    Court of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other judgment, to a court of a
    province or territory designated by Canada pursuant to Article XII.

- [3]{.lawlabel} The practice and procedure governing registration
  (including notice to the judgment debtor and applications to set
  registration aside) shall, except as otherwise provided in this
  Convention, be governed by the law of the registering court.

- [4]{.lawlabel} The registering court may require that an application
  for registration be accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the judgment of the original court or a certified
    copy thereof;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a certified translation of the judgment, if given
    in a language other than the language of the territory of the
    registering court;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} proof of the notice given to the defendant in the
    original proceedings, unless this appears from the judgment; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} particulars of such other matters as may be
    required by the rules of the registering court.

### Article VII {#article-vii .SchedHeadL2}

All matters concerning

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conversion of the sum payable under a registered
  judgment into the currency of the territory of the registering court,
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the interest payable on the judgment with respect to
  the period following its registration,

  shall be determined by the law of the registering court.

## **PART V** Recognition of Judgments {#part-v-recognition-of-judgments .SchedHeadL1}

### Article VIII {#article-viii .SchedHeadL2}

Any judgment given by a court of one Contracting State for the payment
of a sum of money which could be registered under this Convention,
whether or not the judgment has been registered, and any other judgment
given by such a court, which if it were a judgment for the payment of a
sum of money could be registered under this Convention, shall, unless
registration has been or would be refused or set aside on any ground
other than that the judgment has been satisfied or could not be enforced
in the territory of origin, be recognised in a court of the other
Contracting State as conclusive between the parties thereto in all
proceedings founded on the same cause of action.

## **PART VI** Recognition and Enforcement of Third State Judgments {#part-vi-recognition-and-enforcement-of-third-state-judgments .SchedHeadL1}

### Article IX {#article-ix .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} The United Kingdom undertakes, in the circumstances
  permitted by Article 59 of the 1968 Convention, not to recognise or
  enforce under that Convention any judgment given in a third State
  which is a Party to that Convention against a person domiciled or
  habitually resident in Canada.

- [2]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an individual shall be treated as domiciled in
    Canada if and only if he is resident in Canada and the nature and
    circumstances of his residence indicate that he has a substantial
    connection with Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation or association shall be treated as
    domiciled in Canada if and only if it is incorporated or formed
    under a law in force in Canada and has a registered office there, or
    its central management and control is exercised in Canada.

## **PART VII** Final Provisions {#part-vii-final-provisions .SchedHeadL1}

### Article X {#article-x .SchedHeadL2}

This Convention shall not affect any conventions, international
instruments or reciprocal arrangements to which both Contracting States
are or will be parties and which, in relation to particular matters,
govern the recognition or enforcement of judgments.

### Article XI {#article-xi .SchedHeadL2}

Either Contracting State may, on the exchange of instruments of
ratification or at any time thereafter, declare that it will not apply
the Convention to a judgment that imposes a liability which that State
is under a treaty obligation toward any other State not to recognise or
enforce. Any such declaration shall specify the treaty containing the
obligation.

### Article XII {#article-xii .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} On the exchange of instruments of ratification, Canada
  shall designate the provinces or territories to which this Convention
  shall extend and the courts of the provinces and territories concerned
  to which application for the registration of a judgment given by a
  court of the United Kingdom may be made.

- [2]{.lawlabel} The designation by Canada may be modified by a further
  designation given at any time thereafter.

- [3]{.lawlabel} Any designation shall take effect three months after
  the date on which it is given.

### Article XIII {#article-xiii .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} The United Kingdom may at any time while this
  Convention is in force declare that this Convention shall extend to
  the Isle of Man, any of the Channel Islands, Gibraltar or the
  Sovereign Base Areas of Akrotiri and Dhekelia (being territories to
  which the 1968 Convention may be applied pursuant to Article 60 of
  that Convention).

- [2]{.lawlabel} Any declaration pursuant to paragraph (1) shall specify
  the courts of the territories to which application for the
  registration of a judgment given by a court of Canada shall be made.

- [3]{.lawlabel} Any declaration made by the United Kingdom pursuant to
  this Article may be modified by a further declaration given at any
  time thereafter.

- [4]{.lawlabel} Any declaration pursuant to this Article shall take
  effect three months after the date on which it is given.

### Article XIV {#article-xiv .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} This Convention shall be ratified; instruments of
  ratification shall be exchanged at London.

- [2]{.lawlabel} This Convention shall enter into force three months
  after the date on which instruments of ratification are exchanged.

- [3]{.lawlabel} This Convention may be terminated by notice in writing
  by either Contracting State and it shall terminate three months after
  the date of such notice.
