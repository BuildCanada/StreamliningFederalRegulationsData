## Canadian Jewish Heritage Month Act {#canadian-jewish-heritage-month-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2018, c. 5
:::

Assented to 2018-03-29

An Act respecting Canadian Jewish Heritage Month
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the Jewish population in Canada is approximately 400,000 people,
making it the fourth-largest Jewish population in the world;

Whereas the Canadian Jewish community has made significant contributions
to the growth and prosperity of Canada while overcoming tremendous
obstacles;

Whereas the month of May is meaningful for the Jewish community around
the world;

Whereas, by designating the month of May as Canadian Jewish Heritage
Month, the Parliament of Canada recognizes the important contributions
that Jewish Canadians have made to Canada's social, economic, political
and cultural fabric;

And whereas Canadian Jewish Heritage Month would provide an opportunity
to remember, celebrate and educate future generations about the
inspirational role that Jewish Canadians have played and continue to
play in communities across the country;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73087 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canadian Jewish Heritage Month Act](/eng/acts/C-18.4).

## [Canadian Jewish Heritage Month]{.HTitleText1} {#h-73090 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian Jewish Heritage Month

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Throughout Canada, in each
and every year, the month of May is to be known as "Canadian Jewish
Heritage Month".
