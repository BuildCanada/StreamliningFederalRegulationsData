## Purple Day Act {#purple-day-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2012, c. 13
:::

Assented to 2012-06-28

An Act respecting a day to increase public awareness about epilepsy
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the Parliament of Canada wishes to assist in efforts to educate
and increase awareness, among members of the public, about people living
with epilepsy;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-410313 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Purple Day Act](/eng/acts/P-38.8).

## [Purple Day]{.HTitleText1} {#h-410316 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purple Day

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Throughout Canada, in each
and every year, the 26th day of March is to be known as "Purple Day".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Wearing purple

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** On the 26th day of March,
people are encouraged to wear the colour purple to indicate their
support for people with epilepsy and to increase public awareness of
this disorder.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not a legal holiday

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
Purple Day is not a legal holiday or a non-juridical day.
