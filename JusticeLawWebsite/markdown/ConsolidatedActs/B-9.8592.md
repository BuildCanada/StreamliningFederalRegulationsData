### [Coordination with the [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5)]{.HTitleText2} {#h-39547 .Subheading}

- **[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  Bill C-45, introduced in the 1st session of the 42nd Parliament and
  entitled the [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) (referred to in this
  section and section 118 as the "other Act"), receives royal assent,
  then for the purposes of this section and sections 117 and 118,
  [[commencement day]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} means the day on which
  subsection 204(1) of the other Act comes into force.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the other Act receives royal assent, then sections
  69 to 78, subsection 79(1), section 84, subsection 85(2), sections 87,
  89 to 106 and 108 to 113 and subsection 115(1) come into force on the
  first day on which both this Act and the other Act have received royal
  assent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), if the other Act receives
  royal assent, then sections 158.02, 158.09 to 158.12, 158.15 and
  158.16 of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), as enacted by
  section 73, come into force on commencement day.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the other Act receives royal assent, then
  subsection 79(2), sections 80 to 83, subsections 85(1) and (3),
  sections 86, 88, 107 and 114, subsection 115(2) and section 116 come
  into force on commencement day.
