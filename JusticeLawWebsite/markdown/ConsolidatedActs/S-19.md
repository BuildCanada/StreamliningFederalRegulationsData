## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-440828 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 15, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-440833 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Chief Statistician]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chief Statistician]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chief
    Statistician of Canada appointed under subsection
    4(1); ([statisticien en chef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[departments of
    government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any department, board, bureau
    or other division of the Government of Canada or of the government
    of a province or any agency of either; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[identifying information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[identifying information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
    information that makes it possible to identify an individual person,
    business or organization; ([renseignement
    identificateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada as is designated by the Governor in Council
    as the Minister for the purposes of this
    Act; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person in respect of whom
    or in respect of whose activities any report or information is
    sought or provided pursuant to this
    Act. ([intéressé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Statistics Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-440843 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statistics bureau

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** There shall continue to be
a statistics bureau under the Minister, to be known as Statistics
Canada, the duties of which are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to collect, compile, analyse, abstract and publish
  statistical information relating to the commercial, industrial,
  financial, social, economic and general activities and condition of
  the people;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to collaborate with departments of government in the
  collection, compilation and publication of statistical information,
  including statistics derived from the activities of those departments;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to take the census of population of Canada and the
  census of agriculture of Canada as provided in this Act;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to promote the avoidance of duplication in the
  information collected by departments of government; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} generally, to promote and develop integrated social
  and economic statistics pertaining to the whole of Canada and to each
  of the provinces thereof and to coordinate plans for the integration
  of those statistics.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 15, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chief Statistician

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall appoint the Chief Statistician of Canada to
  be the deputy head of Statistics Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Statistician holds office for a term of not
  more than five years during good behaviour, but may be removed for
  cause by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Statistician is eligible to be reappointed
  for an additional term of not more than five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interim appointment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  Chief Statistician, or if that office is vacant, the Governor in
  Council may appoint any qualified person to hold that office in the
  interim for a term not exceeding six months, and that person shall,
  while holding office, be paid the salary or other remuneration and
  expenses that may be fixed by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Statistician shall, in addition to any
  other duties imposed on him or her under any other provision of this
  Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} decide, based strictly on professional statistical
    standards that he or she considers appropriate, the methods and
    procedures for carrying out statistical programs regarding

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the collection, compilation, analysis,
      abstraction and publication of statistical information that is
      produced or is to be produced by Statistics Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the content of statistical releases and
      publications issued by Statistics Canada, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the timing and methods of dissemination of
      statistics compiled by Statistics Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} advise on matters pertaining to statistical
    programs of the departments and agencies of the Government of
    Canada, and confer with those departments and agencies to that end;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} control the operations and staff of Statistics
    Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Minister

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} In each fiscal year the Chief Statistician shall make
  a report to the Minister on the activities of Statistics Canada in the
  preceding fiscal year, and that report shall be included as a separate
  part of the Minister's annual report to Parliament.

::: HistoricalNote

- 2017, c. 31, s. 2

  each House of Parliament.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the House is not sitting, in order to comply with
  subsection (2), the order shall be sent to the Clerk of the House

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directives on statistical programs

- **[[4.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may issue directives to the Chief Statistician on the
  statistical programs that aim to collect, compile, analyse, abstract
  and publish statistics on all or any of the matters referred to in
  section 22.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of directives

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Statistician may require that any directive
  issued to him or her under subsection (1) be made in writing and made
  public before the Chief Statistician acts on it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary employees

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Statistician may employ, in the manner authorized by law, any
  commissioners, enumerators, agents or other persons that are necessary
  to collect for Statistics Canada the statistics and information that
  the Minister considers useful and in the public interest relating to
  the commercial, industrial, financial, social, economic and other
  activities that the Minister may determine. The duties of the
  commissioners, enumerators, agents or other persons shall be those
  duties prescribed by the Chief Statistician.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public servants

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, for such periods as the Minister
  may determine, use the services of any employee of the federal public
  administration in the exercise or performance of any duty, power or
  function of Statistics Canada or an officer of Statistics Canada under
  this Act or any other Act, and any person whose services are so used
  shall, for the purposes of this Act, be deemed to be a person employed
  under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contracted services

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any persons retained under contract to perform
  special services for the Chief Statistician under this Act and the
  employees and agents of those persons are, for the purposes of this
  Act, deemed to be persons employed under this Act while performing
  those services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory or voluntary requests for
information

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Statistician shall determine whether a request for information
  is mandatory or voluntary, with the exception of the census of
  population and census of agriculture, both of which are mandatory.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Statistician shall publish any mandatory
  request for information before the request is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Statistician shall notify the Minister of
  any new mandatory request for information at least 30 days before the
  day on which it is published.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voluntary requests for
  information --- paragraph 31(a) does not apply

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph 31(a) does not apply to a person to whom a
  voluntary request for information is made.

## [Canadian Statistics Advisory Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-440930 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  advisory council, to be known as the Canadian Statistics Advisory
  Council, is established

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to advise the Minister and Chief Statistician in a
    transparent manner on any question that either of them has referred
    to the Council on the overall quality of the national statistical
    system, including the relevance, accuracy, accessibility and
    timeliness of its data; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to make public an annual report on the state of the
    national statistical system.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Membership

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Council is composed of, in addition to the Chief
  Statistician, not more than 10 other members appointed by the Governor
  in Council to hold office during pleasure, including one Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ex officio

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Statistician is an [ex officio]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} member of the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure of Chairperson

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson is appointed to a term of not more
  than five years and may be reappointed for an additional term of three
  years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure of other members

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The other members are appointed to a term of three
  years and may be reappointed for an additional term of three years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and expenses

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The members appointed under subsection (2) shall be
  paid the remuneration that is fixed by the Governor in Council and are
  entitled to be paid any reasonable travel and living expenses that are
  incurred while absent from their ordinary place of residence in the
  course of performing their duties under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2017, c. 31, s. 5
:::

  those individuals or companies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of sampling methods

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite any other provision of this Act, the Chief

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arrangements with provincial governments

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may enter into any arrangement with the government of a
  province providing for any matter necessary or convenient for the
  purpose of carrying out or giving effect to this Act, and in
  particular for all or any of the following matters:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the execution by provincial officers of any power
    or duty conferred or imposed on any officer pursuant to this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the collection by any provincial department or
    provincial officer of any statistical or other information required
    for the purpose of this Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the supplying of statistical information by any
    provincial department or provincial officer to the Chief
    Statistician.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial officers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All provincial officers executing any power or duty
  conferred or imposed on any officer pursuant to this Act, in pursuance
  of any arrangement entered into under this section, shall, for the
  purposes of the execution of that power or duty, be deemed to be
  employed under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 15, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements with provincial governments

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, with the approval of the Governor in Council and subject
  to this section, enter into an agreement with the government of a
  province for the exchange with, or transmission to, a statistical
  agency of the province of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} replies to any specific statistical inquiries;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} replies to any specific classes of information
    collected under this Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any tabulations and analyses based on replies
    referred to in paragraph (a) or (b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Type of statistical agency

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An agreement with a province for the purposes of this
  section shall apply only in respect of a statistical agency of the
  province

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that has statutory authority to collect the
    information that is intended to be exchanged or transmitted pursuant
    to the agreement from a respondent who is subject to statutory
    penalties for refusing or neglecting to furnish information to the
    agency or for falsifying information furnished by him to the agency;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is prohibited by law from disclosing any
    information of a kind that Statistics Canada, its officers and
    employees would be prohibited from disclosing under section 17, if
    the information were furnished to Statistics Canada; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whose officers and employees are subject to
    statutory penalties for the disclosing of any information of the
    kind described in paragraph (b), subject to exceptions authorized by
    law that are substantially the same as those provided under section
    17.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of agreement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Except in respect of information described in
  subsection 17(2), no agreement entered into under this section applies
  to any reply made to or information collected by Statistics Canada or
  an agency of the government of a province before the date that the
  agreement was entered into or is to have effect, whichever is the
  later date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informing respondent

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where any information in respect of which an
  agreement under this section applies is collected by Statistics Canada
  from a respondent, Statistics Canada shall, when collecting
  information, advise the respondent of the names of any statistical
  agencies in respect of which the Minister has an agreement under this
  section and to which the information received from the respondent may
  be communicated under that agreement.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 15, s. 10
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sharing of information

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  corporation on behalf of both of them and for the subsequent
  tabulation or publication based on that information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An agreement under subsection (1) shall provide that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the respondent be informed by notice that the

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the respondent gives notice in writing to the

    the department or corporation unless the department or corporation

    information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}What information may be shared

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Information shared pursuant to this section may,
  subject to subsection (2), include replies to original inquiries and
  supplementary information provided by a respondent to Statistics
  Canada or the department or corporation.

## [Secrecy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-441013 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition against divulging information

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  for the purpose of communicating information in accordance with any
  conditions of an agreement made under section 11 or 12 and except for
  the purposes of a prosecution under this Act but subject to this
  section,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no person, other than a person employed or deemed
    to be employed under this Act, and sworn under section 6, shall be
    permitted to examine any return or identifying information obtained
    for the purposes of this Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no person who has been sworn under section 6 shall
    disclose or knowingly cause to be disclosed, by any means, any
    information obtained under this Act in a manner that it is possible
    from the disclosure to relate the information obtained to any
    identifiable individual person, business or organization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception to prohibition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Statistician may, by order, authorize the
  following information to be disclosed:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} information collected by persons, organizations or
    departments for their own purposes and communicated to Statistics
    Canada before or after May 1, 1971, but that information when
    communicated to Statistics Canada shall be subject to the same
    secrecy requirements to which it was subject when collected and may
    only be disclosed by Statistics Canada in the manner and to the
    extent agreed on by the collector thereof and the Chief
    Statistician;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information relating to a person or organization in
    respect of which disclosure is consented to in writing by the person
    or organization concerned;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} information relating to a business in respect of
    which disclosure is consented to in writing by the owner for the
    time being of the business;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} information available to the public under any
    statutory or other law;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} information relating to any hospital, mental
    institution, library, educational institution, welfare institution
    or other similar non-commercial institution except particulars
    arranged in such a manner that it is possible to relate the
    particulars to any individual patient, inmate or other person in the
    care of any such institution;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} information in the form of an index or list of
    individual establishments, firms or businesses, showing any, some or
    all of the following in relation to them:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their names and addresses,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the telephone numbers at which they may be
      reached in relation to statistical matters,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the official language in which they prefer to
      be addressed in relation to statistical matters,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the products they produce, manufacture, process,
      transport, store, purchase or sell, or the services they provide,
      in the course of their business, or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} whether they are within specific ranges of
      numbers of employees or persons engaged by them or constituting
      their work force; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} information relating to any carrier or public
    utility.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section,

  [[carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person or association of
      persons that owns, operates or manages an undertaking that carries
      or moves persons or commodities by any form of land, sea or air
      transport; ([transporteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[public utility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[public utility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person or
      association of persons that owns, operates or manages an
      undertaking

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the supply of petroleum or petroleum
        products by pipeline,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the supply, transmission or distribution of
        gas, electricity, steam or water,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the collection and disposal of garbage or
        sewage or for the control of pollution,

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for the transmission, emission, reception or
        conveyance of information by any telecommunication system, or

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} for the provision of postal
        services. ([entreprise d'utilité publique]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

## [Population Census and Agriculture Census]{.HTitleText1} {#h-441062 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Population census

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  census of population of Canada shall be taken by Statistics Canada in
  the month of June in the year 1971, and every fifth year thereafter in
  a month to be fixed by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Counts of electoral divisions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The census of population shall be taken in such a
  manner as to ensure that counts of the population are provided for
  each federal electoral district of Canada, as constituted at the time
  of each census of population.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decennial census

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A reference in any Act of Parliament, in any order,
  rule or regulation or in any contract or other document made
  thereunder to a decennial census of population shall, unless the
  context otherwise requires, be construed to refer to the census of
  population taken by Statistics Canada in the year 1971 or in any tenth
  year thereafter.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 15, s. 18
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agriculture census

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** A census of agriculture
of Canada shall be taken by Statistics Canada

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the year 1971 and in every tenth year thereafter;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the year 1976 and in every tenth year thereafter,
  unless the Governor in Council otherwise directs in respect of any
  such year.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 15, s. 19
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Census questions

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall, by order, prescribe the questions to be
  asked in any census taken by Statistics Canada under section 19 or 20.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every order made under subsection (1) shall be
  published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) not later
  than thirty days after it is made.

::: HistoricalNote

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-441153 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Desertion or false declaration

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Every person who, after
taking the oath set out in subsection 6(1),

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deserts from his duty, or wilfully makes any false
  declaration, statement or return in the performance of his duties,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the pretended performance of his duties, obtains
  or seeks to obtain information that the person is not duly authorized
  to obtain, or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} contravenes subsection 17(1)

is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not
exceeding one thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not
exceeding six months or to both.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 15, s. 28
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False or unlawful information

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** Every person is guilty of
an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine of not more than
\$500 who, without lawful excuse,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} refuses or neglects, following a request for
  information under this Act,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to provide any requested information to the best of
    their knowledge and belief, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to provide any requested information when and as
    required under this Act; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} knowingly gives false or misleading information or
  practises any other deception under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. S-19, s. 31
- 2017, c. 31, s. 15
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to grant access to records

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** Every person is guilty of
an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine of not more than
\$1,000

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} who, having the custody or charge of any documents or
  records that are maintained in any department or in any municipal
  office, corporation, business or organization, from which information

  would aid in the completion or correction of the information, refuses

  authorized for the purpose by the Chief Statistician, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} who otherwise in any way wilfully obstructs or seeks
  to obstruct any person employed in the execution of any duty under
  this Act.
