## [Canadian International Trade Tribunal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-71652 .Part}

### [Establishment of Tribunal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-71653 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tribunal established

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  established a tribunal, to be known as the Canadian International
  Trade Tribunal, consisting of up to seven permanent members, including
  a Chairperson and a Vice-chairperson, to be appointed by the Governor
  in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the members who may be appointed under
  subsection (1), the Governor in Council may, whenever in the opinion
  of the Governor in Council the workload of the Tribunal so requires,
  appoint temporary members of the Tribunal on such terms and conditions
  as the Governor in Council may specify, but the number of temporary
  members holding office shall not at any time exceed five.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each permanent member shall be appointed to hold
  office for a term not exceeding five years and each temporary member
  shall be appointed to hold office for a term not exceeding three
  years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Each permanent member and temporary member holds
  office during good behaviour and may be removed by the Governor in
  Council at any time for cause.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment of permanent members

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A permanent member or former permanent member is
  eligible to be re-appointed as a permanent member for one further term
  in the same capacity as in the previous term or in another capacity,
  but may hold office as a permanent member for no more than ten years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(5.1)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a change during a permanent
  member's term of office in the capacity to which the member is
  appointed --- whether as Chairperson, Vice-chairperson or one of the
  other permanent members --- is not a re-appointment to a further term
  of office for the purposes of subsection (5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment of temporary members

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A temporary member is eligible to be re-appointed on
  the expiration of a first or subsequent term of office.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Full-time occupation

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Each permanent member shall
devote the whole of the member's time to the performance of the member's
duties under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Holding other office, etc., prohibited

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A member shall not, during
the term of office of the member, accept or hold any office or
employment inconsistent with the member's duties under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  member shall be paid such remuneration as is fixed by the Governor in
  Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each permanent member is entitled to be paid
  reasonable travel and living expenses incurred by the member while
  absent from the member's ordinary place of work in the course of
  performing duties under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each temporary member and substitute member is
  entitled to be paid reasonable travel and living expenses incurred by
  the member in the course of performing duties under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of Chairperson

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Chairperson has
supervision over and direction of the work of the Tribunal including,
without restricting the generality of the foregoing,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the allocation of work among the members and the
  assignment of members to sit at, and to preside at, hearings of the
  Tribunal; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} generally, the conduct of the work of the Tribunal
  and the management of its internal affairs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence, etc. --- Chairperson

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  event of the absence or incapacity of the Chairperson or if the office
  of Chairperson is vacant, the Vice-chairperson shall act as
  Chairperson and may exercise all the powers and perform all the duties
  and functions of the Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence, etc. --- Chairperson and
  Vice-chairperson

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} If subsection (1) does not apply owing to the
  absence or incapacity of the Vice-chairperson or to the office of
  Vice-chairperson being vacant, the Minister may authorize another
  permanent member to act as Chairperson, and that member may exercise
  all the powers and perform all the duties and functions of the
  Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence, etc. --- Vice-chairperson

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  Vice-chairperson or if the office of Vice-chairperson is vacant, the
  Minister may authorize another permanent member to act as
  Vice-chairperson, and that member may exercise all the powers and
  perform all the duties and functions of the Vice-chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of Governor in Council

  [(1.3)]{.lawlabel} No permanent member may be authorized by the
  Minister to act under subsection (1.1) or (1.2) for a period exceeding
  60 days without the approval of the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence, etc. --- other members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of a
  temporary member or a permanent member other than the Chairperson or
  Vice-chairperson, the Governor in Council may appoint a person, on any
  terms and conditions that the Governor in Council specifies, to act as
  a substitute member for the time being.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting after termination of appointment

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a person who has ceased to be a member, for any
  reason other than removal, may, with the authorization of the
  Chairperson, perform and complete any duties or responsibilities that
  the person would otherwise have had if the person had not ceased to be
  a member and that are in connection with any matter in which that
  person became engaged while holding office as a member, and a person
  so authorized is, for that purpose, deemed to be a member of the
  Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person who has ceased to be a member may, after
  the expiration of one hundred and twenty days after ceasing to be a
  member, take part in the disposition of any matter pursuant to the
  authority granted by the Chairperson under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where member is unable to act

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a person to whom subsection (1) applies or any
  member has taken part in any matter and has died or for any reason is
  unable or unwilling to take part in the disposition of the matter, the
  remaining members, if any, who took part in the matter may, with the
  authorization of the Chairperson, make the disposition notwithstanding
  that the quorum of members required to dispose of the matter was lost
  as a result, and the remaining members where so authorized shall, for
  that purpose, be deemed to constitute a quorum.

### [Powers, Duties and Functions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-71730 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties and functions

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The duties and functions
of the Tribunal are to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} conduct inquiries and report on matters referred to
  the Tribunal for inquiry by the Governor in Council or the Minister
  under this Act;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} conduct mid-term reviews under section 19.02 and
  report on the reviews;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} consider complaints and extension requests filed with
  the Tribunal under this Act by domestic producers of like or directly
  competitive goods or by trade unions whose members are engaged in the
  Canadian production of like or directly competitive goods and, if
  appropriate, conduct inquiries into the complaints and extension
  requests and report on them;

- [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} receive complaints, conduct inquiries and make
  determinations under sections 30.1 to 30.19;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} hear, determine and deal with all appeals that,
  pursuant to any other Act of Parliament or regulations thereunder, may
  be made to the Tribunal, and all matters related thereto; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} exercise and perform such other duties or functions
  that, pursuant to any other Act of Parliament or regulations
  thereunder, shall or may be exercised or performed by the Tribunal.

the Tribunal under any of subsections 23(1) to (1.097) and, for the
purposes of those sections, a complaint is properly documented if the

Definition of [[complaint]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** In sections 23 to 30,
[[complaint]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a written complaint filed with
the Tribunal under subsection 23(1) or (1.1), and, for the purposes of
those sections, a complaint is properly documented if the Tribunal is
satisfied that it contains or is accompanied by the information required
by section 23.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of complaint

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any of
  the following may file a written complaint with the Tribunal alleging
  that goods are being imported into Canada in such increased quantities
  and under such conditions as to cause or threaten serious injury to
  domestic producers of like or directly competitive goods:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a domestic producer of the like or directly
    competitive goods;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person or association acting on behalf of the
    domestic producer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a trade union whose members are engaged in the
    Canadian production of the like or directly competitive goods.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of complaint

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A complaint shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state in reasonable detail the facts on which the
    allegations are based;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a complaint filed by or on behalf of
    a domestic producer, state an estimate of the total percentage of
    Canadian production of the like or directly competitive goods that
    is produced by the producer;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a complaint filed by a trade
    union,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} state an estimate of the total percentage of
      Canadian production of the like or directly competitive goods that
      is produced by its members, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} provide evidence that one or more domestic
      producers of the like or directly competitive goods support the
      complaint and state an estimate of the total percentage of
      Canadian production of the like or directly competitive goods that
      is produced by those producers; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} make any other representations that the complainant
    considers relevant to the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accompanying information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A complaint shall be accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} such information as is available to the complainant
    to prove the facts referred to in paragraph (2)(a) and to
    substantiate the estimates referred to in paragraph (2)(b) or (b.1);
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} such other information as may be required by the
    rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt to be acknowledged

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal shall, forthwith after the receipt of a
  complaint, notify the complainant in writing of the receipt and date
  of receipt thereof.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for additional information

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may, within twenty-one days after the date of receipt of a
  complaint, by notice in writing, request the complainant to provide
  such additional information as the Tribunal considers necessary in
  order for the complaint to be properly documented.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further requests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Tribunal receives additional information in
  relation to a complaint pursuant to a request made under
  subsection (1) or this subsection, the Tribunal may, within twenty-one
  days after the date of receipt of the additional information, by
  notice in writing, request the complainant to provide such additional
  information as the Tribunal considers necessary in order for the
  complaint to be properly documented.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tribunal shall determine if complaint is
properly documented

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal shall, within twenty-one days after the date of receipt of a
  complaint or, where the Tribunal has requested the complainant to
  provide additional information pursuant to subsection 24(1) or (2),
  within twenty-one days after the receipt of the additional information
  requested, determine whether the complaint is properly documented.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Tribunal determines under subsection (1)
  that a complaint is properly documented, it shall forthwith

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the complainant in writing that the
    complaint is properly documented;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify each other interested party in writing of
    the receipt of the complaint and that the complaint is properly
    documented; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a complaint filed under subsection
    23(1.06), (1.08), (1.083), (1.09), (1.091), (1.092) or (1.096), send
    to the Minister a copy of the complaint and the information examined
    by the Tribunal in making its determination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Tribunal determines under subsection (1)
  that a complaint is not properly documented, it shall forthwith notify
  the complainant in writing that the complaint is not properly
  documented and of its reasons for so concluding.

## [Safeguard Measures in Respect of China]{.HTitleText1} {#h-72601 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[30.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30.2 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in this section and in sections 30.21 to 30.25.

[[action]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[action]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any action, including a provisional action, taken

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by the People's Republic of China to prevent or
        remedy market disruption in a WTO Member other than Canada, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by a WTO Member other than Canada to withdraw
        concessions under the World Trade Organization Agreement or
        otherwise to limit imports to prevent or remedy market
        disruption in that Member caused or threatened by the
        importation of goods originating in the People's Republic of
        China; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any combination of actions referred to in
      paragraph (a). ([mesure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[market disruption]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[market disruption]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a rapid increase in
    the importation of goods that are like or directly competitive with
    goods produced by a domestic industry, in absolute terms or relative
    to the production of those goods by a domestic industry, so as to be
    a significant cause of material injury, or threat of material
    injury, to the domestic industry. ([désorganisation du
    marché]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[significant cause]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[significant cause]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
    material injury or threat thereof, an important cause that need not
    be as important as, or more important than, any other cause of the
    material injury or threat. ([cause importante]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[WTO Member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[WTO Member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Member of the World Trade
    Organization established by Article I of the Agreement Establishing
    the World Trade Organization, signed at Marrakesh on April 15,
    1994. ([membre de l'OMC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- 2002, c. 19, s. 4

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of complaint --- market disruption

- **[[30.22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30.22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any domestic producer of goods that
  are like or directly competitive with goods originating in the
  People's Republic of China being imported into Canada, or any person
  or association acting on behalf of any such domestic producer, may
  file a written complaint with the Tribunal alleging that the imported
  goods are being imported in such increased quantities or under such
  conditions as to cause or threaten to cause market disruption to
  domestic producers of like or directly competitive goods.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of complaint

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A complaint shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state in reasonable detail the facts on which the
    allegations are based;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} state an estimate of the total percentage of
    Canadian production of the like or directly competitive goods that
    is produced by the domestic producers by whom or on whose behalf the
    complaint is filed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by any information that is available
    to the complainant to support the facts referred to in paragraph (a)
    and to substantiate the estimate referred to in paragraph (b);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by any other information that may be
    required by the rules; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} make any other representations that the complainant
    deems relevant to the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement of inquiry

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a complaint that meets the requirements
  of subsection (2), the Tribunal shall commence an inquiry into the
  complaint if it is satisfied

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the information provided by the complainant
    and any other information examined by the Tribunal discloses a
    reasonable indication that the goods originating in the People's
    Republic of China that are the subject of the complaint are being
    imported in such increased quantities or under such conditions as to
    cause or threaten to cause market disruption to domestic producers
    of like or directly competitive goods;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the complaint is made by or on behalf of
    domestic producers who produce a major proportion of the domestic
    production of the like or directly competitive goods; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where an inquiry under this section and sections
    30.21 and 30.23 to 30.25 in relation to like or directly competitive
    goods has been completed or terminated during the twelve-month
    period preceding the date of receipt of the complaint, that the
    circumstances are sufficiently different to warrant a new inquiry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision to commence inquiry

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Tribunal decides to commence an inquiry
  into the complaint, it shall immediately

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the complainant and each other interested
    party in writing of its decision, of the reasons for it and of the
    date on which any hearing in the inquiry shall commence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cause a notice of its decision and the date on
    which any hearing in the inquiry shall commence to be published in
    the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} send to the Minister a copy of its decision, a copy
    of the complaint, and the information accompanying the complaint,
    and a copy of any other relevant information examined by the
    Tribunal in relation to the complaint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision not to commence
  inquiry

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the Tribunal decides not to commence an inquiry
  into the complaint, it shall immediately

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the complainant and each other interested
    party in writing of its decision, of the reasons for its refusal to
    commence an inquiry and, where the reasons for its decision are
    based in whole or in part on information that was obtained from a
    source other than the complainant, of the fact that the decision was
    based in whole or in part on such information; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cause a notice of its decision to be published in

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination by Tribunal

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal shall, in the inquiry into the
  complaint, determine whether, having regard to any regulations made
  pursuant to paragraphs 40(a) and (k.1), the goods originating in the
  People's Republic of China that are the subject of the complaint are

    production of the like or directly competitive goods.

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Where the Tribunal decides to commence an inquiry
  into the extension request, it shall immediately

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the requester and each other interested

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cause a notice of its decision and the date on

    the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} send to the Minister a copy of its decision, a copy

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision not to commence
  inquiry

    commence an inquiry and, where the reasons for its decision are

    based in whole or in part on such information; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cause a notice of its decision to be published in

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing necessity of order

## [Safeguard Measures in Respect of Korea]{.HTitleText1} {#h-72759 .Part}

Definition of [[complaint]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[30.27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30.27 .sectionLabel}** In sections 30.28
to 30.32, [[complaint]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a written complaint
filed with the Tribunal under subsection 23(1.097) and, for the purposes
of those sections, a complaint is properly documented if the Tribunal is
satisfied that it contains or is accompanied by the information required
by subsection 30.28(2).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 28, s. 42
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Critical circumstances

- **[[30.28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30.28
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any domestic producer of goods that
  are like or directly competitive with any goods being imported into
  Canada and that are entitled to the Korea Tariff, or any person or
  association acting on behalf of such a domestic producer, may, if
  filing a complaint under subsection 23(1.097), include in that
  complaint a written allegation that as a result of that entitlement,
  the imported goods are being imported in such increased quantities, in
  absolute terms, and under such conditions as to give rise to critical
  circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of allegation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The allegation must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state in reasonable detail the facts on which it is
    based;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} state an estimate of the total percentage of
    Canadian production of the like or directly competitive goods that
    is produced by the domestic producers by whom or on whose behalf the
    allegation is filed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by any information that is available
    to the complainant to support the facts referred to in paragraph (a)
    and to substantiate the estimate referred to in paragraph (b);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by any other information that may be
    required by the rules; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} make any other representations that the complainant
    considers relevant to the matter.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 28, s. 42
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for additional information

- **[[30.29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30.29
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may, within seven days
  after the day on which a complaint under subsection 23(1.097) is
  received, by notice in writing, request the complainant to provide any
  additional information that the Tribunal considers necessary in order
  for an allegation that is included in the complaint to be properly
  documented.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent request for additional
  information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Tribunal receives additional information under
  subsection (1) or this subsection, the Tribunal may, within seven days
  after the day on which the additional information is received, by
  notice in writing, request the complainant to provide any additional
  information that the Tribunal considers necessary in order for the
  allegation to be properly documented.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 28, s. 42
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement of inquiry

- **[[30.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30.3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal shall, within seven
  days after the day on which an allegation is received or, if the
  Tribunal has requested the complainant to provide additional
  information under subsection 30.29(1) or (2), within seven days after
  the day on which the additional information is received, determine
  whether the allegation is properly documented and, if the Tribunal
  determines that it is, commence an inquiry into the allegation if it
  is satisfied

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the information provided by the complainant
    and any other information examined by the Tribunal discloses a
    reasonable indication that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the goods that are entitled to the Korea Tariff
      are, as a result of that entitlement, being imported in such
      increased quantities, in absolute terms, and under such conditions
      as to constitute a principal cause of serious injury, or threat of
      serious injury, to domestic producers of like or directly
      competitive goods, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} delay in taking action would cause serious
      injury to domestic producers of like or directly competitive goods
      that would be difficult to repair; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the allegation is made by or on behalf of
    domestic producers that produce a major proportion of the domestic
    production of the like or directly competitive goods.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of decision to commence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Tribunal decides to commence an inquiry, it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the complainant and each other interested

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cause a notice of its decision and the date on
    which any hearing in the inquiry will commence to be published in
    the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} send to the Minister a copy of the complaint and
    the information accompanying the complaint, the allegation and the
    information accompanying the allegation and any other relevant
    information examined by the Tribunal in relation to the allegation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of decision not to
  commence inquiry

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Tribunal decides not to commence an inquiry,
  it shall immediately notify the complainant of its decision, of the
  reasons for it and, if the reasons are based in whole or in part on

  complainant, of the fact that the decision was based in whole or in
  part on that information.

::: HistoricalNote

  Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15), other than an appeal made

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member by whom evidence relating to any matter has
  been received pursuant to subsection (1) shall make a report thereon
  to the Tribunal and a copy of the report, modified in such manner as
  in the opinion of the member is necessary to comply with sections 45
  and 49, shall be provided to every person who is a party to the
  proceedings before the Tribunal in the matter.

  matter and after holding such hearing, re-hearing or further hearing

  may make its order, finding or report or take such other action in
  relation to the matter as is authorized to be taken by it in relation
  to the matter under this or any other Act of Parliament, as if the
  evidence received by the member had been received by the Tribunal.

  purposes of subsection (3) or (3.1).

- Definition of [[counsel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (3), [[counsel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
  in relation to a party to proceedings, includes any person, other than
  a director, servant or employee of the party, who acts in the
  proceedings on behalf of the party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons who may be recognized as
  experts

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In subsections (3) and (3.1), "expert" includes any
  of the following persons whom the Tribunal recognizes as an expert:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} persons whose duties involve the carrying out of
    the [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34) and who are referred to in
    section 25 of that Act, other than persons authorized by the
    Governor in Council to exercise the powers and perform the duties of
    the Director of Investigation and Research;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the determination of damages and
    costs in procurement review proceedings, persons employed in the
    government institution involved in the procurement under review;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} persons engaged under subsection 11(2) of the
    [Administrative Tribunals Support Service of Canada
    Act](/eng/acts/A-1.5) to advise or assist the Tribunal; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any prescribed person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every person commits an offence who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} uses information disclosed to the person by the
    Tribunal under subsection (3) or (3.1) for any purpose other than
    the purpose for which the information was disclosed under that
    subsection; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contravenes any condition imposed by the Tribunal
    under subsection (3) or (3.1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Every person who commits an offence under
  subsection (6) is guilty of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an indictable offence and liable to a fine of not
    more than \$1,000,000; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an offence punishable on summary conviction and
    liable to a fine of not more than \$100,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} No proceedings for an offence under subsection (6)
  shall be instituted without the consent in writing of the Attorney
  General of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bar from appearing before Tribunal

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} In addition to any punishment imposed under
  subsection (7), counsel or an expert who commits an offence under
  subsection (6) may be barred by the Tribunal from any further
  appearance before it in respect of any proceedings before the Tribunal
  for the period that the Tribunal considers appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of information as
confidential

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a person who provides information to the Tribunal for the purposes of
  proceedings before the Tribunal wishes some or all of the information
  to be kept confidential, the person shall submit to the Tribunal, at
  the time the information is provided,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement designating as confidential the
    information that the person wishes to be kept confidential, together
    with an explanation as to why that information is designated as
    confidential; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a non-confidential edited version or
    non-confidential summary of the information designated as
    confidential pursuant to paragraph (a) in sufficient detail to
    convey a reasonable understanding of the substance of the
    information or a statement

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that such a non-confidential edited version or
      non-confidential summary cannot be made, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that such a non-confidential edited version or
      non-confidential summary would disclose facts that the person has
      a proper reason for wishing to keep confidential,

    together with an explanation that justifies the making of the
    statement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who designates information as confidential
  pursuant to paragraph (1)(a) fails to comply with paragraph (1)(b)
  where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person does not provide the non-confidential
    edited version, the non-confidential summary or the statement
    referred to in paragraph (1)(b);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person provides a non-confidential edited
    version or a summary of the information designated as confidential
    pursuant to paragraph (1)(a) but the Tribunal is satisfied that it
    does not comply with paragraph (1)(b);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person provides a statement referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b), but does not provide an explanation that justifies
    the making of the statement; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person provides a statement referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b), but the Tribunal is satisfied that the explanation
    given as justification for the making of the statement does not
    justify the making thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 47 (4th Supp.), s. 46
- 1994, c. 47, s. 45
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where there has been failure to comply

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a person has designated information as confidential pursuant to
  paragraph 46(1)(a) and the Tribunal considers that such a designation
  is warranted, but the person has failed to comply with paragraph
  46(1)(b), the Tribunal shall cause the person to be informed of the
  failure, of the ground on which the person has so failed and of the
  application of subsection 48(3) if the person fails to take, within
  the time limited therefor by or pursuant to that subsection, such
  action as is necessary for the person to take in order to comply with
  paragraph 46(1)(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where Tribunal considers designation
  unwarranted

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a person has designated information as
  confidential pursuant to paragraph 46(1)(a) and the Tribunal considers
  that, by reason of its nature, extent or availability from other
  sources or of the failure of the person to provide any explanation as
  to why it was designated as confidential, the designation of that
  information as confidential is unwarranted, the Tribunal shall cause
  the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to be notified of the fact that the Tribunal
    considers the designation to be unwarranted and of its reasons for
    so considering; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the person has failed to comply with
    paragraph 46(1)(b), to be informed as provided in subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of designation or submission
of explanation

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a person is notified pursuant to paragraph 47(2)(a) with respect to
  any information that the person has designated as confidential
  pursuant to paragraph 46(1)(a), the person may, within fifteen days
  after being so notified,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} withdraw the designation, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} submit to the Tribunal an explanation or further
    explanation as to why the information was designated as confidential

  and, where the person does neither of those things within those
  fifteen days, that information shall not thereafter be taken into
  account by the Tribunal in the proceedings for the purposes for which
  it was provided, unless the Tribunal obtains that information from a
  source other than that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tribunal to reconsider

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, pursuant to subsection (1), a person submits
  to the Tribunal, within the fifteen days referred to in that
  subsection, an explanation or further explanation as to why the
  information was designated as confidential, the Tribunal shall again
  consider whether, taking into account that explanation or further
  explanation, the designation of the information as confidential is
  warranted and, if it decides that it is not warranted, shall cause the
  person to be notified that the information will not thereafter be
  taken into account by the Tribunal in the proceedings for the purposes
  for which it was provided, in which case the information shall not
  thereafter be taken into account by the Tribunal in those proceedings,
  unless the Tribunal obtains the information from a source other than
  that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where failure to comply not rectified

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), where a person who has
  been informed pursuant to section 47 that the person has failed to
  comply with paragraph 46(1)(b) with respect to any information does
  not, within fifteen days after being so informed or within such longer
  time not exceeding thirty days after being so informed as the
  Tribunal, either before or after the expiration of the fifteen days,
  in its discretion allows, take such action as is necessary for the
  person to take in order to comply with paragraph 46(1)(b), the
  Tribunal shall cause the person to be notified that the information
  will not thereafter be taken into account by the Tribunal in the
  proceedings for the purposes for which it was provided, in which case
  the information shall not thereafter be taken into account by the
  Tribunal in those proceedings, unless the Tribunal obtains the
  information from a source other than that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (3) does not apply in respect of any
  information that the Tribunal is prohibited by subsection (1) or (2)
  from taking into account in the proceedings for the purposes for which
  the information was provided.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other information

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** If

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} information or material given or elicited in the
  course of any proceedings before the Tribunal is, in the opinion of
  the Tribunal, in its nature confidential, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the President indicates to the Tribunal in writing
  that subsection 84(1) of the [Special Import Measures
  Act](/eng/acts/S-15) applies to information or material filed with it
  under paragraph 37(a) or 38(3)(b) or subsection 76.03(9) of that Act,

the information or material shall not knowingly be disclosed by any
member or person employed in the federal public administration who comes
into possession of the information in any manner that is calculated or
likely to make it available for the use of any business competitor or
rival of any person to whose business or affairs the information
relates.

- --- 1999, c. 12, s. 63

  - ###### Disposition of notified complaints {#disposition-of-notified-complaints .MarginalNote}

    - [63]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section, if,
      before the commencement day, notice of a complaint respecting the
      dumping or subsidizing of goods that is properly documented,
      within the meaning assigned to that expression by subsection 2(1)
      of the old [Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15), has been
      given under paragraph 32(1)(a) of that Act, any proceeding,
      process or action in respect of the goods shall be continued and
      disposed of in accordance with that Act, the old [Canadian
      International Trade Tribunal Act](/eng/acts/C-18.3) and the old
      rules and regulations.

    - ###### Proceedings re goods subject to order made after commencement day {#proceedings-re-goods-subject-to-order-made-after-commencement-day .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Canadian International Trade Tribunal
      makes an order or finding under subsection 43(1) of the [Special
      Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15) on or after the commencement
      day with respect to goods that are the subject of a complaint
      referred to in subsection (1), any subsequent proceeding, process
      or action in relation to any of those goods other than the
      following shall be disposed of in accordance with the new [Special
      Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15), the new [Canadian
      International Trade Tribunal Act](/eng/acts/C-18.3) and the new
      rules and regulations:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a judicial review or dispute settlement under
        Part I.1 or II of the [Special Import Measures
        Act](/eng/acts/S-15) in relation to that order or finding and
        any proceeding, process or action in relation to the judicial
        review or dispute settlement;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a proceeding, process or action in relation to
        any of those goods that were released before the commencement
        day;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a proceeding, process or action in relation to
        any of those goods that were released on or after the
        commencement day but on or before the day on which the Tribunal
        made the order or finding; or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a proceeding, process or action under section
        45 of the [Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15) in
        relation to that order or finding.

    - ###### Effect of order or finding {#effect-of-order-or-finding .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, any order or finding that
      was made before the commencement day and is in effect on that day
      shall, for the purposes of sections 3 to 6 of the new [Special
      Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15), have the same force and
      effect as if it were made under that Act.

    - ###### Review in accordance with old Acts, rules and regulations {#review-in-accordance-with-old-acts-rules-and-regulations .MarginalNote}

      [(4)]{.lawlabel} If notice of a review under subsection 76(2) of
      the old [Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15) has been
      given by the Canadian International Trade Tribunal before the
      commencement day, the review shall be disposed of in accordance
      with that Act, the old [Canadian International Trade Tribunal
      Act](/eng/acts/C-18.3) and the old rules and regulations.

    - ###### Review in accordance with new Acts, rules and regulations {#review-in-accordance-with-new-acts-rules-and-regulations .MarginalNote}

      [(5)]{.lawlabel} If notice of an interim review under section
      76.01 of the new [Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15), or
      an expiry review under section 76.03 of that Act, of an order or
      finding that was made before the commencement day and is in effect
      on that day has been given by the Canadian International Trade
      Tribunal on or after the commencement day, the review shall be
      disposed of in accordance with that Act, the new [Canadian
      International Trade Tribunal Act](/eng/acts/C-18.3) and the new
      rules and regulations.

    - ###### New Act does not justify review {#new-act-does-not-justify-review .MarginalNote}

      [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection 76.01(3) of the new
      [Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15), the Canadian
      International Trade Tribunal may not be satisfied that an interim
      review of an order or finding that was made before the
      commencement day is warranted by reason only of the coming into
      force of that Act, the new [Canadian International Trade Tribunal
      Act](/eng/acts/C-18.3) or the new rules and regulations.

    - ###### Determination of normal value, etc., when undertaking {#determination-of-normal-value-etc.-when-undertaking .MarginalNote}

      [(7)]{.lawlabel} Any determination, on or after the commencement
      day, of a normal value, export price, amount of subsidy or margin
      of dumping in relation to any goods that are subject to an
      undertaking accepted before the commencement day shall be made in
      accordance with the new [Special Import Measures
      Act](/eng/acts/S-15).

    - ###### Determination of normal value, etc. {#determination-of-normal-value-etc. .MarginalNote}

      [(8)]{.lawlabel} A normal value, export price, amount of subsidy
      or margin of dumping determined in relation to goods under the old
      [Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15) is, for the purposes
      of goods released on or after the commencement day, other than
      goods to which paragraph (2)(c) applies, deemed to have been made
      under the new [Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15).

    - ###### Re-determination of normal value, etc. {#re-determination-of-normal-value-etc. .MarginalNote}

      [(9)]{.lawlabel} A re-determination of a normal value, export
      price, amount of subsidy or margin of dumping referred to in
      subsection (8) shall be made in accordance with the new [Special
      Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15).
