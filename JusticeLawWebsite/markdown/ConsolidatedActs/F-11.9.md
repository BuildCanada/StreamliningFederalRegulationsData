## First Nations Oil and Gas and Moneys Management Act {#first-nations-oil-and-gas-and-moneys-management-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2005, c. 48
:::

Assented to 2005-11-25

An Act to provide first nations with the option of managing and
regulating oil and gas exploration and exploitation and of receiving
moneys otherwise held for them by Canada
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the White Bear First Nation entered into Treaty Number Four with
Her Majesty on September 23, 1875 and the Blood and Siksika (Blackfoot)
First Nations entered into Treaty Number Seven with Her Majesty on
September 22, 1877;

WHEREAS existing aboriginal and treaty rights of the aboriginal peoples
of Canada were recognized and affirmed by section 35 of the
[Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/);

WHEREAS the White Bear, Blood and Siksika First Nations wish to assume
control of oil and gas in their reserve lands and related revenues, and
control of current and future moneys that would otherwise be held for
them by Canada;

WHEREAS those first nations have, since 1994, entered into a series of
agreements with the Government of Canada for the co-management of oil
and gas in their reserve lands;

WHEREAS the same first nations have participated with the Government of
Canada in the development of proposals for legislation for the transfer
of control of oil and gas and of moneys held for them;

AND WHEREAS it is recognized that such legislation could also benefit
other first nations if they chose to avail themselves of it;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-227567 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [First Nations Oil and Gas and Moneys Management
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.9).

## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-227570 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence, permit or lease
      granting a surface or subsurface right or interest in land --- or
      an instrument evidencing an easement, a right-of-way, a right of
      entry or any other disposition of a right or interest in land ---
      that is granted, issued or made for the purposes of oil or gas
      exploration or exploitation. ([contrat]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[decision-making authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[decision-making authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to
      a project, means a person or body having power under section 34 or
      under a first nation's oil and gas laws to make any decision that
      is required in order for the project to be undertaken. ([autorité
      décisionnelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[environmental assessment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[environmental assessment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a
      project in a managed area, means an assessment of the project's
      effects on the environment conducted in one or more stages in
      accordance with a first nation's oil and gas laws. ([évaluation
      environnementale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[exploitation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[exploitation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to oil or gas,
      means its extraction, production, storage, distribution,
      processing or refining, or its use to generate electrical energy,
      to the extent that those activities relate to lands in a managed
      area. ([exploitation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[exploration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[exploration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes surveying, mapping,
      test drilling and geological, geophysical and geochemical
      examinations carried out in pursuit of oil or
      gas. ([exploration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bank or a
      trust company. ([institution financière]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[first nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[first nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a band within the
      meaning of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([première
      nation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[first nation member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[first nation member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person whose
      name appears on the band list of a first nation or who is entitled
      to have their name appear on that list. ([membre de la première
      nation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[gas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[gas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means natural gas that can be produced
      from a well, including natural gas from a coal bed, and includes
      marketable gas as well as condensate and other fluid components of
      natural gas that are not oil. ([gaz]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[managed area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[managed area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a managed area as
      described in section 25. ([secteur aménagé]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Indigenous
      Services. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any hydrocarbon that can be
      extracted or recovered in liquid form from surface or subsurface
      deposits, other than condensate, or that can be extracted or
      recovered from bitumen, bituminous sands, oil sands or oil
      shale. ([pétrole]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[oil and gas law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[oil and gas law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a law made by a
      first nation under section 35. ([texte pétrolier ou
      gazier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[oil and gas moneys]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[oil and gas moneys]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means moneys derived
      from oil or gas exploration or exploitation within the managed
      area of a first nation after the first nation's transfer date, and
      includes fines recovered for offences under oil and gas
      laws. ([recettes pétrolières ou gazières]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any proposed construction, operation,
        modification, decommissioning or abandonment of an installation
        used for oil or gas exploration or exploitation, or any other
        proposed undertaking in relation to such an installation; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any proposed exploration activity of a kind
        that is defined, by the regulations or by a first nation's oil
        and gas laws, as a project for the purposes of environmental
        assessments. ([projet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transfer date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transfer date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a first
      nation, means the date on which the first nation's name is added
      to Schedule 1 under subsection 22(1). ([date de
      transfert]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[trust company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[trust company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a trust company under
      subsection 57(2) of the [Trust and Loan Companies
      Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), or a company incorporated by or under a
      provincial Act that carries on the business of a trust company,
      that is a member institution within the meaning of section 2 of
      the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation
      Act](/eng/acts/C-3). ([société de fiducie]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expressions in [Impact Assessment
  Act](/eng/acts/I-2.75)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Act, [[environment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has
  the same meaning as in section 2 of the [Impact Assessment
  Act](/eng/acts/I-2.75) and [[environmental
  effects]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 81 of
  that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expressions in [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context otherwise requires, words and
  expressions used in this Act have the same meaning as in the [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty's title to lands in a managed area is not
  affected by this Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} lands in a managed area continue to be set apart by
  Her Majesty for the use and benefit of the first nation for which they
  were set apart;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} lands in a managed area continue to be lands reserved
  for the Indians within the meaning of Class 24 of section 91 of the
  [Constitution Act, 1867](/eng/Const/); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} this Act does not affect the ability of a first
  nation to participate in and benefit from any federal government
  program.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Territorial lands

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act does not apply in
respect of reserve lands in Yukon or in respect of frontier lands within
the meaning of the [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Individual moneys

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Act does not apply in
respect of moneys collected, received or held by Her Majesty in right of
Canada under the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) for the use and benefit of
an individual.

## [Requests for Transfer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-227610 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Council resolution --- oil and gas

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The council of a first
nation may submit to the Minister a written resolution of the council
requesting the transfer to the first nation of the management and
regulation of oil and gas exploration and exploitation in its reserve
lands.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Council resolution --- moneys

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The council of a first
nation may submit to the Minister a written resolution of the council
requesting the payment to the first nation of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} moneys held by Her Majesty for the use and benefit of
  the first nation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} moneys to be collected or received in future by Her
  Majesty for the use and benefit of the first nation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information from Minister --- oil and gas

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (3) and (4), following receipt of a resolution under
  section 6, the Minister shall provide to the first nation, if the
  first nation does not already possess them,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} copies of all contracts issued in respect of the
    first nation's reserve lands that are in force and of any documents
    evidencing designations of those lands under the [Indian
    Act](/eng/acts/I-5);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} copies of any documents in the Minister's
    possession respecting contracts referred to in paragraph (a) that
    relate to periods after the issuance of those contracts;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} particulars of all moneys payable under contracts
    referred to in paragraph (a); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any documents in the Minister's possession with
    respect to contaminated sites on the first nation's reserve.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timetable for providing information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Following receipt of a resolution under section 6,
  the Minister shall provide the first nation with a timetable for
  delivery of the documents and information referred to in subsection
  (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of confidential information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not provide information that is
  subject to solicitor-client privilege or is otherwise privileged at
  law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of confidential information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is not required to provide information
  that may, or must, be withheld under any federal law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (4) does not apply in respect of
  information provided to the Minister by a contract holder.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information from Minister --- moneys

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Following receipt of a
resolution under section 7, the Minister shall inform the first nation
of the amount of the moneys held by Her Majesty for the first nation at
the time of the receipt of the request and of the outstanding amount of
any loans referred to in subsection 30(2).

## [Requirements for Transfer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-227634 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oil and gas code

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  a vote is conducted on a transfer requested under section 6, a first
  nation shall prepare an oil and gas code that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribes the procedure to be followed by the
    council of the first nation in the making, amendment and publication
    of oil and gas laws;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provides for the accountability of the council to
    first nation members for the management and regulation of oil and
    gas exploration and exploitation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} establishes procedures for disclosing and
    addressing conflicts of interest of members of the council and
    employees of the first nation in the management and regulation of
    oil and gas exploration and exploitation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the first nation shares a reserve with another
    first nation, provides for the coordination of the management and
    regulation of oil and gas exploration and exploitation by the two
    first nations; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} provides for the amendment of the code by the first
    nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial code for oil and gas moneys

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before a vote is conducted on a transfer requested
  under section 6, a first nation shall prepare a financial code that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} specifies the mode of holding oil and gas moneys,
    either by their deposit in an account with a financial institution
    or their payment to a trust of which the first nation is settlor and
    sole beneficiary, and prescribing the conditions governing
    subsequent changes from one mode to the other;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provides for the manner of collecting oil and gas
    moneys and the manner of expending moneys held in the account or
    received by the first nation from the trust;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provides for the accountability of the council to
    first nation members for the management of oil and gas moneys;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} establishes procedures for disclosing and
    addressing conflicts of interest of members of the council and
    employees of the first nation in the expenditure of those moneys;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} provides for the amendment of the code by the first
    nation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial code for moneys received from
Canada

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Before a vote is
conducted on a payment requested under section 7, a first nation shall
prepare a financial code

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} specifying the mode of holding moneys paid by Her
  Majesty to the first nation under sections 30 and 31 by their deposit
  in an account with a financial institution or payment to a trust of
  which the first nation is settlor and sole beneficiary, and
  prescribing the conditions governing future changes from one mode to
  the other;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the manner of expending moneys held by the
  first nation in the account or received by it from the trust;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the accountability of the council of the
  first nation to first nation members for the expenditure of those
  moneys;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} establishing procedures for disclosing and addressing
  conflicts of interest of members of the council and employees of the
  first nation in the expenditure of those moneys; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} providing for the amendment of the code by the first
  nation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Law of trusts

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Laws
  of general application in relation to trusts and trustees in force in
  the province in which the trust instrument is executed apply in
  respect of a trust established for the purposes of this Act except to
  the extent that those laws conflict with this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules against perpetuities and
  accumulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A trust established for the purposes of this Act is
  exempt from the application of any rule of common law that limits the
  period within which a beneficial interest must vest or the period
  during which income may be accumulated.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Councillors as trustees

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member of the council of a first nation may not be made a trustee *ex
  officio* of a trust established for the purposes of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not preclude a member of a
  council from serving as a trustee in the member's personal capacity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security required of trustees

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Every trustee of a trust
established for the purposes of this Act other than a trust company
shall, before moneys are paid into a trust referred to in section 10 or
11, and at any other time when the council so requests, provide to the
council of the first nation

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} evidence of bonding, insurance or other form of
  security, in an amount equal to the amount held in the trust, for the
  recovery of any loss occasioned by theft or by breach of the
  conditions of the trust; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} evidence that the trustee fulfils the requirements of
  provincial law, if any, as to the minimum financial worth of trustees
  generally.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oil and gas transfer agreement

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** After a first nation has
prepared the codes referred to in section 10, the Minister and the first
nation may conclude a transfer agreement setting out arrangements for
the transfer to the first nation of the management and regulation of oil
and gas.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Moneys payment agreement

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** After a first nation has
prepared the codes referred to in section 11, the Minister and the first
nation may conclude a payment agreement setting out arrangements for the
payment of moneys to the first nation.

## [Approval Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-227675 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of transfer

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** After a transfer
agreement has been concluded under section 15, the first nation may
conduct a vote among its eligible voters, in accordance with the
regulations, on the ratification of the oil and gas code and the
financial code referred to in section 10 and the approval of the
transfer to the first nation of the management and regulation of oil and
gas exploration and exploitation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of payment

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** After a payment agreement
has been concluded under section 16, the first nation may conduct a vote
among its eligible voters, in accordance with the regulations, on the
ratification of the financial code referred to in section 11 and the
approval of the payment of moneys to the first nation in accordance with
the code.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Combined vote on oil, gas and moneys

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** If agreements have been
concluded under both sections 15 and 16, a single vote may be conducted
for the purposes of sections 17 and 18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility to vote

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a first
  nation member, whether resident on the reserve or not, is an eligible
  voter if the member's name appears on the band list and the member has
  reached the age of eighteen years on the date of the vote.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a first nation that is not the subject
  of an order made under section 74 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5),
  a first nation member, whether resident on the reserve or not, is an
  eligible voter if the member's name appears on the band list and the
  member has, on the date of the vote, reached the eligible age for
  participation in the selection of the first nation's council according
  to the custom of the first nation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval by majority vote

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  a vote conducted under section 17 or 18 is affirmative when

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a majority of eligible voters participates in the
    vote and a majority of those who participate votes affirmatively; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the council has provided for the registration of
    eligible voters who signify their intention to participate in the
    vote, and a majority of those who are so registered votes
    affirmatively.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum participation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A vote is not affirmative unless more than
  twenty-five per cent of all eligible voters vote affirmatively.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Increased percentage

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The council of a first nation may, by resolution
  adopted before the vote, increase the proportion of votes required
  under paragraph (1)(a) or (b) or subsection (2).

## [Transfers to First Nations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-227696 .Part}

### [Oil and Gas]{.HTitleText2} {#h-227697 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additions to Schedule 1

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by order, add a first nation's name to Schedule 1
  following

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an affirmative vote by the first nation's eligible
    voters on the ratification of the codes prepared under section 10
    and the approval of the transfer of the management and regulation of
    oil and gas exploration and exploitation to that first nation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the making of laws by the council under paragraphs
    35(1)(a) to (d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of name

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a resolution of a first nation's
  council advising of a change in the name of the first nation, the
  Minister may, by order, amend Schedule 1 accordingly.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of contracts

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On a
  first nation's transfer date, the rights and obligations of Her
  Majesty under existing contracts in respect of the first nation's
  managed area --- as well as under any agreements related to those
  contracts --- are assigned to the first nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall provide written notice to contract
  holders of the assignment of their contracts and any agreements
  referred to in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing designations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, after a first nation's
  transfer date, any designations made under the [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5) in respect of oil and gas in the first nation's
  managed area cease to have effect, and the rights and obligations of
  Her Majesty under those designations are superseded by the rights and
  obligations of the first nation under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a designation under the [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5) relates both to oil and gas and to other rights
  and interests unrelated to oil and gas, subsection (3) applies in
  respect of the designation only insofar as it relates to oil and gas.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and interests under contracts

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Oil
  and gas laws that come into force on a first nation's transfer date
  may not impair the rights or interests of a contract holder under a
  contract assigned by section 23.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of first nation to amend

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of the
  making of laws by a first nation after its transfer date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Managed area

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  managed area of a first nation consists of the lands set apart, as of
  the transfer date, as the reserve or reserves of the first nation, and
  includes any lands subsequently added to those lands, unless the first
  nation and the Minister agree otherwise at the time they are added.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excluded lands

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any reserve lands whose boundaries have been surveyed
  under Part II of the [Canada Lands Surveys Act](/eng/acts/L-6) may be
  excluded from a managed area if the transfer agreement so provides.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restoration of excluded lands

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister and the first nation may agree, at any
  time after a first nation's transfer date, to include in the first
  nation's managed area any of the lands excluded from it by the
  transfer agreement, in which case an amendment shall be made
  accordingly to the legal description of the managed area in the
  appli-cable register referred to in subsection 26(1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration of lands

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  legal description of a first nation's managed area shall be entered,
  as of the first nation's transfer date, in the Reserve Land Register
  maintained under section 21 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) or, in
  the case of a first nation to which the [Framework Agreement on First
  Nation Land Management Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46) applies, the [[First
  Nation Lands Register]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, within the meaning of
  subsection 2(2) of that Act. If lands are subsequently added to the
  managed area, the applicable register shall be amended accordingly.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration of existing contracts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All contracts in respect of a first nation's managed
  area that are registered in the Surrendered and Designated Lands
  Register maintained under subsection 55(1) of the [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5) shall be entered, as of the transfer date, in the
  register established by the regulations or, if none has been
  established, in the applicable register referred to in subsection (1).

## [Powers Relating to Oil and Gas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-227763 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ownership and management

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any other provision of
  this Act, a first nation named in Schedule 1 has the powers, rights
  and privileges of an owner in relation to oil and gas in the first
  nation's managed area, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the power, in accordance with its oil and gas laws,
    to manage oil and gas exploration and exploitation in the managed
    area and to issue contracts in respect of the managed area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the power, in accordance with its financial code,
    to collect oil and gas moneys from contract holders and to manage
    and expend those moneys.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exercise of powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The powers of a first nation under subsection (1)
  shall be exercised by the council of the first nation or by any
  person, body or government to which the powers are delegated by the
  first nation's oil and gas laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration of contracts

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A first nation shall send particulars relating to
  each contract issued by the first nation to the Minister for
  registration in the register established by the regulations or, if
  none has been established, in the applicable register referred to in
  subsection 26(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of contracts

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No contract may be issued for any activity in a
  managed area before oil and gas laws for the regulation of that
  activity are in force in the managed area.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laws re exploration and exploitation

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 36 to 41 and 45,
  the council of a first nation named in Schedule 1 has the power, in
  accordance with its oil and gas code, to make laws respecting oil and
  gas exploration and exploitation in the first nation's managed area,
  to the extent that those laws are not in relation to matters coming
  within the exclusive jurisdiction of a provincial legislature and may,
  in particular, make laws

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the issuance and the terms and
    conditions of contracts, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any fees, rates, rents and royalties, including
      royalties in kind, to be reserved to the first nation by contract
      holders,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the interest payable on amounts owing to the
      first nation under a contract, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} administrative monetary penalties that may be
      assessed for failure to comply with the terms of a contract;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting environmental assessments of projects in
    the managed area, and specifying circumstances in which an order may
    be made prohibiting the proponent of a project from undertaking work
    before the completion of an environmental assessment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the protection of the environment from
    the effects of oil and gas exploration and exploitation in the
    managed area;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the conservation of oil and gas in the
    managed area;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} establishing offences punishable on summary
    conviction and imposing fines, impris-onment, restitution and
    community service for the contravention of oil and gas laws and
    orders referred to in paragraph (b);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} respecting the inspection, search, seizure and
    detention of property within or outside the managed area for the
    purpose of ensuring compliance with oil and gas laws and for the
    enforcement of those laws; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} respecting the auditing of records of contract
    holders within or outside the managed area for the purposes of
    contract administration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A first nation may exercise its power to make oil and
  gas laws at any time after the date of an affirmative vote under
  section 21, but no oil and gas law has effect before the first
  nation's transfer date except to the extent necessary to make it
  effective on that date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excluded jurisdiction

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
the power of a first nation to make oil and gas laws does not extend to
laws in relation to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} criminal law and criminal procedure;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} labour relations, working conditions and occupational
  health and safety;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} fish and fish habitat, within the meaning of the
  [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14), migratory birds, within the meaning
  of the [Migratory Birds Convention Act, 1994](/eng/acts/M-7.01), and
  species at risk, within the meaning of the [Species at Risk
  Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3); or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} international and interprovincial trade, including
  customs tariffs and export and import controls.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Projects to be subject to environmental
assessment

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  first nation's oil and gas laws must provide that no project, unless
  exempted by regulations made under subsection 63(2), may be undertaken
  until an environmental assessment of it has been conducted under those
  laws and every decision-making authority for the project has taken the
  results of the assessment into account in making any decision that
  would enable the project to be undertaken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laws in conformity with regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The content of laws respecting environmental
  assessments of projects must conform with regulations made under
  subsection 63(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint assessments and delegation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A decision-making authority for a project may, in
  respect of an environmental assessment of the project,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter into an agreement for the joint assessment of
    the project with any other person or body responsible for
    environmental assessments of that project under this Act or any
    other federal law; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} delegate to another person or body any power,
    except the power to determine whether a project is to be carried
    out.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Environmental protection

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the
regulations, oil and gas laws must provide protection for the
environment that is at least equal to that provided by the laws of the
province applicable to oil and gas exploration and exploitation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oil and gas conservation

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the
regulations, oil and gas laws with respect to the conservation of oil
and gas must not conflict with or be inconsistent with the laws of the
province applicable to oil and gas exploration and exploitation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation on penalties

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** The punishment that may
be imposed by an oil and gas law for an offence is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the act or omission constituting the offence
  results in damage to lands in the managed area,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a first offence, a fine not
    exceeding \$300,000 or imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
    months, or both, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a second or subsequent offence, a
    fine not exceeding \$600,000 or imprisonment for a term not
    exceeding one year, or both; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, a fine not exceeding \$100,000 or
  a term of imprisonment not exceeding three months, or both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers re inspections, searches, etc.

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** Oil and gas laws made
under paragraph 35(1)(f) may not establish procedures inconsistent with
those provided by the laws of the province in which the managed area is
situated, nor may they confer powers greater than those of a public
officer within the meaning of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** Oil and gas laws may
incorporate by reference laws of the province in which the managed area
is located as they are amended from time to time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement with province

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** An oil and gas law may,
in accordance with an agreement between a first nation and the
government of the province in which its managed area is located,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} specify the respective responsibilities of the first
  nation and the province for the administration and enforcement of oil
  and gas laws; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide for access by officers of the first nation
  and the province, respectively, to the managed area for the
  enforcement of oil and gas laws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public access to oil and gas laws

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  first nation shall maintain at its principal administrative office the
  originals of its oil and gas code and its oil and gas laws, and all
  persons shall be given reasonable access to them during normal
  business hours.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certified copies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the request of any person and on payment of a fee
  not exceeding the cost of the service, a first nation shall provide a
  copy of its oil and gas code or an oil and gas law certified to be a
  true copy by a person so authorized by the first nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of laws

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the absence of evidence to the contrary, a
  certified copy of an oil and gas code or an oil and gas law is proof
  of the original without proof of the signature or official character
  of the person appearing to have certified the copy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply in respect of an oil and gas code or the oil and gas laws of
  a first nation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}International obligations

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** In exercising powers or
making laws under this Act, a first nation shall comply with Canada's
international legal obligations and, if the exercise of any power or the
making of any law is found to be inconsistent with Canada's
international legal obligations by a body established under an
international treaty or by a court or tribunal of competent
jurisdiction, the first nation shall remedy the inconsistency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11)

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** Moneys paid out of the
Consolidated Revenue Fund to a first nation under section 30 or 31 are
not public moneys within the meaning of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-227913 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} governing the conduct of a vote under section 17 or
  18, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the content of the question to be put to voters,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the information that must be provided to first
    nation members and other persons, respectively, before a vote and
    the periods during which it must be provided, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the extent to which legal and financial advice
    must be provided or made available to first nation members before a
    vote;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsections 26(2) and 34(3),

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} establishing a register for the registration of
    contracts,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} providing for the transfer of existing contracts
    to that register from the Reserve Land Register maintained under
    section 21 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) or, in the case of a
    first nation to which the [Framework Agreement on First Nation Land
    Management Act](/eng/acts/F-31.46) applies, from the [[First Nation
    Lands Register]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, within the meaning of
    subsection 2(2) of that Act, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} respecting the effects of registering contracts
    in the register, including the priorities attached to registration;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} setting the minimal requirements of oil and gas laws
  made in relation to the protection of the environment, including by
  the incorporation by reference of provincial laws as amended from time
  to time;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} setting the minimal requirements of oil and gas laws
  made in relation to the conservation of oil and gas, including by the
  incorporation by reference of provincial laws as amended from time to
  time; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the absence of an agreement with the government of
  a province referred to in section 43, establishing bodies, or
  designating existing bodies, to administer oil and gas laws that
  incorporate laws of the province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Environmental assessment laws

- **[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of the Minister and the
  Minister of the Environment, make regulations respecting the content
  of oil and gas laws made in relation to environmental assessments,
  including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the types or stages of environmental assessments,
    the circumstances in which each type or stage is to be conducted and
    the required elements of each type or stage;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the appointment of persons or bodies that are to
    carry out each type or stage of assessment and the qualifications of
    persons appointed to those bodies;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the factors to be considered in an assessment in
    determining whether a project should be recommended to proceed, to
    proceed with conditions or not to proceed or be referred to another
    stage of assessment;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the powers and duties of decision-making
    authorities in relation to a project that has been assessed;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the powers of persons or bodies referred to in
    paragraph (b) to compel witnesses to appear before them and give
    evidence, and to compel the production of documents;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the participation of the public in each type or
    stage of assessment;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} access by the public to, and the confidentiality
    of, information that is submitted, collected or generated in an
    assessment;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the immunity from liability of persons or bodies
    referred to in paragraph (b); and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the kinds of exploration activities that are
    defined as projects for the purposes of assessments.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemptions from assessment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of
  the Minister and the Minister of the Environment, make regulations
  authorizing a first nation in its oil and gas laws to exempt from
  environmental assessment any class of project that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ought not to be assessed, in the opinion of the
    Governor in Council, for reasons of national security;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} relates to installations used for oil or gas
    exploration or exploitation and will, in the opinion of the Governor
    in Council, have insignificant environmental effects; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has a total cost that is less than an amount
    prescribed by those regulations and meets environmental conditions
    prescribed by those regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Comparable standards

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The requirements of any regulations made under
  subsection (1) must be generally comparable with those applicable in
  similar circumstances under the [Impact Assessment
  Act](/eng/acts/I-2.75).
