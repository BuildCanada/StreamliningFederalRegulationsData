## British Columbia Treaty Commission Act {#british-columbia-treaty-commission-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1995, c. 45
:::

Assented to 1995-12-15

An Act respecting the establishment of the British Columbia Treaty
Commission
::::

:::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33847 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [British Columbia Treaty Commission Act](/eng/acts/B-8.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33850 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the British Columbia Treaty
    Commission Agreement executed among the Summit, Her Majesty in right
    of Canada and Her Majesty in right of British Columbia on September
    21, 1992 and includes any amendments to it that may be made from
    time to time; ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the British Columbia Treaty
    Commission established as set out in section
    4; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[first nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[first nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an aboriginal governing
    body, however organized and established by aboriginal people within
    their traditional territory in British Columbia, that has been
    mandated by its constituents to enter into treaty negotiations on
    their behalf with Her Majesty in right of Canada and Her Majesty in
    right of British Columbia; ([première nation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Lieutenant Governor in Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Lieutenant Governor in Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Lieutenant Governor in Council of British
    Columbia; ([lieutenant-gouverneur en conseil]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada as may be designated by the Governor in
    Council as the Minister for the purposes of this
    Act; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[principals]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[principals]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Her Majesty in right of
    Canada, Her Majesty in right of British Columbia and the
    Summit; ([signataires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Summit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Summit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the body that is established to
    represent the first nations in British Columbia that agree to
    participate in the process provided for in the Agreement to
    facilitate the negotiation of treaties among first nations, Her
    Majesty in right of Canada and Her Majesty in right of British
    Columbia. ([Sommet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Purpose of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33860 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of Act

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to establish the British Columbia Treaty Commission as undertaken in the
Agreement.

## [Establishment of Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33863 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission jointly established

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  established, by the joint operation of this Act, an Act of the
  Legislature of British Columbia and a resolution of the Summit, the
  British Columbia Treaty Commission consisting of the Chief
  Commissioner and not more than four other commissioners.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission is provincial entity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 14, the Commission shall for all
  purposes be treated as having been established by or under an Act of
  the Legislature of British Columbia.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not agent

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission is not an agent of any of the
  principals.

## [Purpose and Powers of Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33871 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of Commission

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  purpose of the Commission is to facilitate, in British Columbia, the
  negotiation of treaties among one or more first nations, Her Majesty
  in right of Canada and Her Majesty in right of British Columbia.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of the Commission

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission has the capacity and powers of a
  natural person, including the power to sue and be sued, to enter into
  contracts and to acquire, hold and dispose of property for its
  purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In furthering its purpose, the Commission shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assess the readiness, in accordance with the
    Agreement, of Her Majesty in right of Canada, Her Majesty in right
    of British Columbia and one or more first nations to begin
    negotiations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} allocate funds that have been provided to enable
    first nations to participate in negotiations, in accordance with
    criteria agreed to by the principals;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} encourage timely negotiations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the parties to a negotiation all agree,
    assist them in obtaining dispute resolution services;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} prepare and maintain a public record of the status
    of negotiations; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} perform any other duties that are consistent with
    the Agreement and this Act.

## [Commissioners]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33885 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of [[prior
commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  section, [[prior commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person
  who, at any time before the coming into force of this Act, was a
  commissioner pursuant to an appointment made by resolution of the
  Summit, an order of the Governor in Council or an order of the
  Lieutenant Governor in Council or jointly by the Governor in Council,
  the Lieutenant Governor in Council and the Summit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior commissioners continue

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The prior commissioners in office on the coming into
  force of this Act are confirmed and continue as commissioners on and
  after the date of the establishment of the Commission as though they
  had been appointed pursuant to subsection 7(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and obligations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission has all the rights, property and
  interests in existence on the coming into force of this Act that were
  acquired by, and is, instead of any prior commissioner, responsible
  for all the debts, liabilities and obligations in existence on the
  coming into force of this Act that were incurred by, a prior
  commissioner in the performance of that person's duties as a
  commissioner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of commissioners

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} One
  commissioner is to be appointed by order of the Governor in Council,
  one commissioner is to be appointed by order of the Lieutenant
  Governor in Council, two commissioners are to be appointed by
  resolution of the Summit and the Chief Commissioner is to be appointed
  jointly by the Governor in Council, the Lieutenant Governor in Council
  and the Summit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Commissioner may be appointed for a term
  not exceeding three years and each of the other commissioners may be
  appointed for a term not exceeding two years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal of Chief Commissioner

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Commissioner holds office during pleasure
  and may be removed by the Governor in Council, the Lieutenant Governor
  in Council and the Summit jointly.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal of other commissioners

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A commissioner other than the Chief Commissioner
  holds office during pleasure and may be removed by the person or body
  that appointed the commissioner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A commissioner or former commissioner may be
  re-appointed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chief executive officer

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Commissioner is the chief executive officer of the Commission
  and has supervision over and direction of the day to day operations of
  the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  Chief Commissioner or if that office is vacant, the other
  commissioners may designate, by unanimous agreement, one of them to
  act as Chief Commissioner during the absence, incapacity or vacancy,
  and the commissioner so designated has and may exercise all of the
  powers and perform all of the duties and functions of the Chief
  Commissioner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms and conditions of appointment

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** After the principals have
reached agreement, the remuneration and other terms and conditions of
the appointment of the commissioners are to be fixed by order of the
Governor in Council, order of the Lieutenant Governor in Council and
resolution of the Summit.

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33911 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Office

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The office of the
Commission shall be in British Columbia.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Commissioner, one commissioner appointed by the Governor in
  Council, one commissioner appointed by the Lieutenant Governor in
  Council and one commissioner appointed by the Summit constitute a
  quorum of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where acting Chief Commissioner

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of determining a quorum, an acting
  Chief Commissioner shall be counted both as Chief Commissioner and as
  a commissioner appointed by the person or body that appointed the
  commissioner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chief Commissioner to preside at meetings

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Commissioner shall preside at meetings of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decisions of the Commission

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Decisions of the Commission shall be made by
  agreement of at least one commissioner appointed by each of the
  Governor in Council, the Lieutenant Governor in Council and the
  Summit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decisions of the Summit

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Her Majesty in right of
Canada is entitled to rely on resolutions of the Summit that are
certified in the manner specified by the Summit as proof of the
decisions of the Summit that are set out in the resolutions as
certified.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to information

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Commission is deemed
to be a government institution as that term is defined in section 3 of
the [Access to Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1) and section 3 of the
[Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21) for the purposes of those Acts.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection of Commission and employees

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Her Majesty in right of
Canada shall not make any civil claim against the Commission, any
commissioner or any employee of or person retained by the Commission for
anything done, omitted, reported or said in the course of the exercise
or performance or purported exercise or performance of any power, duty
or function under this Act, unless the claim arises from the wilful
misconduct or gross negligence of the commissioner, employee or person
retained.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissioners to be indemnified

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Her Majesty in right of
Canada shall indemnify, in proportion to Her funding obligations as set
out in the Agreement, the commissioners against all claims, damages and
penalties that are made against or incurred by the commissioners in the
exercise of their powers or performance of their duties and functions
under this Act, except where the claims, damages or penalties arise from
the commissioners' wilful misconduct or gross negligence.

## [Personnel]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33932 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personnel of Commission

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may employ or retain persons who are necessary to perform
  the duties and functions of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person employed or retained pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall, by virtue of being so employed or retained, be
  considered to be employed in the federal public administration.

## [By-laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33940 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may make
by-laws consistent with this Act and the Agreement respecting the
carrying out of the work of the Commission, the management of its
internal affairs and the duties of its officers and employees and of the
persons retained by it.

## [Financial Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33943 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual budget

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Commission shall
submit its budget for each financial year to the principals for review
and approval before the deadline set by the principals.

## [Audit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33946 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audit

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The accounts and
financial transactions of the Commission shall be audited annually by a
qualified independent auditor designated by the Commission, and a report
of the audit shall be made to the Commission.

## [Annual Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33949 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission shall, as soon as possible but in any case within six
  months after the end of each financial year, submit to the principals
  a report of all activities under this Act for that financial year,
  including the financial statements and the auditor's report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report submitted pursuant to subsection (1) must
  contain information with respect to the progress of negotiations
  facilitated by the Commission, the other operations of the Commission
  and any other matter that the Commission deems appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling in Parliament

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause a copy of the report to be
  laid before each House of Parliament within the first thirty days that
  that House is sitting after the report is made.

## [Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-33957 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principals may amend Agreement

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act shall be
  interpreted as preventing the principals from amending the Agreement
  from time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies of Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall keep a copy of the Agreement at
  its office and make it available for inspection by any person at any
  time during usual business hours.
