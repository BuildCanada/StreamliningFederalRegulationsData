## Nunavut Land Claims Agreement Act {#nunavut-land-claims-agreement-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1993, c. 29
:::

Assented to 1993-06-10

An Act respecting an Agreement between the Inuit of the Nunavut
Settlement Area and Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the Inuit of the Nunavut Settlement Area have asserted an
aboriginal title to that Area based on their traditional and current use
and occupation of the lands, waters and land-fast ice therein in
accordance with their own customs and usages;

WHEREAS the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/) recognizes and affirms
the existing aboriginal and treaty rights of the aboriginal peoples of
Canada;

WHEREAS Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada and the Inuit of the
Nunavut Settlement Area have negotiated an Agreement based on and
reflecting the following objectives:

to provide for certainty and clarity of rights to ownership and use of
lands and resources and of rights for Inuit to participate in
decision-making concerning the use, management and conservation of land,
water and resources, including the offshore,

to provide Inuit with wildlife harvesting rights and rights to
participate in decision-making concerning wildlife harvesting,

to provide Inuit with financial compensation and means of participating
in economic opportunities,

to encourage self-reliance and the cultural and social well-being of
Inuit;

WHEREAS Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada and the Inuit of the
Nunavut Settlement Area, through their duly mandated representatives,
have entered into the Agreement through which Inuit shall receive
defined rights and benefits in exchange for the surrender of certain
claims, rights, title and interests, based on their assertion of an
aboriginal title;

WHEREAS the Agreement provides that it will be a land claims agreement
within the meaning of section 35 of the [Constitution Act,
1982](/eng/Const/);

WHEREAS the Inuit of the Nunavut Settlement Area have ratified the
Agreement in accordance with the provisions of the Agreement;

AND WHEREAS the ratification by Her Majesty under Article 36 of the
Agreement requires the enactment by Parliament of a statute ratifying
the Agreement;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-370415 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Nunavut Land Claims Agreement Act](/eng/acts/N-28.7).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-370418 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the land claims agreement
    between the Inuit of the Nunavut Settlement Area and Her Majesty the
    Queen in right of Canada, signed on May 25, 1993, and tabled in the
    House of Commons for the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern
    Development on May 26, 1993, and includes any amendments to that
    agreement made pursuant to the agreement; ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Nunavut Settlement Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Nunavut Settlement Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area
    described in section 3.1.1 of the Agreement. ([région du
    Nunavut]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-370423 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-370426 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement is hereby ratified, given effect and declared valid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Agreement is binding on
  all persons and bodies that are not parties to the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, any person or body on which
  the Agreement confers a right, privilege, benefit or power or imposes
  a duty or liability may exercise the right, privilege, benefit or
  power, shall perform the duty or is subject to the liability, to the
  extent provided for by the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No merger

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The rights and benefits of
the Inuit of the Nunavut Settlement Area under the Agreement do not
merge in this Act or any other law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency with Agreement

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  event of an inconsistency or conflict between the Agreement and any
  law, including this Act, the Agreement prevails to the extent of the
  inconsistency or conflict.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency with Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of an inconsistency or conflict between
  this Act and any other law, this Act prevails to the extent of the
  inconsistency or conflict.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deposit

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Indian
Affairs and Northern Development shall cause a certified copy of the
Agreement and any amendments to the Agreement to be deposited in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Library and Archives of Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the library of the Department of Indian Affairs and
  Northern Development that is situated in the National Capital Region;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the legislative library of the territorial government
  that has jurisdiction over the Nunavut Settlement Area; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} such other places as the Minister considers
  advisable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make such orders and regulations as are necessary for the purpose of
carrying out any of the provisions of the Agreement.

## [Appropriation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-370451 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriation

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** There shall be paid out of
the Consolidated Revenue Fund the sums required to meet the monetary
obligations of Her Majesty under Articles 25 and 29 of the Agreement
that arise after the coming into force of this Act.

## [Nunavut Wildlife Management Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-370454 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation of Board

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Nunavut Wildlife Management Board established by the Agreement is
  hereby constituted as a corporation and, as such, the Board has, for
  the purposes of carrying out its functions under the Agreement, the
  capacity, rights, powers and privileges of a natural person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not an agent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Nunavut Wildlife Management Board is not an agent
  of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the
Nunavut Wildlife Management Board shall be in Iqaluit or in such other
place in the Nunavut Settlement Area as the Governor in Council may
designate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration of members

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The remuneration of the
members of the Nunavut Wildlife Management Board shall be set by the
Governor in Council.
