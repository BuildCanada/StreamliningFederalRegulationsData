## Canadian Association of Former Parliamentarians Act {#canadian-association-of-former-parliamentarians-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1996, c. 13
:::

Assented to 1996-05-29

An Act establishing the Canadian Association of Former Parliamentarians
::::

:::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-60944 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canadian Association of Former Parliamentarians
Act](/eng/acts/C-11.7).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-60947 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Association
    of Former Parliamentarians established by this
    Act; ([Association]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Board of Directors of the
    Association established under section
    12; ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[former parliamentarian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[former parliamentarian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
    was, but is not at present, a member of the Senate or House of
    Commons of Canada; ([ex-parlementaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of the Association and
    unless otherwise specified does not include an honorary
    member. ([associé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-60954 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of Act

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to establish a non-profit corporation to be known as the Canadian
Association of Former Parliamentarians.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Association established

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby established
a non-profit corporation to be known as the Canadian Association of
Former Parliamentarians.

## [Objects]{.HTitleText1} {#h-60959 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The objects of the
Association are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to put the knowledge and experience of its members at
  the service of parliamentary democracy in Canada and elsewhere;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to serve the public interest by providing
  non-partisan support for the parliamentary system of government in
  Canada;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to foster a spirit of community among former
  parliamentarians;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to foster good relations between members of the
  Senate and House of Commons of Canada and former parliamentarians; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} to protect and promote the interests of former
  parliamentarians.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-partisan

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Association shall not
pursue its objects for any partisan political purpose.

## [Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-60969 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Association has the
capacity of a natural person and, without restricting the generality of
the foregoing, the Association may, in furtherance of its objects,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} initiate, finance and administer programs and
  activities relevant to its objects, including programs and activities
  by government, public or private organizations and agencies or
  individuals;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into contracts or agreements with governments,
  public or private organizations and agencies or individuals;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} publish or otherwise disseminate information of all
  kinds related to its objects;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} establish and award scholarships or fellowships for
  study related to its objects;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} give recognition, by such means as it deems
  appropriate, for outstanding contributions to the promotion and
  understanding of Canada's parliamentary system of government;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} borrow money on the credit of the Association;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} acquire property, money or securities by gift,
  bequest or otherwise and subject to the terms, if any, on which the
  property, money or securities is given, bequeathed or otherwise
  acquired; hold, expend, invest, administer or dispose of any such
  property, money or securities; or create any security interest in such
  property, money or securities to secure any obligation of the
  Association;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} acquire, establish and manage any charitable or
  benevolent work, undertaking or foundation as it considers
  appropriate;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} expend all amounts received from individuals,
  corporations and organizations as contributions to the Association for
  its activities; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} do such other things as are conducive to the
  fulfilment of its purpose and to the exercise of its objects.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity in Canada

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Association may carry on its activities throughout Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extraterritorial capacity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Association has the capacity to carry on its
  activities and affairs and to exercise its powers in any jurisdiction
  outside Canada to the extent that the laws of that jurisdiction
  permit.

## [By-laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-60987 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may make by-laws respecting

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} membership in the Association;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the election of directors, their term of office and
    the expenses, if any, to be paid to directors;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the employment, engagement or appointment and
    remuneration, expenses and duties of officers, employees and agents
    of the Association;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the procedure in all business at meetings of the
    Board and committees of the Association, including the manner of
    voting at such meetings;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the appointment of patrons and honorary officers of
    the Association; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} generally, the administration and management of the
    business and affairs of the Association and the delegation of its
    powers to the Board or committee of the Board or Association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws to be approved

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No by-law of the Association is valid or shall be
  acted on until it is approved at a general meeting of the members by
  at least two-thirds of those present and entitled to vote at the
  meeting.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Present by-laws

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The present constitution,
by-laws, rules and regulations of the unincorporated association known
as the Canadian Association of Former Parliamentarians, insofar as they
are not contrary to law or the provisions of this Act, shall be the
constitution and by-laws of the Association until altered or repealed in
accordance with this Act.

## [Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61001 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the
Association shall be at such place in Canada as the Board may determine.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board of Directors

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The affairs of the
Association shall be managed by a Board of Directors comprised of a
Chairperson and directors elected or appointed in accordance with the
by-laws of the Association.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Speakers of both Houses

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Speaker of the Senate
and the Speaker of the House of Commons shall be honorary members and
co-chairpersons of the Association.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role of Chairperson

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Chairperson of the
Board shall be appointed or elected as the by-laws of the Association
may provide and shall preside at meetings of the Board and may perform
such other duties as may be assigned by the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vice-Chairperson

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Board may elect from
among its membership a Vice-Chairperson who, in the event of the absence
or incapacity of the Chairperson or if the office of Chairperson is
vacant, shall have all the duties and functions of the Chairperson.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board meetings

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the Board
  shall meet at such times and places as the Chairperson deems
  necessary, but it shall meet at least twice in each year with at least
  one meeting at the head office of the Association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic meetings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), where the Board
  holds a meeting and some directors participate in the business of the
  meeting by electronic means only, such directors are deemed to be
  fully present at such meeting for purposes of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Place of electronic board meetings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless the Board decides otherwise, a meeting under
  subsection (2) is deemed to take place at the place where a majority
  of the directors is gathered or, in the absence of a majority at one
  place, where the largest number of directors is gathered or the
  Chairperson or other director presiding is in attendance, as the Board
  may determine.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61019 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Income revenue and profits

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Subject to any by-law of
the Association providing for the remuneration of officers, employees
and agents of the Association, any profits or accretions to the value of
property of the Association shall be used to further the activities of
the Association and no part of the property or profits of the
Association may be distributed, directly or indirectly, to any member of
the Association.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dissolution

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  petition by the Association and in the manner specified in the
  petition, the minister of the Government of Canada having
  responsibility for the regulation of federal corporations may dissolve
  the Association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debts and liabilities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of a dissolution of the Association, any
  property of the Association that remains after the payment of its
  debts and liabilities or after the making of an adequate provision for
  the payment of its debts and liabilities shall be transferred to such
  person or institution as the Association may specify in the petition
  submitted under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notice of a dissolution under subsection (1) shall be
  published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of Association

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Association is not an
agent of Her Majesty and the Chairperson, the other directors and the
officers and employees of the Association are not part of the federal
public administration.

## [Transition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61033 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Executive Committee

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Upon
  the coming into force of this Act and subject to the provisions of
  this Act, the Executive Committee of the unincorporated association
  known as the "Canadian Association of Former Parliamentarians"
  acquires all the powers of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The present members of the Executive Committee
  referred to in subsection (1) and the present officers of the
  unincorporated association shall, subject to the by-laws, rules or
  regulations of such association, continue to hold office as if they
  had been appointed or elected in accordance with the provisions of
  this Act and of the by-laws made under this Act until their successors
  in office have been so appointed or elected.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61039 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** This Act comes into force
on the day on which it is assented to.
