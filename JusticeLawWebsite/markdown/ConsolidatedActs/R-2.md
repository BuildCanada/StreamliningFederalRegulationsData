the [Radiocommunication Act](/eng/acts/R-2).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-423767 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[broadcasting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[broadcasting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any radiocommunication in
    which the transmissions are intended for direct reception by the
    general public; ([radiodiffusion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[broadcasting certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[broadcasting certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a certificate
    issued by the Minister under subparagraph 5(1)(a)(ii); ([certificat
    de radiodiffusion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[broadcasting undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[broadcasting undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any
    distribution undertaking, programming undertaking and network
    operation to which the [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01)
    applies; ([entreprise de radiodiffusion]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[distribution undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[distribution undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
    meaning as in the [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01); ([entreprise
    de distribution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[encrypted]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[encrypted]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means treated electronically or
    otherwise for the purpose of preventing intelligible
    reception; ([encodage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[harmful interference]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[harmful interference]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an adverse effect
    of electromagnetic energy from any emission, radiation or induction
    that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} endangers the use or functioning of a
      safety-related radiocommunication system, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} significantly degrades or obstructs, or
      repeatedly interrupts, the use or functioning of radio apparatus
      or radio-sensitive equipment; ([brouillage
      préjudiciable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[interference-causing equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[interference-causing equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
    device, machinery or equipment, other than radio apparatus, that
    causes or is capable of causing interference to
    radiocommunication; ([matériel brouilleur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[jammer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[jammer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any device or combination of
    devices that transmits, emits or radiates electromagnetic energy and
    that is designed to cause, causes or is capable of causing
    interference or obstruction to radiocommunication, other than a
    device or combination of devices for which standards have been
    established under paragraph 5(1)(d) or 6(1)(a) or for which a radio
    authorization has been issued. ([brouilleur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[lawful distributor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lawful distributor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an
    encrypted subscription programming signal or encrypted network feed,
    means a person who has the lawful right in Canada to transmit it and
    authorize its decoding; ([distributeur légitime]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Industry; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[network]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[network]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in the
    [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01); ([réseau]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[network feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[network feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any radiocommunication
    that is transmitted

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by a network operation to its affiliates,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to a network operation for retransmission by it
      to its affiliates, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by a lawful distributor to a programming
      undertaking; ([alimentation réseau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 1989, c. 17, s.
    3\]]{.Repealed}

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by
    regulations; ([Version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[programming undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[programming undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in the [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01); ([entreprise de
    programmation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[public]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes persons who occupy
    apartments, hotel rooms or dwelling units situated in multi-unit
    buildings; ([public]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[public switched telephone network]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public switched telephone network]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    telecommunication facility the primary purpose of which is to
    provide a land line-based telephone service to the public for
    compensation; ([réseau téléphonique public commuté]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[radio apparatus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radio apparatus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device or
    combination of devices intended for, or capable of being used for,
    radiocommunication; ([appareil radio]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[radio authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radio authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence,
    certificate or authorization issued by the Minister under paragraph
    5(1)(a); ([autorisation de radiocommunication]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[radio-based telephone communication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radio-based telephone communication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    any radiocommunication that is made over apparatus that is used
    primarily for connection to a public switched telephone
    network; ([communication radiotéléphonique]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[radiocommunication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radiocommunication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
    [[radio]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any transmission, emission or
    reception of signs, signals, writing, images, sounds or intelligence
    of any nature by means of electromagnetic waves of frequencies lower
    than 3 000 GHz propagated in space without artificial
    guide; ([radiocommunication]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou
    [radio]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[radio licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radio licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence issued by the
    Minister under subparagraph 5(1)(a)(i); ([licence
    radio]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[radio operator certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radio operator certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    certificate issued by the Minister under subparagraph
    5(1)(a)(iii); ([certificat d'opérateur radio]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[radio-sensitive equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radio-sensitive equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any device,
    machinery or equipment, other than radio apparatus, the use or
    functioning of which is or can be adversely affected by
    radiocommunication emissions; ([matériel
    radiosensible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[radio station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radio station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
    [[station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a place in which radio
    apparatus is located; ([station de
    radiocommunication]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou
    [station]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[spectrum licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[spectrum licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence issued by
    the Minister under subparagraph 5(1)(a)(i.1); ([licence de
    spectre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[subscription programming signal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[subscription programming signal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    radiocommunication that is intended for reception either directly or
    indirectly by the public in Canada or elsewhere on payment of a
    subscription fee or other charge; ([signal
    d'abonnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[technical acceptance certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[technical acceptance certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    certificate issued by the Minister under subparagraph
    5(1)(a)(iv). ([certificat d'approbation technique]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[telecommunication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[telecommunication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 1993, c.
    38, s. 91\]]{.Repealed}

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-423806 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to Her Majesty and Parliament

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), this Act is binding on Her Majesty in right of
  Canada, on the Senate, House of Commons, Library of Parliament, office
  of the Senate Ethics Officer, office of the Conflict of Interest and
  Ethics Commissioner, Parliamentary Protective Service and office of
  the Parliamentary Budget Officer and on Her Majesty in right of a
  province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may by order exempt Her
  Majesty in right of Canada, or the Senate, House of Commons, Library
  of Parliament, office of the Senate Ethics Officer, office of the
  Conflict of Interest and Ethics Commissioner, Parliamentary Protective
  Service or office of the Parliamentary Budget Officer, as represented
  by the person or persons named in the order, from any or all
  provisions of this Act or the regulations, and such an exemption may
  be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an exemption of Her Majesty in right
    of Canada, in respect of Her Majesty in right of Canada generally,
    or only in respect of a department or other body named in the order;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} either absolute or qualified; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} of either general or specific application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Geographical application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} This Act applies within Canada and on board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any ship, vessel or aircraft that is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} registered or licensed under an Act of
      Parliament, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} owned by, or under the direction or control of,
      Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any spacecraft that is under the direction or
    control of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a citizen or resident of Canada, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a corporation incorporated or resident in
      Canada; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any platform, rig, structure or formation that is
    affixed or attached to land situated in the continental shelf of
    Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions of
  Minister

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any power, duty or function of the Minister under
  this Act or the regulations may be exercised or performed by any
  person authorized by the Minister to do so and, if so exercised or
  performed, shall be deemed to have been exercised or performed by the
  Minister.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-423829 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibitions

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall, except under and in accordance with a radio
  authorization, install, operate or possess radio apparatus, other than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} radio apparatus exempted by or under regulations
    made under paragraph 6(1)(m); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} radio apparatus that is capable only of the
    reception of broadcasting and that is not a distribution
    undertaking.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall manufacture, import, distribute,
  lease, offer for sale or sell any radio apparatus,
  interference-causing equipment or radio-sensitive equipment for which
  a technical acceptance certificate is required under this Act,
  otherwise than in accordance with such a certificate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall manufacture, import, distribute,
  lease, offer for sale or sell any radio apparatus,
  interference-causing equipment or radio-sensitive equipment for which
  technical standards have been established under paragraph 6(1)(a),
  unless the apparatus or equipment complies with those standards.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other prohibitions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall install, use, possess, manufacture,
  import, distribute, lease, offer for sale or sell a jammer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other prohibitions --- subsection
  5.01(1)

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 5.01(2), no person shall
  manufacture, import, distribute, lease, offer for sale, sell or
  possess any radio apparatus, or equipment or device, or component of
  any equipment or device, or any class of radio apparatus, or equipment
  or device, or component of any equipment or device, specified by the
  Minister under subsection 5.01(1).

## [Minister's Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-423843 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's powers

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to any regulations made under section 6, the Minister may, taking into
  account all matters that the Minister considers relevant for ensuring
  the orderly establishment or modification of radio stations and the
  orderly development and efficient operation of radiocommunication in
  Canada,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} issue

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} radio licences in respect of radio apparatus,

    - [(i.1)]{.lawlabel} spectrum licences in respect of the utilization
      of specified radio frequencies within a defined geographic area,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} broadcasting certificates in respect of radio
      apparatus that form part of a broadcasting undertaking,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} radio operator certificates,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} technical acceptance certificates in respect of
      radio apparatus, interference-causing equipment and
      radio-sensitive equipment, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any other authorization relating to
      radiocommunication that the Minister considers appropriate,

    and may fix the terms and conditions of any such licence,
    certificate or authorization including, in the case of a radio
    licence and a spectrum licence, terms and conditions as to the
    services that may be provided by the holder thereof;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} amend the terms and conditions of any licence,
    certificate or authorization issued under paragraph (a);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} make available to the public any information set
    out in radio licences or broadcasting certificates;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} establish technical requirements and technical
    standards in relation to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} radio apparatus,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} interference-causing equipment, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} radio-sensitive equipment,

    or any class thereof;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} plan the allocation and use of the spectrum;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} approve each site on which radio apparatus,
    including antenna systems, may be located, and approve the erection
    of all masts, towers and other antenna-supporting structures;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} test radio apparatus for compliance with technical
    standards established under this Act;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} require holders of, and applicants for, radio
    authorizations to disclose to the Minister such information as the
    Minister considers appropriate respecting the present and proposed
    use of the radio apparatus in question and the cost of installing or
    maintaining it;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} require holders of radio authorizations to inform
    the Minister of any material changes in information disclosed
    pursuant to paragraph (h);

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} appoint inspectors for the purposes of this Act;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} take such action as may be necessary to secure, by
    international regulation or otherwise, the rights of Her Majesty in
    right of Canada in telecommunication matters, and consult the
    Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission with
    respect to any matter that the Minister deems appropriate;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} make determinations as to the existence of harmful
    interference and issue orders to persons in possession or control of
    radio apparatus, interference-causing equipment or radio-sensitive
    equipment that the Minister determines to be responsible for the
    harmful interference to cease or modify operation of the apparatus
    or equipment until such time as it can be operated without causing
    or being affected by harmful interference;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} undertake, sponsor, promote or assist in research
    relating to radiocommunication, including the technical aspects of
    broadcasting; and

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} do any other thing necessary for the effective
    administration of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian telecommunications policy

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} In exercising the powers conferred by subsection
  (1), the Minister may have regard to the objectives of the Canadian
  telecommunications policy set out in section 7 of the
  [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bidding system for radio authorizations

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} In exercising the power under paragraph (1)(a) to
  issue radio authorizations, the Minister may use a system of
  competitive bidding to select the persons to whom radio authorizations
  will be issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments pursuant to bids

  [(1.3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister accepts a bid for a radio
  authorization under a system of competitive bidding, any moneys
  payable to Her Majesty pursuant to the bid are in lieu of any fees
  fixed under this or any other Act for the radio authorization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedures for bidding system

  [(1.4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may establish procedures, standards
  and conditions, including, without limiting the generality of the
  foregoing, bidding mechanisms, minimum bids, bidders' qualifications,
  acceptance of bids, application fees for bidders, deposit
  requirements, withdrawal penalties and payment schedules, applicable
  in respect of a system of competitive bidding used under subsection
  (1.2) in selecting the person to whom a radio authorization will be
  issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation

  [(1.5)]{.lawlabel} Any person who is subject to the procedures,
  standards and conditions applicable in respect of a system of
  competitive bidding used under subsection (1.2) shall comply with all
  of them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension or revocation of radio
  authorization

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may suspend or revoke a radio
  authorization

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with the consent of the holder thereof;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} after giving written notice to the holder and
    giving the holder a reasonable opportunity to make representations
    to the Minister with respect thereto, where the Minister is
    satisfied that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the holder has contravened this Act, the
      regulations or the terms or conditions of the radio authorization,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the radio authorization was obtained through
      misrepresentation; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on giving written notice of suspension or
    revocation to the holder, without having to give the holder an
    opportunity to make representations to the Minister with respect
    thereto, where the holder has failed to comply with a request to pay
    fees or interest due under paragraph 6(1)(l).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 4(5) --- devices

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 4(5),

  or device, or component of any equipment or device, or any class of

  also, by order, exempt any person, or class of persons, in whole or in
  part, on the conditions that the Minister may specify, from the
  application of that subsection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

- **[[5.02]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.02 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  order made under subsection 5.01(1) or (2) may incorporate by
  reference any document, regardless of its source, either as it exists
  on a particular date or as it is amended from time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessibility of incorporated documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall ensure that any document that is
  incorporated by reference in the order is accessible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No finding of guilt or administrative
  penalty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person is not liable to be found guilty of an
  offence or subjected to an administrative monetary penalty for any
  contravention in respect of which a document that is incorporated by
  reference in the order is relevant unless, at the time of the alleged
  contravention, the document was accessible as required by
  subsection (2) or it was otherwise accessible to the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No registration or publication required

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a document that is
  incorporated by reference in the order is not required to be
  transmitted for registration or published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) by reason only that it is
  incorporated by reference.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing power not limited

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the express power in this
  section to incorporate a document by reference does not limit the
  power that otherwise exists to incorporate a document by reference in
  a regulation made under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information sharing --- Canada

- **[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Information that has been collected
  or obtained by the Minister in the administration of this Act may be
  disclosed by the Minister to a federal department, a provincial or
  municipal government in Canada, or an agency of that federal,
  provincial or municipal government, to the extent that the disclosure
  is necessary for the administration of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information sharing --- Government of
  foreign state and international organization

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information may also be disclosed by the Minister
  under an agreement, a memorandum of understanding or an arrangement in
  writing between the Government of Canada and the government of a
  foreign state, an international organization of states or an
  international organization established by the governments of states,
  or any institution of that government or organization, if the Minister
  believes that the information may be relevant to an investigation or
  proceeding in respect of a contravention under this Act or of the laws
  of that foreign state that address conduct that is substantially
  similar to conduct that would be in contravention of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The agreement, memorandum of understanding or
  arrangement must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} restrict the use of the information to purposes
    relevant to an investigation or proceeding in respect of a
    contravention of the laws of the foreign state that address conduct
    referred to in subsection (2);

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-424026 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibitions

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} knowingly send, transmit or cause to be sent or
    transmitted any false or fraudulent distress signal, message, call
    or radiogram of any kind;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} without lawful excuse, interfere with or obstruct
    any radiocommunication;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} decode an encrypted subscription programming signal
    or encrypted network feed otherwise than under and in accordance
    with an authorization from the lawful distributor of the signal or
    feed;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} operate a radio apparatus so as to receive an
    encrypted subscription programming signal or encrypted network feed
    that has been decoded in contravention of paragraph (c); or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} retransmit to the public an encrypted subscription
    programming signal or encrypted network feed that has been decoded
    in contravention of paragraph (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Except as prescribed, no person shall make use of
  or divulge a radio-based telephone communication

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the originator of the communication or the
    person intended by the originator of the communication to receive it
    was in Canada when the communication was made; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} unless the originator, or the person intended by
    the originator to receive the communication consents to the use or
    divulgence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as prescribed, no person shall intercept and
  make use of, or intercept and divulge, any radiocommunication, except
  as permitted by the originator of the communication or the person
  intended by the originator of the communication to receive it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply in respect of
  radiocommunication that consists of broadcasting, a subscription
  programming signal or a network feed.

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contravenes section 4 or paragraph 9(1)(a) or (b),

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} without lawful excuse, manufactures, imports,

  - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} contravenes subsection 5(1.5), or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} contravenes or fails to comply with a regulation,

  is guilty of an offence punishable on summary conviction and is
  liable, in the case of an individual, to a fine not exceeding five

  or to both, or, in the case of a corporation, to a fine not exceeding
  twenty-five thousand dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person is guilty of an offence punishable on

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not submit the information required by the

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes paragraph 9(1)(c)
  or (d) is guilty of an offence punishable on summary conviction and is
  liable, in the case of an individual, to a fine not exceeding ten
  thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
  months, or to both, or, in the case of a corporation, to a fine not
  exceeding twenty-five thousand dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2.2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes paragraph 9(1)(e) is
  guilty of an offence punishable on summary conviction and is liable,

  both, or, in the case of a corporation, to a fine not exceeding two
  hundred thousand dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2.3)]{.lawlabel} No person who decodes an encrypted subscription

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Due diligence

  [(2.5)]{.lawlabel} No person shall be convicted of an offence under
  paragraph 9(1)(c), (d) or (e) if the person exercised all due

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an offence under this section is committed or

  the offence is committed or continued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Injunctions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a court of competent jurisdiction is satisfied,

  appropriate, ordering any person to cease or refrain from any activity

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Federal Court

  is a court of competent jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A prosecution for an offence under this Act may be
  commenced within, but not after, three years after the day on which
  the subject-matter of the offence arose.

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  case of a conviction for an offence under paragraph 10(1)(a), any
  radio apparatus in relation to which or by means of which the offence
  was committed may be forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada by
  order of the Minister for such disposition, subject to subsections (2)
  to (6), as the Minister may direct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of forfeiture

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a radio apparatus is ordered to be forfeited
  under subsection (1), the Minister shall cause a notice of the
  forfeiture to be published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application by person claiming interest

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any person, other than a party to the proceedings
  that resulted in a forfeiture under subsection (1), who claims an
  interest in the apparatus as owner, mortgagee, lien holder or holder
  of any like interest may, within thirty days after the making of the
  order of forfeiture, apply to any superior court of competent
  jurisdiction for an order under subsection (6), whereupon the court
  shall fix a day for the hearing of the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An applicant for an order under subsection (6) shall,
  at least thirty days before the day fixed for the hearing of the
  application, serve a notice of the application and of the hearing on
  the Minister and on all other persons claiming an interest in the
  apparatus that is the subject-matter of the application as owner,
  mortgagee, lien holder or holder of any like interest of whom the
  applicant has knowledge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of intervention

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every person, other than the Minister, who is served
  with a notice under subsection (4) and who intends to appear at the
  hearing of the application to which the notice relates shall, at least
  ten days before the day fixed for the hearing, file an appropriate
  notice of intervention in the record of the court and serve a copy
  thereof on the Minister and on the applicant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order declaring nature and extent of
  interests

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where, on the hearing of an application under this
  section, the court is satisfied that the applicant, or the
  interveners, if any, or any of them,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are innocent of any complicity and collusion in any
    conduct that caused the apparatus to be subject to forfeiture, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of owners, exercised all reasonable
    care in respect of the persons permitted to obtain possession and
    use of the apparatus to satisfy themselves that it was not likely to
    be used in the commission of an offence under paragraph 10(1)(a),

  any applicant or intervener in respect of whom the court is so
  satisfied is entitled to an order declaring that his interest is not
  affected by the forfeiture and declaring the nature and extent of his
  interest and the priority of his interest in relation to other
  interests recognized pursuant to this subsection, and the court may,
  in addition, order that the apparatus to which the interests relate be
  delivered to one or more of the persons found to have an interest
  therein, or that an amount equal to the value of each of the interests
  so declared be paid to the persons found to have those interests.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1989, c. 17, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemptions

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by order, subject to any terms and conditions that he or
  she may specify, exempt any person, class of persons or entity from
  the application of subsection 4(4) or paragraph 9(1)(b), for any of
  the following purposes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} national security;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} public safety, including with respect to
    penitentiaries and prisons;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} customs and immigration;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} national defence;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} international relations;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the investigation or prosecution of offences in
    Canada, including the preservation of evidence;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the protection of property, or the prevention of
    serious harm to any person; or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} for any other purpose prescribed by regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make regulations for the
  purpose of paragraph (1)(h).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-2, s. 14
- 1989, c. 17, s. 6
- 2014, c. 39, s. 181

## [Administrative Monetary Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-424141 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission of violation

**[[15.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.1 .sectionLabel}** Every contravention
of any of subsections 4(1), (3) to (5) and 5(1.5) constitutes a
violation and the person who commits the violation is liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to an administrative
  monetary penalty not exceeding \$25,000 and, for a subsequent
  contravention, a penalty not exceeding \$50,000; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, to an administrative monetary
  penalty not exceeding \$10,000,000 and, for a subsequent
  contravention, a penalty not exceeding \$15,000,000.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 182
- [2024, c. 17, s. 382](#1468438-1482168){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1468438-1482168.html"
  role="button"}
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-15.1-20141216.html "Link to previous version of section 15.1"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria for penalty

- **[[15.11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the penalty is to be
  determined by taking into account the following factors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature and scope of the violation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the history of compliance with this Act by the
    person who committed the violation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any benefit that the person obtained from the
    commission of the violation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person's ability to pay the penalty;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any factors established by the regulations; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other relevant factor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of penalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of the penalty is to promote compliance
  with this Act and not to punish.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 182
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of Minister --- violation

**[[15.12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.12 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} designate any person, or any person who is a member
  of a class of persons, as being authorized to issue notices of
  violation or to accept undertakings; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish, in respect of each violation, a short-form
  description to be used in notices of violation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 182
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entry into undertaking

- **[[15.13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person may enter into an
  undertaking after a notice of violation is served on them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The undertaking

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall be accepted by a person who is authorized to
    accept an undertaking;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall identify every act or omission that
    constitutes a violation and that is covered by the undertaking;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} shall identify every provision at issue;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} may contain any conditions that the person who is
    authorized to accept an undertaking considers appropriate; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} may include a requirement to pay a specified
    amount.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of undertaking

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person enters into an undertaking, the
  proceeding that is commenced by the notice of violation is ended in
  respect of that person in connection with any act or omission referred
  to in the undertaking.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to respect undertaking

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Failure to respect an undertaking constitutes a
  violation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 182
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance and service

- **[[15.14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person who is authorized to issue
  notices of violation and who believes, on reasonable grounds, that a
  person has committed a violation may issue, and shall cause to be
  served on the person, a notice of violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of violation shall name the person who is
  believed to have committed the violation, identify the violation and
  include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the penalty that the person is liable to pay;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement as to the right of the person, within
    30 days after the day on which the notice is served, or within any
    longer period that the Minister specifies, to pay the penalty or to
    make representations with respect to the violation and the penalty,
    and the manner for doing so; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that if the person does not
    pay the penalty or make representations in accordance with the
    notice, the person is deemed to have committed the violation and the
    penalty is to be imposed.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 182
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

- **[[15.15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a person who is served with a
  notice of violation pays the penalty proposed in the notice, the
  person is deemed to have committed the violation and the proceedings
  in respect of it are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representations to Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person who is served with a notice of violation
  makes representations in accordance with the notice, the Minister
  shall decide, on a balance of probabilities, after considering any
  other representations that the Minister considers appropriate, whether
  the person committed the violation and may, if the Minister so
  decides, impose the penalty set out in the notice, a lesser penalty or
  no penalty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to pay or make representations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person who is served with a notice of violation
  neither pays the penalty nor makes representations in accordance with
  the notice, the person is deemed to have committed the violation and
  the penalty is to be imposed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of decision and notice of rights

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause a copy of any decision made
  under subsection (2) to be issued and served on the person together
  with a notice of the person's right to appeal under section 15.2.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 182
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

**[[15.16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.16 .sectionLabel}** In a proceeding in
respect of a violation, a notice purporting to be served under
subsection 15.14(1) or a copy of a decision purporting to be served
under subsection 15.15(4) is admissible in evidence without proof of the
signature or official character of the person appearing to have signed
it.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 182
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

- **[[15.17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is a defence for a person in a
  proceeding in relation to a violation to establish that they exercised
  due diligence to prevent the violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Common law principles

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every rule and principle of the common law that
  renders any circumstance a justification or excuse in relation to a
  charge for an offence under this Act applies in respect of a violation
  to the extent that it is not inconsistent with this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 182
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vicarious liability --- acts of employees
and agents and mandataries

**[[15.18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.18 .sectionLabel}** A person is liable
for a violation that is committed by an employee of the person acting in
the course of the employee's employment, or by an agent or mandatary of
the person acting within the scope of the agent's or mandatary's
authority, whether or not the employee or agent or mandatary who
actually committed the violation is identified or proceeded against.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 182
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officer, director or agent or mandatary
of corporations

**[[15.19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.19 .sectionLabel}** An officer,
director or agent or mandatary of a corporation that commits a violation
is liable for the violation if they directed, authorized, assented to,
acquiesced in or participated in the commission of the violation,
whether or not the corporation is proceeded against.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 182
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal to Federal Court

- **[[15.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), an appeal
  may be brought in the Federal Court from a decision made under
  subsection 15.15(2) within 30 days after the day on which the decision
  is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal on question of fact

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An appeal on a question of fact may be brought only
  with the leave of the Federal Court, an application for which shall be
  made within 30 days after the day on which the decision is made. The
  appeal may not be brought later than 30 days after the day on which
  leave to appeal is granted.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 182
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debts due to Her Majesty

- **[[15.21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following amounts are debts due
  to Her Majesty in right of Canada that may be recovered in the Federal
  Court:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount payable under an undertaking entered
    into under subsection 15.13(1), beginning on the day specified in
    the undertaking or, if no day is specified, beginning on the day on
    which the undertaking is entered into;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the penalty set out in a notice of
    violation, beginning on the day on which it is required to be paid
    in accordance with the notice, unless representations are made in
    accordance with the notice;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if representations are made, either the amount of
    the penalty that is imposed by the Minister or on appeal, as the
    case may be, beginning on the day specified by the Minister or the
    court or, if no day is specified, beginning on the day on which the
    decision is made; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any reasonable expenses incurred in
    attempting to recover an amount referred to in any of paragraphs (a)
    to (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit or prescription

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A proceeding to recover such a debt may not be
  commenced later than five years after the day on which the debt
  becomes payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receiver General

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A penalty paid or recovered in relation to a
  violation is payable to the Receiver General.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 182
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of default

- **[[15.22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may issue a certificate
  for the unpaid amount of any debt referred to in subsection 15.21(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of registration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Registration of a certificate in the Federal Court
  has the same effect as a judgment of that Court for a debt of the
  amount set out in the certificate and all related registration costs.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 182
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit or prescription

- **[[15.23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.23
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A proceeding in respect of a
  violation may not be commenced later than three years after the day on
  which the subject-matter of the proceedings becomes known to the
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document appearing to have been issued by the
  Minister, certifying the day on which the subject-matter of any
  proceedings became known to him or her, is admissible in evidence
  without proof of the signature or official character of the person
  appearing to have signed the document and is, in the absence of
  evidence to the contrary, proof of the matter asserted in it.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 182
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

**[[15.24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.24 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
make public

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of a person who committed a violation, the
  nature of the violation including the acts or omissions, or the
  provisions at issue and the amount of the penalty; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of a person who enters into an undertaking,
  the nature of the undertaking including the acts or omissions, or the
  provisions at issue, the conditions included in the undertaking and,
  if applicable, the amount of the penalty.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 182
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How act or omission may be proceeded with

**[[15.25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.25 .sectionLabel}** If an act or
omission may be proceeded with either as a violation or as an offence,
proceeding in one manner precludes proceeding in the other.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 182
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

**[[15.26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.26 .sectionLabel}** For greater
certainty, a violation is not an offence and, accordingly, section 126
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) does not apply.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 182
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[15.27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.27 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in
Council may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} designating provisions of this Act whose
  contravention constitutes a separate violation in respect of each day
  during which it continues;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of paragraph 15.11(1)(e),
  establishing other factors to be considered in determining the amount
  of the penalty; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting undertakings entered into under section
  15.13.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 182
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-424278 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificates or reports of inspectors

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In any
  proceeding under this Act, or in any other proceeding to which the
  legislative jurisdiction of Parliament extends, a certificate or
  report purporting to have been given by an inspector who did an
  inspection or test pursuant to this Act and to have been signed by
  that inspector is admissible in evidence and, in the absence of any
  evidence to the contrary, is proof of the matters stated therein
  relating to the inspection or test, without proof of the signature,
  official character or capacity of the person appearing to have signed
  the certificate or report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No admissibility without notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No certificate or report shall be received in
  evidence pursuant to subsection (1) unless the party who intends to
  produce it has given to the party against whom it is intended to be
  produced reasonable notice of that intention, together with a copy of
  the certificate or report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attendance of inspector

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party who receives notice under subsection (2) may,
  with leave of the court, require the attendance of the inspector for
  the purposes of cross-examination.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-2, s. 16
- 1989, c. 17, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection from personal liability

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  action or other proceeding for damages lies or may be instituted
  against a Minister, servant or agent of the Crown for or in respect of
  anything done or omitted to be done, or purported to be done or
  omitted to be done, in good faith under this Act or any order or
  regulation issued or made under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crown not relieved of liability

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not relieve the Crown of
  liability for the acts or omissions described therein, and the Crown
  is liable under the Crown Liability Act or any other law as if that
  subsection had not been enacted.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1989, c. 17, s. 7
:::

## [Civil Action]{.HTitleText1} {#h-424295 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of civil action

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} holds an interest in the content of a subscription
    programming signal or network feed, by virtue of copyright ownership
    or a licence granted by a copyright owner,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is authorized by the lawful distributor of a
    subscription programming signal or network feed to communicate the
    signal or feed to the public,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} holds a licence to carry on a broadcasting
    undertaking issued by the Canadian Radio-television and
    Telecommunications Commission under the [Broadcasting
    Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01), or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} develops a system or technology, or manufactures or
    supplies to a lawful distributor equipment, for the purpose of
    encrypting a subscription programming signal or network feed, or
    manufactures, supplies or sells decoders, to enable authorized
    persons to decode an encrypted subscription programming signal or
    encrypted network feed

  may, where the person has suffered loss or damage as a result of
  conduct that is contrary to paragraph 9(1)(c), (d) or (e) or 10(1)(b),
  in any court of competent jurisdiction, sue for and recover damages
  from the person who engaged in the conduct, or obtain such other
  remedy, by way of injunction, accounting or otherwise, as the court
  considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules applicable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In an action under subsection (1) against a person,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a monetary judgment may not exceed one thousand
    dollars where the person is an individual and the conduct engaged in
    by the person is neither contrary to paragraph 9(1)(e) or 10(1)(b)
    nor engaged in for commercial gain; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the costs of the parties are in the discretion of
    the court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence of prior proceedings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In an action under subsection (1) against a person,
  the record of proceedings in any court in which that person was
  convicted of an offence under paragraph 9(1)(c), (d) or (e) or
  10(1)(b) is, in the absence of any evidence to the contrary, proof
  that the person against whom the action is brought engaged in conduct
  that was contrary to that paragraph, and any evidence given in those
  proceedings as to the effect of that conduct on the person bringing
  the action is evidence thereof in the action.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction of Federal Court

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of an action under subsection (1),
  the Federal Court is a court of competent jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An action under subsection (1) may be commenced
  within, but not after, three years after the conduct giving rise to
  the action was engaged in.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Copyright Act](/eng/acts/C-42)

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section affects any right or remedy
  that an aggrieved person may have under the [Copyright
  Act](/eng/acts/C-42).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1991, c. 11, s. 85
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of civil action

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who has made or received a radio-based telephone communication
  that the person believes on reasonable grounds will be or has been
  divulged or will be used or has been made use of contrary to
  subsection 9(1.1) may, in any court of competent jurisdiction, bring
  an action to prevent the divulgence or use of or to recover damages
  from the person who will divulge or has divulged or who will make use
  of or has made use of the radio-based telephone communication, and in
  any such action the court may grant any remedy, by way of injunction,
  damages, accounting or otherwise, as the court considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence of prior proceedings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In an action under subsection (1) against a person,
  the record of proceedings in any court in which that person was
  convicted of an offence under subsection 9(1.1) is, in the absence of
  any evidence to the contrary, proof that the person against whom the
  action is brought divulged or made use of the radio-based telephone
  communication and any evidence given in those proceedings as to the
  effect of the divulgence or use on the person bringing the action is
  evidence thereof in the action.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction of Federal Court

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of an action under subsection (1),
  the Federal Court is a court of competent jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An action under subsection (1) may be commenced
  within, but not after, three years after the conduct giving rise to
  the action was engaged in.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remedies not affected

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section affects any other right or
  remedy that an aggrieved person might otherwise have.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1993, c. 40, s. 26

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 1989, c. 17, s. 16

  - ###### Radio licences, etc. {#radio-licences-etc. .MarginalNote}

    [16]{.lawlabel} Radio licences, technical construction and operating
    certificates and radio operator certificates that were in force
    under the Radio Act immediately before this Act comes into force
    continue in force thereafter as if they had been issued in
    accordance with the Radio Act as amended by this Act.

- --- 1995, c. 1, s. 62(4)

  - ###### Idem {#idem .MarginalNote}

    - [62]{.lawlabel} [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every reference to the Minister
      of Communications in any order, regulation or other instrument
      made under the [Radiocommunication Act](/eng/acts/R-2) or the
      [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4) shall, unless the
      context otherwise requires, be read as a reference to the Minister
      of Industry.

    - ###### Exception {#exception .MarginalNote}

      [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (3), any dispute as to
      the compensation to be paid for the taking of possession of a
      radio station on settlement land as defined in section 2 of the
      [Yukon First Nations Land Claims Settlement Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.3),
      land identified as such in a self-government agreement as defined
      in the [Yukon First Nations Self-Government Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.6)
      or on Tetlit Gwich'in Yukon land may be heard and determined only
      by the body established under the laws of the Legislature of Yukon
      having jurisdiction with respect to surface rights and in
      accordance with those laws.

    - ###### Settlement land {#settlement-land .MarginalNote}

      [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the Yukon first nation concerned does not
      consent to it, no interest in settlement land as defined in
      section 2 of the [Yukon First Nations Land Claims Settlement
      Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.3) or identified as such in a self-government
      agreement as defined in the [Yukon First Nations Self-Government
      Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.6) may be taken possession of under this
      section without the consent of the Governor in Council.
