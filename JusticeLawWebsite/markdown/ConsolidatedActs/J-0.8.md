### [DIVISION 3]{.HLabel2}[PPP Canada Inc.]{.HTitleText2} {#h-335599 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[209]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-209 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
this Division, [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[Crown
corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
have the same meanings as in section 2 of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acquisition of shares

- **[[209.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-209.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph
  90(1)(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the
  Minister of Infrastructure, Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs
  may acquire the shares of PPP Canada Inc.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shares held by appropriate Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate Minister holds the shares acquired
  under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriate Minister --- other
  transactions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate Minister may, with the approval of
  the Governor in Council, conduct any transaction referred to in any of
  paragraphs 90(1)(c) to (e) of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11) in respect of PPP Canada Inc.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization --- parent Crown
  corporation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} PPP Canada Inc. may, with the approval of the
  Governor in Council, sell or otherwise dispose of all or substantially
  all of its assets.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization --- wholly-owned
  subsidiaries

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any of the corporations that are part of the group of
  corporations that consists of PPP Canada Inc. and of all of its
  wholly-owned subsidiaries may, with the approval of the Governor in
  Council, sell or otherwise dispose of any of the assets of the
  corporations, even if the assets to be sold or otherwise disposed of
  constitute all or substantially all of the total assets of that group.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2016, c. 7, s. 233
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of PPP Canada Inc.

**[[210]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-210 .sectionLabel}** Except as provided in
this Division, PPP Canada Inc., incorporated under the [Canada Business
Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44), is not an agent of Her Majesty in
right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agent for certain activities

**[[211]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-211 .sectionLabel}** PPP Canada Inc. is an
agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada in relation to the following
activities:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} assessing public-private partnership opportunities
  for departments and Crown corporations in accordance with criteria
  established by the Treasury Board;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} advising departments and Crown corporations on the
  implementation of public-private partnership projects;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} acting as a source of expertise and advice for
  departments and Crown corporations on public-private partnership
  issues; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} conducting any activity specified in an order made
  under section 211.1.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2012, c. 19, s. 211
- 2016, c. 7, s. 234
:::

**[[211.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-211.1 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in

Canada Inc. is an agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Her Majesty not liable

**[[212]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-212 .sectionLabel}** Her Majesty in right of

to in section 211.

  Democratic Development Act](/eng/acts/I-17.3).

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Five directors constitute a quorum at any meeting of
  the Board.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite the provisions of any contract, agreement or
  order, no person appointed to hold office as a director, other than
  the President, has any right to claim or receive any compensation,
  damages, indemnity or other form of relief from Her Majesty in right
  of Canada or from any employee or agent of Her Majesty for ceasing to
  hold that office or for the abolition of that office by the operation
  of this Division.

- **[[494]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-494 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Centre is authorized to sell or otherwise dispose of all or
  substantially all of its assets and do everything necessary for or
  incidental to closing out its affairs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debts and liabilities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Centre must apply any proceeds from the disposal
  of its assets in satisfaction of its debts and liabilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may give directions to the Centre to do
  anything that in his or her opinion is necessary

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the exercise of its powers under
    subsection 5(1) of the [International Centre for Human Rights and
    Democratic Development Act](/eng/acts/I-17.3); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to sell or otherwise dispose of all or
    substantially all of its assets, satisfy its debts and liabilities,
    manage its expenses or otherwise close out its affairs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions binding

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Centre must comply with the directions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of records

**[[495]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-495 .sectionLabel}** The President must
transfer to the Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development the
following items, including any electronic versions of them:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the books of account and other financial records, as
  well as any information that the Centre collected in order to produce
  them; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any studies that the Centre has under its control,
  and any other information that it has collected through research.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2012, c. 31, s. 177

  - ###### Ministerial authorizations {#ministerial-authorizations .MarginalNote}

    - [177]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} An authorization issued by the
      Minister under section 32 or subsection 35(2) of the [Fisheries
      Act](/eng/acts/F-14) as it existed before June 29, 2012, or under
      paragraph 32(2)(c) or paragraph 35(2)(b) of the [Fisheries
      Act](/eng/acts/F-14) as it existed before the coming into force of
      subsection 142(2) of the [Jobs, Growth and Long-term Prosperity
      Act](/eng/acts/J-0.8), and that is still valid on the day on which
      that subsection 142(2) comes into force, is deemed to be an
      authorization issued by the Minister under paragraph 35(2)(b) of
      the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) after that coming into force.

    - ###### Amendment {#amendment .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the request of the holder of an authorization
      referred to in subsection (1) that is made within 90 days after
      the day on which subsection 142(2) of the [Jobs, Growth and
      Long-term Prosperity Act](/eng/acts/J-0.8) comes into force, the
      Minister must examine the authorization, and the Minister may,
      within 210 days after the day on which that subsection 142(2)
      comes into force, confirm or amend the authorization or, if the
      Minister is of the opinion that the holder no longer needs an
      authorization, cancel it.

    - ###### Conditions of authorizations {#conditions-of-authorizations .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph 40(3)(a) of the [Fisheries
      Act](/eng/acts/F-14) does not apply to the holder of an
      authorization referred to in subsection (1) until 90 days after
      the day on which subsection 142(2) of the [Jobs, Growth and
      Long-term Prosperity Act](/eng/acts/J-0.8) comes into force.
      However, if the holder makes a request under subsection (2), then
      that paragraph does not apply to that holder until the day on
      which that holder receives notice of the Minister's decision to
      confirm, amend or cancel the authorization or until 210 days after
      the day on which that subsection 142(2) comes into force,
      whichever is earlier.
