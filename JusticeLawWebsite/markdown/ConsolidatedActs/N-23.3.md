## Nisga'a Final Agreement Act {#nisgaa-final-agreement-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2000, c. 7
:::

Assented to 2000-04-13

An Act to give effect to the Nisga'a Final Agreement
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the reconciliation between the prior presence of aboriginal
peoples and the assertion of sovereignty by the Crown is of significant
social and economic importance to Canadians;

Whereas Canadian courts have stated that this reconciliation is best
achieved through negotiation and agreement, rather than through
litigation or conflict;

Whereas representatives of the Nisga'a Nation, Her Majesty in right of
Canada and Her Majesty in right of British Columbia have negotiated the
Nisga'a Final Agreement to achieve this reconciliation and to establish
a new relationship among them;

Whereas the Constitution of Canada is the supreme law of Canada;

Whereas the Nisga'a Final Agreement states that the Agreement does not
alter the Constitution of Canada;

Whereas the Nisga'a Final Agreement states that the Canadian Charter of
Rights and Freedoms applies to Nisga'a Government in respect of all
matters within its authority, bearing in mind the free and democratic
nature of Nisga'a Government as set out in the Agreement;

And Whereas the enactment of federal legislation is required by the
Nisga'a Final Agreement to give effect to the Agreement;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, Therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365218 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Nisga'a Final Agreement Act](/eng/acts/N-23.3).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365221 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in this Act.

  [[Nisga'a Final Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Nisga'a Final Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Nisga'a
      Final Agreement signed on behalf of the Nisga'a Nation and Her
      Majesty in right of British Columbia on April 27, 1999 and on
      behalf of Her Majesty in right of Canada on May 4, 1999 and laid
      before the House of Commons on October 19, 1999, and includes any
      amendments made to that Agreement from time to time in accordance
      with its provisions. ([Accord définitif nisga'a]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[settlement legislation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[settlement legislation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Acts of
      Parliament and the Acts of the Legislature of British Columbia
      that give effect to the Nisga'a Final Agreement, including this
      Act and the [Nisga'a Final Agreement Act](/eng/acts/N-23.3)
      (British Columbia). ([législation de mise en
      vigueur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Taxation Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Taxation Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Nisga'a
      Nation Taxation Agreement, laid before the House of Commons on
      October 19, 1999, and entered into on the effective date of the
      Nisga'a Final Agreement by the Nisga'a Nation, Her Majesty in
      right of Canada and Her Majesty in right of British Columbia, as
      provided for by paragraph 21 of the Taxation Chapter of the
      Nisga'a Final Agreement, and includes any amendments made to the
      Nisga'a Nation Taxation Agreement from time to time in accordance
      with its provisions. ([accord fiscal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expressions defined in Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Words and expressions used in this Act have the same
  meaning as in the Nisga'a Final Agreement, unless the context requires
  otherwise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/)

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Nisga'a Final Agreement
is a treaty and a land claims agreement within the meaning of
sections 25 and 35 of the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

## [Nisga'a Final Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365232 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nisga'a Final Agreement

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Nisga'a Final Agreement is approved, given effect and declared valid
  and has the force of law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and duties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Without limiting the application of subsection (1), a
  person or body has the powers, rights, privileges and benefits
  conferred on the person or body by the Nisga'a Final Agreement and
  shall perform the duties and is subject to the liabilities imposed on
  the person or body by that Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No provision made by this Act for a matter that is
  already provided for in the Nisga'a Final Agreement limits the
  application of this section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement binding

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Nisga'a Final Agreement
is binding on, and can be relied on by, all persons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict between Agreement and laws

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** In the event of an
inconsistency or conflict between the Nisga'a Final Agreement and the
provisions of any federal or provincial law, including this Act, that
Agreement prevails to the extent of the inconsistency or conflict.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aboriginal rights

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding the common law, as a
  result of the Nisga'a Final Agreement and the settlement legislation,
  the aboriginal rights, including the aboriginal title, of the Nisga'a
  Nation, as they existed anywhere in Canada before the effective date
  of that Agreement, including their attributes and geographic extent,
  are modified, and continue as modified, as set out in that Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aboriginal title

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the aboriginal title of the
  Nisga'a Nation anywhere that it existed in Canada before the effective
  date of the Nisga'a Final Agreement is modified and continues as the
  estates in fee simple to those areas identified in that Agreement as
  Nisga'a Lands or Nisga'a Fee Simple Lands.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The express derogation from the common law contained
  in subsection (1), which is also contained in paragraph 24 of the
  General Provisions Chapter of the Nisga'a Final Agreement, shall not
  be construed so as to limit the effect on the common law of any other
  provision of this or any other Act that does not contain an express
  derogation from the common law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee simple estate

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** On the effective date of
the Nisga'a Final Agreement, the Nisga'a Nation owns the estate in fee
simple, as set out in the Lands Chapter of that Agreement, in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Nisga'a Lands identified in paragraphs 1 and 2 of
  the Lands Chapter of that Agreement; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Category A Lands and Category B Lands.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365255 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriation

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** There shall be paid out of
the Consolidated Revenue Fund the sums that are required to meet the
obligations of Canada under the Capital Transfer and Negotiation Loan
Repayment Chapter and the Fisheries Chapter of the Nisga'a Final
Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make any regulations or orders that the Governor in Council
considers necessary or advisable for the purpose of carrying out any of
the provisions of the Nisga'a Final Agreement or of the Taxation
Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice of Agreements

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice shall be taken of
  the Nisga'a Final Agreement and the Taxation Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of Agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Nisga'a Final Agreement and the Taxation
  Agreement shall be published by the Queen's Printer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the Nisga'a Final Agreement or the Taxation
  Agreement published by the Queen's Printer is evidence of that
  Agreement, and a copy purporting to be published by the Queen's
  Printer shall be deemed to be so published, unless the contrary is
  shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice of Nisga'a laws

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice shall be taken of
  Nisga'a laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence of Nisga'a law

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of a Nisga'a law purporting to be deposited in
  the public registry of Nisga'a laws referred to in the Nisga'a
  Government Chapter of the Nisga'a Final Agreement is evidence of that
  law and of its contents, unless the contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Harvest Agreement

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Fisheries and Oceans has the authority, on behalf of Her
  Majesty in right of Canada, to enter into the Harvest Agreement
  described in the Fisheries Chapter of the Nisga'a Final Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not a treaty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Harvest Agreement does not form part of the
  Nisga'a Final Agreement, and it is not a treaty or a land claims
  agreement within the meaning of section 25 or 35 of the [Constitution
  Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taxation Agreement

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Taxation Agreement is approved, given effect and declared valid and,
  during the period that the Taxation Agreement is, by its terms, in
  force, it has the force of law.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2022, c. 10, s. 177\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in the Taxation Agreement or in this Act
  limits any entitlement of the Nisga'a Nation, a Nisga'a Village or a
  Nisga'a government corporation to any benefit available to it under a
  federal law of general application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfers of capital

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A transfer of Nisga'a capital, other than cash,
  between or among two or more of the Nisga'a Nation, Nisga'a Villages
  and Nisga'a government corporations, is not taxable under a federal
  law during the period that the Taxation Agreement, by its terms, is in
  force.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2022, c. 10, s. 177\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not a treaty

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Taxation Agreement does not form part of the
  Nisga'a Final Agreement, and it is not a treaty or a land claims
  agreement within the meaning of sections 25 and 35 of the
  [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

## [Laws of British Columbia]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365290 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of laws

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** To the extent that a law
of British Columbia does not apply of its own force to the Nisga'a
Nation, Nisga'a Villages, Nisga'a Institutions, Nisga'a Corporations or
Nisga'a citizens, that law applies, subject to this Act and any other
Act of Parliament, in accordance with the Nisga'a Final Agreement to the
Nisga'a Nation, Nisga'a Villages, Nisga'a Institutions, Nisga'a
Corporations or Nisga'a citizens, as the case may be.

## [Application of Other Acts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365293 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the Indian Act
Transition Chapter and paragraphs 5 and 6 of the Taxation Chapter of the
Nisga'a Final Agreement, the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) does not apply
to the Nisga'a Nation, Nisga'a Villages, Nisga'a Institutions or Nisga'a
citizens as of the effective date of that Agreement, except for the
purpose of determining whether an individual is an "Indian".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Section 126 of [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46)

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
section 126 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) does not apply in
respect of anything required to be done or forbidden to be done by or
under the Nisga'a Final Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
neither Nisga'a laws nor any instruments made under the Nisga'a Final
Agreement are statutory instruments within the meaning of the [Statutory
Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7)

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, no
Nisga'a Institution or Nisga'a Court, and no body or person appointed by
Nisga'a Government having, exercising or purporting to exercise
jurisdiction or powers conferred by or under a Nisga'a law, is a federal
board, commission or other tribunal within the meaning of the [Federal
Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7).

## [Legal Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-365304 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of issues arising

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, in
  any judicial or administrative proceeding, an issue arises in respect
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the interpretation or validity of the Nisga'a Final
    Agreement, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the validity or applicability of any settlement
    legislation or any Nisga'a law,

  the issue shall not be decided until the party raising the issue has
  properly served notice on the Attorney General of Canada, the Attorney
  General of British Columbia and the Nisga'a Lisims Government.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice required under subsection (1) must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} describe the judicial or administrative proceeding
    in which the issue arises;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} state whether the issue arises in respect of the
    matters referred to in paragraph (1)(a) or (b) or both;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state the day on which the issue is to be argued;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} give particulars necessary to show the point to be
    argued; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be served at least fourteen days before the day of
    argument, unless the court or tribunal authorizes a shorter period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation in proceedings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In any judicial or administrative proceeding to which
  subsection (1) applies, the Attorney General of Canada, the Attorney
  General of British Columbia and the Nisga'a Lisims Government may
  appear and participate in the proceeding as parties with the same
  rights as any other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsections (2) and (3) do not
  require that an oral hearing be held where one is not otherwise
  required.
