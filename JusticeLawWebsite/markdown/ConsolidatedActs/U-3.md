## United States Wreckers Act {#united-states-wreckers-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. U-3
:::

An Act respecting aid by United States wreckers in Canadian waters
::::

::::::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [United States Wreckers Act](/eng/acts/U-3).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. U-4, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privilege granted

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** United States vessels and
wrecking appliances may salve any property wrecked, and may render aid
and assistance including all necessary towing incident thereto to any
vessels wrecked, disabled or in distress, in the waters of Canada
contiguous to the United States.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. U-4, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Customs and coasting laws

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in the customs or
coasting laws of Canada restricts the salving operations of the vessels
or wrecking appliances referred to in section 2.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. U-4, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of Act

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act ceases to be in
force on a date to be fixed in a proclamation of the Governor General,
which may be issued after the Governor General is advised that the
reciprocal privilege with respect to Canadian vessels or wrecking
appliances salving any property wrecked or aiding any vessels wrecked,
disabled or distressed in United States waters contiguous to Canada has
been withdrawn, revoked or rendered inoperative.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. U-4, s. 4
:::
