## New Bridge for the St. Lawrence Act {#new-bridge-for-the-st.-lawrence-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2014, c. 20, s. 375
:::

Assented to 2014-06-19

An Act respecting a new bridge in Montreal to replace the Champlain
Bridge and the Nuns' Island Bridge

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 375 of chapter 20 of the Statutes of Canada, 2014,
in force on assent June 19, 2014.\]
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364467 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [New Bridge for the St. Lawrence Act](/eng/acts/N-22.6).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364470 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[bridge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bridge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a structure that spans the St.
    Lawrence River and connects the Island of Montreal to the City of
    Brossard, and includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a bridge that replaces the existing Champlain
      Bridge and connects Nuns' Island to the City of Brossard;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a bridge that replaces the existing Nuns' Island
      Bridge and connects Montreal Island to Nuns' Island; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the approaches to both
      bridges. ([pont]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[construction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[construction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in relation to the bridge or a
    related work, includes demolition of existing structures and any
    other work or activity related to its
    construction. ([construction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada designated under section
    3. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to the bridge or a
    related work, includes its maintenance and
    repair. ([exploitation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual, corporation,
    partnership or joint venture. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[related work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[related work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any work that is useful to the operation of the
      bridge, including a toll facility;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any work that is accessory to the bridge or to
      any work referred to in paragraph (a), including any portion of
      Highway 15 situated between the approach to the Nuns' Island
      Bridge and the Atwater interchange that is reconstructed and
      widened. ([ouvrage connexe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364484 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to designate Minister

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
designate a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada to be the
Minister for the purposes of this Act.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364487 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role of Minister

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided in this Act, the Minister is responsible for the administration
of this Act, and the Minister's powers, duties and functions include all
matters relating to the bridge and related works.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The bridge and related
works are declared to be works for the general advantage of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- [Bridges
Act](/eng/acts/B-8)

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  [Bridges Act](/eng/acts/B-8) does not apply to the bridge and related
  works.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- [Service Fees
  Act](/eng/acts/S-8.4)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The [Service Fees Act](/eng/acts/S-8.4) does not
  apply in respect of the tolls, fees and other charges fixed by
  regulations made under paragraph 12(b).

## [Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364499 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister of Public Works and Government
Services

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Public Works and Government Services may enter into an
  agreement with any person for any purpose relating to the design,
  construction or operation of the bridge or any related work, including
  an agreement respecting the collection of tolls, fees or other charges
  that may be imposed under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to carry out agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Public Works and Government Services
  may take any measures that he or she considers appropriate to carry
  out the agreement or to protect the interests or enforce the rights of
  Her Majesty in right of Canada under the agreement, including
  accepting and holding on behalf of Her Majesty any security granted
  under the agreement or releasing or realizing on that security.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not agent of Her Majesty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who enters into an agreement with the
  Minister of Public Works and Government Services under this section is
  not an agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may enter into
any agreement that relates to the bridge or related work, or that is
necessary for the implementation of an agreement entered into under
section 7, with any person or with the government of the Province of
Quebec or any municipality of that Province or any of their agencies or
mandataries.

## [Tolls, Fees or Other Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364509 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Any owner of a vehicle
using the bridge must pay any toll, fee or other charge that is
applicable to the vehicle under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charges recoverable

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A toll, fee or other
charge charged under this Act constitutes a debt owing to Her Majesty in
right of Canada, and the amount of that debt is recoverable in any court
of competent jurisdiction.

## [Order in Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364514 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other exemptions

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, by order, exempt any person, on any condition
  that the Governor in Council considers to be in the public interest,
  from any requirement under any federal Act to obtain a permit,
  licence, approval or other authorization in relation to the
  construction of the bridge or any related work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply to the order. However, the order must be published in the
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorizations deemed issued

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} After completion of the construction of the bridge or
  the related work, as the case may be, any authorization that would
  have been required in relation to its construction but for an
  exemption granted under subsection (1) is deemed to have been issued
  for the purpose of the application of the federal Act for which the
  exemption was granted.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364522 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial regulations

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may make
regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} designating the contravention of any provision of
  this Act as an offence punishable on summary conviction and fixing the
  maximum fine payable for each offence; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} fixing any tolls, fees or other charges to be charged
  with respect to vehicles, or categories of vehicles, using the bridge.
