## Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials Act {#freezing-assets-of-corrupt-foreign-officials-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2011, c. 10
:::

Assented to 2011-03-23

An Act to provide for the taking of restrictive measures in respect of
the property of officials and former officials of foreign states and of
their family members
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-237470 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials
Act](/eng/acts/F-31.6).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-237473 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is a citizen
      within the meaning of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29) or a
      corporation incorporated or continued by or under the laws of
      Canada or of a province. ([Canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation, trust,
      partnership, fund, an unincorporated association or organization
      or a foreign state. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[foreign state]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[foreign state]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a country other than
      Canada, and includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any of its political subdivisions;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its government and any of its departments, or
        the government or any department of any of its political
        subdivisions; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any of its agencies or any agency of any of its
        political subdivisions. ([État étranger]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Foreign
      Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual or an
      entity. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[politically exposed foreign person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[politically exposed foreign person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      person who holds or has held one of the following offices or
      positions in or on behalf of a foreign state and includes any
      person who, for personal or business reasons, is or was closely
      associated with such a person, including a family member:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} head of state or head of government;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} member of the executive council of government
        or member of a legislature;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} deputy minister or equivalent rank;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} ambassador or attaché or counsellor of an
        ambassador;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} military officer with a rank of general or
        above;

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} president of a state-owned company or a
        state-owned bank;

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} head of a government agency;

      - [(h)]{.lawlabel} judge;

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} leader or president of a political party
        represented in a legislature; or

      - [(j)]{.lawlabel} holder of any prescribed office or
        position. ([étranger politiquement vulnérable]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by
      regulation. ([Version anglaise
      seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

  [[property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any real, personal, movable
      or immovable property. ([bien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property of a person

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, the property of a
  person includes property controlled, directly or indirectly, by the
  person.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-237500 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Orders and Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-237503 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  foreign state, in writing, asserts to the Government of Canada that a
  person has misappropriated property of the foreign state or acquired
  property inappropriately by virtue of their office or a personal or
  business relationship and asks the Government of Canada to freeze
  property of the person, the Governor in Council may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make any orders or regulations with respect to the
    restriction or prohibition of any of the activities referred to in
    subsection (3) in relation to the person's property that the
    Governor in Council considers necessary; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by order, cause to be seized, frozen or
    sequestrated in the manner set out in the order any of the person's
    property situated in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make the order or
  regulation only if the Governor in Council is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is, in relation to the foreign state, a
    politically exposed foreign person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is internal turmoil, or an uncertain
    political situation, in the foreign state; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the making of the order or regulation is in the
    interest of international relations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restricted or prohibited activities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Orders and regulations may be made under paragraph
  (1)(a) with respect to the restriction or prohibition of any of the
  following activities, whether carried out in or outside Canada:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dealing, directly or indirectly, by any person
    in Canada or Canadian outside Canada in any property, wherever
    situated, of the politically exposed foreign person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the entering into or facilitating, directly or
    indirectly, by any person in Canada or Canadian outside Canada, of
    any financial transaction related to a dealing referred to in
    paragraph (a); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the provision by any person in Canada or Canadian
    outside Canada of financial services or other related services in
    respect of property of the politically exposed foreign person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An order or regulation made under subsection (1) may
  exclude, individually or by class, any person, property or transaction
  from the application of the order or regulation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permits

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may issue to any person in Canada or Canadian outside Canada
  a permit to carry out a specified activity or transaction, or any
  class of activity or transaction, that is restricted or prohibited by
  order or regulation made under section 4. The permit may be subject to
  any terms and conditions that are, in the opinion of the Minister,
  consistent with this Act and any order or regulations made under
  section 4.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension, revocation, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may amend, suspend, revoke or reinstate
  the permit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An order or regulation made
under section 4 in respect of a politically exposed foreign person
ceases to have effect on the day that is five years after the day on
which it comes into force unless the Governor in Council, by order,
extends it for the period specified in the order. It may be extended
more than once.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of order

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A copy of each order or
regulation made under section 4 must be tabled in each House of
Parliament within 15 days after it is made. It may be sent to the Clerk
of the House if the House is not sitting.

## [Duty To Determine]{.HTitleText1} {#h-237530 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Each of the following
entities must determine on a continuing basis whether it is in
possession or control of property that they have reason to believe is
the property of a politically exposed foreign person who is the subject
of an order or regulation made under section 4:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} authorized foreign banks, as defined in section 2 of
  the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), in respect of their business in
  Canada or banks to which that Act applies;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cooperative credit societies, savings and credit
  unions and caisses populaires regulated by a provincial Act and
  associations regulated by the [Cooperative Credit Associations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} foreign companies, as defined in subsection 2(1) of
  the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), in respect of their
  insurance business in Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} companies, provincial companies and societies, as
  those terms are defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fraternal benefit societies regulated by a provincial
  Act in respect of their insurance activities and insurance companies
  and other entities engaged in the business of insuring risks that are
  regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} companies to which the [Trust and Loan Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) applies;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} trust companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} loan companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} entities that engage in any activity described in
  paragraph 5(h) of the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and
  Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501) if the activity involves
  the opening of an account for a client;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} entities authorized under provincial legislation to
  engage in the business of dealing in securities or to provide
  portfolio management or investment counselling services; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} other entities of a prescribed class of entities.

## [Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-237544 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person in Canada and every Canadian outside Canada must, without
  delay, disclose to the Commissioner of the Royal Canadian Mounted
  Police

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the existence of property in their possession or
    control that they have reason to believe is the property of any
    politically exposed foreign person who is the subject of an order or
    regulation under section 4; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information about a transaction or proposed
    transaction in respect of property referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No criminal or civil proceedings lie against a person
  for disclosure made in good faith under subsection (1).

## [Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-237552 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who wilfully contravenes an order or regulation made under
  section 4

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an indictable offence and is liable to
    imprisonment for a term of not more than five years; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
    conviction and is liable to a fine of not more than \$25,000 or to
    imprisonment for a term of not more than one year, or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who wilfully contravenes section 8 or 9

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an indictable offence and is liable to
    imprisonment for a term of not more than five years; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
    conviction and is liable to a fine of not more than \$25,000 or to
    imprisonment for a term of not more than one year, or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  proceedings in respect of an offence under this Act may be tried and
  determined by the court having jurisdiction at the place in Canada
  where the offence was committed or at the place in Canada in which the
  person charged with the offence is, resides or has an office or place
  of business at the time of commencement of the proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings if Canadians outside Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a Canadian is alleged to have committed, outside
  Canada, an offence under this Act, any proceedings in respect of that
  offence may, whether or not that person is in Canada, be commenced in
  any territorial division in Canada and the accused may be tried and
  punished in respect of that offence in the same manner as if the
  offence had been committed in that territorial division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appearance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the provisions of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) relating to requirements that an
  accused appear at and be present during proceedings and the exception
  to those requirements apply to proceedings commenced under
  subsection (2) in any territorial division.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent of Attorney General required

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Proceedings with respect
to an offence under this Act may be commenced only with the consent of
the Attorney General of Canada.

## [Rights of Persons Who Are Subject to an Order or Regulation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-237571 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is the subject of an order or regulation made under section
  4 may apply in writing to the Minister to cease being the subject of
  the order or regulation on the grounds that the person is not a
  politically exposed foreign person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe
  that the applicant is not a politically exposed foreign person, the
  Minister must recommend to the Governor in Council that the order or
  regulation be amended or repealed, as the case may be, so that the
  applicant is no longer the subject of the order or regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice if application rejected

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must give notice without delay to the
  applicant of any decision to reject the application.

## [Application for a Certificate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-237579 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mistaken identity

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person claiming not to be a politically exposed foreign person may
  apply to the Minister in writing for a certificate stating that they
  are not a politically exposed foreign person who is the subject of an
  order or regulation made under section 4.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate --- ​time frame

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines that the person is not a
  politically exposed foreign person, the Minister must issue a
  certificate to the applicant as soon as feasible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasonable expenses

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is the subject of an order or regulation made under section
  4 may apply to the Minister in writing for a certificate to exempt
  property from the application of the order or regulation if the
  property is necessary to meet the reasonable expenses of the person
  and their dependants.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate --- time frame

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines that the property is
  necessary to meet the reasonable expenses of the applicant and their
  dependants, the Minister must issue a certificate to the applicant as
  soon as feasible.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-237590 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No civil liability

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A person who, in relation
to any property that is the subject of an order or regulation made under
section 4, acts reasonably in taking, or omitting to take, measures to
comply with the order or regulation is not liable in any civil action
arising from having taken or omitted to take the measures if they took
all reasonable steps to satisfy themselves that the property was
property that is the subject of the order or regulation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing equities maintained

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** All secured and unsecured
rights and interests in any property that is the subject of an order or
regulation made under section 4 that are held by a person, other than
the politically exposed foreign person who is the subject of the order
or regulation, are entitled to the same ranking that they would have
been entitled to had the order or regulation not been made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings not precluded

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The making of an order or
regulation under section 4 does not preclude the commencement of
proceedings under any Act of Parliament other than this Act, or any
civil proceedings, in respect of any property that is subject of the
order or regulation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations for carrying out the purposes and provisions of
this Act, including regulations prescribing anything that by this Act is
to be prescribed.

## [Review and Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-237599 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  five years after this section comes into force, a comprehensive review
  of the provisions and operation of this Act and of the [Special
  Economic Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-14.5) must be undertaken by such
  committee of the Senate and of the House of Commons as may be
  designated or established by the Senate and the House of Commons for
  that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The committees referred to in subsection (1) must,
  within a year after a review is undertaken pursuant to that subsection
  or within such further time as may be authorized by the Senate or the
  House of Commons, as the case may be, submit a report on the review to
  Parliament, including a statement of any changes that the committees
  recommend.
