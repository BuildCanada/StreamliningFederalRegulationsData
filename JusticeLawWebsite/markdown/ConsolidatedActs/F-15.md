## Department of Fisheries and Oceans Act {#department-of-fisheries-and-oceans-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. F-15
:::

An Act respecting the Department of Fisheries and Oceans
::::

:::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232256 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Department of Fisheries and Oceans Act](/eng/acts/F-15).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1978-79, c. 13, s. 2
:::

## [Establishment of the Department]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232261 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Department established

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a department of the Government of Canada called the
  Department of Fisheries and Oceans over which the Minister of
  Fisheries and Oceans appointed by commission under the Great Seal
  shall preside.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister holds office during pleasure and has the
  management and direction of the Department.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1978-79, c. 13, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy head

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
appoint an officer called the Deputy Minister of Fisheries and Oceans to
hold office during pleasure and to be the deputy head of the Department.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1978-79, c. 13, s. 4
:::

## [Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232273 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions of Minister

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  powers, duties and functions of the Minister extend to and include all
  matters over which Parliament has jurisdiction, not by law assigned to
  any other department, board or agency of the Government of Canada,
  relating to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sea coast and inland fisheries;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fishing and recreational harbours;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} hydrography and marine sciences; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the coordination of the policies and programs of
    the Government of Canada respecting oceans.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The powers, duties and functions of the Minister also
  extend to and include such other matters, relating to oceans and over
  which Parliament has jurisdiction, as are by law assigned to the
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Security of Canada Information Sharing
  Act](/eng/acts/S-6.9)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In carrying out activities in relation to the
  maritime domain, the Minister may receive information that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} relates to an activity that undermines the security
    of Canada, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Security of Canada
    Information Disclosure Act](/eng/acts/S-6.9); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is relevant to his or her support of a Government
    of Canada institution, as defined in that section, that has
    jurisdiction or responsibilities under an Act of Parliament or
    another lawful authority in respect of those activities, including
    their detection, identification, analysis, prevention, investigation
    or disruption.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-15, s. 4
- 2015, c. 20, s. 4
- [2019, c. 13, s. 122](#1159219-1175755){.wb-lbx

  role="button"}

## [Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232289 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, with the
approval of the Governor in Council, enter into agreements with the
government of any province or any agency thereof respecting the carrying
out of programs for which the Minister is responsible.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1978-79, c. 13, s. 6
:::

## [Annual Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-232294 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall, on or
before the 31st day of January next following the end of each fiscal
year or, if Parliament is not then sitting, on any of the first five
days next thereafter that either House of Parliament is sitting, submit
to Parliament a report showing the operations of the Department for that
fiscal year.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1978-79, c. 13, s. 7
:::
