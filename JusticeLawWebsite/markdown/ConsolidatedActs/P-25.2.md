## Prohibition on the Purchase of Residential Property by Non-Canadians Act {#prohibition-on-the-purchase-of-residential-property-by-non-canadians-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2022, c. 10, s. 235
:::

Assented to 2022-06-23

An Act to prohibit the purchase of residential property by non-Canadians

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 235 of chapter 10 of the Statutes of Canada, 2022,
in force January 1, 2023.\]
:::
:::::

::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Prohibition on the Purchase of Residential Property by
Non-Canadians Act](/eng/acts/P-25.2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an
    individual, means a person who is cohabiting with the individual in
    a conjugal relationship, having so cohabited for a period of at
    least one year. ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[control]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[control]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by the
    regulations. ([contrôle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dwelling unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dwelling unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a residential unit that
    contains private kitchen facilities, a private bath and a private
    living area. ([local d'habitation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the federal minister
    designated under section 3. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an individual who is neither a Canadian citizen
      nor a person registered as an Indian under the [Indian
      Act](/eng/acts/I-5) nor a permanent resident;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that is incorporated otherwise than
      under the laws of Canada or a province;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a corporation incorporated under the laws of
      Canada or a province whose shares are not listed on a stock
      exchange in Canada for which a designation under section 262 of
      the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) is in effect and that is
      controlled by a person referred to in paragraph (a) or (b); and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a prescribed person or
      entity. ([non-Canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5). ([résident permanent]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by
    regulation. ([Version anglaise seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[residential property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[residential property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any real property
    or immovable, other than a prescribed real property or immovable,
    that is situated in Canada and that is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a detached house or similar building, containing
      not more than three dwelling units, together with that proportion
      of the appurtenances to the building and the land subjacent or
      immediately contiguous to the building that is reasonably
      necessary for its use and enjoyment as a place of residence for
      individuals;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a part of a building that is a semi-detached
      house, rowhouse unit, residential condominium unit or other
      similar premises that is, or is intended to be, a separate parcel
      or other division of real property or immovable owned, or intended
      to be owned, apart from any other unit in the building, together
      with that proportion of any common areas and other appurtenances
      to the building and the land subjacent or immediately contiguous
      to the building that is attributable to the house, unit or
      premises and that is reasonably necessary for its use and
      enjoyment as a place of residence for individuals; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any prescribed real property or
      immovable. ([immeuble résidentiel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of Minister

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate any federal minister to be the Minister for the
purposes of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite
  section 34 of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29), it is prohibited
  for a non-Canadian to purchase, directly or indirectly, any
  residential property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- persons

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a temporary resident within the meaning of the
    [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) who
    satisfies prescribed conditions;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a protected person within the meaning of subsection
    95(2) of that Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an individual who is a non-Canadian and who
    purchases residential property in Canada with their spouse or
    common-law partner if the spouse or common law-partner is a Canadian
    citizen, person registered as an Indian under the [Indian
    Act](/eng/acts/I-5), permanent resident or person referred to in
    paragraph (a) or (b); or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a person of a prescribed class of persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- circumstances

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in prescribed
  circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foreign state

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, nothing in subsection (1) is
  to be construed as hindering a foreign state from purchasing
  residential property for diplomatic or consular purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if the non-Canadian
  becomes liable or assumes liability under an agreement of purchase and
  sale of the residential property before the day on which this Act
  comes into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The contravention of
section 4 does not affect the validity of the sale of the residential
property to which the contravention relates.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  non-Canadian that contravenes section 4 and every person or entity
  that counsels, induces, aids or abets or attempts to counsel, induce,
  aid or abet a non-Canadian to purchase, directly or indirectly, any
  residential property knowing that the non-Canadian is prohibited under
  this Act from purchasing the residential property is guilty of an
  offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine of not more than
  \$10,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Party to offence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a corporation or entity commits an offence, any of
  the following persons that directed, authorized, assented to,
  acquiesced in or participated in the commission of the offence is a
  party to and liable for the offence whether or not the corporation or
  entity has been prosecuted or convicted:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an officer, director or agent or mandatary of the
    corporation or entity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a senior official of the corporation or entity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any individual authorized to exercise managerial or
    supervisory functions on behalf of the corporation or entity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  non-Canadian is convicted of having contravened section 4, the
  superior court of the province in which the residential property to
  which the contravention relates is situated may, on application of the
  Minister, order the residential property to be sold in the prescribed
  manner and under prescribed conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, the superior court may
  make the order subject to any terms that it considers appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of the Minister after
  consultation with the Minister of Finance, make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} defining "control" for the purposes of this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting what constitutes a purchase for the
    purposes of this Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the making of orders under section 7;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that by this Act is to be
    prescribed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paragraph (1)(c)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Regulations made under paragraph (1)(c) must provide
  that no non-Canadian receive from the proceeds that results from a
  sale of a residential property ordered under section 7 more than the
  purchase price they paid for the residential property.
