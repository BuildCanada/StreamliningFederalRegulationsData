## Oil Tanker Moratorium Act {#oil-tanker-moratorium-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2019, c. 26
:::

Assented to 2019-06-21

An Act respecting the regulation of vessels that transport crude oil or
persistent oil to or from ports or marine installations located along
British Columbia's north coast
::::

:::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1150791 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Oil Tanker Moratorium Act](/eng/acts/O-8.3).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1150794 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[authorized representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorized representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
    meaning as in section 2 of the [Canada Shipping Act,
    2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15). ([représentant autorisé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[crude oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[crude oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any liquid hydrocarbon
    mixture that occurs naturally in the earth --- including a liquid
    hydrocarbon mixture from which distillate fractions have been
    removed or to which distillate fractions have been added --- whether
    or not it has been treated to render it suitable for
    transportation. ([pétrole brut]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Indigenous peoples of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indigenous peoples of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
    assigned by the definition [[aboriginal peoples of
    Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 35(2) of the
    [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/). ([peuples autochtones du
    Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[marine installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marine installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an installation
    that is used or is capable of being used in the loading or unloading
    of oil to or from vessels and that is on, or is connected to,
    land. ([installation maritime]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[master]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[master]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 2
    of the [Canada Shipping Act,
    2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15). ([capitaine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Transport. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means petroleum in any form, including
    crude oil, fuel oil, sludge, oil refuse and refined
    products. ([hydrocarbure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[oil tanker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[oil tanker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that is
    constructed or adapted to carry oil in bulk in liquid form in its
    hold. ([pétrolier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a vessel, means the
    registered owner of the vessel or, in the absence of registration,
    the person having for the time being, either by law or by contract,
    the rights of the owner of the vessel in respect of its possession
    and use. ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[persistent oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[persistent oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an oil, or an oil of a
    class of oils, set out in the schedule. ([hydrocarbure
    persistant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 2
    of the [Canada Shipping Act,
    2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15). ([bâtiment]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1150820 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mooring or anchoring at port, etc.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for an oil tanker that is carrying crude oil or persistent
  oil, or any combination of the two, in an amount greater than 12 500
  metric tons, in bulk in its hold, to moor or anchor at a port or
  marine installation that is in Canada, on the coast of British
  Columbia, north of 50°53′00′′ north latitude and west of 126°38′36′′
  west longitude.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unloading

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for an oil tanker that is carrying
  crude oil or persistent oil, or any combination of the two, in an
  amount greater than 12 500 metric tons, in bulk in its hold, to unload
  any of that oil at a port that is within the area described in
  subsection (1) or to a marine installation that is within that area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loading

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for an oil tanker to load into its
  hold any crude oil or persistent oil that is at a port or marine
  installation that is within the area described in subsection (1) if,
  when the loading is about to begin, the oil tanker is carrying crude
  oil or persistent oil, or any combination of the two, in an amount
  greater than 12 500 metric tons, in bulk in its hold, or if loading it
  would result in the oil tanker carrying, at any time, crude oil or
  persistent oil, or any combination of the two, in bulk in its hold, in
  an amount greater than 12 500 metric tons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transport to port, etc.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for any person or any vessel to
  transport, by water, any crude oil or persistent oil --- that an oil
  tanker is carrying in its hold --- from the oil tanker to a port or
  marine installation that is within the area described in
  subsection (1) for the purpose of aiding the oil tanker to circumvent
  the prohibition in subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transport from port, etc.

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for any person or any vessel to
  transport, by water, any crude oil or persistent oil from a port or
  marine installation that is within the area described in
  subsection (1) to an oil tanker for the purpose of aiding the oil
  tanker to circumvent the prohibition in subsection (3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 4(1) does not apply to an
  oil tanker that moors or anchors at a port or marine installation

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to ensure the safety of the oil tanker;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to render assistance to a vessel in distress or if
    necessary after rendering that assistance; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to obtain emergency medical assistance for any
    person on board the oil tanker.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- [Canada Shipping Act,
  2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Section 4 does not apply in respect of a vessel
  carrying out an activity in order to comply with a direction given
  under subsection 111(2) or 114(2), paragraph 180(1)(c) or 189(1)(d) or
  subsection 211(3) of the [Canada Shipping Act,
  2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15).

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1150840 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial exemption

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by order, exempt an identified oil tanker from the
  application of any of subsections 4(1) to (3) on any terms and for any
  period that he or she considers appropriate, if he or she is of the
  opinion that the exemption is essential for the purpose of community
  or industry resupply or is otherwise in the public interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of [Statutory
  Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply to an order made under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} After making an order under subsection (1), the
  Minister must make it accessible to the public on the Internet or by
  any other means that he or she considers appropriate.

## [Administration and Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1150848 .Part}

### [Reporting Requirement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1150849 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-arrival information

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of an oil tanker that is constructed or adapted to carry more
  than 12 500 metric tons of oil in bulk in liquid form in its hold must
  report pre-arrival information to the Minister, in accordance with
  subsections (2) and (3), before the oil tanker moors or anchors at a
  port or marine installation that is within the area described in
  subsection 4(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be reported

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The pre-arrival information that must be reported in
  respect of an oil tanker is the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its country of registry;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its owner's name;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} its operator's name;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} its master's name;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the name of the port or the marine installation;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the type and the amount of any oil that it is
    carrying in bulk in its hold; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the type and the amount of any oil to be unloaded
    from or loaded into its hold at the port or the marine installation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of reporting

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The pre-arrival information must be reported

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least 24 hours before the oil tanker moors or
    anchors at the port or the marine installation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible after the oil tanker departs
    from its last port of call, if there is less than 24 hours between
    the moment the oil tanker departs from that port and the moment the
    oil tanker is expected to moor or anchor at the port or the marine
    installation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change in pre-arrival information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If there is any change in the pre-arrival information
  reported under this section, the master of the oil tanker must report
  the change to the Minister without delay.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direction not to moor or anchor

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** If the Minister has
reasonable grounds to believe that the master of an oil tanker has
failed to comply with subsection 7(1) or (4), the Minister may direct
the oil tanker not to moor or anchor at a port or marine installation
that is within the area described in subsection 4(1).

### [Designated Persons]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1150871 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate any person or member of a class of persons for
  the purposes of the administration and enforcement of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must provide every designated person
  with a certificate of their designation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A designated person is not personally liable for
  anything they do or omit to do in good faith under this Act.

#### [Powers]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1150879 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direction to provide information --- oil
tankers

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  designated person may direct an oil tanker that is within the area
  described in subsection 4(1) to provide any information that the
  designated person reasonably requires for the purposes of this Act if
  they have reasonable grounds to believe that the oil tanker is or will
  be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} unloading any oil from its hold at a port that is
    within that area or to a marine installation that is within that
    area; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} loading into its hold any oil that is at a port or
    marine installation that is within that area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direction to provide information ---
  persons and vessels

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A designated person may direct a person or a vessel
  to provide any information that the designated person reasonably
  requires for the purposes of this Act if they have reasonable grounds
  to believe that the person or the vessel is or will be transporting,
  by water,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} from an oil tanker that is within the area
    described in subsection 4(1) to a port or marine installation that
    is within that area, any oil that the oil tanker is carrying in its
    hold; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} from a port or marine installation that is within
    the area described in subsection 4(1) to an oil tanker that is
    within that area, any oil.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to enter a place

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  designated person may, for a purpose related to verifying compliance
  with this Act, enter

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any vessel that is at a port or marine installation
    that is within the area described in subsection 4(1), if the
    designated person has reasonable grounds to believe that the vessel
    is carrying oil as cargo;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any marine installation that is within the area
    described in subsection 4(1), if the designated person has
    reasonable grounds to believe that oil is located there; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other place, if the place is at a port that is
    within the area described in subsection 4(1) and the designated
    person has reasonable grounds to believe that there is, located in
    that place, oil that is to be loaded onto a vessel as cargo or oil
    that was unloaded from a vessel that was carrying it as cargo.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Living quarters

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A designated person is not permitted to enter living
  quarters without the occupant's consent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers on entry

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The designated person may, for the purpose referred
  to in subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine anything in the place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} use any means of communication in the place or
    cause it to be used;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} use any computer system in the place, or cause it
    to be used, to examine data contained in or available to it;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prepare or cause to be prepared any record, in the
    form of a print-out or other intelligible output, from the data;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} use any copying equipment in the place or cause it
    to be used;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} conduct tests or analyses of anything in the place;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} take measurements or samples of anything in the
    place;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} take photographs or make recordings or sketches of
    anything in the place;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} remove anything from the place for the purpose of
    examination, testing or copying;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} direct any person to put anything in the place into
    operation or to cease operating it; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} prohibit or limit access to all or part of the
    place or to anything in the place.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entry on private property

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The designated person and any person accompanying
  them may enter private property -- other than a dwelling-house -- and
  pass through it in order to gain entry to the place. For greater
  certainty, they are not liable for doing so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stoppage or relocation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A designated person may, for the purpose of entering
  a vessel that is at a port or marine installation that is within the
  area described in subsection 4(1), direct the master

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to stop the vessel or proceed to the place that the
    designated person may select, and to moor or anchor or remain there;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to not move the vessel.

#### [General]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1150916 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to assist

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The owner, the operator,
the master and any agent or charterer of a vessel that is entered under
subsection 11(1) and the owner and the operator of a marine installation
or any other place that is entered under that subsection, as well as
every person who is found there, must give a designated person who is
carrying out powers or functions under section 11 all reasonable
assistance and provide them with any information that they may
reasonably require.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition of samples

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  designated person who takes a sample under paragraph 11(3)(g) may
  dispose of it in any manner that they consider appropriate or may
  submit it for analysis or examination to any person the Minister
  considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate or report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who has made an analysis or examination may
  issue a certificate or report that sets out the results of the
  analysis or examination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate or report admissible in
  evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (4) and (5), the certificate
  or the report is admissible in evidence in any prosecution for an
  offence under this Act and, in the absence of any evidence to the
  contrary, is proof of the statements contained in it without proof of
  the signature or official character of the person appearing to have
  signed it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attendance of person

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The party against whom the certificate or the report
  is produced may, with leave of the court, require for the purposes of
  cross-examination the attendance of the person who issued it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The certificate or the report may be admitted in
  evidence only if the party who intends to produce it has given to the
  party against whom it is intended to be produced reasonable notice of
  that intention, together with a copy of the certificate or the report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of anything removed

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Anything removed under
paragraph 11(3)(i) must be returned as soon as feasible after it is no
longer required for the purpose for which it was taken.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for a
person to knowingly give false or misleading information either orally
or in writing to, or otherwise obstruct or hinder, a designated person
who is carrying out powers or functions under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interference

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Unless authorized by a
designated person, it is prohibited for a person to remove, alter or
interfere in any way with a sample taken under paragraph 11(3)(g) or
anything removed under paragraph 11(3)(i).

### [Detention of Vessels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1150936 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Detention order

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  designated person may order the detention of a vessel if they have
  reasonable grounds to believe that the vessel has committed an offence
  under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order in writing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A detention order must be in writing and be addressed
  to every person empowered to grant clearance to the vessel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of detention order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notice of a detention order must be served on the
  master of the vessel

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by delivering a copy of the notice personally to
    the master; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if service cannot reasonably be effected in the
    manner provided in paragraph (a), by leaving a copy of the notice
    with the person who is, or appears to be, in charge of the vessel
    or, if there is no such person, by fixing a copy of the notice to a
    prominent part of the vessel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Movement of vessel prohibited

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 19, it is prohibited for a person
  to move a vessel that is subject to a detention order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Granting clearance prohibited

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (6), it is prohibited for a
  person to whom a detention order is addressed, after they have
  received notice of the order, to grant clearance to the vessel that is
  subject to the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Granting clearance

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A person to whom a detention order is addressed who
  has received notice of the order must grant clearance to the vessel
  that is subject to the order if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} security in an amount and a form satisfactory to
    the Minister is given to Her Majesty in right of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vessel has not been charged with an offence
    under this Act within 30 days after the day on which the order was
    made; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the vessel has been charged with an offence under
    this Act within 30 days after the day on which the order was made
    and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} security in a form satisfactory to the Minister
      for payment of the maximum fine that might be imposed for that
      offence, or payment of any lesser amount that is satisfactory to
      the Minister, is given to Her Majesty in right of Canada, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} proceedings in respect of that offence are
      discontinued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of foreign state

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If the vessel that is subject to a detention order is
  registered in a foreign state, that state is to be notified that the
  order was made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability for expenses

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The authorized representative of a vessel that is
  subject to a detention order or, if there is no authorized
  representative, the vessel's owner is liable for all expenses incurred
  in respect of the detention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interference with service of notice

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for a
person to intentionally interfere with the service of a notice of a
detention order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permission or direction to move vessel

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, in respect of a vessel that is subject to a detention
  order,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on application made by the authorized
    representative of the vessel or, if there is no authorized
    representative, by the vessel's owner, permit the master to move the
    vessel in accordance with the Minister's directions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on application made by the owner of a dock or wharf
    --- or by the person in charge of a port or marine installation ---
    at which the vessel is located, direct the person who is, or appears
    to be, in charge of the vessel to move it in accordance with the
    Minister's directions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-compliance with paragraph (1)(b)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person does not comply with a direction given to
  them under paragraph (1)(b) and the Minister is satisfied that the
  applicant for the direction has sufficient insurance in place to cover
  any incident that may arise from the moving of the vessel, the
  Minister may authorize the applicant to move the vessel in accordance
  with the Minister's directions and at the expense of the authorized
  representative or, if there is no authorized representative, the
  owner.

### [Sale of Vessels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1150970 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No appearance and no security

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may apply to a court of competent jurisdiction for an order
  authorizing the Minister to sell a vessel that is subject to a
  detention order made under subsection 17(1) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vessel has been charged with an offence under
    this Act within 30 days after the day on which the order was made;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no one appeared in Canada on behalf of the vessel
    within 30 days after the day on which the vessel was charged with
    the offence to answer to the charge and security referred to in
    subparagraph 17(6)(c)(i) has not been not given.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appearance but no security

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may apply to a court of competent
  jurisdiction for an order authorizing the Minister to sell a vessel
  that is subject to a detention order made under subsection 17(1) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vessel has been charged with an offence under
    this Act within 30 days after the day on which the order was made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} someone appeared in Canada on behalf of the vessel
    within 30 days after the day on which the vessel was charged with
    the offence to answer to the charge but security referred to in
    subparagraph 17(6)(c)(i) has not been given; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the vessel has been convicted and a fine has been
    imposed but not paid.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As
  soon as feasible after making an application under subsection 20(1) or
  (2), the Minister must send, by registered mail, notice of the
  application to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person responsible for maintaining any register
    in which the vessel is registered, listed or recorded;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder of any mortgage or hypothec registered
    against the vessel on the register in which the vessel is
    registered, listed or recorded; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the holder of any maritime lien or like interest or
    right against the vessel, if the holder is known to the Minister at
    the time the application is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Day notice deemed given

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice is deemed to have been given to the person
  to whom it was sent on the day on which an acknowledgement of receipt
  is received by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Relief from giving notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the court seized of an application made under
  subsection 20(1) or (2) is satisfied that it is appropriate to do so,
  the court may relieve the Minister of the obligation to give the
  notice or authorize the Minister to give the notice in any other
  manner that the court considers appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claiming interest or right

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  application is made under subsection 20(1) or (2) in respect of a
  vessel

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person referred to in paragraph 21(1)(b) or (c)
    may, within 30 days after the day on which the notice of the
    application is sent to that person, apply to the court seized of the
    application for an order referred to in subsection (2); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other person who claims an interest or a right
    in the vessel as mortgagee or hypothecary creditor or as the holder
    of a maritime lien or any like interest or right may, within 30 days
    after the day on which the application is made, apply to the court
    seized of the application for an order referred to in subsection
    (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A court seized of an application made under
  subsection (1) must grant to the applicant an order declaring the
  nature and extent of the applicant's interest or right at the time the
  detention order was made if the court is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant acquired the interest or the right in
    good faith before the making of the detention order; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is innocent of any complicity or
    collusion in respect of the alleged offence that gave rise to the
    making of the detention order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Priority of applications

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application made under subsection 20(1) or (2)
  must not be heard until all applications made under subsection (1) in
  respect of the vessel have been heard.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization of sale

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  court seized of an application made under subsection 20(1) or (2) may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} authorize the Minister to sell the vessel in any
    manner and subject to any terms that the court considers
    appropriate; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on application by the Minister, give directions to
    the Minister as to the ranking of the interests or the rights of
    persons who have obtained an order referred to in subsection 22(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of proceeds

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the court has authorized the sale of a vessel and
  that vessel is sold, any surplus remaining from the proceeds of the
  sale --- after deducting the amount of the maximum fine that could
  have been imposed for the offence, if subsection 20(1) applies, or the
  fine actually imposed, if subsection 20(2) applies, and all expenses
  incurred in respect of the detention and the costs of the sale ---
  must, to the extent of the surplus, be paid in accordance with the
  interests or the rights of persons who have obtained an order referred
  to in subsection 22(2) and any surplus remaining after that must be
  paid to the owner of the vessel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clear title

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On selling a vessel whose sale has been authorized by
  the court, the Minister may, by bill of sale, give the purchaser a
  valid title to the vessel free from any mortgage or hypothec or other
  interest or right that is in existence at the time of the sale.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registrability of vessel

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in subsection (3) is to be construed as
  meaning that the vessel may be registered in the name of the
  purchaser.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

may, by regulation, amend the schedule by adding or deleting any oil or

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1151016 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of section 4

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Every person or vessel
that contravenes any of subsections 4(1) to (5) is guilty of an offence
and liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine of not more
  than \$5,000,000; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine of not more than
  \$1,000,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of other provisions

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who contravenes any of the following is guilty of an offence:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subsection 7(1) or (4);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 12;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} section 15;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} section 16;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} subsection 17(4) or (5); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} section 18.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of a direction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person or vessel that contravenes a direction
  given under section 8 or 10, paragraph 11(3)(j) or subsection 11(5) is
  guilty of an offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person or vessel that is guilty of an offence
  under subsection (1) or (2) is liable on summary conviction to a fine
  of not more than \$1,000,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more
  than 18 months, or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of offence by vessel

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** In a prosecution of a
vessel for an offence under section 25, it is sufficient proof that the
vessel has committed the offence to establish that the act that
constitutes the offence was committed by the master or any person on
board, whether or not the person on board has been identified.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of owners, operators, etc.

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  vessel commits an offence under section 25 and the owner, the
  operator, the master, an employee or an agent of the vessel directed,
  authorized, assented to, acquiesced in or participated in the
  commission of the offence, the owner, the operator, the master, the
  employee or the agent, as the case may be, is a party to and guilty of
  the offence and liable on conviction to the punishment provided for
  under that section for the offence, whether or not the vessel has been
  prosecuted or convicted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of directors and officers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a vessel commits an offence under section 25,
  every director or officer of a corporation that is an owner or an
  operator of the vessel who directed or influenced the corporation's
  policies or activities in respect of the conduct that is the subject
  matter of the offence is a party to and guilty of the offence and
  liable on conviction to the punishment provided for under that section
  for the offence, whether or not the vessel has been prosecuted or
  convicted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions deemed given to vessel

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
prosecuting a vessel for contravening a direction given under this Act,
a direction given to the master, a crew member or any person on board
who is, or appears to be, in charge of the vessel is deemed to have been
given to the vessel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Due diligence --- persons

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person must not be convicted of an offence under section 25, paragraph
  26(1)(a), (b), (d) or (e) or subsection 26(2) if they establish that
  they exercised due diligence to prevent its commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Due diligence --- vessels

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A vessel must not be convicted of an offence under
  section 25 or subsection 26(2) if the person who committed the act or
  omission that constitutes the offence establishes that they exercised
  due diligence to prevent its commission.

## [Summary Conviction Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1151048 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Proceedings by way of summary
  conviction under this Act may be instituted within two years after the
  day on which the Minister becomes aware of the subject matter of the
  proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document that purports to have been issued by the
  Minister and that certifies the day on which the Minister became aware
  of the subject matter of the proceedings is admissible in evidence
  without proof of the signature or official character of the person
  appearing to have signed it and, in the absence of any evidence to the
  contrary, is evidence that the Minister became aware of the subject
  matter on that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defendant outside Canada

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the proceedings cannot be commenced within two
  years because the proposed defendant is outside Canada, the
  proceedings may be commenced not later than 60 days after the day on
  which they arrive in Canada.

## [Review and Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1151056 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During
  the fifth year after the day on which this section comes into force, a
  review of the provisions and operation of this Act must be undertaken
  by any committee of the Senate, of the House of Commons or of both
  Houses of Parliament that is designated or established for that
  purpose, including a review of the impact of this Act on the
  environment, on social and economic conditions and on the Indigenous
  peoples of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report on review

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The committee referred to in subsection (1) must
  submit a report of the results of the review to the Senate, the House
  of Commons or both Houses of Parliament, as the case may be, on any of
  the first 15 days on which the Senate or the House of Commons, as the
  case may be, is sitting after the report is completed.
