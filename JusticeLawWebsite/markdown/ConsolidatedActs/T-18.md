## Department of Transport Act {#department-of-transport-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. T-18
:::

An Act respecting the Department of Transport
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451963 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Department of Transport Act](/eng/acts/T-18).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-15, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451968 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[canal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[canal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means every canal and lock that
    belongs to Canada and includes every canal and lock acquired,
    constructed, extended, enlarged, repaired or improved at the expense
    of Canada, or for the acquisition, construction, repairing,
    extending, enlarging or improving of which any public money is voted
    and appropriated by Parliament, except works for which money has
    been appropriated as a subsidy only, and all works and property
    appertaining or incidental to such a canal or that are placed under
    the management, charge and direction or the control of the Minister
    of Transport by the Governor in Council; ([canaux]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[railway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[railway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means every railway and the rolling
    stock thereon that belongs to Canada, and includes every railway and
    the rolling stock thereon acquired, constructed, extended, enlarged,
    repaired or improved at the expense of Canada, or for the
    acquisition, construction, repairing, extending, enlarging or
    improving of which any public money is voted and appropriated by
    Parliament, except works for which money has been appropriated as a
    subsidy only, and all works and property appertaining or incidental
    to such a railway, or that are placed under the management, charge
    and direction or the control of the Minister of Transport by the
    Governor in Council. ([chemins de fer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-15, s. 2
:::

## [Establishment of the Department]{.HTitleText1} {#h-451975 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Department established

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a department of the Government of Canada called the
  Department of Transport over which the Minister of Transport appointed
  by commission under the Great Seal shall preside.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister holds office during pleasure and has the
  management and direction of the Department.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-15, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy head

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
appoint an officer called the Deputy Minister of Transport to hold
office during pleasure and to be the deputy head of the Department.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-15, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Secretary of the Department

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  secretary of the Department may be appointed, in the manner authorized
  by law, to hold office during pleasure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting Secretary

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In case of the absence of the Secretary or of the
  Secretary's inability to act, the Minister may, in writing, authorize
  any other officer of the Department to act as Secretary.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-15, s. 4
- 1984, c. 40, s. 72
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of Secretary

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Secretary shall, unless
otherwise directed in any case by the Minister,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep separate accounts of the moneys appropriated for
  and expended on each railway or canal under the management, charge and
  direction of the Minister;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} submit those accounts to be audited in such manner as
  is determined by law or by the Governor in Council;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have charge of all plans, contracts, estimates,
  documents, titles, models and other like things relating to any
  railway or canal under the management, charge and direction of the
  Minister;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} keep proper accounts with each contractor or other
  person employed by or under the Department;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} ensure that all contracts are properly drawn up and
  executed;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} prepare all certificates on which any certificate for
  the payment of money is to issue;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} keep minutes of all proceedings of the Department;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} prepare reports and conduct, under the direction of
  the Minister, the correspondence of the Department; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} generally, do and perform all such acts and things
  appertaining to the business of the Department as are required by the
  Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-15, s. 5
:::

## [Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-452007 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's responsibilities for canals

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister has the management, charge and direction of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all Government canals and works and property
    pertaining or incidental to them;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the collection of tolls on the canals and matters
    incidental to their collection; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the officers and persons employed on the canals.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister has and may exercise and carry out any
  of the powers, duties and functions vested, immediately prior to
  November 2, 1936, in the Minister of Marine and with respect to civil
  aviation in the Minister of National Defence, by any Act, order or
  regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister to have supervision over
  boards, etc.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The powers, duties and functions of the Minister
  extend and apply to such boards and other public bodies, subjects,
  services and properties of the Crown as may be designated or assigned
  to the Minister by the Governor in Council, over and of which the
  Minister shall have the control, regulation, management and
  supervision.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-18, s. 7
- 1996, c. 10, s. 271.1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inquiries

- **[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, with the approval of the Governor in Council, appoint
  and direct any person to inquire into and report on any matter or
  thing that the Minister is authorized to deal with under the [Canada
  Transportation Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4) or under a Special Act as
  defined in section 87 of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers on inquiries

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Section 39 of the [Canada Transportation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4) applies, with such modifications as the
  circumstances require, in respect of any inquiry under subsection (1)
  and every person summoned to attend before a person making any such
  inquiry is entitled to receive the like fees and allowances for so
  doing as if summoned to attend before the Federal Court.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 28 (3rd Supp.), s. 353
- 1996, c. 10, s. 272
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction or repair of works

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall direct
the construction, maintenance and repair of all railways and canals and
of all other works appertaining or incidental thereto that are
constructed or maintained at the expense of Canada and are placed under
the Minister's management, charge and direction.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-15, s. 9
:::

## [Examination Under Oath]{.HTitleText1} {#h-452031 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons and documents

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} summon and examine, on oath, all such persons as
    the Minister deems necessary with respect to any matter on which the
    Minister's action is required;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cause those persons to bring with them such papers,
    plans, books, documents and things as it is necessary to examine
    with reference to the matter; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} pay those persons a reasonable compensation for
    their time and disbursements.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment for neglecting to attend

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person summoned by the Minister under
  subsection (1) who, after due notice of the summons, neglects or
  refuses to attend and be examined is guilty of an offence and liable
  on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding twenty dollars.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-15, s. 12
:::

## [Contracts, Tender and Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-452042 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security to be required

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister, in all cases in which any public work under the Minister's
  control is being carried out by contract, shall take all reasonable
  care that good and sufficient security is given to and in the name of
  Her Majesty for the due performance of the work within the amount and
  time specified for its completion.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Passing by lowest tender

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In all cases in which it seems to the Minister not to
  be expedient to let any work described in subsection (1) to the lowest
  tenderer, the Minister shall report the same and obtain the authority
  of the Governor in Council before passing by the lowest tender.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-15, s. 13
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No payment without contract and security

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** No sum of money shall be
paid to the contractor on any contract and no work commenced until the
contract has been signed by all the parties therein named and the
requisite security has been given.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-15, s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signatures binding on Her Majesty

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  deed, contract, document or writing relating to any matter under the
  control or direction of the Minister shall be binding on Her Majesty
  unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is signed by the Minister;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is signed by the Deputy Minister and
    countersigned by the Secretary; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is signed by a person specially authorized in
    writing by the Minister for that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Questioning Minister's authorization

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The authorization given by the Minister under
  paragraph (1)(c) to a person professing to act for the Minister shall
  not be called in question except by the Minister or by a person acting
  for the Minister or for Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Real property and immovables excluded

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply in respect of any
  instrument or act the execution of which is provided for by or under
  the [Federal Real Property and Federal Immovables
  Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-18, s. 12
- 1991, c. 50, s. 46
- 2001, c. 4, s. 171
:::

## [Evidence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-452066 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A copy of any map, plan
or other document in the custody of the Secretary, certified by the
Secretary to be a true copy, shall be held to be authentic, and is, in
the absence of evidence to the contrary, of the same legal effect as the
original.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-15, s. 17
:::

## [Tolls on Canals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-452071 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Imposition of tolls

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may impose and authorize the collection of tolls
  and dues on any canal, alter and change them and declare exemptions
  therefrom.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tolls payable in advance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The tolls and dues imposed under subsection (1) are,
  if so demanded by the collector thereof, payable before the right to
  the use of the canal in respect of which they are incurred accrues.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} All tolls and dues imposed under this Act may be
  recovered, with costs, in any court of competent jurisdiction by the
  collector or person appointed to receive them, in the name of the
  collector or of that person or in the name of Her Majesty, and by any
  form of proceeding by which debts to the Crown are recoverable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Goods on board liable

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The goods on board any steamboat, vessel, raft, crib
  or other craft, to whomever they belong, are liable for any tolls,
  dues or fines imposed and levied under this Act and may be seized,
  detained and sold in the same manner as the steamboat, vessel, raft,
  crib or other craft in which they are, and as if they belonged to the
  person contravening any regulation in that respect, saving the
  recourse of the real owner thereof against the person who is deemed
  the owner for the purposes of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tolls on the upper St. Lawrence

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The same tolls are payable on steamboats or vessels
  of any kind, and passengers, taken down the St. Lawrence River past
  any of the canals between Montreal and Kingston, as would be payable
  on those steamboats, vessels or passengers, if they had been taken
  through the canal or canals past which they are so taken down, and
  those tolls shall be levied in like manner and under the like fines
  and forfeitures for the non-payment thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-15, s. 23
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tolls paid to Receiver General

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** All tolls, dues or other
revenues imposed and collected under this Act shall be paid by the
persons receiving them to the Receiver General in the manner and at the
intervals, not exceeding one month, determined by the Receiver General.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-15, s. 24
:::

## [Regulations for Use of Canals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-452089 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Management of canals and tolls

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make such regulations as the Governor in Council deems necessary for
the management, maintenance, proper use and protection of the canals or
other works under the management, charge and direction or the control of
the Minister, and for the ascertaining and collection of the tolls, dues
and revenues thereon.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-15, s. 25
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fines and enforcement

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, by the regulations mentioned in section 16,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} impose such fines, not exceeding in any one case
    four hundred dollars, for any contravention of a regulation, as the
    Governor in Council deems necessary for ensuring its observance and
    the payment of the tolls and dues imposed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide for the non-passing or detention and
    seizure at the risk of the owner of any steamboat, vessel, raft,
    crib or other craft, timber or goods

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on which tolls or dues have accrued and have not
      been paid,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which a regulation has otherwise
      been contravened or any injury done to a canal and not paid for,
      or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for or on account of which a fine remains
      unpaid; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide for the sale of anything detained or seized
    under a regulation pursuant to paragraph (b) if the tolls, dues,
    damages or fine are not paid by the time fixed therefor, and for the
    payment of the amounts due out of the proceeds of the sale.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surplus proceeds

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The surplus proceeds, if any, of a sale referred to
  in paragraph (1)(c) shall be returned to the owner or the owner's
  agent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crown's rights not impaired

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No regulation made under section 16 and this section
  impairs the right of the Crown to recover in the ordinary course of
  law unpaid tolls, dues, fines or damages.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-15, s. 26
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-452109 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial contracts enure to Canada

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** All contracts, bonds,
agreements or leases for or respecting any railway or canal now the
property of Canada, or for any tolls imposed for their use, entered into
by the Commissioner of Public Works of the former Province of Canada, or
by the Board of Works of the Province of Nova Scotia or of the Province
of New Brunswick, or by any commissioners or other persons duly
authorized to enter into them in any province of Canada, enure to the
use of Her Majesty, and may be enforced as if they had been entered into
with Her Majesty under the authority of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-15, s. 32
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Custody of documents

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may require any person or any provincial authority having the possession
or custody of any maps, plans, specifications, estimates, reports or
other papers, books, drawings, instruments, models, contracts, documents
or records that are not private property and that relate to any railway,
building or property connected therewith, or to any canal under the
management, charge and direction or the control of the Minister, to
deliver them without delay to the Secretary.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-15, s. 33
:::

## [Annual Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-452118 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall make
and submit to the Governor General an annual report on all the railways
and canals under the management, charge and direction or the control of
the Minister, and the annual report shall be laid before each House of
Parliament within twenty-one days after the commencement of the session,
showing the state of each work and the amounts received and expended in
respect thereof, with such further information as is requisite.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. T-15, s. 34
:::
