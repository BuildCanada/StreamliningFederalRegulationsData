## An Act respecting the mandatory reporting of Internet child pornography by persons who provide an Internet service {#an-act-respecting-the-mandatory-reporting-of-internet-child-pornography-by-persons-who-provide-an-internet-service .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2011, c. 4
:::

Assented to 2011-03-23

An Act respecting the mandatory reporting of Internet child pornography
by persons who provide an Internet service
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-277506 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[child pornography]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[child pornography]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in subsection 163.1(1) of the [Criminal
      Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([pornographie juvénile]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[child pornography offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[child pornography offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an offence
      under any of the following provisions of the [Criminal
      Code](/eng/acts/C-46):

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subsection 163.1(2) (making child pornography);

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subsection 163.1(3) (distribution, etc., of
        child pornography);

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subsection 163.1(4) (possession of child
        pornography); or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} subsection 163.1(4.1) (accessing child
        pornography). ([infraction relative à la pornographie
        juvénile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[computer data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[computer data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means representations,
      including signs, signals or symbols, that are in a form suitable
      for processing in a computer system.  ([données
      informatiques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Internet service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Internet service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a service providing
      Internet access, Internet content hosting or electronic
      mail. ([services Internet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual, a corporation,
      a partnership or an unincorporated association or
      organization. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Description with cross-reference

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The descriptive words in parentheses that follow the
  reference to a provision of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) in the
  definition [[child pornography offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
  subsection (1) do not form part of that definition but are inserted
  for convenience of reference only.

## [Duties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-277521 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to report Internet address

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** If a person is advised, in
the course of providing an Internet service to the public, of an
Internet Protocol address or a Uniform Resource Locator where child
pornography may be available to the public, the person must report that
address or Uniform Resource Locator to the organization designated by
the regulations, as soon as feasible and in accordance with the
regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to notify police officer

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** If a person who provides an
Internet service to the public has reasonable grounds to believe that
their Internet service is being or has been used to commit a child
pornography offence, the person must notify an officer, constable or
other person employed for the preservation and maintenance of the public
peace of that fact, as soon as feasible and in accordance with the
regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preservation of computer data

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who makes a notification under section 3 must preserve all computer
  data related to the notification that is in their possession or
  control for 21 days after the day on which the notification is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Destruction of preserved computer data

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person must destroy the computer data that would
  not be retained in the ordinary course of business and any document
  that is prepared for the purpose of preserving computer data under
  subsection (1) as soon as feasible after the expiry of the 21-day
  period, unless the person is required to preserve the computer data by
  a judicial order made under any other Act of Parliament or the
  legislature of a province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No disclosure

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A person must not disclose
that they have made a report under section 2 or a notification under
section 3, or disclose the contents of a report or notification, if the
disclosure could prejudice a criminal investigation, whether or not a
criminal investigation has begun.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No seeking out of child pornography

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act
requires or authorizes a person to seek out child pornography.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A civil proceeding cannot
be commenced against a person for making a report in good faith under
section 2 or for making a notification in good faith under section 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Self-incrimination

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act affects any right of a person to be protected
against self-incrimination.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial or foreign jurisdiction

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A person who has reported
information in compliance with an obligation to report child pornography
under the laws of a province or a foreign jurisdiction is deemed to have
complied with section 2 of this Act in relation to that information.

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-277541 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
knowingly contravenes any of sections 2 to 5 is guilty of an offence and
liable on summary conviction,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine of not more than
    \$1,000,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a second offence, to a fine of not more than
    \$5,000, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for each subsequent offence, to a fine of not
    more than \$10,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than
    six months, or to both; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine of not more than
    \$10,000,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a second offence, to a fine of not more than
    \$50,000, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for each subsequent offence, to a fine of not
    more than \$100,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A prosecution for an
offence under this Act cannot be commenced more than two years after the
time when the act or omission giving rise to the prosecution occurred.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-277554 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} designating an organization for the purpose of
  section 2;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the role, functions and activities of the
  designated organization in relation to information received under this
  Act, including any security measures to be taken;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the making of a report under section 2;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting a notification under section 3;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} respecting security measures to be taken in relation
  to computer data preserved under section 4; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} generally, for carrying out the purposes and
  provisions of this Act.
