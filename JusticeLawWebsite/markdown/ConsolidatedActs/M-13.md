## Payments in Lieu of Taxes Act {#payments-in-lieu-of-taxes-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. M-13
:::

An Act respecting payments in lieu of taxes to municipalities, provinces
and other bodies exercising functions of local government that levy real
property taxes
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-355021 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Payments in Lieu of Taxes Act](/eng/acts/M-13).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. M-13, s. 1
- 2000, c. 8, s. 2
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-355026 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[assessed dimension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[assessed dimension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the frontage,
      area, other dimension or other attribute of real property or
      immovables established by an assessment authority for the purpose
      of computing a frontage or area tax; ([dimensions
      fiscales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[assessed value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[assessed value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the value established
      for any real property or immovable by an assessment authority for
      the purpose of computing a real property tax; ([valeur
      fiscale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[assessment authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[assessment authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an authority
      that has power by or under an Act of Parliament or the legislature
      of a province to establish the assessed dimension or assessed
      value of real property or immovables; ([autorité
      évaluatrice]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[business occupancy tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[business occupancy tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a tax levied
      on occupants in respect of their use or occupation of real
      property or immovables for the purpose of or in connection with a
      business; ([taxe d'occupation commerciale]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any department named in Schedule I to the
        [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11),

      - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} any division or branch of the federal public
        administration named in Schedule I.1 to that Act,

      - [(a.2)]{.lawlabel} any commission under the [Inquiries
        Act](/eng/acts/I-11) designated as a department for the purposes
        of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11),

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any corporation established by or under an Act
        of Parliament or performing a function on behalf of the
        Government of Canada included in Schedule I to this
        Act; ([ministères]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[effective rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[effective rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rate of real
      property tax or of frontage or area tax that, in the opinion of
      the Minister, would be applicable to any federal property if that
      property were taxable property; ([taux effectif]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[federal property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, subject to
      subsection (3),

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} real property and immovables owned by Her
        Majesty in right of Canada that are under the administration of
        a minister of the Crown,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} real property and immovables owned by Her
        Majesty in right of Canada that are, by virtue of a lease to a
        corporation included in Schedule III or IV, under the
        management, charge and direction of that corporation,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} immovables held under emphyteusis by Her
        Majesty in right of Canada that are under the administration of
        a minister of the Crown,

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a building owned by Her Majesty in right of
        Canada that is under the administration of a minister of the
        Crown and that is situated on tax exempt land owned by a person
        other than Her Majesty in right of Canada or administered and
        controlled by Her Majesty in right of a province, and

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} real property and immovables occupied or used
        by a minister of the Crown and administered and controlled by
        Her Majesty in right of a province; ([propriété
        fédérale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[frontage or area tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[frontage or area tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any tax levied
      on the owners of real property or immovables that is computed by
      applying a rate to all or part of the assessed dimension of the
      property and includes any tax levied on the owners of real
      property or immovables that is in the nature of a local
      improvement tax, a development tax or a redevelopment tax, but
      does not include a tax in respect of mineral rights; ([impôt sur
      la façade ou sur la superficie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Public
      Works and Government Services; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[minister of the Crown]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[minister of the Crown]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of
      the Queen's Privy Council for Canada in that member's capacity of
      managing and directing or having responsibility for a
      department; ([ministre fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[other attribute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[other attribute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a tax levy for the purpose of
        financing all or part of the capital cost of a service to real
        property or immovables,

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the actual or estimated cost of constructing
          a new building,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the number of rooms, living units or beds in
          a building, or

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the number of persons in occupancy of a
          building, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a tax levy for the purpose of
        financing all or part of the operating cost of a service to real
        property or immovables, any prescribed criteria; ([autre
        élément]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by regulations
      made by the Governor in Council under section 9; ([Version
      anglaise seulement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[property dimension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[property dimension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the frontage,
      area, other dimension or other attribute that, in the opinion of
      the Minister, would be established by an assessment authority in
      respect of federal property as the basis for computing the amount
      of any frontage or area tax that would be applicable to that
      property if it were taxable property; ([dimensions
      effectives]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[property value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[property value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the value that, in
      the opinion of the Minister, would be attributable by an
      assessment authority to federal property, without regard to any
      mineral rights or any ornamental, decorative or non-functional
      features thereof, as the basis for computing the amount of any
      real property tax that would be applicable to that property if it
      were taxable property; ([valeur effective]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[real property tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[real property tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a tax of general
      application to real property or immovables or any class of them
      that is

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} levied by a taxing authority on owners of real
        property or immovables or, if the owner is exempt from the tax,
        on lessees or occupiers of real property or immovables, other
        than those lessees or occupiers exempt by law, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} computed by applying a rate to all or part of
        the assessed value of taxable property; ([impôt
        foncier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[taxation year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[taxation year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the fiscal year of a
      taxing authority; ([année d'imposition]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[taxable property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[taxable property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means real property and
      immovables in respect of which a person may be required by a
      taxing authority to pay a real property tax or a frontage or area
      tax; ([propriété imposable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[taxing authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[taxing authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any municipality, province, municipal or
        provincial board, commission, corporation or other authority
        that levies and collects a real property tax or a frontage or
        area tax pursuant to an Act of the legislature of a province,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any council of a band within the meaning of the
        [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) that levies and collects a real
        property tax or a frontage or area tax pursuant to an Act of
        Parliament,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the [[band,]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined
        in subsection 2(1) of the [Naskapi and the Cree-Naskapi
        Commission Act](/eng/acts/C-45.7), if it levies and collects a
        tax on interests in [[Category IA-N land]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
        as defined in that subsection,

      - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} any [[Cree First
        Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(2) of
        the [Cree Nation of Eeyou Istchee Governance Agreement
        Act](/eng/acts/C-45.75) that levies and collects a tax on
        interests in [[Category IA land]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
        defined in that subsection,

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the [[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as
        defined in subsection 2(1) of the [shíshálh Nation
        Self-Government Act](/eng/acts/S-6.6), if it levies and collects
        a real property tax or a frontage or area tax in respect of
        [[shíshálh lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in that
        subsection,

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a first nation named in Schedule II to the
        [Yukon First Nations Self-Government Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.6), if
        it levies and collects a real property tax or a frontage or area
        tax in respect of settlement land, as defined in that Act, or in
        respect of lands in which an interest is transferred or
        recognized under section 21 of that Act,

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the Nisga'a Nation or a Nisga'a Village, as
        defined in the Nisga'a Final Agreement given effect by the
        [Nisga'a Final Agreement Act](/eng/acts/N-23.3), if it levies
        and collects a real property tax or a frontage or area tax in
        respect of Nisga'a Lands, as defined in that Agreement,

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Tlicho Government, as defined in section 2
        of the [Tlicho Land Claims and Self-Government
        Act](/eng/acts/T-11.3), if it levies and collects a real
        property tax or a frontage or area tax in respect of Tlicho
        lands, as defined in section 2 of the [Mackenzie Valley Resource
        Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2),

      - [(g.1)]{.lawlabel} the Déline Got'ine Government, as defined in
        section 2 of the [Déline Final Self-Government Agreement
        Act](/eng/acts/D-1.5), if it levies and collects a real property
        tax or a frontage or area tax in respect of Déline Lands, as
        defined in 22.1.1 of the Déline Final Self-Government Agreement
        that is approved, given effect and declared valid by that Act,

      - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the Nunatsiavut Government, as defined in
        section 2 of the [Labrador Inuit Land Claims Agreement
        Act](/eng/acts/L-4.3), or an Inuit Community Government, as
        defined in section 1.1.1 of the Labrador Inuit Land Claims
        Agreement approved by that Act, if it levies and collects a real
        property tax or a frontage or area tax in respect of Labrador
        Inuit Lands or Community Lands, as defined in section 1.1.1 of
        that Agreement, as the case may be,

      - [(h.1)]{.lawlabel} the Tla'amin Government, as defined in
        subsection 2(2) of the [Tla'amin Final Agreement
        Act](/eng/acts/T-11.2), if it levies and collects a real
        property tax or a frontage or area tax in respect of Tla'amin
        Lands, as defined in that subsection,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Tsawwassen Government, as defined in
        subsection 2(2) of the [Tsawwassen First Nation Final Agreement
        Act](/eng/acts/T-21.5), if it levies and collects a real
        property tax or a frontage or area tax in respect of Tsawwassen
        Lands, as defined in that subsection,

      - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a Maanulth Government, within the meaning of
        subsection 2(2) of the [Maanulth First Nations Final Agreement
        Act](/eng/acts/M-0.55), if it levies and collects a real
        property tax or a frontage or area tax in respect of Maanulth
        Lands, within the meaning of that subsection, or

      - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the [[Whitecap Dakota
        Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of
        the [Self-Government Treaty Recognizing the Whitecap Dakota
        Nation / Wapaha Ska Dakota Oyate Act](/eng/acts/S-8.36), if it
        levies and collects a real property tax or a frontage or area
        tax in respect of [[Whitecap Dakota reserve
        lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of that
        Act. ([autorité taxatrice]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed authority for purposes of the
  definition [[taxing authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the definition [[taxing
  authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection (1), where one
  authority collects a real property tax or a frontage or area tax that
  is levied by another authority, the authority that collects the tax
  shall be deemed to be the authority that levies and collects the tax.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property not included in the definition
  [[federal property]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the definition [[federal
  property]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection (1), federal property
  does not include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any structure or work, unless it is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a building designed primarily for the shelter of
      people, living things, fixtures, personal property or movable
      property,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an outdoor swimming pool,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a golf course improvement,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a driveway for a single-family dwelling,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} paving or other improvements associated with
      employee parking, or

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} an outdoor theatre;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any structure, work, machinery or equipment that is
    included in Schedule II;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any real property or immovable developed and used
    as a park and situated within an area defined as
    [[urban]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} by Statistics Canada, as of the most
    recent census of the population of Canada taken by Statistics
    Canada, other than national parks of Canada, national marine parks
    of Canada, national park reserves of Canada, national marine park
    reserves of Canada, national historic sites of Canada, national
    battlefields or heritage canals;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any Indian reserve, or any land referred to in any
    of paragraphs (c) to (e) of the definition [[taxing
    authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1), except for the
    part

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that is occupied for residential purposes by an
      employee of Her Majesty in right of Canada who would not, but for

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is occupied by a minister of the Crown;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any real property or immovable for which an
    original Crown grant has not issued, except to the extent that it

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is designated for a specific use by or under an
      Act of Parliament, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is used by an Indian within the meaning of the
      [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) or an Inuk and is prescribed under
      paragraph 9(1)(e);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any real property for which an original Crown grant
    has not issued, except to the extent that it

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is reserved in the records of the Department of
      Indigenous Services at Whitehorse or Yellowknife for the use of a
      department or an agency of the Government of Canada, and is either
      situated within a municipality or, in the case of real property
      that is not situated within a municipality, used in accordance
      with the reservation, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is situated within a municipality and is
      reserved in the records of the Department of Indigenous Services
      at Whitehorse or Yellowknife for the use of an Indian within the
      meaning of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) or an Inuk;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any real property or immovable developed or used as
    a public highway that, in the opinion of the Minister, does not
    provide, as its primary function, immediate access to real property
    or immovables owned by Her Majesty in right of Canada; or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} unless otherwise prescribed, any real property or
    immovable leased to or occupied by a person or body, whether
    incorporated or not, that is not a department.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective rate --- experimental farms

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In determining the effective rate in respect of an
  experimental farm station, agricultural research station or other
  similar facility on federal property, the Minister shall take into
  account the rates of tax applicable to farms operated by agricultural
  enterprises.

## [Purpose of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-355105 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act
is to provide for the fair and equitable administration of payments in
lieu of taxes.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 8, s. 4
:::

## [Authority to Make Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-355110 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to make payments

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, on receipt of an application in a form provided or
  approved by the Minister, make a payment out of the Consolidated
  Revenue Fund to a taxing authority applying for it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in lieu of a real property tax for a taxation year,
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in lieu of a frontage or area tax

  in respect of federal property situated within the area in which the
  taxing authority has the power to levy and collect the real property
  tax or the frontage or area tax.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delayed payments

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is of the opinion that a payment
  under subsection (1) or part of one has been unreasonably delayed, the
  Minister may supplement the payment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum payable

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} The supplement shall not exceed the product
  obtained by multiplying the amount not paid by the rate of interest
  prescribed for the purpose of section 155.1 of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), calculated over the period that,
  in the opinion of the Minister, the payment has been delayed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to make payments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything in this Act, if real
  property or immovables are prescribed to be included in the definition
  [[federal property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} under paragraph 9(1)(d) or
  (e), a payment may be made in respect of that property for the entire
  taxation year in which the prescription is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to Schedule I corporations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In respect of a corporation included in Schedule I, a
  payment may be made under this section only in respect of the real
  property or immovables of the corporation specified in that Schedule
  or prescribed by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taxing authority

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), a taxing authority
  in respect of federal property described in paragraph 2(3)(d) means a
  council, band or first nation referred to in any of paragraphs (b)
  to (e) of the definition [[taxing authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
  subsection 2(1).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. M-13, s. 3
- 2000, c. 8, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments for leased property

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** Real property and
immovables referred to in paragraph 2(3)(h) are deemed to be federal
property for a taxation year if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} as of the day following the last day of the taxation
  year, all or part of the real property tax or the frontage or area tax
  on the property for that taxation year remains unpaid; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is of the opinion that the taxing
  authority has made all reasonable efforts to collect the tax and there
  is no likelihood that the authority will ever be able to collect it.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 8, s. 5
:::

## [Calculation of Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-355135 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments in lieu of real property tax

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3) and 5(1) and (2), a payment referred to in
  paragraph 3(1)(a) shall not exceed the product of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the effective rate in the taxation year applicable
    to the federal property in respect of which the payment may be made,
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the property value in the taxation year of that
    federal property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rate for school purposes

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where all or part of the real property tax levied by
  a taxing authority in a taxation year is for school purposes and is
  levied at different rates

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for taxpayers of different religious denominations,
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for taxpayers of different religious denominations
    and for different classes of taxable property,

  there shall be substituted for the effective rate referred to in
  paragraph (1)(a) a rate equal to the rate calculated in accordance
  with subsection (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of rate to be substituted

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), the rate
  substituted for the effective rate referred to in paragraph (1)(a)
  shall be a rate equal to the aggregate of that part of the effective
  rate in the taxation year that is used in determining the amount of
  the real property tax that is levied for purposes other than school
  purposes, and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if paragraph (2)(a) applies, a rate for school
    purposes determined by dividing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the portion of the real property tax levied for
      school purposes by the taxing authority in the taxation year,

    by

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the assessed value of all taxable property under
      the jurisdiction of the taxing authority in respect of which such
      portion of the real property tax for school purposes is levied in
      the taxation year, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if paragraph (2)(b) applies, a rate for school
    purposes for each class of taxable property determined by dividing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the portion of the real property tax levied for
      school purposes by the taxing authority in respect of property of
      that class in the taxation year,

    by

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the assessed value of all taxable property of
      that class under the jurisdiction of the taxing authority in
      respect of which that portion of the real property tax for school
      purposes is levied in the taxation year.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. M-13, s. 4
- 2000, c. 8, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Full amount of payment not budgeted

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, for
  any taxation year,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a real property tax rate is established by a taxing
    authority without taking into account all federal property situated
    within the area in which the taxing authority has the power to levy
    and collect a real property tax, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the property value of the federal property not
    taken into account exceeds 25 per cent of the total assessed value
    of taxable property situated within the area in which the taxing
    authority has the power to levy and collect a real property tax,

  the Minister may, in determining the amount of any payment to that
  taxing authority, make an adjustment in the effective rate or in any
  portion of it so that the payment will not exceed the amount described
  in subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}The amount over which a grant will not
  exceed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount referred to in subsection (1) is the
  amount that would have been determined by the Minister if the property
  value referred to in paragraph (1)(b) that is in excess of twenty-five
  per cent of the total assessed value of the taxable property situated
  within the area in which the taxing authority referred to in
  subsection (1) has the power to levy and collect a real property tax,
  had been taken into account by the taxing authority in establishing
  the real property tax rate for the taxation year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where one authority collects a real property tax that
  is established by another authority, the authority that collects the
  tax shall, for the purposes of subsections (1) and (2), be deemed to
  be the authority that establishes the real property tax rate.

::: HistoricalNote

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  payment referred to in paragraph 3(1)(b) shall not exceed the product
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the effective rate applicable to federal property
    in respect of which the payment may be made, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the property dimension of that federal property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Option

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a frontage or area tax is payable over a period of
  more than one year, the Minister may make a payment in lieu of that
  tax in annual instalments, together with interest, or in a lump sum
  without interest.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. M-13, s. 6
- 2000, c. 8, s. 9

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deductions

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** In determining the amount
of a payment for a taxation year under paragraph 3(1)(a), there may be
deducted

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if there is in effect a special arrangement for the
  provision or financing of an educational service by Her Majesty in
  right of Canada, the amount established by that arrangement;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if there is in effect a special arrangement for an
  alternative means of compensating a taxing authority, or a body on
  behalf of which the authority collects a real property tax, for
  providing a service, the amount established by that arrangement;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if a taxing authority, or a body on behalf of which
  the authority collects a real property tax, is, in the opinion of the
  Minister, unable or unwilling to provide federal property with a
  service, and no special arrangement exists, an amount that, in the
  opinion of the Minister, does not exceed reasonable expenditures
  incurred or expected to be incurred by Her Majesty in right of Canada
  to provide the service; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an amount that, in the opinion of the Minister, is

  property tax that the Minister considers would be applicable to the
