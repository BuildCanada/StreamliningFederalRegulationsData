  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may establish offices or employ
  agents in any part of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-3, s. 4

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board of Directors

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There shall be a Board of Directors
  of the Corporation consisting of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person appointed as the Chairperson under
    subsection 6(1);

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} the person appointed as the President and Chief
    Executive Officer of the Corporation under subsection 105(5) of the

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the persons who for the time being hold the offices

    Commissioner of the Financial Consumer Agency of Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} not more than six other members appointed by the
    Minister with the approval of the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disqualifications

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} A person is not eligible to be appointed under
  paragraph (1)(c) or, having been appointed under that paragraph, to
  continue as a member of the Board, if the person is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} employed in any capacity in the federal public
    administration or holds any office or position for which any salary
    or other remuneration is payable out of public moneys;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Senate or House of Commons of
    Canada or a member of a provincial legislature; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a director, officer or employee of a federal
    institution, a provincial institution or a local cooperative credit
    society.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternate director

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A director referred to in paragraph (1)(b) may, from
  time to time with the approval of the Minister, designate in writing
  an alternate to attend in the director's absence at any meeting of the
  Board of Directors, and the alternate shall be deemed to be a member
  of the Board while so attending a meeting of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancy

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A vacancy on the Board does not impair the right of
  the remaining directors to act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting chairperson

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the office of Chairperson is vacant, the
  Minister may appoint, for a period not exceeding ninety days, an
  acting Chairperson who shall, while so acting, be a member of the
  Board and have all the powers of the Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses of directors

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A director shall be paid by the Corporation
  reasonable travel and living expenses incurred by the director while
  absent from his or her ordinary place of residence in the course of
  his or her duties as a director but no director referred to in
  paragraph (1)(b) shall receive any other remuneration for his or her
  services on the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration of certain directors

  [(5.1)]{.lawlabel} A director referred to in paragraph (1)(c) shall be
  paid by the Corporation for attendance at meetings of the Board such
  remuneration as may be fixed by the Governor in Council.

## [Objects, Powers and Duties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-83409 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-7 .sectionLabel}** The objects of the
Corporation are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to provide insurance against the loss of part or all
  of deposits;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to promote and otherwise contribute to the stability
  of the financial system in Canada;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to pursue the objects set out in paragraphs (a)
  and (b) for the benefit of persons having deposits with member
  institutions and in such manner as will minimize the exposure of the
  Corporation to loss; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to act as the resolution authority for its members.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-3, s. 7
- R.S., 1985, c. 18 (3rd Supp.), s. 49
- 1996, c. 6, s. 22
- 2005, c. 30, s. 98
- 2017, c. 20, s. 108
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of Governor in Council

- **[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-7.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by
  order, exempt the Corporation from the requirement that it pursue its
  objects in a manner that will minimize its exposure to loss when it
  takes any action to address a situation that is specified in the
  order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition precedent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make the order only if
  the Minister is of the opinion, after consultation with the Board, the
  Governor of the Bank of Canada and the Superintendent, that the
  requirement that the Corporation pursue its objects in a manner that
  will minimize its exposure to loss, in respect of a situation that
  will be specified in the order, might have an adverse effect on the
  stability of the financial system in Canada or public confidence in
  that stability.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repeal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may repeal the order only if
  the Minister is of the opinion that the requirement that the
  Corporation pursue its objects in a manner that will minimize its
  exposure to loss, in respect of the situation specified in the order,
  will no longer have an adverse effect on the stability of the
  financial system in Canada or public confidence in that stability.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 2, s. 234
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

- **[[7.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-7.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  order made under subsection 7.1(1) has effect from the time that it is
  made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply to the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause a notice to be published in
  the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) that the order has
  been made or repealed as soon as the Minister is of the opinion that
  the publication of the notice will not have an adverse effect on the
  stability of the financial system in Canada or public confidence in
  that stability.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 2, s. 234
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of loss

**[[7.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-7.3 .sectionLabel}** After the publication
of a notice in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) that an
order was made under subsection 7.1(1), the Corporation shall, in
accordance with its by-laws, collect a special premium from member
institutions or any class of member institutions in order to recover the
loss that the Corporation determines it incurred as a result of pursuing
its objects without regard to the requirement that it do so in a manner
that minimizes its exposure to loss.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 2, s. 234
:::

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a person has deposits with two

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation regarding insured amount

                              s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As
  soon as possible after the Corporation is obliged to make payment in
  respect of a deposit insured by deposit insurance, it shall make
  available to the person that in its opinion appears to be entitled to
  be paid in respect of the deposit an amount of money equal to so much

  [(1.01)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation shall not make any money available
  under subsection (1) to a nominee broker or a professional trustee in

  monetary payment to the person or a deposit to the credit of the

  appropriate to do so.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation shall, in the manner described in

  subsection (1.1), make payment in respect of any deposit insured by

  hundred thousand dollars.

  [(2.91)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of calculating the payment to be

  the member institution's records in accordance with the member
  institution's regular transaction posting process as of the applicable
  date referred to in subsection (2.9).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foreign currency exchange

  [(2.92)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of calculating the payment to be

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discharge of liability

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Payment under this section by the Corporation in
  respect of any deposit insured by deposit insurance discharges the

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Corporation makes a payment under this

  made, to all the rights and interests of the depositor and may
  maintain an action in respect of those rights and interests in the
  name of the depositor or in the name of the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subrogation --- returned payment

  [(4.01)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a returned payment, at the expiry

  may maintain an action in respect of those rights and interests in the
  name of the depositor or in the name of the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Priority

  [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} If the Corporation is subrogated to the rights and

    respect of interest accruing and payable on their deposit after the

  claim by the Corporation on the estate of the member institution in
  respect of that payment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assignment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the Corporation deems it advisable, it may

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limitation for claims

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} No action may be taken against the Corporation in
  respect of the obligation of the Corporation to make a payment in
  relation to a deposit held by a member institution that is being wound

  the commencement of the winding-up.

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[commencement of the

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} arrange for the assumption by any person of all
      or any part of the federal member institution's liabilities,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} carry on the business of the federal member
      institution to the extent that the Corporation deems is necessary
      or beneficial to the receivership,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} sue for, defend, compromise and settle, in the
      name of the federal member institution, any claim made by or
      against it,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} in the name of the federal member institution,
      do all acts and execute all receipts and other documents and for
      that purpose, when necessary, use its seal, and

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} do all such other things as may be necessary
      or incidental to the exercise of the Corporation's rights, powers,
      privileges and immunities as receiver; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} gives the Corporation the right to recover, out of
    the assets of the federal member institution and in priority to all
    other claims, all the costs, charges and expenses properly incurred
    by the Corporation in furtherance of the order, including those
    related to its operation of the institution.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, shares and subordinated debt
  that are subject to an order made under paragraph (1)(a) and that,
  immediately before the making of the order, are vested in a trustee in
  bankruptcy under the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3)
  are vested in the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty --- exercising
  rights

  [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, an order made under
  paragraph (1)(a) or (b) prevents any person, other than the
  Corporation, who is the holder of shares or subordinated debt or other
  debts or liabilities of the federal member institution or who is a
  party to or a beneficiary of a contract with the institution, and any
  secured creditor or assignee or successor in interest of such a
  person, from exercising any voting or other rights arising from the
  person's status in any manner that could defeat or interfere with the
  rights, powers, privileges and immunities of the Corporation as holder
  of shares or subordinated debt or as receiver, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receiver order dealings

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where an order is made under paragraph (1)(b),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Corporation, as receiver, may exercise its
    powers, rights, privileges and immunities without leave, approval or
    other intervention of a court, but may seek the assistance of a
    superior court in order to give effect to those powers, rights,
    privileges and immunities,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an asset of the federal member institution that is
    acquired from the Corporation, as receiver, shall, except to the
    extent that it is an asset referred to in subparagraph (3)(b)(iii),
    be acquired free of any adverse claim of the federal member
    institution or any other person, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Corporation, as receiver, may cause or refrain
    from causing any obligation of the federal member institution to be
    performed and may cause the federal member institution to incur an
    obligation or do so on its behalf,

  and the Corporation shall not, by reason of its appointment as
  receiver or any action taken by it, be held to have assumed or
  incurred any obligation of the federal member institution for its own
  account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-liability --- environmental matters

  [(5.1)]{.lawlabel} Without limiting the generality of subsection (5)
  and despite anything in federal or provincial law, the Corporation, as
  receiver, is not liable in that capacity for any environmental
  condition that arose or environmental damage that occurred

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the Corporation's appointment as receiver;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} after the Corporation's appointment as receiver
    unless it is established that the condition arose or the damage
    occurred as a result of the Corporation's gross negligence or wilful
    misconduct or, in Quebec, the Corporation's gross or intentional
    fault.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports, etc., still required

  [(5.2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in subsection (5.1) exempts the
  Corporation, as receiver, from any duty to report or make disclosure
  imposed by a law referred to in that subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-liability --- certain orders

  [(5.3)]{.lawlabel} Without limiting the generality of subsection (5)
  and despite anything in federal or provincial law but subject to
  subsection (5.2), if an order is made that has the effect of requiring
  the Corporation, as receiver, to remedy any environmental condition or
  environmental damage affecting property involved in the receivership,
  the Corporation, as receiver, is not liable for failure to comply with
  the order, and is not liable for any costs that are or would be
  incurred by any person in carrying out the terms of the order,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if, within the time that is specified in the order,

    receiver if the order is in effect when the Corporation is appointed
    as receiver, the Corporation, as receiver,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} complies with the order, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on notice to the person who issued the order,

      condition or damage; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Corporation, as receiver, had, before the
    order was made, abandoned, disposed of or otherwise released any
    interest in any real property, or any right in any immovable,
    affected by the condition or damage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-liability --- employees

  [(5.4)]{.lawlabel} Without limiting the generality of subsection (5)
  and despite anything in federal or provincial law, the Corporation, as
  receiver, is not liable in respect of a liability, including one as a
  successor employer,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is in respect of the employees or former
    employees of the federal member institution or a predecessor of the
    federal member institution or in respect of a pension plan for the
    benefit of those employees or former employees; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that existed before the Corporation is appointed as

    Corporation's appointment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of other successor employers

  [(5.5)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (5.4) does not affect the liability of a
  successor employer other than the Corporation, as receiver.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order conclusive

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} An order made under this section and any action taken
  or decision made in furtherance of such an order are for all purposes
  final and conclusive and shall not be questioned or reviewed in any
  court.

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a conversion under subsection 39.2(2.3) in respect
    of the federal member institution; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a conversion of any of the federal member
    institution's shares or liabilities in accordance with the
    contractual terms of those shares or liabilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stay terminated --- notice

  [(7.101)]{.lawlabel} If the Corporation considers that all or
  substantially all of the federal member institution's assets will be
  transferred to a third party and that an eligible financial contract
  of that institution will not be assigned to a third party, it may give
  notice to that effect to the parties to that contract, in which case
  paragraphs (7.1)(a) and (c) cease to apply in respect of that contract
  at the date and time the notice is issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stay terminated

  [(7.102)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (7.1)(a) and, if the order made under
  subsection 39.13(1) directs the incorporation of a bridge institution,
  paragraph (7.1)(c) cease to apply to an eligible financial contract at
  5:00 p.m. at the location of the Corporation's head office on the
  second business day after the day on which the order is made under
  subsection 39.13(1), unless the Corporation has undertaken, before
  that time, to assign the contract to a bridge institution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(7.103)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, paragraph (7.1)(a) and, if
  applicable, paragraph (7.1)(c) cease to apply in respect of an
  eligible financial contract to which both subsection (7.101) and
  (7.102) apply on the earlier of the date and time that a notice is
  issued under subsection (7.101) and the date and time set out in
  subsection (7.102).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insolvency or deteriorated financial
  condition

  [(7.104)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (7.101) and (7.102), an
  action set out in paragraph (7)(a), (b) or (f) may only be taken by
  reason of the insolvency or deteriorated financial condition described
  in paragraph (7.1)(a) if that insolvency or deteriorated financial
  condition exists on the date and time paragraph (7.1)(a) ceases to
  apply.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(7.105)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in paragraphs (7.1)(b) to (e) prevents a
  person from relying on the facts that led to the making of an order
  under subsection 39.13(1) as evidence of the insolvency or
  deteriorated financial condition described in paragraph (7.1)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements overridden

  [(7.11)]{.lawlabel} Any stipulation in an eligible financial contract
  is of no force or effect if it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has the effect of providing for or permitting
    anything that, in substance, is contrary to subsection (7.1); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provides, in substance, that, by reason of the
    occurrence of any circumstance described in paragraphs (7.1)(a) to
    (e), the federal member institution ceases to have the rights ---
    or, in the case of a bridge institution, does not have the rights
    --- to use or deal with assets that the federal member institution
    or bridge institution would otherwise have.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(7.12)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (7.1) does not apply in respect of an
  eligible financial contract between the federal member institution and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the government of a foreign country;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a central bank; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a clearing house, unless the Corporation has given
    the undertaking referred to in subsection (3.3) in respect of the
    institution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assignment of eligible financial
  contracts

  [(7.2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (7.21), the Corporation may
  assign to a bridge institution or a third party eligible financial
  contracts --- including any claim under such contracts --- that are
  between a federal member institution and an entity or any of the
  following entities if the Corporation assigns all of those eligible
  financial contracts to the bridge institution or the third party:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} another entity that is controlled --- directly or
    indirectly --- by the entity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} another entity that controls --- directly or
    indirectly --- the entity; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} another entity that is controlled --- directly or
    indirectly --- by the entity referred to in paragraph (b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligible third parties

  [(7.21)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may assign eligible financial
  contracts to a third party under subsection (7.2) if the third party
  has met any condition that may be prescribed in the by-laws and has
  certified in writing that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it maintains all material licences and
    registrations that are required for the continued operation of its
    business and, if applicable, that it is in good standing in respect
    of those licences and registrations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it has, on its balance sheet, assets that exceed
    its liabilities;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is able to discharge its obligations in respect
    of the assigned eligible financial contracts as they become due; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} its creditworthiness, taking into account any
    credit support or guarantee in respect of its obligations under the
    assigned contracts, is at least as good as the federal member
    institution's creditworthiness was immediately before the order was
    made under subsection 39.13(1), taking into account any credit
    support or guarantee in respect of the federal member institution's
    obligations under those contracts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effects of assignment of eligible
  financial contracts

  [(7.3)]{.lawlabel} If any of the eligible financial contracts are
  assigned to or assumed by a bridge institution or a third party,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Corporation shall assign all of the federal
    member institution's obligations arising from the eligible financial
    contracts, and the bridge institution or the third party shall
    assume those obligations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the federal member institution's interest or right
    in property that secures its obligations under the eligible
    financial contracts is transferred to the bridge institution or the
    third party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of this section

  [(7.4)]{.lawlabel} A federal member institution that is part of a
  class prescribed by the by-laws must ensure in accordance with the
  by-laws that this section --- or provisions that have substantially
  the same effect as this section --- applies to any eligible financial
  contract to which the institution is a party and that is part of a
  class prescribed by the by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

  [(7.5)]{.lawlabel} The Board may make by-laws respecting the manner in
  which a federal member institution referred to in subsection (7.4) is
  to ensure that this section --- or provisions that have substantially
  the same effect as this section --- applies to an eligible financial
  contract referred to in that subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Different treatment

  [(7.6)]{.lawlabel} The by-laws made under subsection (7.5) may
  distinguish among classes of federal member institutions and classes
  of eligible financial contracts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} kinds of agreements for the purposes of the

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a transaction that involves the amalgamation of the
    federal member institution;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a transaction or series of transactions that

    involves the sale or other disposition of all or part of the assets

  (2), other than one described in subsection (2.1), requires the

  the Minister approves it after consultation with the Superintendent.

  [(2.3)]{.lawlabel} An order made under paragraph 39.13(1)(d) gives the

    a registered disability savings plan that is for the benefit of an
    individual, then the aggregate of those moneys and any other moneys
    that constitute a deposit or part of a deposit received from the
    same depositor under any other registered disability savings plan
    that is for the benefit of that individual is considered to be a
    single deposit separate from any other deposit for the benefit of
    that individual.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Government moneys

    [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (4) and (5) moneys
    received by a member institution from a depositor in accordance with
    a registered education savings plan or a registered disability
    savings plan are considered to include moneys received from the
    government of Canada or the government of a province under that
    plan.

### [Trust Deposits]{.HTitleText1} {#trust-deposits .SchedHeadL1}

#### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#general .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Trust deposits

  - **6** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a member institution is obligated to repay
    moneys to a depositor who is acting as a trustee for another person
    and the trusteeship is disclosed on the records of the member
    institution, in accordance with the by-laws, the deposit of the
    depositor is, for the purposes of deposit insurance with the
    Corporation, considered to be a deposit separate from any deposit of
    the depositor acting in their own right, in a co-ownership capacity
    or acting in a trust capacity for another person with the member
    institution.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Separate trust deposit

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a member institution is obligated to repay
    moneys to a depositor who is acting as trustee for a beneficiary and
    the trusteeship is disclosed on the records of the member
    institution, in accordance with the by-laws, the interest or right
    of the beneficiary in the deposit is, for the purposes of deposit
    insurance with the Corporation, considered to be a deposit separate
    from any deposit of the beneficiary made with the member institution
    in their own right for their own use and separate from any interest
    or right of the beneficiary in respect of any other trust deposit
    made by another depositor of which the beneficiary is a beneficiary.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deposit of beneficiary

    [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 10, if a member institution is
    obligated to repay moneys to a depositor who is acting as trustee
    for two or more beneficiaries and the interest or right of a
    beneficiary in the deposit is disclosed on the records of the member
    institution, in accordance with the by-laws, the interest or right
    of that beneficiary in the deposit is, for the purposes of deposit
    insurance with the Corporation, considered to be a separate deposit.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Multiple deposits

    [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, if a member institution is
    obligated to repay moneys to a depositor who is acting as trustee
    for two or more trusteeships under which the same beneficiary is a
    beneficiary, the aggregate of the interest or right of that
    beneficiary in those deposits shall be insured to a maximum of one
    hundred thousand dollars.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to trustees

    [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may, in accordance with the
    by-laws, require a member institution who is obligated to repay
    moneys to a depositor who is acting as a trustee for another person
    to provide the trustee with the information specified by the
    Corporation.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Error or omission

    [(6)]{.lawlabel} An error or omission that results in a failure to
    meet a requirement of this schedule does not prevent a deposit from
    being considered a separate deposit under subsection (1), (2) or (3)
    if

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the trustee is aware of the error or omission and
      has made their best efforts to correct it; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the trustee is not aware of the error or omission
      but has exercised due diligence to be in compliance with the
      requirements of this schedule.

- **6.1** [\[Repealed, 2021, c. 23, s. 132\]]{.Repealed}

#### [Nominee Broker Deposits]{.HTitleText2} {#nominee-broker-deposits .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional conditions

  - **7** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following conditions apply to nominee
    broker deposits in addition to those set out in section 6:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the time the deposit is made and each time a
      change is made to the deposit, the nominee broker must provide the
      following information to the member institution for it to disclose
      on its records

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the fact that the deposit is made by a nominee
        broker,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the unique alphanumeric code for each
        beneficiary of the deposit, assigned in accordance with the
        by-laws,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the amount or percentage of the interest or
        right of the beneficiary associated with that code, and

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any other information specified in the
        by-laws; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the nominee broker must provide, in accordance
      with the by-laws, the following to the Corporation, within three
      business days after the day on which a request is made by the
      Corporation:

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} each unique alphanumeric code provided under
        subparagraph (a)(ii),

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the current name and address of the
        beneficiary associated with that code, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any other information specified in the
        by-laws respecting the deposit.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Co-ownership

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} If two or more persons are co-owners of a deposit,
    they are, for the purposes of assigning a unique alphanumeric code,
    considered to be a single beneficiary, separate from any of the
    persons as a beneficiary --- in their own right, in a trust capacity
    or in another co-ownership capacity --- of a deposit made by the
    same nominee broker with the same member institution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Member institution requirements ---
  agreements and arrangements

  - **8** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a member institution enters into an
    agreement or arrangement respecting nominee broker deposits, the
    member institution must ensure that the agreement or arrangement
    includes provisions that require the nominee broker

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to provide information to the Corporation in
      accordance with paragraph 7(1)(b);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to make an initial attestation to the Corporation
      and updated attestations in accordance with the bylaws that state
      whether the nominee broker is, or is not, capable of fullfilling
      the obligations set out in paragraph 7(1)(b); and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to provide and update contact information in
      accordance with the by-laws to the member institution.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Updates

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} The member institution must ask the depositor to
    make an updated attestation under paragraph (1)(b) and to provide
    updated contact information under paragraph (1)(c) each April.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

    [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a member institution enters into an agreement or
    arrangement respecting nominee broker deposits, the member
    institution must notify the Corporation in accordance with the
    by-laws.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice --- end of agreement or
    arrangement

    [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a member institution that has entered into an
    agreement or arrangement respecting nominee broker deposits is no
    longer a party to that agreement or arrangement, the member
    institution must notify the Corporation in accordance with the
    by-laws.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to comply with paragraph
    7(1)(a)

    [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the member institution receives moneys from or
    holds moneys in a nominee broker deposit and the nominee broker
    fails to comply with paragraph 7(1)(a), the member institution must,
    as soon as possible after the failure, provide the nominee broker
    with the information specified in the by-laws.

#### [Professional Trustee Accounts]{.HTitleText2} {#professional-trustee-accounts .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Professional trustee account

  **9** A member institution must identify an account as a professional
  trustee account if the depositor

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} makes an attestation, in accordance with the
    by-laws, stating that they are a professional trustee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provides contact information in accordance with the
    by-laws; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} requests that the account to be identified as a
    professional trustee account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deposit of beneficiary

  **10** If a deposit referred to in subsection 6(3) is held in an
  account identified as a professional trustee account, the interest or
  right of each beneficiary in the deposit does not have to be disclosed
  on the records of the member institution. However, the interest or
  right of each beneficiary in the deposit is not, for the purposes of
  deposit insurance with the Corporation, considered to be a separate
  deposit unless the depositor meets the conditions set out in section
  11.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional conditions

  **11** In respect of a deposit held in an account identified as a
  professional trustee account, the following conditions apply in
  addition to those set out in subsections 6(1) and (2):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the depositor must, in accordance with the by-laws,
    maintain a record that sets out the current name and address of each
    beneficiary of a deposit in the account and the amount or percentage
    of the interest or right of each beneficiary;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the depositor must provide, in accordance with the
    by-laws, information respecting deposits in the account to the
    Corporation if a request is made by the Corporation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} each April, the depositor must provide the member
    institution with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an attestation, in accordance with the by-laws,
      stating that they continue to be a professional trustee,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} confirmation that the account is to continue to
      be identified as a professional trustee account, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} updated contact information in accordance with
      the by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Updates

  - **12** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member institution that has identified an
    account as a professional trustee account must ask the depositor to
    carry out the actions referred to in paragraph 11(c) each March.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of status

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} A depositor who has requested that the account to
    be identified as a professional trustee account and who is no longer
    a professional trustee, must notify the member institution that they
    are no longer a professional trustee and request that the
    designation be removed.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation removed

    [(3)]{.lawlabel} A member institution must remove the designation of
    an account identified as a professional trustee account if

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the depositor requests that the designation be
      removed; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the depositor fails to carry out the actions
      referred to in paragraph 11(c) by April 30.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information privileged

  **13** For greater certainty, the provision by a depositor to the
  Corporation of information respecting deposits held in an account
  identified as a professional trustee account that is subject to
  solicitor-client privilege or the professional secrecy of advocates
  and notaries does not constitute a waiver of the privilege or secrecy.

### [By-laws]{.HTitleText1} {#by-laws .SchedHeadL1}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

  **14** For the purposes of the provisions of this schedule, the Board
  may make by-laws

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting information that is to be disclosed on
    the records of a member institution in respect of a co-ownership
    interest, a trusteeship or the interest or right of a beneficiary in
    a deposit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the assignment of unique alphanumeric
    codes for beneficiaries of nominee broker deposits;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} specifying information that is to be disclosed on
    the records of a member institution in respect of a nominee broker
    deposit;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} specifying the information a nominee broker is to
    provide, and the form and manner in which it is to be provided,
    under paragraph 7(1)(b);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} respecting attestations by nominee brokers and
    professional trustees;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} respecting contact information referred to in
    paragraph 8(1)(c) and 9(b) and subparagraph 11(c)(iii);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} respecting notification requirements for agreements
    or arrangements respecting nominee broker deposits;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} specifying information for the purposes of
    subsection 8(4);

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} respecting records to be maintained under paragraph
    11(a);

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} specifying the information a professional trustee
    is to provide, and the form and manner in which it is to be
    provided, under paragraph 11(b); and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} respecting the provision of information to trustees
    under subsection 6(5).
