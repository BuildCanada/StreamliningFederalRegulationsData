## Addition of Lands to Reserves and Reserve Creation Act {#addition-of-lands-to-reserves-and-reserve-creation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2018, c. 27, s. 675
:::

Assented to 2018-12-13

An Act to facilitate the setting apart of lands as reserves for the use
and benefit of First Nations and the addition of lands to reserves

\[Enacted by section 675 of chapter 27 of the Statutes of Canada, 2018,
in force August 27, 2019, see SI/2019-92.\]

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-14 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Addition of Lands to Reserves and Reserve Creation
Act](/eng/acts/A-1.3).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-17 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a band, or an Indigenous
    group that is party to a self-government agreement implemented by an
    Act of Parliament. ([première nation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[governing body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[governing body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in relation to a First Nation
    that is a band, means the [[council of the
    band]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
    [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), or, in relation to a First Nation that
    is an Indigenous group that is party to a self-government agreement
    implemented by an Act of Parliament, means the council, government
    or other entity that is referred to in the agreement as being
    authorized to act on behalf of the Indigenous group. ([corps
    dirigeant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to lands in Canada
    elsewhere than in Quebec, means any estate, interest or right of any
    nature in or to the lands and includes an easement, a servitude, a
    lease and a [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section
    2 of the [Federal Real Property and Federal Immovables
    Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4). ([intérêt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Crown-Indigenous Relations. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Indian
    Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([réserve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[right]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[right]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to lands in Quebec, means
    any right of any nature in or to the lands and includes a
    [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the
    [Federal Real Property and Federal Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4),
    and the rights of a lessee. ([droit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Setting Apart of Lands]{.HTitleText1} {#h-30 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Setting lands apart

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by order, at the request of the governing body of a
  First Nation, set apart as a reserve any lands the title to which is
  vested in Her Majesty in right of Canada or for which Her Majesty in
  right of Canada has the administration and control.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interests or rights

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Lands set apart as a reserve under this section are
  subject to any interest or right of a person or entity in or to the
  lands if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an agreement between the First Nation and Her
    Majesty in right of Canada --- including an agreement in respect of
    which Part 2 of the [Manitoba Claim Settlements Implementation
    Act](/eng/acts/M-0.53) applied, as that Part read immediately before
    its repeal, or in respect of which the [Claim Settlements (Alberta
    and Saskatchewan) Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-31.7) applied, as
    it read immediately before its repeal --- contemplates the
    continuation of interests or rights of that kind, and any
    requirement of the agreement with respect to the continuation of the
    interest or right has been satisfied;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the interest or right has been granted or conceded
    to the person or entity under the [Federal Real Property and Federal
    Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the interest or right is granted to the person or
    entity in accordance with section 5 or 6.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest or right in reserve land

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} As of the time when the Minister sets apart any lands
  as a reserve under this section, an interest or right referred to in
  paragraph (2)(a) or (b) is deemed to be an interest or right in or to
  the lands in the reserve.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Moneys paid to Her Majesty

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any moneys paid to Her Majesty in right of Canada as
  a result of an interest or right referred to in subsection (2) are
  deemed to be moneys collected, received or held by her Majesty for the
  use and benefit of the First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of [Statutory
  Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply in respect of an order made under subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  governing body of a First Nation has requested that the Minister set
  apart certain lands as a reserve, the First Nation may designate,
  conditionally or unconditionally, any interest or right in or to the
  lands, including for the purpose of the replacement of an existing
  interest or right in or to those lands. The designation may be made
  either

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the title to the lands or the administration
    and control of the lands are transferred to Her Majesty in right of
    Canada; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} before the lands are set apart as a reserve under
    section 4.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 39.1, 40.1 and 41 of the [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5) apply in respect of a designation under subsection
  (1), any references to Minister in those sections being read as
  references to Minister, as defined in this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On the acceptance by the Minister of a designation
  made under subsection (1), the Minister may grant the designated
  interest or right to a person or entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a designation made under subsection (1) is
  accepted by the Minister, the designation takes effect at the time the
  lands are set apart as a reserve under section 4. Any resulting grant
  of the designated right or interest, if the grant is made before the
  lands are set apart as a reserve, also takes effect at the time the
  lands are set apart as a reserve.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acts deemed to have been done under
  [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} As of the time when the Minister sets apart any lands
  as a reserve under section 4, any designation made under subsection
  (1), and any resulting grant that was made under subsection (3), are
  deemed to have been designated or made, as the case may be, under the
  [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of permits by Minister

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  governing body of a First Nation has requested that the Minister set
  apart certain lands as a reserve, the Minister may authorize, by
  permit, a person or entity for a period not exceeding one year or,
  with the consent of the governing body of the First Nation, for any
  longer period, to occupy, use or reside on any of those lands or
  exercise any other right on them, including for the purpose of
  replacing an existing interest or right of that person or entity in or
  to those lands. The permit may be issued either

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the title to the lands or the administration
    and control of the lands are transferred to Her Majesty in right of
    Canada; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} before the lands are set apart as a reserve under
    section 4.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Permits issued under subsection (1) and the rights
  granted by those permits take effect at the time the lands are set
  apart as a reserve under section 4.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acts deemed to have been done under
  [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} As of the time when the Minister sets apart any lands
  as a reserve under section 4, a permit issued under subsection (1) and
  any consent given under that subsection, are deemed to have been
  issued or given, as the case may be, under the [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization --- transfer or grant

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  governing body of a First Nation has requested that the Minister set
  apart certain lands as a reserve and an Act of Parliament or a
  provincial legislature authorizes Her Majesty in right of a province,
  a municipal or local authority or a corporation to take or to use
  lands or any interest or right in lands without the consent of the
  owner, the Minister may authorize, with the consent of the governing
  body, a transfer or grant of any of the lands that the governing body
  has requested be set apart as a reserve, or any interest or right in
  or to those lands, to a province, authority or corporation, subject to
  any terms that may be prescribed by the Minister. The authorization
  may be made either

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the title to the lands or the administration
    and control of the lands are transferred to Her Majesty in right of
    Canada; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} before the lands are set apart as a reserve under
    section 4.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acts deemed to have been done under
  [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister has made an authorization under
  subsection (1), as of the time when the Minister sets apart any lands
  as a reserve under section 4, the Governor in Council is deemed to
  have consented to the taking or using of the lands under subsection
  35(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) and the authorization is
  deemed to have been made, and any terms prescribed by the Minister
  under subsection (1) are deemed to have been prescribed, under
  subsection 35(3) of that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exchange

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** In the case where a First
Nation has entered into an agreement that proposes that certain lands in
the First Nation's reserve be exchanged for lands that are to be set
apart as a reserve, references to the Governor in Council in paragraph
39(1)(c) and section 40 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) are to be
read as references to the Minister if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has approved the terms of the exchange;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the surrender made in respect of the exchanged lands
  is assented to by a majority of the electors of the First Nation in
  accordance with subparagraph 39(1)(b)(iii) of that Act.
