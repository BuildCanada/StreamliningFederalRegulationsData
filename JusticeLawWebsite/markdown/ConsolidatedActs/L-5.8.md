## Canada Lands Surveyors Act {#canada-lands-surveyors-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1998, c. 14
:::

Assented to 1998-06-11

An Act respecting Canada Lands Surveyors

::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344098 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada Lands Surveyors Act](/eng/acts/L-5.8).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344101 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Act.

[[Association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Association of Canada
    Lands Surveyors continued by section
    4. ([Association]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[boundary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[boundary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a natural or artificial line
    on the surface of the earth, below the surface of the earth, in
    airspace or in or on any structure, the purpose of which is to
    indicate the extent of a legal interest in land, land under water,
    water, airspace or natural resources, or in a structure. ([ligne de
    démarcation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[by-law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[by-law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a by-law of the Association
    made under section 18. ([règlement administratif]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[cadastral surveying]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cadastral surveying]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means surveying in
    relation to

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identification, establishment, documentation
      or description of a boundary or the position of anything relative
      to a boundary; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the generation, manipulation, adjustment,
      custody, storage, retrieval or display of spatial information that
      defines a boundary. ([arpentage cadastral]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Canada Lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canada Lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Canada Lands within the
    meaning of subsection 24(1) of the [Canada Lands Surveys
    Act](/eng/acts/L-6). ([terres du Canada]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Canada Lands Surveyor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canada Lands Surveyor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
    holds a commission. ([arpenteur des terres du
    Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commission granted under
    section 49 or deemed to have been granted under that section by
    section 48. ([brevet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Council of the Association
    referred to in section 13. ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation, partnership or
    unincorporated organization or association, but does not include a
    department or agency of the government of Canada or of a
    province. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence issued under section
    53. ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Natural
    Resources. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit issued under section
    58. ([licence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[surveying]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[surveying]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the determination of the
    form of the earth or the position of natural or artificial things,
    boundaries or points on, above or under the surface of the earth or
    the collection, storage, management, integration, analysis or
    representation of spatial and spatially related information
    pertaining to the earth or the interpreting of or reporting or
    advising on that information. ([arpentage]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Surveyor General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Surveyor General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Surveyor General
    within the meaning of subsection 2(1) of the [Canada Lands Surveys
    Act](/eng/acts/L-6). ([arpenteur général]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344120 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Association of Canada Lands Surveyors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344123 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Association is continued

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Association of Canada
Lands Surveyors, a corporation incorporated under Part II of the [Canada
Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8), being chapter C-32 of the Revised
Statutes of Canada, 1970, is hereby continued under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status and composition of Association

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Association is a body
corporate consisting of a governing body and members.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The objects of the
Association are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to establish and maintain standards of qualifications
  for Canada Lands Surveyors;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to regulate Canada Lands Surveyors;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to establish and maintain standards of conduct,
  knowledge and skill among members of the Association and permit
  holders;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to govern the activities of members of the
  Association and permit holders;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} to cooperate with other organizations for the
  advancement of surveying; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} to perform the duties and exercise the powers that
  are imposed or conferred on the Association by this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may act in place of Association

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may take such
measures as the Minister considers appropriate to fulfil any object of
the Association if the Minister is of the opinion that the Association
is not fulfilling it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity of Association

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** In carrying out its
objects, the Association has the capacity of a natural person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Association not agent of Her Majesty

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Association is not an
agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the
Association shall be located in Canada at the place stated in the
by-laws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual meeting

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Association shall hold an annual general meeting, within six months
  after the end of its fiscal year, at a time and place within Canada as
  may be designated by the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other meetings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Association may hold other general or special
  meetings as it considers necessary.

## [President and Vice-president of the Association]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344149 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President and Vice-President

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President and Vice-President of the Association shall be appointed or
  elected in accordance with the by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  President of the Association, or if that office is vacant, the
  Vice-President of the Association has all the powers of and may
  exercise and perform all of the duties of the President of the
  Association.

## [Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344155 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition and role

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Council is the
governing body of the Association and consists of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the President and Vice-President of the Association;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the most recent past President of the Association as
  defined in the by-laws;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} members of the Association, in a number set out in
  the by-laws, which number may not be less than three, who are elected
  in accordance with the by-laws;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Surveyor General; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} two persons who are not members of the Association
  and who are appointed by the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson of the Council

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President of the Association is the Chairperson of the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of Chairperson

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson of the Council shall preside at
  meetings of the Council and perform the duties that the Council
  specifies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may review activities

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may review
the activities of the Council and may request that the Council undertake
activities that, in the opinion of the Minister, are advisable to carry
out the objects of the Association.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filling of vacancies

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  vacancy on the Council caused by the death, resignation, removal or
  incapacity to act of a Council member referred to in paragraph 13(c)
  shall be filled by a member of the Association in the manner set out
  in the by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person appointed or elected to fill a vacancy on
  the Council shall hold office for the unexpired portion of the term of
  the Council member being replaced.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall not appoint anyone to the Council under paragraph
  13(e), or revoke the appointment of a person appointed under that
  paragraph, without having first consulted the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Council member appointed under paragraph 13(e)
  shall be appointed for a term of not more than three years and may be
  reappointed for one or more terms of not more than three years each.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees and travelling and living expenses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall pay Council members appointed
  under paragraph 13(e), and the Surveyor General, such fees as may be
  fixed by the Minister and reasonable travelling and living expenses
  incurred by them in the performance of their duties while absent from
  their usual place of residence.

## [By-laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344182 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Council may make by-laws respecting any matter necessary to carry on
  the business and activities of the Association, including by-laws
  regarding any matter for which by-laws are required by this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When by-law becomes effective

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A by-law is not effective until it is approved in
  accordance with the by-laws.

## [Council Committees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344188 .Part}

### [Establishment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-344189 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory committees

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Council shall establish the following committees:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Executive Committee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Board of Examiners;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Complaints Committee; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Discipline Committee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other committees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Council may from time to time as it considers
  necessary establish committees other than those referred to in
  subsection (1).

### [Executive Committee]{.HTitleText2} {#h-344199 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Executive Committee shall perform such powers of the Council as the
  Council may delegate to it, other than the power to make regulations
  or by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Urgent matters

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to ratification by the Council at its next
  meeting, the Executive Committee may take any action on any matter
  that requires immediate action between meetings of the Council, other
  than making regulations or by-laws.

### [Board of Examiners]{.HTitleText2} {#h-344205 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 22, the Board of
  Examiners has control over all matters relating to the admission and
  qualifications of candidates for a commission and to their
  examination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Admission of candidates

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board of Examiners may admit a person to be a
  candidate for a commission only if the person has complied with the
  requirements specified in the regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions from Council

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The Board of Examiners
shall comply with any direction given to it by the Council with respect
to the exercise of the Board of Examiners' powers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendations for commissions

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Board of Examiners
shall recommend to the Council that a commission be granted to every
candidate for a commission who has complied with the requirements
specified in the regulations.

### [Complaints Committee]{.HTitleText2} {#h-344215 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Complaints Committee shall consider all complaints in writing that are
  received by the Association regarding the conduct or actions of any
  member of the Association, Canada Lands Surveyor or permit holder.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights of Committee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Complaints Committee has reasonable grounds
  to believe that a member of the Association, Canada Lands Surveyor or
  permit holder against whom a complaint is made has committed an act of
  professional misconduct or is incompetent, the Complaints Committee,
  or a person appointed by it for the purpose, may inquire into and
  examine the surveying activities of the member, Canada Lands Surveyor
  or permit holder and may enter, at any reasonable time, the business
  premises of the member, Canada Lands Surveyor or permit holder, other
  than a dwelling-house, and examine documents and things relative to
  the subject-matter of the complaint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operation of data processing systems
  and copying equipment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In carrying out an investigation under this section,
  the Complaints Committee, or a person appointed by it for the purpose,
  may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any data processing system
    at the place to examine any data contained in or available to the
    data processing system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reproduce any record or cause it to be reproduced
    from the data in the form of a printout or other intelligible output
    and take the printout or other output for examination or copying;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any copying equipment at
    the place to make copies of any record or other document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to assist

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every member of the Association, Canada Lands
  Surveyor or permit holder in respect of whom a complaint has been
  received by the Association shall give all reasonable assistance to
  the Complaints Committee, or to the person appointed by it to conduct
  the investigation, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision on complaint

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  having considered a complaint, the Complaints Committee shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} recommend to the Council that the complaint be
    dismissed if it is satisfied that the evidence disclosed by the
    investigation does not support a finding of professional misconduct
    or incompetence; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} recommend to the Council that the complaint be
    heard by the Discipline Committee if it is satisfied that there is
    sufficient evidence disclosed by the investigation that could
    support a finding of professional misconduct or incompetence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opportunity to be heard

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Complaints Committee shall not make a
  recommendation under paragraph (1)(b) unless the Association has
  notified, in writing, the member of the Association, Canada Lands
  Surveyor or permit holder who is the subject of the complaint of the
  receipt of the complaint and the member, Canada Lands Surveyor or
  permit holder has been provided with at least 30 days in which to
  submit in writing any explanations or representations that the member,
  Canada Lands Surveyor or permit holder may wish to make concerning the
  complaint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where complaint dismissed by Council

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Council decides not to deal with a
  complaint by either accepting a recommendation to dismiss the
  complaint or by rejecting a recommendation to have the complaint heard
  by the Discipline Committee, the Association shall so notify the
  complainant and the member of the Association, Canada Lands Surveyor
  or permit holder who is the subject of the complaint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opportunity to be heard

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Council shall not reject a recommendation to
  dismiss a complaint unless the Association has notified, in writing,
  the member of the Association, Canada Lands Surveyor or permit holder
  who is the subject of the complaint of the receipt of the complaint
  and the member, Canada Lands Surveyor or permit holder has been
  provided with at least 30 days in which to submit in writing any
  explanations or representations that the member, Canada Lands Surveyor
  or permit holder may wish to make concerning the complaint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where recommendation under paragraph
  (1)(b) accepted

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the Council accepts a recommendation to have a
  complaint heard by the Discipline Committee, the Association shall so
  notify the complainant and the member of the Association, Canada Lands
  Surveyor or permit holder who is the subject of the complaint. The
  notice to the complainant shall also advise the complainant that the
  complainant may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make a request to the Association that a charge
    under oath be made in the name of the Association and that, if the
    Association agrees to the request, the Association shall have the
    responsibility for the payment of expenses and costs of proceeding
    with the charge; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} personally swear a charge under oath and that, if
    the complainant does so, the complainant shall have the
    responsibility for the payment of expenses and costs of proceeding
    with the charge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Association may proceed on its own

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a complainant fails to make a request under
  paragraph (5)(a) or to swear a charge under paragraph (5)(b) within 30
  days after being notified of the right to do so, the Association may,
  on its own initiative, appoint a person to swear a charge under oath
  on its behalf and in its name, in which case the Association shall
  have the responsibility for the payment of expenses and costs of
  proceeding with the charge.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearings not required

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The Complaints Committee
is not required to hold a hearing or to afford to any person an
opportunity to make oral submissions before making a recommendation in
respect of a complaint.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing jurisdiction

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** The Complaints Committee
has jurisdiction to deal with a complaint regarding the conduct or
actions of any member of the Association, Canada Lands Surveyor or
permit holder notwithstanding that the person or entity who is the
subject of the complaint ceases to be a member of the Association or
permit holder, as the case may be, after receipt of the complaint by the
Association.

### [Discipline Committee]{.HTitleText2} {#h-344249 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The Discipline Committee
shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 29, hear and determine allegations
  of professional misconduct or incompetence in complaints submitted to
  it by the Council;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} hear and determine matters referred to it by the
  Registrar with respect to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the reinstatement of a member in the Association,
    or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the issuance of a new licence or permit to any
    person or entity whose licence or permit has been revoked; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} perform the duties assigned to it by the Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charge necessary

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** No complaint may be
referred to the Discipline Committee unless it has been investigated by
the Complaints Committee and converted to a charge sworn under oath.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing jurisdiction

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** The Discipline Committee
has jurisdiction to deal with a charge regarding the conduct or actions
of any member of the Association, Canada Lands Surveyor or permit holder
notwithstanding that the person or entity who is the subject of the
charge ceases to be a member of the Association or permit holder, as the
case may be, after receipt of the complaint by the Association.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Discipline Committee finds a member of the Association, Canada
  Lands Surveyor or permit holder guilty of professional misconduct or
  incompetence, the Discipline Committee may take one or more of the
  following actions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cancel the member's membership in the Association;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} revoke the member's licence, the Canada Lands
    Surveyor's commission or the permit holder's permit;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} suspend the member's licence, the Canada Lands
    Surveyor's commission or the permit holder's permit for a period not
    exceeding two years;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} accept an undertaking that limits the member's,
    Canada Lands Surveyor's or permit holder's surveying activities to
    the extent specified in the undertaking;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} impose any of the terms and conditions specified in
    the regulations on the member's licence or the permit holder's
    permit;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} impose on the member, Canada Lands Surveyor or
    permit holder a penalty not in excess of \$10,000, payable to the
    Association;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} reprimand the member, Canada Lands Surveyor or
    permit holder and direct that the reprimand be recorded in the
    register maintained by the Registrar;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} direct the member, Canada Lands Surveyor or permit
    holder to reimburse the complainant, the Association or both all or
    any part of the costs associated with the complaint;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} direct that the members of the Association be
    notified of any action taken by the Discipline Committee under this
    section; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} take any other action that the Discipline Committee
    considers appropriate in the circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision must be in writing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A decision of the Discipline Committee to take any
  action under this section must be in writing and set out reasons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Discipline Committee shall cause a copy of every
  decision to take action under this section to be given to the Council
  and the Association shall cause a copy of the decision to be served on
  the member of the Association, Canada Lands Surveyor or permit holder
  to whom the decision relates and on the complainant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of decision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Discipline Committee is of the opinion that
  circumstances warrant it, the Discipline Committee may, subject to
  such terms and conditions as it directs, suspend any action under
  subsection (1) for such period as it considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of members

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall notify the members of the
  Association whenever a membership in the Association is cancelled or a
  commission, licence or permit is revoked or suspended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of unfounded allegation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where the Discipline Committee finds that an
  allegation of professional misconduct or incompetence against a member
  of the Association, Canada Lands Surveyor or permit holder is
  unfounded, the Registrar shall, on the request of the member, Canada
  Lands Surveyor or permit holder, notify the members of the Association
  of that finding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reimbursement of costs

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Where the Discipline Committee is of the opinion that
  proceedings before it in relation to any member of the Association,
  Canada Lands Surveyor or permit holder are unwarranted, it may
  recommend to the Council that the Association reimburse the whole or
  part of the member's, Canada Lands Surveyor's or permit holder's
  costs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** A party to a proceeding
before the Discipline Committee may, within 30 days after the day on
which a notice of a decision is served, appeal the decision to the
Federal Court.

## [Registrar]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344288 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** The Council shall appoint
a Registrar for the purposes of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Register of surveyors

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Registrar shall maintain a register in which is recorded the name of
  every Canada Lands Surveyor and all other information required to be
  recorded by the regulations in that register.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Register of other information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall maintain a register in which is
  recorded

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of every member of the
    Association who holds a licence, every other member of the

## [Duties and Powers of Members]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344324 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standards of conduct

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** A member of the
Association who directs the provision of surveying services is in all
respects liable for maintaining the standards of conduct and competence
in respect of the provision of those services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to enter

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** A licence holder and any
person acting under the direction of a licence holder may, for the
purpose of cadastral surveying, enter, pass over and measure the land of
any person but shall take all reasonable precautions to avoid causing
damage in so doing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witnesses and evidence

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  licence holder who is engaged in a cadastral survey reasonably
  believes that a person has knowledge of any matter relating to that
  survey, the licence holder may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} request the person to appear before the licence
    holder as a witness;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} apply to a justice of the peace for a subpoena
    compelling the person to appear before the licence holder to give
    evidence and to bring any documents as may be specified in the
    subpoena; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} take evidence from any person requested or
    compelled to appear before the licence holder under oath to be
    administered by the licence holder.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subpoena

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On application by a licence holder supported by an
  affidavit setting out the reasons for requiring the attendance of a
  witness, a justice of the peace may issue the subpoena referred to in
  subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service and contents of subpoena

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A subpoena shall either be personally served on the
  person named in the subpoena or left with an adult person at the
  residence of the person so named and shall state the time and place at
  which the hearing before the licence holder will be held.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses of witnesses

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A licence holder shall tender to a subpoenaed witness
  conduct money in an amount that will compensate the witness for
  reasonable expenses in attending before the licence holder and, in the
  event of a dispute as to the amount, the licence holder shall refer
  the matter to a justice of the peace whose decision shall be final.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant for non-appearance

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a person named in a subpoena refuses or fails
  to appear before the licence holder at the time and place named in the
  subpoena, the licence holder may apply to a justice of the peace for a
  warrant against the person and the justice of the peace may issue the
  warrant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence to be put into writing

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** All evidence produced at
a hearing before a licence holder under section 44 shall be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} put into writing; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} read to the witness by whom it is given and affirmed
  by the witness and the licence holder.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permit required

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** No licence holder shall
provide cadastral surveying services as an employee of an entity unless
the entity holds a permit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certification of documents and drawings

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** Members of the
Association must certify their documents and drawings in accordance with
the regulations.

## [Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344425 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unlawful use of words or conduct

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** Every person, other than
a Canada Lands Surveyor, who

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} uses the title "Canada Lands Surveyor" or "arpenteur
  des terres du Canada", or any addition to or abbreviation of that
  title, or any words, name or designation that leads to the belief that
  the person is a Canada Lands Surveyor, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} advertises or purports, in any way or by any means,
  to be a Canada Lands Surveyor,

is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not
exceeding \$10,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
months, or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Engaging in cadastral surveying without a
licence

**[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** Every person, other than
a licence holder or a person acting under the direction of a licence
holder, who engages or purports to be engaged in cadastral surveying on
Canada Lands or on private lands in a territory is guilty of an offence
and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding \$10,000 or to
imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False statements

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** Every person who

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} makes or causes to be made any wilful falsification
  in any matter relating to a register maintained by the Registrar under
  this Act, or issues a false licence, a false permit or a false
  document with respect to a register, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} attempts to obtain a licence or a permit by knowingly
  making any representation or declaration that is false in a material
  respect,

is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not
exceeding \$10,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
months, or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction

**[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** Every person who

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} obstructs a person conducting an investigation under
  subsection 24(2), or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} hinders or obstructs a Canada Lands Surveyor engaged
  in cadastral surveying, or a person assisting a Canada Lands Surveyor
  engaged in cadastral surveying,

is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not
exceeding \$10,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
months, or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time for instituting proceedings

**[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** Proceedings to obtain a
conviction for an offence under sections 63 to 66 shall not be commenced
on a date more than five years after the date on which the offence was
committed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Injunctions

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** Where any provision of
this Act is contravened, the Association may, notwithstanding any other
remedy or any penalty imposed, apply to a court for an order prohibiting
the continuation or repetition of the contravention or the carrying out
of any activity that will result, or will likely result, in the
continuation or repetition of the contravention by the person who has
committed the contravention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Civil remedy not affected

**[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** No civil remedy for any
act or omission is suspended or affected by reason that the act or
omission is an offence under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decisions remain in force

**[[89]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-89 .sectionLabel}** Every decision of the
former Board of Examiners, including those relating to the cancellation
or suspension of commissions, that is in force immediately before the
day on which this section comes into force remains in force and is
deemed to be a decision of the new Association.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaints

**[[90]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-90 .sectionLabel}** Where, after the day on
which this section comes into force, a complaint is made regarding the
conduct or actions of a Canada Lands Surveyor before that day, the
Discipline Committee may suspend or cancel the Canada Lands Surveyor's
commission only if it determines that the Canada Lands Surveyor is
guilty of anything mentioned in subsection 16(1) of the [Canada Lands
Surveys Act](/eng/acts/L-6), as that subsection read immediately before
that day.
