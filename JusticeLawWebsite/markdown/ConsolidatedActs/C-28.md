## Children of Deceased Veterans Education Assistance Act {#children-of-deceased-veterans-education-assistance-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. C-28
:::

An Act to provide assistance for the higher education of children of
certain deceased members of the armed forces and of other persons
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80666 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Children of Deceased Veterans Education Assistance
Act](/eng/acts/C-28).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-28, s. 1
- 1990, c. 43, s. 43
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80671 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[educational institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[educational institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a technical or
    vocational school, a university, college or other school of higher
    education in Canada, approved by the Minister for the education or
    instruction of students pursuant to this Act; ([établissement
    d'enseignement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Veterans
    Affairs; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[student]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[student]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a child on whose behalf a pension is being paid
      under or by virtue of any of the enactments set out in the
      schedule at a rate set out for a child in Schedule II to the
      [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6),

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a child on whose behalf payment of a pension was
      being made under or by virtue of any of the enactments set out in
      the schedule but was, either before, on or after July 1, 1953,
      discontinued pursuant to the enactment under or by virtue of which
      the payment was being made,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a child on whose behalf no pension has been paid
      under or by virtue of any of the enactments set out in the
      schedule and who is, except for age, eligible for a pension under
      or by virtue of any of the enactments set out in the schedule at a
      rate provided for a child in Schedule II to the [Pension
      Act](/eng/acts/P-6),

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a child who, but for the operation of section 25
      or 26 of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6), would be included in
      paragraph (a) of this definition,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a child of a member or a veteran, as those terms
      are defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Veterans Well-being
      Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8), if the member or veteran dies

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} of an injury or a disease that was attributable
        to or was incurred during special duty service or arose out of
        or was directly connected with service in the Canadian Forces,
        within the meaning of that Act, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} of an injury or a disease that was aggravated,
        if the aggravation was attributable to or was incurred during
        special duty service or arose out of or was directly connected
        to service in the Canadian Forces, within the meaning of that
        Act, or

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a child of a member or a veteran, as those terms
      are defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Veterans Well-being
      Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8), if

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the member or veteran is deceased,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a disability award or pain and suffering
        compensation has been granted under that Act in respect of the
        member or veteran, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the member's or veteran's extent of
        disability, in respect of the aggregate of all of the member's
        or veteran's disability assessments under that Act and, if
        applicable, the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6), is equal to or
        greater than the lowest extent of disability set out in column 2
        of Schedule 3 to the [Veterans Well-being Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8)
        in respect of a rate of 50%. ([étudiant]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

## [Education Assistance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80690 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allowances and costs of instruction

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, in
accordance with this Act and the regulations,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} make allowances to or in respect of students to
  enable them to continue their education or instruction within an
  educational institution; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pay in whole or in part the cost of such education or
  instruction.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-28, s. 3
- R.S., 1985, c. 12 (2nd Supp.), s. 2
:::

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, 2003, c.
27, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of allowance

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of the monthly allowance that may be paid to or in respect of a
  student during the period in which the student pursues a full-time
  course of study in an educational institution is the aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$300.00, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if no pension is being paid on behalf of the
    student under or by virtue of any of the enactments set out in the
    schedule, an additional amount equal to the monthly rate of pension
    for one orphan child provided in Schedule II to the [Pension
    Act](/eng/acts/P-6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Total period covered

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The total period for which an allowance and costs may
  be paid to or in respect of a student under this Act shall not exceed
  four academic years or thirty-six months, whichever is the lesser.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The costs of education or instruction that may be
  paid in respect of a student under this Act shall include such tuition
  and other fees and costs as may be prescribed by regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may extend

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may extend the total period for which an
  allowance and costs may be paid to or in respect of a student under
  this Act where the Minister is of the opinion that the student's
  progress and achievements in his course of study are such that it
  would be in the interest both of the student and of the public that
  the payments under section 3 be continued during a further period.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-28, s. 4
- 1990, c. 43, s. 45
- 2003, c. 27, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Age limit

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No allowance or costs shall
be paid under this Act in respect of a student who

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} has attained the age of twenty-five years, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where, pursuant to subsection 4(4), the Minister has
  extended the total period for which an allowance and costs may be paid
  beyond the year in which the student attains the age of twenty-five
  years, has attained the age of thirty years,

except in so far as may be necessary to enable the student to complete
the academic year in which he attains that age.

- [(2) and (3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1990, c. 43, s. 46\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-28, s. 5
- 1990, c. 43, s. 46
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, 1990, c. 43,
s. 47\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to be promoted

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No allowance or costs shall
be paid to or in respect of a student for any education or instruction
obtained after the student fails to meet the requirements of an
educational institution for promotion to the next succeeding level of
the full-time course of study that the student is pursuing at that
educational institution, unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the student failed to meet the requirements for
  promotion, in the opinion of the Minister, for reasons beyond the
  student's control, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the student is subsequently, in the opinion of the
  Minister, promoted to the next succeeding level of that full-time
  course of study or of another full-time course of study having
  equivalent levels at that or another educational institution.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-28, s. 7
- 1990, c. 43, s. 48
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs to educational institution

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, in
accordance with the regulations, pay to the educational institution in
which any student receiving assistance under this Act is taking his
course of education or instruction any of the costs of the course that
are payable to the educational institution.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-18, s. 8
:::

## [Annual Adjustment of Allowances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80730 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual adjustment

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  any allowance has become payable under this Act, the basic monthly
  amount of that allowance shall be adjusted annually, in such manner as
  may be prescribed by the Governor in Council, so that the amount
  payable for a month in any following year is an amount equal to the
  product obtained by multiplying

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount that would have been payable for that
    month if no adjustment had been made under this section with respect
    to that following year,

    by

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the ratio that the Consumer Price Index for the
    twelve month period ending on the thirty-first day of October
    immediately before that following year bears to the Consumer Price
    Index for the twelve month period immediately before that twelve
    month period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of certain references

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section and section 11,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a reference to the Consumer Price Index for any
    twelve month period means the average of the Consumer Price Index
    for Canada, as published by Statistics Canada under the authority of
    the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19), for each month in that twelve
    month period; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a reference to the basic monthly amount of any
    allowance shall be construed as a reference to the amount of that
    allowance, expressed in terms of a monthly amount and calculated
    without regard to the provisions of this section.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-28, s. 9
- 1990, c. 43, s. 49
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding anything
in section 9, the amount of any allowance that may be paid to a person
for a month in any calendar year shall not, by reason only of that
section, be less than the amount of the allowance that was or may be
paid to that person for any month in the immediately preceding calendar
year.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1972, c. 12, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where basis of Consumer Price Index
changed

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Where at any time the
Consumer Price Index for Canada, as published by Statistics Canada under
the authority of the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19), is adjusted to
reflect a new time basis or a new content basis, a corresponding
adjustment shall be made in the Consumer Price Index for any twelve
month period that is used for the purpose of calculating the amount of
any allowance that may be paid.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1972, c. 12, s. 7
:::

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-80751 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the manner of payment of the allowances
  and costs under this Act to or in respect of students and the manner
  of computing the amount of allowances and costs payable in respect of
  any period;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the maximum amount of costs under this
  Act payable in respect of a student, and providing for the annual
  adjustment of that amount as a function of the Consumer Price Index;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the conditions in addition to those
  specified in this Act under which the payment of allowances or costs
  may be terminated; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} generally, for carrying out the purposes and
  provisions of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-28, s. 12
- 2003, c. 27, s. 4
:::
