## Newfoundland and Labrador Additional Financial Assistance Act {#newfoundland-and-labrador-additional-financial-assistance-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. N-23
:::

An Act to give effect to Term 29 of the Terms of Union of Newfoundland
with Canada
::::

::::::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Newfoundland and Labrador Additional Financial Assistance
Act](/eng/acts/N-23).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-23, s. 1
- 2015, c. 3, s. 135
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-1-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 1"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual payment to Newfoundland and
Labrador by way of additional financial assistance

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In addition to all other
payments, grants, subsidies and allowances payable to the Province of
Newfoundland and Labrador, the Minister of Finance, on behalf of the
Government of Canada, shall, out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund, cause
to be paid to the Province of Newfoundland and Labrador in each and
every fiscal year, until otherwise provided by any agreement in that
behalf entered into between the Government of Canada and the Government
of Newfoundland and Labrador, an annual amount, by way of additional
financial assistance as contemplated by Term 29 of the Terms of Union of
Newfoundland with Canada, of eight million dollars.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-23, s. 2
- 2015, c. 3, s. 135
