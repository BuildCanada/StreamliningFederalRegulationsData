## Bell Canada Act {#bell-canada-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1987, c. 19
:::

Assented to 1987-06-25

An Act respecting the reorganization of Bell Canada
::::

::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-24286 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Bell Canada Act](/eng/acts/B-3.6).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-24289 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[affiliate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[affiliate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of the Company, means
    any person that controls or is controlled by the Company or that is
    controlled by the same person that controls the Company; ([personne
    du même groupe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian
    Radio-television and Telecommunications
    Commission; ([Conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Bell Telephone Company of
    Canada, established by an Act of Parliament and continued as a
    corporation under the names "The Bell Telephone Company of Canada",
    "La Compagnie de Téléphone Bell du Canada" and "Bell Canada" under
    the [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44) on April 21,
    1982, whether or not the name of the Company is subsequently
    changed; ([Compagnie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[control]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[control]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes control in fact, whether or
    not through one or more persons. ([contrôle]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistencies

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In the event of any
inconsistency between the provisions of this Act and any other Act of
Parliament providing for the incorporation of corporations, or anything
issued, made or established under that other Act, the provisions of this
Act prevail.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-24298 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, 1993, c. 38,
s. 78\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration re works

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The works of the Company
are hereby declared to be works for the general advantage of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Company to furnish service in certain
cases

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  telephone service is requested by any person or organization for any
  lawful purpose in a municipality or other territory within which a
  general telephone service is provided by the Company, the Company
  shall, with all reasonable dispatch,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} furnish the service; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to any order of the Commission under
    section 13 that restricts the right or ability of the Company to be
    a supplier of telephones, furnish telephones of the latest improved
    design then in use by the Company in the municipality or territory.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in subsection (1) requires the Company to
  furnish the service or a telephone where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the premises for which the service is requested are
    not fronting on a highway, street, lane or other area along, over,
    under or on which the Company has a main or branch telephone service
    or system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the telephone on the premises would be situated
    more than 62 metres or such other distance as the Commission may
    specify from the highway, street, lane or other area; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the Commission has not otherwise specified, the
    Company has not received therefor a tender or payment of the lawful
    rates semi-annually in advance.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, 1997, c. 3, s.
1\]]{.Repealed}

**[[8 and 9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8_and_9 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
1993, c. 38, s. 79\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain investments prohibited

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Company shall not acquire or hold directly or indirectly any shares,
  bonds, debentures or other securities of another body corporate that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is engaged in research and development work in
    areas of inquiry that relate to the business activities of the
    Companies; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} manufactures products for sale to the Company or to
    other customers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to the Company in
  respect of another body corporate that was, on March 7, 1968, a
  subsidiary of the Company.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of disposal of certain shares
required

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  voting shares of the Company held by or on behalf of any person that
  controls the Company shall be sold or disposed of to any other person
  without the prior approval of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of disposal of facilities
  required

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except in the ordinary course of the business of the
  Company, no facilities of the Company that are integral and necessary
  for the carrying on of telecommunications activities shall be sold or
  otherwise disposed of, or leased or loaned, without the prior approval
  of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval form

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An approval referred to in subsection (1) or (2) may
  be specific or general and may be granted on such terms and conditions
  as the Commission deems expedient.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of subsection (1)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of the sale
  or disposal of voting shares of the Company where, if the sale or
  disposal occurred, the person that controls the Company on the day on
  which this Act comes into force would retain the ownership of not less
  than eighty per cent of the total number of all the issued and
  outstanding voting shares of the Company.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1987, c. 19, s. 11
- 2001, c. 4, s. 61
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Unconditional approval
shall be deemed to be given under subsection 11(2) thirty days after
written notification of a proposed transaction to the Commission,
without prejudice to the exercise of the Commission's powers under the
[Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1987, c. 19, s. 12
- 1992, c. 1, s. 22
- 1993, c. 38, s. 80
:::

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, 1993, c. 38,
s. 80\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deposit in office of the Registrar
General

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A deed
  of trust creating mortgages, charges or encumbrances --- or, in the
  Province of Quebec, an act constituting hypothecs --- on the whole or
  any part of the property of the Company, present or future, as may be
  described in the deed or act and an assignment or other instrument or
  act in any way affecting the mortgage, hypothec or security shall be
  deposited in the office of the Registrar General of Canada and notice
  of the deposit shall immediately be given in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of compliance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If subsection (1) has been complied with, it shall
  not be necessary for any purpose that the mortgage, hypothec, charge,
  encumbrance or assignment or any other instrument or act in any way
  affecting it be otherwise deposited, registered or filed under the
  provisions of any law respecting the deposit, registration or filing
  of instruments or acts affecting property.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1987, c. 19, s. 14
- 2001, c. 4, s. 62
:::
