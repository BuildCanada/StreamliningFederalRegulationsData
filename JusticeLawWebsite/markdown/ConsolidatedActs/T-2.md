[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
this Act, [[the aggregate of all amounts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
total of all amounts assessed or determined by the Minister of National
Revenue under the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), but does not
include any amount of interest or any amount of loss determined by that
Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 51 (4th Supp.), s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

- **[[2.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of this Act, the [[aggregate of supplies for the prior
  fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of a person means the total value of
  all supplies, within the meaning of Part IX of the [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15), made by the person in the last complete fiscal
  year, within the meaning of that Part, that ended at least six months
  before the day the notice of appeal for a particular appeal of that
  person was filed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[amount in
  dispute]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, the [[amount in
  dispute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in an appeal means

  - [(a )]{.lawlabel} in the case of an appeal under Part V.1 of the
    [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) , the total of all amounts assessed
    by the Minister of National Revenue under section 97.44 of that Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an appeal under the [Excise Act,
    2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount of duty, refund or relief that is in
      issue in the appeal,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any interest under that Act that is in issue in
      the appeal, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any amount of duty, refund or relief under that
      Act, that is likely to be affected by the appeal in any other
      appeal, assessment or proposed assessment of the person who has
      brought the appeal; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an appeal under Part IX of the
    [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount of tax, net tax and rebate, within the
      meaning of that Part, that is in issue in the appeal,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any penalty under that Part that is in issue in
      the appeal, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any amount of tax, net tax or rebate, within
      the meaning of that Part, that is likely to be affected by the
      appeal in any other appeal, assessment or proposed assessment of
      the person who has brought the appeal.

## [The Judges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-458974 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Constitution of Court

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Tax
  Court of Canada shall consist of the following judges:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a chief justice called the Chief Justice of the Tax
    Court of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an associate chief justice called the Associate
    Chief Justice of the Tax Court of Canada; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} not more than 23 other judges.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of judges

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The judges of the Court shall be appointed by the
  Governor in Council by commission under the Great Seal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Who may be appointed judge

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), any person may be
  appointed a judge of the Court who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is or has been a judge of a superior court in
    Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is or has been a barrister or advocate of at least
    ten years standing at the bar of any province, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has, for an aggregate of at least ten years,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} been a barrister or advocate at the bar of any
      province, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} after becoming a barrister or advocate at the
      bar of any province, exercised powers and performed duties and
      functions of a judicial nature on a full-time basis in respect of
      a position held pursuant to a law of Canada or a province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chief Justice or Associate Chief
  Justice to be from Quebec

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Either the Chief Justice or the Associate Chief
  Justice shall be a person who is or was a member of the bar of the
  Province of Quebec.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chief Justice and Associate Chief Justice
to have rank and precedence over all judges

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Justice, and after the Chief Justice the Associate Chief
  Justice, has rank and precedence over all the other judges.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rank and precedence among other judges

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The other judges have rank and precedence after the
  Chief Justice and the Associate Chief Justice and among themselves
  according to seniority determined by reference to the respective times
  when they became judges of the Court or members of the Tax Review
  Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incapacity of Chief Justice, etc.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the office of Chief Justice is vacant, or the
  Chief Justice is for any reason unable to act, the powers of the Chief
  Justice shall be exercised and the duties of the Chief Justice shall
  be performed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by the Associate Chief Justice;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the event of the incapacity of the Associate
    Chief Justice or if the office of Associate Chief Justice is vacant,
    by a judge designated by the Chief Justice for that purpose; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the event of the incapacity of the judge
    referred to in paragraph (b), or if the Chief Justice has not
    designated a judge under that paragraph, by the senior judge who is
    in Canada and is able and willing to act and who has not elected to
    hold office as a supernumerary judge under section 28 of the [Judges
    Act](/eng/acts/J-1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure of office

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a judge holds office during good behaviour, but is
  removable by the Governor General on address of the Senate and House
  of Commons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A judge shall cease to hold office on attaining the
  age of seventy-five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transitional

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A judge of the Court who holds office on March 1,
  1987 may retire at the age of seventy years.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oath of office

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  judge shall, before entering on the duties of their office, take an
  oath that they will duly and faithfully, and to the best of their
  skill and knowledge, execute the powers and trusts reposed in them as
  a judge of the Court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How administered

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The oath referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  administered to the Chief Justice before the Governor General, and to
  the other judges by the Chief Justice or, in the absence or incapacity
  of the Chief Justice, by any other judge.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-2, s. 8
- 2002, c. 8, s. 64

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy judges of the Court

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (3), any judge or former judge of a superior court in
  Canada or any judge or former judge of any other court who was
  appointed under an Act of the legislature of a province may, at the
  request of the Chief Justice made with the approval of the Governor in
  Council, act as a judge of the Court and while so acting has all the
  powers of a judge of the Court and shall be referred to as a deputy
  judge of the Court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent required

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No request may be made under subsection (1) to a
  judge of a court in a province without the consent of the chief
  justice or chief judge of the court of which he or she is a member, or
  of the attorney general of the province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of Governor in Council

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may approve the making of
  requests pursuant to subsection (1) in general terms or for particular
  periods or purposes, and may limit the number of persons who may act
  under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salary

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person who acts as a judge for a period under
  subsection (1) shall be paid a salary for the period at the rate fixed
  by the [Judges Act](/eng/acts/J-1) for a judge of the Court, other
  than the Chief Justice or the Associate Chief Justice, less any amount
  otherwise payable to the person under that Act in respect of the
  period, and shall also be paid the travel allowances that a judge is
  entitled to be paid under that Act.

::: HistoricalNote

## [Associate Judges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1367759 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Associate judges

- **[[11.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may appoint
  as associate judges of the Court any fit and proper persons who are
  barristers or advocates in a province and who are, in the opinion of
  the Governor in Council, necessary for the efficient performance of
  the work of that court that, under the rules of the Court, is to be
  performed by them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Number of associate judges

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by regulation, fix the
  number of associate judges that may be appointed under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supernumerary associate judges

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For each office of associate judge there shall be the
  additional office of supernumerary associate judge that an associate
  judge of the Court may elect under the [Judges Act](/eng/acts/J-1) to
  hold.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers and duties

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The powers, duties and functions of the associate
  judges shall be determined by the rules of the Court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salary, allowances and annuities

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Each associate judge shall be paid a salary, and the
  allowances and annuities, provided for under the [Judges
  Act](/eng/acts/J-1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Workload --- supernumerary associate
  judges

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by regulation, fix the
  workload of a supernumerary associate judge as a percentage of the
  workload of an associate judge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity from liability

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} An associate judge shall have the same immunity from
  liability as a judge of the Court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} An associate judge shall hold office during good
  behaviour but may be removed by the Governor in Council for cause.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of office

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} An associate judge shall cease to hold office on
  becoming 75 years old.

## [Jurisdiction and Powers of the Court]{.HTitleText1} {#h-459047 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Court has exclusive original jurisdiction to hear and determine
  references and appeals to the Court on matters arising under the
  [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), the [Cultural Property Export
  and Import Act](/eng/acts/C-51), Part IX of the [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9), the
  [Petroleum and Gas Revenue Tax Act](/eng/acts/P-12), Part V.1 of the
  [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), the [Employment Insurance
  Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6), the [Air Travellers Security Charge
  Act](/eng/acts/A-10.5), the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), the
  [Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55),
  the [Disability Tax Credit Promoters Restrictions
  Act](/eng/acts/D-2.8), Part 1 of the [Greenhouse Gas Pollution Pricing
  Act](/eng/acts/G-11.55), the [Underused Housing Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/U-0.5), the [Select Luxury Items Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/S-8.35), the [Digital Services Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/D-1.65) and the [Global Minimum Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/G-3.3) when references or appeals to the Court are
  provided for in those Acts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction --- [Canada Disability
  Benefit Act](/eng/acts/C-3.45)

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The Court has exclusive original jurisdiction to
  hear and determine references on matters arising under the [Canada
  Disability Benefit Act](/eng/acts/C-3.45) and referred to in
  subsection 66(2) of the [Department of Employment and Social
  Development Act](/eng/acts/H-5.7).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Court has exclusive original jurisdiction to hear
  and determine appeals on matters arising under the [War Veterans
  Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3) and the [Civilian War-related Benefits
  Act](/eng/acts/C-31) and referred to in section 33 of the [Veterans
  Review and Appeal Board Act](/eng/acts/V-1.6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further jurisdiction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Court has exclusive original jurisdiction to hear
  and determine questions referred to it under section 310 or 311 of the
  [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), section 97.58 of the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), section 173 or 174 of the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), section 51 or 52 of the [Air Travellers
  Security Charge Act](/eng/acts/A-10.5), section 204 or 205 of the
  [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), section 62 or 63 of the
  [Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55),
  section 121 or 122 of the [Greenhouse Gas Pollution Pricing
  Act](/eng/acts/G-11.55), section 45 or 46 of the [Underused Housing
  Tax Act](/eng/acts/U-0.5), section 105 or 106 of the [Select Luxury
  Items Tax Act](/eng/acts/S-8.35), section 80 or 81 of the [Digital
  Services Tax Act](/eng/acts/D-1.65) or section 94 or 95 of the [Global
  Minimum Tax Act](/eng/acts/G-3.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extensions of time

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Court has exclusive original jurisdiction to hear
  and determine applications for extensions of time under subsection
  28(1) of the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), section 33.2 of the
  [Cultural Property Export and Import Act](/eng/acts/C-51), section 304
  or 305 of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), section 97.51 or 97.52
  of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), section 166.2 or 167 of the
  [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), subsection 103(1) of the
  [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6), section 45 or 47 of the
  [Air Travellers Security Charge Act](/eng/acts/A-10.5), section 197 or
  199 of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), section 115 or 117 of
  the [Greenhouse Gas Pollution Pricing Act](/eng/acts/G-11.55), section
  39 or 41 of the [Underused Housing Tax Act](/eng/acts/U-0.5), section
  99 or 101 of the [Select Luxury Items Tax Act](/eng/acts/S-8.35),
  section 74 or 76 of the [Digital Services Tax Act](/eng/acts/D-1.65)
  or section 88 or 90 of the [Global Minimum Tax Act](/eng/acts/G-3.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Postponements of suspensions to issue
  tax receipts

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Court has exclusive original jurisdiction to hear
  and determine applications referred to in subsection 188.2(4) of the
  [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) by a registered charity for a
  postponement of a period of suspension of the authority of the charity
  to issue official receipts referred to in Part XXXV of the [Income Tax
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._945).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-459520 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vexatious proceedings

- **[[19.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the Court is satisfied, on application, that a person has persistently
  instituted vexatious proceedings or has conducted a proceeding in a
  vexatious manner, it may order that no further proceedings be
  instituted by the person in the Court or that a proceeding previously
  instituted by the person in the Court not be continued, except by
  leave of the Court, and may award costs against the person in
  accordance with the rules of the Court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attorney General of Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application under subsection (1) may be made only
  with the consent of the Attorney General of Canada, who is entitled to
  be heard on the application and on any application made under
  subsection (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for rescission or leave to
  proceed

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person against whom the Court has made an order
  under subsection (1) may apply to the Court for rescission of the
  order or for leave to institute or continue a proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court may grant leave

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If an application is made to the Court under
  subsection (3) for leave to institute or continue a proceeding, the
  Court may grant leave if it is satisfied that the proceeding is not an
  abuse of process and that there are reasonable grounds for the
  proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No appeal

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A decision of the Court under subsection (4) is final
  and is not subject to appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When effective

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No rule made under this section has effect until it

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of rules

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A copy of every rule made under this section shall be
  laid before Parliament on any of the first fifteen days after the
  making thereof that either House of Parliament is sitting.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules committee

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  rules committee shall be composed of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Chief Justice;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Associate Chief Justice;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} three judges and one associate judge of the Court
    that are designated from time to time by the Chief Justice;

  - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} the Chief Administrator of the Courts
    Administration Service;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} one representative designated from time to time by
    the Attorney General of Canada; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} two barristers, advocates, attorneys or solicitors
    designated from time to time by the Attorney General of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Justice or, in the Chief Justice's absence,
  the Associate Chief Justice shall preside over the rules committee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules procedure

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the rules committee proposes to amend, vary or
  revoke any rule made under section 20 or continued under section 21,
  or continued under section 28, 29 or 30 of An Act to amend the Tax
  Court of Canada Act and other Acts in consequence thereof, chapter 51
  of the 4th Supplement to the Revised Statutes of Canada, the rules
  committee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall give notice of the proposed rule by
    publishing it in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) and
    shall, in the notice, invite any interested person to make
    representations in writing with respect thereto within sixty days
    after the date of the publication; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may, after the expiration of the sixty days
    referred to in paragraph (a) and subject to the approval of the
    Governor in Council, implement the proposed rule either as
    originally published or as revised in such manner as the rules
    committee deems advisable having regard to any representations made
    to the rules committee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Each person referred to in paragraph (1)(e) is
  entitled to be paid, from the budget of the Court, travel and living
  expenses incurred in carrying out duties as a member of the rules
  committee while absent from the person's ordinary place of residence,
  but those expenses shall not exceed the maximum limits authorized by
  Treasury Board directive for employees of the Government of Canada.

## [Administration of Court]{.HTitleText1} {#h-459595 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of Judicial Administrator

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Justice may designate an employee of the Courts Administration
  Service as the Judicial Administrator of the Court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Judicial Administrator of the Court shall perform
  any non-judicial work that may be delegated to him or her by the Chief
  Justice of the Court, in accordance with the instructions given by the
  Chief Justice, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the making of an order fixing the time and place of
    a hearing, or adjourning a hearing; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} arranging for the distribution of judicial business
    in the Court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of designation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A designation made under subsection (1) may be
  revoked at any time and is automatically revoked when the Chief
  Justice by whom it was made ceases to hold office as Chief Justice.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. T-2, s. 23
- 2002, c. 8, s. 80

## [Transitional]{.HTitleText1} {#h-459611 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility for supernumerary status and
annuities

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of sections 28 and 42 of the [Judges Act](/eng/acts/J-1),
  a judge shall be deemed to have held judicial office during any period
  of time during which the judge held office as a member of the Tax
  Review Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem for Associate Chief Judge

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of an election under section 31 and
  of section 42 of the [Judges Act](/eng/acts/J-1), the Associate Chief
  Judge shall be deemed to have held office as such during any period of
  time during which the Associate Chief Judge held office as Assistant
  Chairman of the Tax Review Board or as Chief Judge of the Court.
