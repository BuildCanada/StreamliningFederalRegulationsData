## Canada Travelling Exhibitions Indemnification Act {#canada-travelling-exhibitions-indemnification-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1999, c. 29
:::

Assented to 1999-06-17

An Act to establish an indemnification program for travelling
exhibitions
::::

:::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada Travelling Exhibitions Indemnification
Act](/eng/acts/C-10.5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Act.

[[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a museum, art gallery,
    archives or library that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is publicly owned;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} operates for educational or cultural purposes;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} operates solely for the benefit of the public;
      and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} exhibits objects to the
      public. ([établissement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada as may be designated by the Governor in
    Council as the Minister for the purposes of this
    Act. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[travelling exhibition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[travelling exhibition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an exhibition
    that meets at least one of the following criteria:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total fair market value of the objects in the
      exhibition that are borrowed from outside Canada exceeds the total
      fair market value of those borrowed from inside Canada; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exhibition is shown in at least two
      provinces. ([exposition itinérante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements for indemnification

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  application by an institution situated in Canada that organizes or
  hosts a travelling exhibition, the Minister may, subject to and in
  accordance with the regulations, enter into agreements with the
  respective owners of objects that are to be in the exhibition, and
  with the respective owners of appurtenances that are integral to the
  display of objects in the exhibition, under which Her Majesty agrees
  to indemnify those owners totally or partially for damage to or loss
  of the objects or appurtenances during

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the period of the loan of the object or
    appurtenance, if the exhibition is organized by an institution
    situated in Canada; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the period of the loan of the exhibition, if the
    exhibition is organized by an institution situated outside Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Agreements referred to in subsection (1) may not
  provide for indemnification in respect of any period during which the
  exhibition is shown outside Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum liability

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty's liability under agreements referred to
  in subsection (1) may not exceed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$600,000,000, or any other amount that is provided
    by an appropriation Act or other Act of Parliament, in respect of
    each travelling exhibition; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$3,000,000,000, or any other amount that is
    provided by an appropriation Act or other Act of Parliament, at any
    given time in respect of all travelling exhibitions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expert advice

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may engage
experts to advise the Minister on any matter relating to the
implementation of this Act and may, subject to any applicable Treasury
Board requirements, policies or guidelines, fix their remuneration and
expenses.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations for carrying out the purposes and provisions of this
Act and, without limiting the generality of the foregoing, may make
regulations respecting

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the minimum total fair market value of all the
  objects and appurtenances in a travelling exhibition in order for an
  institution to be eligible to apply under subsection 3(1);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the assessment criteria for applications under
  subsection 3(1), including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the standards of security, transportation,
    environmental control and handling with respect to indemnified
    objects and appurtenances,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the educational and professional quality of the
    exhibition,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the significance and relevance to Canadians of
    the exhibition's theme and contents, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the extent of public access to the exhibition;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the scope of coverage of agreements referred to in
  subsection 3(1), including limitations, exclusions, deductibles and
  maximum time periods;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the process and requirements governing applications
  under subsection 3(1);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the process for assessing or verifying the fair
  market value of each indemnified object and appurtenance;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the process for submitting and settling claims; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the conditions governing the payment of claims, and
  the manner of payment of claims.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of Act by parliamentary committee

- **[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  administration of this Act shall, five years after the coming into
  force of this Act, be reviewed by the parliamentary committee that may
  be designated or established by Parliament for that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review and report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The committee designated or established by Parliament
  for the purpose of subsection (1) shall undertake a review of the
  provisions and operation of this Act and shall, within a year after
  the review is undertaken or within any further time that may be
  authorized, submit a report to Parliament thereon including a
  statement of any changes to this Act or its administration that the
  committee would recommend.
