[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2022, c. 14, s. 3

An Act respecting benefits in relation to rental housing

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1379657 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Rental Housing Benefit Act](/eng/acts/R-5.44).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1379660 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Revenue
      Agency. ([Agence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[cohabiting spouse or common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cohabiting spouse or common-law
      partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
      122.6 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([époux ou
      conjoint de fait visé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 2 of the [Canada Revenue Agency
      Act](/eng/acts/C-10.11). ([commissaire]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Mortgage and
      Housing Corporation. ([Société]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[His Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[His Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means His Majesty in right of
      Canada. ([Sa Majesté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the member of the King's
      Privy Council for Canada designated as Minister for the purposes
      of the [Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation
      Act](/eng/acts/C-7). ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[reference day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[reference day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the later of December
      1, 2022 and the day on which this Act comes into force. ([date de

  [[rent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[rent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a payment made under a lease,
      licence or similar arrangement to occupy a principal residence,
      including any part of the payment that is allocated to pay for
      utilities or property taxes. It does not include any such payment,
      or part of any such payment,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is paid in respect of utilities or
        property taxes to a person or entity who is not a party to the
        lease, licence or arrangement;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is to be deducted from income from
        business under the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3);

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that is specified and that is allocated to pay
        for board or other services;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that is paid by a person to a [[related
        person]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, within the meaning of paragraph
        251(2)(a) and subsection 251(6) of the [Income Tax
        Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), and that is not income of the related
        person for the purposes of that Act; or

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} that is paid with respect to a capital
        lease. ([loyer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Spouse or common-law partner living
  separate and apart

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, a person is not
  considered to be living separate and apart from their spouse or
  common-law partner at any time unless they are living separate and
  apart at that time, because of a breakdown of their marriage or
  common-law partnership, for a period of at least 90 days that includes
  that time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[adjusted
  income]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In section 4, [[adjusted income]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  has the same meaning as in section 122.6 of the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), except that the reference to "at the end of the
  year" is to be read as a reference to "on the reference day".

## [Rental Housing Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1379681 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of benefit

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must pay a
one-time rental housing benefit of \$500 to a person who is eligible to
receive it and who makes an application under section 5.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant is eligible to receive a rental housing benefit if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are at least 15 years of age on the reference
    day;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they are alive on the day on which their
    application is made in accordance with section 5;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} they are resident in Canada in 2022 for the
    purposes of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) and their
    principal residence is, on the reference day, situated in Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} they --- and, if any, their cohabiting spouse or
    common-law partner --- have filed a [[return of
    income]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 122.6 of the
    [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), for 2021 no later than the 120th
    day after the reference day;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} they have an adjusted income for 2021 of no more
    than

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} \$35,000, if they

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} have a cohabiting spouse or common-law partner
        on the reference day,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} reside on the reference day with a [[qualified
        dependant]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 122.6
        of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} claimed a deduction for 2021 under subsection
        118(1) of that Act, because of paragraph (b) of the description
        of B in that subsection, and are entitled to that deduction, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} \$20,000, in any other case;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} they --- or, if any, their cohabiting spouse or
    common-law partner --- paid, in 2022, rent that is in respect of
    2022 for the applicant's principal residence situated in Canada; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} subject to any of subsections (2) to (6) that apply
    in the circumstances, the total amount of the rent referred to in
    paragraph (f) is at least 30% of the applicant's adjusted income for
    2021.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unspecified amount --- board or other
  services

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a payment for room and either board or other
  services is made in respect of a principal residence and it includes
  an unspecified amount for the board or other services, only 90% of the
  payment is to be taken into account as rent for the purposes of
  paragraph (1)(g).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Separation from cohabiting spouse or
  common-law partner

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an applicant had a cohabiting spouse or common-law
  partner during 2022 but is living separate and apart from them on the
  reference day, the total amount of rent paid by the applicant to be
  taken into account for the purposes of paragraph (1)(g) is the total
  amount of rent paid in 2022 by the applicant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Different principal residences

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the applicant and their cohabiting spouse or
  common-law partner live in two different principal residences at any
  point in 2022, the applicant may take into account, for the purposes
  of paragraph (1)(g), the total amount of the rent paid in 2022 by
  either of them for both of those principal residences while they were
  each other's cohabiting spouse or common-law partner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Part-year residents

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of determining the applicant's
  adjusted income in paragraphs (1)(e) and (g), if a person was
  non-resident in Canada for the purposes of the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) at any time in 2021, the person's income for the
  year is deemed to be the amount that would have been the person's
  income for the year had the person been resident in Canada throughout
  the year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bankruptcy

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of determining the applicant's
  adjusted income in paragraphs (1)(e) and (g), if a person becomes
  bankrupt in 2021, their income for that year is to include their
  income for the taxation year that begins on January 1 of 2021.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Only one cohabiting spouse or
  common-law partner eligible

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If two applicants who are each other's cohabiting
  spouse or common-law partner on the reference day both apply under
  section 5 for a rental housing benefit and both would, but for this
  subsection, be eligible for the benefit, only the applicant that the
  Minister designates is eligible to receive the benefit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An application for the
benefit must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be in the form and manner established by the
  Minister; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be made no earlier than the reference day and no
  later than the 120th day after the reference day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attestation

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An applicant must, in their
application, attest to the accuracy of the information that they provide
in it, including the rent paid during 2022 for the principal residence,
the address of the principal residence and the name and contact
information of the person to whom the rent was paid.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1379717 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation, on behalf
of His Majesty and in the place of the Minister,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} has all the rights and liabilities of His Majesty and
  the Minister under this Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} exercises all the powers and performs all the duties
  and functions of the Minister under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister of National Revenue

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of National
Revenue may provide services and carry out activities to support the
Minister in the administration and enforcement of this Act and may
authorize the Commissioner or any other person who is employed or
engaged by the Agency or who occupies a position of responsibility in
the Agency to provide those services or carry out those activities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements or arrangements

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may enter into
agreements or arrangements with any department, board or agency of the
Government of Canada to assist the Minister in carrying out the purposes
and provisions of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
authorize the Commissioner or any other person or body, or member of a
class of persons or bodies, to exercise any power or perform any duty or
function of the Minister under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Availability of information

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Information obtained, or prepared
  from information obtained, under this Act may be made available to the
  Minister of National Revenue for the administration and enforcement of
  the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) if the Minister considers it
  advisable and the information is made available subject to conditions
  that are agreed on by the Minister and the Minister of National
  Revenue.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Secondary release of information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information must not be made available to any
  other person or body unless the Minister considers it advisable, the
  information is made available for the same purpose and it is subject
  to conditions that are agreed on by the Minister and the Minister of
  National Revenue.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment out of Consolidated Revenue Fund

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  amounts to be expended in relation to this Act, including amounts
  payable by the Minister under section 3 and the costs of administering
  and enforcing this Act, are to be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue
  Fund to the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special account

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may pay or cause to be paid all or
  any part of the money paid out to it under subsection (1) to the
  Receiver General, to be paid into the Consolidated Revenue Fund and
  credited to a special account in the accounts of Canada in the
  Corporation's name.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments charged to special account

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If money is credited to the special account under
  subsection (2),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of National Revenue may, at the
    request and on behalf of the Corporation, make payments out of the
    Consolidated Revenue Fund for the purposes of this Act and those
    payments are to be charged to the special account; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} money owing to His Majesty under this Act is to be
    deposited into the Consolidated Revenue Fund and, unless the special
    account has been closed under paragraph 28(2)(b), credited to the
    special account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Social Insurance Number

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is
authorized to collect and use, for the purposes of the administration
and enforcement of this Act, the Social Insurance Number of a person who
makes an application under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information and documents

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, for any purpose related to verifying compliance or
  preventing non-compliance with this Act, require that any person
  provide any information or document within the reasonable time that
  the Minister may specify.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligibility

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may decide that an applicant who fails
  to fulfill or comply with a requirement under subsection (1) is
  ineligible for a rental housing benefit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefit cannot be charged, etc.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A rental housing benefit

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not subject to the operation of any law relating
  to bankruptcy or insolvency;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cannot be assigned, charged, attached or given as
  security;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} cannot be retained by way of deduction, set-off or
  compensation under any Act of Parliament other than this Act; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} is not garnishable moneys for the purposes of the
  [Family Orders and Agreements Enforcement Assistance
  Act](/eng/acts/F-1.4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reconsideration of application

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), the
  Minister may reconsider an application within 24 months after the
  120th day after the reference day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister decides on reconsideration that a
  person has received money by way of a rental housing benefit to which
  they were not entitled, or has not received money to which they were
  entitled, the Minister must calculate the amount of the money and
  notify the person of the Minister's decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount repayable

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Section 18 applies if the Minister decides that a
  person has received money by way of a benefit to which they were not
  entitled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount payable

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister decides that a person was entitled to
  receive money by way of a rental housing benefit, and the money was
  not paid, the amount calculated under subsection (2) is payable to the
  person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extended time to reconsider claim

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If, in the opinion of the Minister, a false or
  misleading statement or representation has been made in connection
  with an application for a rental housing benefit, the Minister has 72
  months within which to reconsider the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for review

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is the subject of a decision of the Minister made under
  this Act may make a request to the Minister, in the form and manner
  established by the Minister, for a review of that decision at any time
  within 90 days after the day on which they are notified of the
  decision or any further time that the Minister may allow.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must review the decision if a request
  for its review is made under subsection (1). On completion of the
  review, the Minister must confirm, vary or rescind the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must notify the person who made the
  request of the result of the Minister's review under subsection (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of overpayment or erroneous
payment

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister determines that a person has received a rental housing
  benefit to which they are not entitled, or an amount in excess of the
  amount of such a benefit to which they are entitled, the person must
  repay the amount of the payment or the excess amount, as the case may
  be, as soon as feasible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery as debt due to His Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the erroneous payment or overpayment,
  as determined by the Minister, constitutes a debt due to His Majesty
  as of the day on which the amount was paid and the debt is payable and
  may be recovered by the Minister as of the day the Minister determined
  the amount of the erroneous payment or overpayment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation or prescription period

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section, no action
  or proceedings may be taken to recover money owing under this Act
  after the end of the six-year limitation or prescription period that
  begins on the day on which the money becomes due and payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction, set-off or compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Money owing by a person under this Act may be
  recovered at any time by way of deduction from, or set-off or
  compensation against, any sum of money, including a rental housing
  benefit, that may be due or payable by His Majesty to the person,
  other than an amount payable under section 122.61 of the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment of liability

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person's liability for money owing under this
  Act is acknowledged in accordance with subsection (5), the time during
  which the limitation or prescription period has run before the
  acknowledgment does not count in the calculation of that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment after limitation or
  prescription period

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a person's liability for money owing under this
  Act is acknowledged in accordance with subsection (5) after the end of
  the limitation or prescription period, an action or proceedings to
  recover the money may, subject to subsections (3) and (6), be brought
  within six years after the date of the acknowledgment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of acknowledgments

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An acknowledgment of liability means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a written promise to pay the money owing, signed by
    the person or their agent or other representative;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written acknowledgment of the money owing, signed
    by the person or their agent or other representative, whether or not
    a promise to pay can be implied from it and whether or not it
    contains a refusal to pay;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a part payment by the person or their agent or
    other representative of any money owing; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any acknowledgment of the money owing made by the
    person, their agent or other representative or the trustee or
    administrator in the course of proceedings under the [Bankruptcy and
    Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3) or any other legislation dealing with
    the payment of debts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation or prescription period
  suspended

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The running of a limitation or prescription period in
  respect of money owing under this Act is suspended during any period
  in which it is prohibited to commence or continue an action or other
  proceedings against the person to recover money owing under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply in respect of an action
  or proceedings relating to the execution, renewal or enforcement of a
  judgment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No interest payable

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** No interest is payable on
any amount owing to His Majesty under this Act as a result of an
overpayment or an erroneous payment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violations

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person commits a violation if they

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} knowingly make, in relation to an application for a
    rental housing benefit, a representation that is false or
    misleading; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make an application for, and receive, a rental
    housing benefit knowing that they are not eligible to receive it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may impose a penalty on a person if the
  Minister is of the opinion that the person has committed a violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of penalty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may set the amount of the penalty for
  each violation at not more than 50% of the benefit that was or would
  have been paid as a result of committing the violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, no penalty may be imposed on a
  person if they mistakenly believe that a representation is true or
  that they were eligible to receive the benefit, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of penalty

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of a penalty is to promote compliance
  with this Act and not to punish.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation of imposition of penalties

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** A penalty must not be
imposed under section 21 if more than three years have passed after the
day on which the act that would constitute the violation occurred.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rescission or reduction of penalty

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may rescind
the imposition of a penalty under section 21, or reduce the penalty, on
the presentation of new facts or on being of the opinion that the
penalty was imposed without knowledge of, or on the basis of a mistake
as to, some material fact.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery as debt due to His Majesty

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A penalty imposed under
section 21 constitutes a debt due to His Majesty and the debt is payable
and may be recovered by the Minister as of the day on which the penalty
is imposed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person commits an offence who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of obtaining a rental housing
    benefit for themselves, knowingly uses false identity information or
    another person's identity information; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with intent to steal all or a substantial part of
    another person's rental housing benefit, counsels that person to
    apply for the benefit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is guilty of an offence under
  subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction to a fine of not more
  than the sum of \$5,000 plus an amount of not more than double the
  amount of the benefit that was or would have been paid as a result of
  committing the offence, to imprisonment for a term of not more than
  two years less a day, or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[identity
  information]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In paragraph (1)(a), [[identity
  information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
  402.1 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- investigators

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate as an investigator any person or class of
  persons for the purpose of enforcing section 25.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may authorize the Commissioner to
  designate as an investigator any employee or class of employees of the
  Agency for the purpose of enforcing section 25.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Proceedings in respect of
an offence under this Act may be instituted at any time within, but not
later than, five years after the day on which the Minister becomes aware
of the subject matter of the prosecution.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sunset provision

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite any other provision of this
  Act, a payment out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund must not be made
  under section 12 after the fifth anniversary of the day on which this
  Act comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consolidated Revenue Fund

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the day after that fifth anniversary,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any money paid out under subsection 12(1) and held
    on that day by the Corporation is to be paid into the Consolidated
    Revenue Fund and credited to the Receiver General; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any special account referred to in subsection 12(2)
    is closed and any money in that account on closing is to remain in
    the Consolidated Revenue Fund and is credited to the Receiver
