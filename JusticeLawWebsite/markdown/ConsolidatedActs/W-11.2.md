[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1996, c. 15
:::

Assented to 1996-06-20

An Act respecting the establishment and operation of a federal program
for the protection of certain persons providing information or
assistance and respecting the protection of persons admitted to certain
provincial or municipal protection programs

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-464731 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commissioner of the
    Force; ([commissaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a provincial or
    municipal program that is set out in the schedule; ([programme
    désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated program protectee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated program protectee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
    who is receiving protection under a designated
    program; ([bénéficiaire d'un programme désigné]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[federal security, defence or safety organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal security, defence or safety
    organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a federal department,
    agency or service that has a mandate relating to national security,
    national defence or public safety matters; ([organisation fédérale
    de sécurité ou de défense]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police; ([Gendarmerie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Public Safety
    and Emergency Preparedness; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Witness Protection Program
    established by section 4; ([programme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[protected person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[protected person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a current or former
    protectee or a current or former designated program
    protectee; ([personne protégée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[protectee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[protectee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is receiving
    protection under the Program; ([bénéficiaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[protection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[protection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} may include relocation,
    accommodation and change of identity as well as counselling and
    financial support for those or any other purposes in order to ensure
    the security of a person or to facilitate the person's
    re-establishment or becoming
    self-sufficient; ([protection]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[protection agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[protection agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement
    referred to in paragraph 6(1)(c) that applies in respect of a
    protectee; ([accord de protection]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[provincial official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, with respect to a
    designated program, the official designated under subsection 10.1(2)
    or (3); ([fonctionnaire provincial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[substantial harm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[substantial harm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any injury, whether
    physical or psychological, that interferes in a substantial way with
    a person's health or well-being; ([préjudice
    sérieux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[witness]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[witness]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who has given or has agreed to give
      information or evidence, or participates or has agreed to
      participate in a matter, relating to an inquiry or the
      investigation or prosecution of an offence and who may require
      protection because of risk to the security of the person arising
      in relation to the inquiry, investigation or prosecution,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who has assisted or has agreed to assist
      a federal security, defence or safety organization and who may
      require protection because of a risk to his or her security
      arising in relation to the assistance, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who, because of their relationship to or
      association with a person referred to in paragraph (a) or (b), may
      also require protection for the reasons referred to in that
      paragraph. ([témoin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Purpose of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-464758 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection of persons

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to promote law enforcement, national security, national defence and
public safety by facilitating the protection of persons

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} who are involved directly or indirectly in providing
  assistance in law enforcement matters in relation to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} activities conducted by the Force, other than
    activities arising under an arrangement entered into under section
    20 of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10), or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} activities conducted by any law enforcement agency
    or international criminal court or tribunal in respect of which an
    agreement or arrangement has been entered into under section 14;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} who are involved directly or indirectly in providing
  assistance to a federal security, defence or safety organization; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} who have been admitted to a designated program.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1996, c. 15, s. 3
- 2000, c. 24, s. 71
- 2013, c. 29, s. 5

## [Witness Protection Program]{.HTitleText1} {#h-464768 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A program to facilitate the
protection of witnesses is hereby established called the Witness
Protection Program to be administered by the Commissioner.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1996, c. 15, s. 4
- 2013, c. 29, s. 20(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-4-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Admission to Program

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Act, the
Commissioner may determine whether a witness should be admitted to the
Program and the type of protection to be provided to any protectee in
the Program.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Admission to Program

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  witness shall not be admitted to the Program unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a recommendation for the admission has been made by
    a law enforcement agency, a federal security, defence or safety
    organization or an international criminal court or tribunal;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Commissioner has been provided by the witness
    with such information, in accordance with any regulations made for
    the purpose, concerning the personal history of the witness as will
    enable the Commissioner to consider the factors referred to in
    section 7 in respect of the witness; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an agreement has been entered into by or on behalf
    of the witness with the Commissioner setting out the obligations of
    both parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergency situations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Commissioner may, in a
  case of emergency, and for a period of not more than 90 days, provide
  protection to a person who has not entered into a protection
  agreement. The Commissioner may, if the emergency persists, provide
  protection for one additional period of not more than 90 days.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1996, c. 15, s. 6
- 2000, c. 24, s. 72
- 2013, c. 29, s. 6
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-6-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 6"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to be considered

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner shall
consider the following factors in determining whether a witness should
be admitted to the Program:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the risk to the security of the
  witness;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the danger to the community if the witness is
  admitted to the Program;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the inquiry, investigation or
  prosecution involving the witness --- or the nature of the assistance
  given or agreed to be given by the witness to a federal security,
  defence or safety organization --- and the importance of the witness
  in the matter;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the value of the witness's participation or of the
  information, evidence or assistance given or agreed to be given by the
  witness;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the likelihood of the witness being able to adjust to
  the Program, having regard to the witness's maturity, judgment and
  other personal characteristics and the family relationships of the
  witness;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the cost of maintaining the witness in the Program;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} alternate methods of protecting the witness without
  admitting the witness to the Program; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} such other factors as the Commissioner deems
  relevant.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1996, c. 15, s. 7
- 2013, c. 29, ss. 7, 20(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-7-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 7"){.PITLink}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed terms of protection agreement

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A protection agreement is
deemed to include an obligation

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the part of the Commissioner, to take such
  reasonable steps as are necessary to provide the protection referred
  to in the agreement to the protectee; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the part of the protectee,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to give the information or evidence or participate
    as required in relation to the inquiry, investigation or prosecution
    --- or to give to the federal security, defence or safety
    organization the assistance --- in respect of which protection is
    provided under the agreement,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to meet all financial obligations incurred by the
    protectee at law that are not by the terms of the agreement payable
    by the Commissioner,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to meet all legal obligations incurred by the
    protectee, including any obligations regarding the custody and
    maintenance of children,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} to refrain from activities that constitute an
    offence against an Act of Parliament or that might compromise the
    security of the protectee, another protectee or the Program, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} to accept and give effect to reasonable requests
    and directions made by the Commissioner in relation to the
    protection provided to the protectee and the obligations of the
    protectee.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1996, c. 15, s. 8
- 2013, c. 29, ss. 8, 20(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for termination of protection

- **[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  protectee may request that the Commissioner terminate the protection
  provided to the protectee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall meet in person with the
  protectee to discuss their request and shall terminate the protection
  provided to them if, either at the meeting or afterwards, they confirm
  their request in the form and manner that the Commissioner considers
  appropriate in the circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective day

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The termination takes effect on the day on which the
  request is confirmed or on a later day that the protectee may specify.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 29, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of protection

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner may terminate the protection provided to a protectee if
  the Commissioner has evidence that there has been

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a material misrepresentation or a failure to
    disclose information relevant to the admission of the protectee to
    the Program; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a deliberate and material contravention of the
    obligations of the protectee under the protection agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of proposed termination

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall, before terminating the
  protection provided to a protectee, take reasonable steps to notify
  the protectee and allow the protectee to make representations
  concerning the matter.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1996, c. 15, s. 9
- 2013, c. 29, s. 20(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons for certain decisions

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Where a decision is taken

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to refuse to admit a witness to the Program, the
  Commissioner shall provide the body that recommended the admission or,
  in the case of a witness recommended by the Force, the witness, with
  written reasons to enable the body or witness to understand the basis
  for the decision; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to terminate protection without the consent of a
  protectee, the Commissioner shall provide the protectee with written
  reasons to enable the protectee to understand the basis for the
  decision.

## [Designated Programs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-464832 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- program

- **[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At
  the request of a provincial minister who is responsible for a
  provincial or municipal program that facilitates the protection of
  witnesses and on the Minister's recommendation, the Governor in
  Council may, by regulation, designate the program by adding its name
  to the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- provincial official

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In his or her request, the provincial minister shall
  designate an official of the applicable province or municipality to be
  the program's provincial official.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of provincial official

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The provincial minister may replace the provincial
  official by designating another official of the applicable province or
  municipality and shall notify the Minister without delay of any such
  replacement.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 29, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deletion from schedule

**[[10.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.2 .sectionLabel}** On the Minister's
recommendation, the Governor in Council may, by regulation, delete the
name of a program from the schedule.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 29, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coordination --- change of identity

- **[[10.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At
  a provincial official's request, the Commissioner shall coordinate the
  activities of federal departments, agencies and services in order to
  facilitate a change of identity for a designated program protectee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The provincial official shall provide any information
  that the Commissioner requires for the purpose of subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 29, s. 11
:::

## [Protection of Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-464853 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosures prohibited

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 11.1 to 11.5, no
  person shall directly or indirectly disclose

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any information that reveals, or from which may be
    inferred, the location or a change of identity of a person that they
    know is a protected person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any information about the means and methods by
    which protected persons are protected, knowing that or being
    reckless as to whether the disclosure could result in substantial
    harm to any protected person; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the identity and role of a person who provides
    protection or directly or indirectly assists in providing
    protection, knowing that or being reckless as to whether the
    disclosure could result in substantial harm to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that person,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a member of that person's family, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any protected person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means and methods of protection

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(b), information
  about the means and methods by which protected persons are protected
  includes information about

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} covert operational methods used to provide
    protection;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} covert administrative methods used to support the
    provision of protection;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any means used to record or exchange confidential
    information relating to protection or used to gain access to that
    information; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the location of facilities used to provide
    protection.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1996, c. 15, s. 11
- 2013, c. 29, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- protected or other
person

**[[11.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.1 .sectionLabel}** Paragraph 11(1)(a)
does not apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a protected person who discloses information about
  themselves, if the disclosure could not result in substantial harm to
  any protected person; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who discloses information that was disclosed
  to them by a protected person, if the disclosure could not result in
  substantial harm to any protected person.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 29, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- Commissioner

- **[[11.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may make a
  disclosure described in paragraph 11(1)(a) that relates to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a protectee, for the purpose of providing
    protection to them; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a designated program protectee, for the purpose of
    facilitating a change of identity for them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may make a disclosure described in
  paragraph 11(1)(a) that relates to a protected person if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the protected person consents to the disclosure;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the protected person has previously made such a
    disclosure or acted in a manner that results in such a disclosure;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Commissioner has reasonable grounds to believe
    that the disclosure is essential for the purposes of the
    administration of justice, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} investigating a serious offence if there is
      reason to believe that the protected person can provide material
      information or evidence in relation to, or has been involved in
      the commission of, the offence,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} preventing the commission of a serious offence,
      or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} establishing the innocence of a person in
      criminal proceedings;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Commissioner has reasonable grounds to believe
    that the disclosure is essential for the purposes of national
    security or national defence; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure is made in accordance with an
    agreement or arrangement entered into under subsection 14.1(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated program protectee

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner is only permitted to make a
  disclosure under paragraph (2)(c) that relates to a current or former
  designated program protectee if, in the Commissioner's opinion, the
  urgency of the situation requires the disclosure or if the appropriate
  provincial official consents to it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- other information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may make a disclosure described in
  paragraph 11(1)(b) or (c)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that relates to the Program or a person associated
    with it, if the Commissioner has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the disclosure is essential for the purposes of the administration
    of justice, national security, national defence or public safety;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that relates to a current or former designated
    program or a person associated with it, if the Commissioner has
    reasonable grounds to believe that the disclosure is essential for
    the purposes of national security or national defence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior notification --- protected person

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall, before making a disclosure
  under paragraph (2)(b), (c) or (e), take reasonable steps to notify
  the protected person and allow them to make representations concerning
  the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (5) does not apply if, in the
  Commissioner's opinion, the notification would impede the
  investigation of an offence, could compromise national security,
  national defence or public safety or could result in substantial harm
  to any protected person or to any person providing protection or
  directly or indirectly assisting in providing protection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior notification --- provincial
  official

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall notify the appropriate
  provincial official of a disclosure under paragraph (2)(a) or (b) that
  relates to a current or former designated program protectee before the
  disclosure is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification --- provincial official

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall notify the appropriate
  provincial official of a disclosure referred to in subsection (3) as
  soon as feasible after making it, if it was made on the basis of the
  urgency of the situation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 29, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- provincial official

- **[[11.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate provincial official
  may make a disclosure described in paragraph 11(1)(a) that relates to
  a designated program protectee for the purpose of providing protection
  to them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate provincial official may make a
  disclosure described in paragraph 11(1)(a) that relates to a current
  or former designated program protectee if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the current or former designated program protectee
    consents to the disclosure;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the current or former designated program protectee
    has previously made such a disclosure or acted in a manner that
    results in such a disclosure;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the provincial official has reasonable grounds to
    believe that the disclosure is essential for the purposes of the
    administration of justice, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} investigating a serious offence if there is
      reason to believe that the current or former designated program
      protectee can provide material information or evidence in relation
      to, or has been involved in the commission of, the offence,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} preventing the commission of a serious offence,
      or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} establishing the innocence of a person in
      criminal proceedings; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure is made in accordance with an
    agreement or arrangement entered into under subsection 14.1(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- other information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A provincial official may make a disclosure described
  in paragraph 11(1)(b) or (c) that relates to their designated program
  or to a person associated with it if the provincial official has
  reasonable grounds to believe that the disclosure is essential for the
  purposes of the administration of justice or public safety.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior notification --- designated
  program protectee

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The provincial official shall, before making a
  disclosure under any of paragraphs (2)(b) to (d), take reasonable
  steps to notify the current or former designated program protectee and
  allow them to make representations concerning the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (4) does not apply if, in the provincial
  official's opinion, the notification would impede the investigation of
  an offence, could compromise public safety or could result in
  substantial harm to any current or former designated program protectee
  or to any person providing protection or directly or indirectly
  assisting in providing protection.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 29, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- agreement or arrangement

- **[[11.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party, other than the Commissioner or a provincial official, who has
  entered into an agreement or arrangement under subsection 14.1(1)
  or (2) may make a disclosure described in paragraph 11(1)(a) in
  accordance with the agreement or arrangement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior notification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party shall, before making a disclosure under
  subsection (1), take reasonable steps to notify the protected person
  and allow them to make representations concerning the matter.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 29, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further disclosures prohibited

- **[[11.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section, a person to
  whom information is disclosed under sections 11.2 to 11.4 is not
  authorized to disclose that information to anyone else.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- Commissioner's request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person may, for the purpose of providing protection
  to a protectee or of changing a designated program protectee's
  identity, disclose that information to the extent that is necessary to
  give effect to a Commissioner's request that is made for that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- provincial official's
  request

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person may, for the purpose of providing protection
  to a designated program protectee, disclose that information to the
  extent that is necessary to give effect to a provincial official's
  request that is made for that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court --- confidentiality

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Once a disclosure described in subsection 11(1) is
  made to a court, the court shall take any measures that it considers
  necessary to ensure that the information remains confidential.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- court

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A court may make a disclosure described in subsection
  11(1) for the purpose of preventing a miscarriage of justice, but in
  doing so it shall disclose only the information that it considers
  necessary for that purpose and shall disclose the information only to
  persons who require it for that purpose.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 29, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to be considered

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner or a
provincial official shall consider the following factors in determining
whether to make a disclosure under section 11.2 or 11.3, as the case may
be, except for a disclosure under paragraph 11.2(2)(e) or 11.3(2)(d):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for the disclosure;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the danger or adverse consequences of the disclosure
  in relation to a person and the integrity of the Program or of a
  designated program, as the case may be;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the likelihood that the information will be used
  solely for the purpose for which the disclosure is made;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether the need for the disclosure can be
  effectively met by another means; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} whether there are effective means available to
  prevent further disclosure of the information.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1996, c. 15, s. 12
- 2013, c. 29, s. 13
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informer privilege

**[[12.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.1 .sectionLabel}** Informer privilege at
common law prevails over any authority to disclose information provided
under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 29, s. 14

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claim --- new identity

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A person shall not be
liable or otherwise punished for making a claim that the new identity of
a protected person, whose identity has been changed as a consequence of
the protection provided under the Program or under a designated program,
is and has been the protected person's only identity.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1996, c. 15, s. 13

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claim --- providing protection

**[[13.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13.1 .sectionLabel}** A person who provides
protection or directly or indirectly assists in providing protection
under the Program or under a designated program shall not be liable or
otherwise punished for making a claim that they do not

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide or assist in providing protection; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} know a protected person or know that a person is a
  protected person.

## [Agreements and Arrangements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-464971 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissioner's agreements

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner may enter into an agreement

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with a law enforcement agency to enable a witness
    who is involved in activities of the law enforcement agency to be
    admitted to the Program;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with the Attorney General of a province in respect
    of which an arrangement has been entered into under section 20 of
    the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10) to enable a
    witness who is involved in activities of the Force in that province
    to be admitted to the Program; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with any provincial authority in order to obtain
    documents and other information that may be required for the
    protection of a protectee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial arrangements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may enter into a reciprocal arrangement
  with the government of a foreign jurisdiction to enable a witness who
  is involved in activities of a law enforcement agency in that
  jurisdiction to be admitted to the Program, but no such person may be
  admitted to Canada pursuant to any such arrangement without the
  consent of the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration nor admitted to
  the Program without the consent of the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arrangements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may enter into an arrangement with an
  international criminal court or tribunal to enable a witness who is
  involved in activities of that court or tribunal to be admitted to the
  Program, but no such person may be admitted to Canada pursuant to any
  such arrangement without the consent of the Minister of Citizenship
  and Immigration, nor admitted to the Program without the consent of
  the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1996, c. 15, s. 14
- 2000, c. 24, s. 74
- 2013, c. 29, s. 20(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement or arrangement --- Commissioner

- **[[14.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may enter into an
  agreement or arrangement with a federal department, agency or service
  regarding disclosures --- that are described in paragraph 11(1)(a) and
  that relate to a current or former protectee --- to each other or to
  another federal department, agency or service or any provincial
  department, agency or service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement or arrangement --- provincial
  official

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A provincial official may enter into an agreement or
  arrangement with a provincial department, agency or service of the
  same province as that official regarding disclosures --- that are
  described in paragraph 11(1)(a) and that relate to a current or former
  designated program protectee --- to each other or to another
  department, agency or service of the same province or any federal
  department, agency or service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to be considered

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner or a provincial official shall
  consider the factors set out in paragraphs 12(a) to (e) in determining
  whether to enter into an agreement or arrangement and in establishing
  its terms.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory terms

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An agreement or arrangement shall include the
  following terms:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a party is not permitted to make a disclosure
    unless it is for a purpose that is essential for the administration
    of justice or public safety and that is specified in the agreement
    or arrangement; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the parties shall take whatever steps are
    necessary, including any steps specified in the agreement or
    arrangement, to protect information that is disclosed from being
    disclosed beyond what is strictly necessary in the circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Multiple departments, agencies or
  services

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} More than one department, agency or service may be
  party to a single agreement or arrangement.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 29, s. 16
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-464999 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation --- Commissioner

  Commissioner may delegate to any member of the Force any of his or her
  powers, duties and functions under this Act, except the power to
  delegate under this section and the power to

  Commissioner to be responsible for the Program and, despite paragraph
  (1)(d), may delegate the powers set out in that paragraph to that
  Assistant Commissioner.

## [Offence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-465041 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure offence

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes subsection 11(1) is guilty of an offence and liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine not exceeding
  \$50,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years, or to
  both; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding
  \$5,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, or to
  both.
