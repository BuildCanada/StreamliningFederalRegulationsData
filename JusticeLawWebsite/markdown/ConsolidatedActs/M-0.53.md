[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2000, c. 33

An Act respecting an agreement with the Norway House Cree Nation for the
settlement of matters arising from the flooding of land

of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Norway House Cree Nation Flooded Land]{.HTitleText1} {#h-348396 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Part.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the agreement concluded
    between

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Manitoba,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Manitoba Hydro-Electric Board, and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Norway House Cree Nation,

    pursuant to negotiations relating to the comprehensive
    implementation of the Flood Agreement. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[first nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[first nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Norway House Cree
    Nation. ([première nation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Flood Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Flood Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the agreement
    concerning the flooding of land concluded between

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Manitoba,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Manitoba Hydro-Electric Board, and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Northern Flood Committee, Inc.

    and signed on December 16, 1977. ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indian moneys

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Amounts
  paid to the first nation pursuant to the Agreement, whether before or
  after the coming into force of this Part, are not Indian moneys within
  the meaning of subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of amounts held

  [[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#M-0.53_en_1){#M-0.53_en_1-ID0EBCAA}[(2)]{.lawlabel} As
  soon as practicable after the coming into force of this Part, all
  amounts collected, received or held by Her Majesty in right of Canada
  for the use or benefit of the first nation pursuant to the Agreement
  shall be transferred to the first nation in accordance with the
  Agreement.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#M-0.53_en_1-ID0EBCAA){#M-0.53_en_1}\[Note:
    Part 1 in force on assent October 20, 2000.\]
    :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 35(4) of the [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 35(4) of the
[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) does not apply in respect of any amount
paid, whether before or after the coming into force of this Part, to the
first nation pursuant to the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Section 36 of the [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5)

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where land is held or
transferred pursuant to the Agreement, section 36 of the [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5) does not apply in respect of that land if the holder
or transferee of the land, or any subsequent holder or transferee of the
land, is not Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claims

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A claim provided for by
both the Flood Agreement and the Agreement may be exercised by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the council of the first nation,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the first nation,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a member of the first nation,

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any group or unincorporated association that is
  wholly or substantially comprised of members of the first nation,

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any unincorporated association established by the
  council of the first nation,

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any share capital corporation the shares of which are
  wholly or substantially owned and controlled by the first nation or
  members of the first nation, and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} any non-share capital corporation the membership of
  which consists wholly or substantially of the first nation or members
  of the first nation,

only in accordance with the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arbitration

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided in the Agreement, the legislation of Manitoba relating to
arbitration applies in respect of any dispute between the parties to the
Agreement that, under the terms of the Agreement, is to be settled by
way of arbitration.

  - ###### [Manitoba Claim Settlements Implementation Act](/eng/acts/M-0.53) {#manitoba-claim-settlements-implementation-act .MarginalNote}

    [677]{.lawlabel} If the council of a First Nation has, by
    resolution, requested that the Minister set apart certain lands as a
    reserve under the [Manitoba Claim Settlements Implementation
    Act](/eng/acts/M-0.53) and, on the day on which this section comes
    into force, those lands have not been set apart as a reserve,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any designation or grant of an interest or right
      made under section 12 of the [Manitoba Claim Settlements
      Implementation Act](/eng/acts/M-0.53), as it read immediately
      before this section comes into force, is deemed to have been made
      under section 5 of the [Addition of Lands to Reserves and Reserve
      Creation Act](/eng/acts/A-1.3);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any ongoing process conducted under the [Indian
      Act](/eng/acts/I-5), in accordance with [Manitoba Claim
      Settlements Implementation Act](/eng/acts/M-0.53), for the
      purposes of a proposed designation, is continued in accordance
      with the [Addition of Lands to Reserves and Reserve Creation
      Act](/eng/acts/A-1.3); and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any permit issued or any consent given under
      section 13 of the [Manitoba Claim Settlements Implementation
      Act](/eng/acts/M-0.53), as it read immediately before this section
      comes into force, is deemed to have been issued or given, as the
      case may be, under section 6 of the [Addition of Lands to Reserves
      and Reserve Creation Act](/eng/acts/A-1.3).
