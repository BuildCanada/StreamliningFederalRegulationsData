## Marriage (Prohibited Degrees) Act {#marriage-prohibited-degrees-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1990, c. 46
:::

Assented to 1990-12-17

An Act respecting the laws prohibiting marriage between related persons
::::

:::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Marriage (Prohibited Degrees) Act](/eng/acts/M-2.1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No prohibition

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), persons related by consanguinity, affinity or
  adoption are not prohibited from marrying each other by reason only of
  their relationship.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall marry another person if they are
  related lineally, or as brother or sister or half-brother or
  half-sister, including by adoption.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1990, c. 46, s. 2
- 2005, c. 33, s. 13
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 2"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marriage not invalid

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a marriage between persons related by
  consanguinity, affinity or adoption is not invalid by reason only of
  their relationship.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marriage void

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A marriage between persons who are related in the
  manner described in subsection 2(2) is void.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1990, c. 46, s. 3
- 2005, c. 33, s. 14
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complete code

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act contains all of
the prohibitions in law in Canada against marriage by reason of the
parties being related.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** \[Repeal\]
