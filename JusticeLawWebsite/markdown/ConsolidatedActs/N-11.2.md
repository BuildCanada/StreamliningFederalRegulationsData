## National Housing Strategy Act {#national-housing-strategy-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2019, c. 29, s. 313
:::

Assented to 2019-06-21

An Act respecting a national housing strategy

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 313 of chapter 29 of the Statutes of Canada, 2019,
in force July 9, 2019, see SI/2019-62.\]
:::
:::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas housing is essential to the inherent dignity and well-being of
the person and to building sustainable and inclusive communities as well
as a strong national economy in which the people of Canada can prosper
and thrive;

Whereas access to affordable housing contributes to achieving beneficial
social, economic, health and environmental outcomes;

Whereas improved housing outcomes are best achieved through cooperation
between governments and civil society as well as the meaningful
involvement of local communities;

Whereas national goals, timelines and initiatives relating to housing
and homelessness are essential to improving the quality of life of the
people of Canada, particularly persons in greatest need;

Whereas a national housing strategy would support a common vision, key
principles and a coordinated approach to achieving improved housing
outcomes;

Whereas a national housing strategy would contribute to meeting the
Sustainable Development Goals of the United Nations;

And whereas a national housing strategy would support the progressive
realization of the right to adequate housing as recognized in the
International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights, to which
Canada is a party;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1168972 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [National Housing Strategy Act](/eng/acts/N-11.2).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1168975 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister designated under
section 3.

## [Designation of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1168978 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate a member of the Queen's Privy Council for
Canada as the Minister for the purposes of this Act.

## [Housing Policy Declaration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1168981 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** It is declared to be the
housing policy of the Government of Canada to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} recognize that the right to adequate housing is a
  fundamental human right affirmed in international law;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} recognize that housing is essential to the inherent
  dignity and well-being of the person and to building sustainable and
  inclusive communities;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} support improved housing outcomes for the people of
  Canada; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} further the progressive realization of the right to
  adequate housing as recognized in the International Covenant on
  Economic, Social and Cultural Rights.

## [National Housing Strategy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1168988 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Development and maintenance

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must develop and maintain a national housing strategy to
  further the housing policy, taking into account key principles of a
  human rights-based approach to housing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The National Housing Strategy is to, among other
  things,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out a long-term vision for housing in Canada
    that recognizes the importance of housing in achieving social,
    economic, health and environmental goals;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish national goals relating to housing and
    homelessness and identify related priorities, initiatives, timelines
    and desired outcomes;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} focus on improving housing outcomes for persons in
    greatest need; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide for participatory processes to ensure the
    ongoing inclusion and engagement of civil society, stakeholders,
    vulnerable groups and persons with lived experience of housing need,
    as well as those with lived experience of homelessness.

## [National Housing Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1168998 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties and functions

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  council, to be known as the National Housing Council, is established
  for the purpose of furthering the housing policy and the National
  Housing Strategy by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} providing advice to the Minister, on its own
    initiative or at the request of the Minister, including, among other
    things, on the effectiveness of the National Housing Strategy; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} undertaking any other activity that the Minister
    specifies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Membership

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Council is composed of two co-chairpersons and
  not fewer than 9, but not more than 15, other members.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Ex officio]{.otherLang lang="la"}
members

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The [ex officio]{.otherLang
lang="la"} members of the National Housing Council are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Federal Housing Advocate, appointed under section
  14;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Deputy Minister of the department for which the
  Minister is responsible;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Deputy Minister of Indigenous Services; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the President of the Canada Mortgage and Housing
  Corporation, appointed under subsection 7(1) of the [Canada Mortgage
  and Housing Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-7).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of other members

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  other members of the National Housing Council are to be appointed by
  the Minister to hold office during pleasure for a term, not exceeding
  three years, that will ensure, as far as possible, the expiry in any
  one year of the terms of office of not more than one half of the
  members who are appointed by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further term

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Council who are appointed by the
  Minister are eligible to be reappointed for one further term.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to consider

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In appointing members, the Minister is to take into
  consideration the importance of representation on the Council of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} persons who are members of vulnerable groups;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} persons with lived experience of housing need, as
    well as those with lived experience of homelessness;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} persons who reflect the diversity of Canadian
    society; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} persons who have expertise in human rights.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Part-time

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Council who are appointed by the
  Minister perform their duties and functions on the Council on a
  part-time basis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and other expenses

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Council who are appointed by the
  Minister are to be paid the remuneration that may be fixed by the
  Minister and are entitled to be reimbursed, in accordance with
  Treasury Board directives, for the travel, living and other expenses
  incurred in connection with their work while absent from their
  ordinary place of residence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Federal public administration

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Council who are appointed by the
  Minister are deemed to be employed in the federal public
  administration for the purposes of the [Government Employees
  Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and for the purposes of any
  regulations made under section 9 of the [Aeronautics
  Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Ex officio]{.otherLang lang="la"}
co-chairperson

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President of the Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation is the [ex
  officio]{.otherLang lang="la"} co-chairperson of the National Housing
  Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of other co-chairperson

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must designate the other co-chairperson
  from among the members of the Council who are appointed by the
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Co-chairperson absent or unable to act

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the co-chairperson designated under subsection (2)
  is absent or unable to act, the Minister may designate another person
  from among the members of the Council who are appointed by the
  Minister to act as that co-chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of co-chairpersons

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The co-chairpersons have supervision over and
  direction of the work of the Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternate [ex officio]{.otherLang
lang="la"} co-chairperson

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President of the Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation may designate
  in writing an alternate to perform his or her duties and functions in
  respect of the National Housing Council and that alternate is
  considered to be a co-chairperson of the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternate [ex officio]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} member

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each [ex officio]{.otherLang lang="la"} member, other
  than the Federal Housing Advocate, may designate an alternate to
  perform his or her duties and functions in respect of the Council.
  That alternate is considered to be a member of the Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  National Housing Council must meet four times each year or as the
  Minister may specify.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of telecommunication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A meeting may be held by any means of
  telecommunication that allows the members to communicate with each
  other during the meeting.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrative support

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Canada Mortgage and
Housing Corporation is to provide the National Housing Council with any
administrative services and facilities that are necessary to assist the
Council in performing its duties and functions.

## [Federal Housing Advocate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169050 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties and functions

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** There is to be a Federal
Housing Advocate whose mandate is to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} monitor the implementation of the housing policy and
  assess its impact on persons who are members of vulnerable groups,
  persons with lived experience of housing need and persons with lived
  experience of homelessness;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} monitor progress in meeting the goals and timelines
  --- and in achieving the desired outcomes --- set out in the National
  Housing Strategy;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} analyze and conduct research, as the Advocate sees
  fit, on systemic housing issues, including barriers faced by persons
  referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} initiate studies, as the Advocate sees fit, into
  economic, institutional or industry conditions --- respecting matters
  over which Parliament has jurisdiction --- that affect the housing
  system;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} consult with persons referred to in paragraph (a) and
  civil society organizations with respect to systemic housing issues;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} receive submissions with respect to systemic housing
  issues;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} provide advice to the Minister;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} submit a report to the Minister on the Advocate's
  findings and any recommendations to take measures respecting matters
  over which Parliament has jurisdiction, to further the housing policy,
  including the progressive realization of the right to adequate
  housing, and the National Housing Strategy; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} participate in the work of the National Housing
  Council as an [ex officio]{.otherLang lang="la"} member.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submissions -- power to review issues

- **[[13.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Federal Housing Advocate may
  conduct a review of any systemic housing issue that is raised in a
  submission received under paragraph 13(f).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submissions -- power to request
  establishment of review panel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Federal Housing Advocate may request that the
  National Housing Council establish a review panel to hold a hearing to
  review any systemic housing issue within the jurisdiction of
  Parliament that is raised in a submission received under paragraph
  13(f).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Federal Housing Advocate must inform the person
  or group that presented the submission whether or not any action will
  be taken under subsection (1) or (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Federal Housing Advocate conducts a review of
  the systemic housing issue, he or she must, at the conclusion of the
  review, provide the Minister and the person or group that presented
  the submission with a report setting out the Advocate's opinion on the
  issue and any recommendation to take measures --- respecting matters
  over which Parliament has jurisdiction --- to further the housing
  policy, including the progressive realization of the right to adequate
  housing, or the National Housing Strategy.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to request establishment of review
panel

- **[[13.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the Federal Housing Advocate, at any time, identifies a systemic
  housing issue that is within the jurisdiction of Parliament and that
  is not the subject of a submission, he or she may request that the
  National Housing Council establish a review panel to hold a hearing to
  review the issue.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summary of information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Federal Housing Advocate must provide a review
  panel with a summary of the information that formed the Advocate's
  basis for identifying the systemic housing issue.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Federal Housing Advocate is to be appointed by the Governor in Council
  to hold office during pleasure for a term of not more than three
  years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further term

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The term may be renewed for one further term.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advocate absent or unable to act

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Federal Housing Advocate is absent or unable
  to act, the Minister may designate another person to act as the
  Federal Housing Advocate, but that person may act for a period of more
  than 90 days only with the approval of the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Full-time

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Federal Housing Advocate performs his or her
  duties and functions on a full-time basis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and other expenses

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Federal Housing Advocate is to be paid the
  remuneration fixed by the Governor in Council and is entitled to be
  reimbursed in accordance with Treasury Board directives for the
  travel, living and other expenses incurred in connection with his or
  her work while absent from his or her ordinary place of work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Federal public administration

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Federal Housing Advocate is deemed to be employed
  in the public service for the purposes of the [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and to be employed in the federal
  public administration for the purposes of the [Government Employees
  Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and for the purposes of any
  regulations made under section 9 of the [Aeronautics
  Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrative support

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Canadian Human Rights Commission is to provide the Federal Housing
  Advocate with any administrative services and facilities that are
  necessary to assist the Advocate in performing his or her duties and
  functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contractual assistance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may, for the purposes of subsection
  (1), enter into contracts for the services of persons to assist the
  Advocate in performing his or her duties and functions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Federal Housing Advocate must, within 30 days after the end of each
  fiscal year, submit a report to the Minister for that year that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provides a summary of his or her activities, any
    submissions received, the results of the consultations and of any
    analysis, research and study; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contains recommended measures --- respecting
    matters over which Parliament has jurisdiction and that take into
    account the housing policy --- to address systemic housing issues.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling in Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must cause the report to be laid before
  each House of Parliament on any of the first 30 days after the
  Minister receives the report or, if either House is not sitting on the
  last day of that period, on any of the first 15 days on which that
  House of Parliament is sitting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Federal Housing Advocate may publish the report
  only after the report is laid before each House of Parliament.

## [Review Panels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169103 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to establish review panel

**[[16.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16.1 .sectionLabel}** The National Housing
Council must establish a review panel if requested to do so by the
Federal Housing Advocate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Membership

- **[[16.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  review panel is to consist of three members of the National Housing
  Council, other than [ex officio]{.otherLang lang="la"} members, to be
  appointed by the National Housing Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to consider

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In appointing members to a review panel, the National
  Housing Council is to take into consideration the importance of
  representation on the review panel of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} persons who are members of vulnerable groups;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} persons with lived experience of housing need, as
    well as those with lived experience of homelessness; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} persons who have expertise in human rights.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

**[[16.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16.3 .sectionLabel}** A review panel must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} hold a hearing to review the systemic housing issue
  in respect of which it was established;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} hold the hearing in a manner that offers the public,
  particularly members of communities that are affected by the issue and
  groups that have expertise in human rights and housing, an opportunity
  to participate;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prepare a report that sets out the panel's opinion on
  the issue and any recommendation to take measures --- respecting
  matters over which Parliament has jurisdiction --- to address the
  issue; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} submit the report to the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representations

**[[16.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16.4 .sectionLabel}** The Federal Housing
Advocate is entitled to make representations and present proposals for
recommendations to a review panel and may, for the purpose of doing so,
work with communities that are affected by the issue that is before the
review panel and with experts.

## [Accountability]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169122 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's response

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must respond to the annual report of the Federal Housing
  Advocate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of response

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must cause the response to be tabled in
  each House of Parliament on any of the first 120 days after the
  Minister has caused the annual report to be tabled in both Houses of
  Parliament or, if either House is not sitting on the last day of that
  period, on any of the first 15 days on which that House of Parliament
  is sitting.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's response to Advocate's reports

**[[17.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17.1 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must
respond to each report provided by the Federal Housing Advocate under
paragraph 13(h) and subsection 13.1(4) within 120 days after the day on
which it is received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's response to review panel
reports

- **[[17.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must respond to a
  report submitted by a review panel under paragraph 16.3(d) within 120
  days after the day on which it is received.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling in Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must cause the response to be laid
  before each House of Parliament on any of the first 30 days after the
  day on which it is provided to the review panel or, if either House is
  not sitting on the last day of that period, on any of the first 15
  days on which that House of Parliament is sitting.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Triennial report

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must, before March 31, 2021 and within every three years
  after that date, cause a report to be made on the effectiveness of the
  National Housing Strategy, with respect to the achievement of the
  desired outcomes, and the initiatives related to its implementation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling in Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must cause the report to be laid before
  each House of Parliament on any of the first 30 days on which that
  House is sitting after the report is made.
