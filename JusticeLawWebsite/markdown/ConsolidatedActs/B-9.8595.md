### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1356630 .Subheading}

- **[[128]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-128
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections 158.35, 158.51 to 158.53,
  158.68 and 158.69 of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), as
  enacted by section 59, subsection 64(2), sections 65 to 69,
  subsections 70(2) and (4), sections .71, 72 and 75, subsection 81(2)
  and sections 82, 87 to 105, 115 and 122 come into force on October 1,
  2022.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 158.41, 158.57 and 158.58 of the [Excise
  Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), as enacted by section 59, apply in
  respect of vaping products manufactured in Canada that are packaged on
  or after October 1, 2022 and to vaping products that are imported into
  Canada or [[released]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} (as defined in subsection
  2(1) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)) on or after that day.
  Those sections of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1) also apply
  in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} vaping products manufactured in Canada that are
    packaged before October 1, 2022 if the vaping products are stamped
    after the day on which this Act receives royal assent; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} vaping products that are imported into Canada or
    [[released]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} (as defined in subsection 2(1) of
    the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)) after the day on which this Act
    receives royal assent but before October 1, 2022 if the vaping
    products are stamped when they are reported under the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 158.42 to 158.47 and 158.49 and subsection
  158.5(2) of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), as enacted by
  section 59, subsection 63(1) and sections 107 to 109 come into force
  on October 1, 2022. However, those provisions of the [Excise Act,
  2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), subsection 63(1) and sections 107 to 109 do
  not apply before 2023 in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} vaping products manufactured in Canada that are
    packaged before October 1, 2022 and that are not stamped; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} vaping products that are imported into Canada or
    [[released]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} (as defined in subsection 2(1) of
    the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)) before October 1, 2022 and that
    are not stamped.

- [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} Sections 158.54 to 158.56, 158.6 and 158.61 of the
  [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), as enacted by section 59, come
  into force on October 1, 2022. However, those sections of the [Excise
  Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1) do not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} vaping products manufactured in Canada that are
    packaged before October 1, 2022 and that are not stamped; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} vaping products that are imported into Canada or
    [[released]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} (as defined in subsection 2(1) of
    the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)) before October 1, 2022 and that
    are not stamped.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} In applying sections 158.57 and 158.58 of the [Excise
  Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), as enacted by section 59, in respect of
  vaping products manufactured in Canada that are packaged before
  October 1, 2022, paragraph (a) of each of those sections 158.57 and
  158.58 is to be read as follows:

  > ::: ReadAsText
  > - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of vaping products manufactured in
  >   Canada, by the vaping product licensee that packaged the vaping
  >   products and at the later of the beginning of October 1, 2022 and
  >   the time they are stamped; and

## [SCHEDULE 8]{.scheduleLabel}[(Sections 158.57, 158.6, 158.61, 218.2, 233.2, 234.2, 237 and 238.01)]{.OriginatingRef}[Duty on Vaping Products]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#schedule-8sections-158.57-158.6-158.61-218.2-233.2-234.2-237-and-238.01duty-on-vaping-products .scheduleLabel}
:::

- [1]{.lawlabel} Vaping products that are vaping devices that contain
  vaping substances or that are vaping substances in immediate
  containers: for each vaping device or immediate container of vaping
  substance

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the vaping substance is in liquid form, the
    amount equal to the total of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the first 10 millilitres of vaping substance
      in the vaping device or immediate container, \$1.00 per 2
      millilitres of vaping substance or fraction thereof, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for any additional amount of vaping substance in
      the vaping device or immediate container, \$1.00 per 10
      millilitres of vaping substance or fraction thereof; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the vaping substance is in solid form, the
    amount equal to the total of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the first 10 grams of vaping substance in the
      vaping device or immediate container, \$1.00 per 2 grams of vaping
      substance or fraction thereof, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for any additional amount of vaping substance in
      the vaping device or immediate container, \$1.00 per 10 grams of
      vaping substance or fraction thereof.

- [2]{.lawlabel} Vaping products that are vaping substances not in any
  vaping device or immediate container:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the vaping substance is in liquid form, the
    amount equal to the total of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the first 10 millilitres of vaping substance,
      \$1.00 per 2 millilitres of vaping substance or fraction thereof,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for any additional amount of vaping substance,
      \$1.00 per 10 millilitres of vaping substance or fraction thereof;
      and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the vaping substance is in solid form, the
    amount equal to the total of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the first 10 grams of vaping substance,
      \$1.00 per 2 grams of vaping substance or fraction thereof, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for any additional amount of vaping substance,
      \$1.00 per 10 grams of vaping substance or fraction thereof.
