## Labrador Inuit Land Claims Agreement Act {#labrador-inuit-land-claims-agreement-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2005, c. 27
:::

Assented to 2005-06-23

An Act to give effect to the Labrador Inuit Land Claims Agreement and
the Labrador Inuit Tax Treatment Agreement
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/) recognizes and affirms
the existing aboriginal and treaty rights of the aboriginal peoples of
Canada;

WHEREAS the Inuit of Labrador are an aboriginal people of Canada;

WHEREAS the Inuit of Labrador claim aboriginal rights in and to the
Labrador Inuit Land Claims Area, as defined in the Agreement, based on
their traditional and current use and occupancy of the lands, waters and
sea ice of the Labrador Inuit Land Claims Area in accordance with their
own customs and traditions;

WHEREAS the Inuit of Labrador, as represented by the Labrador Inuit
Association, the Government of Newfoundland and Labrador and the
Government of Canada have negotiated the Agreement;

WHEREAS the Inuit of Labrador, by a vote held on May 26, 2004, approved
the Agreement;

WHEREAS on December 6, 2004, the Legislature of the Province of
Newfoundland and Labrador enacted the [Labrador Inuit Land Claims
Agreement Act](/eng/acts/L-4.3) to ratify the Agreement;

WHEREAS the Agreement was signed on behalf of the Inuit of Labrador, Her
Majesty the Queen in right of Newfoundland and Labrador and Her Majesty
the Queen in right of Canada on January 22, 2005;

AND WHEREAS the Agreement requires that legislation be enacted by the
Parliament of Canada in order for the Agreement to be ratified;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-343890 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Labrador Inuit Land Claims Agreement Act](/eng/acts/L-4.3).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-343893 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the land claims agreement
    signed on behalf of the Inuit of Labrador, Her Majesty the Queen in
    right of Newfoundland and Labrador and Her Majesty the Queen in
    right of Canada on January 22, 2005, including any amendments made
    to it. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Inuit bylaw]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Inuit bylaw]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Bylaw as defined in
    section 1.1.1 of the Agreement. ([règlement inuit]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Inuit law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Inuit law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an Inuit Law as defined in
    section 1.1.1 of the Agreement. ([loi inuite]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Nunatsiavut Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Nunatsiavut Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the government
    established pursuant to subsection 17.3.3(a) of the
    Agreement. ([gouvernement nunatsiavut]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Tax Treatment Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Tax Treatment Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the tax
    treatment agreement signed on behalf of the Inuit of Labrador on
    March 15, 2005, Her Majesty the Queen in right of Newfoundland and
    Labrador on March 24, 2005 and Her Majesty the Queen in right of
    Canada on April 12, 2005, including any amendments made to
    it. ([accord sur le traitement fiscal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of Agreement

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement is a treaty
and a land claims agreement within the meaning of sections 25 and 35 of
the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-343903 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act binding on Her Majesty

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This Act
  is binding on Her Majesty in right of Canada or of a province so as to
  give effect to the Agreement in accordance with its terms.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inuit laws and bylaws

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in subsection (1) has the effect of rendering
  Her Majesty in right of Canada or of a province bound by Inuit laws or
  Inuit bylaws.

## [Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-343909 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement given effect

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement is approved, given effect and declared valid and has the
  force of law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and obligations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, any person or body has the
  powers, rights, privileges and benefits conferred on the person or
  body by the Agreement and shall perform the duties, and is subject to
  the liabilities, imposed on the person or body by the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Third parties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Agreement is binding on,
  and may be relied on by, all persons and bodies that are not parties
  to it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (3), sections 17.27.8 and 17.27.9
  of the Agreement may be invoked only by Her Majesty in right of Canada
  or by the Nunatsiavut Government.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency with Agreement

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  event of an inconsistency or a conflict between the Agreement and any
  federal or provincial law, including this Act, the Agreement prevails
  to the extent of the inconsistency or conflict.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency with Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of an inconsistency or a conflict
  between this Act and any other federal or provincial law, this Act
  prevails to the extent of the inconsistency or conflict.

## [Appropriation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-343924 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of C.R.F.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** There shall be paid out of
the Consolidated Revenue Fund any sums that are required to meet the
monetary obligations of Her Majesty in right of Canada under chapters
18, 19 and 23 of the Agreement.

## [Taxation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-343927 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tax Treatment Agreement given effect

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Tax Treatment Agreement
is approved, given effect and declared valid and has the force of law
during the period that it is in effect.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not a treaty

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Tax Treatment Agreement
does not form part of the Agreement and is not a treaty or a land claims
agreement within the meaning of sections 25 and 35 of the [Constitution
Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-343932 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice of Agreements

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice shall be taken of
  the Agreement and the Tax Treatment Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of Agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Agreement and the Tax Treatment Agreement shall
  be published by the Queen's Printer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the Agreement or the Tax Treatment
  Agreement published by the Queen's Printer is evidence of that
  agreement and of its contents, and a copy purporting to be published
  by the Queen's Printer is deemed to be so published, unless the
  contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice of Inuit laws and Inuit
bylaws

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice shall be taken of
  Inuit laws and Inuit bylaws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence of Inuit laws and bylaws

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of an Inuit law or an Inuit bylaw purporting
  to be deposited in a public registry of laws referred to in section
  17.5.1 or 17.5.2 of the Agreement is evidence of that law or bylaw and
  of its contents, unless the contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
Inuit laws and Inuit bylaws are not statutory instruments within the
meaning of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make any orders and regulations that are necessary for the purpose
of carrying out any of the provisions of the Agreement or of the Tax
Treatment Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chapter 22 of Agreement

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsection 5(1),
chapter 22 of the Agreement is deemed to have effect from August 29,
2003.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of issues arising

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, in
  any judicial or administrative proceeding, an issue arises in respect
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the interpretation or validity of the Agreement, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the validity or applicability of this Act, the
    enactment by the Legislature of the Province of Newfoundland and
    Labrador entitled the [Labrador Inuit Land Claims Agreement
    Act](/eng/acts/L-4.3) or any Inuit law or Inuit bylaw,

  the issue shall not be decided until the party raising the issue has
  served notice on the Attorney General of Canada, the Attorney General
  of Newfoundland and Labrador and the Nunatsiavut Government.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} describe the judicial or administrative proceeding
    in which the issue arises;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} state whether the issue arises in respect of the
    matters referred to in paragraph (1)(a) or (b) or both;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state the day on which the issue is to be argued;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} give particulars necessary to show the point to be
    argued; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be served at least 14 days before the day of
    argument, unless the court or tribunal authorizes a shorter period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation in proceedings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In any judicial or administrative proceeding to which
  subsection (1) applies, the Attorney General of Canada, the Attorney
  General of Newfoundland and Labrador and the Nunatsiavut Government
  may appear and participate in the proceeding as parties with the same
  rights as any other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsections (2) and (3) do not
  require that an oral hearing be held if one is not otherwise required.
