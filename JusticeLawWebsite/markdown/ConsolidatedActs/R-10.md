## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Constitution and Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-420861 .Part}

### [Composition of Force]{.HTitleText2} {#h-420862 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Police Force for Canada

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** There shall continue to be
a police force for Canada, which shall consist of officers and other
members and be known as the Royal Canadian Mounted Police.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. R-9, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employment of Force

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Force may be employed
in such places within or outside Canada as the Governor in Council
prescribes.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. R-9, s. 4
:::

### [Commissioner]{.HTitleText2} {#h-420871 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may appoint an officer, to be known as the
  Commissioner of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, to hold office
  during pleasure, who, under the direction of the Minister, has the
  control and management of the Force and all matters connected with the
  Force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may delegate to any member, subject
  to any terms and conditions that the Commissioner directs, any of the
  Commissioner's powers, duties or functions under this Act, except the
  power to delegate under this subsection, the power to make rules under
  this Act and the powers, duties or functions under subsections 45.4(5)
  and 45.41(10).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-10, s. 5
- R.S., 1985, c. 8 (2nd Supp.), s. 2
- 2013, c. 18, ss. 3, 77
:::

### [Officers and Members]{.HTitleText2} {#h-420879 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other officers

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  officers of the Force, in addition to the Commissioner, shall consist
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Deputy Commissioners,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Assistant Commissioners,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Chief Superintendents,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Superintendents,

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Inspectors,

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, R.S., 1985, c. 8 (2nd Supp.), s.
    3\]]{.Repealed}

  and such other ranks as are prescribed by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum number

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The maximum number of officers in each rank shall be
  as prescribed by the Treasury Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Deputy Commissioners

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may appoint any person to the
  rank of Deputy Commissioner to hold office during pleasure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of other officers

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may appoint any person to any other
  rank of officer and, by way of promotion, appoint an officer to a
  higher rank, other than to the rank of Deputy Commissioner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may authorize the issue of a
  commission under the Great Seal to an officer on the officer's first
  appointment to the rank of an officer or on the recommendation of the
  Commissioner.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-10, s. 6
- R.S., 1985, c. 8 (2nd Supp.), ss. 3, 24(E)
- 2013, c. 18, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commanding Officers

- **[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, in respect of each Division of the Force, on
  the recommendation of the Minister, designate an officer to be the
  Division's Commanding Officer to hold office during pleasure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister's recommendation is to be made on the
  recommendation of the Commissioner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of a
  Commanding Officer or if a position of Commanding Officer is vacant,
  the Commissioner may authorize another officer to act as the
  Commanding Officer, but no officer may act as the Commanding Officer
  for more than 180 days without the Governor in Council's approval.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of designation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An officer who holds office as a Commanding Officer
  ceases to hold that office if the officer ceases to be an officer but
  nothing in this subsection precludes the termination of the officer's
  designation for any other reason.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 18, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment and designation

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner may appoint members of the Force other than officers and,
  by way of promotion, appoint a member other than an officer to a
  higher rank, other than to the rank of Deputy Commissioner, or to a
  higher level, for which there is a vacancy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ranks and levels

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The ranks and levels of members other than officers
  and the maximum numbers of persons that may be appointed to each rank
  and level shall be as prescribed by the Treasury Board.

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The term

### [Qualifications]{.HTitleText2} {#h-420929 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications

- **[[9.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), no person
  shall be appointed to be a member unless that person is a Canadian
  citizen, is of good character and has the necessary physical qualities
  and, in the case of a member other than an officer, that person meets
  such other qualifications for appointment to the Force as the
  Commissioner may, by rule, prescribe.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When no person who meets the qualifications described
  in subsection (1) is available for appointment as a member, any person
  who is not a Canadian citizen but meets the other qualifications
  described in that subsection that are applicable to that person may be
  appointed to be a member.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 8 (2nd Supp.), s. 4
:::

### [Revocation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-420937 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of appointment

**[[9.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.2 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner's
power to appoint a person as a member or to appoint a member, by way of
promotion, to a higher rank or level, includes the power to revoke the
appointment and to take corrective action whenever the Commissioner is
satisfied that an error, an omission or improper conduct affected the
selection of the person or member for appointment.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 18, s. 10
:::

### [Probation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-420942 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Probationary period

- **[[9.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person appointed as a member is on probation for a period established
  by rules of the Commissioner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member's probationary period is not terminated by
  any appointment, demotion or transfer from one position to another
  during that period.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 18, s. 10
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discharge

- **[[9.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} While a member is on probation, the
  Commissioner may discharge the member by notifying the member that the
  member will be discharged at the end of the notice period established
  by rules of the Commissioner. The member ceases to be a member at the
  end of that notice period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation in lieu of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Instead of giving the notice referred to in
  subsection (1), the Commissioner may notify the member that the member
  is discharged on the date specified by the Commissioner and that the
  member will be paid an amount equal to the salary the member would
  have been paid during the notice period referred to in subsection (1).
  The member ceases to be a member at that specified date.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 18, s. 10
:::

### [Resignation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-420957 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resignation

**[[9.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.5 .sectionLabel}** A member may resign
from the Force by giving the Commissioner notice in writing of their
intention to resign, and the member ceases to be a member on the date
specified by the Commissioner in writing on accepting the resignation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 18, s. 10
:::

### [Supernumerary Special Constables]{.HTitleText2} {#h-420962 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

- **[[9.6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner may, on the request of any department as defined in
  section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) or if
  he or she considers it necessary or in the public interest, appoint,
  for periods of not more than 12 months, special constables
  supernumerary to the strength of the Force for the purpose of
  maintaining law and order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of appointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may at any time revoke the
  appointment of any supernumerary special constable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No entitlement to pecuniary privileges
  or benefits

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Supernumerary special constables are not entitled to
  any pecuniary privileges or benefits under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 18, s. 10
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment or employment

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The civilian employees
that are necessary for carrying out the functions and duties of the
Force shall be appointed or employed under the [Public Service
Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

::: HistoricalNote

### [Reserve]{.HTitleText2} {#h-420977 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations providing for the
  establishment of a Royal Canadian Mounted Police Reserve, for the
  appointment of persons as reservists and for defining their powers,
  duties and functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of this Act to Reserve

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as provided by the regulations made under
  subsection (1), this Act does not apply to reservists.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Royal Canadian Mounted Police
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 3(3) of the [Royal Canadian
  Mounted Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11), for the purposes
  of that Act, the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36)
  and the [Canadian Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17), a
  reservist is not deemed to be employed in the public service, as
  defined in subsection 3(1) of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-10, s. 11
- 2013, c. 18, s. 11

### [Peace Officers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-420987 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers

- **[[11.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every officer is a peace officer in
  every part of Canada and has all the powers, authority, protection and
  privileges that a peace officer has by law until the officer ceases to
  be an officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of others as peace officers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may designate any member, other than
  an officer, any supernumerary special constable appointed under
  subsection 9.6(1), any person appointed as a reservist under the
  regulations and any other person who is under the Commissioner's
  jurisdiction as a peace officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, authority, etc.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person designated under subsection (2) has the
  same powers, authority, protection and privileges as officers under
  subsection (1) until the person's designation expires or is revoked or
  the designated person ceases to be a member, supernumerary special
  constable or reservist, or a person under the Commissioner's
  jurisdiction, as the case may be.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 18, s. 11
:::

### [Certificates]{.HTitleText2} {#h-420997 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificates

- **[[11.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may issue

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a certificate to any member stating that the person
    to whom it is issued is a member of the Force and, if that person is
    also a peace officer, that the person is a peace officer; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a certificate to any other person under the
    Commissioner's jurisdiction stating that the person to whom it is
    issued is a peace officer, if that person has been designated as
    such under subsection 11.1(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence of appointment or designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any document purporting to be a certif-icate referred
  to in subsection (1) is evidence in all courts and in all proceedings
  of the facts stated in it.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 18, s. 11
:::

### [Suspension]{.HTitleText2} {#h-421007 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Every member who has
contravened, is found contravening or is suspected of contravening any
provision of the Code of Conduct or of an Act of Parliament, or of the
legislature of a province, may be suspended from duty by the
Commissioner.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-10, s. 12
- R.S., 1985, c. 8 (2nd Supp.), s. 6
- 2013, c. 18, s. 11

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Headquarters

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The headquarters of the
Force and the offices of the Commissioner shall be at Ottawa.

### [Oaths]{.HTitleText2} {#h-421018 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oaths

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  member shall, before entering on the duties of the member's office,
  take the oath of allegiance and the oaths set out in the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to administer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The oaths prescribed by subsection (1), and any other
  oath or declaration that may be necessary or required, may be taken by
  the Commissioner before any judge, provincial court judge or justice
  of the peace having jurisdiction in any part of Canada, and by any
  other member before the Commissioner or any officer or person having
  authority to administer oaths or take and receive affidavits.

### [Absence of Commissioner]{.HTitleText2} {#h-421026 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority where Commissioner absent

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  event that the Commissioner is absent or unable to act or the office
  is vacant, the senior Deputy Commissioner at the headquarters of the
  Force has, for the time being, the control and management of the Force
  and all matters connected therewith, and for such purposes the senior
  Deputy Commissioner may exercise all the powers of the Commissioner
  under this Act or any other Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority where Commissioner and Deputy
  Commissioners absent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event that the Commissioner and all the Deputy
  Commissioners are absent or unable to act or the offices are vacant,

### [Boards of Inquiry]{.HTitleText2} {#h-421162 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board of Inquiry

- **[[24.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister or the Commissioner may
  appoint such persons as the Minister or Commissioner considers
  appropriate as a board of inquiry to investigate and report on any
  matter connected with the organization, training, conduct, performance
  of duties, discipline, efficiency, administration or government of the
  Force or affecting any member or other person appointed or employed
  under the authority of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the power to appoint under
  subsection (1) includes the power to appoint all or any of the members
  of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matter to be investigated

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister or the Commissioner appoints a
  board of inquiry under subsection (1), the Minister or Commissioner
  shall specify in writing the matter that the board is to investigate
  and report on.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of board of inquiry

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A board of inquiry has, in relation to the matter
  before it, power

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to summon any person before the board and to
    require that person to give oral or written evidence on oath and to
    produce such documents and things under that person's control as the
    board deems requisite to the full investigation and consideration of
    that matter;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to administer oaths;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to receive and accept on oath or by affidavit such
    evidence and other information as the board sees fit, whether or not
    such evidence or information is or would be admissible in a court of
    law; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to make such examination of records and such
    inquiries as the board deems necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights of persons interested

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any person whose conduct or affairs are being
  investigated by a board of inquiry or who satisfies a board of inquiry
  that the person has a substantial and direct interest in the matter
  before the board shall be afforded a full and ample opportunity, in
  person or by counsel or a representative, to present evidence, to
  cross-examine witnesses and to make representations before the board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation of witnesses

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A board of inquiry shall permit any person who gives
  evidence in the investigation by the board to be represented by
  counsel or a representative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (3), a board of inquiry
  may not receive or accept in an investigation

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (7), any evidence or other
    information that would be inadmissible in a court of law by reason
    of any privilege under the law of evidence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any answer or statement made in response to a
    question described in subsection 35(8), 40(2), 45.1(5) or 45.65(2);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any answer or statement made in response to a
    question described in subsection (7) before any other board of
    inquiry appointed under this section; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any answer or statement made in the course of
    attempting to informally dispose of or resolve a complaint made
    under Part VII or VII.2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witness not excused from testifying

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In an investigation by a board of inquiry, no witness
  shall be excused from answering any question relating to the matter
  before the board when required to do so by the board on the ground
  that the answer to the question may tend to criminate the witness or
  subject the witness to any proceeding or penalty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Answer not receivable

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If the witness is a member, no answer or statement
  made in response to a question described in subsection (7) shall be
  used or receivable against the witness under any proceeding under Part
  IV, other than a proceeding regarding an allegation that with intent
  to mislead the witness gave the answer or statement knowing it to be
  false.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation and hearing in private

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} Unless the Minister or the Commissioner directs
  otherwise, an investigation and any hearing by a board of inquiry
  appointed by the Minister or Commissioner, as the case may be, shall
  be conducted in private.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (9),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} while a child is testifying in an investigation or
    at a hearing by a board of inquiry, the child's parent or guardian
    may be present; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when authorized by a board of inquiry, a member may
    attend a hearing before the board as an observer for the purpose of
    familiarizing the member with procedures under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of documents, etc.

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} Any document or thing produced pursuant to this
  section to a board of inquiry shall, on the request of the person
  producing the document or thing, be released to that person within a
  reasonable time after completion of the board's investigation and
  report.

  Council.

  to continue as a member of the Committee.

### [Presentation of Grievances]{.HTitleText2} {#h-421283 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Member's right

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (1.01) to
  (3), if a member is aggrieved by a decision, act or omission in the
  administration of the affairs of the Force in respect of which no
  other process for redress is provided by this Act, the regulations or
  the Commissioner's standing orders, the member is entitled to present
  the grievance in writing at each of the levels, up to and including
  the final level, in the grievance process provided for by this Part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(1.01)]{.lawlabel} A grievance that relates to the interpretation or
  application, in respect of a member, of a provision of a collective
  agreement or arbitral award must be presented under the [Federal
  Public Sector Labour Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-33.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} A member is not entitled to present a grievance in
  respect of which an administrative procedure for redress is provided
  under any other Act of Parliament, other than one provided for in the
  [Canadian Human Rights Act](/eng/acts/H-6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1.1), a member is not entitled
  to present a grievance in respect of the right to equal pay for work
  of equal value.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(1.3)]{.lawlabel} A member is not entitled to present a grievance
  relating to any action taken under any instruction, direction or
  regulation given or made by or on behalf of the Government of Canada
  in the interest of the safety or security of Canada or any state
  allied or associated with Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order to be conclusive proof

  [(1.4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1.3), an order made
  by the Governor in Council is conclusive proof of the matters stated
  in the order in relation to the giving or making of an instruction,
  direction or regulation by or on behalf of the Government of Canada in
  the interest of the safety or security of Canada or any state allied
  or associated with Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A grievance under this Part must be presented

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the initial level in the grievance process,
    within thirty days after the day on which the aggrieved member knew
    or reasonably ought to have known of the decision, act or omission
    giving rise to the grievance; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the second and any succeeding level in the
    grievance process, within fourteen days after the day the aggrieved
    member is served with the decision of the immediately preceding
    level in respect of the grievance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No appointment by the Commissioner to a position
  prescribed pursuant to subsection (7) may be the subject of a
  grievance under this Part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4.1) and any limitations
  specified under paragraph 36(b), a member presenting a grievance shall
  be granted access to any written or documentary information under the
  Force's control and relevant to the grievance that the member
  reasonably requires to properly present it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to standardized test

  [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} A member is not entitled to have access to a
  standardized test used by the Force, or to information concerning such
  a test, if in the opinion of the Commissioner, its disclosure would
  affect its validity or continued use or would affect the results of
  such a test by giving an unfair advantage to any person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[standardized
  test]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(4.2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[standardized
  test]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} has the meaning assigned by rules
  established by the Commissioner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No penalty for presenting grievance

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} No member shall be disciplined or otherwise penalized
  in relation to employment or any term of employment in the Force for
  exercising the right under this Part to present a grievance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} As soon as feasible after the presentation and
  consideration of a grievance at any level in the grievance process,
  the person constituting the level shall render a decision in writing
  as to the disposition of the grievance, including reasons for the
  decision, and serve the member presenting the grievance and, if the
  grievance has been referred to the Committee under section 33, the
  Committee Chairperson with a copy of the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excluded appointments

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make regulations
  prescribing for the purposes of subsection (3) any position in the
  Force that reports to the Commissioner either directly or through one
  other person.

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (4) but subject to

## [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Conduct]{.HTitleText1} {#h-421406 .Part}

### [Purposes of Part]{.HTitleText2} {#h-421407 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purposes

**[[36.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36.2 .sectionLabel}** The purposes of this
Part are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to establish the responsibilities of members;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to provide for the establishment of a Code of Conduct
  that emphasizes the importance of maintaining the public trust and
  reinforces the high standard of conduct expected of members;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to ensure that members are responsible and
  accountable for the promotion and maintenance of good conduct in the
  Force;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to establish a framework for dealing with
  contraventions of provisions of the Code of Conduct, in a fair and
  consistent manner, at the most appropriate level of the Force; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} to provide, in relation to the contravention of any
  provision of the Code of Conduct, for the imposition of conduct
  measures that are proportionate to the nature and circumstances of the
  contravention and, where appropriate, that are educative and remedial
  rather than punitive.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 18, s. 26
:::

### [Responsibilities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-421417 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibilities

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** It is the responsibility
of every member

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to respect the rights of all persons;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to maintain the integrity of the law, law enforcement
  and the administration of justice;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to perform the member's duties promptly, impartially
  and diligently, in accordance with the law and without abusing the
  member's authority;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to avoid any actual, apparent or potential conflict
  of interests;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} to ensure that any improper or unlawful conduct of
  any member is not concealed or permitted to continue;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} to be incorruptible, never accepting or seeking
  special privilege in the performance of the member's duties or
  otherwise placing the member under any obligation that may prejudice
  the proper performance of the member's duties;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} to act at all times in a courteous, respectful and
  honourable manner; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} to maintain the honour of the Force and its
  principles and purposes.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

- **[[40.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application that has been approved by
  an officer designated by the Commissioner for the purposes of this
  section, a justice may issue a warrant if the justice is satisfied by
  information on oath that there are reasonable grounds to believe that
  there is in any receptacle or place not under the control of the Force
  anything that will afford evidence with respect to the contravention
  of a provision of the Code of Conduct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dwelling-house

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must indicate whether or not the
  place is a dwelling-house.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers under warrant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The warrant may authorize a peace officer, and any
  other individual named in the warrant, to enter and search the place
  and to seize anything specified in the warrant, subject to any
  conditions specified in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Telewarrant provisions to apply

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A warrant may be issued under this section by
  telephone or other means of telecommunication on application submitted
  by a peace officer by one of those means and section 487.1 of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) applies for that purpose with any
  modifications that the circumstances require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Execution of search warrant

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A warrant issued under this section shall be executed
  by day, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the justice is satisfied that there are reasonable
    grounds for it to be executed by night;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reasonable grounds are included in the
    information; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the warrant authorizes that it be executed by
    night.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operation of computer system and
  copying equipment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A person authorized under this section to search a
  computer system in a place for data may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any computer system at the
    place to search any data contained in or available to the computer
    system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reproduce or cause to be reproduced any data in the
    form of a print-out or other intelligible output;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} seize the print-out or other output for examination
    or copying; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any copying equipment at
    the place to make copies of the data.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt and report

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A person who seizes a thing under this section shall
  give a receipt to the person from whom the thing was seized and shall
  as soon as feasible, make a report of the seizure to a justice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return or detention

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If a report of the seizure is made to the justice,
  the justice shall,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the lawful owner or person who is lawfully
    entitled to possession of the thing seized is known, order it to be
    returned to that owner or person, unless the person having custody
    of the thing seized satisfies the justice that the detention of the
    thing seized is required for the purposes of any proceeding under
    this Part; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person having custody of the thing seized
    satisfies the justice that the thing seized should be detained,
    order that it be detained in the Force's care until the conclusion
    of the proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for order of return

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} On application by the person from whom a thing was
  seized and on three clear days notice to the Commissioner, a justice
  may make an order for the release of the thing to the person if the
  justice is satisfied that the thing is no longer necessary for the
  purposes of the investigation or any proceeding under this Part
  arising from the investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage and removal

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} A thing seized under this section may be stored in
  the place where it was seized or it may, at the discretion of a peace
  officer, be removed to any other place for storage.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 18, s. 29

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for making order

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is unreasonable to require the applicant to

### [Conduct Boards]{.HTitleText2} {#h-421566 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  being notified under subsection 41(1) of an alleged contravention of a
  provision of the Code of Conduct by a member, the officer designated
  for the purpose of that subsection shall, subject to the regulations,
  appoint one or more persons as members of a conduct board to decide
  whether the member contravened the provision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} As soon as feasible after making the appointment or
  appointments, the conduct authority who initiated the hearing shall
  serve the member with a notice in writing informing the member that a
  conduct board is to determine whether the member contravened a
  provision of the Code of Conduct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice may allege more than one contravention of
  any provision of the Code of Conduct and is to contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a separate statement of each alleged contravention;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the particulars of the act or
    omission constituting each alleged contravention;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the names of the members of the conduct board; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the member's right to object to the
    appointment of any person as a member of the conduct board as
    provided in section 44.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement of particulars

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The statement of particulars contained in the notice
  is to contain sufficient details, including, if practicable, the place
  and date of each contravention alleged in the notice, to enable the
  member who is served with the notice to identify each contravention in
  order that the member may prepare a response and direct it to the
  occasion and events indicated in the notice.

  following conduct measures on the member, namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} recommendation for dismissal from the Force, if the

    member is not a Deputy Commissioner,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} direction to resign from the Force and, in default

    recommendation for dismissal from the Force, if the member is a
    Deputy Commissioner, or dismissal from the Force, if the member is
    not a Deputy Commissioner, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one or more of the conduct measures provided for in
    the rules.

### [Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-421611 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parties

- **[[45.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The parties to a hearing initiated
  under subsection 41(1) are the conduct authority who initiated it and
  the member whose conduct is the subject of the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing in public

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The hearing shall be held in public but the conduct
  board, on its own initiative or at the request of any party, may order
  that the hearing or any part of it is to be held [in
  camera]{.otherLang lang="la"} if it is of the opinion

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that information, the disclosure of which could
    reasonably be expected to be injurious to the defence of Canada or
    any state allied or associated with Canada or to the detection,
    prevention or suppression of subversive or hostile activities, will
    likely be disclosed during the course of the hearing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that information, the disclosure of which could
    reasonably be expected to be injurious to law enforcement, will
    likely be disclosed during the course of the hearing;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that information respecting a person's financial or
    personal affairs, if that person's interest or security outweighs
    the public's interest in the information, will likely be disclosed
    during the course of the hearing; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that it is otherwise required by the circumstances
    of the case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation of witnesses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The conduct board shall permit any person who gives
  evidence at the hearing to be represented by legal counsel or a
  representative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 45(2), but subject to subsection
  (5), the conduct board is not authorized to receive or accept any
  evidence or other information that would be inadmissible in a court of
  law by reason of any privilege under the law of evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witness not excused from testifying

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In the hearing, no witness shall be excused from
  answering any question relating to the case before the conduct board
  when required to do so by the conduct board on the grounds that the
  answer to the question may tend to criminate the witness or subject
  the witness to any criminal, civil or administrative action or
  proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Answer not receivable

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the witness is a member, no answer or statement
  made in response to a question described in subsection (5) shall be
  used or receivable against the witness under any proceeding under Part
  IV regarding an allegation of a contravention of a provision of the
  Code of Conduct by the witness, other than a proceeding regarding an
  allegation that with intent to mislead the witness gave the answer or
  statement knowing it to be false.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order restricting publication

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The conduct board may, on its own initiative or at
  the request of any person, make an order directing that any of the
  following information shall not be published in any document or
  broadcast or transmitted in any way:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} information that could identify a complainant, a
    witness or a person under the age of 18; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information disclosed during any part of the
    hearing held [in camera]{.otherLang lang="la"}.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence of member

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The conduct board may conduct the hearing in the
  absence of the member whose conduct is the subject of the hearing in
  the circumstances set out in the rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Medical examination

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} If the member whose conduct is the subject of the
  hearing indicates that they are unable to attend the hearing for
  medical reasons, the conduct board may direct the member to undergo a
  medical examination or an assessment by a qualified person specified
  by the conduct board to determine if the member is unable to
  participate in the hearing for medical reasons. If the member fails to
  undergo the medical examination or assessment, the conduct board may
  conduct the hearing in the absence of the member.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 8 (2nd Supp.), s. 16
- 2013, c. 18, s. 29

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal of appeal against conduct
board's finding

- **[[45.16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45.16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may dispose of an
  appeal in respect of a conduct board's finding by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dismissing the appeal and confirming the finding
    being appealed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} allowing the appeal and either ordering a new
    hearing into the allegation giving rise to the finding or making the
    finding that, in the Commissioner's opinion, the conduct board
    should have made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal of appeal against conduct
  authority's finding

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may dispose of an appeal in respect
  of a conduct authority's finding by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dismissing the appeal and confirming the finding
    being appealed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} allowing the appeal and making the finding that, in
    the Commissioner's opinion, the conduct authority should have made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal of appeal against conduct
  measure

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may dispose of an appeal in respect
  of a conduct measure imposed by a conduct board or a conduct authority
  by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dismissing the appeal and confirming the conduct
    measure; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} allowing the appeal and either rescinding the
    conduct measure or, subject to subsection (4) or (5), imposing
    another conduct measure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the appeal is in respect of a conduct measure
  imposed by a conduct authority, the Commissioner may only impose under
  paragraph (3)(b) a conduct measure that is provided for in the rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the appeal is in respect of a conduct measure
  imposed by a conduct board, the Commissioner may impose any conduct
  measure under paragraph (3)(b) that the conduct board could have
  imposed, including the power to recommend dismissal from the Force, if
  the member is a Deputy Commissioner, or dismissal from the Force, if
  the member is not a Deputy Commissioner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New hearing

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the Commissioner orders a new hearing into an
  allegation under subsection (1), a conduct board shall be appointed in
  accordance with this Part to conduct the hearing and the new hearing
  shall be held in accordance with this Part as if it were the first
  hearing into that allegation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall as soon as feasible render a
  decision in writing on an appeal, including reasons for the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committee's or Committee Chairperson's
  report

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If a case has been referred to the Committee under
  section 45.15, the Commissioner shall take into consideration the
  findings or recommendations set out in the report of the Committee or
  the Committee Chairperson in respect of the case, but the Commissioner
  is not bound to act on any findings or recommendations set out in the
  report. However, if the Commissioner does not so act, the Commissioner
  shall include in the decision on the appeal the reasons for not so
  acting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissioner's decision final

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} A Commissioner's decision on an appeal is final and
  binding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rescission or amendment of decision

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (9), the Commissioner may rescind
  or amend the Commissioner's decision on an appeal under section 45.11
  on the presentation to the Commissioner of new facts or if, with
  respect to the finding of any fact or the interpretation of any law,
  the Commissioner determines that an error was made in reaching the
  decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may delegate any of the
  Commissioner's powers, duties or functions under this section to any
  person under the Commissioner's jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sub-delegation

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} A person to whom powers, duties or functions are
  delegated under subsection (11) may not sub-delegate any of them.

## [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Management Advisory Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-421724 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[45.18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45.18
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Management Advisory Board is
  established.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The mandate of the Management Advisory Board is to
  provide the Commissioner --- on its own initiative or at the
  Commissioner's request --- with advice, information and reports on the
  administration and management of the Force, including with respect to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the development and implementation of
    transformation and modernization plans;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the effective and efficient use of resources;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the actions to be taken to reduce corporate risks;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the development and implementation of policies and
    management controls that support the operation of the Force;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the development and implementation of corporate and
    strategic plans; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the development and implementation of operating and
    capital budgets.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} In carrying out its mandate, the Management
  Advisory Board shall consider the impact of its advice on women, men
  and gender-diverse people by taking into account the intersection of
  sex and gender with other identity factors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy or summary to Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Management Advisory Board may provide the
  Minister with a copy or a summary of any advice, information or report
  that it provides to the Commissioner.

  consist of not more than 13 members to be appointed by the Governor in
  Council on the recommendation of the Minister.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before making a recommendation to the Governor in

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} When recommending members, the Minister shall
  consider the importance of having a Management Advisory Board that is
  representative of the diversity of Canadian society and that is
  comprised of members who have the experience and the capacity required
  to carry out the Board's mandate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The members are to be appointed to hold office on a
  part-time basis during pleasure for a renewable term of not more than
  four years that will ensure, as far as possible, the expiry in any one
  year of the terms of office of not more than one half of the members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson and Vice-chairperson

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council shall designate, from among
  the members of the Management Advisory Board, one person to be the
  Chairperson and another person to be the Vice-chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence of Chairperson

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the Chairperson is absent or unable to act or if
  the office of Chairperson is vacant, the Vice-chairperson is to act as
  Chairperson, but he or she is not entitled to act for a period of more
  than 90 days without the Governor in Council's approval.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence of Chairperson and
  Vice-chairperson

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the Chairperson and the Vice-chairperson are
  absent or unable to act or if those offices are vacant, the Minister
  may designate a member of the Management Advisory Board to act as
  Chairperson, but no member so designated is entitled to act for a
  period of more than 90 days without the Governor in Council's
  approval.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security clearance

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Every member of the Management Advisory Board shall
  obtain and maintain the necessary security clearance from the
  Government of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligibility

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} A person is not eligible to be appointed or to
  continue as a member of the Management Advisory Board if the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a member or other person appointed or employed
    under the authority of Part I;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not a Canadian citizen or a [[permanent
    resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of
    the [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is a [[public office holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
    as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Conflict of Interest
    Act](/eng/acts/C-36.65), unless the person is a public office holder
    only by virtue of their appointment as a member of the Management
    Advisory Board;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is employed on a full-time basis in the federal
    public administration or by a provincial or municipal authority; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} is a member of the Senate, the House of Commons,
    the legislature of a province or a municipal council or is on the
    staff of such a member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Management Advisory Board are to

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Travel and living expenses

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Management Advisory Board are

Definition of [[privileged information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[45.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45.4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section and sections 45.41 to 45.48, [[privileged
  information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means information that is subject to
  any type of privilege that exists and may be claimed, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} information that is protected by the privilege that
    exists between legal counsel and their client or that is subject to
    informer privilege;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information the disclosure of which is described in
    subsection 11(1) of the [Witness Protection Program
    Act](/eng/acts/W-11.2);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2013, c. 29, s. 23\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} special operational information as defined in
    subsection 8(1) of the [Foreign Interference and Security of
    Information Act](/eng/acts/O-5);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} information or intelligence that is similar in
    nature to information or intelligence referred to in any of
    paragraphs (a) to (f) of the definition [[special operational
    information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 8(1) of the [Foreign
    Interference and Security of Information Act](/eng/acts/O-5) and
    that is in relation to, or is received from, any police force or
    Interpol or other similar international police organization; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} medical information about a member or other person
    appointed or employed under the authority of Part I.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to privileged information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite any privilege that exists and may be claimed,
  the Commission is entitled to have access to privileged information
  under the control, or in the possession, of the Force if that
  information is relevant and necessary to the matter before the
  Commission when it is conducting a review under section 45.34 or 45.35
  or is conducting an investigation, review or hearing under Part VII.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to records

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The entitlement to access includes the right to
  examine all or any part of a record and, subject to the Commissioner's
  approval, to be given a copy of all or any part of a record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal and reasons

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Commissioner refuses access to privileged
  information sought by the Commission under this section, the
  Commissioner shall, without disclosing the privileged information,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicate to the Commission why the privileged
    information is not relevant or necessary to the matter before the
    Commission; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the Commission with information about the
    nature and date of the privileged information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Memorandum of understanding

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson and the Commissioner may enter into a
  memorandum of understanding setting out principles and procedures
  respecting access to privileged information under this section and
  principles and procedures to protect that information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make regulations
  respecting procedures that govern the Commission's access to
  privileged information under this section and procedures to protect
  that information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Except as provided by any other Act of Parliament
  that expressly refers to this section, this section, or any regulation
  made under subsection (6), applies despite any other Act of
  Parliament.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Former judge or other individual

- **[[45.41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45.41
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the Commissioner refuses access
  to privileged information sought by the Commission under subsection
  45.4(2), the Minister shall, at the request of the Commission, appoint
  a former judge of a superior court of a province or the Federal Court
  or an individual who is a member of a prescribed category of
  individuals to review the information and make observations to the
  Commission and the Commissioner. In order to be appointed, the former
  judge or other individual shall obtain a security clearance from the
  Government of Canada and shall take the oath of secrecy referred to in
  paragraph 45.45(1)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of appointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall provide notice to the Chairperson
  and the Commissioner when a former judge or other individual has been
  appointed in accordance with subsection (1). The Chairperson and the
  Commissioner shall make their representations to the former judge or
  other individual within 30 days after the day on which the notice is
  sent or within any longer period, not exceeding 60 days, that the
  former judge or other individual may permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Former judge or other individual to
  have access

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The former judge or other individual shall have
  access to privileged information for the purposes of the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Observations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The former judge or other individual shall review the
  privileged information and provide his or her observations to the
  Chairperson and the Commissioner

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} regarding the privileged nature of the information;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} regarding the relevance and necessity of the
    information to the matter before the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The former judge or other individual shall not
  include information that reveals privileged information or from which
  it may be inferred in the observations provided under subsection (4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to consider

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The former judge or other individual shall, before
  making any observations, consider the following factors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for which the Commission is seeking
    access to the information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Commissioner's reasons for refusing access to
    the information; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the Commission can properly exercise its
    powers or perform its duties and functions without access to the
    information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The observations of the former judge or other
  individual shall be made within 30 days after the day on which the
  period referred to in subsection (2) expires or within any longer
  period, not exceeding 60 days, that the Minister permits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The observations of the former judge or other
  individual are confidential and shall not be disclosed by the judge or
  other individual, the Commission or the Force, except to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity and no summons

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} Section 45.5 applies to the former judge or other
  individual as if he or she were a member of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Observations to be taken into account

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} After receiving the observations of the former judge
  or other individual, the Chairperson shall review the Commission's
  decision to seek access and the Commissioner shall review his or her
  decision to refuse access, taking those observations into account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} An application for judicial review shall not be made
  in connection with the Commission's decision to seek access to
  privileged information, or the Commissioner's refusal to allow access
  to privileged information, until the former judge or other individual
  has made his or her observations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by regulation,
  prescribe categories of individuals for the purposes of subsection
  (1).

## [PART VII]{.HLabel1}[Investigation, Review and Hearing of Complaints]{.HTitleText1} {#h-422021 .Part}

### [Complaints]{.HTitleText2} {#h-422022 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaints

- **[[45.53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45.53
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any individual may make a complaint
  concerning the conduct, in the performance of any duty or function
  under this Act or the [Witness Protection Program
  Act](/eng/acts/W-11.2), of any person who, at the time that the
  conduct is alleged to have occurred, was a member or other person
  appointed or employed under Part I.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission's discretion

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may refuse to deal with the complaint
  if, in the Commission's opinion, the complaint

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has been adequately dealt with, or could more
    appropriately be dealt with, according to a procedure provided for
    under this Act or any other Act of Parliament;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is trivial, frivolous, vexatious or made in bad
    faith; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is from an individual who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is not an individual at whom the conduct was
      directed,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is not the guardian, tutor, curator, mandatary
      in case of incapacity or any other person authorized to act on
      behalf of the individual at whom the conduct was directed,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} did not see or hear the conduct or its effects
      as a result of not being physically present at the time and place
      that the conduct or its effects occurred,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} has not been given written permission to make
      the complaint from the individual at whom the conduct was
      directed, or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} has not suffered loss, damage, distress, danger
      or inconvenience as a result of the conduct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaints involving decisions made
  under Part IV

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall refuse to deal with a complaint
  concerning any decision under Part IV.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaint by members or certain other
  persons

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall refuse to deal with a complaint
  made under subsection (1) by a member or other person appointed or
  employed under Part I if the complaint has been or could have been
  adequately dealt with, or could more appropriately be dealt with,
  according to a procedure provided for under this Act or any other Act
  of Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National security

  [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall refuse to deal with a
  complaint concerning an activity that is closely related to national
  security and shall refer such a complaint to the National Security and
  Intelligence Review Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of referral

  [(4.2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall notify the Commissioner if it
  refers a complaint to the National Security and Intelligence Review
  Agency. After doing so, it shall also notify the complainant of the
  referral.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The complaint shall be made within one year after the
  day on which the conduct is alleged to have occurred or any longer
  period permitted under subsection (6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of time limit

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Commission or the Commissioner may extend the
  time limit for making a complaint if the Commission or the
  Commissioner, as the case may be, is of the opinion that there are
  good reasons for doing so and that it is not contrary to the public
  interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If a complaint is made more than one year after the
  day on which the conduct is alleged to have occurred and the
  Commissioner does not extend the time limit for the making of the
  complaint, the Commissioner shall so notify the complainant and the
  Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reception of complaint

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} A complaint shall be made to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Commission;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any member or other person appointed or employed
    under Part I; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the provincial authority that is responsible for
    the receipt of complaints against police in the province in which
    the subject matter of the complaint arose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall, on the request of an individual
  who wishes to make a complaint, arrange for the provision of
  assistance to that individual in making the complaint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgement and notification

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} As soon as feasible after a person or entity
  referred to in subsection (8) receives a complaint, the person or
  entity shall acknowledge the complaint in writing to the complainant
  and shall provide written notice of the complaint to the Commissioner
  and to the entities referred to in paragraphs (8)(a) and (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Covert operations

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} The Commission and the Force are authorized to
  acknowledge a complaint or otherwise deal with a complainant in a
  manner that does not reveal, or from which may not be inferred,
  information concerning

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether a place, person, agency, group, body or
    other entity was, is or is intended to be the object of a covert
    investigation or a covert collection of information or intelligence;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the identity of any person who is, has been or is
    intended to be engaged in a covert collection of information or
    intelligence.

### [Withdrawal of Complaints]{.HTitleText2} {#h-422065 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal

- **[[45.55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45.55
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A complainant may withdraw a
  complaint at any time by sending a written notice to the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall, on the request of an individual
  who wishes to withdraw a complaint, arrange for the provision of
  assistance to that individual in withdrawing the complaint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of withdrawal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} As soon as feasible after the Commission receives a
  notice that a complaint has been withdrawn, the Commission shall give
  written notice of the withdrawal to the Commissioner and the

  complaint arose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to member or other person

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} When the Commissioner receives a notice under
  subsection (3), he or she shall notify in writing the member or other
  person whose conduct is the subject matter of the complaint that the
  complaint has been withdrawn.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation or hearing into withdrawn
  complaint

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite the withdrawal of the complaint, the
  complaint may be the subject of an investigation, review or hearing
  conducted under this Part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preservation of evidence

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall ensure the protection and
  preservation of any evidence relating to a withdrawn complaint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make regulations
  respecting the period during which the evidence is to be protected and
  preserved.

### [Informal Resolution]{.HTitleText2} {#h-422083 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informal resolution

- **[[45.56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45.56
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As soon as feasible after being
  notified of a complaint, the Commissioner shall consider whether the
  complaint can be resolved informally and, with the consent of the
  complainant and the member or other person whose conduct is the
  subject matter of the complaint, may attempt to resolve it informally.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inadmissibility

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An answer or statement made in the course of
  attempting to resolve a complaint informally, by the complainant or
  the member or other person whose conduct is the subject matter of the
  complaint, may be used or received against that person only in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a prosecution under section 132 or 136 of the
    [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a civil or administrative proceeding regarding an
    allegation that with intent to mislead the witness gave the answer
    or statement knowing it to be false.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement to informal resolution in
  writing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The terms of every informal resolution of a complaint
  as well as the agreement of the complainant and the member or other
  person whose conduct is the subject matter of the complaint to those
  terms shall be signified in writing. A copy of everything so signified
  in writing is to be provided to the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make regulations
  prescribing the categories of complaints that are not to be resolved
  informally by the Commissioner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, nothing in this section
  prevents the Commission from informally resolving a complaint of which
  it is seized.

### [Powers of the Commission in Relation to Complaints]{.HTitleText2} {#h-422168 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

- **[[45.65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45.65
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may, in relation to a
  complaint before it,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the same manner and to the same extent as a
    superior court of record, summon and enforce the attendance of
    witnesses before the Commission and compel them to give oral or
    written evidence on oath and to produce any documents and things
    that the Commission considers relevant for the full investigation,
    hearing and consideration of the complaint;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} administer oaths;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} receive and accept any evidence and other
    information, whether on oath or by affidavit or otherwise, that the
    Commission sees fit, whether or not that evidence or information is
    or would be admissible in a court of law; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} make any examination of records and any inquiries
    that the Commission considers necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No excuse

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No witness shall be excused from answering any
  question or producing any document or thing, when compelled to do so
  by the Commission, on the grounds that the answer or statement made in
  response to the question, or the document or thing given by the
  witness, may tend to criminate him or her or subject him or her to any
  criminal, civil or administrative action or proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inadmissibility

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Evidence given, or a document or thing produced, by a
  witness who is compelled by the Commission to give or produce it, and
  any evidence derived from it, may be used or received against the
  witness only in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a prosecution under section 132 or 136 of the
    [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a civil or administrative proceeding in respect of
    an allegation that, with intent to mislead, the witness gave the
    answer or statement knowing it to be false.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Commission shall not
  receive or accept

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any answer or statement made in response to a
    question described in subsection 24.1(7), 35(8), 40(2) or 45.1(5);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any answer or statement made in response to a
    question described in subsection (2) in any investigation or hearing
    with respect to any other complaint; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any answer or statement made in the course of
    attempting to dispose of a complaint under section 45.56.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(a), the Commission shall not
  enforce the production of written evidence or any document or thing to
  which the Commission has a right of access under subsection 45.4(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witness fees

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Any witness, other than a member, who is summoned is
  entitled, at the discretion of the Commission, to receive the same
  fees and allowances as those paid to witnesses summoned to attend
  before the Federal Court.

### [Hearings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-422253 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing

- **[[45.73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45.73
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the Commission decides, under
  section 45.66 or paragraph 45.71(3)(c), to institute a hearing to
  inquire into a complaint, the Chairperson shall assign one or more
  members of the Commission to conduct the hearing and shall send a
  notice in writing of the decision to the Minister, the Commissioner,
  the complainant and the member or other person whose conduct is the
  subject matter of the complaint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the member or
  members of the Commission who are conducting the hearing are deemed to
  be the Commission.

- Meaning of [[parties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[parties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means the officer designated by the Commissioner for the purposes of
  this Part, the member or other person whose conduct is the subject
  matter of the complaint and the complainant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall serve a notice in writing of the
  time and place set for the hearing on the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sittings of Commission

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may sit at any place in Canada and at
  any time that may be fixed by the Commission, taking into account the
  convenience of the parties who wish to appear before the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearings in public

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A hearing to inquire into a complaint shall be held
  in public but the Commission, on its own initiative or at the request
  of any party or witness, may order a hearing or any part of a hearing
  to be held [in camera]{.otherLang lang="la"} or [ex parte]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} if it is of the opinion

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that information that could reasonably be expected
    to be injurious to the defence of Canada or any state allied or
    associated with Canada or the detection, prevention or suppression
    of subversive or hostile activities will likely be disclosed during
    the course of the hearing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that information that could reasonably be expected
    to be injurious to law enforcement will likely be disclosed during
    the course of the hearing;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that information respecting a person's financial or
    personal affairs, if that person's interest or security outweighs
    the public's interest in the information, will likely be disclosed
    during the course of the hearing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that information that could reasonably be expected
    to reveal privileged information, as defined in subsection 45.4(1),
    will likely be disclosed during the course of the hearing; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} that it is otherwise required by the circumstances
    of the case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights of persons interested

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The parties and any other person who satisfies the
  Commission that the person has a substantial and direct interest in a
  complaint before the Commission shall be allowed an opportunity, in
  person or by legal counsel, to present evidence, cross-examine
  witnesses and make representations at the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation of witnesses

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall permit any person who gives
  evidence at a hearing to be represented by legal counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated officer

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The officer designated by the Commissioner for the
  purposes of this Part may be represented or assisted at a hearing by
  any other person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privilege

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} If the officer referred to in subsection (9) is
  represented or assisted by another person, communications passing in
  confidence between them in relation to the hearing are, for the
  purposes of this Act, privileged as if they were communications
  passing in professional confidence between the officer and their legal
  counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} If the Commission sits at a place in Canada that is
  not the ordinary place of residence of the complainant, of the member
  or other person whose conduct is the subject matter of the complaint
  or of the legal counsel of any of those persons, then that person or
  their legal counsel is entitled, at the discretion of the Commission,
  to receive, in accordance with Treasury Board directives, the travel
  and living expenses incurred by that person or their legal counsel in
  appearing before the Commission.

- 2013, c. 18, ss. 35, 77

## [PART VII.1]{.HLabel1}[Serious Incidents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-422319 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[45.79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45.79
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in
  this Part.

  [[designated authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to a
      province, means the person, body or authority that is designated
      by the lieutenant governor in council of that province under
      subsection (2). ([autorité désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[investigative body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[investigative body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a provincial
      entity, other than a police force, whose authority includes the
      power to investigate a serious incident for the purpose of
      determining whether an offence under federal or provincial law has
      occurred. ([organisme d'enquête]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[serious incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[serious incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an incident in
      which the actions of a member or other person appointed or
      employed under Part I or any person assisting the Force in
      exercising its powers or performing its duties and functions under
      this Act

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} may have resulted in serious injury to, or the
        death of, any person; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may have constituted an offence under federal
        or provincial law that any of the following persons decides
        would be in the public interest to be investigated by an
        investigative body or by a police force other than the Force:

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Minister,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the provincial minister who has the primary
          responsibility for policing in the province in which the
          incident is alleged to have occurred if there is an
          arrangement between the government of that province and the
          Minister under section 20, or

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Commissioner. ([incident
          grave]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[serious injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[serious injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prescribed physical
      or psychological injury. ([blessure grave]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The lieutenant governor in council of a province may
  designate any person, body or authority as the designated authority
  for that province for the purposes of this Part and Part VII.2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by regulation, prescribe
  physical or psychological injuries for the purposes of the definition
  [[serious injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 18, ss. 35, 81
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification

**[[45.8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45.8 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner
shall, as soon as feasible, notify the designated authority for a
province of a serious incident that is alleged to have occurred in that
province.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 18, s. 35
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to consider investigative body

- **[[45.81]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45.81
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If there is an investigative body in
  the province in which the serious incident is alleged to have
  occurred, the designated authority for that province shall first
  consider appointing that investigative body to investigate the serious
  incident.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of police force

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If there is no investigative body or, after
  consideration, the designated authority does not appoint one, the
  designated authority may appoint a police force to investigate the
  serious incident.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Referral by the Force

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the designated authority appoints an investigative
  body or police force to investigate the serious incident, the Force
  shall, as soon as feasible, refer the investigation of the serious
  incident to that investigative body or police force.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 18, s. 35
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request to police force

- **[[45.82]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45.82
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If there is no designated authority
  for a province or the designated authority for a province notifies the
  Force that no investigative body or police force will be appointed to
  investigate the serious incident, the Force shall, as soon as
  feasible, request an investigative body or a police force to
  investigate it having taken into account the available expertise and
  resources of that investigative body or police force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation by the Force

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the investigative body, or the police force that
  receives the request, notifies the Force that it will not investigate
  the serious incident and the Force does not consider any other
  investigative body or police force to be appropriate to receive such a
  request, the Force shall, as soon as feasible,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the Commission that it will investigate the
    serious incident; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} investigate the serious incident.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasonable efforts

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Force shall make reasonable efforts under this
  section to identify an investigative body or police force to
  investigate the serious incident and shall keep a written record of
  the efforts made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall provide the Chairperson with a
  report outlining the efforts made by the Force under subsection (3).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2013, c. 18, s. 35

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Observer --- investigation by another
police force

- **[[45.83]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45.83
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a police force is appointed under
  subsection 45.81(2) --- or accepts, following a request made under
  subsection 45.82(1) --- to investigate a serious incident and no
  observer is appointed by a designated authority,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Commissioner shall, as soon as feasible, notify
    the Commission of the serious incident; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Commission may, with the agreement of the
    provincial minister who has the primary responsibility for policing
    in the province in which the incident is alleged to have occurred,
    appoint an observer to assess the impartiality of the investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Observer --- Force

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Force investigates a serious incident, the
  Force shall permit an observer appointed by a designated authority or
  by the Commission under subsection (3) to assess the impartiality of
  the investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of observer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Force investigates a serious incident and no
  observer is appointed by a designated authority, the Commission may
  appoint an observer to assess the impartiality of the investigation
  conducted by the Force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No observer appointed

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If no observer is appointed to an investigation of a
  serious incident under subsection (2) or (3), the Commissioner shall
  provide the Chairperson with a report that sets out all measures that
  have been or will be taken by the Force to ensure the impartiality of
  the investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An observer appointed by a designated authority for

### [Offences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-422598 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bribes, etc.

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, R.S., 1985, c. 8 (2nd Supp.), s.
    19\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} makes any agreement with any member to induce the
    member in any way to forego the member's duty, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} concerts or connives at any act whereby any rule,
    order or regulation made under Part I may be evaded,

  is guilty of an offence punishable on summary conviction.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, R.S., 1985, c. 8 (2nd Supp.), s.
  19\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-10, s. 48
- R.S., 1985, c. 8 (2nd Supp.), ss. 19, 24(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unlawful use of name of Force

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person is guilty of an offence punishable on summary conviction who,
  without the authority of the Commissioner, uses

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the Force or any abbreviation thereof
    or any words or letters likely to be mistaken therefor,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any picture or other representation of a member of
    the Force, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any mark, badge or insignia of the Force,

  as all or any part of the name of any corporation, company,
  partnership or unincorporated association, in any advertising, for any
  business or trade purpose, or in such a way as to represent or imply
  that the Force uses or approves or endorses the use of any goods or
  services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personation of former member

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person not being a former member who, without
  the authority of the Commissioner, uses any clothing, equipment,
  badge, medal, ribbon, document or other thing in such a manner as to
  lead to a reasonable belief that the person was a member of the Force
  is guilty of an offence punishable on summary conviction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent to prosecution

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No proceedings in respect of an offence under this
  section shall be instituted without the consent of the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-10, s. 49
- R.S., 1985, c. 8 (2nd Supp.), s. 20
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attendance of witnesses, etc.

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person commits an offence punishable on summary conviction who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on being duly summoned as a witness or otherwise
    under this Act, makes default in attending;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} being in attendance as a witness in any proceeding
    under this Act,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} refuses to take an oath or solemn affirmation
      required of that person,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} refuses to produce any document or thing under
      that person's control or in that person's possession and required
      to be produced by that person, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} refuses to answer any question;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at any proceeding under this Act uses insulting or
    threatening language or causes any interference or disturbance;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} without lawful justification or excuse, prints
    observations or uses words in relation to an ongoing criminal, civil
    or administrative action or proceeding with intent

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to injure the reputation of a member of a board
      of inquiry under Part I, the Committee under Part III, IV or V, an
      adjudication board under Part IV, a discharge and demotion board
      under Part V or the Commission under Part VII or VII.2 or a
      witness before any of those entities by exposing that member or
      witness to contempt, insult or ridicule, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to dissuade a witness in any proceedings before
      an entity referred to in subparagraph (i) from testifying; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} fails to comply with an order made under subsection
    45.1(7).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(a) and subparagraphs (1)(b)(ii)
  and (iii) do not apply to a designated officer as defined in
  subsection 45.88(1) who was appointed under subsection 8(1) of the
  [Integrated Cross-border Law Enforcement Operations
  Act](/eng/acts/I-14.7).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is convicted of an offence under
  subsection (1) is liable to a fine of not more than \$5,000 or to
  imprisonment for a term of not more than six months or to both.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-10, s. 50
- R.S., 1985, c. 8 (2nd Supp.), s. 21
- 2013, c. 18, ss. 40, 77, 78, 83, 84

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences --- harassment, obstruction,
destroying documents etc.

- **[[50.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} harass, intimidate or threaten any person with the
    intent to compel that other person to abstain from making a
    complaint under Part VII or VII.2;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} harass, intimidate or threaten

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an individual who makes a complaint under Part
      VII or VII.2,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an individual at whom the conduct that is the
      subject of a complaint made under any of those Parts was directed,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a person whom the person has reasonable grounds
      to believe will be questioned or summoned by the Commission when
      it deals with a complaint made under any of those Parts, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a person who is carrying out any power, duty or
      function under any of Parts VI to VII.2;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} wilfully obstruct a person who is carrying out any
    power, duty or function under any of Parts VI to VII.2, or knowingly
    make any false or misleading statement or knowingly provide false or
    misleading information to that person;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} destroy, mutilate, alter, falsify or conceal a
    document or thing, or make a false document or thing, knowing that
    the document or thing is likely to be relevant to an investigation
    of, or hearing to inquire into, a complaint made under Part VII or
    VII.2 or to a review under any of those Parts; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} direct, counsel or cause, in any manner, any person
    to do anything mentioned in any of paragraphs (a) to (d), or
    propose, in any manner, to any person that they do anything
    mentioned in any of those paragraphs.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an offence punishable on summary conviction and
    liable to a fine of not more than \$5,000 or to imprisonment for a
    term of not more than six months, or to both.
