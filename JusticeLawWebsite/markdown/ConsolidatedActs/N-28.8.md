## Nunavut Waters and Nunavut Surface Rights Tribunal Act {#nunavut-waters-and-nunavut-surface-rights-tribunal-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2002, c. 10
:::

Assented to 2002-04-30

An Act respecting the water resources of Nunavut and the Nunavut Surface
Rights Tribunal and to make consequential amendments to other Acts
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada and the Inuit of the
Nunavut Settlement Area have entered into a land claims agreement that
was ratified by Her Majesty when it was signed on Her behalf and when
the [Nunavut Land Claims Agreement Act](/eng/acts/N-28.7) came into
force and by the Inuit when it was signed on their behalf following a
ratification vote;

WHEREAS the agreement came into force on July 9, 1993 on its
ratification by both parties;

AND WHEREAS the Government of Canada has undertaken in the agreement to
establish the Nunavut Water Board and the Nunavut Surface Rights
Tribunal as institutions of public government and to set out by statute
all of their substantive powers, functions, objectives and duties;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-372321 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Nunavut Waters and Nunavut Surface Rights Tribunal
Act](/eng/acts/N-28.8).

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Nunavut Waters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-372353 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-372354 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Part.

[[appurtenant undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appurtenant undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an undertaking
    in relation to which a use of waters or a deposit of waste is
    permitted by a licence. ([entreprise principale]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Nunavut Water Board
    established by section 14. ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[domestic purpose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[domestic purpose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the use of waters for
    the following purposes:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} household requirements, including sanitation and
      fire prevention;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the watering of domestic animals; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the irrigation of a garden that adjoins a
      dwelling-house and is not ordinarily used in the growth of produce
      for market. ([domestique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[instream use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[instream use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a use of waters by a
    person, other than for a domestic purpose or as described in
    paragraph (a), (b) or (c) of the definition
    [[use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, to earn income or for subsistence
    purposes. ([ordinaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, unless the context otherwise
    requires, a type A or type B licence, in accordance with the
    criteria prescribed by the regulations, issued for the use of waters
    or the deposit of waste, or both, in Nunavut under section
    42. ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person to whom a licence is
    issued or assigned. ([titulaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[marine area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marine area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any waters, including
    those that are ice-covered, of the Nunavut Settlement Area, other
    than inland waters, and the seabed and subsoil below those
    waters. ([zones marines]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[national park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[national park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a park within the
    meaning of the [Canada National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01), or
    lands set aside as a reserve for a park under that Act. ([parc
    national]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Nunavut Impact Review Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Nunavut Impact Review Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Nunavut Impact Review Board referred to in section 18 of the
    [Nunavut Planning and Project Assessment
    Act](/eng/acts/N-28.75). ([Commission d'examen des projets de
    développement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Nunavut Planning Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Nunavut Planning Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Nunavut Planning Commission referred to in section 10 of the
    [Nunavut Planning and Project Assessment
    Act](/eng/acts/N-28.75). ([Commission
    d'aménagement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[penalty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[penalty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an administrative monetary
    penalty imposed for a violation. ([pénalité]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to waters, means a direct
    or indirect use of any kind, including, but not limited to,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any use of water power and geothermal resources;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any diversion or obstruction of waters;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any alteration of the flow of waters; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any alteration of the bed or banks of a river,
      stream, lake or other body of water, whether or not the body of
      water is seasonal.

    However, it does not include navigation or any other use connected
    with shipping activities that are governed by the [Canada Shipping
    Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15). ([utilisation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any substance that, by itself or
    in combination with other substances found in water, would have the
    effect of altering the quality of any water to which the substance
    is added to an extent that is detrimental to its use by people or by
    any animal, fish or plant, or any water that would have that effect
    because of the quantity or concentration of the substances contained
    in it or because it has been treated or changed, by heat or other
    means, and includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any substance or water that, for the purposes of
      the [Canada Water Act](/eng/acts/C-11), is deemed to be waste;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any substance or class of substances specified by
      the regulations;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} water containing any substance or class of
      substances in a quantity or concentration that is equal to or
      greater than that prescribed by the regulations; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} water that has been subjected to a treatment or
      change described by the regulations. ([déchet]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means inland waters, whether in a
    liquid or solid state, on or below the surface of
    land. ([eaux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Scope and Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-372385 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other Acts

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Part, the
regulations or a licence authorizes a person to contravene any other Act
of Parliament or a regulation or order made under any other Act of
Parliament.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights preserved

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Part, the
regulations or a licence constitutes a defence to a claim for loss or
damage sustained by any person by reason of the construction or
operation of any work forming part of an appurtenant undertaking.

### [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText2} {#h-372390 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** This Part is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province, except that Her Majesty in
right of Canada is not required to pay any fee prescribed by the
regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waters vested in Her Majesty

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to any rights granted by or under any other Act of Parliament in
  respect of waters in Nunavut, the property in and the right to the use
  of all waters in Nunavut are vested in Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights of designated Inuit organization

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the designated Inuit
  organization has, in respect of waters in Nunavut, the rights that are
  provided in the Agreement, including the exclusive right to the use of
  water on, in, or flowing through Inuit-owned land and the right to
  have water flow through that land substantially unaffected in quality,
  quantity and flow.

### [Delegation and Agreements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-372398 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation to territorial minister

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, in
writing, delegate to the territorial minister responsible for water
resources any of the Minister's functions under sections 14, 16, 17, 19
and 21, subsection 55(5), section 56, subsection 77(1) and section 84,
either generally or as otherwise provided in the instrument of
delegation, except that the delegation cannot abrogate or derogate from
any rights of Inuit under the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements with provinces

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister and the
territorial minister responsible for water resources shall, with the
assistance of the Board, use their best efforts to negotiate an
agreement, subject to any agreement entered into under section 5 or 11
of the [Canada Water Act](/eng/acts/C-11), with a provincial government
providing for the management of any waters situated partially in Nunavut
and partially in a province, or flowing between Nunavut and a province.
The Minister shall not enter into an agreement without the approval of
the Governor in Council.

### [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-372403 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of waters

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), no person
  shall use, or permit the use of, waters in Nunavut except in
  accordance with the conditions of a licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any unlicensed use of waters that is authorized by
    the regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the use of waters

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a domestic purpose, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of extinguishing a fire or, on
      an emergency basis, controlling or preventing a flood; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the use of waters in a national park.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties in certain cases

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a person diverts waters for a purpose referred
  to in subparagraph (2)(b)(ii), the person shall, when the need for the
  diversion has ceased, discontinue the diversion and, in so far as
  possible, restore the waters to their original channel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deposit of waste

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2) and except
  in accordance with the conditions of a licence, no person shall
  deposit or permit the deposit of waste

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in waters in Nunavut; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other place in Nunavut under conditions in
    which the waste, or any other waste that results from the deposit of
    that waste, may enter waters in Nunavut.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any unlicensed deposit of waste that is authorized
    by the regulations; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the deposit of waste in a national park.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to report deposits

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where waste is deposited in contravention of this
  section, every person who owns or has the charge, management or
  control of the waste, or who caused or contributed to the deposit,
  shall, subject to the regulations, without delay report the deposit to
  an inspector.

### [Compensation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-372427 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to compensation

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as otherwise provided by a compensation agreement referred to in this
  Part, a person, including the designated Inuit organization, who is
  adversely affected by a licensed use of waters or deposit of waste, or
  by an unlicensed use of waters or deposit of waste authorized by the
  regulations, is entitled to be compensated in respect of that adverse
  effect by the licensee or the person so authorized and to recover the
  compensation in any court of competent jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person, including the designated Inuit
  organization, is entitled to recover compensation under subsection (1)
  only to the extent that the person is not paid compensation under any
  other provision of this Part in respect of the adverse effect.

### [DIVISION 1]{.HLabel2}[Nunavut Water Board]{.HTitleText2} {#h-372433 .Subheading}

#### [Establishment and Organization of Board]{.HTitleText3} {#h-372434 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of Board

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  is hereby established the Nunavut Water Board, the members of which
  are to be appointed by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Number of members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 16 and 17, the Board consists of
  nine members, including the Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proportions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The following rules apply to the appointment of
  members, other than the Chairperson:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one half of the members shall be appointed on the
    nomination of the designated Inuit organization; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one quarter of the members shall be appointed on
    the nomination of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the territorial minister responsible for
      renewable resources, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the territorial minister or ministers
      designated, by an instrument of the Executive Council of Nunavut,
      for the purposes of this paragraph.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Chairperson

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson shall be appointed after consultation
  with the other members.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member of the Board shall be appointed to hold office for a term of
  three years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting after expiry of term

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the term of a member expires before the member has
  made a decision in a matter for which a public hearing is held, the
  member may, with the authorization of the Chairperson, continue to act
  as a member only in relation to that matter until the hearing is
  concluded and a decision is made. The office of the member is deemed
  to be vacant as soon as the term expires for the purpose of the
  appointment of a replacement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional members

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Additional members may be
appointed to the Board for the performance of a specified purpose, or
for a term of less than three years, in the manner and the proportions
provided by subsection 14(3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inuit of northern Quebec

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During
  any period preceding the ratification by the parties of an agreement
  to settle the offshore land claims of the Inuit of northern Quebec,
  the Minister shall appoint, on the nomination of Makivik, a number of
  substitute members of the Board equal to one-half the number appointed
  on the nomination of the designated Inuit organization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role of substitute members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In respect of licensing decisions of the Board that
  apply to any area of equal use and occupancy described in Schedule
  40-1 to the Agreement, the substitute members shall act in the place
  of such members appointed on the nomination of the designated Inuit
  organization as are identified by the Minister, after consultation
  with that organization, at the time of the appointment of the
  substitute members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 15(2), the term of a substitute
  member is three years, except that the term of that member expires on
  the ratification referred to in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of substitute members

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Substitute members shall not be considered to be
  members, except in respect of decisions referred to in subsection (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oath of office

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Before taking up their
duties, members of the Board shall take and subscribe the oath of office
set out in Schedule 2 before a person authorized by law to administer
oaths.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Any member of the Board
may be removed for cause, but before a member who was nominated by the
designated Inuit organization, Makivik or the territorial ministers is
removed, the Minister shall consult the designated Inuit organization,
Makivik or the territorial ministers, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** A member is eligible to
be reappointed to the Board in the same or another capacity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancies

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Where the office of a
member becomes vacant, the Minister shall, without delay, appoint a new
member to that office, and if the vacancy occurs during the term of a
member, a replacement shall be appointed only for the remainder of that
term.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of Chairperson

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The Chairperson is the
chief executive officer of the Board and has such powers, duties and
functions as are prescribed by the rules or by-laws of the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  member of the Board may participate in a decision on a matter in which
  that member has a material conflict of interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of Inuk or interest in land

### [DIVISION 2]{.HLabel2}[Licences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-372595 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this Act and on

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may not issue a licence in respect of a use

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to issue licence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board may not refuse to issue a licence merely

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this Act, the Board may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on application by the licensee, renew a licence,
    with or without changes to the conditions of the licence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} amend, for a specified term or otherwise, any
    condition of a licence

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on application by the licensee,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to deal with a water shortage, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where the Board considers the amendment to be
      in the public interest; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} cancel a licence

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on application by the licensee,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the licensee, for three successive years,
      fails to exercise the licensee's rights under the licence, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where the Board considers the cancellation to
      be in the public interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Renewal and amendment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 57 to 76 apply in relation to the renewal or

  Nunavut.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if the Board, with the

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Guidelines for applicant

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the qualitative and quantitative effects of the use

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the program the applicant proposes to undertake to
    monitor the impact of the use of waters or the deposit of waste;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the interests in and rights to lands and waters
    that the applicant has obtained or seeks to obtain; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the options available for the use of waters or the
    deposit of waste.

#### [Procedure]{.HTitleText3} {#h-372661 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standing

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** In the exercise of its

standing to the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Tunngavik, or any other Organization, within the
  meaning of section 1.1.1 of the Agreement, designated by Tunngavik, to
  make representations on behalf of the Inuit of Nunavut;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Makivik to make representations respecting the
  interests of the Inuit of northern Quebec in relation to islands and
  marine areas of the Nunavut Settlement Area traditionally used and
  occupied by those Inuit;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the councils of the Fort Churchill Indian Band and
  Northlands Indian Band to make representations respecting their
  interests in relation to the areas that those bands have traditionally
  used and continue to use; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the councils of the Black Lake Indian Band, Hatchet
  Lake Indian Band and Fond du Lac Indian Band to make representations
  respecting their interests in relation to the areas that those bands
  have traditionally used and continue to use.

The Board shall take the representations into account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public hearing required

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 37(2), a

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any application in relation to a licence, unless
    the application is of a class that is exempted by the regulations

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an application for permission to expropriate under

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A public hearing need not be held

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant or licensee consents in writing to

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} before an application is rejected under subsection

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for the amendment of

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors in determining compensation

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the periodic review of the security, including by
    taking into account any material changes to the undertaking or the

    the security as a result of the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of arrangement to be provided to

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, as soon as possible after

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall take into account the written

#### [Expropriation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-372892 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permission to expropriate

- **[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant for a licence, or a licensee, may apply to the Board for
  permission from the Minister to expropriate, in accordance with the
  [Expropriation Act](/eng/acts/E-21), land or an interest in land in
  Nunavut, and the Minister may grant that permission where the
  Minister, on the recommendation of the Board, is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the land or interest is reasonably required in
    relation to an appurtenant undertaking by the applicant or licensee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant or licensee has been unable to
    acquire the land or interest despite reasonable efforts to do so;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is in the public interest that such permission
    be granted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to appropriate minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister grants permission under subsection
  (1), the applicant or licensee shall so advise the appropriate
  minister in relation to Part I of the [Expropriation
  Act](/eng/acts/E-21).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Expropriation Act](/eng/acts/E-21)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the [Expropriation
  Act](/eng/acts/E-21), land or an interest in land in respect of which
  the Minister has granted permission to expropriate is deemed to be an
  interest in land that, in the opinion of the appropriate minister in
  relation to Part I of the [Expropriation Act](/eng/acts/E-21), is
  required for a public work or other public purpose, and a reference to
  the Crown in that Act shall be read as a reference to the applicant or
  licensee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inuit-owned land

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} When Inuit-owned land is expropriated and the
  designated Inuit organization and the applicant or licensee do not
  agree on the compensation to be paid, notwithstanding subsection (1),
  subsections 30(3) to (6) of the [Expropriation Act](/eng/acts/E-21) do
  not apply and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if both parties make a request to the Arbitration
    Board established under Article 38 of the Agreement, the Arbitration
    Board shall

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} appoint a person who is acceptable to both
      parties to act as a negotiator for the purposes of section 30 of
      the [Expropriation Act](/eng/acts/E-21), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} fix the remuneration and expenses, to be paid in
      equal shares by both parties to that person, for the period, not
      to exceed eight hours unless the parties agree to an extension of
      the number of hours, that the person performs the duties described
      in paragraphs (b) and (c);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the negotiator shall, on reasonable notice to the
    parties, meet with them or their authorized representatives, make
    any inspection of the land that the negotiator believes necessary,
    receive and consider appraisals, valuations or other written or oral
    evidence on which the parties rely for their estimation of the
    amount of compensation payable, whether or not the evidence would be
    admissible in proceedings before a court, and endeavour to effect a
    settlement;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the negotiator shall, within sixty days after the
    service of the notice to negotiate, report in writing to each of the
    parties and to the Arbitration Board concerning the success or
    failure in the matter of the negotiation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the negotiation is not successful, or if there
    are no negotiations,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} sections 31 to 33 of the [Expropriation
      Act](/eng/acts/E-21) do not apply, and compensation shall be
      determined by arbitration as set out in Article 38 of the
      Agreement,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in section 35 and subsection 36(1) of that Act,
      the term "adjudged" shall be read as "determined",

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in section 35 and in subsections 36(1) and (5)
      of that Act, the term "the Court" shall be read as "the
      arbitration panel", and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in subsections 36(2) and (3) of that Act, the
      term "judgment" shall be read as "the arbitration determination";
      and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} evidence of anything said or of any admission made
    in the course of a negotiation under this subsection is not
    admissible in any proceedings before a court for the recovery of the
    compensation payable to the designated Inuit organization, or before
    an arbitration panel established under Article 38 of the Agreement
    for the determination of the compensation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charges for services

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate minister in relation to Part I of the
  [Expropriation Act](/eng/acts/E-21) may make regulations prescribing
  fees or charges to be paid by an applicant or a licensee in respect of
  an expropriation referred to in subsection (1), and rates of interest
  payable in respect of those fees and charges.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt due to Her Majesty

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The fees or charges referred to in subsection (5) are
  a debt due to Her Majesty in right of Canada by the applicant or
  licensee, and shall bear interest at the prescribed rate from the date
  they are payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate minister in relation to Part I of the
  [Expropriation Act](/eng/acts/E-21) may require the applicant or
  licensee to provide security, in an amount determined by that minister
  and subject to any terms and conditions that the minister may specify,
  for the payment of any fees or charges that are or may become payable
  under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mitigation of damages --- enforcement
  of undertaking

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Where the applicant or licensee, in mitigation of any
  injury or damage caused or likely to be caused to lands by an
  expropriation, undertakes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to abandon or grant, to the owner of the lands or a
    person interested in the lands, any portion of its lands or of the
    land being taken or any easement, servitude or privilege over or in
    respect of the lands, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to construct and maintain any work for the benefit
    of the owner or interested person,

  and the owner or interested person accepts the undertaking, the
  undertaking is deemed to be an undertaking referred to in paragraph
  28(1)(b) of the [Expropriation Act](/eng/acts/E-21) and it may be
  enforced by the Board as if it were a condition of the licensee's
  licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the document evidencing the permission
  granted by the Minister under subsection (1), certified as such by the
  Chairperson of the Board, shall be deposited with the registrar of
  land titles for the registration district in which the affected lands
  are situated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of registrars

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} The provisions of section 210 of the [Canadian
  Energy Regulator Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1) relating to plans, profiles
  and books of reference deposited with land registrars under that Act
  and the duties of land registrars with respect to those plans,
  profiles and books, in so far as they are reasonably applicable and
  not inconsistent with this Part, apply in respect of copies of
  documents deposited under subsection (9).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply in respect of lands in
  Nunavut that are vested in Her Majesty in right of Canada or of which
  the Government of Canada has power to dispose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expropriation subject to Agreement

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} The expropriation of Inuit-owned land under this
  section is subject to the terms of Part 9 of Article 21 of the
  Agreement.

#### [Enforcement]{.HTitleText3} {#h-373022 .Subheading}

- **[[86]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-86 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of ensuring compliance with this Part, the regulations or

  reasonable time,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter any place in Nunavut in which the inspector
    believes, on reasonable grounds,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a work is being constructed that, on completion,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any alteration or extension is being carried out

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} conduct such inspections of a work described in

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} enter any place in Nunavut, except in a national

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} there is any waste that may be added to waters,

  examination under that subsection.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The owner or person in charge of any place referred

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of inspector

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of Her Majesty's costs

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any portion of the reasonable costs incurred by Her

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False statements

    and any of their parts or products. It does not include trees
    suitable for commercial production of lumber or other building

    végétales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    defined in section 2 of the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14),
    netting, egging, picking, collecting, gathering, spearing, killing,
    capturing or taking by any means. ([exploitation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

:   [[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Nunavut Surface Rights
    Tribunal established by section 99. ([Tribunal]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[wildlife]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wildlife]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} means terrestrial, aquatic, avian and amphibian
      fauna in their wild state and any of their parts or products; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection 152(2), includes
      flora. ([ressources fauniques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [DIVISION 1]{.HLabel2}[Establishment and Organization of Tribunal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-373307 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[99]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-99 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  is hereby established the Nunavut Surface Rights Tribunal consisting
  of a Chairperson and not fewer than two nor more than ten other
  members to be appointed by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Odd number

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall make such appointments as are
  necessary to ensure that an odd number of members holds office at any
  time.

- **[[100]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-100 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At
  least two of the members shall be resident in Nunavut.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of ceasing to be resident

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines that a member has ceased
  to be resident in Nunavut and that the condition imposed by
  subsection (1) is not satisfied, the appointment of the member is
  terminated as of the date on which the member receives written
  notification from the Minister that the determination has been made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

- **[[101]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-101 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member shall be appointed to hold office for a term not exceeding
  three years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting after expiry of term

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the term of a member expires before the member has
  made a decision in a matter for which a hearing is held, the member
  may, with the authorization of the Chairperson, continue to act as a
  member only in relation to that matter until the hearing is concluded
  and a decision is made. The office of the member is deemed to be
  vacant as soon as the term expires for the purpose of the appointment
  of a replacement.

be reappointed to the Tribunal in the same or another capacity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of Chairperson

chief executive officer of the Tribunal and has such powers, duties and
functions as are prescribed by the by-laws of the Tribunal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and expenses

- **[[104]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-104 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  members of the Tribunal shall receive fair remuneration, as determined
  by the Minister, for the performance of their duties and shall be paid
  such travel and living expenses incurred while absent from their
  ordinary place of residence in the course of performing their duties
  as are consistent with Treasury Board directives for public servants.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Workers' compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member is deemed to be an employee for the purposes
  of the [Government Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and to

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Translation of orders

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal shall, on the request of a party to any

  order.

#### [Applications and Hearings]{.HTitleText3} {#h-373404 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informal and expeditious

**[[119]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-119 .sectionLabel}** An application before
the Tribunal shall be dealt with as informally and expeditiously as the
circumstances and considerations of fairness permit and, in particular,
the Tribunal

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not bound by any strict rules of evidence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} may take into account any material it considers
  relevant;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} shall give due weight to Inuit knowledge of wildlife
  and the environment; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} shall, in hearing an application under Division 5,
  take into account the social, cultural and economic importance of
  wildlife to Inuit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General powers of Tribunal

**[[120]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-120 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal has, with
respect to the attendance and examination of witnesses, the production
and inspection of documents and all other matters necessary or proper in
relation to applications before the Tribunal, all the powers, rights and
privileges of a superior court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parties to a hearing

**[[121]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-121 .sectionLabel}** The following are
parties to a hearing before the Tribunal:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant and any other person who may apply for
  an order in respect of which the hearing is held; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the owner of land that would be subject to an order
  in respect of which the hearing is held and any occupant of that land.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing in absence of party

**[[122]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-122 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may not
hear an application in the absence of any party unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that party consents to the holding of the hearing in
  their absence; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} notice of the hearing was given to that party in
  accordance with the rules of the Tribunal or, in the absence of rules
  respecting the giving of such notice, in a manner satisfactory to the
  Tribunal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Location of hearing

**[[123]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-123 .sectionLabel}** Unless the parties
agree otherwise,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application under section 155 or 167 in relation
  to an order made under section 155 shall be heard in a community that
  is convenient to the claimant; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other application shall be heard in the community
  that is closest to the land involved.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing of applications

- **[[124]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-124 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application to the Tribunal shall be heard by a panel consisting of
  three members or, if the parties consent, by one member. If one of the
  members of a panel is absent, the hearing may continue with only one
  of the members if the parties consent, but if the parties do not
  consent, the application shall be reheard by another panel or member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member who is not present during the entire hearing
  of an application may not participate in the disposition of the
  application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Residency requirement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an application involves Inuit-owned land, at
  least two of the members of the panel hearing the application, or in
  the case of an application heard by one member, that member, shall be
  resident in Nunavut.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assignment of members

- **[[125]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-125
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Members shall be assigned to panels
  in accordance with the by-laws of the Tribunal or, in the absence of
  by-laws respecting the assignment of members, by the Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member shall not be assigned to, or continue to,
  hear an application if doing so would place the member in a material
  conflict of interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of Inuk or interest in land

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A member is not placed in a material conflict of
  interest merely because the member has the status under the Agreement
  of an Inuk or has an interest in land in Nunavut.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions

- **[[126]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-126 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  panel, or member hearing an application, has all of the powers, and
  shall perform all of the duties and functions, of the Tribunal in
  relation to the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any order disposing of an application is an order of
  the Tribunal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information made available

**[[127]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-127 .sectionLabel}** Before disposing of an
application, the Tribunal shall make any information that it intends to
use in the disposition available to the parties and provide them with a
reasonable opportunity to respond to the information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References to Federal Court

**[[128]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-128 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may, at
any stage of its proceedings, refer to the Federal Court any question or
issue of law or of jurisdiction, other than a question or issue that has
been referred to an arbitration panel established under Article 38 of
the Agreement.

### [DIVISION 2]{.HLabel2}[Entry Orders for Inuit-owned Lands]{.HTitleText2} {#h-373485 .Subheading}

#### [Exercise of Mineral Rights]{.HTitleText3} {#h-373486 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use and occupation

**[[133]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-133 .sectionLabel}** On application by any
person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} who has a mineral right granted by Her Majesty in
  right of Canada in relation to Inuit-owned land, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} who has been unable to obtain the consent of the
  designated Inuit organization,

the Tribunal shall make an entry order setting out the terms and
conditions for the use and occupation of that land to the extent
necessary for the purpose of exercising the mineral right.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prospecting right

- **[[134]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-134 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who has a right to prospect for minerals and who applies under
  section 133 for an entry order to exercise that right on Inuit-owned
  land shall make a separate application in respect of each parcel of
  that land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In disposing of an application made by a person who
  has a right to prospect for minerals, the Tribunal shall take into
  account the need to provide confidentiality for that person.

- Definition of [[parcel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (1), [[parcel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means the portion of land represented by a code of letters and numbers
  in the property description, as defined in section 19.1.1 of the
  Agreement, used for the purposes of conveying title to Inuit-owned
  land.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to other land

- **[[135]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-135
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), on
  application by any person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} who requires access to Inuit-owned land in order to
    exercise a mineral right, granted under an Act of Parliament, in
    relation to any other land, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} who has been unable to obtain the consent of the
    designated Inuit organization,

  the Tribunal shall make an entry order setting out the terms and
  conditions for access to that Inuit-owned land to the extent necessary
  for the purpose of exercising the mineral right.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal shall not make an entry order under
  subsection (1) unless the applicant satisfies the Tribunal that the
  access is reasonably required.

#### [Other Commercial Purposes]{.HTitleText3} {#h-373507 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to cross

- **[[136]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-136
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), on
  application by any person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} who requires access across Inuit-owned land for a
    commercial purpose, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} who has been unable to obtain the consent of the
    designated Inuit organization,

  the Tribunal shall make an entry order setting out the terms and
  conditions for the access.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal shall not make an entry order under
  subsection (1) unless an arbitration panel established under Article
  38 of the Agreement has, in accordance with the Agreement,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} established that the applicant attempted for a
    period of not less than sixty days to negotiate the access in good
    faith;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determined that the access is essential to the
    commercial purposes of the applicant and that access by any other
    means is physically or financially impractical; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} designated a route of access that will minimize any
    damage to the Inuit-owned land and interference with Inuit use of
    that land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms and conditions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An entry order made under subsection (1) shall
  include terms and conditions to minimize any damage to the Inuit-owned
  land and interference with Inuit use of that land.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation dispute

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the designated Inuit organization has consented
  to permit a person to cross Inuit-owned land for commercial purposes
  but that organization and that person are unable to agree on
  appropriate compensation, the Tribunal shall, on application by that
  organization or person, make an order resolving the matter.

#### [Construction Materials]{.HTitleText3} {#h-373523 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to remove materials

- **[[137]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-137
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), on
  application by the Minister or the territorial minister designated by
  an instrument of the Executive Council of Nunavut, in any case where
  the designated Inuit organization has refused entry on Inuit-owned
  land to remove sand, gravel or other like construction materials, the
  Tribunal shall make an entry order setting out the terms and
  conditions, including the payment of compensation, for entry on that
  land by agents of the Government of Canada or of the Government of
  Nunavut to remove those construction materials.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal shall not make an entry order under
  subsection (1) unless it determines that the construction materials
  are required for public purposes and that no alternative supply is
  reasonably available.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms and conditions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An entry order made under subsection (1) shall
  include terms and conditions to minimize any damage to the Inuit-owned
  land and interference with Inuit use of that land and shall require
  the rehabilitation of the site by the government that removed the
  construction materials.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In determining the amount of compensation that is
  payable as a term or condition of an entry order made under subsection
  (1), the Tribunal shall not take into account any amount that is
  payable for the construction materials.

#### [General Rules for Orders]{.HTitleText3} {#h-373533 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offer of compensation

**[[138]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-138 .sectionLabel}** An applicant for an
entry order shall file with the application a copy of the most recent
written offer of compensation made to the designated Inuit organization
or to the occupant of the land that would be subject to the order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms and conditions

**[[139]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-139 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may
include in an entry order, in addition to the terms and conditions
required by this Part,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} terms and conditions respecting any of the following
  matters, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the times when the right may be exercised,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the giving of notice,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} limitations on the location in which the right
    may be exercised and on routes of access,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} limitations on the number of persons exercising
    the right,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} limitations on the activities that may be carried
    on and the equipment that may be used,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the giving of security in accordance with the
    regulations and the purposes for which the security is given,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} abandonment and restoration work, and

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the right of the designated Inuit organization
    or occupant of the land to verify, by inspection or otherwise,
    whether the other terms and conditions have been complied with; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other terms and conditions that the Tribunal
  considers appropriate to minimize any damage to or interference with
  the use and peaceful enjoyment of the land by the occupant of the land
  or Inuit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation factors

- **[[140]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-140
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), in
  determining the amount of compensation that is payable under an order,
  the Tribunal may consider such factors as it considers appropriate
  and, without limiting the generality of the foregoing, shall consider

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the market value of the land;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the loss of the use of the land to the designated
    Inuit organization, the occupant of the land and Inuit;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the effect on wildlife harvesting by Inuit;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the adverse effect of the use or occupancy on any
    other Inuit-owned land;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any damage that may be caused to the land;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any nuisance and inconvenience, including noise, to
    the designated Inuit organization, the occupant of the land and
    Inuit;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the cultural attachment of Inuit to the land;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the peculiar and special value of the land to
    Inuit;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any reasonable expenses that may be incurred by the
    designated Inuit organization or occupant of the land as or on
    account of costs of an inspection under subparagraph 139(a)(viii);
    and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} any reasonable costs incurred by the designated
    Inuit organization in connection with the application and the
    hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In determining the amount of compensation payable,
  the Tribunal shall not consider the reversionary value of the land or

#### [Liability of Developers]{.HTitleText3} {#h-373652 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loss or damage

- **[[153]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-153
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section, a developer
  is absolutely liable, without proof of fault or negligence, for any of
  the following losses or damage suffered by a claimant as a result of a
  development activity of the developer:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} loss of or damage to property or equipment used in
    harvesting wildlife or to wildlife that has been harvested;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} present and future loss of income from the
    harvesting of wildlife; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} present and future loss of wildlife harvested for
    personal use by claimants.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A developer is not liable under subsection (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the developer establishes that the loss or
    damage was wholly the result of an act of war, hostilities, a civil
    war, an insurrection or a natural phenomenon of an exceptional,
    inevitable and irresistible character;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the loss or damage was caused by a ship, to
    the extent that the developer would not, but for subsection (1),
    have been liable as a result of a defence or limitation of liability
    available at law; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to the extent that the aggregate loss or damage for
    each incident exceeds the applicable limit of liability prescribed
    by, or determined pursuant to, regulations under paragraph 170(e).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claim

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any claim for compensation by a claimant, or by a
  designated Inuit organization or a Hunters and Trappers Organization,
  within the meaning assigned to that expression by section 1.1.1 of the
  Agreement, acting on behalf of the claimant, for loss or damage
  described in subsection (1) shall be made in writing to the developer
  within three years after the later of the date on which the loss or
  damage occurs and the date on which it comes to the knowledge of the
  claimant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The following principles apply to the determination
  of the amount of compensation payable as a result of loss or damage
  described in subsection (1):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a claimant is required to make all reasonable
    attempts to mitigate any loss or damage; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in general, compensation shall not be a guaranteed
    annual income in perpetuity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of Minister

- **[[154]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-154
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Without limiting the liability of
  the Minister where the Minister is the person engaged in the
  development activity or the owner of the ship that caused the loss or
  damage, the Minister is liable, in relation to any loss or damage that
  is attributable to marine transportation as described in paragraph (c)
  of the definition [[development activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
  subsection 152(1) other than that resulting from a discharge of oil
  from a ship, for any portion of the loss or damage for which a
  developer is not liable because of the application of paragraph
  153(2)(b) and for which no other person is liable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of Fund

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In relation to loss or damage resulting from a
  discharge of oil from a ship that is engaged in marine transportation
  as described in paragraph (c) of the definition [[development
  activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 152(1), the Ship-source
  Oil Pollution Fund established under Part 7 of the [Marine Liability
  Act](/eng/acts/M-0.7) is liable to the same extent that a developer
  would be liable under section 153 if paragraph 153(2)(b) did not
  apply.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subrogation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Administrator of the Ship-source Oil Pollution
  Fund is subrogated, to the extent of any payment made by the Fund
  under subsection (2), to any rights of the claimant in respect of the
  loss or damage for which that payment was made and, for that purpose,
  the Administrator may maintain an action in the Administrator's name
  or in the name of the claimant.

  same body.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acts and decisions of the Board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any act of the Board taken, or any decision made by
  it under the Agreement, before the day on which this Act is assented
  to is deemed, to the extent that the act or decision would have been
  valid under this Act, to have been validly taken or made under this
  Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial approval

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any approval to the issuance of a licence by the
  Board that was given by the Minister before the day on which this Act
  is assented to is deemed to have been validly given under this Act, to
  the extent that the approval would have been validly given under this
  Act with the exception of subsections 56(2) to (2.2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Actions of inspectors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any actions taken in Nunavut by inspectors under the
  [Northwest Territories Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-27.3), for the period
  beginning on July 9, 1996 and ending on the day before the day on
  which this Act is assented to, are deemed, to the extent that the
  actions would have been valid under this Act, to have been validly
  taken under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licences

- **[[172]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-172 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Act does not affect licences for the use of waters or the deposit of
  waste in Nunavut issued under the [Northwest Territories Waters
  Act](/eng/acts/N-27.3) that were in force immediately before the day
  on which this Act is assented to. The licences are deemed to have been
  issued by the Nunavut Water Board under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pending applications

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Nunavut Water Board shall dispose of any
  application respecting a licence in relation to a use of waters or a
  deposit of waste to which Part 1 applies that was made to the
  Northwest Territories Water Board and was pending on July 9, 1996.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing regulations

- **[[173]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-173
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Until they have been replaced or
  repealed under this Act, the regulations and orders made under
  sections 33 and 34 of the [Northwest Territories Waters
  Act](/eng/acts/N-27.3) that were in force on July 9, 1996 are binding
  on the Nunavut Water Board from that date, and continue to apply from
  that date in Nunavut, except in a national park, and the Board shall
  exercise the powers of the Northwest Territories Water Board under
  those regulations and orders in relation to Nunavut.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Instream use

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The regulations made under paragraph 33(1)(m) of the
  [Northwest Territories Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-27.3) are deemed to
  authorize the unlicensed instream use of waters in Nunavut, except in
  a national park.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Board

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} During the period of one year following the day on
  which this Act is assented to, the Nunavut Water Board may, by order,
  provide that any provision of the regulations made under paragraph
  33(1)(m) or (n) of the [Northwest Territories Waters
  Act](/eng/acts/N-27.3) ceases to apply in relation to Nunavut.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications not requiring public
hearings

- **[[174]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-174 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  regulations made under paragraph 33(1)(c) of the [Northwest
  Territories Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-27.3) are deemed, in relation to
  the use of waters or the deposit of waste in Nunavut, to prescribe, as
  classes of applications that are exempted from the requirement of a
  public hearing, the classes of applications in relation to the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a Type A licence,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any amendment that does not affect the use, flow
      or quality of waters or alter the term of the licence,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any amendment that affects the use, flow or
      quality of waters or alters the term of the licence, where the
      Nunavut Water Board, with the consent of the Minister, is of the
      opinion that an emergency exists that requires the amendment, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} one or several renewals of a total duration not
      exceeding sixty days; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a Type B licence, its issuance,
    amendment, renewal or cancellation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within one year after the day on which this Act is
  assented to, the Minister shall, unless regulations have been made
  under paragraph 82(1)(f) before that time to replace the regulations
  referred to in subsection (1), consult the Board on the application of
  subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of Nunavut Surface Rights
Tribunal

**[[175]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-175 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal
established by section 99 and the Nunavut Surface Rights Tribunal
established under the Agreement before this Act is assented to are
hereby declared for all purposes to be one and the same body.
