## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-252196 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Governor General's Act](/eng/acts/G-9).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-14, s. 1
:::

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Office of Governor General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-252201 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation sole

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Governor General of
Canada or other chief executive officer or administrator carrying on the
Government of Canada on behalf and in the name of the Sovereign, by
whatever title designated, is a corporation sole.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-14, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Instruments taken to Governor General

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  bonds, recognizances and other instruments by law required to be taken
  to the Governor General in his public capacity shall be taken to the
  Governor General by his name of office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suit and recovery

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The instruments referred to in subsection (1) may be
  sued for and recovered by the Governor General by his name of office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not to vest in personal representatives

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The instruments referred to in subsection (1) shall
  in no case go to or vest in the personal representatives of the
  Governor General, chief executive officer or administrator of the
  Government in whose name they were taken.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-14, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salary

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be payable to the Governor General for the 12-month period
  beginning on January 1, 2013 a salary of \$270,602.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Second charge on C.R.F.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The salary of the Governor General is payable out of
  the Consolidated Revenue Fund, and forms the second charge thereon.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. G-9, s. 4
- R.S., 1985, c. 50 (1st Supp.), s. 1
- 1990, c. 5, s. 1
- 2012, c. 19, s. 16
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual adjustment of salary

- **[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the 12-month period beginning on January 1, 2014 and for each 12-month
  period after that, the Governor General's salary shall be the amount
  obtained by multiplying

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that salary for the twelve month period immediately
    preceding the twelve month period in respect of which the salary is
    to be determined

    by

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lesser of one hundred and seven per cent and
    the percentage that the Industrial Aggregate for the first
    adjustment year is of the Industrial Aggregate for the second
    adjustment year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of certain expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to any twelve month period in respect
    of which the salary of the Governor General is to be determined, the
    "first adjustment year" is the most recent twelve month period for
    which the Industrial Aggregate is available on the first day of the
    period in respect of which the salary is to be determined, and the
    "second adjustment year" is the twelve month period immediately
    preceding the first adjustment year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the "Industrial Aggregate" for an adjustment year
    is the average weekly wages and salaries of the Industrial Aggregate
    in Canada for that year as published by Statistics Canada under the
    authority of the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rounding of amounts and prorating

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A salary determined pursuant to subsection (1) that
  is not a multiple of one hundred dollars shall be rounded to the
  closest multiple of one hundred dollars that is lower than the salary
  so determined and the salary so determined shall be prorated for any
  period that is less than one year.

- [(4) and (5)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2012, c. 19, s. 17\]]{.Repealed}

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Governor General's Retiring Annuity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-252237 .Part}

Definition of [[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[4.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
this Part, [[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who was married

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to a Governor General or former Governor General
    immediately before the death of the Governor General or former
    Governor General, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a former Governor General, to him
    or her immediately before the time when he or she ceased to be a
    Governor General; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who establishes that the person was
  cohabiting in a relationship of a conjugal nature

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} with a Governor General or former Governor General
    for a period of at least one year immediately before the death of
    the Governor General or former Governor General, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a former Governor General, with him
    or her immediately before he or she ceased to be a Governor General.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 12, s. 127
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of males and females

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Males and females have
equality of status and equal rights and obligations under this Part.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 81, s. 95
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annuity

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  Governor General ceases to hold office as such, there shall be paid to
  him an annuity equal to the aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one-third of the salary annexed to the office of
    Governor General on March 1, 1967; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} such amount, in addition to the amount determined
    under paragraph (a), as would be paid to him as a supplementary
    retirement benefit under the [Supplementary Retirement Benefits
    Act](/eng/acts/S-24) in the year in which he ceases to hold office
    if that benefit were calculated on the basis that he had ceased to
    hold office on January 1, 1952.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of annuity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An annuity payable under this section shall commence
  on the day the annuitant ceases to hold the office of Governor General
  and shall continue thereafter during his life.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. G-9, s. 6
- R.S., 1985, c. 50 (1st Supp.), s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annuity to survivor of annuitant

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  former Governor General who is in receipt of an annuity under section
  6 dies, an annuity equal to one-half of the annuity shall be paid to
  his or her survivor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annuity to survivor of Governor General

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a Governor General dies while holding office,
  his or her survivor shall be paid an annuity equal to one-half of the
  annuity that would have been paid if the Governor General had retired
  on the day on which he or she died.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of annuity to survivor

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An annuity payable to a survivor under this section
  shall commence immediately after the death of the Governor General or
  former Governor General and shall continue during the life of the
  survivor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Apportionment when two survivors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} When an annuity is payable under this section and
  there are two survivors, the total amount of the annuity shall be
  apportioned so that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the survivor referred to in paragraph 4.2(a)
    receives an amount, if any, equal to the total amount less any
    amount determined under paragraph (b) of this subsection; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the survivor referred to in paragraph 4.2(b)
    receives an amount equal to that proportion of the total amount that
    the number of years that the survivor cohabited with the annuitant
    while the annuitant was Governor General is of the number of years
    that the annuitant was Governor General.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Years

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In determining a number of years for the purpose of
  paragraph (4)(b), a part of a year shall be counted as a full year if
  the part is six or more months and shall be ignored if it is less.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. G-9, s. 7
- 2000, c. 12, s. 128
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election for former Governor General

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  person to whom a former Governor General is married or with whom he or
  she is cohabiting in a relationship of a conjugal nature, having so
  cohabited for a period of at least one year, would not be entitled to
  an annuity under section 7 in the event of the former Governor
  General's death, the former Governor General may make an election, in
  accordance with the regulations, to reduce the amount of the annuity
  to which he or she is entitled in order that the person could become
  entitled to an annuity under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person referred to in subsection (1) is entitled to
  an annuity in an amount determined in accordance with the election and
  the regulations if the former Governor General dies and the election
  is not revoked or deemed to have been revoked, and the person was
  married to the former Governor General at the time of his or her
  death, or was cohabiting with the former Governor General in a
  relationship of a conjugal nature for a period of at least one year
  immediately before his or her death.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make regulations
  respecting

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the time, manner and circumstances in which an
    election may be made, revoked or deemed to have been revoked;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reduction to be made in the amount of a former
    Governor General's pension when an election is made;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the annuity to be paid under
    subsection (2); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other matter that the Governor in Council
    considers necessary for carrying out the purposes and provisions of
    this section.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. G-9, s. 8
- 2000, c. 12, s. 128
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of annuities

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An annuity payable under
this Part shall be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund by monthly
instalments, and for any period less than a month shall be paid pro
rata.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-15, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment not affected by other benefits

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The payment of an annuity
under this Part shall not affect or be affected by the payment of any
pension, annuity or other benefit under any other Act of Parliament.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. G-15, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Diversion of payments to satisfy
financial support order

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  any court in Canada of competent jurisdiction has made an order
  requiring a person in receipt of an annuity under subsection 6(1) to
  pay financial support, amounts payable to the annuitant under that
  subsection are subject to being diverted to the person named in the
  order in accordance with Part II of the [Garnishment, Attachment and
  Pension Diversion Act](/eng/acts/G-2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment deemed to be to annuitant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Part, any payment made
  pursuant to subsection (1) shall be deemed to have been made to the
  annuitant in respect of whom the payment was made.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. G-9, s. 11
- 2000, c. 12, s. 129
