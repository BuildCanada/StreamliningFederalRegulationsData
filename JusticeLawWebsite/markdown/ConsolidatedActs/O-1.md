## Oaths of Allegiance Act {#oaths-of-allegiance-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. O-1
:::

An Act respecting oaths of allegiance
::::

:::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-382909 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Oaths of Allegiance Act](/eng/acts/O-1).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. O-1, s. 1
:::

## [Oath of Allegiance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-382914 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oath of allegiance

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who, either of his own accord or in compliance with any lawful
  requirement made of the person, or in obedience to the directions of
  any Act or law in force in Canada, except the [Constitution Act,
  1867](/eng/Const/) and the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29), desires
  to take an oath of allegiance shall have administered and take the
  oath in the following form, and no other:

  I, \...\...\...\...\...\....., do swear that I will be faithful and
  bear true allegiance to Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth the Second, Queen
  of Canada, Her Heirs and Successors. So help me God.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Demise of the Crown

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where there is a demise of the Crown, there shall be
  substituted in the oath of allegiance the name of the Sovereign for
  the time being.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. O-1, s. 2
- 1974-75-76, c. 108, s. 39
:::

## [Solemn Affirmation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-382922 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Solemn affirmation of allegiance may be
substituted

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every person allowed by law
in civil cases to solemnly affirm instead of taking an oath shall be
permitted to take a solemn affirmation of allegiance in the like terms,
with such modifications as the circumstances require, as the oath of
allegiance, and that affirmation, taken before the proper officer, shall
in all cases be accepted from the person in lieu of the oath and has the
like effect as the oath.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. O-1, s. 5
:::

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-382927 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations respecting oath of allegiance
and oath of office

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations requiring any person appointed to or holding an office
that is under the legislative authority of Parliament

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to take the oath of allegiance set out in section 2
  notwithstanding that the taking of the oath is not required by any
  other law; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to take an oath of office in the form prescribed by
  the regulations for the faithful performance of the duties of that
  office, in any case in which the form of such oath is not prescribed
  by any other law.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. O-1, s. 2
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-382934 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No other oath necessary

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** It is not necessary for any
person appointed to any public office, for any mayor or other officer or
member of any corporation, or for any person admitted, called or
received as a barrister, advocate, notary public, attorney, solicitor or
proctor, to make any declaration or subscription, or to take or
subscribe any oath other than the oath of allegiance set out in section
2 and such other oath for the faithful performance of the duties of the
office, or for the due exercise of the profession or calling, as is
required by any law.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. O-1, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time for taking oath

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The oath of allegiance set
out in section 2, together with the oath of office or oath for the due
exercise of any profession or calling, shall be taken within the period
and in the manner, and subject to the disabilities and penalties for the
omission thereof, provided by law with respect to such oaths.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. O-1, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By whom administered

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** All justices of the peace
and other officers lawfully authorized either by virtue of their office
or by special commission from the Crown may administer the oath of
allegiance set out in section 2 or receive a solemn affirmation of
allegiance.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. O-1, s. 6
:::
