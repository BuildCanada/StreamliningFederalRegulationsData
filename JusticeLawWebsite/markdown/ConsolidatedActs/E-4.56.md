## Emergency Management Act {#emergency-management-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2007, c. 15

Assented to 2007-06-22

An Act to provide for emergency management and to amend and repeal
certain Acts

of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-214335 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Emergency Management Act](/eng/acts/E-4.56).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-214338 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[emergency management]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[emergency management]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the prevention
    and mitigation of, preparedness for, response to and recovery from
    emergencies. ([gestion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[emergency management plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[emergency management plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a program,
    arrangement or other measure

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for dealing with an emergency by the civil
      population; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for dealing with a civil emergency by the
      Canadian Forces in accordance with the [National Defence
      Act](/eng/acts/N-5). ([plan de gestion des
      urgences]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[government institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[government institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any department,
    branch, office, board, agency, commission, corporation or other body
    for the administration or affairs of which a minister of the Crown
    is accountable to Parliament. ([institution
    fédérale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Public Safety
    and Emergency Preparedness. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[provincial emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an emergency
    occurring in a province if the province or a local authority in the
    province has the primary responsibility for dealing with the
    emergency. ([urgence provinciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Minister's Responsibilities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-214348 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial responsibilities --- general

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is responsible
for exercising leadership relating to emergency management in Canada by
coordinating, among government institutions and in cooperation with the
provinces and other entities, emergency management activities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibilities --- Canada

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister's responsibilities under section 3 include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establishing policies, programs and other measures
    respecting the preparation, maintenance, testing and implementation
    by a government institution of emergency management plans;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} providing advice to government institutions
    respecting the preparation, maintenance, testing and implementation
    of emergency management plans;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} analysing and evaluating emergency management plans
    prepared by government institutions;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} monitoring potential, imminent and actual
    emergencies and advising other ministers accordingly;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} coordinating the Government of Canada's response to
    an emergency;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} coordinating the activities of government
    institutions relating to emergency management with those of the
    provinces --- and supporting the emergency management activities of
    the provinces --- and through the provinces, those of local
    authorities;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} establishing arrangements with each province
    whereby any consultation with its lieutenant governor in council
    with respect to a declaration of an emergency under an Act of
    Parliament may be carried out effectively;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} coordinating the provision of assistance to a
    province in respect of a provincial emergency, other than the
    provision of financial assistance and the calling out of the
    Canadian Forces for service in aid of the civil power under Part VI
    of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5);

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} providing assistance other than financial
    assistance to a province if the province requests it;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} providing financial assistance to a province if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a provincial emergency in the province has been
      declared to be of concern to the federal government under section
      7,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is authorized under that section to
      provide the assistance, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the province has requested the assistance;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} participating, in accordance with Canada's foreign
    relations policies, in international emergency management
    activities;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} establishing the necessary arrangements for the
    continuity of constitutional government in the event of an
    emergency;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} establishing policies and programs respecting
    emergency management;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} conducting exercises and providing education and
    training related to emergency management;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} promoting a common approach to emergency
    management, including the adoption of standards and best practices;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} conducting research related to emergency
    management;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} promoting public awareness of matters related to
    emergency management; and

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} facilitating the authorized sharing of information
    in order to enhance emergency management.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other responsibilities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister has any other responsibilities in
  relation to emergency management that the Governor in Council may
  specify.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibilities --- United States

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In consultation with the
Minister of Foreign Affairs, the Minister may develop joint emergency
management plans with the relevant United States' authorities and, in
accordance with those plans, coordinate Canada's response to emergencies
in the United States and provide assistance in response to those
emergencies.

## [Ministers' Responsibilities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-214379 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministers' responsibilities

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  emergency management responsibilities of each minister accountable to
  Parliament for a government institution are to identify the risks that
  are within or related to his or her area of responsibility ---
  including those related to critical infrastructure --- and to do the
  following in accordance with the policies, programs and other measures
  established by the Minister:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prepare emergency management plans in respect of
    those risks;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintain, test and implement those plans; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} conduct exercises and training in relation to those
    plans.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of emergency management plans

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each minister shall include in an emergency
  management plan

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any programs, arrangements or other measures to
    assist provincial governments and, through the provincial
    governments, local authorities;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any federal-provincial regional plans;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any programs, arrangements or other measures to
    provide for the continuity of the operations of the government
    institution in the event of an emergency; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of war or other armed conflict, the
    programs, arrangements or other measures that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} support the overall defence effort,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} support the Canadian Forces and the armed forces
      of Canada's allies in the conduct of military operations,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} contribute to meeting Canada's military and
      civil wartime obligations to its allies, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} mitigate the effects of foreign armed conflict
      on Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial emergencies

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A government institution may not respond to a
  provincial emergency unless the government of the province requests
  assistance or there is an agreement with the province that requires or
  permits the assistance.

## [Orders or Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-214398 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders or regulations

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
on the recommendation of the Minister make orders or regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the preparation, maintenance, testing and
  implementation of emergency management plans;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the use of federal civil resources in
  response to civil emergencies;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} declaring a provincial emergency to be of concern to
  the federal government; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} authorizing the Minister to provide financial
  assistance to a province under paragraph 4(1)(j).
