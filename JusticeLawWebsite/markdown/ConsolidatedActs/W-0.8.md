## Wage Earner Protection Program Act {#wage-earner-protection-program-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2005, c. 47, s. 1
:::

An Act to establish a program to provide for payments to individuals in
respect of wages owed to them by employers who are insolvent

force July 7, 2008, see SI/2008-78.\]

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-464276 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Wage Earner Protection Program Act](/eng/acts/W-0.8).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-464279 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Industrial
      Relations Board established by section 9 of the [Canada Labour
      Code](/eng/acts/L-2). ([Conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[eligible wages]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[eligible wages]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} wages other than termination pay and severance
        pay that were earned during the longer of the following periods:

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the six-month period ending on the date of
          the bankruptcy or the first day on which there was a receiver
          in relation to the former employer,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the period beginning on the day that is six
          months before one of the following days and ending on the date
          of the bankruptcy or the first day on which there was a
          receiver in relation to the former employer:

          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the day on which a proposal is filed by or
            in respect of the employer under Division I of Part III of
            the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3) or, if a
            notice of intention to make a proposal is filed by or in
            respect of the employer under that Division, the day on
            which the notice of intention is filed,

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the most recent
            proceedings under the [Companies' Creditors Arrangement
            Act](/eng/acts/C-36) are commenced, and

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the period beginning on the day that is six
          months before one of the following days and ending on the day
          on which a court makes a determination under subsection 5(5):

          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the day on which a proposal is filed by or
            in respect of the employer under Division I of Part III of
            the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3) or, if a
            notice of intention to make a proposal is filed by or in
            respect of the employer under that Division, the day on
            which the notice of intention is filed,

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the most recent
            proceedings under the [Companies' Creditors Arrangement
            Act](/eng/acts/C-36) are commenced; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} termination pay and severance pay that relate
        to employment that ended

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} during the period referred to in paragraph
          (a), or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} during the period beginning on the day after
          the day on which the period referred to in paragraph (a) ends
          and ending on the day on which the trustee is discharged or
          the receiver completes their duties, as the case may
          be. ([salaire admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[wages]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[wages]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes salaries, commissions,
      compensation for services rendered, vacation pay, termination pay,
      severance pay and any other amounts prescribed by
      regulation. ([salaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Precision

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of the definition [[eligible
  wages]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, a proposal does not include a proposal
  for which a certificate is given under section 65.3 of the [Bankruptcy
  and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3) and a notice of intention to make a
  proposal does not include a notice of intention in respect of a
  proposal for which such a certificate is given.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of
  [[trustee]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} In this Act, [[trustee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  includes a [[monitor]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection
  2(1) of the [Companies' Creditors Arrangement Act](/eng/acts/C-36).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employers subject to a receivership

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, an employer is subject
  to a receivership when any property of the employer is under the
  possession or control of a receiver.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of
  [[receiver]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this Act, [[receiver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
  receiver within the meaning of subsection 243(2) of the [Bankruptcy
  and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Words and expressions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise provided, words and expressions used
  in this Act have the same meaning as in the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency
  Act](/eng/acts/B-3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Related persons

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 4(5) of the [Bankruptcy and
  Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of paragraph 6(d), an individual
    is considered to deal at arm's length with a related person if the
    Minister is satisfied that, having regard to the circumstances ---
    including the terms and conditions of the individual's employment
    with the former employer, their remuneration and the duration,
    nature and importance of the work performed for the former employer
    --- it is reasonable to conclude that the individual would have
    entered into a substantially similar contract of employment with the
    former employer if they had been dealing with each other at arm's
    length; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsection 21(4), individuals
    who are related to each other are, in the absence of evidence to the
    contrary, deemed not to deal with each other at arm's length while
    so related.

## [Eligibility for Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-464313 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of eligibility

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual is eligible to receive a payment if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the individual's employment ended for a reason
    prescribed by regulation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one of the following applies:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the former employer is bankrupt,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the former employer is subject to a
      receivership,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the former employer is the subject of a foreign
      proceeding that is recognized by a court under subsection 270(1)
      of the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3) and

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the court determines under subsection (2) that
        the foreign proceeding meets the criteria prescribed by
        regulation, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a trustee is appointed, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the former employer is the subject of
      proceedings under Division I of Part III of the [Bankruptcy and
      Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3) or under the [Companies' Creditors
      Arrangement Act](/eng/acts/C-36) and a court determines under
      subsection (5) that the criteria prescribed by regulation are met;
      and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the individual is owed eligible wages by the former
    employer.

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2009, c. 2, s. 343\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed criteria --- foreign
  proceeding

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On application by any person, a court may, in a
  proceeding under Part XIII of the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency
  Act](/eng/acts/B-3), determine that the foreign proceeding meets the
  criteria prescribed by regulation. If the court determines that the
  foreign proceeding meets the prescribed criteria, the court may
  appoint a trustee for the purposes of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employment in Canada

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An individual who is eligible to receive a payment
  because of subparagraph (1)(b)(iii) is only eligible to receive a
  payment in respect of eligible wages earned for employment in Canada
  and termination pay and severance pay that relate to that employment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed bankruptcy

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, if all of the
  conditions set out in subparagraph (1)(b)(iii) are met, the former
  employer is deemed to be bankrupt and the date of the bankruptcy is
  deemed to be the day on which all of those conditions are met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed criteria --- other
  proceedings

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} On application by any person, a court may, in
  proceedings under Division I of Part III of the [Bankruptcy and
  Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3) or under the [Companies' Creditors
  Arrangement Act](/eng/acts/C-36), determine that the former employer
  meets the criteria prescribed by regulation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An individual is not
eligible to receive a payment in respect of any wages earned during, or
that otherwise relate to, a period in which the individual

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} was an officer or director of the former employer;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} had a controlling interest within the meaning of the
  regulations in the business of the former employer;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} occupied a managerial position within the meaning of
  the regulations with the former employer; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} was not dealing at arm's length with

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an officer or director of the former employer,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a person who had a controlling interest within the
    meaning of the regulations in the business of the former employer,
    or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an individual who occupied a managerial position
    within the meaning of the regulations with the former employer.

## [Amounts Covered by Program]{.HTitleText1} {#h-464333 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of payment

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount that may be paid under this Act to an individual is the amount
  of eligible wages owing to the individual up to a maximum of an amount
  equal to seven times the maximum weekly insurable earnings under the
  [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reduction

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Except in the circumstances prescribed by
  regulation, the amount that may be paid under this Act to an
  individual is to be reduced by any amounts provided for by regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Greatest amount

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If more than one situation that is described in
  paragraph 5(1)(b) applies to the former employer, the amount that may
  be paid is the greatest of the amounts determined in respect of each
  of those situations.

## [Administration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-464401 .Part}

### [Duties of Trustees and Receivers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-464402 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General duties

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of this Act, a trustee or a receiver, as the case may be,
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} identify each individual who is owed eligible
    wages;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine the amount of eligible wages owing to
    each individual;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} inform each individual other than one who is in a
    class prescribed by regulation of the existence of the program
    established by section 4 and of the conditions under which payments
    may be made under this Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide the Minister and each individual other than
    one who is in a class prescribed by regulation with the amount of
    eligible wages owing to the individual and any other information
    prescribed by regulation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} inform the Minister of when the trustee is
    discharged or the receiver completes their duties, as the case may
    be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with directions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A trustee or receiver shall comply with any
  directions of the Minister relating to the administration of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to assist

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person, other than one described in subsection (4),
  who has or has access to information described in paragraph (1)(d)
  shall, on request, provide it to the trustee or the receiver, as the
  case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to assist --- payroll contractors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person who is dealing at arm's length with and
  providing payroll services to a bankrupt or insolvent person and who
  has or has access to information described in paragraph (1)(d) shall,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if requested by the trustee or receiver, provide
    the trustee or receiver with a description of the information
    described in paragraph (1)(d) that they have or have access to and
    an estimate of the cost of providing the information; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if requested by the trustee or receiver, provide
    the trustee or receiver with the information described in paragraph
    (1)(d) that they have or have access to.

### [Powers of Minister]{.HTitleText2} {#h-464429 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions to trustees and receivers

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may give directions to trustees and receivers in respect of
  the performance of their duties under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions not statutory instruments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A direction given by the Minister is not a statutory
  instrument within the meaning of the [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to summon, etc.

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of the administration of this Act, the Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} summon any person before him or her and require the
    person to give evidence, orally or in writing, and on oath or, if
    the person is entitled to affirm in civil matters, on solemn
    affirmation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require any person to provide the Minister with any
    information or document that the Minister considers necessary; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} require any person to provide an affidavit or a
    statutory declaration attesting to the truth of any information
    provided by the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taking oaths, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person, if designated by the Minister for the
  purpose, may administer oaths and take and receive affidavits,
  statutory declarations and solemn affirmations for the purpose of or
  incidental to the administration of this Act. Every person so
  designated has, with respect to any such oath, affidavit, declaration
  or affirmation, all the powers of a commissioner for administering
  oaths or taking affidavits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acceptance of oaths, etc.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, for the purposes of administering
  this Act, accept any oath administered or any affidavit, statutory
  declaration or solemn affirmation taken or received by any person who
  has the powers of a commissioner for taking affidavits and who is an
  officer or employee of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a department or other portion of the federal public
    administration specified in any of Schedules I, IV and V to the
    [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a department of the government of a province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspections

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person designated by the Minister for the purpose may, at any
  reasonable time, enter any place in which he or she reasonably
  believes there is any information or document relevant to the
  administration of this Act and may, in that place,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} inspect any books, records, electronic data or
    other documents that he or she reasonably believes may contain
    information that is relevant to the administration of this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any computer system to
    examine any data contained in or available to the computer system;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} reproduce or cause to be reproduced any record from
    the data in the form of a print-out or other intelligible output;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} take any document or other thing from the place for
    examination or, in the case of a document, for copying; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any copying equipment to
    make copies of any documents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior authorization

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If any place referred to in subsection (1) is a
  dwelling-house, the designated person may not enter the dwelling-house
  without the consent of the occupant, except under the authority of a
  warrant issued under subsection (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant to enter dwelling-house

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A judge may issue a warrant authorizing the
  designated person to enter a dwelling-house subject to the conditions
  specified in the warrant if, on [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  application, the judge is satisfied by information on oath that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there are reasonable grounds to believe that the
    dwelling-house is a place referred to in subsection (1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entry into the dwelling-house is necessary for any
    purpose related to the administration of this Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} entry into the dwelling-house has been, or there
    are reasonable grounds to believe that entry will be, refused.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders if entry not authorized

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the judge is not satisfied that entry into the
  dwelling-house is necessary for any purpose related to the
  administration of this Act, the judge may, to the extent that access
  was or may be expected to be refused and that information or documents
  are or may be expected to be kept in the dwelling-house,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} order the occupant of the dwelling-house to provide
    the Minister, or a person designated by the Minister for the
    purpose, with reasonable access to any information or document that
    is or should be kept in the dwelling-house; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make any other order that is appropriate in the
    circumstances to carry out the purposes of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to assist

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The owner or person in
charge of a place that is entered by the designated person and every
person found there must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} give the designated person all reasonable assistance
  to enable him or her to exercise his or her powers and perform his or
  her duties; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the designated person with any information
  relevant to the administration of this Act that he or she requires.

  payment is made under this Act to an individual in respect of eligible

  of the payment, subrogated to any rights the individual may have in
  respect of the eligible wages against

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the bankrupt or insolvent employer; and

  prescribed by regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister --- decision or
  order

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the Minister directs otherwise, an
  individual who received a payment under this Act shall also notify the
  Minister, in writing, of any final decision or order, of which they
  are aware, respecting the recovery of eligible wages. The notice shall
  contain any information prescribed by regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintaining an action

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), Her Majesty in
  right of Canada may maintain an action in the name of the individual
  or Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment to Her Majesty in right of Canada

- **[[36.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, under a court judgment or for
  any other reason, a trustee, receiver or any other person is required
  to pay eligible wages to an individual who the trustee, receiver or
  other person has reason to believe has received a payment under this
  Act, the trustee, receiver or other person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ascertain whether Her Majesty in right of Canada is
    subrogated to any rights the individual may have in respect of the
    eligible wages; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if Her Majesty in right of Canada is subrogated,
    pay to Her Majesty the amount in respect of which Her Majesty is
    subrogated before making any payment to the individual in respect of
    eligible wages.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Components of wages

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A trustee, receiver or other person who makes a
  payment under paragraph (1)(b) shall provide the Minister with
  information respecting the components of wages to which the payment
  relates.

### [Offences and Penalties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-464593 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person commits an offence who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} makes a false or misleading entry, or omits to
    enter a material particular, in any record or book of account that
    contains information that supports an application under this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to an application under this Act, makes
    a representation that the person knows to be false or misleading;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in relation to an application under this Act, makes
    a declaration that the person knows to be false or misleading
    because of the nondisclosure of facts;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} being required under this Act to provide
    information, does not provide it or makes a representation that the
    person knows to be false or misleading;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} obtains a payment under this Act by false pretence;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} being the payee of any cheque issued as a payment
    under this Act, knowingly negotiates or attempts to negotiate it
    knowing that the person is not entitled to the payment or any part
    of the payment; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} participates in, consents to or acquiesces in an
    act or omission mentioned in any of paragraphs (a) to (f).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Trustees and receivers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who fails to comply with any of the
  requirements of subsection 21(1), (3) or (4) commits an offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation of prosecutions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A prosecution for an offence under subsection (1)
  or (2) may be commenced at any time within six years after the day on
  which the subject matter of the prosecution arose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Due diligence

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person may be convicted of an offence under
  subsection (2) if the person establishes that they exercised due
  diligence to prevent the commission of the offence.
