## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1159491 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants
Act](/eng/acts/C-33.6).

## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1159494 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the board of directors referred
    to in subsection 17(1). ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[College]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[College]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the College of Immigration and
    Citizenship Consultants continued under section 84 or established
    under section 86. ([Collège]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[immigration and citizenship consultant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[immigration and citizenship consultant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    a person --- other than a person referred to in paragraph 21.1(2)(a)
    or (b) or subsection 21.1(3) or (4) of the [Citizenship
    Act](/eng/acts/C-29) or paragraph 91(2)(a) or (b) or subsection
    91(3) or (4) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) --- who, directly or indirectly, represents or
    advises persons for consideration --- or offers to do so --- in
    connection with a proceeding or application under the [Citizenship
    Act](/eng/acts/C-29), the submission of an expression of interest
    under subsection 10.1(3) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) or a proceeding or application under that
    Act. ([consultant en immigration et en citoyenneté]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence issued under this
    Act. ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the federal minister
    designated under section 3. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by the
    regulations. ([Version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[privileged]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[privileged]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means subject to a privilege
    under the law of evidence, litigation privilege, solicitor-client
    privilege or the professional secrecy of advocates and
    notaries. ([protégé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Registrar of the College
    appointed under section 30. ([registraire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of Minister

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate any federal minister to be the Minister
referred to in this Act.

## [Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1159507 .Part}

### [College]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1159508 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of the College
is to regulate immigration and citizenship consultants in the public
interest and protect the public, including by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} establishing and administering qualification
  standards, standards of practice and continuing education requirements
  for licensees;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} establishing and providing training and development
  programs for licensees;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensuring compliance with the code of professional
  conduct; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} undertaking public awareness activities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The College's head office
is to be at the place in Canada that is designated in the College's
by-laws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  carrying out its purpose, the College has the capacity and the rights,
  powers and privileges of a natural person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extraterritorial capacity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the College may exercise the
  rights, powers and privileges referred to in subsection (1) in any
  jurisdiction outside Canada to the extent that the laws of that
  jurisdiction permit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The College is not an agent
of Her Majesty in right of Canada, and the College's directors, the
members of its committees, the Registrar, the investigators and any
officers, employees and agents and mandataries of the College are not
part of the federal public administration.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Canada Not-for-profit Corporations
Act](/eng/acts/C-7.75)

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The [Canada Not-for-profit
Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-7.75) does not apply to the College.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Official Languages
Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01)

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The [Official Languages
Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01) applies to the College.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The members of the
College are the licensees.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual general meeting

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The College must hold an
annual general meeting of members within six months after the end of
each of its fiscal years, at a time and place in Canada fixed by the
Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The College must give
members notice of the time and place of the annual general meeting in
accordance with the by-laws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation fund

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The College must
establish a fund to compensate persons who have been adversely affected
by the conduct or activities of a licensee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public records

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  College must prepare and maintain, at its head office or at any other
  place in Canada designated by the Board, records that are made
  available to the public on the College's website and in any other
  manner that the College considers appropriate and that contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its by-laws, and amendments to them;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the minutes of the Board's public meetings;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a register of directors;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a register of officers; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} its audited annual financial statements as approved
    by the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accounting records and financial
  statements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The College must prepare and maintain adequate
  accounting records and annual financial statements.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any other Act of Parliament or of the
  legislature of a province that provides for a longer retention period,
  the College must retain, for the prescribed period, the accounting
  records and audited annual financial statements as approved by the
  Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  College must submit to the Minister, within 120 days after the end of
  each of its fiscal years, a report on the College's activities during
  the preceding fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must cause a copy of the report to be
  tabled in each House of Parliament on any of the first 15 days on
  which that House is sitting after the day on which the Minister
  receives it.

### [Board of Directors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1159552 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties and powers of Board

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board must manage, or supervise the management of, the College's
  activities and affairs and may, for those purposes, exercise all of
  the powers conferred on the College under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may delegate any of the powers, duties and
  functions conferred on it under this Act, except the powers

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to amend or repeal regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to make by-laws; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to approve audited annual financial statements.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  College has a board of directors composed of at least seven directors,
  including the Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order fixing number of directors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by order, fix the number of
  directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointed directors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), the Minister may, by
  order, fix the number of directors that are to be appointed to the
  Board and appoint those directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum number of appointed directors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The number of appointed directors fixed under
  subsection (3) must not be greater than the minimum number that is
  required to constitute a majority of directors on the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elected directors

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The remaining directors are to be licensees elected
  in accordance with the by-laws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mailing address

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Each director must
provide the College with their mailing address and with notice of any
change in that address.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  director is to be appointed or elected for a term of not more than
  three years, and may be reappointed or --- subject to the by-laws ---
  re-elected for subsequent terms of not more than three years each.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of term

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The length of a director's term is to be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out by the Minister in the order appointing the
    director, if the director is appointed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determined in accordance with the by-laws, if the
    director is elected.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staggered terms

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, it is not necessary that all
  directors hold office for terms that begin or end on the same day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation in office --- appointed
  directors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1) and subject to section 23, an
  appointed director continues to hold office until they are reappointed
  or their successor is appointed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligibility

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** An individual is not
eligible to be appointed or elected as a director if they

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} are less than 18 years of age;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} are neither a Canadian citizen nor a [[permanent
  resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
  [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have the status of bankrupt;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an appointment to the Board,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are a licensee, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are employed in any capacity in the federal public
    administration;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of election to the Board, have a licence
  that is suspended; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} are ineligible under any other criteria set out in
  the regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity of acts of directors and
officers

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** An act of a director or
an officer is valid despite their ineligibility or an irregularity in
their election or appointment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal --- appointed directors

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appointed director holds office during good behaviour and may be
  removed for cause by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal --- elected directors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An elected director may be removed in accordance with
  the by-laws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ceases to be director

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** A director ceases to be a
director if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} they die;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} they resign;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} they are removed from office under section 22; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any prescribed circumstance applies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and expenses

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The College must pay to
the directors the remuneration and expenses that are determined in
accordance with the by-laws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson is to be elected by the Board from among the directors in
  accordance with the by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson must preside over the Board's
  meetings and perform any other duties that are assigned to the
  Chairperson by the by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board may remove the Chairperson in accordance
  with the by-laws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board must hold a meeting at least once every calendar year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A majority of the number of directors fixed by the
  Minister under subsection 17(2) constitutes a quorum at any meeting of
  the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of meeting

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson of the Board must give notice of the
  time and place of meetings of the Board to the directors and any
  observer designated under section 76.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public meetings

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the by-laws, meetings of the Board are to
  be open to the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Observer --- [in camera]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} meetings

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An observer designated under section 76 is entitled
  to attend meetings of the Board that are held [in camera]{.otherLang
  lang="la"}.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Telecommunications

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the by-laws, a director may participate
  in, and any observer designated under section 76 may observe, a
  meeting of the Board by telephone or by an electronic or other
  communication facility that permits them to communicate adequately
  with each other during the meeting. A director so participating in a
  meeting is deemed for the purposes of this Act to be present at that
  meeting.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity of signed resolutions

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  resolution in writing, signed by all the directors entitled to vote on
  that resolution at a meeting of the Board, is as valid as if it had
  been passed at a meeting of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing resolution

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the by-laws made under paragraph 80(1)(z),
  a copy of every resolution referred to in subsection (1) is to be kept
  with the minutes of the public meetings of directors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chief Executive Officer

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The Board may appoint a
Chief Executive Officer, who is to exercise the powers and perform the
duties and functions that are delegated to him or her by the Board.

### [Committees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1159637 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaints Committee and Discipline
Committee

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Two
  committees of the College are established, to be known as the
  Complaints Committee and the Discipline Committee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The members of each committee are to be appointed by
  the Board in accordance with the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Discipline Committee are to hold
  office for a term of not more than five years. The members may be
  reappointed for subsequent terms of not more than five years each.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting after expiry of term

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A member of the Discipline Committee whose term
  expires may, in accordance with the rules referred to in section 59,
  conclude any proceeding that the member has begun.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Discipline Committee hold office
  during good behaviour and may be removed for cause by the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} An individual is not permitted to be a member of the
  Complaints Committee and the Discipline Committee at the same time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other committees

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Board may establish other committees of the
  College.

### [Registrar]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1159653 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board is to appoint a Registrar of the College for a term of not more
  than five years. The Registrar may be reappointed for subsequent terms
  of not more than five years each.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar holds office during good behaviour and
  may be removed for cause by the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar is responsible for issuing licences,
  establishing and maintaining a register of licensees and verifying
  compliance or preventing non-compliance with this Act by licensees.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Register available to public

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  register of licensees must be made available to the public on the
  College's website in a searchable format and, subject to the
  regulations, in any other manner that the Registrar considers
  appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Updated information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar must ensure that the information
  included in the register is updated in a timely manner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar must,
within the prescribed period and in the prescribed form and manner,
provide notice to the Minister in the following circumstances:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a licence is suspended;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a licence is revoked;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a licence is surrendered; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other prescribed circumstance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  application, the Registrar must issue to an individual who, in the
  Registrar's opinion, meets the eligibility requirements set out in the
  by-laws for the class of licence for which the individual applied, a
  licence of that class.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licence issued under this section is subject to any
  conditions or restrictions imposed under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application for a licence must be made in the form
  and manner specified by the Registrar and must contain the information
  specified by the Registrar.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surrender of licence

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar may, in
accordance with the by-laws, approve the surrender of a licence on
application by a licensee made in accordance with the by-laws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Registrar --- verification

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, the
  Registrar may, for a purpose related to verifying compliance or
  preventing non-compliance with this Act by licensees,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), enter a licensee's
    business premises, without notice, at any reasonable time and
    require the production of any document or other thing that is
    relevant, and examine or copy the document or thing; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require the licensee, or a business partner or
    employee of the licensee or any person employed by the same employer
    as the licensee, to provide any information that is relevant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dwelling-house

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the licensee's business premises are in a
  dwelling-house, the Registrar may enter them only with the occupant's
  consent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privileged information

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the
regulations, the powers set out in section 35 must not be exercised in
respect of privileged information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Referral to Complaints Committee

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** If the Registrar is of
the opinion that there may be reasonable grounds to suspect that a
licensee has committed professional misconduct or was incompetent, the
Registrar may, subject to the regulations, initiate a complaint and
refer it to the Complaints Committee for consideration.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision of Registrar

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** If the Registrar
determines that a licensee has contravened a provision of this Act, of
the regulations or of the by-laws, the Registrar may, in his or her
decision, in the prescribed circumstances,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend the licensee's licence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} revoke the licensee's suspended licence; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} take or require any other action set out in the
  regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with decision

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** A licensee who is the
subject of a decision made under section 38 must comply with it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing decision in Federal Court

- **[[39.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The College may file in the Federal
  Court a certified copy of a decision made under section 38.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of filing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the certified copy being filed, the decision
  becomes and may be enforced as an order of the Federal Court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to licensees --- revocation or
suspension

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Registrar must notify all licensees of every decision made under
  subsection 69(3) to revoke or suspend a licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to licensees --- dismissal of
  complaint

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar must, if requested by the licensee who
  was the subject of a complaint that was dismissed by the Discipline
  Committee, notify all licensees of the dismissal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the
regulations, the Registrar may delegate any of the powers, duties and
functions conferred on the Registrar under this Act.

## [Licensees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1159706 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Professional liability insurance

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a
  licensee must be insured against professional liability.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensee may be exempted by the by-laws from the

## [Investigations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1159734 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigator

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Complaints Committee may designate an individual as an investigator to
  conduct an investigation under the Committee's direction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Complaints Committee may revoke the designation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to require information and
documents

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  investigator may, for the purpose of investigating a licensee's
  conduct and activities, require any person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to provide any information that, in the
    investigator's opinion, the person may be able to provide in
    relation to the matter being investigated; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to produce, for examination or copying by the
    investigator, any document or other thing that, in the
    investigator's opinion, relates to the matter being investigated and
    that may be in the possession or under the control of that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to enter

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 52(1), an investigator may, for
  that purpose, enter a place in which they have reasonable grounds to
  believe that an activity regulated under this Act is conducted or any
  document or other thing relevant to the investigation is located.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other powers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The investigator may, for that purpose,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine anything in the place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} remove the thing for examination or copying;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} use any copying equipment in the place, or cause it
    to be used;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} open or order any person to open any container or
    package found in the place;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} use any means of communication in the place, or
    cause it to be used;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} use any computer system or other device in the
    place, or cause it to be used, to examine data contained in or
    available to it;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} prepare a document, or cause one to be prepared,
    based on the data; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} take photographs or make recordings or make a copy
    of anything in the place.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to assist

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The owner or person in charge of the place and every
  person in the place must give all assistance that is reasonably
  required to enable the investigator to perform their functions under
  subsections (2) and (3) and must provide any documents or information,
  and access to any data, that are reasonably required for that purpose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant to enter dwelling-house

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  place is a dwelling-house, the investigator may enter it without the
  occupant's consent only under the authority of a warrant issued under
  subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application, a
  justice of the peace may issue a warrant authorizing the investigator
  to enter a dwelling-house, subject to any conditions specified in the
  warrant, if the justice is satisfied by information on oath that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dwelling-house is a place referred to in
    subsection 51(2);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entry to the dwelling-house is necessary for the
    purposes of the investigation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} entry was refused by the occupant or there are
    reasonable grounds to believe that entry will be refused by, or that
    consent to entry cannot be obtained from, the occupant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** In executing a warrant to
enter a dwelling-house, an investigator may use force only if the use of
force has been specifically authorized in the warrant and they are
accompanied by a peace officer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privileged information

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the
regulations, the powers set out in section 51 must not be exercised in
respect of privileged information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction and false statements

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited to
obstruct or hinder, or make a false or misleading statement either
orally or in writing, to an investigator who is performing their
functions.

## [Powers of College]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1417627 .Part}

### [Administration of Licensee's Property]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1417628 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order

  a licensee is not able to perform their activities as an immigration

  in order to carry out its purpose, including to ensure the
  continuation of services to a licensee's client, apply, without
  notice, to a court of competent jurisdiction, including the Federal
  Court, for an order authorizing the College or a person appointed by
  the court to administer any property in relation to those activities
  that is or should be in the licensee's possession or under their
  control.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the court concludes that it is necessary to ensure
  the continuation of services to the licensee's clients or to otherwise
  permit the College to carry out its purpose, it may, for the purposes
  of subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} authorize the College or person appointed by the
    court to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} enter a place, including a vehicle, in which the
      College or person has reasonable grounds to believe property
      referred to in subsection (1) is located,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} examine anything in the place,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} open or order any person to open any container
      or package found in the place,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} require the owner or person in charge of the
      place and any person in the place to give all assistance that is
      reasonably required to enable the College or person appointed by
      the court to execute the order and to provide any property
      referred to in subsection (1) or any information, and access to
      any data, that are reasonably required for that purpose, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} seize any document or other thing related to a
      client's files and transfer it to the client or a licensee or
      person referred to in paragraph 21.1(2)(a) or (b) or subsection
      21.1(3) or (4) of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29) or
      paragraph 91(2)(a) or (b) or subsection 91(3) or (4) of the
      [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} order that any property referred to in
    subsection (1) is not to be dealt with by any person without leave
    of the court or is to be held in trust by the College or person
    appointed by the court;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} require the licensee to account to the College or
    person appointed by the court for any property referred to in
    subsection (1);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide for the compensation of the College or
    person appointed by the court for administering the licensee's
    property and the reimbursement of any expenses the College or person
    incurs in doing so; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide for any other matter that it considers
    appropriate in the circumstances, including imposing conditions on
    the manner in which the College or person appointed by the court is
    to execute the order.

  winding up their practice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Former licensee

  consultant," "international student immigration advisor" or a
  variation or abbreviation of any of those titles, or any words, name
  or designation, in a manner that leads to a reasonable belief that the
  person is a licensee;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} represent themselves, in any way or by any means, to

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} unless the person is a person referred to in

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the holder may provide advice only in relation to
      authorizations to study in Canada and authorizations to enter and
      remain in Canada as a student and the manner in which those
      authorizations relate to submissions of an expression of interest
      under subsection 10.1(3) of the [Immigration and Refugee
      Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) and applications under that Act,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the holder must not represent any person in
      connection with submissions of an expression of interest under
      subsection 10.1(3) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
      Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), proceedings or applications under that Act
      or proceedings or applications under the [Citizenship
      Act](/eng/acts/C-29);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the code of professional ethics regulating members
    of the Council and the code of ethics regulating persons registered
    with the Council as Regulated International Student Immigration
    Advisors that were in effect immediately before the date of
    transition apply until the day before the day on which the Minister
    establishes a code of professional conduct for licensees under
    subsection 43(1) and, until that day, a reference to the code of
    professional conduct in this Act, other than in section 43, is to be
    read as a reference to that code of professional ethics and that
    code of ethics;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the College may deal with any matter related to
    complaints and discipline that was pending before the Council
    immediately before the date of transition;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the College's Complaints Committee has jurisdiction
    to consider a complaint and conduct an investigation in respect of
    the conduct or activities of a member referred to in paragraph (a)
    or a person referred to in paragraph (b) that were engaged in before
    the date of transition;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the College's Discipline Committee has jurisdiction
    to hear and determine complaints in respect of the conduct or
    activities of a member referred to in paragraph (a) or a person
    referred to in paragraph (b) that were engaged in before the date of
    transition and to take or require the actions that would have been
    applicable at the time that the conduct or activities were engaged
    in;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of paragraphs (e) and (f), a
    member referred to in paragraph (a) or a person referred to in
    paragraph (b) committed professional misconduct or was incompetent
    if they failed to meet the standards of professional conduct and
    competence that were established by the code of professional ethics
    regulating members of the Council or the code of ethics regulating
    persons registered with the Council as Regulated International
    Student Immigration Advisors, as the case may be, that was in effect
    when the conduct or activities were engaged in;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the Registrar may, until the day before the day on
    which a regulation prescribing the circumstances referred to in
    section 38 comes into force, suspend a licence if the licensee fails
    to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} pay the annual fee, or any other fee or amount
      that the licensee is required to pay under this Act, in accordance
      with the by-laws,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} provide, in accordance with the by-laws, any
      information or document required by the by-laws,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} comply with any requirement that is imposed by
      the by-laws with respect to continuing professional development,
      or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} comply with any requirement with respect to
      professional liability insurance that is imposed under this Act;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed period referred to in paragraph
    69(3)(b) is deemed to be two years until the day before the day on
    which a regulation made under section 81 prescribing that period
    comes into force;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed amount referred to in paragraph
    69(3)(d) is deemed to be \$10,000 until the day before the day on
    which a regulation made under section 81 prescribing that amount
    comes into force; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} every decision of the Council that suspends a
    member referred to in paragraph (a) or a person referred to in
    paragraph (b), or imposes conditions or restrictions on their
    membership or registration, and that was in force immediately before
    the date of transition becomes a suspension of a licence or a
    condition or restriction to which a licence is subject, as the case
    may be, and continues to be such until the date specified in the
    decision or until the College lifts the suspension, conditions or
    restrictions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration fees

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} A member of the Council referred to in paragraph
  (7)(a) or a person referred to in paragraph (7)(b) continues to be a
  holder of a licence only if, within two months after the date of
  transition, or within any longer period specified by the College and
  posted on its website, they pay the registration fee specified by the
  College and posted on its website.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} A by-law must not be made under paragraph 80(1)(n) on
  or before the date of transition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transitional provisions --- new classes
  of licence

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the by-laws made under
  subsection 80(1) may contain provisions in respect of the transition
  from the classes of licence referred to in paragraphs (7)(a) and (b)
  to the new classes of licence established by the by-laws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[88]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-88 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations providing for any other transitional matters
arising from the coming into force of this Act.
