## Western Economic Diversification Act {#western-economic-diversification-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. 11 (4th
Supp.)
:::

An Act to promote the development and diversification of the economy of
Western Canada, to establish the Department of Western Economic
Diversification and to make consequential amendments to other Acts

::: ReaderNote
\[1988, c. 17, assented to 8th June, 1988\]
:::
:::::

::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467971 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Western Economic Diversification Act](/eng/acts/W-6.8).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467974 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of Western
    Economic Diversification established by subsection
    4(1); ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Western
    Economic Diversification; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Western Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Western Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Provinces of
    Manitoba, British Columbia, Saskatchewan and Alberta. ([Ouest
    canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467980 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to promote the development and diversification of the economy of Western
Canada and to advance the interests of Western Canada in national
economic policy, program and project development and implementation.

## [Department]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467983 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Department established

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a department of the Government of Canada called the
  Department of Western Economic Diversification over which the Minister
  of Western Economic Diversification, appointed by commission under the
  Great Seal, shall preside.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister holds office during pleasure and has the
  management and direction of the Department.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may appoint an officer called
  the Deputy Minister of Western Economic Diversification to be the
  deputy head of the Department.

## [Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-467991 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions of the
Minister

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  powers, duties and functions of the Minister extend to and include all
  matters over which Parliament has jurisdiction, not by law assigned to
  any other department, board or agency of the Government of Canada,
  relating to the promotion of the development and diversification of
  the economy of Western Canada and the advancement of the interests of
  Western Canada in national economic policy, program and project
  development and implementation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} guide, promote and coordinate the policies and
    programs, including those related to industrial benefits, of the
    Government of Canada in relation to the development and
    diversification of the economy of Western Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} lead and coordinate the efforts of the Government
    of Canada to establish cooperative relationships with the provinces
    constituting Western Canada, business, labour and other public and
    private organizations for the development and diversification of the
    economy of Western Canada; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} compile detailed information on all programs and
    projects undertaken by the Minister for the purpose of measuring
    trends, development and progress in the development and
    diversification of the economy of Western Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further duties and functions

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} formulate plans and integrated strategies to
    support development and diversification of the economy of Western
    Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} oversee the implementation of programs and projects
    in pursuance of plans and strategies referred to in paragraph (a)
    and, where those programs and projects will not be undertaken by
    another Minister or department or agency of the Government of
    Canada, initiate or implement those programs and projects;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} plan, direct, manage and implement programs and
    projects intended to contribute directly or indirectly to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the establishment, development, support and
      promotion of enterprises, and more particularly, small and
      medium-sized enterprises, in Western Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the economic prosperity of that region;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} plan, direct, manage and implement programs and
    projects to improve the business environment in Western Canada,
    including programs and projects

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} of support to business associations, conferences,
      studies, consultations, trade shows, demonstration projects and
      market research,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} related to the development of business
      opportunity data banks and networks, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to improve business communication and
      cooperation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} initiate, implement, sponsor, promote and
    coordinate policy research, policy development and economic analysis
    to support development and diversification of the economy of Western
    Canada;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} enter into any agreement with the government of any
    province, or with any agency of such a government, respecting the
    exercise of the Minister's powers and the carrying out of the
    Minister's duties and functions; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} do all such other things as are necessary or
    incidental to the attainment of the purpose of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial assistance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, in accordance with terms and
  conditions approved by the Treasury Board, and with the approval of
  the Minister of Finance,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make loans to any person with respect to the
    establishment or development of enterprises, and more particularly,
    small and medium-sized enterprises, in Western Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} guarantee the repayment of, or provide loan
    insurance or credit insurance in respect of, any financial
    obligation undertaken by any person in respect of the establishment
    and development of enterprises, and more particularly, small and
    medium-sized enterprises, in Western Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, in accordance with terms and
  conditions approved by the Treasury Board, make grants and
  contributions in support of programs and projects undertaken by the
  Minister.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468023 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offices

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  principal office of the Department shall be in Edmonton, Alberta, but
  the Department shall maintain at least one office in each other
  province in Western Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Government services and facilities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Department shall, where appropriate, make use of
  the services and facilities of other departments and of boards and
  agencies of the Government of Canada.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468029 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may make
regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} specifying programs and projects in addition to those
  referred to in paragraph 6(1)(d) to improve the business environment
  in Western Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} generally for carrying out the purposes and
  provisions of this Act.

## [Annual Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468034 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall cause to
be laid before each House of Parliament on any of the first fifteen days
on which that House is sitting after January 31 next following the end
of each fiscal year, a report on the administration of this Act for that
fiscal year.

## [Transitional]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468037 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriations based on Estimates

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The provisions made by
any appropriation Act for the fiscal year in which this section comes
into force, based on the Estimates for that year to defray the charges
and expenses of the public service of Canada within the Department of
Regional Industrial Expansion and the Department of Indian Affairs and
Northern Development in relation to any matter in Western Canada to
which the powers, duties or functions of the Minister extend by law,
shall be applied to such classifications of the public service within
the Department of Western Economic Diversification as the Governor in
Council may determine.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of powers, duties and functions

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Wherever under any Act,
order, rule or regulation, or any contract, lease, licence or other
document, any power, duty or function is vested in or exercisable by the
Minister of Regional Industrial Expansion, the Deputy Minister of
Regional Industrial Expansion or any other officer of the Department of
Regional Industrial Expansion in relation to any matter in Western
Canada to which the powers, duties or functions of the Minister extend
by law, the power, duty or function is vested in and shall be exercised
by the Minister of Western Economic Diversification, the Deputy Minister
of Western Economic Diversification or the appropriate officer of the
Department of Western Economic Diversification, as the case may be,
unless the Governor in Council by order designates another Minister,
deputy head or officer of a department or a portion of the public
service of Canada to exercise that power, duty or function.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed appointment

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Every indeterminate
employee in the public service of Canada who is transferred to a
position in the Department from the public service of Canada within
ninety days after the day on which this section comes into force is
deemed to have been appointed to the Department in accordance with the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).
