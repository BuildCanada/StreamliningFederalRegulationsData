## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-146665 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada Infrastructure Bank Act](/eng/acts/C-6.18).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-146668 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Infrastructure Bank
    established by subsection 5(1). ([Banque]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the board of directors of the
    Bank. ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chairperson of the
    Board. ([président]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Chief Executive Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chief Executive Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the chief
    executive officer of the Bank. ([premier dirigeant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[designated Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the member of the
    Queen's Privy Council for Canada who is designated as the Minister
    under section 3. ([ministre désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of the
    Board. ([administrateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[infrastructure projects]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[infrastructure projects]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means projects
    described in section 6. ([projets
    d'infrastructures]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[joint venture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[joint venture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an association of
    persons, when the relationship among those associated persons does
    not constitute a corporation, partnership or
    trust. ([coentreprise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a trust, a partnership, a
    joint venture and an association of natural persons or
    corporations. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[subsidiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[subsidiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a subsidiary within the
    meaning of subsection 83(6) of the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11). ([filiale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[wholly-owned subsidiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wholly-owned subsidiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in subsection 83(1) of the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11). ([filiale à cent pour cent]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Designation and Appropriate Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-146682 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of Minister

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
designate a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada to be the
Minister for the purposes of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriate Minister

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The designated Minister is
the appropriate Minister for the Bank for the purposes of Part X of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

## [Establishment and Organization of the Bank]{.HTitleText1} {#h-146687 .Part}

### [Status of the Bank]{.HTitleText2} {#h-146688 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  corporation is established to be known as the Canada Infrastructure
  Bank.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The head office of the Bank is to be at a place in
  Canada that is designated by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Bank has the capacity and, subject to the
  provisions of this Act and of the regulations, the rights, powers and
  privileges of a natural person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not a Crown agent

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Bank is not an agent of Her Majesty in right of
  Canada, except when

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} giving advice about investments in infrastructure
    projects to ministers of Her Majesty in right of Canada, to
    departments, boards, commissions and agencies of the Government of
    Canada and to Crown corporations as defined in subsection 83(1) of
    the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} collecting and disseminating data in accordance
    with paragraph 7(1)(g);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} acting on behalf of the government of Canada in the
    provision of services or programs, and the delivery of financial
    assistance, specified in paragraph 18(h); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} carrying out any activity conducive to the carrying
    out of its purpose that the Governor in Council may, by order,
    specify.

### [Purpose and Functions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-146702 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of Bank

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of the Bank is
to invest, and seek to attract investment from private sector investors
and institutional investors, in infrastructure projects in Canada or
partly in Canada that will generate revenue and that will be in the
public interest by, for example, supporting conditions that foster
economic growth or by contributing to the sustainability of
infrastructure in Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Functions of Bank

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In order
  to carry out its purpose, the Bank may do only the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} structure proposals and negotiate agreements, with
    the proponents of infrastructure projects and with investors in
    infrastructure projects, with regard to the Government of Canada's
    support of those projects;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} invest in infrastructure projects, including by
    means of innovative financial tools, and seek to attract investment
    from private sector investors and institutional investors in
    infrastructure projects;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} receive unsolicited proposals for infrastructure
    projects that come from private sector investors or from
    institutional investors;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} support infrastructure projects by, among other
    things, fostering evidence-based decision making;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} act as a centre of expertise on infrastructure
    projects in which private sector investors or institutional
    investors are making a significant investment;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} provide advice to all levels of governments with
    regard to infrastructure projects;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} collect and disseminate data, in collaboration with
    the federal, provincial and municipal governments, in order to
    monitor and assess the state of infrastructure in Canada and to
    better inform investment decisions in regards to infrastructure
    projects; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} perform any other function conducive to the
    carrying out of its purpose that the Governor in Council may, by
    order, specify.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of order's effect

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An order made under paragraph (1)(h) ceases to have
  effect on the second anniversary of the day on which it is made.

### [Board and Chief Executive Officer]{.HTitleText2} {#h-146718 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Membership of Board

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Bank
  has a board of directors composed of the Chairperson and not fewer
  than eight, but not more than 11, other directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of directors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each director, other than the Chairperson, is to be
  appointed by the Governor in Council to hold office during pleasure
  for a term of not more than four years that will ensure, to the extent
  possible, the expiry in any one year of the terms of office of not
  more than one half of the directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Chairperson

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson is to be appointed by the Governor in
  Council to hold office during pleasure for a term that the Governor in
  Council considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committee to advise designated Minister

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The designated Minister may establish a committee to
  provide him or her with advice on the appointment of directors. The
  committee is to include representation from the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The designated Minister may undertake any other
  consultations as to the appointment of directors that he or she
  considers appropriate, including with the provinces.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of appointment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may terminate the appointment
  of, or remove or suspend, any director. However, before terminating
  the appointment of or removing or suspending the Chairperson, the
  Governor in Council must consult with the Board. The Board may also,
  with the approval of the Governor in Council, terminate the
  appointment of or remove or suspend any director.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A director is eligible for reappointment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation in office

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), if a director is not
  appointed to take office on the expiry of the term of an incumbent
  director, other than the Chairperson, the incumbent director continues
  in office until their successor is appointed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} If the Chairperson is absent or unable to act or the
  office of Chairperson is vacant, the Board may authorize one of the
  other directors to act as Chairperson, but that person is not
  authorized to act as Chairperson for a period of more than 180 days
  without the approval of the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Chief Executive Officer

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A Chief
  Executive Officer is to be appointed by the Board to hold office
  during pleasure for a term to be fixed by the Board. The appointment
  and term of office are subject to the approval of the Governor in
  Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of appointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, after consulting with
  the Board, terminate the appointment of or remove or suspend the Chief
  Executive Officer. The Board may also, with the approval of the
  Governor in Council, terminate the appointment of or remove or suspend
  the Chief Executive Officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Full-time office

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Executive Officer must carry out the duties
  and functions of his or her office on a full-time basis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Chief Executive Officer is absent or unable to
  act or the office of Chief Executive Officer is vacant, the designated
  Minister may appoint a person to act as Chief Executive Officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attendance at Board meetings

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any by-law of the Board, the Chief
  Executive Officer may attend meetings of the Board or any committee of
  the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligibility for appointment

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** An individual is not
eligible to be appointed as the Chief Executive Officer, the Chairperson
or a director if the individual

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is less than 18 years of age;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} has the status of bankrupt;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is employed in the federal public administration or
  by a provincial, municipal or local authority; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} is a member of the Senate or House of Commons or a
  member of the legislature of a province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No overlapping offices

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An individual is not
entitled to hold the offices of Chairperson and Chief Executive Officer
at the same time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Executive Officer, the Chairperson and each of the other
  directors are to be paid by the Bank remuneration for their services
  in respect of that office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rate of remuneration --- directors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The rate of any remuneration paid to the Chairperson
  and the other directors is to be fixed by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rate of remuneration --- Chief
  Executive Officer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The rate of any remuneration paid to the Chief
  Executive Officer is to be fixed by the Governor in Council on the
  recommendation of the Board. In making its recommendation, the Board
  is to take into account the skills required for the position and the
  remuneration paid to persons in comparable positions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses of directors

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  director is to be paid by the Bank reasonable travel and living
  expenses incurred in connection with their services in respect of that
  office while absent from their ordinary place of residence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses of Chief Executive Officer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Executive Officer is to be paid by the Bank
  reasonable travel and living expenses incurred in connection with his
  or her services in respect of that office while absent from his or her
  ordinary place of work.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accident compensation

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The directors and the
officers and the employees of the Bank are deemed to be employees for
the purposes of the [Government Employees Compensation
Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and to be employed in the federal public
administration for the purposes of any regulations made under section 9
of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committees of Board

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may establish any committee of the Board that it considers
  advisable and may determine the committee's composition and duties and
  the tenure of its members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation of powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may delegate powers to any of its
  committees to act in all matters that are not by this Act or any
  by-law or resolution specifically reserved to the Board.

## [Financial Management and Control]{.HTitleText1} {#h-146776 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporate plans

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Bank must annually
submit a corporate plan to the designated Minister, who may, with the
concurrence of the Minister of Finance, recommend it for the approval of
the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operating budgets

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Bank must annually submit an operating budget for its next financial
  year to the designated Minister, who may, with the concurrence of the
  Minister of Finance, recommend it for the approval of the Treasury
  Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capital budgets

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Bank must annually submit a capital budget for
  its next financial year to the designated Minister, who may, with the
  concurrence of the Minister of Finance, recommend it for the approval
  of the Treasury Board.

## [Certain Bank Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-146784 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investments, etc.

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** In particular, the Bank
may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} make investments in any person, including by way of
  equity investment in, or by making a loan to or acquiring a derivative
  from, the person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} extend credit or provide liquidity to, or in relation
  to, any person;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} acquire and deal with as its own any investment made
  by another person;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} acquire and hold security or a security interest,
  including, in Quebec, a right in a security, of any kind and in any
  form for the due discharge of obligations under an investment or
  agreement that it makes;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} surrender the security, security interest or right in
  the security and acquire and hold, in exchange, security or a security
  interest, including, in Quebec, a right in a security, of any kind and
  in any form;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} realize the security, security interest or right in
  the security made, acquired or held by it on the investment or
  agreement;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} exchange, sell, assign, convey or otherwise dispose
  of, or lease, the investment, agreement, security, security interest
  or right in a security;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} enter into arrangements or agreements with, and act
  as agent or mandatary for, any department or agency of the government
  of Canada or a province, or any other body or person, for the
  provision of services or programs to, by, on behalf of or jointly with
  that body or person, and deliver financial assistance on their behalf
  under the arrangement or agreement;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} accept any interest or rights in real property or
  personal property or any rights in immovables or movables as security
  for the due performance of any arrangement or agreement with the Bank;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} determine and charge interest and any other form of
  compensation for services provided by the Bank in the exercise of its
  powers or the performance of its functions under this Act;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} acquire and dispose of any interest or right in any
  entity by any means; and

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} acquire, hold, exchange, sell or otherwise dispose
  of, or lease, any interest or rights in real property or personal
  property or any right in immovables or movables and retain and use the
  proceeds of disposition.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loan guarantees --- limitation

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Bank may provide loan guarantees only in accordance with this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Bank may recommend to the designated Minister
  that the Minister of Finance approve a loan guarantee with respect to
  an infrastructure project. If the designated Minister concurs with the
  recommendation, he or she is to recommend to the Minister of Finance
  that the Minister of Finance approve the loan guarantee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to provide

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Bank may provide a loan guarantee with respect to
  an infrastructure project only if the Minister of Finance approves the
  loan guarantee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of provision

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Section 91 of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) does not apply to or in
respect of the Bank or a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Bank.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsidiaries --- Ministerial
authorization

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The Bank or a
wholly-owned subsidiary of the Bank may procure the incorporation,
dissolution or amalgamation of its subsidiaries, and acquire or dispose
of any shares in its subsidiaries, only with the concurrence of the
designated Minister.

## [Powers of Minister of Finance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-146810 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendation for loan or loan guarantee

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Bank may recommend to the designated Minister that the Minister of
  Finance make a loan or provide a loan guarantee with respect to an
  infrastructure project. If the designated Minister concurs with the
  recommendation, he or she is to recommend to the Minister of Finance
  that the Minister of Finance make the loan or provide the loan
  guarantee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to make loan or provide loan
  guarantee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Finance, on the recommendation of the
  designated Minister, may make a loan or provide a loan guarantee with
  respect to the infrastructure project.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capital payments

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Finance
may pay to the Bank, out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund, amounts of
not more than \$35,000,000,000 in the aggregate, or any greater
aggregate amount that may be authorized from time to time under an
appropriation Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loans to the Bank

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** At the request of the
Bank, the Minister of Finance may lend money to the Bank, on the
recommendation of the designated Minister, out of the Consolidated
Revenue Fund, on any terms and conditions that the Minister of Finance
may fix.

## [Miscellaneous Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-146820 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capital and shares

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  capital of the Bank is \$100, divided into 10 shares having a par
  value of \$10 each. The shares are to be issued to the designated
  Minister to be held on behalf of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The shares issued to the designated Minister are to
  be registered by the Bank in the name of the designated Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial year

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The financial year of the
Bank is the period of 12 months beginning on April 1 and ending on the
next March 31, unless the Governor in Council directs otherwise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Five-year review

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  five years beginning on the day on which this Act comes into force,
  the designated Minister must have a review of the provisions and
  operation of this Act undertaken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within one year after the review is undertaken, the
  designated Minister must cause a copy of the report on the review to
  be laid before each House of Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The report must be reviewed by any committee of the
  Senate or of the House of Commons, or any joint committee, that may be
  designated or established for the purpose of reviewing the report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privileged information

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), all
  information obtained by the Bank, by any of the Bank's subsidiaries or
  by any of the subsidiaries of the Bank's wholly-owned subsidiaries in
  relation to the proponents of, or private sector investors or
  institutional investors in, infrastructure projects is privileged and
  a director, officer, employee, or agent or mandatary of, or adviser or
  consultant to, the Bank, any of its subsidiaries, or any of the
  subsidiaries of its wholly-owned subsidiaries must not knowingly
  communicate, disclose or make available the information, or permit it
  to be communicated, disclosed or made available.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized disclosure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Privileged information may be communicated, disclosed
  or made available in the following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is communicated, disclosed or made available for
    the purpose of the administration or enforcement of this Act and
    legal proceedings related to it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is communicated, disclosed or made available for
    the purpose of prosecuting an offence under this Act or any other
    Act of Parliament;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is communicated, disclosed or made available to
    the Minister of National Revenue solely for the purpose of
    administering or enforcing the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) or
    the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15); or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} it is communicated, disclosed or made available
    with the written consent of the person to whom the information
    relates.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of Bank's name, initials or acronyms

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** Except with the written
consent of the Bank, a person must not in any prospectus or
advertisement, or for any other business purpose, use the name of the
Bank, the initials "C.I.B." or "B.I.C." or the acronyms "CIB" or "BIC"
in reference to any function of the Bank under section 7.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Auditors

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** The Auditor General of
Canada and an auditor appointed annually by the Governor in Council
under subsection 134(1) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11) are the auditors of the Bank.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** A person who contravenes
section 28 or 29 is guilty of an offence and liable on summary
conviction to a fine of not more than \$10,000 or to imprisonment for a
term of not more than six months, or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations for the purpose of carrying out the purposes and
provisions of this Act, including regulations setting out requirements
in respect of the exercise of the Bank's powers under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** In the event of any
inconsistency between this Act and Part X of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), this Act prevails to the extent of
the inconsistency.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-146854 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First Chief Executive Officer

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  first Chief Executive Officer is to be appointed by the Governor in
  Council to hold office during pleasure for a term that the Governor in
  Council considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before the first Chief Executive Officer is
  appointed, the designated Minister must consult with any directors
  then in office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson's interim powers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} During the period before the first day on which there
  is a Chairperson and at least eight other directors in office, the
  Chairperson, once appointed, comprises the Board and may exercise all
  the powers of the Board, and during the period before the first Chief
  Executive Officer is appointed, the Chairperson may exercise all the
  powers of the Chief Executive Officer.
