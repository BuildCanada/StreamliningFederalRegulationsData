## Seized Property Management Act {#seized-property-management-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1993, c. 37
:::

Assented to 1993-06-23

An Act respecting the management of certain property seized or
restrained in connection with certain offences, the disposition of
certain property on the forfeiture thereof and the sharing of the
proceeds of disposition therefrom in certain circumstances
::::

::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## [Purposes of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-445129 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purposes of Act

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purposes of this Act
are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to authorize the Minister to provide consultative and
  other services to law enforcement agencies in relation to the seizure
  or restraint of property in connection with designated offences, or
  property that is or may be proceeds of crime or offence-related
  property;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} to authorize the Minister to provide consultative
  and other services to any person employed in the federal public
  administration or by a provincial or municipal authority in relation
  to the seizure, restraint, custody, management, forfeiture or disposal
  of property

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by means of or in respect of which an offence or
    violation is committed,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is used in any manner in connection with the
    commission of an offence or violation, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that is intended for use for the purpose of
    committing an offence or violation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to authorize the Minister to manage property

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} seized or restrained under any Act of Parliament or
    of the legislature of a province by a person employed in the federal
    public administration or by a provincial or municipal authority,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} forfeited under any Act of Parliament or of the
    legislature of a province, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} paid under subsection 18(2) of the [Proceeds of
    Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing
    Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to authorize the Minister to dispose of property
  referred to in paragraph (b) when it is forfeited to Her Majesty and,
  with the consent of the government of the province, when it is
  forfeited to Her Majesty in right of a province;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where property referred to in paragraph (c) is
  forfeited to Her Majesty and disposed of, or where a fine is imposed
  pursuant to subsection 462.37(3) of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46), to provide authority for the sharing, in
  certain circumstances, of the proceeds of disposition therefrom or the
  fine, as the case may be, with jurisdictions the law enforcement
  agencies of which participated in the investigations of the offences
  that led to the forfeiture or the imposition of the fine; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} if property referred to in paragraph (c) is forfeited
  to Her Majesty in right of a province and disposed of by the Minister
  with the consent of the government of the province, to provide
  authority for the sharing of the proceeds of disposition in accordance
  with directions given by that government.

## [Management of Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-445143 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister to be responsible for management
of property

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  taking possession or control thereof, the Minister shall be
  responsible for the custody and management of all property that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} seized under a warrant issued under section 83.13,
    462.32, 462.321 or 487 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46),
    section 11 of the [Controlled Drugs and Substances
    Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8) or section 87 of the [Cannabis
    Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) on the application of the Attorney General
    and that the Minister is appointed to manage under subsection
    83.13(3), 462.331(2) or 490.81(2) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46), subsection 15.1(2) of the [Controlled Drugs
    and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8) or subsection 93(2) of the
    [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5), as the case may be;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to a restraint order made under section
    83.13, 462.33 or 490.8 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46),
    section 14 of the [Controlled Drugs and Substances
    Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8) or section 91 of the [Cannabis
    Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) on the application of the Attorney General
    and that the Minister is appointed to manage under subsection
    83.13(3), 462.331(2) or 490.81(2) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46), subsection 15.1(2) of the [Controlled Drugs
    and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8) or subsection 93(2) of the
    [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5), as the case may be;

  - [(b.01)]{.lawlabel} seized or restrained under any Act of Parliament
    or of the legislature of a province if the Minister agrees to be
    responsible for the custody and management of the property;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} forfeited under subsection 14(5) or 39.03(5),
    seized under subsection 18(1) or 39.06(1) or paid under subsection
    18(2) of the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist
    Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501);

  - [(b.2)]{.lawlabel} forfeited under subparagraph 715.34(1)(e)(i) of
    the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

  - [(b.3)]{.lawlabel} if the Minister agrees to be responsible for its
    custody and management, forfeited under any Act of Parliament, other
    than under subsection 14(5) or 39.03(5) of the [Proceeds of Crime
    (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501)
    or subparagraph 715.34(1)(e)(i) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or forfeited under any Act of the legislature
    of a province; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to a management order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibility continues after
  forfeiture

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where property referred to in subsection (1) that is
  in the possession or under the control of the Minister is forfeited to
  Her Majesty, the Minister shall continue to be responsible for the
  custody and management thereof until the property is disposed of.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional responsibility

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In addition to being responsible for the custody and
  management of property referred to in subsections (1) and (2), the
  Minister shall be responsible, until the property is disposed of, for
  the custody and management of all proceeds of crime, offence-related
  property and property that was the subject of an application under
  section 83.14 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), that were
  forfeited to Her Majesty as a result of proceedings conducted by the
  Attorney General and that were not in the possession or under the
  control of the Minister prior to their forfeiture.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of other Acts

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section precludes the operation of
  the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the [Controlled Drugs and
  Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8), the [Cannabis
  Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) or any other Act of Parliament in respect of
  any property that is in the possession or under the control of the
  Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of property

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who has control of any property that is subject to a management
  order issued under subsection 83.13(2), 462.331(1) or 490.81(1) of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), subsection 15.1(1) of the [Controlled
  Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8), subsection 93(1) of the
  [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) or subsection 7(1) of this Act shall,
  as soon as practicable after the order is issued, transfer the control
  of the property to the Minister, except for any property or any part
  of the property that is needed as evidence or is necessary for the
  purposes of an investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister to report location

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister takes control of property pursuant
  to subsection (1) and detains the property in a location other than
  the location referred to in the report required to be filed pursuant
  to paragraph 462.32(4)(b) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the
  Minister shall prepare a report in the prescribed form identifying the
  location of the property and cause the report to be filed in the
  prescribed manner with the clerk of the court that issued the warrant.

## [Management Order]{.HTitleText1} {#h-445168 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for management order

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Attorney General, or any other person with the written consent of the
  Attorney General, may apply to any judge or justice for a management
  order in respect of any seized property, other than a [[controlled
  substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
  [Controlled Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8) or
  [[cannabis]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of
  the [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order under section 490 of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for a management order in respect of
  any seized property may be heard at the same time as an order is
  sought under paragraph 490(1)(b) of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46) in respect of the seized property.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Management order

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an
  application for a management order is made, the judge or justice
  hearing the application shall make an order allowing the Minister to
  take possession and control of, and to manage or otherwise deal with,
  the seized property referred to in the order if the judge or justice
  is of the opinion that the seized property may be required for the
  purposes of any provision respecting forfeiture in any Act of
  Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to manage

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The power of the Minister in respect of any seized
  property that is the subject of a management order includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the power to make an interlocutory sale of
    perishable or rapidly depreciating property;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the power to destroy, in accordance with
    subsections (2.1) to (2.4), property that has little or no value;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the power to have property, other than real
    property or a conveyance, forfeited to Her Majesty in accordance
    with subsection (2.5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for destruction order

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} Before the Minister destroys property that has
  little or no value, he or she shall apply to a court for a destruction
  order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2.2)]{.lawlabel} Before making a destruction order, a court shall
  require notice in accordance with subsection (2.3) to be given to and
  may hear any person who, in the court's opinion, appears to have a
  valid interest in the property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of giving notice

  [(2.3)]{.lawlabel} A notice shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be given in the manner that the court directs or
    that may be specified in the rules of the court; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specify the effective period of the notice that the
    court considers reasonable or that may be set out in the rules of
    the court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Destruction order

  [(2.4)]{.lawlabel} A court shall order that the property be destroyed
  if it is satisfied that the property has little or no financial or
  other value.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture order

  [(2.5)]{.lawlabel} On application by the Minister, a court shall order
  that the property, other than real property or a conveyance, be
  forfeited to Her Majesty and disposed of or otherwise dealt with in
  accordance with the law if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a notice is given or published in the manner that
    the court directs or that may be specified in the rules of the
    court;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the notice specifies a period of 60 days during
    which a person may make an application to the court asserting their
    interest in the property; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} during that period, no one makes such an
    application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When management order ceases to have
  effect

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A management order ceases to have effect when the
  property that is the subject of the management order is returned in
  accordance with the law, destroyed or forfeited to Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, if property that is the
  subject of a management order is sold, the management order applies to
  the net proceeds of the sale.

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

## [Powers of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-445210 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial powers

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** In carrying out the
purposes of this Act, the Minister may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide consultative and other services to law
  enforcement agencies in relation to the seizure or restraint of any
  property in connection with designated offences, or any property that
  is or may be proceeds of crime or offence-related property;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} provide consultative and other services to any
  person employed in the federal public administration or by a
  provincial or municipal authority in relation to the seizure,
  restraint, custody, management, forfeiture or disposal of property

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by means of or in respect of which an offence or
    violation is committed,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is used in any manner in connection with the
    commission of an offence or violation, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that is intended for use for the purpose of
    committing an offence or violation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the
  [Controlled Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8), the [Cannabis
  Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) and any other Act of Parliament, manage any
  property referred to in any of subsections 4(1) to (3) in the manner
  that the Minister considers appropriate including, without restricting
  the generality of the foregoing, by advancing money at a commercial
  rate of interest to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} maintain the ongoing operation of the property,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} satisfy the terms of any order concerning
    environmental, industrial, labour or property standards to which the
    property is subject, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} make improvements to the property to preserve the
    property and its economic worth;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} notwithstanding any other Act of Parliament, but
  subject to the regulations and to the provisions of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) relating to public money, dispose
  of any property referred to in any of subsections 4(1) to (3) that is
  forfeited to Her Majesty;

- [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} if property is forfeited to Her Majesty in right of
  a province, dispose of the property on behalf of Her Majesty in right
  of the province with the consent of the government of that province,
  and share the proceeds of disposition in accordance with directions
  given by that government;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} notwithstanding subsection 734.4(2) of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46) and sections 125 and 126 of the [Excise
  Act](/eng/acts/E-14), where property referred to in any of subsections
  4(1) to (3) is forfeited to Her Majesty, share the proceeds of
  disposition therefrom in accordance with this Act, the regulations and
  any agreement entered into pursuant to section 11;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} notwithstanding subsection 734.4(2) of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46) and sections 125 and 126 of the [Excise
  Act](/eng/acts/E-14), if a fine, or any portion of a fine, imposed
  under subsection 462.37(3) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) in
  relation to proceedings commenced at the instance of the Government of
  Canada is paid or recovered or if a penalty is paid under subsection
  18(2) of the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist
  Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501), share the amount of the fine or
  penalty in accordance with this Act, the regulations and any agreement

## [Sharing of Proceeds]{.HTitleText1} {#h-445226 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sharing within Canada

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a law enforcement agency in Canada has participated in the
  investigation of an offence that leads to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the forfeiture to Her Majesty of property under
    section 83.14, subsection 462.37(1), (2) or (2.01) or 462.38(2),
    subparagraph 462.43(c)(iii) or subsection 490.1(1) or 490.2(2) of
    the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), subsection 16(1) or 17(2) of
    the [Controlled Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8) or
    subsection 94(1) or 95(2) of the [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5),

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the forfeiture to Her Majesty pursuant to
    subsection 490(9) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) of any
    property that is or was the subject of a management order, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the imposition of a fine pursuant to subsection
    462.37(3) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) in relation to
    proceedings commenced at the instance of the Government of Canada,

  the Minister shall, in accordance with the regulations, share the
  proceeds of disposition of that forfeited property, or any amount paid
  or recovered on account of the fine, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering)
  and Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the participation of a law enforcement agency in
  Canada has led to the forfeiture to Her Majesty of property under the
  [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing
  Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501) or the payment of a penalty under subsection
  18(2) of that Act, the Minister shall, in accordance with the
  regulations, share the proceeds of disposition of that forfeited
  property or the penalty, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sharing outside Canada

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Attorney General may,
with the approval of the Governor in Council and in accordance with the
regulations, enter into an agreement with the government of any foreign
state respecting the reciprocal sharing of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proceeds of disposition of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} property forfeited to Her Majesty under section
    83.14, subsection 462.37(1), (2) or (2.01) or 462.38(2),
    subparagraph 462.43(c)(iii) or subsection 490.1(1) or 490.2(2) of
    the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), subsection 16(1) or 17(2) of
    the [Controlled Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8) or
    subsection 94(1) or 95(2) of the [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5),
    or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} property that is or was the subject of a
    management order and that was forfeited under subsection 490(9) of
    the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)

  and the proceeds arising from the disposition of property by that
  foreign state, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} amounts paid or recovered on account of fines imposed
  under subsection 462.37(3) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) in
  relation to proceedings commenced at the instance of the Government of
  Canada, penalties paid under subsection 18(2) of the [Proceeds of
  Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing
  Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501) and amounts paid or recovered on account of
  fines imposed in lieu of forfeiture under the laws of that foreign
  state,

if law enforcement agencies of that foreign state, or of Canada, as the
case may be, have participated in the investigation of the offence or
offences that led to the forfeiture of the property or the imposition of
the fine or if the law enforcement agencies\' participation led to the
forfeiture of the property or the payment of the penalty under the
[Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing
Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-445294 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advances are debts owing

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Advances made pursuant to
paragraph 9(b) and any interest thereon are debts owing to Her Majesty
and may be recovered as such in any court of competent jurisdiction or
in any manner provided for by law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnity

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the
regulations, the Minister may indemnify any person referred to in
paragraph 9(g) against any claim made against the person in respect of
anything done, or omitted to be done, in good faith by the person in
relation to any property referred to in any of subsections 4(1) to (3)
that is in the possession or under the control of the Minister.

## [Review]{.HTitleText1} {#h-445315 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review after three years

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  expiration of three years after the coming into force of this Act, the
  provisions contained herein shall be referred to such committee of the
  House of Commons, of the Senate, or of both Houses of Parliament as
  may be designated or established by Parliament for that purpose.
