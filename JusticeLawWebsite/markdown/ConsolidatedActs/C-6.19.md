[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in section 2 of the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11). ([ministre compétent]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the board of directors of the
    Corporation. ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Innovation
    Corporation continued under section
    5. ([Corporation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[federal institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - ::: hiddenMsg
      The following provision is not in force.
      :::

      ::: nif
      [(a)]{.lawlabel} a [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
      defined in section 2 of the [Financial Administration
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11); or
      :::

    - ::: hiddenMsg
      The following provision is not in force.
      :::

      ::: nif
      [(b)]{.lawlabel} a [[Crown corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
      defined in subsection 83(1) of that Act. ([institution
      fédérale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})
      :::

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Industry or,
    if another federal minister is designated under section 4, that
    minister.‍ ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of the
