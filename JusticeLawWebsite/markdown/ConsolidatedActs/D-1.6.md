## Depository Bills and Notes Act {#depository-bills-and-notes-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1998, c. 13
:::

Assented to 1998-06-11

An Act respecting depository bills and depository notes and to amend the
Financial Administration Act
::::

:::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-171632 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Depository Bills and Notes Act](/eng/acts/D-1.6).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-171635 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in this Act.

  [[acceptor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[acceptor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person to whom a
      depository bill is addressed and who signs
      it. ([accepteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[clearing house]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[clearing house]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation,
      association, partnership, agency or other entity that carries on
      in Canada the business of providing its participants with the
      clearing and settlement of transactions in securities that are
      deposited with it. ([chambre de compensation]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[depository bill]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[depository bill]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an order described
      in section 4. ([lettre de dépôt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[depository note]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[depository note]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a promise described
      in section 5. ([billet de dépôt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[drawer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[drawer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person who addresses a
      depository bill. ([tireur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[endorser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[endorser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who, in the case
      of a depository bill, signs the depository bill otherwise than as
      drawer or acceptor, or in the case of a depository note, signs the
      depository note otherwise than as
      maker. ([endosseur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[issue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[issue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the first delivery of a
      depository bill or note, complete in form, to the person to whom
      it is payable. ([émission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[maker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[maker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person who makes a
      depository note. ([souscripteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who has entered
      into a contract of membership with a clearing
      house. ([établissement participant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who signs a
      depository bill or note. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a natural person, a
      corporation, a trust, a partnership, a fund, an unincorporated
      association or organization, Her Majesty in right of Canada or of
      a province or the government of a foreign country or of any
      political subdivision of a foreign
      country. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a transfer, pledge,
      assignment or hypothecation of a depository bill or note or of an
      interest in a depository bill or
      note. ([opération]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deposit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, a depository bill or
  note is deposited with a clearing house if it is accepted for deposit
  by the clearing house to which it is payable and is in the possession
  of that clearing house or, subject to the instructions of the clearing
  house, in the possession of the clearing house's custodian, or a
  nominee of either of them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payable to clearing house

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, a depository bill or
  note is payable to a clearing house if it is made payable to the
  clearing house or its nominee, originally or by endorsement.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-171655 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Depository Bills and Notes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-171658 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Depository bill

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A depository bill is an
unconditional order in writing that is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} signed by the drawer and addressed to another person,
  requiring the person to whom it is addressed to pay, at a fixed or
  determinable future time, a sum certain in money to, or to the order
  of, a specified person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} accepted unconditionally by the signature of the
  person to whom it is addressed;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} marked prominently and legibly on its face and within
  its text, at or before the time of issue, with the words "This is a
  depository bill subject to the [Depository Bills and Notes
  Act](/eng/acts/D-1.6)" or "Lettre de dépôt assujettie à la [Loi sur
  les lettres et billets de dépôt](/eng/acts/D-1.6)";

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} not marked with any words prohibiting negotiation,
  transfer or assignment of it or of an interest in it;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} made payable, originally or by endorsement, to a
  clearing house; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} deposited with the clearing house to which it is made
  payable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Depository note

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A depository note is an
unconditional promise in writing that is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} signed by the maker, promising to pay, at a fixed or
  determinable future time, a sum certain in money to, or to the order
  of, a specified person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} marked prominently and legibly on its face and within
  its text, at or before the time of issue, with the words "This is a
  depository note subject to the [Depository Bills and Notes
  Act](/eng/acts/D-1.6)" or "Billet de dépôt assujetti à la [Loi sur les
  lettres et billets de dépôt](/eng/acts/D-1.6)";

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} not marked with any words prohibiting negotiation,
  transfer or assignment of it or of an interest in it;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} made payable, originally or by endorsement, to a
  clearing house; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} deposited with the clearing house to which it is made
  payable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment from assets of partnership, etc.

**[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 4 and 5, an order or a promise to pay is not conditional by
reason only that it is limited to payment from the assets of a
partnership, unincorporated association, trust or estate.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 9, s. 581
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of [Bills of Exchange
Act](/eng/acts/B-4)

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The [Bills of Exchange
Act](/eng/acts/B-4) does not apply in respect of depository bills and
notes.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signature presumed valid

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** In the absence of evidence
to the contrary, a signature on a depository bill or note is presumed to
be genuine and authorized.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transactions effected by entries in
records of clearing house

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  transaction related to a depository bill or note recorded by a
  clearing house, or related to an interest in such a depository bill or
  note, may be effected by the making of appropriate entries in the
  records of the clearing house.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of transactions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The transaction has the same effect as a delivery of
  a bill of exchange in bearer form under the [Bills of Exchange
  Act](/eng/acts/B-4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicable law

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The transaction, including the time it takes effect,
  is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction agreed to by the clearing
  house and its participants.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Possession

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A participant in whose favour the transaction is
  effected is deemed to be in possession of the depository bill or note.
  If the transaction relates to an interest in a depository bill or
  note, the participant in whose favour the transaction is effected is
  deemed to be in possession of a depository bill or note representing
  the interest.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fungible bulk of depository bills or
notes

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Transactions related to like
  depository bills or notes, or related to interests in like depository
  bills or notes, may be recorded by a clearing house as part of a
  fungible bulk and the entries may refer merely to a quantity of like
  depository bills or notes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entries on net basis

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When there is more than one transaction in depository
  bills or notes that are part of a fungible bulk, a clearing house may
  make entries in respect of those transactions on a net basis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fungible bulk

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, "fungible bulk"
  means a bulk of depository bills or notes of which any unit is, by
  usage of trade, the equivalent of any other unit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Errors in records

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Errors in the records of
a clearing house do not affect the validity or effect of the entries or
the liabilities or obligations of the clearing house to persons
adversely affected by the errors.

## [Liabilities and Actions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-171700 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of acceptor

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The acceptor of a
depository bill is liable to provide the clearing house to which it is
payable with funds so that the clearing house can pay, on the depository
bill's due date and in accordance with its terms, the participants who
have an interest in the depository bill.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of drawer

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If a depository bill is
dishonoured by the acceptor, the drawer is liable to pay the amount of
the depository bill to the clearing house to which it is payable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of endorser of depository bill

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** If a depository bill is
dishonoured by the drawer and acceptor, every endorser is jointly and
severally liable to pay the amount of the depository bill to the
clearing house to which it is payable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of maker

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The maker of a depository
note is liable to provide the clearing house to which it is payable with
funds so that the clearing house can pay, on the depository note's due
date or dates, the principal amount of the depository note, and the
interest payable by its terms, to the participants who have an interest
in the depository note.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of endorser of depository note

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** If a depository note is
dishonoured by the maker, every endorser is jointly and severally liable
to pay the principal amount of the depository note, and the interest
payable by its terms, to the clearing house to which it is payable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment from assets of partnership, etc.

**[[15.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 13, 14 and 15, a depository bill or a depository note whose
order or promise to pay is limited to payment from the assets of a
partnership, unincorporated association, trust or estate is not
dishonoured by its acceptor, drawer or maker if that person provides
funds to the clearing house to which it is payable in accordance with
the order or promise to pay and section 17.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 9, s. 582
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability in absence of contract

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A party is liable to pay
in accordance with sections 11 to 15 whether or not the depository bill
or note constitutes a binding contractual obligation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discharge of liability

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A party who is liable to
pay in accordance with sections 11 to 15 and who makes final and
irrevocable payment of the amounts owing to the clearing house on the
depository bill's or note's due date or dates is discharged of that
liability to pay.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of clearing house

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  clearing house must enforce the payment of a depository bill or note
  payable to it and it must do so for the account and benefit and at the
  expense of each participant who has an interest in the depository bill
  or note.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A clearing house need not enforce the payment of a
  depository bill or note if its contract of membership with its
  participants provides that it is not required to do so or that the
  duty to enforce is conditional on the fulfilment of conditions
  specified in the contract and those conditions have not been
  fulfilled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action in name of clearing house

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a clearing house that is required to enforce the
  payment of a depository bill or note does not do so within a
  reasonable time, any participant who has an interest in the depository
  bill or note may bring an action in the name of the clearing house.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No beneficial interest

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A clearing house has no
beneficial interest in a depository bill or note by reason only of the
fact that the depository bill or note is payable to it, but nothing in
this Act precludes the clearing house from acquiring, in its own right,
a depository bill or note or any interest in a depository bill or note.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No defences

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  clearing house holds a depository bill or note payable to it, and any
  money received as payment of principal or interest with respect to the
  depository bill or note, free from any defence that affects the
  depository bill or note or any defence that affects the liability of
  any party to it, except a defence that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the party liable to pay it is
    forged or unauthorized; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the depository bill or note is counterfeit or has
    been materially altered without the consent of the party liable to
    pay it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participants

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) applies to a participant who brings an
  action in the name of a clearing house in accordance with subsection
  18(3).
