## Canada Emergency Student Benefit Act {#canada-emergency-student-benefit-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2020, c. 7
:::

Assented to 2020-05-01

An Act respecting Canada emergency student benefits (coronavirus disease
2019)
::::

::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada Emergency Student Benefit Act](/eng/acts/C-3.75).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Employment
    and Social Development. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[student]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[student]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is a Canadian
    citizen, a person registered as an Indian under the [Indian
    Act](/eng/acts/I-5), a [[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
    as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee
    Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) or a protected person within the
    meaning of subsection 95(2) of that Act and who

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is enrolled, at any time between December 1, 2019
      and August 31, 2020, in a post-secondary educational program that
      leads to a degree, diploma or certificate;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has graduated from secondary school in 2020, has
      applied for enrollment in such a post-secondary educational
      program that is scheduled to begin before February 1, 2021 and
      plans to enroll in the program if their application is accepted;
      or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is a member of a class of persons that is
      prescribed by regulation. ([étudiant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period of seven consecutive
    days beginning on and including Sunday. ([semaine]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations --- definition of
[[student]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** With the consent of the
Minister of Finance, the Minister may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the meaning of the term "post-secondary
  educational program" for the purposes of the definition
  [[student]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing classes of persons for the purposes of
  paragraph (c) of that definition.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of benefit

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must pay a
Canada emergency student benefit to a student who makes an application
under section 5 and who is eligible for the benefit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  student may, in the form and manner established by the Minister, apply
  for a Canada emergency student benefit for any four-week period
  falling within the period prescribed by regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attestation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A student who applies on the basis that they are
  seeking work, whether as an employee or in self-employment, but are
  unable to find it must, in their application, attest to the fact that
  they are seeking work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No student is permitted to file an application after
  September 30, 2020, and the Minister must not pay a Canada student
  emergency benefit to a student who files an application after that
  day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An applicant must provide the Minister with any
  information that the Minister may require in respect of the
  application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} With the consent of the Minister of Finance, the
  Minister may, by regulation, prescribe periods for the purposes of
  subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  student is eligible for a Canada emergency student benefit if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for reasons related to the coronavirus disease 2019
    and whether as an employee or in self-employment, they are unable to
    work, are seeking work and unable to find it, or are working but are
    paid less than the amount determined under the regulations, during
    the four-week period for which they apply for the benefit; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they do not, in respect of any part of that
    four-week period, receive

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} subject to the regulations, income from
      employment or self-employment,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} [[benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined
      in subsection 2(1) of the [Employment Insurance
      Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6), or an employment insurance emergency
      response benefit referred to in section 153.7 of that Act,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} allowances, money or other benefits that would
      be paid to the student under a provincial plan because of
      pregnancy or in respect of the care by the student of one or more
      of their new-born children or one or more children placed with
      them for the purpose of adoption,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an income support payment under the [Canada
      Emergency Response Benefit Act](/eng/acts/C-3.7), or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any other income that is prescribed by
      regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} With the consent of the Minister of Finance, the
  Minister may make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the meaning of terms referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a) and the amount referred to in that paragraph;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} excluding income from the application of
    subparagraph (1)(b)(i); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing any other income for the purposes of
    subparagraph (1)(b)(v).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information --- employment
  opportunities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is to make available to eligible
  students information about employment opportunities through a
  government-managed job posting system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of benefit

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of a Canada emergency student benefit for a week is the amount
  determined for that week under the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} With the consent of the Minister of Finance, the
  Minister may make regulations respecting the amount of a Canada
  emergency student benefit for a week specified in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distinguishing --- classes

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Regulations made under subsection (2) may distinguish
  among different classes of students.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum number of weeks

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  maximum number of weeks for which a student may receive Canada
  emergency student benefits is the number of weeks that is determined
  under the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} With the consent of the Minister of Finance, the
  Minister may make regulations respecting the number of weeks for the
  purpose of subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retroactive effect

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Regulations made under this
Act may, if they so provide, have retroactive effect.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Social Insurance Number

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is
authorized to collect and use, for the purposes of the administration
and enforcement of this Act, the Social Insurance Number of a person who
makes an application under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information and documents

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, for any
purpose related to verifying compliance or preventing non-compliance
with this Act, by notice served personally or by confirmed delivery
service, require that any person provide any information or document
within the reasonable time that is stated in the notice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefits cannot be charged, etc.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A Canada emergency
student benefit

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not subject to the operation of any law relating
  to bankruptcy or insolvency;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cannot be assigned, charged, attached or given as
  security;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} cannot be retained by way of deduction, set-off or
  compensation under any Act of Parliament other than this Act; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} is not garnishable moneys for the purposes of the
  [Family Orders and Agreements Enforcement Assistance
  Act](/eng/acts/F-1.4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of erroneous payment or
overpayment

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister determines that a person has been paid a Canada emergency
  student benefit to which the person is not entitled, or an amount in
  excess of the amount of such a benefit to which the person is
  entitled, the person must repay the amount of the benefit or the
  excess amount, as the case may be, as soon as is feasible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery as debt due to Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the erroneous payment or overpayment,
  as determined by the Minister, constitutes a debt due to Her Majesty
  in right of Canada, as of the day on which it was paid, that may be
  recovered by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of default

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amount of any debt referred to in subsection (2)
  may be certified by the Minister, and registration of the certificate
  in the Federal Court has the same effect as a judgment of that Court
  for the amount specified in the certificate and all related
  registration costs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation or prescription period

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) to (7),
  no action or proceedings are to be taken to recover money owing under
  this Act after the expiry of the six-year limitation or prescription
  period that begins on the day on which the money becomes due and
  payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction, set-off or compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Money owing by a person under this Act may be
  recovered at any time by way of deduction from, set-off against or
  compensation against any sum of money, including a Canada emergency
  student benefit under this Act, that may be due or payable by Her
  Majesty in right of Canada to the person, other than an amount payable
  under section 122.61 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment of liability

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person's liability for money owing under this
  Act is acknowledged in accordance with subsection (5), the time during
  which the limitation or prescription period has run before the
  acknowledgment does not count in the calculation of that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment after expiry of
  limitation or prescription period

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a person's liability for money owing under this
  Act is acknowledged in accordance with subsection (5) after the expiry
  of the limitation or prescription period, an action or proceedings to
  recover the money may, subject to subsections (3) and (6), be brought
  within six years after the date of the acknowledgment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of acknowledgment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An acknowledgment of liability means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a promise to pay the money owing, made by the
    person or their agent, mandatary or other representative;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgment of the money owing, made by the
    person or their agent, mandatary or other representative, whether or
    not a promise to pay can be implied from it and whether or not it
    contains a refusal to pay;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a part payment by the person or their agent,
    mandatary or other representative of any money owing; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgment of the money owing, made in the
    course of proceedings under the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency
    Act](/eng/acts/B-3) or any other legislation dealing with the
    payment of debts by the person, their agent, mandatary or other
    representative or the trustee or administrator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation or prescription period
  suspended

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The running of a limitation or prescription period in
  respect of money owing under this Act is suspended during any period
  in which it is prohibited to commence or continue an action or other
  proceedings against the person to recover money owing under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement proceedings

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply in respect of an action
  or proceedings relating to the execution, renewal or enforcement of a
  judgment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No interest payable

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** No interest is payable on
any amount owing to Her Majesty in right of Canada under this Act as a
result of an erroneous payment or overpayment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt of benefits, allowances or money

- **[[15.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, for any four-week period, the
  Minister determines that a student received a Canada emergency student
  benefit for which they were not eligible by reason only that they
  received one or more payments of benefits, allowances or money
  referred to in subparagraph 6(1)(b)(ii) or (iii), the Minister is
  deemed to have determined under subsection 13(2) that the amount that
  the worker must repay under subsection 13(1) is, despite subsection
  13(1), the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#1368284 .Subsection}
  A × (B ÷ 4)

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$2,000, in the case of a student with a
        dependant or a student with a disability, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$1,250, in any other case; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of weeks for which the student received such
      benefits, allowances or money during that four-week period.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of an
  employment insurance emergency response benefit received by the
  student if the Canada Employment Insurance Commission informs the
  Minister that subsection (1) should not apply in respect of that
  benefit and, if the Commission does so, the student is, despite
  subparagraph 6(1)(b)(ii), deemed to have been eligible to receive the
  income support payment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parliamentary review

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** No later than September
30, 2021, a comprehensive review of the provisions and operation of this
Act is to be undertaken and completed by a committee of the Senate, of
the House of Commons or of both Houses of Parliament that may be
designated or established by the Senate or the House of Commons, or by
both Houses of Parliament, as the case may be, for that purpose.
