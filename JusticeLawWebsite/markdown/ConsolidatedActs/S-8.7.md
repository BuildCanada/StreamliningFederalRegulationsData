## Sex Offender Information Registration Act {#sex-offender-information-registration-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2004, c. 10

offenders, to amend the Criminal Code and to make consequential
amendments to other Acts

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-445935 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Sex Offender Information Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7).

## [Purpose and Principles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-445938 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  purpose of this Act is to help police services prevent and investigate
  crimes of a sexual nature by requiring the registration of certain
  information relating to sex offenders.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principles

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Act shall be carried out in recognition of, and
  in accordance with, the following principles:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the interest of protecting society through the
    effective prevention and investigation of crimes of a sexual nature,
    police services must have rapid access to certain information
    relating to sex offenders;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the collection and registration of accurate
    information on an ongoing basis is the most effective way of
    ensuring that such information is current and reliable; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the privacy interests of sex offenders and the
    public interest in their rehabilitation and reintegration into the
    community as law-abiding citizens require that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the information be collected only to enable
      police services to prevent or investigate crimes of a sexual
      nature, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} access to the information, and use and
      disclosure of it, be restricted.

- 2010, c. 17, s. 28

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-445951 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[database]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[database]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the database that contains
      the information that is registered under this Act. ([banque de
      données]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[finding of not criminally responsible on account of mental disorder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[finding of not criminally responsible on account of mental
      disorder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a verdict of not criminally
      responsible on account of mental disorder within the meaning of
      subsection 672.1(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or a
      finding of not responsible on account of mental disorder within
      the meaning of subsection 2(1) of the [National Defence
      Act](/eng/acts/N-5), as the case may be. ([verdict de
      non-responsabilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes characteristics
      recorded and photographs taken under subsection 5(3) and
      fingerprints taken under subsection
      9(2). ([renseignements]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[main residence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[main residence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the place in Canada
      where a person lives most often or, if there is no such place, the
      place in Canada where they may be found most often. ([résidence
      principale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[member of a police service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[member of a police service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an officer or non-commissioned member of the
        Canadian Forces who is appointed for the purposes of section 156
        of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5); and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in an area in which an aboriginal police
        service is responsible for policing, a member of that police
        service. ([membre d'un service de police]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[Ontario Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Ontario Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 490.011(1) of the [Criminal
      Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([loi ontarienne]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an order under section 490.012
      of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or section 227.01 of the
      [National Defence
      Act](/eng/acts/N-5). ([ordonnance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[person who collects information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[person who collects information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      person who is authorized to collect information under paragraph
      18(1)(b) or subsection 19(1) of this Act or paragraph 227.2(c) of
      the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5). ([préposé à la
      collecte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[person who registers information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[person who registers information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      person who is authorized to register information under paragraph
      18(1)(c) or subsection 19(1) of this Act or paragraph 227.2(d) of
      the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5). ([préposé à
      l'enregistrement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[registration centre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[registration centre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a place that is
      designated as a registration centre under paragraph 18(1)(d) or
      subsection 19(1) of this Act or paragraph 227.2(e) of the
      [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5). ([bureau
      d'inscription]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[retained]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[retained]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means retained under a contract
      for services, whether the contract is entered into with an
      individual, or with their employer or another person to whom the
      individual provides services. ([agent
      contractuel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[secondary residence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[secondary residence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a place in
      Canada, other than a main residence, where a person regularly
      lives. ([résidence secondaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[sex offender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[sex offender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is subject
      to an order or to an obligation under section 490.019 or 490.02901
      of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), section 227.06 of the
      [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) or section 36.1 of the
      [International Transfer of Offenders
      Act](/eng/acts/I-20.6). ([délinquant sexuel]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[sexual offence against a child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[sexual offence against a child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a designated offence as defined in subsection
        490.011(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) that is
        committed against a person who is under 18 years of age and as a
        result of which the offender is required to comply with this
        Act; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an offence that is committed outside Canada
        against a person who is under 18 years of age and as a result of
        which the offender is required to comply with this
        Act. ([infraction sexuelle visant un enfant]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, a crime is of a sexual
  nature if it consists of one or more acts that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are either sexual in nature or committed with the
    intent to commit an act or acts that are sexual in nature; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} constitute an offence.

## [Obligations of Sex Offenders]{.HTitleText1} {#h-445979 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First obligation to report

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who is subject to an order shall report to a registration centre
  referred to in section 7.1 within seven days --- or, if they are
  required to report to a registration centre designated under the
  [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), within 15 days --- after

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the order is made, if they are convicted of the
    offence in connection with which the order is made and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} they are not given a custodial sentence,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} they are ordered to serve a sentence of
      imprisonment intermittently under subsection 732(1) of the
      [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} they are the subject of a conditional sentence
      order made under section 742.1 of the [Criminal
      Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they receive an absolute or conditional discharge
    under Part XX.1 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), if they are
    found not criminally responsible on account of mental disorder for
    the offence in connection with which the order is made;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} they receive an absolute or conditional discharge
    or are released from custody under Division 7 of Part III of the
    [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), if they are found not
    criminally responsible on account of mental disorder for the offence
    in connection with which the order is made;

  - [(b.2)]{.lawlabel} the imprisonment or detention to which they are
    sentenced for the offence in connection with which the order is made
    is suspended under section 215 or 216 of the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} they are released from custody pending the
    determination of an appeal relating to the offence in connection
    with which the order is made; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} they are released from custody after serving the
    custodial portion of a sentence for the offence in connection with
    which the order is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First obligation to report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who is subject to an obligation under
  section 490.019 or 490.02901 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46),
  section 227.06 of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) or section
  36.1 of the [International Transfer of Offenders
  Act](/eng/acts/I-20.6) shall report to a registration centre referred
  to in section 7.1

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if they are not in custody on the day on which they
    become subject to the obligation, within seven days --- or, if they
    are required to report to a registration centre designated under the
    [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), within 15 days --- after that
    day; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, within seven days --- or, if
    they are required to report to a registration centre designated
    under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), within 15 days ---
    after

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} they receive an absolute or conditional discharge
      under Part XX.1 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46),

    - [(i.1)]{.lawlabel} they receive an absolute or conditional
      discharge or are released from custody under Division 7 of Part
      III of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5),

    - [(i.2)]{.lawlabel} an imprisonment or a detention to which they
      are sentenced is suspended under section 215 or 216 of the
      [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} they are released from custody pending the
      determination of an appeal, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} they are released from custody after serving
      the custodial portion of a sentence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of reporting

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a sex offender is required to report to a
  registration centre designated under this Act, they shall report in
  person. If they are required to report to a registration centre
  designated under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), they shall
  report in person unless regulations are made under paragraph 227.2(a)
  of that Act, in which case they shall report in accordance with those
  regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance before leaving Canada

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A sex offender shall not leave Canada before they
  report under this section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent obligation to report

- **[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  sex offender shall subsequently report to the registration centre
  referred to in section 7.1,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within seven days after they change their main
    residence or any secondary residence or, if they are required to
    report to a registration centre designated under the [National
    Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), within 15 days after the change;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within seven days after they change their given
    name or surname or, if they are required to report to a registration
    centre designated under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5),
    within 15 days after the change;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} within seven days after they receive a driver's
    licence or, if they are required to report to a registration centre
    designated under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), within
    15 days after they receive it;

  - [(b.2)]{.lawlabel} within seven days after they receive a passport
    or, if they are required to report to a registration centre
    designated under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), within
    15 days after they receive it; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at any time between 11 months and one year after
    they last reported to a registration centre under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of reporting

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a sex offender is required to report to a
  registration centre designated under this Act, they shall report in
  person or in accordance with regulations made under paragraph 18(1)(a)
  or subsection 19(1). If they are required to report to a registration
  centre designated under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5),
  they shall report in person unless regulations are made under
  paragraph 227.2(a) of that Act, in which case they shall report in
  accordance with those regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to provide information

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  sex offender reports to a registration centre, they shall provide the
  following information to a person who collects information at the
  registration centre:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their given name and surname, and every alias that
    they use;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} their date of birth and gender;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the address of their main residence and every
    secondary residence or, if there is no such address, the location of
    that place;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the address of every place at which they are
    employed or retained or are engaged on a volunteer basis --- or, if
    there is no address, the location of that place --- the name of
    their employer or the person who engages them on a volunteer basis
    or retains them and the type of work that they do there;

  - [(d.1)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, their status as an officer or a
    non-commissioned member of the Canadian Forces within the meaning of
    subsection 2(1) of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) and the
    address and telephone number of their unit within the meaning of
    that subsection;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the address of every educational institution at
    which they are enrolled or, if there is no such address, the
    location of that place;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a telephone number at which they may be reached, if
    any, for every place referred to in paragraphs (c) and (d), and the
    number of every mobile telephone or pager in their possession;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} their height and weight and a description of every
    physical distinguishing mark that they have;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the licence plate number, make, model, body type,
    year of manufacture and colour of the motor vehicles that are
    registered in their name or that they use regularly;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the licence number and the name of the issuing
    jurisdiction of every driver's licence that they hold; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the passport number and the name of the issuing
    jurisdiction of every passport that they hold.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a sex offender reports to a registration centre,
  the person who collects the information from them may ask them when
  and where they were convicted of, or found not criminally responsible
  on account of mental disorder for, an offence in connection with an
  order or with an obligation under section 490.019 or 490.02901 of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), section 227.06 of the [National
  Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) or section 36.1 of the [International
  Transfer of Offenders Act](/eng/acts/I-20.6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When a sex offender reports to a registration centre
  in person, the person who collects the information referred to in
  subsection (1) may record any observable characteristic that may
  assist in identification of the sex offender, including their eye
  colour and hair colour, and may require that their photograph be
  taken.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of absence

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (1.1), a sex offender other than one who is referred to
  in subsection (1.01) shall notify a person who collects information at
  the registration centre referred to in section 7.1

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least 14 days before the sex offender's
    departure --- of the dates of their departure and return and of
    every address or, if the address is unknown, every location at which
    they expect to stay in Canada or outside Canada --- if they expect
    not to be at their main residence or any of their secondary
    residences for a period of seven or more consecutive days;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within seven days after their departure --- of the
    date of their return and of every address or, if the address is
    unknown, every location at which they are staying in Canada or
    outside Canada --- if they decide, after departure, not to be at
    their main residence or any of their secondary residences for a
    period of seven or more consecutive days or if they have not given a
    notification required under paragraph (a); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} before departure or, if it is later, within seven
    days after the day on which the change is made --- of a change in
    address, location or date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sex offender convicted of sex offence
  against child

  [(1.01)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (1.1), a sex offender who is
  convicted of a sexual offence against a child shall notify a person
  who collects information at the registration centre referred to in
  section 7.1

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least 14 days before the sex offender's
    departure --- of the dates of their departure and return and of
    every address or, if the address is unknown, every location at which
    they expect to stay in Canada --- if they expect not to be at their
    main residence or any of their secondary residences for a period of
    seven or more consecutive days;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at least 14 days before their departure --- of the
    dates of their departure and return and of every address or, if the
    address is unknown, every location at which they expect to stay
    outside Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} within seven days after their departure --- of the
    date of their return and of every address or, if the address is
    unknown, every location at which they are staying in Canada --- if
    they decide, after departure, not to be at their main residence or
    any of their secondary residences for a period of seven or more
    consecutive days or if they have not given a notification required
    under paragraph (a);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} without delay, after their departure --- of the
    date of their return and of every address or, if the address is
    unknown, every location at which they are staying outside Canada ---
    if they decide, after departure, to extend their stay beyond the
    date of return that they indicated in the notification they gave
    under paragraph (b) or if they have not given a notification under
    paragraph (b); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} of a change in address, location or date, before
    their departure or

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the change is made after their departure and
      they are staying in Canada, within seven days after the date on
      which the change is made, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the change is made after their departure and
      they are staying outside Canada, without delay after the date on
      which the change is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(1.02)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(a) or (1.01)(a) or (b), as
  the case may be, a sex offender who has a reasonable excuse for not
  complying with the requirement to give at least 14 days' notice shall
  notify a person who collects information at the registration centre
  referred to in section 7.1 as soon as feasible before their departure
  of the dates of their departure and return and of every address or, if
  the address is unknown, every location at which they expect to stay in
  Canada or outside Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian Forces

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} A sex offender who is required to notify a person
  who collects information at a registration centre designated under the
  [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) and who requests the Chief of
  the Defence Staff to make a determination under section 227.16 of that
  Act shall provide the information relating to the operation within
  seven days after the date of their departure unless the determination
  is made during that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of notification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a sex offender is required to provide notification
  to a registration centre designated under this Act, they shall provide
  the notification by registered mail or in accordance with regulations
  made under paragraph 18(1)(a) or subsection 19(1). If they are
  required to provide notification to a registration centre designated
  under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), they shall provide
  the notification by registered mail unless regulations are made under
  paragraph 227.2(a) of that Act, in which case they shall provide the
  notification in accordance with those regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization for research

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police may authorize a
  person to consult information that is registered in the database,
  compare the information with other information or, by electronic
  means, combine the information with, or link it to, any other
  information contained in a computer system within the meaning of
  subsection 342.1(2) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), for
  research or statistical purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall not provide the authorization
  unless the Commissioner

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is satisfied that those purposes cannot reasonably
    be accomplished without consulting the information or without
    comparing or combining the information with, or linking it to, the
    other information, as the case may be; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} obtains from the person a written undertaking that
    no subsequent disclosure of the information or of any information
    resulting from the comparison or combination of the information
    with, or the linking of the information to, other information will
    be made, or be allowed to be made, in a form that could reasonably
    be expected to identify any individual to whom it relates.

## [Authority To Collect or Disclose Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-446239 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Correctional Service of Canada

- **[[15.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Correctional Service of Canada
  may disclose to a person who registers information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which a sex offender is received into a
    penitentiary as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Corrections and
    Conditional Release Act](/eng/acts/C-44.6);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a sex offender is expected to be temporarily
    outside penitentiary for seven or more days, the days on which they
    are expected to be outside, the days on which they are outside, the
    address or location at which they are expected to stay and the
    address or location at which they stay; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date of a sex offender's release or discharge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial correctional authority

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person in charge of a provincial correctional
  facility may disclose to a person who registers information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which a sex offender is received into
    the facility;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a sex offender is expected to be temporarily
    outside the facility for seven or more days, the days on which they
    are expected to be outside, the days on which they are outside, the
    address or location at which they are expected to stay and the
    address or location at which they stay; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date of a sex offender's release or discharge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian Forces

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person in charge of a service prison or detention
  barrack as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [National Defence
  Act](/eng/acts/N-5) may disclose to a person who registers information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which a sex offender is received into
    the prison or barrack;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a sex offender is expected to be temporarily
    outside the prison or barrack for seven or more days, the days on
    which they are expected to be outside, the days on which they are
    outside, the address or location at which they are expected to stay
    and the address or location at which they stay; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date of a sex offender's release or discharge.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2010, c. 17, s. 43
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canada Border Services Agency ---
collection of information

- **[[15.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Canada Border Services Agency
  may assist a member or an employee of, or a person retained by, a
  police service in the prevention or investigation of a crime of a
  sexual nature or an offence under section 490.031 or 490.0311 of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or in the laying of a charge for such
  an offence by collecting the information disclosed to it under
  paragraph 16(4)(j.2) or (j.3) as well as the following information
  with respect to any sex offender who is the subject of a disclosure
  made under those paragraphs:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of their departure from Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date of their return to Canada; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} every address or location at which they have stayed
    outside Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canada Border Services Agency ---
  disclosure of information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Canada Border Services Agency may, in assisting
  the member or employee of, or person retained by, a police service
  referred to in subsection (1), disclose to the Commissioner of the
  Royal Canadian Mounted Police any information collected under
  paragraphs (1)(a) to (c).

- 2015, c. 23, s. 27

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-446268 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unauthorized persons

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall exercise any function or perform any duty under this Act
  that they are not authorized under this Act to exercise or perform.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unauthorized consultation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall consult any information that is
  collected under this Act or registered in the database, unless they
  are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member or employee of, or a person retained by, a
    police service who consults the information for the purpose of
    preventing or investigating a crime of a sexual nature or an offence
    under section 490.031, 490.0311 or 490.0312 of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a member or employee of, or a person retained by, a
    police service in the province in which a sex offender's main
    residence is located who consults the information to verify
    compliance by the sex offender with an order or with an obligation
    under section 490.019 or 490.02901 of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46), section 227.06 of the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5) or section 36.1 of the [International Transfer
    of Offenders Act](/eng/acts/I-20.6);

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} a person who collects information at a
    registration centre designated under the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5) who consults the information to verify
    compliance by a sex offender who is subject to the Code of Service
    Discipline --- or who is an officer, or non-commissioned member, of
    the primary reserve as defined in section 227 of the [National
    Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) --- with an order or with an obligation
    under section 490.019 or 490.02901 of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46), section 227.06 of the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5) or section 36.1 of the [International Transfer
    of Offenders Act](/eng/acts/I-20.6);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who collects or registers information who
    consults the information in order to exercise the functions or
    perform the duties assigned to them under an Act of Parliament;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a person who is authorized under section 13 to
    consult information that is registered in the database for research
    or statistical purposes and who does so for those purposes;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Commissioner of the Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police or a person authorized by the Commissioner who consults
    information that is collected under this Act or registered in the
    database in order to perform the duties of the Commissioner under
    this Act, under subsection 490.03(1) or (2) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or under subsection 227.18(1) or 227.19(1) of
    the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5); or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a member or employee of, or a person retained by,
    the Royal Canadian Mounted Police who is authorized to consult the
    information for the purpose of administering the database and who
    does so for that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unauthorized comparison of information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall compare any information that is
  collected under this Act or registered in the database with any other
  information unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information was consulted in accordance with
    paragraph (2)(a) and they compare it with other information for the
    purpose of preventing or investigating a crime of a sexual nature;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information was consulted in accordance with
    paragraph (2)(b) or (b.1) and they compare it with other information
    for the purpose of verifying compliance by the sex offender with an
    order or with an obligation under section 490.019 or 490.02901 of
    the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), section 227.06 of the [National
    Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) or section 36.1 of the [International
    Transfer of Offenders Act](/eng/acts/I-20.6) or for the purpose of
    preventing or investigating an offence under section 490.031,
    490.0311 or 490.0312 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) --- or
    an offence under any of those provisions that is punishable under
    section 130 of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) --- or an
    offence under section 119.1 of that Act;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} the information was collected under subsection
    15.2(1) and they compare it with other information for the purpose
    of assisting a member or an employee of, or a person retained by, a
    police service in the prevention or investigation of a crime of a
    sexual nature or an offence under section 490.031 or 490.0311 of the
    [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or in the laying of a charge for
    such an offence; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} they compare the information in accordance with an
    authorization under section 13.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unauthorized combination or linking of
  information

  [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} No person shall, by electronic means, combine any
  information that is collected under this Act or registered in the
  database with, or link it to, any other information contained in a
  computer system within the meaning of subsection 342.1(2) of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they combine the information that is registered in
    the database with, or link it to, information contained in the sex
    offender registry established under the Ontario Act, for the purpose
    of registering information under section 8, 8.1 or 10;

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} the information was consulted in accordance with
    paragraph (2)(a) and they combine it with, or link it to, law
    enforcement information for the purpose of preventing or
    investigating a crime of a sexual nature;

  - [(a.2)]{.lawlabel} the information was consulted in accordance with
    paragraph (2)(b) or (b.1) and they combine it with, or link it to,
    law enforcement information for the purpose of verifying the sex
    offender's compliance with an order or with an obligation under
    section 490.019 or 490.02901 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46),
    section 227.06 of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) or
    section 36.1 of the [International Transfer of Offenders
    Act](/eng/acts/I-20.6) or for the purpose of preventing or
    investigating an offence under section 490.031 or 490.0311 of the
    [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), an offence under either of those
    provisions that is punishable under section 130 of the [National
    Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) or an offence under section 119.1 of
    that Act;

  - [(a.3)]{.lawlabel} the information was collected under subsection
    15.2(1) and they combine it with information contained in a computer
    system of the Canada Border Services Agency for the purpose of
    assisting a member or an employee of, or a person retained by, a
    police service in the prevention or investigation of a crime of a
    sexual nature or an offence under section 490.031 or 490.0311 of the
    [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or in the laying of a charge for
    such an offence; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they combine or link information in accordance with
    an authorization under section 13.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unauthorized disclosure

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall disclose any information that is
  collected under this Act or registered in the database or the fact
  that information relating to a person is collected under this Act or
  registered in the database, or allow it to be disclosed,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} unless the disclosure is to the sex offender, or
    the person served with a notice under section 490.021 of the
    [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or section 227.08 of the [National
    Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), to whom the information relates;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} unless the disclosure is expressly authorized under
    this Act, the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or the [National
    Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} unless the disclosure is to a member or an employee
    of, or a person retained by, a police service and is necessary

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to enable them to investigate an offence under
      section 17 or to lay a charge for such an offence,

    - [(i.1)]{.lawlabel} to enable them to verify the sex offender's
      compliance with section 5,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to enable them to prevent or investigate a crime
      of a sexual nature, an offence under section 119.1 of the
      [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), an offence under section
      490.031 or 490.0311 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or an
      offence under either of those provisions that is punishable under
      section 130 of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) or to
      enable them to lay a charge for such an offence,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to enable them to investigate a criminal
      offence or a service offence within the meaning of subsection 2(1)
      of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) or to lay a charge
      for such an offence, as long as the investigation or charge
      results from an investigation referred to in subparagraph (ii), or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} to enable them to obtain a warrant under
      subsection 490.03121(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) and
      execute the warrant;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} unless the disclosure is to a prosecutor and is
    necessary to enable the prosecutor to determine whether a charge for
    an offence resulting from an investigation referred to in
    paragraph (c) should be laid;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} unless the disclosure is to a person who is
    responsible under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) for
    laying, referring or preferring a charge for a service offence and
    to a person who provides legal advice with respect to the charge,
    and the disclosure is necessary to enable them to determine whether
    a charge for a service offence resulting from an investigation
    referred to in paragraph (c) should be laid, referred or preferred;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} unless the disclosure is to a prosecutor, judge or
    justice in a proceeding relating to an application for a search
    warrant in connection with an investigation referred to in paragraph
    (c), and the information is relevant to the application;

  - [(f.1)]{.lawlabel} unless the disclosure is to a prosecutor, judge
    or justice in a proceeding relating to an application for a warrant
    referred to in subparagraph (c)(iv), and the information is relevant
    to the application;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} unless the disclosure is to a person who is
    authorized under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) to issue
    a search warrant in connection with the investigation of a service
    offence and to a person who provides legal advice with respect to
    the issuance of the search warrant, and the information is relevant
    to an application for a search warrant in connection with an
    investigation referred to in paragraph (c);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} unless the information disclosed is relevant to the
    proceeding, appeal or review and the disclosure is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to a prosecutor in connection with a proceeding
      that results from an investigation referred to in paragraph (c)
      and that is before a [[court of criminal
      jurisdiction]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or a [[superior court of
      criminal jurisdiction]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
      section 2 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), or a [[court
      martial]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of
      the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to the Attorney General within the meaning of
      section 2 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), or the Minister
      of National Defence or counsel instructed by the Minister, in
      connection with an appeal of a decision made in such a proceeding,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to the court or military judge presiding over
      the proceeding or appeal, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} to a review authority referred to in section
      163.6 of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) for its review
      under that section and to a person who provides legal advice to
      the review authority in connection with that review;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} unless the disclosure to the person is necessary to
    assist an investigation of any act or omission referred to in
    subsection 7(4.1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) by a police
    service in the state where the act or omission was committed;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} unless the disclosure is to an employee of, or a
    person retained by, a person referred to in any of paragraphs (d)
    to (i) who is authorized by that person to receive information
    disclosed under that paragraph on their behalf;

  - [(j.1)]{.lawlabel} unless the disclosure is to a member or an
    employee of, or a person retained by, a police service outside
    Canada and is necessary to assist them in the prevention or
    investigation of a crime of a sexual nature;

  - [(j.2)]{.lawlabel} unless the disclosure is to the Canada Border
    Services Agency, is limited to the information referred to in
    paragraphs 5(1)(a), (b), (i) and (j) and is necessary to assist a
    member or an employee of, or a person retained by, a police service
    in the prevention or investigation of a crime of a sexual nature or
    an offence under section 490.031 or 490.0311 of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or in the laying of a charge for such an
    offence;

  - [(j.3)]{.lawlabel} unless the disclosure is to the Canada Border
    Services Agency, relates to a sex offender who is convicted of a
    sexual offence against a child and who poses a high risk of
    committing a crime of a sexual nature, is limited to the information
    referred to in paragraphs 5(1)(a), (b), (i) and (j) and is made for
    the purpose of assisting a member or an employee of, or a person
    retained by, a police service in the prevention or investigation of
    a crime of a sexual nature or an offence under section 490.031 or
    490.0311 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or in the laying of
    a charge for such an offence; or

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} unless the disclosure is by a person who is
    authorized under section 13 to consult information that is
    registered in the database or to compare or combine that information
    with, or link it to, other information, the disclosure is for
    research or statistical purposes and it is not made, or allowed to
    be made, in a form that could reasonably be expected to identify any
    individual to whom it relates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unauthorized use

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} No person shall use any information that is collected
  under this Act or registered in the database, or allow it to be used,
  for a purpose other than that for which it is consulted, compared,
  combined, linked or disclosed, as the case may be, under this section.

## [Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-446321 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
knowingly contravenes any of subsections 16(1) to (5) is guilty of an
offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine of not more than
\$10,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than six months, or
to both.
