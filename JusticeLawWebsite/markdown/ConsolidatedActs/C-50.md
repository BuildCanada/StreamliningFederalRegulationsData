## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Liability]{.HTitleText1} {#h-138361 .Part}

### [Liability and Civil Salvage]{.HTitleText2} {#h-138362 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Crown is liable for the
damages for which, if it were a person, it would be liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Quebec, in respect of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the damage caused by the fault of a servant of the
    Crown, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the damage resulting from the act of a thing in
    the custody of or owned by the Crown or by the fault of the Crown as
    custodian or owner; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other province, in respect of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a tort committed by a servant of the Crown, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a breach of duty attaching to the ownership,
    occupation, possession or control of property.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 3
- 2001, c. 4, s. 36
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motor vehicles

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Crown is liable for the
damage sustained by anyone by reason of a motor vehicle, owned by the
Crown, on a highway, for which the Crown would be liable if it were a
person.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 4
- 2001, c. 4, s. 37
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Civil salvage

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the law relating to civil salvage, whether of life
  or property, applies in relation to salvage services rendered in
  assisting any Crown ship or aircraft, or in saving life from a Crown
  ship or aircraft, or in saving any cargo or apparel belonging to the
  Crown, in the same manner as if the ship, aircraft, cargo or apparel
  belonged to a private person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claims in Federal Court

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All claims against the Crown under subsection (1)
  shall be heard and determined by a judge of the Federal Court.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 5
- 2001, c. 4, s. 38, c. 26, s. 296
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving in respect of prerogative and
statutory powers

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in sections 3 to 7
makes the Crown liable in respect of anything done or omitted in the
exercise of any power or authority that, if those sections had not been
passed, would have been exercisable by virtue of the prerogative of the
Crown, or any power or authority conferred on the Crown by any statute,
and, in particular, but without restricting the generality of the
foregoing, nothing in those sections makes the Crown liable in respect
of anything done or omitted in the exercise of any power or authority
exercisable by the Crown, whether in time of peace or of war, for the
purpose of the defence of Canada or of training, or maintaining the
efficiency of, the Canadian Forces.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-38, s. 3

### [Special Provisions respecting Liability]{.HTitleText2} {#h-138395 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No proceedings lie where pension payable

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No proceedings lie against
the Crown or a servant of the Crown in respect of a claim if a pension
or compensation has been paid or is payable out of the Consolidated
Revenue Fund or out of any funds administered by an agency of the Crown
in respect of the death, injury, damage or loss in respect of which the
claim is made.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 9
- 2001, c. 4, s. 39(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability for acts of servants

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** No proceedings lie
against the Crown by virtue of subparagraph 3(a)(i) or (b)(i) in respect
of any act or omission of a servant of the Crown unless the act or
omission would, apart from the provisions of this Act, have given rise
to a cause of action for liability against that servant or the servant's
personal representative or succession.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 10
- 2001, c. 4, s. 40
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motor vehicles

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** No proceedings lie
against the Crown by virtue of section 4 in respect of damage sustained
by any person by reason of a motor vehicle on a highway unless the
driver of the motor vehicle or the driver's personal representative or
succession is liable for the damage so sustained.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 11
- 2001, c. 4, s. 40
:::

### [Invasion of Privacy]{.HTitleText2} {#h-138428 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** In this section and
sections 17 and 18,

[[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 1993, c. 40, s.
    19\]]{.Repealed}

[[electro-magnetic, acoustic, mechanical or other device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[electro-magnetic, acoustic, mechanical or other
    device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any device or apparatus that is
    used or is capable of being used to intercept a private
    communication, but does not include a hearing aid used to correct
    subnormal hearing of the user to not better than normal
    hearing; ([dispositif d'interception]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[intercept]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[intercept]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes listen to, record or
    acquire a communication or acquire the substance, meaning or purport
    thereof; ([interception]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[private communication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[private communication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any oral
    communication or any telecommunication that is made by an originator
    who is in Canada or is intended by the originator to be received by
    a person who is in Canada and that is made under circumstances in
    which it is reasonable for the originator to expect that it will not
    be intercepted by any person other than the person intended by the
    originator to receive it, and includes any radio-based telephone
    communication that is treated electronically or otherwise for the
    purpose of preventing intelligible reception by any person other
    than the person intended by the originator to receive
    it; ([communication privée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[public switched telephone network]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public switched telephone network]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    telecommunication facility the primary purpose of which is to
    provide a land line-based telephone service to the public for
    compensation; ([réseau téléphonique public commuté]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[radio-based telephone communication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radio-based telephone communication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    any radiocommunication within the meaning of the [Radiocommunication
    Act](/eng/acts/R-2) that is made over apparatus that is used
    primarily for connection to a public switched telephone
    network. ([communication radiotéléphonique]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 16
- 1990, c. 8, s. 26
- 1993, c. 40, s. 19
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crown liable for interception

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), where a
  servant of the Crown, by means of an electro-magnetic, acoustic,
  mechanical or other device, intentionally intercepts a private
  communication, in the course of that servant's employment, the Crown
  is liable for all loss or damage caused by or attributable to that
  interception, and for punitive damages in an amount not exceeding five
  thousand dollars, to each person who incurred that loss or damage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving provision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Crown is not liable under subsection (1) for loss
  or damage or punitive damages referred to therein where the
  interception complained of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} was lawfully made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} was made with the consent, express or implied, of
    the originator of the private communication or of the person
    intended by the originator thereof to receive it; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} was made by an officer or servant of the Crown who
    engages in radio frequency spectrum management, for the purpose of
    identifying, isolating or preventing an unauthorized or interfering
    use of a frequency or of a transmission.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1993, c. 40, s. 20\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 17
- 1993, c. 40, s. 20
- 2001, c. 4, s. 43(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crown liable for disclosure

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), where a
  servant of the Crown who has obtained, in the course of that servant's
  employment, any information respecting a private communication or a
  radio-based telephone communication that has been intercepted by means
  of an electro-magnetic, acoustic, mechanical or other device without
  the consent, express or implied, of the originator thereof or of the
  person intended by the originator thereof to receive it, intentionally

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} uses or discloses that private communication or
    radio-based telephone communication or any part thereof or the
    substance, meaning or purport thereof or of any part thereof, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} discloses the existence thereof,

  the Crown is liable for all loss or damage caused thereby, and for
  punitive damages in an amount not exceeding five thousand dollars, to
  each person who incurred that loss or damage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving provision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Crown is not liable for loss or damage or
  punitive damages referred to in subsection (1) where a servant of the
  Crown discloses a private communication or a radio-based telephone
  communication or any part thereof or the substance, meaning or purport
  thereof or of any part thereof or the existence of a private
  communication or of a radio-based telephone communication

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with the consent of the originator of the
    communication or of the person intended by the originator to receive
    it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the course of or for the purpose of giving
    evidence in any civil or criminal proceedings or in any other
    proceedings in which the servant of the Crown may be required to
    give evidence on oath;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the course of or for the purpose of any criminal
    investigation if the private communication or radio-based telephone
    communication was not unlawfully intercepted;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in giving notice under section 189 of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or furnishing further particulars pursuant to
    an order under section 190 of that Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of identifying, isolating or
    preventing an unauthorized or interfering use of a frequency or of a
    transmission, where the duties of the servant of the Crown include
    engaging in radio frequency spectrum management; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} where disclosure is made to a peace officer or
    prosecutor in Canada or to a person or authority with responsibility
    in a foreign state for the investigation or prosecution of offences
    and is intended to be in the interests of the administration of
    justice in Canada or elsewhere.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 18
- R.S., 1985, c. 30 (4th Supp.), s. 46
- 1993, c. 40, s. 21
- 2001, c. 4, s. 44(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent to interception

**[[18.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18.1 .sectionLabel}** Where a private
communication or a radio-based telephone communication is originated by
more than one person or is intended by the originator to be received by
more than one person, a consent to the interception thereof by any one
of those persons is sufficient for the purposes of paragraph 17(2)(b)
and subsection 18(2).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1993, c. 40, s. 22
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No punitive damages

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** No award for punitive
damages shall be made under section 17 or 18 where punitive damages have
been ordered to be paid to the person claiming those damages pursuant to
subsection 194(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1973-74, c. 50, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indemnity

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Where a judgment has been
given against the Crown by reason of its liability under section 17 or
18, the servant in respect of whose conduct the Crown has been found
liable is accountable to the Crown for the amount of the judgment and
the Crown may recover that amount from that servant.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 20
- 1990, c. 8, s. 27

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement proceedings

- **[[20.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20.3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) to (5), a
  panel determination that is made an order of the Federal Court is

  Crown only in that Court and only by the appropriate party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearings in summary way

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any proceedings referred to in subsection (2) shall
  be heard and determined in a summary way.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to panel

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If any question of fact or interpretation of a panel
  determination arises in any proceedings referred to in subsection (2),
  the Federal Court shall refer the question to the panel that made the
  determination, and the decision of the panel on the question is
  binding on that Court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No interventions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} No person or body may intervene in any proceedings
  referred to in subsection (2).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1994, c. 11, s. 1
- 2009, c. 16, s. 27
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determinations, orders and decisions are
final

- **[[20.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20.4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Panel determinations, including
  panel determinations that are made orders of the Federal Court, and
  orders and decisions made by the Federal Court in any proceedings
  referred to in subsection 20.3(2) are final and binding and are not
  subject to appeal to any court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation on proceedings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 20.3, no panel determination,
  including a panel determination that is made an order of the Federal
  Court, no determination or proceedings of a panel made or carried on
  or purporting to be made or carried on under any environmental
  cooperation treaty or labour cooperation treaty, no order or decision
  made by the Federal Court in any proceedings referred to in subsection
  20.3(2) and no proceedings of that Court made or carried on or
  purporting to be made or carried on under that subsection shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} questioned, reviewed, set aside, removed,
    prohibited or restrained, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} made the subject of any proceedings in, or any
    process or order of, any court, whether by way of or in nature of
    injunction, [certiorari]{.otherLang lang="la"}, prohibition, [quo
    warranto]{.otherLang lang="la"}, declaration or otherwise,

  on any ground, including the ground that the determination,
  proceedings, order or decision is beyond the jurisdiction of the panel
  or the Federal Court, as the case may be, or that, in the course of
  any proceedings, the panel or the Federal Court for any reason
  exceeded or lost its jurisdiction.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1994, c. 11, s. 1
- 1997, c. 14, s. 34
- 2009, c. 16, s. 28

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-138528 .Part}

### [Jurisdiction]{.HTitleText2} {#h-138529 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Concurrent jurisdiction of provincial
court

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In all
  cases where a claim is made against the Crown, except where the
  Federal Court has exclusive jurisdiction with respect to it, the
  superior court of the province in which the claim arises has
  concurrent jurisdiction with respect to the subject-matter of the
  claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where proceedings pending in Federal
  Court

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No court in a province has jurisdiction to entertain
  any proceedings taken by a person if proceedings taken by that person
  in the Federal Court in respect of the same cause of action, whether
  taken before or after the proceedings are taken in the court, are
  pending.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 21
- R.S., 1985, c. 40 (4th Supp.), s. 2
- 1990, c. 8, s. 28
- 2001, c. 4, s. 45
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration of rights

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  in proceedings against the Crown any relief is sought that might, in
  proceedings between persons, be granted by way of injunction or
  specific performance, a court shall not, as against the Crown, grant
  an injunction or make an order for specific performance, but in lieu
  thereof may make an order declaratory of the rights of the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Servants of Crown

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A court shall not in any proceedings grant relief or
  make an order against a servant of the Crown that it is not competent
  to grant or make against the Crown.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 22
- 1990, c. 8, s. 28
- 2001, c. 4, s. 46(F)
:::

### [Procedure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-138544 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taking of proceedings against Crown

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Proceedings against the Crown may be
  taken in the name of the Attorney General of Canada or, in the case of
  an agency of the Crown against which proceedings are by an Act of
  Parliament authorized to be taken in the name of the agency, in the
  name of that agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of originating document

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where proceedings are taken against the Crown, the
  document originating the proceedings shall be served on the Crown by
  serving it on the Deputy Attorney General of Canada or the chief
  executive officer of the agency in whose name the proceedings are
  taken, as the case may be.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 23
- 1990, c. 8, s. 29
- 2001, c. 4, s. 47(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defences

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** In any proceedings
against the Crown, the Crown may raise

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any defence that would be available if the
  proceedings were a suit or an action between persons in a competent
  court; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any defence that would be available if the
  proceedings were by way of statement of claim in the Federal Court.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 24
- 1990, c. 8, s. 30
- 2001, c. 4, s. 48
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No judgment by default without leave

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** In any proceedings
against the Crown, judgment shall not be entered against the Crown in
default of appearance or pleading without leave of the court obtained on
an application at least fourteen clear days notice of which has been
given to the Deputy Attorney General of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 25
- 1990, c. 8, s. 31
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No jury trials

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** In any proceedings
against the Crown, trial shall be without a jury.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 26
- 1990, c. 8, s. 31
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules of court

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided by this Act or the regulations, the rules of practice and
procedure of the court in which proceedings are taken apply in those
proceedings.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 27
- 1990, c. 8, s. 31
:::

### [Costs]{.HTitleText2} {#h-138570 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In any
  proceedings to which the Crown is a party, costs may be awarded to or
  against the Crown.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs awarded to Crown

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Costs awarded to the Crown shall not be disallowed or
  reduced on taxation by reason only that the solicitor or counsel who
  earned the costs, or in respect of whose services the costs are
  charged, was a salaried officer of the Crown performing those services
  in the discharge of the officer's duty and was remunerated therefor by
  a salary, or for that or any other reason was not entitled to recover
  any costs from the Crown in respect of the services so rendered.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2012, c. 31, s. 305\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 28
- 1990, c. 8, s. 31
- 1996, c. 17, s. 15
- 2012, c. 31, s. 305
:::

### [Execution of Judgment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-138579 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No execution against Crown

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** No execution shall issue
on a judgment against the Crown.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 29
- 1990, c. 8, s. 31
- 2001, c. 4, s. 49(F)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of judgment

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of a certificate of judgment against the Crown issued under
  the regulations or the [Federal Courts
  Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106), the Minister of Finance shall
  authorize the payment out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund of any
  money awarded by the judgment to any person against the Crown.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crown costs to be paid to Receiver
  General

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any money or costs awarded to the Crown in any
  proceedings shall be paid to the Receiver General.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 30
- 1990, c. 8, s. 31

### [Interest]{.HTitleText2} {#h-138591 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prejudgment interest, cause of action
within province

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as otherwise provided in any other Act of Parliament and subject to
  subsection (2), the laws relating to prejudgment interest in
  proceedings between subject and subject that are in force in a
  province apply to any proceedings against the Crown in any court in
  respect of any cause of action arising in that province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prejudgment interest, cause of action
  outside province

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who is entitled to an order for the payment
  of money in respect of a cause of action against the Crown arising
  outside any province or in respect of causes of action against the
  Crown arising in more than one province is entitled to claim and have
  included in the order an award of interest thereon at such rate as the
  court considers reasonable in the circumstances, calculated

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the order is made on a liquidated claim, from
    the date or dates the cause of action or causes of action arose to
    the date of the order; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the order is made on an unliquidated claim,
    from the date the person entitled gave notice in writing of the
    claim to the Crown to the date of the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special damages and pre-trial pecuniary
  losses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When an order referred to in subsection (2) includes
  an amount for, in the Province of Quebec, pre-trial pecuniary loss or,
  in any other province, special damages, the interest shall be
  calculated under that subsection on the balance of the amount as
  totalled at the end of each six month period following the notice in
  writing referred to in paragraph (2)(b) and at the date of the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Interest shall not be awarded under subsection (2)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on exemplary or punitive damages;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on interest accruing under this section;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on an award of costs in the proceeding;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} on that part of the order that represents pecuniary
    loss arising after the date of the order and that is identified by a
    finding of the court;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} where the order is made on consent, except by
    consent of the Crown; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} where interest is payable by a right other than
    under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial discretion

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A court may, where it considers it just to do so,
  having regard to changes in market interest rates, the conduct of the
  proceedings or any other relevant consideration, disallow interest or
  allow interest for a period other than that provided for in
  subsection (2) in respect of the whole or any part of the amount on
  which interest is payable under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} This section applies in respect of the payment of
  money under judgment delivered on or after the day on which this
  section comes into force, but no interest shall be awarded for a
  period before that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian maritime law

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply in respect of any case in
  which a claim for relief is made or a remedy is sought under or by
  virtue of Canadian maritime law within the meaning of the [Federal
  Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 31
- 1990, c. 8, s. 31
- 2001, c. 4, s. 51
- 2002, c. 8, s. 182
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judgment interest, causes of action
within province

- **[[31.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided in any
  other Act of Parliament and subject to subsection (2), the laws
  relating to interest on judgments in causes of action between subject
  and subject that are in force in a province apply to judgments against
  the Crown in respect of any cause of action arising in that province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judgment interest, causes of action
  outside or in more than one province

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A judgment against the Crown in respect of a cause of
  action outside any province or in respect of causes of action arising
  in more than one province shall bear interest at such rate as the
  Court considers reasonable in the circumstances, calculated from the
  time of the giving of the judgment.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1990, c. 8, s. 31
- 2001, c. 4, s. 52(E)

### [Tenders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-138624 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenders

- **[[31.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Crown may, in any proceeding,
  plead a tender without paying the money tendered into court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written offer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every tender of a sum of money on behalf of the Crown
  shall be deemed to be legally made if made by a written offer to pay
  the sum, given under the hand of a minister of the Crown, or a person
  acting for that minister in that behalf, and notified to the person
  having the claim to that sum.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1990, c. 8, s. 31
:::

### [Prescription and Limitation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-138632 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial laws applicable

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided in this Act or in any other Act of Parliament, the laws
relating to prescription and the limitation of actions in force in a
province between subject and subject apply to any proceedings by or
against the Crown in respect of any cause of action arising in that
province, and proceedings by or against the Crown in respect of a cause
of action arising otherwise than in a province shall be taken within six
years after the cause of action arose.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 32
- 1990, c. 8, s. 31
:::

### [Application of Statutes to the Crown]{.HTitleText2} {#h-138637 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of statutes to Crown

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
expressly provided in this Act, nothing in this Act affects any rule of
evidence or any presumption relating to the extent to which the Crown is
bound by an Act of Parliament.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-38, s. 20
:::

### [Regulations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-138642 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing rules of practice and procedure in
  respect of proceedings by, against or involving the Crown, including
  tariffs of fees and costs;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing forms for the purposes of proceedings
  referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the issue of certificates of judgments
  against the Crown;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} making applicable to any proceedings by, against or
  involving the Crown all or any of the rules of evidence applicable in
  similar proceedings between subject and subject; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} generally respecting proceedings by, against or
  involving the Crown.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 34
- 1990, c. 8, s. 32
- 2006, c. 11, s. 18
:::

### [Agencies and Servants of the Crown]{.HTitleText2} {#h-138652 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings against Crown agencies

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Act, except section 22, applies in respect of any proceedings against
  an agency of the Crown taken in accordance with any Act of Parliament
  that authorizes the proceedings to be taken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of award

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any money awarded to any person by a judgment in any
  proceedings referred to in subsection (1), or the interest thereon,
  may be paid out of any funds administered by the agency of the Crown.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 35
- 1990, c. 8, s. 32
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of Canadian Forces and R.C.M.P.

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
determining liability in any proceedings by or against the Crown, a
person who was at any time a member of the Canadian Forces or of the
Royal Canadian Mounted Police shall be deemed to have been at that time
a servant of the Crown.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-50, s. 36
- 1990, c. 8, s. 32
