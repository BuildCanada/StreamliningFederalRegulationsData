## Food and Drugs Act {#food-and-drugs-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. F-27
:::

An Act respecting food, drugs, cosmetics and therapeutic devices

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233997 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-27, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-234002 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[advanced therapeutic product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[advanced therapeutic product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    therapeutic product that is described in Schedule G or that belongs
    to a class of therapeutic products that is described in that
    Schedule; ([produit thérapeutique innovant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[advertisement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[advertisement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any representation by
    any means whatever for the purpose of promoting directly or
    indirectly the sale or disposal of any food, drug, cosmetic or
    device; ([publicité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou
    [annonce]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[analyst]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[analyst]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual designated as an
    analyst for the purposes of this Act under section 28 or under
    section 13 of the [Canadian Food Inspection Agency
    Act](/eng/acts/C-16.5); ([analyste]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[clinical trial]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[clinical trial]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a study, involving
    human subjects, for the purpose of discovering or verifying the
    effects of a drug, a device or a food for a special dietary
    purpose; ([essai clinique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[confidential business information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[confidential business information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in
    respect of a person to whose business or affairs the information
    relates, means --- subject to the regulations --- business
    information

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is not publicly available,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which the person has taken measures
      that are reasonable in the circumstances to ensure that it remains
      not publicly available, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that has actual or potential economic value to
      the person or their competitors because it is not publicly
      available and its disclosure would result in a material financial
      loss to the person or a material financial gain to their
      competitors; ([renseignements commerciaux
      confidentiels]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contraceptive device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contraceptive device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any instrument,
    apparatus, contrivance or substance other than a drug, that is
    manufactured, sold or represented for use in the prevention of
    conception; ([moyen anticonceptionnel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cosmetic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cosmetic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any substance or mixture
    of substances manufactured, sold or represented for use in
    cleansing, improving or altering the complexion, skin, hair or
    teeth, and includes deodorants and
    perfumes; ([cosmétique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of
    Health; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an instrument, apparatus,
    contrivance or other similar article, or an [in vitro]{.otherLang
    lang="la"} reagent, including a component, part or accessory of any
    of them, that is manufactured, sold or represented for use in

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} diagnosing, treating, mitigating or preventing a
      disease, disorder or abnormal physical state, or any of their
      symptoms, in human beings or animals,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} restoring, modifying or correcting the body
      structure of human beings or animals or the functioning of any
      part of the bodies of human beings or animals,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} diagnosing pregnancy in human beings or animals,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} caring for human beings or animals during
      pregnancy or at or after the birth of the offspring, including
      caring for the offspring, or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} preventing conception in human beings or animals;

    however, it does not include such an instrument, apparatus,
    contrivance or article, or a component, part or accessory of any of
    them, that does any of the actions referred to in paragraphs (a)
    to (e) solely by pharmacological, immunological or metabolic means
    or solely by chemical means in or on the body of a human being or
    animal; ([instrument]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any substance or mixture of
    substances manufactured, sold or represented for use in

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the diagnosis, treatment, mitigation or
      prevention of a disease, disorder or abnormal physical state, or
      its symptoms, in human beings or animals,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} restoring, correcting or modifying organic
      functions in human beings or animals, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} disinfection in premises in which food is
      manufactured, prepared or kept; ([drogue]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[food]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[food]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any article manufactured, sold
    or represented for use as food or drink for human beings, chewing
    gum, and any ingredient that may be mixed with food for any purpose
    whatever; ([aliment]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[food for a special dietary purpose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[food for a special dietary purpose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    food that has been specially processed or formulated

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to meet the particular requirements of an
      individual in whom a physical or physiological condition exists as
      a result of a disease, disorder or abnormal physical state, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to be the sole or primary source of nutrition for
      an individual; ([aliment à des fins diététiques
      spéciales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[foreign regulatory authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foreign regulatory authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    government agency or other entity outside Canada that controls the
    manufacture, use or sale of therapeutic products or foods within its
    jurisdiction; ([autorité réglementaire étrangère]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual designated as
    an inspector for the purposes of this Act under subsection 22(1) or
    under section 13 of the [Canadian Food Inspection Agency
    Act](/eng/acts/C-16.5); ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any legend, word or mark
    attached to, included in, belonging to or accompanying any food,
    drug, cosmetic, device or package; ([étiquette]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Health; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any thing in which any
    food, drug, cosmetic or device is wholly or partly contained, placed
    or packed; ([emballage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual or an
    [[organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 2 of
    the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46); ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by the
    regulations; ([Version anglaise seulement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} offer for sale, expose for sale or have in
      possession for sale --- or distribute to one or more persons,
      whether or not the distribution is made for consideration, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} lease, offer for lease, expose for lease or have
      in possession for lease; ([vente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[therapeutic product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[therapeutic product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a drug or device
    or any combination of drugs and devices; ([produit
    thérapeutique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[therapeutic product authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[therapeutic product authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an authorization, including a licence, that

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} authorizes the conduct of a clinical trial in
        respect of a therapeutic product and is issued under the
        regulations, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} authorizes, as the case may be, the import,
        sale, advertisement, manufacture, preparation, preservation,
        packaging, labelling, storage or testing of a therapeutic
        product and is issued under subsection 21.92(1) or the
        regulations, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an authorization, including a licence, that would
      fall within paragraph (a) if the authorization were not
      suspended; ([autorisation relative à un produit
      thérapeutique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unsanitary conditions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unsanitary conditions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such conditions
    or circumstances as might contaminate with dirt or filth, or render
    injurious to health, a food, drug or cosmetic. ([conditions non
    hygiéniques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classification --- thing

- **[[2.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the Minister believes that a thing falls within two or more of the
  definitions [[food]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
  [[drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[cosmetic]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
  [[device]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2, the Minister may, by
  order, add a description of the thing to a single part of Schedule A
  that corresponds to one of the definitions in which the thing is
  believed to fall.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classification --- class of things

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister believes that every thing in a class
  of things falls within the same two or more definitions
  [[food]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
  [[cosmetic]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
  [[device]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2, the Minister may, by
  order, add a description of the class to a single part of Schedule A
  that corresponds to one of the definitions in which the things in the
  class are believed to fall.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before adding a description of a thing or a class of
  things to a part of Schedule A, the Minister shall consider the
  following factors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the risk of injury that the thing or things present
    to human health;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the protection and promotion of health;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the possibility of a person being deceived or
    misled;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the purposes for which the thing or things are
    sold, represented or used and the history of use of the thing or
    things;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the treatment of similar things that have been
    regulated as foods, drugs, cosmetics or devices under this Act; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed factors, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of addition

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A thing that either is described in or belongs to a
  class of things that is described in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Part 1 of Schedule A falls within the definition
    [[food]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 and does not fall within
    any of the other definitions referred to in subsections (1) and (2);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Part 2 of Schedule A falls within the definition
    [[drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 and does not fall within
    any of the other definitions referred to in subsections (1) and (2);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Part 3 of Schedule A falls within the definition
    [[cosmetic]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 and does not fall
    within any of the other definitions referred to in subsections (1)
    and (2); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Part 4 of Schedule A falls within the definition
    [[device]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 and does not fall
    within any of the other definitions referred to in subsections (1)
    and (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deletion from Schedule A

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by order, delete the description of
  a thing or a class of things from Schedule A.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- definitions

  [[cosmetic]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the definitions

  [[device]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 are subject to

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Foods, Drugs, Cosmetics and Devices]{.HTitleText1} {#h-234053 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-234054 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited advertising

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall advertise any food, drug, cosmetic or device to the
  general public as a treatment, preventative or cure for any of the
  diseases, disorders or abnormal physical states referred to in
  Schedule A.1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited sales

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall sell any food, drug, cosmetic or
  device if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is represented by label as a treatment,
    preventative or cure for any of the diseases, disorders or abnormal
    physical states referred to in Schedule A.1; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person advertises it to the general public as a
    treatment, preventative or cure for any of the diseases, disorders
    or abnormal physical states referred to in Schedule A.1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unauthorized advertising of
  contraceptive device prohibited

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Except as authorized by regulation, no person shall
  advertise to the general public any contraceptive device or any drug
  manufactured, sold or represented for use in the prevention of
  conception.

### [Food]{.HTitleText2} {#h-234067 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited sales of food

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall sell an article of food that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has in or on it any poisonous or harmful substance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is unfit for human consumption;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} consists in whole or in part of any filthy, putrid,
    disgusting, rotten, decomposed or diseased animal or vegetable
    substance;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is adulterated; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} was manufactured, prepared, preserved, packaged or
    stored under unsanitary conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A food does not have a poisonous or harmful substance
  in or on it for the purposes of paragraph (1)(a) --- or is not
  adulterated for the purposes of paragraph (1)(d) --- by reason only
  that it has in or on it a pest control product as defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the [Pest Control Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01),
  or any of its components or derivatives, if the amount of the pest
  control product or the components or derivatives in or on the food
  being sold does not exceed the maximum residue limit specified under
  section 9 or 10 of that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-27, s. 4
- 2005, c. 42, s. 1
- 2012, c. 19, s. 412
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deception, etc., regarding food

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall label, package, treat, process, sell or advertise any
  food in a manner that is false, misleading or deceptive or is likely
  to create an erroneous impression regarding its character, value,
  quantity, composition, merit or safety.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Food labelled or packaged in
  contravention of regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An article of food that is not labelled or packaged
  as required by, or is labelled or packaged contrary to, the
  regulations shall be deemed to be labelled or packaged contrary to
  subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-27, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Importation and interprovincial movement
of food

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  standard for a food has been prescribed, no person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} import into Canada,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} send, convey or receive for conveyance from one
    province to another, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} have in possession for the purpose of sending or
    conveying from one province to another

  any article that is intended for sale and that is likely to be
  mistaken for that food unless the article complies with the prescribed
  standard.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not applicable to carriers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraphs (1)(b) and (c) do not apply to an operator
  of a conveyance that is used to carry an article or to a carrier of an
  article whose sole concern, in respect of the article, is the
  conveyance of the article unless the operator or carrier could, with
  reasonable diligence, have ascertained that the conveying or receiving
  for conveyance of the article or the possession of the article for the
  purpose of conveyance would be in contravention of subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Labelling, etc., of food that is
  imported or moved interprovincially

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a standard for a food has been prescribed, no
  person shall label, package, sell or advertise any article that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has been imported into Canada,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has been sent or conveyed from one province to
    another, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is intended to be sent or conveyed from one
    province to another

  in such a manner that it is likely to be mistaken for that food unless
  the article complies with the prescribed standard.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-27, s. 6
- R.S., 1985, c. 27 (3rd Supp.), s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Governor in Council may identify standard
or portion thereof

- **[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, by regulation, identify a standard prescribed
  for a food, or any portion of the standard, as being necessary to
  prevent injury to the health of the consumer or purchaser of the food.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where standard or portion thereof is
  identified

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a standard or any portion of a standard
  prescribed for a food is identified by the Governor in Council
  pursuant to subsection (1), no person shall label, package, sell or
  advertise any article in such a manner that it is likely to be
  mistaken for that food unless the article complies with the standard
  or portion of a standard so identified.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 27 (3rd Supp.), s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unsanitary manufacture, etc., of food

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No person shall
manufacture, prepare, preserve, package or store for sale any food under
unsanitary conditions.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-27, s. 7

### [Drugs]{.HTitleText2} {#h-234115 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited sales --- animal testing

- **[[16.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall sell a cosmetic unless the person can establish the
  safety of the cosmetic without relying on data derived from a test
  conducted on an animal that could cause pain, suffering or injury,
  whether physical or mental, to the animal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The prohibition in subsection (1) does not apply if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Government of Canada has published the data in
    a scientific journal or on a Government of Canada website;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the data is publicly available and is derived from
    a test that was not sponsored by or conducted by or on behalf of a
    person who manufactures, imports or sells the cosmetic;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the following conditions are met:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the data is derived from a test that was
      conducted on a substance in order to meet

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a requirement under a provision of an Act of
        Parliament or any of its regulations that applied at the time
        that the test was conducted, except a requirement that relates
        only to cosmetics under a provision of this Act or the
        regulations, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a requirement that does not relate to cosmetics
        under the law that applied in a foreign state at the time that
        the test was conducted,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the substance is or has been used in a product
      that is not a cosmetic and that is or has been legally sold in the
      country where the requirement in question applied, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the test was necessary to meet the requirement
      in order to sell the product in that country;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the data is derived from a test that was conducted
    before the day on which this section comes into force;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the cosmetic was sold in Canada at any time before
    the day on which this section comes into force; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any prescribed circumstance applies.

- **[[21.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the Minister believes that a therapeutic product may present a serious
  risk of injury to human health, the Minister may order a person to
  provide the Minister with information that is in the person's control
  and that the Minister believes is necessary to determine whether the
  product presents such a risk.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure ---  serious risk

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may disclose confidential business
  information about a therapeutic prod­uct without notifying the person
  to whose business or affairs the information relates or obtaining
  their consent, if the Minister believes that the product may present a
  serious risk of injury to human health.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure ---  health or safety

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may disclose confidential business
  information about a therapeutic prod­uct without notifying the person
  to whose business or affairs the information relates or obtaining
  their consent, if the purpose of the disclosure is related to the
  protection or promotion of human health or the safety of the public
  and the disclosure is to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a government;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person from whom the Minister seeks advice; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who carries out functions relating to the
    protection or promotion of human health or the safety of the public.

- Definition of [[government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means any of the following or their institutions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the federal government;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation named in Schedule III to the
    [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a provincial government or a public body
    established under an Act of the legislature of a province;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an aboriginal government as defined in subsection
    13(3) of the [Access to Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a government of a foreign state or of a subdivision
    of a foreign state; or

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's powers --- risk of injury to

- **[[21.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21.3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the Minister believes that a therapeutic product presents a serious or
  imminent risk of injury to health, he or she may order a person who
  sells the product to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} recall the product; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} send the product, or cause it to be sent, to a

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recall order --- corrective action

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, if the Minister makes an order
  under paragraph (1)(a) and believes that corrective action is an
  effective means of dealing with the risk, the order may require the
  person who sells the product to, instead of requesting the product's
  return, request the product's owner or user to allow corrective action
  to be taken in respect of the product and then take that corrective
  action, or cause it to be taken, if the request is accepted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- selling

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), no person shall sell a

  to recall.

  has ordered them, or another person, to recall it.

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person does not contravene subsection (3) if they
  sell a therapeutic product that they have been authorized under
  subsection (4) to sell, provided that they sell it in accordance with
  any conditions that the Minister establishes.

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} No person shall be convicted of an offence for the
  contravention of subsection (3) unless it is proved that, at the time
  of the alleged contravention, the person had been notified of the
  recall order or reasonable steps had been taken to bring the purport
  of the recall order to the notice of those persons likely to be
  affected by it.

    grounds includes information relevant to the administration of this
    Act or the regulations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} cause to be reproduced any electronic data referred
    to in paragraph (c);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} use, or cause to be used, any computer system or

### [Removal, Forfeiture or Destruction of Unlawful Imports]{.HTitleText2} {#h-234355 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unlawful imports

- **[[27.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector who has reasonable grounds to believe that an imported food,
  drug, cosmetic or device does not meet the requirements of the
  regulations or was imported in contravention of a provision of this
  Act or the regulations may decide whether to give the owner or
  importer, or the person having possession, care or control of the
  food, drug, cosmetic or device, the opportunity to take a measure in
  respect of it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In making a decision under subsection (1), the
  inspector shall consider, among other factors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the food, drug, cosmetic or device presents
    a risk of injury to health or safety; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other prescribed factors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of inspector

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the inspector decides under subsection (1) not to
  give the owner or importer, or the person having possession, care or
  control of the food, drug, cosmetic or device the opportunity to take
  a measure in respect of it, the inspector shall exercise, in respect
  of the food, drug, cosmetic or device, any of the powers conferred by
  the provisions of this Act, other than this section, or of the
  regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures that may be taken and notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} However, if the inspector decides under
  subsection (1) to give the owner or importer, or the person having
  possession, care or control of the food, drug, cosmetic or device the
  opportunity to take a measure in respect of it, the inspector, or any
  other inspector who is informed of the decision, shall decide whether
  the owner or importer, or the person having possession, care or
  control of it may remove it from Canada at their expense, consent to
  its forfeiture or take either of these measures, and shall notify or
  cause to be notified the owner or importer, or the person having
  possession, care or control of the food, drug, cosmetic or device that
  they may take that measure within the period specified by the
  inspector or other inspector, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a person is notified under subsection (4) that
  they may consent to the forfeiture of the food, drug, cosmetic or
  device and the person consents to its forfeiture, the food, drug,
  cosmetic or device is forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada and
  may be disposed of, as the Minister may direct, at the person's
  expense.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2016, c. 9, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal or destruction

- **[[27.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector who has reasonable grounds to believe that an imported food,
  drug, cosmetic or device does not meet the requirements of the
  regulations or was imported in contravention of a provision of this
  Act or the regulations may, by notice, whether the food, drug,
  cosmetic or device is seized or not, order its owner or importer, or
  the person having possession, care or control of it, to remove it from
  Canada at their expense or, if removal is not possible, to destroy it
  at their expense.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must be delivered personally to the owner
  or importer of the food, drug, cosmetic or device, or the person
  having possession, care or control of it, or sent to the owner,
  importer or person by any method that provides proof of delivery or by
  any prescribed method.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the food, drug, cosmetic or device is not removed
  from Canada, or destroyed, within the period specified in the
  notice --- or, if no period is specified, within 90 days after the day
  on which the notice was delivered or sent --- it is, despite section
  26, forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada and may be disposed
  of, as the Minister may direct, at the expense of the person to whom
  the notice was delivered or sent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of application of subsection
  (3)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An inspector may, for the period specified by the
  inspector, suspend the application of subsection (3) if the inspector
  is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the food, drug, cosmetic or device does not present
    a risk of injury to health;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the food, drug, cosmetic or device will not be sold
    within that period;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the measures that should have been taken for the
    food, drug, cosmetic or device not to have been imported in
    contravention of a provision of this Act or the regulations will be
    taken within that period; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the food, drug, cosmetic or device does not meet
    the requirements of the regulations, it will be brought into
    compliance with those requirements within that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An inspector may cancel the notice if the inspector
  is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the food, drug, cosmetic or device does not present
    a risk of injury to health;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the food, drug, cosmetic or device has not been
    sold within the period referred to in subsection (6);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the measures referred to in paragraph (4)(c) were
    taken within that period; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the food, drug, cosmetic or device did not meet
    the requirements of the regulations when it was imported, it was
    brought into compliance with those requirements within that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The period for the purposes of subsection (5) is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the application of subsection (3) was suspended
    under subsection (4), the period of the suspension; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the application of subsection (3) was not
    suspended, the period specified in the notice or, if no period was
    specified, the period of 90 days after the day on which the notice
    was delivered or sent.

### [Interim Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-234517 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interim orders

- **[[30.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may make an interim
  order that contains any provision that may be contained in a
  regulation made under this Act if the Minister believes that immediate
  action is required to deal with a significant risk, direct or
  indirect, to health, safety or the environment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of effect

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An interim order has effect from the time that it is
  made but ceases to have effect on the earliest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 14 days after it is made, unless it is approved by
    the Governor in Council,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which it is repealed,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which a regulation made under this Act,
    that has the same effect as the interim order, comes into force, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} one year after the interim order is made or any
    shorter period that may be specified in the interim order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of unpublished order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall be convicted of an offence consisting
  of a contravention of an interim order that, at the time of the
  alleged contravention, had not been published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) unless it is proved that, at the
  time of the alleged contravention, the person had been notified of the
  interim order or reasonable steps had been taken to bring the purport
  of the interim order to the notice of those persons likely to be
  affected by it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An interim order

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is exempt from the application of sections 3, 5 and
    11 of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall be published in the [Canada
    Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) within 23 days after it is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of any provision of this Act other
  than this section and section 30.05, any reference to regulations made
  under this Act is deemed to include interim orders, and any reference
  to a regulation made under a specified provision of this Act is deemed
  to include a reference to the portion of an interim order containing
  any provision that may be contained in a regulation made under the
  specified provision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of order

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A copy of each interim order must be tabled in each
  House of Parliament within 15 days after it is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}House not sitting

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In order to comply with subsection (6), the interim
  order may be sent to the Clerk of the House if the House is not
  sitting.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sentencing considerations

**[[31.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31.5 .sectionLabel}** A court that imposes
a sentence for an offence under section 31.2 or 31.4 shall take into
account, in addition to any other principles that it is required to
consider, the following factors:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the harm or risk of harm caused by the commission of
  the offence; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vulnerability of consumers of the therapeutic
  product.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parties to offence

**[[31.6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31.6 .sectionLabel}** If a person other

offence under section 31.4 by reason of contravening section 21.6, then
any of the person's directors, officers or agents or mandataries who
directs, authorizes, assents to or acquiesces or participates in the
commission of the offence is a party to the offence and is liable on
conviction to the punishment provided for by this Act, even if the
person is not prosecuted for the offence.

constitutes a separate offence for each day on which it is committed or
continued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions under which exports exempt

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Act does not apply to any packaged food, drug, cosmetic or device if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is manufactured or prepared in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is intended for export and is not manufactured
    or prepared for consumption or use in Canada nor sold for
    consumption or use in Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a certificate that the package and its contents do
    not contravene any known requirement of the law of the country to
    which it is or is about to be consigned has been issued in respect
    of the package and its contents in prescribed form and manner; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the packaged food, drug, cosmetic or device meets
    any other prescribed requirement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- Act

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} section 4, subsection 5(1) and section 7 apply to
    any food;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 8, subsection 9(1) and section 11 apply to
    any drug that is not a [[natural health
    product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} within the meaning of the [Natural
    Health Products Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sections 16 and 18 apply to any cosmetic; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} section 19 and subsection 20(1) apply to any
    device.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- regulations

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), any prescribed provision of
  the regulations applies to any food, drug, cosmetic or device.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- General Council Decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), this Act applies in respect
  of any drug or device to be manufactured for the purpose of being
  exported in accordance with the [[General Council
  Decision]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 30(6), and
  the requirements of the Act and the regulations apply to the drug or
  device as though it were a drug or device to be manufactured and sold
  for consumption or use in Canada, unless the regulations provide
  otherwise.

        416, has the same effect as the interim marketing authorization;
        and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} two years after the day on which the interim
        marketing authorization is published in the [Canada
        Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

    - ###### Exemption from [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) {#exemption-from-statutory-instruments-act .MarginalNote}

  - ###### Impact of remote proceedings {#impact-of-remote-proceedings .MarginalNote}

    - [78.1]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Justice must,
      no later than three years after the day on which this Act receives
      royal assent, initiate one or more independent reviews on the use
      of remote proceedings in criminal justice matters that must
      include an assessment of whether remote proceedings

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enhance, preserve or adversely affect access to
        justice;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintain fundamental principles of the
        administration of justice; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} adequately address the rights and obligations
        of participants in the criminal justice system, including
        accused persons.

    - ###### Report {#report .MarginalNote}

      years after the day on which a review is initiated, cause a report
      on the review --- including any findings or recommendations
      resulting from it --- to be laid before each House of Parliament.

  - ###### Review by committee {#review-by-committee .MarginalNote}

    - [78.2]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the start of the fifth year

      provisions enacted or amended by this Act are to be referred to a
      committee of the Senate and a committee of the House of Commons
      that may be designated or established for the purpose of reviewing
      the provisions.

    - ###### Report {#report-1 .MarginalNote}

      referred are to review them and the use of remote proceedings in
      criminal justice matters and submit reports to the Houses of
      Parliament of which they are committees, including statements
      setting out any changes to the provisions that they recommend.
