## Prevention of Terrorist Travel Act {#prevention-of-terrorist-travel-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2015, c. 36, s. 42
:::

Assented to 2015-06-23

An Act respecting the protection of information in relation to certain
decisions made under the Canadian Passport Order

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 42 of chapter 36 of the Statutes of Canada, 2015,
in force on assent June 23, 2015.\]

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-396720 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Prevention of Terrorist Travel Act](/eng/acts/P-19.45).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-396723 .Part}

Definition of [[judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,
[[judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chief Justice of the Federal
Court or a judge of that Court designated by the Chief Justice.

## [Designation of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-396726 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate a minister of the Crown to be the Minister
referred to in this Act.

## [Appeals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-396729 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellations under [Canadian Passport
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-81-86) --- terrorism or national security

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  passport has been cancelled as a result of a decision of the Minister
  under the [Canadian Passport Order](/eng/regulations/SI-81-86) that
  the passport is to be cancelled on the grounds that the cancellation
  is necessary to prevent the commission of a terrorism offence, as
  defined in section 2 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), or for
  the national security of Canada or a foreign country or state, the
  person to whom the passport was issued may appeal that decision to a
  judge within 30 days after the day on which the person receives notice
  of the Minister's decision in respect of an application that was made
  under that Order to have the cancellation reconsidered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the person may appeal the
  Minister's decision that the passport is to be cancelled within any
  further time that a judge may, before or after the end of those 30
  days, fix or allow.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination and disposition

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an appeal is made, the judge must, without delay,
  determine whether cancelling the passport is reasonable on the basis
  of the information available to him or her and may, if he or she finds
  that cancelling it is unreasonable, quash the Minister's decision that
  the passport is to be cancelled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The following rules apply to appeals under this
  section:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2024, c. 16, s. 96\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2024, c. 16, s. 96\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2024, c. 16, s. 96\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the judge must provide the appellant and the
    Minister with an opportunity to be heard;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the judge may receive into evidence anything that,
    in the judge's opinion, is reliable and appropriate, even if it is
    inadmissible in a court of law, and may base his or her decision on
    that evidence;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2024, c. 16, s. 96\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the judge determines that evidence or other
    information provided by the Minister is not relevant or if the
    Minister withdraws the evidence or other information, the judge must
    not base his or her decision on that evidence or other information
    and must return it to the Minister; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the judge must ensure the confidentiality of all
    evidence and other information that the Minister withdraws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, sections 38.2 to 38.45 of the
  [Canada Evidence Act](/eng/acts/C-5) apply to appeals under this
  section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection of information on an appeal

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subsections 4(3) and (4)
apply to any appeal of a decision made under section 4 and to any
further appeal, with any necessary modifications.

## [Judicial Review]{.HTitleText1} {#h-396749 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusals or revocations under [Canadian
Passport Order](/eng/regulations/SI-81-86) --- terrorism or national
security

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  rules set out in subsection (2) apply to judicial review proceedings
  in respect of the following decisions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a decision of the Minister under the [Canadian
    Passport Order](/eng/regulations/SI-81-86) that a passport is not to
    be issued or is to be revoked on the grounds that the refusal to
    issue or the revocation is necessary to prevent the commission of a
    terrorism offence, as defined in section 2 of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46), or for the national security of Canada or a
    foreign country or state; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a decision of the Minister under that Order that
    passport services are not to be delivered to a person on a ground
    referred to in paragraph (a) if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Minister's decision is made after he or she
      decides, on the same ground, that a passport is not to be issued
      to the person or is to be revoked, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Minister's decision is made after the
      passport issued to the person has expired, but, based on facts
      that occurred before the expiry date, he or she could have decided
      that the passport is to be revoked on the same ground had it not
      expired.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following rules apply for the purposes of this
  section:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2024, c. 16, s. 97\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2024, c. 16, s. 97\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2024, c. 16, s. 97\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the judge must provide the applicant and the
    Minister with an opportunity to be heard;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2024, c. 16, s. 97\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the judge determines that evidence or other
    information provided by the Minister is not relevant or if the
    Minister withdraws the evidence or other information, the judge must
    not base his or her decision on that evidence or other information
    and must return it to the Minister; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the judge must ensure the confidentiality of all
    evidence and other information that the Minister withdraws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, sections 38.2 to 38.45 of the
  [Canada Evidence Act](/eng/acts/C-5) apply to judicial review
  proceedings referred to in subsection (1).
