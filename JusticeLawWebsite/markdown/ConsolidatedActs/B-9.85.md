## Budget Implementation Act, 2001 {#budget-implementation-act-2001 .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2002, c. 9
:::

Assented to 2002-03-27

An Act to implement certain provisions of the budget tabled in
Parliament on December 10, 2001
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
