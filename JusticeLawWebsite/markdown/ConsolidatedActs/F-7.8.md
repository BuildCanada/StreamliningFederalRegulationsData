## Federal-Provincial Fiscal Arrangements Act {#federal-provincial-fiscal-arrangements-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"} 1970, c. F-6
:::

An Act to authorize the making of certain fiscal payments to provinces,
and to authorize the entry into tax collection agreements with provinces
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-238871 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Federal-Provincial Fiscal Arrangements Act](/eng/acts/F-7.8).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1966-67, c. 89, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-238876 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act

[[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period of twelve
    months commencing on the 1st day of April and ending on the 31st day
    of March next following; ([année financière]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[former Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[former Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Federal-Provincial
    Fiscal Arrangements Act](/eng/acts/F-7.8), chapter 58 of the
    Statutes of Canada, 1960-61; ([loi antérieure]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Finance; ([Ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by
    regulation; ([prescrit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include the Northwest
    Territories or the Yukon Territory. ([province]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- 1966-67, c. 89, s. 2
:::

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Equalization, Stabilization and Succession Duty Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-238886 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial revenue equalization payments

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Act, the
Minister may pay to a province, for each fiscal year in the period
commencing on the 1st day of April 1967 and ending on the 31st day of
March 1972, a provincial revenue equalization payment not exceeding the
amount computed in accordance with section 7.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1966-67, c. 89, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial revenue stabilization payments

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Act, the
Minister may pay to a province, for each fiscal year commencing on or
after the 1st day of April 1967, a provincial revenue stabilization
payment not exceeding the amount computed in accordance with section 10.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1966-67, c. 89, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Succession duty payments

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, for each fiscal year commencing on or after the 1st day
  of April 1967, pay to a province that does not levy a succession duty
  as defined by regulation in respect of successions or transmissions
  consequent upon, or on property passing upon, any death occurring in
  the fiscal year, an amount equal to the basic estate tax applicable to
  the province for the fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, for each fiscal year commencing on
  or after the 1st day of April 1967, pay to a province that levies a
  succession duty as defined by regulation in respect of successions or
  transmissions consequent upon, or on property passing upon, any death
  occurring in the fiscal year, if that province does not increase its
  succession duties in the fiscal year beyond the rates thereof in
  effect on the 31st day of March 1964, an amount equal to one-third of
  the basic estate tax applicable to the province for the fiscal year.

- [[Basic estate tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section [[basic estate
  tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} as applied to a province for a fiscal year
  means the amount, as determined by the Minister, that would be derived
  from a tax equal to seventy-five per cent of the total amount of
  estate tax payable pursuant to the Estate Tax Act in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} property situated in the province and included in
    the estates of persons dying in the fiscal year domiciled in the
    province,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} property situated in the province and included in
    the estates of persons dying in the fiscal year domiciled outside
    the province, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} property (other than real property) situated
    outside Canada, passing to persons domiciled or resident in the
    province and included in the estates of persons dying in the fiscal
    year domiciled in the province.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1966-67, c. 89, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}C.R.F.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The amounts authorized to
be paid by sections 3, 4 and 5 shall be paid out of the Consolidated
Revenue Fund at such times and in such manner as may be prescribed.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1966-67, c. 89, s. 6
:::

### [Provincial Revenue Equalization Payments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-238911 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial revenue equalization payments

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The provincial revenue
equalization payment that may be paid to a province for a fiscal year is
an amount equal to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the general equalization applicable to the province
  for the fiscal year computed in accordance with section 8,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a province to which a grant was paid
  under subsection 3(2) of the former Act in respect of the fiscal year
  commencing on the 1st day of April 1966, the guaranteed equalization
  applicable to the province for the fiscal year computed in accordance
  with subsection 9(1), or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the Province of Saskatchewan and in
  respect of each fiscal year in the period commencing on the 1st day of
  April 1967 and ending on the 31st day of March 1971, the guaranteed
  equalization applicable to the province for the fiscal year computed
  in accordance with subsection 9(2),

whichever is the greatest.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1966-67, c. 89, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General equalization

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  general equalization applicable to a province for a fiscal year is an
  amount, as determined by the Minister, equal to the product obtained
  by multiplying the population of the province for the fiscal year by
  the amount, if any, that will cause

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the per capita amount derived by dividing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of the products obtained by
      multiplying the national average provincial revenue rate for each
      revenue source for the fiscal year by the revenue base for that
      revenue source for the province for the fiscal year,

    by

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the population of the province for the fiscal
      year,

  to equal

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the per capita amount derived by dividing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of the products obtained by
      multiplying the national average provincial revenue rate for each
      revenue source for the fiscal year by the revenue base for that
      revenue source for all the provinces for the fiscal year,

    by

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the total population of all the provinces for
      the fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section

  [[fiscal period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fiscal period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} for a revenue source means
      such period as may be prescribed, in the case of that revenue
      source, for the purpose of determining the national average
      provincial revenue rate for that revenue source for a fiscal
      year; ([exercice financier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[national average provincial revenue rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[national average provincial revenue
      rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} for a revenue source for a fiscal year
      is the rate derived by dividing

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total revenue, as determined by the
        Minister, from that revenue source for all the provinces for the
        fiscal period prescribed in the case of that revenue source,

      by

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total revenue base, as determined by the
        Minister, for that revenue source for all the provinces for the
        fiscal period prescribed in the case of that revenue
        source; ([taux national moyen du revenu
        provincial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[revenue base]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[revenue base]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} for a revenue source for a
      province for a fiscal year has the meaning given to that
      expression by regulation; ([base de revenu]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[revenue source]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[revenue source]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following
      sources, as more particularly defined by regulation, from which
      provincial revenues are or may be derived, namely:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} personal income tax,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} corporation income tax,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} succession duties and shares of estate tax,

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} general sales tax,

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} motor fuel tax,

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} motor vehicle revenues,

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} alcoholic beverage revenues,

      - [(h)]{.lawlabel} forestry revenues,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} oil royalties,

      - [(j)]{.lawlabel} natural gas royalties,

      - [(k)]{.lawlabel} sales of Crown leases and reservations on oil
        and natural gas lands,

      - [(l)]{.lawlabel} oil and gas revenues, other than those
        described in paragraphs (i), (j) and (k),

      - [(m)]{.lawlabel} metallic and non-metallic mineral revenues,

      - [(n)]{.lawlabel} water power rentals,

      - [(o)]{.lawlabel} provincial taxes, other than those described in
        any of paragraphs (a) to (n), and

      - [(p)]{.lawlabel} miscellaneous provincial revenues. ([source de
        revenu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In determining for the purposes of the definition
  [[national average provincial revenue rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
  subsection (2) the revenue from the revenue source described in
  paragraph (a) of the definition [[revenue source]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  in subsection (2) for a province for a fiscal year, the Minister may
  deduct from the amount that but for this subsection would have been
  the revenue therefrom the estimated amount, as determined by the
  Minister, by which the revenues derived by Canada from individual
  income taxes for the taxation year ending in that fiscal year under
  the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) are less than the revenues that
  would have been derived from such taxes for that taxation year if no
  additional amounts had been deductible under section 33 of that Act,
  in the case of individuals resident in that province in that taxation
  year,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in consequence of the province providing schooling
    allowances within the meaning of the Youth Allowances Act, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in consequence of the application to that province
    of the Established Programs (Interim Arrangements) Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of population

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For purposes of this section

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the population of a province

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a calendar year in which a census thereof was
      taken, is the population as ascertained by the census, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for any other calendar year, is the population
      on the 1st day of June in that year as estimated by the Dominion
      Statistician on the assumption that the population changed by the
      same number of persons annually between censuses; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the population of a province for a fiscal year is
    the population determined in accordance with this subsection for the
    calendar year in which the fiscal year begins.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S.C. 1970, c. F-6, s. 8
- 1970-71-72, c. 43, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Guaranteed equalization

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  guaranteed equalization applicable to a province referred to in
  paragraph 7(b) for a fiscal year is an amount equal to the aggregate
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amounts payable to the province under
    subsections 3(1) and (2) of the former Act in respect of the fiscal
    year commencing on the 1st day of April 1966,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the Provinces of Nova Scotia, New
    Brunswick and Newfoundland, the amount of ten and one-half million
    dollars each, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the Province of Prince Edward
    Island, the amount of three and one-half million dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saskatchewan transitional payments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The guaranteed equalization applicable to the
  Province of Saskatchewan for a fiscal year is an amount, as determined
  by the Minister, equal to the product obtained by multiplying the
  amount payable to the Province of Saskatchewan under subsection 3(1)
  of the former Act in respect of the fiscal year commencing on the 1st
  day of April 1966, by the following fraction:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the fiscal year commencing on April
    1, 1967, four-fifths;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the fiscal year commencing on April
    1, 1968, three-fifths;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the fiscal year commencing on April
    1, 1969, two-fifths; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the fiscal year commencing on April
    1, 1970, one-fifth.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1966-67, c. 89, s. 9
:::

### [Provincial Revenue Stabilization Payments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-238983 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial revenue stabilization payments

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  provincial revenue stabilization payment that may be paid to a
  province for a fiscal year is the amount, as determined by the
  Minister, by which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ninety-five per cent of the net general revenue of
    the province for the immediately preceding fiscal year

  exceeds

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the adjusted current net general revenue of the
    province for the fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section

  [[adjusted current net general revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[adjusted current net general revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, as
      applied to a province for a fiscal year, means the net general
      revenue for the fiscal year adjusted in prescribed manner on the
      basis of the revenue rates and revenue structure of the province
      in effect for the immediately preceding year; ([revenu général net
      courant ajusté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[net general revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[net general revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, as applied to a
      province for a fiscal year, means the net general revenue for the
      fiscal year as shown in the table "Net General Revenue for Fiscal
      Year" in the publication of the Dominion Bureau of Statistics
      entitled "Provincial Government Finance, Revenue and Expenditure",
      altered in prescribed manner to reflect

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the average revenue of the province from the
        revenue source described in paragraph (c) of the definition
        [[revenue source]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 8(2) for
        that and the two immediately preceding fiscal years, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the average revenue of the province from the
        revenue source described in paragraph (k) of the definition
        [[revenue source]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 8(2) for
        that and the four immediately preceding fiscal years,

      and minus any amount deductible under subsection 8(3) in
      determining the revenue of the province for the fiscal year from
      the revenue source referred to in that subsection. ([revenu
      général net]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to be made

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A provincial revenue stabilization payment may be
  paid to a province in respect of a fiscal year only upon receipt by
  the Minister, not later than eighteen months after the end of the
  fiscal year, of an application by the province therefor containing
  such information as may be prescribed.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1966-67, c. 89, s. 10
:::

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Post-Secondary Education Adjustment Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-239001 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this Part

  [[adjustment payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[adjustment payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a post-secondary

      de rajustement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[educational institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[educational institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      institution of learning that offers courses at a post-secondary
      level; ([établissement d'enseignement]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[federal revenue abatement relating to post-secondary education]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal revenue abatement relating to post-secondary
      education]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} applicable to a province for a

      15; ([abattement du revenu fédéral relatif à l'enseignement

  [[junior matriculation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [[operating expenditures]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[operating expenditures]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} for post-secondary

  :   [[post-secondary education]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} requires for admission the attainment of a

        province,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is of not less than twenty-four weeks duration,
        and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has been certified as a course of studies at a

        designated by the Lieutenant Governor in Council of the province
        for such purpose; ([enseignement
        post-secondaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[post-secondary level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[post-secondary level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning given

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of population and
  financial year

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Part

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the population of a province for a calendar year is
    the population on the 1st day of June in that year as estimated by
    the Dominion Statistician; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a financial year of an educational institution is
    related to a fiscal year if more than one-half of the total number
    of days in the financial year fall within the fiscal year.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1966-67, c. 89, s. 11

      post-secondary education applicable to the province for the fiscal
      year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustment payments after 1967

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the adjustment payment
  that may be paid to a province for a fiscal year commencing after the
  1st day of April 1967 is an amount equal to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a province in respect of which the
    amount determined under subparagraph (1)(a)(i) is greater than the
    amount determined under subparagraph (1)(a)(ii), the amount, as
    determined by the Secretary of State of Canada, that is equal to
    fifty per cent of the operating expenditures for post-secondary
    education in the province in the fiscal year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other province, the product
    obtained by multiplying

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined for the immediately

    by

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quotient derived by dividing

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of the amounts, as determined by

      by

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of the amounts, as determined by

  minus

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the federal revenue abatement relating to

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a province described in paragraph

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the portion determined in the manner described

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the amount, if any, determined in the manner

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other province, the amount

  minus

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate determined in the manner described in

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operating expenditures

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  the operating expenditures for post-secondary education in a province
  in a fiscal year are the aggregate of the operating expenditures
  incurred for post-secondary education by or in respect of each
  educational institution in the province during the financial year of
  the institution related to the fiscal year, but do not include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any amount expended in respect of student financial
    aid;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any amount expended as or on account of the capital
    cost of land, buildings, physical plant, facilities or equipment,
    except as otherwise proviced by regulation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any amount expended as or on account of interest;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any amount expended in payment of a capital debt;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any provision for depreciation on buildings,
    physical plant, facilities or equipment;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any amount expended in respect of a prescribed
    ancillary enterprise undertaken or operated by an educational
    institution; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} such portion of any amount expended as or on
    account of rent on land, buildings, physical plant, facilities or
    equipment as may be prescribed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In determining the operating expenditures incurred
  for post-secondary education by or in respect of an educational
  institution during a financial year of the institution, there shall be
  deducted from the amount thereof otherwise determined

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any amount received by the institution in the year
    for assisted, sponsored or contract research; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any amount received by the institution in the year
    from Her Majesty in right of Canada or any agent thereof or from the
    Canada Council;

  except as otherwise provided by regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In determining the operating expenditures for
  post-secondary education in a province in a fiscal year, there shall
  be deducted from the amount thereof otherwise determined any amount
  paid to the province in the year by Her Majesty in right of Canada or
  any agent thereof, otherwise than pursuant to this Act, that is
  prescribed for purposes of this subsection to be an amount paid in
  respect of post-secondary education.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1966-67, c. 89, s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Federal revenue abatement

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The federal revenue
abatement relating to post-secondary education applicable to a province
for a fiscal year is the amount of the revenue loss sustained by Canada
in respect of the fiscal year, by virtue of certain deductions permitted
by Part I of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) in computing income
taxes payable for a taxation year ending in the fiscal year, calculated
as the aggregate of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount, as determined by the Minister, that would
  be derived from a tax

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on the incomes (other than incomes from businesses)
    of individuals resident in the province on the last day of that
    taxation year, within the meaning of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on the incomes (other than incomes from
    businesses) earned in the province in that taxation year by
    individuals not resident in Canada at any time during that taxation
    year, within the meaning of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3),
    and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} on the incomes from businesses earned in the
    province in that taxation year by individuals, within the meaning of
    the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3),

  equal to four one-hundredths of the tax otherwise payable, within the
  meaning of section 33 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), on
  those incomes; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount, as determined by the Minister, that would
  be derived from a tax on the income earned in the province by each
  corporation (other than a non-resident owned investment corporation as
  defined in the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) or a corporation
  specified in Schedule D to the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11) that is an agent of Her Majesty in right of
  Canada) that maintained a permanent establishment in the province at
  any time during its taxation year, for the purposes of the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), ending in the calendar year that ends in the
  fiscal year, at the rate of one per cent of its taxable income earned
  in the province in that taxation year, such tax to be computed as if
  imposed under the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) and regulations
  thereunder.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S.C. 1970, c. F-6, s. 15
- 1970-71-72, c. 43, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of operating expenditures

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  determining the operating expenditures for post-secondary education in
  a province in a fiscal year, the Secretary of State of Canada shall
  have recourse to and be guided by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any provincial return of operating expenditures
    submitted to him for the fiscal year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any information contained in a report made to him
    by the Minister of Supply and Services in connection with such
    operating expenditures; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} such additional information as he may consider
    appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial return

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section a provincial return
  of operating expenditures for a fiscal year is a statistical return,
  in prescribed form, relating to the operating expenditures for
  post-secondary education in the province in the fiscal year,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that has been signed by the Deputy Minister of
    Education of the province or such other provincial officer as may be
    designated by the Lieutenant Governor in Council for the purpose;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that has been certified by the provincial auditor
    as having been examined by him, and, to the best of his knowledge
    and belief,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} as accurately setting forth the operating
      expenditures for post-secondary education in the province in the
      fiscal year, calculated in accordance with the requirements of
      this Act and the regulations, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} as being based on

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} financial returns for the fiscal year in
        respect of each educational institution in the province that is
        not a secondary institution, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a financial report for the fiscal year in
        respect of all secondary institutions in the province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial returns and report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a financial return for a fiscal year in respect of
    an educational institution that is not a secondary institution is a
    statement relating to the operating expenditures incurred for
    post-secondary education by that institution during its financial
    year related to the fiscal year, that has been certified by an
    independent auditor as having been examined by him, and, to the best
    of his knowledge and belief, as accurately setting forth the
    operating expenditures incurred for post-secondary education by that
    institution during its financial year, calculated in accordance with
    the requirements of this Act and the regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a financial report for a fiscal year in respect of
    all secondary institutions in a province is a statement setting
    forth the operating expenditures incurred for post-secondary
    education by or in respect of all such institutions during their
    financial years related to the fiscal year, that has been signed by
    the Deputy Minister of Education of the province or such other
    provincial officer as may be designated by the Lieutenant Governor
    in Council for the purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section

  [[independent auditor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[independent auditor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an auditor who
      is a member in good standing of an institution or association of
      accountants incorporated by or under the authority of the
      legislature of a province but who is not, except as otherwise
      provided by regulation, the same person or a member of the same
      firm or office of auditors as, or employed by the same employer
      as, the provincial auditor; ([vérificateur
      indépendant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[provincial auditor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[provincial auditor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such person as is
      charged by law with the audit of the accounts of a
      province; ([vérificateur provincial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[secondary institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[secondary institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
      province, means an institution in that province that offers at a
      post-secondary level only prescribed programs of
      study. ([établissement secondaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- 1966-67, c. 89, s. 16
- 1968-69, c. 28, s. 105
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to file return

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Where a provincial return
of operating expenditures for a fiscal year as described in subsection
16(2) has not been submitted to the Secretary of State of Canada,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the fiscal year commencing on the 1st
  day of April 1967, within twelve months after the end of the fiscal
  year, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any subsequent fiscal year, within
  nine months after the end of the fiscal year,

the Secretary of State of Canada, in determining the operating
expenditures for post-secondary education in the province in the fiscal
year, shall have recourse to and be guided by such information available
to him as he considers appropriate, but in no such case shall the amount
determined by the Secretary of State of Canada to be equal to fifty per
cent of the operating expenditures for post-secondary education in the
province in the fiscal year be less than the product obtained by
multiplying \$15 by the population of the province for the calendar year
ending in the fiscal year.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1966-67, c. 89, s. 17
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report of Minister of Supply and Services

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Supply

  16(3), and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} request and receive from the independent auditor who
  certified any financial return for a fiscal year in respect of an
  educational institution, as described in subsection 16(3), such
  information, reports and explanations as he deems necessary in order
  to satisfy himself as to the method and procedure employed by such
  auditor in determining the operating expenditures incurred for
  post-secondary education by that institution during its financial year
  related to the fiscal year,

and shall make such report to the Secretary of State of Canada in
respect thereof as he considers appropriate.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1966-67, c. 89, s. 18
- 1968-69, c. 28, s. 105

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-239135 .Part}

### [Tax Collection Agreements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-239136 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tax collection agreements

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a province imposes taxes on the income of individuals or corporations
  or both, the Minister, with the approval of the Governor in Council,
  may, on behalf of the Government of Canada, enter into an agreement
  with the government of the province pursuant to which the Government
  of Canada will collect the provincial taxes on behalf of the province
  and will make payments to the province in respect of the taxes so
  collected, in accordance with such terms and conditions as the
  agreement prescribes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments to agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister, with the approval of the Governor in
  Council, may, on behalf of the Government of Canada, enter into an
  agreement amending the terms and conditions of an agreement entered
  into pursuant to subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of requirements of
  provincial law to certain persons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the law of a province that imposes a tax on
  income as described in subsection (1) contains provisions requiring
  every person making a payment of a specified kind to another person to
  deduct or withhold therefrom an amount and to remit that amount on
  account of such tax, effect may be given to those provisions, in
  accordance with the regulations, in relation to persons to whom such
  payments are made out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund or by an agent
  of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1966-67, c. 89, s. 20
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements under former Act

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Where an agreement was
entered into pursuant to subsection 6(1) or (2) of the former Act, the
agreement shall be deemed to have been entered into pursuant to
subsection 20(1).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1966-67, c. 89, s. 21
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advance payments under agreements

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Where a province has
entered into a tax collection agreement, the Minister, in accordance
with the regulations, may make advance payments to the province out of
the Consolidated Revenue Fund on account of any amount that may become
payable to the province pursuant to the agreement.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1966-67, c. 89, s. 22
:::
