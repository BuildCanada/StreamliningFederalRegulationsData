## Wild Animal and Plant Protection and Regulation of International and Interprovincial Trade Act {#wild-animal-and-plant-protection-and-regulation-of-international-and-interprovincial-trade-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1992, c. 52
:::

Assented to 1992-12-17

An Act respecting the protection of certain species of wild animals and
plants and the regulation of international and interprovincial trade in
those species
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468884 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Wild Animal and Plant Protection and Regulation of International
and Interprovincial Trade Act](/eng/acts/W-8.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468887 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any specimen, whether living or
    dead, of any species of animal that is listed as "fauna" in an
    appendix to the Convention, and includes any egg, sperm, tissue
    culture or embryo of any such animal; ([animal]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on
    international trade in endangered species of wild fauna and flora,
    made on March 3, 1973 in Washington, D.C., United States and
    ratified by Canada on April 10, 1975, as amended from time to time,
    to the extent that the amendment is binding on
    Canada; ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any vehicle, aircraft or
    water-borne craft or any other contrivance that is used to move
    persons or goods; ([moyen de transport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[distribute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[distribute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes
    sell; ([distribuer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of the
    Environment; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person, or a person who
    belongs to a class of persons, designated pursuant to section
    12; ([agent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any specimen, whether living or
    dead, of any species of plant that is listed as "flora" in an
    appendix to the Convention, and includes any seed, spore, pollen or
    tissue culture of any such plant; ([végétal]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by
    regulation; ([Version anglaise seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[transport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes
    send. ([acheminer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Binding on Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468899 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468902 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to protect certain species of animals and plants, particularly by
implementing the Convention and regulating international and
interprovincial trade in animals and plants.

## [Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468905 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Federal-provincial agreements

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may enter into
an agreement with the government of any province to provide for the
cooperative management and administration of this Act and to avoid
conflict between, and duplication in, federal and provincial regulatory
activity.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468908 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Importation

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall import into Canada any animal or plant that was taken, or
  any animal or plant, or any part or derivative of an animal or plant,
  that was possessed, distributed or transported in contravention of any
  law of any foreign state.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Importation and exportation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, no person shall, except
  under and in accordance with a permit issued pursuant to subsection
  10(1), import into Canada or export from Canada any animal or plant,
  or any part or derivative of an animal or plant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interprovincial transport

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, no person shall, except
  under and in accordance with a permit issued pursuant to subsection
  10(1), transport from one province to another province any animal or
  plant, or any part or derivative of an animal or plant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transport requiring provincial
authorization

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the transportation out of a province of an animal or plant, or any
  part or derivative of an animal or plant, is permitted by the province
  only if the person who transports it holds a permit issued by a
  competent authority in that province, no person shall, except under
  and in accordance with such a permit, transport any animal, plant or
  part or derivative of an animal or plant from that province to another
  province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial prohibitions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall transport from a province to another
  province any animal or plant, or any part or derivative of an animal
  or plant, where the animal or plant was taken, or the animal, plant,
  part or derivative was possessed, distributed or transported, in
  contravention of any provincial Act or regulation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Possession

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the regulations,
no person shall knowingly possess an animal or plant, or any part or
derivative of an animal or plant,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that has been imported or transported in
  contravention of this Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of transporting it from one province
  to another province in contravention of this Act or exporting it from
  Canada in contravention of this Act; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of distributing or offering to
  distribute it if the animal or plant, or the animal or plant from
  which the part or derivative comes, is listed in Appendix I to the
  Convention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Every person who imports
into Canada, exports from Canada or transports from one province to
another province an animal or plant, or any part or derivative of an
animal or plant, shall keep in Canada, in the prescribed manner and for
the prescribed period, any documents that are required to be kept by the
regulations.

## [Federal Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468928 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, on application and on such terms and conditions as the
  Minister thinks fit, issue a permit authorizing the importation,
  exportation or interprovincial transportation of an animal or plant,
  or any part or derivative of an animal or plant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for permit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application shall be made in the form and on the
  terms and conditions that the Minister requires, contain all the
  information that the Minister requires and be accompanied by the
  prescribed fees.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation or suspension

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, after giving a person who holds a
  permit an opportunity to make representations, revoke or suspend the
  permit for contravention of any term or condition of the permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation by Minister

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may delegate to any minister of the
  Crown in right of Canada or of a province or to any person who is
  employed by the Government of Canada, the government of a province or
  any other government in Canada any power conferred on the Minister
  under this section relating to permits. The minister or other person
  to whom the power is delegated may then exercise the power subject to
  any terms and conditions that the Minister specifies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Misrepresentation

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** No person shall knowingly
furnish any false or misleading information or make any
misrepresentation with respect to any matter in this Act.

## [Administration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468942 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers and analysts

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate such persons or classes of persons as the
  Minister considers necessary to act as officers or analysts for the
  purposes of this Act or any provision of this Act, and if the person
  to be designated is an employee, or the class of persons to be
  designated consists of employees, of the government of a province, the
  Minister shall only designate that person or class with the agreement
  of that government.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of peace officers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Officers designated under subsection (1) have, for
  the purposes of this Act, all the powers of a peace officer, but the
  Minister may limit, in any manner the Minister considers appropriate,
  the powers that certain officers may exercise for the purposes of this
  Act and, where those powers are so limited, they shall be specified in
  the certificate referred to in subsection (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate to be shown

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On entering any place under this Act, an officer or
  analyst shall, on request, show the person in charge or the occupant
  of the place a certificate, in the form approved by the Minister,
  certifying that the officer or analyst, as the case may be, has been
  designated under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall knowingly make any false or
  misleading statement either orally or in writing to, or obstruct or
  hinder, an officer or analyst who is carrying out duties or functions
  under this Act or the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Officers and analysts are not personally liable for
  anything they do or omit to do in good faith under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents admissible in evidence

- **[[12.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document made, given or issued under this Act and appearing to be
  signed by an analyst is admissible in evidence and, in the absence of
  evidence to the contrary, is proof of the statements contained in the
  document without proof of the signature or official character of the
  person appearing to have signed the document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attendance of analyst

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party against whom the document is produced may,
  with leave of the court, require the attendance of the analyst who
  signed it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No document referred to in subsection (1) may be
  received in evidence unless the party intending to produce it has
  given to the party against whom it is intended to be produced
  reasonable notice of that intention together with a copy of the
  document.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Detention

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Any thing that has been
imported into or is about to be exported from Canada, or has been
transported, or is about to be transported, from a province to another
province, may be detained by an officer until the officer is satisfied
that the thing has been dealt with in accordance with this Act and the
regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspections

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of ensuring compliance with this Act and the regulations,
  an officer may at any reasonable time enter and inspect any place in
  which the officer believes, on reasonable grounds, there is any thing
  to which this Act applies, or there are any documents relating to the
  administration of this Act or the regulations, and the officer may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} open or cause to be opened any container that the
    officer believes, on reasonable grounds, contains such a thing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inspect any such thing and take samples free of
    charge;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} require any person to produce for inspection or
    copying, in whole or in part, any document that the officer
    believes, on reasonable grounds, contains any information relevant
    to the administration of this Act or the regulations; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} seize any thing by means of or in relation to which
    the officer believes, on reasonable grounds, this Act or the
    regulations have been contravened or that the officer believes, on
    reasonable grounds, will afford evidence of a contravention of this
    Act or the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Analysts

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} An analyst may, for the purposes of this Act,
  accompany an officer who is carrying out an inspection of a place
  under this section, and the analyst may, when accompanying the
  officer, enter the place and exercise any of the powers described in
  paragraphs (1)(a) and (b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conveyance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of carrying out the inspection, an
  officer may stop a conveyance or direct that it be moved by the route
  and in the manner that the officer may specify, to a place specified
  by the officer where the inspection can be carried out.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dwelling-place

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An officer may not enter a dwelling-place except with
  the consent of the occupant or under the authority of a warrant issued
  under subsection (4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where on [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application
  a justice of the peace is satisfied by information on oath that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conditions for entry described in
    subsection (1) exist in relation to a dwelling-place,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entry to the dwelling-place is necessary for any
    purpose in relation to the administration of this Act or the
    regulations, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} entry to the dwelling-place has been refused or
    there are reasonable grounds for believing that entry will be
    refused,

  the justice may issue a warrant authorizing an officer to enter the
  dwelling-place subject to any conditions that may be specified in the
  warrant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of passage

**[[14.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14.1 .sectionLabel}** While carrying out
duties or functions under this Act, officers and analysts, and any
persons accompanying them, may enter on and pass through or over private
property without being liable for doing so and without any person having
the right to object to that use of the property.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 118

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance

**[[14.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14.2 .sectionLabel}** The owner or person
in charge of a place being inspected under section 14, and every person
found in the place, shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} give the officer or analyst all reasonable assistance
  to enable the officer or analyst to carry out their duties or
  functions under this Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the officer or analyst with any information
  with respect to the administration of this Act that he or she may
  reasonably require.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 118
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Search without warrant

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
ensuring compliance with this Act and the regulations, an officer may
exercise the powers of search and seizure provided for in section 487 of
the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) without a warrant if the conditions
for obtaining a warrant exist but, by reason of exigent circumstances,
it would not be feasible to obtain a warrant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Custody of things seized

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  officer who detains or seizes a thing under section 13, 14 or 15 or
  under a warrant issued under the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) may
  retain custody of the thing or transfer custody of it to such person
  as the officer may designate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Perishable things

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a thing referred to in subsection (1) is
  perishable, the officer may dispose of it or destroy it and any
  proceeds realized from its disposition shall be paid to the lawful
  owner of the thing unless proceedings under this Act are instituted
  within ninety days after the detention or seizure.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Abandonment

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The owner, importer or
exporter of any thing detained or seized under this Act may abandon the
thing to Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to remove

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an officer believes, on reasonable grounds, that any thing is being or
  has been imported into Canada in contravention of this Act or the
  regulations, the officer may, whether or not the thing is detained or
  seized, require, by delivering a notice in the prescribed form and
  manner, that it be removed from Canada in accordance with the
  regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline for removal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a notice to remove a thing is delivered, the
  removal shall be carried out within the period specified in the notice
  or, if no such period is specified in the notice, within ninety days
  after its delivery.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a person is convicted of an offence under this Act, the convicting
  court may, in addition to any punishment imposed, order that any thing
  detained or seized, or any proceeds realized from its disposition, be
  forfeited to Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture on consent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the owner of a thing detained or seized under
  this Act consents to its forfeiture, it is thereupon forfeited to Her
  Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Automatic forfeiture

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a thing is detained or seized under this Act,
  it, or the proceeds realized from its disposition, is forfeited to Her
  Majesty

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a thing that has been detained under
    section 13, if the thing has not been removed within the period
    prescribed by the regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a thing that has been seized, if
    ownership of the thing cannot be ascertained within thirty days
    after the seizure; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a thing that is the subject of a
    notice under section 18, if the thing has not been removed from
    Canada in compliance with that section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of thing where no forfeiture
  ordered

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the convicting court does not order the
  forfeiture of a detained or seized thing under subsection (1), the
  thing, or the proceeds realized from its disposition, shall be
  returned to the lawful owner of the thing or the person in whose
  possession the thing was at the time of the detention or seizure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a person is convicted of an offence under this
  Act, any thing seized or any proceeds realized from its disposition,
  may be retained until the fine is paid or the thing may be sold under
  execution in satisfaction of the fine and the proceeds applied, in
  whole or in part, in payment of the fine.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition by Minister

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a sample has been taken pursuant to paragraph 14(1)(b) or a thing has
  been forfeited or abandoned under this Act, it shall be dealt with and
  disposed of as the Minister may direct.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability for costs

**[[20.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20.1 .sectionLabel}** If a thing is seized
under this Act or under a warrant issued under the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the importer or exporter, as the case may be, and
the person who owned the thing at the time it was seized, the person who
had charge or control of the thing immediately before it was seized and
the person who possessed it immediately before it was seized are jointly
and severally, or solidarily, liable for all the costs of seizure,
detention, maintenance and forfeiture, including any destruction or
disposal costs, incurred by Her Majesty in right of Canada in relation
to the thing in excess of any proceeds of its disposition, if any.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 120

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the exemption of animals and plants, and
    parts and derivatives of animals and plants, from the operation of
    any provision of this Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} amending the definitions
    [[animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsection 6(1),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsection 6(2),

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsection 6(3),

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} in order to protect species that are subject to
        the legislative authority of Parliament, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} at the request of the minister who is
        responsible for the protection of wild animal or plant species
        of the government of the province into which the animal or plant

        the transport would be harmful to the environment of that
        province,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of section 7, in order to
      protect species of animals and plants in a province, other than
      those species that are subject to the legislative authority of
      Parliament, at the request of the minister who is responsible for
      the protection of wild animal or plant species of the government
      of the province, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of section 8;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} specifying the places and times at which, and the
    manner in which, animals and plants, classes of animals and plants
    and parts and derivatives of animals and plants may be imported into
    Canada and exported from Canada;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} respecting the marking of animals and plants, and
    parts and derivatives of animals and plants, and the packaging for
    animals, plants and parts and derivatives of animals and plants for
    importation into or exportation from Canada or for transportation
    from one province to another province;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the documents to be kept by persons
    mentioned in section 9 and the manner of keeping the documents and
    the period for which they are to be kept;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} specifying the terms and conditions under which
    animals and plants, and parts and derivatives of animals and plants,
    are to be removed from Canada under section 18;

  - [(g.1)]{.lawlabel} designating provisions of the regulations for the
    purposes of paragraph 22(1)(b);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the manner in which the proceeds
    resulting from the payment of fines or the execution of orders under
    this Act shall be distributed;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the fees or charges to be paid in
    connection with the administration of this Act and the terms and
    conditions of paying such fees and charges; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} generally to implement the Convention.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council shall make regulations

  later than ninety days after any change to a list in an appendix to
  the Convention, amend the regulations to reflect that change.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, on the

## [Offence and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-469072 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence --- persons

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person commits an offence who contravenes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any provision of this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any provision of the regulations designated by
    regulations made under paragraph 21(1)(g.1); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any order made under this Act by a court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty --- individuals

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every individual who commits an offence under
  subsection (1) is liable,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine of not less than
      \$15,000 and not more than \$1,000,000 or to imprisonment for a
      term of not more than five years, or to both, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a second or subsequent offence, to a fine of
      not less than \$30,000 and not more than \$2,000,000 or to
      imprisonment for a term of not more than five years, or to both;
      or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine of not less than
      \$5,000 and not more than \$300,000 or to imprisonment for a term
      of not more than six months, or to both, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a second or subsequent offence, to a fine of
      not less than \$10,000 and not more than \$600,000 or to
      imprisonment for a term of not more than six months, or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty --- other persons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person, other than an individual or a
  corporation referred to in subsection (4), that commits an offence
  under subsection (1) is liable,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine of not less than
      \$500,000 and not more than \$6,000,000, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a second or subsequent offence, to a fine of
      not less than \$1,000,000 and not more than \$12,000,000; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine of not less than
      \$100,000 and not more than \$4,000,000, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a second or subsequent offence, to a fine of
      not less than \$200,000 and not more than \$8,000,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty --- small revenue corporations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every corporation that commits an offence under
  subsection (1) and that the court determines under section 22.02 to be
  a small revenue corporation is liable,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine of not less than
      \$75,000 and not more than \$4,000,000, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a second or subsequent offence, to a fine of
      not less than \$150,000 and not more than \$8,000,000; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine of not less than
      \$25,000 and not more than \$2,000,000, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a second or subsequent offence, to a fine of
      not less than \$50,000 and not more than \$4,000,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Relief from minimum fine

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The court may impose a fine that is less than the
  minimum amount provided for in this section if it is satisfied, on the
  basis of evidence submitted to the court, that the minimum fine would
  cause undue financial hardship. The court shall provide reasons if it
  imposes a fine that is less than the minimum amount provided for in
  this section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fundamental purpose of sentencing

**[[22.07]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.07 .sectionLabel}** The fundamental
purpose of sentencing for offences under this Act is to contribute, in
light of the numerous serious threats to plants and animals and their
importance to the environment, to respect for the law regulating
international and interprovincial trade in animals and plants through
the imposition of just sanctions that have as their objectives

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to deter the offender and any other person from
  committing offences under this Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to denounce the unlawful trade in certain animals and
  plants and to make it unprofitable; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to recover, if possible, certain species of animals
  and plants unlawfully traded.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sentencing principles

- **[[22.08]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.08
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the principles and
  factors that the court is otherwise required to consider, including
  those set out in sections 718.1 to 718.21 of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the court shall consider the following
  principles when sentencing a person who is convicted of an offence
  under this Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the fine should be increased to
    account for every aggravating factor associated with the offence,
    including the aggravating factors set out in subsection (2); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the fine should reflect the gravity
    of each aggravating factor associated with the offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aggravating factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The aggravating factors are the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the offence caused damage or risk of damage,
    directly or indirectly, to animals or plants;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the offence caused damage or risk of damage to a
    unique, rare, particularly important or vulnerable species of animal
    or plant or population of animals or plants;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the offender committed the offence intentionally or
    recklessly;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the offender profited, or intended to profit, by
    committing the offence;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the offender has a history of non-compliance with
    federal or provincial legislation that relates to environmental or
    wildlife conservation or protection; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the offence involved a high degree of planning.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence of aggravating factor

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The absence of an aggravating factor set out in
  subsection (2) is not a mitigating factor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of
  [[damage]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraphs (2)(a) and (b),
  [[damage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes loss of use value and non-use
  value.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the court is satisfied of the existence of one or
  more of the aggravating factors set out in subsection (2) but decides
  not to increase the amount of the fine because of that factor, the
  court shall give reasons for that decision.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 14, s. 122
:::

## [Ticketable Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-469248 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  addition to the procedures set out in the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46) for commencing a proceeding, proceedings in
  respect of any prescribed offence may be commenced by an officer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} completing a ticket that consists of a summons
    portion and an information portion;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} delivering the summons portion of the ticket to the
    accused or mailing it to the accused at the accused's latest known
    address; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} filing the information portion of the ticket with a
    court of competent jurisdiction before or as soon as practicable
    after the summons portion has been delivered or mailed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of ticket

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The summons and information portions of a ticket
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out a description of the offence and the time
    and place of its alleged commission;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} include a statement, signed by the officer who
    completes the ticket, that the officer has reasonable grounds to
    believe that the accused committed the offence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set out the amount of the prescribed fine for the
    offence and the manner in which and period within which it may be
    paid;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} include a statement that if the accused pays the
    fine within the period set out in the ticket, a conviction will be
    entered and recorded against the accused; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} include a statement that if the accused wishes to
    plead not guilty or for any other reason fails to pay the fine
    within the period set out in the ticket, the accused must appear in
    the court and at the time set out in the ticket.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of forfeiture

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where any thing is seized under this Act and
  proceedings relating to the thing are commenced by way of the
  ticketing procedure described in subsection (1), the officer who
  completes the ticket shall give written notice to the accused that if
  the accused pays the prescribed fine within the period set out in the
  ticket, the thing, or any proceeds realized from its disposition,
  shall thereupon be forfeited to Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consequences of payment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where an accused to whom the summons portion of a
  ticket is delivered or mailed pays the prescribed fine within the
  period set out in the ticket,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the payment constitutes a plea of guilty to the
    offence described in the ticket and a conviction shall be entered
    against the accused and no further action shall be taken against the
    accused in respect of that offence; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any thing seized from the accused under this Act
    relating to the offence described in the ticket, or any proceeds
    realized from its disposition, are forfeited to Her Majesty and may
    be disposed of as the Minister directs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make regulations
  prescribing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} offences under this Act to which this section
    applies and the manner in which those offences may be described in
    tickets; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the fine for a prescribed offence,
    which amount shall not exceed one thousand dollars.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences by employees or agents

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** In any prosecution for an
offence under this Act, it is sufficient proof of the offence to
establish that it was committed by an employee or agent of the accused,
whether or not the employee or agent is identified or has been
prosecuted for the offence, unless the accused establishes that the
offence was committed without the knowledge or consent of the accused
and that the accused exercised all due diligence to prevent its
commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Venue

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** A prosecution for an
offence under this Act may be instituted, heard and determined in the
place where the offence was committed or the subject-matter of the
prosecution arose, where the accused was apprehended or where the
accused happens to be, or is carrying on business.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unpaid fees or charges

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Where any fee or charge
imposed under this Act is unpaid, the fee or charge, as the case may be,
may be recovered from the person on whom it was imposed as a debt due to
Her Majesty.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of information about
contraventions

- **[[27.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of encouraging
  compliance with this Act and the regulations, the Minister shall
  maintain, in a registry accessible to the public, information about
  all convictions of corporations for offences under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Information in the registry is to be maintained for a
  minimum of five years.

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall
annually prepare a report with respect to the administration of this Act
during the preceding calendar year and shall cause a copy of the report
to be laid before each House of Parliament on any of the first fifteen
days that the House is sitting after its completion.
