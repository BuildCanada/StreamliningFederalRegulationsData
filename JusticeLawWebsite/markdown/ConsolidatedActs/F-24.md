## Fishing and Recreational Harbours Act {#fishing-and-recreational-harbours-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. F-24
:::

An Act respecting the administration and development of certain fishing
and recreational harbours in Canada
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233419 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Fishing and Recreational Harbours Act](/eng/acts/F-24).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233424 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a province, includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any incorporated or unincorporated city, town,
      municipality, village, township, rural municipality, regional
      district administration or local improvement district
      administration,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any council of a band within the meaning of the
      [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5),

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the [[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined
      in subsection 2(1) of the [shíshálh Nation Self-Government
      Act](/eng/acts/S-6.6), and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a first nation named in Schedule II to the [Yukon
      First Nations Self-Government
      Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.6); ([organisme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fishing or recreational harbour]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fishing or recreational harbour]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any harbour, wharf, pier, breakwater, slipway,
      marina or part thereof, together with machinery, works, land and
      structures related or attached thereto, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other facility, installation, works or part
      thereof located on or adjacent to water

    where accommodation or services are provided principally for fishing
    or recreational vessels or the occupants thereof; ([port de pêche ou
    de plaisance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means tangible personal property other
    than vessels; ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada as is designated by the Governor in Council
    as the Minister for the purposes of this
    Act; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[scheduled harbour]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[scheduled harbour]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any fishing or
    recreational harbour or portion thereof included in a schedule
    prescribed by the regulations; ([ports inscrits]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes every description of ship,
    boat, craft, floating home or other structure used or capable of
    being used solely or partly for aquatic navigation without regard to
    method or lack of propulsion. ([bateau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-24, s. 2
- R.S., 1985, c. 20 (2nd Supp.), s. 3
- 1994, c. 35, s. 34
- [2022, c. 9, s. 42](#1365914-1368004){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1365914-1368004.html"
  role="button"}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Act

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Nothing
  in this Act applies to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any port, as defined in section 5 of the [Canada
    Marine Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7), or any harbour, works or property
    under the jurisdiction of a harbour commission established under an
    Act of Parliament; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any harbour, wharf, pier or breakwater under the
    control and management of any member of the Queen's Privy Council
    for Canada other than the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Act does not affect any of the powers or duties
  of the Minister of Transport or the Minister of Public Works and
  Government Services under any other Act of Parliament or regulations
  made pursuant thereto.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-24, s. 3
- 1998, c. 10, s. 181
- 1999, c. 31, s. 125
:::

## [Powers of the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233451 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Harbours under control of Minister

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The use, management and
maintenance of every scheduled harbour, the enforcement of regulations
relating thereto and the collection of charges for the use of every
scheduled harbour are under the control and administration of the
Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may undertake projects

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may undertake projects for the acquisition, development,
  construction, improvement or repair of any scheduled harbour or any
  fishing or recreational harbour to which this Act applies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements with provinces

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, subject to the regulations, enter
  into an agreement with any province to provide for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the undertaking jointly with the government of the
    province or any agency thereof of any project that the Minister is
    authorized to undertake under subsection (1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the payment to the province of contributions or
    grants in respect of the cost or any part of the cost of any project
    that is undertaken by the government of the province or any agency
    thereof and that the Minister is authorized to undertake under
    subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements with persons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, subject to the regulations, enter
  into an agreement with any person to provide for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the undertaking jointly with that person of any
    project that the Minister is authorized to undertake under
    subsection (1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the payment to that person of contributions or
    grants in respect of the cost or any part of the cost of any project
    that is undertaken by that person and that the Minister is
    authorized to undertake under subsection (1) and in respect of the
    cost of any planning studies and advisory services relating to any
    such project.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Studies

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of assisting the formulation and
  assessment of projects undertaken under subsection (1), the Minister
  may, subject to the regulations, undertake economic or other studies
  alone or jointly with the government of any province or agency thereof
  or with any person or educational institution.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Services of other departments

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall, in
carrying out any project or study under this Act, make use, wherever
possible, of the services and facilities of other departments, boards or
agencies of the Government of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions to be included in agreements

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every agreement entered
into pursuant to subsection 5(2) or (3) shall specify

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the respective proportions of the cost of any project
  to which the agreement relates that shall be paid by the Minister and
  the province or person or the contributions or grants in respect of
  that project that shall be paid by the Minister, and the times at
  which those amounts to be paid by the Minister or the province or
  person shall be paid;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the authority that shall be responsible for the
  undertaking, operation and maintenance of any project or any part
  thereof to which the agreement relates;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the respective proportions of the revenues from any
  project to which the agreement relates that are to be paid to the
  Minister and the province or person; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the terms and conditions respecting the operation and
  maintenance of any project to which the agreement relates and the
  charges, if any, to be charged to persons to whom any of the benefits
  of the project are made available.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Leases, licences and agreements for use
of scheduled harbours

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, subject
to the regulations,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} lease any scheduled harbour or any part thereof to
  any person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} grant a licence to any person for the use of any
  scheduled harbour or any part thereof; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} enter into an agreement with the government of any
  province or any agency thereof for the occupancy and use of any
  scheduled harbour or any part thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 8
:::

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233490 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing schedules naming and delimiting or
  describing the fishing or recreational harbours or portions thereof
  belonging to Her Majesty in right of Canada that are under the control
  and administration of the Minister for the purposes of this Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the maintenance of order and the safety of
  persons and property at any scheduled harbour;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} not inconsistent with any other Act of Parliament or
  regulations made thereunder, for the control of mooring, berthing,
  loading and discharging of vessels at any scheduled harbour;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} not inconsistent with any other Act of Parliament or
  regulations made thereunder, for the control of pollution at any
  scheduled harbour;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} prescribing standards for the accommodation and
  services provided or to be provided at any scheduled harbour;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} prescribing charges for the use of any scheduled
  harbour;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the duties or functions of persons
  appointed or designated under this Act or any other Act of Parliament
  to supervise or manage any fishing or recreational harbour to which
  this Act applies;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} governing inquiries into accidents and incidents held
  under section 26;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} prescribing terms and conditions of agreements
  entered into pursuant to subsection 5(2) or (3);

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the manner of undertaking economic or
  other studies pursuant to subsection 5(4);

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} prescribing terms and conditions of leases, licences
  and agreements entered into or granted pursuant to section 8;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the form of the tickets that may be
  issued pursuant to paragraph 25(1)(a);

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} respecting the detention and safe-keeping of vessels
  and goods seized under this Act and the payment of any reasonable
  costs incidental thereto;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the manner of disposing of anything
  forfeited under this Act; and

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} generally for carrying out the purposes and
  provisions of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 9
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 47, s. 53(F)
- 1984, c. 40, s. 30
:::

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233510 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement officers

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of enforcing this Act and the regulations, the Minister
  may designate as an enforcement officer any person who is, in the
  opinion of the Minister, qualified to be so designated and furnish
  that person with a certificate of his designation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Production of certificate of
  designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In carrying out the duties and functions of an
  enforcement officer under this Act, an enforcement officer shall, if
  so requested, produce the certificate of designation of the
  enforcement officer as such to the person appearing to be in charge of
  any vessel, vehicle, premises or goods in respect of which the officer
  is acting.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 10
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of enforcement officers

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Where an enforcement
officer believes on reasonable grounds that a provision of this Act or
the regulations is not being complied with, the officer may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} with a warrant issued under section 11.1, board any
  vessel or enter any vehicle or premises where or in respect of which
  the officer believes on reasonable grounds there may be evidence of
  the non-compliance and make any inspection the officer deems
  necessary;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} require the person appearing to be in charge of the
  vessel, vehicle or premises to produce for inspection, or for the
  purpose of making copies or extracts therefrom, any log book, document
  or paper that may, in the officer's opinion, provide evidence of that
  non-compliance;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} require any person found on board any vessel or in
  any vehicle or premises to give all reasonable assistance to enable
  the officer to carry out his duties and functions under this Act; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} prohibit the use of any scheduled harbour by any
  person, vessel or vehicle that the officer believes on reasonable
  grounds to be involved in that non-compliance and direct the removal
  of that person, vessel or vehicle from the harbour.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-24, s. 11
- R.S., 1985, c. 31 (1st Supp.), s. 36
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

**[[11.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.1 .sectionLabel}** Where on [ex
parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application a justice of the peace is
satisfied by information on oath that there are reasonable grounds to
believe that there is in any vessel, vehicle or premises

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} anything on or in respect of which any contravention
  under this Act has been or is suspected to have been committed, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} anything that there are reasonable grounds to believe
  will afford evidence with respect to the commission of any
  contravention under this Act,

the justice of the peace may issue a warrant under his hand authorizing
the enforcement officer named therein to enter that vessel, vehicle or
premises subject to such conditions as may be specified in the warrant
in order to make any inspection the enforcement officer deems necessary.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 31 (1st Supp.), s. 37
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

**[[11.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.2 .sectionLabel}** In executing a
warrant issued under section 11.1, the enforcement officer named therein
shall not use force unless the officer is accompanied by a peace officer
and the use of force has been specifically authorized in the warrant.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 31 (1st Supp.), s. 37
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where warrant not necessary

**[[11.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.3 .sectionLabel}** An enforcement
officer may exercise the powers referred to in paragraph 11(a) without a
warrant issued under section 11.1 if the conditions for obtaining the
warrant exist but by reason of exigent circumstances it would not be
practical to obtain the warrant.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 31 (1st Supp.), s. 37
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exigent circumstances

**[[11.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 11.3, exigent circumstances include circumstances in which the
delay necessary to obtain a warrant under section 11.1 would result in
danger to human life or safety or the loss or destruction of evidence.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 31 (1st Supp.), s. 37
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction of enforcement officer

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Where an enforcement
officer is carrying out the duties and functions of an enforcement
officer under this Act, no person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fail to comply with any reasonable requirement or
  direction of the officer;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} knowingly make a false or misleading statement,
  either orally or in writing, to the officer; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} otherwise obstruct or hinder the officer.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 12
:::

## [Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233552 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt due to Her Majesty

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  charges prescribed by regulations made under this Act for the use of
  any scheduled harbour constitute a debt due to Her Majesty in right of
  Canada jointly and severally by the owner and any other person in
  charge of the vessel or goods in respect of which the charges are
  payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other remedies available

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act prevents a debt payable under
  this Act from being recovered, together with all costs, from the
  debtor in any court of competent jurisdiction or the exercise against
  the debtor of any other right or remedy available at law.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 13
:::

## [Removal, Seizure, Detention and Sale]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233560 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction of scheduled harbours

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  any vessel or goods are left or abandoned at any scheduled harbour and
  an enforcement officer believes on reasonable grounds that the vessel
  or goods impede, interfere with or render difficult or dangerous the
  use of the harbour, the officer may direct the person appearing to be
  in charge of the vessel or goods to remove the vessel or goods from
  the harbour to such place as the officer deems suitable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seizure and removal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the person to whom a direction is given pursuant
  to subsection (1) fails to comply with the direction or if no person
  appears to be in charge of the vessel or goods, the enforcement
  officer may seize the vessel or goods and remove them to such place as
  the officer deems suitable, and the costs of the seizure and removal
  are recoverable in the same manner as a debt due to Her Majesty in
  right of Canada for charges under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-24, s. 14
- R.S., 1985, c. 31 (1st Supp.), s. 98
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seizure and detention

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Where an enforcement
officer believes on reasonable grounds that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any amount is due and payable under this Act for
  charges in respect of any vessel or goods, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any provision of this Act or the regulations relating
  to any vessel or goods has been contravened,

the officer may seize and detain the vessel or goods.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 15
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for order directing
forfeiture

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  at the time any vessel or goods are seized under this Act, no person
  appears to be in possession thereof or to have the right to the
  possession thereof, the Minister may apply to any court of competent
  jurisdiction for an order directing the forfeiture of the vessel or
  goods to Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture granted

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, on the hearing of an application made under
  subsection (1), the court is satisfied that no person appears to be in
  possession or to have the right to the possession of the vessel or
  goods seized, it may order the forfeiture thereof to Her Majesty in
  right of Canada and on such forfeiture the vessel or goods may be
  disposed of as the Minister directs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection of persons claiming interest

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 74 to 77 of the [Fisheries
  Act](/eng/acts/F-14) applies, with such modifications as the
  circumstances require, in respect of anything forfeited under
  subsection (2) as though it were an article forfeited under subsection
  72(1) of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture refused

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where, on the hearing of an application made under
  subsection (1), the court is not satisfied that the vessel or goods
  seized should be forfeited, it shall order the vessel or goods to be
  dealt with according to the other provisions of this Act and the
  regulations applicable thereto.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 16
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale of perishable goods

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  in the opinion of the enforcement officer, any goods seized and
  detained under this Act are likely to rot, spoil or otherwise perish,
  the officer may sell or otherwise dispose of the goods in such manner
  and for such price as is reasonable in the circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceeds of sale

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The proceeds of a sale or other disposition referred
  to in subsection (1) shall be paid to the Receiver General or
  deposited to the credit of the Receiver General in a bank.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 17
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of property seized

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), any
  vessel or goods seized under this Act and any proceeds of a sale or
  other disposition thereof made under section 17 shall be returned to
  the person from whom the vessel or goods were seized or to any other
  person entitled to the possession thereof after

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the provisions of this Act and the regulations that
    apply to the vessel or goods have, in the opinion of an enforcement
    officer, been complied with; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the expiration of twenty-one days from the day of
    the seizure, unless before that time an application for an order
    authorizing the sale of the vessel or goods has been made pursuant
    to section 19.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of any
  vessel or goods forfeited pursuant to section 16.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 18
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for order authorizing sale

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, before the expiration of twenty-one days from the day of
  the seizure of any vessel or goods under this Act, apply to any court
  of competent jurisdiction for an order authorizing the sale of the
  vessel or goods.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Redelivery on security

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The court may order the redelivery of any vessel or
  goods seized under this Act to the person from whom the vessel or
  goods were seized or to any other person entitled to the possession
  thereof after security by bond, with two sureties, in such amount and
  form as is satisfactory to the Minister, is given to Her Majesty in
  right of Canada or after security of a cash deposit, in an amount
  satisfactory to the Minister, is given to Her Majesty in right of
  Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of sale refused

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where, on the hearing of an application made under
  subsection (1), the court is not satisfied that the vessel or goods
  seized should be sold, it may order that the vessel or goods be
  returned or, if a sale or other disposition of the goods has been made
  pursuant to section 17, it may order that any proceeds thereof be
  remitted to the person from whom the vessel or goods were seized or to
  any other person entitled to the possession thereof.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of sale granted

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where, on the hearing of an application made under
  subsection (1), the court is satisfied that the vessel or goods seized
  should be sold, it may order the sale thereof and thereupon the
  Minister may sell the vessel or goods in such manner and for such
  price as the court determines is reasonable in the circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceeds of sale

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the total amount realized from a sale made
  under subsection (4) or any other disposition made under section 17,
  or both, exceeds the amount payable for rates and charges under this
  Act together with all costs and reasonable expenses incurred in
  connection with the sale or other disposition and any removal, seizure
  or detention under this Act, the Minister shall remit the excess
  amount to the person then appearing to be entitled thereto.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where it appears to the Minister that no person is
  entitled to the excess amount mentioned in subsection (5), that amount
  shall be paid to the Receiver General.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 19
:::

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233617 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravening section 12 or regulations

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes section 12 or the regulations is guilty of an offence and
liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding twenty-five
thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months
or to both.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 20
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers, etc., of corporations

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Where a corporation
commits an offence under this Act, any officer, director or agent of the
corporation who directed, authorized, assented to, acquiesced in or
participated in the commission of the offence is a party to and guilty
of the offence and is liable on conviction to the punishment provided
for the offence whether or not the corporation has been prosecuted or
convicted.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 21
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Trial of offences

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** A complaint or
information in respect of an offence under this Act may be heard, tried
or determined by a court if the accused is resident or carrying on
business within the territorial jurisdiction of that court although the
matter of the complaint or information did not arise in that territorial
jurisdiction.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 22
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence by employee or agent

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** In any prosecution for an
offence under this Act, it is sufficient proof of the offence to
establish that it was committed by an employee or agent of the accused
whether or not the employee or agent is identified or has been
prosecuted for the offence, unless the accused establishes that the
offence was committed without the knowledge or consent of the accused
and that the accused exercised all due diligence to prevent its
commission.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 23
:::

## [Evidence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233634 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies and extracts as evidence

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  a copy or extract made by an enforcement officer pursuant to paragraph
  11(b) and purporting to be certified under the signature of that
  enforcement officer as a true copy or extract is admissible in
  evidence in any prosecution for an offence under this Act without
  proof of the signature or official character of the person appearing
  to have signed the copy or extract and, in the absence of any evidence
  to the contrary, has the same probative force as the original document
  would have if it had been proved in the ordinary way.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attendance of enforcement officer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party against whom any copy or extract is
  produced pursuant to subsection (1) may, with leave of the court,
  require the attendance of the enforcement officer for the purposes of
  cross-examination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No copy or extract referred to in subsection (1)
  shall be admitted in evidence unless the party intending to produce it
  has given to the party against whom it is intended to be produced
  reasonable notice of that intention together with a duplicate of the
  copy or extract.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 24
:::

## [Payment of Fines]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233644 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations relating to offences

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may by regulation designate any offence under this
  Act or the regulations as an offence with respect to which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notwithstanding the provisions of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46), any person designated as an enforcement
    officer pursuant to section 10 may lay an information and issue and
    serve a summons by completing a ticket in the form prescribed by
    regulation, affixing that person's signature thereto and delivering
    the ticket to the person alleged to have committed the offence
    specified therein at the time the offence is alleged to have been
    committed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the summons may be served on an accused by mailing
    the summons to the accused at the latest known address of the
    accused.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voluntary payment of fine

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under this section shall establish
  a procedure for voluntarily entering a plea of guilty and paying a
  fine in respect of each offence to which the regulation relates and
  shall fix the amount of the fine that may be so paid in respect of
  each such offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fixing of fines

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A fine fixed in respect of an offence by a regulation
  made under this section may be lower for a first offence than for any
  subsequent offence but in no case shall it be greater than fifty
  dollars.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 25
- 1984, c. 40, s. 79(F)
:::

## [Inquiries into Accidents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233656 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may direct inquiry

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may direct an inquiry to be made, subject to the [Canadian
  Transportation Accident Investigation and Safety Board
  Act](/eng/acts/C-23.4), whenever an accident or incident that results
  or is likely to result in death or injury to any person, damage to
  property or danger to the health or safety of the public has occurred
  at any scheduled harbour and authorize any qualified persons to
  conduct the inquiry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of persons conducting inquiries

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person authorized by the Minister to conduct an
  inquiry under subsection (1) shall have all the powers of a
  commissioner appointed under Part I of the [Inquiries
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1989, c. 3, s. 44\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-24, s. 26
- 1989, c. 3, s. 44
:::

## [Officers and Employees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233665 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of officers or employees

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Act, the Minister may appoint and fix the remuneration of such officers
or employees as the Minister thinks proper for the operation,
administration and management of any scheduled harbours, and the
officers or employees so appointed shall perform such duties or
functions as the Minister may direct or as may be prescribed by
regulations made under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1977-78, c. 30, s. 27
:::
