## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-462243 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Universal Child Care Benefit Act](/eng/acts/U-3.2).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-462246 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[eligible individual]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible individual]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is an
    eligible individual for the purpose of Subdivision a.1 of Division E
    of Part I of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([particulier
    admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Employment
    and Social Development. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualified dependant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualified dependant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is a
    qualified dependant for the purpose of Subdivision a.1 of Division E
    of Part I of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([personne à
    charge admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shared-custody parent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shared-custody parent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
    assigned by section 122.6 of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([parent ayant la garde
    partagée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-462255 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to assist families by supporting their child care choices through direct
financial support to a maximum of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$1,920 per year in respect of each of their children
  who is under six years of age; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$720 per year in respect of each of their children
  who is six years of age or older but who is under 18 years of age.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2006, c. 4, s. 168 "3"
- 2015, c. 36, s. 36

## [Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-462262 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of payment --- child under six
years

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  respect of every month before January 1, 2015, the Minister shall pay
  to an eligible individual, for each month at the beginning of which he
  or she is an eligible individual, for each child who, at the beginning
  of that month, is under six years of age and is a qualified dependant
  of the eligible individual,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a benefit of \$50, if the eligible individual is a
    shared-custody parent of the qualified dependant; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a benefit of \$100 in any other case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Child under six years --- January 2015
  to June 2016

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} In respect of every month as of January 1, 2015 but
  before July 1, 2016, the Minister shall pay to an eligible individual,
  for each month at the beginning of which he or she is an eligible
  individual, for each child who, at the beginning of that month, is
  under the age of six years and is a qualified dependant of the
  eligible individual,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a benefit of \$80, if the eligible individual is a
    shared-custody parent of the qualified dependant; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a benefit of \$160 in any other case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other children --- January 2015 to June
  2016

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} In respect of every month as of January 1, 2015 but
  before July 1, 2016, the Minister shall pay to an eligible individual,
  for each month at the beginning of which he or she is an eligible
  individual, for each child who, at the beginning of that month, is six
  years of age or older and is a qualified dependant of the eligible
  individual,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a benefit of \$30, if the eligible individual is a
    shared-custody parent of the qualified dependant; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a benefit of \$60 in any other case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The benefit may not be paid in respect of any month
  before July, 2006.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefit cannot be charged, etc.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A benefit

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not subject to the operation of any law relating
  to bankruptcy or insolvency;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cannot be assigned, charged, attached or given as
  security;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} cannot be retained by way of deduction, set-off or,
  in Quebec, compensation, under any Act of Parliament other than this
  Act; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} is not garnishable moneys for the purposes of the
  [Family Orders and Agreements Enforcement Assistance
  Act](/eng/acts/F-1.4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of overpayment or erroneous
payment

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who has received or obtained a benefit to which the person is not
  entitled, or a benefit in excess of the amount of the benefit to which
  the person is entitled, shall, as soon as possible, repay the amount
  of the benefit or the excess amount, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery as a debt due to Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the overpayment or erroneous payment
  constitutes a debt due to Her Majesty, as of the day on which it was
  paid, that may be recovered by the Minister of National Revenue.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this section, no action or proceedings shall be taken to recover
  money owing under this Act after the expiry of the six-year limitation
  period that begins on the day on which the money becomes due and
  payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction and set-off

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Money owing by a person under this Act may be
  recovered at any time by way of deduction from, set-off against or, in
  Quebec, compensation against, any sum of money, including a benefit
  under this Act, that may be due or payable by Her Majesty in right of
  Canada to the person, other than an amount payable under section
  122.61 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment of liability

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person's liability for money owing under this
  Act is acknowledged in accordance with subsection (5), the time during
  which the limitation period has run before the acknowledgment does not
  count in the calculation of that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment after expiry of
  limitation period

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a person's liability for money owing under this
  Act is acknowledged in accordance with subsection (5) after the expiry
  of the limitation period, an action or proceedings to recover the
  money may, subject to subsections (3) and (6), be brought within six
  years after the date of the acknowledgment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of acknowledgments

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An acknowledgment of liability means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a written promise to pay the money owing, signed by
    the person or his or her agent or other representative;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written acknowledgment of the money owing, signed
    by the person or his or her agent or other representative, whether
    or not a promise to pay can be implied from it and whether or not it
    contains a refusal to pay;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a part payment by the person or his or her agent or
    other representative of any money owing; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any acknowledgment of the money owing made by the
    person, his or her agent or other representative or the trustee or
    administrator in the course of proceedings under the [Bankruptcy and
    Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3) or any other legislation dealing with
    the payment of debts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period suspended

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The running of a limitation period in respect of
  money owing under this Act is suspended during any period in which it
  is prohibited to commence or continue an action or other proceedings
  against the person to recover money owing under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement proceedings

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply in respect of an action
  or proceedings relating to the execution, renewal or enforcement of a
  judgment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No interest payable

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No interest is payable on
any amount owing to Her Majesty under this Act as a result of an
overpayment or an erroneous payment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to enter agreements

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may enter into
agreements or arrangements with any department, board or agency of the
Government of Canada to assist the Minister in carrying out the purposes
and provisions of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment out of C.R.F.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** All amounts payable by
the Minister under section 4 shall be paid out of the Consolidated
Revenue Fund.
