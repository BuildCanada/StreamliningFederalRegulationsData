## Canadian Victims Bill of Rights {#canadian-victims-bill-of-rights .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2015, c. 13, s. 2
:::

Assented to 2015-04-23

An Act for the Recognition of Victims Rights

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 2 of chapter 13 of the Statutes of Canada, 2015, in
force July 23, 2015.\]
:::
:::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas crime has a harmful impact on victims and on society;

Whereas victims of crime and their families deserve to be treated with
courtesy, compassion and respect, including respect for their dignity;

Whereas it is important that victims' rights be considered throughout
the criminal justice system;

Whereas victims of crime have rights that are guaranteed by the Canadian
Charter of Rights and Freedoms;

Whereas consideration of the rights of victims of crime is in the
interest of the proper administration of justice;

Whereas the federal, provincial and territorial governments share
responsibility for criminal justice;

Whereas, in 1988, the federal, provincial and territorial governments
endorsed the Canadian Statement of Basic Principles of Justice for
Victims of Crime and, in 2003, the Canadian Statement of Basic
Principles of Justice for Victims of Crime, 2003;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-76029 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canadian Victims Bill of Rights](/eng/acts/C-23.7).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-76032 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an offence under the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the [Youth Criminal Justice
    Act](/eng/acts/Y-1.5) or the [Crimes Against Humanity and War Crimes
    Act](/eng/acts/C-45.9), a [[designated substance
    offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
    [Controlled Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8), a
    [[designated offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) or an
    offence under section 91 or Part 3 of the [Immigration and Refugee
    Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5). ([infraction]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[victim]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[victim]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who has suffered
    physical or emotional harm, property damage or economic loss as the
    result of the commission or alleged commission of an
    offence. ([victime]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting on victim's behalf

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Any of the following
individuals may exercise a victim's rights under this Act if the victim
is dead or incapable of acting on their own behalf:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the victim's spouse or the individual who was at the
  time of the victim's death their spouse;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the individual who is or was at the time of the
  victim's death, cohabiting with them in a conjugal relationship,
  having so cohabited for a period of at least one year;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a relative or dependant of the victim;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an individual who has in law or fact custody, or is
  responsible for the care or support, of the victim;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an individual who has in law or fact custody, or is
  responsible for the care or support, of a dependant of the victim.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An individual is not a
victim in relation to an offence, or entitled to exercise a victim's
rights under this Act, if the individual is charged with the offence,
found guilty of the offence or found not criminally responsible on
account of mental disorder or unfit to stand trial in respect of the
offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criminal justice system

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of this
Act, the criminal justice system consists of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the investigation and prosecution of offences in
  Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the corrections process and the conditional release
  process in Canada; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proceedings of courts and Review Boards, as those
  terms are defined in subsection 672.1(1) of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46), in respect of accused who are found not
  criminally responsible on account of mental disorder or unfit to stand
  trial.

## [Rights]{.HTitleText1} {#h-76053 .Part}

### [Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-76054 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General information

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every victim has the right,
on request, to information about

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the criminal justice system and the role of victims
  in it;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the services and programs available to them as a
  victim, including restorative justice programs; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} their right to file a complaint for an infringement
  or denial of any of their rights under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation and proceedings

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every victim has the right,
on request, to information about

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the status and outcome of the investigation into the
  offence; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the location of proceedings in relation to the
  offence, when they will take place and their progress and outcome.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information about offender or accused

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Every victim has the right,
on request, to information about

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} reviews under the [Corrections and Conditional
  Release Act](/eng/acts/C-44.6) relating to the offender's conditional
  release and the timing and conditions of that release; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} hearings held for the purpose of making dispositions,
  as defined in subsection 672.1(1) of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46), in relation to the accused, if the accused is
  found not criminally responsible on account of mental disorder or
  unfit to stand trial, and the dispositions made at those hearings.

### [Protection]{.HTitleText2} {#h-76068 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Every victim has the right
to have their security considered by the appropriate author- ities in
the criminal justice system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection from intimidation and
retaliation

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every victim has the
right to have reasonable and necessary measures taken by the appropriate
authorities in the criminal justice system to protect the victim from
intimidation and retaliation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privacy

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every victim has the
right to have their privacy considered by the appropriate authorities in
the criminal justice system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identity protection

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Every victim has the
right to request that their identity be protected if they are a
complainant to the offence or a witness in proceedings relating to the
offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Testimonial aids

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Every victim has the
right to request testimonial aids when appearing as a witness in
proceedings relating to the offence.

### [Participation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-76079 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Views to be considered

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Every victim has the
right to convey their views about decisions to be made by appropriate
authorities in the criminal justice system that affect the victim's
rights under this Act and to have those views considered.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Victim impact statement

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Every victim has the
right to present a victim impact statement to the appropriate
authorities in the criminal justice system and to have it considered.

### [Restitution]{.HTitleText2} {#h-76084 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restitution order

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Every victim has the
right to have the court consider making a restitution order against the
offender.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Every victim in whose
favour a restitution order is made has the right, if they are not paid,
to have the order entered as a civil court judgment that is enforceable
against the offender.

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-76089 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Act applies in respect of a victim of an offence in their interactions
  with the criminal justice system

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} while the offence is investigated or prosecuted;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} while the offender is subject to the corrections
    process or the conditional release process in relation to the
    offence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} while the accused is, in relation to the offence,
    under the jurisdiction of a court or a Review Board, as those terms
    are defined in subsection 672.1(1) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46), if they are found not criminally responsible
    on account of mental disorder or unfit to stand trial.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reporting of offence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), if an offence is
  reported to the appropriate authorities in the criminal justice
  system, the investigation of the offence is deemed to begin at the
  time of the reporting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), this Act does not apply in
  respect of offences that are [[service
  offences]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
  [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), that are investigated or
  proceeded with under that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} This Act applies in respect of an offender who is
  convicted of a [[service offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined
  in subsection 2(1) of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) and
  who is committed to a [[penitentiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or a
  [[civil prison]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms are defined in
  that subsection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exercise of rights

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  rights of victims under this Act are to be exercised through the
  mechanisms provided by law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Connection to Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A victim is entitled to exercise their rights under
  this Act only if they are present in Canada or they are a Canadian
  citizen or a permanent resident within the meaning of subsection 2(1)
  of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation of this Act

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** This Act is to be
construed and applied in a manner that is reasonable in the
circumstances, and in a manner that is not likely to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} interfere with the proper administration of justice,
  including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by causing interference with police discretion or
    causing excessive delay in, or compromising or hindering, the
    investigation of any offence, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by causing interference with prosecutorial
    discretion or causing excessive delay in, or compromising or
    hindering, the prosecution of any offence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} interfere with ministerial discretion;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} interfere with the discretion that may be exercised
  by any person or body authorized to release an offender into the
  community;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} endanger the life or safety of any individual; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} cause injury to international relations or national
  defence or national security.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation of other Acts,
regulations, etc.

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** To the extent that it is
possible to do so, every Act of Parliament enacted --- and every order,
rule or regulation made under such an Act --- before, on or after the
day on which this Act comes into force must be construed and applied in
a manner that is compatible with the rights under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Primacy in event of inconsistency

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If,
  after the application of sections 20 and 21, there is any
  inconsistency between any provision of this Act and any provision of
  any Act, order, rule or regulation referred to in section 21, the
  provision of this Act prevails to the extent of the inconsistency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- Acts, regulations, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of the
  [Canadian Bill of Rights](/eng/acts/C-12.3), the [Canadian Human
  Rights Act](/eng/acts/H-6), the [Official Languages
  Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01), the [Access to Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1)
  and the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21) and in respect of orders, rules
  and regulations made under any of those Acts. It also does not apply
  in respect of Division 1.1 of Part III of the [National Defence
  Act](/eng/acts/N-5) and in respect of any orders, rules and
  regulations made under that Act to the extent that they apply in
  relation to that Division.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No adverse inference

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** No adverse inference is
to be drawn against a person who is charged with an offence from the
fact that an individual has been identified as a victim in relation to
the offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entering or remaining in Canada

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act is to
be construed so as to permit any individual to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter Canada or to remain in Canada beyond the end of
  the period for which they are authorized to so remain;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} delay any removal proceedings or prevent the
  enforcement of any removal order; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} delay any extradition proceedings or prevent the
  extradition of any person to or from Canada.

## [Remedies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-76128 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaint --- federal entity

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  victim who is of the opinion that any of their rights under this Act
  have been infringed or denied by a federal department, agency or body
  has the right to file a complaint in accordance with its complaints
  mechanism.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaint to authority

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every victim who has exhausted their recourse under
  the complaints mechanism and who is not satisfied with the response of
  the federal department, agency or body may file a complaint with any
  authority that has jurisdiction to review complaints in relation to
  that department, agency or body.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaints mechanism

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every federal department, agency or body that is
  involved in the criminal justice system must have a complaints
  mechanism that provides for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a review of complaints involving alleged
    infringements or denials of rights under this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the power to make recommendations to remedy such
    infringements and denials; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the obligation to notify victims of the result of
    those reviews and of the recommendations, if any were made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaint --- provincial or territorial
entity

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Every victim who is of
the opinion that their rights under this Act have been infringed or
denied by a provincial or territorial department, agency or body may
file a complaint in accordance with the laws of the province or
territory.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act is to
be construed as granting to, or removing from, any victim or any
individual acting on behalf of a victim the status of party, intervenor
or observer in any proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No cause of action

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** No cause of action or
right to damages arises from an infringement or denial of a right under
this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No appeal

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** No appeal lies from any
decision or order solely on the grounds that a right under this Act has
been infringed or denied.
