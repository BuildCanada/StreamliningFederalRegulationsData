## Legislative Instruments Re-enactment Act {#legislative-instruments-re-enactment-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2002, c. 20
:::

Assented to 2002-06-13

An Act to re-enact legislative instruments enacted in only one official
language
::::

:::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Legislative Instruments Re-enactment Act](/eng/acts/L-7.5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[enacted]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[enacted]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes issued, made and
    established. ([édicter]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[government publication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[government publication]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada
    Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) or any other official
    publication of the Government of Canada in which legislative
    instruments were published. ([publication
    gouvernementale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[legislative instrument]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[legislative instrument]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an instrument enacted before the coming into
      force of section 7 of the [Official Languages
      Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01) on September 15, 1988 by, or with the
      approval of, the Governor in Council or a minister of the Crown in
      the execution of a legislative power conferred by or under an Act
      of Parliament; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an instrument that amends or repeals an
      instrument referred to in paragraph (a). ([texte
      législatif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Instruments published in both languages

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  legislative instrument that was originally enacted in only one
  official language and was, at the time of its enactment, published in
  both official languages in a government publication is hereby
  re-enacted in both official languages in the same form as that in
  which the legislative instrument was published.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-enactment retroactive

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The provisions of an instrument re-enacted under
  subsection (1) are retroactive to, and are deemed to have come into
  force on, the day or days on which the corresponding provisions of the
  legislative instrument it replaces came into force, and those
  corresponding provisions are deemed to be repealed as at that time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Instruments not published or published in
one language

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  legislative instrument was originally enacted in only one official
  language and, at the time of its enactment, was published in only one
  official language or was exempted by law from the requirement to be
  published in a government publication, the Governor in Council may, by
  regulation, repeal the legislative instrument and re-enact it in both
  official languages, without change to the version of the legislative
  instrument in the language in which it was originally enacted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulation retroactive

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under subsection (1) shall provide
  that the provisions of the re-enacted instrument are retroactive to,
  and are deemed to have come into force on, the day or days on which
  the corresponding provisions of the legislative instrument it replaces
  came into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall be convicted of an offence consisting
  of a contravention of a provision of an instrument re-enacted under
  subsection (1) unless the contravention occurred after the instrument
  was re-enacted and published in both official languages.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Governor in Council

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may repeal and re-enact a
  legislative instrument under subsection (1) notwithstanding that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the power under which the legislative instrument
    was originally enacted no longer exists; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the office or body that originally enacted the
    legislative instrument no longer exists.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of re-enactment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The re-enactment of a legislative instrument under
  subsection (1) is not subject to the same conditions, if any, that
  applied to the enactment of the legislative instrument it replaces.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under subsection (1) shall be
  published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), unless
  the regulation is a regulation of a class referred to in subsection
  15(3) of the [Statutory Instruments
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1509).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repeal of legislative instruments

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Upon the expiration of six years after this Act comes
  into force, any legislative instrument described in subsection (1)
  that has not been re-enacted in both official languages is repealed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming and citation

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  instrument re-enacted under section 3 or 4 is deemed to be, and to
  have always been, the legislative instrument it replaces and, subject
  to subsection (3), shall be cited in the same manner as that
  legislative instrument.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to amend or repeal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the holder of an office, or a
  body, that has the power to amend or repeal a legislative instrument
  that was re-enacted under section 3 or 4 may use that power to amend
  or repeal the re-enacted instrument.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to title

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a legislative instrument was not published at
  the time of its enactment or was at that time published in only one
  official language, the re-enacted instrument that replaces it may be
  referred to by its title in either official language.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Both versions equally authoritative

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The English and French
versions of an instrument re-enacted under section 3 or 4 are equally
authoritative.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repealed instruments not revived

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An instrument that was
repealed or that otherwise ceased to have effect on or before the day on
which this Act comes into force is not by virtue of this Act or any
regulation made under this Act revived in respect of any period
subsequent to its repeal or ceasing to have effect.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does not apply to an
  instrument re-enacted under section 3 or to a regulation made under
  section 4.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Referral for scrutiny

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Instruments re-enacted under section 3 and
  regulations made under section 4 stand permanently referred to the
  Committee referred to in section 19 of the [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22) for review and scrutiny.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  five years after the day on which this Act comes into force, the
  Minister of Justice shall complete a review of the implementation and
  operation of section 4.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), within one year after the
  review is completed pursuant to subsection (1), or within such further
  time as may be authorized by both Houses of Parliament, the Minister
  of Justice shall submit a report on the review to each House of
  Parliament that includes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures taken to identify
    legislative instruments referred to in subsection 4(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a list of any legislative instruments that have
    been repealed and re-enacted under subsection 4(1); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a list of any legislative instruments referred to
    in that subsection that have been identified but that have not been
    repealed and re-enacted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Number of certain instruments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The report referred to in subsection (2) shall, in
  respect of legislative instruments of a class referred to in
  subsection 15(3) of the [Statutory Instruments
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1509), set out only the
  number of such instruments that are of the types described in
  paragraphs (2)(b) and (c).
