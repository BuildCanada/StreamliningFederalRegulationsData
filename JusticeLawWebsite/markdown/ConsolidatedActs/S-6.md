## Seals Act {#seals-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. S-6
:::

An Act to make provision for the sealing of royal instruments
::::

::::::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Seals Act](/eng/acts/S-6).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. S-6, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[counter-signature]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[counter-signature]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the endorsement on a
    royal instrument or on a document under the sign-manual of the
    signature of Her Majesty's responsible Canadian
    minister; ([contreseing]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[document under the sign-manual]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[document under the sign-manual]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    instrument, in respect of Canada, that, under the present practice,
    is issued in the name and under the signature of Her Majesty the
    Queen without any seal; ([document revêtu du seing
    royal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Great Seal of the Realm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Great Seal of the Realm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Great Seal
    of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland for
    which provision was made in Article XXIV of An Act for an Union of
    the two Kingdoms of England and Scotland, 5 Anne, 1706, chapter VIII
    \[Statutes at Large, Volume IV\], and includes the wafer
    seal; ([grand sceau du Royaume]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[royal instrument]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[royal instrument]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an instrument, in
    respect of Canada, that, under the present practice, is issued by
    and in the name of the Queen and passed under the Great Seal of the
    Realm or under one of the signets; ([instrument
    royal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[royal seals]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[royal seals]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} include the Great Seal of Canada
    and any other seals or signets that may, with the approval of Her
    Majesty the Queen, be authorized under this Act; ([sceaux
    royaux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[signet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[signet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the seal that, under the
    existing practice in the United Kingdom, is delivered by Her Majesty
    the Queen to each of her Principal Secretaries of State in the
    United Kingdom, and includes the lesser signet or second secretarial
    seal and the cachet. ([signet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. S-6, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issue of royal instruments

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding any law in
force in Canada, any royal instrument may be issued by and with the
authority of Her Majesty the Queen and passed under the Great Seal of
Canada, or under any other royal seal approved by Her Majesty the Queen
for the purpose.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. S-6, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding any law in
force in Canada, the Governor in Council may, subject to the approval of
Her Majesty the Queen, make orders and regulations relating to royal
seals, the use thereof, royal instruments and documents under the
sign-manual, and, without restricting the generality of the foregoing,
in relation to the following matters:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the specification of the instruments or classes of
  instruments that are to be passed under the royal seals;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the authorization of royal seals, the naming of those
  seals and the specification of the purposes for which they are to be
  used;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the custody of the royal seals;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the procedure governing the use of the royal seals;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} counter-signature of royal instruments;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the issuing and counter-signature of documents under
  the sign-manual;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the procedure whereby the approval of Her Majesty the
  Queen and her authority for the issuing of royal instruments and
  documents under the sign-manual is to be given; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the authentication and proof of royal instruments and
  documents under the sign-manual, including the conditions under which
  certification by an official, or publication by the Queen's Printer,
  constitutes authentication and proof.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. S-6, s. 4
