## Heritage Lighthouse Protection Act {#heritage-lighthouse-protection-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2008, c. 16
:::

Assented to 2008-05-29

An Act to protect heritage lighthouses
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS lighthouses have long graced Canada's rugged coastlines and
majestic shores, providing and symbolizing direction, hope and safe
harbour to generations of mariners;

AND WHEREAS lighthouses form an integral part of Canada's identity,
culture and heritage, and are of historic and aesthetic interest and
significance to our communities and our nation;

AND WHEREAS communities in the areas in which lighthouses are situated
have an important role in the conservation and protection of heritage
lighthouses and in ensuring a public purpose for them;

AND WHEREAS it is important to provide access to heritage lighthouses in
order for people to understand and appreciate the contribution of those
lighthouses to Canada's maritime heritage;

AND WHEREAS the Parliament of Canada recognizes that measures must be
taken to conserve and protect our heritage lighthouses;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-253791 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Heritage Lighthouse Protection Act](/eng/acts/H-3.4).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-253794 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[advisory committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[advisory committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the advisory
    committee established by the Minister under section 10. ([comité
    consultatif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[alter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[alter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes to restore or renovate, but
    does not include to perform routine maintenance and
    repairs. ([modifier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[established criteria]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[established criteria]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the criteria
    established by the Minister under paragraph 16(a). ([critère
    établi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[heritage lighthouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[heritage lighthouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a lighthouse
    designated as a heritage lighthouse under this Act, and includes any
    related building that is included in the designation. ([phare
    patrimonial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[lighthouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lighthouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a tower or other structure,
    including its fixtures, that was built to contain, contains, or once
    contained a beacon light or other signal to warn or guide marine
    vessels, whether or not it is now in use as an aid to
    navigation. ([phare]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister responsible for
    the Parks Canada Agency. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[related building]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[related building]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a heritage
    lighthouse, means any building on the site on which the lighthouse
    is situated that contributes to the heritage character of the
    lighthouse. ([bâtiment connexe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Purpose and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-253804 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to conserve and protect heritage lighthouses by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} providing for the selection and designation of
  heritage lighthouses;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} preventing the unauthorized alteration or disposition
  of heritage lighthouses;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} requiring that heritage lighthouses be reasonably
  maintained; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} facilitating sales or transfers of heritage
  lighthouses in order to ensure the lighthouses' public purpose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act applies to
lighthouses that are the property of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada.

## [Designation of Heritage Lighthouses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-253815 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation by the Minister

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, at any time, taking into account the established
  criteria, designate a lighthouse to be a heritage lighthouse for the
  purposes of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inclusion of related structure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designation of a lighthouse may include any
  related building that the Minister considers should be included in the
  designation, taking into account the established criteria.

Definition of [[petition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  section, [[petition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a petition that a
  specified lighthouse be designated as a heritage lighthouse that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} signed by at least 25 persons who are resident in
    Canada and are 18 years of age or older, and whose names and
    addresses appear in printed form on the petition; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} received by the Minister within two years after the
    coming into force of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration within five years

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within five years after the coming into force of this
  Act, the Minister must, taking into account the established criteria,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} consider all lighthouses in respect of which the
    Minister receives a petition; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine which of them should be designated as
    heritage lighthouses and whether any related buildings should be
    included in the designations, and make the appropriate designations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surplus lighthouses

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For two
  years beginning with the coming into force of this Act, every Minister
  of the Crown in right of Canada who has the administration of
  lighthouses must maintain and make available to the public a list of
  those lighthouses that he or she considers to be surplus to the
  operational requirements of the portion of the federal public
  administration for which he or she is responsible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commitment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A surplus lighthouse may only be designated as a
  heritage lighthouse if a person or body submits a written commitment
  to buy or otherwise acquire the lighthouse and to protect its heritage
  character in the event that it is designated as a heritage lighthouse.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of list of lighthouses
considered

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Within 90 days after the
expiration of the five-year period referred to in subsection 7(2), the
Minister must publish, in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), a list of all lighthouses that the
Minister has considered for designation as heritage lighthouses under
this Act, indicating, for each lighthouse, whether or not it has been
designated as a heritage lighthouse.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advisory committee

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must
establish an advisory committee to advise and assist the Minister on
matters relating to heritage lighthouses, including the designation and
protection of heritage lighthouses and the establishment of criteria for
their designation, alteration and maintenance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation with the advisory committee

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must consult
with the advisory committee, and may consult with any other persons or
bodies that the Minister considers appropriate, before determining
whether a lighthouse should be designated as a heritage lighthouse and
whether any related building should be included in the designation.

## [Protection of Heritage Lighthouses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-253841 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alterations

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  heritage lighthouse, or any part of it, may only be altered in
  accordance with the criteria and procedures established under
  paragraph 16(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of an
  alteration made to a heritage lighthouse

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in response to an emergency situation or an urgent
    operational requirement; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for operational reasons, if the alteration does not
    affect the heritage character of the heritage lighthouse.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of transfer or sale

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  heritage lighthouse, or any part of it, may only be transferred to Her
  Majesty in right of a province or sold if a notice is published at
  least 90 days before the transfer or sale in one or more newspapers of
  general circulation in the area in which the lighthouse is situated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public meeting

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A heritage lighthouse, or any part of it, may only be
  sold if a public meeting is held on the matter in the area in which
  the lighthouse is situated, unless the sale is to a municipality or to
  a person or body that is acquiring the heritage lighthouse for a
  public purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection of heritage character

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any transaction effecting a transfer to Her Majesty
  in right of a province or a sale must provide for the protection of
  the heritage character of the heritage lighthouse by any means that
  the Minister may authorize.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of demolition

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  heritage lighthouse, or any part of it, may only be demolished if
  there is no reasonable alternative and if a notice is published at
  least 90 days before the demolition in one or more newspapers of
  general circulation in the area in which the lighthouse is situated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public meeting

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A heritage lighthouse, or any part of it, may only be
  demolished if a public meeting is held on the matter in the area in
  which the lighthouse is situated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply in respect of
  the demolition of a heritage lighthouse in response to an emergency
  situation or an urgent operational requirement.

## [Maintenance of Heritage Lighthouses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-253863 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to maintain

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a heritage
lighthouse must maintain it in accordance with the criteria established
under paragraph 16(c).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-253866 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish criteria to be taken into account in
  considering whether a lighthouse should be designated as a heritage
  lighthouse and whether any related building should be included in the
  designation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish criteria and procedures respecting the
  alteration of heritage lighthouses that are in keeping with national
  and international standards for the conservation of heritage
  properties; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} establish criteria for the maintenance of heritage
  lighthouses that are in keeping with national and international
  standards for the conservation of heritage properties.
