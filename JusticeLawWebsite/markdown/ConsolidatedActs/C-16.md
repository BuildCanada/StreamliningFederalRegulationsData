## [Constitution of Telefilm Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69415 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation established

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby established
a corporation, to be known as Telefilm Canada, consisting of six members
to be appointed by the Governor in Council as provided in section 4 and
the Government Film Commissioner appointed under the [National Film
Act](/eng/acts/N-8).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-16, s. 3
- 2002, c. 17, s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment and tenure of office of
members

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each of
  the members of the Corporation, other than the Government Film
  Commissioner, shall be appointed to hold office for a term of five
  years, except that, of those members first appointed, three shall be
  appointed for a term of three years and three shall be appointed for a
  term of five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council shall designate one of the
  members of the Corporation to serve as chairperson of the Corporation
  during pleasure and the chairperson may receive a yearly honorarium to
  be determined by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility for re-appointment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A retiring member of the Corporation is eligible for
  re-appointment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filling vacancy

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} When a member of the Corporation ceases to be a
  member before the end of the term for which he was appointed, the
  Governor in Council shall appoint a person to be a member of the
  Corporation for the remainder of that term.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-16, s. 4
- 1995, c. 29, s. 20(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility for membership

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person who has, directly
or indirectly and individually or as a shareholder, partner or
otherwise, any pecuniary interest in the audio-visual industry is
eligible to be appointed or to hold office as a member of the
Corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-16, s. 5
- 2005, c. 14, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and expenses

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Each member of the
Corporation, other than the Government Film Commissioner or other member
of the federal public administration, shall be paid such remuneration
for each day that member of the Corporation attends any meeting thereof
as may be fixed by by-law of the Corporation, and each member of the
Corporation is entitled to be paid reasonable travel and living expenses
incurred while absent from his ordinary place of residence in the course
of his duties under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-16, s. 6
- 2003, c. 22, s. 224(E)
:::

[Previous
Version](section-6-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 6"){.PITLink}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A majority of the members
of the Corporation constitutes a quorum for the transaction of the
business of the Corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-8, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to act not impaired by vacancy

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A vacancy in the membership
of the Corporation does not impair the right of the remaining members to
act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-8, s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vice-chairperson

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation may
designate one of its members to be vice-chairperson of the Corporation
and the vice-chairperson shall, in the event of the absence or
incapacity of the chairperson of the Corporation or if the office of
chairperson is vacant, act as chairperson.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-16, s. 9
- 1995, c. 29, s. 20(E)
:::

## [Mandate and Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69451 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandate

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  mandate of the Corporation is to foster and promote the development of
  the audio-visual industry in Canada and to act in connection with
  agreements entered into under subsection (8).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General capacity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of carrying out its mandate, the
  Corporation has the capacity, rights, powers and privileges of a
  natural person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specific powers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Without limiting the generality of subsection (2),
  the Corporation may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} invest in individual Canadian audio-visual
    productions in return for a share in the proceeds from those
    productions;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make loans to producers of individual Canadian
    audio-visual productions and charge interest on those loans;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} make awards for outstanding accomplishments in
    Canadian audio-visual productions;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} make grants to audio-visual industry professionals
    resident in Canada to assist them in improving their craft; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} advise and assist Canadian audio-visual producers
    in the distribution of their works and in the administrative
    functions of audio-visual production.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Borrowing

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation shall not borrow money within the
  meaning of Part X of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11) otherwise than from the Crown.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loan guarantees

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (4), the Corporation may, in
  accordance with terms and conditions approved by the Treasury Board
  and the Minister of Finance, guarantee loans for audio-visual
  productions and their distribution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian content, ownership, etc.

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, a [[Canadian
  audio-visual production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} is an audio-visual
  production in respect of which the Corporation has determined

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the completed production will, in the judgment
    of the Corporation, have a significant Canadian creative, artistic
    and technical content, and that arrangements have been made to
    ensure that the copyright in the completed production will be owned
    by an individual resident in Canada, by a corporation incorporated
    under the laws of Canada or a province or by any combination of
    owners described in this paragraph; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that provision has been made for the production
    under a co-production agreement entered into between Canada and
    another country.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation not a partner

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation shall not be regarded as a partner in
  any production in which it may invest, and its liability shall be
  limited to the amount of its investment in the production.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contribution agreements

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may enter into agreements with the
  Department of Canadian Her­itage for the provision of services or
  programs relating to the audio-visual or sound recording industries.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation and cooperation

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation shall, to the greatest possible
  extent consistent with the performance of its duties under this Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} carry out its mandate in the broader context of the
    policies of the Government of Canada with respect to culture; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} consult and cooperate with any departments, boards
    and agencies of the Government of Canada and the governments of the
    provinces that have a mandate related to the mandate of the
    Corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-16, s. 10
- 1994, c. 25, s. 1
- 2005, c. 14, s. 4
:::

## [Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69482 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The members of the
Corporation shall meet at such times and places as they deem necessary
but shall meet at least six times a year.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-8, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Executive director

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of the Corporation,
  appoint an executive director of the Corporation, who shall hold
  office during pleasure and shall be paid such salary as is fixed by
  the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Functions of executive director

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The executive director is the chief executive officer
  of the Corporation, has supervision over and direction of the work and
  staff of the Corporation and may attend the meetings of the
  Corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-16, s. 12
- 1995, c. 29, s. 19
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation may,
subject to the approval of the Minister, make by-laws for the regulation
of its proceedings and generally for the conduct and management of its
activities.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-8, s. 13
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advisory Group

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, on the
recommendation of the Corporation, appoint an Advisory Group, broadly
representative of the professional associations, exhibitors,
distributors and unions in the Canadian film industry and including
other qualified persons, to advise the Corporation on matters that the
Minister or the Corporation may refer to it.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-8, s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff and special advisers

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation may
employ such officers and employees and such technical and professional
advisers as it considers necessary for the proper conduct of its
activities, at such remuneration and on such other terms and conditions
as it deems fit.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-8, s. 15
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No application of [Public Service
Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) to members

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The [Public Service
Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) does not apply to the members of the
Corporation as such.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-8, s. 16
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation agent of Her Majesty

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation is for all its purposes an agent of Her Majesty and it may
  exercise its powers only as an agent of Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contracts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may, on behalf of Her Majesty, enter
  into contracts in the name of Her Majesty or in the name of the
  Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Property acquired by the Corporation is the property
  of Her Majesty and title thereto may be vested in the name of Her
  Majesty or in the name of the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Actions, suits or other legal proceedings in respect
  of any right or obligation acquired or incurred by the Corporation on
  behalf of Her Majesty, whether in its name or in the name of Her
  Majesty, may be brought or taken by or against the Corporation in the
  name of the Corporation in any court that would have jurisdiction if
  the Corporation were not an agent of Her Majesty.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-8, s. 17
:::

## [Financial]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69521 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriation

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby
appropriated for the purposes of this Act the sum of twenty-five million
dollars to be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund from time to
time as required pursuant to this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-8, s. 18
- 1970-71-72, c. 58, Sch. (SS) vote 63a
- 1974-75-76, c. 74, Sch. (SS) vote 62a
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advance Account for corporation

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be established in the accounts of Canada a special account to be
  known as the Telefilm Canada Advance Account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts to be charged to Account

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All amounts required for the purposes of paragraphs
  10(3)(a) and (b) and subsection 10(5) shall be paid

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} out of the amount appropriated by section 18, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} out of amounts credited to the Telefilm Canada
    Advance Account under subsection (3),

  and shall be charged to that Account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts to be credited to Account

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation shall pay to the Receiver General, to
  be deposited in the Consolidated Revenue Fund and credited to the
  Telefilm Canada Advance Account, all amounts received by the
  Corporation as or on account of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} proceeds from any production in which the
    Corporation has invested under paragraph 10(3)(a);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the principal amount of or interest on any loan
    made by the Corporation under paragraph 10(3)(b); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fees charged by the Corporation in respect of loan
    guarantees provided under subsection 10(5).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-16, s. 19
- 1994, c. 25, s. 2
- 2002, c. 17, s. 9
- 2005, c. 14, s. 5
:::

## [Audit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69555 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual audit

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The accounts and
financial transactions of the Corporation shall be audited annually by
the Auditor General of Canada, and a report of the audit shall be made
to the Corporation and to the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-8, s. 19
- 1976-77, c. 34, s. 30(F)
:::

## [Report to Parliament]{.HTitleText1} {#h-69560 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  chairperson of the Corporation shall, within three months after the
  termination of each fiscal year, submit to the Minister a report in
  such form as the Minister may prescribe relating to the activities of
  the Corporation for that fiscal year, including the financial
  statement of the Corporation and the report of the Auditor General of
  Canada thereon.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause the report submitted under
  subsection (1) to be laid before Parliament within fifteen days after
  the receipt thereof by the Minister or, if Parliament is not then
  sitting, on any of the first fifteen days next thereafter that either
  House of Parliament is sitting.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-16, s. 23
- 1995, c. 29, s. 20(E)

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2002, c. 17, s. 10

  - ###### Powers, duties and functions {#powers-duties-and-functions .MarginalNote}

    [10]{.lawlabel} Wherever, under any Act of Parliament, any
    instrument made under an Act of Parliament or any contract, lease,
    licence or other document, a power, duty or function is vested in or
    exercisable by the Canadian Film Development Corporation, that
    power, duty or function is vested in or exercisable by Telefilm
    Canada.

- --- 2002, c. 17, s. 11

  - ###### References {#references .MarginalNote}

    [11]{.lawlabel} Every reference to the Canadian Film Development
    Corporation in any deed, contract or other document executed by the
    Canadian Film Development Corporation in its own name shall, unless
    the context otherwise requires, be read as a reference to Telefilm
    Canada.

- --- 2002, c. 17, s. 12

  - ###### Rights and obligations transferred {#rights-and-obligations-transferred .MarginalNote}

    [12]{.lawlabel} All rights and property of the Canadian Film
    Development Corporation, rights and property held in its name or
    held in trust for it and all its obligations and liabilities are
    deemed to be rights, property, obligations and liabilities of
    Telefilm Canada.

- --- 2002, c. 17, s. 13

  - ###### Continuation of legal proceedings {#continuation-of-legal-proceedings .MarginalNote}

    [13]{.lawlabel} Any legal proceeding to which the Canadian Film
    Development Corporation is party pending in any court immediately
    before the day on which this section comes into force may be
    continued by or against Telefilm Canada in the same manner and to
    the same extent as it could have been continued by or against the
    Canadian Film Development Corporation.

- --- 2005, c. 14, s. 9

  - ###### Validation {#validation .MarginalNote}

    [9]{.lawlabel} Everything done by Telefilm Canada before the coming
    into force of this Act is valid to the same extent as it would have
    been were it done after that coming into force.
