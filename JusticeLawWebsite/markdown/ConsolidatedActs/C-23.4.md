[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1989, c. 3

and Safety Board and to amend certain Acts in consequence thereof
::::

of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-75348 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canadian Transportation Accident Investigation and Safety Board
Act](/eng/acts/C-23.4).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-75351 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any machine, including a
    rocket, capable of deriving support in the atmosphere from reactions
    of the air, other than a machine designed to derive support in the
    atmosphere from reactions against the earth's surface of air
    expelled from the machine; ([aéronef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[aviation occurrence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aviation occurrence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any accident or incident associated with the
      operation of an aircraft, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any situation or condition that the Board has
      reasonable grounds to believe could, if left unattended, induce an
      accident or incident described in paragraph (a); ([accident
      aéronautique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Transportation
    Accident Investigation and Safety Board established by section
    4; ([Bureau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chairperson of the
    Board; ([président]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commodity pipeline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commodity pipeline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 1998, c.
    20, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[commodity pipeline occurrence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commodity pipeline occurrence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    1998, c. 20, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[coroner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[coroner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} medical examiner, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} other person

    carrying out the duties and functions of a
    coroner; ([coroner]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any department of the Government of Canada,
      including the Minister responsible therefor and any person acting
      on behalf of the Minister,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any body listed in the schedule, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any fact-finding authority, body or person
      established or appointed by any such department, Minister, person
      or body; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Department of National Defence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department of National Defence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of National Defence and any person
      acting on behalf of the Minister of National Defence, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any fact-finding authority, body or person

      the Minister of National Defence or a person acting on behalf of

      nationale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dynamically supported craft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dynamically supported craft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a craft
    that is designed, used or capable of being used solely or partly for
    marine navigation and that possesses either of the following
    characteristics:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the weight, or a significant part thereof, is
      balanced in one mode of operation by forces other than hydrostatic
      forces, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the craft is able to operate at speeds such that
      the function v/sqrt(gL) is equal to or greater than 0.9, where "v"
      is the maximum speed, "L" is the waterline length and "g" is the
      acceleration due to gravity, all in consistent units; ([engin à
      portance dynamique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[investigator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[investigator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person referred to in
    paragraph 10(1)(a) or (b); ([enquêteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any accident or incident associated with the

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any situation or condition that the Board has

[[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[pipeline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pipeline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pipeline that is used for
    the transportation of commodities and includes all branches,
    extensions, pumps, racks, compressors, loading facilities, storage
    facilities, reservoirs, tanks, preparation plants, separation
    plants, interstation systems of communication and property and works
    connected therewith; ([pipeline]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pipeline occurrence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pipeline occurrence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any accident or incident associated with the
      operation of a pipeline, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any situation or condition that the Board has
      reasonable grounds to believe could, if left unattended, induce an
      accident or incident referred to in paragraph (a); ([accident de
      pipeline]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[railway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[railway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes all branches, extensions,

:   [[railway occurrence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any accident or incident associated with the
      operation of rolling stock on a railway, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any situation or condition that the Board has
      reasonable grounds to believe could, if left unattended, induce an
      accident or incident described in paragraph (a); ([accident
      ferroviaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[rolling stock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[rolling stock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means every description of car
    or other railway equipment that is designed for movement on its
    wheels along a line of railway and, without limiting the generality
    of the foregoing, includes any locomotive, engine, track motor car,
    tender, snow-plough, flanger and railway crane that is so
    designed; ([matériel roulant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} every description of vessel, boat or craft
      designed, used or capable of being used solely or partly for
      marine navigation without regard to method or lack of propulsion,
      and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a dynamically supported
      craft; ([navire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transportation occurrence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transportation occurrence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an aviation
    occurrence, a railway occurrence, a marine occurrence or a pipeline
    occurrence. ([accident de transport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-75397 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This Act
  applies in respect of aviation occurrences

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in or over Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in or over any place that is under Canadian air
    traffic control; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in or over any other place, if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Canada is requested to investigate the aviation
      occurrence by an appropriate authority, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the aviation occurrence involves an aircraft in
      respect of which, or that is operated by a person to whom, a
      Canadian aviation document has been issued under Part I of the
      [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Act applies in respect of marine occurrences

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other place, including waters described in
    subsection (3), if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Canada is requested to investigate the marine
      occurrence by an appropriate authority,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the marine occurrence involves a ship registered
      or licensed in Canada, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a competent witness to, or person having
      information concerning a matter that may have contributed to, the
      marine occurrence arrives or is found at any place in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} This Act also applies in respect of marine
  occurrences or pipeline occurrences related to an activity concerning
  the exploration or exploitation of the continental shelf.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} This Act applies in respect of railway occurrences
  and pipeline occurrences

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in Canada, if the railway or pipeline is within the
    legislative authority of Parliament; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} outside Canada, if Canada is requested to
    investigate the occurrence by an appropriate authority.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1996, c. 31, s. 64\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrictions re national security

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The application of this Act, or any provision
  thereof, in respect of transportation occurrences referred to in
  subsection 18(3) is subject to such restrictions in the interests of
  national security as are provided by the [National Defence
  Act](/eng/acts/N-5) or by regulations made under that Act or as the
  Governor in Council is hereby authorized to prescribe by order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act binding on Her Majesty

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} This Act is binding on Her Majesty in right of Canada
  or a province.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1989, c. 3, s. 3
- 1996, c. 31, s. 64
- 1998, c. 20, ss. 2, 24, 25(E)
:::

## [Canadian Transportation Accident Investigation and Safety Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-75426 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board established

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a board to be known as the Canadian Transportation
  Accident Investigation and Safety Board, consisting of not more than
  five members appointed by the Governor in Council, at least three of
  whom shall be full-time members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications of members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council shall appoint as members
  persons who, in the opinion of the Governor in Council, are
  collectively knowledgeable about air, marine, rail and pipeline
  transportation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of office

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A member shall be appointed to hold office during
  good behaviour for a term not exceeding seven years, but may be
  removed by the Governor in Council at any time for cause.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A member is eligible for re-appointment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuity of membership

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In fixing a term of appointment or re-appointment,
  the Governor in Council shall endeavour to ensure, to the extent that
  it is practical to do so, that, notwithstanding periodic changes in
  the membership of the Board, it remains constituted at all times at
  least partly of persons who have had previous experience as members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and fees

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A full-time member shall be paid the remuneration,
  and a part-time member shall be paid the fees, that are fixed by the
  Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The members are entitled to be paid reasonable travel
  and living expenses incurred by them in the course of their duties
  under this Act while absent from, in the case of full-time members,
  their ordinary place of work and, in the case of part-time members,
  their ordinary place of residence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retirement pensions

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The full-time members are deemed to be employed in
  the public service for the purposes of the [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The members are deemed to be employees for the
  purposes of the [Government Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5)
  and to be employed in the federal public administration for the
  purposes of any regulations made pursuant to section 9 of the
  [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall designate one member to be the Chairperson
  of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of the Chairperson

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson is the chief executive officer of the
  Board and has exclusive responsibility for managing personnel matters,
  financial matters and property matters and for all other aspects of
  the internal management of the Board, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} directing the staff and apportioning its work,
    subject to subsections 10(2) and (3);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} directing the members and apportioning their work,
    subject to section 8; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} convening meetings in accordance with section 12
    and presiding at meetings in accordance with any by-laws made under
    paragraph 8(1)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson may delegate powers and
  duties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson may, subject to any limitations
  specified in the instrument of delegation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} delegate to any member any power or duty of the
    Chairperson relating to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} convening or presiding at meetings, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} directing the members or apportioning their
      work; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} delegate to the staff of the Board any power or
    duty of the Chairperson under this Act, except the matters described
    in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A delegation under subsection (3) may be revoked in
  writing at any time by the Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity of Chairperson

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  Chairperson, or if the office of Chairperson is vacant, a member
  designated, either before or after that absence, incapacity or
  vacancy, by the Governor in Council shall act as Chairperson during
  the continuance of that absence or incapacity or until a new
  Chairperson is designated.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1989, c. 3, s. 5
- 1998, c. 20, s. 4(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest prohibited

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member
  shall not, directly or indirectly, as owner, shareholder, director,
  officer, partner or otherwise,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be engaged in a transportation undertaking or
    business, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have an interest in a transportation undertaking or
    business or an interest in the manufacture or distribution of
    transportation plant or equipment, except where the distribution is
    merely incidental to the general merchandising of goods,

  and in this subsection, [[transportation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
  air, marine, rail or pipeline transportation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal of conflict of interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where any interest referred to in subsection (1)
  vests in a member for the benefit of the member by gift, will,
  succession or otherwise, the interest shall, within three months after
  the vesting, be absolutely disposed of by that member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest prohibited

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} During the term of office of a member, in addition to
  the prohibitions described in subsection (1), the member shall not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} accept or hold any office or employment, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} carry on any activity

  inconsistent with the performance of the member's duties under this
  Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1989, c. 3, s. 6
- 1998, c. 20, s. 24

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Object of the Board

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  object of the Board is to advance transportation safety by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} conducting independent investigations, including,
    when necessary, public inquiries, into selected transportation
    occurrences in order to make findings as to their causes and
    contributing factors;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identifying safety deficiencies as evidenced by
    transportation occurrences;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} making recommendations designed to eliminate or
    reduce any such safety deficiencies; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} reporting publicly on its investigations and on the
    findings in relation thereto.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In making its findings as to the causes and
  contributing factors of a transportation occurrence, it is not the
  function of the Board to assign fault or determine civil or criminal
  liability, but the Board shall not refrain from fully reporting on the
  causes and contributing factors merely because fault or liability
  might be inferred from the Board's findings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No finding of the Board shall be construed as
  assigning fault or determining civil or criminal liability.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Findings not binding

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The findings of the Board are not binding on the
  parties to any legal, disciplinary or other proceedings.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1989, c. 3, s. 7
- 1998, c. 20, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of members

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  members, at meetings convened in accordance with section 12,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall make by-laws respecting the conduct of
    business at meetings of the Board;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall establish policies respecting the classes of
    transportation occurrences to be investigated;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} shall establish, either generally or in relation to
    specific classes of transportation occurrences, policies to be
    followed in the conduct of investigations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} shall review reports submitted to them by the
    Directors of Investigations mentioned in paragraph 10(1)(a) and,
    after such review, may require the Directors of Investigations to
    conduct further investigation with respect to any aspect of a
    transportation occurrence;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} shall determine the Board's findings as to the
    causes and contributing factors of transportation occurrences;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} shall identify any safety deficiencies as evidenced
    by transportation occurrences; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} shall make such recommendations as they consider
    appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Policies to be available to public

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall make available to the public any
  policies established pursuant to paragraph (1)(b) or (c).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1989, c. 3, s. 8

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Such
  employees as are necessary for the proper conduct of the work of the
  Board shall be appointed in accordance with the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Technical assistance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a conflict of interest would not thereby be
  created, the Chairperson may engage on a temporary basis the services
  of persons having technical or specialized knowledge to assist the
  Board in carrying out its duties under this Act, and may pay them such
  remuneration and expenses as the Board, with the approval of the
  Treasury Board, may fix.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors of Investigations and other
investigators

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} From
  among the employees appointed under subsection 9(1), there shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Director of Investigations (Air), a Director of
    Investigations (Marine) and a Director of Investigations (Rail and
    Pipelines); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} other investigators.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Directors of Investigations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each of the three Directors mentioned in paragraph
  (1)(a) has exclusive authority to direct the conduct of investigations
  on behalf of the Board under this Act in relation to aviation
  occurrences, marine occurrences, and railway and pipeline occurrences,
  respectively, but

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Directors' authority under this subsection must
    be exercised in accordance with any policies established under
    paragraphs 8(1)(b) and (c); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Directors shall report to the Board with
    respect to their investigations and shall conduct such further
    investigation as the Board requires under paragraph 8(1)(d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role of other investigators

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The investigators mentioned in paragraph (1)(b) shall
  assist the Directors of Investigations in the conduct of
  investigations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificates of appointment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson shall provide each person mentioned
  in paragraphs (1)(a) and (b) with a certificate of appointment.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1989, c. 3, s. 10
- 1998, c. 20, ss. 7, 25(E)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of Board

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board is a body corporate and is for all purposes an agent of Her
  Majesty, and the duties and powers of the Board under this Act may be
  exercised only as an agent of Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contracts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Contracts shall be entered into by the Chairperson on
  behalf of the Board, either in the name of Her Majesty or in the name
  of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Property acquired by the Board is the property of Her
  Majesty and title thereto may be vested in the name of Her Majesty or
  in the name of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal proceedings

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Actions, suits or other legal proceedings in respect
  of any right or obligation acquired or incurred by the Board on behalf
  of Her Majesty, whether in its name or in the name of Her Majesty, may
  be brought or taken by or against the Board in the name of the Board
  in any court that would have jurisdiction if the Board were not an
  agent of Her Majesty.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson to convene meetings

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson shall convene meetings of the Board whenever the work of
  the Board so requires or whenever the Chairperson is requested in
  writing to do so by two or more other members, and the Chairperson
  shall preside at the meetings.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1998, c. 20, s. 8\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  head office of the Board shall be in the National Capital Region as
  described in the schedule to the [National Capital
  Act](/eng/acts/N-4).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2012, c. 19, s. 180\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall report to Parliament through the
  Minister on its activities, findings and recommendations for each
  fiscal year and, for that purpose,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Board shall submit its report to the Minister
    within three months after the end of each fiscal year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall cause the report to be laid
    before each House of Parliament on any of the first twenty days on
    which that House is sitting after the Minister receives the report.

## [Investigations and Public Inquiries]{.HTitleText1} {#h-75561 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction of Board

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding any other Act of
  Parliament but subject to section 18, the Board may, and if so
  requested by the Governor in Council shall, investigate any
  transportation occurrence for the purpose of carrying out the object
  of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request by a department or province

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 18, the Board may investigate a
  transportation occurrence where a department, the lieutenant governor
  in council of a province or the Commissioner of the Northwest
  Territories or Nunavut, or the Commissioner of Yukon with the consent
  of the Executive Council of that territory, requests the Board to
  investigate and undertakes to be liable to the Board for any
  reasonable costs incurred by the Board in the investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusive jurisdiction of Board

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding any other Act of Parliament,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no department, other than the Department of
    National Defence, may commence an investigation into a
    transportation occurrence for the purpose of making findings as to
    its causes and contributing factors if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that transportation occurrence is being or has
      been investigated by the Board under this Act, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the department has been informed that that
      transportation occurrence is proposed to be investigated by the
      Board under this Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where an investigation into a transportation
    occurrence is commenced by the Board under this Act after an
    investigation into that transportation occurrence has been commenced
    by a department, other than the Department of National Defence, the
    department shall forthwith discontinue its investigation, to the
    extent that it is an investigation for the purpose of making
    findings as to the causes and contributing factors of the
    transportation occurrence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving provision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in subsection (3)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prevents a department from commencing an
    investigation into or continuing to investigate a transportation
    occurrence for any purpose other than that of making findings as to
    its causes and contributing factors, or from investigating any
    matter that is related to the transportation occurrence and that is
    not being investigated by the Board; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prevents the Royal Canadian Mounted Police from
    investigating the transportation occurrence for any purpose for
    which it is empowered to conduct investigations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where Board does not investigate

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, where the Board does not
  investigate a transportation occurrence, no department is prevented
  from investigating any aspect of the transportation occurrence that it
  is empowered to investigate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of investigators

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an investigator believes on reasonable grounds that there is, or may
  be, at or in any place, any thing relevant to the conduct of an
  investigation of a transportation occurrence, the investigator may,
  subject to subsection (2), enter and search that place for any such
  thing, and seize any such thing that is found in the course of that
  search.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for exercise of powers under
  subsection (1)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An investigator shall not exercise the powers
  referred to in subsection (1) in relation to a particular place
  without the consent of the person apparently in charge of that place
  unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} those powers are so exercised in relation to that
    place under the authority of a warrant, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by reason of exigent circumstances, it would not be
    practical for the investigator to obtain a warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issue of warrant authorizing exercise
  of powers under subsection (1)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a justice of the peace is satisfied by
  information on oath that an investigator believes on reasonable
  grounds that there is, or may be, at or in any place, any thing
  relevant to the conduct of an investigation of a transportation
  occurrence, the justice may, on [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  application, issue a warrant signed by the justice authorizing the
  investigator to enter and search that place for any such thing and to
  seize any such thing found in the course of that search.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrants may be obtained by telephone,
  etc.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The procedure set out in section 487.1 of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) applies in relation to the obtaining
  of warrants under this section, subject to regulations made under
  paragraph 34(1)(h).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to test things seized, etc.

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where any thing is seized by an investigator under
  subsection (1), the investigator

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} may, subject to paragraph (b), cause such tests,
    including tests to destruction, to be conducted on the thing as are
    necessary for the purposes of the investigation in respect of which
    the thing was seized;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall, to the extent that it is practical and safe
    to do so and does not unreasonably impede the progress of the
    investigation,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} take all reasonable measures to invite the owner
      of the thing, and any person who appears on reasonable grounds to
      be entitled to it, to be present at any tests referred to in
      paragraph (a), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} allow persons referred to in subparagraph (i) to
      be present at those tests; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to the need to conduct such tests, shall
    cause the thing to be preserved pending its return in accordance
    with section 20.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to exclude persons from
  particular areas

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} An investigator may, for the purposes of preserving
  and protecting any thing involved or likely to have been involved in a
  transportation occurrence, whether or not the thing has been seized
  under this section, prohibit or limit access to the area immediately
  surrounding the place at which the thing is located for such period as
  is necessary for the purposes of the investigation of the
  transportation occurrence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disruption to be minimized

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In exercising the power conferred by subsection (6),
  an investigator shall have regard to the desirability of minimizing
  any resulting disruption to transportation services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence in respect of exclusion order

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} No person shall knowingly enter an area in
  contravention of a prohibition or limitation of access pursuant to
  subsection (6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional powers of investigators

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} An investigator who is investigating a transportation
  occurrence may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the investigator believes on reasonable
    grounds that a person is in possession of information relevant to
    that investigation,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by notice in writing signed by the investigator,
      require the person to produce the information to the investigator
      or to attend before the investigator and give a statement referred
      to in section 30, under oath or solemn affirmation if required by
      the investigator, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} make such copies of or take such extracts from
      the information as the investigator deems necessary for the
      purposes of the investigation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the investigator believes on reasonable
    grounds that the medical examination of a person who is directly or
    indirectly involved in the operation of an aircraft, ship, rolling
    stock or pipeline is, or may be, relevant to the investigation, by
    notice in writing signed by the investigator, require the person to
    submit to a medical examination;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the investigator believes on reasonable
    grounds that a physician or other health practitioner has
    information concerning a patient that is relevant to that
    investigation, by notice in writing signed by the investigator,
    require the physician or practitioner to provide that information to
    the investigator; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the investigator believes on reasonable
    grounds that the performance of an autopsy on the body of a deceased
    person, or the carrying out of other medical examinations of human
    remains, is, or may be, relevant to the conduct of the
    investigation, cause such an autopsy or medical examination to be
    performed and, for that purpose, by notice in writing signed by the
    investigator, require the person having custody of the body of the
    deceased person or other human remains to permit the performance of
    that autopsy or that medical examination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons to comply with requirements
  imposed under paragraph (9)(a), (c) or (d)

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} No person shall refuse or fail to produce
  information to an investigator, or to attend before an investigator
  and give a statement, in accordance with a requirement imposed under
  paragraph (9)(a), or to provide information in accordance with a
  requirement imposed under paragraph (9)(c) or to make the body of a
  deceased person or other human remains available for the performance
  of an autopsy or medical examination in accordance with a requirement
  imposed under paragraph (9)(d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons to comply with requirements
  imposed under paragraph (9)(b)

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} No person shall refuse or fail to submit to a
  medical examination in accordance with a requirement imposed under
  paragraph (9)(b), but information obtained pursuant to such an
  examination is privileged and, subject to the power of the Board to
  make such use of it as the Board considers necessary in the interests
  of transportation safety, no person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} knowingly communicate it or permit it to be
    communicated to any person; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be required to produce it or give evidence relating
    to it in any legal, disciplinary or other proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate to be produced

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} Before acting under this section, an investigator
  shall, on request, produce the investigator's certificate of
  appointment to any person in relation to whom the investigator acts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of medical examination for
  certain purposes

  [(13)]{.lawlabel} The requirement under subsection (9) that a person
  submit to a medical examination shall not be construed as a
  requirement that the person submit to any procedure involving surgery,
  perforation of the skin or any external tissue or the entry into the
  body of any drug or foreign substance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(14)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section shall be taken

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to imply that a thing seized pursuant to
    subsection (1) may not be an aircraft, a ship, an item of rolling
    stock or a pipeline, or any part thereof; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to authorize the exercise of a power by an
    investigator in circumstances where the exercise of that power would
    be inconsistent with section 18.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

  [(15)]{.lawlabel} In executing a warrant under this section, an
  investigator shall not use force unless the investigator is
  accompanied by a peace officer and the use of force is specifically
  authorized in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to produce document, etc.

  [(15.1)]{.lawlabel} Where an investigator has required a person to do

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report made public

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  completion of any investigation, the Board shall prepare and make
  available to the public a report on its findings, including any safety
  deficiencies that it has identified and any recommendations that it
  considers appropriate in the interests of transportation safety.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representations on draft report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before making public a report under subsection (1),
  the Board shall, on a confidential basis, send a copy of the draft
  report on its findings and any safety deficiencies that it has
  identified to each Minister and any other person who, in the opinion
  of the Board, has a direct interest in the findings of the Board, and
  shall give that Minister or other person a reasonable opportunity to
  make representations to the Board with respect to the draft report
  before the final report is prepared.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality of draft report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall communicate or use the draft report,
  or permit its communication or use, for any purpose, other than the
  taking of remedial measures, not strictly necessary to the study of,
  and preparation of representations concerning, the draft report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of dealing with representations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} receive representations made pursuant to
    subsection (2) in any manner the Board considers appropriate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} keep a record of those representations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} consider those representations before preparing its
    final report; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} notify in writing each of the persons who made
    those representations, indicating how the Board has disposed of that
    person's representations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection of representations

  [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} A representation is privileged, except for one made
  by a minister responsible for a department having a direct interest in
  the findings of the Board. Subject to other provisions of this Act or
  to a written authorization from the author of a representation, no
  person, including any person to whom access is provided under this
  section, shall knowingly communicate it or permit it to be
  communicated to any person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use by Board

  [(4.2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may use representations as it considers
  necessary in the interests of transportation safety.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Making representations available to
  coroner

  [(4.3)]{.lawlabel} If requested to do so by a coroner conducting an
  investigation into any circumstances in respect of which
  representations were made to the Board, the Board shall make them
  available to the coroner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition of use

  [(4.4)]{.lawlabel} Except for use by a coroner for the purpose of an
  investigation, no person shall use representations in any legal,
  disciplinary or other proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of findings and
  recommendations

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} during its investigation of a transportation
    occurrence, notify forthwith in writing any minister or person who,
    in the opinion of the Board, has a direct interest in the findings
    of the Board of any of its findings and recommendations, whether
    interim or final, that, in the opinion of the Board, require urgent
    action; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on completion of its investigation of a
    transportation occurrence, notify forthwith in writing any minister
    or person who, in the opinion of the Board, has a direct interest in
    the findings of the Board of its findings as to the causes and
    contributing factors of the transportation occurrence, any safety
    deficiencies it has identified and any recommendations resulting
    from its findings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister to reply to Board

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A Minister who is notified of the findings and
  recommendations of the Board under paragraph (5)(a) or (b) shall,
  within ninety days after being so notified,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} advise the Board in writing of any action taken or
    proposed to be taken in response to those findings and
    recommendations, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide written reasons to the Board if no action
    will be taken or if the action to be taken differs from the action
    that was recommended,

  and, in either case, the Minister shall make that reply available to
  the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrictions

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Where obligations are imposed on the Minister of
  National Defence under subsection (6), those obligations are subject
  to such restrictions in the interests of national security as are

  regulations made under that Act or as the Governor in Council is
  hereby authorized to prescribe by order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of time

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board is satisfied that a Minister is
  unable to reply to the Board within the period referred to in
  subsection (6), the period may be extended as the Board deems

## [Privilege]{.HTitleText1} {#h-75791 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[on-board
recording]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[on-board recording]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
  whole or any part of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a recording of voice communications originating
    from, or received on or in,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the flight deck of an aircraft,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the bridge or a control room of a ship,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the cab of a locomotive, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the control room or pumping station of a
      pipeline, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a video recording of the activities of the
    operating personnel of an aircraft, ship, locomotive or pipeline

  that is made, using recording equipment that is intended to not be
  controlled by the operating personnel, on the flight deck of the
  aircraft, on the bridge or in a control room of the ship, in the cab
  of the locomotive or in a place where pipeline operations are carried
  out, as the case may be, and includes a transcript or substantial
  summary of such a recording.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privilege for on-board recordings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every on-board recording is privileged and, except as
  provided by this section, no person, including any person to whom
  access is provided under this section, shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} knowingly communicate an on-board recording or
    permit it to be communicated to any person; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be required to produce an on-board recording or
    give evidence relating to it in any legal, disciplinary or other
    proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access by Board

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any on-board recording that relates to a
  transportation occurrence being investigated under this Act shall be
  released to an investigator who requests it for the purposes of the
  investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use by Board

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board may make any use of any on-board recording
  obtained under this Act that it considers necessary in the interests
  of transportation safety, but, subject to subsections (5) and (5.1),
  shall not knowingly communicate or permit to be communicated to anyone
  any portion of the recording that is unrelated to the causes or
  contributing factors of the transportation occurrence under
  investigation or to the identification of safety deficiencies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access by peace officers, coroners and
  other investigators

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall make available any on-board recording
  obtained under this Act to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1998, c. 20, s. 17\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a coroner who requests access thereto for the
    purpose of an investigation that the coroner is conducting; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person carrying out a coordinated investigation
    under section 18.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to provide access to certain
  persons

  [(5.1)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a transportation occurrence that is
  required to be reported under this Act to the Board and that is
  investigated under this Act, the Board may make an on-board recording
  related to the occurrence available to a person who is expressly
  authorized under the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), the [Canadian
  Energy Regulator Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1), the [Railway Safety
  Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2) or the [Canada Shipping Act,
  2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) to use or communicate it and, if the Board
  does so, the person may only use or communicate it in accordance with
  the express authorization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization under another Act

  [(5.2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section prevents the use or
  communication of an on-board recording if that use or communication is
  expressly authorized under the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), the
  [Canadian Energy Regulator Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1), the [Railway Safety
  Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2) or the [Canada Shipping Act,
  2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there has been no transportation occurrence that is
    required to be reported under this Act to the Board that involves
    the means of transportation to which the recording relates; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there has been a transportation occurrence that is
    required to be reported under this Act to the Board that involves
    the means of transportation to which the recording relates but that
    is not investigated under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of court or coroner

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything in this section, where, in
  any proceedings before a court or coroner, a request for the
  production and discovery of an on-board recording is made, the court
  or coroner shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cause notice of the request to be given to the
    Board, if the Board is not a party to the proceedings;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [in camera]{.otherLang lang="la"}, examine the
    on-board recording and give the Board a reasonable opportunity to
    make representations with respect thereto; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the court or coroner concludes in the
    circumstances of the case that the public interest in the proper
    administration of justice outweighs in importance the privilege
    attached to the on-board recording by virtue of this section, order
    the production and discovery of the on-board recording, subject to
    such restrictions or conditions as the court or coroner deems
    appropriate, and may require any person to give evidence that
    relates to the on-board recording.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use prohibited

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} An on-board recording may not be used against any of
  the following persons in disciplinary proceedings, proceedings
  relating to the capacity or competence of an officer or employee to
  perform the officer's or employee's functions, or in legal or other
  proceedings, namely, air or rail traffic controllers, marine traffic
  regulators, aircraft, train or ship crew members (including, in the
  case of ships, masters, officers, pilots and ice advisers), airport
  vehicle operators, flight service station specialists, persons who
  relay messages respecting air or rail traffic control, marine traffic
  regulation or related matters and persons who are directly or
  indirectly involved in the operation of a pipeline.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[court]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (6),
  [[court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a person or persons appointed
  or designated to conduct a public inquiry into a transportation
  occurrence pursuant to this Act or the [Inquiries
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11).

this Act into, or into matters that include, the causes and contributing
factors of an accident or loss of life or property occurring on a
railway, where that inquiry was commenced but not completed before the
day on which section 51 of this Act comes into force,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that person continues to have the same powers and
  duties under the [Railway Safety Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2) as the person
  had before the person's appointment under this Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Transportation Accident Investigation
  and Safety Board shall make the services of that person available to
  the Minister of Transport; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} that person shall be paid remuneration and expenses
  in respect of the performance of duties under the [Railway Safety
  Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2) as if those duties were being performed under
  this Act.
