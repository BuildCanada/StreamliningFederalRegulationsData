## An Act respecting constitutional amendments {#an-act-respecting-constitutional-amendments .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1996, c. 1
:::

Assented to 1996-02-02

An Act respecting constitutional amendments
::::

:::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent to constitutional amendments

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  Minister of the Crown shall propose a motion for a resolution to
  authorize an amendment to the Constitution of Canada, other than an
  amendment in respect of which the legislative assembly of a province
  may exercise a veto under section 41 or 43 of the [Constitution Act,
  1982](/eng/Const/) or may express its dissent under subsection 38(3)
  of that Act, unless the amendment has first been consented to by a
  majority of the provinces that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Ontario;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Quebec;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} British Columbia;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} two or more of the Atlantic provinces that have,
    according to the then latest general census, combined populations of
    at least fifty per cent of the population of all the Atlantic
    provinces; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} two or more of the Prairie provinces that have,
    according to the then latest general census, combined populations of
    at least fifty per cent of the population of all the Prairie
    provinces.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section,

  [[Atlantic provinces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Atlantic provinces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the provinces of
      Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island and Newfoundland
      and Labrador; ([provinces de l'Atlantique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Prairie provinces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Prairie provinces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the provinces of
      Manitoba, Saskatchewan and Alberta. ([provinces des
      Prairies]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})
