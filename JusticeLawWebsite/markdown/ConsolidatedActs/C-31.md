## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-85939 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Civilian War-related Benefits Act](/eng/acts/C-31).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-31, s. 1
- 1992, c. 24, s. 2
- 1999, c. 10, s. 19
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-85944 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In Parts
  I to X,

  [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 1995, c. 18, s.
      79\]]{.Repealed}

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Veterans
      Affairs or such other member of the Queen's Privy Council for
      Canada as may be designated by the Governor in Council as the
      Minister for the purposes of this
      Act; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[War]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[War]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the war waged by His Majesty and
      His Majesty's Allies against Germany and Germany's Allies, which
      for the purposes of this Act shall be deemed to have commenced on
      September 1, 1939 and to have terminated on April 1,
      1947. ([guerre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References to pension and allowance
  rates

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A reference in this Act to any rate or rates set out
  in Schedule I, II or III of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) shall be
  construed as a reference to that rate or those rates as increased by
  virtue of Part V of that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-31, s. 2
- 1995, c. 18, s. 79
- 1999, c. 10, s. 20
- 2000, c. 34, s. 94(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons considered to be members of
certain groups

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** A person who served in
a theatre of actual war, within the meaning of paragraph 37(8)(b) or (c)
of the [War Veterans Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3), with a group or
organization mentioned in Part II.1, III, VII.1 or X or paragraph (e),
(f), (h) or (i) of the definition [[civilian]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
subsection 56(1) in an auxiliary, reserve, junior, support or ground
crew capacity, whether or not the person was enrolled as a member of
that group or organization, shall, for the purposes of that Part or
paragraph, be considered to have been a member of that group or
organization if the person functioned closely with, or functioned
alongside, that group or organization under conditions of service that
were substantially similar to wartime conditions of service of a member
of the forces.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 34, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claims to be dealt with as claims under
[Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6)

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** All claims for pensions,
allowances and compensation under Parts I to X shall be dealt with and
adjudicated on in like manner as claims under the [Pension
Act](/eng/acts/P-6) and all the provisions of the [Pension
Act](/eng/acts/P-6) not inconsistent with Parts I to X, with such
modifications as the circumstances require, apply to every claim under
Parts I to X.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-20, s. 3
:::

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Canadian Salt Water Fishers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-85969 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-85970 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Part.

[[Canadian salt water fisher]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian salt water fisher]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a British
    subject who served on a ship engaged in the salt water fishing
    industry of Canada or Newfoundland. ([pêcheur canadien en eau
    salée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[enemy action or counteraction against the enemy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[enemy action or counteraction against the
    enemy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes marine hazards occasioned by
    the War and encountered by a ship engaged in the salt water fishing
    industry of Canada or Newfoundland, when it was employed on a voyage
    that in the opinion of the Minister was essential to the prosecution
    of the War on behalf of His Majesty or His Majesty's
    allies. ([opération de l'ennemi ou
    contre-opération]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-31, s. 6
- 1992, c. 24, s. 3
- 1999, c. 10, s. 22
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9) to
apply

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Part, the status of a vessel and of the members of its crew, and the
class of voyage in which a vessel was engaged, shall be determined
according to the Canada Shipping Act, 1934 and regulations made
thereunder, as that Act and those regulations read at the relevant time.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-31, s. 7
- 1999, c. 10, s. 22
:::

### [Evidence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-85981 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Part, where official records of a person's service, or of injuries or
disease suffered or contracted by a person during service, are not
available, the Minister may accept a statutory declaration or like
statement from anyone if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} information about the existence of any registered or
  licensed ship on which the person claims to have served is
  corroborated by official records;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} no information in the declaration or statement is
  contradicted by other evidence; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} after taking into consideration such corroboration as
  may be available, the Minister is satisfied, on the balance of
  probabilities, that the information in the declaration or statement is
  true.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-31, s. 8
- 1992, c. 24, s. 4
- 1995, c. 18, s. 80
- 1999, c. 10, s. 23
:::

### [Pensions and Allowances for Disability and Death]{.HTitleText2} {#h-85989 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pensions and allowances

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Part, a
pension, attendance allowance, clothing allowance and exceptional
incapacity allowance in accordance with the rates set out in Schedules
I, II and III to the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) may be awarded to or
in respect of any person, other than a pensioner within the meaning of
that Act, who, during the War and as a direct result of enemy action or
counteraction against the enemy, suffered or contracted injury or
disease or aggravation thereof resulting in disability or death, while
that person was a Canadian salt water fisher.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-31, s. 9
- 1992, c. 24, s. 4
- 1999, c. 10, s. 24
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation otherwise payable

**[[9.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.1 .sectionLabel}** No pension shall be
awarded under this Part in respect of any disability or death for which
compensation is payable under the [Merchant Seamen Compensation
Act](/eng/acts/M-6) or the [Government Employees Compensation
Act](/eng/acts/G-5) or any workers' compensation or similar laws unless
evidence satisfactory to the Minister is provided that a claim for that
compensation has not been made and unless the person entitled to that
compensation submits to the Minister a waiver, in a form approved by the
Minister, of all claims for that compensation in respect of disability
or death.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1999, c. 10, s. 24
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption of death

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, for the
purposes of this Part, presume death in every case where, according to
the evidence available as to the circumstances surrounding the
disappearance of the person whose death is in question or the loss of
the ship on which the person was serving, the Minister is satisfied
beyond a reasonable doubt that the death has in fact occurred.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-31, s. 10
- 1995, c. 18, s. 85
:::

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, 1999, c. 10,
s. 25\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person not a Canadian citizen or resident
of Canada

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding anything
in this Part, where a person entitled to a pension under this Part is
not a Canadian citizen and is not a resident of Canada, the Minister
may, in lieu of that pension, award such pension or such lump sum as the
Minister deems commensurate with the pension that would be payable under
this Part to the person if he were a Canadian citizen or a resident of
Canada, having regard to comparative living costs and such other matters
that may affect the value of the pension, but no pension or amount
awarded under this section shall exceed the amount of pension that would
be payable to the person under this Part if he were a Canadian citizen
or a resident of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-31, s. 12
- 1995, c. 18, s. 85
:::

## [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Royal Canadian Mounted Police Special Constables]{.HTitleText1} {#h-86053 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-86054 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[special
constable]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** In this Part, [[special
constable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person specially engaged and
employed by the Royal Canadian Mounted Police under the authority of the
Governor in Council for the particular duty of mounting guard at
vulnerable points throughout Canada or for any other similar duty during
the War.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-20, s. 25
:::

### [Pensions for Disability and Death]{.HTitleText2} {#h-86059 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pensions of special constables

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Part,
pensions shall be awarded to or in respect of special constables who,
during the War and as a direct result of the performance of their duties
as special constables, suffered injury or disease or aggravation thereof
resulting in disability or death.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-20, s. 26
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rate and manner

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The pension to be awarded
under this Part in respect of disability shall be awarded at such rate
and in such manner as the Governor in Council may prescribe under
section 5 of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Pension Continuation
Act](/eng/acts/R-10.6), chapter R-10 of the Revised Statutes of Canada,
1970.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-20, s. 27
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions applicable

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The pensions to be
awarded under this Part in respect of death shall be awarded in
accordance with section 45 of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Pension
Continuation Act](/eng/acts/R-10.6), chapter R-10 of the Revised
Statutes of Canada, 1970, and for the purposes of that section the pay
and allowances that would have been permitted for pension purposes shall
be the actual pay and allowances of which the special constable was in
receipt at the time of his death.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-20, s. 28
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of pension

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Where a special constable
is in receipt of a disability pension under the [Pension
Act](/eng/acts/P-6), the amount of pension payable under this Part shall
not at any time exceed the amount by which the pension authorized by
that Act for total disability exceeds the pension of which he is in
receipt under that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-20, s. 29
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not applicable if [Pension
Act](/eng/acts/P-6) applies

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** No pension shall be
awarded under this Part for any disability in respect of which a pension
was awarded under the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-20, s. 30
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Award on ceasing to be special constable

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** No pension shall be
awarded under this Part to or in respect of a special constable until he
ceases to be a special constable.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-20, s. 31
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surviving spouse, common-law partner and
children only

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** No pension for death
shall be awarded under this Part to or in respect of any person other
than the surviving spouse, surviving common-law partner and surviving
children of the special constable on account of whose death pension is
claimed.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-31, s. 29
- 2000, c. 12, s. 80
:::

## [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Air Raid Precautions Workers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-86088 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-86089 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

[[air raid precautions worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[air raid precautions worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
    registered as a volunteer worker in a designated area by an official
    body organized for air raid precautions purposes, a duly registered
    voluntary evacuation worker or a person designated as such by the
    Minister pursuant to section 40; ([engagé de la défense
    passive]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any area that has been
    designated as such by the Governor in Council; ([région
    désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[serious or prolonged disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[serious or prolonged disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not
    include a disability of a degree less than twenty per cent estimated
    in the manner provided by subsection 35(2) of the [Pension
    Act](/eng/acts/P-6); ([invalidité grave ou
    prolongée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[war service injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[war service injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in the case of an
    air raid precautions worker other than a duly registered voluntary
    evacuation worker, any physical injury sustained during the War and
    arising out of and in the course of his duties as such as a direct
    result of enemy action, counteraction against the enemy or action in
    apprehension of enemy attack or during a blackout, test or period of
    training duly authorized by the senior air raid precautions officer
    in the designated area in which the injury was sustained, and, in
    the case of a duly registered voluntary evacuation worker, means
    injuries arising out of and in the course of his duties as an
    evacuation worker. ([blessure de service de guerre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-31, s. 30
- 1995, c. 18, s. 85
:::

### [Pensions and Allowances for Disability and Death]{.HTitleText2} {#h-86098 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rates applicable

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Part,
pensions and allowances shall be awarded in accordance with the rates
set out in Schedules I, II and III of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6)
in respect of serious or prolonged disability or death caused by a war
service injury.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-31, s. 31
- 1999, c. 10, s. 29
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Wilful negligence or improper conduct

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** No pension shall be
awarded under this Part in respect of a war service injury sustained by
reason of the wilful negligence or improper conduct of the air raid
precautions worker by or in respect of whom pension is claimed.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-20, s. 36
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If other pension or payment made

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** No pension in respect of
a war service injury shall be paid under this Part to or in respect of
any person in any period during which the person receives or is entitled
to receive in respect of the same injury any grant, allowance,
compensation, pension or other payment of a like nature, payable out of
any public funds to which the person has not made a direct financial
contribution, unless the grant, allowance, compensation, pension or
other payment is less than the amount of the pension that would
otherwise be payable under this Part, in which case a pension equal to
the amount by which the pension that would otherwise be payable under
this Part exceeds the other grant, allowance, compensation, pension or
other payment may be paid under this Part during that period.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-20, s. 37
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pension to surviving spouse or surviving
common-law partner

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  pension shall be awarded under this Part to the surviving spouse or
  surviving common-law partner of any person in respect of the death of
  the person unless the surviving spouse or surviving common-law partner
  wholly or to a substantial extent maintained or was maintained by that
  person at the time of that person's death and unless the surviving
  spouse or surviving common-law partner was that person's spouse or
  common-law partner, as the case may be, prior to the day the war
  service injury in respect of which a pension is claimed was sustained.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional pension in respect of spouse
  or common-law partner

## [PART VII]{.HLabel1}[Injury During Remedial Treatment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-86136 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rates of pensions and allowances

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** Pensions and allowances
shall be awarded in accordance with the rates set out in Schedules I, II
and III of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) to or in respect of persons
who

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} were called up for training, service or duty under
  The National Resources Mobilization Act, 1940, chapter 13 of the
  Statutes of Canada, 1940;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} accepted and underwent treatment of any kind
  prescribed by the Department of Veterans Affairs for the purpose of
  improving their physical condition and rendering them fit for
  training, service or duty; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} suffered injury or disease or aggravation thereof
  resulting in disability or death arising out of or directly connected
  with that treatment.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-31, s. 41
- 1999, c. 10, s. 30
- 2000, c. 34, s. 94(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rates of pensions and allowances

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** Pensions and allowances
shall be awarded in accordance with the rates set out in Schedules I, II
and III of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) to or in respect of persons
who

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} volunteered for active service in the Canadian naval,
  army or air forces but were not accepted owing to their physical
  condition;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} were furnished with remedial treatment by the
  Department of Veterans Affairs, under the conditions prescribed by the
  Governor in Council, for the purpose of rendering them fit for active
  service in those forces; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} suffered injury or disease or aggravation thereof
  resulting in disability or death arising out of or directly connected
  with that treatment.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-31, s. 42
- 1999, c. 10, s. 31
- 2000, c. 34, s. 94(F)

## [PART VIII]{.HLabel1}[Voluntary Aid Detachment (World War II)]{.HTitleText1} {#h-86162 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

[[member of the Voluntary Aid Detachment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[member of the Voluntary Aid Detachment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    a member of the Nursing Auxiliary Canadian Red Cross Corps or the
    Nursing Division of the St. John Ambulance Brigade of Canada who,
    with the approval of the Adjutant-General, served with the Royal
    Canadian Army Medical Corps during the War; ([membre du détachement
    des auxiliaires volontaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[serious or prolonged disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[serious or prolonged disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not
    include a disability of a degree less than twenty per cent estimated
    in the manner provided by subsection 35(2) of the [Pension
    Act](/eng/acts/P-6); ([invalidité grave ou
    prolongée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[war service injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[war service injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an injury arising
    out of and in the course of duties as a member of the Voluntary Aid
    Detachment. ([blessure de service de guerre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-20, s. 48
:::

pensions and allowances shall be awarded in accordance with Schedules I,
II and III of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) in respect of serious or
prolonged disability or death caused by a war service injury.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-31, s. 44
- 1999, c. 10, s. 32
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Improper conduct

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** No pension shall be
awarded under this Part in respect of a war service injury sustained by
reason of the wilful negligence or improper conduct of the member of the
Voluntary Aid Detachment by or in respect of whom pension is claimed.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-20, s. 50
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Avoidance of duplication of benefits

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** No pension in respect of
a war service injury shall be paid under this Part to or in respect of
any person in any period during which the person receives or is entitled
to receive in respect of the same injury any grant, allowance,
compensation, pension or other payment of a like nature, payable out of
any public funds to which that person has not made a direct financial
contribution, unless the grant, allowance, compensation, pension or
other payment is less than the amount of the pension that would
otherwise be payable under this Part, in which case a pension equal to
the amount by which the pension that would otherwise be payable under
this Part exceeds the other grant, allowance, compensation, pension or
other payment may be paid under this Part during that period.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-20, s. 51

## [PART XI]{.HLabel1}[Civilian Allowances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-86213 .Part}

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} during the period of service referred to in

        I in a theatre of actual war within the meaning of paragraph
        37(8)(b) of the [War Veterans Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3),

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} was an overseas welfare worker within the

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 1999, c. 10, s. 35(5)

  - - [35]{.lawlabel} [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, on the coming
      into force of this subsection, cease to pay an allowance referred
      to in subsection 57(1) of the Merchant Navy Veteran and Civilian
      War-related Benefits Act to or in respect of any civilian who
      would not have been entitled to receive the allowance had
      subsection 56(3.1) of that Act, as enacted by subsection (4), been
      in force when the allowance became payable.

- --- 1999, c. 10, s. 48

  - [48]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any benefit that, immediately before the coming
      into force of section 24, was payable under section 9 of the
      Merchant Navy Veteran and Civilian War-related Benefits Act
      continues to be payable under the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) as
      amended by this Act, and any application for such a benefit that
      had not been adjudicated before the coming into force of section
      24 shall be continued and adjudicated under the [Pension
      Act](/eng/acts/P-6) as amended by this Act;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any benefit that, immediately before the coming
      into force of section 27, was payable under section 15.2 of the
      Merchant Navy Veteran and Civilian War-related Benefits Act by
      virtue of paragraph 15.2(1)(a) or (b) continues to be payable
      under the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) as amended by this Act, and
      any application for such a benefit that had not been adjudicated
      before the coming into force of section 27 shall be continued and
      adjudicated under the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) as amended by
      this Act;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any benefit that, immediately before the coming
      into force of section 27, was payable under section 15.2 of the
      Merchant Navy Veteran and Civilian War-related Benefits Act by
      virtue of paragraph 15.2(1)(c) continues to be payable under
      section 9 of that Act as amended by this Act, and any application
      for such a benefit that had not been adjudicated before the coming
      into force of section 27 shall be continued and adjudicated under
      section 9 of that Act as amended by this Act; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any benefit that, immediately before the coming
      into force of section 36, was payable under subsection 57(1) of
      the Merchant Navy Veteran and Civilian War-related Benefits Act
      continues to be payable under the [War Veterans Allowance
      Act](/eng/acts/W-3) as amended by this Act, and any application
      for such a benefit that had not been adjudicated before the coming
      into force of section 36 shall be continued and adjudicated under
      the [War Veterans Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3) as amended by this
      Act.

- --- 1999, c. 10, s. 49

  - [49]{.lawlabel} After the coming into force of this section, a
    reference in any enactment or other document to the Merchant Navy
    Veteran and Civilian War-related Benefits Act is deemed to be a
    reference to the [Civilian War-related Benefits Act](/eng/acts/C-31)
    or to whichever of the following is appropriate in the
    circumstances:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6), in relation to
      a person who was, immediately before the coming into force of this
      section, a merchant navy veteran within the meaning of the
      Merchant Navy Veteran and Civilian War-related Benefits Act;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6), in relation to
      a person who, immediately before the coming into force of this
      section, was eligible to be awarded a benefit under section 15.2
      of the Merchant Navy Veteran and Civilian War-related Benefits Act
      by virtue of paragraph 15.2(1)(a) or (b);

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the [Civilian War-related Benefits
      Act](/eng/acts/C-31), in relation to a person who, immediately
      before the coming into force of this section, was eligible to be
      awarded a benefit under section 15.2 of the Merchant Navy Veteran
      and Civilian War-related Benefits Act by virtue of paragraph
      15.2(1)(c); or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the [War Veterans Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3),
      in relation to a person who, immediately before the coming into
      force of this section, was a merchant navy veteran referred to in
      section 57 of the Merchant Navy Veteran and Civilian War-related
      Benefits Act.
