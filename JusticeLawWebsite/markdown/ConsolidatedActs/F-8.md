[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[aboriginal government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[aboriginal government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an Indian, an
      Inuit or a Métis government or the [[council of the

  [[administration agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[administration agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an agreement between the Government of Canada
        and the government of a province or an aboriginal government
        under which

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Government of Canada will administer and
          enforce an Act of the legislature of the province, or
          legislation made by an aboriginal government, that imposes a
          tax and will make payments to the province or the aboriginal
          government in respect of the taxes collected, in accordance
          with the terms and conditions of the agreement, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the government of the province will
          administer and enforce an Act of Parliament that imposes a tax

        and the government of a province under which the government of
        the province will administer and enforce a First Nation law that

        in respect of the taxes collected, in accordance with the terms

        d'application]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[province]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of population

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, the population of a
  province, or, before April 1, 1999, of the part of the Northwest
  Territories that became Nunavut or the part that remained as the
  Northwest Territories, for a fiscal year is the population of the
  province or the part for that fiscal year as determined by the Chief
  Statistician of Canada in the prescribed manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of gross domestic product

  [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, the gross domestic
  product of Canada for a calendar year is the gross domestic product of
  Canada for the year as determined by the Chief Statistician of Canada
  in prescribed manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References to "Act"

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the definition [[administration
  agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection (1) and in Part III, a
  reference to an Act shall be read as a reference to an Act or a part
  thereof.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General rule

- **[[3.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the other provisions of
  this Part, the fiscal equalization payment that may be paid to a
  province for a fiscal year is the amount, as determined by the
  Minister, equal to the greater of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

    ::: {#240781 .Paragraph}
    (A + B) × C

    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is the aggregate of the amounts obtained by subtracting, for
        each revenue source except the one referred to in paragraph (e)
        of the definition [[revenue source]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
        subsection 3.5(1), the average annual per capita yield in that
        province for the revenue source for that fiscal year from the
        average annual per capita national yield for the revenue source
        for that fiscal year,

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is 50% of the amount obtained by subtracting, for the revenue
        source referred to in paragraph (e) of the definition [[revenue
        source]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 3.5(1), the average
        annual per capita revenue to be equalized for that province for
        that fiscal year from the average annual per capita national
        revenue to be equalized for that fiscal year, and

    [C]{.dfn}
    :   is the average annual population of that province for that
        fiscal year, and
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

    ::: {#240787 .Paragraph}
    A × C

    where

    [A and C]{.dfn}
    :   have the same meaning as in paragraph (a).
    :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Province may elect otherwise

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a province may elect, at the
  prescribed time and in the prescribed manner, that the fiscal
  equalization payment that may be paid to that province for a fiscal
  year be equal to the amount determined by the formula set out in
  paragraph (1)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Negative amount

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Part, if the amount of a
  fiscal equalization payment computed in accordance with subsection (1)
  or (2) is negative, that amount is considered to be zero.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

- **[[3.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this section and in sections 3 to 3.4.

  [[average annual per capita national revenue to be equalized]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[average annual per capita national revenue to be
      equalized]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, for a fiscal year for a
      revenue source, the amount determined by the formula

      ::: {#240864 .Definition}
      (A/2 + B/4 + C/4) / D

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is the national revenue to be equalized for that revenue
          source for the fiscal year that is two years prior to that
          fiscal year;

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the national revenue to be equalized for that revenue
          source for the fiscal year that is three years prior to that
          fiscal year;

      [C]{.dfn}
      :   is the national revenue to be equalized for that revenue
          source for the fiscal year that is four years prior to that

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fiscal equalization amount

- **[[3.72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.72
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of this section and
  section 3.71, the fiscal equalization amount for a province for a
  fiscal year is the average of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the greater of zero and the amount determined by
    the formula

    ::: {#240958 .Paragraph}
    (A - B) × C

    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is the per capita equalization standard for that fiscal year,

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is the aggregate of the average annual per capita yield in that
        province for each revenue source for that fiscal year, and

    [C]{.dfn}
    :   is the average annual population of that province for that
        fiscal year, and
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

    ::: {#240964 .Paragraph}
    D × \[(E - F) × G\] / H

    where

    [D]{.dfn}
    :   is
        - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the fiscal year beginning on April 1,
          2006, the product obtained by multiplying \$10,900,000,000 by
          1.035, and

        - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each subsequent fiscal year, the product
          obtained by multiplying the amount computed for the
          immediately preceding fiscal year by 1.035,

    [E]{.dfn}
    :   is the five-province per capita equalization standard for that
        fiscal year determined under subsection (3),

    [F]{.dfn}
    :   is the aggregate of the average annual per capita yield in that
        province for each revenue source for that fiscal year,

    [G]{.dfn}
    :   is the average annual population of that province for that
        fiscal year, and

    [H]{.dfn}
    :   is the aggregate of the amount for each of the provinces
        determined by the formula
        ::: {#240973 .NestedFormula}
        (I - J) × K

        where

        [I]{.dfn}
        :   is the five-province per capita equalization standard for
            that fiscal year determined under subsection (3),

        [J]{.dfn}
        :   is the aggregate of the average annual per capita yield in
            the province for each revenue source for that fiscal year,
            and

        [K]{.dfn}
        :   is the average annual population of the province for that
            fiscal year.
        :::
    :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fiscal stabilization payment

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Act, the
Minister may pay to a province, for each fiscal year that begins after

computed in accordance with section 6.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Computation of payments

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (8) to (10), the fiscal stabilization payment that may
  be paid to a province for a fiscal year is the amount determined by
  the formula

  ::: {#241295 .Subsection}
  (0.95 × A) -- B + (C × D) -- (E × F)

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the non-natural resource revenue of the province for the
      preceding fiscal year;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the non-natural resource revenue of the province for the fiscal
      year;

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is equal to
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 95%, if the natural resource revenue of the
        province for the fiscal year is greater than 95% of the natural
        resource revenue of the province for the preceding fiscal year,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 50%, if the natural resource revenue of the
        province for the fiscal year is less than one half of the
        natural resource revenue of the province for the preceding
        fiscal year, or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} zero, in any other case;

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the natural resource revenue of the province for the preceding
      fiscal year;

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is equal to
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one, if the natural resource revenue of the
        province for the fiscal year is either greater than 95% of the
        natural resource revenue of the province for the preceding
        fiscal year or less than one half of the natural resource
        revenue of the province for the preceding fiscal year, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} zero, in any other case; and

  [F]{.dfn}
  :   is the natural resource revenue of the province for the fiscal
      year.
  :::

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2010, c. 25, s. 173\]]{.Repealed}

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Administration Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-241351 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administration agreement

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister or the Minister of National Revenue, with the approval of the
  Governor in Council, may on behalf of the Government of Canada enter
  into an administration agreement with the government of a province or
  an aboriginal government.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} An administration agreement referred to in
  paragraph (b) of the definition [[administration
  agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) can only be
  entered into if the government of the province that is to administer
  and enforce the First Nation law also administers and enforces Part IX
  of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) under an administration
  agreement referred to in paragraph (a) of that definition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amending agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister or the Minister of National Revenue,
  with the approval of the Governor in Council, may on behalf of the
  Government of Canada enter into an agreement amending the terms and
  conditions of an administration agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amending agreement --- exception to
  general rule

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply to an amendment made
  to an administration agreement if the agreement authorizes the
  Minister or the Minister of National Revenue to make the amendment and
  the amendment does not fundamentally alter the terms and conditions of
  the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confirmation of past amendments

  [(2.2)]{.lawlabel} Amendments that were made to an administration
  agreement before the day on which the Technical Tax Amendments Act,
  2012 received royal assent and that, if subsection (2.1) had been in
  force on the date those amendments were made, would have been
  authorized under that subsection are, for greater certainty, ratified
  and confirmed and all actions taken and payments made as a result of
  those amendments are ratified and confirmed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of provincial laws

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an Act of the legislature of a province in
  respect of which an administration agreement has been entered into and
  that imposes a tax contains provisions requiring every person making a
  payment of a specified kind to another person to deduct or withhold
  therefrom an amount, and to remit that amount on account of the tax,
  effect may be given to those provisions in relation to persons to whom
  those payments are made out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund or by an
  agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Depositing payments

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A government that is a party to an administration
  agreement in respect of an Act may, in accordance with the terms and
  conditions of the agreement and, if the Act is the Act of the
  legislature of a province, subject to the law of the province, deposit
  to an account under its control any form of payment, including a
  cheque or money order, received in the course of administering and
  enforcing the Act, that is payable to the government that is the other
  party to the administration agreement.

  sufficiently similar to a tax imposed under Part I.3 or VI of the

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incentive to eliminate capital taxes

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As an
  incentive for a province to eliminate capital taxes imposed by the
  province, the province is eligible to receive a payment under this
  Part if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before January 2, 2011, it eliminates a capital tax
    that is imposed under a law of the province that was in force on
    March 18, 2007; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any legislation that is required to give effect to
    the elimination is enacted after March 18, 2007 and before January
    2, 2011.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of elimination

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Part, a capital tax is
  considered to be eliminated if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} under the law of the province, the tax ceases
    before January 2, 2011 to be imposed on all corporations, except
    that the tax may continue to be imposed on any corporation that is
    exempt from tax under any of paragraphs 149(1)(d) to (d.4) of the
    [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) on all of its taxable income; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the tax is imposed only on
    financial institutions, the law under which the tax is imposed is
    amended, before January 2, 2011, to replace the tax with a new
    capital tax imposed only on financial institutions that meets the
    following criteria:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} no financial institution becomes subject to the
      new capital tax that was not subject to the replaced tax,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} every financial institution on which the new
      capital tax is imposed must be permitted to reduce the amount of
      the new capital tax payable by it for a taxation year by the
      amount of income tax payable by it to the province for the year
      and, if the amount of income tax so payable exceeds the amount of
      the new capital tax so payable, the financial institution must be
      permitted to apply the amount of the excess to reduce capital tax
      payable by it in other taxation years in a manner satisfactory to
      the Minister, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is satisfied that the total amount
      of revenue that would be raised by the new capital tax from
      financial institutions if there were no reduction for income tax
      payable is intended to be broadly commensurate with the total
      amount of revenue raised from those financial institutions by the
      province's income tax.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of payment

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  period fixed by the Minister, is equal to 17% of the estimated
  foregone revenue for that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preliminary payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A province is eligible to receive a preliminary

  preliminary determination of the estimated foregone revenue for that
  period. The Minister shall try to make a preliminary payment to the
  province on or before the last day of the period if the Minister
  receives the information in a timely manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final determination

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} After finalized information that is consistent with a
  province's public accounts becomes available so as to enable the
  Minister to make a final determination of the amount under
  subsection (1) in respect of a period, the Minister shall do so and
  reconcile the final determination with any preliminary payment paid to
  the province. If the amount of the final determination is greater than
  the preliminary payment, the Minister shall, without delay, pay the
  difference to the province. However, if the amount of the final
  determination is less than the preliminary payment, the difference may
  be deducted from any amount payable to the province under this Act or
  be recovered from the province as a debt due to Her Majesty in right
  of Canada.
