## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-364602 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[designated work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2019, c. 28, s.
    47\]]{.Repealed}

[[ferry cable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ferry cable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2012, c. 31, s.
    317\]]{.Repealed}

[[Indigenous knowledge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indigenous knowledge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Indigenous
    knowledge of the Indigenous peoples of Canada. ([connaissances
    autochtones]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Indigenous peoples of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indigenous peoples of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
    assigned by the definition [[aboriginal peoples of
    Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 35(2) of the
    [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/). ([peuples autochtones du
    Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[major work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[major work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any work designated under
    paragraph 28(2)(b). ([ouvrage majeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Transport. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[minor water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minor water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2019, c. 28, s.
    47\]]{.Repealed}

[[minor work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minor work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any work designated under
    paragraph 28(2)(a). ([ouvrage secondaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[navigable water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[navigable water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a body of water,
    including a canal or any other body of water created or altered as a
    result of the construction of any work, that is used or where there
    is a reasonable likelihood that it will be used by vessels, in full
    or in part, for any part of the year as a means of transport or
    travel for commercial or recreational purposes, or as a means of
    transport or travel for Indigenous peoples of Canada exercising
    rights recognized and affirmed by section 35 of the [Constitution
    Act, 1982](/eng/Const/), and

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is public access, by land or by water;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is no such public access but there are two
      or more riparian owners; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province is
      the only riparian owner. ([eaux navigables]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[obstruction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[obstruction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any thing, including a
    vessel that is left anchored, moored or adrift or a wreck, that
    obstructs or impedes navigation or renders it more difficult or
    dangerous, but does not include a thing of natural origin unless a
    person causes the thing of natural origin to obstruct or impede
    navigation or to render it more difficult or
    dangerous. ([obstacle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a work, means the
    actual or reputed owner of the work or that owner's agent or
    mandatary. It includes a person who is in possession or claiming
    ownership of the work and a person who is authorizing or otherwise
    responsible for the construction, placement, alteration, rebuilding,
    removal, decommissioning, repair, maintenance, operation, use or
    safety of the work. It also includes a person who proposes to
    construct or place a work. ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[person in charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person in charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to an
    obstruction, includes the owner of the obstruction and, in the case
    of a vessel, or part of one, that is an obstruction, the registered
    owner or other owner at the time the obstruction was occasioned, as
    well as the managing owner, master and any subsequent
    purchaser. ([responsable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Transportation Appeal
    Tribunal of Canada established by subsection 2(1) of the
    [Transportation Appeal Tribunal of Canada
    Act](/eng/acts/T-18.5). ([Tribunal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any description of ship,
    boat or floating craft designed, used or capable of being used for
    navigation, without regard to method or lack of propulsion,
    including everything forming part of its machinery, tackle,
    equipment, cargo, stores or ballast. ([bâtiment]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any structure, device or other thing, whether
      temporary or permanent, that is made by humans, including a
      structure, device or other thing used for the repair or
      maintenance of another work; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any dumping of fill in any navigable water, or
      any excavation or dredging of materials from the bed of any
      navigable water. ([ouvrage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Works

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Except in accordance with
this Act, it is prohibited to construct, place, alter, rebuild, remove
or decommission a work in, on, over, under, through or across any
navigable water.

### [Major Works in any Navigable Water and Works in Navigable Waters Listed in Schedule]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1198467 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

**[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** An owner who proposes
to construct, place, alter, rebuild, remove or decommission one of the
following works may do so if the work, or its construction, placement,
alteration, rebuilding, removal or decommissioning, would not interfere
with navigation and the owner, before beginning the construction,
placement, alteration, rebuilding, removal or decommissioning, deposits
any information specified by the Minister in any place specified by the
Minister and publishes a notice in any manner, and including any
information, specified by the Minister:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a major work in, on, over, under, through or across
  any navigable water; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a work --- other than a minor work --- in, on, over,
  under, through or across any navigable water that is listed in the
  schedule.

::: HistoricalNote
- [2019, c. 28, s. 49](#1157701-1198473){.wb-lbx

  role="button"}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for approval

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An owner
  who proposes to construct, place, alter, rebuild, remove or
  decommission one of the following works must make an application for
  an approval to the Minister --- in the form and manner, and containing
  the information, specified by the Minister --- if the work, or its
  construction, placement, alteration, rebuilding, removal or
  decommissioning, may interfere with navigation:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a major work in, on, over, under, through or across
    any navigable water; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a work --- other than a minor work --- in, on,
    over, under, through or across any navigable water that is listed in
    the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Related works

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is of the opinion that two or more
  works are related, the Minister may consider them to be a single work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of determining whether the work or
  its construction, placement, alteration, rebuilding, removal or
  decommissioning may interfere with navigation, the Minister may
  require from the owner any additional information that the Minister
  considers appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interference with navigation

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister is of the opinion that a work that is the subject of an
  application made under subsection 5(1), or its construction,
  placement, alteration, rebuilding, removal or decommissioning, may
  interfere with navigation, including by changing the water level or
  water flow of a navigable water, he or she must inform the owner, in
  writing, of that opinion and the owner may only construct, place,
  alter, rebuild, remove or decommission that work if the Minister
  issues an approval for the work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner must deposit any information specified by
  the Minister in any place specified by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The owner must publish a notice containing any
  information specified by the Minister in any manner specified by the
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Comment period

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The notice referred to in subsection (3) must invite
  interested persons to provide written comments on the owner's proposal
  to the Minister within 30 days after publication of the notice or
  within any other period specified by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is satisfied that the owner has
  already deposited sufficient information in a place specified by the
  Minister or published a sufficient notice, the Minister may exempt the
  owner from the application of subsection (2) or (3), as the case may
  be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may issue an approval for the work,
  including its site and plans, if he or she considers it appropriate in
  the circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment --- factors

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In determining whether to issue the approval, the
  Minister must consider the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the characteristics of the navigable water in
    question;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the safety of navigation in that navigable water;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the current or anticipated navigation in that
    navigable water;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the impact of the work on navigation, including as
    a result of its construction, placement, alteration, rebuilding,
    removal, decommissioning, repair, maintenance, operation or use;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the impact of the work, in combination with other
    works, on navigation, if the Minister is provided with, or has in
    his or her possession, information relating to that cumulative
    impact;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any Indigenous knowledge that has been provided to
    the Minister;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any comments that he or she receives from
    interested persons within the period provided for under subsection
    (4);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the record of compliance of the owner under this
    Act; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any other information or factor that he or she
    considers relevant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of determining whether to issue the
  approval, the Minister may require from the owner any additional
  information that the Minister considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms and conditions

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may attach any term or condition that he
  or she considers appropriate to an approval including one that
  requires the owner to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain the water level or water flow necessary
    for navigation purposes in a navigable water; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give security in the form of a letter of credit,
    guarantee, suretyship or indemnity bond or insurance or in any other
    form that is satisfactory to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of approval

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} An approval issued under this section in relation to
  a work replaces any approval previously issued in relation to that
  work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contiguous area

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, in an approval, designate an area
  contiguous to a work that is necessary for the safety of persons and
  navigation and, for the purposes of the approval, that area is
  considered to be part of the work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with requirements

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} The owner must comply with the approval and
  maintain, operate and use the work in accordance with the requirements
  under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval after activity begins

  [(13)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, if he or she considers that it is
  justified in the circumstances, approve the construction, placement,
  alteration, rebuilding, removal or decommissioning of a work after the
  construction, placement, alteration, rebuilding, removal or
  decommissioning begins or is completed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of approval

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may amend an approval by amending or revoking any term or
  condition of the approval.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment resulting from order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may otherwise amend the approval,
  including by adding terms and conditions, to reflect any changes to
  the work resulting from an order made under section 11 or 13.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other amendments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may otherwise amend the approval,
  including by adding terms and conditions, if he or she considers that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the work that is the subject of the approval
    interferes more with navigation at the time in question than it did
    when the approval was issued;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the work causes or is likely to cause a serious and
    imminent danger to navigation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amendment of the approval is in the public
    interest; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the owner consents to the amendment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension or cancellation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may suspend or cancel an approval if he
  or she considers that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the owner has not complied with the approval;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the approval was obtained by a fraudulent or
    improper means or by the misrepresentation of a material fact;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the owner has not paid a fine or penalty imposed
    under this Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the owner has contravened this Act; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the suspension or cancellation is in the public
    interest, including by reason of the record of compliance of the
    owner under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must give the owner 30 days' notice
  setting out the grounds on which the Minister relies for the
  amendment, except in the case of an amendment made under
  subsection (2) or paragraph (3)(b), suspension or cancellation of the
  approval of a work if he or she is amending it without the owner's
  consent or suspending or cancelling it.

### [Works in Navigable Waters not Listed in Schedule]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1198553 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

**[[9.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.1 .sectionLabel}** An owner who proposes
to construct, place, alter, rebuild, remove or decommission a work ---
other than a major work or a minor work --- in, on, over, under, through
or across any navigable water that is not listed in the schedule may do
so if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the work, or its construction, placement, alteration,
  rebuilding, removal or decommissioning, would not interfere with
  navigation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} before beginning the construction, placement,
  alteration, rebuilding, removal or decommissioning, the owner deposits
  any information specified by the Minister in any place specified by
  the Minister and publishes a notice in any manner, and including any
  information, specified by the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- [2019, c. 28, s. 49](#1157701-1198560){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1157701-1198560.html"

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application or notice

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  owner who proposes to construct, place, alter, rebuild, remove or
  decommission a work --- other than a major work or a minor work ---
  in, on, over, under, through or across any navigable water that is not
  listed in the schedule must take one of the following steps if the
  work, or its construction, placement, alteration, rebuilding, removal
  or decommissioning, may interfere with navigation:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make an application for an approval to the
    Minister, in the form and manner, and containing the information,

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  believes that immediate action is required to deal with a significant

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of effect

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An interim order has effect from the time that it is
  made but ceases to have effect on the earliest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 14 days after it is made, unless it is approved by
    the Governor in Council,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which it is repealed,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which a regulation made under this Act,
    that has the same effect as the interim order, comes into force, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} one year after the interim order is made or any
    shorter period that may be specified in the interim order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of unpublished order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall be convicted of an offence consisting
  of a contravention of an interim order that, at the time of the
  alleged contravention, had not been published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) unless it is proved that, at the
  time of the alleged contravention, the person had been notified of the
  interim order or reasonable steps had been taken to bring the purport
  of the interim order to the notice of those persons likely to be
  affected by it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An interim order

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is exempt from the application of sections 3, 5 and
    11 of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall be published in the [Canada
    Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) within 23 days after it is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of any provision of this Act other

  deemed to include interim orders, and any reference to a regulation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The designated person may, for the purposes referred

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} remove any document or other thing from the place

    output and remove the printout or other output for examination or
    copying;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any copying equipment at
    the place to make copies of any books, records, electronic data or
    other documents; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} order any person in the place to establish their

#### [Proceedings]{.HTitleText3} {#h-365037 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of violation

- **[[39.11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39.11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A designated person may issue a
  notice of violation and cause it to be provided to a person if the
  designated person has reasonable grounds to believe that the person
  has committed a violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of violation shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name the person believed to have committed the
    violation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identify the acts or omissions that constitute the
    alleged violation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set out the penalty for the violation that the
    person is liable to pay;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} set out the particulars concerning the time and
    manner of payment; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} set out a lesser amount that may be paid as
    complete satisfaction of the penalty if it is paid within the
    prescribed time and in the prescribed manner that are specified in
    the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summary of rights

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice of violation shall summarize, in plain
  language, the rights and obligations under this section and sections
  39.12 to 39.23 of the person to whom it is provided, including the
  right to request a review of the acts or omissions that constitute the
  alleged violation or of the amount of the penalty and the procedure
  for requesting the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short-form descriptions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may establish a short-form description
  of each violation to be used in notices of violation.

#### [Penalties]{.HTitleText3} {#h-365054 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of payment

- **[[39.12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39.12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the person who is named in a
  notice of violation pays, within the prescribed time and in the
  prescribed manner that are specified in the notice, the amount of the
  penalty --- or, if applicable, the lesser amount that may be paid as
  complete satisfaction of the penalty --- set out in the notice,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is deemed to have committed the
    violation to which the amount paid relates;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall accept the amount paid as
    complete satisfaction of the penalty; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proceedings commenced in respect of the
    violation under section 39.11 are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternatives to payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Instead of paying the penalty or, if applicable, the
  lesser amount that may be paid as complete satisfaction of the
  penalty, the person who is named in the notice may, within the
  prescribed time and in the prescribed manner that are specified in the
  notice, request a review by the Tribunal of the acts or omissions that
  constitute the alleged violation or of the amount of the penalty, as
  the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the person who is named in the notice does not pay
  the penalty or, if applicable, the lesser amount that may be paid as
  complete satisfaction of the penalty, within the prescribed time and
  in the prescribed manner and does not exercise the right referred to
  in subsection (2) within the prescribed time and in the prescribed
  manner, the person is deemed to have committed the violation
  identified in the notice.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2012, c. 31, s. 328
:::

#### [Review by Tribunal]{.HTitleText3} {#h-365067 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review --- violation

- **[[39.13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39.13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After completing a review requested
  under subsection 39.12(2) with respect to the acts or omissions that
  constitute the alleged violation identified in the notice of
  violation, the Tribunal shall determine whether the person who is
  named in the notice committed the violation and, if the Tribunal
  determines that the person did so but considers that the amount of the
  penalty for the violation was not established in accordance with the
  regulations, the Tribunal shall correct that amount and cause the
  person to be provided with a notice of the Tribunal's decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review --- penalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} After completing a review requested under subsection
  39.12(2) with respect to the amount of the penalty set out in the
  notice of violation, the Tribunal shall determine whether the amount
  of the penalty was established in accordance with the regulations and,
  if the Tribunal determines that it was not, the Tribunal shall correct
  that amount and cause the person to be provided with a notice of the
  Tribunal's decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person who is provided with a notice of the
  Tribunal's decision is liable to pay the amount of the penalty that is
  set out in it within the prescribed time and in the prescribed manner
  that are specified in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of payment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the person pays the amount of the penalty that is
  set out in the notice of the Tribunal's decision within the prescribed
  time and in the prescribed manner that are specified in the notice,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Tribunal shall accept the amount as complete
    satisfaction of the penalty; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proceedings commenced in respect of the
    violation under section 39.11 are ended.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2012, c. 31, s. 328
:::

### [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-365150 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person is guilty of an offence, if the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contravenes section 3, subsection 4(1) or (2), 5(1)
    or 7(12), section 8, subsection 10(1), 10.1(2), 10.2(1) or (2),
    10.3(1) or 15(1), section 21, 22, 23 or 35 or subsection 37(1) or
    (2);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contravenes an order given under paragraph
    11(2)(a), (b) or (d), subsection 13(1) or (2), 15(3), 15.1(1) or
    16(1) or paragraph 25.1(1)(a), (b), (d) or (e);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not take the measures required under
    subsection 10.3(2) or paragraph 15(2)(a) or (b);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2019, c. 28, s. 72\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2019, c. 28, s. 72\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} contravenes a regulation or order made under
    section 28; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} contravenes an interim order made under section 32.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalties

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is guilty of an offence under
  subsection (1) is liable, on summary conviction,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine of not more than
      \$100,000, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a second or subsequent offence, to a fine of
      not more than \$200,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more
      than six months, or to both; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine of not more than
      \$500,000, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a second or subsequent offence, to a fine of
      not more than \$1,000,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vessel liable for fine

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If any materials referred to in section 22 are thrown
  from or deposited by a vessel and a conviction is obtained for that
  activity, the vessel is liable for any fine that is imposed and may be
  detained by any port warden or the chief officer of customs at any
  port until the fine is paid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the offence under subsection (1) is committed or
  continued on more than one day, the person who commits it is liable to
  be convicted for a separate offence for each day on which it is
  committed or continued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers, etc., of corporations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a corporation commits an offence under this Act,
  any officer, director or agent or mandatary of the corporation who
  directed, authorized, assented to, acquiesced in or participated in
  the commission of the offence is a party to, and guilty of, the
  offence and liable on conviction to the punishment provided for the
  offence, whether or not the corporation has been prosecuted or
  convicted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to ensure compliance

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every director and officer of a corporation shall
  take all reasonable care to ensure that the corporation complies with
  this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vicarious liability --- acts of
  employees, agents and mandataries

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A person is liable for an offence that is committed
  by that person's employee or agent or mandatary who is acting in the
  course of the employee's employment or within the scope of the agent
  or mandatary's authority, whether or not the employee or agent or
  mandatary who actually committed the offence is identified or
  proceeded against in accordance with this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Due diligence defence

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A person is not to be found guilty of an offence
  under subsection (1), other than for a contravention of subsection
  37(1) or (2), if they establish that they exercised due diligence to
  prevent the commission of the offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders of court

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** If a person is convicted
of an offence under this Act, in addition to any punishment imposed, the
court may, having regard to the nature of the offence and the
circumstances surrounding its commission, make an order imposing any one
or more of the following prohibitions, directions or requirements on the
person:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a prohibition from doing any act or engaging in any
  activity that may, in the opinion of the court, result in the
  continuation or repetition of the offence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a direction to publish, in any manner the court
  considers appropriate, the facts relating to the commission of the
  offence;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a direction to pay the Minister an amount of money as
  compensation, in whole or in part, for the cost of any remedial or
  preventive action taken by or caused to be taken on behalf of the
  Minister as a result of the commission of the offence;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a direction to perform community service in
  accordance with any reasonable conditions that may be specified in the
  order;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a direction to post a bond or provide surety or pay
  into court an amount of money the court considers appropriate for the
  purpose of ensuring compliance with any prohibition, direction or
  requirement mentioned in this section;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a direction to submit to the Minister, on application
  by the Minister within three years after the date of the conviction,
  any information respecting the activities of the person that the court
  considers appropriate in the circumstances; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a requirement to comply with any other conditions
  that the court considers appropriate for securing the person's good
  conduct and for preventing the person from repeating the offence or
  committing other offences under this Act.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2012, c. 31, s. 332

  - ###### Approvals {#approvals .MarginalNote}

    - [332]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every work in respect of which
      an approval was granted under the Navigable Waters Protection Act
      as it read immediately before the coming into force of this
      Division is deemed to be approved under section 6 of the
      [Navigation Protection Act](/eng/acts/N-22), except if it is
      stated in the approval that the work interferes, other than
      substantially, with navigation, in which case the work is deemed
      to be validly constructed or placed in accordance with section 9
      of the [Navigation Protection Act](/eng/acts/N-22).

    - ###### Terms and conditions {#terms-and-conditions .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any term or condition imposed on an approval
      granted under the Navigable Waters Protection Act as it read
      immediately before the coming into force of this Division remains
      in effect.

    - ###### Designated works {#designated-works .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every work, the construction or placement of
      which was permitted under section 5.1 of the Navigable Waters
      Protection Act as it read immediately before the coming into force
      of this Division, is deemed to be validly constructed or placed in
      accordance with section 10 of the [Navigation Protection
      Act](/eng/acts/N-22).

    - ###### Lawful works {#lawful-works .MarginalNote}

      [(4)]{.lawlabel} A work referred to in subsection 4(1) or (2) or
      section 8 of the Navigable Waters Protection Act as it read
      immediately before the coming into force of this Division remains
      validly constructed or placed under the [Navigation Protection
      Act](/eng/acts/N-22).

    - ###### Works --- navigable waters {#works-navigable-waters .MarginalNote}

      [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every work referred to in subsection (1), (3)
      or (4) that is constructed or placed in, on, over, under, through
      or across any navigable water other than any navigable water
      listed in the schedule to the [Navigation Protection
      Act](/eng/acts/N-22) is deemed to be constructed or placed in, on,
      over, under, through or across a navigable water listed in the
      schedule.

    - ###### Opt out {#opt-out .MarginalNote}

      [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (5) does not apply to a work on
      receipt by the Minister of Transport of a notice to that effect.
      The notice shall be given by the owner, as defined in section 2 of
      the [Navigation Protection Act](/eng/acts/N-22), no later than
      five years after the coming into force of this Division.

- --- 2012, c. 31, s. 333

  - ###### Request for approval not decided {#request-for-approval-not-decided .MarginalNote}

    [333]{.lawlabel} Every request for approval of a work submitted
    under the Navigable Waters Protection Act as it read immediately
    before the coming into force of this Division that has not been
    decided before that coming into force is deemed to be a notice
    referred to in subsection 5(1) of the [Navigation Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/N-22).

- --- 2012, c. 31, s. 334

  - ###### Minor Works and Waters (Navigable Waters Protection Act) Order {#minor-works-and-waters-navigable-waters-protection-act-order .MarginalNote}

    - [334]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} The works established as classes
      of works in the Minor Works and Waters (Navigable Waters
      Protection Act) Order are deemed to be minor works designated
      under paragraph 28(2)(a) of the [Navigation Protection
      Act](/eng/acts/N-22) until an order is made under that paragraph.

    - ###### Terms and conditions {#terms-and-conditions-1 .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every term and condition imposed under paragraph
      13(1)(b) of the Navigable Waters Protection Act as it read
      immediately before the coming into force of this Division with
      respect to the minor works referred to in subsection (1) is deemed
      to be made under paragraph 28(2)(c) of the [Navigation Protection
      Act](/eng/acts/N-22) until an order is made under that paragraph.

    - ###### Minor Works and Waters (Navigable Waters Protection Act) Order {#minor-works-and-waters-navigable-waters-protection-act-order-1 .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} The navigable waters established as classes of
      navigable waters in the Minor Works and Waters (Navigable Waters
      Protection Act) Order are deemed to be minor waters designated
      under paragraph 28(2)(b) of the [Navigation Protection
      Act](/eng/acts/N-22) until an order is made under that paragraph.

    - ###### Terms and conditions {#terms-and-conditions-2 .MarginalNote}

      [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every term and condition imposed under paragraph
      13(1)(b) of the Navigable Waters Protection Act as it read
      immediately before the coming into force of this Division with
      respect to the minor waters referred to in subsection (3) is
      deemed to be made under paragraph 28(2)(d) of the [Navigation
      Protection Act](/eng/acts/N-22) until an order is made under that
      paragraph.

- --- 2019, c. 1, s. 132

  - ###### Section 20 of [Navigation Protection Act](/eng/acts/N-22) {#section-20-of-navigation-protection-act .MarginalNote}

    [132]{.lawlabel} A notice given under section 20 of the [Navigation
    Protection Act](/eng/acts/N-22) before the day on which section 138
    comes into force is deemed to have been given under subsection
    38(2).

- --- 2019, c. 28, s. 76

  - ###### Deemed approval {#deemed-approval .MarginalNote}

    - [76]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} A work is deemed to have been
      approved under section 7 of the [Canadian Navigable Waters
      Act](/eng/acts/N-22), as enacted by section 49 of this Act, if it
      was or was deemed to be

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} approved under section 6 of the [Navigation
        Protection Act](/eng/acts/N-22), as it read immediately before
        the coming into force of section 46 of this Act; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} permitted under section 9 of the [Navigation
        Protection Act](/eng/acts/N-22), as it read immediately before
        the coming into force of section 46 of this Act.

    - ###### Terms and conditions {#terms-and-conditions-3 .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any term or condition attached to an approval
      under section 6 of the [Navigation Protection Act](/eng/acts/N-22)
      as it read immediately before the coming into force of section 46
      of this Act --- or imposed under section 9 of that Act as it read
      at that time --- remains in effect.

    - ###### Validity period of approval {#validity-period-of-approval .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 332(2) of the Jobs and Growth
      Act, 2012, a term or condition relating to a validity period that
      was imposed on an approval granted under the Navigable Waters
      Protection Act before April 1, 2014 is of no force or effect if
      the validity period in question had not expired before that date.

- --- 2019, c. 28, s. 77

  - ###### Minor works {#minor-works .MarginalNote}

    - [77]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} A work is deemed to be validly
      constructed or placed in accordance with section 4 of the
      [Canadian Navigable Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-22), as enacted by
      section 49 of this Act, if it was, or was deemed to be, validly
      constructed or placed in accordance with section 10 of the
      [Navigation Protection Act](/eng/acts/N-22), as it read
      immediately before the coming into force of section 46 of this
      Act.

    - ###### Deemed designation {#deemed-designation .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any work designated or deemed to have been
      designated as a minor work under paragraph 28(2)(a) of the
      [Navigation Protection Act](/eng/acts/N-22), as it read
      immediately before the coming into force of section 46 of this
      Act, is deemed to be designated as a minor work under paragraph
      28(2)(a) of the [Canadian Navigable Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-22)
      until an order is made under that paragraph.

    - ###### Terms and conditions {#terms-and-conditions-4 .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every term and condition imposed, or deemed to
      have been imposed, under paragraph 28(2)(c) of the [Navigation
      Protection Act](/eng/acts/N-22), as it read immediately before the
      coming into force of section 46 of this Act, with respect to the
      minor works referred to in subsection (1), is deemed to be imposed
      under paragraph 28(2)(c) of the [Canadian Navigable Waters
      Act](/eng/acts/N-22) until an order is made under that paragraph.

- --- 2019, c. 28, s. 78

  - ###### Navigable waters not listed in the schedule {#navigable-waters-not-listed-in-the-schedule .MarginalNote}

    [78]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the [Canadian Navigable Waters
    Act](/eng/acts/N-22), any work that is referred to in subsection
    332(3) or (4) of the Jobs and Growth Act, 2012 and that was deemed
    to be constructed or placed in, on, over, under, through or across a
    navigable water listed in the schedule to the [Navigation Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/N-22) in accordance with subsection 332(5) of the
    Jobs and Growth Act, 2012 is not constructed or placed in, on, over,
    under, through or across a navigable water listed in the schedule to
    the [Canadian Navigable Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-22).

  - ###### Validly constructed or placed {#validly-constructed-or-placed .MarginalNote}

    placed under the [Canadian Navigable Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-22).
