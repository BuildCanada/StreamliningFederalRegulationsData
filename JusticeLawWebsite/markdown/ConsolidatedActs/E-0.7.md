### [DIVISION 7]{.HLabel2}[Dominion Coal Blocks]{.HTitleText2} {#h-174905 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[239]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-239 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in this Division.

[[Crow's Nest Pass Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Crow's Nest Pass Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means An Act to
    authorize a Subsidy for a Railway through the Crow's Nest Pass,
    chapter 5 of the Statutes of Canada 1897. ([Loi du
    Nid-de-Corbeau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Dominion Coal Blocks]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Dominion Coal Blocks]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the selected land
    referred to in paragraph 1(i.) of the Crow's Nest Pass Act, being

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Parcel Identifier 014-832-020, Parcel 73, shown
      on Plan DD 729 (F25(2)), District Lot 4589, Kootenay District,
      British Columbia; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Parcel Identifier 014-832-038, Parcel 82, shown
      on Plan DD 729 (F25(1)), District Lot 4589, Kootenay District,
      British Columbia, except Part included in Plan 6844. ([Réserve
      fédérale de charbon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister designated under
    section 240, or, if none is designated, the Minister of Natural
    Resources. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of Minister

**[[240]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-240 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate any federal minister to be the Minister
referred to in this Division.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dominion Coal Blocks

**[[241]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-241 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in the Crow's
Nest Pass Act, in the agreement mentioned in that Act or in any covenant
in the instrument conveying the Dominion Coal Blocks to His Majesty in
right of Canada operates so as to limit the power of Her Majesty in
right of Canada to hold, dispose of or otherwise deal with the Dominion
Coal Blocks, or any part of the Dominion Coal Blocks or any interest in
them, in any manner and on any conditions that Her Majesty in right of
Canada considers appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister

- **[[242]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-242 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} With
  the approval of the Governor in Council, and subject to any terms that
  the Governor in Council considers appropriate, the Minister may
  dispose of the Dominion Coal Blocks, or any part of the Dominion Coal
  Blocks or any interest in them, in any manner and on any conditions
  established under section 241.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- easements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Minister may grant an
  easement over any part of the Dominion Coal Blocks without the
  approval of the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may do anything that he or she considers
  necessary to prepare the Dominion Coal Blocks, or any part of the
  Dominion Coal Blocks or any interest in them, for disposition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administration

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister has the administration of the Dominion
  Coal Blocks.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of provincial law

**[[243]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-243 .sectionLabel}** A disposition under
section 241 or subsection 242(1) or (2) may be effected by any
instrument by which an interest in real property may be disposed of by a
private person under the laws in force in British Columbia.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Her Majesty's obligations are terminated

**[[244]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-244 .sectionLabel}** All obligations and
liabilities of Her Majesty in right of Canada arising out of any of the
matters referred to in paragraph 1(i.) of the Crow's Nest Pass Act, and
all rights acquired by any other party under any covenant in the
instrument conveying the Dominion Coal Blocks to His Majesty in right of
Canada relating to those matters, are extinguished.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No liability

**[[245]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-245 .sectionLabel}** No action or other
proceeding, including any action or proceeding in restitution, or for
damages of any kind, that is based on or is in relation to any agreement
in respect of the Dominion Coal Blocks that existed on or before the
coming into force of this section lies or may be instituted by anyone
against Her Majesty in right of Canada or any minister or any employee
or agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada, or any person engaged to
provide advice or services to Her Majesty in right of Canada in relation
to such an agreement, for anything done or omitted to be done or for
anything purported to have been done or omitted to be done, in the
exercise or performance of their powers, duties and functions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No compensation

**[[246]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-246 .sectionLabel}** No one is entitled to
any compensation from Her Majesty in right of Canada in connection with
the coming into force of section 244.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of money from disposition

**[[247]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-247 .sectionLabel}** The money from a sale
or other disposition made under section 241 or subsection 242(1) or (2)
minus an amount equal to the amount paid or payable by Her Majesty in
right of Canada, or any agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada, under
an agreement relating to the disposition, including the management of
that disposition, is public money for the purposes of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Federal Real Property and Federal
Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4)

**[[248]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-248 .sectionLabel}** The [Federal Real
Property and Federal Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4) does not apply to
any disposition of the Dominion Coal Blocks.
