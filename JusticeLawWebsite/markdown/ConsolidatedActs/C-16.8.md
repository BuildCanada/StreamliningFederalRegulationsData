## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-68175 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[aggravated by service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[aggravated by service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
      injury or a disease, means an injury or a disease that has been
      aggravated, if the aggravation

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} was attributable to or was incurred during
        special duty service; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} arose out of or was directly connected with
        service in the Canadian Forces. ([due au
        service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Veterans Review and Appeal
      Board established by section 4 of the [Veterans Review and Appeal
      Board Act](/eng/acts/V-1.6). ([Tribunal]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian Forces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian Forces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the armed forces
      referred to in section 14 of the [National Defence
      Act](/eng/acts/N-5), and includes any predecessor naval, army or
      air forces of Canada or Newfoundland. ([Forces
      canadiennes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a
      member or a veteran, means a person who is cohabiting with the
      member or veteran in a conjugal relationship, having so cohabited
      for a period of at least one year. ([conjoint de
      fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[compensation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[compensation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following
      benefits under this Act, namely, an education and training
      benefit, an education and training completion bonus, an income
      replacement benefit, a Canadian Forces income support benefit, a
      critical injury benefit, a disability award, pain and suffering
      compensation, additional pain and suffering compensation, a death
      benefit, a clothing allowance, a detention benefit or a caregiver
      recognition benefit. ([indemnisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[dependent child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[dependent child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a member
      or a veteran, means their child, or a child of their spouse or
      common-law partner who is ordinarily residing in the member's or
      veteran's household, who is

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} under the age of 18 years;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} under the age of 25 years and following a
        course of instruction approved by the Minister; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} over the age of 18 years and prevented by
        physical or mental incapacity from earning a livelihood, if the
        incapacity occurred

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} before the child attained the age of 18
          years, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} after the age of 18 years and before the age
          of 25 years while the child was following a course of
          instruction approved by the Minister. ([enfant à
          charge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the loss or lessening of
      the power to will and to do any normal mental or physical
      act. ([invalidité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[disability award]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[disability award]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a disability award
      paid under section 45, 47 or 48 of this Act as it read immediately
      before April 1, 2019. ([indemnité d'invalidité]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[medical assistance in dying]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[medical assistance in dying]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in section 241.1 of the [Criminal
      Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([aide médicale à mourir]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[medical rehabilitation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[medical rehabilitation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any
      physical or psychological treatment whose object is to stabilize
      and restore the basic physical and psychological functions of a
      person. ([réadaptation médicale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an officer or a
      non-commissioned member of the Canadian Forces, as those terms are
      defined in subsection 2(1) of the [National Defence
      Act](/eng/acts/N-5). ([militaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Veterans
      Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[orphan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[orphan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a deceased member
      or a deceased veteran, means their child, or a child of their
      survivor who at the time of the member's or veteran's death was
      ordinarily residing in the member's or veteran's household, who is

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} under the age of 18 years;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} under the age of 25 years and following a
        course of instruction approved by the Minister; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} over the age of 18 years and prevented by
        physical or mental incapacity from earning a livelihood, if the
        incapacity occurred

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} before the child attained the age of 18
          years, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} after the age of 18 years and before the age
          of 25 years while the child was following a course of
          instruction approved by the
          Minister. ([orphelin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[personal information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[personal information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
      as in section 3 of the [Privacy
      Act](/eng/acts/P-21). ([renseignements
      personnels]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by regulation.

  [[psycho-social rehabilitation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[psycho-social rehabilitation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any
      psychological or social intervention whose object is to restore a
      person to a state of independent functioning and to facilitate
      their social adjustment. ([réadaptation
      psychosociale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[rehabilitation services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[rehabilitation services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all services
      related to the medical rehabilitation, psycho-social
      rehabilitation or vocational rehabilitation of a
      person. ([services de réadaptation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[service-related injury or disease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[service-related injury or disease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      injury or a disease that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} was attributable to or was incurred during
        special duty service; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} arose out of or was directly connected with
        service in the Canadian Forces. ([liée au
        service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[special duty service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[special duty service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means service as a
      member in a special duty area designated under section 69, or as a
      member as part of a special duty operation designated under
      section 70, during the period in which the designation is in
      effect. It includes any of the following that occurred during that
      period but not earlier than September 11, 2001:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} periods of training for the express purpose of
        service in that area or as part of that operation, wherever that
        training takes place;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} travel to and from the area, the operation or
        the location of training referred to in paragraph (a); and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} authorized leave of absence with pay during
        that service, wherever that leave is taken. ([service
        spécial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a deceased member
      or a deceased veteran, means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their spouse who was, at the time of the
        member's or veteran's death, residing with the member or
        veteran; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person who was, at the time of the member's
        or veteran's death, the member's or veteran's common-law
        partner. ([survivant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a former
      member. ([vétéran]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[vocational assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vocational assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes
      employability assessments, career counselling, training,
      job-search assistance and job-finding assistance, whose object is
      to help a person to find appropriate employment. ([assistance
      professionnelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[vocational rehabilitation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vocational rehabilitation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any
      process designed to identify and achieve an appropriate
      occupational goal for a person with a physical or a mental health
      problem, given their state of health and the extent of their
      education, skills and experience. ([réadaptation
      professionnelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Couples who are living apart

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, a spouse is deemed to
  be residing with a member or a veteran, and a person does not cease to
  be a member's or a veteran's common-law partner, if it is established
  that they are living apart by reason only of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one or both of them having to reside in a health
    care facility;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} circumstances of a temporary nature; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} other circumstances not within the control of the
    member or veteran or the spouse or common-law partner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References to spouses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A reference in this Act to a member's or a veteran's
  spouse is a reference to a member's or a veteran's spouse who is
  residing with the member or veteran.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recent marriage

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} This Act does not apply to a member's or a veteran's
  surviving spouse if the member or veteran dies within one year after
  the date of the marriage, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the opinion of the Minister, the member or
    veteran was at the time of that marriage in such a condition of
    health as to justify their having an expectation of life of at least
    one year; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the time of the member's or veteran's death, the
    spouse was cohabiting with the member or veteran in a conjugal
    relationship, having so cohabited for a period of at least one year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct of member or veteran

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} This Act does not apply in respect of a member's or a
  veteran's physical or mental health problem, disability or death if it
  is caused by a wilful self-inflicted injury or improper conduct on the
  member's or veteran's part, including wilful disobedience of an order
  and vicious or criminal conduct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- medical assistance
  in dying

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, a member or veteran has
  neither inflicted wilful self-injury nor engaged in improper conduct
  by reason only that they receive medical assistance in dying, if the
  requirement set out in paragraph 241.2(3)(a) of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46) has been met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming --- medical assistance in dying

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, if a member or a
  veteran receives medical assistance in dying, that member or veteran
  is deemed to have died as a result of the illness, disease or
  disability for which they were determined to be eligible to receive
  that assistance, in accordance with paragraph 241.2(3)(a) of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-68239 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act
is to recognize and fulfil the obligation of the people and Government
of Canada to show just and due appreciation to members and veterans for
their service to Canada. This obligation includes providing services,
assistance and compensation to members and veterans who have been
injured or have died as a result of military service and extends to
their spouses or common-law partners or survivors and orphans. This Act
shall be liberally interpreted so that the recognized obligation may be
fulfilled.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2015, c. 36, s. 207
:::

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Career Transition Services]{.HTitleText1} {#h-68244 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this section, the Minister may, on application, provide career
  transition services to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member who has completed basic training;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a veteran who completed basic training and who was
    released from the Canadian Forces on or after April 1, 2006;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a veteran who is entitled to a Canadian Forces
    income support benefit;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a spouse or common-law partner of a veteran who
    completed basic training and who was released from the Canadian
    Forces on or after April 1, 2006;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a survivor of a member who completed basic training
    and who died on or after April 1, 2006;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a survivor of a veteran who completed basic
    training and who was released from the Canadian Forces on or after
    April 1, 2006; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a survivor who is entitled to a Canadian Forces
    income support benefit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- member

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Career transition services may be provided to a
  member only if the member resides in Canada and the Minister is
  satisfied that they require assistance in making the transition to the
  civilian labour force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- veteran

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Career transition services may be provided to a
  veteran only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the veteran resides in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is satisfied that the veteran requires
    assistance in making the transition to the civilian labour force;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the veteran is not receiving rehabilitation
    services under Part 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- spouse, common-law
  partner or survivor

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Career transition services may be provided to a
  spouse, common-law partner or survivor only if they reside in Canada
  and are not receiving rehabilitation services or vocational assistance
  under Part 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period --- spouse or common-law partner
  of veteran

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A spouse or common-law partner of a veteran who
  completed basic training and who was released from the Canadian Forces
  on or after April 1, 2006 may receive career transition services until
  the later of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} March 31, 2020, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the second anniversary of the day on which the
    veteran was released.

## [PART 1.1]{.HLabel1}[Education and Training Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-68289 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[5.11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.11 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in this Part.

[[regular force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regular force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5). ([force régulière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reserve force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reserve force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5). ([force de réserve]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Supplementary Reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Supplementary Reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
    assigned by article 2.034 of the Queen's Regulations and Orders for
    the Canadian Forces. ([Réserve supplémentaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a former member or a member of
    the Supplementary Reserve. ([vétéran]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility --- veterans

- **[[5.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, on application, pay an education and training benefit to
  a veteran in accordance with section 5.3 or 5.5 if the veteran

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} served for a total of at least six years in the
    regular force, in the reserve force or in both; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} was honourably released from the Canadian Forces on
    or after April 1, 2006 or was transferred from the regular force or
    another subcomponent of the reserve force to the Supplementary
    Reserve on or after that date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum cumulative amount

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The maximum cumulative amount that the Minister may
  pay to a veteran is \$40,000 or, if the veteran served for a total of
  at least 12 years in the regular force, in the reserve force or in
  both, \$80,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Course of study at educational
institution

- **[[5.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  education and training benefit may be paid to a veteran entitled to a
  benefit under this Part in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} education or training received from an educational
    institution as part of a course of study leading to the completion
    of a degree, diploma, certification or designation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any expenses, including living expenses, that may
    be incurred by the veteran while enrolled at the institution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A veteran requesting payment in respect of education
  or training described in paragraph (1)(a) shall provide the Minister
  with proof of acceptance, enrolment or registration at the institution
  for an upcoming period of study and with any prescribed information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may request that the veteran provide the
  Minister with additional information for the purpose of making the
  determination under subsection (4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's determination

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} On being provided with the proof and information, the
  Minister shall, if he or she is satisfied that the requested payment
  may be made to the veteran, determine

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the payment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the period of study to which that amount is
    allocated; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the payment is to be made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment day

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The day on which the payment is to be made must be no
  earlier than the 60th day before

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which fees for the education or training
    are due to be paid to the institution in respect of the period of
    study; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the period of study begins, if the
    institution fixes no day on which the fees are due.

::: HistoricalNote

**[[5.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.4 .sectionLabel}** On application, the
Minister may pay, over and above an education and training benefit, an
education and training completion bonus in the prescribed amount to a
veteran who receives a degree, diploma, certification or designation in
respect of which they received a payment of an education and training
benefit under section 5.3.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2017, c. 20, s. 274

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other education or training

- **[[5.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  education and training benefit may be paid to a veteran entitled to a
  benefit under this Part for fees charged by the provider of any
  education or training, other than education or training described in
  paragraph 5.3(1)(a), that is approved by the Minister.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Rehabilitation Services, Vocational Assistance and Financial Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-68385 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-68386 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of this Part

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Part does not apply in
respect of a physical or a mental health problem that resulted primarily
from

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} service in the Canadian Forces on or before April 1,
  1947; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} service in the Korean War, as that term is defined in
  subsection 3(1) of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consequential injury or disease

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Part, an injury or a disease is deemed to be a service-related injury or
disease if the injury or disease is, in whole or in part, a consequence
of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a service-related injury or disease;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a non-service related injury or disease that was
  aggravated by service;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an injury or a disease that is itself the consequence
  of an injury or a disease described in paragraph (a) or (b); or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an injury or a disease that is a consequence of an
  injury or a disease described in paragraph (c).

### [Rehabilitation Services and Vocational Assistance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-68397 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility --- rehabilitation need

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, on application, provide rehabilitation services to a
  veteran who has a physical or a mental health problem resulting
  primarily from service in the Canadian Forces that is creating a
  barrier to re-establishment in civilian life.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors Minister may consider

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (1) and 18(1), in
  deciding whether a veteran has a physical or a mental health problem
  that is creating a barrier to re-establishment in civilian life, and
  whether that health problem resulted primarily from service in the
  Canadian Forces, the Minister may consider any factors that the
  Minister considers relevant, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} medical reports or records that document the
    veteran's physical or mental health problem;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} documentation that indicates the nature of the
    veteran's service in the Canadian Forces;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} documentation provided by the veteran as to the
    circumstances of their health problem; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} research that establishes the prevalence of
    specific health problems in military populations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (1) and 18(1), a
  veteran's physical or mental health problem is deemed to have resulted
  primarily from service in the Canadian Forces if, as a result of the
  health problem, the veteran suffers from a disability for which a
  disability award has been granted, pain and suffering compensation has
  been granted under section 45 or a pension has been granted under the
  [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consequential injury or disease

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 45(1), an injury or a disease is deemed to
  be a service-related injury or disease if the injury or disease is, in
  whole or in part, a consequence of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a service-related injury or disease;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a non-service-related injury or disease that was
    aggravated by service;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an injury or a disease that is itself a consequence
    of an injury or a disease described in paragraph (a) or (b); or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an injury or a disease that is a consequence of an
    injury or a disease described in paragraph (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensable fraction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a disability results from an injury or a disease
  that is deemed to be a service-related injury or disease, pain and
  suffering compensation may be paid under subsection 45(1) only in
  respect of that fraction of the disability, measured in fifths, that
  represents the extent to which that injury or disease is a consequence
  of another injury or disease that is, or is deemed to be, a
  service-related injury or disease.

**[[56.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56.1 .sectionLabel}** If pain and suffering
compensation is payable to a survivor or a person who was, at the time
of a member's or veteran's death, a dependent child, the following rules
apply:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if there is a survivor but no person who was a
  dependent child, the survivor is entitled to 100% of the pain and
  suffering compensation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if there is a survivor and one or more persons who
  were dependent children,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the survivor is entitled to 50% of the pain and
    suffering compensation, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the persons who were dependent children are
    entitled, as a class, to 50% of the pain and suffering compensation,
    divided equally among them;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if there are one or more persons who were dependent
  children but no survivor, each of those children is entitled to the
  amount obtained by dividing the pain and suffering compensation by the
  number of those children.

### [Additional Pain and Suffering Compensation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1120193 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

- **[[56.6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56.6
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, on application,

  suffers from one or more disabilities that are creating a permanent
  and severe impairment and a barrier to re-establishment in civilian
  life if the veteran, in respect of each of those disabilities, has
  been granted a disability award or pain and suffering compensation or
  a disability pension under the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of subsection (1)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a
  disability resulting from an injury or disease, or the aggravation of
  an injury or disease, that resulted from

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} service in the Canadian Forces on or before April
    1, 1947; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} service in the [[Korean
    War]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 3(1) of the
    [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligibility --- exceptional
  incapacity allowance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A veteran who is receiving an exceptional incapacity
  allowance under the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) is not eligible for
  additional pain and suffering compensation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment of extent of impairment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall assess the extent of the veteran's
  permanent and severe impairment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of additional pain and suffering
  compensation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The monthly amount of additional pain and suffering
  compensation that is payable is the amount set out in column 2 of
  Schedule 4 that corresponds to the extent of the veteran's permanent
  and severe impairment as set out in column 1 of that Schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When additional pain and suffering
  compensation payable

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (8), additional pain and
  suffering compensation begins to be payable on the later of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the first day of the month in which the application
    for additional pain and suffering compensation is made,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day that is one year before the first day of
    the month in which the veteran is determined to be entitled to the
    additional pain and suffering compensation, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the first day of the month in which the veteran is
    released from the Canadian Forces or, if the veteran is released on
    the last day of the month, the first day of the month after the
    month in which the veteran is released.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reassessment of extent of impairment

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If there has been a change in circumstances relating
  to a veteran to whom additional pain and suffering compensation is
  payable, the Minister may, on application, reassess the extent of the
  veteran's permanent and severe impairment. The Minister may, on his or
  her own initiative, reassess the extent of the veteran's permanent and
  severe impairment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When additional pain and suffering
  compensation payable --- reassessment

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If, as a result of the reassessment, a determination
  is made that the extent of the veteran's permanent and severe
  impairment has changed, the resulting additional pain and suffering
  compensation begins to be payable,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the reassessment is conducted on application and
    the veteran's extent of impairment has worsened, on the later of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the first day of the month in which the
      application for a reassessment is made, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the day that is one year before the first day of
      the month in which the determination is made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the reassessment is conducted on application and
    the veteran's extent of impairment has lessened, on the first day of
    the month after the month in which the determination is made; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the reassessment is conducted on the Minister's
    own initiative, on the first day of the month after the month in
    which the determination is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of additional pain and
  suffering compensation

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 56.8, additional pain and
  suffering compensation ceases to be payable on the earlier of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the first day of the month after the month in which
    the Minister determines that the veteran is no longer entitled to
    the additional pain and suffering compensation, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the first day of the month after the month in which
    the veteran dies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of operations

**[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraphs 69(1)(b) and 70(1)(a), the types of operations are as
follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an armed conflict;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an operation authorized under the Charter of the
  United Nations, the North Atlantic Treaty, the North American
  Aerospace Defence Command Agreement or any other similar treaty
  instrument;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an international or a multinational military
  operation;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an operation authorized to deal with a national
  emergency, as that term is defined in section 3 of the [Emergencies
  Act](/eng/acts/E-4.5), in respect of which a declaration of emergency
  is made under that Act;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an operation authorized under section 273.6 or Part
  VI of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), or other similar
  operation authorized by the Governor in Council;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} an operation that, in the opinion of the Minister of
  National Defence, is a search and rescue operation;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} an operation that, in the opinion of the Minister of
  National Defence, is a disaster relief operation;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} an operation that, in the opinion of the Minister of
  National Defence, is a counter-terrorism operation; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} an operation involving a level of risk that, in the
  opinion of the Minister of National Defence, is comparable to that
  normally associated with an operation referred to in paragraphs (a) to
  (e).

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister intends to waive the requirement for

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person may accept to have the requirement for an
  application waived by notifying the Minister in the prescribed manner

  shall, in any period specified by the Minister, provide him or her
  with any information or document that he or she requests.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The requirement for an application is waived on the

  decision to accept the waiver of the requirement.

  the period that he or she specifies, require that the person make an
  application and, in that case, the Minister shall notify the person in
  writing of that requirement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waiver cancelled

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A waiver is cancelled on the day on which the
  Minister notifies the person that they are required to make an

### [Overpayments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-69240 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[overpayment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[88]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-88 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[overpayment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
  compensation, or a part of it, that was paid to a person or their
  estate or succession and to which the person or the estate or
  succession had no entitlement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of overpayment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, through any cause, an overpayment is paid to a
  person or their estate or succession, the overpayment is a debt due to
  Her Majesty by the person or by their estate or succession, and may be
  recovered

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by deduction from any future payments made under
    this Act to the person or their estate or succession;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with section 155 of the [Financial
    Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by proceedings in any court of competent
    jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission of overpayment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person or their estate or succession has
  received or obtained an overpayment, the Minister may, unless that
  person or the executor of the estate or the liquidator of the
  succession has been convicted of an offence under the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46) in connection with the receiving or obtaining of
  the overpayment, remit all or any portion of the overpayment if the
  Minister is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the overpayment cannot be recovered within the
    reasonably foreseeable future;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the administrative costs of recovering the
    overpayment are likely to equal or exceed the amount to be
    recovered;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the repayment of the overpayment would cause undue
    hardship to the person or a beneficiary; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the overpayment is the result of an administrative
    error, a delay or an oversight on the part of an officer or employee
    of the federal public administration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Erroneous payments

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite anything in this Act, the Minister may
  continue the payment of an education and training benefit, an income
  replacement benefit, a Canadian Forces income support benefit, pain
  and suffering compensation, additional pain and suffering
  compensation, a clothing allowance or a caregiver recognition benefit,
  in whole or in part, to a person who is not entitled to it, or not
  entitled to a portion of it, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount paid to the person to which the person
    was not entitled is the result of an administrative error, a delay
    or an oversight on the part of an officer or employee of the federal
    public administration and has been remitted under paragraph (3)(d);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no part of the amount paid to the person to which
    the person was not entitled was the result of a misrepresentation or
    of concealment of a material fact on the part of the person, in the
    opinion of the Minister;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the cancellation or reduction of the benefit,
    allowance, pain and suffering compensation or additional pain and
    suffering compensation would, in the opinion of the Minister, cause
    undue hardship to the person; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the benefit, allowance, pain and suffering
    compensation or additional pain and suffering compensation has been

    education and training benefit, for three years or more.

##### [Members and Veterans]{.HTitleText4} {#h-1120298 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Veterans' deemed entitlement to income
replacement benefit

- **[[99]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-99 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If,
  before April 1, 2019, the Minister determined, as a result of an
  assessment of a veteran's needs under subsection 10(1) of the former
  Act, that a rehabilitation plan or a vocational assistance plan should
  be developed for the veteran in respect of a physical or a mental
  health problem and if, as a result of the Minister's determination, an
  earnings loss benefit was payable to the veteran on March 31, 2019
  under section 18 of the former Act, then the veteran is deemed, on
  April 1, 2019, to be entitled to an income replacement benefit under
  section 18 of the new Act in respect of that health problem and the
  following rules apply in respect of the veteran:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsection 18(2) of the new
    Act, the veteran is deemed, on April 1, 2019, to be informed of
    their entitlement to the income replacement benefit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} despite subsection 18(3) of the new Act, the
    benefit begins to be payable to the veteran on April 1, 2019;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the physical or mental health problem in respect of
    which the veteran is deemed to be entitled to the benefit is deemed
    to be the physical or mental health problem referred to in
    subsection 18(5) or (7) of the new Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if, on a day before April 1, 2019, the Minister
    determined that the veteran has a diminished earning capacity that
    is due to the physical or mental health problem in respect of which
    the veteran is deemed to be entitled to the benefit, the Minister is
    deemed to have made that determination, on that day, under
    subsection 18(5) of the new Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if, on March 31, 2019, the veteran was entitled to
    continue to receive the earnings loss benefit under subsection 18(4)
    of the former Act, the veteran is deemed, on April 1, 2019, to be
    entitled to continue to receive the income replacement benefit under
    subsection 18(7) of the new Act; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the monthly amount of the income replacement
    benefit that is payable to the veteran under section 18 of the new
    Act is to be determined under subsection 19(1) or 19.1(1) of that
    Act, subject to subsections (2) to (5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- career progression
  factor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the physical or mental health problem in respect
  of which the veteran is deemed to be entitled to the income
  replacement benefit under subsection (1) did not result primarily from
  service in the Canadian Forces and an increase to the career impact
  allowance was not payable to the veteran, under subsection 38(3) of
  the former Act, on March 31, 2019, then the monthly amount of the
  income replacement benefit that is payable to the veteran under
  section 18 of the new Act is determined under subsection 19(1) of that
  Act without any periodic adjustment in accordance with a career
  progression factor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protected amount --- veteran under age

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, in determining the amount of the income
  replacement benefit that is payable to the veteran for a given month,

  benefit is payable, then the amount determined for A is to be replaced
  by that sum:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined for A in subsection 19(1) of
    the former Act that was used to calculate the earnings loss benefit
    payable to the veteran under section 18 of that Act for the month of
    March 2019, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if an increase to the career impact allowance was
    payable to the veteran, under subsection 38(3) of the former Act, on
    March 31, 2019, the amount of the increase that the veteran received
    for the month of March 2019 or, if the increase was payable only for
    a portion of that month, the amount of the increase that the veteran
    would have received under that subsection for that month if the
    increase had been payable for the whole month.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protected amount --- veteran 65 years
  or older

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, in determining the amount of the income
  replacement benefit that is payable to the veteran for a given month,

  indexed until the time the benefit is payable, is less than 70% of the
  sum of the following amounts, indexed until the time the benefit is
  payable, then the amount determined for A is to be replaced by 70% of
  the sum of the following amounts, indexed until the time the benefit
  is payable:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined for A in subsection 19(1) of
    the former Act that was used to calculate the earnings loss benefit

    March 2019, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if an increase to the career impact allowance was
    payable to the veteran, under subsection 38(3) of the former Act, on
    March 31, 2019, the amount of the increase that the veteran received
    for the month of March 2019 or, if the increase was payable only for
    a portion of that month, the amount of the increase that the veteran
    would have received under that subsection for that month if the
    increase had been payable for the whole month.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum amount of income replacement
  benefit

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (3) and (4), if an increase to
  the career impact allowance was payable to the veteran, under
  subsection 38(3) of the former Act, on March 31, 2019, then the
  monthly amount of the income replacement benefit that is payable to
  the veteran under section 18 of the new Act is not to be less than,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the increase was payable for the whole month of

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the increase was payable only for a portion of

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the monthly amount of the income replacement
    benefit that is payable to the survivor or orphan under section 22

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the veteran is deemed, on April 1, 2019, to be
    entitled to continue to receive the income replacement benefit under
    subsection 18(7) of the new Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the monthly amount of the income replacement

    March 31, 2019, 70% of the amount of the increase that the veteran
    received for the month of March 2019 or, if the increase was payable
    only for a portion of that month, 70% of the amount of the increase

    month if the increase had been payable for the whole month.

### [Transitional Provisions Relating to Additional Pain and Suffering Compensation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1120839 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[133]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-133 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this section.

  [[former Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[former Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means this Act as it read
      immediately before April 1, 2019. ([ancienne loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[new Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[new Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means this Act as it reads on
      April 1, 2019. ([nouvelle loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed entitlement to additional pain
  and suffering compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a career impact allowance was payable to a veteran
  on March 31, 2019 under section 38 of the former Act, then the veteran
  is deemed, on April 1, 2019, to be entitled to additional pain and
  suffering compensation under section 56.6 of the new Act and the
  following rules apply in respect of the veteran:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the physical or mental health problems in respect
    of which the career impact allowance was payable to the veteran are
    deemed to be the disabilities in respect of which the veteran is
    deemed to be entitled to additional pain and suffering compensation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the monthly amount of additional pain and suffering
    compensation that is payable to the veteran under section 56.6 of
    the new Act is determined under subsection 56.6(5) of that Act,
    subject to subsections (3) and (4); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} despite subsection 56.6(6) of the new Act,
    additional pain and suffering compensation begins to be payable to
    the veteran on April 1, 2019.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extent of veteran's permanent and
  severe impairment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The extent of the veteran's permanent and severe
  impairment that is used to determine the amount of additional pain and
  suffering compensation that is payable to the veteran for the month of
  April 2019 is assessed in accordance with the following rules:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a veteran to whom the maximum amount of career
    impact allowance was payable under section 38 of the former Act on
    March 31, 2019 --- without taking into account the amount of any
    increase to the career impact allowance referred to in subsection
    38(3) of that Act --- is assessed as having a Grade 1 extent of
    permanent and severe impairment as set out in Schedule 4 to the new
    Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a veteran to whom the minimum amount of career
    impact allowance was payable under section 38 of the former Act on
    March 31, 2019 --- without taking into account the amount of any
    increase to the career impact allowance referred to in subsection
    38(3) of that Act --- is assessed as having a Grade 3 extent of
    permanent and severe impairment as set out in Schedule 4 to the new
    Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a veteran who is not referred to in paragraph (a)
    or (b) is assessed as having a Grade 2 extent of permanent and
    severe impairment as set out in Schedule 4 to the new Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protected amount

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For every month after the month of April 2019 for
  which the veteran is entitled, as a result of subsection (2), to
  additional pain and suffering compensation under section 56.6 of the
  new Act, the amount of additional pain and suffering compensation that
  is payable to the veteran under that section is not to be less than
  the amount of additional pain and suffering compensation that is
  payable for the month of April 2019.

      survivor or a person who was, at the time of a member's or a
      veteran's death, a dependent child made an application for a
      disability award under subsection 50(1) of the former Act but the
      Minister did not make a determination in respect of the
      application before that date, then the survivor or person is
      deemed to have made an application for pain and suffering
      compensation under section 56 of the new Act on April 1, 2019.

    - ###### No determination {#no-determination-1 .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the Minister
      did not make a determination in respect of an application if the
      Minister did not assess the member's or veteran's extent of
      disability in respect of the application.

- --- 2018, c. 12, s. 176

  - ###### Deceased member or veteran {#deceased-member-or-veteran .MarginalNote}

    [176]{.lawlabel} If a member or a veteran who made an application
    for a disability award under section 45, 47 or 48 of the former Act
    dies before the Minister makes a determination in respect of the
    application and, on March 31, 2019, the Minister has not made a
    determination to pay, under subsection 50(2) of the former Act, the
    disability award to a survivor or a person who was, at the time of
    the member's or veteran's death, a dependent child, the Minister may
    pay pain and suffering compensation to the survivor or person under
    subsection 55(1) of the new Act as if the member or veteran had made
    an application for pain and suffering compensation under section 45,
    47 or 48 of the new Act, as the case may be, on April 1, 2019.

- --- 2018, c. 12, s. 177

  - ###### Review, appeal and reconsideration --- disability award {#review-appeal-and-reconsideration-disability-award .MarginalNote}

    [177]{.lawlabel} Any review, appeal or reconsideration that is
    continued on or commenced on or after April 1, 2019, in relation to
    a determination made before that date in respect of an application
    for a disability award made under the former Act is to be conducted
    as if the determination was a determination in respect of an
    application for pain and suffering compensation made on that date
    under the new Act.

- --- 2020, c. 11, s. 10

  - ###### Payment out of Consolidated Revenue Fund {#payment-out-of-consolidated-revenue-fund .MarginalNote}

    [10]{.lawlabel} Any amount payable by the Minister of Employment and
    Social Development in relation to the administration of a program to
    provide a one-time payment to persons with disabilities for reasons
    related to the coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19), including any
    administrative cost, is to be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue
    Fund.
