## Department of Indigenous Services Act {#department-of-indigenous-services-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2019, c. 29, s. 336
:::

Assented to 2019-06-21

An Act respecting the Department of Indigenous Services

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 336 of chapter 29 of the Statutes of Canada, 2019,
in force July 15, 2019.\]
:::
:::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the Government of Canada is committed to

achieving reconciliation with First Nations, the Métis and the Inuit
through renewed nation-to-nation, government-to-government and
Inuit-Crown relationships based on affirmation and implementation of
rights, respect, cooperation and partnership,

promoting respect for the rights of Indigenous peoples recognized and
affirmed by section 35 of the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/), and

implementing the United Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous
Peoples;

And whereas it is appropriate to establish a Department of Indigenous
Services and that the Department, in carrying out its activities,

ensures that Indigenous individuals have access --- in accordance with
transparent service standards and the needs of each Indigenous group,
community or people --- to services for which those individuals are
eligible,

takes into account socio-economic gaps that persist between Indigenous
individuals and other Canadians with respect to a range of matters as
well as social factors having an impact on health and well-being,

recognizes and promotes Indigenous ways of knowing, being and doing,

collaborates and cooperates with Indigenous peoples and with the
provinces and territories, and

implements the gradual transfer of departmental responsibilities to
Indigenous organizations;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169251 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Department of Indigenous Services Act](/eng/acts/I-7.88).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169254 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the department established
    under section 3. ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Indigenous governing body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indigenous governing body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a council,
    government or other entity that is authorized to act on behalf of an
    Indigenous group, community or people that holds rights recognized
    and affirmed by section 35 of the [Constitution Act,
    1982](/eng/Const/). ([corps dirigeant autochtone]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Indigenous organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indigenous organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an Indigenous
    governing body or any other entity that represents the interests of
    an Indigenous group and its members. ([organisation
    autochtone]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Indigenous peoples]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indigenous peoples]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned
    by the definition [[aboriginal peoples of Canada
    ]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}in subsection 35(2) of the [Constitution
    Act, 1982](/eng/Const/). ([peuples autochtones]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Indigenous
    Services. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Establishment of Department]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169262 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Department established

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** There is established a
department of the Government of Canada, called the Department of
Indigenous Services, over which the Minister presides.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister, appointed by
commission under the Great Seal, holds office during pleasure and has
the management and direction of the Department.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy Minister

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
appoint a Deputy Minister of Indigenous Services to hold office during
pleasure and to be the deputy head of the Department.

## [Powers, Duties and Functions of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169269 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister's powers, duties and functions extend to and include all
  matters over which Parliament has jurisdiction --- and that are not by
  law assigned to any other department, board or agency of the
  Government of Canada --- relating to the provision of services to
  Indigenous individuals who, and Indigenous governing bodies that, are
  eligible to receive those services under an Act of Parliament or a
  program of the Government of Canada for which the Minister is
  responsible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial duty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is to ensure that services with respect
  to the following matters are provided to Indigenous individuals who,
  and Indigenous governing bodies that, are eligible to receive those
  services under an Act of Parliament or a program of the Government of
  Canada for which the Minister is responsible --- including, in the
  case of Indigenous individuals, services provided by Indigenous
  governing bodies, if applicable:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} child and family services;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} education;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} health;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} social development;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} economic development;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} housing;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} infrastructure;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} emergency management;

  - [(h.1)]{.lawlabel} governance; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any other matter designated by order of the
    Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collaboration and transfer of
responsibilities

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** In exercising the powers
and performing the duties and functions under this Act, the Minister is
to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide Indigenous organizations with an opportunity
  to collaborate in the development, provision, assessment and
  improvement of the services referred to in subsection 6(2); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with any agreements respecting the
  transfer of responsibilities that are entered into under section 9,
  take the appropriate measures to give effect to the gradual transfer
  to Indigenous organizations of departmental responsibilities with
  respect to the development and provision of those services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitations

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
subsection 6(2) and section 7 apply subject to the transfer of
responsibilities referred to in paragraph 7(b) and to agreements with
Indigenous organizations, including land claims, self-government or
self-determination agreements.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may enter into
agreements with Indigenous organizations and other entities regarding
the provision of services referred to in subsection 6(2) and the
transfer of responsibilities referred to in paragraph 7(b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special representatives

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may appoint special representatives to provide him or her
  with advice on any matter relating to his or her powers, duties and
  functions under this Act or to any other matter relating to this Act,
  or to represent him or her in the context of, among other things, any
  consultation or engagement with an Indigenous organization or
  Indigenous group, community or people with respect to such a matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may also establish advisory and other
  committees to provide him or her with advice on any matter referred to
  in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and expenses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is to fix the remuneration and expenses
  to be paid to special representatives and to committee members.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of services between departments

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Department may
provide services to and receive services from the Department of
Crown-Indigenous Relations and Northern Affairs --- including services
to support policy and program implementation --- as provided in a
written agreement between the Minister and the Minister of
Crown-Indigenous Relations or the Minister of Northern Affairs, as the
case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collection and use of information

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, in exercising his or her powers and performing his or
  her duties or functions under this Act, collect, analyze and interpret
  documents, information or data in respect of matters under his or her
  administration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, in exercising his or her powers and
  performing his or her duties or functions under this Act, disclose or
  permit to be disclosed, in whole or in part, those documents and that
  information or data to, among others,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Department of Crown-Indigenous Relations and
    Northern Affairs;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a department named in Schedule I to the [Financial
    Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), a division or branch of the
    federal public administration set out in column I of Schedule I.1 to
    that Act, a corporation named in Schedule II to that Act or a
    [[Crown corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection
    83(1) of that Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an Indigenous organization; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a not-for-profit entity controlled by Indigenous
    individuals.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions related to disclosure

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Paragraphs (2)(c) and (d) do not apply in respect of
  any information, or to any documents or data that would reveal any
  information,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is [[personal
    information]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 3 of the
    [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21), unless

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the personal information is publicly available,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the individual to whom the personal information
      relates consents to its disclosure, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure is for a purpose referred to in
      any of paragraphs 8(2)(a), (b), (f), (j), (k) or (m) of the
      [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is protected by solicitor-client privilege or
    professional secrecy of advocates and notaries or by litigation
    privilege;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure of which is restricted under any
    provision of any other Act of Parliament set out in Schedule II to
    the [Access to Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1); or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that is [[a confidence of the Queen's Privy Council
    for Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 39(2) of
    the [Canada Evidence Act](/eng/acts/C-5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Support for Indigenous bodies

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may support
Indigenous bodies that specialize in research or statistics, in relation
to their activities involving the collection, analysis, interpretation,
publication and distribution of documents, information or data relating
to the provision of services to Indigenous individuals.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may delegate
any of his or her powers, duties and functions under this Act or any
other Act of Parliament, except the power to delegate under this
section, to the Minister of Crown-Indigenous Relations or the Minister
of Northern Affairs, for any period and under any terms that the
Minister considers suitable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report to Parliament

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must cause
to be tabled in each House of Parliament, within three months after the
end of the fiscal year or, if the House is not then sitting, on any of
the first 15 days of the next sitting of the House, a report on

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the socio-economic gaps between First Nations
  individuals, Inuit, Métis individuals and other Canadians and the
  measures taken by the Department to reduce those gaps; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the progress made towards the transfer of
  responsibilities referred to in paragraph 7(b).

      immediately before the day on which this section comes into force,
      the office of Minister of State styled Minister of Indigenous
      Services is deemed, as of that day, to be the minister referred to
      in section 4 of the [Department of Indigenous Services
      Act](/eng/acts/I-7.88), as enacted by section 336.

    - ###### Deputy Minister {#deputy-minister .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person who holds, immediately before the day
      on which this section comes into force, the office of Special
      Adviser to the Minister of Indigenous Services, styled as Deputy
      Minister of Indigenous Services, is deemed to have been appointed
      on that day under section 5 of the [Department of Indigenous
      Services Act](/eng/acts/I-7.88), as enacted by section 336, as the
      Deputy Minister of Indigenous Services.

    - ###### Persons who occupy a position {#persons-who-occupy-a-position .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act is to be construed as
      affecting the status of any person who, immediately before the day
      on which this section comes into force, occupies or is assigned to
      a position in the Department of Indigenous Services Canada, except
      that the person, as of that day, occupies or is assigned to their
      position in the Department of Indigenous Services.

  - ###### Transfer of appropriations {#transfer-of-appropriations .MarginalNote}

    Services.

    including any approval given in relation to the issuance of a

    [Department of Indigenous Services Act](/eng/acts/I-7.88) or that

      and Northern Development in a land claims, specific claim or

      otherwise, to be read as a reference to the Minister of
      Crown-Indigenous Relations or Department of Crown-Indigenous

      and Northern Development or Department of Indian Affairs and
      Northern Development in any contract, instrument or act, agreement
      --- other than an agreement referred to in subsection (1) --- or
      other document is, unless the context requires otherwise, to be
      read as a reference to the Minister of Crown-Indigenous Relations,
      Minister of Northern Affairs, Minister of Indigenous Services,
      Department of Crown-Indigenous Relations and Northern Affairs or
      Department of Indigenous Services, as the case may be.

- --- 2019, c. 29, s. 346

  - ###### Regulations {#regulations .MarginalNote}

    [346]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may make any regulations
    that the Governor in Council considers necessary to provide for any
    other transitional matter arising from the coming into force of this
    Act.
