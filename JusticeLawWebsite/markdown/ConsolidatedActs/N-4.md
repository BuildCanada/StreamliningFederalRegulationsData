## [Commission Continued]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374252 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation continued

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  corporation called the National Capital Commission is continued,
  consisting of fifteen members including a Chairperson and a Chief
  Executive Officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each member, other than the Chairperson and the Chief
  Executive Officer, shall be appointed by the Minister, with the
  approval of the Governor in Council, to hold office during pleasure
  for a term not exceeding four years that will ensure, as far as
  possible, the expiration in any one year of the terms of office of not
  more than one half of the members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Chairperson and Chief
  Executive Officer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson and the Chief Executive Officer shall
  each be appointed by the Governor in Council to hold office during
  pleasure for a term that the Governor in Council considers
  appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The members, other than the Chairperson and the Chief
  Executive Officer, shall be appointed as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} three, ordinarily resident in the National Capital
    Region, from local municipalities in Ontario, at least two of whom
    must be from the city of Ottawa;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} two, ordinarily resident in the National Capital
    Region, from local municipalities in Quebec, at least one of whom
    shall be from the section of the city of Gatineau that is west of
    the Gatineau River; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} eight from Canada generally, other than from a city
    or municipality referred to in either of paragraphs (a) or (b).

  - [(d) and (e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2002, c. 17, s.
    19\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A member is eligible to be appointed from a local
  municipality if, at the time of the appointment, the member normally
  resides in that municipality.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A person who has served two consecutive terms as a
  member, other than as Chief Executive Officer, is not eligible to be
  re-appointed to the Commission, during the twelve months following the
  completion of the person's second term, in the same capacity in which
  the person served.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancy

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A vacancy in the membership of the Commission does
  not impair the right of the remaining members to act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members not contributors for
  superannuation

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36) does not apply to a member unless the Governor in
  Council otherwise directs.

## [Officers and Employees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374300 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General Manager

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may appoint a General Manager to hold office
  during pleasure who shall be paid a salary to be fixed by the Governor
  in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the plan of organization and terms and
  conditions of employment approved under subsection (3), the Commission
  may employ such officers and employees and such consultants and
  advisers as it deems necessary for the purpose of this Act and may fix
  their remuneration and terms and conditions of employment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Plan of organization and terms and
  conditions of employment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may approve

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a plan of organization for the establishment and
    classification of the continuing positions necessary for the proper
    functioning of the Commission; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the establishment of rates of compensation for each
    class of position, and such other terms and conditions of employment
    as are considered desirable.

## [Committees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374312 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Executive Committee

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be an Executive Committee of the Commission consisting of the
  Chairperson, the Chief Executive Officer and three other members to be
  appointed by the Commission, at least one of whom shall be from the
  Province of Quebec.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Executive Committee shall exercise such of the
  powers and perform such of the functions of the Commission as are
  delegated to it by the Commission and shall submit at each meeting of
  the Commission minutes of its proceedings since the last preceding
  meeting of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other committees

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may establish a National Capital
  Planning Committee and such other committees as it considers necessary
  or desirable for the administration of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses of committee members

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Each member of the Executive Committee, National
  Capital Planning Committee or other committee established under this
  section is entitled to be paid reasonable travel and other expenses
  incurred by the member in the performance of his duties.

## [Objects, Purposes and Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374324 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects and purposes of Commission

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  objects and purposes of the Commission are to prepare plans for and
  assist in the development, conservation and improvement of the
  National Capital Region in order that the nature and character of the
  seat of the Government of Canada may be in accordance with its
  national significance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may, for the purposes of this Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} acquire, hold, administer or develop property;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sell, grant, convey, lease or otherwise dispose of
    or make available to any person any property, subject to such
    conditions and limitations as it considers necessary or desirable;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} construct, maintain and operate parks, squares,
    highways, parkways, bridges, buildings and any other works;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} maintain and improve any property of the
    Commission, or any other property under the control and management
    of a department, at the request of the authority or Minister in
    charge thereof;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} cooperate or engage in joint projects with, or make
    grants to, local municipalities or other authorities for the
    improvement, development or maintenance of property;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} construct, maintain and operate, or grant
    concessions for the operation of, places of entertainment,
    amusement, recreation, refreshment, or other places of public
    interest or accommodation on any property of the Commission;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} administer, preserve and maintain any historic
    place or historic museum;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} conduct investigations and researches in connection
    with the planning of the National Capital Region; and

  - [(h.1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2013, c. 33, s. 213\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} generally, do and authorize such things as are
    incidental or conducive to the attainment of the objects and
    purposes of the Commission and the exercise of its powers.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-4, s. 10
- R.S., 1985, c. 45 (4th Supp.), s. 3
- 2013, c. 33, s. 213

## [Development]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374342 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coordination of development

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Commission shall, in
accordance with general plans prepared under this Act, coordinate the
development of public lands in the National Capital Region.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-3, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Development proposals

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any department proposes to erect, alter, extend or
    demolish a building or other work on any lands in the National
    Capital Region,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person proposes to erect, alter, extend or
    demolish a building or other work on public lands in the National
    Capital Region, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any department or person proposes to change the use
    of public lands in the National Capital Region,

  the department or person shall, prior to the commencement of the
  project, submit a proposal therefor to the Commission for approval.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of proposals

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In determining whether to approve a proposal
  submitted under subsection (1), the Commission shall consider the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a proposal to erect, alter or extend
    a building or other work, the site, location, design and plans
    thereof and the use to be made of the building or other work as
    erected, altered or extended;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a proposal to demolish a building or
    other work, the site, location, design and use made of the building
    or other work and the plans for the demolition; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a proposal to change the use of
    public lands, the site, location, existing use and proposed use of
    the lands.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No department or person shall commence any project in
  relation to which a proposal is required to be submitted to the
  Commission under subsection (1) unless a proposal has been so
  submitted and has been approved by the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interior alterations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply to any alteration of the
  interior of a building or other work unless the alteration is made to
  accommodate a change in the use of the building or work.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-4, s. 12
- R.S., 1985, c. 45 (4th Supp.), s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proposals for sale of public lands

- **[[12.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Proposals by any department for the
  sale of public lands in the National Capital Region shall be submitted
  to the Commission for approval prior to the sale.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No public lands in the National Capital Region shall
  be sold without the approval of the Commission.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 45 (4th Supp.), s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Governor in Council may approve

- **[[12.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where the Commission does not give
  its approval to a proposal made under section 12 or 12.1, the Governor
  in Council may give such approval and any such approval given by the
  Governor in Council shall, for the purposes of that section, be deemed
  to have been given by the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms and conditions of approval

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any approval given under section 12, 12.1 or this
  section may be subject to such terms and conditions as are considered
  desirable by the Commission or the Governor in Council, as the case
  may be.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 45 (4th Supp.), s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to construct railway

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may construct in the National Capital Region, in accordance
  with plans prepared under this Act, a railway and related facilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale, lease, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sell, convey or lease any railway and related
    facilities, or any portion thereof, constructed pursuant to
    subsection (1) to any railway company; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into agreements with any railway company for

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the sole, joint or several use of the railway or
      facilities, or any portion thereof,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the maintenance by the company of the railway or
      facilities, or any portion thereof, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the operation of the railway or facilities, or
      any portion thereof.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Part III of the [Canada
  Transportation Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Part III of the [Canada Transportation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4), with such modifications as the circumstances
  require, is applicable to the exercise of the powers conferred by this
  section, but nothing in this section is deemed to constitute the
  Commission a railway company except for the purpose of subsection (2).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-4, s. 13
- 1996, c. 10, s. 236
:::

## [Expropriation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374393 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expropriation

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  in the opinion of the Commission the acquisition of any land or
  interest therein by the Commission without the consent of the owner is
  required for the purposes of this Act, the Commission shall so advise
  the appropriate Minister in relation to Part I of the [Expropriation
  Act](/eng/acts/E-21).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the [Expropriation
  Act](/eng/acts/E-21), any land or interest therein that, in the
  opinion of the Minister mentioned in subsection (1), is required for
  the purposes of this Act shall be deemed to be land or an interest
  therein that, in his opinion, is required for a public work or other
  public purpose, and, in relation thereto, a reference to the Crown in
  that Act shall be construed as a reference to the Commission.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-3, s. 13
- R.S., c. 16(1st Supp.), s. 42
:::

## [Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374401 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrictions on transactions

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  with the approval of the Governor in Council, the Commission shall not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} acquire any real property for a consideration in
    excess of a value of twenty-five thousand dollars; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into a lease enduring for a period in excess
    of five years or grant an easement enduring for a period in excess
    of forty-nine years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall not dispose of real property for
  a consideration in excess of ten thousand dollars, except in
  accordance with subsection 99(2) of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contract regulations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection 41(2) of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the Governor in Council may make
  regulations under subsection 41(1) of that Act that apply in respect
  of the Commission.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-4, s. 15
- R.S., 1985, c. 45 (4th Supp.), s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments in lieu of taxes

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may pay grants to a local municipality not exceeding the
  taxes that might be levied by the municipality in respect of any real
  property of the Commission if the Commission were not an agent of Her
  Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to parks or squares, to
  highways or parkways, or to bridges or similar structures.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Gatineau Park

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may pay grants to the appropriate
  authorities in respect of real property of the Commission situated in
  Gatineau Park not exceeding in any tax year the amounts estimated by
  the Commission to be sufficient to compensate such authorities for the
  loss of tax revenue during that tax year in respect of municipal and
  school taxes by reason of the acquisition of the property by the
  Commission.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-3, s. 15
:::

## [Financial]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374422 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loans

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Finance
may out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund advance to the Commission such
amounts by way of loan as are authorized by Parliament, subject to such
terms and conditions respecting interest, terms of repayment and
otherwise as are approved by the Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-3, s. 16

:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General fund

the Commission may expend for any of the purposes of this Act any money
appropriated by Parliament for the use of the Commission, received by it
through the conduct of its operations or received by it by bequest,
donation or otherwise.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-3, s. 17

## [By-laws and Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374431 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may make
by-laws for the conduct and management of its activities and for
carrying out the purposes and provisions of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-3, s. 18
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations for the protection of any
  property of the Commission and for preserving order or preventing
  accidents on any property of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may by regulation prescribe
  the punishment that may be imposed on summary conviction for the
  contravention of any regulation made under subsection (1), but the
  punishment so prescribed shall not exceed that set out in subsection
  787(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-4, s. 20
- 2002, c. 13, s. 87
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of owner

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a vehicle is operated or parked in contravention of any regulation
  made under subsection 20(1), the owner of the vehicle is liable to the
  punishment prescribed by the Governor in Council for such
  contravention, unless at the time of such contravention the vehicle
  was not operated or parked, as the case may be, by the owner or by any
  other person with the express or implied consent of the owner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Damage to property

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a person is convicted for the contravention of
  any regulation made under subsection 20(1), the convicting court may,
  at the time sentence is imposed, order that person to pay to the
  Commission an amount by way of satisfaction or compensation for loss
  of or damage to property suffered by the Commission as a result of the
  contravention for which that person was convicted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order enforced as a judgment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An order made under subsection (2) in respect of a
  person convicted for the contravention of any regulation made under
  subsection 20(1) may, on the filing of the order in the superior court
  of the province in which the trial of the contravention was held, be
  enforced as a judgment of that court.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-3, s. 19
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** In a prosecution for the
contravention of any regulation made under subsection 20(1), a
certificate stating that any property described in it is under the
control of the Commission and purporting to be certified by the
Commission or the Chief Executive Officer, General Manager, Chief
Engineer, or Secretary of the Commission shall be admitted in evidence
without proof of the signature or official character of the person
appearing to have signed the certificate and without further proof and,
in the absence of evidence to the contrary, is proof that the property
is under the control of the Commission.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-4, s. 22
- 1995, c. 29, s. 55(E)
- 2006, c. 9, s. 289
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374456 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Gifts

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may, if it
sees fit, accept any property by way of gift, bequest or devise and may,
subject to paragraph 10(2)(b) and subsection 15(1) but notwithstanding
any other provision of this Act, expend, administer or dispose of any
such property for the purposes of this Act, subject to the terms, if
any, on which such property was given, bequeathed or devised to the
Commission.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-3, s. 20
- 1984, c. 31, s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Auditor

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The Auditor General of
Canada is the auditor of the Commission.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-3, s. 22
- 1976-77, c. 34, s. 30(F)
- 1984, c. 31, s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Works for general advantage of Canada

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** All works of the
Commission, whether constructed or executed before or after February 6,
1959, are hereby declared to be for the general advantage of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-3, s. 23

Mississippi Mills in the County of Lanark, part of the Township of

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 1995, c. 29, s. 56

  - ###### Directors cease to hold office {#directors-cease-to-hold-office .MarginalNote}

    [56]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection 105(4) of the [Financial
    Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), every person who was a director
    of the National Capital Commission immediately before the coming
    into force of section 54 of this Act by reason of that subsection
    shall cease to hold office on the coming into force of section 54 of
    this Act.

- --- 2013, c. 33, s. 216

  - ###### Definitions {#definitions .MarginalNote}

    [216]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in sections 217 to
    220.

    [[activity and event mandate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[activity and event mandate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
        organization, sponsoring or promotion of any public activities
        and events, in the National Capital Region as defined in section
        2 of the [National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4), that will enrich
        the cultural and social fabric of Canada. ([mandat en matière
        d'activités et de manifestations]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the National Capital
        Commission continued by section 3 of the [National Capital
        Act](/eng/acts/N-4). ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- --- 2013, c. 33, s. 217

  - ###### Appointment of employees {#appointment-of-employees .MarginalNote}

    [217]{.lawlabel} Every employee of the Commission whose functions
    relate to the activity and event mandate is deemed, on the day on
    which this section comes into force, to be a person appointed to a
    position in the Department of Canadian Heritage under the [Public
    Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) and to be an employee as
    defined in subsection 2(1) of that Act.

- --- 2013, c. 33, s. 218

  - ###### Credits transferred {#credits-transferred .MarginalNote}

    [218]{.lawlabel} Any money that is appropriated by an Act of
    Parliament for the fiscal year in which this section comes into
    force to defray any expenditures of the Commission related to the
    activity and event mandate and that is unexpended is deemed to have
    been appropriated to defray any operating expenditures of the
    Department of Canadian Heritage.

- --- 2013, c. 33, s. 219

  - ###### Assets, obligations and authorizations {#assets-obligations-and-authorizations .MarginalNote}

    [219]{.lawlabel} On the day on which this section comes into force,
    and to the extent that they relate to the activity and event
    mandate,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Commission's assets are transferred to Her
      Majesty in right of Canada as represented by the Minister of
      Canadian Heritage;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Commission's obligations are assumed by Her
      Majesty in right of Canada as represented by the Minister of
      Canadian Heritage;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} permits, licences and other authorizations issued
      to the Commission are transferred to Her Majesty in right of
      Canada as represented by the Minister of Canadian Heritage; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} permits, licences and other authorizations issued
      by the Commission are deemed to have been issued by Her Majesty in
      right of Canada as represented by the Minister of Canadian
      Heritage.

- --- 2013, c. 33, s. 220

  - ###### Continuation of legal proceedings {#continuation-of-legal-proceedings .MarginalNote}

    [220]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal proceeding to which
    the Commission is party that is pending in any court on the day on
    which this section comes into force and that relates to the activity
    and event mandate may be continued by or against Her Majesty in
    right of Canada in the same manner and to the same extent as it
    could have been continued by or against the Commission.
