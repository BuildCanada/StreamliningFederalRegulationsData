## Protection of Residential Mortgage or Hypothecary Insurance Act {#protection-of-residential-mortgage-or-hypothecary-insurance-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2011, c. 15, s. 20
:::

Assented to 2011-06-26

An Act to authorize, in certain circumstances, the making of payments or
the purchase of replacement insurance by Her Majesty in respect of
certain types of mortgage or hypothecary insurance provided by an
insurance company in respect of which a winding-up order is made and to
terminate certain agreements relating to mortgage or hypothecary
insurance

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 20 of chapter 15 of the Statutes of Canada, 2011,
in force January 1, 2013, see SI/2012-87.\]
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-400619 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Protection of Residential Mortgage or Hypothecary Insurance
Act](/eng/acts/P-25.7).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-400622 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[approved mortgage insurer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approved mortgage insurer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mortgage
    insurer that is designated under section 4. ([assureur hypothécaire
    agréé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Guaranty Mortgage
    Insurance Company, the Genworth Financial Mortgage Insurance Company
    Canada, the PMI Mortgage Insurance Company Canada or any successor
    to any of those companies. ([société]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in Ontario, the Superior Court of Justice;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in Quebec, the Superior Court of the province;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in Nova Scotia and British Columbia, the Supreme
      Court of the province;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in New Brunswick, Manitoba, Saskatchewan and
      Alberta, the Court of Queen's Bench for the province;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in Prince Edward Island and Newfoundland and
      Labrador, the trial division of the Supreme Court of the province;
      and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in Yukon and the Northwest Territories, the
      Supreme Court of the territory, and in Nunavut, the Nunavut Court
      of Justice. ([tribunal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible mortgage loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible mortgage loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mortgage or
    hypothecary loan that meets the criteria established by regulations
    made under subsection 42(1). ([prêt hypothécaire
    admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Her Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Her Majesty]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Her Majesty in right of
    Canada. ([Sa Majesté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[liquidator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[liquidator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a liquidator appointed
    under the [Winding-up and Restructuring Act](/eng/acts/W-11) or a
    similar official appointed under any other Act of Parliament
    relating to insolvency or
    bankruptcy. ([liquidateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Finance. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mortgage insurer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mortgage insurer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation
    referred to in subsection 13(1) of the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) and that is approved by the Superintendent to
    sell mortgage or hypothecary insurance in Canada.  ([assureur
    hypothécaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any written contract of
    insurance in respect of an eligible mortgage loan, whether contained
    in one or more documents, entered into by an approved mortgage
    insurer and a qualified mortgage lender that has been designated as
    such by the approved mortgage insurer. ([police]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by regulations
    made under section 41. ([Version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[qualified mortgage lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualified mortgage lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mortgage
    or hypothecary lender that has been designated under section
    10. ([prêteur hypothécaire qualifié]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Superintendent of
    Financial Institutions appointed under the [Office of the
    Superintendent of Financial Institutions
    Act](/eng/acts/O-2.7). ([surintendant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[winding-up order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[winding-up order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a winding-up order
    made under the [Winding-up and Restructuring Act](/eng/acts/W-11) or
    a similar order made under any other Act of Parliament relating to
    insolvency or bankruptcy. ([ordonnance de mise en
    liquidation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Purposes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-400644 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purposes

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purposes of this Act
are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to authorize the Minister to provide protection in
  respect of certain mortgage or hypothecary insurance contracts in
  order to support the efficient functioning of the housing finance
  market and the stability of the financial system in Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to mitigate the risks arising from the provision of
  that protection.

## [Approved Mortgage Insurers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-400649 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  written request of a mortgage insurer, the Minister may, after
  consulting with the Superintendent, designate the mortgage insurer as
  an approved mortgage insurer for the purposes of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designation or its renewal are in effect for any
  period that the Minister specifies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of designation

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  consulting with the Superintendent, the Minister may, by notice sent
  to an approved mortgage insurer, suspend the approved mortgage
  insurer's designation if the Minister is of the opinion that the
  approved mortgage insurer has failed to comply with any provision of
  this Act or the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date and length

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must set out the effective date and the
  length of the suspension.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An approved mortgage insurer that has had its
  designation suspended must not insure any new mortgage or hypothecary
  loans until the suspension expires or is cancelled by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations during suspension

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For as long as the designation of an approved
  mortgage insurer is suspended, the approved mortgage insurer remains
  subject to the provisions of this Act and the regulations and must
  meet any conditions imposed by the Minister and satisfy any
  undertakings required by him or her.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of suspension

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may cancel a suspension if he or she
  considers it appropriate to do so.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of designation

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  consulting with the Superintendent, the Minister may, by notice sent
  to an approved mortgage insurer, cancel the approved mortgage
  insurer's designation if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is of the opinion that the approved
    mortgage insurer has failed to comply with any provision of this Act
    or the regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a winding-up order is made in respect of the
    approved mortgage insurer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the approved mortgage insurer ceases to be a
    mortgage insurer; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the approved mortgage insurer requests in writing
    that its designation be cancelled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date of cancellation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must set out the effective date of the
  cancellation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} As soon as feasible after sending the notice, the
  Minister must cause the name of the approved mortgage insurer whose
  designation is cancelled and the effective date of the cancellation to
  be published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations after cancellation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} After the designation of a corporation as an approved
  mortgage insurer is cancelled and for as long as the Minister has any
  actual or potential obligations under section 16 in respect of the
  policies the corporation issued or the contracts of insurance it
  entered into that could be deemed to be policies under section 19, the
  corporation remains subject to sections 8 and 15 and the regulations
  related to those sections, as if it were still an approved mortgage
  insurer, and it must meet any conditions imposed by the Minister and
  satisfy any undertakings required by him or her.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Company never designated

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  period referred to in subsection (2), a company that has never been
  designated as an approved mortgage insurer is subject to sections 8
  and 15 and the regulations related to those sections, as if it were an
  approved mortgage insurer, and must meet any conditions imposed by the
  Minister and satisfy any undertakings required by him or her.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The period is the length of time that the Minister
  has any actual or potential obligations under section 16 in respect of
  the contracts of insurance the company entered into that could be
  deemed to be policies under section 19.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adequacy of capital

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In order
  to mitigate the risks to Her Majesty that arise from the provision of
  mortgage or hypothecary insurance protection under this Act, an
  approved mortgage insurer must, in relation to its business and in
  addition to the capital it is required to maintain under the
  [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), maintain adequate
  capital.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adequacy determined by Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The adequate level of capital is to be determined by
  the Minister after considering the advice of the Superintendent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees for risk exposure

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An approved mortgage
insurer must, in accordance with the regulations, pay fees to the
Receiver General to compensate Her Majesty for Her exposure to the risks
arising from the provision of the mortgage or hypothecary insurance
protection under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of qualified mortgage lenders

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  approved mortgage insurer may designate as a qualified mortgage lender
  for the purposes of this Act any mortgage or hypothecary lender that
  meets prescribed criteria.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designation or its renewal are in effect for any
  period that the approved mortgage insurer specifies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of designation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The approved mortgage insurer may, by notice sent to
  a qualified mortgage lender that it has designated as such, cancel the
  qualified mortgage lender's designation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date of cancellation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The notice must set out the effective date of the
  cancellation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insurance restricted

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as permitted by the regulations, an approved mortgage insurer must not
  insure any risk unless the risk is in respect of an eligible mortgage
  loan made by a qualified mortgage lender that has been designated as
  such by the approved mortgage insurer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reinsurance restricted

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as permitted by the regulations, an approved
  mortgage insurer must not cause itself to be reinsured against any
  risk that it has undertaken under its policies or reinsure any risk
  that another insurer has undertaken under that insurer's contracts of
  insurance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsidiaries

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  approved mortgage insurer must obtain the Minister's approval before
  establishing or acquiring a subsidiary as defined in subsection 2(1)
  of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions and undertakings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An approved mortgage insurer must meet any conditions
  imposed by the Minister and satisfy any undertakings required by him
  or her in respect of its business activities with any of its
  subsidiaries.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions and undertakings --- business
with affiliates, etc.

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may impose conditions on and require undertakings from an
  approved mortgage insurer in respect of its business activities with a
  person or entity that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an affiliate, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
    [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), of the approved
    mortgage insurer;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a related party, within the meaning of section 518
    of that Act, of the approved mortgage insurer; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in a prescribed relationship with the approved
    mortgage insurer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions and undertakings ---
  increased risks

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may also impose conditions on and
  require undertakings from an approved mortgage insurer if the Minister
  is of the opinion that the activities of a person or entity referred
  to in subsection (1) could materially increase the risks to Her
  Majesty that arise from the provision of mortgage or hypothecary
  insurance protection under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation of approved mortgage insurer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An approved mortgage insurer must meet any conditions
  imposed on it and satisfy any undertakings required from it under
  subsection (1) or (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited policies --- affiliates, etc.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Except as permitted by
the regulations, an approved mortgage insurer must not be a party to a
policy under which the beneficiary is a person or entity referred to in
subsection 13(1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to retain information, books
and records

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  approved mortgage insurer must keep and retain books and records and
  retain information in respect of its business that are relevant for
  the purposes of this Act or that are prescribed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to provide information or
  copies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the request of the Minister or the Superintendent,
  an approved mortgage insurer must, without delay, provide the
  requester with any information or copies of any books or records that
  the approved mortgage insurer is required to retain.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister and the Superintendent may disclose to
  each other any information or copies of any books or records received
  under subsection (2) and may also disclose them to the Governor of the
  Bank of Canada, the Chief Executive Officer of the Canada Deposit
  Insurance Corporation and the Commissioner of the Financial Consumer
  Agency of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of conditions and
  undertakings

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may disclose to the Superintendent any
  conditions imposed by the Minister and any undertakings required by
  him or her under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public information

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An approved mortgage insurer must make available to
  the public prescribed books, records and information.

## [Mortgage or Hypothecary Insurance Protection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-400735 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's obligations

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (2) to (4) apply if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a winding-up order is made in respect of a
    corporation that had, before the making of the order, issued a
    policy to a qualified mortgage lender in respect of an eligible
    mortgage loan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the policyholder or a beneficiary under the policy
    has made a claim to the liquidator that is related to the policy;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the liquidator has allowed the claim and determined
    that it will not be paid in whole.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Event has occurred

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an event that is insured against under the policy
  has occurred before the conditions set out in subsection (1) are met,
  the Minister must make a payment out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund
  to the beneficiary under the policy in an amount equal to the amount
  calculated in accordance with section 22.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Event has not occurred

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an event that is insured against under the policy
  has not occurred before the conditions set out in subsection (1) are
  met, the Minister must elect to either

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make a payment out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund
    to an approved mortgage insurer or the Canada Mortgage and Housing
    Corporation, with the payee's consent, in an amount sufficient to
    allow the payee to enter into, with the policyholder, a contract of
    insurance that replaces the policy in accordance with subsection
    23(1), or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} satisfy, as required by subsection 24(1), future
    claims arising from the occurrence, if any, of such an event.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other election

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister makes an election under paragraph
  (3)(a), no further election is to be made, but the making of an
  election under paragraph (3)(b) does not preclude the making of an
  election under paragraph (3)(a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's discretion

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (2) and (3) apply if a
  winding-up order is made in respect of a corporation that had, before
  the making of the order, issued a policy to a qualified mortgage
  lender in respect of an eligible mortgage loan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Event has occurred

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an event that is insured against under the policy
  has occurred before the condition set out in subsection (1) is met,
  the Minister may make a payment referred to in subsection 16(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Event has not occurred

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an event that is insured against under the policy
  has not occurred before the condition set out in subsection (1) is
  met, the Minister may make a payment referred to in paragraph 16(3)(a)
  or satisfy future claims referred to in paragraph 16(3)(b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligible mortgage loan

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A contract of insurance
entered into by an approved mortgage insurer and a qualified mortgage
lender --- that has been designated as such by the approved mortgage
insurer --- in respect of a mortgage or hypothecary loan that is not an
eligible mortgage loan is deemed, for the purposes of sections 16, 17
and 20 to 25 to be a policy in respect of an eligible mortgage loan if
the Minister is satisfied that the approved mortgage insurer and the
qualified mortgage lender believed the loan to be an eligible mortgage
loan and that the ineligibility of the loan was not due to negligence or
bad faith by either of them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-existing contracts

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** If a winding-up order is
made in respect of a company, a contract of insurance that the company
entered into before the coming into force of this Act is deemed, in
respect of a mortgage or hypothecary loan that is the subject of the
contract of insurance, to be a policy issued to a qualified mortgage
lender in respect of an eligible mortgage loan for the purposes of
sections 16, 17 and 20 to 25, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty would have been bound to make a payment
  in respect of the contract of insurance had section 44 not come into
  force; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the mortgage or hypothecary loan meets the criteria
  established by regulations made under subsection 42(1) that relate to
  a guarantee of payment referred to in subsection 14(1) of the
  [National Housing Act](/eng/acts/N-11).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subrogation

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** If the Minister is
obligated, or decides under section 17, to make a payment referred to in
subsection 16(2) or paragraph 16(3)(a) or satisfy future claims in
accordance with paragraph 16(3)(b) in respect of a policy, Her Majesty
is subrogated to all the rights the corporation had under the policy
immediately before the making of the winding-up order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subrogation --- subsection 16(2)

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister is obligated, or decides under section 17, to make a payment
  referred to in subsection 16(2) in respect of a policy, Her Majesty is
  subrograted, once the payment is made, to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the rights that the policyholder or the beneficiary
    under the policy has to make a claim related to the policy;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a right to any proceeds payable in respect of the
    policy as a result of the making of the winding-up order; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the lender's rights under the loan covered by the
    policy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subrogation --- subsection 16(3)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is obligated, or decides under
  section 17, to make a payment referred to in paragraph 16(3)(a) or
  satisfy future claims in accordance with paragraph 16(3)(b) in respect
  of a policy, Her Majesty is subrogated to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the rights that the policyholder has to make a
    claim related to the policy; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a right to any proceeds paid or payable in respect
    of the policy as a result of the making of the winding-up order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subrogation --- subsection 24(1)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister makes a payment under subsection
  24(1) in respect of a policy, Her Majesty is subrogated to the
  lender's rights under the loan covered by the policy.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of amount --- event has
occurred

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The amount for the
purpose of subsection 16(2) is the amount determined by the formula

::: {#400781 .Section}
A -- B -- C

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the total of the amounts payable to the beneficiary under the
    policy immediately before the winding-up order is made;

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the amount of any proceeds the beneficiary has received in
    respect of the policy since the making of the winding-up order; and

[C]{.dfn}
:   is 10% of the original principal amount of the first eligible
    mortgage loan in respect of which the premium for the insurance was
    paid.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacement insurance

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  insurance referred to in paragraph 16(3)(a) must be sufficient to
  cover the amount of benefits determined by the formula

  ::: {#400789 .Subsection}
  A -- B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of benefits payable under the replaced policy; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is 10% of the original principal amount of the first eligible
      mortgage loan in respect of which the premium for the original
      insurance was paid.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No liability --- replaced policy

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister makes a payment referred to in
  paragraph 16(3)(a), the Minister is not liable to make any other
  payment under this Act in respect of the replaced policy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection for replacement insurance
  contracts

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the payment is made to an approved mortgage
  insurer, the contract of insurance entered into as a result of the
  payment is a policy for the purposes of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations --- replacement policy
  payments

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite the formulas set out in section 22 and
  subsections 23(1) and 24(1), the Governor in Council may make
  regulations prescribing the manner of calculating the amount of any
  payment referred to in subsection 16(2), paragraph 16(3)(a) or
  subsection 24(1) in respect of the policy referred to in subsection
  (3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Satisfaction of future claims

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister is obligated, or decides under section 17, to satisfy future
  claims in accordance with paragraph 16(3)(b) and a claim is
  subsequently made to the Minister by reason of the occurrence of an
  event that was insured against under the policy, the Minister must
  make a payment out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund to the beneficiary
  under the policy in an amount equal to the amount determined by the
  formula

  ::: {#400802 .Subsection}
  A -- B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the total of the amounts that would have been payable to the
      beneficiary under the policy; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is 10% of the original principal amount of the first eligible
      mortgage loan in respect of which the premium for the insurance
      was paid.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is not obligated to pay the amount
  determined in accordance with the formula unless he or she is
  satisfied that the beneficiary would have been entitled to a payment
  under the policy and that the policyholder has discharged its
  obligations under the policy and the obligations it would have had
  under the policy had the winding-up order not been made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affiliates, etc.

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** A payment referred to in
subsection 16(2), paragraph 16(3)(a) or subsection 24(1) must not be
made in respect of a policy if the corporation in respect of which the
winding-up order was made was, immediately before that order was made, a
party to the policy in contravention of section 14.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of payment --- subsection 16(2)

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  payment required to be made under subsection 16(2) must be made before
  the expiry of two years or of any other prescribed period beginning on
  the day on which the conditions referred to in paragraphs 16(1)(a)
  to (c) have been met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of payment --- subsection 24(1)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A payment required to be made under subsection 24(1)
  must be made before the expiry of two years or of any other prescribed
  period beginning on the day on which the Minister is satisfied that
  the conditions referred to in subsection 24(2) have been met.

## [Protected Loan Limit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-400815 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** The aggregate outstanding
principal amount of the following loans must not at any time exceed
\$300,000,000,000 or any other amount that is authorized for the
purposes of this section under an appropriation Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all mortgage or hypothecary loans that are insured by
  corporations that are or were approved mortgage insurers; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all mortgage or hypothecary loans that are insured
  under contracts of insurance that could be deemed to be policies under
  section 19 and that are entered into by companies that have never been
  designated as approved mortgage insurers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation of limit

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by notice in writing, at any time allocate any portion
  of the amount applicable under section 27 to any approved mortgage
  insurer in order to support the efficient functioning of the housing
  finance market and the stability of the financial system in Canada and
  to mitigate the risks to Her Majesty that arise from the provision of
  mortgage or hypothecary insurance protection under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An approved mortgage insurer to whom an amount is
  allocated must not insure any new mortgage or hypothecary loans if
  doing so would cause the outstanding principal amount of all mortgage
  or hypothecary loans that are insured by it to exceed that allocated
  amount.

## [Examination and Reporting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-400825 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The Superintendent must,
from time to time, make or cause to be made any examination and inquiry
into the business and affairs of a corporation that the Superintendent
considers to be necessary or expedient to determine whether the
corporation is complying with sections 5 to 8, 10 to 15 and 28 and the
regulations related to those sections and may, after the conclusion of
each examination and inquiry, report on it to the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  corporation is not, or is not likely to remain, in compliance with any
  of sections 5 to 8, 10 to 15 and 28 or the regulations related to
  those sections, the Superintendent must notify the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to management and board of
  directors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a corporation is not, or is not likely to remain,
  in compliance with any of sections 5 to 8, 10 to 15 and 28 or the
  regulations related to those sections, the Superintendent must notify
  the management or board of directors of the corporation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court order to comply

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** If a corporation is not
in compliance with a provision of this Act or the regulations, the
Minister may apply to a court for an order directing the corporation to
comply with the provision. On such an application the court may make the
order and any other order that it thinks fit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister --- section 27

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The Superintendent must,
from time to time but at least once in each calendar year, notify the
Minister of the aggregate outstanding principal amount referred to in
section 27.

## [Sanctions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-400837 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** Every corporation that,
without reasonable cause, contravenes any provision of this Act or the
regulations is guilty of an offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False or misleading information

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
knowingly provides false or misleading information in relation to any
matter relating to a provision of this Act or the regulations is guilty
of an offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who is guilty of an offence under this Act is liable to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, a fine of not more
    than \$5,000,000; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, a fine of not more than
    \$500,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order to comply

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a corporation has been convicted of an offence
  under this Act, the court may, in addition to any punishment it may
  otherwise impose, order it to comply with the provisions of this Act
  and the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional fine

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a corporation has been convicted of an offence
  under this Act, the court may, if it is satisfied that as a result of
  the commission of the offence the corporation acquired any monetary
  benefits, order the corporation to pay, despite the maximum amount of
  any fine that may otherwise be imposed under this Act, an additional
  fine in an amount equal to three times the court's estimation of the
  amount of those monetary benefits.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of officers, directors, etc.

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** If a corporation commits
an offence under this Act, any of its officers, directors, agents or
mandataries who directed, authorized, assented to, acquiesced in or
participated in the commission of the offence is a party to and guilty
of the offence and liable, whether or not the corporation has been
prosecuted or convicted, to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, a fine of not more than
  \$1,000,000 or imprisonment for a term of not more than five years, or
  both; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, a fine of not more than
  \$100,000 or imprisonment for a term of not more than one year, or
  both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period or prescription

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Proceedings by way of summary
  conviction in respect of an offence under this Act may be commenced at
  any time within, but not later than, two years after the day on which
  the subject matter of the proceedings became known to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document appearing to have been issued by the
  Minister certifying the day on which the subject matter of any
  proceedings became known to the Minister is admissible in evidence
  without proof of the signature or official character of the person
  appearing to have signed it and is, in the absence of evidence to the
  contrary, proof of the matter asserted in it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal with leave

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** An appeal lies to the
court of appeal of a province from any order made under this Act by a
court of that province, only with leave of the court of appeal in
accordance with the rules applicable to that court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery and application of fines

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** All fines payable under
this Act are recoverable and enforceable, with costs, at the suit of Her
Majesty, instituted by the Attorney General of Canada, and, when
recovered, belong to Her Majesty.

## [Legal Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-400864 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No liability

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** No action lies against
Her Majesty, the Minister, the Superintendent or any employee or agent
of Her Majesty for anything done or omitted to be done in good faith in
the exercise or performance of any powers, duties or functions that
under this Act are intended or authorized to be exercised or performed.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-400867 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations for carrying out the purposes and provisions of
this Act, including regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} imposing conditions on an approved mortgage insurer
  in order to mitigate the risks to Her Majesty that arise from the
  provision of mortgage or hypothecary insurance protection under this
  Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the payment of fees under section 9,
  including the amount of those fees or the manner of calculating them;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the designation, by approved mortgage
  insurers, of mortgage or hypothecary lenders as qualified mortgage
  lenders;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the exceptions to each of the
  requirements under section 11;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the exceptions to each of the
  prohibitions under section 14;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} prescribing, for the purposes of subsection 15(1),
  the manner in which books and records are to be kept and retained and
  the manner in which information is to be retained;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} prescribing, for the purposes of subsection 15(5),
  the manner in which the books, records and information are to be made
  available to the public; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that is required or authorized
  by this Act to be prescribed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial regulations

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, after consulting with the Governor of the Bank of Canada
  and the Superintendent, make regulations establishing criteria that a
  mortgage or hypothecary loan must meet in order for it to be an
  eligible mortgage loan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criterion  --- guarantee

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Any criterion established by a regulation made
  under subsection (1) that relates to a guarantee of payment referred
  to in subsection 14(1) of the [National Housing Act](/eng/acts/N-11)
  may apply to an existing insured mortgage or hypothecary loan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial regulations --- agreements
  and undertakings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may make regulations specifying, for the
  purposes of paragraph 43(k), agreements entered into by Her Majesty
  and a company in respect of mortgage or hypothecary insurance, and
  undertakings given by a company under an agreement as defined in
  section 43.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under this section comes into force
  on the day on which it is published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) or on any later day that is
  specified in the regulation unless the regulation provides otherwise
  and it gives effect to a budgetary or other public announcement, in
  which case the regulation must not come into force before the day on
  which the announcement was made.

## [Termination of Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-400889 .Part}

Definition of [[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** In sections 44 to 48,
[[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the agreement entered into by Her Majesty and The
  Mortgage Insurance Company of Canada that was made on January 1, 1991
  and subsequently assigned to GE Capital Mortgage Insurance Company
  (Canada);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Management Agreement entered into by Her Majesty,
  The Mortgage Insurance Company of Canada, Royal Bank Investment
  Management Inc., and Royal Trust Corporation of Canada that was made
  on November 1, 1991;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the agreement entered into by Her Majesty and AIG
  United Guaranty Mortgage Insurance Company Canada that was made on
  November 21, 2006;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Custody and Security Agreement entered into by
  Her Majesty, AIG United Guaranty Mortgage Insurance Company Canada and
  RBC Dexia Investor Services Trust that was made on November 21, 2006;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Management Agreement entered into by Her Majesty,
  AIG United Guaranty Mortgage Insurance Company Canada and RBC Dominion
  Securities Inc. that was made on November 21, 2006;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the agreement entered into by Her Majesty and PMI
  Mortgage Insurance Company Canada that was made on July 12, 2007;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Custody and Security Agreement entered into by
  Her Majesty, PMI Mortgage Insurance Company Canada and Citibank Canada
  that was made on July 12, 2007;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the Management Agreement entered into by Her Majesty,
  PMI Mortgage Insurance Company Canada and TD Asset Management Inc.
  that was made on July 12, 2007;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Management Agreement entered into by Her Majesty,
  Canada Guaranty Mortgage Insurance Company and Phillips, Hager & North
  Investment Management Ltd. that was made on May 5, 2010;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} any reinsurance agreement to which Her Majesty and a
  company or a predecessor to a company are parties;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} any agreement or undertaking specified in regulations
  made under subsection 42(2); or

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} any amendment to an agreement referred to in any of
  paragraphs (a) to (k).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements are terminated

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** The agreements are
terminated and all obligations and liabilities arising out of the
agreements and all rights acquired under them are extinguished.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No liability

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** No action or other
proceeding, including any action or proceeding in restitution, or for
damages of any kind, that is based on or is in relation to any
agreement, lies or may be instituted by anyone against Her Majesty or
any minister or any employee or agent of Her Majesty, or any person
engaged to provide advice or services to Her Majesty in relation to any
agreement, for anything done or omitted to be done or for anything
purported to have been done or omitted to be done, in the exercise or
performance of their powers, duties and functions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No compensation

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** No one is entitled to any
compensation from Her Majesty in connection with the coming into force
of section 44.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to send statement

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  30 days after the day on which this Act comes into force or any longer
  period that the Minister allows, each company must send to the
  Minister a written statement indicating, as of the time immediately
  before this Act comes into force, the following amounts:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of cash on deposit in any custodial
    account, trust account, guarantee fund or similar account or fund
    established under an agreement in respect of the company; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the current market value of all securities held in
    those accounts or funds.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audit of statement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may cause the statement to be audited.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ownership

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
the cash on deposit in, and the securities held in, any custodial
account, trust account, guarantee fund or similar account or fund
established under an agreement in respect of a company belong to the
company on the coming into force of this Act.
