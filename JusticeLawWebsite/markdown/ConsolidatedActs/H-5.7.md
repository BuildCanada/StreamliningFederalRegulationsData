## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-255856 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Board of Appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board of Appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Employment
    Insurance Board of Appeal established under section 43.01. ([Conseil
    d'appel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Employment
    Insurance Commission continued by section
    20. ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Employment
    and Social Development. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[partner entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[partner entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a department or any other body, other than a
      parent Crown corporation, referred to in a schedule to the
      [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a [[parent Crown
      corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and any [[wholly-owned
      subsidiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms are defined in
      section 83 of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the government of a province, a public body
      created under the law of a province, or a municipality;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a council, government or other entity authorized
      to act on behalf of either a [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
      defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), or
      a First Nation, Aboriginal people or Aboriginal organization that
      is a party to a land claim agreement or any other treaty,
      self-government agreement or settlement agreement; and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a non-profit corporation or public body
      performing a function for an entity referred to in any of
      paragraphs (a) to (d). ([entité partenaire]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Social Security Tribunal
    established under section 44. ([Tribunal]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Department of Employment and Social Development]{.HTitleText1} {#h-255870 .Part}

### [Continuation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-255871 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Department continued

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Department of Human Resources and Skills Development is continued
  under the name of the Department of Employment and Social Development
  over which the Minister of Employment and Social Development,
  appointed by commission under the Great Seal, shall preside.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employment and Social Development
  Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The expressions "Employment and Social Development
  Canada" and "Emploi et Développement social Canada" in any document
  issued or executed in relation to the powers, duties and functions
  conferred on the Minister by this or any other Act are deemed to be
  references to the "Department of Employment and Social Development"
  and the "ministère de l'Emploi et du Développement social",
  respectively.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister holds office during pleasure and has the
  management and direction of the Department.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 34, s. 3
- 2013, c. 40, s. 208
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy Minister

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may appoint an officer called the Deputy Minister
  of Employment and Social Development to hold office during pleasure
  and to be the deputy head of the Department.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Associate Deputy Ministers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may appoint one or more

  hold office during pleasure and each shall have the rank and status of
  a deputy head of a department and exercise, under the Deputy Minister
  of Employment and Social Development, the powers and perform the
  duties and functions as deputies of the Minister and otherwise that

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may designate the Deputy
  Minister of Employment and Social Development or one of the Associate
  Deputy Ministers to be Deputy Minister of Labour.

### [Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister]{.HTitleText2} {#h-255890 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister's powers, duties and functions extend to and include all
  matters relating to human resources and skills development in Canada
  or the social development of Canada over which Parliament has
  jurisdiction and which are not by law assigned to any other Minister,
  department, board or agency of the Government of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exercise of powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall exercise the powers and perform
  the duties and functions

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} relating to human resources and skills development
    with a view to improving the standard of living and quality of life
    of all Canadians by promoting a highly skilled and mobile workforce
    and an efficient and inclusive labour market; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} relating to social development with a view to
    promoting social well-being and income security.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 34, s. 5
- 2012, c. 19, s. 687
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service delivery to public

- **[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may provide support for service delivery to the public and,
  in so doing, he or she may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide the following services and facilities to
    any partner entity and perform activities related to those services
    and facilities:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} services and facilities in support of its
      delivery of its programs and services, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any other service or facility that is authorized
      by the Governor in Council;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the following services to any department or
    body listed in Schedule I, I.1 or II to the [Financial
    Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) and to any other partner entity
    authorized by the Governor in Council and perform activities related
    to those services:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the administration of websites that are
      accessible to the public,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the administration of social media accounts,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the publication of mobile applications on
      third-party mobile application stores and on the Government of
      Canada website,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} online citizen engagement, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any other related electronic or digital service;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} administer the Government of Canada website; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide information services relating to programs
    and services of the Government of Canada and of any other person,
    organization or government authorized by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization --- Governor in Council

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may provide any service or facility that
  is described in subsection (1), other than the services described in
  paragraph (1)(c) or (d), and that is authorized by the Governor in
  Council to any other person, organization or government that the
  Governor in Council authorizes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exercise of powers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, in supporting service delivery to
  the public, do so with a view to better serving the needs of
  Canadians.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2018, c. 12, s. 269

- **[[5.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may charge for any service or facility provided under
  subsection 5.1(1), except the services provided under paragraph
  5.1(1)(c) or (d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Spending authority

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may spend revenues obtained under
  subsection (1).

### [Committees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-255941 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committees

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may establish advisory and other committees and provide for
  their membership, duties, functions and operation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Members of a committee shall be paid, in connection
  with their work for the committee, the remuneration that may be fixed
  by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Travel, living and other expenses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Members of a committee are entitled to be reimbursed,
  in accordance with Treasury Board directives, for the travel, living
  and other expenses incurred in connection with their work for the
  committee while absent, in the case of full-time members, from their
  ordinary place of work or, in the case of part-time members, from
  their ordinary place of residence.

### [General Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-255949 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
facilitating the formulation, coordination and implementation of any
program or policy relating to the powers, duties and functions conferred
by this Act, the Minister may enter into agreements with a province or a
provincial public body, financial institutions and other persons or
bodies that the Minister considers appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
authorize the Minister of Labour, the Commission or any other person or
body, or member of a class of persons or bodies, to exercise any power
or perform any duty or function of the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oaths and affidavits

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A person or member of a
class of persons employed by the Department and authorized by the Deputy
Minister and any other person or member of a class of persons authorized
by the Minister may, in the course of their duties, administer oaths and
take and receive affidavits, declarations and affirmations for the
purposes of or incidental to the performance of their duties, and every
person so authorized has for those purposes all the powers of a
commissioner for administering oaths or taking affidavits.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees for services and use of facilities

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, subject to any regulations that the Treasury Board may
  make for the purposes of this section, fix the fees to be paid for a
  service or the use of a facility provided by the Minister, the
  Department, the Commission or any other board or agency of the
  Government of Canada for which the Minister has responsibility.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount not to exceed cost

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Fees that are fixed under subsection (1) may not
  exceed the cost to Her Majesty in right of Canada of providing the
  service or the use of the facility.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees for products, rights and privileges

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, subject
to any regulations that the Treasury Board may make for the purposes of
this section, fix the fees to be paid for products, rights and
privileges provided by the Minister, the Department, the Commission or
any other board or agency of the Government of Canada for which the
Minister has responsibility.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees for regulatory processes and
approvals

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, subject to any regulations that the Treasury Board may
  make for the purposes of this section, fix the fees to be paid for
  regulatory processes or approvals provided by the Minister, the
  Department, the Commission or any other board or agency of the
  Government of Canada for which the Minister has responsibility.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Fees that are fixed under subsection (1) may not in
  the aggregate exceed an amount sufficient to compensate Her Majesty in
  right of Canada for any reasonable outlays incurred by Her Majesty for
  providing the regulatory processes or approvals.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall, within thirty days after the day on which the Minister
  fixes a fee under any of sections 13 to 15, publish the fee in the
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to Scrutiny Committee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A fee fixed under any of sections 13 to 15 shall
  stand referred to the Committee referred to in section 19 of the
  [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) to be reviewed and
  scrutinized as if it were a statutory instrument.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to make regulations

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Treasury Board may
make regulations for the purposes of sections 13 to 15.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Minister of Labour]{.HTitleText1} {#h-255975 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister of Labour

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  Minister of Labour may be appointed by commission under the Great Seal
  to hold office during pleasure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The powers, duties and functions of the Minister of
  Labour extend to and include all matters relating to labour over which
  Parliament has jurisdiction and which are not by law assigned to any
  other department, board or agency of the Government of Canada. They
  are to be exercised with the objective of promoting safe, healthy,
  fair, stable, cooperative and productive workplaces.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No Minister of Labour appointed

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If no Minister of Labour is appointed under
  subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall exercise the powers and perform
    the duties and functions of the Minister of Labour; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} every reference to the Minister of Labour in any
    Act of Parliament or in any order, regulation or other instrument
    made under an Act of Parliament shall, unless the context otherwise
    requires, be read as a reference to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of departmental services and
  facilities

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Labour shall make use of the services
  and facilities of the Department and may authorize employees of the
  Department to exercise any power or perform any duty or function of
  the Minister of Labour.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Programs

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Labour
may, in exercising the powers and performing the duties and functions
assigned by subsection 18(2), establish and implement programs designed
to support projects or other activities that promote safe, healthy,
fair, stable, cooperative and productive workplaces, and the Minister
may make grants and contributions in support of the programs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Business numbers --- Minister of Labour

**[[19.01]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19.01 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of
Labour may collect any [[business number]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
defined in subsection 248(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3),
and use it as an identifier for the purposes of the administration or
enforcement of any Act, program or activity in respect of which the
administration or enforcement is the responsibility of that Minister.

- 2018, c. 12, s. 273
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Social Insurance Numbers --- Minister of

**[[19.02]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19.02 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of

their identity for the purposes of the administration or enforcement of

::: HistoricalNote
- [2021, c. 23, s. 262](#1307661-1311725){.wb-lbx

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crediting to Labour Cooperation Treaties
Account

- **[[19.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All moneys received by Her Majesty
  as a result of a monetary assessment by a panel determination under a
  treaty, or chapter of a treaty, respecting labour cooperation referred
  to in the schedule shall be paid into the Consolidated Revenue Fund
  and, if required under the treaty, credited to a special account in
  the accounts of Canada to be known as the Labour Cooperation Treaties
  Account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional credits to Account

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an amount is assessed against Canada by a panel
  determination under a treaty, or chapter of a treaty, respecting
  labour cooperation referred to in the schedule, an amount equal to
  that amount shall be credited to the Account if the Account has been
  designated for the purposes of the treaty in accordance with its
  provisions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On April 1 of every year there shall be credited to
  the Account an amount representing interest, at the rate fixed for the
  purposes of subsection 21(2) of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11), on the balance to the credit of the Account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of moneys

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} One or more amounts not exceeding in total the amount
  standing in the Account in relation to a treaty, including interest,
  may, on the requisition of the Minister of Labour, be paid out of the
  Consolidated Revenue Fund in accordance with the provisions of the
  treaty. The amount of all such payments is to be charged to the
  Account.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 16, s. 50
- 2014, c. 14, ss. 49, 51, c. 28, s. 57

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Canada Employment Insurance Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-256004 .Part}

### [Continuation of the Commission]{.HTitleText2} {#h-256005 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Canada Employment Insurance Commission is continued. It consists of
  four commissioners.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissioners

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The four commissioners shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Deputy Minister of Employment and Social
    Development, who shall be the Chairperson of the Commission;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an Associate Deputy Minister of Employment and
    Social Development, who shall be the Vice-Chairperson of the
    Commission;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person appointed by the Governor in Council after
    consultation with organizations representative of workers; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a person appointed by the Governor in Council after
    consultation with organizations representative of employers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employment Insurance Canada

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The expressions "Employment Insurance Canada" and
  "[Assurance-emploi Canada]{.otherLang lang="fr"}" in any document
  issued or executed in relation to the matters set out in section 24
  are deemed to be references to the "Canada Employment Insurance
  Commission" and the "[Commission de l'assurance-emploi du
  Canada]{.otherLang lang="fr"}", respectively.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2005, c. 34, s. 20
- 2010, c. 12, s. 1723
- 2013, c. 40, s. 210

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure, remuneration and conditions of
office

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Commissioners, other than the
  Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall be appointed by the Governor in Council to
    hold office during good behaviour for a term not exceeding five
    years but may be removed by the Governor in Council at any time for
    cause;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are eligible for reappointment on the expiration of
    each term of office;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} shall receive the remuneration that is fixed by the
    Governor in Council;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} are deemed to be employed in the public service for
    the purposes of the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and to be employed in the federal public
    administration for the purposes of the [Government Employees
    Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and any regulations made pursuant

### [Social Insurance Number]{.HTitleText2} {#h-256086 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

- **[[28.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any person who is employed in
  insurable employment as defined in the [Employment Insurance
  Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6) or who is a self-employed person in respect of
  whom Part VII.1 of that Act applies must be registered with the
  Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Register

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall maintain a register containing
  the names of the persons referred to in subsection (1) and any other
  information that it determines is necessary to accurately identify
  them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Social Insurance Number

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall assign to each person registered
  with it a number that is suitable for use as a file number or account
  number or for data processing purposes, and that number is the
  person's Social Insurance Number for any purpose for which a Social
  Insurance Number is required.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Social Insurance Number card

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may issue to each person registered
  with it a card containing the person's name and Social Insurance
  Number.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Social Insurance Register

- **[[28.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may maintain a Social
  Insurance Register containing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the names of persons registered in the registry
    referred to in section 28.1;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the names of persons to whom a Social Insurance
    Number has been assigned under the [Canada Pension
    Plan](/eng/acts/C-8); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the names of persons for whom an application has
    been made to the Commission for a Social Insurance Number.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Social Insurance Register may, subject to any
  regulations that the Governor in Council may make in that regard,
  contain any other information in addition to the names and Social
  Insurance Numbers of persons that is necessary to accurately identify
  all persons who are registered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuing number and card

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When a Social Insurance Number is assigned to a
  person by the Commission in the course of maintaining the registers
  mentioned in this section and section 28.1, the Commission may issue a
  Social Insurance Number card to that person and that number is the
  person's Social Insurance Number for all purposes for which a Social
  Insurance Number is required.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may, with the approval of the Governor
  in Council, make regulations respecting

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the registration of persons under this section and
    section 28.1, including applications for that registration;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the assignment and use of Social Insurance Numbers;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the issuance, custody, production and use of Social
    Insurance Number cards;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} periods of validity, and the extension of those
    periods, of Social Insurance Numbers and Social Insurance Number
    cards; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the replacement of Social Insurance Number cards
    that have been lost, destroyed or defaced.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Availability of information --- social
  insurance registers

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may make available any information
  contained in the registers maintained under this section and section
  28.1 that the Commission considers necessary for the accurate
  identification of persons and for the effective use by those persons
  of Social Insurance Numbers to any persons that the Commission thinks
  appropriate to accomplish that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Secondary release of information

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Information obtained under subsection (5) shall not
  be made available to a person or body unless the Commission considers
  it advisable, the information is made available for the same purpose
  and it is subject to conditions agreed on by the Commission and the
  person who obtained the information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements still in force

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (6) does not apply to information made
  available in accordance with an agreement entered into by the
  Commission before the coming into force of this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New Social Insurance Number

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} A person who has been assigned a Social Insurance
  Number may subsequently be assigned a new Social Insurance Number, in
  accordance with and subject to any regulations that the Commission may
  make, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number first assigned has been assigned to
    another person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} wrongful use by another person of the number first
    assigned has created a situation in which the person to whom the
    number was first assigned is or may be caused embarrassment or
    hardship; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} there are other special or unusual circumstances
    that would make the issuance of a new number desirable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voiding

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} When a new Social Insurance Number is assigned to a
  person, any number previously assigned to that person becomes void.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}More than one number assigned

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} If a person has inadvertently been assigned more
  than one Social Insurance Number, the Commission shall determine which
  number is the official number and shall void the others.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2012, c. 19, s. 304

**[[28.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28.3 .sectionLabel}** When the name of a

because of marriage or otherwise, the person shall inform the Commission
of their new name within 60 days after the day on which the change of
name becomes effective, unless they have already so informed another
authority empowered to receive that information.

- 2012, c. 19, s. 304

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibitions

- **[[28.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28.4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall, knowing that they already have a Social
    Insurance Number, make an application to be again assigned a Social
    Insurance Number, whether the person gives information that is the
    same as or different from that contained in their previous
    application on which the Social Insurance Number had been assigned;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall, with intent to defraud or deceive any
    person, present, loan or use a Social Insurance Number or Social
    Insurance Number card;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} shall, without the authority of the Commission,
    manufacture a Social Insurance Number card or a substantially
    similar card, or duplicate a Social Insurance Number card, except by
    making a paper photocopy for record purposes only; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} shall, without the authority of the Commission,
    sell a Social Insurance Number, a Social Insurance Number card or a
    substantially similar card.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes subsection (1) is guilty
  of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine of not more
  than \$1,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than one year,
  or to both.

- 2012, c. 19, s. 304

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** This Part sets out the
rules that apply to the protection and the making available of
information that is obtained by the Minister or the Commission under a
program or prepared from that information. This Part also sets out
principles for the use of information for research purposes.

  the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or paragraph 40(1)(a) of the
  [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it may be made available under any of paragraphs
  33.1(a) to (c) of the [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment --- Executive Head

- **[[43.02]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43.02
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Executive Head of the Board of
  Appeal is to be appointed by the Governor in Council, on the
  recommendation of the Minister after consultation by the Minister with
  the Commission, to hold office during pleasure on a full-time basis
  for a renewable term of not more than five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment --- regional coordinators

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the recommendation of the Minister after
  consultation by the Minister with the Commission, the Governor in
  Council may appoint not more than six regional coordinators to hold
  office during pleasure on a full-time or part-time basis for a
  renewable term of not more than five years that will ensure, to the
  extent possible, the end in any one year of the terms of office of not
  more than one half of the regional coordinators.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Diversity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Recommendations under subsection (2) are to be made
  having regard to the importance of having regional coordinators who
  are representative of the diversity of Canadian society.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition

- **[[43.03]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43.03
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Board of Appeal consists of the
  Executive Head, the regional coordinators and the following members:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} members to be appointed by the Governor in Council,
    on the recommendation of the Minister after consultation by the
    Minister with the Commission, to hold office during pleasure on a
    part-time basis for a renewable term of not more than five years;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} members to be appointed by the Commission to hold
    office during pleasure on a part-time basis for a renewable term of
    not more than five years, each of whom must be an
    [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, within the meaning of subsection
    2(1) of the [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6), or, at the
    time of the appointment, a representative of such employers; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} members to be appointed by the Commission to hold
    office during pleasure on a part-time basis for a renewable term of
    not more than five years, each of whom must be an [[insured
    person]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, within the meaning of subsection
    2(1) of the [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6), or, at the
    time of the appointment, a representative of such insured persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms of members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Members are to be appointed for terms that will
  ensure, to the extent possible, the end in any one year of the terms
  of office of not more than one half of the members appointed under
  each of paragraphs (1)(a) to (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equal number

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} To the extent possible, an equal number of members is
  to be appointed under each of paragraphs (1)(a) to (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regional representation and diversity

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Recommendations under paragraph (1)(a) and
  appointments under paragraphs (1)(b) and (c) are to be made having
  regard to the importance of regional representation in the membership
  of the Board of Appeal as well as the importance of having members who
  are representative of the diversity of Canadian society.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Completion of ongoing matters

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person who ceases to be a member for any reason
  other than removal may, at the request of the Executive Head, within
  12 weeks after ceasing to be a member, carry out and complete any
  duties and functions that they would otherwise have had in connection
  with any matter that came before the Board of Appeal while they were
  still a member and in respect of which there was any proceeding in
  which they participated as a member. For that purpose, the person is
  deemed to be a member.

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Executive Head has supervision

  particular, the Executive Head is responsible for the management of
  the regional coordinators and the members of the Board of Appeal,
  including providing them with training and guidance with respect to
  their duties and functions and evaluating their performance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report on overall performance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Executive Head is to report regularly to the
  Commission on the overall performance of the Board of Appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regional coordinators

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Regional coordinators assist the Executive Head and
  perform any duties and functions that the Executive Head may assign.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation --- regional coordinators

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Executive Head may delegate to any regional
  coordinator any of the Executive Head's powers, duties or functions,
  except the power to delegate under this subsection, the duty to report
  under subsection (2), the duty to select members under subsection
  43.05(1) and the duty to select a regional coordinator under
  subsection 43.05(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members assigned to region

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Executive Head is to assign each member of the
  Board of Appeal to a particular region to hear appeals in that region,
  taking into account the member's ordinary place of residence. However,
  a member may be selected to hear appeals in other regions for
  operational reasons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence --- Executive Head

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  Executive Head or a vacancy in that office, the Chairperson of the
  Commission may authorize a person to act as Executive Head, on any
  terms and conditions that the Chairperson may specify, but no person
  so authorized has authority to act for a term of more than 90 days
  without the Governor in Council's approval given on the recommendation
  of the Minister after consultation by the Minister with the
  Commission.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} hear an application to reopen an appeal that has

## [PART 6]{.HLabel1}[Electronic Administration or Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-256524 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[70.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70.1 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to
any Act, program or activity whose administration or enforcement is the
responsibility of the Minister, the Minister of Labour or the
Commission.

- **[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, if the
  Minister, the Minister of Labour or the Commission is responsible for
  an Act, program or activity, that responsible Minister or Commission
  may administer or enforce it electronically, including for the
  purposes of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} creating, communicating, making available,
    collecting, receiving, storing, or managing or otherwise dealing
    with documents or information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} providing any services, benefits or other
    assistance;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} providing notifications;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} verifying the identity of a person or entity;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} entering into agreements or arrangements; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} making, receiving or verifying an electronic
    signature.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister and the Commission must not require
  persons or entities to apply electronically for, or receive
  electronically, services, benefits or other assistance except for
  classes of persons or entities and those services, benefits or other
  assistance that are prescribed by the regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic manner of filing documents

- **[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  another manner of filing a document or information is expressly
  required by a provision of an Act or a regulation, or by a term or
  condition of a program, the filing of an electronic version of the
  document or information is to be considered as the filing of a
  document or information in accordance with the provision or the term
  or condition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to prescribe form or manner of
  filing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A provision of an Act or a regulation, or a term or
  condition of a program, that provides for a power to issue, prescribe
  or in any other manner establish a form or to establish the manner of
  filing a document or information includes the power to do so with
  respect to an electronic document or information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written documents or information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A requirement under a provision of an Act or a
  regulation, or a term or condition of a program, that a document be in
  writing or information be provided in writing is satisfied by an
  electronic document or information if the electronic document or
  information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is readable or perceivable so as to be usable for
    subsequent reference;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is in a format that does not prevent it from being
    retained by the recipient; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} meets the prescribed requirements, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signatures

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A requirement under a provision of an Act or a
  regulation, or a term or condition of a program, to provide a
  signature is satisfied by an electronic signature if the electronic
  signature

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is reliable for the purposes for which it is
    required;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is reliably linked with the electronic document in
    respect of which the signature is required; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} meets the prescribed requirements, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of
  [[filing]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[filing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  includes all manner of transmitting, regardless of how it is
  designated.

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date and hour when an electronic document or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} whether an electronic document must be signed

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} establishing criteria for the validity of an
    agreement or arrangement entered into electronically;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} respecting terms and conditions for providing or
    receiving electronically services, benefits or other assistance,
    including the payment of amounts electronically;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} respecting the technology to be used and the
    process to be followed for verifying electronically the identity of
    any person or entity;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} respecting the establishment and operation of
    electronic systems or any other technology to be used in the
    administration or enforcement of an Act or regulation to which this
    Part applies or in the administration or enforcement of a program or
    activity to which this Part applies, and respecting the manner in
    which and the extent to which any provision of that Act or
    regulation, or any term or condition of that program applies to the
    electronic systems; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that by this Part is to be
    prescribed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under this section may incorporate
  by reference any document, regardless of its source, either as it
  exists on a particular date or as it is amended from time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessibility

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister, the Minister of Labour or the
  Commission, as the case may be, shall ensure that any document that is
  incorporated by reference in a regulation in respect of which the
  administration or the enforcement is their responsibility is
  accessible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person is not liable to be found guilty of an
  offence for any contravention in respect of which a document that is
  incorporated by reference in the regulation is relevant unless, at the
  time of the alleged contravention, the document was accessible as
  required by subsection (3) or it was otherwise accessible to the
  person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No registration or publication

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a document that is
  incorporated by reference in the regulation is not required to be
  transmitted for registration or published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) by reason only that it is
  incorporated by reference.
