## Canada-Costa Rica Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act {#canada-costa-rica-free-trade-agreement-implementation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2001, c. 28
:::

Assented to 2001-12-18

An Act to implement the Free Trade Agreement between the Government of
Canada and the Government of the Republic of Costa Rica
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the Government of Canada and the Government of the Republic of
Costa Rica have entered into a Free Trade Agreement having resolved to

strengthen the special bonds of friendship and cooperation among their
peoples,

contribute to the harmonious development and expansion of world and
regional trade and provide a catalyst to broader international
cooperation,

create new employment opportunities and improve working conditions and
living standards in their respective territories,

recognize the differences in the level of development and the size of
their respective economies and create opportunities for economic
development,

create an expanded and secure market for the goods and services produced
in their territories,

reduce distortions to trade,

establish clear and mutually advantageous rules governing their trade,

ensure a predictable commercial framework for business planning and
investment,

recognize the importance of trade facilitation in promoting efficient
and transparent procedures to reduce costs and to ensure predictability
for their importers and exporters,

build on their respective rights and obligations under the Marrakesh
Agreement Establishing the World Trade Organization and other
multilateral and bilateral instruments of cooperation,

promote regional integration with an instrument that will contribute to
the establishment of the Free Trade Area of the Americas (FTAA),

enhance the competitiveness of their firms in global markets,

ensure that the benefits of trade liberalization are not undermined by
anticompetitive activities,

promote sustainable development,

undertake each of the preceding in a manner consistent with
environmental protection and conservation,

preserve their flexibility to safeguard the public welfare,

recognize that States have the ability to preserve, develop and
implement their cultural policies for the purpose of strengthening
cultural diversity, and

recognize the increased cooperation between Canada and Costa Rica on
labour and environmental cooperation;

WHEREAS the Government of Canada has entered into the Agreement having
further resolved to

strengthen Canada's national identity while at the same time protecting
vital national characteristics and qualities, and

strengthen the Canadian economy and Canada's competitiveness as a
trading nation;

WHEREAS the Agreement applies generally throughout Canada;

AND WHEREAS it is necessary, in order to give effect to the Agreement,
to make related or consequential amendments to certain Acts;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-49444 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the Canada --- Costa Rica Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-49447 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in this Act.

  [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Free Trade Agreement
      entered into between the Government of Canada and the Government
      of the Republic of Costa Rica and signed on April 23, 2001, and
      includes any rectifications to it made before its ratification by
      Canada. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Free Trade Commission
      established under Article XIII.1 of the
      Agreement. ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the whole or any portion
      of an Act of Parliament or a regulation, order or other instrument
      issued, made or established in the exercise of a power conferred
      by or under an Act of Parliament. ([texte législatif
      fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of any provision of
      this Act, means the member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada
      designated under section 10 as the Minister for the purposes of
      that provision. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[territory]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[territory]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to Canada, the territory to which
        its customs laws apply, including any areas beyond the
        territorial seas of Canada within which, in accordance with
        international law and its domestic law, Canada may exercise
        rights with respect to the seabed and subsoil and their natural
        resources; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to Costa Rica, the territory and
        air space, and the maritime areas, including the seabed and
        subsoil adjacent to the outer limit of the territorial sea, over
        which it exercises, in accordance with international law and its
        domestic law, sovereign rights with respect to the natural
        resources of such areas. ([territoire]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Agreement shall be published in the Canada Treaty
  Series.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation consistent with Agreement

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, this
Act, any provision of an Act enacted or amended by Part 2 and any other
federal law that implements a provision of the Agreement or fulfils an
obligation of the Government of Canada under the Agreement shall be
interpreted in a manner consistent with the Agreement.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-49462 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to implement the Agreement, the objectives of which, as elaborated more
specifically through its provisions, are to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish a free trade area in accordance with the
  Agreement;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} promote regional integration through an instrument
  that contributes to the establishment of the Free Trade Area of the
  Americas (FTAA) and to the progressive elimination of barriers to
  trade and investment;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} create opportunities for economic development;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} eliminate barriers to trade in, and facilitate the
  cross-border movement of, goods and services between the territories
  of Canada and Costa Rica;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} substantially increase investment opportunities in
  the territories of Canada and Costa Rica;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} facilitate trade in services and investment with a
  view to developing and deepening the relations of Canada and Costa
  Rica under the Agreement;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} promote conditions of fair competition in the
  free-trade area established by the Agreement;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} establish a framework for further bilateral, regional
  and multilateral cooperation to expand and enhance the benefits of the
  Agreement; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} create effective procedures for the implementation
  and application of the Agreement, for its joint administration and for
  the resolution of disputes.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-49474 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-49477 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Causes of action under Part 1

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person has any cause of action and no proceedings of any kind shall be
  taken, without the consent of the Attorney General of Canada, to
  enforce or determine any right or obligation that is claimed or arises
  solely under or by virtue of Part 1 or an order or regulation made
  under Part 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Causes of action under the Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person has any cause of action and no proceedings
  of any kind shall be taken, without the consent of the Attorney
  General of Canada, to enforce or determine any right or obligation
  that is claimed or arises solely under or by virtue of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of Agreement to water

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act or the Agreement applies to natural surface or
ground water in liquid, gaseous or solid state.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act, by specific mention or omission, shall be construed
to affect in any manner the right of Parliament to enact legislation to
implement any provision of the Agreement or fulfil any of the
obligations of the Government of Canada under the Agreement.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Implementation of Agreement Generally]{.HTitleText1} {#h-49487 .Part}

### [Approval of Agreement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-49488 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement approved

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement is hereby
approved.

### [Designation of Minister]{.HTitleText2} {#h-49491 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order designating Minister

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate any member of the Queen's Privy Council for
Canada to be the Minister for the purposes of any provision of this Act.

### [Administrative and Institutional Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-49494 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Minister for
International Trade is the principal representative of Canada on the
Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of expenditures

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
shall pay its appropriate share of the aggregate of any expenditures
incurred by or on behalf of the Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrative support

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Minister for
International Trade shall designate an agency, division or branch of the
Government of Canada to facilitate the operation of, and provide
administrative assistance to panels established under, Chapter XIII of
the Agreement.

### [Panels, Committees, Subcommittees and Working Groups]{.HTitleText2} {#h-49501 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointments to roster

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, in accordance with a consensus reached under Article XIII.9 of the
Agreement, appoint any person to be a member of the roster established
under that Article.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointments to committees, subcommittees
and working groups

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may appoint
any person to be a representative of Canada on any committee or
subcommittee referred to in Annex XIII.2.2 of the Agreement or on any
committee, subcommittee or working group established under Article
XIII.1.5 of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
shall, in accordance with Annex XIII.3.2 of the Agreement, pay the costs
of or its appropriate share of the costs of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the remuneration and expenses payable to panellists,
  members of committees, subcommittees and working groups, including
  persons appointed under section 15;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the remuneration and expenses payable to assistants;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the general expenses incurred by panels, committees,
  subcommittees and working groups.

### [Orders and Regulations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-49511 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations re Article III.8

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, if the Governor in Council is of the opinion
  that, for the purpose of giving effect in a province to Article III.8
  of the Agreement, regulations are necessary in relation to any matter
  dealt with by that Article, make regulations for that purpose
  including, without limiting the generality of the foregoing,
  regulations requiring or prohibiting the doing of anything in relation
  to which a regulation may be made under this subsection and
  prescribing penalties for the contravention of any such regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicability of regulations in respect
  of a province

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under subsection (1) shall not come
  into force in respect of a province if the Governor in Council is of
  the opinion that the province has, by or under provincial law, enacted
  provisions or is carrying on practices that are in conformity with the
  portion of Article III.8 of the Agreement in respect of which the
  regulation was made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation with provincial
  governments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall consult with the government of a
  province before the making of a regulation under subsection (1) in
  respect of that province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expiration

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under subsection (1) or any of its
  provisions ceases to be in force in respect of a province on a day or
  days to be fixed, in respect of that province, by order of the
  Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on province

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under subsection (1) in respect of
  a province is binding on Her Majesty in right of that province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders re Article XIII.18

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, for the purpose of suspending concessions or
  other obligations in accordance with Article XIII.18 of the Agreement,
  by order, do any one or more of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend rights or privileges granted by Canada to
    Costa Rica or to goods, service providers, suppliers, investors or
    investments of Costa Rica under the Agreement or any federal law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} modify or suspend the application of any federal
    law enacted by Part 2, with respect to Costa Rica or to goods,
    service providers, suppliers, investors or investments of Costa
    Rica;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} extend the application of any federal law to Costa
    Rica or to goods, service providers, suppliers, investors or
    investments of Costa Rica; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} take any other measure that the Governor in Council
    considers necessary for that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless revoked, an order made under subsection (1)
  has effect for the period specified in the order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

- **[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#C-1.9_en_1){#C-1.9_en_1-ID0EBCA}[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  provisions of this Act, or any provisions of an Act as enacted or
  amended by this Act, come into force on a day or days to be fixed by
  order of the Governor in Council.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No order may be made under subsection (1) unless the
  Governor in Council is satisfied that the Government of the Republic
  of Costa Rica has taken satisfactory steps to implement the Agreement.
