## Fisheries Improvement Loans Act {#fisheries-improvement-loans-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. F-22
:::

An Act respecting loans to assist fishermen engaged in a primary fishing
enterprise
::::

::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233255 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Fisheries Improvement Loans Act](/eng/acts/F-22).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-22, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233260 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an application for a
    guaranteed loan; ([Version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a fisherman to whom a
    guaranteed loan has been made; ([emprunteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[engine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[engine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a steam or an internal
    combustion engine and includes the clutch, reduction gears, shaft,
    propeller, control and such accessories as properly form part of a
    mechanism for the propulsion of a fishing
    vessel; ([machines]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes shellfish, crustaceans and all
    forms of aquatic life whether animal or
    vegetable; ([poisson]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fisherman]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fisherman]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who has or intends to acquire a
      prescribed interest in a fishing vessel or a proprietory share in
      a fishing vessel, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who has a prescribed interest in a weir,
      or similar fish catching or trapping device, that is affixed to
      the ground, or a proprietory share in such a weir or similar fish
      catching or trapping device,

    and whose principal occupation is participation in a primary fishing
    enterprise; ([pêcheur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fishing equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fishing equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means equipment of a
    prescribed class or kind used in connection with a primary fishing
    enterprise but does not include a shore installation; ([matériel de
    pêche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fishing vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fishing vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any ship or boat or any
    other description of a vessel, for use in a primary fishing
    enterprise, that has an engine for its propulsion, and includes the
    equipment affixed thereto for use in the operation thereof but does
    not include prescribed electronic equipment; ([navire de
    pêche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[guaranteed loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[guaranteed loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a loan made in
    accordance with the requirements of section 3; ([prêt
    garanti]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a bank or an authorized foreign bank within the
      meaning of section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01),

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a credit union, *caisse populaire* or other
      cooperative credit society that is designated by the Minister on
      the application of that society as a lender for the purposes of
      this Act, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that carries on the business of a
      trust company within the meaning of the Trust Companies Act, the
      business of a loan company within the meaning of the Loan
      Companies Act or the business of insurance within the meaning of
      the Canadian and British Insurance Companies Act, and that is
      designated by the Minister on the application of that corporation
      as a lender for the purposes of this
      Act; ([prêteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Fisheries and
    Oceans; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by
    regulation; ([Version anglaise seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[primary fishing enterprise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[primary fishing enterprise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    enterprise that is carried on for the purpose of catching or
    trapping fish for sale, and does not include the processing of fish
    except as prescribed; ([entreprise de pêche
    primaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shore installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shore installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means structures,
    appliances or machinery affixed to the ground of a prescribed class
    or kind used in connection with a primary fishing enterprise, but
    does not include fishing equipment. ([installations au
    sol]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-22, s. 2
- 1999, c. 28, s. 165
:::

## [Guaranteed Loans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233284 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister to pay losses of lenders

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall, subject
to this Act, pay to a lender the amount of loss sustained by it as a
result of a loan made to a fisherman pursuant to an application by the
fisherman in any case where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application stated that the loan was required by
  the fisherman for any of the following purposes:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the purchase or construction of a fishing vessel,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the purchase or construction of fishing equipment,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the major repair or major overhaul of a fishing
    vessel or its hull, superstructure or engine,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the purchase or construction of a shore
    installation,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the purchase, construction, repair or alteration of
    or making of additions to any building used or to be used in
    carrying on a primary fishing enterprise, or

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} any prescribed development or improvement of a
    primary fishing enterprise;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application was in prescribed form and was signed
  by the fisherman making the application;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a responsible officer of the lender certified that he
  had scrutinized and checked the application for the loan with the care
  required by the lender in the conduct of its ordinary business;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of the principal amount of the loan and
  the amount that is owing in respect of previous guaranteed loans that
  were made to the borrower, or to the borrower and all other fishermen
  with whom the borrower is associated in the same primary fishing
  enterprise, if made in respect of that primary fishing enterprise, and
  that is disclosed in the application, or of which the lender had
  knowledge, did not exceed one hundred and fifty thousand dollars;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the loan was repayable in full by the terms thereof
  in not more than fifteen years;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} no fee, service charge or charge of any kind other
  than simple interest at the rate prescribed and a prescribed charge
  for insurance was, by the terms of the loan, payable to the lender in
  respect of the loan as long as the fisherman was not in default on the
  loan;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} repayment of the loan was secured in prescribed
  manner;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the loan was made on such terms and in accordance
  with such provisions in addition to those specified in paragraphs (a)
  to (g) as may be prescribed; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the loan was made prior to the termination of the
  liability of the Minister under section 5 or 6.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-22, s. 3
- 1974, c. 10, s. 9
- 1976-77, c. 23, s. 8
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 11, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister need not pay if non-compliance

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is not liable
to pay any amount under section 3 with respect to any loan made after
June 28, 1983 if the lender has failed to comply with any regulation
made pursuant to this Act respecting the registration of the loan, the
making of reports in respect of the loan or the procedure to be followed
in making a claim for loss in respect of the loan and, where the
Minister decides not to pay, the Minister shall so notify the lender by
notice in writing to the lender's head office.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 154, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of liability

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, with the
approval of the Governor in Council, by notice in writing to the head
office of a lender terminate the Minister's liability to the lender
under this Act with respect to guaranteed loans made by the lender after
a day fixed by the notice, being a day not less than fourteen days after
the date of dispatch of the notice.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-22, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination by proclamation

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may by proclamation terminate the liability of the
  Minister in respect of guaranteed loans made in any locality for any
  of the purposes specified by or under this Act after a day fixed by
  the proclamation, being a day not less than fourteen days after the
  publication of the proclamation in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of termination

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a proclamation has been issued under
  subsection (1) terminating the liability of the Minister in respect of
  any guaranteed loans, the Governor in Council may by further
  proclamation revoke the termination in respect of any of those loans.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-22, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation of Minister's liability

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is not liable
under this Act to pay to a lender, in respect of losses sustained by it
as a result of guaranteed loans made by it after September 12, 1968, a
total amount in excess of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} ninety per cent of that part of the aggregate
  principal amount of the guaranteed loans made by the lender that does
  not exceed one hundred and twenty-five thousand dollars;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} fifty per cent of that part of the aggregate
  principal amount of the guaranteed loans made by the lender that
  exceeds one hundred and twenty-five thousand dollars but does not
  exceed two hundred and fifty thousand dollars; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} ten per cent of that part of the aggregate principal
  amount of the guaranteed loans made by the lender that exceeds two
  hundred and fifty thousand dollars.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-22, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further limitation of liability

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister is not liable under this Act to make any payment to a lender
  in respect of loss sustained by it as a result of a guaranteed loan

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} made during the period commencing on July 1, 1983
    and ending on June 30, 1985, after the aggregate principal amount of
    the guaranteed loans made by all lenders during that period exceeds
    one hundred million dollars; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} made during the period commencing on July 1, 1985
    and ending on June 30, 1987, after the aggregate principal amount of
    the guaranteed loans made by all lenders during that period exceeds
    thirty million dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is not liable under this Act to make a
  payment to any lender in respect of a guaranteed loan made after June
  30, 1987.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-22, s. 8
- R.S., 1985, c. 37 (1st Supp.), s. 1, c. 41 (2nd Supp.), s. 1
:::

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233335 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, subject to subsection (2), on the
  recommendation of the Minister, make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that, by this Act, is to be
    prescribed by regulation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} defining for the purposes of this Act the
    expressions

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} "principal occupation",

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} "hull" and "superstructure",

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} "repair", "alteration" and "additions", and
      "major" in relation to "repair" and "overhaul", and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} "responsible officer of the lender";

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the forms of receipts, notes and
    documents to be used in connection with the guaranteed loans or for
    the effective operation of this Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the registration of guaranteed loans;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} providing, notwithstanding anything to the contrary
    in this Act, that in the event of impending or actual default in the
    repayment of a guaranteed loan the lender may with the approval of
    the borrower alter or revise such of the terms of the guaranteed
    loan or any document connected therewith as are prescribed;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} prescribing, in the event of default in the
    repayment of a guaranteed loan, the legal or other measures to be
    taken by the lender and the procedure to be followed for the
    collection of the amount of the loan outstanding and the disposal or
    realization of any security for the repayment thereof held by the
    lender;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the method of determination of the
    amount of any loss sustained by a lender as a result of a guaranteed
    loan;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the procedure to be followed by a
    lender in making a claim for any loss sustained by it as a result of
    a guaranteed loan;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the steps to be taken by a lender to
    effect collection on behalf of the Minister of any guaranteed loan
    in respect of which payment has been made by the Minister to the
    lender under this Act, and to provide that on failure by the lender
    to take such steps the amount of the payment may be recovered by the
    Minister;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} requiring reports to be made periodically to the
    Minister by a lender in respect of guaranteed loans; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} generally for carrying the purposes and provisions
    of this Act into effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations re certain terms

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A regulation by the Governor in Council under
  subsection (1) respecting a rate of interest that may be charged as a
  term on which a guaranteed loan is to be made shall be made on the
  recommendation of the Minister and the Minister of Finance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of alteration on Minister's
  liability

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where any of the terms of a guaranteed loan or any
  document connected therewith have been altered or revised under
  paragraph (1)(e), the alteration or revision does not discharge the
  liability of the Minister in respect of the guaranteed loan.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-22, s. 7
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 11, s. 3, c. 154, s. 4
:::

## [Special Powers of a Bank]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233360 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bank security

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything in the
  [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) or any other Act of Parliament, if a bank
  makes a guaranteed loan in respect of which it is required by
  regulation to take security on real or personal property, the bank may
  at the time of making the loan take as security for the repayment
  thereof and the payment of interest thereon,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a mortgage or hypothec on real or personal property
    whether or not all or part of the proceeds of the guaranteed loan
    are to be expended in respect thereof, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an assignment of the rights and interest of a
    purchaser under

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an agreement for sale of real or personal
      property, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a lien or conditional sale contract for personal
      property,

    whether or not all or part of the proceeds of the guaranteed loan
    are to be expended in respect thereof.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights in respect of security

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A bank has and may exercise, in respect of any
  mortgage, hypothec or assignment taken under this Act and the real or
  personal property affected thereby, all rights and powers that it
  would have or might exercise if the mortgage, hypothec or assignment
  had been taken by the bank by way of subsequent security under the
  [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of "bank"

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
  bank or an authorized foreign bank within the meaning of section 2 of
  the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-22, s. 10
- 1999, c. 28, s. 166
:::

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233375 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False statements or misuse of loan

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} makes in an application for a guaranteed loan a
    statement that is false in any material respect,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} uses the proceeds of a guaranteed loan for a
    purpose other than that stated in his application, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} while indebted to a lender under a guaranteed loan,
    without the consent in writing of the lender, encumbers or disposes
    of any property in respect of which the proceeds of the loan were
    expended,

  is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not
  exceeding five hundred dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a person is convicted of an offence under
  subsection (1), there shall be imposed on that person, in addition to
  any fine, a penalty equal to such amount of the guaranteed loan made
  to that person in respect of which the offence was committed as has
  not been repaid by him, with interest thereon to the date of payment
  of the penalty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment to lender or Receiver General

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The penalty referred to in subsection (2) shall be
  paid to the lender by which the guaranteed loan was made, or, if
  payment has been made by the Minister to that lender in respect of the
  guaranteed loan, to the Receiver General, and the payment of the
  penalty to the lender or the Receiver General under this section
  discharges the liability of the person so convicted to pay the loan.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-22, s. 9
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-233389 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister subrogated in lender's rights

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  under this Act the Minister has paid to a lender the amount of any
  loss sustained by the lender as a result of a guaranteed loan, the
  lender shall execute a receipt in favour of the Minister in prescribed
  form and the Minister is thereupon subrogated in all rights of the
  lender in respect of the loan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Without limiting the generality of subsection (1),
  the subrogation mentioned in that subsection vests in the Minister all
  rights and powers of the lender in respect of the guaranteed loan, of
  any judgment concerning the loan obtained by the lender and of any
  security taken by the lender for the repayment of the loan, and the
  Minister is entitled to exercise all the rights, powers and privileges
  that the lender had or might exercise in respect of the loan, judgment
  or security, including the right to commence or continue any action or
  proceeding, to execute any release, transfer, sale or assignment or in
  any way to collect, realize or enforce the loan, judgment or security.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt evidence of payment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any document purporting to be a receipt in prescribed
  form and purporting to be signed on behalf of the lender is evidence
  of the payment by the Minister to the lender under this Act in respect
  of the guaranteed loan therein mentioned and of the execution of that
  document on behalf of the lender.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-22, s. 10
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment out of C.R.F.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may pay any
amount payable to a lender under this Act out of the Consolidated
Revenue Fund.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-22, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall, as soon as possible after the termination of each
  fiscal year, and in any event within three months thereafter, prepare
  a report with regard to the administration of this Act during that
  fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to be laid before Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall lay before Parliament the report
  prepared under subsection (1) within fifteen days after it is prepared
  or, if Parliament is not then sitting, on any of the first fifteen
  days next thereafter that either House of Parliament is sitting.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-22, s. 12
:::
