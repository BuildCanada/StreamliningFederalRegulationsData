## An Act to confirm the authority of the Federal District Commission to have acquired certain lands {#an-act-to-confirm-the-authority-of-the-federal-district-commission-to-have-acquired-certain-lands .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1979, c. 7
:::

Assented to 1979-12-06

An Act to confirm the authority of the Federal District Commission to
have acquired certain lands
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acquisition confirmed

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, the
acquisition by the Federal District Commission of parts of lots 3a, 3b
and 4b, Range 5 of the Official Cadastre for the Township of Hull,
Registry Division of Gatineau, Province of Quebec, by indenture made on
the 31st day of May, 1954 and registered in the Office of the Registry
Division of Gatineau under number 34984 is deemed, for all purposes, to
have had, and to continue to have, the same force and effect as if it
had been made with the previous consent of the Governor in Council
pursuant to subsection 16(1) of the Federal District Commission Act,
chapter 112 of the Revised Statutes of Canada, 1952, repealed by section
28 of chapter 37 of the Statutes of Canada, 1958.
