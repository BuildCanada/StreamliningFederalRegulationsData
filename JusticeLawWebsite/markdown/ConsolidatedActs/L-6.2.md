## Language Skills Act {#language-skills-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2013, c. 36
:::

Assented to 2013-06-26

An Act respecting language skills
::::

:::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344525 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This act may be cited as
the [Language Skills Act](/eng/acts/L-6.2).

## [Language Skills]{.HTitleText1} {#h-344528 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any person appointed to any
of the following offices must, at the time of his or her appointment, be
able to speak and understand clearly both official languages:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Auditor General of Canada, appointed pursuant to
  subsection 3(1) of the [Auditor General Act](/eng/acts/A-17);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Chief Electoral Officer, appointed pursuant to
  subsection 13(1) of the [Canada Elections Act](/eng/acts/E-2.01);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Commissioner of Official Languages for Canada,
  appointed pursuant to subsection 49(1) of the [Official Languages
  Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Privacy Commissioner, appointed pursuant to
  subsection 53(1) of the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Information Commissioner, appointed pursuant to
  subsection 54(1) of the [Access to Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the Senate Ethics Officer, appointed pursuant to
  section 20.1 of the [Parliament of Canada Act](/eng/acts/P-1);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Conflict of Interest and Ethics Commissioner,
  appointed pursuant to subsection 81(1) of the [Parliament of Canada
  Act](/eng/acts/P-1);

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the Commissioner of Lobbying, appointed pursuant to
  subsection 4.1(1) of the [Lobbying Act](/eng/acts/L-12.4);

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Public Sector Integrity Commissioner, appointed
  pursuant to subsection 39(1) of the [Public Servants Disclosure
  Protection Act](/eng/acts/P-31.9);

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the President of the Public Service Commission,
  appointed pursuant to subsection 4(5) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01);

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the Parliamentary Budget Officer, appointed under
  subsection 79.1(1) of the [Parliament of Canada Act](/eng/acts/P-1).
