## Human Pathogens and Toxins Act {#human-pathogens-and-toxins-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2009, c. 24
:::

Assented to 2009-06-23

An Act to promote safety and security with respect to human pathogens
and toxins
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the Parliament of Canada recognizes the objective of protecting
the health and safety of the public;

Whereas the Parliament of Canada recognizes that human pathogens and
toxins pose varying levels of risk to the health and safety of the
public;

Whereas the Parliament of Canada recognizes that a lack of full
scientific certainty regarding the risks posed by certain human
pathogens and toxins is not to be used as a reason to postpone measures
that protect the health and safety of the public;

And whereas the Parliament of Canada recognizes that human pathogens and
toxins evolve and can be altered and that new human pathogens and toxins
appear continually, therefore creating unique challenges in meeting the
objective of protecting the health and safety of the public;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-254961 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Human Pathogens and Toxins Act](/eng/acts/H-5.67).

## [Purpose of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-254964 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to establish a safety and security regime to protect the health and
safety of the public against the risks posed by human pathogens and
toxins.

## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-254967 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[controlled activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[controlled activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an activity
      referred to in subsection 7(1). ([activité
      réglementée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel, aircraft,
      train, motor vehicle, trailer or other means of transportation,
      including a cargo container. ([véhicule]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[disease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[disease]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes
      intoxication. ([maladie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[human pathogen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[human pathogen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a micro-organism,
      nucleic acid or protein that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is listed in any of Schedules 2 to 4 or in Part
        2 of Schedule 5; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not listed in any of the Schedules but falls
        into Risk Group 2, Risk Group 3 or Risk Group 4. ([agent
        pathogène humain]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence issued under
      section 18. ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
      Health. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual or an
      organization as defined in section 2 of the [Criminal
      Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[personal information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[personal information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
      as in section 3 of the [Privacy
      Act](/eng/acts/P-21). ([renseignements
      personnels]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[possession]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[possession]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 4(3) of the [Criminal
      Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([possession]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[produce]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[produce]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a human pathogen or
      toxin, means to create it by any method or process, including

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by manufacturing, cultivating, developing,
        reproducing or synthesizing it; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by converting or refining a substance,
        micro-organism, nucleic acid or protein, or by using any other
        means of altering its physical or chemical
        properties. ([production]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[release]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[release]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any discharge, anywhere, and
      includes leaking, spraying, depositing, dumping or
      vaporizing. ([rejet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Risk Group 2]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Risk Group 2]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a category of human
      pathogens that pose a moderate risk to the health of individuals
      and a low risk to public health and includes the human pathogens
      listed in Schedule 2. They are able to cause serious disease in a
      human but are unlikely to do so. Effective treatment and
      preventive measures are available and the risk of spread of
      disease caused by those pathogens is low.  ([groupe de risque
      2]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Risk Group 3]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Risk Group 3]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a category of human
      pathogens that pose a high risk to the health of individuals and a
      low risk to public health and includes the human pathogens listed
      in Schedule 3. They are likely to cause serious disease in a
      human. Effective treatment and preventive measures are usually
      available and the risk of spread of disease caused by those
      pathogens is low. ([groupe de risque 3]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Risk Group 4]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Risk Group 4]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a category of human
      pathogens that pose a high risk to the health of individuals and a
      high risk to public health and includes the human pathogens listed
      in Schedule 4. They are likely to cause serious disease in a
      human. Effective treatment and preventive measures are not usually
      available and the risk of spread of disease caused by those
      pathogens is high. ([groupe de risque 4]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[security clearance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[security clearance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a security
      clearance issued under section 34. ([habilitation de
      sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[toxin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[toxin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance that is listed in
      Schedule 1 or in Part 1 of Schedule 5. ([toxine]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Included

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, a human pathogen or
  toxin includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a substance that contains a human pathogen or
    toxin; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any synthetic form of the human pathogen or toxin.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excluded

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act does not apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a human pathogen or toxin that is in an environment
  in which it naturally occurs if it has not been cultivated or
  intentionally collected or extracted, including a human pathogen or
  toxin that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is in or on a human suffering from a disease caused
    by that human pathogen or toxin,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has been expelled by a human suffering from a
    disease caused by that human pathogen or toxin, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is in or on a cadaver, a body part or other human
    remains; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a drug in dosage form whose sale is permitted or
  otherwise authorized under the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27) or
  a human pathogen or toxin contained in such a drug.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-255005 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act binding on Her Majesty

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Obligation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-255008 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasonable precautions

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every person who knowingly
conducts any activity referred to in section 7 involving a human
pathogen or toxin shall take all reasonable precautions to protect the
health and safety of the public against the risks posed by that
activity.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-255011 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Controlled activities

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall knowingly conduct any of the following activities unless
  a licence has been issued by the Minister that authorizes the
  activity:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} possessing, handling or using a human pathogen or
    toxin;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} producing a human pathogen or toxin;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} storing a human pathogen or toxin;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} permitting any person access to a human pathogen or
    toxin;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} transferring a human pathogen or toxin;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} importing or exporting a human pathogen or toxin;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} releasing or otherwise abandoning a human pathogen
    or toxin; or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} disposing of a human pathogen or toxin.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other Acts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any activity to which the [Transportation of
    Dangerous Goods Act, 1992](/eng/acts/T-19.01) applies; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the export of human pathogens or toxins authorized
    under the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Human pathogens and toxins --- Schedule 5

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 7, no
person shall conduct any activity referred to in that section in
relation to a human pathogen or toxin listed in Schedule 5.

## [Schedules 1 to 4]{.HTitleText1} {#h-255029 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Addition of items --- toxins

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister is of the opinion that a substance is produced by, or derived
  from, a micro-organism and is able to cause disease in a human, the
  Minister may, by regulation, add it to Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Addition of items --- human pathogens

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is of the opinion that a
  micro-organism, nucleic acid or protein is able to cause disease in a
  human, the Minister may, by regulation, add it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to Schedule 2 if the Minister is of the opinion
    that it falls into Risk Group 2;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to Schedule 3 if the Minister is of the opinion
    that it falls into Risk Group 3; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to Schedule 4 if the Minister is of the opinion
    that it falls into Risk Group 4.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deletion of items

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by regulation, delete

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a substance from Schedule 1 if the Minister is of
    the opinion that it is not produced by, or derived from, a
    micro-organism or is not able to cause disease in a human; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a micro-organism, nucleic acid or protein from any
    of Schedules 2 to 4 if the Minister is of the opinion that it does
    not fall into the risk group to which that Schedule relates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advisory Committee

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall consult an advisory committee
  established under subsection 14(1) of the [Public Health Agency of
  Canada Act](/eng/acts/P-29.5) before making any regulation under
  subsection (1), (2) or (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The advisory committee shall make available to the
  public the advice given to the Minister.

## [Schedule 5]{.HTitleText1} {#h-255046 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Addition of items

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, by regulation, on the Minister's
  recommendation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} add a substance to Part 1 of Schedule 5 if the
    Governor in Council is of the opinion that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is produced by, or derived from, a
      micro-organism and is able to cause disease in a human, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all activities referred to in section 7 should
      be prohibited in relation to it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} add a micro-organism, nucleic acid or protein to
    Part 2 of Schedule 5 if the Governor in Council is of the opinion
    that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is able to cause disease in a human, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all activities referred to in section 7 should
      be prohibited in relation to it; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} delete a substance, micro-organism, nucleic acid or
    protein from any of Schedules 1 to 4 if the Governor in Council adds
    it to Schedule 5.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deletion of items

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by regulation, on the
  Minister's recommendation, delete a substance, micro-organism, nucleic
  acid or protein from Schedule 5 if the Governor in Council is of the
  opinion that it is in the public interest to allow one or more of the
  activities referred to in section 7 to be authorized in relation to
  that substance, micro-organism, nucleic acid or protein.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advisory Committee

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall consult an advisory committee
  established under subsection 14(1) of the [Public Health Agency of
  Canada Act](/eng/acts/P-29.5) before making any recommendation under
  subsection (1) or (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The advisory committee shall make available to the
  public the advice given to the Minister.

## [Consequences of Adding to Schedules]{.HTitleText1} {#h-255063 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited possession --- Schedules 1 to
4

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  30 days after the date of publication of a regulation made under
  subsection 9(1) or (2), every person who, as a result of the
  regulation, no longer has lawful possession of a human pathogen or
  toxin shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dispose of it in accordance with the regulations,
    if any;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} transfer it to a facility where controlled
    activities in relation to it are authorized; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} obtain from the Minister a licence, or a variation
    of the conditions of their existing licence, authorizing possession
    of it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited possession --- Schedule 5

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within 14 days after the date of publication of a
  regulation made under subsection 10(1), every person who, as a result
  of the regulation, no longer has lawful possession of a human pathogen
  or toxin shall dispose of it in accordance with the regulations, if
  any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No contravention

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person contravenes subsection 7(1) or section 8 by
  reason only that they possess a human pathogen or toxin in the
  circumstances described in subsection (1) or (2) if they transfer or
  dispose of it in accordance with subsection (1) or (2).

## [Obligation to Inform Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-255074 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inadvertent release

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  licence holder has reason to believe that a human pathogen or toxin
  has been released inadvertently from the facility in the course of an
  activity that is otherwise authorized by the licence, the licence
  holder shall, without delay, inform the Minister of the release and
  provide the Minister with the information referred to in
  subsection (3) that is under the licence holder's control.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inadvertent production

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person is in possession of a human pathogen or
  toxin in contravention of subsection 7(1) or section 8 as a result of
  the inadvertent production of that human pathogen or toxin in the
  course of an activity that is otherwise lawful, the person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} without delay, inform the Minister of the
    inadvertent production and provide the Minister with the information
    referred to in subsection (3) that is under the person's control;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} dispose of the inadvertently produced human
    pathogen or toxin in accordance with the regulations, if any, or, if
    it is not listed in Schedule 5, transfer it to a facility where
    controlled activities in relation to that human pathogen or toxin
    are authorized.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The information that is to be provided under
  subsections (1) and (2) is the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any information that supports the conclusion that a
    human pathogen or toxin has been released or produced;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the human pathogen or toxin released or
    produced;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the quantity released or produced;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the place and time of the release or production;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other information relating to the release or
    production that the Minister may require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No contravention

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person contravenes subsection 7(1) or section 8 by
  reason only that they possess a human pathogen or toxin in the
  circumstances described in subsection (2) if they transfer or dispose
  of it in accordance with that subsection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disease

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** If a licence holder has
reason to believe that an incident involving a human pathogen or toxin
that is in their possession has, or may have, caused disease in an
individual, the licence holder shall, without delay, inform the Minister
of the incident and provide the Minister with the following information
that is under the licence holder's control:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the incident;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the human pathogen or toxin; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other information relating to the incident that
  the Minister may require.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Missing human pathogen or toxin

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** If a licence holder has
reason to believe that a human pathogen or toxin that was in their
possession has been stolen or is otherwise missing, the licence holder
shall, without delay, inform the Minister and provide the Minister with
any information relating to the incident that is under their control and
that the Minister may require. The licence holder shall also take
reasonable measures to locate the missing human pathogen or toxin.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person conducting activities

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** If a person conducting
activities under the authority of a licence has reason to believe that
any of the incidents described in subsection 12(1) or (2) or section 13
or 14 has occurred, the person shall, without delay, inform the licence
holder.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of information

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** No information provided
under sections 12 to 15 by a licence holder or a person conducting
activities under the authority of a licence may be used or received
against that person in any criminal proceedings that are subsequently
instituted against them, other than with respect to a contravention of
section 17.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False or misleading information

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** No person shall knowingly
communicate or cause to be communicated to the Minister false or
misleading information in relation to a matter under this Act or the
regulations.

## [Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-255104 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, in accordance with the regulations, if any, issue a
  licence that authorizes any controlled activity in any facility if the
  Minister is of the opinion that the conduct of the controlled activity
  in the facility poses no undue risk to the health or safety of the
  public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for a licence must be filed with the
  Minister and made in the form and manner specified by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to issue licence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister refuses to issue a licence, the
  Minister shall notify the applicant in writing of the reasons for the
  refusal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A licence authorizes the controlled activities that
  are specified in it and is subject to any conditions that the Minister
  considers appropriate to protect the health and safety of the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other conditions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A licence must also set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licence holder's name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the period during which the licence is in effect;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the facility in which controlled
    activities are authorized under it;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of each part of the facility that is
    subject to section 33; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the toxins, human pathogens, or the risk groups of
    the human pathogens, in respect of which controlled activities are
    authorized under it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation of licence holder

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The licence holder shall inform all persons
  conducting the controlled activities authorized by the licence of its
  conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with licence conditions

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A licence holder and all persons conducting the
  controlled activities authorized by the licence shall comply with the
  licence conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} A licence is not a statutory instrument within the
  meaning of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Variation of licence conditions

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, on the Minister's own initiative or on the application
  of a licence holder, vary the licence conditions if the Minister is of
  the opinion that the variation poses no undue risk to the health or
  safety of the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may vary the licence conditions on the
  Minister's own initiative only if the Minister first gives the licence
  holder a reasonable opportunity to make representations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures specified by Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister varies the licence conditions, the
  Minister may specify in writing any measures to be taken to protect
  the health and safety of the public that the variation in conditions
  may necessitate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension or revocation of licence

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may suspend or revoke a licence if the Minister is of the
  opinion that a controlled activity authorized by the licence is
  conducted in a manner that is contrary to this Act or the regulations
  or poses an undue risk to the health or safety of the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opportunity to make representations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may suspend or revoke a licence only if
  the Minister first gives the licence holder a reasonable opportunity
  to make representations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures specified by Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister suspends or revokes a licence, the
  Minister may specify in writing any measures to be taken to protect
  the health and safety of the public that the suspension or revocation
  may necessitate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the measures specified by the Minister,
  the licence holder shall, within five days after the day on which the
  Minister's decision to suspend or revoke the licence takes effect,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dispose of the human pathogen or toxin in
    accordance with the regulations, if any; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} transfer the human pathogen or toxin to a facility
    where controlled activities in relation to the human pathogen or
    toxin are authorized.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall notify a licence holder by registered mail of any
  decision made under section 19 or 20.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons for decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall give reasons for the decision in
  the notice and shall advise the licence holder of their right to
  request a review of the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 23(2), the Minister's decision
  takes effect 31 days after the day on which the notice is received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Serious and imminent danger

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister is of the opinion that there is a serious and imminent danger
  to the health or safety of the public, the Minister shall notify the
  licence holder orally of the decision to suspend or revoke their
  licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The decision takes effect at the time that the
  licence holder is notified of it and the licence holder is advised
  that it is effective immediately.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opportunity to make representations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is not required to give the licence
  holder an opportunity to make representations in respect of the
  decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall send the notice referred to in
  subsection 21(1) within five days after the day on which the licence
  holder is notified orally of the decision.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of decision

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  30 days after the day on which the notice is received, the person
  whose licence is affected by the decision may request in writing,
  stating their reasons, that the Minister refer the decision to a
  committee for review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request for a review suspends the application of
  the decision, unless the Minister notified the licence holder orally
  of the decision under the circumstances described in subsection 22(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures specified by Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When a request for a review is made, the Minister may
  specify in writing any measures to be taken to protect the health and
  safety of the public pending the Minister's final decision.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation of measures

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person whose licence is affected by the decision shall ensure that any
  measures specified by the Minister under subsection 19(3), 20(3) or
  23(3) are implemented.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No contravention

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person contravenes subsection 7(1) or section 8 by
  reason only that they have implemented those measures.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The measures are not statutory instruments within the
  meaning of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Referral of decision to committee

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of a request for a review, the Minister shall, within a
  reasonable time, refer the decision to which the request relates to a
  committee that is to consist of three individuals who have expertise
  in the area of human pathogens or toxins.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of committee members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} One of the members of the committee is to be
  designated by the Minister and another by the person who requests the
  review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The two members designated under subsection (2) shall
  designate a third member of the committee, who shall be its
  chairperson. If they are unable to designate the third member within a
  reasonable time, the Minister shall make the designation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The members of the committee may be paid for the
  carrying out of their functions any remuneration that the Governor in
  Council may determine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Travel, living and other expenses

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The members of the committee are entitled to be paid,
  in accordance with Treasury Board directives, reasonable travel,
  living and other expenses incurred in the carrying out of their
  functions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to be considered

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** A committee to which a
decision is referred shall consider

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for the decision;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reasons stated by the person who requested the
  review; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any representations, information or material
  submitted to it by the Minister or the person who requested the
  review.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection of information

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** The committee members
shall not disclose any information or material submitted to them as part
of the review to any person other than the Minister unless the
disclosure is authorized by the person to whom the information or
material relates or is otherwise authorized or required by law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committee's report

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Within 60 days after the
referral of a decision to a committee, or within any longer period that
the Minister may allow, the committee shall report its findings and
recommendations to the Minister and to the person who requested the
review.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final decision

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  60 days after receiving a committee's report, the Minister shall,
  taking into account its findings and recommendations,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} reconsider the decision in respect of which the
    report was made; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} send the Minister's final decision by registered
    mail to the person who requested the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister's final decision takes effect on the day
  after the day on which it is received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to inform

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person whose licence is suspended or revoked shall, without delay,
  inform all persons conducting controlled activities authorized by it
  of its suspension or revocation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of revoked licence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person whose licence is revoked shall return it by
  registered mail to the Minister as soon as feasible after the
  Minister's decision takes effect or, if the decision is reviewed, as
  soon as feasible after the Minister's final decision takes effect.

## [Access to Facility to Which Licence Applies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-255207 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of authorized persons

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** A licence holder shall
establish and maintain a list of all persons authorized by the licence
holder to access the facility to which the licence applies, including
persons holding a security clearance for that facility and visitors. The
licence holder shall provide the Minister with that list if requested to
do so.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to inform Minister

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** If a licence holder
decides to prohibit the holder of a security clearance from having
access to the facility to which the licence applies, the licence holder
shall, without delay, inform the Minister in writing of their decision.

## [Security Clearances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-255212 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to facilities

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** No person shall enter the
part of a facility in which controlled activities are authorized in
relation to human pathogens that fall into Risk Group 3 or Risk Group 4
and are prescribed by regulation or toxins prescribed by regulation
unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} they hold a security clearance for that part of the
  facility; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} they are, in accordance with the regulations, if any,
  accompanied and supervised by a person who holds a security clearance
  for that part of the facility.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance, suspension and revocation

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, in accordance with the regulations, issue a security
  clearance to any individual or suspend or revoke a security clearance.
  The Minister shall notify the individual concerned in writing of the
  decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by way of an agreement, authorize
  any federal department or agency to issue, suspend or revoke security
  clearances and section 35 applies with any necessary modifications.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reconsideration of decision

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister refuses to issue a security clearance or suspends or revokes
  a security clearance, the individual concerned may, within 30 days
  after the day on which the notice is received, request in writing that
  the Minister reconsider the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for reconsideration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request for reconsideration must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the decision that is the subject of the request;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for the request, including any new
    information that the individual concerned wishes the Minister to
    consider; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any information prescribed by regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opportunity to make representations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a request made in accordance with this
  section, the Minister shall give the individual concerned a reasonable
  opportunity to make representations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confirmation or variation of decision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Within a reasonable time after representations have
  been made or an opportunity to do so has been given, the Minister
  shall reconsider the decision, in accordance with the regulations, if
  any, and confirm or vary it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall notify the individual concerned in
  writing of the decision made following the reconsideration.

## [Biological Safety Officers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-255236 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant shall, before a licence may be issued, designate an
  individual as a biological safety officer for the requested licence.
  The individual designated may also be the applicant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations not limited

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A designation does not have the effect of limiting
  the obligations of the licence holder or any other person under this
  Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An individual may be designated as a biological
  safety officer only if the individual has the qualifications set out
  in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of designation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A designation takes effect on the day on which the
  designated individual provides the Minister with their written consent
  to the designation or the day on which the individual begins to act as
  a biological safety officer, whichever is earlier.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers and functions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The biological safety officer may exercise the powers
  and shall carry out the functions set out in the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacement

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If an individual ceases to act as a biological safety
  officer, the licence holder shall, without delay, designate another
  individual and inform the Minister of the new designation.

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-255250 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 7(1) and
section 8 do not apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an inspector or analyst carrying out their functions
  under this Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a peace officer carrying out their functions under
  any federal or provincial Act or a person providing assistance to that
  peace officer;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person who, in the course of their employment,
  outside a facility in which controlled activities are authorized,
  collects a sample for the purpose of laboratory analysis or diagnostic
  testing; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in exigent circumstances, any person carrying out
  their functions under any federal or provincial Act.

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-255257 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information to Minister

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may order an applicant, a licence holder or a biological
  safety officer to provide the Minister, in accordance with any
  conditions that the Minister may specify, with any information that is
  under that person's control, including personal information and
  confidential business information, and that the Minister believes, on

  the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

  information regarding

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the facility in which the controlled activities are
    authorized or in respect of which an application for a licence has
    been submitted; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the controlled activities that are authorized by a
    licence or in respect of which an application for a licence has been
    submitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to provide information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An applicant, a licence holder or a biological safety
  officer shall provide the Minister with the information, in accordance
  with any conditions that the Minister may specify.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excluded information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (1) to (3), the Minister of
  National Defence may refuse to disclose any information the disclosure

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure by Minister

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, without the consent of the person to whom the
  information relates, disclose personal information and confidential
  business information obtained under this Act to a person from whom the
  Minister seeks advice, to a department or agency of the government of
  Canada or a province, to a foreign government or to an international
  organization if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure is necessary for the administration
    or enforcement of this Act or the regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that

    to the health or safety of the public; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure is necessary to enable Canada to
    fulfil its international obligations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adequate protection

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except in the circumstances described in paragraph
  (1)(b), before disclosing the information to any person other than Her
  Majesty in right of Canada or an agent of Her Majesty, the Minister
  must obtain the person's written agreement that they will maintain the
  confidentiality of the information unless they are required by law to
  disclose it.

## [Administration and Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-255279 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of inspectors

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate any individual, or class of individuals, as an
  inspector for the administration and enforcement of this Act and the
  regulations and may restrict in any manner that the Minister considers
  appropriate the powers that an inspector may exercise under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate to be produced

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall provide an inspector with a
  certificate of designation and, on entering any place or conveyance
  under subsection 41(1), the inspector shall produce the certificate to
  the person in charge of that place or conveyance if requested to do
  so.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entry by inspectors

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 42, an inspector
  may, for the purpose of verifying compliance or preventing
  non-compliance with this Act or the regulations, enter at any
  reasonable time any place or conveyance in which the inspector
  believes on reasonable grounds that an activity to which this Act or
  the regulations apply is conducted or that there is any material,
  equipment or document relevant to the administration of this Act or
  the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspector's powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An inspector who enters a place or conveyance may,
  for the purpose referred to in subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine the place --- including any building --- or
    conveyance and any material or equipment found there;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require any person in the place or conveyance to
    produce, in the manner and form requested by the inspector, any
    material or equipment found there;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} seize and detain for any time that may be necessary
    any material, equipment or document found there, or any conveyance;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} open and examine any receptacle or package found
    there;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} take, or require any person in the place or
    conveyance to produce, free of charge, a sample of any material
    found there;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} direct the owner or the person having possession,
    care or control of any material, equipment or document found in the
    place or conveyance --- or of the conveyance --- to move it or, for
    any time that may be necessary, not to move it or to restrict its
    movement;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} conduct, or require any person in the place or
    conveyance to conduct, any test or analysis or take any measurement
    of any material or equipment found there;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} take photographs or make recordings or sketches;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} examine and make copies, in whole or in part, of
    any book, document or other record found there;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} require any person in the place or conveyance to
    produce any book, document or other record found there for
    examination or copying;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any computer system or
    other device found there to examine information that is contained in
    or available to the computer system or device;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} reproduce any information in the form of a printout
    or other intelligible output for examination or copying; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used any copying equipment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conveyance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of entering the conveyance, the
  inspector may order the owner or person having possession, care or
  control of the conveyance to stop it or move it to a place where the
  inspector can enter it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excluded information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), the Minister of National
  Defence may refuse to disclose any information the disclosure of which
  could reasonably be expected to be injurious to the defence or
  security of Canada or of a state allied or associated with Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to inspector

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The owner or person in charge of a place or
  conveyance that is entered by an inspector who is carrying out their
  functions and every person in that place or conveyance shall give the
  inspector all reasonable assistance and provide them with any
  information that they may reasonably require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction and false statements

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} No person shall knowingly obstruct or hinder, or make
  a false or misleading statement either orally or in writing to, an
  inspector who is carrying out their functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Private property

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} An inspector who is carrying out their functions and
  any person accompanying the inspector may enter on and pass through or
  over private property, and they are not liable for doing so.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant to enter dwelling-house

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector may not enter a dwelling-house without the occupant's
  consent, except under the authority of a warrant issued under
  subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application, a
  justice of the peace may issue a warrant authorizing the inspector
  named in it to enter a dwelling-house, subject to any conditions that
  may be specified in the warrant, if the justice of the peace is
  satisfied by information on oath that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dwelling-house is a place referred to in
    subsection 41(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entry to the dwelling-house is necessary for a
    purpose referred to in subsection 41(1); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} entry to the dwelling-house has been refused or
    there are reasonable grounds for believing that entry will be
    refused.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In executing the warrant, the inspector named in it
  may not use force unless they are accompanied by a peace officer and
  the use of force is specifically authorized in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of telecommunication

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An application for a warrant may be submitted, and
  the warrant may be issued, by a means of telecommunication and section
  487.1 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) applies for those
  purposes with any necessary modifications.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Serious and imminent danger

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, in
  the course of an inspection, an inspector has reasonable grounds to
  believe that a controlled activity is conducted in a manner that poses
  a serious and imminent danger to the health or safety of the public,
  the inspector may order the licence holder, or any other person in the
  place or conveyance, to carry out any measure that the inspector
  considers necessary to reduce or eliminate that danger.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licence holder or any other person who is ordered
  by an inspector to carry out such a measure shall comply with the
  order and, in doing so, does not contravene subsection 7(1) or section
  8.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An inspector who orders a measure to be carried out
  shall, if appropriate, withdraw the order if they are satisfied that
  the controlled activity is no longer conducted in a manner that poses
  a serious and imminent danger to the health or safety of the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review by Minister

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An inspector shall, without delay, refer any decision
  to make or withdraw an order to the Minister for review. After the
  review, the Minister may amend, replace or rescind the order if the
  Minister is of the opinion that it is necessary to do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of order not suspended

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The referral of a decision to the Minister for review
  does not suspend the application of the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to comply

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a licence holder or any other person who is
  ordered to carry out a measure fails to comply with the order, the
  inspector may carry out the measure or require another person to do
  so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informing of action

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} After the measure is carried out, the inspector
  shall, as soon as feasible, advise the person who failed to comply
  with the order that the measure was carried out.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance not required

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} No person is required to carry out a measure ordered
  by an inspector if doing so would expose them to a danger, as defined
  in subsection 122(1) of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cost

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} A licence holder, or if no licence has been issued in
  respect of the controlled activity, the person who is responsible for
  the conduct of the controlled activity, shall bear the cost of
  carrying out any measure ordered by an inspector.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage and removal

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector may order that a thing seized under this Act be kept or
  stored in the place where it was seized or be removed to any other
  appropriate place.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interference

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except with the inspector's authority, no person
  shall remove, alter or interfere in any way with the seized thing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of seized thing

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** An inspector who seizes a
thing under this Act shall release it if they are satisfied that the
provisions of this Act and the regulations that apply to that thing have
been complied with.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for restoration

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  reasonable notice in writing to the Minister, the owner of a thing
  seized under this Act, or the person in possession of it at the time
  of its seizure, may, within 60 days after the day of its seizure,
  apply to a provincial court judge within whose jurisdiction the
  seizure was made for an order of restoration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of restoration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The provincial court judge may order that the seized
  thing be restored immediately to the applicant if, on hearing the
  application, the judge is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is entitled to possession of it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it does not pose a serious and imminent danger to
    the health or safety of the public; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it will not be required as evidence in a
    prosecution for an offence that is subsequently instituted under
    this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of later restoration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, on hearing an application, the provincial court
  judge is satisfied that the applicant is entitled to possession of the
  seized thing and that it does not pose a serious and imminent danger
  to the health or safety of the public but is not satisfied with
  respect to paragraph (2)(c), the judge may order that the thing be
  restored to the applicant

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the expiry of 180 days after the day of its
    seizure if no prosecution for an offence under this Act has been
    instituted before that day; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the final conclusion of proceedings under this
    Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The provincial court judge may not make an order for
  the restoration of the seized thing if it has been forfeited by
  consent under subsection 47(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If no
  application is made for the restoration of a thing seized under this
  Act within 60 days after the day of its seizure, or an application has
  been made but no order of restoration is made after the application
  has been heard, the seized thing is forfeited to Her Majesty in right
  of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture with consent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an inspector has seized a thing and its owner, or
  the person in possession of it at the time of its seizure, consents in
  writing to its forfeiture, the thing is forfeited to Her Majesty in
  right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 48, the Minister may dispose of a
  seized thing that is forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada in
  any manner that the Minister directs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preservation

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall make
reasonable efforts to preserve any thing seized under this Act pending
its disposition.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a thing
seized under this Act, or the person in possession of it at the time of
its seizure, shall bear any associated seizure, storage, transfer,
preservation or disposition costs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of analyst

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
designate any individual, or class of individuals, as an analyst for the
administration and enforcement of this Act and the regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Analysis and examination

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector may submit to an analyst, for analysis or examination, any
  thing seized or taken by the inspector.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate or report of analyst

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An analyst who has made an analysis or examination
  may issue a certificate or report setting out the results of the
  analysis or examination.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Admissibility

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  analyst's certificate or report is admissible in evidence in any
  prosecution for an offence under this Act if it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appears to be signed by the analyst;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} states that a thing has been analyzed or examined
    by the analyst; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} states the results of that analysis or examination.

  In the absence of evidence to the contrary, the certificate or report
  is proof of the statements contained in it without proof of the
  signature or official character of the person appearing to have signed
  it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attendance of analyst required

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party against whom a certificate or report is
  produced may, with leave of the court, require the analyst's
  attendance for the purpose of cross-examination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of intention to produce
  certificate or report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No certificate or report may be admitted in evidence
  unless, before the trial, the party intending to produce it has given
  reasonable notice of that intention, together with a copy of the
  certificate or report, to the party against whom it is intended to be
  produced.

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-255394 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 54 to
58, every person who contravenes this Act or the regulations is guilty
of an offence and liable, on summary conviction,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a contravention with respect to a
  human pathogen that falls into Risk Group 2,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine of not more than
    \$50,000, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a subsequent offence, to a fine of not more
    than \$250,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than three
    months, or to both; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine of not more than
    \$250,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than three
    months, or to both, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a subsequent offence, to a fine of not more
    than \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than six
    months, or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Breach of duty

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes section 6 and, as a result, creates a risk to the health or
safety of the public is guilty of an indictable offence and liable to
imprisonment for a term of not more than two years.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Wanton or reckless breach of duty

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes section 6 and who shows wanton or reckless disregard for the
health or safety of other persons and, as a result, creates a risk to
the health or safety of the public is guilty of an indictable offence
and liable to imprisonment for a term of not more than five years.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of subsection 7(1) or 18(7)

**[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes subsection 7(1) or 18(7) with respect to a human pathogen
that falls into Risk Group 3 or Risk Group 4 or a toxin is guilty of an
offence and liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, for a first offence, to
  a fine of not more than \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not
  more than six months, or to both, and, for a subsequent offence, to a
  fine of not more than \$1,000,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not
  more than two years, or to both; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, for a first offence, to a fine
  of not more than \$250,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more
  than three months, or to both, and, for a subsequent offence, to a
  fine of not more than \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not
  more than six months, or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of section 8 --- knowingly

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who knowingly contravenes section 8 is guilty of an offence and
  liable on conviction on indictment to a fine of not more than
  \$1,000,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than five years,
  or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of section 8

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes section 8 is guilty of
  an offence and liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, for a first offence,
    to a fine of not more than \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term
    of not more than six months, or to both, and, for a subsequent
    offence, to a fine of not more than \$1,000,000 or to imprisonment
    for a term of not more than two years, or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, for a first offence, to a
    fine of not more than \$250,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not
    more than three months, or to both, and, for a subsequent offence,
    to a fine of not more than \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term
    of not more than six months, or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intentional release

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
intentionally releases or otherwise abandons a human pathogen or toxin
in contravention of this Act or the regulations and, as a result,
creates a risk to the health or safety of the public is guilty of an
indictable offence and liable to imprisonment for a term of not more
than 10 years.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

**[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** No person who establishes
that they exercised all due diligence to prevent the commission of an
offence under this Act may be convicted of that offence, except for an
offence under

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} section 53, with respect to a contravention of
  subsection 7(1), section 17 and subsection 41(6);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 55;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} section 56, with respect to a contravention of
  subsection 7(1);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} subsection 57(1); and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} section 58.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Place of trial

**[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** A prosecution for an
offence under this Act may be instituted, heard and determined in the
place where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the offence was committed or the subject matter of
  the prosecution arose;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the accused was apprehended; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the accused happens to be or is carrying on business.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offences

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** If an offence under this
Act is committed or continued on more than one day, the person who
committed the offence is liable to be convicted for a separate offence
for each day on which it is committed or continued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

- **[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Summary conviction proceedings for
  an offence under this Act may be instituted no later than two years
  after the day on which the subject matter of the proceedings becomes
  known to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document that appears to be issued by the Minister,
  certifying the day on which the subject matter of the proceedings
  became known to the Minister, is admissible in evidence without proof
  of the signature or official character of the person appearing to have
  signed it. In the absence of evidence to the contrary, it is proof of
  the matters asserted in it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors, officers, etc.

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** If a person other than an
individual commits an offence under this Act, any of the person's
directors, officers, agents or mandataries who directed, authorized,
assented to, acquiesced in or participated in the commission of the
offence is a party to and guilty of the offence and is liable on
conviction to the punishment provided for the offence, whether or not
the person has been prosecuted or convicted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences by employees, agents or
mandataries

**[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** In a prosecution for an
offence under this Act, it is sufficient proof of the offence to
establish that it was committed by the accused's employee acting within
the scope of their employment, or the accused's agent or mandatary
acting within the scope of their authority, whether or not the employee,
agent or mandatary is identified or prosecuted for the offence, unless
the accused establishes that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the offence was committed without the accused's
  knowledge or consent; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the accused exercised all due diligence to prevent
  its commission.

  of the opinion that prompt measures are required to address a serious

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The interim order has effect from the day on which it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 14 days after the day on which it is made, unless

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which it is repealed,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which a regulation made under section 66

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} one year after the day on which it is made or any

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of any provision of this Act other

  reference to the portion of an interim order containing any provision

  provision.

  House of Parliament within 15 days after the day on which it is made.

  order may be sent to the Clerk of the House if the House is not
  sitting.

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an organization established for the purpose of
    writing standards, including an organization accredited by the
    Standards Council of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an industrial or trade organization; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a government.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A regulation may incorporate by reference documents
  that the Minister reproduces or translates from documents that are
  produced by a person or body other than the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with any adaptations of form or reference that
    would facilitate the incorporation of those documents into the
    regulation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a form that sets out only those parts that apply
    for the purposes of the regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jointly produced documents

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A regulation may incorporate by reference documents
  that the Minister produces jointly with another government for the
  purpose of harmonizing the regulation with other laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Internally produced standards

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A regulation may incorporate by reference technical
  or explanatory documents that the Minister produces, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} specifications, classifications, illustrations,
    graphs or other information of a technical nature; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} test methods, procedures, operational standards,
    laboratory safety standards or performance standards of a technical
    nature.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ambulatory incorporation by reference

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A document may be incorporated by reference as
  amended from time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporated document not a regulation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A document that is incorporated by reference in a
  regulation is not a regulation for the purposes of the [Statutory
  Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) to (5) do not limit any authority to
  make regulations incorporating documents by reference that exists
  apart from those subsections.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

**[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** If a provision of a

convicted of an offence, or subjected to a penalty, for the
contravention of the provision unless it is proved that, at the time of
the alleged contravention,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the document was reasonably accessible to the person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} reasonable steps had been taken to ensure that the
  document was accessible to persons likely to be affected by the
  regulation; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the document had been published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2022, c. 17, s. 76

  - ###### Clarification --- immediate application {#clarification-immediate-application .MarginalNote}

    [76]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, but subject to sections 77
    and 78, the amendments made by this Act also apply with respect to
    proceedings that are ongoing on the day on which this Act comes into
    force.

- --- 2022, c. 17, par. 77(2)(g)

  - ###### Certain applications for warrants {#certain-applications-for-warrants .MarginalNote}

    - [77]{.lawlabel} [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each of the following provisions,
      as it read immediately before the day on which this Act comes into
      force, continues to apply with respect to an application made for
      a warrant under the provision if the application is submitted, and
      no decision has been made in respect of the application, before
      that day:

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} subsection 42(4) of the [Human Pathogens and
        Toxins Act](/eng/acts/H-5.67);

- --- 2022, c. 17, s. 78.1

  - ###### Impact of remote proceedings {#impact-of-remote-proceedings .MarginalNote}

    - [78.1]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Justice must,
      no later than three years after the day on which this Act receives
      royal assent, initiate one or more independent reviews on the use
      of remote proceedings in criminal justice matters that must
      include an assessment of whether remote proceedings

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enhance, preserve or adversely affect access to
        justice;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintain fundamental principles of the
        administration of justice; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} adequately address the rights and obligations
        of participants in the criminal justice system, including
        accused persons.

    - ###### Report {#report .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Justice must, no later than five
      years after the day on which a review is initiated, cause a report
      on the review --- including any findings or recommendations
      resulting from it --- to be laid before each House of Parliament.

- --- 2022, c. 17, s. 78.2

  - ###### Review by committee {#review-by-committee .MarginalNote}

    - [78.2]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the start of the fifth year
      after the day on which this Act receives royal assent, the
      provisions enacted or amended by this Act are to be referred to a
      committee of the Senate and a committee of the House of Commons
      that may be designated or established for the purpose of reviewing
      the provisions.

    - ###### Report {#report-1 .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} The committees to which the provisions are
      referred are to review them and the use of remote proceedings in
      criminal justice matters and submit reports to the Houses of
      Parliament of which they are committees, including statements
      setting out any changes to the provisions that they recommend.
