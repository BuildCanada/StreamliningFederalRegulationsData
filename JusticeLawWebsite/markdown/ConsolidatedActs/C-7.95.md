## Canada--Panama Economic Growth and Prosperity Act {#canadapanama-economic-growth-and-prosperity-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2012, c. 26
:::

Assented to 2012-12-14

An Act to implement the Free Trade Agreement between Canada and the
Republic of Panama, the Agreement on the Environment between Canada and
the Republic of Panama and the Agreement on Labour Cooperation between
Canada and the Republic of Panama
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-164970 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada--Panama Economic Growth and Prosperity
Act](/eng/acts/C-7.95).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-164973 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Free Trade Agreement
    between Canada and the Republic of Panama, done at Ottawa on May 14,
    2010. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the whole or any portion
    of an Act of Parliament or a regulation, order or other instrument
    issued, made or established in the exercise of a power conferred by
    or under an Act of Parliament. ([texte législatif
    fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Joint Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Joint Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Joint Commission
    established under Article 21.01 of the Agreement. ([Commission
    mixte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister for
    International Trade. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[related agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[related agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Agreement on the Environment between Canada
      and the Republic of Panama, done at Ottawa on May 13, 2010; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Agreement on Labour Cooperation between
      Canada and the Republic of Panama, done at Ottawa on May 13,
      2010. ([accord connexe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation consistent with agreements

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, this
Act and any federal law that implements a provision of the Agreement or
a related agreement or fulfils an obligation of the Government of Canada
under the Agreement or a related agreement is to be interpreted in a
manner consistent with the Agreement or related agreement, as the case
may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of Act or Agreement to
water

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act or the Agreement applies to natural surface or
ground water in liquid, gaseous or solid state.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Act, by specific mention or omission, is to be construed
to affect in any manner the right of Parliament to enact legislation to
implement any provision of the Agreement or a related agreement or
fulfil any of the obligations of the Government of Canada under the
Agreement or a related agreement.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-164989 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-164992 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to implement the Agreement and the related agreements, the objectives of
which, as elaborated more specifically through their provisions, are to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish a free trade area in accordance with the
  Agreement;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} promote, through the expansion of recip­rocal trade,
  the harmonious development of the economic relations between Canada
  and the Republic of Panama in order to foster, in both countries, the
  advancement of economic activity;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide fair conditions of competition affecting
  trade between Canada and the Republic of Panama;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} substantially increase investment opportunities in
  Canada and the Republic of Panama;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} contribute, by the removal of barriers to trade, to
  the harmonious development and expansion of world and regional trade;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} enhance and enforce environmental laws and
  regulations and strengthen cooperation between Canada and the Republic
  of Panama on environmental matters;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} protect, enhance and enforce basic workers' rights,
  strengthen cooperation and build on the respective international
  commitments of Canada and the Republic of Panama on labour matters;
  and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} promote sustainable development.

## [Causes of Action]{.HTitleText1} {#h-165003 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Causes of action under Part 1

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person has any cause of action and no proceedings of any kind are to
  be taken, without the consent of the Attorney General of Canada, to
  enforce or determine any right or obligation that is claimed or arises
  solely under or by virtue of Part 1 or an order made under that Part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Causes of action under the Agreement or
  a related agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to Section C of Chapter 9 of the Agreement
  and Part Three and Annex 3 of the Agreement on Labour Cooperation
  between Canada and the Republic of Panama, done at Ottawa on May 13,
  2010, no person has any cause of action and no proceedings of any kind
  are to be taken, without the consent of the Attorney General of
  Canada, to enforce or determine any right or obligation that is
  claimed or arises solely under or by virtue of the Agreement or a
  related agreement.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Implementation of the Agreement and the Related Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-165009 .Part}

### [Approval]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165010 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements approved

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement and the
related agreements are approved.

### [Administrative and Institutional Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165013 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian representative on Joint
Commission

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is the
principal representative of Canada on the Joint Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of expenditures

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
is to pay its appropriate share of the aggregate of any expenditures
incurred by or on behalf of the Joint Commission.

### [Panels, Committees, Subcommittees, Working Groups and Expert Groups]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165018 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint representatives of Canada to any committee,
    subcommittee or working group referred to in paragraph 6 of Article
    21.01 of the Agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} appoint a panellist in accordance with Article
    22.08 of the Agreement; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} propose candidates to serve as the chair of a
    panel, or select the chair, in accordance with that Article 22.08.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister of the Environment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of the Environment may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint representatives of Canada to the committee
    referred to in Article 17 of the Agreement on the Environment
    between Canada and the Republic of Panama, done at Ottawa on May 13,
    2010;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} appoint a panellist in accordance with paragraph 11
    of Annex I to that Agreement; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} propose candidates to serve as the chair of a
    panel, or select the chair, in accordance with that paragraph.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of the Minister of Labour

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Labour may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint representatives of Canada to any committee,
    working group or expert group referred to in Article 7 of the
    Agreement on Labour Cooperation between Canada and the Republic of
    Panama, done at Ottawa on May 13, 2010;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} appoint a panellist in accordance with paragraph 4
    of Annex 2 to that Agreement; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} propose candidates to serve as the chairperson of a
    panel, or select the chairperson, in accordance with that paragraph.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrative support

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is to
designate an agency, division or branch of the Government of Canada to
facilitate the operation of Chapter 22 of the Agreement and to provide
administrative assistance to panels established under that Chapter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of costs

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Government of Canada
is to pay the costs of or its appropriate share of the costs of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the remuneration and expenses payable to members of
  panels, committees, subcommittees, working groups and expert groups,
  to independent experts and to the assistants of panel members; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the general expenses incurred by panels, committees,
  subcommittees, working groups and expert groups.

### [Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-165041 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders re Article 22.13 of Agreement

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, for the purpose of suspending benefits in
  accord­ance with Article 22.13 of the Agreement, by order, do any one
  or more of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend rights or privileges granted by Canada to
    the Republic of Panama or to goods of the Republic of Panama under
    the Agreement or any federal law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} modify or suspend the application of any federal
    law, with respect to the Republic of Panama or to goods of the
    Republic of Panama;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} extend the application of any federal law to the
    Republic of Panama or to goods of the Republic of Panama; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} take any other measure that the Governor in Council
    considers necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless repealed, an order made under subsection (1)
  has effect for the period specified in the order.
