## Dental Benefit Act {#dental-benefit-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2022, c. 14, s. 2
:::

Assented to 2022-11-17

Cost of Living Relief Act, No. 2 (Targeted Support for Households)

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 2 of chapter 14 of the Statutes of Canada, 2022, in
force on assent November 17, 2022.\]
:::
:::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the cost of dental care services is of particular concern for
Canadians, as many have no access to a dental services plan and do not
use those services because of the cost;

Whereas a lack of access to dental care services not only causes harm to
children but also has an impact on the health care system, with dental
surgeries being regularly performed in pediatric hospitals;

And whereas the Government of Canada recognizes the need to provide
interim dental benefits for children under 12 years old while working
towards the development of a long term national dental care program;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1379426 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Dental Benefit Act](/eng/acts/D-1.55).

## [Dental Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1379450 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of benefit

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must pay a
dental benefit to a person who is eligible for the benefit and who makes
an application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (4), a person is eligible for a dental benefit if they
  meet the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are entitled to apply for the dental benefit
    under section 5, 6 or 7;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they make an application in accordance with section
    8 and provide the Minister with any information that the Minister
    may require in respect of the application;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} their adjusted income is less than \$90,000 for the
    base taxation year in relation to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section
      5 or paragraph 7(a), December 1, 2022, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section
      6 or paragraph 7(b), July 1, 2023;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} they make the application in respect of a person
    who has received or will receive dental care services the costs of
    which have not been and will not be fully paid or reimbursed under a
    program or plan established by the government of Canada or of a
    province; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} they make the application in respect of a person
    who is not insured under a dental services plan and who does not
    have access to a dental care insurance plan obtained on the basis of
    the employment of the applicant, their cohabiting spouse or common
    law partner or any other person

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person who has received dental
      care services on or before the date of the application, on the
      date the person received the services, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person who will receive dental
      care services after the date of the application, on the date of
      the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Part-year residents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of determining the applicant's
  adjusted income in paragraph (1)(c), if a person was a non-resident in
  Canada for the purposes of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) at
  any time in a taxation year, the person's income for the year is
  deemed to be the amount that would have been the person's income for
  the year had the person been resident in Canada throughout the year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bankruptcy

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of determining the applicant's
  adjusted income in paragraph (1)(c), if in a taxation year a person
  becomes bankrupt, their income for the taxation year is to include
  their income for the taxation year that begins on January 1 of the
  calendar year that includes the date of bankruptcy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligibility

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may decide that an applicant is
  ineligible for a dental benefit if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister determines that their eligibility for
    a dental benefit for which they previously applied under section 5
    was based on a misrepresentation or the concealment of a material
    fact on the part of the applicant; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has decided that an applicant has
    committed a violation in relation to an application for a dental
    benefit previously made under section 5 and no decision to rescind
    that decision has been made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application --- first period

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An eligible parent may,
during the period beginning on December 1, 2022 and ending on June 30,
2023, apply to the Minister for a dental benefit in respect of each of
their qualified dependants

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} who has received or will receive dental care services
  in Canada during the period beginning on October 1, 2022 and ending on
  June 30, 2023;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} who is under 12 years of age on December 1, 2022; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of whom the parent is in receipt of a
  Canada child benefit on that date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application --- second period

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An eligible parent may,
during the period beginning on July 1, 2023 and ending on June 30, 2024,
apply to the Minister for a dental benefit in respect of each of their
qualified dependants

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} who has received or will receive dental care services
  in Canada during that period;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} who is under 12 years of age on July 1, 2023;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of whom the parent is in receipt of a
  Canada child benefit on that date; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in respect of whom no person who is entitled to apply
  for an additional dental benefit under paragraph 7(a) and who is
  eligible for that benefit has made an application under that
  paragraph.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application --- additional dental benefit

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A person may, during the
period beginning on July 1, 2023 and ending on June 30, 2024, apply to
the Minister for an additional dental benefit if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} they make the application in respect of a person

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in respect of whom they made an application for a
    dental benefit under section 5 and were eligible for that benefit,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of whom no eligible parent who is
    entitled to apply for a dental benefit under section 6 and who is
    eligible for that benefit has made an application under that
    section, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} who received dental care services during the
    period beginning on October 1, 2022 and ending on June 30, 2023, the
    cost of which exceeded \$650; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} they are an eligible parent, their adjusted income
  was less than \$90,000 for the base taxation year in relation to
  December 1, 2022 and they make the application in respect of their
  qualified dependant

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in respect of whom a dental benefit was not
    received for the period beginning on December 1, 2022 and ending on
    June 30, 2023,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of whom they made an application for a
    dental benefit under section 6 and are eligible for that benefit,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} who has received or will receive dental care
    services during the period beginning on July 1, 2023 and ending on
    June 30, 2024, the cost of which has exceeded or will exceed \$650,
    and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} who is not insured under a dental services plan
    and who does not have access to a dental care insurance plan
    obtained on the basis of the employment of the applicant, their
    cohabiting spouse or common law partner or any other person

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a dependant who has received
      dental care services on or before the date of the application, on
      the date the dependant received the services, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a dependant who will receive
      dental care services after the date of the application, on the
      date of the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application --- form, manner and contents

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An application for a dental
benefit must be made in the form and manner established by the Minister
and must contain the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name and address;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the attestation referred to in section 10;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the
  dentist, denturist or dental hygienist who provided, or who the
  applicant intends to have provide, dental care services for the person
  in respect of whom the application is made, as well as the month
  during which the services were provided or during which the applicant
  intends to have the services provided;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the
  employer, if any, of the applicant and of their cohabiting spouse or
  common-law partner

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person who has received dental
    care services on or before the date of the application, on the date
    that person received the services, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person who will receive dental
    care services after the date of the application, on the date of the
    application; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other information that the Minister may require.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of benefit

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the amount of a dental benefit is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$650, if an applicant's adjusted income is less
    than \$70,000 for the base taxation year in relation to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section
      5 or paragraph 7(a), December 1, 2022, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section
      6 or paragraph 7(b), July 1, 2023;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$390, if an applicant's adjusted income is equal
    to or greater than \$70,000 but less than \$80,000 for the base
    taxation year in relation to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section
      5 or paragraph 7(a), December 1, 2022, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section
      6 or paragraph 7(b), July 1, 2023; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$260, if an applicant's adjusted income is equal
    to or greater than \$80,000 but less than \$90,000 for the base
    taxation year in relation to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section
      5 or paragraph 7(a), December 1, 2022, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section
      6 or paragraph 7(b), July 1, 2023.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount --- shared-custody parent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, at the beginning of the relevant period, an
  applicant is a shared-custody parent of the person in respect of whom
  the application is made, the amount of a dental benefit is 50% of the
  amount referred to in paragraph (1)(a), (b) or (c), as the case may
  be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Relevant period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), the relevant
  period is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section 5
    or paragraph 7(a), the period beginning on October 1, 2022 and
    ending on June 30, 2023; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section 6
    or paragraph 7(b), the period beginning on July 1, 2023 and ending
    on June 30, 2024.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}December 1, 2022

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2) and paragraph
  (3)(a), the period beginning on October 1, 2022 and ending on June 30,
  2023 is deemed to begin on December 1, 2022.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} No more than two dental benefits --- or, in the case
  of shared-custody parents, no more than four dental benefits in the
  amount referred to in subsection (2) --- may be paid in respect of a
  person who has received or will receive dental services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attestation

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  applicant must, in their application, attest that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person in respect of whom the application is
    made has received dental care services in Canada during the relevant
    period or the applicant intends for the person to receive dental
    care services in Canada during the relevant period; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person in respect of whom the application is
    made is not insured under a dental services plan and does not have
    access to a dental care insurance plan obtained on the basis of the
    employment of the applicant, their cohabiting spouse or common law
    partner or any other person

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person who has received dental
      care services on or before the date of the application, on the
      date that person received the services, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person who the applicant
      intends to receive dental care services after the date of the
      application, on the date of the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Relevant period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the relevant
  period is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section 5
    or paragraph 7(a), the period beginning on October 1, 2022 and
    ending on June 30, 2023; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made under section 6
    or paragraph 7(b), the period beginning on July 1, 2023 and ending
    on June 30, 2024.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1379539 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister of National Revenue

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of National
Revenue may provide services and carry out activities to support the
Minister in the administration and enforcement of this Act and may
authorize the Commissioner or any other person who is employed or
engaged by the Agency or who occupies a position of responsibility in
the Agency to provide those services or carry out those activities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements or arrangements

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may enter
into agreements or arrangements with any department, board or agency of
the Government of Canada to assist the Minister in carrying out the
purposes and provisions of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
authorize the Commissioner or any other person or body, or member of a
class of persons or bodies, to exercise any power or perform any duty or
function of the Minister under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment out of Consolidated Revenue Fund

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** All amounts payable by
the Minister under section 3 are to be paid out of the Consolidated
Revenue Fund.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Social Insurance Number

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is
authorized to collect and use, for the purposes of the administration
and enforcement of this Act, the Social Insurance Number of a person who
makes an application under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information and documents

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, for any purpose related to verifying compliance or
  preventing non-compliance with this Act, require that any person
  provide any information or document, including a receipt for payment
  in respect of dental care services, within the reasonable time that
  the Minister may specify.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligibility

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may decide that an applicant who fails
  to fulfill or comply with a requirement under subsection (1) is
  ineligible for a dental benefit in respect of the benefit period to
  which the application relates.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefit cannot be charged, etc.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A dental benefit

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not subject to the operation of any law relating
  to bankruptcy or insolvency;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cannot be assigned, charged, attached or given as
  security;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} cannot be retained by way of deduction, set-off or
  compensation under any Act of Parliament other than this Act; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} is not garnishable moneys for the purposes of the
  [Family Orders and Agreements Enforcement Assistance
  Act](/eng/acts/F-1.4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reconsideration of application

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), the
  Minister may reconsider an application within 24 months after the day
  on which the benefit period to which the application relates ends.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister decides on reconsideration that a
  person has received money by way of a dental benefit to which they
  were not entitled, or has not received money to which they were
  entitled, the Minister must calculate the amount of the money and
  notify the person of the Minister's decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount repayable

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Section 20 applies if the Minister decides that a
  person has received money by way of a dental benefit to which they
  were not entitled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount payable

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister decides that a person was entitled to
  receive money by way of a dental benefit, and the money was not paid,
  the amount calculated under subsection (2) is payable to the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extended time to reconsider claim

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If, in the opinion of the Minister, a false or
  misleading statement or representation has been made in connection
  with an application for a dental benefit, the Minister has 72 months
  within which to reconsider the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for review

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is the subject of a decision of the Minister made under
  this Act may make a request to the Minister, in the form and manner
  established by the Minister, for a review of that decision at any time
  within 90 days after the day on which they are notified of the
  decision or any further time that the Minister may allow.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must review the decision if a request
  for its review is made under subsection (1). On completion of the
  review, the Minister must confirm, vary or rescind the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must notify the person who made the
  request of the result of the Minister's review under subsection (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of overpayment or erroneous
payment

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister determines that a person has received a dental benefit to
  which they are not entitled, or an amount in excess of the amount of
  such a benefit to which they are entitled, the person must repay the
  amount of the payment or the excess amount, as the case may be, as
  soon as feasible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery as debt due to His Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the erroneous payment or overpayment,
  as determined by the Minister, constitutes a debt due to His Majesty
  as of the day on which the amount was paid and the debt is payable and
  may be recovered by the Minister as of the day the Minister determined
  the amount of the erroneous payment or overpayment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation or prescription period

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section, no action
  or proceedings may be taken to recover money owing under this Act
  after the end of the six-year limitation or prescription period that
  begins on the day on which the money becomes due and payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction, set-off or compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Money owing by a person under this Act may be
  recovered at any time by way of deduction from, or set-off or
  compensation against, any sum of money, including a dental benefit,
  that may be due or payable by His Majesty to the person, other than an
  amount payable under section 122.61 of the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment of liability

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person's liability for money owing under this
  Act is acknowledged in accordance with subsection (5), the time during
  which the limitation or prescription period has run before the
  acknowledgment does not count in the calculation of that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment after limitation or
  prescription period

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a person's liability for money owing under this
  Act is acknowledged in accordance with subsection (5) after the end of
  the limitation or prescription period, an action or proceedings to
  recover the money may, subject to subsections (3) and (6), be brought
  within six years after the date of the acknowledgment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of acknowledgments

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An acknowledgment of liability means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a written promise to pay the money owing, signed by
    the person or their agent or other representative;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written acknowledgment of the money owing, signed
    by the person or their agent or other representative, whether or not
    a promise to pay can be implied from it and whether or not it
    contains a refusal to pay;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a part payment by the person or their agent or
    other representative of any money owing; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any acknowledgment of the money owing made by the
    person, their agent or other representative or the trustee or
    administrator in the course of proceedings under the [Bankruptcy and
    Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3) or any other legislation dealing with
    the payment of debts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation or prescription period
  suspended

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The running of a limitation or prescription period in
  respect of money owing under this Act is suspended during any period
  in which it is prohibited to commence or continue an action or other
  proceedings against the person to recover money owing under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply in respect of an action
  or proceedings relating to the execution, renewal or enforcement of a
  judgment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No interest payable

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** No interest is payable on
any amount owing to His Majesty under this Act as a result of an
overpayment or an erroneous payment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violations

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person commits a violation if they

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} knowingly make, in relation to an application for a
    dental benefit, a representation that is false or misleading; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make an application for, and receive, a dental
    benefit knowing that they are not eligible to receive it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may impose a penalty on a person if the
  Minister is of the opinion that the person has committed a violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of penalty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may set the amount of the penalty for
  each violation at not more than 50% of the dental benefit that was or
  would have been paid as a result of committing the violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The maximum amount of all penalties that may be
  imposed on a particular person under this section is \$5,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, no penalty may be imposed on a
  person if they mistakenly believe that a representation is true or
  that they were eligible to receive the dental benefit, as the case may
  be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of penalty

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of a penalty is to promote compliance
  with this Act and not to punish.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation of imposition of penalties

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A penalty must not be
imposed under section 23 if more than three years have passed after the
day on which the act that would constitute the violation occurred.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rescission or reduction of penalty

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may rescind
the imposition of a penalty under section 23, or reduce the penalty, on
the presentation of new facts or on being of the opinion that the
penalty was imposed without knowledge of, or on the basis of a mistake
as to, some material fact.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery as debt due to His Majesty

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** A penalty imposed under
section 23 constitutes a debt due to His Majesty and the debt is payable
and may be recovered by the Minister as of the day on which the penalty
is imposed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person commits an offence who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of obtaining a dental benefit for
    themselves, knowingly uses false identity information or another
    person's identity information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with intent to steal all or a substantial part of
    another person's dental benefit, counsels that person to apply for
    the benefit; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} knowingly makes, in relation to one or more
    applications under this Act, three or more representations that are
    false or misleading, if the total amount of the dental benefits that
    were or would have been paid as a result of the applications is at
    least \$5,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is guilty of an offence under
  subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction to a fine of not more
  than the sum of \$5,000 plus an amount of not more than double the
  amount of the dental benefit that was or would have been paid as a
  result of committing the offence, to imprisonment for a term of not
  more than two years less a day, or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[identity
  information]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In paragraph (1)(a), [[identity
  information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
  402.1 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- investigators

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate as an investigator any person or class of
  persons for the purpose of enforcing section 27.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may authorize the Commissioner to
  designate as an investigator any employee or class of employees of the
  Agency for the purpose of enforcing section 27.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** Proceedings in respect of
an offence under this Act may be instituted at any time within, but not
later than, five years after the day on which the Minister becomes aware
of the subject matter of the prosecution.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sunset provision

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Despite any other
provision of this Act, a payment out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund
must not be made under this Act after the fifth anniversary of the day
on which this Act comes into force.
