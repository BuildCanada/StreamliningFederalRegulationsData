## Parks Canada Agency Act {#parks-canada-agency-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1998, c. 31
:::

Assented to 1998-12-03

An Act to establish the Parks Canada Agency and to amend other Acts as a
consequence
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the Government of Canada wishes to establish an Agency for the
purpose of ensuring that Canada's national parks, national historic
sites and related heritage areas are protected and presented for this
and future generations and in order to further the achievement of the
national interest as it relates to those parks, sites and heritage areas
and related programs;

Whereas the Government of Canada wishes to establish an Agency that,
through the exercise of its responsibilities in relation to those parks,
sites and heritage areas and related programs, will reflect Canada's
values and identity and contribute to enhancing pride in Canada;

Whereas the Government of Canada wishes to establish an Agency to
provide quality services to visitors and the Canadian public through
alternative human resource and administrative regimes and financial
authorities;

And Whereas it is in the national interest

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to protect the nationally significant examples of
  Canada's natural and cultural heritage in national parks, national
  historic sites, national marine conservation areas and related
  heritage areas in view of their special role in the lives of Canadians
  and the fabric of the nation,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to present that heritage through interpretive and
  educational programs for public understanding, appreciation and
  enjoyment, both for international visitors and the Canadian public,
  thereby enhancing pride, encouraging stewardship and giving expression
  to our identity as Canadians,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to carry out Canada's international obligations and
  agreements to protect, conserve and present that heritage and to
  contribute towards the protection and presentation of the global
  heritage and biodiversity,

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to include representative examples of Canada's land
  and marine natural regions in the systems of national parks and
  national marine conservation areas,

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} to commemorate places, people and events of national
  historic significance, including Canada's rich and ongoing aboriginal
  traditions,

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} to ensure the commemorative integrity of national
  historic sites,

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} to maintain or restore the ecological integrity of
  national parks,

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} to ensure the ecologically sustainable use of
  national marine conservation areas,

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} to protect heritage railway stations and the heritage
  character of federal heritage buildings,

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} to provide leadership and support to the Canadian
  Heritage Rivers System,

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} to provide Canadians with an opportunity to enjoy
  Canada's special places,

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} to maintain ecological and commemorative integrity as
  a prerequisite to the use of national parks and national historic
  sites, and

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} to manage visitor use and tourism to ensure both the
  maintenance of ecological and commemorative integrity and a quality
  experience in such heritage and natural areas for this and future
  generations;

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-388876 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this section apply in this Act.

  [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Parks Canada Agency
      established by section 3. ([Agence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Chief Executive Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Chief Executive Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chief
      Executive Officer appointed under section 10. ([directeur
      général]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee appointed under
      subsection 13(1). ([employé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  :   [[heritage protection programs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes
      programs that relate to

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} heritage railway stations, heritage lighthouses
        and federal heritage buildings;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Canadian heritage rivers and federal
        archaeology; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other matters within the jurisdiction of
        the Minister that relate to areas of Canadian natural or
        historical significance that the Minister may, with the approval
        of the Governor in Council, specify for the purposes of this
        definition. ([programmes de protection du
        patrimoine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of the
      Environment. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[national historic site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[national historic site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a place
      designated under subsection (2) or a national historic site of
      Canada to which the [Canada National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01)
      applies. ([lieu historique national]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[national marine conservation area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[national marine conservation area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      marine conservation area or reserve as defined in subsection 2(1)
      of the [Canada National Marine Conservation Areas
      Act](/eng/acts/C-7.3). ([aire marine nationale de
      conservation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[national park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[national park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a park or park reserve
      as defined in section 2 of the [Canada National Parks
      Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01). ([parc national]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[other protected heritage areas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[other protected heritage areas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} historic canals;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} historic museums that may be established by the
        Minister under the [Historic Sites and Monuments
        Act](/eng/acts/H-4);

      - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} the Saguenay-St. Lawrence Marine Park
        established by section 5 of the [Saguenay-St. Lawrence Marine
        Park Act](/eng/acts/S-1.3);

      - [(b.2)]{.lawlabel} the Rouge National Urban Park; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other areas within the jurisdiction of the
        Minister that are of Canadian natural or historical significance
        and that the Minister may, with the approval of the Governor in
        Council, specify for the purposes of this definition. ([autres
        lieux patrimoniaux protégés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may designate any historic place as
  defined in section 2 of the [Historic Sites and Monuments
  Act](/eng/acts/H-4) as a national historic site for the purposes of
  this Act.

## [Establishment of Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-388901 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby established
a body corporate to be called the Parks Canada Agency, that may exercise
powers and perform duties and functions only as an agent of Her Majesty
in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister responsible

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister is responsible for the Agency and the powers, duties and
  functions of the Minister, in that capacity, extend to and include all
  matters over which Parliament has jurisdiction, not by law assigned to
  any other department, board or agency of the Government of Canada,
  relating to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} areas of natural or historical significance to the
    nation, including national parks, national marine conservation
    areas, national historic sites, historic canals, historic museums
    established under the [Historic Sites and Monuments
    Act](/eng/acts/H-4), Saguenay-St. Lawrence Marine Park and Rouge
    National Urban Park;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} heritage railway stations, heritage lighthouses,
    federal heritage buildings, historic places in Canada, federal
    archaeology and Canadian heritage rivers; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the design and implementation of programs that
    relate primarily to built heritage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial direction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister has the overall direction of the Agency,
  which shall comply with any general or special direction given by the
  Minister with reference to the carrying out of its responsibilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (2), no direction may be
  given with respect to the matters referred to in section 13.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exercise of powers conferred on Minister

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to any direction given by the Minister, the Agency may exercise the
  powers and shall perform the duties and functions that relate to
  national parks, national historic sites, national marine conservation
  areas, other protected heritage areas and heritage protection programs
  that are conferred on, or delegated, assigned or transferred to, the
  Minister under any Act or regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers and employees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An officer or employee of the Agency may exercise any
  power and perform any duty or function referred to in subsection (1)
  if the officer or employee is appointed to serve in the Agency in a
  capacity appropriate to the exercise of the power or the performance
  of the duty or function, and in so doing, shall comply with any
  general or special direction given by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any power, duty or function of the Minister under
    this Act, other than those referred to in subsection 4(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a power to make regulations under this or any other
    Act; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a power to make designations or appointments under
    the [Historic Sites and Monuments Act](/eng/acts/H-4) or the
    [Heritage Railway Stations Protection Act](/eng/acts/H-3.5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibilities --- subject-matter

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  sites, national marine conservation areas, other protected heritage
  areas and heritage protection programs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}System plans

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Agency shall ensure that there are long-term
  plans in place for establishing systems of national parks, national
  historic sites and national marine conservation areas.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New protected heritage areas

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Agency is responsible for negotiating, and
  recommending to the Minister, the establishment of new national parks,
  national marine conservation areas and other protected heritage areas
  and the acquisition of national historic sites.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibilities --- Acts

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Agency is responsible for the administration and
  enforcement of the Acts listed in Part 1 of the schedule and any
  regulations made under those Acts and the regulations listed in Part 2

## [Organization of Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-388976 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall appoint an officer, to be called the Chief
  Executive Officer, to hold office during pleasure for a term of not
  more than five years, which term may be renewed for one or more
  further terms.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Executive Officer shall be paid the
  remuneration that may be fixed by the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** In the event of the
absence or incapacity of the Chief Executive Officer or a vacancy in
that office, the Minister may appoint any person to exercise the powers
and perform the duties and functions of the Chief Executive Officer, but
no person may be so appointed for a term of more than ninety days
without the approval of the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role of Chief Executive Officer

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Executive Officer, under the direction of the Minister, has the
  control and management of the Agency and all matters connected with
  it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rank of deputy head

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Executive Officer has the rank and all the
  powers of a deputy head of a department.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Guiding principles and operational
  policies

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Executive Officer is responsible for
  preparing, for the Minister's approval, guiding principles and
  operational policies for matters within the Agency's responsibilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Executive Officer may delegate to any
  person any power, duty or function conferred on the Chief Executive
  Officer under this Act or any other Act or regulation.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-389019 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charter for the Agency

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Executive Officer is responsible for establishing a charter for
  the Agency that sets out the values and principles governing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the provision of services by the Agency to the
    public; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the management of the human resources of the
    Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Available to the public

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The charter established under subsection (1) shall be
  made available to the public.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principal office

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The principal office of
the Agency shall be in the National Capital Region described in the
schedule to the [National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal proceedings

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Actions, suits or other legal
  proceedings in respect of any right or obligation acquired or incurred
  by the Agency, whether in its own name or in the name of Her Majesty
  in right of Canada, may be brought or taken by or against the Agency
  in the name of the Agency in any court that would have jurisdiction if
  the Agency were not an agent of Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Property acquired by the Agency is the property of
  Her Majesty in right of Canada and title to it may be held in the name
  of Her Majesty or in the name of the Agency.

## [Financial Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-389034 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriations

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Money
  may be appropriated by Parliament from time to time by way of a vote
  in an appropriation Act or any other Act of Parliament for the
  purposes of making operating and capital expenditures of the Agency

  contributions.

    acquired, held or administered by the Agency;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} proceeds from any of the following transactions in
    respect of federal real property and federal immovables under the
    administration of the Minister for the purposes of the Agency:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the lease or giving of a licence,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the transfer to Her Majesty in any right other
      than Canada of administration and control, otherwise than in
      perpetuity, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a disposition of any right or interest, other
      than a disposition referred to in paragraph 21(2)(a);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} proceeds from, including fees paid under any Act of
    Parliament in respect of, the provision by the Agency of a service,
    of the use of a facility or of a product, right or privilege;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} proceeds from any money recovered under section 29;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any refund of expenditures made in previous fiscal
    years.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of New Parks and Historic
Sites Account

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  is hereby established an account in the accounts of Canada to be
  called the New Parks and Historic Sites Account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts credited to Account

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} There shall be credited to the New Parks and Historic
  Sites Account money appropriated by Parliament from time to time by
  way of an appropriation Act or any other Act of Parliament for the
  purposes specified in subsection (3), and any revenues of the Agency
  resulting, including proceeds, from

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any of the following transactions in respect of
    federal real property and federal immovables under the
    administration of the Minister for the purposes of the Agency:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the sale or any other disposition in perpetuity
      of any right or interest,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the transfer of administration to another
      minister or to an agent corporation, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the transfer of administration and control in
      perpetuity to Her Majesty other than in right of Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a gift, bequest or other form of donation made for
    the purposes of the Agency or for any purpose specified in
    subsection (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of New Parks and Historic
  Sites Account

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite any other Act of Parliament, amounts may be
  paid out of the New Parks and Historic Sites Account for the following
  purposes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to acquire any historic place, or lands for
    historic museums, or any interest therein, for the purpose of
    paragraph 3(d) of the [Historic Sites and Monuments
    Act](/eng/acts/H-4);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to acquire any real property or immovables for the
    purpose of establishing, enlarging or designating, as the case may
    be, any national park, national historic site, national marine
    conservation area or other protected heritage area;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to develop or maintain any national park, national
    historic site, national marine conservation area or other protected
    heritage area that is being established, enlarged or designated, and
    to make any related contribution or other payment;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to implement a decision by the Minister to
    recommend the establishment of a national park, national historic
    site, national marine conservation area or other protected heritage
    area, or to commemorate a historic place under section 3 of the
    [Historic Sites and Monuments Act](/eng/acts/H-4), and to make any
    related contribution or other payment; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} to repay advances made under subsection 22(2).

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-389086 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees for services and use of facilities

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, subject to any regulations that the Treasury Board may
  make for the purposes of this section, fix the fees or the manner of
  calculating the fees to be paid for a service or the use of a facility
  provided by the Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount not to exceed cost

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Fees for a service or the use of a facility that are
  fixed under subsection (1) may not exceed the cost to Her Majesty in
  right of Canada of providing the service or the use of the facility.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees for products, rights and privileges

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, subject
to any regulations that the Treasury Board may make for the purposes of
this section, fix the fees or the manner of calculating fees in respect
of products, rights or privileges provided by the Agency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  fixing a fee under section 23 or 24, the Minister must consult with
  any persons and organizations that the Minister considers to be
  interested in the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must publish any fee fixed under section
  23 or 24 in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) within
  thirty days after fixing it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to Scrutiny Committee

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A fee fixed under section 23 or 24 stands permanently
  referred to the Committee referred to in section 19 of the [Statutory
  Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) to be reviewed and scrutinized as if
  it were a statutory instrument.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The Treasury Board may
make regulations for the purposes of sections 23 and 24.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collection of fees

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** The Agency may enter into
an agreement with any person respecting the collection of fees fixed
under this Act or any other Act under which the Agency provides
services, facilities, products, rights or privileges and,
notwithstanding subsections 17(1) and (4) of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), authorizing that person to withhold
amounts from those fees.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission of fees

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may remit or
refund all or part of a fee fixed under section 23 or 24 or under any
other Act under which the Agency provides services, facilities,
products, rights or privileges, and the interest on it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of fees and costs

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The Agency may recover,
as a debt due to Her Majesty, any fee or charge fixed under this or any
other Act, any costs incurred by the Agency, and any interest on those
fees, charges or costs, that relate to providing a service, facility,
product, right or privilege by the Agency under this or any other Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may delegate
to the Agency any power, duty or function conferred on the Minister
under sections 23 to 25 and 28.

## [Application of the Official Languages Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-389131 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Official Languages
Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01) applies

**[[36.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36.1 .sectionLabel}** For greater
certainty, the [Official Languages Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01) applies to the
Agency and the Agency has the duty, under section 25 of that Act, to
ensure that, where services are provided or made available by another
person or organization on its behalf, any member of the public in Canada
or elsewhere can communicate with and obtain those services from that
person or organization in either official language, in any case where
those services, if provided by the Agency, would be required under Part
IV of the [Official Languages Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01) to be provided in
either official language.

## [Transitional]{.HTitleText1} {#h-389134 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriation Act

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** The provisions made by

into force or a subsequent fiscal year, based on the Estimates for that
year, to defray the charges and expenses of the public service of Canada
within the Parks Canada Sector of the Department of Canadian Heritage in
relation to any matter for which the Agency is responsible by virtue of
this Act are deemed to be an amount appropriated for defraying the
charges and expenses of the Agency in the amount that the Treasury Board
may, on the recommendation of the Minister, determine.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this

[[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person whose employment in
    the Department of Canadian Heritage is terminated under paragraph
    11(2)(g.1) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) and
    who is appointed to the Agency following an offer of employment made
    by the Agency as a result of a transfer of any work, undertaking or
    business from the Department of Canadian Heritage to the
    Agency. ([employé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[grievance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[grievance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service Staff Relations
    Act](/eng/acts/P-35). ([grief]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pending competitions and appointments

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** A competition being
conducted or an appointment being or about to be made under the [Public
Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) in respect of a position
within the Department of Canadian Heritage the duties and functions of
which are assigned to a position within the Agency, may continue to be
conducted or made as if the Agency were a department for the purposes of
that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pending appeals

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appeal made under section 21 of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) by any person against an appointment to a
  position within the Department of Canadian Heritage the duties and
  functions of which are assigned to a position within the Agency, and
  not finally disposed of on the assignment, must be dealt with and
  disposed of in accordance with that Act as if the Agency were a
  department for the purposes of that Act and the person continued to be
  an employee for the purposes of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other recourse

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any recourse commenced by an employee under the
  [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) that has not been
  finally dealt with on the appointment of the employee to the Agency
  must be dealt with and disposed of in accordance with that Act as if
  the Agency were a department for the purposes of that Act and the
  person continued to be an employee for the purposes of that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employees on probation

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  employee who was considered to be on probation under section 28 of the
  [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) immediately before
  being appointed to the Agency continues on probation with the Agency
  until the end of any period established by the Public Service
  Commission by regulation for that employee or a class of persons of
  which that employee is a member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rejection

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 28(2) of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) applies to an employee of the Agency who is on
  probation but the reference to deputy head in that subsection is to be
  read as a reference to the Chief Executive Officer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated positions

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** If an employee,
immediately before being appointed to the Agency, occupied a position
that was a designated position in the Department of Canadian Heritage,
that employee is, on appointment to the Agency, deemed to occupy a
designated position in the Agency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pending grievances

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  grievance commenced by an employee under the [Public Service Staff
  Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-35) that has not been finally dealt with on
  the appointment of the employee to the Agency, must be dealt with and
  disposed of in accordance with that Act as if the employee's
  employment in the Department of Canadian Heritage had not been
  terminated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation of decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A final decision with respect to a grievance referred
  to in subsection (1) that provides for the reinstatement of or payment
  of money to a person must be implemented by the Agency as soon as
  practicable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Successor rights

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
section 48.1 of the [Public Service Staff Relations
Act](/eng/acts/P-35), the Governor in Council may fix the date on which
the Parks Canada Sector and any other portion of the Department of
Canadian Heritage are deemed to be severed from the Department and to
become a part of the Agency, and that date is to be used for the
purposes of subsections 48.1(3), (6) and (7) of that Act.
