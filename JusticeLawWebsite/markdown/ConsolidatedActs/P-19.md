## Precious Metals Marking Act {#precious-metals-marking-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. P-19
:::

An Act respecting the marking of articles containing precious metals
::::

:::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-396861 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Precious Metals Marking Act](/eng/acts/P-19).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-19, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-396866 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[apply]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[apply]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a mark, includes any
    application or attachment thereof to, or any use thereof on, in
    connection with or in relation to

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an article,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} anything attached to an article,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} anything to which an article is attached,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} anything in or on which an article is, or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} anything so used or placed as to lead to a
      reasonable belief that the mark thereon is meant to be taken as a
      mark on an article; ([appliquer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[article]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[article]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any article of merchandise,
    and includes any portion of any article of merchandise, whether a
    distinct part thereof or not, other than an article or a part
    thereof designated by the regulations; ([article]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commissioner of
    Competition appointed under the [Competition
    Act](/eng/acts/C-34); ([commissaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is a manufacturer
    or an importer of any article to which this Act applies and any
    person who traffics by wholesale or retail in any such article and
    includes any director, manager, officer or agent or mandatary of
    that person; ([commerçant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an inspector appointed or
    designated in accordance with section
    6; ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mark]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mark]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any mark, sign, device,
    imprint, stamp, brand, label, ticket, letter, word or
    figure; ([marque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada as is designated by the Governor in Council
    as the Minister for the purposes of this
    Act; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[plated article]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[plated article]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an article composed of
    any substance on the surface of which a layer or plating of a
    precious metal is deposited or plated by means of a chemical,
    electrical, mechanical or metallurgical process or by means of a
    combination of any of those processes, and an article composed of an
    inferior metal to the surface of which a covering or sheeting of a
    precious metal is fixed by brazing, soldering or by any mechanical
    means; ([article plaqué]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[precious metal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[precious metal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means gold, palladium,
    platinum and silver and an alloy of any of those metals and any
    other metal and an alloy thereof that is designated by the
    regulations as a precious metal for the purposes of this
    Act; ([métal précieux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[precious metal article]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[precious metal article]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an article
    wholly or partly, or purporting to be wholly or partly, composed of
    a precious metal and includes a plated article, and the word
    "article" when used in association with the name of a precious metal
    has a corresponding meaning; ([article de métal
    précieux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[quality mark]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[quality mark]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mark indicating or
    purporting to indicate the quality, quantity, fineness, weight,
    thickness, proportion or kind of precious metal in an
    article; ([marque de qualité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sell, offer for sale, expose for sale and have in
      possession for sale,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} distribute or offer, whether as a premium or
      prize, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} display in such manner as to lead to a reasonable
      belief that the article so displayed is intended for
      sale. ([vendre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-19, s. 2
- 1999, c. 2, s. 48
- 2011, c. 21, s. 154(E)
:::

## [Marking]{.HTitleText1} {#h-396891 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unauthorized markings

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Except as authorized by
this Act, no dealer shall apply to an article, or bring into Canada an
article that has applied to it, a mark that suggests or indicates that
the article is a precious metal article.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-19, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When quality mark may be applied

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  quality mark that truly and correctly indicates the quality of the
  precious metal of which an article is in whole or in part composed may
  be applied to the article if the precious metal content of the article
  and the quality of the precious metal content meet standards provided
  in the regulations with respect to that precious metal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quality marks and manner of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A quality mark applied to a precious metal article
  shall be a mark authorized by the regulations for use in association
  with the precious metal of which the article is in whole or in part
  composed and shall be applied in a manner authorized by the
  regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Trademark to be applied

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a quality mark is applied to a precious metal
  article in accordance with this section, a mark that is a registered
  trademark within the meaning of the [Trademarks Act](/eng/acts/T-13),
  or in respect of which an application for registration that is
  acceptable to the Minister has been filed in accordance with that Act,
  shall be applied to the article in a manner authorized by the
  regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marks authorized by law of foreign
  country

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a precious metal article

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is hallmarked in accordance with the laws of the
    United Kingdom, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has applied to it by the government of any foreign
    country a mark authorized to be applied thereto under the laws of
    that country that truly and correctly indicates the quality of the
    precious metal of which the article is in whole or in part composed,

  subsection (3) is not applicable thereto and a quality mark may be
  applied to the article in accordance with subsections (1) and (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other marks

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A precious metal article to which a quality mark is
  applied or to which a mark described in paragraph (4)(a) or (b) is
  applied may also have applied thereto

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} numerals intended to identify the article or
    pattern and not calculated to mislead or deceive;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name or initials of a dealer; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other mark not calculated to mislead or
    deceive.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-19, s. 4
- [2014, c. 20, s. 366(E)](#1034903-1145734){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1034903-1145734.html"
  role="button"}
:::

## [National Marks]{.HTitleText1} {#h-396915 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian mark on articles composed of a
precious metal

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an
  article that is wholly manufactured in Canada is composed of a
  precious metal of a quality prescribed in the regulations and has a
  quality mark applied to it in accordance with section 4, there may be
  applied to the article, by a dealer authorized by an order made by the
  Commissioner under subsection (2), a national mark consisting of a
  representation of a maple leaf surrounded by the letter "C".

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order authorizing application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may, by order, authorize any dealer
  to apply the national mark described in subsection (1) in accordance
  with that subsection.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-19, s. 5
- 1999, c. 2, s. 49
:::

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-396923 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspectors

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may appoint or
designate any person as an inspector for the purposes of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-19, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of inspectors

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (1.1), an inspector may at any reasonable time enter the
  premises of any dealer, require the production for inspection of any
  precious metal article on the premises of a dealer and seize any
  precious metal article that the inspector believes on reasonable
  grounds is marked otherwise than in accordance with this Act and the
  regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant required to enter
  dwelling-house

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Where any premises referred to in subsection (1) is
  a dwelling-house, an inspector may not enter that dwelling-house
  without the consent of the occupant except under the authority of a
  warrant issued under subsection (1.2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} Where on [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  application a justice of the peace is satisfied by information on oath

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that entry to a dwelling-house is necessary for any
    purpose relating to the administration or enforcement of this Act,
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that entry to the dwelling-house has been refused
    or that there are reasonable grounds for believing that entry
    thereto will be refused,

  the justice of the peace may issue a warrant under his hand
  authorizing the inspector named therein to enter that dwelling-house
  subject to such conditions as may be specified in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of force

  [(1.3)]{.lawlabel} In executing a warrant issued under subsection
  (1.2), the inspector named therein shall not use force unless the
  inspector is accompanied by a peace officer and the use of force has
  been specifically authorized in the warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of seized articles

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any article seized pursuant to subsection (1) may be
  retained for a period of ninety days and, if before the expiration of
  that period any proceedings in respect of the article are taken under
  this Act, the article may be retained until the proceedings are
  finally disposed of.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-19, s. 7
- R.S., 1985, c. 31 (1st Supp.), s. 19
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate to be produced

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner shall furnish every inspector with a certificate of his
  designation as an inspector and on entering the premises of any dealer
  an inspector shall, if required to do so, produce the certificate to
  the person in charge of those premises.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to inspectors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A dealer or the person appearing to be in charge of
  the premises of a dealer and every person found therein shall, if
  requested to do so by an inspector, give an inspector all reasonable
  assistance to enable him to carry out his duties and functions under
  this Act and the regulations and shall furnish him with any
  information he may reasonably require with respect to any article
  found in the premises.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction of inspectors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall obstruct or hinder an inspector
  engaged in carrying out his duties and functions under this Act and
  the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False statements

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall knowingly make a false or misleading
  statement either orally or in writing to an inspector engaged in
  carrying out his duties and functions under this Act and the
  regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-19, s. 8
- 1999, c. 2, s. 49
:::

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-396955 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} designating articles that are exempt, conditionally
  or unconditionally, from the application of this Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designating parts of articles that are exempt,
  conditionally or unconditionally, from assay for the purposes of this
  Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} designating any metal and any alloy thereof to be a
  precious metal for the purposes of this Act;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} designating quality marks that may be applied to
  plated articles and quality marks that may be applied to other
  precious metal articles;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} prescribing marks indicating the nature of the base
  metal content of a plated article and prescribing the circumstances in
  which the use of those marks is required;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} establishing minimum precious metal content and
  quality standards and precious metal quality tolerances in respect of
  precious metal articles to which quality marks may be applied;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the methods by which quality marks and
  trademarks may be applied to precious metal articles;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the meaning to be attributed to quality
  marks designated pursuant to paragraph (d); and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} with respect to any other matter concerning which he
  deems regulations necessary or desirable to carry out the purposes and
  provisions of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-19, s. 9

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-396969 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences and punishment

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  dealer who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} applies to a precious metal article a mark not
    authorized by this Act or the regulations to be applied thereto,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} applies a mark to a precious metal article
    otherwise than in accordance with this Act and the regulations,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} omits or neglects to apply to a precious metal
    article any mark required by this Act or the regulations to be
    applied thereto,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} sells or imports into Canada a precious metal
    article to which a mark not authorized by this Act or the
    regulations has been applied or that is marked otherwise than in
    accordance with this Act and the regulations or that has not applied
    thereto any mark required by this Act or the regulations to be
    applied thereto,

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} destroys, defaces or in any manner renders
    indecipherable any mark applied to a precious metal article in
    accordance with this Act and the regulations,

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} applies to a plated article a mark that guarantees
    or purports to guarantee or induces or tends to induce a belief that
    the precious metal with which the article is plated will wear for a
    period of time, whether specified or not,

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} sells or imports into Canada a plated article to
    which a mark described in paragraph (f) has been applied,

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} prints, causes to be printed, issues, publishes,
    imports or in any manner makes use of any printed or written matter
    in the nature of an advertisement guaranteeing or purporting to
    guarantee or inducing or tending to induce a belief that the
    precious metal with which a plated article is plated will wear or
    last for a period of time, whether specified or not,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in any advertisement of a precious metal article or
    in any publication related to a precious metal article uses any mark
    the application of which to the article is not authorized by this
    Act or the regulations, or

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} in any manner not provided for in this subsection
    or subsection (2) contravenes or fails to comply with any provision
    of this Act or the regulations,

  is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not
  exceeding five hundred dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes or fails to comply with
  any provision contained in subsections 8(2) to (4) is guilty of an
  offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding five
  hundred dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A prosecution under subsection (1) may be instituted
  at any time within but not later than one year after the time when the
  subject-matter of the complaint arose.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-19, s. 10
- 1984, c. 40, s. 59
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition of articles on conviction

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  article in respect of which a conviction is had under this Act shall
  be broken, broken down or defaced and every part of the article, other
  than precious and semi-precious stones, watch movements and any other
  part designated by the regulations, is, on such conviction, in
  addition to any other punishment imposed, forfeited to Her Majesty
  and, subject to subsection (3), shall be disposed of in such manner
  and at such time and place as the Commissioner may direct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition of printed or written
  matter on conviction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All printed or written matter in respect of which a
  conviction is had under this Act is on the conviction, in addition to
  any other punishment imposed, forfeited to Her Majesty, and, subject
  to subsection (3), shall be disposed of in such manner and at such
  time and place as the Commissioner may direct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection of persons claiming interest

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 74 to 76 of the [Fisheries
  Act](/eng/acts/F-14) applies, with such modifications as the
  circumstances require, in respect of any article or printed or written
  matter forfeited under subsection (1) or (2) as though that article or
  matter were an article forfeited under subsection 72(1) of that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-19, s. 11
- 1999, c. 2, s. 49
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of Master or assayer

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** In any prosecution under
this Act, a document purporting to be a certificate of the Master or any
assayer of the Royal Canadian Mint stating that he has assayed or
examined an article and stating the result of his assay or examination
is admissible in evidence and in the absence of any evidence to the
contrary is proof of the statements contained in the certificate without
proof of the signature or official character of the person appearing to
have signed the certificate.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. P-19, s. 12
:::
