## Regional Development Incentives Act {#regional-development-incentives-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"} 1970, c. R-3
:::

An Act to provide incentives for the development of productive
employment opportunities in regions of Canada determined to require
special measures to facilitate economic expansion and social adjustment
::::

::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-426565 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Regional Development Incentives Act](/eng/acts/R-4.9).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1968-69, c. 56, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-426570 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an applicant for a
    development incentive or an applicant for a loan guarantee under
    this Act; ([requérant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[approved capital costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approved capital costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the capital
    costs, as determined by the Minister, of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establishing, expanding or modernizing a facility
      in respect of which a development incentive is authorized or a
      loan guarantee is authorized under this Act, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establishing a commercial facility in respect of
      which a loan guarantee is authorized under this Act; ([coût
      d'immobilisation approuvé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[capital to be employed in the operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[capital to be employed in the
    operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to the
    establishment or expansion of a facility, the aggregate of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approved capital costs,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the value, as accepted by the Minister, of the
      fixed assets that are to be employed in the operation and that are
      not included in the approved capital costs, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} such amount in respect of the working capital
      required for the purposes of the operation as is approved by the
      Minister; ([capital affecté à l'entreprise]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[commercial facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the structures,
    machinery and equipment that constitute the necessary components of
    a commercial operation; ([établissement commercial]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[designated region]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated region]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a region designated
    pursuant to section 3; ([région désignée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[development incentive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[development incentive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a primary
    development incentive, a secondary development incentive or a
    special development incentive described in section 4; ([subvention
    au développement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the structures, machinery and
    equipment that constitute the necessary components of a
    manufacturing or processing operation, other than an initial
    processing operation in a resource-based
    industry; ([établissement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to any class of
    matters to which this Act extends in any province or region of a
    province, means such member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada
    as is designated by the Governor in Council as the Minister for the
    purposes of this Act in relation to that class of matters in that
    province or region of a province; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a facility, the manufacturing or
      processing operation of which the facility constitutes the
      necessary components, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a commercial facility, the
      commercial undertaking of which the commercial facility
      constitutes the necessary
      components; ([entreprise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[total capital costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[total capital costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approved capital costs,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the value, as accepted by the Minister, of the
      fixed assets that are to be employed in the operation and that are
      not included in the approved capital costs, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the value, as accepted by the Minister, of the
      capitalized expenses incurred in bringing a new facility or
      commercial facility into commercial production or operation or in
      bringing an expanded or modernized facility into commercial
      production. ([coût d'immobilisation total]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. R-3, s. 2
- R.S., 1970, c. 25(2nd Supp.), s. 1
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 167, s. 34
- 1988, c. 17, s. 16

## [Designation of Regions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-426595 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of regions

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Governor in Council, after consultation with
  the government of any province or provinces, may for the purposes of
  this Act by order designate as a designated region, for the period set
  out in the order, any region comprising the whole of that province or
  those provinces, or any portion thereof not less than 12 500 square
  kilometres in size, that is determined to require special measures to
  facilitate economic expansion and social adjustment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria for designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A region may be designated pursuant to subsection (1)
  only if the Governor in Council, upon the report of the Minister, is
  satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} existing opportunities for productive employment in
    the region are exceptionally inadequate; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the provision of development incentives under this
    Act for the establishment of new facilities or the expansion or
    modernization of existing facilities in the region will make a
    significant contribution to economic expansion and social adjustment
    within the region.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. R-3, s. 3
- 1976-77, c. 55, s. 7
:::

## [Development Incentives]{.HTitleText1} {#h-426605 .Part}

### [Authorization of Incentives]{.HTitleText2} {#h-426606 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incentives for new and existing
facilities

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Upon application therefor
to the Minister by an applicant proposing to establish a new facility or
to expand or modernize an existing facility in a designated region, the
Minister may authorize the provision to the applicant, subject to this
Act and upon such terms and conditions as are prescribed by the
regulations, of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a primary development incentive by way of financial
  assistance to the applicant for the establishment, expansion or
  modernization of the facility;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a proposal to establish a new facility
  or to expand an existing facility to enable the manufacturing or
  processing of a product not previously manufactured or processed in
  the operation, a secondary development incentive by way of additional
  financial assistance to the applicant for the establishment of the new
  facility or the expansion of the existing facility for that purpose;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a special development incentive by way of financial
  assistance to the applicant for the establishment, expansion or
  modernization of the facility.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. R-3, s. 4
- R.S., 1970, c. 25(2nd Supp.), s. 2
:::

### [Maximum Amounts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-426614 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum primary development incentive

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of a primary development incentive shall be based on the
  approved capital costs of establishing, expanding or modernizing the
  facility in respect of which the primary development incentive is
  authorized and shall not exceed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 20% of those approved capital costs, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$6,000,000,

  whichever is the lesser amount.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum secondary development incentive

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of a secondary development incentive shall
  be based on the approved capital costs of establishing or expanding
  the facility in respect of which the secondary development incentive
  is authorized and on the number of jobs created directly in the
  operation and shall not exceed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 5% of those approved capital costs,

  plus

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$5,000 for each job determined by the Minister to
    have been created directly in the operation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum special development incentive

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amount of a special development incentive shall
  be based

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the approved capital costs of establishing,
    expanding or modernizing the facility in respect of which the
    special development incentive is authorized, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where such incentive is authorized in respect of a
    new facility or for the expansion of an existing facility to enable
    the manufacturing or processing of a product not previously
    manufactured or processed in the operation, on the approved capital
    costs of establishing or expanding the facility and on the number of
    jobs created directly in the operation,

  and shall not exceed,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in a case to which paragraph (a) applies and to
    which paragraph (b) does not apply, 10% of those approved capital
    costs, or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in a case to which paragraph (b) applies,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 10% of those approved capital costs,

    plus

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} \$2,000 for each job determined by the Minister
      to have been created directly in the operation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum incentive or combined incentive
  in certain cases

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No development incentive or combination of
  development incentives authorized in respect of a new facility or for
  the expansion of an existing facility to enable the manufacturing or
  processing of a product not previously manufactured or processed in
  the operation shall exceed the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$30,000 for each job determined by the Minister to
    have been created directly in the operation, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one-half of the capital to be employed in the
    operation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. R-3, s. 5
- R.S., 1970, c. 25(2nd Supp.), s. 3
:::

### [Determination of Amount of Incentive]{.HTitleText2} {#h-426642 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria for determining amount of
incentive

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Act, the
Minister may authorize the provision of a development incentive in the
maximum amount provided for by this Act or in any lesser amount, and in
determining whether to authorize the provision of a development
incentive in the maximum amount so provided for or in any lesser amount,
the Minister shall take into consideration the following factors:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the extent of the contribution that the
  establishment, expansion or modernization of the facility would make
  to economic expansion and social adjustment in the designated region;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the probable cost to provincial, municipal or other
  public authorities of providing services or utilities required for or
  in connection with the facility;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount or present value of any federal,
  provincial or municipal assistance given or to be given, other than
  under this Act, in respect of the establishment, expansion or
  modernization of the facility;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the probable cost of preventing or eliminating any
  significant air, water or other pollution that could result from the
  establishment, expansion or modernization of the facility;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any proposal to establish or expand a
  facility constituting the necessary components of a processing
  operation, whether the resources to be exploited would be adequate, on
  a sustained-yield basis, to support the facility together with
  existing facilities that utilize the same resources; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} such other factors relating to the economic and
  social benefits and costs of the facility as the Minister considers
  relevant.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1968-69, c. 56, s. 6
:::

### [Ineligible Facilities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-426653 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligible facilities

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  development incentive may be authorized under this Act for the
  establishment, expansion or modernization of any facility if, in the
  opinion of the Minister,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is probable that the facility would be
    established, expanded or modernized without the provision of such an
    incentive; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the establishment, expansion or modernization of
    the facility would not make a significant contribution to economic
    expansion and social adjustment within the designated region.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No development incentive may be authorized under this
  Act for the establishment, expansion or modernization of any facility
  the capital costs of which would not, in the opinion of the Minister,
  exceed such minimum amount as is prescribed by the regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1968-69, c. 56, s. 7
:::

### [Limiting Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-426663 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Servicing expenditures as approved
capital costs

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  calculating the amount of any development incentive for the
  establishment, expansion or modernization of any facility, there may
  be included in the approved capital costs of establishing, expanding
  or modernizing the facility any capital expenditures made by the
  applicant to provincial, municipal or other public authorities for the
  provision of services or utilities required for or in connection with
  the facility, if the Minister is of the opinion that the expenditures
  were reasonably and responsibly made, but no such expenditures shall
  be so included in excess of 20% of the total amount of the approved
  capital costs of establishing, expanding or modernizing the facility,
  after deducting from those approved capital costs all federal,
  provincial and municipal grants or other financial assistance made or
  to be made in connection therewith or for which the applicant would
  ordinarily have been eligible by reason of the establishment,
  expansion or modernization of the facility.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation re capital to be employed in
  the operation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In calculating the amount of any secondary
  development incentive for the expansion of any facility, there may be
  included in the capital to be employed in the operation only such part
  of that capital as is to be employed in connection with the
  manufacturing or processing of a product not previously manufactured
  or processed in the operation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1968-69, c. 56, s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior contractual commitment

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  development incentive may be authorized under this Act for the
  establishment, expansion or modernization of a facility for which a
  contractual commitment was made, whether or not the commitment remains
  in force, before

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the 1st day of July 1969, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which an application for the development
    incentive is received by the Minister,

  whichever is the later date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), where an application
  for a development incentive is received by the Minister before the 1st
  day of January 1970 in respect of a facility for which a contractual
  commitment was made on or after the 1st day of July 1969, provision of
  the development incentive may be authorized and the development
  incentive may be paid in accordance with this Act as if the
  contractual commitment had not been so made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limitation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No primary development incentive or secondary
  development incentive may be provided under this Act

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the establishment of a facility that is not
    brought into commercial production until after the 31st day of
    December, 1984; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the expansion or modernization of a
    facility, if the expanded or modernized facility is not brought into
    commercial production until after the 31st day of December, 1984.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} No special development incentive may be provided
  under this Act

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the establishment of a facility that is not
    brought into commercial production until after the 31st day of
    December 1973, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the expansion or modernization of a
    facility, if the expanded or modernized facility is not brought into
    commercial production until after the 31st day of December 1973,

  unless, in the opinion of the Minister, the facility or the expanded
  or modernized facility was not brought into commercial production
  until after the 31st day of December 1973 for reasons beyond the
  control of the applicant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insurance

  [(3.2)]{.lawlabel} No development incentive may be provided under this
  Act unless, at the request of the Minister or at such times as are
  provided by the regulations, the applicant therefor provides to the
  Minister evidence that the facility in respect of which the
  development incentive is authorized is insured to the satisfaction of
  the Minister or in accordance with the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where incentive previously authorized

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No development incentive may be authorized for the
  modernization of any facility in respect of which a development
  incentive has previously been authorized under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. R-3, s. 9
- R.S., 1970, c. 25(2nd Supp.), s. 4
- 1974-75-76, c. 84, s. 1
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 14, s. 1
:::

### [Payment of Incentives]{.HTitleText2} {#h-426694 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time for payment of development
incentives

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** When the Minister is
satisfied that a facility for the establishment, expansion or
modernization of which a development incentive has been authorized, the
amount of which was based on

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approved capital costs of establishing, expanding
  or modernizing the facility, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the approved capital costs of establishing or
  expanding the facility and the number of jobs created directly in the
  operation,

has been brought into commercial production or, in the case of a
facility for the expansion or modernization of which a development
incentive has been authorized, the expanded or modernized facility has
been brought into commercial production, the Minister shall pay to the
applicant an amount on account of the development incentive not
exceeding 80% of the amount estimated by the Minister to be the amount
of the development incentive, and the remainder of the incentive shall
be paid in such amounts and within such period,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} not longer than 30 months from the day the facility
  or the expanded or modernized facility was brought into commercial
  production, in a case to which paragraph (a) applies and to which
  paragraph (b) does not apply, or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} not longer than 42 months from the day the facility
  or the expanded facility was brought into commercial production, in a
  case to which paragraph (b) applies,

as are prescribed by the regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. R-3, s. 10
- R.S., 1970, c. 25(2nd Supp.), s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where region ceases to be designated

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Where, before a facility
for the establishment of which a development incentive was authorized is
brought into commercial production or, in the case of a facility for the
expansion or modernization of which a development incentive was
authorized, before the expanded or modernized facility is brought into
commercial production, the region in which the facility or the expanded
or modernized facility is or is to be situated ceases to be a designated
region, no amount on account of the development incentive shall be paid
under this Act

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} unless the facility or the expanded or modernized
  facility is brought into commercial production within 18 months after
  the region ceases to be a designated region; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, unless the Minister is satisfied
  that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} substantial progress was made in establishing,
    expanding or modernizing the facility before the region ceased to be
    a designated region, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} after the region ceased to be a designated region,
    the establishment, expansion or modernization of the facility was
    completed with all reasonable speed.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. R-3, s. 11
- R.S., 1970, c. 25(2nd Supp.), s. 6
:::

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** An amount payable to an
applicant on account of a development incentive under this Act is exempt
from income tax.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1968-69, c. 56, s. 12

## [Manpower Services]{.HTitleText1} {#h-426718 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition respecting utilization of
manpower services

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  a condition precedent to the payment of any amount on account of a
  development incentive that the applicant keep the Canada Employment
  and Immigration Commission informed of the employment vacancies and
  requirements of the applicant in the designated region in which the
  facility for the establishment, expansion or modernization of which
  the development incentive was authorized is or is to be situated, and
  that the applicant further, from time to time, at the request of that
  Commission or as required by the regulations,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} discuss with the Commission the long-term plans of
    the applicant for the recruitment and training of employees in the
    designated region; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} participate in and cooperate with the Commission in
    respect of any programs of the Commission related to employment
    counselling, placement and manpower adjustment, mobility and
    training.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of condition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The condition prescribed by this section shall be
  effective for the period ending on the day the final payment on
  account of the development incentive is made or the 31st day of
  December, 1984, whichever is later.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. R-3, s. 13
- 1974-75-76, c. 84, s. 2
- 1976-77, c. 54, s. 74
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 14, s. 2

## [Loan Guarantees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-426728 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loan guarantees

- **[[13.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Upon application therefor to the
  Minister by an applicant proposing to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish a new facility or to expand or modernize
    an existing facility, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish a commercial facility,

  the Minister may, with the concurrence of the Minister of Finance,
  subject to this Act and upon such terms and conditions as are
  prescribed by the regulations, authorize the guarantee by Her Majesty
  in right of Canada of the repayment by the applicant of a proportion
  of any loan made to him in respect of the establishment, expansion or
  modernization of the facility or the establishment of the commercial
  facility and the payment of interest by the applicant on the
  proportion of the loan so guaranteed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligible facilities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No loan guarantee may be authorized under this Act in
  respect of the establishment of a new facility or the expansion or
  modernization of an existing facility unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a development incentive

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has been or could be authorized under this Act
      for the establishment of the facility or the expansion or
      modernization thereof, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} could not be authorized under this Act for the
      establishment of the facility or the expansion or modernization
      thereof by reason only that the Minister is of the opinion that
      the facility could be established, expanded or modernized without
      the provision of a development incentive; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is of the opinion that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the applicant could not obtain sufficient
      financing on reasonable terms for the establishment of the
      facility or the expansion or modernization thereof without such
      guarantee, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the establishment of a new
      facility, it will be brought into commercial production on or
      before the 31st day of December, 1984 and, in the case of the
      expansion or modernization of a facility, the expanded or
      modernized facility will be brought into commercial production on
      or before that date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligible commercial facilities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No loan guarantee may be authorized under this Act in
  respect of the establishment of a commercial facility unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the commercial services to be provided are of a
    class prescribed by the regulations and the facility will be located
    within an area prescribed by the regulations for the purposes of
    this section that is within a designated region; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is of the opinion that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the establishment of the commercial facility
      would make a significant contribution to economic expansion and
      social adjustment within the designated region,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the capital costs of the commercial facility
      will exceed such minimum amount as is prescribed by the
      regulations,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the applicant could not obtain sufficient
      financing on reasonable terms for the establishment of the
      commercial facility without such guarantee, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the commercial facility will be brought into
      commercial operation on or before the 31st day of December, 1984.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum loan a proportion of which may
  be guaranteed

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No loan guarantee may be authorized under this Act
  where the amount of the loan to the applicant exceeds 80% of the
  amount estimated by the Minister to be the total capital costs of
  establishing, expanding or modernizing the facility or of establishing
  the commercial facility in respect of which the loan is made minus an
  amount estimated by the Minister to be the aggregate of any
  development incentive and all other federal, provincial and municipal
  grants or other financial assistance given or to be given in
  connection therewith or for which the applicant would ordinarily have
  been eligible by reason of the establishment, expansion or
  modernization of the facility or of the establishment of the
  commercial facility.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insurance

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A loan guarantee authorized under this Act ceases to
  be of any force or effect if the lender by whom the loan was made
  fails to provide to the Minister, at his request or at such times as
  are provided by the regulations, evidence that the facility or
  commercial facility in respect of which the guaranteed loan was made
  is insured to the satisfaction of the Minister or in accordance with
  the regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. 25(2nd Supp.), s. 7
- 1974-75-76, c. 84, ss. 3, 4
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 14, s. 3
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-426757 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitations on agreements under
Department of Regional Economic Expansion Act

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Where, in the opinion of
the Minister, a development incentive could be provided under this Act
in respect of an undertaking referred to in subsection 10(1) of the
[Special Areas Act](/eng/acts/S-14), notwithstanding anything in section
10 of that Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an agreement providing for a guarantee described in
  paragraph 10(1)(a) of that Act may be entered into only if, in the
  opinion of the Minister, the approved capital costs of the undertaking
  would exceed

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} \$75,000 for each job that the Minister estimates
    would be created directly in the undertaking, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} \$30,000,000; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} no agreement providing for a payment described in
  paragraph 10(1)(b) or (c) of that Act may be entered into in respect
  of the undertaking.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. R-3, s. 14
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 167, s. 34
:::

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-426766 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} defining for the purposes of this Act the expressions
  "manufacturing or processing operation", "initial processing
  operation", "resource-based industry" and "commercial operation";

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing, for any designated region or for any
  class of manufacturing or processing operation, an amount less than
  the maximum amount of a development incentive provided for by this
  Act, which lesser amount shall, in relation to that region or any
  manufacturing or processing operation of that class, be deemed to be
  the maximum amount provided for by this Act;

- [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} prescribing any one or more development incentives
  that may not be authorized by the Minister in respect of facilities
  within a designated region or regions prescribed for the purposes of
  this paragraph;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing classes of fixed assets the value of
  which may be included in the capital to be employed in the operation
  or in the total capital costs of a facility or commercial facility;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the determination of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the working capital required for the purposes of
    any class of operation, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the capitalized expenses incurred in bringing a
    new facility or commercial facility into commercial production or
    operation or in bringing an expanded or modernized facility into
    commercial production;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} for determining whether a product is a product not
  previously manufactured or processed in an operation;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} respecting the determination of the amount or present
  value of any federal, provincial or municipal assistance given or to
  be given in respect of a facility or commercial facility;

- [(f.1)]{.lawlabel} prescribing terms and conditions on which a
  guarantee by Her Majesty in right of Canada may be authorized pursuant
  to section 13.1, including terms and conditions relating to the
  payment to Her Majesty by any lender of a guarantee fee;

- [(f.2)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the maximum proportion of any loan the
  repayment of which and the payment of interest on which may be
  guaranteed pursuant to section 13.1;

- [(f.3)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the nature and extent of the insurance
  to be maintained in force on any facility in respect of which a
  development incentive is authorized or on any facility or commercial
  facility in respect of which a loan guarantee is authorized under this
  Act and the times at which evidence of such insurance shall be
  provided to the Minister;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} respecting the determination of any matter that under
  this Act is to be determined by the Minister;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} respecting any other matter or thing that under this
  Act is to be prescribed by the regulations; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} generally for carrying into effect the purposes and
  provisions of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1970, c. R-3, s. 15
- R.S., 1970, c. 25(2nd Supp.), s. 8
:::

**[[15.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.1 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall

this Act.
