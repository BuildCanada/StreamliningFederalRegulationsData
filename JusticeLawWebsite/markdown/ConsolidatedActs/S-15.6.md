## Spirit Drinks Trade Act {#spirit-drinks-trade-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2005, c. 39
:::

Assented to 2005-11-03

An Act respecting the implementation of international trade commitments
by Canada regarding spirit drinks of foreign countries
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS Canada has made international commitments regarding the use of
names of spirit drinks of foreign countries;

AND WHEREAS those commitments are contained in the Agreement between
Canada and the European Community on trade in wines and spirit drinks,
signed on September 16, 2003, and in side letters to that Agreement, in
the North American Free Trade Agreement, signed on December 17, 1992,
and in commitments made by Canada at the Commonwealth Heads of
Government meeting in Nassau in 1985 to establish an economic and trade
development program for the Commonwealth Caribbean countries and
territories;

NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Spirit Drinks Trade Act](/eng/acts/S-15.6).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Agriculture
    and Agri-Food. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes offer for sale, expose for
    sale and have in possession for sale. ([vendre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall use the name of a spirit drink referred to in sections 1
  to 5 of the schedule to sell a product as a spirit drink except in
  accordance with those sections.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not prevent the use of the name
  of a spirit drink to sell the spirit drink if it has been blended or
  modified in accordance with the laws of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not prevent the use of any
  registered trademark that was applied for before January 1, 1996.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

- **[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall use the name of a spirit drink referred to in sections 6
  to 14 of the schedule to sell a product as that spirit drink except in
  accordance with those sections.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not prevent the use of the name
  of a spirit drink to sell the spirit drink if it has been blended or
  modified in accordance with the laws of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of schedule

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, amend the schedule to implement Canada's international
trade obligations regarding the use of names of spirit drinks to sell
products as spirit drinks.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspectors

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate any persons, or classes of persons, as
  inspectors for the purpose of the enforcement of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate to be produced

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every inspector shall be given a certificate in a
  form established by the Minister attesting to the inspector's
  designation and, on entering any place under subsection 6(1), an
  inspector shall, if so requested, produce the certificate to the
  person in charge of that place.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of inspectors

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector may at any reasonable time enter any place, including a
  conveyance but excluding a dwelling-house, where the inspector
  believes on reasonable grounds any article to which this Act or the
  regulations apply is manufactured, prepared, preserved, packaged or
  stored, and may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine the article and take samples of it, and
    examine anything that the inspector believes on reasonable grounds
    is used or capable of being used for the article's manufacture,
    preparation, preservation, packaging or storing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} open and examine any receptacle or package that the
    inspector believes on reasonable grounds contains any article to
    which this Act or the regulations apply;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} examine and make copies of, or extracts from, any
    books, documents or other records found in any place referred to in
    this subsection that the inspector believes on reasonable grounds
    contain any information with respect to any article to which this
    Act or the regulations apply; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} seize and detain for such time as may be necessary
    any article by means of or in relation to which the inspector
    believes on reasonable grounds any provision of this Act or the
    regulations has been contravened.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance and information to be given
  inspector

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner or person in charge of a place entered by
  an inspector under subsection (1) and every person found in it shall
  give the inspector all reasonable assistance and furnish the inspector
  with any information that they may reasonably require.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction and false statements

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall obstruct or hinder, or knowingly make any false or
  misleading statement either orally or in writing to, an inspector
  while the inspector is carrying out their duties or functions under
  this Act or the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except with the authority of an inspector, no person
  shall remove or alter in any way any article seized under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage and removal

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Any article seized under
this Act may, at the option of an inspector, be kept or stored in the
place where it was seized or, at the direction of an inspector, the
article may be removed to any other place.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Release of seized articles

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An inspector who has seized
any article under this Act shall release it when they are satisfied that
all the provisions of this Act and the regulations with respect to it
have been complied with.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Destruction with consent

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  inspector seizes an article under this Act and its owner or the person
  in whose possession the article was at the time of seizure consents to
  its destruction, the article is immediately forfeited to Her Majesty
  and may be destroyed or otherwise disposed of as the Minister may
  direct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person is convicted of a contravention of this
  Act or the regulations, the court or judge may order that any article
  by means of or in relation to which the offence was committed, and any
  thing of a similar nature belonging to or in the possession of the
  person or found with the article, be forfeited. On the making of the
  order, the article and thing are forfeited to Her Majesty and may be
  disposed of as the Minister may direct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order for forfeiture on application of
  inspector

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Without prejudice to subsection (2), a judge of a
  superior court of the province in which any article is seized under
  this Act may, on the application of an inspector and on any notice to
  those persons that the judge directs, order that the article and any
  thing of a similar nature found with it be forfeited to Her Majesty,
  if the judge finds, after making any inquiry that the judge considers
  necessary, that the article is one by means of or in relation to which
  any of the provisions of this Act or the regulations have been
  contravened. On the making of the order, the article or thing may be
  disposed of as the Minister may direct.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Analysts

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
designate any persons, or classes of persons, as analysts for the
purpose of the enforcement of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Analysis and examination

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector may submit to an analyst for analysis or examination any
  article seized by the inspector, any sample from it or any sample
  taken by the inspector.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate or report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An analyst after making the analysis or examination
  may issue a certificate or report setting out the results of the
  analysis or examination.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, on the recommendation of the Minister, make regulations for
carrying out the purposes and provisions of this Act and, in particular,
may make regulations respecting the powers and duties of inspectors and
analysts and the taking of samples and the seizure, detention,
forfeiture and disposition of articles.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of Act or regulations

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes any provision of this Act or the regulations is guilty of an
offence and liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding
  \$50,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to
  both; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine not exceeding
  \$250,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three years or
  to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** No person shall be found
guilty of an offence under this Act, other than an offence under section
7, if the person establishes that they exercised all due diligence to
prevent its commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of analyst

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section, in any
  prosecution for an offence under this Act, a certificate purporting to
  be signed by an analyst and stating that an article, sample or
  substance has been submitted to, and analysed or examined by, the
  analyst and stating the results of the analysis or examination is
  admissible in evidence and, in the absence of evidence to the
  contrary, is proof of the statements contained in the certificate
  without proof of the signature or official character of the person
  appearing to have signed it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requiring attendance of analyst

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party against whom the analyst's certificate is
  produced may, with leave of the court, require the attendance of the
  analyst for the purposes of cross-examination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of intention to produce
  certificate

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No certificate shall be admitted in evidence unless,
  before the trial, the party intending to produce the certificate has
  given reasonable notice of that intention, together with a copy of the
  certificate, to the party against whom it is intended to be produced.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** This Act comes into force
on June 1, 2006.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Sections 3, 3.1 and 4)]{.OriginatingRef}[Use of Spirit Drink Names]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-437150 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- [1 (1)]{.lawlabel} A spirit drink may be sold using the name Grappa if
  it has been produced exclusively in Italy.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} A spirit drink may be sold using the name *Grappa
    di Ticino* if it has been produced in the Ticino region of
    Switzerland.

- [2]{.lawlabel} A spirit drink may be sold using the name *Jägertee*,
  *Jagertee* or *Jagatee* if it has been produced exclusively in
  Austria.

- [3]{.lawlabel} A spirit drink may be sold using the name *Korn* or
  *Kornbrand* if it has been produced exclusively in Germany or Austria.

- [4]{.lawlabel} A spirit drink may be sold using the name Ouzo or
  *Oύζo* if it has been produced exclusively in Greece.

- [5]{.lawlabel} A spirit drink may be sold using the name *Pacharán* if
  it has been produced exclusively in Spain.

- [6]{.lawlabel} Scotch whisky may be sold under that name if it has
  been distilled in Scotland as Scotch whisky for domestic consumption
  in accordance with the laws of the United Kingdom.

- [7]{.lawlabel} Irish whisky may be sold under that name if it has been
  distilled in Northern Ireland or in the Republic of Ireland as Irish
  whisky for domestic consumption in accordance with the laws of
  Northern Ireland or the Republic of Ireland.

- [8]{.lawlabel} Armagnac brandy may be sold under that name if it has
  been manufactured in the Armagnac district of France in accordance
  with the laws of the French Republic for consumption in that country.

- [9]{.lawlabel} Cognac brandy may be sold under that name if it has
  been manufactured in the Cognac district of France in accordance with
  the laws of the French Republic for consumption in that country.

- [10]{.lawlabel} Bourbon whiskey may be sold under that name if it has
  been manufactured in the United States in accordance with the laws and
  regulations of the United States governing the manufacture of Bourbon
  whiskey.

- [11]{.lawlabel} Tennessee whiskey may be sold under that name if it
  has been manufactured in the United States in accordance with the laws
  and regulations of the United States governing the manufacture of
  Tennessee whiskey.

- [12]{.lawlabel} Tequila may be sold under that name if it has been
  manufactured in Mexico in accordance with the laws and regulations of
  Mexico governing the manufacture of Tequila.

- [13]{.lawlabel} Mezcal may be sold under that name if it has been
  manufactured in Mexico in accordance with the laws and regulations of
  Mexico governing the manufacture of Mezcal.

- [14 (1)]{.lawlabel} Caribbean rum may be sold under that name if it
  has been

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} made from sugar cane products of a Commonwealth
    Caribbean country and distilled and fermented in a Commonwealth
    Caribbean country; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} imported in bulk from a Commonwealth Caribbean
    country for bottling and sale in Canada as Caribbean rum and blended
    or modified by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} blending it with other rum of a Commonwealth
      Caribbean country,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} blending it with Canadian rum in proportions
      that result in 1 to 1.5% Canadian rum by volume in the final
      product,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} adding distilled or otherwise purified water to
      adjust the rum to the strength stated on the label applied to the
      container, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} adding caramel.

  <!-- -->

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[Commonwealth Caribbean
    country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Anguilla, Antigua and Barbuda,
    the Bahamas, Barbados, Belize, Bermuda, the British Virgin Islands,
    the Cayman Islands, Dominica, Grenada, Guyana, Jamaica, Montserrat,
    St. Christopher and Nevis, Saint Lucia, St. Vincent and the
    Grenadines, Trinidad and Tobago and the Turks and Caicos Islands.
