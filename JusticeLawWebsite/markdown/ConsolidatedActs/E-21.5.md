## Expungement of Historically Unjust Convictions Act {#expungement-of-historically-unjust-convictions-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2018, c. 11
:::

Assented to 2018-06-21

An Act to establish a procedure for expunging certain historically
unjust convictions and to make related amendments to other Acts
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas it is now recognized that the criminalization of certain
activities constitutes a historical injustice;

And whereas the criminalization of an activity may constitute a
historical injustice because, among other things, were it to occur
today, it would be inconsistent with the Canadian Charter of Rights and
Freedoms;
:::

::: Enacts
Now therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-211749 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Expungement of Historically Unjust Convictions
Act](/eng/acts/E-21.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-211752 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Parole Board of
    Canada. ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chairperson of the
    Board designated under section 104 of the [Corrections and
    Conditional Release
    Act](/eng/acts/C-44.6). ([président]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Powers, Duties and Functions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-211757 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Board

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Board may, in
accordance with this Act, order or refuse to order expungement of a
conviction in respect of the offences listed in the schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Board, subject to the
approval of the Chairperson, may confer on any of its employees or class
of its employees any of its powers, duties or functions under this Act.

## [Expungement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-211762 .Part}

### [Effects]{.HTitleText2} {#h-211763 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of expungement

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Board orders expungement of a conviction in respect of an offence
  listed in the schedule, the person convicted of the offence is deemed
  never to have been convicted of that offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lawful act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act has the effect of making unlawful
  a lawful act or omission related to the expunged conviction that was
  committed prior to the expungement order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Royal prerogative

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act in any
manner limits or affects Her Majesty's royal prerogative of mercy.

### [Procedure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-211771 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who has been convicted of an offence listed in the schedule or a
  person referred to in subsection (2) may apply to the Board for an
  expungement order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application on person's behalf

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person who has been convicted of an offence
  listed in the schedule is deceased, any of the following may apply for
  an expungement order on the person's behalf:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person's spouse or the individual who, at the
    time of the person's death, was cohabiting with the person in a
    conjugal relationship, having so cohabited for a period of at least
    one year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person's child;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person's parent;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person's brother or sister;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the person's agent or mandatary, attorney,
    guardian, trustee, committee, tutor or curator, or any other person
    who was appointed to act in a similar capacity before his or her
    death;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the person's executor or the administrator or
    liquidator of the person's estate;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any other individual who, in the opinion of the
    Board, is an appropriate representative of the person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and manner of application

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for an expungement order must be in the form and manner
  determined by the Board and in accordance with subsections (2) and
  (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), if an application is in
  respect of an offence for which criteria are set out in section 25 or
  in an order, the application must include documents that provide
  evidence that those criteria are satisfied.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sworn statement or solemn declaration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If it is not possible to obtain the documents
  referred to in subsection (2), the applicant must submit a sworn
  statement or solemn declaration

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that explains the reasonable efforts made by the
    applicant to obtain the documents, and the reasons why they could
    not be obtained, including because they were lost or destroyed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that affirms the evidence referred to in section 25
    or in an order that could not otherwise be provided.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board must reject an application that does not relate to an offence
  listed in the schedule or for which the applicant is not authorized by
  section 7.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The applicant must be advised in writing of the
  rejection of their application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incomplete application

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** If the Board determines
that an application is incomplete, the Board may return it to the
applicant at any time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inquiries

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** On receipt of an
application, the Board may cause inquiries to be made

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of determining whether the applicant
  is authorized to apply for an expungement order under section 7; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of the review under section 12.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 9 and
10, the Board must review the application and the evidence gathered
through any inquiries and determine whether there is evidence

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the application is in respect of an offence for
  which criteria are set out in section 25 or in an order, that any of
  those criteria are not satisfied; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the activity in respect of which the application
  is made is prohibited under the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) at the
  time the application is reviewed.

### [Order]{.HTitleText2} {#h-211808 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order to expunge

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 9 and
10, if the Board's review reveals no evidence referred to in paragraph
12(a) or (b), the Board must order expungement of the conviction in
respect of the offence for which the application was made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 9 and
10, if the Board's review reveals evidence referred to in paragraph
12(a) or (b), the Board must refuse to order expungement of the
conviction in respect of the offence for which the application was made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to applicant

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Board must notify the
applicant in writing of the expungement order or of its refusal to order
expungement.

### [Destruction and Removal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-211815 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification to RCMP

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Board must notify the
Royal Canadian Mounted Police of any expungement order. The Board must
also notify any superior, provincial or municipal court that, to its
knowledge, has custody of any judicial record of the conviction to which
the expungement order relates.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Destruction or removal by RCMP

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** As soon as feasible after
receipt of the notification from the Board, the Royal Canadian Mounted
Police must destroy or remove any judicial record of the conviction to
which the expungement order relates that is in its repositories or
systems.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification by RCMP

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** As soon as feasible after
receipt of the notification from the Board, the Royal Canadian Mounted
Police must provide notification of the expungement order to any federal
department or agency, and to any provincial or municipal police force,
that, to its knowledge, has custody of any judicial record of the
conviction to which the expungement order relates.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Destruction or removal by departments and
agencies

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** As soon as feasible after
receipt of a notification from the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, a
federal department or agency must destroy or remove any judicial record
of the conviction to which the expungement order relates that is in its
repositories or systems.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Sections 17 and 19 apply
despite sections 12 and 13 of the [Library and Archives of Canada
Act](/eng/acts/L-7.7), subsections 6(1) and (3) of the [Privacy
Act](/eng/acts/P-21) and any other provision of an Act of Parliament.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
sections 17 to 20 do not apply to documents submitted or produced in
respect of an application under this Act.

### [Disclosure of Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-211828 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Perjury investigation

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The Board may, for the
purpose of the investigation or prosecution of any offence under section
131 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) (perjury), disclose any
information submitted or produced in respect of an application under
this Act.

## [Amendment of Schedule]{.HTitleText1} {#h-211831 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Schedule

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the conditions referred
  to in subsection (2), the Governor in Council may, by order, add to
  the schedule any item or portion of an item.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In order to provide for expungement of convictions
  arising from an activity, the Governor in Council may add any item or
  portion of an item to the schedule if the activity no longer
  constitutes an offence under an Act of Parliament and the Governor in
  Council is of the opinion that the criminalization of the activity
  constitutes a historical injustice.

## [Criteria]{.HTitleText1} {#h-211837 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, establish the criteria related to an offence listed in
the schedule that must be satisfied for expungement of a conviction to
be ordered.

## [Same-Sex Sexual Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-211840 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences listed in items 1 to 6 of
schedule

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** An application for an
expungement order for a conviction in respect of the offences listed in
items 1 to 6 of the schedule must include evidence that the following
criteria are satisfied:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the activity for which the person was convicted was
  between persons of the same sex;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the persons other than the person who was convicted
  had given their consent to participate in the activity; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the persons who participated in the activity were 16
  years of age or older at the time the activity occurred or the person
  who was convicted would have been able to rely on a defence under
  section 150.1 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), had that defence
  been available in respect of the offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 25, consent means the voluntary agreement of the persons to
engage in the sexual activity in question, within the meaning of section
273.1 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).
