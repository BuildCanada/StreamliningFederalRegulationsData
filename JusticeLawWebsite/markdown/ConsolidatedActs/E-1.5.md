## Eeyou Marine Region Land Claims Agreement Act {#eeyou-marine-region-land-claims-agreement-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2011, c. 20
:::

Assented to 2011-11-29

An Act to give effect to the Agreement between the Crees of Eeyou
Istchee and Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada concerning the
Eeyou Marine Region
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Recognizing that

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  :::

  ::: listItemText0
  the Crees of Eeyou Istchee assert aboriginal rights in and title to
  the Eeyou Marine Region as defined in the Agreement;
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  :::

  ::: listItemText0
  the Eeyou Marine Region is a fundamental and integral component of
  Eeyou Istchee as defined in the Agreement;
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  :::

  ::: listItemText0
  the Crees of Eeyou Istchee are an aboriginal people of Canada;
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  :::

  ::: listItemText0
  the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/) recognizes and affirms the
  existing aboriginal and treaty rights of the aboriginal peoples of
  Canada;
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  :::

  ::: listItemText0
  the Crees of Eeyou Istchee, as represented by the Grand Council of the
  Crees, and the Government of Canada have negotiated the Agreement;
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  :::

  ::: listItemText0
  the Crees of Eeyou Istchee, by a vote held from March 15 to 26, 2010,
  have approved the Agreement;
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  :::

  ::: listItemText0
  the Agreement was signed on behalf of the Crees of Eeyou Istchee and
  Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada on July 7, 2010; and
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  :::

  ::: listItemText0
  the Agreement requires that legislation be enacted by the Parliament
  of Canada in order for the Agreement to be ratified;
  :::
  :::::

Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-176829 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Eeyou Marine Region Land Claims Agreement Act](/eng/acts/E-1.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-176832 .Part}

Definition of [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act, [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement between the
  Crees of Eeyou Istchee and Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada
  concerning the Eeyou Marine Region, signed on July 7, 2010, including
  any amendments made to it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions in Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Act, [[Crees of Eeyou
  Istchee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[Legislation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  have the same meaning as in Chapter 1 of the Agreement, and [[Grand
  Council of the Crees]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
  [[Grand Council of the Crees (Eeyou Istchee)]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
  that Chapter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of Agreement

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement is a treaty
within the meaning of sections 25 and 35 of the [Constitution Act,
1982](/eng/Const/).

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-176840 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act binding on Her Majesty

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province so as to give effect to the
Agreement in accordance with its terms.

## [Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-176843 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement given effect

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement is approved, given effect and declared valid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and obligations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, any person or body has the
  powers, rights, privileges and benefits conferred on them by the
  Agreement and must perform the duties, and is subject to the
  liabilities, imposed on them by the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Third parties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Agreement is binding on, and may be relied on by,
  all persons and bodies that are not parties to it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency with Agreement

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement prevails over this Act and other Legislation to the extent
  of an inconsistency or conflict between them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency with Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Act prevails over other Legislation to the
  extent of an inconsistency or conflict between them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal capacity

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Eeyou Marine Region Planning Commission, the Eeyou Marine Region
  Wildlife Board and the Eeyou Marine Region Impact Review Board
  established by the Agreement each have the capacity, rights, powers
  and privileges of a natural person for the purposes of carrying out
  their objectives.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not agents of Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The bodies referred to in subsection (1) are not
  agents of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

## [Appropriation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-176861 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of C.R.F.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The sums that are required
to meet the monetary obligations of Her Majesty in right of Canada under
Chapters 13, 22, 23 and 25 of the Agreement are to be paid out of the
Consolidated Revenue Fund.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-176864 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial
  notice is to be taken of the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Agreement is to be published by the Queen's
  Printer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the Agreement published by the Queen's
  Printer is evidence of the Agreement and of its contents, and a copy
  purporting to be published by the Queen's Printer is presumed to be so
  published unless the contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of issues arising

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  issue arises in any judicial or administrative proceeding in respect
  of the interpretation, validity or application of the Agreement or of
  this Act, the issue is not to be decided unless the party raising it
  has served notice on the Attorney General of Canada and the Grand
  Council of the Crees.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of issues arising --- Nunavut

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the issue affects the interests of the Government
  of Nunavut, it is not to be decided unless the party raising it has
  also served notice on the Minister of Justice of Nunavut.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} describe the judicial or administrative proceeding
    in which the issue arises;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specify what the issue arises in respect of;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state the day on which the issue is to be argued;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} give particulars necessary to show the point to be
    argued; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be served at least 14 days before the day of
    argument, unless the court or tribunal authorizes a shorter period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation in proceedings

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In any proceeding to which subsection (1) applies,
  the Attorney General of Canada and the Grand Council of the Crees ---
  and, if subsection (2) applies, the Minister of Justice of Nunavut ---
  may appear and participate in the proceeding as parties with the same
  rights as any other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsections (3) and (4) do not
  require that an oral hearing be held if one is not otherwise required.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An instrument made under
the Agreement is not a statutory instrument for the purposes of the
[Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make any orders and regulations that are necessary for the purpose
of carrying out any of the provisions of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review by Grand Council of the Crees

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  10 years after this Act receives royal assent, a review of the
  implementation of this Act and the Agreement may be undertaken by the
  Grand Council of the Crees.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Grand Council of the Crees may submit a report on
  any review undertaken under subsection (1) to the Minister referred to
  in subsection 14(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must cause any report submitted under
  subsection (2) to be laid before each House of Parliament on any of
  the first 15 days on which that House is sitting after the Minister
  receives it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review by Minister

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  10 years after this Act receives royal assent, a comprehensive review
  of the implementation of this Act and the Agreement must be undertaken
  by the Minister designated by the Governor in Council for the purposes
  of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must cause a report on the review to be
  laid before each House of Parliament on any of the first 15 days on
  which that House is sitting after the report is completed.
