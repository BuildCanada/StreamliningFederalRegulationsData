## Prince Rupert Grain Handling Operations Act {#prince-rupert-grain-handling-operations-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1988, c. 1
:::

Assented to 1988-01-20

An Act to provide for the resumption of grain handling operations at the
port of Prince Rupert, British Columbia
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-396781 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Prince Rupert Grain Handling Operations Act](/eng/acts/P-19.6).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-396784 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[arbitrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[arbitrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the arbitrator appointed
      pursuant to subsection 7(1); ([arbitre]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[collective agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[collective agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the collective
      agreement between the employer and the union that expired on
      December 31, 1983; ([convention collective]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person employed by the
      employer who is bound by the collective
      agreement; ([employé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Prince Rupert Grain
      Ltd.; ([employeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Grain Workers' Union,
      Local 333. ([syndicat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Words and expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise provided, words and expressions used
  in this Act have the same meaning as in Part V of the [Canada Labour
  Code](/eng/acts/L-2).

## [Grain Handling Operations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-396795 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resumption of grain handling operations

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#P-19.6_en_1){#P-19.6_en_1-ID0EBEA}[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
.sectionLabel}** On the coming into force of this Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the employer shall forthwith resume grain handling
  operations at the grain terminal located on Ridley Island in the port
  of Prince Rupert, British Columbia; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} every employee shall, when so required, forthwith
  resume the duties of that employee's employment.

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The union and each officer and
  representative of the union shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} forthwith on the coming into force of this Act,
    give notice to the employees that, by reason of that coming into
    force, grain handling operations are forthwith to be resumed at the
    grain terminal located on Ridley Island in the port of Prince
    Rupert, British Columbia and the employees, when so required, are
    forthwith to resume the duties of their employment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} take all reasonable steps to ensure that employees
    comply with paragraph 3(b); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} refrain from any conduct that may encourage
    employees not to comply with paragraph 3(b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of collective agreement

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The term
  of the collective agreement is extended to include the period
  beginning on January 1, 1984 and ending on December 31, 1989.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collective agreement amended

  [[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#P-19.6_en_4){#P-19.6_en_4-ID0EBCBA}[(2)]{.lawlabel} On
  the coming into force of this Act, the collective agreement shall be
  deemed to be amended by the incorporation therein of the terms and
  conditions of the collective agreement between the B.C. Terminal
  Elevator Operators' Association and the union and in effect for the
  period beginning on January 1, 1987 and ending on December 31, 1989,
  other than any terms and conditions relating to the matters referred
  to the arbitrator pursuant to subsection 7(1).

    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#P-19.6_en_4-ID0EBCBA){#P-19.6_en_4}\[Note:

    the twelfth hour after the time at which this Act is assented to,
    *see* section 12.\]
    :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collective agreement binding for
  extended term

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The collective agreement, as amended by or pursuant
  to this Act, is effective and binding on the parties thereto for the
  period for which the agreement is extended by subsection (1)
  notwithstanding anything in Part V of the [Canada Labour
  Code](/eng/acts/L-2) or in the agreement, and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Part V of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2)
    applies in respect of the agreement, as so amended, as if that
    period were the term of the agreement; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the agreement, as so amended, shall, for the
    purposes of any Act of Parliament, be deemed to be an agreement
    between the employer and each of its employees on all matters
    relating to their employment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Strikes and lockouts prohibited

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** During the term of the
collective agreement, as extended by subsection 5(1),

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the employer shall not declare or cause a lockout
  against the union;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} no officer or representative of the union shall
  declare or authorize a strike against the employer; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} no employee shall participate in a strike against the
  employer.

## [Arbitration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-396824 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arbitration

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall appoint an arbitrator and refer to the arbitrator all
  matters relating to staffing at the Grain Centre, job classification
  and security personnel that, at the time of the appointment, are in
  dispute between the employer and the union.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of arbitrator

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The arbitrator has, with such modifications as the
  circumstances require, all the powers and duties of an arbitrator
  under section 157 of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of arbitrator

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The arbitrator shall decide all matters referred to
  the arbitrator pursuant to subsection (1) within forty-five days after
  the arbitrator's appointment or such longer period as the employer and
  union may agree upon.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of decision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The arbitrator shall set out the decision on the
  matters referred to the arbitrator in such form as will enable the
  decision to be incorporated into the collective agreement in
  accordance with section 8.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collective agreement amended

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** When the arbitrator has
decided all matters referred to the arbitrator pursuant to subsection
7(1), the collective agreement shall be deemed to be amended by the
incorporation therein of the decision of the arbitrator and of such
other amendments as may have been agreed upon by the parties thereto
prior to that decision.

## [Amendment of Collective Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-396836 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parties may amend collective agreement

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act shall
be construed so as to limit the rights of the parties to the collective
agreement to agree to amend any provision of the agreement as amended by
or pursuant to this Act, other than a provision relating to the term of
the agreement, and to give effect thereto.

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-396839 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence by individuals

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual who contravenes any provision of this Act is guilty of an
  offence punishable on summary conviction and is liable, for each day
  or part of a day during which the offence continues, to a fine

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} of not less than \$10,000 or more than \$50,000,
    where the individual was acting in the capacity of an officer or
    representative of the employer or the union when the offence was
    committed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} of not less than \$500 or more than \$1,000, in any
    other case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence by employer or union

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the employer or the union contravenes any
  provision of this Act, it is guilty of an offence punishable on
  summary conviction and is liable, for each day or part of a day during
  which the offence continues, to a fine of not less than \$20,000 or
  more than \$100,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
enforcement proceedings under this Act, the union is deemed to be a
person.
