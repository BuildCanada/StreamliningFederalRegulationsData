## Vimy Ridge Day Act {#vimy-ridge-day-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2003, c. 6
:::

Assented to 2003-04-03

An Act respecting a national day of remembrance of the Battle of Vimy
Ridge
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the Battle of Vimy Ridge of April 9, 1917 was the first time
that Canadians from coast to coast fought in a battle together against a
common enemy;

AND WHEREAS the Battle of Vimy Ridge is considered by many to be a
turning point for our country and the beginning of Canada's march
towards nationhood;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-463808 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Vimy Ridge Day Act](/eng/acts/V-1.8).

## [Vimy Ridge Day]{.HTitleText1} {#h-463811 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vimy Ridge Day

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Throughout Canada, in each
and every year, the ninth day of April shall be known as "Vimy Ridge
Day".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian flag at half-mast

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In each and every year, on
the ninth day of April, the Canadian flag on the Peace Tower shall be
lowered at half-mast.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not a legal holiday

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, Vimy
Ridge Day will not be considered a legal holiday or a non-juridical day.
