## Labour Adjustment Benefits Act {#labour-adjustment-benefits-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. L-1
:::

An Act to provide for the payment of benefits to laid-off employees
::::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-338478 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Labour Adjustment Benefits Act](/eng/acts/L-1).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 89, s. 1

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-338483 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[average weekly insurable earnings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[average weekly insurable earnings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in
      respect of an employee, means the weekly average of the employee's
      insurable earnings, as determined under the [Employment Insurance
      Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6); ([rémunération hebdomadaire assurable
      moyenne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Labour Adjustment Review
      Board established by section 6; ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[board of referees]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[board of referees]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2012, c.
      19, s. 273\]]{.Repealed}

  [[Canadian establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
      establishment in Canada engaged in the production of goods or the
      provision of services; ([établissement canadien]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Employment
      Insurance Commission; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated industry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated industry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an industry
      designated pursuant to section 3; ([secteur d'activités
      désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[effective date of lay-off]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[effective date of lay-off]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of
      an employee, means the date the employee was laid off as
      determined by the Board pursuant to subsection 11(3); ([date de
      mise à pied]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any individual who has been
      employed at a Canadian establishment; ([employé]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[industrial restructuring]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[industrial restructuring]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes
      technological change; ([restructuration
      industrielle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[labour adjustment benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[labour adjustment benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      benefits payable under this Act; ([prestations
      d'adaptation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[lay-off]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[lay-off]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the separation, for an
      indefinite period, of an employee from employment at a Canadian

      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Employment

  :   [[qualified employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee who
      has been determined by the Commission pursuant to this Act to be

      admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Social Security Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Social Security Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Social
      Security Tribunal established under section 44 of the [Department
      of Employment and Social Development
      Act](/eng/acts/H-5.7); ([Tribunal de la sécurité
      sociale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a period of seven consecutive
      days commencing on Sunday; ([semaine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a calendar
      year. ([année]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of any provision of this Act in

  attains that age.

## [Designation of Industries]{.HTitleText1} {#h-338507 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of industries

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of this Act, the Governor in Council may, by order, designate
  any industry either generally or with respect to any region of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria for general designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An industry may be designated generally pursuant to
  subsection (1) if the Governor in Council is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the industry in Canada generally is undergoing
    significant economic adjustment of a non-cyclical nature by reason
    of import competition or by reason of industrial restructuring
    implemented pursuant to a policy or program of the Government of
    Canada to encourage such restructuring; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the economic adjustment referred to in
    paragraph (a) is resulting in a significant loss of employment in
    the industry in Canada generally.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria for regional designation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An industry may be designated with respect to any
  region of Canada pursuant to subsection (1) if the Governor in Council
  is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the industry in that region is undergoing
    significant economic adjustment of a non-cyclical nature; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the economic adjustment referred to in
    paragraph (a) is resulting in a severe economic disruption in that
    region and in a significant loss of employment in the industry in
    that region.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 89, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of general designation

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An order
  made under subsection 3(1) that designates an industry generally is in
  force for such period, not exceeding three years from the date the
  order is made, as is specified in the order unless, before the
  expiration of the period so specified, the Governor in Council makes a
  continuation order continuing the order in force for such period, not
  exceeding three years, as is specified in the continuation order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No further extension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Not more than one continuation order may be made
  under subsection (1) in respect of any one order made under subsection
  3(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term of regional designation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An order made under subsection 3(1) that designates
  an industry with respect to a region in Canada is in force for one
  year from the date the order is made unless, before the expiration of
  that one year, the Governor in Council makes a continuation order
  continuing the order in force for such period, not exceeding six
  months, as is specified in the continuation order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further extension

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Governor in Council has made a continuation
  order under subsection (3) continuing an order in force, he may,
  before the expiration of the period for which the order is so
  continued, make one further continuation order continuing the order in
  force for such further period, not exceeding six months, as is
  specified in the further continuation order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entitlement continued

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The revocation or expiration of an order made under
  this section or section 3 does not affect the entitlement, after the
  revocation or expiration, of any person laid off while the order was
  in force to make an application under section 11 or 13 in relation to
  the order or to receive labour adjustment benefits by virtue of the
  order.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 89, s. 3, c. 169, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retroactive application

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Governor in Council may, in any order made
  under section 3 or 4, declare

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the designation of the industry in the order
    is retroactive in effect and applies as of such day, before the date
    of the order, as is specified in the order; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that this Act applies in respect of lay-offs from a
    Canadian establishment in the industry designated in the order
    occurring on or after the day specified pursuant to paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may not specify pursuant to
  paragraph (1)(a) a day that is earlier than May 1, 1978.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 89, s. 4, c. 169, s. 3(F)
:::

## [Labour Adjustment Review Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-338543 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board established

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  hereby established a board, to be known as the Labour Adjustment
  Review Board, consisting of not more than five members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment and tenure of members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each member of the Board shall be appointed by the
  Minister to hold office during pleasure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representatives

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} One member of the Board shall be appointed after
  consultation with such organizations representative of employees as
  the Minister deems appropriate and shall be a representative of
  employees and another member of the Board shall be appointed after
  consultation with such organizations representative of employers as
  the Minister deems appropriate and shall be a representative of
  employers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary substitute members

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If any member of the Board is absent or unable to
  act, the Minister may appoint a person to act as a member for the time
  being, but no person so appointed has authority to act as a member for
  a period exceeding ninety days without the approval of the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A member appointed pursuant to subsection (4) holds
  office during pleasure and may exercise all the powers and carry out
  all the duties and functions of a member of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and expenses

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Board who are not employed in the
  federal public administration are entitled to be paid for their
  services in connection with the work of the Board such remuneration
  and expenses as are authorized by the Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. L-1, s. 6
- 2003, c. 22, s. 224(E)
:::

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chief executive officer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chairman of the Board is the chief executive

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head Office

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The head
  office of the Board shall be at such place in Canada as the Governor
  in Council may, by order, designate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sittings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may sit at such time and at such place in
  Canada as it considers necessary or desirable for the proper conduct
  of its business.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board may make rules respecting

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sittings of the Board;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the manner of dealing with matters and business
    before the Board; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the exercise of the powers and performance of the

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, on request of the Board, provide

  other assistance as is necessary for the proper conduct of the
  business of the Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 89, s. 7

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall exercise

imposed on it by, or as may be incidental to the attainment of the
objects of, this Act.

## [Certification by Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-338591 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for certification

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  employee who has been laid off may apply, directly or through an
  employer, trade union or any person, to the Board for certification
  that he is eligible to apply to the Commission for labour adjustment
  benefits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application under subsection (1) shall be
  submitted in such form and manner as the Board may direct and shall
  set out the name of the employee in respect of whom the application is
  being made and such other information as the Board may require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of an application under subsection (1),
  the Board shall carry out such investigation as it considers necessary
  for the purpose of determining the eligibility of the employee named
  in the application to apply to the Commission for labour adjustment
  benefits and for that purpose the Board shall determine the date the
  employee was laid off.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision on application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} On completion of its investigation under subsection
  (3), the Board shall determine whether or not the employee is eligible
  to apply to the Commission for labour adjustment benefits and shall
  notify the applicant in writing of its decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certification

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board determines pursuant to subsection (4)
  that an employee is eligible to apply to the Commission for labour
  adjustment benefits, it shall so certify in writing to the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of certification

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Certification under subsection (5) shall be submitted
  in such form and manner as the Commission may direct and shall set out
  the name of the employee certified, his effective date of lay-off and
  such other information respecting the lay-off of the employee as the
  Commission may require.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 89, s. 10
- 1984, c. 40, s. 79(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for certification

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Board may certify
that an employee named in an application under section 11 is eligible to
apply to the Commission for labour adjustment benefits if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} he was laid off;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian establishment from which he was laid off
  was, at the time of his lay-off, part of a designated industry;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number of employees at the Canadian establishment
  referred to in paragraph (b) was reduced as a result of lay-offs, in
  any twelve month period including the employee's effective date of
  lay-off, by at least ten per cent or fifty employees, whichever is the
  lesser; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} his lay-off resulted from the economic adjustment
  referred to in subsection 3(2) or (3), as the case may be.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 89, s. 11, c. 169, s. 4
- 1984, c. 40, s. 79(E)
:::

## [Application to Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-338615 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligible applicants

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  employee who has been certified under section 11 may apply to the
  Commission for labour adjustment benefits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application under subsection (1) shall be
  submitted in such form and manner as the Commission may direct and
  shall set out such information as the Commission may require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attendance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the Commission may
  require an applicant to attend at a suitable time and place in order
  to make an application in person or to provide information required
  under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of an application under subsection (1),
  the Commission shall carry out such investigation as it considers
  necessary for the purpose of determining whether or not the applicant
  is qualified to receive labour adjustment benefits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision on application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (6), on completion of its
  investigation under subsection (4), the Commission shall determine
  whether or not the applicant is qualified to receive labour adjustment
  benefits and shall notify him in writing of its decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to Social Security Tribunal

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may at any time within 14 days after
  receiving an application under subsection (1) refer the application or
  a question arising from it to the General Division of the Social
  Security Tribunal for a decision on it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings before Social Security
  Tribunal

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If an application or question is referred to the
  General Division of the Social Security Tribunal under subsection (6),
  the Tribunal must conduct its proceedings in respect of that
  application or question in accordance with the procedure for appeals
  under the [Department of Employment and Social Development
  Act](/eng/acts/H-5.7).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to comply with requirements

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding any other provision of this Act, an
  applicant who fails to comply with a requirement under subsection (2)
  or (3) that has not been waived under subsection (9) is not qualified
  to receive labour adjustment benefits as long as such failure to
  comply continues.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waiver or variation of requirement

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may waive or vary any requirement
  under subsection (2) or (3) if in its opinion the circumstances
  warrant such waiver or variation for the benefit of the applicant in a
  particular case or class or group of cases.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. L-1, s. 13
- 1996, c. 23, s. 187
- 2012, c. 19, s. 274
- 2013, c. 40, s. 236

## [Qualification for Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-338637 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications for benefits

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may determine that an employee who has been certified under
  section 11 is qualified to receive labour adjustment benefits if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the employee is a Canadian citizen resident in
    Canada or a permanent resident within the meaning of subsection 2(1)
    of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} he has had at least ten years of employment in the
    relevant designated industry in the fifteen years immediately
    preceding his effective date of lay-off;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} he was, on his effective date of lay-off, not less
    than fifty-four years of age but under sixty-five years of age;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the employee has claimed and exhausted all benefits
    under Part I of the [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6) to
    which the employee was entitled subsequent to his or her lay-off;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} he has not attained sixty-five years of age; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} he has no present prospect of employment, whether
    with or without training or relocation assistance, or has accepted
    employment with earnings that are less than his average weekly
    insurable earnings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), where the Commission
  is of the opinion that an employee certified under section 11 who
  would be qualified under subsection (1) to receive labour adjustment
  benefits but for the requirements set out in paragraphs (1)(b) and (c)
  or either paragraph will suffer severe financial hardship unless he
  receives those benefits, the Commission may determine that the
  employee is qualified to receive labour adjustment benefits if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} he meets the requirements set out in paragraphs
    (1)(a), (d), (e) and (f);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} his age on his effective date of lay-off, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} his years of employment in the relevant
      designated industry preceding his effective date of lay-off

    equals eighty or more; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} he was, on his effective date of lay-off, under
    sixty-five years of age.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), where an employee
  certified under section 11 would be qualified under subsection (1) to
  receive labour adjustment benefits but for the requirement set out in
  paragraph (1)(b), the Commission may determine that the employee is
  qualified to receive labour adjustment benefits if he shows that he is
  in substantial compliance with the requirement and that he does not
  meet such requirement by reason only of illness, disability, lay-off
  or any other good cause whatever.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section,

  [[relevant designated industry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[relevant designated industry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect
      of an employee, means the designated industry that included, at
      the time of his lay-off, the Canadian establishment from which the
      employee was laid off; ([secteur d'activités désigné en
      question]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[year of employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[year of employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
      employee, means a year in which the employee was paid for at least
      one thousand hours of employment. ([année
      d'emploi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. L-1, s. 14
- 1996, c. 23, s. 178
- 2001, c. 27, s. 260
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employment in other designated industries

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Where the Canadian
establishment from which an employee was laid off was, at the time of
his lay-off, part of an industry designated generally pursuant to
section 3, the Commission shall, in applying paragraph 14(1)(b) or
14(2)(b) in respect of the employee, take into account as employment in
that industry any period of employment of that employee preceding his
effective date of lay-off in any other industry so designated, whether
or not the designation was in force on that date.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 169, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Semi-annual review

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission shall at least once every six months review the
  circumstances of every qualified employee to determine if he continues
  to meet the requirement set out in paragraph 14(1)(f) and, if the
  Commission determines he does not meet that requirement, it shall
  notify him in writing of its decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attendance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the Commission
  may require a qualified employee to attend at a suitable time and
  place in order to provide such information as the Commission may
  require to conduct its review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to comply with requirements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding any other provision of this Act, a
  qualified employee who fails to comply with a requirement to attend
  under subsection (2) that has not been waived under subsection (4) is
  not qualified to receive labour adjustment benefits as long as the
  failure to comply continues.

## [Labour Adjustment Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-338680 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation and payment of benefits

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Commission determines that an employee who has been certified
  under section 11 is qualified to receive labour adjustment benefits,
  the Commission shall, in accordance with this Act, calculate the
  amount of the benefits on a weekly basis in arrears and pay the
  benefits to the qualified employee every two weeks in arrears.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement of benefits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Labour adjustment benefits are payable to a qualified
  employee commencing on the later of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the week immediately following the week the
    employee's benefits under Part I of the [Employment Insurance
    Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6) subsequent to his or her lay-off are
    exhausted, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the week during which he applies to the Commission
    under section 13 for labour adjustment benefits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional benefits

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a qualified employee was certified by the Board
  pursuant to section 11 after the week his or her benefits under Part I
  of the [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6) subsequent to the
  lay-off were exhausted, in addition to the labour adjustment benefits
  otherwise payable to the employee under this Act, labour adjustment
  benefits are payable from the later of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that week, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the week during which he applied to the Board for
    certification

  to the week during which he was so certified.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lump sum payment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), the amount of any
  labour adjustment benefits payable to a qualified employee for a
  period prior to the first two week period for which payment is made to
  him in accordance with that subsection shall be paid in one sum.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. L-1, s. 17
- 1996, c. 23, s. 179
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of benefits

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Labour
  adjustment benefits are not payable to a qualified employee notified
  of a decision pursuant to subsection 16(1) from the week during which
  the notice is sent to the qualified employee to the week during which
  any subsequent notice of decision pursuant to subsection 16(5) to
  revoke that decision is sent to the qualified employee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of benefits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Labour adjustment benefits are not payable to a
  qualified employee after the week during which he attains sixty-five
  years of age.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Labour adjustment benefits shall cease with the
  payment for the week in which the qualified employee dies.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 89, s. 14, c. 169, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial amount of benefit

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  initial amount of labour adjustment benefit payable to a qualified
  employee is a weekly amount equal to sixty per cent of his average
  weekly insurable earnings, which amount shall be rounded to the
  nearest cent or, if the amount is equidistant from two cents, to the
  greater thereof.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Work sharing agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, the weekly insurable
  earnings of a qualified employee for any week of employment under a
  work sharing agreement approved pursuant to section 24 of the
  [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6) is the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total amount the employee would have earned for
    that week if he had worked a full working week for the employer he
    worked for under the agreement, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the maximum weekly insurable earnings, as
    determined pursuant to that Act, for that week.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. L-1, s. 19
- 1996, c. 23, s. 180
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual adjustment

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  weekly amount of labour adjustment benefit payable to a qualified
  employee shall be adjusted each year so that the amount of the benefit
  payable to the employee for a week in any year following the year in
  which his effective date of lay-off falls is equal to the amount
  obtained

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by multiplying

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the initial amount of the benefit as determined
      pursuant to subsection 19(1)

    by

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the ratio, rounded to the nearest one-thousandth
      or, where the ratio is equidistant from two one-thousandths, to
      the greater thereof, that the Pension Index for that following
      year bears to the Pension Index for the year in which the
      effective date of lay-off occurs; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by rounding the product obtained under
    paragraph (a) to the nearest cent or, if the product is equidistant
    from two cents, to the greater thereof.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pension Index

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, the Pension Index for any year means
  the Pension Index for that year calculated in accordance with section
  43 of the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 89, s. 16

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deductions

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be deducted from the weekly amount of labour adjustment benefit
  payable to a qualified employee an amount equal to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sixty cents for each dollar that is received by the
    employee as

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} vacation pay, severance pay, salary, wages or
      other remuneration, including gratuities but excluding any worker
      compensation benefits or other disability payments, in respect of
      any office or employ- ment continued or commenced after the
      commencement of such benefit, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} income from any business carried on by him,
      either alone or with others, that is continued or commenced after
      the commencement of such benefit,

    and that is allocated to that week in accordance with the
    regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one dollar for each dollar that is received by the
    employee as

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} benefits under an employer pension plan earned by
      the employee as a result of any office or employment,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a retirement pension under the [Canada Pension
      Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or a provincial pension plan,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} benefits under Part I of the [Employment
      Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6),

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1996, c. 23, s. 181\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} vacation pay, severance pay, salary, wages or
      other remuneration, including gratuities but excluding any worker
      compensation benefits or other disability payments, in respect of
      any office or employment terminated prior to the commencement of
      such benefit, or

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} income from any business carried on by him,
      either alone or with others, that is terminated prior to the
      commencement of such benefit,

    and that is allocated to that week in accordance with the
    regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (1),

  [[business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a profession, calling,
      trade, manufacture or undertaking of any kind and an adventure or

      employment; ([entreprise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the position of an
      individual in the service of some other person, including Her
      Majesty or a foreign state or
      sovereign; ([emploi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the position of an individual
      entitling him to a fixed or ascertainable stipend or remuneration
      and includes any office the incumbent of which is elected by
      popular vote or is elected or appointed in a representative
      capacity and also includes the position of a corporation

::: HistoricalNote

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed benefit

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a qualified employee to whom labour adjustment benefits are payable
  has, after his effective date of lay-off and before the commencement
  of those benefits, received a lump sum payment, under or in respect of

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report of earnings

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to comply

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding any other provision of this Act, a

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waiver or variation of requirement

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may waive or vary any requirement

## [Administration and Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-338764 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False or misleading statements

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Board becomes aware of facts establishing, in its opinion, that a
  person, in relation to an application under section 11, has made a
  statement or representation that he knew to be false or misleading or
  has participated or acquiesced in the making of such a statement or
  representation, the Board may revoke the certification of any employee
  named in the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of intention

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before the Board revokes the certification of an
  employee pursuant to subsection (1), it shall notify the employee in
  writing of its intention to do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to make written representations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An employee who is notified pursuant to
  subsection (2) may within thirty days after the date of the notice, or
  within such further time as the Board may allow, make representations
  in writing to the Board with respect to the proposed revocation of his
  certification.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of decision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board revokes the certification of an
  employee pursuant to subsection (1), it shall notify the employee and
  the Commission in writing of the revocation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding any other provision of this Act, a
  qualified employee whose certification is revoked pursuant to
  subsection (1) is not qualified at any time before or after the
  revocation to receive labour adjustment benefits by virtue of the
  revoked certification.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} No certification shall be revoked pursuant to
  subsection (1) more than seventy-two months after the date on which
  the false or misleading statement was made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recertification not precluded

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section shall be construed as
  preventing an employee whose certification is revoked pursuant to
  subsection (1) from re-applying to, and being certified by, the Board
  under section 11.

  showing the number, during the three months, of applications under
  sections 11 and 13 and of persons to whom labour adjustment benefits
  were paid and the Minister shall cause the report to be laid before
  Parliament on any of the first fifteen days that either House of
  Parliament is sitting after the day he completes it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board and the Commission shall, on the request of
  the Minister, provide the Minister with such information on the
  administration of this Act as he may require to prepare his report
  under subsection (1).
