## Tlicho Land Claims and Self-Government Act {#tlicho-land-claims-and-self-government-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2005, c. 1
:::

Assented to 2005-02-15

An Act to give effect to a land claims and self-government agreement
among the Tlicho, the Government of the Northwest Territories and the
Government of Canada, to make related amendments to the Mackenzie Valley
Resource Management Act and to make consequential amendments to other
Acts
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the Tlicho is an aboriginal people of Canada that has used and
occupied lands in and adjacent to the Northwest Territories from time
immemorial;

WHEREAS the Tlicho, as represented by the Dogrib Treaty 11 Council, the
Government of the Northwest Territories and the Government of Canada
negotiated a land claims and self-government agreement in order to
define and provide certainty in respect of rights of the Tlicho relating
to lands, resources and self-government;

WHEREAS the Tlicho, by a vote held on June 26 and 27, 2003, approved the
agreement;

WHEREAS the Tlicho, as represented by the Dogrib Treaty 11 Council, the
Government of the Northwest Territories and the Government of Canada
signed the agreement on August 25, 2003;

WHEREAS on October 10, 2003 the Commissioner in Council of the Northwest
Territories made an ordinance entitled the Tlicho Land Claims and
Self-Government Agreement Act approving the agreement;

AND WHEREAS the agreement provides that the agreement will be a land
claims agreement within the meaning of section 35 of the [Constitution
Act, 1982](/eng/Const/) and that approval by Parliament is a condition
precedent to the validity of the agreement;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448925 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Tlicho Land Claims and Self-Government Act](/eng/acts/T-11.3).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448928 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Land Claims and
    Self-Government Agreement among the Tlicho, the Government of the
    Northwest Territories and the Government of Canada, signed on August
    25, 2003, including any amendments made to it from time to
    time. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Tax Treatment Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Tax Treatment Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Tlicho Tax
    Treatment Agreement among the Government of Canada, the Government
    of the Northwest Territories and the Tlicho First Nation, signed on
    behalf of the Government of Canada on February 6, 2003, on behalf of
    the Government of the Northwest Territories on February 27, 2003 and
    on behalf of the Tlicho First Nation on March 3, 2003, including any
    amendments made to it from time to time. ([accord sur le traitement
    fiscal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Tlicho Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Tlicho Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the government of
    the Tlicho First Nation established in accord­ance with chapter 7 of
    the Agreement. ([gouvernement tlicho]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Tlicho law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Tlicho law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a law enacted by the Tlicho
    Government. ([loi tlicho]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448935 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement given effect

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement is approved, given effect and declared valid and has the
  force of law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and obligations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, any person or body may
  exercise the powers, rights, privileges and benefits conferred on the
  person or body by the Agreement and shall perform the duties, and is
  subject to the liabilities, imposed on the person or body by the
  Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement binding

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Agreement is binding on,
  and may be relied on by, all persons and bodies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of Agreement and amendments

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Indian
Affairs and Northern Development shall cause a copy of the Agreement and
of any amendments made to it to be deposited in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Library of Parliament;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the library of the Legislative Assembly of the
  Northwest Territories;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the main office of the Tlicho Government;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the library of the Department of Indian Affairs and
  Northern Development that is situated in the National Capital Region;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the office of the Registrar of Land Titles for the
  Northwest Territories;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the regional office of the Department of Indian
  Affairs and Northern Development that is situated in the Northwest
  Territories; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} any other places that that Minister considers
  necessary.

## [Other Laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448952 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict with Agreement or this Act

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  event of an inconsistency or conflict between the Agreement or this
  Act, or any regulations made under this Act, and the provisions of any
  other Act of Parliament, any law of the Legislature of the Northwest
  Territories, any regulations made under any of those other Acts or
  laws, or any Tlicho law, then the Agreement or this Act, or
  regulations made under this Act, as the case may be, prevail to the
  extent of the inconsistency or conflict.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict between the Agreement and this
  Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of an inconsistency or conflict between
  the Agreement and the provisions of this Act or any regulations made
  under this Act, the Agreement prevails to the extent of the
  inconsistency or conflict.

## [Appropriation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448960 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of C.R.F.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** There shall be paid out of
the Consolidated Revenue Fund any sums that are required to meet the
monetary obligations of Canada under chapters 9, 18 and 24 to 26 of the
Agreement.

## [Taxation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448963 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tax Treatment Agreement given effect

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Tax
  Treatment Agreement is approved, given effect and declared valid and
  has the force of law during the period that it is in effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not a treaty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tax Treatment Agreement does not form part of the
  Agreement and is not a treaty or a land claims agreement within the
  meaning of section 35 of the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

## [Wekeezhii Renewable Resources Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448969 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal capacity

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
carrying out its objectives, the Wekeezhii Renewable Resources Board
established by chapter 12 of the Agreement has the capacity, rights,
powers and privileges of a natural person.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-448972 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice of Agreements

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial
  notice shall be taken of the Agreement and the Tax Treatment
  Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of Agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Agreement and the Tax Treatment Agreement shall
  be published by the Queen's Printer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the Agreement or the Tax Treatment
  Agreement published by the Queen's Printer is evidence of that
  Agreement, and a copy purporting to be published by the Queen's
  Printer is deemed to be so published, unless the contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice of Tlicho laws

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial notice shall be taken of
  Tlicho laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence of Tlicho laws

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of a Tlicho law purporting to be deposited in
  the public registry of Tlicho laws referred to in chapter 7 of the
  Agreement is evidence of that law and of its contents, unless the
  contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
Tlicho laws are not statutory instruments within the meaning of the
[Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make any orders and regulations that are necessary for the purpose
of carrying out the Agreement or the Tax Treatment Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility Committee

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Despite having been
established before the effective date of the Agreement, the Eligibility
Committee referred to in chapter 3 of the Agreement is deemed to have
been validly established under the Agreement and to have had, since it
was established, the jurisdiction, powers and authority provided by the
Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of issues arising

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, in
  any judicial or administrative proceeding, an issue arises in respect
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the interpretation, validity or applicability of
    the Agreement, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the validity or applicability of this Act, the law
    of the Legislature of the Northwest Territories entitled the Tlicho
    Land Claims and Self-Government Agreement Act or any Tlicho law,

  the issue shall not be decided until the party raising the issue has
  served notice on the Attorney General of Canada, the Attorney General
  of the Northwest Territories and the Tlicho Government.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} describe the judicial or administrative proceeding
    in which the issue arises;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} state whether the issue arises in respect of the
    matters referred to in paragraph (1)(a) or (b) or both;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state the day on which the issue is to be argued;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} give particulars necessary to show the point to be
    argued; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be served at least 14 days before the day of
    argument, unless the court or tribunal authorizes a shorter period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation in proceedings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In any judicial or administrative proceeding to which
  subsection (1) applies, the Attorney General of Canada, the Attorney
  General of the Northwest Territories and the Tlicho Government may
  appear and participate in the proceeding as parties with the same
  rights as any other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsections (2) and (3) do not
  require that an oral hearing be held if one is not otherwise required.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity of ordinances of the Northwest
Territories

**[[96]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-96 .sectionLabel}** The following ordinances
of the Northwest Territories are deemed for all purposes to have been
validly made if they were made before the coming into force of this Act
and would have been validly made if they had been made after that coming
into force, and everything done under any of those ordinances before
that coming into force has the effect that it would otherwise have if
the ordinance had been validly made after that coming into force:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ordinance of the Northwest Territories entitled
  the Tlicho Land Claims and Self-Government Agreement Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an ordinance that establishes community governments
  as required by chapter 8 of the Agreement; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an ordinance that establishes a community services
  agency as required by the first intergovernmental services agreement
  referred to in 7.10 of chapter 7 of the Agreement.
