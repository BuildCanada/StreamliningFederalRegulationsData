## Energy Monitoring Act {#energy-monitoring-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. E-8
:::

An Act respecting energy monitoring
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-220160 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Energy Monitoring Act](/eng/acts/E-8).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-220165 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2001, c. 34, s.
      42\]]{.Repealed}

  [[Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the continental shelf of
      Canada; ([Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[energy commodity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[energy commodity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means oil and gas and any
      prescribed product resulting from the processing or refining of
      oil or gas and, where there is a designation in respect of coal,
      thorium and uranium, or any of those substances, under section 10,
      includes all those substances, or the designated substance, as the
      case may be, and any prescribed product resulting from the
      processing or refining of the designated substance or
      substances; ([produit énergétique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[energy enterprise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[energy enterprise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any individual,
      corporation, partnership, trust or organization engaged in the
      exploration for, or the development, production, processing or
      refining of, any energy commodity in Canada; ([entreprise
      énergétique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[gas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[gas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any hydrocarbon or mixture of
      hydrocarbons that, at a temperature of 15°C and a pressure of
      101.325 kPa, is in a gaseous state; ([gaz]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Natural
      Resources; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any hydrocarbon or mixture of
      hydrocarbons other than coal or gas; ([pétrole]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[oil and gas dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[oil and gas dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person or
      association of persons who supplies, transports or stores oil, gas
      or petroleum products and includes, without limiting the
      generality of the foregoing, any exporter, importer, refiner,
      processor, wholesale marketer, jobber, distributor, terminal
      operator or broker who supplies oil, gas or petroleum
      products; ([négociant en pétrole et en gaz]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned to
      that term by the regulations; ([organisme]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[petroleum product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[petroleum product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prescribed
      product resulting from the production, processing or refining of
      oil or gas; ([produit pétrolier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by the
      regulations. ([Version anglaise
      seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of control

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 5(2) and paragraphs
  6(f) and 7(b), a corporation (in this section referred to as the
  "controlled corporation") is controlled by an energy enterprise or
  another corporation if voting securities of the controlled corporation
  to which are attached more than fifty per cent of the votes that may
  be cast to elect directors are held, directly or indirectly, other
  than by way of security only, by or for the benefit of the energy
  enterprise or other corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. E-8, s. 2
- 1994, c. 41, s. 37
- 1996, c. 31, s. 80
- 2001, c. 34, s. 42
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada and in right of any province.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 3
:::

## [Energy Enterprise Monitoring]{.HTitleText1} {#h-220188 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 7, this
Act applies to every energy enterprise that has

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} gross annual revenues derived from the activities
  referred to in the definition "energy enterprise" in subsection 2(1),
  determined in prescribed manner, in excess of ten million dollars or
  such higher amount as is prescribed; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} assets related to its activities referred to in the
  definition "energy enterprise" in subsection 2(1), determined in
  prescribed manner, in excess of ten million dollars or such higher
  amount as is prescribed.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. E-8, s. 4
- 1993, c. 34, s. 62(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of Canadian energy enterprise

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  exempted by the regulations, every energy enterprise shall file with
  the Minister, in prescribed form and manner, for each prescribed
  reporting period that applies to that energy enterprise, a return
  setting out statistics and information relating to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its ownership and control;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its sources and application of funds;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its financial position and performance;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} its exploration for, development, production,
    processing, refining and marketing of energy commodities;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} its costs and revenues in connection with each of
    its activities referred to in paragraph (d);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the exploration expenditures, development
    expenditures, resource allowances, capital cost allowances,
    non-capital losses and the other deductions, credits and allowances
    deducted under the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) in the
    computation of income from and taxes payable on each of its
    activities referred to in paragraph (d);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the amounts with respect to the expenditures,
    allowances, losses, deductions and credits referred to in
    paragraph (f) that can be carried forward in accordance with the
    provisions of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the distribution and disposition of its revenues
    and profits;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its energy commodity resources, reserves and
    properties;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} its ownership of or interest in any corporation,
    partnership, trust or organization;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} its research and development programs; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} such other matters concerning each of its
    activities referred to in paragraph (d) and the funds derived from
    those activities as are prescribed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional statistics and information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the statistics and information
  referred to in subsection (1), every energy enterprise required to
  file a return under subsection (1) shall set out, in prescribed form
  and manner, in every return required by that subsection, statistics
  and information relating to the matters referred to in paragraphs
  (1)(b) to (l) in respect of every corporation it controls.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply to any energy enterprise
  required to file a return under section 6.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of foreign energy enterprise

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Unless exempted by the
regulations, where an energy enterprise is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an individual not ordinarily resident in Canada,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation incorporated elsewhere than in Canada,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a partnership all the partners of which are
  individuals not ordinarily resident in Canada or corporations
  incorporated elsewhere than in Canada, or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a trust the trustee and the beneficiary of which are,
  with respect to their status as such, governed by the laws of a
  jurisdiction other than that of Canada or a province,

that energy enterprise shall file with the Minister, in prescribed form
and manner, for each prescribed reporting period that applies to that
energy enterprise, a return setting out statistics and information
relating to

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} its ownership and control, and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the matters set out in paragraphs 5(1)(b) to (l) in
  respect of its holdings and operations in Canada and the holdings and
  operations in Canada of every corporation it controls.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may require return

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where the Minister is of
the opinion that it is in the public interest, he may require

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any energy enterprise to which this Act does not
  apply to file, in prescribed form and manner, a return setting out the
  statistics and information referred to in section 5 or 6, as the case
  may be; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any corporation that controls a corporation that is
  an energy enterprise to file, in prescribed form and manner, a return
  setting out statistics and information relating to the matters set out
  in paragraphs 5(1)(a), (b), (h) and (j).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supplementary statistics, information and
documentation

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Every energy enterprise
required to file a return by or under this Act shall submit such
additional statistics, information and documentation as may be required
by the Minister for any purpose related to the administration or
enforcement of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 8
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, 2001, c. 34,
s. 43\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate coal, thorium and uranium, or any of those
substances, as an energy commodity for the purposes of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 10
:::

## [Oil and Gas Dealer Monitoring]{.HTitleText1} {#h-220242 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 13,
this Act does not apply, with respect to any reporting period, to any
oil and gas dealer who supplied, transported or stored less than one
hundred million litres of oil, gas or petroleum products in the twelve
months that immediately preceded that period.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of oil and gas dealer

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Unless exempted by the
regulations, every oil and gas dealer shall file with the Minister, in
prescribed form and manner, for each prescribed reporting period that
applies to that oil and gas dealer, a return setting out statistics and
information relating to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} his production, processing, refining, storage,
  marketing, sale and use of oil, gas and petroleum products; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} his costs, revenues, prices and profits in connection
  with each activity referred to in paragraph (a).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 12
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may require return

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Where the Minister is of
the opinion that it is in the public interest, he may require any oil
and gas dealer to whom this Act does not apply to file, in prescribed
form and manner, a return setting out the statistics and information
referred to in section 12.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 13
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supplementary statistics, information and
documentation

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Every oil and gas dealer
required to file a return by or under this Act shall submit such
additional statistics, information and documentation as may be required
by the Minister for any purpose related to the administration or
enforcement of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statistics, information and documentation
available to Board

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may make the
statistics, information and documentation obtained by him under this Act
that relate to oil and gas dealers available to the Energy Supplies
Allocation Board established under the [Energy Supplies Emergency
Act](/eng/acts/E-9).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 15
:::

**[[16 to 28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16_to_28 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
2001, c. 34, s. 44\]]{.Repealed}

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-220266 .Part}

### [Reports Regarding Energy Commodities and Energy Enterprises]{.HTitleText2} {#h-220267 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  Minister may publish any reports that the Minister considers
  appropriate and necessary in respect of energy commodities and energy
  enterprises and their holdings and operations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of certain information only

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not disclose in any report
  published under subsection (1) any statistic, information or
  documentation obtained by the Minister under this Act or any other Act
  of Parliament that identifies or permits the identification of the
  individual, corporation, partnership, trust or organization to which
  the statistic, information or documentation relates without the
  written consent of that individual, corporation, partnership, trust or
  organization.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. E-8, s. 29
- 2001, c. 34, ss. 44, 45
:::

### [Documents, Records and Books of Account]{.HTitleText2} {#h-220275 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents, records and books of account

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Every energy enterprise
and every oil and gas dealer required by or under this Act to file a
return shall keep documents, records and books of account at his or its
place of business or other prescribed place in Canada in such form and
containing such statistics and information as will enable the Minister
to verify the accuracy and completeness of the statistics, information
and documentation that the energy enterprise or oil and gas dealer
submits to the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 30
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal of records

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** Every energy enterprise
and every oil and gas dealer required by section 30 to keep documents,
records and books of accounts shall, unless authorized by the Minister,
retain every such document, record or book of account until the
expiration of six years from the end of the reporting period to which
the document, record or book of account relates.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 31
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to authorized persons

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** Every energy enterprise
and every oil and gas dealer required by section 30 to keep documents,
records and books of account shall, for the purpose of audit or
examination,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} make those documents, records and books of account
  available at all reasonable times to any person authorized by the
  Minister for the purpose; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} give all reasonable assistance to a person authorized
  by the Minister to carry out the audit or examination, provide access
  to all relevant sites, answer, orally or in writing, as required, all
  questions relating to the audit or examination and provide all
  statistics, information, documentation and copies required for the
  purpose of the audit or examination.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 32
:::

### [Disclosure of Statistics and Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-220290 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privilege

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** The statistics,
information and documentation obtained by the Minister under this Act,
by the Energy Supplies Allocation Board under section 15 or by the
persons referred to in paragraphs 34(a) and (b) are privileged and shall
not knowingly be or be permitted to be communicated, disclosed or made
available without the written consent of the person from whom they were
obtained.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. E-8, s. 33
- 2001, c. 34, s. 46
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The statistics,
information and documentation obtained under this Act may be
communicated, disclosed or made available for the purposes of the
administration or enforcement of this Act, legal proceedings related
thereto or criminal proceedings under this Act or any other Act of
Parliament and may be communicated, disclosed or made available

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the Minister of Finance solely for the purposes of
  evaluating and formulating tax policy in relation to energy matters;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the Chief Statistician of Canada for the purposes
  of the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 34
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidentiary privilege

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding any other
Act or law, no person who obtains any statistic, information or
documentation under this Act shall be required, in connection with any
legal proceedings, other than proceedings relating to the administration
or enforcement of this Act or criminal proceedings under this Act or any
other Act of Parliament, to give evidence relating to any statistic,
information or documentation that is privileged under this Act or to
produce any statement, document, writing or portion thereof containing
any such statistic, information or documentation.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 35
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure to public

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything in this
  Act, the Minister may disclose any statistic, information or
  documentation obtained under this Act where he considers the
  disclosure to be in the public interest, taking into account the
  competitive position of the individual, corporation, partnership,
  trust, organization or association of persons affected by the
  disclosure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification and opportunity to make
  representations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister proposes to disclose any
  statistic, information or documentation pursuant to subsection (1) in
  a form that identifies or permits the identification of the
  individual, corporation, partnership, trust, organization or
  association of persons to which the statistic, information or
  documentation relates, the Minister shall so notify that individual,
  corporation, partnership, trust, organization or association of
  persons and afford him or it a reasonable opportunity to make
  representations with respect to the effect that the disclosure of the
  statistic, information or documentation might have on his or its
  competitive position.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 36
:::

### [Enforcement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-220312 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation or duty on persons

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  obligation or duty imposed by or under this Act on any energy
  enterprise that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a partnership shall be carried out or complied with
    by the partners of the partnership;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a trust shall be carried out or complied with by
    the trustee of the trust; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an organization shall be carried out or complied
    with by such persons responsible for the administration and
    management of the organization as are prescribed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any obligation or duty imposed by or under this Act
  on any association of persons shall be carried out or complied with by
  the persons forming part of that association.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 37
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to file return or provide
supplementary information

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** Every person who

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fails to file a return as and when required by this
  Act,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} fails to comply with a requirement of the Minister
  pursuant to section 7 or 13,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} fails to comply with section 8, 14, 30, 31 or 32, or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} contravenes or fails to comply with section 33

is guilty of an offence punishable on summary conviction and liable to a
fine not exceeding twenty-five thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a
term not exceeding one year or to both.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 38
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False information

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** Every person who, under
this Act, submits any statistic, information or documentation, makes any
statement or answers any question, whether in connection with any return
filed under this Act or any audit or examination carried out pursuant to
this Act, knowing that the statistic, information, documentation,
statement or answer is false or misleading or misrepresents or fails to
disclose a material fact, is guilty of an offence and liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding fifty
  thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six
  months or to both; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine not exceeding
  two hundred thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not
  exceeding five years or to both.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 39
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers, etc., of corporation

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** Where a corporation
commits an offence under this Act, any officer, director or agent of the
corporation who directed, authorized, assented to, acquiesced in or
participated in the commission of the offence is a party to and guilty
of the offence and is liable on conviction to the punishment provided
for the offence whether or not the corporation has been prosecuted or
convicted.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 40
:::

### [Regulations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-220342 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} defining the term "organization" for the purposes of
  this Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} exempting any energy enterprise or oil and gas
  dealer, or any class thereof, from the requirement of filing a return
  under this Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing any matter or thing that is by this Act
  to be prescribed; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting such other matters or things as are
  necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 41
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act referred to Committee of House of
Commons

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** This Act shall be deemed
to be referred, for review and report, to the first sitting of the
Committee of the House of Commons that normally considers oil and gas
matters, following February 18, 1988.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 112, s. 42
:::
