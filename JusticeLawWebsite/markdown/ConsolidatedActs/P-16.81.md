## Poverty Reduction Act {#poverty-reduction-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2019, c. 29, s. 315
:::

Assented to 2019-06-21

An Act respecting the reduction of poverty

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 315 of chapter 29 of the Statutes of Canada, 2019,
sections 9 to 12 in force July 9, 2019, see SI/2019-58.\]
:::
:::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas Canada aspires to be a world leader in the eradication of
poverty;

Whereas poverty reduction contributes to meeting Canada's international
human rights obligations, including under the International Covenant on
Economic, Social and Cultural Rights, the Convention on the Rights of
the Child, the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities and
the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against
Women;

And whereas the progress made by Canada in the reduction of poverty
contributes to meeting the Sustainable Development Goals of the United
Nations;
:::

::: Enacts
Now, therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169158 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Poverty Reduction Act](/eng/acts/P-16.81).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169161 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the National Advisory Council
    on Poverty established under section 9. ([Conseil]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada designated under section
    4. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Official Poverty Line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Official Poverty Line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Market
    Basket Measure, as published by Statistics Canada under the
    authority of the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19). ([seuil officiel
    de la pauvreté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169167 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of the Act is
to support continuous efforts in, and continuous monitoring of, poverty
reduction in Canada.

## [Designation of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169170 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order in council

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate a member of the Queen's Privy Council for
Canada to be the Minister for the purposes of this Act.

## [Poverty Reduction Strategy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169173 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Strategy development and implementation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must develop
and implement a poverty reduction strategy.

## [Targets]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169176 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Poverty reduction targets

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The targets for poverty
reduction in Canada to which the Government of Canada aspires are the
following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 20% below the level of poverty in 2015 by 2020; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 50% below the level of poverty in 2015 by 2030.

## [Official Poverty Line and Other Metrics]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169181 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Official metric

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Official Poverty Line is Canada's official metric to measure the level
  of poverty in Canada and to assess progress in meeting the targets set
  out in section 6.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Official Poverty Line is to be reviewed, on a
  regular basis as determined by Statistics Canada, to ensure that it
  reflects the up-to-date cost of a basket of goods and services
  representing a modest, basic standard of living in Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other metrics

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  metrics set out in the schedule are to be used, in addition to the
  Official Poverty Line, to measure the level of poverty in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of schedule

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by order, amend the
  schedule by adding or deleting a metric.

## [National Advisory Council on Poverty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169192 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  council is established, to be known as the National Advisory Council
  on Poverty, consisting of 8 to 10 members, including a Chairperson and
  a member with particular responsibilities for children's issues.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deputy Minister --- [ex
  officio]{.otherLang lang="la"} member

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Deputy Minister of the department for which the
  Minister is responsible is [ex officio]{.otherLang lang="la"} a member
  of the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of other members

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Council, other than the [ex
  officio]{.otherLang lang="la"} member, are to be appointed by the
  Governor in Council to hold office during pleasure for a term not
  exceeding three years and are eligible for reappointment on the expiry
  of a first or subsequent term of office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Full-time or part-time membership

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson is to be appointed as a full-time
  member and the other members are to be appointed as either full-time
  members or part-time members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson's role

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson has supervision over and direction of
  the work of the Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson absent or unable to act

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the Chairperson is absent or unable to act or if
  the office of Chairperson is vacant, the Minister may designate
  another member of the Council to act as Chairperson, but that member
  may act as Chairperson for a period of more than 90 days only with the
  approval of the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Council, other than the [ex
  officio]{.otherLang lang="la"} member, are to be paid, in connection
  with their work for the Council, the remuneration that may be fixed by
  the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Travel and living expenses

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Council are entitled to be
  reimbursed for the travel, living and other expenses incurred, in
  connection with their work for the Council, while absent from, in the
  case of full-time members, their ordinary place of work or, in the
  case of part-time members, their ordinary place of residence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed employment

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Council are deemed to be employees
  for the purposes of the [Government Employees Compensation
  Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and to be employed in the federal public
  administration for the purposes of any regulations made under section
  9 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2). Full-time members are also
  deemed to be employed in the public service for the purposes of the
  [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Functions

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Council must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide advice to the Minister on poverty reduction
  in Canada, including with respect to programs, funding and activities
  that support poverty reduction;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} undertake consultations with the public, including
  the academic community and other experts, Indigenous persons and
  persons with lived experience in poverty;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} within six months after the end of each fiscal year,
  submit a report to the Minister

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on the progress being made in meeting the targets
    referred to in section 6 and the progress being made in poverty
    reduction measured by, among other things, the metrics set out in
    the schedule, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on the advice that the Council provided to the
    Minister under paragraph (a) during the fiscal year; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} undertake any activity specified by the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dissolution of Council

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, dissolve the Council, on the recommendation of the
Minister made after the Minister is of the opinion that the level of
poverty in Canada has been reduced by 50% below the level of poverty in
2015.

## [Tabling of Report in Parliament]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1169222 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's duty

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must cause
the report referred to in paragraph 10(c) to be tabled in each House of
Parliament on any of the first 15 days on which that House is sitting
after the Minister receives the report.
