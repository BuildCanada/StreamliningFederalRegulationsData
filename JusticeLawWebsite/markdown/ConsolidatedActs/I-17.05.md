## International Bridges and Tunnels Act {#international-bridges-and-tunnels-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2007, c. 1
:::

Assented to 2007-02-01

An Act respecting international bridges and tunnels and making a
consequential amendment to another Act

::::::::::::::::::::::: section

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270805 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [International Bridges and Tunnels Act](/eng/acts/I-17.05).

## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270808 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[alteration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[alteration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a conversion, an
    extension and a change in the use of an international bridge or
    tunnel but does not include its operation, maintenance and
    repair. ([modification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[debt obligation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[debt obligation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bond, debenture,
    note or other evidence of indebtedness or guarantee of an entity,
    whether secured or unsecured. ([titre de créance]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation, partnership,
    trust, joint venture or unincorporated association or
    organization. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[international bridge or tunnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international bridge or tunnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    bridge or tunnel, or any part of it, that connects any place in
    Canada to any place outside Canada, and includes the approaches and
    facilities related to the bridge or tunnel. ([pont ou tunnel
    international]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport.
     ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a corporation, a share of any
      class of shares of the corporation or a debt obligation of the
      corporation, and includes a warrant of the corporation, but does
      not include a deposit with a financial institution or any
      instrument evidencing such a deposit; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to any other entity, any ownership
      interest in or debt obligation of the
      entity. ([titre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Relationship with certain Acts

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  event of any inconsistency or conflict between this Act or any
  regulations made under it and any Act listed in the schedule, this Act
  and the regulations prevail to the extent of the inconsistency or
  conflict.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of schedule

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of
  the Minister, make regulations amending the schedule by adding,
  chang­ing or deleting the name of an Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of other Acts

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act or any regulations made under it
  affects the application of any other Act of Parliament, including any
  requirement for a person to obtain a licence, permit or other
  authorization in respect of an international bridge or tunnel.

## [Construction and Alteration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270833 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No person shall construct
or alter an international bridge or tunnel without the approval of the
Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for approval

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Approval
  for the construction or alteration of an international bridge or
  tunnel may only be obtained by submitting an application to the
  Minister for approval by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, if in the opinion of the Minister
  it is necessary having regard to all the circumstances, consult with
  the other levels of government that have jurisdiction over the place
  of the proposed construction or alteration and with any person who, in
  the opinion of the Minister, has a direct interest in the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents or information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who submits an application shall provide the
  Minister with any document or information that is required under
  guidelines issued by the Minister and any other document or
  information that is required by the Minister after receipt of the
  application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not statutory instruments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The guidelines referred to in subsection (2) are not
  statutory instruments within the meaning of the [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

- 2007, c. 1, s. 7
- 2015, c. 3, s. 122(F)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of Governor in Council

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of the Minister,
  approve the proposed construction or alteration of an international
  bridge or tunnel subject to any terms and conditions that the Governor
  in Council considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Variation of terms and conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may vary or rescind the terms
  and conditions or impose new terms and conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with terms and conditions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is subject to terms and conditions
  shall comply with them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial order

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  international bridge or tunnel is constructed or altered without the
  approval of the Governor in Council, the Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} order any person to refrain from proceeding with
    the construction or alteration of the bridge or tunnel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} order the owner of the bridge or tunnel to remove
    or alter the bridge or tunnel; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the owner of the bridge or tunnel fails to
    comply with an order made under paragraph (b), remove and destroy
    the bridge or tunnel and sell, give away or otherwise dispose of the
    materials contained in the bridge or tunnel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs of removal, destruction or
  disposal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The costs of and incidental to anything done by the
  Minister under paragraph (1)(c) are --- after deducting any sum that
  may be realized by sale or otherwise --- recoverable with costs by Her
  Majesty in right of Canada from the owner as a debt due to Her
  Majesty.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who contravenes section 6 or subsection 8(3) or fails to comply
  with an order of the Minister under paragraph 9(1)(a) or (b) is guilty
  of an offence and is liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine not
    exceeding \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two
    years, or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding
    \$200,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or
    to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the offence is committed or continued on more than
  one day, the person who commits it is liable to be convicted for a
  separate offence for each day on which it is committed or continued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers, etc., of corporations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the offence is committed by a corporation, any
  officer, director or agent or mandatary of the corporation who
  directed, authorized, assented to, acquiesced in or participated in
  the commission of the offence is a party to and guilty of the offence
  and is liable on conviction to the punishment provided for the offence
  whether or not the corporation has been prosecuted or convicted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court may order forfeiture

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person is convicted on indictment of an offence referred to in
  subsection 10(1), the court may, in addition to any other punishment
  that it may impose, order that the international bridge or tunnel, or
  anything used in its construction or alteration, (in this section
  referred to as the "property") be forfeited and, on the making of the
  order, the property is forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application by person claiming interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If any property is forfeited under subsection (1),
  any person, other than a person convicted of the offence that resulted
  in the forfeiture, who claims a right or interest in the property,
  may, within 30 days after the forfeiture, apply by notice in writing
  to a judge of the superior court of the province where the property is
  situated for an order under subsection (5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of hearing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A judge to whom the application is made shall fix a
  day not less than 30 days after the date of filing of the application
  for the hearing of the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An applicant shall serve on the Minister a notice of
  the application and the day fixed for the hearing of the application
  at least 15 days before the day fixed for the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order by judge

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The applicant is entitled to an order by a judge
  declaring that the applicant's right or interest is not affected by
  the forfeiture if, on the hearing of the application, the judge is
  satisfied that the applicant

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is innocent of any complicity in the offence that
    resulted in the forfeiture and of any collusion in relation to the
    offence with the person convicted of the offence; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} exercised reasonable care to prevent the property
    from being constructed, altered or used, as the case may be, in
    contravention of this Act.

  The judge shall include in the order a declaration of the nature and
  extent of the applicant's right or interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} An appeal from an order or refusal to make an order
  under subsection (5) lies to the court to which an appeal may be taken
  from an order of the superior court in the province in which the
  forfeiture occurred, and the appeal shall be asserted, heard and
  decided according to the ordinary procedure governing appeals to the
  court from orders or judgments of a judge of the superior court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to Minister

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, on application made to the
  Minister by any person who has obtained an order under subsection (5),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} direct that the property to which the applicant's
    right or interest relates be returned to the applicant; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} direct that an amount equal to the value of the
    applicant's right or interest, as declared in the order, be paid to
    the applicant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal of forfeited property

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If no application is made under this section for an
  order in relation to a right or interest in any property or an
  application is made and the judge or, on appeal, the court refuses to
  make an order referred to in subsection (5), the property shall be
  disposed of in any manner that the Minister may direct.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expropriation

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If a person requires an
interest in land or an immovable real right within the meaning of
section 2 of the [Expropriation Act](/eng/acts/E-21), for the purposes
of the construction or alteration of an international bridge or tunnel
and has unsuccessfully attempted to purchase the interest in land or the
immovable real right, the person may request the Minister to have the
Minister of Public Works and Government Services have the interest in
land or the immovable real right expropriated by the Crown and section
4.1 of that Act applies to that person, with any modifications that are
necessary, as if the person were a railway company.

## [Maintenance and Repair]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270897 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial order

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may order
the owner or operator of an international bridge or tunnel to take any
action that the Minister considers appropriate to ensure that it is kept
in good condition.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, on the recommendation of the Minister, make regulations respecting
the maintenance and repair of international bridges and tunnels,
including regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} requiring the owner or operator of an international
  bridge or tunnel to prepare reports for the Minister on the condition
  of the bridge or tunnel;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} specifying the information that is to be included in
  the reports and when they must be prepared and sent to the Minister;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} requiring any person or class of persons to provide
  to the Minister any information related to the maintenance and repair
  of international bridges and tunnels; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} providing for the inspection of international bridges
  and tunnels by the Minister or a person designated by the Minister.

## [Operation and Use]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270906 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of the Minister, make
  regulations respecting the operation and use of international bridges
  and tunnels, including regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the use that may be made of
    international bridges or tunnels by different types of vehicles;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting any matter relating to complaints in
    respect of tolls, fees and other charges, including the procedures
    to be followed and the person or body who is responsible for dealing
    with complaints;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the level of service that is to be
    provided by owners or operators of international bridges or tunnels;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} requiring any person or class of persons to provide
    to the Minister any information related to the operation and use of
    international bridges and tunnels.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before recommending that a regulation be made under
  subsection (1), the Minister shall, if in the opinion of the Minister
  it is necessary having regard to all the circumstances, consult with
  the other levels of government that have jurisdiction over any place
  where an international bridge or tunnel is situated and with any
  person who, in the opinion of the Minister, has a direct interest in
  the matter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial order

**[[15.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.1 .sectionLabel}** If the Minister is of
the opinion that a change in the tolls, fees or other charges for the
use of an international bridge or tunnel is resulting in adverse effects
on the flow of traffic, the Minister may, with the approval of the
Governor in Council, order the owner or operator of the bridge or tunnel
to impose the tolls, fees or other charges that, in the opinion of the
Minister, would not result in adverse effects on the flow of traffic.
Before ordering an owner or operator to impose specified tolls, fees or
other charges, the Minister shall consult with the owner or operator
concerning the impact that those tolls, fees or other charges could have
on their financial situation.

## [Security and Safety]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270918 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, on the recommendation of the Minister, make regulations respecting
the security and safety of international bridges and tunnels, including
regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} requiring persons who own or operate international
  bridges or tunnels to develop and implement security plans and
  establish security management systems;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} specifying what must be included in the security
  plans and requiring persons who own or operate international bridges
  or tunnels to make the additions, changes or deletions to their
  security plans that the Minister considers appropriate; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} requiring any person or class of persons to provide
  to the Minister any information related to the security and safety of
  international bridges and tunnels.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergency directions

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** If the Minister is of the
opinion that there is an immediate threat to the security or safety of
any international bridge or tunnel, the Minister may make directions ---
including directions respecting the evacuation of the bridge or tunnel
and the diversion of traffic or persons --- requiring any person to do,
or to refrain from doing, anything that in the opinion of the Minister
it is appropriate to do or refrain from doing in order to respond to the
threat.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized officer may make emergency
direction

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
authorize any officer of the Department of Transport to make, subject to
any restrictions or conditions that the Minister may specify, any
direction that the Minister may make under section 17 whenever the
officer is of the opinion that there is a threat referred to in that
section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A direction comes into
force immediately when it is made but ceases to have force 30 days after
it is made, unless the Minister or the officer who made it repeals it
before the expiry of the 30 days.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Relationship with regulations

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  direction may provide that it applies in lieu of or in addition to any
  regulation made under section 16.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of any inconsistency or conflict between
  a regulation and a direction, the direction prevails to the extent of
  the inconsistency or conflict.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not statutory instrument

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A direction made under
section 17 or 18 is not a statutory instrument within the meaning of the
[Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who contravenes a regulation made under section 16 or a
  direction made under section 17 or 18 is guilty of an offence and is
  liable on summary conviction

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine not
    exceeding \$5,000; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, to a fine not
    exceeding \$100,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the offence is committed or continued on more than
  one day, the person who commits it is liable to be convicted for a
  separate offence for each day on which it is committed or continued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers, etc., of corporations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the offence is committed by a corporation, any
  officer, director or agent or mandatary of the corporation who
  directed, authorized, assented to, acquiesced in or participated in
  the commission of the offence is a party to and guilty of the offence
  and is liable on conviction to the punishment provided for the offence
  whether or not the corporation has been prosecuted or convicted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No offence

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person authorized by the Minister to verify
  compliance with a regulation or a direction referred to in
  subsection (1) does not commit an offence if the person commits any
  act or omission that is required in the course of verifying compliance
  and that would otherwise constitute a contravention of a regulation or
  direction referred to in that subsection.

## [Change of Ownership, Operator or Control]{.HTitleText1} {#h-270948 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall, without the approval of the Governor in Council,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} purchase or otherwise acquire an international
    bridge or tunnel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} operate an international bridge or tunnel; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} acquire control of an entity that owns or operates
    an international bridge or tunnel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of this section and sections 26 and
  28,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person controls a corporation that owns or
    operates an international bridge or tunnel if securities of the
    corporation to which are attached more than 50 per cent of the votes
    that may be cast to elect directors of the corporation are
    beneficially owned by that person and the votes attached to those
    securities are sufficient, if exercised, to elect a majority of the
    directors of the corporation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person controls an unincorporated entity, other
    than a limited partnership, that owns or operates an international
    bridge or tunnel if more than 50 per cent of the ownership
    interests, however designated, into which the entity is divided are
    beneficially owned by that person and the person is able to direct
    the business and affairs of the entity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the general partner of a limited partnership that
    owns or operates an international bridge or tunnel controls the
    limited partnership; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a person controls an entity that owns or operates
    an international bridge or tunnel if the person has any direct or
    indirect influence that, if exercised, would result in control in
    fact of the entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed control

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person is deemed to control an entity referred in
  paragraph 2(a) or (b) if that person and any entities controlled by
  that person beneficially own securities of the entity and that person
  and those entities, if they were considered as one person, would
  control the entity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for approval

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Approval for anything referred to in
  paragraphs 23(1)(a) to (c) may only be obtained by submitting an
  application to the Minister for approval by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, if in the opinion of the Minister
  it is necessary having regard to all the circumstances, consult with
  the other levels of government that have jurisdiction over the place
  where the international bridge or tunnel that is the subject of the
  application is situated and with any person who, in the opinion of the
  Minister, has a direct interest in the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents or information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who submits an application shall provide the
  Minister with any document or information that is required under
  guidelines issued by the Minister and any other document or
  information that is required by the Minister after receipt of the
  application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not statutory instruments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The guidelines referred to in subsection (2) are not
  statutory instruments within the meaning of the [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of Governor in Council

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of the Minister,
  approve an application referred to in section 24 subject to any terms
  and conditions that the Governor in Council considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Variation of terms and conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may vary or rescind the terms
  and conditions or impose new terms and conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with terms and conditions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is subject to terms and conditions
  shall comply with them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial order

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, in accordance with any terms and conditions that the
  Minister considers appropriate, order a person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to sell, assign, transfer or otherwise dispose of
    an international bridge or tunnel if the person purchased or
    otherwise acquired it without the approval of the Governor in
    Council;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to cease operating an international bridge or
    tunnel if the person is operating it without the approval of the
    Governor in Council; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to relinquish control of an entity that owns or
    operates an international bridge or tunnel if the person acquired
    control of the entity without the approval of the Governor in
    Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interim manager

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, if an order is made under
  subsection (1), appoint a person to manage and operate the
  international bridge or tunnel on an interim basis and in accordance
  with any terms and conditions that the Minister may specify.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who contravenes section 23 or subsection 25(3) or fails to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine not
    exceeding \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding
    \$200,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or
    to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the offence is committed or continued on more than
  one day, the person who commits it is liable to be convicted for a
  separate offence for each day on which it is committed or continued.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the offence is committed by a corporation, any
  officer, director or agent or mandatary of the corporation who
  directed, authorized, assented to, acquiesced in or participated in
  the commission of the offence is a party to and guilty of the offence
  and is liable on conviction to the punishment provided for the offence
  whether or not the corporation has been prosecuted or convicted.

## [Incorporation by Letters Patent]{.HTitleText1} {#h-271000 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Letters patent

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of the Minister, issue
  letters patent of incorporation for the establishment of a
  corporation, with or without share capital, for any purpose relating
  to the construction or operation of an international bridge or tunnel.
  Letters patent take effect on the date stated in them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of letters patent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The letters patent may include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the corporate name of the corporation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the objects of the corporation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provisions specifying the activities that may, or
    may not, be carried on by the corporation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the place where the registered office of the
    corporation is located;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} provisions regarding the keeping of, and access to,
    records of the corporation;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number of directors to be appointed and
    provisions governing their appointment, term of office,
    remuneration, indemnification and removal;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} provisions regarding the powers, duties and
    functions of the directors and meetings of the directors;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a code of conduct governing the conduct of the
    directors and officers of the corporation;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} provisions for the holding of meetings of members
    or shareholders of the corporation, including an annual public
    meeting;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} provisions regarding the ownership of, or
    membership in, the corporation;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} provisions regarding the management and control of
    the corporation;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} provisions regarding financial statements and
    corporate finance, including provisions regarding the power of the
    corporation to borrow money on the credit of the corporation and to
    issue debt obligations and provide security for its obligations;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} provisions regarding subsidiaries of the
    corporation, including the management and control of subsidiaries
    and their activities;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} provisions regarding any fundamental changes to the
    corporation, including the amalgamation, continuance, liquidation or
    dissolution of the corporation; and

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} any other provision that the Governor in Council
    considers appropriate to include in the letters patent and that is
    not inconsistent with this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supplementary letters patent

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of
  the Minister, issue supplementary letters patent amending the letters
  patent of a corporation, and the supplementary letters patent take
  effect on the date stated in them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of letters patent

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of
  the Minister, revoke the letters patent or supplementary letters
  patent of a corporation by issuing a notice to that effect. The
  revocation takes effect on the date stated in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not statutory instruments

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Letters patent, supplementary letters patent and a
  notice of revocation are not statutory instruments within the meaning
  of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22), but shall be
  published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) and are
  valid with respect to third parties as of the date of publication.

## [Administrative Monetary Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-271123 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, by
regulation,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} designate as a provision or requirement the
  contravention of which may be proceeded with as a violation in
  accordance with sections 45 to 55

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any provision of this Act or any regulation made
    under this Act,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any order made under section 9, 13, 15.1 or 26, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any directive made under section 17 or 18; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribe the maximum amount payable for each
  violation, but the amount shall not exceed \$5,000, in the case of an
  individual, and \$25,000, in the case of a corporation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notices of violation

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
establish the form and content of notices of violation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violation

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who contravenes a provision or requirement designated under
  paragraph 43(a) commits a violation and is liable to a penalty not
  exceeding the maximum prescribed under paragraph 43(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing violation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A violation that is committed or continued on more
  than one day constitutes a separate violation for each day on which it
  is committed or continued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How contraventions may be proceeded
  with

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If any act or omission can be proceeded with as a
  violation or as an offence, proceedings may be commenced in respect of
  that act or omission as a violation or as an offence, but proceeding
  with it as a violation precludes proceeding with it as an offence, and
  proceeding with it as an offence precludes proceeding with it as a
  violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nature of violation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a violation is not an offence
  and, accordingly, section 126 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)
  does not apply.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of notice of violation

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** If a person designated by
the Minister under subsection 39(1) believes on reasonable grounds that
a person has committed a violation, he or she may issue and serve on the
person a notice of violation that names the person, identifies the
violation and sets out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the penalty for the violation that the person is
  liable to pay; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} particulars concerning the time for paying and the
  manner of paying the penalty.

Definition of [[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 48 to 54, [[Tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
Transportation Appeal Tribunal of Canada established by subsection 2(1)
of the [Transportation Appeal Tribunal of Canada Act](/eng/acts/T-18.5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Option

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** A person who has been
served with a notice of violation must either pay the amount of the
penalty specified in the notice or file with the Tribunal a written
request for a review of the facts of the alleged contravention or of the
amount of the penalty.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of specified amount precludes
further proceedings

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** If a person who is served
with a notice of violation pays the amount specified in the notice in
accordance with the particulars set out in the notice, the Minister
shall accept the amount as and in complete satisfaction of the amount of
the penalty for the contravention by that person of the designated
provision and no further proceedings under this Act shall be taken
against the person in respect of that contravention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for review of determination

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is served with a notice of violation and who wishes to have
  the facts of the alleged contravention or the amount of the penalty
  reviewed shall, on or before the date specified in the notice or
  within any further time that the Tribunal on application may allow,
  file a written request for a review with the Tribunal at the address
  set out in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time and place for review

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a request filed under subsection (1),
  the Tribunal shall appoint a time and place for the review and shall
  notify the Minister and the person who filed the request of the time
  and place in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review procedure

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The member of the Tribunal assigned to conduct the
  review shall provide the Minister and the person who filed the request
  with an opportunity consistent with procedural fairness and natural
  justice to present evidence and make representations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Burden of proof

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The burden of establishing that a person has
  contravened a designated provision is on the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person not compelled to testify

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person who is alleged to have contravened a
  designated provision is not required, and shall not be compelled, to
  give any evidence or testimony in the matter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** If a person fails to pay
the amount of the penalty specified in a notice of violation within the
time specified in the notice and does not file a request for a review,
the person is deemed to have committed the contravention alleged in the
notice, and the Minister may obtain from the Tribunal a certificate in
the form that may be established by the Governor in Council that
indicates the amount of the penalty specified in the notice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination by Tribunal member

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** If, at the conclusion of
a review, the member of the Tribunal who conducts the review determines
that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person has not contravened the designated
  provision that the person is alleged to have contravened, the member
  of the Tribunal shall without delay inform the person and the Minister
  of the determination and, subject to section 53, no further
  proceedings under this Act shall be taken against the person in
  respect of the alleged contravention; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person has contravened the designated provision
  that the person is alleged to have contravened, the member of the
  Tribunal shall without delay inform the person and the Minister of the
  determination and, subject to any regulations made under paragraph
  43(b), of the amount determined by the member of the Tribunal to be
  payable by the person in respect of the contravention and, if the
  amount is not paid to the Tribunal by or on behalf of the person
  within the time that the member of the Tribunal may allow, the member
  of the Tribunal shall issue to the Minister a certificate in the form
  that may be established by the Governor in Council, setting out the
  amount required to be paid by the person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of appeal

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister or a person affected by a determination under section 52 may,
  within 30 days after the determination, appeal it to the Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loss of right of appeal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party that does not appear at a review hearing is
  not entitled to appeal a determination, unless they establish that
  there was sufficient reason to justify their absence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition of appeal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The appeal panel of the Tribunal assigned to hear the
  appeal may dispose of the appeal by dismissing it or allowing it and,
  in allowing the appeal, the panel may substitute its decision for the
  determination appealed against.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the appeal panel finds that a person has
  contravened the designated provision, the panel shall without delay
  inform the person of the finding and, subject to any regulations made
  under paragraph 43(b), of the amount determined by the panel to be
  payable by the person in respect of the contravention and, if the
  amount is not paid to the Tribunal by or on behalf of the person
  within the time allowed by the Tribunal, the Tribunal shall issue to
  the Minister a certificate in the form that may be established by the
  Governor in Council, setting out the amount required to be paid by the
  person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration of certificate

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  time limit for the payment of an amount determined in a notice has
  expired, the time limit for the request for a review has expired, the
  time limit for an appeal has expired, or an appeal has been disposed
  of, on production in any superior court, a certificate issued under
  section 51, paragraph 52(b) or subsection 53(4) shall be registered in
  the court. When it is registered, a certificate has the same force and
  effect, and proceedings may be taken in connection with it, as if it
  were a judgment in that court obtained by Her Majesty in right of
  Canada against the person named in the certificate for a debt of the
  amount set out in the certificate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of costs and charges

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All reasonable costs and charges attend­ant on the
  registration of the certificate are recoverable in like manner as if
  they had been certified and the certificate had been registered under
  subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts received deemed public moneys

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An amount received by the Minister or the Tribunal
  under this section is deemed to be public money within the meaning of
  the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for proceedings

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** Proceedings in respect of

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must cause a report of the results of
  the review to be laid before each House of Parliament on any of the
  first 15 days on which that House is sitting after the report has been
  completed.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-271193 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proposals for construction or alteration

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
this Act applies in respect of any proposal for the construction or
alteration of an international bridge or tunnel that has been submitted
to any department, agency or regulatory authority of the Government of
Canada before the coming into force of this section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing operators of international
bridges and tunnels

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsection 23(1),
a person who operates an international bridge or tunnel immediately
before the day on which this section comes into force is not required to
obtain the approval of the Governor in Council to continue to operate
the bridge or tunnel on and after the day this section comes into force.
