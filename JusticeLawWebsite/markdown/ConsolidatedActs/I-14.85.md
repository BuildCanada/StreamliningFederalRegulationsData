## Intelligence Commissioner Act {#intelligence-commissioner-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2019, c. 13, s. 50
:::

Assented to 2019-06-21

An Act respecting the office of the Intelligence Commissioner

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 50 of chapter 13 of the Statutes of Canada, 2019,
in force July 12, 2019, see SI/2019-67.\]

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170182 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Intelligence Commissioner Act](/eng/acts/I-14.85).

## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170185 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,
[[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Intelligence Commissioner
appointed under subsection 4(1).

## [Designation of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170188 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order in council

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate a federal minister as the Minister responsible
for this Act.

## [Commissioner]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170191 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, is
  to appoint a retired judge of a superior court as the Intelligence
  Commissioner, to hold office during good behaviour for a term of not
  more than five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person appointed to be Commissioner is eligible to
  be reappointed for one additional term of not more than five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Part-time

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner is to perform his or her duties and
  functions on a part-time basis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner is to be paid the remuneration that
  may be fixed by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Travel and living expenses

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner is entitled to be paid, in
  accordance with Treasury Board directives, reasonable travel and
  living expenses incurred in the exercise of his or her powers and the
  performance of his or her duties and functions under this or any other
  Act of Parliament, while absent from his or her ordinary place of
  residence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed employment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner is deemed to be an employee for the
  purposes of the [Government Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5)
  and to be employed in the federal public administration for the
  purposes of any regulations made under section 9 of the [Aeronautics
  Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting Commissioner

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the Commissioner's absence or
  incapacity, or if that office is vacant, the Governor in Council is to
  appoint a retired judge of a superior court to act as Commissioner
  and, while acting as Commissioner, that person has all the
  Commissioner's powers, duties and functions under this or any other
  Act of Parliament.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rank of deputy head

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner has the
rank and all the powers of a deputy head of a department and has control
and management of his or her office and all matters connected with it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personnel

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner has exclusive authority to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint or lay off employees, revoke their
    appointment or terminate their employment; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish standards, procedures and processes
    governing staffing, including governing the appointment of
    employees, lay-off of employees, revocation of their appointment or
    termination of their employment otherwise than for cause.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of employer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in the [Federal Public Sector Labour
  Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-33.3) is to be construed so as to affect
  the Commissioner's right or authority to deal with the matters
  referred to in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Political activities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Part 7 of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) applies to the Commissioner and to his or her
  employees. For the purposes of that Part, the Commissioner is deemed
  to be a deputy head, and his or her employees are deemed to be
  employees, as those terms are defined in subsection 2(1) of that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Commissioner

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** In the exercise of his or
her authority under subsection 6(1), the Commissioner may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine his or her human resources requirements and
  provide for the allocation and effective utilization of human
  resources;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide for the classification of positions and
  employees;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} after consultation with the President of the Treasury
  Board, determine and regulate the pay to which employees are entitled
  for services rendered, the hours of work and leave of the employees
  and any related matters;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} after consultation with the President of the Treasury
  Board, determine and regulate the payments that may be made to
  employees by way of reimbursement for travel or other expenses and by
  way of allowances in respect of expenses and conditions arising out of
  their employment;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} determine employees' learning, training and
  development requirements and fix the terms on which the learning,
  training and development may be carried out;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} provide for the awards that may be made to employees
  for outstanding performance of their duties, for other meritorious
  achievement in relation to their duties or for inventions or practical
  suggestions for improvements;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} establish standards of discipline and set penalties,
  including termination of employment, suspension, demotion to a
  position at a lower maximum rate of pay and financial penalties;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} provide for the termination of employment, or the
  demotion to a position at a lower maximum rate of pay, for reasons
  other than breaches of discipline or misconduct;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} establish policies respecting the exercise of the
  powers granted by this section; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} provide for any other matters, including terms and
  conditions of employment not otherwise specifically provided for in
  this section, that the Commissioner considers necessary for effective
  human resources management.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Negotiation of collective agreements

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Before entering into
collective bargaining with the bargaining agent for a bargaining unit
composed of his or her employees, the Commissioner must have his or her
negotiating mandate approved by the President of the Treasury Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Technical assistance

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner may engage
on a temporary basis the services of persons having technical or
specialized knowledge to advise and assist the Commissioner in the
exercise of his or her powers and the performance of his or her duties
and functions under this or any other Act of Parliament and, with the
approval of Treasury Board, may fix and pay the remuneration and
expenses of those persons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oath or solemn affirmation

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner must, before commencing the duties and functions of
  office, take the following oath or make the following solemn
  affirmation:

  I, ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-dot}, swear (solemnly
  affirm) that I will, to the best of my ability, discharge my
  responsibilities as the Intelligence Commissioner and will not
  communicate or use without due authority any information obtained in
  confidence by me in that capacity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security clearance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every employee of the Commissioner and every person
  whose services are engaged under section 9 must maintain the security
  clearance required by the Government of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security requirements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner and every person referred to in
  subsection (2) must follow established procedures or practices,
  including any requirement found in a Treasury Board policy, guideline
  or directive, for the secure handling, storage, transportation and
  transmission of information or documents.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure limited

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner, a
former Commissioner, any current or former employee and any person whose
services are or were engaged under section 9 may disclose information
that they obtained, or to which they had access, in the course of
exercising their powers or performing their duties and functions under
this or any other Act of Parliament only for the purpose of exercising
those powers or performing those duties and functions or as required by
any other law.

## [Duties and Functions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170243 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review and approval

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner is
responsible, as set out in sections 13 to 20, for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} reviewing the conclusions on the basis of which
  certain authorizations are issued or amended, and certain
  determinations are made, under the [Communications Security
  Establishment Act](/eng/acts/C-35.3) and the [Canadian Security
  Intelligence Service Act](/eng/acts/C-23); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if those conclusions are reasonable, approving those
  authorizations, amendments and determinations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foreign Intelligence Authorization

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner must
review whether the conclusions --- made under subsections 34(1) and (2)
of the [Communications Security Establishment Act](/eng/acts/C-35.3) and
on the basis of which a Foreign Intelligence Authorization was issued
under subsection 26(1) of that Act --- are reasonable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cybersecurity Authorization

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner must
review whether the conclusions --- made under subsections 34(1) and (3)
of the [Communications Security Establishment Act](/eng/acts/C-35.3) and
on the basis of which a Cybersecurity Authorization was issued under
subsection 27(1) or (2) of that Act --- are reasonable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amended Authorization

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner must
review whether the conclusions --- made under paragraph 39(2)(a) of the
[Communications Security Establishment Act](/eng/acts/C-35.3) and on the
basis of which an authorization referred to in section 13 is amended, or
made under paragraph 39(2)(b) of that Act and on the basis of which an
authorization referred to in section 14 is amended --- are reasonable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classes of Canadian datasets

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner must
review whether the conclusions --- made under subsection 11.03(2) of the
[Canadian Security Intelligence Service Act](/eng/acts/C-23) and on the
basis of which a class of Canadian datasets is determined under
subsection 11.03(1) of that Act --- are reasonable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of foreign datasets

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner must
review whether the conclusions --- made under subsection 11.17(1) of the
[Canadian Security Intelligence Service Act](/eng/acts/C-23) and on the
basis of which the retention of a foreign dataset was authorized --- are
reasonable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Query or exploitation of dataset in
exigent circumstances

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner must
review whether the conclusions --- made under subsection 11.22(1) of the
[Canadian Security Intelligence Service Act](/eng/acts/C-23) and on the
basis of which a query or exploitation of a dataset in exigent
circumstances was authorized --- are reasonable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classes of acts or omissions

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner must
review whether the conclusions --- made under subsection 20.1(3) of the
[Canadian Security Intelligence Service Act](/eng/acts/C-23) and on the
basis of which a class of acts or omissions is determined --- are
reasonable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissioner's decision

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  conducting a review under any of sections 13 to 16, 18 and 19, the
  Commissioner, in a written decision,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} must approve the authorization, amendment or
    determination if he or she is satisfied that the conclusions at
    issue are reasonable, and must set out his or her reasons for doing
    so; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} must not approve the authorization, amendment or
    determination if he or she is not so satisfied, and must set out his
    or her reasons for doing so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foreign datasets

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} After conducting a review under section 17, the
  Commissioner, in a written decision,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} must approve the authorization if he or she is
    satisfied that the conclusions at issue are reasonable, and must set
    out his or her reasons for doing so;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} must approve the authorization with conditions ---
    respecting the querying or exploitation of the foreign dataset or
    the retention or destruction of the dataset or of a portion of it
    --- and provide reasons for doing so, if he or she is satisfied that
    those conclusions are reasonable once the conditions are attached;
    or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} must not approve the authorization in any other
    case and must set out his or her reasons for doing so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner must provide the decision to the
  person whose conclusions are being reviewed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as soon as feasible, in the case of an
    authorization referred to in section 18; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 30 days after the day on which the
    Commissioner receives notice of the authorization, amendment or
    determination, or within any other period that may be agreed on by
    the Commissioner and the person, in any other case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a decision of the Commissioner
  is not a statutory instrument within the meaning of the [Statutory
  Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision provided to Review Agency

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner must
provide a copy of every decision made by him or her under section 20 to
the National Security and Intelligence Review Agency for the purpose of
assisting that Agency in fulfilling its mandate under paragraphs 8(1)(a)
to (c) of the [National Security and Intelligence Review Agency
Act](/eng/acts/N-16.62).

## [Public Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170280 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Prime Minister

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner must, each calendar year, submit to the Prime Minister a
  report with respect to the Commissioner's activities during the
  previous calendar year. The report must include statistics, that the
  Commissioner considers appropriate, relating to the authorizations,
  amendments and determinations that were approved and not approved.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection of confidential information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner must consult with the Director of
  the Canadian Security Intelligence Service and the Chief of the
  Communications Security Establishment in preparing the report in order
  to ensure that it does not contain information the disclosure of which
  would be injurious to national security, national defence or
  international relations or information that is subject to
  solicitor-client privilege or the professional secrecy of advocates
  and notaries or to litigation privilege.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Prime Minister must cause to be laid before each
  House of Parliament, on any of the first 15 days on which that House
  is sitting after a report is submitted under subsection (1), a copy of
  the report.

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1170288 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information to Commissioner

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite any other Act of Parliament
  and subject to section 26, the person whose conclusions are being
  reviewed by the Commissioner under any of sections 13 to 19 must, for
  the purposes of the Commissioner's review, provide the Commissioner
  with all information that was before the person in issuing or amending
  the authorization or making the determination at issue, including
  information that is subject to any privilege under the law of
  evidence, solicitor-client privilege or the professional secrecy of
  advocates and notaries or to litigation privilege.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No waiver

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the disclosure to the
  Commissioner under this section of any information that is subject to
  solicitor-client privilege or the professional secrecy of advocates
  and notaries or to litigation privilege does not constitute a waiver
  of those privileges or that secrecy.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entitlement to reports

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner is
entitled to receive a copy of the following reports, or of a part of the
reports, if the report or part in question relates to the Commissioner's
powers, duties or functions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a special report submitted by the National Security
  and Intelligence Committee of Parliamentarians under subsection 21(2)
  of the [National Security and Intelligence Committee of
  Parliamentarians Act](/eng/acts/N-16.6); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a report submitted by the National Security and
  Intelligence Review Agency under subsection 32(1) or 33(1) or section
  34 or 35 of the [National Security and Intelligence Review Agency
  Act](/eng/acts/N-16.62).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of information to Commissioner

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Despite any other Act of
Parliament and any privilege under the law of evidence and subject to
section 26, the following persons or bodies may --- for the purpose of
assisting the Commissioner in the exercise of his or her powers and the
performance of his or her duties and functions --- disclose to the
Commissioner any information that is not directly related to a specific
review under any of sections 13 to 19:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
  Preparedness;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
  section 2 of the [Communications Security Establishment
  Act](/eng/acts/C-35.3);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Security Intelligence Service; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Communications Security Establishment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No entitlement

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner is not
entitled to have access to information that is a confidence of the
Queen's Privy Council for Canada the disclosure of which could be
refused under section 39 of the [Canada Evidence Act](/eng/acts/C-5).

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 51

  - ###### Definitions {#definitions .MarginalNote}

    [51]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in sections 52 to
    59.

    [[former Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[former Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
        Commissioner of the Communications Security Establishment
        appointed under subsection 273.63(1) of the [National Defence
        Act](/eng/acts/N-5), as it read immediately before the coming
        into force of section 68. ([ancien commissaire]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

    [[new Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[new Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Intelligence
        Commissioner under the [Intelligence Commissioner
        Act](/eng/acts/I-14.85). ([nouveau commissaire]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 52

  - ###### Former Commissioner {#former-commissioner .MarginalNote}

    [52]{.lawlabel} The person occupying the position of the former
    Commissioner immediately before the day on which this section comes
    into force is, on that day and for the remainder of the term for
    which he or she was appointed to that position, to become the new
    Commissioner as if he or she had been appointed under subsection
    4(1) of the [Intelligence Commissioner Act](/eng/acts/I-14.85).

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 53

  - ###### Status continued {#status-continued .MarginalNote}

    - [53]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act is to be
      construed as affecting the status of a person who, immediately
      before the day on which this section comes into force, occupied a
      position with the office of the former Commissioner, except that
      the person is to occupy their position in the office of the new
      Commissioner on that day.

    - ###### For greater certainty {#for-greater-certainty .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a person's status includes
      whether or not they occupy a managerial or confidential position.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 54

  - ###### Powers, duties and functions {#powers-duties-and-functions .MarginalNote}

    [54]{.lawlabel} A person referred to in section 53 who was
    authorized, immediately before the day on which that section comes
    into force, by the former Commissioner to exercise certain powers or
    perform certain duties and functions retains the authority to
    exercise those powers and perform those duties and functions in the
    office of the new Commissioner.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 55

  - ###### Appropriations {#appropriations .MarginalNote}

    [55]{.lawlabel} Any money that is appropriated by an Act of
    Parliament, for the fiscal year in which this section comes into
    force, to defray any expenditure of the office of the former
    Commissioner and that, on the day on which this section comes into
    force, is unexpended is deemed, on that day, to be an amount
    appropriated to defray any expenditure of the office of the new
    Commissioner.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 56

  - ###### Rights, property and obligations {#rights-property-and-obligations .MarginalNote}

    [56]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 15, all rights and property of
    the office of the former Commissioner and of Her Majesty in right of
    Canada that are under the administration and control of the office
    of the former Commissioner and all obligations of the office of the
    former Commissioner are transferred to the office of the new
    Commissioner.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 57

  - ###### Contracts {#contracts .MarginalNote}

    - [57]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} A contract respecting the
      provision of materiel or services to the office of the former
      Commissioner that was entered into by that Commissioner is deemed
      to have been entered into by the new Commissioner.

    - ###### References {#references .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context otherwise requires, every
      reference to the former Commissioner in a contract referred to in
      subsection (1) is to be read as a reference to the new
      Commissioner.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 58

  - ###### Commencement of proceedings {#commencement-of-proceedings .MarginalNote}

    - [58]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal
      or administrative proceeding in respect of an obligation or
      liability incurred by the former Commissioner, other than one
      relating to a complaint or a review before him or her, may be
      brought against the new Commissioner in any court that would have
      had jurisdiction if the action, suit or other legal proceeding had
      been brought against the former Commissioner.

    - ###### Pending proceedings {#pending-proceedings .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any action, suit or other legal or administrative
      proceeding to which the former Commissioner is a party, other than
      one relating to a complaint or a review before him or her, and
      that is pending immediately before the day on which this section
      comes into force may be continued by or against the new
      Commissioner in the same manner and to the same extent as it could
      have been continued by or against the former Commissioner.

- --- 2019, c. 13, s. 59

  - ###### No compensation {#no-compensation .MarginalNote}

    [59]{.lawlabel} Despite the provisions of any contract, agreement or
    order, the person appointed to hold office as the former
    Commissioner has no right to claim or receive any compensation,
    damages, indemnity or other form of relief from Her Majesty in right
    of Canada or from any employee or agent of Her Majesty for ceasing
    to hold that office or for the abolition of that office by the
    operation of section 68.

- --- 2019, c. 13, par. 82(1)(g)

  - ###### References {#references-1 .MarginalNote}

    - [82]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} A reference to the former
      department in any of the following is deemed to be a reference to
      the new department:

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the [Intelligence Commissioner
        Act](/eng/acts/I-14.85).
