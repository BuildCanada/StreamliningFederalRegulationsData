## High Risk Child Sex Offender Database Act {#high-risk-child-sex-offender-database-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2015, c. 23, s. 29
:::

Assented to 2015-06-18

An Act respecting the establishment of a publicly accessible database
containing information with respect to persons who are found guilty of
sexual offences against children and who pose a high risk of committing
crimes of a sexual nature

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 29 of chapter 23 of the Statutes of Canada, 2015,
in force December 31, 2024, see SI/2024-64.\]

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-254115 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [High Risk Child Sex Offender Database Act](/eng/acts/H-3.76).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-254118 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Act.

  [[sexual offence against a child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[sexual offence against a child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a [[designated offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
        as defined in subsection 490.011(1) of the [Criminal
        Code](/eng/acts/C-46), that is committed against a person who is
        under 18 years of age, other than a [[secondary
        offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in that subsection,
        for which the prosecutor has not, for the purposes of subsection
        490.012(5) of that Act, established beyond a reasonable doubt
        that the offender committed the offence with the intent to
        commit a [[primary offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined
        in that subsection 490.011(1); or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an offence that is committed outside Canada
        against a person who is under 18 years of age and as a result of
        which the offender is or was required to comply with the [Sex
        Offender Information Registration
        Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7). ([infraction sexuelle visant un
        enfant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commissioner of the
      Royal Canadian Mounted Police. ([commissaire]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- crime of a sexual
  nature

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, a crime is of a sexual
  nature if it consists of one or more acts that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are either sexual in nature or committed with the
    intent to commit an act or acts that are sexual in nature; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} constitute an offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- young person

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, a reference to a person
  who is found guilty of a sexual offence against a child does not
  include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a young person as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
    [Youth Criminal Justice Act](/eng/acts/Y-1.5) who has been found
    guilty of a sexual offence against a child unless they are given an
    adult sentence as defined in that subsection for the offence; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a young person as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
    [Young Offenders Act](/eng/acts/Y-1), chapter Y-1 of the Revised
    Statutes of Canada, 1985, who has been found guilty of a sexual
    offence against a child unless they are found guilty of the offence
    in ordinary court as defined in that subsection.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-254134 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to enhance public safety by establishing a publicly accessible database
that contains information with respect to persons who are found guilty
of sexual offences against children and who pose a high risk of
committing crimes of a sexual nature.

## [Database]{.HTitleText1} {#h-254137 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner must establish and administer a publicly accessible
  database that contains information with respect to persons who are
  found guilty of sexual offences against children and who pose a high
  risk of committing crimes of a sexual nature.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissioner's duties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner's duties under this Act may be
  performed on behalf of the Commissioner by any person who is
  authorized by the Commissioner to perform those duties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of database

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The database must contain
only information, with respect to any person referred to in subsection
4(1), that a police service or other public authority has previously
made accessible to the public, including:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} their given name and surname, and any alias that they
  use;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} their date of birth;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} their gender;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} their physical description, including any physical
  distinguishing mark that they have;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a photograph of them;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of offences they have committed;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} any condition to which they are subject; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the name of the city, town, municipality or other
  organized district in which they reside.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior notification

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner must,
before including the given name and surname of a person referred to in
subsection 4(1) in the database, take reasonable steps to notify that
person that information with respect to them is to be included in the
database and allow that person to make representations concerning the
matter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal of information

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  receipt of an application in writing by a person referred to in
  subsection 4(1), the Commissioner must decide whether there are
  reasonable grounds to remove information with respect to that person
  from the database.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Commissioner does not make a decision on the
  application within the period prescribed by regulation, the
  Commissioner is deemed to have decided not to remove the information
  in question.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision to applicant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner must give notice without delay to
  the applicant of any decision made or deemed to have been made
  respecting the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial review

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Within 60 days after receipt of the notice of the
  decision, the applicant may apply to the Federal Court for judicial
  review of the decision.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regular review of information

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Within the period
prescribed by regulation after the establishment of the database, and at
prescribed intervals after that, the Commissioner must review the
information in the database to determine whether the information should
still be maintained in the database. The review does not affect the
validity of the database.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-derogation

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act affects
any authority of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police under another Act of
Parliament or an Act of a provincial legislature or at common law to
disclose information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** No criminal or civil
proceedings lie against the Commissioner, or against any person acting
on behalf or under the direction of the Commissioner, for anything done
or omitted to be done in good faith under this Act.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-254170 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Governor in Council

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} establishing the criteria for determining whether a
  person who is found guilty of a sexual offence against a child poses a
  high risk of committing a crime of a sexual nature; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that, by this Act, is to be
  prescribed.
