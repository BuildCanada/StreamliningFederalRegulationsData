## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-81604 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 108, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-81609 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[certificate of citizenship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[certificate of citizenship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      certificate of citizenship issued or granted under this Act or
      under the former Act; ([certificat de
      citoyenneté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[certificate of naturalization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[certificate of naturalization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      certificate of naturalization granted under any Act that was in
      force in Canada at any time before January 1, 1947; ([certificat
      de naturalisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[certificate of renunciation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[certificate of renunciation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, unless
      a contrary intention appears, a certificate of renunciation issued
      under this Act; ([certificat de répudiation]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a child adopted or
      legitimized in accordance with the laws of the place where the
      adoption or legitimation took place; ([enfant]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[citizen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[citizen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canadian
      citizen; ([citoyen]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[citizenship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[citizenship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Canadian
      citizenship; ([citoyenneté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[citizenship judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[citizenship judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a citizenship
      judge appointed under section 26; ([juge de la
      citoyenneté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an
      individual, means a person who is cohabiting with the individual
      in a conjugal relationship, having so cohabited for a period of at
      least one year; ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Federal
      Court; ([Cour]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 1992, c. 21, s.
      6\]]{.Repealed}

  [[former Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[former Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Citizenship
      Act, chapter C-19 of the Revised Statutes of Canada,
      1970; ([ancienne loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
      Privy Council for Canada as is designated by the Governor in
      Council as the Minister for the purposes of this
      Act; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[minor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[minor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who has not attained
      the age of eighteen years; ([mineur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[prior legislation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prior legislation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any Act respecting
      naturalization or citizenship that was in force in Canada at any
      time before February 15, 1977. ([législation
      antérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person is deemed to be born in Canada if the
    person is born on a Canadian vessel as defined in section 2 of the
    [Canada Shipping Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15), or on an aircraft
    registered in Canada under the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2) and
    regulations made under that Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who is lawfully present and entitled to
    permanently reside in Canada is deemed to have been lawfully
    admitted to Canada for permanent residence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person against whom a removal order has been made
    remains under that order

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} unless all rights of review by or appeal to the
      Immigration Appeal Division of the Immigration and Refugee Board,
      the Federal Court of Appeal and the Supreme Court of Canada have
      been exhausted with respect to the order and the final result of
      those reviews or appeals is that the order has no force or effect,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} until the order has been executed.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[The Right to Citizenship]{.HTitleText1} {#h-81636 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons who are citizens

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this Act, a person is a citizen if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person was born in Canada after February 14,
    1977;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person was born outside Canada after February
    14, 1977 and at the time of his birth one of his parents, other than
    a parent who adopted him, was a citizen;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person has been granted or acquired citizenship
    pursuant to section 5 or 11 and, in the case of a person who is
    fourteen years of age or over on the day that he is granted
    citizenship, he has taken the oath of citizenship;

  - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} the person has been granted citizenship under
    section 5.1;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person was a citizen immediately before
    February 15, 1977;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the person was entitled, immediately before
    February 15, 1977, to become a citizen under paragraph 5(1)(b) of
    the former Act;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} before the coming into force of this paragraph, the
    person ceased to be a citizen for any reason other than the
    following reasons and did not subsequently become a citizen:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the person renounced his or her citizenship under
      any of the following provisions:

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 19(2)(c) of the Canadian Citizenship
        Act, S.C. 1946, c. 15, as enacted by S.C. 1951, c. 12, s. 1(3),

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 19(2)(c) of the Canadian Citizenship
        Act, R.S.C. 1952, c. 33,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} subparagraph 19(1)(b)(iii) of the Canadian
        Citizenship Act, R.S.C. 1952, c. 33, as enacted by S.C.
        1967-68, c. 4, s. 5,

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} subparagraph 18(1)(b)(iii) of the former Act,

      - [(E)]{.lawlabel} section 8 of the [Citizenship
        Act](/eng/acts/C-29), S.C. 1974-75-76, c. 108, or

      - [(F)]{.lawlabel} section 9 of this Act,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the person's citizenship was revoked for false
      representation, fraud or concealment of material circumstances
      under any of the following provisions:

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 21(1)(b) of the Canadian Citizenship
        Act, S.C. 1946, c. 15,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 19(1)(b) of the Canadian Citizenship
        Act, S.C. 1946, c. 15, as enacted by S.C. 1950, c. 29, s. 8,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 19(1)(b) of the Canadian Citizenship
        Act, R.S.C. 1952, c. 33, as it read before the coming into force
        of An Act to amend the Canadian Citizenship Act, S.C.
        1967-68, c. 4,

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 19(1)(a) of the Canadian Citizenship
        Act, R.S.C. 1952, c. 33, as enacted by S.C. 1967-68, c. 4, s. 5,

      - [(E)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 18(1)(a) of the former Act,

      - [(F)]{.lawlabel} section 9 of the [Citizenship
        Act](/eng/acts/C-29), S.C. 1974-75-76, c. 108, or

      - [(G)]{.lawlabel} section 10 of this Act, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the person failed to make an application to
      retain his or her citizenship under section 8 as it read before
      the coming into force of this paragraph or did make such an
      application that subsequently was not approved;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the person was born outside Canada before February
    15, 1977 to a parent who was a citizen at the time of the birth and
    the person did not, before the coming into force of this paragraph,
    become a citizen;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the person was granted citizenship under section 5,
    as it read before the coming into force of this paragraph, the
    person would have, but for that grant, been a citizen under
    paragraph (g) and, if it was required, he or she took the oath of
    citizenship;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the person had been a citizen other than by way of
    grant, ceased to be a citizen for a reason other than the reasons
    referred to in subparagraphs (f)(i) to (iii), was subsequently
    granted citizenship before the coming into force of this paragraph
    under any of the following provisions and, if it was required, he or
    she took the oath of citizenship:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} subsection 10(1) of the [Citizenship
      Act](/eng/acts/C-29), S.C. 1974-75-76, c. 108,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} subsection 5(1) or (4) or 11(1) of this Act, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 5(2)(a) of this Act, as it read
      before the coming into force of this paragraph;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} under prior legislation, the person had been a
    citizen other than by way of grant, ceased to be a citizen for a
    reason other than the reasons referred to in subparagraphs (f)(i)
    and (ii) and resumed citizenship;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the person, before January 1, 1947, was born or
    naturalized in Canada but ceased to be a British subject, and did
    not become a citizen on that day;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the person, before April 1, 1949, was born or
    naturalized in Newfoundland and Labrador but ceased to be a British
    subject, and did not become a citizen on or before that day;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} the person, on January 1, 1947, was a British
    subject neither born nor naturalized in Canada and was ordinarily
    resident in Canada, and did not become a citizen on that day;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} the person, on April 1, 1949, was a British subject
    neither born nor naturalized in Newfoundland and Labrador and was
    ordinarily resident there, and did not become a citizen on or before
    that day;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} the person was born outside Canada and Newfoundland
    and Labrador before January 1, 1947 to a parent who is a citizen
    under paragraph (k) or (m), and the person did not become a citizen
    on that day;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} the person was born outside Canada and Newfoundland
    and Labrador before April 1, 1949 to a parent who is a citizen under
    paragraph (l) or (n), and the person did not become a citizen on or
    before that day;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} the person was born outside Canada and Newfoundland
    and Labrador before January 1, 1947 to a parent who became a citizen
    on that day under the Canadian Citizenship Act, S.C. 1946, c. 15,
    and the person did not become a citizen on that day; or

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} the person was born outside Canada and Newfoundland
    and Labrador before April 1, 1949 to a parent who became a citizen
    on that day under section 44A of the Canadian Citizenship Act, S.C.
    1946, c. 15, as enacted by S.C. 1949, c. 6, and the person did not
    become a citizen on or before that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  with Canada.

  (1)(b), (g) or (h) if that parent, but for his or her death, would
  have been a citizen under paragraph (1)(f), (i) or (j).

  the coming into force of this subsection, his or her parent ---
  referred to in one of those paragraphs --- is deceased, is a citizen
  under that paragraph if that parent, but for his or her death, would
  have been a citizen under any of paragraphs (1)(k) to (n).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Citizen despite death of parent

  [(1.3)]{.lawlabel} A person who would not become a citizen under
  paragraph (1)(q) for the sole reason that his or her parent died
  before January 1, 1947 and did not become a citizen on that day under
  the Canadian Citizenship Act, S.C. 1946, c. 15, is, nonetheless, a
  citizen under that paragraph if his or her parent would have been a
  citizen if that Act had come into force immediately before their death
  and the date referred to in the provisions of that Act that set out
  the requirements to be met to become a citizen had been the day of
  that coming into force rather than January 1, 1947.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Citizen despite death of parent

  [(1.4)]{.lawlabel} A person who would not become a citizen under
  paragraph (1)(r) for the sole reason that his or her parent died
  before April 1, 1949 and did not become a citizen on that day under

  enacted by S.C. 1949, c. 6, is, nonetheless, a citizen under that
  paragraph if his or her parent would have been a citizen if that Act
  had come into force immediately before their death and the date
  referred to in the provisions of that Act that set out the
  requirements to be met to become a citizen had been the day of that
  coming into force rather than April 1, 1949.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grant of citizenship

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall grant citizenship to any person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} makes application for citizenship;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2017, c. 14, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is a permanent resident within the meaning of
    subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), has, subject to the regulations, no
    unfulfilled conditions under that Act relating to his or her status
    as a permanent resident and has

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} been physically present in Canada for at least
      1,095 days during the five years immediately before the date of
      his or her application, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2017, c. 14, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} met any applicable requirement under the
      [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) to file a return of income in
      respect of three taxation years that are fully or partially within
      the five years immediately before the date of his or her
      application;

  - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2017, c. 14, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if 18 years of age or more but less than 55 years
    of age at the date of his or her application, has an adequate
    knowledge of one of the official languages of Canada;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if 18 years of age or more but less than 55 years
    of age at the date of his or her application, demonstrates in one of
    the official languages of Canada that he or she has an adequate
    knowledge of Canada and of the responsibilities and privileges of
    citizenship; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} is not under a removal order and is not the subject
    of a declaration by the Governor in Council made pursuant to section
    20.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Length of physical presence ---
  calculation

  [(1.001)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subparagraph (1)(c)(i), the
  length of physical presence is calculated in the following manner:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for every day during which the person was
    physically present in Canada as a temporary resident or protected
    person under the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) before becoming a permanent resident, the
    person accumulates half of a day of physical presence, up to a
    maximum of 365 days; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for every day during which the person has been
    physically present in Canada since becoming a permanent resident,
    the person accumulates one day of physical presence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of physical presence --- spouse
  or common-law partner of citizen

  [(1.01)]{.lawlabel} Any day during which an applicant for citizenship
  resided with the applicant's spouse or common-law partner who at the
  time was a Canadian citizen and was employed outside Canada in or with
  the Canadian Armed Forces, the federal public administration or the
  public service of a province, otherwise than as a locally engaged
  person, shall be treated as equivalent to one day of physical presence
  in Canada for the purposes of paragraphs (1)(c) and 11(1)(d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of physical presence ---
  permanent residents

  [(1.02)]{.lawlabel} Any day during which an applicant for citizenship
  was a permanent resident within the meaning of subsection 2(1) of the
  [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) and was
  employed outside Canada in or with the Canadian Armed Forces, the
  federal public administration or the public service of a province,
  otherwise than as a locally engaged person, shall be treated as
  equivalent to one day of physical presence in Canada for the purposes
  of paragraphs (1)(c) and 11(1)(d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of physical presence ---
  residing with permanent residents

  [(1.03)]{.lawlabel} Any day during which an applicant for citizenship
  was the spouse or common-law partner, or child, of a permanent
  resident referred to in subsection (1.02) and resided with that
  permanent resident shall be treated as equivalent to one day of
  physical presence in Canada for the purposes of paragraphs (1)(c) and
  11(1)(d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application --- minor

  [(1.04)]{.lawlabel} When the application referred to in paragraph
  (1)(a) is in respect of a minor, it must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} made by a person who has custody of the minor or
    who is empowered to act on their behalf by virtue of a court order
    or written agreement or by operation of law, unless otherwise
    ordered by a court; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} countersigned by the minor, if the minor has
    attained the age of 14 years on or before the day on which the

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application made by minor

  [(1.05)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister waives the requirement set out in
  paragraph (1.04)(a) under subparagraph (3)(b)(v), the application

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this section and sections 19.1, 19.2
  and 20.

  [[Review Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Review Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the National Security
      and Intelligence Review Agency. ([Office de
      surveillance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[threats to the security of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[threats to the security of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the
      same meaning as in section 2 of the [Canadian Security
      Intelligence Service Act](/eng/acts/C-23). ([menaces envers la
      sécurité du Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Review Agency

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may make a report to the Review Agency
  if the Minister is of the opinion that a person should not be granted
  citizenship under section 5 or subsection 11(1) or administered the
  oath of citizenship or be issued a certificate of renunciation under
  section 9 because there are reasonable grounds to believe that the
  person has engaged, is engaging or may engage in activity

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that constitutes a threat to the security of
    Canada; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is part of a pattern of criminal activity
    planned and organized by a number of persons acting in concert in
    furtherance of the commission of any offence that may be punishable
    under any Act of Parliament by way of indictment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to be sent to person affected

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, within ten days after a report is
  made pursuant to subsection (2), cause a notice to be sent informing
  the person referred to in that subsection of the report and stating
  that following an investigation in relation thereto, a declaration
  with respect to that person may be made by the Governor in Council
  under section 20.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of the [National Security
  and Intelligence Review Agency Act](/eng/acts/N-16.62)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a report is made to the Review Agency pursuant
  to subsection (2), the Review Agency shall investigate the grounds on
  which it is based and for that purpose sections 10 to 12, 20, 25 to 28
  and 30 of the [National Security and Intelligence Review Agency
  Act](/eng/acts/N-16.62) apply, with any necessary modifications, to
  the investigation as if the investigation were conducted in relation
  to a complaint made under subsection 18(3) of that Act, except that a
  reference in any of those provisions to "deputy head" shall be read as
  a reference to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation to cease

  [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} If the Review Agency is of the opinion that it
  cannot perform its duties described in subsections (4), (5) and (6),
  it must cease its investigation and give notice to the Minister and
  the person referred to in subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement to be sent to person affected

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Review Agency shall, as soon as practicable after
  a report is made to it pursuant to subsection (2), send to the person
  with respect to whom the report is made a statement summarizing such
  information available to it as will enable the person to be as fully
  informed as possible of the circumstances giving rise to the report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Review Agency shall, on completion of an
  investigation made pursuant to subsection (4), make a report to the
  Governor in Council on all matters relating thereto and shall, at the
  same time as or after the report is made, provide the complainant with
  the conclusions of the report.

## [PART V.1]{.HLabel1}[Judicial Review]{.HTitleText1} {#h-82217 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for judicial review only with
leave

- **[[22.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for judicial review with respect to any matter under this
  Act may be made only with leave of the Court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for leave

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following provisions govern an application for
  leave:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application must be filed in the Registry of
    the Court and served on the other party within 30 days after the day
    on which the applicant is notified of or otherwise becomes aware of
    the matter;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a judge of the Court may, for special reasons,
    allow an extended time for filing and serving the application;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a judge of the Court shall dispose of the
    application without delay and in a summary way and, unless a judge
    of the Court directs otherwise, without personal appearance; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} no appeal lies from the decision of the Court with
    respect to the application or with respect to an interlocutory
    decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application by Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may make an application in respect of a
  decision of a citizenship judge.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 22, s. 20
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial review

**[[22.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.2 .sectionLabel}** The following
provisions govern the judicial review:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the judge who grants leave shall fix the day and
  place for a hearing;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the hearing shall be held no later than 90 days after
  the day on which leave is granted and, unless the parties agree
  otherwise, no earlier than 30 days after that day;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the judge shall dispose of the application without
  delay and in a summary way; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an appeal to the Federal Court of Appeal may be made
  only if, in rendering judgment, the judge certifies that a serious
  question of general importance is involved and states the question.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 22, s. 20
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules

**[[22.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22.3 .sectionLabel}** With the approval of
the Governor in Council, the rules committee established under section
45.1 of the [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7) may make rules governing
the practice and procedure in relation to applications for leave to
commence an application for judicial review, applications for judicial
review and appeals. The rules are binding despite any rule or practice
that would otherwise apply.

## [PART VII]{.HLabel1}[Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-82353 .Part}

Definition of [[document of citizenship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of this section, [[document of
  citizenship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a certificate of citizenship,
  a document provided under paragraph 12(1)(b) or (2)(b), a certificate
  of naturalization or a certificate of renunciation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indictable offences and punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person is guilty of an indictable offence and
  liable to imprisonment for a term of not more than five years if that
  person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtains or uses a document of citizenship of
    another person to personate that other person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} knowingly permits a document of citizenship
    relating to him or her to be used by another person to personate him
    or her; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is in possession of a document of citizenship that
    he or she knows has been unlawfully issued or altered, or
    counterfeited.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences and punishment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person is guilty of an indictable offence and
  liable to imprisonment for a term of not more than 14 years if that
  person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} without lawful authority issues, provides or alters
    a document of citizenship;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} counterfeits a document of citizenship;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} uses, acts on or causes or attempts to cause any
    person to use or act on a document of citizenship, knowing it to
    have been unlawfully issued, provided or altered or to have been
    counterfeited; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} traffics in documents of citizenship or has such
    documents in his or her possession for the purpose of trafficking.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person who contravenes any provision of this Act or
  the regulations for the contravention of which no fine or imprisonment
  is provided in this Act is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
  conviction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Counselling misrepresentation

  induce, aid or abet any person to directly or indirectly misrepresent
  or withhold material circumstances relating to a relevant matter,

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person commits an offence who knowingly

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for any of the purposes of this Act, directly or

    conceals any material circumstances;

## [PART VIII]{.HLabel1}[Status of Persons in Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-82403 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Citizen of the Commonwealth

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who, under an enactment of a Commonwealth country other than
  Canada, is a citizen or national of that country has in Canada the
  status of a citizen of the Commonwealth.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}British subject

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of any law in force in Canada on and
  after February 15, 1977 that refers to the status of British subject,
  the status so described shall refer to the status of Canadian citizen
  or citizen of the Commonwealth or both as the intent of that law may
  require.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 108, s. 31
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Citizen of Ireland

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** Any law of Canada and any
regulation made thereunder shall, unless it otherwise provides, have
effect in relation to a citizen of Ireland who is not a citizen of the
Commonwealth in like manner as it has in relation to a citizen of the
Commonwealth.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 108, s. 32
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 35,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} real and personal property of every description may
  be taken, acquired, held and disposed of by a person who is not a
  citizen in the same manner in all respects as by a citizen; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a title to real and personal property of every
  description may be derived through, from or in succession to a person
  who is not a citizen in the same manner in all respects as though
  through, from or in succession to a citizen.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 108, s. 33
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to prohibit or restrict
acquisitions of property in a province by non-Canadians

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the
  Lieutenant Governor in Council of a province or such other person or
  authority in the province as is designated by the Lieutenant Governor
  in Council thereof is authorized to prohibit, annul or in any manner
  restrict the taking or acquisition directly or indirectly of, or the
  succession to, any interest in real property located in the province
  by persons who are not citizens or by corporations or associations
  that are effectively controlled by persons who are not citizens.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Lieutenant Governor in Council of a province may
  make regulations applicable in the province for the purposes of
  determining

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} what transactions constitute a direct or an
    indirect taking or acquisition of any interest in real property
    located in the province;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} what constitutes effective control of a corporation
    or association by persons who are not citizens; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} what constitutes an association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) do not operate so as to
  authorize or permit the Lieutenant Governor in Council of a province,
  or such other person or authority as is designated by the Lieutenant
  Governor in Council thereof, to make any decision or take any action
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prohibits, annuls or restricts the taking or
    acquisition directly or indirectly of, or the succession to, any
    interest in real property located in a province by a permanent
    resident within the meaning of subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration
    and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} conflicts with any legal obligation of Canada under
    any international law, custom or agreement;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} discriminates as between persons who are not
    citizens on the basis of their nationalities, except in so far as
    more favourable treatment is required by any legal obligation of
    Canada under any international law, custom or agreement;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} hinders any foreign state in taking or acquiring
    real property located in a province for diplomatic or consular
    purposes; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} prohibits, annuls or restricts the taking or
    acquisition directly or indirectly of any interest in real property
    located in a province by any person in the course or as a result of
    an investment that the Minister is satisfied or is deemed to be
    satisfied is likely to be of net benefit to Canada under the
    [Investment Canada Act](/eng/acts/I-21.8).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-29, s. 35
- R.S., 1985, c. 28 (1st Supp.), s. 49
- 2001, c. 27, s. 232
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences and punishment

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who fails to comply with any prohibition, annulment or
  restriction made pursuant to subsection 35(1) is guilty of an offence
  and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding ten thousand
  dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year or to
  both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers, etc., of corporations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a corporation commits an offence under
  subsection (1), any officer, director or agent of the corporation who
  directed, authorized, assented to, acquiesced in or participated in
  the commission of the offence is a party to and guilty of the offence
  and is liable on conviction to the punishment provided for the offence
  whether or not the corporation has been prosecuted or convicted.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 108, s. 33
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** Sections 35 and 36 shall
come into force in any of the Provinces of Ontario, Quebec, Nova Scotia,
New Brunswick, British Columbia, Prince Edward Island, Saskatchewan and
Newfoundland and Labrador or in Yukon, the Northwest Territories or
Nunavut on a day fixed in a proclamation of the Governor in Council
declaring those sections to be in force in that Province or any of those
territories.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-29, s. 37
- 1993, c. 28, s. 78
- 2002, c. 7, s. 131
- 2015, c. 3, s. 172
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disabilities

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** Sections 34 and 35 do not
operate so as to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} qualify any person for any office or for any
  municipal, parliamentary or other franchise;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} qualify any person to be the owner of a Canadian
  ship;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} qualify any person to take, acquire, hold or dispose
  of any property that under or pursuant to any Act of Parliament may be
  taken, acquired, held or disposed of only by citizens;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} entitle any person to any right or privilege as a
  Canadian citizen except such rights and privileges in respect of
  property as are by this Act expressly given to the person; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} affect any estate or interest in real or personal
  property to which a person has or may become entitled, either
  mediately or immediately, in possession or expectancy, in pursuance of
  any disposition made before July 4, 1883, or in pursuance of any
  devolution by law on the death of any person dying before July 4,
  1883.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 108, s. 33
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Trial

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** A person who is not a
citizen is triable at law in the same manner as if the person were a
citizen.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1974-75-76, c. 108, s. 34
