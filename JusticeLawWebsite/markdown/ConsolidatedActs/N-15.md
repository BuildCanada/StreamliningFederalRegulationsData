## [Establishment of Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363434 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Council established

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There is
  established a Council, to be called the National Research Council of
  Canada, consisting of a President, a Chairperson and not more than 10
  other members, to be appointed by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Council incorporated

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Council is a corporation that has power, for the
  purposes of this Act, to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} acquire, hold, sell or otherwise dispose of and
    loan or lease real, personal, movable and immovable property; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into contracts, agreements, memoranda of
    understanding or other arrangements with a department, board or
    agency of the Government of Canada, with any other government or any
    of its departments, boards or agencies or with any person or
    organization in the name of His Majesty in right of Canada or in its
    own name.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of requirements

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} Despite the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11), including subsection 41(1) of that Act, the
  Council is not subject to any requirement established by the Treasury
  Board under that Act that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} limits, on the basis of financial criteria, the
    power of the Council to enter into contracts for the procurement of
    goods and services; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} relates to limits on liability or indemnification
    in those contracts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procurement of goods and services

  [(2.2)]{.lawlabel} Despite section 9 of the [Department of Public
  Works and Government Services Act](/eng/acts/P-38.2), the Council may
  procure goods and services from outside the federal public
  administration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(2.3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the power to procure goods
  and services includes the power to procure goods and services relating
  to construction and to research-related digital and information
  technology.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal services

  [(2.4)]{.lawlabel} The Council may procure legal services from outside
  the federal public administration only with the approval of the
  Attorney General of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation and acquisition of shares

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Council, as well as a corporation described in
  subsection (4), may do anything in respect of which paragraph 90(1)(a)
  or (b) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) applies
  only with the approval of the Governor in Council and in accordance
  with any conditions that the Governor in Council may impose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (3) applies with respect to a corporation
  if any of its shares, within the meaning of Part X of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), are held by the Council and
  either

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a majority of the corporation's directors or
    members are nominated or appointed by or on behalf of Her Majesty in
    right of Canada or by directors or members who were nominated or
    appointed by or on behalf of Her Majesty; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty otherwise controls the corporation.

## [Duties and Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363442 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of Council

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Council has charge of such matters affecting scientific and industrial
  research in Canada as may be assigned to it by the Governor in
  Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Research-related digital and
  information technology

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The design, development, testing and operation of
  research-related digital and information technology is essential to
  the carrying out of the Council's duties.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-15, s. 4

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Council

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Without
  limiting the general powers conferred on or vested in the Council by
  this Act, the Council may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to the approval of the Governor in Council,
    make by-laws for the conduct of its business;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} control and direct the work of the Council through
    the President;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} undertake, assist or promote scientific and
    industrial research, including, without restricting the generality
    of the foregoing,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the utilization of the natural resources of
      Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} researches with the object of improving the
      technical processes and methods used in the industries of Canada,
      and of discovering processes and methods that may promote the
      expansion of existing or the development of new industries,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} researches with the view of utilizing the waste
      products of those industries,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the investigation and determination of standards
      and methods of measurements, including length, volume, weight,
      mass, capacity, time, heat, light, electricity, magnetism and
      other forms of energy, and the investigation and determination of
      physical constants and the fundamental properties of matter,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the standardization and certification of the
      scientific and technical apparatus and instruments for the
      Government service and for use in the industries of Canada, and
      the determination of the standards of quality of the materials
      used in construction of public works and of the supplies used in
      the various branches of the Government service,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the investigation and standardization, at the
      request of any of the industries of Canada, of the materials used
      or usable in, or the products of, the industries making the
      request, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} researches, the object of which is to improve
      conditions in agriculture;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} have charge of and direction or supervision over
    the researches that may be undertaken, under conditions to be
    determined in each case, by or for single industrial firms or by
    such organizations or persons as may desire to avail themselves of
    the facilities offered for this purpose;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} expend, for the purposes of this Act, any money
    appropriated by Parliament for the work of the Council or received
    by the Council through the conduct of its operations;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} acquire any money, securities or other property by
    gift, bequest or otherwise, and expend, administer or dispose of any
    of the money, securities or other property subject to the terms, if
    any, on which the money, securities or other property is given,
    bequeathed or otherwise made available to the Council;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} appoint such scientific, technical and other
    officers as are nominated by the President, fix the tenure of their
    appointments, prescribe their several duties and, subject to the
    approval of the Governor in Council, fix their remuneration;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} authorize the President or any other officer of the
    Council to appoint persons to perform duties of a temporary nature
    for a period not exceeding six months;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} establish, operate and maintain a national science
    library;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} subject to the approval of the Minister, publish
    and sell or otherwise distribute such scientific and technical
    information as the Council deems necessary;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} carry on work and manufacturing of an experimental
    and developmental nature with respect to the matters referred to in
    paragraphs (c) and (d) so as to render the processes, methods or
    products relating to those matters more available and effective in
    useful arts and manufacturing and for scientific purposes and
    otherwise;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} license, sell or otherwise grant or make available
    to others, and receive royalties, fees and payment for, any
    intellectual property right --- including any patent, copyright,
    industrial design, trademark, trade secret, know-how or other
    similar right and any such future right that is described under a
    written agreement --- that is held, developed, administered or
    controlled by the Council, whether vested in Her Majesty in right of
    Canada or in the Council; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} operate and administer any astronomical
    observatories established or maintained by the Government of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation to President

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may authorize the President to approve
  on the Minister's behalf the publication, sale or other distribution
  by the Council of scientific and technical information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Public Servants Inventions
  Act](/eng/acts/P-32)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite section 9 of the [Public Servants Inventions
  Act](/eng/acts/P-32), the administration and control of any invention
  made by a [[public servant,]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
  section 2 of that Act, who is employed by the Council, and vested in
  Her Majesty by that Act, and any patent issued with respect to the
  invention, are vested in the Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Drugs and devices --- public health

- **[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Council may engage in, and may direct or supervise, the production on
  any scale of [[drugs]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
  [[devices]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms are defined in
  section 2 of the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27), for the purpose
  of protecting or improving public health in Canada or elsewhere.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval or direction of Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, the Council may do anything referred to in
  subsection (1) that is not otherwise authorized under paragraph
  5(1)(k) only with the approval, or under the direction, of the
  Minister given after the Minister has consulted with the Minister of
  Health.

## [Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363476 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure of office

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Each member of the Council,
other than the President, shall be appointed to hold office for a term
of not more than three years.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-14, s. 5
- 1976-77, c. 24, s. 52
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of President

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The President shall be
appointed to hold office for such term, not exceeding five years, as the
Governor in Council may determine.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-14, s. 6
- 1976-77, c. 24, s. 53
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Executive capacity of President

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The President is the chief
executive officer of the Council and has supervision over and direction
of the work of the Council and of the officers, technical and otherwise,
appointed for the purpose of carrying on the work of the Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-14, s. 6
- 1976-77, c. 24, s. 53
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting President

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  President is absent or unable to act or if the office of President is
  vacant, the Minister may appoint another person to act as President,
  but that person may act as President for a period of more than 90 days
  only with the approval of the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting Chairperson

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Chairperson is absent or unable to act or if
  the office of Chairperson is vacant, the Minister may appoint another
  person to act as Chairperson, but that person may act as Chairperson
  for a period of more than 90 days only with the approval of the
  Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. N-15, s. 9
- 2013, c. 40, s. 273
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Any retiring President or
other retiring member of the Council is eligible for re-appointment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salary and expenses of President

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President shall be paid such salary and expenses as are fixed by the
  Governor in Council.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2013, c. 40, s. 274\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Travel and other expenses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Council, other than the President,
  shall serve without remuneration but each of those members is entitled
  to be paid reasonable travel and other expenses incurred while absent
  from his ordinary place of residence in the course of performing
  duties under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration for additional duties

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (3), a member of the
  Council, other than the President, shall, for any period during which
  the member performs with the approval of the Council any duties on its
  behalf in addition to his ordinary duties, be paid such remuneration
  therefor as may be authorized by the Council.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the
Council shall be at such place in Canada as may be designated by the

least three times a year at the times and places in Canada that the

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Executive committee

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  is hereby established an executive committee of the Council consisting
  of the President and at least six other members to be selected by the
  Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of executive committee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The executive committee of the Council may exercise
  the powers of the Council and shall submit, at each meeting of the
  Council, minutes of the proceedings of that committee since the last
  preceding meeting of the Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-14, ss. 5, 11
- 1976-77, c. 24, ss. 52, 57(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agent of Her Majesty

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Council is for all purposes of this Act an agent of Her Majesty and it
  may exercise its powers under this Act only as an agent of Her
  Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings by and against the Council

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Actions, suits or other legal proceedings in respect
  of any right or obligation acquired or incurred by the Council on
  behalf of Her Majesty, whether in its name or in the name of Her
  Majesty, may be brought or taken by or against the Council in the name
  of the Council in any court that would have jurisdiction if the
  Council were not an agent of Her Majesty.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-14, s. 9
:::

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** All the receipts and
expenditures of the Council are subject to examination and audit by the
Auditor General of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote

## [Annual Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363537 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report to Parliament

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The President shall,
within four months after the termination of each fiscal year, transmit
to the Minister a report of the operations of the Council for that
fiscal year and the Minister shall cause that report to be laid before
Parliament within fifteen days after the receipt thereof or, if
Parliament is not then sitting, on any of the first fifteen days next
thereafter that either House of Parliament is sitting.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-14, s. 16
:::

## [Establishment of Procurement Oversight Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1416557 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  is established a board, to be called the Procurement Oversight Board,
  consisting of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} not less than three or more than five members,
    including a Chair, who have a right to vote;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one member who is a person employed by the
    Department of Public Works and Government Services and who does not
    have a right to vote; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one member who is a person employed in the federal
    public administration and who does not have a right to vote, if the
    Minister considers that member necessary to assist the Procurement
    Oversight Board in carrying out its duties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Procurement Oversight Board shall review and
  approve

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Council's policy framework for procuring goods
    and services and any amendments to it; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Council's proposals to enter into contracts for
    the procurement of goods and services if the procurement is complex
    or large-scale when measured by criteria established by the
    Procurement Oversight Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms of reference

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may determine and amend the terms of
  reference of the Procurement Oversight Board.
