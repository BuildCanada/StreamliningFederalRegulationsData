## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-424514 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Railway Safety Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2).

## [Application of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-424517 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This Act
  is binding on Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application generally

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Act applies in respect of railways that are
  within the legislative authority of Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), this Act does not apply in
  respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} railways referred to in section 16 of the [Harbour
    Commissions Act](/eng/acts/H-1); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} railways referred to in section 29 of the [Canada
    Marine Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7), except to the extent provided by
    regulations made under subsection 29(2) of that Act.

## [Objectives]{.HTitleText1} {#h-424529 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objectives

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The objectives of this Act
are to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} promote and provide for the safety and security of
  the public and personnel, and the protection of property and the
  environment, in railway operations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} encourage the collaboration and participation of
  interested parties in improving railway safety and security;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} recognize the responsibility of companies to
  demonstrate, by using safety management systems and other means at
  their disposal, that they continuously manage risks related to safety
  matters; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} facilitate a modern, flexible and efficient
  regulatory scheme that will ensure the continuing enhancement of
  railway safety and security.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 32 (4th Supp.), s. 3
- 1996, c. 10, s. 262
- 1999, c. 9, s. 1
- 2012, c. 7, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's responsibilities respecting
railway safety

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is
responsible for the development and regulation of matters to which this
Act applies, including safety and security, and for the supervision of
all matters connected with railways and, in the discharge of those
responsibilities, the Minister may, among other things,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} promote railway safety and security by means that the
  Minister considers appropriate;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide facilities and services for the collection,
  publication or dissemination of information;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} undertake, and cooperate with persons undertaking,
  projects, technical research, study or investigation;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} inspect, examine and report on activities related to
  railway matters; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} undertake other activities that the Minister
  considers appropriate or that the Governor in Council may direct.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A reference in this Act to a proposed railway work
  shall be construed, in a case where a person proposes to alter an
  existing railway work, as meaning the railway work as proposed to be
  so altered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safe railway operations, etc.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In determining, for the purposes of this Act, whether
  railway operations are safe railway operations, or whether an act or
  thing constitutes a threat to safe railway operations or enhances the
  safety of railway operations, regard shall be had not only to the
  safety of persons and property transported by railways but also to the
  safety of other persons and other property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Threats and immediate threats

  [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, a threat is a hazard
  or condition that could reasonably be expected to develop into a
  situation in which a person could be injured or made to be ill or
  damage could be caused to the environment or property, and a threat is
  immediate if such a situation already exists.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, by facsimile or by

    designated by the Minister; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in either case, by any electronic or other means

    fixed by the Minister.

### [Notice of Certain Proposed Railway Works]{.HTitleText2} {#h-424671 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of certain proposed railway works

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  proposed railway work is of a prescribed kind, the proponent shall not
  undertake the work unless it has first given notice of the work in
  accordance with the regulations. However, it may undertake the work if
  all persons to whom the notice was given file with the proponent a
  response indicating that they do not object to the work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of objections

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person to whom a notice is given under
  subsection (1) may file with the proponent an objection to the
  proposed railway work if the person considers that the proposed
  railway work would prejudice their safety or the safety of their
  property. The objection must include reasons and be filed before the
  expiry of the period specified in the notice for the filing of
  objections, and a copy of it must be filed without delay with the
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of objection

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who has filed an objection under
  subsection (2) and subsequently wishes to withdraw that objection
  shall so notify the proponent and the Minister, and the objection
  shall be deemed to be withdrawn on receipt by the Minister of the
  notice.

### [Undertaking of Proposed Railway Works]{.HTitleText2} {#h-424688 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Railway works for which Ministerial
approval required

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a proposed railway work departs from any applicable
    engineering standards, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a proposed railway work is one in respect of which
    notice has been given under subsection 8(1) and, at the expiration
    of the period specified in that notice for the filing of objections,
    there is an outstanding objection,

  the proponent shall not undertake that work otherwise than in
  accordance with the terms of the Minister's approval given under this
  section pursuant to a written request, filed with the Minister, for
  such approval.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for approval before end of
  notice period

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} A request to the Minister for approval of a
  proposed railway work under subsection (1) may be filed before the end
  of the period specified in the notice given under subsection 8(1) if
  all persons to whom the notice was given have filed a response with
  the proponent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of objection

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} A proposed railway work described in paragraph
  (1)(b) may be undertaken without the Minister's approval if the
  outstanding objection is withdrawn.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Material to accompany request for
  Minister's approval

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request to the Minister for approval of a proposed
  railway work pursuant to subsection (1) must be accompanied

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in all cases, by a plan of the work to which the
    request relates, which plan must include such drawings,
    specifications and other particulars as are prescribed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where paragraph (1)(a) applies, by a statement
    setting out the manner in which the work departs from the applicable
    engineering standards and the reasons for such departure; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where paragraph (1)(b) applies, by the response of
    the proponent to the outstanding objections.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration of requests for approval

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a request for approval of a proposed
  railway work under this section, the Minister shall, having regard to
  the material referred to in subsection (2) and any other factors that
  the Minister deems relevant, consider whether the proposed railway
  work is consistent with safe railway operations and shall, before the
  expiration of the assessment period,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if satisfied that the work is consistent with safe
    railway operations, notify the proponent and any objecting party
    that the Minister approves the work, either absolutely or on such
    terms as are specified in the notice; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if not satisfied that the work is consistent with
    safe railway operations, by notice

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} inform the proponent and any objecting party that
      the Minister refuses to approve the work and of the reasons why
      the Minister is not so satisfied, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} direct the proponent to file with the Minister
      and any objecting party, within the period specified in the
      notice, such further particulars relating to the work as are
      specified in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Late approval

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a railway work has been undertaken without the
  required approval of the Minister having first been given, the
  Minister may nevertheless approve the work in accordance with this
  section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may seek advice

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, in deciding whether to approve a
  proposed railway work, engage any person or organization having
  expertise in matters of safe railway operations to furnish advice to
  the Minister in relation to the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further particulars

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where the proponent is directed to file further
  particulars pursuant to subparagraph (3)(b)(ii) and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fails to file the further particulars within the
    period referred to in that subparagraph, it shall be deemed to have
    withdrawn its request to the Minister for approval of the work; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} files the further particulars within the period
    referred to in that subparagraph, this Act shall thereupon have
    effect as if the request that the Minister approve the work had been
    filed only at the time when the further particulars were filed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period for which approval in force

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Minister's approval of a proposed railway work

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the period specified in the approval; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the approval does not specify a period, one year
    after the approval is given.

- Definition of [[assessment period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[assessment
  period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a request that the
  Minister approve a proposed railway work, means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the period of sixty days commencing on the day on

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if, before the expiration of the period referred to

    requests filed or any other reason, it is not feasible to consider
    the request before the expiration of that period, and so notifies
    the proponent and any objecting party, such period in excess of that
    period of sixty days as the Minister specifies in the notices.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of inquiry not part of
  assessment period

### [Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#h-424951 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Formulation or revision of rules

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by order, require a company

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to formulate rules respecting any matter referred
    to in subsection 18(1) or (2.1) or to revise its rules respecting
    that matter; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within a specified period, to file the formulated
    or revised rules with the Minister for approval.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Company to consult

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company shall not file rules with the Minister
  under subsection (1) unless it has first, during a period of sixty
  days, given a reasonable opportunity for consultation with it on the
  rules to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a railway company, each relevant
    association or organization that is likely to be affected by the
    implementation of the rules; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a local railway company, any railway
    company on whose railway the local railway company operates railway
    equipment and that is likely to be affected by the implementation of
    the rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to accompany rules

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When rules are filed with the Minister by a company
  pursuant to an order under subsection (1), the company shall, by
  notice filed with those rules, identify each association or
  organization or any railway company that was consulted and attach a
  copy of any objection that is made by any of them on grounds of
  safety.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration of rules

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} After rules are filed with the Minister by a company
  pursuant to an order under subsection (1), the Minister shall
  consider, without delay, whether, in the Minister's opinion and after
  having regard to current railway practice, to the views of the company
  and the views of each relevant association or organization or any
  railway company identified under subsection (3) and to any other
  factor that the Minister considers relevant, those rules are conducive
  to safe railway operations by the company, and shall, before the
  expiration of the assessment period in relation to those rules,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the Minister is so satisfied, notify the company
    and each association or organization or any railway company
    identified under subsection (3) that the Minister approves those
    rules, either absolutely or on any terms and conditions that are
    specified in the notice; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Minister is not so satisfied, notify the
    company and each association or organization or any railway company
    identified under subsection (3) that the Minister refuses to approve
    those rules and of the reasons why the Minister is not so satisfied.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for amendment to terms and
  conditions

  [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} A company referred to in subsection (4) may, on the
  basis of new information about the safety of railway operations,
  request the Minister to amend the terms and conditions specified under
  paragraph (4)(a), and shall send a copy of the request to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a railway company, each relevant
    association or organization that is likely to be affected by the
    amendment to the terms and conditions; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a local railway company, any railway
    company on whose railway the local railway company operates railway
    equipment and that is likely to be affected by the amendment to the
    terms and conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments

  [(4.2)]{.lawlabel} After receiving a request from a company under
  subsection (4.1), the Minister may amend the terms and conditions and,

  association or organization, or any railway company, referred to in
  subsection (4.1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may seek advice

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, in deciding whether to approve

  advice in relation to the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date of rules

  [(5.1)]{.lawlabel} Rules approved by the Minister under subsection (4)

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to file rules

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Non-railway Operations Affecting Railway Safety]{.HTitleText1} {#h-425112 .Part}

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the control or prohibition of the construction or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the control of the maintenance

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the control or prohibition of the construction or
      alteration, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the control of the maintenance,

    on land adjoining the land on which a line of railway is situated,
    of drainage systems that would constitute a threat to safe railway
    operations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the control or prohibition of the
    presence or storage, on land adjoining the land on which a line of
    railway is situated, of specified materials;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the removal of anything, including trees or

    employees or agents or mandataries of the railway company concerned,
    or of the local railway company authorized to operate railway
    equipment on the railway --- or by vehicles or animals, if their
    presence on that land would constitute a threat to safe railway
    operations;

  - [(f.1)]{.lawlabel} respecting the construction, alteration and
    maintenance of roads for the purpose of ensuring safe railway
    operations;

  - [(f.2)]{.lawlabel} respecting the control of vehicular and
    pedestrian traffic on road approaches to road crossings for the
    purpose of ensuring safe railway operations; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} respecting the control or prohibition of any other
    activity, on land adjoining the land on which a line of railway is
    situated, that could constitute a threat to safe railway operations.

### [Power of Railway Companies on Adjoining Lands]{.HTitleText2} {#h-425141 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entry onto land adjoining line of railway

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of preventing a threat to safe railway operations on a
  line of railway or restoring safe railway operations on a line of
  railway, a railway company may enter onto any land adjoining the land
  on which the line of railway is situated

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at any time, in order to maintain or alter railway
    works or remove obstructions to them, if no other access to the line
    of railway is reasonably available, and remain on the land for as
    long as is necessary to accomplish that purpose;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at any time, in order to deal with any fire
    occurring on either of those lands;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at any reasonable time, on giving notice in writing

    order to cut down trees or brush that has been permitted to grow on
    that land in contravention of regulations made under paragraph
    24(1)(e); or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} at any time between November 1 and March 31, in
    order to install or maintain a snow fence.

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of preventing a threat to safe

  crossing to cut down trees or brush that has been permitted to grow on
  that land in contravention of regulations made under paragraph
  24(1)(e), if the road authority gives notice in writing to the owner
  of the land of its intention to do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal of snow fences

  land shall cause that snow fence to be removed on or before April 1
  next following the date of its installation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  suffers a loss because of the exercise by a railway company or a road
  authority of a power conferred by this section, the railway company or
  road authority shall pay to that person any compensation in respect of
  that loss that they may agree on or, failing an agreement, that is
  determined under section 26, but the payment of compensation is not a
  condition precedent to the exercise of the power.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspector's powers

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway safety inspector may, at any time,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of ensuring compliance with this
    Act and with the regulations, emergency directives, rules, orders
    and security measures made under this Act, enter any place, other
    than a private dwelling-place, where activities are carried on that
    relate directly or indirectly to the operation or maintenance of a
    railway or the operation of railway equipment, and carry out any
    inspection that the inspector considers necessary in relation to the
    matters designated by the Minister under section 27 in respect of
    which the inspector may exercise the powers of a railway safety
    inspector;

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} require any person appearing to be in charge of
    the place to produce any document, regardless of physical form or
    characteristics, for inspection or for the purpose of making copies
    or taking extracts;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} seize any property found in the course of that
    inspection on the railway work or in the railway equipment that the
    inspector believes, on reasonable grounds, will afford evidence with
    respect to an offence under this Act, and may submit that property
    to reasonable tests; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} require the attendance of persons whom the
    inspector deems relevant to the carrying out of the inspector's
    functions, and may question those persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication authorized

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} A company is authorized to communicate to a railway
  safety inspector the information that it recorded, collected or
  preserved under subsection 17.31(1) and that is contained in a
  document that the railway safety inspector requires it to produce
  under paragraph (1)(a.1), for the purpose of verifying compliance with
  section 17.31 and regulations made under section 17.95.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a railway safety inspector believes, on
  reasonable grounds, that an offence under this Act is being or has
  been committed at or in any place other than a railway work or railway
  equipment and that evidence of that offence is likely to be found at
  that place, the inspector may, if the offence relates to a matter for
  which the inspector is designated, and subject to subsection (3),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter and search that place for evidence of that
    offence; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} seize any property found in the course of the
    search that the inspector believes, on reasonable grounds, will
    afford evidence with respect to that offence, and submit that
    property to reasonable tests.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for exercise of powers under
  subsection (2)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A railway safety inspector shall not exercise the
  powers referred to in subsection (2) unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person apparently in charge of the place
    consents to the entry and search;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the powers are exercised in relation to that place
    under the authority of a warrant; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by reason of exigent circumstances, it would not be
    practicable for the inspector to obtain a warrant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forcible entry must be specifically
  authorized

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In executing a warrant, a railway safety inspector
  shall not use force unless the inspector is accompanied by a peace
  officer and the use of force has been specifically authorized in the
  warrant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seized property to be returned

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  property seized by a railway safety inspector under section 28 shall
  be returned to the person from whom the seizure was made or to any
  other person who appears on reasonable grounds to be entitled thereto,
  after

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the provision of this Act or the regulations, the
    emergency directive, the rule or the order in respect of the

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the expiration of a period of thirty days from the
    day of the seizure,

  whichever first occurs, unless before that time proceedings are
  instituted in respect of an offence under this Act in relation to the
  property seized, in which case it may be detained until the
  proceedings are finally concluded.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for return of seized
  property

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where proceedings referred to in subsection (1) have
  been instituted, a person from whom property that is the
  subject-matter of the proceedings was seized, or any other person who
  appears on reasonable grounds to be entitled to that property, may
  apply to the court before which the proceedings were instituted for an
  order that the property be returned to the applicant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the court is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sufficient evidence for the purposes of the
    proceedings exists or may be produced without detaining the seized
    property, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no threat to safe railway operations would result
    from the release of the property,

  the court may grant the application and order the return of the
  property forthwith to the applicant, subject to any terms and
  conditions that appear necessary or desirable to ensure that the
  property is safeguarded and preserved for any purpose for which it may
  subsequently be required.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where person convicted

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Seized property that is detained until the final
  conclusion of proceedings shall be returned to the person from whom
  the seizure was made, or to any other person who appears on reasonable
  grounds to be entitled thereto, unless that person has been convicted
  of an offence under this Act, in which case the property may be
  detained until any fine imposed on conviction has been paid, or sold
  under execution in satisfaction of the fine or any part thereof.

### [Notices of Railway Safety Inspectors Concerning the Safety or Security of Railway Operations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-425314 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice --- threat

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  railway safety inspector is of the opinion that a person's conduct or
  any thing for which a person is responsible constitutes a threat to
  the safety or security of railway operations or the safety of persons
  or property, the inspector shall inform, by notice sent to the person
  and to any company whose railway operations are affected by the
  threat, the person and the company of that opinion and of the reasons
  for it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice --- immediate threat

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the railway safety inspector is satisfied that the
  threat is immediate, the inspector may, in the notice, order the
  person or any company whose railway operations are affected by the
  threat, to take the measures that are specified in the notice to
  mitigate the threat until it has been removed to the inspector's
  satisfaction.

- [(2.1) to (4)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2015, c. 31, s.
  25\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  contains an order shall indicate the date on or before which the

  to which the request may be filed. The date shall be 30 days after the
  day on which the notice is sent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister to be informed of order

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a notice sent under this section contains an

  inform the Minister of the order and the reasons for it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies sent to company supervisor

  that time in charge of that person or those operations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of order

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} An order contained in a notice under this section has
  effect, in the case of a company, when the company receives the notice

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} An order made by a railway safety inspector under
  this section may be altered or revoked by another railway safety
  inspector only if the inspector who made the order is unable to act.

  order that is reviewable under sections 31.1 to 31.5.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When alteration or revocation effective

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} An alteration or revocation of an order under this
  section has effect when the company or other person to whom the
  original notice was sent receives a notice of the alteration or
  revocation.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any railway work has not been altered in accordance
    with the requirements imposed by or under this Act, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any railway work is not being, or has not been,
    maintained in accordance with the requirements imposed by or under

  the Minister may

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by notice sent to the person responsible for the
    work, order the person to remove or modify the work, and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} where a person fails to comply with an order made

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister removes, destroys or disposes of a

  sale or otherwise, are recoverable, with costs, in the name of Her
  Majesty from the person who failed to remove or modify the work,
  whether or not proceedings are instituted against that person for
  failing to comply with an order of the Minister under this section.

  section 24

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is of the opinion that a person has
  contravened a regulation made under section 24, the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by notice sent to the person,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} shall inform the person of that opinion and of
      the reasons for it, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} may, if the Minister believes that, by reason of

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by notice sent to the railway company concerned,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} shall inform the railway company of that opinion
      and of the reasons for it, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} may, if the Minister believes that, by reason of
      that contravention, there exists an immediate threat to safe
      railway operations, order the railway company to ensure that
      specified railway works or specified railway equipment not be
      used, or not be used otherwise than under terms and conditions
      specified in the notice, until appropriate action to remove the
      threat has, to the Minister's satisfaction, been taken by the
      person referred to in paragraph (a).

### [Emergency Directives]{.HTitleText2} {#h-425437 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may send emergency directives

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister is of the opinion that there is an immediate threat to safe
  railway operations or the security of railway transportation, the
  Minister may, by emergency directive sent to a company, order it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} either absolutely or to the extent specified in the
    directive, to stop using the kind of railway works or railway
    equipment or following the maintenance or operating practice that
    poses the threat; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to follow a maintenance or operating practice
    specified in the directive if the threat is posed by the company's
    failure to follow that practice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directive despite compliance with law

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may issue an emergency directive even
  though

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the construction of the railway work was undertaken
    in accordance with the law in force at the time; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} using the railway equipment or following or not
    following the maintenance or operating practice is in accordance
    with this Act or any regulations or rules made under it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An emergency directive has effect during such period,
  not exceeding six months, as is specified in the directive.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergency directive to contain
  statement of reasons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An emergency directive shall contain a statement of
  the Minister's reasons for holding the opinion by reason of which the
  directive was given.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may rescind emergency
  directives

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by notice sent to the company,
  rescind an emergency directive, in which case the directive ceases to
  have effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency between emergency
  directives, regulations, rules or orders

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In the event that there is an inconsistency between
  an emergency directive and a regulation made under subsection 18(1) or
  (2.1) or a rule in force under section 19 or 20, the emergency
  directive prevails to the extent of the inconsistency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may renew emergency directives

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, before the expiration of the period
  during which an emergency directive has effect, by notice sent to the
  company, renew the directive for a further specified period commencing
  on the expiration of the previous period and not exceeding six months
  and, if the Minister does so, this section, except this subsection,
  applies to the directive as renewed.

### [Security]{.HTitleText2} {#h-425516 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Screening before boarding

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person shall not board the railway equipment or
    enter the restricted area unless the person has undergone the
    authorized screening; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no person shall take the goods, or have them
    placed, on board the railway equipment or in the restricted area
    unless the goods have undergone the authorized screening.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Screening after boarding or in
  restricted areas

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A screening officer may require a person on board
  railway equipment or in a restricted area to undergo authorized
  screening and, if the person refuses,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the officer may order the person to leave the
    railway equipment or restricted area and to remove from it any goods
    that the person took or had placed there; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person shall leave the railway equipment or
    restricted area and remove or permit the removal of the goods
    immediately or, if the railway equipment is moving, at the first
    reasonable opportunity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unaccompanied goods

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A screening officer may carry out authorized
  screening of any goods at a railway work that are intended for
  transport on railway equipment and are not accompanied by a person,
  and the officer may use any force that is reasonably necessary to gain
  access to the goods.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False or misleading information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall knowingly make any false or
  misleading statement to a screening officer, or knowingly provide
  false or misleading information to a screening officer.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no person is obliged to undergo authorized

    equipment or enter a restricted area; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} no person is obliged to permit authorized screening

    placed on board the railway equipment or in the restricted area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Placement and languages of notices

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The notices must be posted in prominent places where

### [Offences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-425684 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of provision of Act

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who contravenes a provision of this Act is guilty of an offence
  and liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, to a fine not
      exceeding one million dollars, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine not
      exceeding fifty thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not
      exceeding one year, or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, to a fine not
      exceeding five hundred thousand dollars, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine not
      exceeding twenty-five thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a
      term not exceeding six months, or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of regulations, orders,
  etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person is guilty of an offence if the person
  contravenes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a regulation made under subsection 7(1) or section
    7.1, 17.95, 18, 24, 37, 47 or 47.1;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an order made by the Minister under subsection 7(2)
    or 19(1), section 32 or 32.01 or contained in a notice sent by a
    railway safety inspector under section 31;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} an engineering standard;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a requirement made by the Agency under subsection
    16(3) or 26(3);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a rule in force under section 19 or 20;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an emergency directive made by the Minister under
    section 33;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a requirement under subsection 39.1(2) to carry out
    a security measure;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a railway operating certificate issued under
    section 17.4; or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} an order made under subsection 36(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} A person who is guilty of an offence under
  subsection (2) is liable on summary conviction

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, to a fine of not more
    than one million dollars; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, to a fine of not more
    than fifty thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term of not
    more than six months, or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offences

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person commits a contravention described in
  subsection (1) or (2) that occurs on more than one day, or is
  continued for more than one day, the person is deemed to have
  committed a separate offence for each day on which it occurs or is
  continued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Venue

  [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} Any complaint or information in respect of an
  offence under this Act may be heard, tried or determined by a court if
  the accused is resident or carrying on business within the territorial
  jurisdiction of that court although the matter of the complaint or
  information did not arise in that territorial jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joinder of complaints

  [(3.2)]{.lawlabel} The judge who hears, tries or determines the
  complaint or information may, at the request of the accused, join it
  with others of the same type against the accused, even if the matter
  of the complaints or informations did not arise in the same
  territorial jurisdiction, and hear them under the same procedure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A prosecution by way of summary conviction under this
  section may be instituted at any time within, but not after, two years
  after the time when the subject-matter of the prosecution arose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of fines

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a person is convicted of an offence under this
  Act and the fine that is imposed is not paid when required, the
  conviction shall, on production in a superior court of the province in
  which the trial was held, be registered in the court and, when
  registered, has the same force and effect, and all proceedings may be
  taken thereon, as if the conviction were a judgment in that court
  obtained by Her Majesty in right of Canada against the convicted
  person for a debt of the amount of the fine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of costs and charges

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} All reasonable costs and charges attendant on the
  registration of the conviction are recoverable in like manner as if
  they had been registered as part of the conviction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Admissibility of evidence

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Evidence relating to the presence or concentration of
  alcohol or a drug in a sample of a bodily substance obtained under the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) is admissible in proceedings taken
  against a person under this Act in respect of a contravention of a
  rule or regulation respecting the use of alcohol or a drug, and
  sections 320.31 to 320.35 of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46)  apply to those proceedings with any
  modifications that the circumstances require.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence by employee or agent

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** In any prosecution for an
offence under this Act, it is sufficient proof of the offence to
establish that it was committed by an employee or agent of the accused,
whether or not the employee or agent has been prosecuted for the
offence, unless the accused establishes that the offence was committed
without the knowledge or consent of the accused and that the accused
exercised all due diligence to prevent its commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers, etc., of corporation

participated in the commission of the offence is a party to and guilty
of the offence, and is liable on conviction to the punishment provided
for the offence, whether or not the corporation has been prosecuted or
convicted.

## [PART IV.1]{.HLabel1}[Police Constables]{.HTitleText1} {#h-425729 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  judge of a superior court may appoint a person as a police constable
  for the enforcement of Part III of the [Canada Transportation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4) and for the enforcement of the laws of Canada
  or a province in so far as their enforcement relates to the protection
  of property owned, possessed or administered by a railway company and
  the protection of persons and property on that property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appointment may only be made on the application
  of a railway company that owns, possesses or administers property
  located within the judge's jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The police constable has jurisdiction on property
  under the administration of the railway company and in any place
  within 500 m of property that the railway company owns, possesses or
  administers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to take persons before a court

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The police constable may take a person charged with
  an offence under Part III of the [Canada Transportation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4), or any law referred to in subsection (1),
  before a court that has jurisdiction in such cases over any area where
  property owned, possessed or administered by the railway company is
  located, whether or not the person was arrested, or the offence
  occurred or is alleged to have occurred, within that area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court's jurisdiction

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The court must deal with the person as though the
  person had been arrested, and the offence had occurred, within the
  area of the court's jurisdiction, but the court may not deal with the
  person if the offence is alleged to have occurred outside the province
  in which the court is sitting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dismissal or discharge of police
  constable

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A superior court judge referred to in subsection (1)
  or the railway company may dismiss or discharge the police constable
  and the dismissal or discharge terminates the powers, duties and
  privileges conferred on the constable by this section.

### [Transitional Provisions Relating to Repeal of Parts II and III of the Railway Relocation and Crossing Act]{.HTitleText2} {#h-425916 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Balance of Part II appropriation

- **[[120]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-120 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  amount that, before the coming into force of this section, had been
  appropriated for the purpose of enabling the Minister to make grants
  under Part II of the [Railway Relocation and Crossing
  Act](/eng/acts/R-4) but had not been authorized by the Minister to be
  so granted shall, after the coming into force of this section, be
  deemed to have been appropriated for the purpose of making grants
  under section 13 of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Balance of Part III appropriation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any amount that, before the coming into force of this
  section, had been appropriated for the purpose of making grants under
  Part III of the [Railway Relocation and Crossing Act](/eng/acts/R-4)
  but had not been authorized pursuant to that Part to be so granted
  shall, after the coming into force of this section, be deemed to have
  been appropriated for the purpose of making grants under section 12 of
  this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Outstanding grant applications

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any application for a grant in respect of a proposed
  railway work that, before the coming into force of this section, had
  been made under Part II or III of the [Railway Relocation and Crossing
  Act](/eng/acts/R-4) but had not been dealt with under that Act may, at
  the request of the party who made the application and with the consent
  of the Minister, be treated as if it were an application duly filed in
  accordance with the requirements of section 12 or 13 of this Act, and
  may be dealt with accordingly by the Minister.
