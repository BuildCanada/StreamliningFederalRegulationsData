## Government Services Resumption Act {#government-services-resumption-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1989, c. 24
:::

Assented to 1989-12-15

An Act to provide for the resumption of certain government services
::::

::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-252079 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Government Services Resumption Act](/eng/acts/G-7.6).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-252082 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[bargaining agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[bargaining agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Public Service
      Alliance of Canada; ([agent négociateur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[conciliation board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[conciliation board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a board
      established pursuant to section 8; ([bureau de
      conciliation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person employed in the
      Public Service who is bound by a group specific
      agreement; ([fonctionnaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Her Majesty in right of
      Canada as represented by the Treasury
      Board; ([employeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[government services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[government services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the services
      provided by that part of the Public Service in which the employees
      are employed; ([services gouvernementaux]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[group specific agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[group specific agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      collective agreement specified in the schedule; ([convention
      particulière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[master agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[master agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the collective
      agreement between the employer and the bargaining agent that
      expired on June 30, 1988. ([convention cadre]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Words and expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise provided, words and expressions used
  in this Act have the same meaning as in the [Public Service Staff
  Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-35), except that "chair" wherever it is
  used in this Act shall mean the Chairman of the Public Service Staff
  Relations Board.

## [Government Services]{.HTitleText1} {#h-252095 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resumption of government services

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#G-7.6_en_1){#G-7.6_en_1-ID0EBDA}[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
.sectionLabel}** On the coming into force of this Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the employer shall forthwith ensure that government
  services are resumed; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} every employee shall, when so required, forthwith
  resume the duties of that employee's employment.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#G-7.6_en_1-ID0EBDA){#G-7.6_en_1}\[Note: Act
    in force on the day immediately after the day on which this Act is
    assented to (assent date being December 15, 1989), but not before
    the twelfth hour after the time at which this Act is assented to,
    *see* section 16.\]
    :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of bargaining agent

- **[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#G-7.6_en_2){#G-7.6_en_2-ID0EBCA}[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The bargaining agent and each
  officer and representative of the bargaining agent

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall, forthwith on the coming into force of this
    Act, give notice to every employee that, by reason of that coming
    into force,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any declaration, authorization or direction to go
      on strike given to them before that coming into force has become
      invalid, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} government services are forthwith to be resumed
      and every employee, when so required, is forthwith to resume the
      duties of that employee's employment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall, in addition to giving notice as required by
    paragraph (a), take all reasonable steps to ensure that every
    employee complies with paragraph 3(b); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} shall refrain from any conduct that may encourage
    any employee not to comply with paragraph 3(b).

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#G-7.6_en_2-ID0EBCA){#G-7.6_en_2}\[Note: Act
      in force on the day immediately after the day on which this Act is
      assented to (assent date being December 15, 1989), but not before
      the twelfth hour after the time at which this Act is assented to,
      *see* section 16.\]
      :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of employer

  [[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#G-7.6_en_3){#G-7.6_en_3-ID0EBDAA}[(2)]{.lawlabel} No
  officer or representative of the employer shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in any manner impede any employee from complying
    with paragraph 3(b); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} discharge or in any other manner discipline, or
    authorize or direct the discharge or discipline of, any employee by
    reason of that employee's having been legally on strike before the
    coming into force of this Act.

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#G-7.6_en_3-ID0EBDAA){#G-7.6_en_3}\[Note:
      Act in force on the day immediately after the day on which this
      Act is assented to (assent date being December 15, 1989), but not
      before the twelfth hour after the time at which this Act is
      assented to, *see* section 16.\]
      :::

## [Collective Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-252112 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collective agreements

- **[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#G-7.6_en_4){#G-7.6_en_4-ID0EBCA}[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this Act, each group
  specific agreement and the master agreement, in its application to the
  bargaining unit bound by that group specific agreement, shall have
  effect on and after the coming into force of this Act and shall expire
  on the date fixed by the conciliation board established in respect of
  that bargaining unit.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#G-7.6_en_4-ID0EBCA){#G-7.6_en_4}\[Note: Act
    in force on the day immediately after the day on which this Act is
    assented to (assent date being December 15, 1989), but not before
    the twelfth hour after the time at which this Act is assented to,
    *see* section 16.\]
    :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into effect where express
  stipulation

  [[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#G-7.6_en_5){#G-7.6_en_5-ID0EBCAA}[(2)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the employer and the bargaining agent have, or the conciliation board
  established in respect of the bargaining unit represented by that
  agent has, stipulated in writing that a provision of a group specific
  agreement or of the master agreement is effective on a day before the
  coming into force of this Act, that provision shall be deemed to have
  effect on and after that day.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#G-7.6_en_5-ID0EBCAA){#G-7.6_en_5}\[Note: Act
    in force on the day immediately after the day on which this Act is
    assented to (assent date being December 15, 1989), but not before
    the twelfth hour after the time at which this Act is assented to,
    *see* section 16.\]
    :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collective agreement binding

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Each group specific
agreement and the master agreement, in its application to the bargaining
unit bound by that group specific agreement, as amended by or pursuant
to this Act, is effective and binding on the parties thereto as provided
for in section 5, notwithstanding anything in the [Public Service Staff
Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-35) or in that agreement and

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that Act applies in respect of the agreement, as so
  amended, as if the term provided for in subsection 5(1) were the term
  of the agreement; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the agreement, as so amended, shall, for the purposes
  of any Act of Parliament, be deemed to be an agreement between the
  employer and each of its employees in that bargaining unit on all
  matters relating to their employment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action prohibited

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the term of a group
specific agreement as provided for in subsection 5(1),

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} no officer or representative of the bargaining agent
  shall declare, authorize or direct a strike by any employees bound by
  that agreement; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} no employee bound by that agreement shall participate
  in a strike against the employer.

## [Conciliation Boards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-252126 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of conciliation boards

- **[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#G-7.6_en_6){#G-7.6_en_6-ID0EBEA}[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Forthwith on the coming into force
  of this Act, the chair shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish two conciliation boards, one in respect
    of the bargaining unit bound by the group specific agreement
    specified in item 1 of the schedule and the other in respect of the
    bargaining units bound by the group specific agreements specified in
    items 2 and 3 of the schedule;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} appoint Michael Bendel, of the City of Ottawa, as
    the chair of the first board referred to in paragraph (a) and
    Vincent Ready, of the City of Vancouver, as the chair of the second
    board therein referred to; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by notice require each of the parties to nominate,
    within two days after receipt of the notice, two persons, one to be
    a member of the first board referred to in paragraph (a) and the
    other to be a member of the second board therein referred to.

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#G-7.6_en_6-ID0EBEA){#G-7.6_en_6}\[Note: Act
      in force on the day immediately after the day on which this Act is
      assented to (assent date being December 15, 1989), but not before
      the twelfth hour after the time at which this Act is assented to,
      *see* section 16.\]
      :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a nomination within the period referred
  to in paragraph (1)(c), the chair shall appoint the nominee as a
  member of the conciliation board for which the person was nominated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If either party fails to nominate a person for a
  conciliation board within the period referred to in paragraph (1)(c),
  the chair shall appoint as a member of that board a person the chair
  deems fit for the purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancy

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If any vacancy occurs in the membership of a
  conciliation board before it reports to the chair, the chair shall
  appoint as a member of that board a person the chair deems fit for the
  purpose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allowances

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The members of a
conciliation board are entitled to be paid such allowances for the
performance of their duties under this Act as the Governor in Council
may fix.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms of reference for Hospital Services
conciliation board

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  chair shall refer to the first conciliation board referred to in
  paragraph 8(1)(a)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all matters relating to the amendment or revision
    of the group specific agreement specified in item 1 of the schedule,
    and of the master agreement, in its application to the bargaining
    unit bound by that group specific agreement, that were referred to a
    conciliation board established under the [Public Service Staff
    Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-35) by the chair on September 22, 1989
    and that remain in dispute between the parties at the time the board
    was established under this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} such other matters as the chair deems necessary or
    advisable; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fixing of a date for the expiry of the
    agreements referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms of reference for Ships' Crews
  conciliation board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The chair shall refer to the second conciliation
  board mentioned in paragraph 8(1)(a)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all matters relating to the amendment or revision
    of the group specific agreements specified in items 2 and 3 of the
    schedule, and of the master agreement, in its application to the
    bargaining units bound by those group specific agreements, that were
    referred to a conciliation board established under the [Public
    Service Staff Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-35) by the chair on
    September 20, 1989 and that remain in dispute between the parties at
    the time the board was established under this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} such other matters as the chair deems necessary or
    advisable; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fixing of a date for the expiry of the
    agreements referred to in paragraph (a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  conciliation board shall, within ninety days after all its members
  have been appointed or such longer period as the chair, after
  consultation with the parties, may allow,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} endeavour to mediate all matters referred to it
    pursuant to section 10 and to bring about agreement between the
    parties on those matters;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the board is unable to bring about agreement in
    respect of any such matter, hear the parties on the matter and
    render a decision in respect thereof;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fix the date for the expiry of the agreements
    referred to in paragraph 10(1)(a) or (2)(a), as the case may be,
    which date shall not be earlier than June 21, 1991, in the case of
    the agreements referred to in paragraph 10(1)(a), or June 30, 1991,
    in the case of the agreements referred to in paragraph 10(2)(a); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} report to the chair on the resolution of all such
    matters and on the date fixed by the board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A conciliation board has all the powers conferred by
  the [Public Service Staff Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-35) on a
  conciliation board established under that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of decision

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  decision of a conciliation board referred to in paragraph 11(1)(b)
  shall be set out in a form that will enable the decision to be
  incorporated in the agreements referred to in paragraph 10(1)(a) or
  (2)(a), as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation in collective agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a conciliation board reports to the chair
  pursuant to subsection 11(1), the agreements referred to in paragraph
  10(1)(a) or (2)(a), as the case may be, shall be deemed to be amended
  by the incorporation therein of any amendments thereto agreed to in
  writing by the parties in the course of the mediation, any decision of
  the board referred to in paragraph 11(1)(b) and the date fixed by the
  board for the expiry of the agreements, and each agreement, as so
  amended, constitutes a new group specific agreement or master
  agreement, as the case may be.

## [Amendment of Collective Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-252166 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of collective agreement

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Act shall
be construed so as to limit or restrict the rights of the parties to the
master agreement or a group specific agreement to agree to amend any
provision of the agreement as amended by or pursuant to this Act, other
than the date fixed for the expiry of the agreement, and to give effect
thereto.

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-252169 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence by individuals

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual who contravenes any provision of this Act is guilty of an
  offence punishable on summary conviction and is liable, for each day
  or part thereof during which the offence continues, to a fine

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} of not less than \$10,000 nor more than \$50,000,
    where the individual was acting in the capacity of an officer or
    representative of the employer or the bargaining agent when the
    offence was committed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} of not less than \$500 nor more than \$1,000, in
    any other case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence by bargaining agent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the bargaining agent contravenes any provision
  of this Act, it is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
  conviction and is liable, for each day or part thereof during which
  the offence continues, to a fine of not less than \$50,000 nor more
  than \$100,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
enforcement proceedings under this Act, the bargaining agent is deemed
to be a person.
