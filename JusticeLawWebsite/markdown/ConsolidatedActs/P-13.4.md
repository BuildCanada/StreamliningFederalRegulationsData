## Physical Activity and Sport Act {#physical-activity-and-sport-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2003, c. 2
:::

Assented to 2003-03-19

An Act to promote physical activity and sport
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the Government of Canada recognizes that physical activity and
sport are integral parts of Canadian culture and society and produce
benefits in terms of health, social cohesion, linguistic duality,
economic activity, cultural diversity and quality of life;

WHEREAS the Government of Canada wishes to increase awareness among
Canadians of the significant benefits of physical activity and the
practice of sport;

WHEREAS the Government of Canada wishes to encourage and assist
Canadians in increasing their level of physical activity and their
participation in sport;

WHEREAS the Government of Canada is committed to promoting physical
activity and sport, having regard to the principles set out in the
[Official Languages Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01);

AND WHEREAS the Government of Canada wishes to encourage cooperation
among the various governments, the physical activity and sport
communities and the private sector in the promotion of physical activity
and sport;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-392630 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Physical Activity and Sport Act](/eng/acts/P-13.4).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-392633 .Part}

Definition of [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the member or members of the
Queen's Privy Council for Canada designated by the Governor in Council
for the purposes of this Act.

## [Policies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-392636 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Physical activity policy

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The objectives of the
Government of Canada's policy regarding physical activity are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to promote physical activity as a fundamental element
  of health and well-being;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to encourage all Canadians to improve their health by
  integrating physical activity into their daily lives; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to assist in reducing barriers faced by all Canadians
  that prevent them from being active.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sport policy --- principles

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Government of Canada's policy regarding sport is founded on the
  highest ethical standards and values, including doping-free sport, the
  treatment of all persons with fairness and respect, the full and fair
  participation of all persons in sport and the fair, equitable,
  transparent and timely resolution of disputes in sport.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sport policy --- objectives

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The objectives of the Government of Canada's policy
  regarding sport are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to increase participation in the practice of sport
    and support the pursuit of excellence in sport; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to build capacity in the Canadian sport system.

## [Objects and Mandate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-392649 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects of Act and mandate of Minister

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The objects of this Act are
to encourage, promote and develop physical activity and sport in Canada.
The Minister may take any measures that the Minister considers
appropriate to further those objects, and in particular may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} undertake or assist in research or studies in respect
  of physical activity and sport;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} arrange for national and regional conferences in
  respect of physical activity and sport;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide for the recognition of achievement in respect
  of physical activity and sport by the grant or issue of certificates,
  citations or awards of merit;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} prepare and distribute information relating to
  physical activity and sport;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} assist, cooperate with and enlist the aid of any
  group interested in furthering the objects of this Act;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} coordinate federal initiatives related to the
  encouragement, promotion and development of physical activity and
  sport, particularly those initiatives related to the implementation of
  the Government of Canada's policy regarding sport, the hosting of
  major sporting events and the implementation of anti-doping measures,
  in cooperation with other departments or agencies of the Government of
  Canada;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} undertake or support any projects or programs related
  to physical activity or sport;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} provide assistance for the promotion and development
  of Canadian participation in national and international sport;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} provide for the training of coaches and any other
  resource persons to further the objects of this Act in relation to
  sport;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} provide bursaries or fellowships to assist
  individuals in pursuing excellence in sport;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} encourage the promotion of sport as a tool of
  individual and social development in Canada and, in cooperation with
  other countries, abroad;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} encourage the private sector to contribute
  financially to the development of sport;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} facilitate the participation of under-represented
  groups in the Canadian sport system;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} encourage provincial and territorial governments to
  promote and develop sport;

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} coordinate the Government of Canada's initiatives and
  efforts with respect to the staging and hosting of the Canada Games;
  and

- [(p)]{.lawlabel} encourage and support alternative dispute resolution
  for sport.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial assistance

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Act, the Minister may provide financial assistance in the form of grants
and contributions to any person, in accordance with Parts IV and VII of
the [Official Languages Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01).

## [Agreements and Arrangements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-392670 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contribution agreements

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister, with the approval of the Governor in Council, may enter into
  an agreement with any province or territory providing for the payment
  of contributions in respect of costs that they incur in undertaking
  programs designed to encourage, promote and develop physical activity
  or sport.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements to implement Canada's policy
  regarding sport

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may enter into an agreement or
  arrangement with any province or territory respecting the
  implementation of the Government of Canada's policy regarding sport.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}International agreements

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister, with the
approval of the Governor in Council, may enter into an agreement or
arrangement with the government of any foreign state in order to
encourage, promote and develop physical activity and sport.

## [Sport Dispute Resolution Centre of Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-392678 .Part}

### [Establishment of Centre]{.HTitleText2} {#h-392679 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Centre established

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  not-for-profit corporation is hereby established to be called the
  Sport Dispute Resolution Centre of Canada, in this Act referred to as
  "the Centre", which shall include a dispute resolution secretariat and
  a resource centre.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not an agent of Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Centre is not an agent of Her Majesty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not a departmental or Crown corporation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Centre is not a departmental corporation or a
  Crown corporation within the meaning of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of arbitrator or mediator

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the [Federal Courts
  Act](/eng/acts/F-7), the Centre or an arbitrator or mediator who
  provides services under the auspices of the Centre is not a federal
  board, commission or other tribunal within the meaning of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Both official languages to be used

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Centre shall offer its services to, and
  communicate with, the public in both official languages of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The head office of the Centre shall be at the place
  in Canada that is designated in the by-laws of the Centre.

### [Mission and Powers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-392695 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mission

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  mission of the Centre is to provide to the sport community

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a national alternative dispute resolution service
    for sport disputes; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} expertise and assistance regarding alternative
    dispute resolution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a sport dispute
  includes disputes among sport organizations and disputes between a
  sport organization and persons affiliated with it, including its
  members.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  carrying out its mission, the Centre has the capacity and powers of a
  natural person, including the power to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} use any funds that may be provided to it, subject
    to any terms on which the funds are provided;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into contracts or agreements in its own name;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} conduct studies with respect to the exercise of its
    powers; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} do any other things that are conducive to the
    fulfilment of its mission and the exercise of its powers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrictions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Centre

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} may not acquire or construct real property or
    immovables for valuable consideration, other than those required for
    its head office;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall expressly state in its contracts and
    agreements that it is entering into the contract or agreement on its
    own behalf;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} may not procure the incorporation of a corporation
    any shares of which, on incorporation, would be held by, on behalf
    of or in trust for the Centre; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} may not acquire shares of a corporation that, on
    acquisition, would be held by, on behalf of or in trust for the
    Centre.

### [Board of Directors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-392716 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The affairs and business
of the Centre shall be managed by a board of directors, and for that
purpose the board may exercise all the powers of the Centre.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  board of directors consists of not more than 12 directors, including
  the chairperson, and the executive director of the Centre who does not
  have a right to vote.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of provisions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 14, 16 and 18 do not apply to the executive
  director.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  directors shall be appointed by the Minister to hold office during
  good behaviour for any term of not more than three years that will
  ensure, as far as possible, the expiry in any one year of the terms of
  office of not more than one half of the directors. A director may be
  appointed to not more than two consecutive terms and may be removed by
  the Minister for cause.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment criteria

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The directors shall be chosen in accordance with
  guidelines that are established by the Minister in consultation with
  the sport community.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Guidelines

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The guidelines shall provide for a board of directors
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is composed of men and women committed to the
    promotion and development of sport who have the experience or
    capacity to enable the Centre to achieve its mission; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is representative of the sport community and of the
    diversity and bilingual character of Canadian society.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does not apply

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Guidelines referred to in subsection (3) are not
  statutory instruments for the purposes of the [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A director may not be
appointed as an officer of the Centre.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No remuneration

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The directors are not
entitled to be paid any remuneration, but are entitled to be paid such
reasonable travel and other expenses incurred by them in connection with
their duties or functions under this Act as may be fixed by the by-laws
of the Centre.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  board of directors may make by-laws with respect to the conduct and
  management of the affairs of the Centre and the carrying out of the
  duties and functions of the board under this Act, including by-laws
  providing for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the establishment of committees of the board of
    directors, including an executive committee, and the duties,
    functions and powers of the committees;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the duties, functions and powers of the chairperson
    and the officers of the Centre, including the executive director;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the appointment and remuneration of the officers of
    the Centre;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the delegation of any functions of the board of
    directors to an executive committee and the manner in which those
    functions are to be performed;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the mandate, duties and functions of the dispute
    resolution secretariat, the resource centre and any other part of
    the Centre;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the terms and conditions of eligibility for
    services provided by the Centre;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the establishment of a policy respecting the
    official languages of Canada that includes

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} principles governing the use of English and
      French by the staff of the Centre in their communications,
      provision of services and daily work, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a mechanism for resolving disputes related to
      the application of the policy;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the fixing of fees and charges to be paid for the
    services and facilities provided by the Centre or the determination
    of a manner for calculating those fees and charges;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the establishment of mediation and arbitration
    procedures for resolving sport disputes, including a mechanism for
    determining the manner in which the parties may select an arbitrator
    or mediator and the language, according to the needs of the parties,
    in which the parties may be heard and the decision rendered;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the qualifications for arbitrators or mediators;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the establishment of a code of ethics for
    directors, officers and employees of the Centre, as well as for
    arbitrators and mediators who provide dispute resolution services
    under the auspices of the Centre; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} personnel management, including terms and
    conditions of employment of persons employed by the Centre.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws available to the public

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of every by-law shall be kept at the head
  office of the Centre. Anyone is entitled, during the usual business
  hours of the Centre, to examine the by-laws and, on payment of a
  reasonable fee, to photocopy them in whole or in part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does not apply

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} By-laws made under subsection (1) are not statutory
  instruments for the purposes of the [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

### [Chairperson]{.HTitleText2} {#h-392760 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Minister, after
consulting with the directors, shall designate one of them as
chairperson to hold office during good behaviour for any term of not
more than three years. The chairperson may be designated for not more
than two consecutive terms and may be removed by the Minister for cause.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The chairperson shall
determine the times and places of the meetings of the board of directors
and presides at those meetings. The chairperson may perform any other
duties or functions that are assigned to the chairperson by the board of
directors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence, etc., of chairperson

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** If the chairperson is
absent or incapacitated or if the office of chairperson is vacant, the
board of directors may designate a director to exercise the powers and
perform the duties and functions of the chairperson during the absence,
incapacity or vacancy, but no person may be so designated for a period
exceeding 90 days without the approval of the Minister.

### [Executive Director]{.HTitleText2} {#h-392767 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The board of directors
shall appoint an executive director of the Centre.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The executive director is
the chief executive officer of the Centre and has, on behalf of the
board of directors, responsibility for the direction and management of
the business and day-to-day operations of the Centre.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence, etc., of executive director

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** If the executive director
is absent or incapacitated or if the office of executive director is
vacant, the chairperson may designate any person to exercise the powers
and perform the duties and functions of the executive director during
the absence, incapacity or vacancy, but no person may be so designated
for a period exceeding 90 days without the approval of the board of
directors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The executive director
may delegate to any person any power, duty or function conferred on the
executive director under this Act.

### [Personnel]{.HTitleText2} {#h-392776 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personnel

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The Centre may engage any
employees and any technical and professional advisers that it considers
necessary for the proper conduct of its activities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Directors, officers and
employees of the Centre are deemed not to be employees of the federal
public administration and, for the purposes of the [Public Service
Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), are deemed not to be employed in
the public service.

### [Audit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-392783 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audit committee

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  board of directors shall establish an audit committee consisting of at
  least three directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of audit committee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The audit committee shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} require the Centre to implement and maintain
    appropriate internal control procedures;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} review, evaluate and approve those internal control
    procedures;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} review the Centre's annual financial statements and
    report to the Centre before those statements are approved by the
    board of directors;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} meet with the Centre's auditor to discuss the
    Centre's annual financial statements and the auditor's report; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} meet with the Centre's auditor and the Centre's
    management to discuss the effectiveness of the internal control
    procedures.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the audit committee is of the opinion that there
  is any information that should be brought to the attention of the
  Minister, it shall make a report of that information to the Minister
  and furnish the board with a copy of the report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meeting of directors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The audit committee may call a meeting of the board
  of directors to consider any matter of concern to the committee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Technical assistance

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The audit committee may engage, on a temporary basis,
  the services of persons having technical or specialized knowledge to
  assist the committee in carrying out its duties under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Independent audit

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The accounts and
financial transactions of the Centre shall be audited annually by an
independent auditor designated by the board of directors, and a written
report of the audit shall be made to that board.

### [Arbitrators and Mediators]{.HTitleText2} {#h-392802 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibilities of the Centre

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The Centre shall ensure
that arbitrators and mediators who provide dispute resolution services
under the auspices of the Centre

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the qualifications established by its by-laws;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} are independent of the Centre; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} are, as a group, able to provide services in one or
  the other of the official languages of Canada or in both, according to
  the needs of the parties.

### [General Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-392808 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of care

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  director and officer of the Centre, in exercising their powers and
  performing their duties and functions, must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} act honestly and in good faith with a view to the
    best interests of the Centre;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} exercise the care, diligence and skill that a
    reasonably prudent person would exercise in comparable
    circumstances; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} comply with this Act and the by-laws of the Centre.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No exculpation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No provision in a contract or resolution relieves a
  director or officer from the duty to act in accordance with this Act,
  the regulations or the by-laws or relieves a director or officer from
  liability for a breach of any of them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reliance on statements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A director or officer is not liable for a breach of
  duty under subsection (1) if the director or officer relies in good
  faith on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} financial statements of the Centre represented to
    the director or officer by an officer of the Centre or in a written
    report of the auditor of the Centre as fairly reflecting the
    financial condition of the Centre; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a report of a lawyer, notary, accountant, engineer,
    appraiser or other person whose position or profession lends
    credibility to a statement made by that person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions of [Canada Business

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subsection 22(1) (form of corporate records);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} section 23 (corporate seal not needed to validate

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} subsection 108(2) (resignation of director);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} subsections 114(1), (2), (5) to (7) and (9)

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} section 116 (validity of acts of directors and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} section 117 (validity of directors' resolutions in

### [Corporate Plan and Annual Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-392849 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporate plan

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Centre shall prepare a corporate plan for each fiscal year, and
  deliver a copy of that plan to the Minister at least 30 days before
  the start of that fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scope and content of corporate plan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The corporate plan shall encompass all the business
  and activities of the Centre and shall include a statement of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Centre's objectives;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strategies that the Centre intends to use to
    achieve its objectives, including its operational and financial
    strategies and its human resource strategies; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Centre's operating and capital budgets for the
    next fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporate plan to be made public

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} After the corporate plan is delivered to the
  Minister, the Centre shall make the plan public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporate plan to be tabled

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause a copy of the corporate plan
  to be tabled in each House of Parliament on any of the first fifteen
  days on which that House is sitting after the Minister receives the
  plan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  chairperson of the board of directors shall, within four months after
  the end of each fiscal year, deliver a report on the operations of the
  Centre in that fiscal year to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the financial statements of the Centre and the
    report of the auditor respecting those statements;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the Centre's corporate plan; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} information about the Centre's performance with
    respect to the objectives established in the corporate plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The total remuneration that each officer receives in
  a fiscal year from the Centre, including any reimbursements or
  monetary benefits, and the amount of any reimbursements or monetary
  benefits that each director receives in a fiscal year from the Centre,
  shall be set out in the annual financial statements for that year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution of report

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} After its annual report is delivered to the Minister,
  the Centre shall make the report public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report to be tabled

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause a copy of the annual report
  to be tabled in each House of Parliament on any of the first fifteen
  days on which that House is sitting after the Minister receives the
  report.

- 2003, c. 2, s. 33
- 2015, c. 3, s. 141(F)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public meeting

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  60 days after the delivery of its annual report to the Minister, the
  Centre shall convene a public meeting at a city in Canada selected by
  the Centre to consider the report and other matters relating to the
  Centre's activities during the current fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of meeting

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At least 30 days before the date of a meeting
  convened under subsection (1), the Centre shall give notice of the
  time and place of the meeting in accordance with its by-laws.

### [Dissolution]{.HTitleText2} {#h-392883 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may order dissolution

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by order, dissolve the Centre

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the Centre has failed to make by-laws in
    accordance with paragraphs 17(1)(e), (g) and (i) to (k) within one
    year after section 9 comes into force;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Minister is satisfied that the Centre has
    failed, for a period of one year, to carry on its affairs and
    business;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the Minister, on the expiry of any period of
    five years after the coming into force of section 9, after having
    made an evaluation of the Centre, is satisfied that the Centre is
    not fulfilling its mission or is no longer necessary; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} on petition by the Centre supported by a resolution
    passed by at least two thirds of the directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debts and liabilities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the dissolution of the Centre, any
  property of the Centre that remains after the payment of its debts and
  liabilities, or after the making of adequate provision for the payment
  of its debts and liabilities, may be transferred to any person or
  institution having a mission similar to that of the Centre that the
  Minister specifies in the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dissolution

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister dissolves the Centre, the affairs of
  the Centre shall be wound up in accordance with this section and any
  regulations made under paragraph 36(b).
