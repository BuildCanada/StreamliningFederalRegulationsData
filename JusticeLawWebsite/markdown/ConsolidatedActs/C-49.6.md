## Crown Corporations Dissolution or Transfer Authorization Act {#crown-corporations-dissolution-or-transfer-authorization-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1991, c. 38
:::

Assented to 1991-11-26

An Act to provide for the dissolution or transfer of certain Crown
corporations and to amend certain Acts in consequence thereof
::::

:::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-135560 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Crown Corporations Dissolution or Transfer Authorization
Act](/eng/acts/C-49.6).

## [Canada Harbour Place Corporation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-135563 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization of disposal

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Transport is hereby authorized to sell or otherwise
  dispose of, on such terms and conditions as are approved by the
  Governor in Council, any or all shares of Canada Harbour Place
  Corporation held by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vesting in Corporation

  [[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#C-49.6_en_1){#C-49.6_en_1-ID0EBFBA}[(2)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of effecting a disposal authorized by subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all property, rights and interests of Her Majesty
    in right of Canada held by Canada Harbour Place Corporation
    immediately before the coming into force of this subsection, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all obligations and liabilities of Her Majesty in
    right of Canada incurred by Canada Harbour Place Corporation and
    existing immediately before the coming into force of this subsection

  are hereby vested in the Corporation.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#C-49.6_en_1-ID0EBFBA){#C-49.6_en_1}\[Note:
    Subsection 2(2) in force March 17, 1993, *see* SI/93-46.\]
    :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization of disposal of assets

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Canada Harbour Place Corporation is hereby authorized
  to sell or otherwise dispose of all or substantially all of the assets
  of the Corporation, including shares of any other corporation held by
  the Corporation, on such terms and conditions as are approved by the
  Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors cease to hold office

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The directors of Canada
Harbour Place Corporation cease to hold office at the close of the first
meeting of shareholders of the Corporation held after the date on which
any or all shares of the Corporation are disposed of pursuant to
subsection 2(1).

into force, 2008, c. 20, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

## [Canadian Livestock Feed Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-135586 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation dissolved

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Canadian Livestock Feed Board is hereby dissolved.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}References

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every reference to the Canadian Livestock Feed Board
  in any deed, contract or other document executed by the Board in its
  own name shall, unless the context otherwise requires, be read as a
  reference to Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Closing out affairs

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister designated for the purposes of the
  [Livestock Feed Assistance Act](/eng/acts/L-10) may do all things and
  perform all acts necessary for or incidental to closing out the
  affairs of the Canadian Livestock Feed Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement of legal proceedings

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  action, suit or other legal proceeding in respect of an obligation or
  liability incurred by the Canadian Livestock Feed Board, or by the
  Minister referred to in subsection 10(3) in closing out the affairs of
  the Board, may be brought against Her Majesty in right of Canada in
  any court that would have had jurisdiction if the action, suit or
  other legal proceeding had been brought against the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of legal proceedings

  [[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#C-49.6_en_2){#C-49.6_en_2-ID0EBCAA}[(2)]{.lawlabel} Any
  action, suit or other legal proceeding to which the Canadian Livestock
  Feed Board is party pending in any court on the coming into force of
  this section may be continued by or against Her Majesty in right of
  Canada in like manner and to the same extent as it could have been
  continued by or against the Board.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#C-49.6_en_2-ID0EBCAA){#C-49.6_en_2}\[Note:
    Section 11 in force November 26, 1991, *see* SI/91-161.\]
    :::

## [Canadian Patents and Development Limited]{.HTitleText1} {#h-135600 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procurement of dissolution

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Industry, Science and Technology is hereby authorized to
  procure the dissolution of Canadian Patents and Development Limited.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed before coming into force, 2008, c. 20, s.
  3\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Closing out affairs

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 34, the Minister of Industry,
  Science and Technology may do all things and perform all acts
  necessary for or incidental to closing out the affairs of Canadian
  Patents and Development Limited.

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-33-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 33"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of administration of property

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may transfer, or direct to be transferred, the
  control, management and administration of any property, rights or
  interests of Her Majesty in right of Canada held by Canadian Patents
  and Development Limited from the Corporation to any Minister,
  department or agency of the Government of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of obligations and liabilities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may transfer, or direct to be
  transferred, any obligations or liability incurred by Canadian Patents
  and Development Limited from the Corporation to any Minister,
  department or agency of the Government of Canada.

## [Harbourfront Corporation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-135613 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procurement of dissolution

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Public Works is hereby authorized to procure, on behalf of
  Her Majesty in right of Canada, the dissolution of Harbourfront
  Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Closing out affairs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Public Works may do all things and
  perform all acts necessary for or incidental to closing out the
  affairs of Harbourfront Corporation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direction to dispose of assets

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  Harbourfront Corporation is dissolved pursuant to section 40, the
  Minister of Public Works may direct the Corporation to sell or
  otherwise dispose of any or all of its assets, including shares of any
  other corporation held by the Corporation, on such terms and
  conditions as are approved by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions not applicable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 189(3) to (9) of the [Canada Business
  Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44) do not apply in respect of any sale
  or other disposal of assets directed pursuant to subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direction to pay proceeds

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Public Works may direct Harbourfront
  Corporation to pay all or any part of the proceeds of any disposition
  of its assets pursuant to a direction under subsection (1) or
  otherwise to such person and on such terms and conditions as are
  approved by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization of disposal of assets

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} On receiving a direction given under subsection (1)
  or (3), Harbourfront Corporation is hereby authorized to sell or
  dispose of its assets in accordance with the direction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with direction

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Harbourfront Corporation shall comply with any
  direction given under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Compliance by Harbourfront Corporation with a
  direction given under this section is deemed to be in the best
  interests of the Corporation and the directors of the Corporation are
  not accountable for any consequences arising from that compliance if
  they act in accordance with section 115 of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1991, c. 38, s. 41
- 1994, c. 24, s. 34(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations transferred

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** All obligations and
liabilities of Harbourfront Corporation are deemed to be obligations and
liabilities of Her Majesty in right of Canada.
