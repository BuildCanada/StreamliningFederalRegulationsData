## Veterans Review and Appeal Board Act {#veterans-review-and-appeal-board-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1995, c. 18
:::

Assented to 1995-06-22

An Act to establish the Veterans Review and Appeal Board, to amend the
Pension Act, to make consequential amendments to other Acts and to
repeal the Veterans Appeal Board Act
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-463464 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Veterans Review and Appeal Board Act](/eng/acts/V-1.6).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-463467 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Veterans Review and Appeal
    Board established by section 4; ([Tribunal]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Bureau]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Veterans

    the purposes of this Act; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The provisions of this Act
and of any other Act of Parliament or of any regulations made under this
or any other Act of Parliament conferring or imposing jurisdiction,
powers, duties or functions on the Board shall be liberally construed
and interpreted to the end that the recognized obligation of the people
and Government of Canada to those who have served their country so well
and to their dependants may be fulfilled.

## [Establishment of Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-463479 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of Board

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** There is established an
independent board, to be known as the Veterans Review and Appeal Board,
consisting of not more than 25 permanent members to be appointed by the
Governor in Council and any number of temporary members that are
appointed under section 6.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 18, s. 4
- 2013, c. 40, s. 276
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permanent members

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  permanent member holds office during good behaviour.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A permanent member shall be appointed for a term not
  exceeding ten years and is eligible to be reappointed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary members

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may appoint temporary members of the Board
  whenever, in the opinion of the Governor in Council, the workload of
  the Board so requires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tenure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A temporary member holds office during good
  behaviour.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A temporary member shall be appointed for a term not
  exceeding two years and is eligible to be reappointed for one
  additional term.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting after ceasing to hold office

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who has resigned or otherwise ceased to hold office as a member may,
  at the request of the Chairperson, at any time within eight weeks
  after that event, make or take part in the disposition of any appeal,
  review, reference or other matter in which the person became engaged
  while holding that office, and a person performing duties under this
  subsection is deemed to be a temporary member of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition where member unable to take
  part

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a person to whom subsection (1) applies or any
  other member by whom a matter has been heard is unable to take part in
  the disposition of the matter or has died, the remaining members, if
  any, who heard the matter may make the disposition and, for that
  purpose, are deemed to constitute the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall designate a Chairperson and a Deputy
  Chairperson from among the permanent members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chief executive officer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson is the chief executive officer of the
  Board and has supervision over and direction of the work of the Board
  including the allocation of work among its members, the conduct of its
  work, the management of its internal affairs and the duties of its
  staff.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson shall make such reports to the
  Minister as the Minister may require respecting the use of the
  resources allocated to the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting Chairperson

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Chairperson is absent or unable to act or the
  office of Chairperson is vacant, the Deputy Chairperson shall act as
  Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting Chairperson

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If both the Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson are
  absent or unable to act or both offices are vacant, a member
  designated by the Minister shall act as Chairperson.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Full-time occupation

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A member shall perform
their duties under this Act on a full-time basis and shall not hold any
office or engage in any occupation inconsistent with those duties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and allowances

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member shall be paid such remuneration and allowances as may be fixed
  by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Travel and living expenses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member is entitled to be paid reasonable travel and
  living expenses incurred by them while absent from their ordinary
  place of work in connection with the work of the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member is deemed to be an employee for the purposes of the [Government
  Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and to be employed in the
  federal public administration for the purposes of any regulations made
  under section 9 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member is deemed to be employed in the public
  service for the purposes of the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

::: HistoricalNote

- 2003, c. 22, s. 224(E), 225(E)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the
Board shall be at such place in Canada as the Governor in Council may
designate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers and employees

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Such officers and
employees as are necessary for the proper conduct of the work of the
Board shall be appointed in accordance with the [Public Service
Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).

## [Powers, Duties and Functions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-463530 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Board and each member
have, with respect to the carrying out of the Board's duties and
functions under this Act, all the powers of a commissioner appointed
under Part I of the [Inquiries Act](/eng/acts/I-11).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to records

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Subject to any other Act
of Parliament and any regulations made under any other Act of
Parliament, the Board may inspect the records of the Department of
Veterans Affairs and all material relating to any proceeding before the
Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 18, s. 15
- 2000, c. 34, s. 94(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional powers, duties and functions

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall exercise
such powers and shall perform such duties and functions as are conferred
or imposed on it by or pursuant to any other Act of Parliament or any
order of the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Panels

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Subject to subsections
19(1), 27(1), 34(2) and 44(2), the Chairperson may establish panels of
one or more members to exercise any power or perform any duty or
function of the Board designated by the Chairperson.

## [Review]{.HTitleText1} {#h-463541 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusive jurisdiction

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Board has full and
exclusive juris­diction to hear, determine and deal with all applications
for review that may be made to the Board under the [Pension
Act](/eng/acts/P-6) or the [Veterans Well-being Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8),
and all matters related to those applications.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 18, s. 18
- 2005, c. 21, s. 110
- 2017, c. 20, s. 292

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review panels

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), an
  application for review shall be heard, determined and dealt with by a
  review panel consisting of not fewer than two members or, with the
  consent of the applicant, one member designated by the Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to establish review panel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson, or any member to whom the
  Chairperson has delegated the authority, may refuse to establish a
  review panel to hear an application for review of a decision
  concerning the amount of an award under the [Pension
  Act](/eng/acts/P-6), or the amount of a critical injury benefit, pain
  and suffering compensation, additional pain and suffering
  compensation, a death benefit, a clothing allowance or a detention
  benefit under Part 3 of the [Veterans Well-being
  Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8), if the Chairperson or member, as the case may
  be, considers the application to be such that no reasonable review
  panel could dispose of it in a manner favourable to the applicant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written and oral submissions

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** An applicant may make a
written submission to the review panel or may appear before it, in
person or by a representative, to present evidence and arguments.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition of application

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A review panel may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} affirm, vary or reverse the decision of the Minister
  being reviewed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} refer any matter back to the Minister for
  reconsideration; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} refer any matter not dealt with in the decision back
  to the Minister for a decision.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision to be made as soon as possible

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  review panel shall make its decision as soon as possible and shall
  notify the applicant of the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision of majority

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A decision of a majority of the members of a review
  panel is a decision of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence of majority decision

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the absence of a majority decision, the decision
  most favourable to the applicant is the decision of the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reconsideration of decisions

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  review panel may, on its own motion, reconsider a decision made by it
  under section 21 or this section and may either confirm the decision
  or amend or rescind the decision if it determines that an error was
  made with respect to any finding of fact or the interpretation of any
  law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board may exercise powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may exercise the powers of a review panel
  under subsection (1) if the members of the review panel have ceased to
  hold office as members.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicant and witnesses to be paid
expenses, etc.

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** An applicant and each
witness called by an applicant who attends a hearing by a review panel
are entitled to be paid

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} travel and living expenses incurred in attending the
  hearing, in accordance with regulations made in relation to veterans
  health care under section 5 of the [Department of Veterans Affairs
  Act](/eng/acts/V-1); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a witness who is a medical
  practitioner, such attendance fee as may be fixed by the Treasury
  Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** An applicant who is
dissatisfied with a decision made under section 21 or 23 may appeal the
decision to the Board.

## [Appeals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-463580 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusive jurisdiction

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The Board has full and
exclusive jurisdiction to hear, determine and deal with all appeals that
may be made to the Board under section 25 or under the [War Veterans
Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3) or any other Act of Parliament, and all
matters related to those appeals.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal panel

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appeal shall be heard, determined and dealt with by an appeal panel
  consisting of not fewer than three members designated by the
  Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member of a review panel may not sit on an appeal
  panel that has been established to hear an appeal of a decision made
  by that review panel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written and oral submissions

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), an
  appellant may make a written submission to the appeal panel or may
  appear before it, in person or by representative and at their own
  expense, to present evidence and oral arguments.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documented evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Only documented evidence may be submitted under
  subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition of appeals

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appeal panel may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} affirm, vary or reverse the decision being
    appealed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} refer any matter back to the person or review panel
    that made the decision being appealed for reconsideration,
    re-hearing or further investigation; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} refer any matter not dealt with in the decision
    back to that person or review panel for a decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where matter cannot be referred to
  review panel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the members of a review panel have ceased to
  hold office or for any other reason a matter cannot be referred to
  that review panel under paragraph (1)(b) or (c), the appeal panel may
  refer the matter to the Chairperson who shall establish a new review
  panel in accordance with subsection 19(1) to consider, hear,
  investigate or decide the matter, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Questions of interpretation

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Where the appellant
raises a question of interpretation relating to this Act, to the
[Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6), to Part 3 of the [Veterans Well-being
Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8), to any other Act of Parliament pursuant to which
an appeal may be taken to the Board or to any regulations made under any
such Act and the appeal panel is of the opinion that the question is not
trivial, frivolous or vexatious, it shall notify the prescribed persons
or organizations and give them an opportunity to present argument on the
question before it makes its decision.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 18, s. 30
- 2005, c. 21, s. 112
- 2017, c. 20, s. 292
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision of majority

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** A decision of the
majority of members of an appeal panel is a decision of the Board and is
final and binding.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reconsideration of decisions

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding section 31, an
  appeal panel may, on its own motion, reconsider a decision made by it
  under subsection 29(1) or this section and may either confirm the
  decision or amend or rescind the decision if it determines that an
  error was made with respect to any finding of fact or the
  interpretation of any law, or may do so on application if the person
  making the application alleges that an error was made with respect to
  any finding of fact or the interpretation of any law or if new
  evidence is presented to the appeal panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board may exercise powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may exercise the powers of an appeal panel
  under subsection (1) if the members of the appeal panel have ceased to
  hold office as members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other sections applicable

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 28 and 31 apply, with such modifications as
  the circumstances require, with respect to an application made under
  subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal to Tax Court of Canada

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding section 31, an
  appeal lies to the Tax Court of Canada from any decision of an appeal
  panel as to income or as to the source of income, for the purposes of
  the [War Veterans Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3) or Part XI of the
  [Civilian War-related Benefits Act](/eng/acts/C-31), of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person or their spouse, or both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person or their common-law partner, or both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of [[common-law
  partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In paragraph (1)(b), [[common-law
  partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} has the same meaning as in subsection
  2(1) of the [War Veterans Allowance Act](/eng/acts/W-3).

## [Compassionate Awards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-463623 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for compassionate award

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who has been refused an award under the [Pension
  Act](/eng/acts/P-6) or a critical injury benefit, a disability award,
  pain and suffering compensation, additional pain and suffering
  compensation, a death benefit, a clothing allowance or a detention
  benefit under Part 3 of the [Veterans Well-being
  Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8), and who has exhausted all procedures for
  review and appeal under this Act may apply to the Board for a
  compassionate award.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Panel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for a compassionate award shall be
  heard, determined and dealt with by a panel consisting of not fewer
  than three members designated by the Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Granting of compassionate award

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A panel may grant a compassionate award if it
  considers the case to be specially meritorious and the applicant is
  unqualified to receive an award under the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6)
  or a critical injury benefit, a disability award, pain and suffering
  compensation, additional pain and suffering compensation, a death
  benefit, a clothing allowance or a detention benefit under Part 3 of
  the [Veterans Well-being Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The amount of a compassionate award shall be any sum
  that the panel may fix, but may not exceed the amount to which the
  applicant would have been entitled if the applicant's claim under the
  [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) or the [Veterans Well-being
  Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8) had been upheld.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review by Minister

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may from time to time vary the amount of
  a compassionate award that is subject to being varied in accordance
  with the applicant's dependent condition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The amount of a compassionate award shall be adjusted
  at the same times and by the same percentage as the basic pension is
  adjusted under subsection 75(1) of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Death of recipient

  [(6.1)]{.lawlabel} On the death of a person to whom a compassionate
  award has been granted, the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) applies in
  respect of a survivor or surviving child of that person in the same
  manner as it would apply if the compassionate award had been an award
  within the meaning of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of
  [[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(6.2)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (6.1),
  [[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
  have the same meaning as in the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reconsideration of decisions

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Board may, on its own motion, reconsider a
  decision made by it under this section and may either confirm the
  decision or amend or rescind the decision if it determines that an
  error was made with respect to any finding of fact or the
  interpretation of any law, or may do so on application if new evidence
  is presented to the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[compassionate
  award]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[compassionate
  award]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a compassionate pension, allowance
  or supplementary award.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-463647 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** In all proceedings under
this Act, an applicant or appellant may choose to be represented by a
pensions advocate from the Bureau, by a service bureau of a veterans'
organization or, at the person's own expense, by any other
representative of the person's choice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Place and time of sittings

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall sit at such places in Canada and at such times as may be
  fixed by the Chairperson, having regard to the convenience of the
  applicant and the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearings to be public

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every hearing before the Board shall be open to the
  public except where the applicant or appellant requests that the
  hearing be closed and the Board is of the opinion that a closed
  hearing would not be contrary to the public interest.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 18, s. 36
- 1999, c. 10, s. 40
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Questions of interpretation

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister, the Chief Pensions Advocate, any veterans' organization
  incorporated by or under an Act of Parliament or any interested person
  may refer to the Board for a hearing and decision on any question of
  interpretation relating to this Act, to the [Pension
  Act](/eng/acts/P-6), to Part 3 of the [Veterans Well-being
  Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8), to any other Act of Parliament pursuant to
  which an appeal may be taken to the Board or to any regulations made
  under any such Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before making a decision on a question referred to
  it, the Board shall notify the prescribed persons or organizations and
  give them an opportunity to present argument on the question.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to hear trivial questions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board may refuse to hear and decide any question
  referred to it under this section that it considers to be trivial,
  frivolous or vexatious.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 18, s. 37
- 2005, c. 21, s. 114
- 2017, c. 20, s. 292
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Medical opinion

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may obtain independent medical advice for the purposes of any
  proceeding under this Act and may require an applicant or appellant to
  undergo any medical examination that the Board may direct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of intention

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before accepting as evidence any medical advice or
  report on an examination obtained pursuant to subsection (1), the
  Board shall notify the applicant or appellant of its intention to do
  so and give them an opportunity to present argument on the issue.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules of evidence

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** In all proceedings under
this Act, the Board shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} draw from all the circumstances of the case and all
  the evidence presented to it every reasonable inference in favour of
  the applicant or appellant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} accept any uncontradicted evidence presented to it by
  the applicant or appellant that it considers to be credible in the
  circumstances; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} resolve in favour of the applicant or appellant any
  doubt, in the weighing of evidence, as to whether the applicant or
  appellant has established a case.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expeditious proceedings

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** All proceedings before
the Board shall be dealt with as informally and expeditiously as the
circumstances and considerations of fairness permit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** No action or other
proceeding lies against any person for or in respect of anything done,
reported or said in good faith in any proceedings before the Board or
anything reported or said in good faith in any material, information or
report made or furnished by any person at the request of the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inquiries

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson may recommend to the Minister that an inquiry be held to
  determine whether a member should be subject to disciplinary or
  remedial measures for any reason set out in any of paragraphs 43(2)(a)
  to (d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judge to conduct inquiry

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister considers it appropriate that an
  inquiry be held, a judge, supernumerary judge or former judge of the
  Federal Court of Canada, the Federal Court of Appeal or the Federal
  Court, in this section and section 43 referred to as a "judge", shall
  conduct the inquiry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A judge conducting an inquiry has all the powers,
  rights and privileges that are vested in a superior court and, without
  restricting the generality of the foregoing, has the power

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to issue to any person a summons requiring them to
    appear at the time and place mentioned in the summons to testify
    with respect to all matters within their knowledge relative to the
    inquiry and to bring and produce any thing that they have or control
    relative to the inquiry; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to administer oaths and examine any person on oath.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inquiry public

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (5) and (6), an inquiry shall
  be conducted in public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A judge conducting an inquiry may, on application,
  take any measures or make any order that the judge considers necessary
  to ensure the confidentiality of the inquiry if the judge is satisfied
  that financial or personal or other matters may be disclosed and are
  of such a nature that the desirability of avoiding public disclosure
  of those matters in the interest of any person affected or in the
  public interest outweighs the desirability of adhering to the
  principle that the inquiry be conducted in public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where the judge considers it appropriate to do so,
  the judge may take any measures or make any order that the judge
  considers necessary to ensure the confidentiality of any hearing held
  in respect of an application referred to in subsection (5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules of evidence

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A judge conducting an inquiry is not bound by any
  legal or technical rules of evidence and, in any proceedings of the
  inquiry, the judge may receive and base a decision on evidence adduced
  in the proceedings and considered credible or trustworthy in the
  circumstances of the case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to be heard

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Every member in respect of whom an inquiry is held
  shall be given reasonable notice of the subject-matter of the inquiry
  and of the time and place of any hearing and shall be given an
  opportunity, in person or by counsel, to be heard at the hearing, to
  cross-examine witnesses and to adduce evidence.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1995, c. 18, s. 42
- 2002, c. 8, s. 179
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report of inquiry

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  an inquiry has been completed, the judge who conducted the inquiry
  shall submit a report of the conclusions of the inquiry to the
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an inquiry has been held and, in the opinion of
  the judge who conducted the inquiry, the member in respect of whom the
  inquiry was held

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has become incapacitated from the due execution of
    their office by reason of infirmity,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of misconduct,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has failed in the due execution of their office, or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} has been placed, by conduct or otherwise, in a
    position that is incompatible with the due execution of their
    office,

  the judge may, in the report of the inquiry, recommend that the member
  be suspended without pay or removed from office or may recommend that
  such remedial measure as the judge considers necessary be taken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Governor in Council may suspend or
  remove

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister receives a report under subsection
  (1), the Minister shall send a copy of the report to the Governor in
  Council, who may suspend the member to whom the report relates without
  pay, remove the member from office or take any remedial measure.

## [Rules and Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-463713 .Part}

  the proceedings, practice and procedures of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

  a quorum for the purpose of making rules under subsection (1).

  connection with an application or appeal;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the information to be included in the
  Board's decisions;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} governing the giving of notice of the Board's
  decisions; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} prescribing persons or organizations for the purposes
  of sections 30 and 37.
