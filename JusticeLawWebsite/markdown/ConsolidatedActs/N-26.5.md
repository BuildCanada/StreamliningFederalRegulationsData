## Northumberland Strait Crossing Act {#northumberland-strait-crossing-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1993, c. 43
:::

Assented to 1993-06-23

An Act respecting the Northumberland Strait Crossing
::::

::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-366105 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the Northumberland Strait Crossing Act.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-366108 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement entered into
      pursuant to section 4; ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[crossing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[crossing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a fixed structure that
      provides a means of crossing the Northumberland Strait and
      includes all facilities and appurtenances related to that fixed
      structure; ([ouvrage de franchissement]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"} ou [ouvrage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Public
      Works and Government Services. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of Consumer Price Index

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, the consumer price
  index for any period shall be determined in accordance with an
  agreement.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1993, c. 43, s. 2
- 1994, c. 26, s. 47(F)
- 1996, c. 16, s. 60
:::

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-366119 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada.

## [Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-366122 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to enter into agreements

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, on behalf of Her Majesty in right of Canada, enter into
  one or more agreements in respect of the crossing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The agreements entered into under this section may
  include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provisions respecting the design, maintenance,
    financing, development, construction and operation of the crossing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provisions respecting the payment of the annual
    subsidy referred to in section 7;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} undertakings in relation to industrial and
    employment benefits;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provisions respecting the tolls, fees or other
    charges that may be imposed in respect of the crossing; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} such other terms and conditions as the Minister
    considers desirable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to carry out agreements

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may do such things as the Minister considers appropriate to
  carry out an agreement or to protect the interests or enforce the
  rights of Her Majesty under an agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Without limiting the generality of subsection (1),
  the Minister may, with respect to an agreement,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} accept and hold on behalf of Her Majesty any
    security granted to Her Majesty under or pursuant to the agreement
    or any security granted in substitution therefor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} release or realize on any security referred to in
    paragraph (a);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} acquire shares of a corporation in trust for Her
    Majesty; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} acquire on behalf of Her Majesty the whole or any
    portion of any interest held in respect of the crossing by any party
    to the agreement and, for that purpose, enter into such agreements
    or arrangements as the Minister considers appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lease of federal real property

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may lease
land, and any other property belonging to Her Majesty in right of Canada
or of which Her Majesty has the power to dispose, that the Minister
considers is required for the construction or operation of the crossing,
on such terms and conditions as the Minister considers desirable.

## [Annual Subsidy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-366144 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriation

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), there shall be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue
  Fund, for the purpose of paying an annual subsidy in respect of the
  crossing, and the operation thereof in accordance with an agreement,
  in each of the thirty-five fiscal years beginning with the fiscal year
  determined for that purpose in accordance with the agreement, on the
  dates set out in the agreement, an amount not exceeding an aggregate
  sum of forty-two million in 1992 dollars, adjusted, in accordance with
  the agreement, using the consumer price index.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Number of fiscal years

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the date fixed by the agreement referred to in
  subsection (1) as the date on which the first payment on account of
  the annual subsidy is to be made falls in a month other than April in
  a fiscal year, there shall be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue
  Fund, for the purpose referred to in that subsection, in each of the
  thirty-six fiscal years beginning with the fiscal year determined for
  that purpose in accordance with the agreement, on the dates set out in
  the agreement, an amount not exceeding

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of both the first fiscal year and the
    thirty-sixth fiscal year, an aggregate sum of forty-two million, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of each of the remaining fiscal years,
    an aggregate sum of forty-two million,

  in 1992 dollars, adjusted, in accordance with the agreement, using the
  consumer price index.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No set-off of subsidy

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding any other
Act of Parliament, where the person to whom the annual subsidy is
payable under an agreement, or any assignee of that person under the
agreement or any other agreement entered into pursuant to the agreement,
is indebted to Her Majesty in right of Canada, the amount of the
indebtedness may not be retained by way of deduction from or set-off
against any sum of money that may be due or payable by Her Majesty to
that person or assignee on account of the annual subsidy.

## [Tolls]{.HTitleText1} {#h-366155 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tolls

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Transport
may, with the approval of the Governor in Council, make regulations
prescribing, or prescribing the manner of calculating, the tolls, fees
or other charges in respect of the use of the crossing that may be
imposed on or after the lease of land and other property referred to in
section 6 terminates in accordance with the terms and conditions of the
lease.
