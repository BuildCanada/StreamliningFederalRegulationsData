## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Canada Emergency Business Account]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1350812 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[CEBA
loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** In this Part, [[CEBA
loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a loan provided by a Canadian financial
institution as part of the Canada Emergency Business Account program
established by Export Development Canada pursuant to an authorization
made under subsection 23(1) of the [Export Development
Act](/eng/acts/E-20).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation or prescription period

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (4) and (6),
  any action or proceedings to recover money owing under a CEBA loan
  must be taken no more than six years after the date of the default.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Default

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the date of the
  default is the earlier of the day on which the person making the claim
  first knew, or ought reasonably to have known, that the default had
  occurred.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction, set-off or compensation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Money owing by a person under a CEBA loan may be
  recovered at any time by way of deduction from, set-off against or
  compensation against any sum of money that may be payable by Her
  Majesty in right of Canada to the person, other than an amount payable
  under section 122.61 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment of liability

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a person's liability for money owing under a CEBA
  loan is acknowledged in accordance with subsection (5) at any time
  during or after the limitation or prescription period described in
  subsection (1), an action or proceedings to recover the money may,
  subject to subsection (6), be brought within six years after the date
  of the acknowledgment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of acknowledgment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An acknowledgment of liability means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a promise to pay the money owing, made by the
    person or their agent or mandatary or other representative;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgment of the money owing, made by the
    person or their agent or mandatary or other representative, whether
    or not a promise to pay can be implied from it and whether or not it
    contains a refusal to pay;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a part payment by the person or their agent or
    mandatary or other representative of the money owing; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgment of the money owing, made in the
    course of proceedings under the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency
    Act](/eng/acts/B-3) or any other legislation dealing with the
    payment of debts, by the person, their agent or mandatary or other
    representative or the trustee or administrator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation or prescription period
  suspended

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The running of a limitation or prescription period is
  suspended during any period in which it is prohibited to commence or
  continue an action or other proceedings against the person to recover
  money owing under a CEBA loan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement proceedings

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply in respect of an action
  or proceeding relating to the execution, renewal or enforcement of a
  judgment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** Section 42 applies
irrespective of whether

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of default is before the day on which this
  Part comes into force or on or after that day; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the limitation or prescription period applicable
  before that day has expired.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[School Ventilation Improvement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1350838 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum payment of \$100 million

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Finance may make the following payments to the provinces
  and territories for the purpose of supporting ventilation improvement
  projects in schools:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to Ontario, a sum not exceeding \$36,226,000;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to Quebec, a sum not exceeding \$21,023,000;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to Nova Scotia, a sum not exceeding \$2,674,000;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to New Brunswick, a sum not exceeding \$2,294,000;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} to Manitoba, a sum not exceeding \$4,465,000;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} to British Columbia, a sum not exceeding
    \$11,906,000;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} to Prince Edward Island, a sum not exceeding
    \$898,000;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} to Saskatchewan, a sum not exceeding \$3,979,000;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to Alberta, a sum not exceeding \$12,983,000;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} to Newfoundland and Labrador, a sum not exceeding
    \$1,631,000;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} to Yukon, a sum not exceeding \$607,000;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} to the Northwest Territories, a sum not exceeding
    \$635,000; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} to Nunavut, a sum not exceeding \$679,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of C.R.F.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any amount payable under subsection (1) may be paid
  by the Minister of Finance out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund, at
  the times and in the manner, and on any terms and conditions, that the
  Minister of Finance considers appropriate.

## [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Proof of Vaccination]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1350857 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum payment of \$300 million

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Health may make payments to the provinces and territories
  not exceeding \$300 million in total for the purpose of supporting
  their coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) proof-of-vaccination
  initiatives, with the amount of each payment to be determined by the
  Minister of Health.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of C.R.F.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any amount payable under subsection (1) may be paid
  by the Minister of Health out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund, at the
  times and in the manner, and on any terms and conditions, that the
  Minister of Health considers appropriate.

## [PART 6]{.HLabel1}[COVID-19 Tests]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1350863 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of C.R.F.

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Health may make payments, the total of which may not
  exceed \$1.72 billion, out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund for any
  expenses incurred on or after April 1, 2021 in relation to coronavirus
  disease 2019 (COVID-19) tests.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within three months after the day on which this
  section comes into force, and every three months after that, the
  Minister of Health must prepare a report setting out the number of
  payments made and the total amount paid under subsection (1) should
  any payments be made under the Act during that period, the number of
  tests purchased and how they were distributed, and cause it to be
  tabled in each House of Parliament on any of the first 15 days on
  which that House is sitting after the report is completed.
