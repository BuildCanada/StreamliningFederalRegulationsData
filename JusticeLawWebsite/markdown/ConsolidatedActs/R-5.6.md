## Representation Act, 1985 {#representation-act-1985 .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1986, c. 8
:::

Assented to 1986-03-04

An Act to amend the Constitution Act, 1867 and the Electoral Boundaries
Readjustment Act and to provide for certain matters in relation to the
1981 decennial census
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427447 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Representation Act, 1985](/eng/acts/R-5.6).

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Constitution Act, 1867]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427450 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** \[Amendment\]

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title and citation

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Part may be cited as
the [Constitution Act, 1985 (Representation)](/eng/Const/) and a
reference to the [Constitution Acts 1867 to 1982](/eng/Const/) shall be
deemed to include a reference to the [Constitution Act, 1985
(Representation)](/eng/Const/).

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Electoral Boundaries Readjustment for 1981 Census]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427454 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transitional

- **[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#R-5.6_en_1){#R-5.6_en_1-ID0EBCA}[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything that was
  done under the [Electoral Boundaries Readjustment Act](/eng/acts/E-3)
  prior to the coming into force of this Act in respect of the decennial
  census of Canada taken in the year 1981, on the coming into force of
  this Act, the [Electoral Boundaries Readjustment Act](/eng/acts/E-3),
  as amended by Part III of this Act, shall be applied as if subsection
  51(1) of the [Constitution Act, 1867](/eng/Const/), as amended by Part
  I of this Act, had been in force immediately following that census
  and, except in respect of the Northwest Territories, electoral
  boundaries commissions shall be established and carry out their duties
  under the [Electoral Boundaries Readjustment Act](/eng/acts/E-3) in
  all respects as though nothing had been done under that Act and no
  time had elapsed following that census.

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Electoral Boundaries Readjustment Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427460 .Part}

**[[5 to 11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5_to_11 .sectionLabel}** \[Amendments\]

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transitional

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Sections 19 and 20 of the
[Electoral Boundaries Readjustment Act](/eng/acts/E-3), as amended by
this Act, apply in respect of the electoral boundary readjustment for
the Northwest Territories in respect of the decennial census of Canada
taken in 1981 notwithstanding anything done under those sections in
respect of that census before the coming into force of this Act and, for
the purposes of those sections, the copy of the report of the electoral
boundaries commission for the Northwest Territories in respect of that
census that was transmitted to and received by the Speaker of the House
of Commons under subsection 19(1) of that Act, as it read immediately
before the coming into force of this Act, shall forthwith after the
coming into force of this Act be referred by the Speaker to the
committee referred to in subsection 19(1) of that Act, as amended by
this Act.

.sectionLabel}** This Act shall come into force on a day to be fixed by
proclamation.
