## International Financial Assistance Act {#international-financial-assistance-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2018, c. 27, s. 659
:::

Assented to 2018-12-13

An Act to support the delivery of international financial assistance

in force June 23, 2019, see SI/2019-45.\]

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-272119 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [International Financial Assistance Act](/eng/acts/I-19.3).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-272122 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[competent
minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act, [[competent
minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Foreign Affairs, or
the Minister for International Development for the purposes of the role
described in section 4 of the [Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and
Development Act](/eng/acts/F-27.5).

## [International Financial Assistance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-272125 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}International assistance --- sovereign
loans

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to the regulations, for the purpose of supporting a federal sovereign
  loans program, the competent minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make loans to a foreign state or to any person or
    entity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} acquire and hold security or a security interest,
    including, in Quebec, a right in a security, of any kind and in any
    form, for the due discharge of obligations with respect to loans
    under paragraph (a);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} surrender the security, security interest or right
    in the security and acquire and hold, in exchange, a security or
    security interest, including, in Quebec, a right in a security, of
    any kind and in any form;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} realize the security, security interest or right in
    the security made, acquired and held for the due discharge of
    obligations with respect to loans made under paragraph (a);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} exchange, assign, sell or otherwise dispose of the
    security, security interest or right in the security, of any kind
    and in any form, for the due discharge of obligations with respect
    to loans under paragraph (a); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} acquire, hold, assign, exchange, sell or otherwise
    dispose of shares within the meaning of paragraph 90(5)(e) of the
    [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Guarantee of sovereign loan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless a loan referred to in paragraph (1)(a) is made
  to the government of a foreign state, it must be guaranteed by the
  government of a foreign state benefiting from the loan in favour of
  Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Innovative financing

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the regulations,
for the purpose of supporting a federal program that promotes
international assistance through the use of innovative financing, the
competent minister may, directly or indirectly,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} guarantee, in whole or in part, any obligation
  undertaken by a person or entity;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} acquire and hold security or a security interest,
  including, in Quebec, a right in a security, of any kind and in any
  form, for the due discharge of obligations guaranteed under paragraph
  (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} surrender the security, security interest or right in
  the security and acquire and hold, in exchange, a security or security
  interest, including, in Quebec, a right in a security, of any kind and
  in any form;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} realize the security, security interest or right in
  the security made, acquired and held with respect to obligations
  guaranteed under paragraph (a);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} exchange, assign, sell or otherwise dispose of the
  security, security interest or right in the security, of any kind and
  in any form, for the due discharge of obligations guaranteed under
  paragraph (a); and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} acquire, hold, assign, exchange, sell or otherwise
  dispose of shares within the meaning of paragraph 90(5)(e) of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Program for climate change

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the regulations,
for the purpose of supporting a federal international assistance program
that promotes the mitigation of or adaptation to climate change through
repayable contributions, the competent minister may, directly or
indirectly, acquire, hold, assign, exchange, sell or otherwise dispose
of shares within the meaning of paragraph 90(5)(e) of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees and interest

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  competent minister may charge fees and interest as determined under
  the regulations for the purposes of sections 3 and 4.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Service Fees Act](/eng/acts/S-8.4)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the [Service Fees
  Act](/eng/acts/S-8.4) does not apply to any fees or interest referred
  to in subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions no longer applicable

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  [Surplus Crown Assets Act](/eng/acts/S-27) and section 61 of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) do not apply with
  respect to the exercising and carrying out of the powers, duties and
  functions conferred to the competent minister under paragraphs 3(1)(b)
  to (f) and 4(b) to (f) and section 5.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision no longer applicable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 90 of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), shares of a corporation that on
  acquisition would be held by, on behalf of or in trust for Her Majesty
  in right of Canada, may be acquired for the purposes of sections 3 to
  5.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foreign exchange loss

**[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** Any foreign exchange
loss in respect of any transaction entered into in relation to a program
referred to in section 3, 4 or 5 must be charged to the Consolidated
Revenue Fund.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum payment

- **[[7.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  competent minister may, in relation to a program referred to in
  section 4 or 5, make payments not exceeding \$720,000,000 in the
  aggregate, or any greater amount that is specified in an appropriation
  Act, to Development Finance Institute Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of Consolidated Revenue
  Fund

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any amount payable under subsection (1) may be paid
  by the competent minister out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund, at the
  times and in the manner that the minister considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Separate account

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Development Finance Institute Canada must maintain a
  separate account of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all amounts received under subsection (1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all amounts received by way of receipts and
    recoveries, and all disbursements made, in connection with all
    transactions involving the amounts received under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repayments

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The competent minister may require Development
  Finance Institute Canada to pay to the Receiver General for credit to
  the Consolidated Revenue Fund any amount referred to in subsection (3)
  at the times and in the manner that the minister considers
  appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[Development Finance
  Institute Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[Development Finance Institute
  Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Development Finance Institute
  Canada (DFIC) Inc., a corporation incorporated under the Canada
  Business Corporation Act, or any successor to that corporation.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-272157 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of the Minister of
  Foreign Affairs and the Minister for International Development and
  with the concurrence of the Minister of Finance, make regulations for
  the purposes of sections 3 to 6, including regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing eligibility criteria for recipients;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establishing the maximum loan term, the terms and
    conditions of repayment and the manner in which applicable rates of
    interest are set;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the type of security referred to in
    paragraphs 3(1)(b) to (e) and 4(b) to (e);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the circumstances and the manner in
    which shares may be acquired, held, assigned, exchanged, sold or
    otherwise disposed of;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the maximum amount of outstanding
    guarantees;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the applicable fees or the manner in
    which fees are set, circumstances in which they are required and the
    manner in which fees are paid; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} providing that certain transactions or classes of
    transactions require consultation with or the approval of the
    Minister of Finance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classes

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The regulations may establish classes of securities
  or guarantees and distinguish among those classes.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, on the recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs and the
Minister for International Development and with the concurrence of the
Minister of Finance, make regulations for the purposes of section 7.2.
