## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-86618 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the board of directors
    established under section 13. ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[College]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[College]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the College of Patent Agents
    and Trademark Agents established by section
    5. ([Collège]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[investigator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[investigator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual designated
    under section 42. ([enquêteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence issued under this
    Act. ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a patent agent or a trademark
    agent. ([titulaire de permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the federal minister
    designated under section 4. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[patent agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[patent agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who holds a
    patent agent licence or a patent agent in training licence issued
    under section 26. ([agent de brevets]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[privileged]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[privileged]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means protected by litigation
    privilege, by solicitor-client privilege, by the professional
    secrecy of advocates and notaries or by the privilege set out in
    section 16.1 of the [Patent Act](/eng/acts/P-4) or section 51.13 of
    the [Trademarks Act](/eng/acts/T-13). ([protégé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Registrar of the College
    appointed under section 22. ([registraire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[trademark agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[trademark agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who
    holds a trademark agent licence or a trademark agent in training
    licence issued under section 29. ([agent de marques de
    commerce]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Establishment and Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-86635 .Part}

### [College]{.HTitleText2} {#h-86636 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  corporation without share capital is established, to be known as the
  College of Patent Agents and Trademark Agents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act not applicable to College

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any regulations made under paragraph
  76(1)(a.1), the [Canada Not-for-profit Corporations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-7.75) does not apply to the College.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of the College
is to regulate patent agents and trademark agents in the public
interest, in order to enhance the public's ability to secure the rights
provided for under the [Patent Act](/eng/acts/P-4) and the [Trademarks
Act](/eng/acts/T-13).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The head office of the
College is to be in the National Capital Region, as described in the
schedule to the [National Capital Act](/eng/acts/N-4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** In carrying out its
purpose, the College has the capacity and the rights, powers and
privileges of a natural person, including the power to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} purchase or otherwise acquire, or lease, any real or
  personal property or immovable or movable;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sell or otherwise dispose of, or lease, any of its
  acquired or leased property; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} borrow money.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not Crown agent

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The College is not an agent
of Her Majesty, and the College's directors, the members of its
committees, the Registrar, the investigators and any officers,
employees, agents and mandataries of the College are not part of the
federal public administration.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Official Languages
Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01)

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The [Official Languages
Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01) applies to the College.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The members of the
College are the licensees.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual general meeting

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The College must hold an
annual general meeting of members within six months after the end of
each calendar year, at a time and place in Canada fixed by the Board.

### [Board of Directors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-86656 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  College has a board of directors composed of at least seven directors,
  including the Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order fixing number of directors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must, by order, fix the number of
  directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointed directors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by order, fix the number of
  directors that are to be appointed to the Board and appoint those
  directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum number of appointed directors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The number of appointed directors fixed under
  subsection (3) must not be greater than the minimum number that is
  required to constitute a majority of directors on the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elected directors

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The remaining directors are to be licensees elected
  in accordance with the by-laws, and at least one elected director must
  be a patent agent and at least one must be a trademark agent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligibility

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** An individual is not
eligible to be appointed or elected as a director if they

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} are less than 18 years of age;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have the status of bankrupt;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} are a member of an association whose primary purpose
  is to represent the interests of persons who provide advice on patents
  or trademarks;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} were, within the preceding 12 months, a member of a
  governing body or a steering committee of an association referred to
  in paragraph (c);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an appointment to the Board,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are a licensee, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are employed by a
    [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the
    [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11); or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of election to the Board,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} have a licence that is suspended, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} meet any other ineligibility criteria set out in
    the by-laws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  director is to be appointed or elected for a term of not more than
  three years, and may be reappointed or re-elected for subsequent terms
  of not more than three years each.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of term

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The length of a director's term is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out by the Minister in the order appointing the
    director, if the director is appointed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determined in accordance with the by-laws, if the
    director is elected.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staggered terms

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, it is not necessary that all
  directors hold office for terms that begin or end on the same day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation in office --- appointed
  directors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1) and subject to section 17, an
  appointed director continues to hold office until they are
  re-appointed or their successor is appointed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vacancies during term

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If an elected director has ceased to hold office
  before the expiry of their term, the Board may, in accordance with the
  by-laws, appoint an individual to fill that vacancy for the unexpired
  portion of that term or for any shorter period that the Board fixes.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal --- appointed directors

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appointed director holds office during good behaviour and may be
  removed for cause by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal --- elected directors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An elected director may be removed in accordance with
  the by-laws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ceases to be director

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A director ceases to be a
director if they

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} die;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} resign;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} become bankrupt;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} become a member of an association whose primary
  purpose is to represent the interests of persons who provide advice on
  patents or trademarks;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} become a member of a governing body or a steering
  committee of an association referred to in paragraph (d);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} are removed from office under section 16;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an appointed director,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} become a licensee, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} become an employee of a
    [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the
    [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11); or

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an elected director,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} have a licence that is suspended,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} cease to be a licensee, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} meet the ineligibility criteria set out in the
    by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An individual is not permitted to be a member of the
  Discipline Committee and the Investigations Committee at the same
  time.

### [Immunity]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1366314 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibility for damages --- directors
and others

**[[23.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23.1 .sectionLabel}** No action or other
proceeding for damages lies or may be instituted against any of the
following persons for anything done or omitted to be done in good faith
in the exercise or purported exercise of any power, or in the
performance or purported performance of any duty or function, conferred
on that person under the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a current or former director of the Board;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a current or former member of a committee of the
  College;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Registrar or a former Registrar;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a current or former investigator;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a current or former officer, employee, agent or
  mandatary of the College; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a person who is or has been engaged by the College.

satisfy a judgment, reasonably incurred by them in respect of any civil,
criminal, administrative or other proceeding in which they are involved

purported exercise of any power, or in the performance or purported
performance of any duty or function, conferred on that person under the

### [Powers of Minister and Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-86741 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} review the Board's activities and require the Board
    to provide reports and information, other than privileged
    information, to the Minister; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require the Board to do anything --- including to

    Minister's opinion, is advisable to carry out the purposes of this
    Act.

  year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must cause a copy of the report to be
  tabled in each House of Parliament on any of the first 15 days on

  receives it.

## [Licensees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-86754 .Part}

### [Patent Agents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-86755 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Patent agent licence

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  application, the Registrar must issue a patent agent licence to an
  individual who meets the requirements under the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Patent agent in training licence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On application, the Registrar must issue a patent
  agent in training licence to an individual who meets the requirements
  under the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A licence issued under this section is subject to any
  conditions imposed under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation before Patent Office

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Subject to any

### [Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-86805 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standards of professional conduct and
competence

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** A licensee must meet the
standards of professional conduct and competence that are established by
the code of professional conduct. A licensee who fails to meet those
standards commits professional misconduct or is incompetent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Code of professional conduct

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must, by regulation, establish a code of professional conduct
  for licensees.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment or repeal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Only the Board may, by regulation and with the
  Minister's prior written approval, amend or repeal the regulations
  establishing the code.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Professional liability insurance

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  a licensee must be insured against professional liability.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- employee of Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensee who is employed by Her Majesty in right of
  Canada or a province, or by an agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada
  or an agent or mandatary of Her Majesty in right of a province, is not
  required to be insured against professional liability for work done as
  a licensee within the scope of that employment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- by-laws

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A licensee may be exempted by the by-laws from the
  application of subsection (1).

### [Suspension, Revocation and Surrender of Licences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-86820 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Registrar may, in accordance with the by-laws, suspend a licence if
  the licensee fails to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pay the annual fee in accordance with the by-laws;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pay, within the time and in the manner provided for
    under this Act, any other fee or amount that the licensee is
    required to pay under this Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide any information or document in accordance
    with the by-laws;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} comply with any requirement that is imposed by the
    by-laws with respect to continuing professional development;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} comply with a condition of their licence that is
    imposed under this Act;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} comply with any requirement with respect to
    professional liability insurance that is imposed under this Act; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} comply with any other requirement that is imposed
    under the by-laws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar must notify a licensee in writing if
  their licence is suspended under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Length of suspension

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A licence is suspended under this section until the
  licensee complies with the requirements set out in the by-laws to
  address the failure that led to the suspension or until the licence is
  revoked by the Registrar under subsection (4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar may, in accordance with the by-laws,
  revoke a suspended licence if it has been suspended under this section
  for the applicable period of time that is specified in the by-laws.

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar may, in

## [Investigations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-86839 .Part}

### [Duty to Investigate]{.HTitleText2} {#h-86840 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** The Investigations
Committee must, on the basis of a complaint or on its own initiative,
conduct an investigation into a licensee's conduct and activities if it
has reasonable grounds to believe that the licensee has committed
professional misconduct or was incompetent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

- **[[37.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Investigations Committee may
  take any of the following actions in respect of a licensee who is
  under investigation if it is satisfied that it is necessary for the
  protection of the public:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} impose conditions on a licence of the licensee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} impose restrictions on the licensee's entitlement
    to represent persons under section 27 or 30;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} suspend a licence of the licensee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Investigations Committee must notify the licensee
  in writing of any action taken in respect of the licensee and must
  inform them of their right to make an application for a review by the
  Discipline Committee at any time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action is provisional

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any action taken under subsection (1) is provisional
  and ceases to have effect if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Discipline Committee makes a decision under
    subsection 37.2(2) that amends or revokes the action;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Investigations Committee dismisses the matter
    under subsection 49(1);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Investigations Committee withdraws the
    application under section 50;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Discipline Committee exercises its powers under
    section 56; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Discipline Committee renders a decision under
    section 57.

### [Conduct of Investigation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-86855 .Subheading}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Investigations Committee may revoke the

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of investigator

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For

  investigator may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (3), enter the licensee's

    investigation, and examine or copy the document or thing or remove

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require the licensee, or a business partner or
    employee of the licensee or any person employed by the same employer
    as the licensee, to provide any information that is relevant to the
    investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privileged information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An investigator may take any action under
  subsection (1) with respect to any privileged information, including
  privileged information contained in a document or other thing, unless
  the information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is subject to litigation privilege or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is not related to a patent, a trademark, a

    (i), (i.1), (i.3), (n) or (n.1) of the [Trademarks
    Act](/eng/acts/T-13).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant to enter dwelling-house

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the licensee's business premises are in a
  dwelling-house, the investigator may enter them only with the
  occupant's consent or under the authority of a warrant issued under
  section 44.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An investigator may remove a document or other thing
  from business premises under paragraph (1)(a) only if the investigator
  gives a person who appears to be in control of the premises a receipt
  for the document or thing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the

    any document or thing that contains information that is privileged;

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} there are reasonable grounds to believe that the

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 43(1)(b) does not authorize the

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} there are reasonable grounds to believe that the
      document or thing contains information that meets, or the
      information meets, all the conditions set out in paragraphs
      43(2)(a) to (c).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sealing and notification --- legal
counsel

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  investigator requires the production of a document or other thing ---
  or is about to examine, copy or remove it --- under section 43, under
  a warrant issued under section 44 or under an order made under section
  45, and the document or thing is in the possession of legal counsel or
  a firm of legal counsel,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the investigator must not examine or copy the
    document or thing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} legal counsel or a representative of the firm must
    place the document or thing in a package and seal and identify the
    package in accordance with the regulations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the investigator must retain and remove the package
    once it is sealed; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} legal counsel or the firm must take all reasonable
    steps

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to notify the holder of a privilege with respect
      to the document or thing within the period prescribed by
      regulation, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the holder cannot be located within that
      period, to immediately notify the law society of the applicable
      province or the Chambre des notaires du Québec, as the case may
      be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objection

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an investigator requires the production of a
  document or other thing --- or is about to examine, copy or remove it
  --- under section 43, under a warrant issued under section 44 or under
  an order made under section 45, a person in possession of the document
  or thing may object to its production, examination, copying or removal
  on one or more of the following grounds:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the document or thing is not relevant to the
    investigation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the investigator is acting under section 43, the
    document or thing contains information that meets all the conditions
    set out in paragraphs 43(2)(a) to (c);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the investigator is acting under the authority
    of a warrant or order, the production, examination, copying or
    removal of the document or thing is not authorized by the warrant or
    order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objection --- sealing and notification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the person makes an objection under subsection
  (2),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the investigator must not examine or copy the
    document or thing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person must place the document or thing in a
    package and seal and identify the package in accordance with the
    regulations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the investigator must retain and remove the package
    once it is sealed; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the person is not the owner of the document or
    thing or the holder of a privilege with respect to it, the person
    must immediately notify the owner or holder of the objection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to Federal Court

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The legal counsel or firm referred to in subsection
  (1), the person who makes the objection under subsection (2) or any
  other person who has a right or interest in the document or other
  thing that is sealed may apply to the Federal Court in accordance with
  the regulations for a determination of whether any of the grounds set
  out in paragraphs (2)(a) to (c) apply with respect to the document or
  thing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Handling of sealed package

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The sealed package is to be retained, opened or
  returned only in accordance with the regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return or application for retention order

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  investigator who, as a result of the application of any of sections 43
  to 45 or the regulations referred to in subsection 46(5), is in
  possession of a document or other thing that is not in a sealed
  package must, within the applicable period determined in accordance
  with the regulations made under paragraph 76(1)(i), return the
  document or thing or apply to the Federal Court for a retention order
  under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On application by the investigator, the Federal Court
  may order that the document or thing may be retained by the
  investigator until no later than the conclusion of the investigation
  or, if an application is made to the Discipline Committee under
  subsection 49(1), until the final disposition of the proceedings
  before the Discipline Committee and any appeals. If the Court does not
  order that the document or thing be retained, the document or thing
  must be returned as soon as feasible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for return

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who has a right or interest in a document or other thing that,
  as a result of the application of any of sections 43 to 45 or the
  regulations referred to in subsection 46(5), is in the possession of
  an investigator and that is not in a sealed package may, on giving
  notice to the Investigations Committee, apply to the Federal Court for
  an order that the document or thing be returned or that any copies of
  the document or thing made by or on behalf of the Committee or an
  investigator be destroyed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Court may order, on any conditions that it
  considers appropriate,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the document or thing be returned; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that any copies of the document or thing made by or
    on behalf of the Investigations Committee or an investigator be
    destroyed, if the removal of the document or thing by an
    investigator was not authorized under this Act.

### [Conclusion of Investigation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-86948 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application or dismissal

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, at
  the conclusion of an investigation, the Investigations Committee is
  satisfied that there is sufficient evidence that the licensee has
  committed professional misconduct or was incompetent, the Committee
  must apply to the Discipline Committee for a decision as to whether
  the licensee committed professional misconduct or was incompetent.
  Otherwise, the Investigations Committee must dismiss the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written representations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Investigations Committee must consider any
  written representations made by the licensee under section 38 before
  the conclusion of the investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of application or dismissal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Investigations Committee must, in writing, notify
  the licensee and any complainant of the application or dismissal and,
  in the case of dismissal, the reasons for it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Investigations Committee is not permitted to
  disclose privileged information in its notice to the licensee or
  complainant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of application

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** The Investigations
Committee may withdraw an application to the Discipline Committee only
if the Investigations Committee is no longer satisfied that there is
sufficient evidence that the licensee has committed professional
misconduct or was incompetent.

## [Disciplinary Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-86960 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral hearing

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** The Discipline Committee
must hold an oral hearing for every application made by the
Investigations Committee in order to determine whether a licensee
committed professional misconduct or was incompetent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearings open to public

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** Except to the extent
necessary to protect privileged or confidential information, the
hearings of the Discipline Committee are open to the public.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parties

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** The Investigations
Committee and the licensee are parties to the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of complainant to make submissions

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** If the application
results from a complaint, the complainant is entitled to make oral and
written submissions to the Discipline Committee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Discipline Committee has the power

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to summon and enforce the appearance of persons and
    compel them to give oral or written evidence on oath and to produce
    any document or other thing that the Committee considers necessary
    to decide the application, in the same manner and to the same extent
    as a superior court of record;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to administer oaths; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to accept any evidence, whether admissible in a
    court of law or not.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privileged information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Discipline Committee may take any action referred
  to in subsection (1) with respect to information that is privileged.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers before decision

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  making a decision under section 57, the Discipline Committee may take
  any action referred to in any of paragraphs 57(3)(a) to (c) if the
  Discipline Committee is satisfied that it is necessary for the
  protection of the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action is provisional

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any action taken under subsection (1) is provisional
  and ceases to have effect

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} after the decision on the application is made,
    unless the action is confirmed in the decision; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the application is withdrawn.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision on application

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  the hearing of an application, the Discipline Committee must decide
  whether the licensee has committed professional misconduct or was
  incompetent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No professional misconduct or
  incompetence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Discipline Committee decides that the licensee
  did not commit professional misconduct or was not incompetent, the
  Committee must dismiss the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Professional misconduct or incompetence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Discipline Committee decides that the licensee
  has committed professional misconduct or was incompetent, the
  Committee may, in its decision,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} impose conditions on a licence of the licensee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} impose restrictions on the licensee's entitlement
    to represent persons under section 27 or 30;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} suspend a licence of the licensee for a period of
    not more than two years or until specified conditions are met, or
    both;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} revoke a licence of the licensee;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} reprimand the licensee;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} require the licensee to pay a penalty of not more
    than \$10,000 to the College;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} require the licensee to pay to the College or any
    complainant all or a portion of the costs incurred by them during
    the application before the Committee;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} require the licensee to reimburse all or a portion
    of the fees or disbursements paid to the licensee by a client; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} take or require any other action that the Committee
    considers appropriate in the circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision and reasons in writing

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Discipline Committee must give its decision and
  the reasons for it in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision and reasons available to
  public

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A decision and the reasons for it, other than any
  privileged and confidential information contained in the decision or
  reasons, must be made available to the public on the College's
  website.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Registrar

- **[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Discipline Committee must provide a copy of every decision made under
  section 57 to the Registrar.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to licensees --- revocation or
  suspension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar must notify all licensees of every
  decision made under subsection 57(3) to revoke or suspend a licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to licensees --- dismissal of
  application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar must, if requested by the licensee who
  was the subject of an application to the Discipline Committee that was
  dismissed, notify all licensees of the dismissal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal to Federal Court

**[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** A party to an application
may appeal a decision of the Discipline Committee made under section 57
to the Federal Court within 30 days after the day on which the decision
is made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of decision in Federal Court

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  College may file in the Federal Court a certified copy of a final
  decision of the Discipline Committee made under section 57 that is not
  subject to appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of filing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the certified copy being filed, the decision
  becomes and may be enforced as an order of the Federal Court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension lifted if conditions met

- **[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Discipline Committee suspends a licence under paragraph 57(3)(c) until
  specified conditions are met, the Committee must, on application by
  the licensee, lift the suspension if the Committee is satisfied that
  the conditions are met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application under subsection (1) may be made [ex
  parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Registrar

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Discipline Committee must provide written notice
  to the Registrar as soon as feasible after it lifts the suspension.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation by Registrar

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar may, at any time after the second
  anniversary of the day on which a licence is suspended under paragraph
  57(3)(c) until specified conditions are met and after giving at least
  30 days' written notice to the licensee, revoke the licence if the
  suspension has not been lifted under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar must notify the licensee in writing as
  soon as feasible after their licence is revoked.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts paid as penalty

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** Amounts paid to the
College as a penalty under paragraph 57(3)(f) may be expended only for
the purpose of supporting the mental health of licensees.

## [Confidentiality]{.HTitleText1} {#h-87033 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No waiver

**[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
the disclosure of privileged information to the College, including to
the Investigations Committee or to the Discipline Committee, or to an
investigator does not constitute a waiver of the privilege.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No disclosure

- **[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  following persons must not disclose a complaint received by the
  College or any privileged or confidential information obtained in the
  course of an investigation or proceeding under this Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a current or former director of the Board;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a current or former member of the Investigations
    Committee or of the Discipline Committee;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Registrar or a former Registrar;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a current or former investigator;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a current or former officer, employee, agent or
    mandatary of the College;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a person who is or has been engaged by the College.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person referred to in subsection (1) may disclose a
  complaint or information referred to in that subsection if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure is for the purpose of permitting the
    exercise of powers or the performance of duties or functions under
    this Act, including in connection with a proceeding under this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information is available to the public;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure is to the person's legal counsel;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person has obtained the written consent of all
    persons whose rights or interests might reasonably be affected by
    the disclosure; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} there are reasonable grounds to believe that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} there is a significant risk of harm to any person
      if the disclosure is not made, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} making the disclosure is likely to reduce the
      risk.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Testimony and production

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person referred to in subsection (1) is not
  required, in any proceeding other than a proceeding under this Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to give testimony on a complaint received by the
    College or on any privileged or confidential information that the
    person is prohibited from disclosing under that subsection; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to produce such a complaint or any document or
    other thing that contains any such privileged or confidential
    information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for order authorizing
disclosure

- **[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  College may apply to the Federal Court for an order authorizing the
  disclosure of confidential information that would be prohibited by
  section 65 to a law enforcement agency or other public authority.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Court must not make an order if the information
  is privileged or if it was obtained by the College as a result of a
  person making, in the course of an investigation or proceeding under
  this Act, an oral or written statement that may tend to incriminate
  the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents and other things

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An order that authorizes the disclosure of
  information may also authorize the delivery of documents or other
  things that are in the possession of the College and that relate to
  the information.

  diligence to prevent the commission of the offence.

  Trademarks.

    notifications, the identification of sealed packages, applications
    to the Federal Court and the retention, opening and return of sealed
    packages; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} respecting the determination of the applicable
    periods referred to in subsection 47(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Regulations made under paragraph (1)(a.2) may
  authorize the Board or any committee of the College, and those made
  under paragraphs (1)(c), (d), (f) and (g) may authorize the Board, the
  Registrar or any committee of the College, to make by-laws with
  respect to all or part of the subject matter of the regulations and,
  for greater certainty, those by-laws are regulations for the purposes
  of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations --- sealed packages

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Regulations made under paragraph (1)(h) may authorize
  the Federal Court to make orders respecting the retention, opening or
  return of sealed packages.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations prevail

**[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77 .sectionLabel}** The regulations prevail
over the by-laws to the extent of an inconsistency or conflict between
them.

### [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-87151 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[coming-into-force
day]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78 .sectionLabel}** In sections 79 to 86,
[[coming-into-force day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the day on which
section 13 comes into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial organization of board

- **[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  the coming-into-force day, the College's board of directors is to be
  composed of five directors appointed by the Minister of Industry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment on recommendation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Two of the directors are to be appointed on the
  recommendation of the Intellectual Property Institute of Canada, with
  one to be an individual whose name is on the register kept under
  section 15 of the [Patent Act](/eng/acts/P-4) and the other to be an
  individual whose name is on the list of trademark agents kept under
  section 28 of the [Trademarks Act](/eng/acts/T-13).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Industry must designate a Chairperson
  from among the directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed election or appointment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The directors appointed on the recommendation of the
  Intellectual Property Institute of Canada who hold office on the
  coming-into-force day are deemed to have been elected under subsection
  13(5) on that day, for a term that ends on the earlier of the day on
  which the first election is held under that subsection and the first
  anniversary of the coming-into-force day. The other directors who hold
  office on the coming-into-force day are deemed to have been appointed
  under subsection 13(3) on that day for a term that ends on the first
  anniversary of that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, section 17 applies as of the
  coming-into-force day with respect to directors who are deemed to be
  elected or appointed under subsection (4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal for cause

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Before the coming-into-force day, a director
  appointed under subsection (1) may be removed for cause by the
  Minister of Industry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Before the coming-into-force day, the College may pay
  the remuneration and expenses that it fixes to the directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to the Board

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Any reference in this Act to the Board is, until the
  coming-into-force day, a reference to the board of directors as
  constituted under this section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confirmation of by-laws

**[[80]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80 .sectionLabel}** Any by-laws that are made
by the Board before the first election is held under subsection 13(5)
are repealed on the 180th day after the day on which the first election
is held, unless they are confirmed by a resolution of the Board, as it
is constituted after that election, before that 180th day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}College not a Crown Corporation

**[[81]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-81 .sectionLabel}** Despite Part X of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the College is not a
[[Crown corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} within the meaning of that
Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed issued patent agent licence

**[[82]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-82 .sectionLabel}** An individual is deemed
to have been issued a patent agent licence under subsection 26(1) on the
coming-into-force day if their name was, on the day before the
coming-into-force day, on the register kept under section 15 of the
[Patent Act](/eng/acts/P-4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed issued trademark agent licence

**[[83]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-83 .sectionLabel}** An individual is deemed
to have been issued a trademark agent licence under subsection 29(1) on
the coming-into-force day if their name was, on the day before the
coming-into-force day, on the list of trademark agents kept under
section 28 of the [Trademarks Act](/eng/acts/T-13).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information --- patents

**[[84]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-84 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner of
Patents may, for the purpose of enabling the College or the Registrar to
exercise their powers or perform their duties and functions under this
Act, provide the Registrar with information relating to an individual or
firm whose name is or was on the register kept under section 15 of the
[Patent Act](/eng/acts/P-4), an individual who has sat for the
qualifying examination for patent agents or an individual who has
notified the Commissioner in writing of their intention to sit for the
examination, including

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the individual's or firm's name and contact
  information and, in the case of an individual, the name of any firm of
  which they are a member;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the Commissioner has refused to recognize the
  individual or firm as a patent agent or attorney under section 16 of
  the [Patent Act](/eng/acts/P-4);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any complaints against the individual or firm; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the results of any qualifying examinations for patent
  agents.

individual or firm whose name is or was on the list of trademark agents

individual who has sat for the qualifying examination for trademark
agents or an individual who has notified the Registrar of Trademarks in
writing of their intention to sit for the examination, including

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the individual's or firm's name and contact
  information and, in the case of an individual, the name of any firm of
  which they are a member;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any complaints against the individual or firm; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the results of any qualifying examinations for
  trademark agents.
