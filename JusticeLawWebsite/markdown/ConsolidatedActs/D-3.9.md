## Donkin Coal Block Development Opportunity Act {#donkin-coal-block-development-opportunity-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2007, c. 33
:::

Assented to 2007-12-14

An Act respecting the exploitation of the Donkin coal block and
employment in or in connection with the operation of a mine that is
wholly or partly at the Donkin coal block, and to make a consequential
amendment to the Canada-Nova Scotia Offshore Petroleum Resources Accord
Implementation Act
::::

::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-173897 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Donkin Coal Block Development Opportunity Act](/eng/acts/D-3.9).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-173900 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Donkin coal block]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Donkin coal block]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the coal and
    coal-bed methane deposits located in the area described in the
    schedule. ([réserve de charbon Donkin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[exploitation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exploitation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the exploration,
    development and reclamation of a coal block, as well as the
    operation of a mine that is wholly or partly at the coal
    block. ([exploitation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[frontier lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[frontier lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means lands that belong to
    Her Majesty in right of Canada, or in respect of which Her Majesty
    in right of Canada has the right to dispose of or exploit the
    natural resources, and that are situated in

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Northwest Territories, Nunavut or Sable
      Island; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} submarine areas, not within a province, in the
      internal waters of Canada, the territorial sea of Canada or the
      continental shelf of Canada.

    However, it does not include the adjoining area, as defined in
    section 2 of the [Yukon Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.01). ([terres
    domaniales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Natural
    Resources. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the development,
    construction and abandonment of a mine, as well as exploration for a
    mine. ([exploitation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Nova
    Scotia. ([province]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, this
Act shall not be interpreted as providing a basis for any claim by or on
behalf of any province in respect of any interest in or legislative
jurisdiction over any frontier lands, or any living or non-living
resources of any frontier lands, or as limiting the application of any
federal law that is not specifically limited under this Act.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-173914 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to provide a legal regime within a cooperative framework to facilitate
the exploitation of the Donkin coal block and to regulate employment in
or in connection with the operation of a mine that is wholly or partly
at the Donkin coal block.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-173917 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada and of the Province.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-173920 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Act applies to the
exploitation of the Donkin coal block and to employment in or in
connection with the operation of a mine that is wholly or partly at the
Donkin coal block.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of certain Acts

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided in the regulations, the [Canada Oil and Gas Operations
Act](/eng/acts/O-7), the [Canada Petroleum Resources
Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5) and the [Federal Real Property and Federal
Immovables Act](/eng/acts/F-8.4), and all regulations made under those
Acts, do not apply to the Donkin coal block.

## [Delegation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-173925 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may delegate

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 15, the
Minister may delegate to any person any power, function or duty that the
Minister considers necessary for the administration of the exploitation
of the Donkin coal block.

## [Royalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-173928 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Royalties

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby reserved to
Her Majesty in right of Canada the royalties on coal and coal-bed
methane from the portions of the Donkin coal block that lie in frontier
lands, as well as related interest and penalties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remittance to Receiver General

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Province shall remit to the Receiver General, in accordance with the
  terms of any agreement referred to in section 12, all royalties,
  interest and penalties reserved to Her Majesty in right of Canada
  under section 9.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remittance to Province

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Canada shall, in accordance
  with the terms of such an agreement, remit to Her Majesty in right of
  the Province an amount equal to each amount remitted to the Receiver
  General under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment out of Consolidated Revenue
  Fund

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} There may be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue
  Fund to Her Majesty in right of the Province, on the requisition of
  the Minister, any amount to be remitted during a fiscal year under
  subsection (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debts due to Her Majesty

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** All royalties, interest
and penalties payable under subsection 10(1) are debts due to Her
Majesty in right of Canada and are recoverable as such from the
Province.

## [Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-173942 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement concerning royalties, etc.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, with
the approval of the Governor in Council, enter on behalf of Her Majesty
in right of Canada into an agreement with the government of the Province
with respect to the collection and administration of the royalties,
interest and penalties referred to in section 9 on behalf of the
Government of Canada, and to the remittance of those amounts by the
Government of Canada to the Province.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-173945 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendation of Minister of Labour

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of the Minister of
  Labour, make regulations respecting employment in or in connection
  with the operation of a mine that is wholly or partly at the Donkin
  coal block, including regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} excluding, in whole or in part, from the
    application of any of the provisions of the [Canada Labour
    Code](/eng/acts/L-2) any employment, or any class or classes of
    employment, in or in connection with the operation of such a mine;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} governing industrial relations, occupational health
    and safety and labour standards in relation to employment that is
    subject to a regulation made under paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendation of Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of
  the Minister, make regulations respecting all other matters relating
  to the exploitation of the Donkin coal block, including regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} amending the schedule to provide for any change
    that is necessary to the description of the area in which the Donkin
    coal block is located;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the application, in whole or in part, of
    any of the Acts referred to in section 7 and the regulations made
    under those Acts; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting royalties, interest and penalties that
    are referred to in section 9.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The regulations referred to in subsections (1)
  and (2) may incorporate by reference in whole or in part any Act of
  the Province or instrument made under such an Act, as amended from
  time to time, with any adaptations that the Governor in Council
  considers necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint recommendation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of
  both the Minister and the Minister of Labour, by regulation provide
  that any Act of the Province or instrument made under such an Act
  relating to the prosecution of provincial offences applies, in whole
  or in part, as amended from time to time and with any adaptations that
  the Governor in Council considers necessary, to prosecutions for
  offences under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Service Fees Act](/eng/acts/S-8.4) does
not apply

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
the [Service Fees Act](/eng/acts/S-8.4) does not apply in respect of any
fees fixed under a provision that is incorporated into the regulations
by reference.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administration and enforcement

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  regulation incorporating --- and, as necessary, adapting --- any Act
  of the Province or instrument made under such an Act shall, after
  consultation with the appropriate provincial minister, be administered
  and enforced by the person or authority that is responsible for the
  administration and enforcement of the incorporated Act or instrument.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not agent of Her Majesty in right of
  Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Persons and authorities referred to in subsection (1)
  are not agents of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and penalty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes a regulation by
  contravening a provision that is incorporated --- and, as necessary,
  adapted --- by the regulation is guilty of an offence against this Act
  and liable to the same punishment as is imposed by or under an Act of
  the Province for the contravention of that provision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The prosecution of an offence described in
  subsection (3) shall be conducted by the Attorney General of the
  Province or his or her lawful deputy.
