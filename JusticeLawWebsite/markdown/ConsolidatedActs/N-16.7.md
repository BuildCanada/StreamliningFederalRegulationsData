## National Sports of Canada Act {#national-sports-of-canada-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1994, c. 16
:::

Assented to 1994-05-12

An Act to recognize hockey and lacrosse as the national sports of Canada
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363929 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [National Sports of Canada Act](/eng/acts/N-16.7).

## [National Sports of Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-363932 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hockey and lacrosse to be national sports

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The game commonly known as
ice hockey is hereby recognized and declared to be the national winter
sport of Canada and the game commonly known as lacrosse is hereby
recognized and declared to be the national summer sport of Canada.
