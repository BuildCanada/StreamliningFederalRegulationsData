## Invest in Canada Act {#invest-in-canada-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2017, c. 20, s. 442
:::

Assented to 2017-06-22

An Act to establish the Invest in Canada Hub

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 442 of chapter 20 of the Statutes of Canada, 2017,
in force March 12, 2018, see SI/2018-15.\]
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-278150 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Invest in Canada Act](/eng/acts/I-21.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-278153 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Invest in Canada Hub]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Invest in Canada Hub]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the corporation
    established under subsection 4(1). ([Investir au
    Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada designated under section
    3. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-278158 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of Minister

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate a member of the Queen's Privy Council for
Canada as the Minister for the purpose of this Act.

## [Invest in Canada Hub Established]{.HTitleText1} {#h-278161 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Invest in Canada Hub

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  corporation is established to be called the Invest in Canada Hub.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agent of Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Invest in Canada Hub is for all purposes an agent
  of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The head office of the Invest in Canada Hub is to be
  at a place in Canada that is designated by the Minister.

## [Mandate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-278169 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandate

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Invest in Canada Hub's
mandate, for the purpose of supporting economic prosperity and
stimulating innovation in Canada, is to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} promote foreign direct investment in Canada and
  attract and facilitate that investment; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} coordinate the efforts of the government, the private
  sector and other stakeholders with respect to foreign direct
  investment in Canada.

## [Functions and Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-278174 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Functions

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** In carrying out its
mandate, the Invest in Canada Hub is to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} develop and implement a national strategy to attract
  foreign direct investment to Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} create and maintain partnerships with any department,
  board or agency of any government in Canada, the private sector in
  Canada or any other Canadian stakeholder with an interest in foreign
  direct investment in order to optimize the benefits of any programs,
  resources and services that are offered with respect to foreign direct
  investment;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} plan, direct, manage and implement activities,
  events, conferences and programs to promote Canada as an investment
  destination;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} collect, prepare and disseminate information to
  support the decisions of foreign investors with respect to foreign
  direct investment in Canada; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide services in a coordinated manner to foreign
  investors with respect to their actual or potential investments in
  Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Invest in Canada Hub

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  carrying out its mandate, the Invest in Canada Hub may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter into contracts, agreements, memoranda of
    understanding or other arrangements with a department, board or
    agency of the Government of Canada, with any other government or any
    of its departments, boards or agencies or with any person or
    organization in the name of Her Majesty in right of Canada or in its
    own name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into contracts, agreements, memoranda of
    understanding or other arrangements with Her Majesty in right of
    Canada as if it were not an agent of Her Majesty;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} acquire, hold, administer, dispose of or lease
    personal property or movables; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} do anything else that it considers necessary or
    incidental to achieving its mandate, exercising its powers or
    performing its duties and functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Title to property

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Title to property acquired by the Invest in Canada
  Hub may be held in the name of Her Majesty or in the name of the
  Invest in Canada Hub.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General authority

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Invest in Canada Hub has authority over all matters relating to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its policies with respect to contracts,
    communications, travel, hospitality, conferences and events and any
    of its other general administrative policies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its organization;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} human resources management, including the
    determination of the terms and conditions of employment of persons
    whom it employs; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} its internal auditing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of regulations and
  requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the Invest in Canada Hub is not subject to any
  regulation or requirement established by the Treasury Board under that
  Act that relates to any matter referred to in subsection (1), except

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the extent that the regulation or requirement
    under that Act relates to financial management; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to any regulation or requirement as it
    relates to any matter referred to in paragraphs (1)(a), (b) and (d),
    to the extent provided by the Governor in Council, by order, on the
    recommendation of the President of the Treasury Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procurement of goods and services

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite
  section 9 of the [Department of Public Works and Government Services
  Act](/eng/acts/P-38.2), the Invest in Canada Hub may procure goods and
  services from outside the federal public administration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal services

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, the Invest in Canada Hub may only procure
  legal services from outside the federal public administration with the
  approval of the Attorney General of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal proceedings

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Actions, suits or other
legal proceedings in respect of any right or obligation acquired or
incurred by the Invest in Canada Hub, whether in its own name or in the
name of Her Majesty in right of Canada, may be brought or taken by or
against the Invest in Canada Hub in its name.

## [Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-278209 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibility of Minister

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister is responsible for the Invest in Canada Hub.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial direction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Invest in Canada Hub must comply with any general
  or special direction given by the Minister in carrying out its
  mandate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's power of inquiry

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may inquire into any of the Invest in
  Canada Hub's activities and require it to provide him or her with any
  information under its control.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may require the Invest in Canada Hub to
  provide him or her with a report on its activities and operations.

## [Board of Directors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-278219 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment and composition

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Invest in Canada Hub is to have a board of directors consisting of not
  more than 11 directors, including a Chairperson and a
  Vice-Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Ex officio]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  director --- Deputy Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Deputy Minister of the department for which the
  Minister is responsible is [ex officio]{.otherLang lang="la"} a
  director of the board of directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson and the other
  directors, except the Deputy Minister, are to be appointed by the
  Governor in Council to hold office on a part-time basis and during
  pleasure for a term not exceeding three years that will ensure, to the
  extent possible, the end in any one year of the terms of office of not
  more than one half of the directors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A director appointed under subsection (3) is eligible
  for reappointment in the same or another capacity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and expenses

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A director appointed under subsection (3) is to be
  paid the remuneration that is fixed by the Governor in Council and is
  entitled to be paid reasonable travel and living expenses incurred
  while absent from their ordinary place of residence in the course of
  their duties under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role of board of directors

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  board of directors is responsible for supervising and managing the
  Invest in Canada Hub's business and affairs and for advising the
  Minister and the Chief Executive Officer on matters relating to the
  Invest in Canada Hub's mandate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The board of directors may make by-laws respecting
  the conduct of its proceedings and the general conduct of its
  activities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A majority of the directors holding office or five
  directors, whichever is greater, constitutes a quorum of the board of
  directors.

## [Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson]{.HTitleText1} {#h-278238 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role of Chairperson

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson presides over meetings of the board of directors and
  performs any other duties that are assigned to them by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting Chairperson

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  Chairperson, or a vacancy in that office, the Vice-Chairperson acts as
  Chairperson.

## [Chief Executive Officer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-278244 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Executive Officer of the Invest in Canada Hub is to be appointed
  by the Governor in Council to hold office during pleasure for a term
  of up to five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reappointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Executive Officer is eligible for
  reappointment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration and expenses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Executive Officer is to be paid the
  remuneration that is fixed by the Governor in Council and is entitled
  to be paid reasonable travel and living expenses while absent from his
  or her ordinary place of work in the course of his or her duties under
  this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role of Chief Executive Officer

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chief Executive Officer is responsible for the Invest in Canada Hub's
  day-to-day operations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rank of deputy head

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Executive Officer has the rank and the
  powers of a deputy head of a department.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual business plan and report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Executive Officer must submit to the board
  of directors, for its approval, an annual business plan and an annual
  report on the Invest in Canada Hub's operations and performance during
  the preceding fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting Chief Executive Officer

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  Chief Executive Officer, or a vacancy in that office, the Minister may
  appoint any person to act as Chief Executive Officer, but that person
  may only act as Chief Executive Officer for a period exceeding 90 days
  with the approval of the Governor in Council.

## [Human Resources]{.HTitleText1} {#h-278261 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Human resources management

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Invest in Canada Hub
may, in the exercise of its responsibilities in relation to human
resources management,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine its requirements with respect to human
  resources and provide for the allocation and effective utilization of
  human resources;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine requirements for the training and
  development of its personnel and fix the terms and conditions on which
  that training and development may be carried out;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide for the classification of the Invest in
  Canada Hub positions and employees;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} after consulting with the President of the Treasury
  Board, determine and regulate the pay to which persons employed by the
  Invest in Canada Hub are entitled for services rendered, the hours of
  work and leave of those persons and any related matters;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide for the awards that may be made to persons
  employed by the Invest in Canada Hub for outstanding performance of
  their duties and for other meritorious achievement in relation to
  those duties;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} establish standards of discipline for its employees
  and prescribe the financial and other penalties, including termination
  of employment and suspension, that may be applied for breaches of
  discipline or misconduct and the circumstances and manner in which and
  the authority by which or by whom those penalties may be applied or
  may be varied or rescinded in whole or in part;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} provide for the termination of employment or the
  demotion to a position at a lower maximum rate of pay, for reasons
  other than breaches of discipline or misconduct, of persons employed
  by the Invest in Canada Hub and establish the circumstances and manner
  in which and the authority by which or by whom those measures may be
  taken or may be varied or rescinded in whole or in part; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} provide for any other matters that the Invest in
  Canada Hub considers necessary for effective personnel management,
  including terms and conditions of employment not otherwise
  specifically provided for in this section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to appoint

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Invest in Canada Hub
has the exclusive authority to appoint any employees, including senior
vice-presidents and account executives, that it considers necessary for
carrying out its mandate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Group insurance and benefit programs

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Invest in Canada Hub may establish or enter into a contract to acquire
  group insurance or benefit programs for its employees and may set any
  terms and conditions in respect of those programs, including those
  relating to premiums, contributions, benefits, management and control
  and expenditures to be made from those contributions and premiums, and
  may audit and make contributions and pay premiums in respect of those
  programs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)
  does not apply to any contributions made or premiums paid by the
  Invest in Canada Hub or the members in respect of any program
  established under subsection (1) or any benefits received by the
  members of such a program.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staffing program

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Invest in Canada Hub must develop a program governing staffing,
  including the appointment of, and recourse for, employees.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collective agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Matters governed by the staffing program are not to
  be covered by a collective agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Negotiation of collective agreements

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Before entering into
collective bargaining with the bargaining agent for a bargaining unit
composed of the Invest in Canada Hub's employees, the Invest in Canada
Hub must have its negotiating mandate approved by the President of the
Treasury Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pension --- Chief Executive Officer

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  the Governor in Council otherwise orders, the Chief Executive Officer
  is deemed not to be employed in the public service for the purposes of
  the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pension --- other employees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Senior vice-presidents and account executives are
  deemed not to be employed in the public service for the purposes of
  the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** A director appointed
under subsection 12(3) and the Chief Executive Officer are deemed to be
employees for the purposes of the [Government Employees Compensation
Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and to be employed in the federal public
administration for the purposes of regulations made under section 9 of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2017, c. 20, s. 443

  - ###### Words and expressions {#words-and-expressions .MarginalNote}

    - [443]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} The words and expressions used
      in this section have the same meaning as in the [Invest in Canada
      Act](/eng/acts/I-21.5), as enacted by section 442.

    - ###### Powers, duties and functions of Chief Executive Officer {#powers-duties-and-functions-of-chief-executive-officer .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any power, duty or function of the Invest in
      Canada Hub under the [Invest in Canada Act](/eng/acts/I-21.5) is
      vested in or may be performed or exercised by the Chief Executive
      Officer of the Invest in Canada Hub until the first meeting of the
      board of directors.

    - ###### Transfer of money {#transfer-of-money .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any money that is appropriated by an Act of
      Parliament, for the fiscal year in which section 442 comes into
      force, to defray any expenditure of the Department of Foreign
      Affairs, Trade and Development related to the Invest in Canada Hub
      and that, on the day on which that section comes into force, is
      unexpended is deemed, on that day, to be an amount appropriated to
      defray any expenditure of the Invest in Canada Hub.
