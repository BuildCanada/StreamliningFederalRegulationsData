## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-82654 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to encourage long term savings through registered disability savings
plans to provide for the financial security of persons with severe and
prolonged impairments in physical or mental functions.

## [Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-82657 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of Minister

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, by order, designate a minister of the Crown to be "the Minister"
for the purposes of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informing Canadians

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may take any
measures that the Minister considers appropriate to make known to
Canadians the existence of Canada Disability Savings Grants and Canada
Disability Savings Bonds.

## [Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-82662 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canada Disability Savings Grants

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this Act and the regulations, on application, the Minister may, in
  respect of any contribution made to a registered disability savings
  plan of a beneficiary, pay a Canada Disability Savings Grant into the
  plan. The grant is to be paid on any terms and conditions that the
  Minister may specify by agreement between the Minister and the issuer
  of the plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of grant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of a Canada Disability Savings Grant that
  may be paid for a particular year is equal to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 300% of the part of the total contributions made in
    the particular year that is less than or equal to \$500, and 200% of
    the part of those contributions that is more than \$500 but less
    than or equal to \$1,500, if the beneficiary is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an individual who is at least 18 years of age on
      December 31 of the year preceding the particular year and whose
      family income for the particular year is less than or equal to the
      second threshold for the particular year,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a qualified dependant of an eligible individual
      whose adjusted income used to determine the amount of a Canada
      child benefit in respect of January in the particular year is less
      than or equal to the second threshold for the particular year, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a person in respect of whom a special allowance
      under the [Children's Special Allowances Act](/eng/acts/C-28.5) is
      payable for at least one month in the particular year; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 100% of the total contributions made in the
    particular year, up to a maximum of \$1,000, in any other case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed year of contribution

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), a contribution
  allocated to a year under subsection (2.2) is deemed to have been made
  in that year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation of contribution

  [(2.2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may allocate a contribution made to
  the beneficiary's registered disability savings plan in a year after
  2010, in parts --- to the year in which it is actually made and to
  each of the previous 10 years that is after 2007 and in which the plan
  was not a specified disability savings plan (other than a year in
  which the plan became a specified disability savings plan) --- in the
  following order:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} up to \$500 to each year in which the beneficiary
    is one referred to in paragraph (2)(a), beginning with the earliest
    year, less any contributions allocated to the year in question;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} up to \$1500 to each year in which the beneficiary
    is one referred to in paragraph (2)(a), beginning with the earliest
    year, less any contributions allocated to the year in question
    including those so allocated under paragraph (a); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} up to \$1000 to each year in which the beneficiary
    is not one referred to in paragraph (2)(a), beginning with the
    earliest year, less any contributions allocated to the year in
    question.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Residency and DTC-eligibility

  [(2.3)]{.lawlabel} No contribution made to the plan in a year may be
  allocated to a previous year unless, during that previous year, the
  beneficiary was resident in Canada and a DTC-eligible individual.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit

  [(2.4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may allocate only the portion of
  contributions made to the plan in a year in respect of which, in
  accordance with subsection (2), a Canada Disability Savings Grant of
  up to \$10,500 may be paid into the plan in that year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contributions made before 2011

  [(2.5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of determining the contributions
  allocated to the year in question under any of paragraphs (2.2)(a) to
  (c), contributions made to the plan in 2008, 2009 or 2010 are
  considered to have been allocated to the year in which they were
  actually made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Family income

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subparagraph (2)(a)(i), the
  family income for a particular year is that income determined for the
  year that ended on December 31 of the second preceding year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No determination for January

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If there has been no determination of eligibility for
  a Canada child benefit in respect of January in a particular year, the
  adjusted income to be used for the purposes of subparagraph (2)(a)(ii)
  is the adjusted income used to determine the amount of a Canada child
  benefit for the first month in the particular year in respect of which
  eligibility has been established.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Beneficiary born in December

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In applying subsection (4) in respect of a
  beneficiary born in December, the reference to "the first month in the
  particular year in respect of which eligibility has been established"
  in that subsection is to be read as a reference to "January of the
  next year".

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2010, c. 12, s. 27\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lifetime cap

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Not more than \$70,000 in Canada Disability Savings
  Grants may be paid in respect of a beneficiary during their lifetime.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual cap

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Not more than \$10,500 in Canada Disability Savings
  Grants may be paid in respect of a beneficiary in a year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual statement to plan holders

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} Once a year, the Minister shall cause each holder of
  a registered disability savings plan to be provided with a statement
  that sets out the amount of Canada Disability Savings Grants that may
  be paid for particular years on the basis of future contributions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canada Disability Savings Bonds

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to this Act and the regulations, on application, the Minister may pay
  a Canada Disability Savings Bond into a registered disability savings
  plan of a beneficiary

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each year after the year in which the plan is
    entered into; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the year in which the plan is entered into and
    for each of the previous 10 years

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that is after 2007,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} during which the beneficiary was resident in
      Canada, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for which a Canada Disability Savings Bond has
      not previously been paid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms and conditions

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} A Canada Disability Savings Bond is to be paid on
  any terms and conditions that the Minister may specify by agreement
  between the Minister and the issuer of the plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of bond

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of a Canada Disability Savings Bond that
  may be paid for a particular year is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$1,000, if the beneficiary is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an individual who is at least 18 years of age on
      December 31 of the year preceding the particular year and whose
      family income for the particular year is less than or equal to the
      phase-out income for the particular year,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a qualified dependant of an eligible individual
      whose adjusted income used to determine the amount of a Canada
      child benefit in respect of January in the particular year is less
      than or equal to the phase-out income for the particular year, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a person in respect of whom a special allowance
      under the [Children's Special Allowances Act](/eng/acts/C-28.5) is
      payable for at least one month in the particular year; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula set out in
    subsection (4), if the beneficiary is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an individual who is at least 18 years of age on
      December 31 of the year preceding the particular year and whose
      family income for the particular year is more than the phase-out
      income for the particular year but less than the first threshold
      for the particular year, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a qualified dependant of an eligible individual
      whose adjusted income used to determine the amount of a Canada
      child benefit in respect of January in the particular year is more
      than the phase-out income for the particular year but less than
      the first threshold for the particular year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Family income

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subparagraphs (2)(a)(i) and
  (b)(i), the family income for a particular year is that income
  determined for the year that ended on December 31 of the second
  preceding year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Formula

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (2)(b), the formula is
  as follows:

  ::: {#82724 .Subsection}
  \$1,000 - \[\$1,000 × (A - B)/(C - B)\]

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is, as the case may be, the family income referred to in
      subparagraph (2)(b)(i) or the adjusted income referred to in
      subparagraph (2)(b)(ii);

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the phase-out income for the particular year; and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the first threshold for the particular year.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rounding of amounts

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If an amount calculated under subsection (4) contains
  a fraction of a cent, the amount is to be rounded to the nearest whole
  cent or, if the amount is equidistant from two whole cents, to the
  higher of them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No determination for January

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If there has been no determination of eligibility for
  a Canada child benefit in respect of January in a particular year, the
  adjusted income to be used for the purposes of subparagraphs
  (2)(a)(ii) and (b)(ii) is the adjusted income used to determine the
  amount of a Canada child benefit for the first month in the particular
  year in respect of which eligibility has been established.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Beneficiary born in December

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In applying subsection (6) in respect of a
  beneficiary born in December, the reference to "the first month in the
  particular year in respect of which eligibility has been established"
  in that subsection is to be read as a reference to "January of the
  next year".

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2010, c. 12, s. 28\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lifetime cap

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} Not more than \$20,000 in Canada Disability Savings
  Bonds may be paid in respect of a beneficiary during their lifetime.

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is provided with, as the case may be,

  in the case of a Canada Disability Savings Grant, at the time the
  contribution to the plan is made and, in the case of a Canada
  Disability Savings Bond, immediately before the payment is made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, in
prescribed circumstances, pay interest, calculated as prescribed, in
respect of Canada Disability Savings Grants or Canada Disability Savings
Bonds.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of CRF

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** All amounts payable by
the Minister under this Act shall be paid out of the Consolidated
Revenue Fund.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waiver

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** On application made by
the holder or the beneficiary, to avoid undue hardship, the Minister
may, in prescribed circumstances, waive any of the prescribed
requirements of this Act or the regulations that relate to the payment
of any amount or the repayment of any amount or earnings generated by
that amount. The application must be in the form and manner approved by
the Minister.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-82756 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt due to Her Majesty

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  amount required to be repaid under this Act, the regulations or an
  agreement entered into under this Act constitutes a debt due to Her
  Majesty in right of Canada as of the date on which the Minister issues
  a written notice to the person responsible for the debt indicating the
  amount that is due.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of payments and interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Debts due to Her Majesty in right of Canada under
  this Act are recoverable, including in the Federal Court or any other
  court of competent jurisdiction, by the Minister of National Revenue.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction and set-off

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 14(1), debts due to Her Majesty in
  right of Canada under this Act may be recovered at any time by way of
  deduction from, set-off against or, in Quebec, compensation against,
  any sum of money that may be due or payable by Her Majesty in right of
  Canada to the person responsible for the debt, other than an amount
  payable under section 122.61 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction and set-off by the Minister

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsections 12(2)
and 14(1), an amount required to be repaid by a person under this Act,
the regulations or an agreement entered into under this Act may be
recovered by the Minister at any time by way of deduction from, set-off
against or, in Quebec, compensation against, any sum of money that may
be due or payable under this Act to the person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation or prescription period

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section, no action
  or proceedings shall be taken to recover debts due to Her Majesty in
  right of Canada under this Act after the expiry of the six-year
  limitation or prescription period that begins on the day on which the
  Minister issues the notice referred to in subsection 12(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgement of liability

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person's liability for debts due to Her Majesty
  in right of Canada under this Act is acknowledged in accordance with
  subsection (4), the time during which the limitation or prescription
  period has run before the acknowledgement does not count in the
  calculation of that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgement after expiry of
  limitation or prescription period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person's liability for debts due to Her Majesty
  in right of Canada under this Act is acknowledged in accordance with
  subsection (4) after the expiry of the limitation or prescription
  period, an action or proceedings to recover the money may, subject to
  subsections (2) and (5), be brought within six years after the date of
  the acknowledgement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of acknowledgements

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An acknowledgement of liability means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a written promise to pay the money owing, signed by
    the person or his or her agent or other representative;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written acknowledgement of the money owing,
    signed by the person or his or her agent or other representative,
    whether or not a promise to pay can be implied from it and whether
    or not it contains a refusal to pay;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a part payment by the person or his or her agent or
    other representative of any money owing; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any acknowledgement of the money owing made by the
    person, his or her agent or other representative or the trustee or
    director in the course of proceedings under the [Bankruptcy and
    Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3) or any other legislation dealing with
    the payment of debts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation or prescription period
  suspended

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The running of a limitation or prescription period is
  suspended during

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the period beginning on the day on which the
    Minister receives an application under section 11 and ending on the
    day on which the Minister issues a decision;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the period beginning on the day on which the
    Minister of National Revenue receives an application concerning
    subsection 146.4(12) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) and
    ending on the day on which that Minister makes a decision;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the period beginning on the day on which an
    application for judicial review, with respect to a decision of the
    Minister to issue a notice under subsection 12(1), is filed and
    ending on the day on which the final decision is rendered; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any period in which it is prohibited to commence or
    continue an action or other proceedings against the person to
    recover debts due to Her Majesty in right of Canada under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement proceedings

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply in respect of an action
  or proceedings relating to the execution, renewal or enforcement of a
  judgment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations for carrying out the purpose and provisions of this
Act and, without limiting the generality of the foregoing, may make
regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} establishing requirements that must be met by a
  registered disability savings plan and by persons in respect of the
  plan before a Canada Disability Savings Grant or a Canada Disability
  Savings Bond may be paid in respect of the plan;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} establishing the manner of determining the amount of
  a Canada Disability Savings Grant that may be paid in respect of
  contributions made to registered disability savings plans or the
  amount of a Canada Disability Savings Bond that may be paid into those
  plans;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} specifying terms and conditions to be included in
  agreements entered into between an issuer of a registered disability
  savings plan and the Minister;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} governing the repayment of any amount paid under this
  Act or earnings generated by those amounts including providing for the
  circumstances under which an amount or earnings must be repaid and the
  manner of calculating such an amount or earnings;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} specifying the circumstances in which the Minister
  may pay interest on Canada Disability Savings Grants or Canada
  Disability Savings Bonds as well as the manner of calculating
  interest;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} specifying the requirements of this Act or the
  regulations relating to the payment of any amount or the repayment of
  any amount or earnings generated by that amount that may be waived by
  the Minister to avoid undue hardship;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} specifying the circumstances in which the Minister
  may waive the requirements provided under paragraph (f);

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} specifying information that the Minister may collect
  under section 15; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} requiring issuers to keep any record, book or other
  document containing any information relevant to the administration or
  enforcement of this Act or the regulations, and respecting where, how
  and how long it is to be kept.

  - - [81]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the [Income

      occurred, in the order that they actually occurred, on December
      31, 2008 and not on the day or days that they actually occurred.

    - [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), [[specified
      RDSP event]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an event occurring after
      2008 and before March 3, 2009 that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establishes a [[disability savings

        of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3);

        the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3);

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} establishes a [[registered disability savings
        plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 146.4(1)

        146.4(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) and who was
        resident in Canada at the end of that year;
