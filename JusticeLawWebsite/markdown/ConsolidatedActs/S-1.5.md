## Sahtu Dene and Metis Land Claim Settlement Act {#sahtu-dene-and-metis-land-claim-settlement-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1994, c. 27
:::

Assented to 1994-06-23

An Act to approve, give effect to and declare valid an agreement between
Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada and the Dene of Colville Lake,
Déline, Fort Good Hope and Fort Norman and the Metis of Fort Good Hope,
Fort Norman and Norman Wells, as represented by the Sahtu Tribal
Council, and to make related amendments to another Act
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the Slavey, Hare and Mountain Dene of the Sahtu region have
traditionally used and occupied lands in the Northwest Territories from
time immemorial;

WHEREAS the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/) recognizes and affirms
the existing aboriginal and treaty rights of the aboriginal peoples of
Canada;

WHEREAS Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada and the Sahtu Dene and
Metis, as represented by the Sahtu Tribal Council, undertook
negotiations in order to achieve certainty and clarity of rights with
respect to ownership and use of those lands and their resources;

WHEREAS the Sahtu Dene and Metis, by a vote held from July 5 to 8, 1993,
approved a comprehensive land claim agreement that, in exchange for the
release of certain rights and claims as set out in the agreement,
defines the rights that the Sahtu Dene and Metis shall have, and
confirms the treaty rights that are unaffected by that release;

WHEREAS Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada and the Sahtu Dene and
Metis, as represented by the Sahtu Tribal Council, signed the agreement
on September 6, 1993;

WHEREAS the Sahtu Tribal Council by resolution approved certain
amendments to the agreement on January 13 and February 11, 1994;

AND WHEREAS the agreement provides that the agreement will be a land
claims agreement within the meaning of section 35 of the [Constitution
Act, 1982](/eng/Const/) and that approval by Parliament is a condition
precedent to the validity of the agreement;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-430476 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Sahtu Dene and Metis Land Claim Settlement Act](/eng/acts/S-1.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-430479 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of \"Agreement\"

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,
[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Comprehensive Land Claim
Agreement between Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada and the Sahtu
Dene and Metis, as represented by the Sahtu Tribal Council, signed on
September 6, 1993 and tabled in the House of Commons by the Minister of
Indian Affairs and Northern Development on March 8, 1994, including any
amendments made to it from time to time.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-430482 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-430485 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement given effect

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement is hereby approved, given effect and declared valid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and obligations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, any person or body may
  exercise the powers, rights, privileges and benefits conferred on the
  person or body by the Agreement and shall perform the duties and is
  subject to the liabilities imposed on the person or body by the
  Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Title to lands

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, title to lands vests in one or
  more designated Sahtu organizations as provided in the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Renewable Resources Board

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
carrying out its objectives, the Renewable Resources Board established
by the Agreement has the capacity, rights, powers and privileges of a
natural person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make such orders and regulations as are necessary for the purpose of
carrying out the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of Agreement and amendments

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Indian
Affairs and Northern Development shall cause a certified copy of the
Agreement and of any amendments made to it to be deposited in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the library of the Department of Indian Affairs and
  Northern Development that is situated in the National Capital Region;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the regional office of the Department of Indian
  Affairs and Northern Development situated in the Northwest
  Territories;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the legislative library of the Government of the
  Northwest Territories; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} such other places as the Minister considers
  necessary.

## [Other Laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-430503 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency or conflict

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where there is an
inconsistency or conflict between this Act or the Agreement and the
provisions of any law, this Act or the Agreement, as the case may be,
prevails to the extent of the inconsistency or conflict.
