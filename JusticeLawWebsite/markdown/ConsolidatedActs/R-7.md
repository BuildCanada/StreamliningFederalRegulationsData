## Resources and Technical Surveys Act {#resources-and-technical-surveys-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. R-7
:::

An Act respecting resources and technical surveys
::::

::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427987 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Resources and Technical Surveys Act](/eng/acts/R-7).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. R-7, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-427992 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of Natural
    Resources; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to technical surveys relating to any
      matter to which the powers, duties and functions of the Minister
      of the Environment extend by law, and any powers, duties and
      functions under this Act that relate to any such matter, means the
      Minister of the Environment,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to technical surveys relating to any
      matter to which the powers, duties and functions of the Minister
      of Fisheries and Oceans extend by law, and any powers, duties and
      functions under this Act that relate to any such matter, means the
      Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to other technical surveys and all
      other powers, duties and functions under this Act, the Minister of
      Natural Resources; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[technical surveys]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[technical surveys]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means geological,
    geophysical, geochemical, geographical, geodetic, topographical,
    hydrographic, hydrogeological, geotechnical, oceanographic,
    meteorological and other similar surveys. ([levés]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-7, s. 2
- 1994, c. 41, s. 33
:::

## [Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-428003 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of the Minister

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} collect and publish statistics of the mineral
  exploration, development and production and of the mining and
  metallurgical industries of Canada, and such data regarding the
  economic minerals of Canada as relate to the processes and activities
  connected with their utilization, and collect and preserve records of
  mines and mining works in Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} make detailed investigations of mining camps and
  areas containing economic minerals or deposits of other economic
  substances, for the purpose of determining the mode of occurrence and
  the extent and character of the ore-bodies and deposits of the
  economic minerals or other economic substances;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} make a full and scientific examination and survey of
  the geological structure and mineralogy of Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} make the chemical, mechanical, metallurgical and
  other researches and investigations that are necessary or desirable to
  carry out the purposes and provisions of this Act and particularly to
  aid the mining and metallurgical industry of Canada;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} collect and prepare for exhibition such specimens of
  the different ores and associated rocks and minerals of Canada and
  other materials as are necessary to afford a knowledge of the geology
  and mineralogy and the mining and metallurgical resources and
  industries of Canada; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} prepare and publish the maps, plans, sections,
  diagrams, drawings, documents and data that are necessary to
  illustrate and elucidate any reports of investigations and surveys
  made pursuant to this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-7, s. 3
- 1994, c. 41, s. 34
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surveys

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, for the
purpose of obtaining a basis for the representation of the mineral and
mining resources and of the geographical and geological features of any
part of Canada, cause the measurements, observations, investigations and
physiographic, exploratory and reconnaissance surveys to be made that
are necessary for or in connection with the preparation of maps,
sketches, plans, sections or diagrams.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. R-7, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution of specimens and
publications

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may authorize
the distribution or sale of products, data, duplicate specimens, maps
and other documents produced or issued by or on behalf of the
Department.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-7, s. 5
- 1994, c. 41, s. 35
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5 of the
[Department of Natural Resources Act](/eng/acts/N-20.8) respecting the
powers, duties and functions of the Minister in relation to matters
mentioned in that section over which Parliament has jurisdiction, the
Minister shall be responsible for coordinating, promoting and
recommending national policies and programs with respect to energy,
mines and minerals, water and other resources, and, in carrying out the
Minister's responsibilities under this section, the Minister may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} conduct or cooperate with persons conducting applied
  and basic research programs and investigations and economic studies in
  relation to those resources, and for that purpose maintain and operate
  research institutes, laboratories, observatories and other facilities
  for exploration and research related to the source, origin,
  properties, development or use of those resources; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} study, keep under review and consider recommendations
  with respect to matters relating to the exploration for, or the
  production, recovery, manufacture, processing, transmission,
  transportation, distribution, sale, purchase, exchange or disposition
  of, any of those resources, and with respect to matters relating to
  the sources of those resources within or outside Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-7, s. 6
- 1994, c. 41, s. 35
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conservation, etc., of resources

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, in exercising the powers and carrying out the duties and
  functions mentioned in section 6, formulate plans for the
  conservation, development and use of the resources specified in that
  section and for related research and the Minister may carry out those
  plans in cooperation with other departments, branches and agencies of
  the Government of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cooperation and agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In formulating and carrying out any plans under
  subsection (1), the Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cooperate with the provinces and with
    municipalities;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into agreements with any person or body,
    including the government of any province or any department, branch
    or agency of such a government, respecting the carrying out of those
    plans; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} make grants and contributions and, with the
    approval of the Governor in Council, provide other forms of
    financial assistance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, in exercising the powers and
  carrying out the duties and functions mentioned in this section,
  including in relation to technical surveys, consult with, and
  inaugurate conferences of representatives of, producers, industry, the
  universities, labour and provincial and municipal authorities.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-7, s. 7
- 1994, c. 41, s. 35
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Logistic support, etc., relating to the
Arctic region

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may coordinate logistics support and provide related
  assistance for the purposes of advancing scientific knowledge of the
  Arctic region and contributing to the exercise of Canada's sovereignty
  in that region and its adjacent waters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of expenditures

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make grants and contributions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make recoverable expenditures on behalf of any
    other department, branch or agency of the Government of Canada or a
    province or any university, organization or person in respect of its
    share of the cost of any logistics support or related assistance.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1994, c. 41, s. 35
:::
