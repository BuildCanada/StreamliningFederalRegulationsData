## Nunavik Inuit Land Claims Agreement Act {#nunavik-inuit-land-claims-agreement-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2008, c. 2
:::

Assented to 2008-02-14

An Act to give effect to the Nunavik Inuit Land Claims Agreement and to
make a consequential amendment to another Act
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the Nunavik Inuit assert aboriginal rights, title, interests and
jurisdiction in and to the Nunavik Inuit Settlement Area as defined in
the Agreement;

WHEREAS the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/) recognizes and affirms
the existing aboriginal and treaty rights of the aboriginal peoples of
Canada;

WHEREAS the Nunavik Inuit, as represented by Makivik, and the Government
of Canada have negotiated the Agreement;

WHEREAS the Nunavik Inuit, by a vote held from October 16 to 20, 2006,
approved the Agreement;

WHEREAS the Agreement was signed on behalf of the Nunavik Inuit and Her
Majesty the Queen in right of Canada on December 1, 2006;

AND WHEREAS the Agreement requires that legislation be enacted by the
Parliament of Canada in order for the Agreement to be ratified;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-369616 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Nunavik Inuit Land Claims Agreement Act](/eng/acts/N-28.5).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-369619 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the land claims agreement
    between the Nunavik Inuit and Her Majesty the Queen in right of
    Canada signed on December 1, 2006, including any amendments made to
    it. ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Makivik]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Makivik]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the corporation established by
    An Act respecting the Makivik Corporation, R.S.Q., c. S-18.1, and
    representing the Inuit of northern
    Quebec. ([Makivik]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status of Agreement

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Agreement is a treaty
within the meaning of section 35 of the [Constitution Act,
1982](/eng/Const/).

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-369626 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Act binding on Her Majesty

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province so as to give effect to the
Agreement in accordance with its terms.

## [Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-369629 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement given effect

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement is approved, given effect and declared valid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and obligations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, any person or body has the
  powers, rights, privileges and benefits conferred on the person or
  body by the Agreement and shall perform the duties, and is subject to
  the liabilities, imposed on the person or body by the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Third parties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Agreement is binding on, and may be relied on by,
  all persons and bodies that are not parties to it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency with Agreement

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  event of an inconsistency or a conflict between the Agreement and this
  Act or any law referred to in section 2.11 of the Agreement, the
  Agreement prevails to the extent of the inconsistency or conflict.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency with Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of an inconsistency or a conflict
  between this Act and any other statute, this Act prevails to the
  extent of the inconsistency or conflict.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal capacity

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of carrying out their objectives, the Nunavik Marine Region
  Wildlife Board, the Nunavik Marine Region Planning Commission and the
  Nunavik Marine Region Impact Review Board established by the Agreement
  each have the capacity, rights, powers and privileges of a natural
  person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not agents of Her Majesty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Nunavik Marine Region Wildlife Board, the Nunavik
  Marine Region Planning Commission and the Nunavik Marine Region Impact
  Review Board are not agents of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

## [Appropriation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-369647 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of C.R.F.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** There shall be paid out of
the Consolidated Revenue Fund any sums that are required to meet the
monetary obligations of Her Majesty in right of Canada under Articles 5,
15, 16 and 23 of the Agreement.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-369650 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judicial notice of Agreement

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Judicial
  notice shall be taken of the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Agreement shall be published by the Queen's
  Printer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the Agreement published by the Queen's
  Printer is evidence of the Agreement and of its contents, and a copy
  purporting to be published by the Queen's Printer is deemed to be so
  published, unless the contrary is shown.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of issues arising

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, in
  any judicial or administrative proceeding, an issue arises in respect
  of the interpretation or validity of the Agreement or the validity or
  applicability of this Act, the issue shall not be decided unless the
  party raising the issue has served notice on the Attorney General of
  Canada and Makivik.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} describe the judicial or administrative proceeding
    in which the issue arises;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} state whether the issue arises in respect of the
    interpretation or validity of the Agreement or the validity or
    applicability of this Act, or both;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state the day on which the issue is to be argued;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} give particulars necessary to show the point to be
    argued; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be served at least 14 days before the day of
    argument, unless the court or tribunal authorizes a shorter period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation in proceedings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In any judicial or administrative proceeding to which
  subsection (1) applies, the Attorney General of Canada and Makivik may
  appear and participate in the proceeding as parties with the same
  rights as any other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsections (2) and (3) do not
  require that an oral hearing be held if one is not otherwise required.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An instrument made under
the Agreement is not a statutory instrument for the purposes of the
[Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make any orders and regulations that are necessary for the purpose
of carrying out any of the provisions of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review by Makivik

- **[[12.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within ten years after this Act
  receives royal assent, a review of the implementation of this Act and
  the Agreement may be undertaken by Makivik.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Makivik may submit a report on any review undertaken
  under subsection (1) to the Minister referred to in sebsection
  12.2(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause any report submitted under
  subsection (2) to be laid before each House of Parliament on any of
  the first 15 days on which that House is sitting after the Minister
  receives it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review by Minister

- **[[12.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within ten years after the Act
  receives royal assent, a comprehensive review of the implementation of
  this Act and the Agreement shall be undertaken by the Minister
  designated by the Governor in Council for the purposes of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause a report on the review to be
  laid before each House of Parliament on any of the first 15 days on
  which that House is sitting after the report is completed.
