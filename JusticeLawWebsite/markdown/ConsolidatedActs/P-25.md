## Prohibition of International Air Services Act {#prohibition-of-international-air-services-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. P-25
:::

An Act to provide for the prohibition of certain international air
services
::::

::::::::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Prohibition of International Air Services Act](/eng/acts/P-25).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 61, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who operates a
    commercial air service; ([transporteur aérien]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any aircraft other than an
    aircraft that is used in military, customs or police
    service; ([aéronef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial air service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial air service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any use of
    aircraft for hire or reward. ([service aérien
    commercial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 61, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 61, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of Governor in Council

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Minister of Foreign Affairs certifies to the Governor in Council
  that a state is in default within the meaning of subsection (2), the
  Governor in Council may, by order, prohibit air carriers from carrying
  out any one or more of the following activities, having regard to the
  circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} operating a commercial air service between Canada
    and the defaulting state;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where an air carrier is an air carrier of the
    defaulting state, operating a commercial air service between Canada
    and any other state;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} flying across Canadian territory, if the flight is
    coming from or going to the defaulting state;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} landing in Canada, if the flight is coming from or
    going to the defaulting state;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} where an air carrier is an air carrier of the
    defaulting state, flying across Canadian territory; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} where an air carrier is an air carrier of the
    defaulting state, landing in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When a state is in default

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a state is in
  default if the state has not extradited or prosecuted any of the
  parties to an unlawful act or has not permitted the continuation of
  the journey of the passengers and crew of an aircraft in respect of
  which an unlawful act has been committed or has not returned an
  aircraft in respect of which an unlawful act has been committed to the
  persons lawfully entitled to possession thereof.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of "unlawful act"

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (2), an [[unlawful
  act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any act by which a person, while on
  board an aircraft in flight, by force or threat of force, seizes or
  exercises control over the aircraft or attempts to do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aircraft in flight

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (3), an aircraft is
  deemed to be in flight from the time all its external doors are closed
  following embarkation until such time as one of the doors is opened
  for disembarkation or, in the case of a forced landing, until such
  time as the competent authorities take responsibility for the aircraft
  and the persons and property on board.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. P-25, s. 4
- 1995, c. 5, s. 25
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment or repeal of order

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
amend or repeal any order made under subsection 4(1).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 61, s. 4
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every air carrier that
fails to comply with an order made under subsection 4(1) is guilty of an
offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding
twenty-five thousand dollars.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 61, s. 5
:::
