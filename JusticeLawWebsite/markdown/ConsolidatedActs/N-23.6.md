## Non-smokers' Health Act {#non-smokers-health-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. 15 (4th
Supp.)
:::

An Act to regulate smoking in the federal workplace and on certain modes
of transportation

::: ReaderNote
\[1988, c. 21, assented to 28th June, 1988\]
:::
:::::

::::::::::::::::::::::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Non-smokers' Health Act](/eng/acts/N-23.6).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[cannabis]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cannabis]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Cannabis
      Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5); ([cannabis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated smoking area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated smoking area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area,
      other than an enclosed room, that is designated for smoking under
      subsection 3(2); ([zone fumeurs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated smoking room]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated smoking room]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an enclosed
      room that is designated for smoking under subsection
      3(2); ([fumoir]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is employed by
      an employer; ([employé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who employs one or
      more persons in employment described in subsection 123(1) of the
      [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2), or

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Treasury Board, in relation to employees in
        any portion of the federal public administration specified in
        Schedule I or IV to the [Financial Administration
        Act](/eng/acts/F-11),

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a separate agency named in Schedule V to the
        [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), in relation to
        employees of that separate agency,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Senate, House of Commons, Library of
        Parliament, office of the Senate Ethics Officer, office of the
        Conflict of Interest and Ethics Commissioner, Parliamentary
        Protective Service or office of the Parliamentary Budget
        Officer, in relation to their employees or the employees of a
        committee of the Senate or House of Commons, as the case may be,
        or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Senate or House of Commons, in
        relation to employees on the staff of the member, including
        persons employed in the office of a Minister, or in relation to
        employees on the staff of a political party housed in
        accommodation provided by the Senate or House of Commons who are
        under the direction and control of the
        member; ([employeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is designated
      as an inspector under section 9; ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[smoke]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[smoke]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to smoke, hold or otherwise
      have control over an ignited tobacco product or ignited cannabis
      or to vape using a vaping product; ([fumer]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[tobacco product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[tobacco product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any product
      manufactured from tobacco and intended for use by
      smoking; ([produit à base de tabac]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[vaping product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vaping product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a device that is intended to be used to
        simulate the act of smoking a tobacco product or cannabis and
        that emits an aerosol that is intended to be inhaled, including
        an electronic cigarette, an electronic cigar and an electronic
        pipe; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a device that is designated to be a vaping
        product by the regulations; ([produit de
        vapotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[work space]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[work space]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any indoor or other
      enclosed space in which employees perform the duties of their
      employment, and includes any adjacent corridor, lobby, stairwell,
      elevator, cafeteria, washroom or other common area frequented by
      such employees during the course of their employment. ([lieu de
      travail]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application outside Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Act, except section 10, applies outside Canada
  in respect of a work space on an aircraft, train or motor vehicle
  being operated between Canada and another country by a Canadian, as
  defined in section 55 of the [Canada Transportation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4), or a work space on a vessel registered or
  listed under the [Canada Shipping Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) that
  is being operated between Canada and another country, to the extent
  that compliance with this Act within the territory of another
  jurisdiction does not result in the contravention of the laws of that
  jurisdiction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application to foreign carriers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} This Act does not apply in respect of a work space on
  an aircraft, train or motor vehicle being operated between Canada and
  another country by a person other than a Canadian, as defined in
  section 55 of the [Canada Transportation Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4) or a
  work space on a ship registered under the laws of another country that
  is being operated between Canada and another country.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application to commuter trains

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} This Act does not apply in respect of a work space on
  a passenger train operated in commuter service by or for the account
  of the government of a province or an agency thereof.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of employers

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  employer, and any person acting on behalf of an employer, shall ensure
  that persons refrain from smoking in any work space under the control
  of the employer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of smoking rooms and areas

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An employer may, to the extent permitted by the
  regulations, designate for smoking

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enclosed rooms under the control of the employer
    other than rooms normally occupied by non-smokers; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} areas under the control of the employer on an
    aircraft, train, motor vehicle or ship or in an airport passenger
    terminal, railway passenger station, interurban bus station or
    marine passenger terminal other than areas normally occupied by
    non-smokers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), an employer may
  require employees, by reason of the nature of their duties, to perform
  those duties in a room or area designated for smoking under subsection
  (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Independent ventilation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where an employer has designated a room for smoking
  under subsection (2) in a building or portion of a building the
  construction of which commenced before January 1, 1990, the employer
  shall, to the extent reasonably practicable, ensure that the room
  conforms to any requirements of the regulations respecting independent
  ventilation of designated smoking rooms.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} No employer shall designate a room for smoking under
  subsection (2) in a building or portion of a building the construction
  of which commenced after December 31, 1989 if the room fails to
  conform to any requirements of the regulations respecting independent
  ventilation of designated smoking rooms.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} No employer shall designate a room or area for
  smoking under subsection (2) in a work place, other than on an
  aircraft, train, motor vehicle or ship that is carrying passengers,
  until after the employer has consulted with the work place committee
  or health and safety representative in respect of that work place or,
  if there is no such committee or representative, with the employees
  employed there.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expressions used in [Canada Labour
  Code](/eng/acts/L-2)

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (6), the expressions "work place
  committee", "health and safety representative" and "work place" have
  the same meaning as in Part II of the [Canada Labour
  Code](/eng/acts/L-2).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 15 (4th Supp.), s. 3
- 1989, c. 7, s. 1
- 2000, c. 20, s. 29
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Smoking prohibited

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall smoke in any work space under the control of an employer
  except in a designated smoking room or designated smoking area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An employer shall, to the extent and in the manner
  required by the regulations, inform employees and members of the
  public of the prohibition imposed by subsection (1) and of the
  location of designated smoking rooms and designated smoking areas
  under the control of the employer.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 15 (4th Supp.), s. 4
- 1989, c. 7, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Smoking areas on aircraft

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  employer may not designate an area for smoking under subsection 3(2)
  on an aircraft carrying passengers for hire or reward except

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on segments of a flight that are longer than two
    hours or such greater period as the regulations prescribe;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on segments of a flight that includes a segment
    extending between Canada and a country other than the United States;
    or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on a flight operated in accordance with a charter
    contract under which the cost of transportation of all passengers is
    paid by one person, company or organization and no charge or other
    financial obligation is imposed on a passenger as a condition of
    carriage or otherwise in connection with the transportation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Smoking areas on trains

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An employer may not, in respect of a train carrying
  passengers, designate for smoking under subsection 3(2)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} areas comprising more than two thirds of the
    seating accommodation of any class;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} areas on more than two thirds of the cars providing
    a class of seating accommodation; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} areas comprising more than two thirds of the
    sleeping car accommodation other than enclosed accommodation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request to stop smoking

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An employee who becomes aware that a passenger is
  smoking in contravention of section 4 on an aircraft, train, motor
  vehicle or ship operated by the employer of the employee shall request
  the passenger to refrain from smoking.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal of passenger

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a passenger fails to comply with a request made
  under subsection (3), the employer shall require the passenger to
  disembark at the next scheduled stop following the passenger's failure
  to comply.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 15 (4th Supp.), s. 5
- 1989, c. 7, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Saving

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in section 4 or 5
affects the operation of any other Act of Parliament, any regulations
made under any Act of Parliament, or any rule of law in relation to the
protection of persons from exposure to tobacco or cannabis smoke or any
emission from a vaping product.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 15 (4th Supp.), s. 6
- 1989, c. 7, s. 1
- 2018, c. 9, s. 83, c. 16, ss. 163, 189

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the size, number, proportionate floor
    space, location, use, number of occupants and other characteristics
    of rooms and areas that may be designated for smoking under
    subsection 3(2);

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} designating any device to be a vaping product for
    the purpose of the definition [[vaping
    product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef};

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the ventilation of designated smoking
    rooms;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} designating outdoor spaces or classes of outdoor
    spaces for the purpose of the definition [[work
    space]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef};

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} permitting the designation of the whole or any part
    of aircraft, trains, motor vehicles or ships as areas or rooms for
    smoking, either generally or on specified routes or in specified
    classes of service and, subject to subsection 5(2), prescribing the
    maximum proportion of accommodation of any class thereon that may be
    designated for smoking;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the maximum proportion of aircraft
    flights, train schedules, motor vehicle trips or ship voyages
    operated for hire or reward in passenger service in respect of which
    designated smoking areas or rooms may be provided during any
    specified travel period, either generally or on specified routes or
    in specified classes of service;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} prescribing, either generally or in respect of
    specified routes or specified classes of service,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a period greater than two hours for the purposes
      of paragraph 5(1)(a), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the minimum duration of an aircraft flight
      referred to in paragraph 5(1)(b) or (c), or of a flight not
      carrying passengers, in respect of which areas may be designated
      for smoking;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} requiring employers to inform employees and members
    of the public of the prohibition imposed by section 4 and of the
    location of designated smoking areas and designated smoking rooms,
    and respecting the manner of so informing them; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the form of tickets and informations
    for the purposes of section 14 and fixing the fine payable in
    proceedings under that section in respect of a first or a subsequent
    contravention of any provision of this Act, not exceeding the amount
    fixed by section 11 for such a contravention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Regulations made pursuant to this section may be made
  applicable to all employers or to a class of employer and in respect
  of all work spaces or a class of work space.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings against Crown

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding any other
Act of Parliament, proceedings in respect of an offence under this Act
may be brought against an employer that is an agent of Her Majesty in
the employer's own name, or against a person acting on behalf of such an
employer, in the same manner as if that employer were a person not an
agent of Her Majesty, and a fine imposed against the employer in such
proceedings may be paid out of any funds held by the employer.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 15 (4th Supp.), s. 8
- 1989, c. 7, s. 1

may by regulation direct that this Act applies in respect of any
employment, or any class or classes of employment, on or in connection
with a work or undertaking set out in the regulation that is, or is part
of, a corporation that is an agent of Her Majesty in right of a province
and whose activities are regulated, in whole or in part, pursuant to the
[Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1996, c. 12, s. 5
- 1997, c. 9, s. 125

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion from application

- **[[8.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may by regulation exclude, in whole or in part,
  from the application of any or all of the provisions of this Act any
  employment, or any class or classes of employment, on or in connection
  with a work or undertaking set out in the regulation whose activities
  are regulated, in whole or in part, pursuant to the [Nuclear Safety
  and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the recommendation of the Minister of Labour, the
  Governor in Council may make regulations respecting smoking in a
  workplace at which is carried on employment that is subject to a
  regulation made under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation of provincial law

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under subsection (2) incorporating
  by reference, in whole or in part, an Act of the legislature of a
  province or an instrument made under such an Act may incorporate the
  Act or instrument as amended to a certain date or from time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of regulation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under subsection (2) may apply

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} generally, with respect to all employment that is
    subject to a regulation made pursuant to subsection (1); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to any class or classes of employment that are
    subject to a regulation made pursuant to subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administration and enforcement

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A regulation made under subsection (2) incorporating
  an Act or instrument shall, after consultation by the Minister of
  Labour with the appropriate provincial minister, be administered and
  enforced by the person or authority that is responsible for the
  administration and enforcement of the Act or instrument.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and penalty

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes a regulation made under
  subsection (2) by contravening a provision of an Act of the
  legislature of a province that, or an instrument made under such an
  Act that, is incorporated by the regulation is guilty of an offence
  against this Act and liable to the same punishment as is imposed by or
  under an Act of that legislature for the contravention of that
  provision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The prosecution of an offence in respect of a
  contravention described in subsection (6) shall be commenced by the
  attorney general of the province in which the offence was committed.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1996, c. 12, s. 5
- 1997, c. 9, s. 125
- 2018, c. 9, s. 85

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of inspectors

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Labour may designate any person to be an inspector for the
  purposes of sections 10 and 14.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial inspectors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Labour may, with the approval of the
  Governor in Council, enter into an agreement with the government of a
  province or an agency thereof stipulating terms and conditions under
  which a person employed by that government or agency may act as an
  inspector for the purposes of this Act, and a person so employed and
  so referred to in the agreement shall be deemed to be designated an
  inspector under this section at the time fixed by or under the
  agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificates

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Labour shall provide an inspector
  with a certificate of the inspector's authority and, on entering any
  work space referred to in section 10, the inspector shall, on demand
  by the person in charge of that place, produce the certificate for
  examination by that person.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 15 (4th Supp.), s. 9
- 1989, c. 7, s. 1

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspection of premises

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of verifying compliance with this Act, an inspector may at
  any reasonable time enter and inspect any work space under the control
  of an employer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance to inspectors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person in charge of a work space entered by an
  inspector pursuant to subsection (1) and every person found in that
  place shall give the inspector all reasonable assistance to enable the
  inspector to carry out the inspector's duties and shall furnish the
  inspector with any information that the inspector has reasonable
  grounds to believe is required to carry out the inspection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall wilfully obstruct an inspector in the
  performance of the inspector's duties under this section.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1989, c. 7, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  employer who contravenes section 3 or subsection 4(2) or 5(4) is
  guilty of an offence and is liable on summary conviction

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine not exceeding one
    thousand dollars; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a subsequent offence, to a fine not exceeding
    ten thousand dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes subsection 4(1) or 5(3)
  is guilty of an offence and is liable on summary conviction

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a first offence, to a fine not exceeding fifty
    dollars; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a subsequent offence, to a fine not exceeding
    one hundred dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes section 10 is guilty of
  an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding
  one thousand dollars.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1989, c. 7, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence outside Canada

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Proceedings in respect of
an offence under this Act alleged to have been committed outside Canada
may be brought in a court of competent jurisdiction for any judicial
division in Canada in which the accused person is found, as if the
offence had been committed in that judicial division.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1989, c. 7, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service on corporations, etc.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Service of a summons
under the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or a ticket under this Act on
an employer that is not an individual may be effected by delivery of the
summons or ticket to the president, secretary or other executive officer
of the employer, to the person appearing to have the control or
management of any establishment of the employer, or to the employee or
agent by whose act or omission the employer is alleged to have committed
the offence.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1989, c. 7, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings commenced by ticket

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector or peace officer who has reasonable grounds to believe that
  an offence has been committed under this Act may serve a ticket in the
  form prescribed by the regulations, completed and signed by the
  inspector or peace officer, by causing the ticket to be delivered to
  the person believed to have committed the offence, if an individual,
  or to a person referred to in section 13 in any other case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laying of information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Either before or after service of a ticket, an
  information under oath shall be laid before a justice in respect of
  the offence alleged in the ticket.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Voluntary guilty plea

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person on whom a ticket is served or, where service
  is effected in accordance with section 13, a person acting on behalf
  of the employer so served may, within fifteen days after it is served,
  complete and sign the plea of guilty set out in the ticket and deliver
  or mail the plea, together with the fine fixed by the regulations and
  specified in the ticket, to the court specified in the ticket.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conviction

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Acceptance by the court of a payment made under
  subsection (3) constitutes a plea of guilty, whether or not the plea
  is signed or the signature is proved, and endorsement of the payment
  on the ticket constitutes the conviction of the person for the
  offence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Service of a ticket may be proved by the oral
  evidence given under oath of the person who served it or by the
  affidavit of that person made before a justice or other person
  authorized to administer oaths or take affidavits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Default conviction

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where no plea is entered in respect of an offence
  that is alleged in a ticket and service of the ticket is proved before
  a justice, the justice shall examine the information laid pursuant to
  subsection (2) and, if it is complete and regular on its face, shall
  enter a conviction in the accused's absence and impose a fine in the
