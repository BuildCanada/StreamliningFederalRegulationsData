## Canadian Commercial Corporation Act {#canadian-commercial-corporation-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. C-14
:::

An Act to establish the Canadian Commercial Corporation
::::

::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61899 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canadian Commercial Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-14).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-6, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61904 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chairperson, the President
    and the other directors of the
    Corporation; ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[by-law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[by-law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a by-law made under section
    6; ([règlement administratif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Commercial
    Corporation established by section 3; ([Société]{.DefinedTermLink

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada as is designated by the Governor in Council
    as the Minister for the purposes of this
    Act. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[President]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

## [Establishment and Organization of the Corporation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61914 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation established

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby established
a corporation to be known as the Canadian Commercial Corporation
consisting of a Chairperson, a President and not more than nine or less
than five other directors.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-14, s. 3
- R.S., 1985, c. 1 (4th Supp.), s. 44(E)
- 2002, c. 4, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Chairperson and President

- **[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson and the President shall be appointed by the Governor in
  Council to hold office during pleasure for any term that the Governor
  in Council considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration of Chairperson and
  President

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The remuneration of the Chairperson and the
  President, if they are not members of the federal public
  administration, shall be fixed by the Governor in Council and paid by
  the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of directors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each director of the Corporation, other than the
  Chairperson and the President, shall be appointed by the Minister,
  with the approval of the Governor in Council, to hold office during
  pleasure for a term not exceeding four years that will ensure, as far
  as possible, the expiry in any one year of the terms of office of not
  more than one half of the directors.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2002, c. 4, s. 2
- 2003, c. 22, s. 224(E)
- 2006, c. 9, s. 238
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3.1-20050401.html "Link to previous version of section 3.1"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of Chairperson

- **[[3.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson shall preside at all meetings of the Board and shall
  perform any other duties that are assigned by the by-laws or by
  resolution of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence of Chairperson

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Chairperson is absent from a meeting, the
  directors that are present at the meeting shall choose a director to
  preside at it. For that purpose, the director has all the powers and
  duties of the Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of President

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The President is the chief executive officer of the
  Corporation and has on behalf of the Board the direction and
  management of the business of the Corporation. The President shall
  perform any other duties that are assigned by the by-laws or by
  resolution of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence or incapacity of President

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the President is absent or unable to act or the
  office of President is vacant, the Board may appoint a director or an
  officer of the Corporation to act as President and shall fix the
  remuneration and terms and conditions of the appointment. No person
  may be appointed for a term of more than 90 days without the approval
  of the Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2002, c. 4, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agent of Her Majesty

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation is for all
its purposes an agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-6, s. 3
- 1984, c. 31, s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office, branches

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The head
  office of the Corporation shall be in Ottawa and the Corporation may
  establish such branch offices, either inside or outside Canada, as it
  deems necessary to carry out this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Office accommodation, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may provide office accommodation,
  supplies and equipment for the Board and persons employed under this
  Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-6, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation may make
such by-laws as it may deem necessary to carry out this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-6, s. 11
- 1984, c. 31, s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation to act by resolution

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation shall act by resolution of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meetings of Board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall meet as required by by-law, or on
  reasonable notice given by the President or any two directors of the
  Corporation at such convenient place as may be specified in the
  notice.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2002, c. 4, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Three members of the Board constitute a quorum.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-14, s. 7
- 2002, c. 4, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employment of officers and employees

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation may employ such officers or employees as it deems
  necessary to carry out this Act and may determine their conditions of
  employment and their remuneration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation pays remuneration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The remuneration determined under subsection (1)
  shall be paid by the Corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control and supervision

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation has, under the Minister, the control
  and supervision of the officers and employees employed under this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-14, s. 8
- 2002, c. 4, s. 4(F)
:::

## [Purposes and Powers of the Corporation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61973 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purposes

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation is established for the following purposes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to assist in the development of trade between
    Canada and other nations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to assist persons in Canada

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to obtain goods and commodities from outside
      Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to dispose of goods and commodities that are
      available for export from Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to exercise or perform, on behalf and under the
    direction of the Minister, any powers or functions vested in the
    Minister by any other Act that authorizes the Minister to employ the
    Corporation to exercise or perform them; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to exercise or perform any other powers or
    functions conferred on it by any other Act or for the exercise or
    performance of which it may be employed under any other Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation shall comply with any general or
  special direction given by the Minister with reference to carrying out
  its purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 89(2) to (6) and section 153 of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) apply, with such
  modifications as the circumstances require, to a direction given under
  subsection (2) as though it were a directive referred to in those
  provisions.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-6, s. 4
- 1984, c. 31, s. 14
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation may do such things as it deems expedient for, or conducive
  to, the attainment of the purposes set out in section 9, and, for
  greater certainty, but not so as to restrict the generality of the
  foregoing, the Corporation may carry on the business of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} importing goods or commodities into Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} exporting goods or commodities from Canada, either
    as principal or as agent, in such manner and to such extent as it
    deems advisable to achieve those purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Generality not restricted

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The generality of subsection (1) is not restricted by
  any provision of this Act other than section 9.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-6, s. 5
:::

## [Financial Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-61998 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grants and advances to Corporation

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Finance shall, on the request of the Minister, from time
  to time deposit to the credit of the Corporation in the Bank of Canada
  or in a bank designated by the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an amount or amounts not exceeding in the aggregate
    ten million dollars to be paid out of unappropriated moneys in the
    Consolidated Revenue Fund at any time while this Act is in force;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other advances or grants to the Corporation
    that are otherwise authorized to be made by Parliament from the
    Consolidated Revenue Fund.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Borrowing power

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may borrow moneys from the
  Consolidated Revenue Fund or any other source for any purpose for
  which the Corporation was established, but the total amount
  outstanding on all of those loans at any time shall not exceed \$90
  million or any greater amount that may be specified in an
  appropriation Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loans to the Corporation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Finance may lend moneys from the
  Consolidated Revenue Fund to the Corporation on any terms and
  conditions that the Minister of Finance may determine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corporation to retain moneys

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to repayment of the loans made under
  subsection (2), the Corporation shall retain, for the purposes of this
  Act, all moneys received by it in the course of its business.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charges

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may charge any amount that it
  considers appropriate for providing services to a person, department
  or agency, including an amount to cover the risk of any loss that the
  Corporation may incur as a result of a default or failure by that
  person, department or agency in respect of a transaction entered into
  with any of them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administration of moneys

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} All moneys that are placed to its credit under this
  section and all moneys received by it in the course of its business
  shall be administered by the Corporation exclusively in furtherance of
  the purposes for which it is constituted.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-14, s. 11
- 2002, c. 4, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excess moneys

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding section 11, the
  Corporation shall, if the Minister so directs, pay to the Receiver
  General any part of the moneys administered by it that the Minister
  considers to be in excess of the amount required for the purposes of
  this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of excess moneys

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Finance shall, on the request of the
  Minister, from time to time deposit to the credit of the Corporation
  in the Bank of Canada or in a bank designated by the Minister, out of
  the Consolidated Revenue Fund, all or any part of the moneys paid to
  the Receiver General under subsection (1), if in the opinion of the
  Minister such moneys are again required for the purposes of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-6, s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Auditor

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Auditor General of
Canada is the auditor of the Corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. C-6, s. 8
- 1984, c. 31, s. 14
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-62027 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Former employee

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a person who was an employee in the public service immediately before
  that person's employment under this Act is retired from employment
  under this Act, that person may, in accordance with the [Public
  Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), be assigned to a position
  in the public service of the class from which that person was so
  retired or for which that person is qualified.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employee benefits continued

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person employed under this Act, who immediately
  prior to that employment held a position in the public service or was
  an employee within the meaning of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), continues to retain and is eligible for all
  the benefits, except salary as an employee in the public service, that
  that person would have been eligible to receive had that person
  remained an employee in the public service.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. C-14, s. 14
- 2003, c. 22, s. 134(E)
:::
