## Canadian Securities Regulation Regime Transition Office Act {#canadian-securities-regulation-regime-transition-office-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2009, c. 2, s. 297
:::

Assented to 2009-03-12

An Act to establish the Canadian Securities Regulation Regime Transition
Office

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 297 of chapter 2 of the Statutes of Canada, 2009,
in force July 13, 2009, see SI/2009-60.\]
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-74905 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canadian Securities Regulation Regime Transition Office
Act](/eng/acts/C-23.05).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-74908 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[Advisory Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Advisory Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the advisory
    committee of participating provinces and territories established
    under section 5. ([comité consultatif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Finance. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[participating province or territory]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[participating province or territory]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    any province or territory that informs the Minister of its
    willingness to participate in the establishment of a Canadian
    securities regulation regime and a Canadian regulatory
    authority. ([province ou territoire participant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Transition Office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Transition Office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian
    Securities Regulation Regime Transition Office established under
    section 3. ([Bureau de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Establishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-74915 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transition Office

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Canadian Securities Regulation Regime Transition Office is
  established.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Transition Office is not an agent of Her Majesty
  nor is it an entity governed by the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11), and its president, officers, employees, agents
  and mandataries, advisers and experts and the members of the Advisory
  Committee are not part of the federal public administration.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}President

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council shall, on the recommendation of the Minister,
  appoint a president, or two co-presidents acting jointly, of the
  Transition Office, to hold office during pleasure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The president is the chief executive officer of the
  Transition Office and has control and supervision over the work,
  officers and employees of that office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting president

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of the
  president or a vacancy in that office, the Minister may designate a
  person to act as president, which person may not act for a period
  exceeding 90 days without the Governor in Council's approval.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting co-president

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the event of the absence or incapacity of a
  co-president or a vacancy in that office, the Minister may designate a
  person to act as co-president, which person may not act for a period
  exceeding 90 days without the Governor in Council's approval. The
  other co-president may act alone until another co-president is
  designated or appointed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In this Act, other than in subsections (1) and (3),
  "president" includes two co-presidents who hold office, or a
  co-president who acts alone under subsection (4), as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advisory Committee

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  advisory committee of participating provinces and territories is
  established within the Transition Office and consists of not more than
  13 members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council shall appoint, on the
  recommendation of the Minister, a member for each participating
  province and territory, from persons nominated by that province or
  territory, to hold office during pleasure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Advisory Committee's role is to provide the
  president with advice on matters related to the Transition Office's
  purpose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Staff

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Transition Office may
employ any officers and employees and engage the services of any agents
and mandataries, advisers and experts that it considers necessary to
carry out its purpose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of employment --- president
and members

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister, shall
  determine the remuneration and allowances to be paid to the president
  and members of the Advisory Committee and any other conditions of
  their employment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of employment --- staff

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Transition Office shall determine the
  remuneration and allowances to be paid to its officers, employees,
  agents and mandataries, advisers and experts and any other conditions
  of their employment or engagement, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Transition Office shall pay the remuneration and
  allowances determined under subsections (1) and (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest --- president and
members

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  president and the members of the Advisory Committee shall not accept
  or hold any office or employment, or carry on any activity, that, in
  the Minister's opinion, is inconsistent with the performance of their
  duties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest --- officers and
  employees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The officers and employees of the Transition Office
  shall not accept or hold any office or employment, or carry on any
  activity, that, in the president's opinion, is inconsistent with the
  performance of their duties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No criminal or civil
proceedings lie against the president, a member of the Advisory
Committee or an officer or employee of the Transition Office for
anything done or omitted to be done by that person in good faith under
this Act.

## [Purpose and Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-74955 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of the
Transition Office is to assist in the establishment of a Canadian
securities regulation regime and a Canadian regulatory authority.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  carrying out its purpose, the Transition Office shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} develop a transition plan with respect to
    administrative and organizational matters, including those relating
    to human, financial, material and informational resources;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} consult with stakeholders, including Canadian
    capital market participants; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} undertake any other activity that the Minister may
    direct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Transition Office shall provide the Minister and
  each participating province and territory with a copy of the
  transition plan no later than one year after the day on which section
  3 comes into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Capacity and powers

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** In order to carry out its
purpose, the Transition Office has the capacity and the rights, powers
and privileges of a natural person and, in particular, it may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} initiate, finance and administer programs and
  activities;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} expend any money it receives for its activities,
  subject to any terms and conditions on which it is provided;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} enter into contracts; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} with the Minister's approval, enter into any
  agreement with the government of a province or territory that the
  president considers necessary or advisable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Transition Office
shall inform the Minister regularly of its activities and its progress
in carrying out its purpose.

## [Financial Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-74974 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum payment

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may make direct payments, in an aggregate amount not
  exceeding \$96,100,000, or any other amount that may be specified in
  an appropriation Act, to the Transition Office for its use.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of C.R.F.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any amount payable under this section may be paid out
  of the Consolidated Revenue Fund, on the requisition of the Minister,
  at the times and in the manner, and on any terms and conditions, that
  the Minister considers appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audit

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The accounts and
financial transactions of the Transition Office shall be audited
annually by the Auditor General of Canada, and a report of the audit
shall be made to the Transition Office and the Minister.

## [Annual Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-74984 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission to Minister

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  president shall, within four months after the end of each fiscal year,
  submit a report of all of the Transition Office's activities for that
  fiscal year, including its financial statements and the report
  referred to in section 15, to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause a copy of the report to be
  tabled in each House of Parliament on any of the first 15 days on
  which that House is sitting after the Minister receives the report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report available to public

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} After the report is tabled in Parliament, the
  Transition Office shall make it available to the public.

## [Dissolution]{.HTitleText1} {#h-74992 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of dissolution

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, by order, on the recommendation of the
  Minister, dissolve the Transition Office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The order shall be published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) before the date of dissolution
  referred to in the order.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2013, c. 33, s. 133\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assets transferred

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} On the Transition Office's dissolution, any of its
  property that remains after the payment of its debts and liabilities,
  or after the making of adequate provision for the payment of its debts
  and liabilities, shall be transferred to Her Majesty in right of
  Canada or to any body that the Governor in Council may specify.
