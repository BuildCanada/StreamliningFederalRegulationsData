## National Symbol of Canada Act {#national-symbol-of-canada-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. N-17
:::

An Act to provide for the recognition of the Beaver (Castor canadensis)
as a symbol of the sovereignty of Canada
::::

:::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [National Symbol of Canada Act](/eng/acts/N-17).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Beaver as a symbol of Canadian
sovereignty

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** It is hereby recognized and
declared that the Beaver (*Castor canadensis*) is a symbol of the
sovereignty of Canada and it is proclaimed that any representation of
the Beaver (*Castor canadensis*) when used by Her Majesty in right of
Canada shall be so used and so regarded.
