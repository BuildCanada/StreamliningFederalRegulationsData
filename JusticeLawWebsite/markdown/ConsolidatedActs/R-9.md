## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-428890 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Royal Canadian Mint Act](/eng/acts/R-9).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-428895 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[base metal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[base metal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any metal other than
    precious metal; ([métal commun]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[base metal coin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    Mint appointed under this Act; ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[circulation coin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[circulation coin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a coin composed of
    base metal that is listed in Part 2 of the schedule and that is put
    into circulation in Canada for use in day-to-day
    transactions; ([monnaie de circulation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Master]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Master]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Master of the
    Mint; ([président]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, 2005, c. 38, s.
    129\]]{.Repealed}

    established by this Act; ([Monnaie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-circulation coin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-circulation coin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a coin composed
    of base metal, precious metal or any combination of those metals
    that is not intended for circulation and that is listed in Part 1 of
    the schedule; ([monnaie hors circulation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

:   [[precious metal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means gold, silver, platinum
    or any of the platinum group of metals. ([métal
    précieux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    4, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

## [Incorporation, Objects and Head Office]{.HTitleText1} {#h-428915 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Master of the Mint and such other persons as constitute the Board of
  Directors of the Mint are hereby incorporated as a body corporate
  under the name of the Royal Canadian Mint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The objects of the Mint are to mint coins in
  anticipation of profit and to carry out other related activities.

- [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2016, c. 12, s. 117\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head office

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The head office of the Mint shall be in the National
  Capital Region as described in the schedule to the [National Capital
  Act](/eng/acts/N-4).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-9, s. 3
- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (3rd Supp.), s. 2
- 2014, c. 39, s. 185
- 2016, c. 12, s. 117
:::

## [Capital]{.HTitleText1} {#h-428926 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized capital

- **[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  authorized capital of the Mint is forty million dollars, divided into
  four thousand shares of ten thousand dollars each.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purchase of shares

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, by order, approve the
  purchase by the Minister of shares of the Mint and the payment
  therefor out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shares

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The shares of the Mint are not transferable and when
  issued to the Minister in accordance with this Act shall be registered
  in the books of the Mint in the name of the Minister and held by the
  Minister in trust for Her Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No shares of the Mint may be issued otherwise than as
  expressly authorized by this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (3rd Supp.), s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Redemption of shares

- **[[3.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Mint shall, at the request of the Minister after consultation with the
  Board, redeem such number of shares issued to the Minister in
  accordance with this Act as the Minister may direct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Redemption price

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The price to be paid for each share redeemed by the
  Mint pursuant to subsection (1) is the issue price of the share.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (3rd Supp.), s. 2
:::

## [Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-428945 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  carrying out its objects, the Mint has the rights, powers and
  privileges and the capacity of a natural person and may if necessary

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} procure the incorporation, dissolution or
    amalgamation of subsidiaries and acquire or dispose of any shares in
    them;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} acquire and dispose of any interest in any entity
    by any means;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} produce and arrange for the production and supply
    of coins of the currency of Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} produce and arrange for the production of coins of
    the currency of countries other than Canada;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} make medals, plaques, tokens and other objects;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} melt, assay and refine gold, silver and other
    metals;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} lease or acquire gold, silver and other metals;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} lend, lease or dispose of gold, silver and other
    metals;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} issue, promote, deal in or trade in financial
    services and products relating to gold, silver and other metals,
    subject to the approval of the Minister and in a manner consistent
    with the Mint's last corporate plan as approved under section 122 of
    the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} securely store and securely ship coins, gold,
    silver and other metals or arrange for their secure storage and
    shipment;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} market minting equipment developed by, or on behalf
    of, the Mint;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} acquire or provide consulting services relating to
    any activity referred to in paragraphs (c) to (k);

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} obtain, acquire, license, sell, or otherwise make
    available or dispose of any patent, copyright, industrial design,
    trademark or other similar property right, or any licence to any of
    them, relating to any activity referred to in paragraphs (c) to (k);

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} engage in marketing, promotion and research and in
    development activities relating to any activity referred to in
    paragraphs (c) to (k);

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} do any of the activities referred to in
    paragraphs (d) to (f) and (j) on behalf of any person or entity; and

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} subject to the approval of the Minister and in a
    manner consistent with the Mint's last corporate plan as approved
    under section 122 of the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11), engage in any other activity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the powers under section 21 of the
  [Interpretation Act](/eng/acts/I-21), the Mint may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} acquire, hold and alienate real property or any
    interest therein; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make grants in lieu of taxes to any municipality in
    Canada in amounts not exceeding the taxes that might be levied by
    that municipality in respect of real property under the
    administration and control of the Mint if the Mint were not an agent
    of Her Majesty.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-9, s. 4
- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (3rd Supp.), s. 3
- 1999, c. 4, s. 2
- 2016, c. 12, s. 118
:::

## [Coins of Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-429013 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coins to be delivered to Minister of
Finance

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  coins of the currency of Canada that are produced at or supplied by
  the Mint shall be delivered to the Minister of Finance or such person
  as the Minister of Finance may designate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage, preparation and movement of
  coins

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Mint shall comply with such instructions as the
  Minister of Finance may give respecting the storage of coins of the
  currency of Canada or the preparation and movement of shipments of
  such coins to or from the Mint.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Payments for the production, storage, preparation or
  movement of coins of the currency of Canada shall be made out of the

  Finance.

## [Master and Board of Directors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-429025 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board of Directors

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Board of Directors of
the Mint shall consist of not less than nine and not more than eleven
directors, including the Chairperson and the Master.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-9, s. 8
- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (3rd Supp.), s. 7
- 1999, c. 4, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chairperson

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Chairperson shall be
appointed by the Governor in Council to hold office during pleasure for
any term that the Governor in Council considers appropriate.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-9, s. 9
- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (3rd Supp.), s. 7
- 1999, c. 4, s. 6(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of Master of the Mint

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Master shall be appointed by the Governor in Council to hold office
  during pleasure for such term as the Governor in Council considers
  appropriate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, R.S., 1985, c. 35 (3rd Supp.), s.
  8\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Master

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Master is the chief executive officer of the Mint
  and shall devote the whole of his time to the business of the Mint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting Master

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board may authorize one of its directors or an
  officer of the Mint to act as Master in the event that the Master is
  absent or incapacitated or if the office of Master is vacant but no
  person so authorized shall act as Master for a period exceeding sixty
  days without the approval of the Governor in Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-9, s. 10
- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (3rd Supp.), s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of directors

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Each director, other than
the Chairperson and the Master of the Mint, shall be appointed by the
Minister, with the approval of the Governor in Council, to hold office
during pleasure for a term not exceeding four years that will ensure, as
far as possible, the expiration in any one year of the terms of office
of not more than one half of the directors.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-9, s. 11
- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (3rd Supp.), s. 9
- 2006, c. 9, s. 298

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Outside interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person is not eligible to be appointed or to
  continue as a director of the Mint if the person is not a Canadian
  citizen ordinarily resident in Canada or if, directly or indirectly,
  the person is engaged in any undertaking involving or associated with

    operated devices.

  interest.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Where the office of a

thereto, the Governor in Council may appoint a director for the
remainder of that term.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. R-8, s. 13

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Salary and benefits

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  directors shall be paid such salary as is fixed by the Governor in
  Council and shall receive such benefits as are fixed by the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expenses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each director is entitled to be paid by the Mint such
  travel and other expenses incurred by the director while absent from
  his ordinary place of residence in the course of his duties under this
  Act as may be fixed by by-law of the Mint.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-9, s. 16

## [Staff of the Mint]{.HTitleText1} {#h-429078 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officers and employees

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Mint may appoint such officers, agents and employees as are necessary
  for the proper conduct of the work of the Mint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remuneration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The remuneration of officers, agents and employees of
  the Mint shall be a charge against the revenues of the Mint.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. R-8, s. 15
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Master, officers and employees not part
of federal public administration

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Master, officers and employees of the Mint are not part of the federal
  public administration but shall be deemed to be employed in the
  federal public administration for the purposes of the [Government
  Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) and any regulations made
  pursuant to section 9 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Master and employees deemed employed in
  public service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Master, officers and employees of the Mint shall
  be deemed to be employed in the public service for the purposes of the
  [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), and the Mint
  shall be deemed to be a Public Service corporation for the purposes of
  that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contracting powers not limited by
  collective agreements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No collective agreement entered into by the Mint with
  its employees pursuant to Part I of the [Canada Labour
  Code](/eng/acts/L-2) shall prohibit or limit the power of the Mint to
  enter into contracts with any person to provide for the procurement by
  the Mint of any goods or services from that person or the minting of
  coins by that person.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-9, s. 18
- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (3rd Supp.), s. 13(F)
- 2003, c. 22, ss. 224(E), 225(E)
:::

## [By-laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-429095 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By-laws

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Board may make
by-laws respecting

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the duties and conduct of officers, agents and
  employees of the Mint;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the conditions of employment and the fixing of the
  remuneration of officers, agents and employees of the Mint;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the time and place for the holding of meetings of the
  Board, the quorum at those meetings and the procedure in all business
  at those meetings; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} generally the conduct and management of the affairs
  of the Mint.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. R-8, s. 14
- 1984, c. 31, s. 14
:::

## [Financial]{.HTitleText1} {#h-429104 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Borrowing power

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Mint may, for the attainment of its objects, borrow money from the
  Consolidated Revenue Fund or any other source, but the total amount
  outstanding at any time may not exceed 75 million dollars or such
  greater amount as may be specified in an appropriation Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Government loans

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Finance may lend money to the Mint
  from the Consolidated Revenue Fund.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of borrowing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Mint shall not enter into any particular
  transaction to borrow money pursuant to subsection (1) without the
  approval of the Minister of Finance with respect to the time and the
  terms and conditions of the transaction.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. R-9, s. 20
- R.S., 1985, c. 35 (3rd Supp.), s. 14
- 1999, c. 4, s. 7
:::

**[[21 to 25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21_to_25 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
R.S., 1985, c. 35 (3rd Supp.), s. 14\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Auditor

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Auditor General of Canada is the auditor of the Mint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspection of stores and metals

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Auditor General of Canada shall inspect the
  inventories of stores and metals of the Mint at least once in each
  year.
