## Returned Soldiers' Insurance Act {#returned-soldiers-insurance-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1920, c. 54
:::

Assented to 1920-07-01

An Act to provide for the Insurance of Returned Soldiers by the Dominion
of Canada
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
The Returned Soldiers' Insurance Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act and in any
regulation, unless the context otherwise requires,

:   - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[brother]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a
      half-brother and [[sister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a
      half-sister; ([frère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} et
      [soeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

:   - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a legally adopted child;

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a stepchild who is designated by the insured
        as a beneficiary and in such designation is described either by
        name or as a stepchild; and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a child acknowledged or maintained by the
        insured or for whom the insured has been judicially ordered to
        provide support; ([enfant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

:   - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in
      relation to an individual, means a person who is cohabiting with
      the individual in a conjugal relationship, having so cohabited for
      a period of at least one year; ([conjoint de
      fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

:   - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[grandchild]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a child
      as above defined of a child as above
      defined; ([petit-enfant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

:   - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      Minister of Veterans Affairs or such other Minister as the
      Governor in Council may from time to time
      determine; ([Ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

:   - [(e)]{.lawlabel} [[parent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a father,
      mother, grandfather, grandmother, stepfather, stepmother,
      foster-father, foster-mother, of either the insured or the spouse
      or common-law partner of the insured; ([parent]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

:   - [(f)]{.lawlabel} [[regulation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      regulation made under the provisions of this
      Act; ([règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

:   - [(g)]{.lawlabel} [[returned soldier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      any person, male or female, who served as an officer or warrant
      officer or who enlisted or was enrolled or was drafted for service
      in the naval, military or air forces of Canada in the Great War,
      or having been domiciled and resident in Canada on the 4th day of
      August, 1914, has served in any of His Majesty's naval, military
      or air forces in the said war; or, having been domiciled and
      resident as aforesaid, has served in the naval, military or air
      forces of one of His Majesty's Allies or Associated Powers in the
      Great War; and who has been retired or obtained honourable
      discharge therefrom; ([soldat de retour]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

:   - [(h)]{.lawlabel} [[widow]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the widow of
      a returned soldier who has died after retirement or honourable
      discharge from service and before the expiration of twelve months
      from the coming into force of this Act; ([veuve]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

:   - [(h.1)]{.lawlabel} [[widower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      widower of a returned soldier who has died after retirement or
      honourable discharge from service and before the expiration of
      twelve months from the coming into force of this
      Act; ([veuf]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

:   - [(i)]{.lawlabel} [[the insured]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
      person with whom the Minister enters into a contract under this
      Act. ([l'assuré]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insurance and limits thereof

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may enter into an insurance contract with any returned
  soldier or with any widow or widower, providing for the payment of
  five hundred dollars or any multiple thereof, not, however, exceeding
  five thousand dollars in the event of the death of the insured.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How payable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Payment under an insurance contract shall be made on
  the death of the insured in an amount that, at the option of the
  insured, is the full face value of the policy or any lesser amount,
  and the remainder, if any, or the portion thereof to which any
  beneficiary is entitled, shall, at the option of the insured, be
  payable as

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an annuity certain for five, ten, fifteen or twenty
    years;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a life annuity; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an annuity guaranteed for five, ten, fifteen or
    twenty years and payable thereafter as long as the beneficiary may
    live.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of annuity in a lump sum or
  otherwise

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything in this Act, where, at the
  death of the insured, any insurance money is being paid or is to be
  paid as an annuity to any beneficiary, such money shall, upon the
  request of the beneficiary, be paid in a lump sum or in any other
  manner provided for in subsection (2) as the beneficiary may request.

- [(4) and (5)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1951, c. 59, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Who are the beneficiaries

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the insured has a spouse, common-law partner or children, the
  beneficiary shall, subject to subsections (4) and (5), be the spouse,
  common-law partner or children of the insured, or some one or more of
  such persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Who are the beneficiaries

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the insured has no spouse, common-law partner
  or children, the beneficiary shall, subject to subsections (4) and (5)
  and section 5, be the future spouse, future common-law partner or
  future children of the insured, or some one or more of such persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Apportionment of insurance money

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the insured designates more than one
  beneficiary, the insured may apportion, and may at any time
  reapportion, the insurance money between or among them as he sees fit,
  and, in default of any such apportionment, the insurance money shall
  be paid in equal shares to the designated beneficiaries surviving the
  insured.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New designation where death of
  beneficiary

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a designated beneficiary dies in the lifetime
  of the insured, the insured may, subject to subsections (1) and (2),
  designate a beneficiary or beneficiaries to whom the share formerly
  apportioned to the deceased beneficiary shall be paid, and, in default
  of any such designation, the said share shall be divided equally among
  the surviving designated beneficiaries, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where no designated beneficiary

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the insured does not designate a beneficiary,
  or where all of the beneficiaries designated by him die within his
  lifetime, the insurance money shall be paid to the spouse, the
  common-law partner and the children of the insured in equal shares,
  and if the insured survives the spouse, the common-law partner and all
  the children of the insured and there is no contingent beneficiary
  within the meaning of section 5 surviving the insured, the insurance
  money shall be paid, as it falls due or otherwise as the Minister may
  determine, to the estate or succession of the insured.

- [(6) and (7)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2000, c. 12, s.
  279\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- 1920, c. 54, s. 4

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of contingent beneficiaries

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  insured may designate as a contingent beneficiary a grandchild,
  parent, brother, sister, uncle, aunt, nephew, niece or first cousin of
  the insured or such other person as may by regulation be prescribed
  for the purposes of this section, or some one or more of such persons,
  to whom the insurance money or any portion thereof shall be paid in
  the event that the insured at the time of his death has no spouse,
  common-law partner or children.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment to contingent beneficiaries or
  to estate or succession

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the insured survives the spouse, the common-law
  partner and all the children of the insured, the insurance money shall
  be paid to the contingent beneficiary or beneficiaries, if any, but in
  default of the designation of a contingent beneficiary, or in the
  event of the death of all the contingent beneficiaries within the
  lifetime of the insured, the insurance money shall be paid, as it
  falls due or otherwise as the Minister may determine, to the estate or
  succession of the insured.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Apportionment among contingent
  beneficiaries

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the insured designates more than one contingent
  beneficiary, the insured may apportion, and may at any time
  reapportion, the insurance money between or among them as he sees fit,
  and, in default of any such apportionment, the insurance money shall
  be paid in equal shares to the contingent beneficiaries surviving the
  insured.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Death of contingent beneficiaries

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a contingent beneficiary dies in the lifetime
  of the insured, the insured may, subject to subsection (1), designate
  a contingent beneficiary or beneficiaries to whom the share formerly
  apportioned to the deceased contingent beneficiary shall be paid, and,
  in default of any such designation, the said share shall be divided
  equally among the contingent beneficiaries, if any, surviving the
  insured.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1920, c. 54, s. 5
- 1951, c. 59, s. 3
- 1958, c. 41, s. 2
- 2000, c. 12, ss. 281, 283, 284(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of beneficiaries, etc.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the provisions
of this Act, the insured may at any time change the beneficiary or
beneficiaries, or the contingent beneficiary or beneficiaries, or vary
the option as to the mode of payment or the apportionment of the
insurance money, by so stating in a document that is satisfactory to the
Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1920, c. 54, s. 6
- 1921, c. 52, s. 3
- 1951, c. 59, s. 3
- 1958, c. 41, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Variation of option by beneficiary

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Any option, chosen by the
insured, as to the mode of payment of the insurance money to a
beneficiary or contingent beneficiary, may, after the death of the
insured, be varied by such beneficiary or contingent beneficiary with
the consent of the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1920, c. 54, s. 7
- 1951, c. 59, s. 3
- 1958, c. 41, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment to estate or succession of
deceased beneficiary

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where a beneficiary or
contingent beneficiary survives the insured but dies before receiving
all of the insurance money to which under the contract of insurance such
beneficiary or contingent beneficiary is entitled, the remaining unpaid
money shall be paid, as it falls due or otherwise as the Minister may
determine, to the estate or succession of the deceased beneficiary or
deceased contingent beneficiary.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1920, c. 54, s. 8
- 1951, c. 59, s. 3
- 1958, c. 41, s. 2
- 2000, c. 12, s. 284(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disability benefits and waiver of
premiums where total and permanent disability

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an
  insured becomes totally and permanently disabled and is thereby
  rendered incapable of pursuing any substantially gainful occupation,
  the premiums thereafter falling due under the contract shall be waived
  during the continuance of such disability and the insured shall be
  entitled to receive as a disability benefit the payment of the sum
  insured in instalments not exceeding one-twentieth of the sum insured
  for each year of total and permanent disability, the said benefit to
  continue during the continuance of such disability but not to exceed
  payment for twenty years in all.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where insured dies before full
  disability benefits paid

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the insured dies before the total payment of
  disability benefits under subsection (1) equals the sum insured, the
  balance of the sum insured shall be payable as a death benefit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disability benefit not payable if
  insured received pension under [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6), etc.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply where the total and
  permanent disability of an insured is due to a disability of the
  insured in respect of which he receives or is entitled to receive

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a pension under the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) or
    under the corresponding pension laws of the United Kingdom or of any
    of His Majesty's Dominions or of His Majesty's Government or of any
    of His Majesty's Allies or Associated Powers in the Great War; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} allowances while receiving treatment by the
    Department of Veterans Affairs on account of war disability.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where deemed to be totally and
  permanently disabled

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where his total disability has existed continuously
  for a period of at least one year, the insured shall, for the purposes
  of this section, be deemed to be totally and permanently disabled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Premiums payable after discontinuance
  of disability based upon reduced amount of insurance

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where, otherwise than by reason of the death of the
  insured, the insured ceases to be entitled to waiver of premiums under
  subsection (1), the premiums payable thereafter shall be based upon
  the reduced amount of insurance under the contract of insurance,
  namely, the sum insured less the aggregate of the disability benefits
  paid to the insured under subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1920, c. 54, s. 9
- 1921, c. 52, s. 4
- 1951, c. 59, s. 3
- 2000, c. 34, s. 93(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waiver of premiums after eighty-fifth
birthday

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding the
provisions of this Act or the terms of any contract of insurance made
thereunder, premiums falling due on or after the anniversary of his
contract nearest the eighty-fifth birthday of the insured shall be
waived.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1920, c. 54, s. 11
- 1951, c. 59, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Premiums

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  insurance contract may provide for the payment of a single premium or
  of premiums uniform throughout the lifetime of the insured, or during
  the lifetime of the insured for a period of ten, fifteen or twenty
  years, or until he attains the age of sixty-five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The premiums payable under the various plans of
  contract shall be those shown in the Schedule to this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1920, c. 54, s. 14
- 1951, c. 59, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insured or beneficiary may be a Member of
Parliament

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding the
Senate and House of Commons Act or any other law, no person, by reason
only of his entering into a contract of insurance or receiving a benefit
under this Act, is liable to any forfeiture or penalty imposed by the
Senate and House of Commons Act or disqualified as a member of the House
of Commons or incapable of being elected to, or of sitting or voting in
the House of Commons.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1920, c. 54, s. 15
- 1951, c. 59, s. 8
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insurance unassignable and not liable to
creditors

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The insurance money
payable under the contract shall be unassignable and shall not be
subject to the claims of creditors of the insured or of the beneficiary.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1920, c. 54, s. 16
- 1951, c. 59, s. 9
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When insured treated as if dead

- **[[14.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the death of the insured has not been proved in accordance with the
  regulations made under paragraph 15(b) but the Minister is satisfied,
  after efforts satisfactory to the Minister have been made, that the
  insured has died or cannot be located, the Minister shall, subject to
  this section, apply this Act as if the insured had died on a date
  specified by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of "Ministerial payment"

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (3), "Ministerial payment" means an
  amount of insurance money that is payable by virtue of the operation
  of subsection (1) and would not be payable in the absence of its
  operation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement to repay Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not make a Ministerial payment
  unless the person to whom that payment is to be made agrees in
  writing, in such form as is prescribed by the Minister, to repay to
  the Minister the amount of that payment in the event that the insured
  referred to in subsection (1) is subsequently determined by the
  Minister to be alive.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 34, s. 45
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When beneficiary or contingent
beneficiary treated as if dead

- **[[14.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, after the insured's death, the
  death of a beneficiary or contingent beneficiary has not been proved
  in accordance with the regulations made under paragraph 15(b) but the
  Minister is satisfied, after efforts satisfactory to the Minister have
  been made, that that beneficiary or contingent beneficiary died within
  the insured's lifetime or cannot be located, the Minister shall,
  subject to this section, apply this Act as if that beneficiary or
  contingent beneficiary had died within the insured's lifetime.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of "Ministerial payment"

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (3), "Ministerial payment" means an
  amount of insurance money that is payable by virtue of the operation
  of subsection (1) and would not be payable in the absence of its
  operation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement to repay Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not make a Ministerial payment
  unless the person to whom that payment is to be made agrees in
  writing, in such form as is prescribed by the Minister, to repay to
  the Minister the amount of that payment in the event that the
  beneficiary or contingent beneficiary referred to in subsection (1) is
  subsequently determined by the Minister to be alive.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2000, c. 34, s. 45
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may, for the purposes of this Act, make regulations,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing such forms as he may consider necessary
  under this Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the mode of proving the age, identity and
  existence or death of persons;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the mode of paying money in connection
  with insurance contracts;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} dispensing with the production of probate of a will
  or letters of administration, either generally or in any particular
  case or class of cases;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the accounts to be kept and their
  management;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} determining before hand the cases or classes of cases
  in which an insurance contract may be surrendered and a cash surrender
  value paid therefor, or a paid-up insurance contract issued instead
  thereof and for prescribing the manner in which such cash surrender
  value or amount of paid-up insurance shall be determined;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} determining the cases, not otherwise provided for in
  this Act, in which a person not originally named as, but who is
  eligible under this Act to be a beneficiary, may be made a
  beneficiary;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} determining the cases, not otherwise provided for in
  this Act, in which an apportionment of the insurance money may be made
  or varied;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the class or classes of persons other
  than those mentioned in sections 4 and 5 who are entitled to be
  beneficiaries;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} determining the cases in which a dependent, other
  than the spouse, common-law partner or child, of the insured may be
  named as a beneficiary under the contract;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} determining in cases not otherwise provided for by
  the contract or by declaration or by this Act, the person or persons
  entitled to the share and the apportionment thereof in the death
  benefit of any beneficiary dying before the payment of instalments of
  the said benefit has been completed; and,

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} any other purpose for which it is deemed expedient to
  make regulations in order to carry this Act into effect.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1920, c. 54, s. 17
- 1951, c. 59, s. 10
- 2000, c. 12, s. 282
:::

the provisions of this Act shall form part of the Consolidated Revenue
Fund, and the moneys payable under the said provisions shall be payable
out of the said Consolidated Revenue Fund.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1920, c. 54, s. 18

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual statement

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall cause a statement to be prepared within three months
  after the end of each fiscal year showing,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the premiums received during the fiscal year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the insurance moneys paid during the fiscal year;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number and amount of contracts in force at the
    end of the fiscal year; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} such further information as the Minister deems
    advisable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}To be laid before Parliament

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every such statement shall be laid before Parliament
  as soon as may be after it is prepared.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1920, c. 54, s. 19
- 1951, c. 59, s. 12
