## Heritage Railway Stations Protection Act {#heritage-railway-stations-protection-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. 52 (4th
Supp.)
:::

An Act to protect heritage railway stations

::: ReaderNote
\[1988, c. 62, assented to 22nd September, 1988\]
:::
:::::

:::::::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Heritage Railway Stations Protection Act](/eng/acts/H-3.5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[alter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[alter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to change in any manner and
      includes to restore, renovate or
      repair; ([modifier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Historic Sites and
      Monuments Board of Canada established by section 4 of the
      [Historic Sites and Monuments
      Act](/eng/acts/H-4); ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[heritage feature]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[heritage feature]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any feature of a
      heritage railway station designated by the Minister on the
      recommendation of the Board as a heritage
      feature; ([caractéristique patrimoniale]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[heritage railway station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[heritage railway station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any railway
      station designated by the Minister on the recommendation of the
      Board as a heritage railway station; ([gare ferroviaire
      patrimoniale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister responsible
      for the Parks Canada Agency; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by regulation
      of the Governor in Council. ([Version anglaise
      seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Part III of the [Canada Transportation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise provided, words and expressions used
  in this Act have the same meaning as in Part III of the [Canada
  Transportation Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act applies to railway
companies to which Part III of the [Canada Transportation
Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4) applies.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 52 (4th Supp.), s. 3
- 1995, c. 24, s. 18
- 1996, c. 10, s. 232
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of stations

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, on the
recommendation of the Board, designate

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} railway stations as heritage railway stations for the
  purposes of this Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} features of heritage railway stations as heritage
  features for the purposes of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 52 (4th Supp.), s. 4
- 1992, c. 1, s. 144(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  authorized by the Governor in Council, no railway company shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} remove, destroy or alter or sell, assign, transfer
    or otherwise dispose of a heritage railway station owned by it or
    otherwise under its control; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} alter any of the heritage features of a heritage
    railway station referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any railway company that contravenes subsection (1)
  is guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of not less than fifty
  thousand dollars and not more than one million dollars.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of the
  alteration of a heritage railway station or of any of the station's
  heritage features where the alteration is made in response to an
  emergency situation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of the sale,
  assignment, transfer or other disposition of a heritage railway
  station to a railway company.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for authorization

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A railway company that
plans

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to remove, destroy or alter or to sell, assign,
  transfer or otherwise dispose of a heritage railway station owned by
  it or otherwise under its control, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to alter any of the heritage features of a heritage
  railway station referred to in paragraph (a)

shall file an application for authorization to do so with the Minister
in accordance with the regulations after having given public notice in
the prescribed manner of its intention to file such an application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of objection

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who objects to an application for authorization filed pursuant to
  section 6 may, within sixty days after the application is filed, serve
  on the Minister a notice of objection in the form established by the
  Minister setting out the reason for the objection and any relevant
  facts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing and report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a notice of objection is served in accordance
  with subsection (1), the Minister may, within sixty days after the
  notice is served, refer the matter to the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of the Board

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Pursuant to a reference by the Minister under this
  section, the Board shall give all interested persons a reasonable
  opportunity to make representations to it and may hold a public
  meeting to determine whether the Minister should recommend that the
  Governor in Council grant the application for authorization filed
  pursuant to section 6.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representations to the Board

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notice of any public meeting held under this section
  shall be published at least ten days prior to the date of the public
  meeting in at least one newspaper of general circulation in the
  municipality in which the heritage railway station is situated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of a reference under this section,
  one member of the Board constitutes a quorum.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} After the conclusion of the Board's consideration of
  an application and in any event within six months after the
  application is filed, the Board shall submit to the Minister a report
  setting out its findings of fact and its recommendations as well as
  any information or knowledge that entered into the formulation of its
  recommendations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendations to the Governor in
  Council

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} After the receipt of the report referred to in
  subsection (6), the Minister may recommend that the Governor in
  Council grant the application for authorization.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. 52 (4th Supp.), s. 7
- 1992, c. 1, s. 144(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of the Minister and on
  such terms and conditions as the Governor in Council considers
  appropriate, authorize a railway company

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to remove, destroy or alter or to sell, assign,
    transfer or otherwise dispose of a heritage railway station owned by
    it or otherwise under its control; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to alter any of the heritage features of a heritage
    railway station referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The authorization referred to in subsection (1) is
  required in addition to and not in substitution for any other
  authorization or approval required by law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations for carrying out the purposes and provisions of this
Act and, without restricting the generality of the foregoing, may make
regulations prescribing anything that by virtue of this Act may be
prescribed by regulation.
