## Extractive Sector Transparency Measures Act {#extractive-sector-transparency-measures-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2014, c. 39, s. 376
:::

Assented to 2014-12-16

An Act to implement Canada's international commitments to participate in
the fight against corruption through the imposition of measures
applicable to the extractive sector

\[Enacted by section 376 of chapter 39 of the Statutes of Canada, 2014,
in force June 1, 2015, see SI/2015-43.\]

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-212470 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Extractive Sector Transparency Measures Act](/eng/acts/E-22.7).

## [Interpretation and General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-212473 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[category of payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[category of payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a category of
    payment set out in any one of paragraphs (a) to (h) of the
    definition [[payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}. ([catégorie de
    paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial development of oil, gas or minerals]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial development of oil, gas or
    minerals]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the exploration or extraction of oil, gas or
      minerals;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the acquisition or holding of a permit, licence,
      lease or any other authorization to carry out any of the
      activities referred to in paragraph (a); or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other prescribed activities in relation to
      oil, gas or minerals. ([exploitation commerciale de pétrole, de
      gaz ou de minéraux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation or a trust,
    partnership or other unincorporated organization

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is engaged in the commercial development of
      oil, gas or minerals in Canada or elsewhere; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that controls a corporation or a trust,
      partnership or other unincorporated organization that is engaged
      in the commercial development of oil, gas or minerals in Canada or
      elsewhere. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[gas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means natural gas and includes all
    substances, other than oil, that are produced in association with
    natural gas. ([gaz]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[minerals]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minerals]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all naturally occurring
    metallic and non-metallic minerals, including coal, salt, quarry and
    pit material, and all rare and precious minerals and
    metals. ([minéraux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada designated under section
    5. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means crude petroleum, bitumen and oil
    shale. ([pétrole]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[payee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any government in Canada or in a foreign state;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a body that is established by two or more
      governments;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any trust, board, commission, corporation or body
      or authority that is established to exercise or perform, or that
      exercises or performs, a power, duty or function of government for
      a government referred to in paragraph (a) or a body referred to in
      paragraph (b); or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other prescribed
      payee. ([bénéficiaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a payment --- whether monetary
    or in kind --- that is made to a payee in relation to the commercial
    development of oil, gas or minerals and that falls within any of the
    following categories of payment:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} taxes, other than consumption taxes and personal
      income taxes;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} royalties;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fees, including rental fees, entry fees and
      regulatory charges as well as fees or other consideration for
      licences, permits or concessions;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} production entitlements;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} bonuses, including signature, discovery and
      production bonuses;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} dividends other than dividends paid as ordinary
      shareholders;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} infrastructure improvement payments; or

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any other prescribed category of
      payment. ([paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules relating to payments

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a payment that is made to an employee or public
  office holder of a payee is deemed to have been made to the payee;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a payment that is due to a payee and that is received
  by a body that is not a payee for the payee is deemed to have been
  made to the payee;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a payment that is made by an entity, other than an
  entity referred to in subsection 8(1), that is controlled by another
  entity is deemed to have been made by the controlling entity;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a payment that is made for an entity is deemed to
  have been made by the entity; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the value of a payment in kind is the cost to the
  entity --- or, if the cost cannot be determined, the fair market value
  --- of the goods or services that it provided.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to the regulations, an entity is controlled by another entity if it is
  controlled by the other entity, directly or indirectly, in any manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed control

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An entity that controls another entity is deemed to
  control any entity that is controlled, or deemed to be controlled, by
  the other entity.

## [Purpose of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-212517 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to implement Canada's international commitments to participate in the
fight against corruption through the implementation of measures
applicable to the extractive sector, including measures that enhance
transparency and measures that impose reporting obligations with respect
to payments made by entities. Those measures are designed to deter and
detect corruption including any forms of corruption under any of
sections 119 to 121 and 341 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) and
sections 3 and 4 of the [Corruption of Foreign Public Officials
Act](/eng/acts/C-45.2).

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-212520 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on her Majesty

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-212523 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entities

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), sections 9 to 13 apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an entity that is listed on a stock exchange in
    Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity that has a place of business in Canada,
    does business in Canada or has assets in Canada and that, based on
    its consolidated financial statements, meets at least two of the
    following conditions for at least one of its two most recent
    financial years:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it has at least \$20 million in assets,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it has generated at least \$40 million in
      revenue,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} it employs an average of at least 250
      employees; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other prescribed entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of obligations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An entity that ceases to be an entity referred to in
  subsection (1) before the end of a period referred to in section 12 or
  13, as the case may be, continues to be subject to the obligations of
  that section until the end of the applicable period.

## [Obligations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-212535 .Part}

### [Reporting Payments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-212536 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  entity must, not later than 150 days after the end of each of its
  financial years, provide the Minister with a report that discloses, in
  accordance with this section, the payments that it has made during
  that year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments to be disclosed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any regulations made under paragraph
  23(1)(d), an entity must disclose any payments within a category of
  payment that are made to the same payee, if the total amount of all
  those payments during the financial year is at least

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount prescribed by regulation for the
    category of payment; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if no amount is prescribed for the
    category, \$100,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed payee

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (2), a payment that is
  made to a payee referred to in paragraph (c) of the definition
  [[payee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} is deemed to have been made to the
  government or body for which it is established to exercise or perform,
  or that for which it exercises or performs, a power, duty or function
  of government.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attestation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The report is to include an attestation made by a
  director or officer of the entity, or an independent auditor or
  accountant, that the information in the report is true, accurate and
  complete.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and manner

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may specify, in writing, the way in
  which payments are to be organized or broken down in the report ---
  including on a project basis --- and the form and manner in which a
  report is to be provided. The Minister is to make those requirements
  available to the public in the manner that he or she considers
  appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Substitution

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, in
  the Minister's opinion, and taking into account any additional
  conditions that he or she may impose, the payment reporting
  requirements of another jurisdiction achieve the purposes of the
  reporting requirements under this Act, the Minister may determine that
  the requirements of the other jurisdiction are an acceptable
  substitute for those set out in section 9. The determination is to be
  in writing and made available to the public in the manner that the
  Minister considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of substitute report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister has determined that the requirements
  of a jurisdiction are an acceptable substitute, an entity that is
  subject to those requirements is deemed to have provided a report in
  accordance with section 9 if the entity

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provides the report required by the jurisdiction to
    the jurisdiction's competent authority;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provides a copy of that report to the Minister, in
    the form and manner that he or she specifies, within any period
    specified in the jurisdiction's reporting requirements for providing
    the report to the competent authority; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} meets any conditions imposed by the Minister under
    subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Wholly owned subsidiary --- consolidated
report

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** If an entity and any
wholly owned subsidiary of the entity are entities referred to in
subsection 8(1), the subsidiary is deemed to have provided a report in
accordance with section 9 for a financial year if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the entity provides the Minister with a report with
  respect to its payments in accordance with section 9 that also
  contains information with respect to the payments made by the
  subsidiary during the subsidiary's financial year or any part of that
  year, which information must meet the reporting requirements of
  section 9;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} not later than 150 days after the end of its
  financial year, the subsidiary notifies the Minister in writing that
  the entity is providing the report; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the subsidiary provides the Minister with a report in
  accordance with section 9 with respect to payments that it has made
  during any part of its financial year that is not covered by the
  report provided by the entity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessibility of report

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  entity must, on providing the Minister with a report in accordance
  with section 9 --- or on being deemed to have done so by the operation
  of subsection 10(2) or section 11 --- make any information required by
  regulations made under paragraph 23(1)(f) available to the public or,
  if no such regulation is made, make the report and any information
  provided to the Minister under paragraph 10(2)(c) available to the
  public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner and period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report and information must be made available to
  the public

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the manner specified by the Minister; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the period prescribed by regulation or, if no
    period is prescribed, for a period of five years.

### [Record Keeping]{.HTitleText2} {#h-212570 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** An entity must keep
records of its payments made in a financial year for a prescribed period
or, if no period is prescribed, for a seven-year period that begins on
the day on which the entity provides the report in accordance with
section 9 for the financial year or is deemed to have done so by the
operation of subsection 10(2) or section 11.

## [Administration and Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-212573 .Part}

### [Information and Independent Audit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-212574 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order --- required information

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of verifying compliance with this Act, the Minister may,
  by order, require an entity to provide to him or her, within the
  period specified in the order, any information or documents, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a list of projects for the commercial development
    of oil, gas, or minerals in which the entity has an interest and the
    nature of that interest;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of how the entity has treated a
    payment for the purpose of preparing a report referred to in
    subsection 9(1) or meeting the requirements set out in paragraphs
    10(2)(a) to (c);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement of any policies that the entity has
    implemented for the purpose of meeting its obligations under this
    Act; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the results of an audit of its report or of the
    records of payments for the financial year to which the report
    relates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The audit is to be carried out in accordance with the
  generally accepted auditing standards specified in the order by an
  independ­ent auditor who meets the requirements specified in it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of [Statutory
  Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply to an order referred to in subsection (1).

### [Designated Person's Powers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-212589 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entry into a place

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  designated person may, for a purpose related to verifying compliance
  with this Act, enter any place in which the person has reasonable
  grounds to believe there is anything to which this Act applies or any
  document relating to the administration of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers on entry

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designated person may, for the purpose referred
  to in subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine anything in the place including any
    document;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} use any means of communication in the place or
    cause it to be used;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} use any computer system in the place, or cause it
    to be used, to examine data contained in or available to it, or
    reproduce the data, or cause it to be reproduced, in the form of a
    printout or other intelligible output and remove any printout or
    output for examination or copying;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prepare a document, or cause one to be prepared,
    based on the data;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} use any copying equipment in the place, or cause it
    to be used;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} take photographs or make recordings or sketches of
    anything in the place;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} direct any person to put any equipment in the place
    into operation or to cease operating it;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} prohibit or limit access to all or part of the
    place or to anything in the place; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} remove anything from the place for the purpose of
    examination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons accompanying designated person

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The designated person may be accompanied by any
  person that they believe is necessary to help them exercise their
  powers or perform their duties or functions under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The owner or person in charge of the place and every
  person in the place must give all assistance that is reasonably
  required to enable the designated person to exercise their powers or
  perform their duties or functions under this section and is to provide
  any documents or information, and access to any data, that is
  reasonably required for that purpose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warrant to enter dwelling-house

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  place referred to in subsection 16(1) is a dwelling-house, the
  designated person may enter it without the occupant's consent only
  under the authority of a warrant issued under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to issue warrant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application, a
  justice of the peace may issue a warrant authorizing the designated
  person to enter a dwelling-house, subject to any conditions specified
  in the warrant, if the justice is satisfied by information on oath
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dwelling-house is a place referred to in
    subsection 16(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entry to the dwelling-house is necessary for a
    purpose related to verifying compliance with this Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} entry was refused by the occupant or there are
    reasonable grounds to believe that entry will be refused by, or that
    consent to entry cannot be obtained from, the occupant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A person must not
obstruct or hinder a person who is exercising powers or performing
duties or functions under this Act.

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything that may, by this Act, be

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the way in which anything that may be

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Regulations made under this section may, in whole or
  in part, incorporate by reference, as it is amended from time to time
  or as it exists on a particular date,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a document produced by a person or body other than
    the Minister; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a technical or explanatory document produced by the

    graphs, test methods, procedures, operational stand­ards and
    performance standards.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessibility

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is to ensure that any document that is
  incorporated by reference in the regulations is accessible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not liable to be found guilty

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person is not liable to be found guilty of an
  offence for any contravention in respect of which a document that is
  incorporated by reference in the regulations is relevant unless, at
  the time of the alleged contravention, the document was accessible as
  required by subsection (3) or it was otherwise accessible to the
  person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration or publication not
  required

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a document that is
  incorporated by reference in a regulation is not required to be
  transmitted for registration or published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) under the [Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22) by reason only that it is incorporated by
  reference.

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-212654 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person or entity that fails to comply with section 9, 12 or 13, an
  order made under section 14, subsection 16(4) or an order made under
  section 19, or that contravenes section 18 or the regulations, is
  guilty of an offence punishable on summary conviction and liable to a
  fine of not more than \$250,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False or misleading statement or
  information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person or entity that knowingly makes any false
  or misleading statement or knowingly provides false or misleading
  information --- including with respect to the category of payment in
  respect of which a payment was made --- to the Minister or a person
  designated under section 15 is guilty of an offence punishable on
  summary conviction and liable to a fine of not more than \$250,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Avoidance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every entity is guilty of an offence punishable on
  summary conviction and liable to a fine of not more than \$250,000 if
  it structures any payments --- or any other financial obligations or
  gifts, whether monetary or in kind, that relate to its commercial
  development of oil, gas or minerals --- with the intention of avoiding
  the requirement to report those payments, obligations or gifts in
  accordance with this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuing offence

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If an offence under this section is committed or
  continued on more than one day, it constitutes a separate offence for
  each day on which the offence is committed or continued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of officers, directors, etc.

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** If a person or an entity
commits an offence under this Act, any officer, director or agent or
mandatary of the person or entity who directed, authorized, assented to,
acquiesced in or participated in its commission is a party to and guilty
of the offence and liable on conviction to the punishment provided for
the offence, whether or not the person or entity has been prosecuted or
convicted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence by employee or agent or mandatary

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** In a prosecution for an
offence under subsection 24(1),

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is sufficient proof of the offence to establish
  that it was committed by an employee or agent or mandatary of the
  accused, whether or not the employee or agent or mandatary is
  identified or has been prosecuted for the offence; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} no person or entity is to be found guilty of the
  offence if they establish that they exercised due diligence to prevent
  its commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limitation

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Proceedings under this
Act may be instituted within, but not after, five years after the time
when the subject matter of the proceedings arose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Admissibility of evidence

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  proceedings for an offence under this Act, a document that is
  purported to have been signed by the Minister or a designated person
  is admissible in evidence without proof of the signature or official
  character of the person appearing to have signed it and, in the
  absence of evidence to the contrary, is proof of the matters asserted
  in it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies and extracts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In proceedings for an offence under this Act, a copy
  of or an extract from any document that is made by the Minister or a
  designated person that appears to have been certified under the
  signature of the Minister or that person as a true copy or extract is
  admissible in evidence without proof of the signature or official
  character of the person appearing to have signed it and, in the
  absence of evidence to the contrary, has the same probative force as
  the original would have if it were proved in the ordinary way.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumed date of issue

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A document referred to in this section is, in the
  absence of evidence to the contrary, presumed to have been issued on
  the date that it bears.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No document referred to in this section is to be
  received in evidence unless the party intending to produce it has
  provided reasonable notice of that intention to the party against whom
  it is intended to be produced together with a copy of the document.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-212681 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aboriginal government in Canada

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The provisions of this
Act do not apply to any payment made by an entity to the following
payees during the two-year period that begins on the day on which
section 2 comes into force:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an Aboriginal government in Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a body established by two or more Aboriginal
  governments in Canada; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any trust, board, commission, corporation or body or
  authority that is established to exercise or perform, or that
  exercises or performs, a power, duty or function of government for a
  government referred to in paragraph (a) or a body referred to in
  paragraph (b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Past and current financial years

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** No entity is required to
comply with section 9, 12 or 13 with respect to the financial year in
progress on the day on which section 9 comes into force and for any
previous financial year.
