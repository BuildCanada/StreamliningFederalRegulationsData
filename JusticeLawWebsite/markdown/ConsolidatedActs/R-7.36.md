## Retail Payment Activities Act {#retail-payment-activities-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2021, c. 23, s. 177

An Act Respecting Retail Payment Activities

###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

Whereas the safe and efficient movement of funds is essential to the
health and strength of the national economy;

Whereas evolving technologies permit retail payment activities to be
performed in new and increasingly complex ways by a larger variety of
payment service providers across Canada;

Whereas Parliament considers that it is desirable and in the national
interest to address risks related to national security that could be
posed by payment service providers;

Whereas Parliament considers that it is desirable and in the national
interest to supervise and regulate retail payment activities performed
by payment service providers in order to mitigate operational risks and
to safeguard end-user funds;

And whereas Parliament considers that it is desirable and in the
national interest to supervise and regulate retail payment activities
performed by payment service providers in order to foster competition
and innovation in payment services by building confidence in the retail
payment sector;
:::

::: Enacts
Now therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1308939 .Part}

as the [Retail Payment Activities Act](/eng/acts/R-7.36).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1308942 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in this Act.

[[Bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Bank of
    Canada. ([Banque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Centre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Centre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Financial Transactions and
    Reports Analysis Centre of Canada. ([Centre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[electronic funds transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[electronic funds transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a placement,
    transfer or withdrawal of funds by electronic means that is
    initiated by or on behalf of an individual or entity. ([transfert
    électronique de fonds]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[end user]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[end user]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual or entity that
    uses a payment service as a payer or payee. ([utilisateur
    final]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation, trust,
    partnership, fund, an unincorporated association or organization,
    the government of a foreign country or of a political subdivision of
    a foreign country, or an agency of a foreign country or of a
    subdivision of a foreign country. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[government authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[government authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the Royal
    Canadian Mounted Police, the Communications Security Establishment
    and the Canadian Security Intelligence Service. ([autorité
    administrative]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Governor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Governor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
    2 of the [Bank of Canada
    Act](/eng/acts/B-2). ([gouverneur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an event or series of related
    events that is unplanned by a payment service provider and that
    results in or could reasonably be expected to result in the
    reduction, deterioration or breakdown of any retail payment activity
    that is performed by the payment service
    provider. ([incident]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Finance. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[operational risk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operational risk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a risk that any of
    the following will result in the reduction, deterioration or
    breakdown of retail payment activities that are performed by a
    payment service provider:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a deficiency in the payment service provider's
      information system or internal process;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a human error;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a management failure; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a disruption caused by an external
      event. ([risque opérationnel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[payment function]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payment function]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the provision or maintenance of an account that,
      in relation to an electronic funds transfer, is held on behalf of
      one or more end users;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holding of funds on behalf of an end user
      until they are withdrawn by the end user or transferred to another
      individual or entity;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the initiation of an electronic funds transfer at
      the request of an end user;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the authorization of an electronic funds transfer
      or the transmission, reception or facilitation of an instruction
      in relation to an electronic funds transfer; or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the provision of clearing or settlement
      services. ([fonction de paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[payment service provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payment service provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual
    or entity that performs payment functions as a service or business
    activity that is not incidental to another service or business
    activity.  ([fournisseur de services de paiement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by
    regulation. ([Version anglaise seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[registered]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registered]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means registered under section
    25. ([enregistré]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[retail payment activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[retail payment activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a payment
    function that is performed in relation to an electronic funds
    transfer that is made in the currency of Canada or another country
    or using a unit that meets prescribed criteria. ([activité associée
    aux paiements de détail]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[third-party service provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[third-party service provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    individual or entity that, under a contract, provides a payment
    service provider with a service related to a payment function and
    that is not an employee or agent or mandatary of the payment service
    provider. ([tiers fournisseur de services]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affiliation

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of section 8 and paragraph 29(1)(d),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one entity is affiliated with another entity if one
    of them is the subsidiary of the other or both are subsidiaries of
    the same entity or each of them is controlled by the same individual
    or entity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} two entities are deemed to be affiliated with each
    other if they are affiliated with the same entity at the same time;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an individual is affiliated with an entity if the
    individual controls the entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsidiary entity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), an entity is a
  subsidiary of another entity if it is controlled by that other entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a corporation is controlled by an individual or
    entity if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} securities of the corporation to which are
      attached more than 50% of the votes that may be cast to elect
      directors of the corporation are held, directly or indirectly,
      whether through one or more subsidiaries or otherwise, otherwise
      than by way of security only, by or for the benefit of that
      individual or entity, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the votes attached to those securities are
      sufficient, if exercised, to elect a majority of the directors of
      the corporation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a limited partnership is controlled by its general
    partner; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an entity other than a corporation or a limited
    partnership is controlled by an individual or entity if the
    individual or entity, directly or indirectly, whether through one or
    more subsidiaries or otherwise, holds an interest in the entity that
    is not a corporation that entitles them to receive more than 50% of
    the profits of that entity or more than 50% of its assets on
    dissolution.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1308985 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1308986 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment service providers in Canada

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 6
to 10, this Act applies in respect of any retail payment activity that
is performed by a payment service provider that has a place of business
in Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment service providers outside of
Canada

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 6
to 10, this Act also applies in respect of any retail payment activity
that is performed for an end user in Canada by a payment service
provider that does not have a place of business in Canada but directs
retail payment activities at individuals or entities that are in Canada.

### [Non-application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1308991 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retail payment activities

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Act does not apply
in respect of the following retail payment activities:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a payment function that is performed in relation to
  an electronic funds transfer that is made with an instrument that is
  issued by a merchant --- or by an issuer that is not a payment service
  provider and has an agreement with a group of merchants --- and that
  allows the holder of the instrument to purchase goods or services only
  from the issuing merchant or any merchant in the group;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a payment function that is performed in relation to
  an electronic funds transfer that is made for the purpose of giving
  effect to an [[eligible financial contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as
  defined in subsection 39.15(9) of the [Canada Deposit Insurance
  Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3) or for the purpose of giving effect to
  a prescribed transaction in relation to securities;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a payment function that is performed in relation to
  an electronic funds transfer that is made for the purpose of a cash
  withdrawal at an automatic teller machine; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a prescribed retail payment activity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated systems

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-7 .sectionLabel}** This Act does not apply
in respect of a payment function that is performed in relation to an
electronic funds transfer if the payment function is performed using a
system that is designated under section 4 of the [Payment Clearing and
Settlement Act](/eng/acts/P-4.4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Internal transactions

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-8 .sectionLabel}** This Act does not apply
in respect of a retail payment activity that is performed by a payment
service provider if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the payment function in question is performed in
  relation to an electronic funds transfer that is made between
  affiliated entities;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the payment service provider is one of the affiliated
  entities; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} no other payment service provider performs payment
  functions in relation to that electronic funds transfer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment service providers

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-9 .sectionLabel}** This Act does not apply
to a payment service provider that performs retail payment activities if
the payment service provider is one of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a bank;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an [[authorized foreign bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
  as defined in section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) in respect
  of its business in Canada;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a cooperative credit society, savings and credit
  union, caisse populaire or central cooperative credit society that is
  regulated by a provincial Act or an association regulated by the
  [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of a province or an agent or
  mandatary of Her Majesty in right of a province, if Her Majesty in
  right of a province or the agent or mandatary accepts deposits
  transferable by order;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a company to which the [Insurance Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies or an insurance company regulated by a
  provincial Act;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a company to which the [Trust and Loan Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) applies;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a trust company that is regulated by a provincial
  Act;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a loan company that accepts deposits transferable by
  order and is regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Payments Association;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the Bank; or

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} a prescribed individual or entity or an individual or
  entity of a prescribed class.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agents and mandataries

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-10 .sectionLabel}** This Act does not
apply to an agent or mandatary of a registered payment service provider
if the agent or mandatary is performing retail payment activities in the
scope of their authority as agent or mandatary and is included on the
list of agents or mandataries that was provided by the payment service
provider under paragraph 29(1)(e) and is updated in accordance with
subsection 59(1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Governor's orders

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  a provision of a federal or provincial Act or regulation applies to or
  in respect of a payment service provider that performs retail payment
  activities or a class of payment service providers that perform retail
  payment activities and the Governor is of the opinion that the
  provision is substantially similar to any of the following provisions
  of this Act or its regulations, then the Governor may, by order,
  specify the provision of this Act or its regulations and the payment
  service provider or class of payment service provider:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sections 17 to 22;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subsection 29(2);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} paragraphs 48(1)(a) to (e) and (g) and 52(a) to (d)
    and (g);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} sections 59, 94, 95 and 99; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a provision of the regulations that is made for the
    purposes of any of the provisions referred to in paragraphs (a) to
    (d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A provision that is specified in an order made under
  subsection (1) does not apply to or in respect of the payment service
  provider or class of payment service provider specified in the order.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Bank and Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1309030 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objects

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Bank's objects under this Act are to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} supervise payment service providers that perform
    retail payment activities in order to determine whether those
    payment service providers are in compliance with this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} promote the adoption by those payment service
    providers of policies and procedures that are designed to implement
    their obligations under this Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} monitor and evaluate trends and issues related to
    retail payment activities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of Bank

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In pursuing these objects, the Bank must consider the
  efficiency of payment services and the interests of end users.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements and arrangements

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Bank may, for the
purpose of exercising its powers or performing its duties and functions
under this Act, enter into an agreement or arrangement with any
government authority or regulatory body.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Guidelines --- Bank

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Bank may issue guidelines respecting the manner in which this Act,
  with the exception of the provisions referred to in subsection (2), is
  to be applied.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Guidelines --- Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may issue guidelines respecting the

  applied.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delegation of Governor's powers, duties
and functions

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Governor may delegate to an officer of the Bank any of the Governor's
  powers, duties or functions under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of delegation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a power, duty or function is delegated, the Bank
  must cause to be published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) a notice that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the title of the officer to whom the power, duty or
    function is delegated;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the power, duty or function that is delegated; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the delegation takes effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of revocation of delegation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Governor revokes a delegation, the Bank must
  cause to be published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) a notice that includes the day on
  which the delegation ceases to have effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The notice referred to in subsection (2) or (3) must
  be published before the day on which the delegation takes effect or
  ceases to have effect, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No liability if in good faith --- Bank

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  action lies against Her Majesty in right of Canada, any officer,
  employee or director of the Bank or any person acting under the
  direction of the Governor for anything done or omitted to be done in
  good faith in the administration or discharge of any powers or duties
  that under this Act are intended or authorized to be executed or
  performed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No liability if in good faith ---
  Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No action lies against Her Majesty in right of
  Canada, the Minister or any person or government authority acting
  under the direction of the Minister for anything done or omitted to be
  done in good faith in the administration or discharge of any powers or
  duties that under this Act are intended or authorized to be executed
  or performed.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Operational and Financial Measures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1309063 .Part}

### [Operational Risk Management and Incident Response]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1309064 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Framework

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of identifying and mitigating operational risks and
  responding to incidents, a payment service provider that performs
  retail payment activities must, in accordance with the regulations,
  establish, implement and maintain a risk management and incident
  response framework that meets prescribed requirements.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment by Bank

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Bank or a person designated by the Bank may
  assess the payment service provider's risk management and incident
  response framework or any portion of it and the Bank may provide the
  payment service provider with a list of corrective measures that the
  Bank considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to assist

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The payment service provider must give all assistance
  that is reasonably required to enable the Bank or the designated
  person to carry out an assessment referred to in subsection (2) and
  must provide any documents or information and access to any data that
  are specified by the Bank or the designated person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to notify

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  a payment service provider that performs retail payment activities
  becomes aware of an incident that has a material impact on any of the
  following individuals or entities, the payment service provider must,
  without delay, notify that individual or entity and the Bank of the
  incident:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an end user;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a payment service provider that performs retail
    payment activities, whether or not this Act applies to the payment
    service provider; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a [[clearing house]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} of a
    [[clearing and settlement system]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those
    expressions are defined in section 2 of the [Payment Clearing and
    Settlement Act](/eng/acts/P-4.4), that is designated under
    subsection 4(1) of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form, manner and content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must be given in the prescribed form and
  manner and contain the prescribed information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Follow-up notices

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Bank may, by order, direct a payment service provider that has given a
  notice in accordance with section 18 to provide any follow-up notice
  that the Bank considers relevant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The order must specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the individuals or entities to be notified;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the follow-up notice is to be given and its
    form and manner; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the information to be contained in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The payment service provider must comply with the
  order.

### [Safeguarding of Funds]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1309090 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accounts

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  a payment service provider performs a retail payment activity that is
  the holding of end-user funds until they are withdrawn by the end user
  or transferred to another individual or entity, the payment service
  provider must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} hold the end-user funds in trust in a trust account
    that is not used for any other purpose;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} hold the end-user funds in a prescribed account or
    in a prescribed manner and take any prescribed measures in relation
    to the funds, the account or the manner; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} hold the end-user funds in an account that is not
    used for any other purpose and hold insurance or a guarantee in
    respect of the funds that is in an amount equal to or greater than
    the amount held in the account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- provincial insurance or
  guarantee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a payment service
  provider in respect of end-user funds it holds in a province if the
  payment service provider accepts deposits that are insured or
  guaranteed under an Act of that province and those end-user funds are
  deposits that are guaranteed or insured under that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No set-off or compensation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No right of set-off or compensation may be asserted
  by an individual or entity that maintains an account that is referred
  to in paragraph (1)(a), (b) or (c) in respect of the funds held in
  that account.

### [Provision of Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1309101 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-21 .sectionLabel}** A payment service
provider that performs retail payment activities must, at the prescribed
time and in the prescribed form and manner, submit an annual report to
the Bank that includes

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed information respecting the payment
  service provider's risk management and incident response framework;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed information in relation to any account
  referred to in subsection 20(1) and the insurance or guarantee
  referred to in paragraph 20(1)(c);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other prescribed information in relation to the
  holding of end-user funds for the purposes of subsection 20(1) and any
  regulations made for the purposes of that subsection; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other prescribed information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice --- significant change or new
activity

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  payment service provider must notify the Bank before the payment
  service provider makes a significant change in the way it performs a
  retail payment activity or before it performs a new retail payment
  activity. The notice must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be given within the prescribed period;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be in the prescribed form and manner; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} include the prescribed information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Significant change

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a change is
  significant if it could reasonably be expected to have a material
  impact on operational risks or the manner in which end-user funds are
  safeguarded.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1309116 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1309117 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration required

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-23 .sectionLabel}** A payment service

### [Administrative Monetary Penalties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1309405 .Subheading}

#### [Notices of Violation and Compliance Agreements]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1309406 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commission of violation

- **[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-76
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every contravention that is
  designated under paragraph 101(1)(h) or (j) constitutes a violation
  and the individual or entity that commits the violation is liable to a
  penalty established in accordance with paragraph 101(1)(k) or (l).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment service provider

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Bank believes on reasonable grounds that a
  payment service provider has committed a violation, the Bank may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} issue and cause to be served on the payment service
    provider a notice of violation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} issue and cause to be served on the payment service
    provider a notice of violation with an offer to reduce by half the
    penalty set out in the notice if the payment service provider enters
    into a compliance agreement with the Bank in respect of the
    provision to which the violation relates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other individual or entity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Bank believes on reasonable grounds that an
  individual or entity other than a payment service provider has
  committed a violation, the Bank may issue and cause to be served on
  the individual or entity a notice of violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of penalty

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of the penalty is to promote compliance
  with this Act and not to punish.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

- **[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-77 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  notice of violation must name the individual or entity believed to
  have committed a violation, identify the violation and set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the penalty to be paid;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the right of the individual or entity, within 30
    days after the day on which the notice is served or within any
    longer period that the Bank specifies, to pay the penalty or to make
    representations to the Governor with respect to the violation and
    the penalty, and the manner for doing so; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fact that, if the individual or entity does not
    pay the penalty or make representations in accordance with the
    notice, the individual or entity will be deemed to have committed
    the violation and is liable to pay the penalty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short-form descriptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Bank may establish, in respect of each violation,
  a short-form description to be used in notices of violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrative corrections

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a notice of violation contains any error or
  omission, the Bank may issue and cause to be served a corrected notice
  of violation on the individual or entity at any time during the period
  referred to in paragraph (1)(b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of penalty

- **[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-78 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the individual or entity pays the penalty set out in the notice of
  violation, the individual or entity is deemed to have committed the
  violation and proceedings in respect of it are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representations to Governor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the individual or entity makes representations in
  accordance with the notice, the Governor must decide, on a balance of
  probabilities, whether the individual or entity committed the
  violation and, if so, may, subject to any regulations made under
  paragraph 101(1)(k) or (l), impose the penalty set out in the notice,
  a lesser penalty or no penalty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to pay or make representations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An individual or entity that neither pays the penalty
  set out in the notice nor makes representations in accordance with the
  notice is deemed to have committed the violation and is liable to pay
  the penalty set out in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision and right of appeal

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Bank must cause notice of any decision made under
  subsection (2) or the penalty that the individual or entity is liable
  to pay under subsection (3) to be issued and served on the individual
  or entity together with notice of the right of appeal under subsection
  84(1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of compliance agreement

- **[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-79 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the Bank offers to enter into a compliance agreement with a payment
  service provider under paragraph 76(2)(b), the agreement must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} identify the provision that was contravened and
    provide that the payment service provider will comply with that
    provision within the period and be subject to the terms and
    conditions specified in the agreement; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out the amount that the payment service
    provider will have to pay as the reduced penalty for the violation
    if the compliance agreement is entered into.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to enter into agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the payment service provider does not enter into
  the compliance agreement and does not pay the reduced penalty within
  30 days after the day on which the payment service provider received
  the notice of violation, the payment service provider is deemed to
  have refused to enter into the agreement and is liable to pay the full
  penalty set out in the notice of violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Bank may extend the period referred to in
  paragraph (1)(a) if it is satisfied that the payment service provider
  is unable to comply with it within that period for reasons beyond
  their control.

                            s-81 .sectionLabel}** If the Bank considers

- **[[83]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-83 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor may confirm the Bank's decision or

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to pay or apply for review

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Individuals and entities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The individuals and entities are any of the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an end user;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a payment service provider that performs retail
    payment activities, whether or not this Act applies to them; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a [[clearing house]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} of a
    [[clearing and settlement system]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those
    expressions are defined in section 2 of the [Payment Clearing and
    Settlement Act](/eng/acts/P-4.4), that is designated under
    subsection 4(1) of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opportunity for representations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), no order is to be made in
  respect of a payment service provider under subsection (1) unless the
  payment service provider is provided with a opportunity to make
  representations in respect of the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary order

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, in the Governor's opinion, the length of time
  required for representations to be made might be prejudicial to the
  public interest, the Governor may make a temporary order that has the
  same effect as an order under subsection (1). The order ceases to have
  effect 30 days after the day on which it is made or after the
  expiration of a shorter period that is specified in the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continued effect

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A temporary order continues to have effect after the
  expiration of the 30-day or the shorter period if no representations
  are made to the Governor within that period or, if representations are
  made, the Governor notifies the payment service provider that the
  Governor is not satisfied that there are sufficient grounds for
  revoking the order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court enforcement

- **[[95]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-95 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  an individual or entity is contravening or has contravened a provision
  of this Act, the regulations or an order made under subsection 94(1)
  or (4), the Governor may, in addition to any other action that may be
  taken under this Act, apply to a superior court for an order requiring
  the individual or entity to cease the contravention or to comply with
  the provision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of court

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The court may make the order and may make any other
  order the court thinks fit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An appeal from an order made under subsection (2)
  lies in the same manner and to the same court as an appeal from any
  other order of the court.

### [National Security]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1309545 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National security order

- **[[96]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-96 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by order, direct a payment service provider that
  performs retail payment activities to take or to refrain from taking
  any measures related to the performance of retail payment activities
  if the Minister is of the opinion that it is necessary to do so for
  reasons related to national security.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opportunity for representations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), no order is to be made
  under subsection (1) unless the payment service provider is provided
  with a opportunity to make representations in respect of the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, in the Minister's opinion, the length of time
  required for representations to be made might be prejudicial to the
  public interest, the Minister may make a temporary order that has the
  same effect as an order made under subsection (1). The temporary order
  ceases to have effect 30 days after the day on which it is made or
  after the expiration of a shorter period that is specified in the
  order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continued effect

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The temporary order continues to have effect after
  the expiration of the 30-day or the shorter period if no
  representations are made to the Minister within that period or, if
  representations are made, the Minister notifies the payment service
  provider that the Minister is not satisfied that there are sufficient
  grounds for revoking the order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy to Bank

**[[97]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-97 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must
provide the Bank with a copy of each order the Minister makes under
subsection 96(1) or (3) and the Bank must, as soon as feasible, provide
a copy to the individual or entity in question.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court enforcement

- **[[98]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-98 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  an individual or entity is not complying with an undertaking provided
  in accordance with section 42, a condition imposed under section 43 or
  an order made under section 96, the Minister may, in addition to any
  other action that may be taken under this Act, apply to a superior
  court for an order requiring the individual or entity to comply with
  the undertaking, condition or order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of court

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The court may make the order and may make any other
  order the court thinks fit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An appeal from an order made under subsection (2)
  lies in the same manner and to the same court as an appeal from any
  other order of the court.

## [PART 8]{.HLabel1}[Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1309617 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[transition
period]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[103]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-103 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,
[[transition period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period that begins
on the day on which section 29 comes into force and ends on the day
before the day on which subsection 25(1) comes into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application required

**[[104]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-104 .sectionLabel}** A payment service
provider that performs or plans to perform retail payment activities
during the transition period must, within the prescribed period that
begins on the day on which section 29 comes into force, apply to the
Bank for registration in accordance with that section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed periods

- **[[105]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-105
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a regulation
  that prescribes a period within which something is to be done or may
  be done under this Act in relation to an application for registration
  may distinguish between applications that are submitted during the
  transition period and applications that are submitted after the end of
  that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period suspended for Centre

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If subsection 53.6(1) of the [Proceeds of Crime
  (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501),
  as enacted by section 181 of the [Budget Implementation Act, 2021, No.
  1](/eng/acts/B-9.8594), comes into force before or during the
  transition period, the 30-day period referred to in that subsection
  is, in relation to an application for registration that is submitted
  during the transition period, suspended until the end of the
  transition period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition on disclosure of outcome of
application

**[[106]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-106 .sectionLabel}** During the transition
period, the Bank must not disclose the outcome or likely outcome of an
application to the applicant unless a directive in relation to the
applicant is issued to the Bank under section 40.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception to subsection 62(1)

**[[107]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-107 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsection
62(1), the Bank may publish the names of applicants that submit
applications during the transition period as well as any prescribed
information in relation to those applications.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of section 23

**[[108]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-108 .sectionLabel}** Starting on the day
on which section 23 comes into force, that section does not apply to a
payment service provider who submits an application during the
transition period until the earlier of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the Bank notifies the payment
  service provider under subsection 25(2) that it has been registered;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the Bank notifies the payment
  service provider under subsection 48(3) or section 49 of a refusal to
  register.
