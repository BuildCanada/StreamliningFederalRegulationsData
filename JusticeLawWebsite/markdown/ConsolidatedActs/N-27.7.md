## Nuclear Fuel Waste Act {#nuclear-fuel-waste-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2002, c. 23
:::

Assented to 2002-06-13

An Act respecting the long-term management of nuclear fuel waste
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-367855 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Nuclear Fuel Waste Act](/eng/acts/N-27.7).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-367858 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Act.

[[economic region]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[economic region]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an economic region
    described by Statistics Canada in its Guide to the Labour Force
    Survey, published on January 31, 2000. ([région
    économique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[management]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[management]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to nuclear fuel
    waste, means long-term management by means of storage or disposal,
    including handling, treatment, conditioning or transport for the
    purpose of storage or disposal. ([gestion]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Natural
    Resources or such member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada as
    the Governor in Council may designate as the Minister for the
    purposes of this Act. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nuclear energy corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear energy corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Ontario Power Generation Inc., Hydro-Québec, New
      Brunswick Power Corporation, and any other body that owns nuclear
      fuel waste resulting from the production of electricity by means
      of a commercial nuclear reactor;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any successor or assignee of a corporation
      mentioned in paragraph (a); and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any assignee of Atomic Energy of Canada Limited,
      being the company incorporated or acquired pursuant to subsection
      10(2) of the Atomic Energy Control Act, chapter A-19 of the
      Revised Statutes of Canada, 1970. ([sociétés d'énergie
      nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nuclear fuel waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuclear fuel waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means irradiated fuel
    bundles removed from a commercial or research nuclear fission
    reactor. ([déchets nucléaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prime rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prime rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, for any day, the rate of
    interest charged by banks to their most credit-worthy borrowers for
    short-term business loans, as determined and published by the Bank
    of Canada for the month in which the day falls. ([taux de
    base]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[waste management organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[waste management organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    corporation established under section 6, regardless of the actual
    name of that corporation. ([société de gestion]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Purpose of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-367871 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Comprehensive, integrated and
economically sound approach

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Act is
to provide a framework to enable the Governor in Council to make, from
the proposals of the waste management organization, a decision on the
management of nuclear fuel waste that is based on a comprehensive,
integrated and economically sound approach for Canada.

## [Application of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-367874 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to nuclear energy
corporations and AECL

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Act applies to a
nuclear energy corporation and to Atomic Energy of Canada Limited only
if it is the owner of nuclear fuel waste.

## [Waste Management Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-367879 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation and purpose

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  nuclear energy corporations shall establish a corporation, in this Act
  referred to as the waste management organization, whose purpose under
  this Act is to do the following on a non-profit basis:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} propose to the Government of Canada approaches for
    the management of nuclear fuel waste; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} implement the approach that is selected under
    section 15 or is approved under subsection 20(5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation in waste management
  organization

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Once the waste management organization has been
  established, every nuclear energy corporation shall become and remain
  a member or shareholder of it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not an agent of Her Majesty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For all purposes the waste management organization is
  not an agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty toward other owners of nuclear fuel
waste

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The waste management
organization shall offer, without discrimination and at a fee that is
reasonable in relation to its costs of managing the nuclear fuel waste
of its members or shareholders, to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Atomic Energy of Canada Limited, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all owners of nuclear fuel waste produced in Canada
  that are neither members nor shareholders of the waste management
  organization

its nuclear fuel waste management services that are set out in the
approach that the Governor in Council selects under section 15 or
approves under subsection 20(5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advisory Council

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  waste management organization shall create an Advisory Council, which
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine the study referred to in subsection 12(1)
    and the triennial reports referred to in section 18 that are to be
    submitted to the Minister; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give written comments on that study and those
    reports to the waste management organization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation on Advisory Council

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Advisory Council shall be
  appointed by the governing body of the waste management organization.
  The governing body shall make all reasonable efforts to ensure that
  the Advisory Council's membership

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} reflects a broad range of scientific and technical
    disciplines related to the management of nuclear fuel waste;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reflects expertise, in matters of nuclear energy,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in public affairs, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} as needed, in other social sciences;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} reflects expertise in traditional aboriginal
    knowledge; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} includes representatives nominated by local and
    regional governments and aboriginal organizations that are affected
    because their economic region is specified for the approach that the
    Governor in Council selects under section 15 or approves under
    subsection 20(5).

## [Financing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-367907 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Trust funds

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  nuclear energy corporation and Atomic Energy of Canada Limited shall
  maintain in Canada, either individually or jointly with one or more of
  the other nuclear energy corporations or Atomic Energy of Canada
  Limited, one trust fund with a financial institution incorporated or
  formed by or under an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a
  province, except, in the case of a nuclear energy corporation, a
  financial institution in relation to which the nuclear energy
  corporation beneficially owns, directly or indirectly, more than ten
  per cent of the outstanding shares of any given class of shares.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents relating to trust funds

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The financial institution that holds a trust fund
  referred to in this section shall maintain in Canada all documents
  relating to that trust fund.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial deposit to trust funds

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  body mentioned in this subsection shall, either directly or through a
  third party, no later than 10 days after the day on which this Act
  comes into force, deposit to its trust fund maintained under
  subsection 9(1) the following respective amounts:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Ontario Power Generation Inc., \$500,000,000;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Hydro-Québec, \$20,000,000;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} New Brunswick Power Corporation, \$20,000,000; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Atomic Energy of Canada Limited, \$10,000,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent deposits to trust funds

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each body mentioned in this subsection shall in each
  year, either directly or through a third party, no later than the
  anniversary of the day on which this Act comes into force, deposit to
  its trust fund maintained under subsection 9(1) the following
  respective amounts:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Ontario Power Generation Inc., \$100,000,000;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Hydro-Québec, \$4,000,000;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} New Brunswick Power Corporation, \$4,000,000; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Atomic Energy of Canada Limited, \$2,000,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When obligation ceases to apply

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) ceases to apply on the day on which
  the Minister approves the amount of the deposit under paragraph
  16(3)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of interest

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Interest accumulates on any portion of a deposit not
  paid by the day referred to in subsection (1) or (2), at the prime
  rate plus two per cent, calculated daily from the day referred to in
  subsection (1) or (2), as the case may be, to the day before the day
  of the deposit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Latest date for deposit

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Each body mentioned in subsection (1) or (2) shall,
  either directly or through a third party, deposit to its trust fund
  maintained under subsection 9(1), no later than 30 days after the date
  of the decision of the Governor in Council under section 15, the
  applicable amount referred to in subsection (1) or (2) plus an amount,
  if any, equal to the interest.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawals from trust funds

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Only
  the waste management organization may withdraw moneys from a trust
  fund maintained under subsection 9(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition for withdrawals

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The waste management organization may make
  withdrawals only for the purpose of implementing the approach that the
  Governor in Council selects under section 15 or approves under
  subsection 20(5), including avoiding or minimizing significant
  socio-economic effects on a community's way of life or on its social,
  cultural or economic aspirations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First withdrawal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The waste management organization may make the first
  withdrawal only for an activity in respect of which a construction or
  operating licence has, after the date of the decision of the Governor
  in Council under section 15, been issued under section 24 of the
  [Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ministerial approval

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is of the view that the waste
  management organization has withdrawn moneys from a trust fund
  contrary to subsection (2) or (3), the Minister may require the
  Minister's prior approval in respect of any future withdrawal from a
  trust fund by the waste management organization.

## [Study by Waste Management Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-367941 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Study within three years

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  three years after the coming into force of this Act, the waste
  management organization shall submit to the Minister a study setting
  out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its proposed approaches for the management of
    nuclear fuel waste, along with the comments of the Advisory Council
    on those approaches; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its recommendation as to which of its proposed
    approaches should be adopted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Methods to manage nuclear fuel waste

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each of the following methods must be the sole basis
  of at least one approach:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deep geological disposal in the Canadian Shield,
    based on the concept described by Atomic Energy of Canada Limited in
    the Environmental Impact Statement on the Concept for Disposal of
    Canada's Nuclear Fuel Waste and taking into account the views of the
    environmental assessment panel set out in the Report of the Nuclear
    Fuel Waste Management and Disposal Concept Environmental Assessment
    Panel dated February 1998;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} storage at nuclear reactor sites; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} centralized storage, either above or below ground.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Technical description, region

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The study must include a detailed technical
  description of each proposed approach and must specify an economic
  region for its implementation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Comparison of risks, etc.

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Each proposed approach must include a comparison of
  the benefits, risks and costs of that approach with those of the other
  approaches, taking into account the economic region in which that
  approach would be implemented, as well as ethical, social and economic
  considerations associated with that approach.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Services to certain owners of waste

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Each proposed approach must include a description of
  the nuclear fuel waste management services to be offered by the waste
  management organization under section 7.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation plan

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Each proposed approach must include an implementation
  plan setting out, as a minimum,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of activities;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a timetable for carrying out the approach;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the means that the waste management organization
    plans to use to avoid or minimize significant socio-economic effects
    on a community's way of life or on its social, cultural or economic
    aspirations; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a program for public consultation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The waste management organization shall consult the
  general public, and in particular aboriginal peoples, on each of the
  proposed approaches. The study must include a summary of the comments
  received by the waste management organization as a result of those
  consultations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial aspects

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  study must set out, with respect to each proposed approach, a formula
  to calculate the annual amount required to finance the management of
  nuclear fuel waste. The report must explain the assumptions behind
  each term of the formula. The formula must include the following
  terms:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the estimated total cost of management of nuclear
    fuel waste, which must take into account natural or other events
    that have a reasonable probability of occurring;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the estimated rate of return on the trust funds
    maintained under subsection 9(1);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the life expectancy of the nuclear reactors of each
    nuclear energy corporation and of Atomic Energy of Canada Limited;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the estimated amounts to be received from owners of
    nuclear fuel waste, other than nuclear energy corporations and
    Atomic Energy of Canada Limited, in return for services of
    management of nuclear fuel waste.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Respective percentages

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The study must set out, with respect to each proposed
  approach, the respective percentage of the estimated total cost of
  management of nuclear fuel waste that is to be paid by each nuclear
  energy corporation and Atomic Energy of Canada Limited, and an
  explanation of how those respective percentages were determined.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial guarantees

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The study must set out the form and amount of any
  financial guarantees for the management of nuclear fuel waste that
  have been provided by the nuclear energy corporations and Atomic
  Energy of Canada Limited under the [Nuclear Safety and Control
  Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may consult public

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may engage in such consultations with the general public on
  the approaches set out in the study as the Minister considers
  necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revision of study

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is of the opinion that the study
  fails in a significant way to meet the requirements of sections 12 and
  13, the Minister shall direct the waste management organization to
  revise the relevant portions of it and submit the revised study to the
  Minister within the period that the Minister specifies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision of Governor in Council

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council,
on the recommendation of the Minister, shall select one of the
approaches for the management of nuclear fuel waste from among those set
out in the study, and the decision of the Governor in Council shall be
published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

## [Reports by Waste Management Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-367984 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to submit annual reports

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  waste management organization shall, within three months after the end
  of each fiscal year of the organization, submit to the Minister a
  report of its activities for that fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of annual report after section
  15 decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each annual report after the date of the decision of
  the Governor in Council under section 15 must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the form and amount of any financial guarantees
    that have been provided during that fiscal year by the nuclear
    energy corporations and Atomic Energy of Canada Limited under the
    [Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3) and relate to
    implementing the approach that the Governor in Council selects under
    section 15 or approves under subsection 20(5);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the updated estimated total cost of the management
    of nuclear fuel waste;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the budget forecast for the next fiscal year;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed formula for the next fiscal year to
    calculate the amount required to finance the management of nuclear
    fuel waste and an explanation of the assumptions behind each term of
    the formula; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the deposit required to be paid
    during the next fiscal year by each of the nuclear energy
    corporations and Atomic Energy of Canada Limited, and the rationale
    by which those respective amounts were arrived at.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's approval of formula and
  deposits

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The formula referred to in paragraph (2)(d) and the
  amount of each deposit referred to in paragraph (2)(e) are subject to
  the approval of the Minister when proposed in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the first annual report after the date of a
    decision of the Governor in Council under section 15 or subsection
    20(5); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the first annual report after the issuance, under
    section 24 of the [Nuclear Safety and Control
    Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3), of a construction or operating licence for
    an activity to implement the approach that the Governor in Council
    selects under section 15 or approves under subsection 20(5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grounds for refusing to approve

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not satisfied that the formula referred to in
    paragraph (2)(d) will provide sufficient funds to implement the
    approach that the Governor in Council selects under section 15 or
    approves under subsection 20(5), or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not satisfied that the amount of each deposit
    referred to in paragraph (2)(e) is consistent with the formula
    referred to in paragraph (2)(d),

  the Minister shall refuse to give the approval referred to in
  subsection (3) and shall direct the waste management organization to
  revise the relevant portions of the annual report and submit the
  revised annual report to the Minister within 30 days.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to deposit

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  nuclear energy corporation and Atomic Energy of Canada Limited shall,
  either directly or through a third party, deposit to its trust fund
  maintained under subsection 9(1) its respective deposit specified in
  the annual report

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the Minister's approval under subsection 16(3)
    is not required, within 30 days after the annual report is submitted
    to the Minister under subsection 16(1); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Minister's approval under subsection 16(3)
    is required, within 30 days after the date of that approval.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of time

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), the Governor in
  Council may, on request by a nuclear energy corporation made before
  the expiration of the 30 day period referred to in that subsection,
  authorize the nuclear energy corporation to defer by one year all or
  part of its deposit required by that subsection, if the Governor in
  Council is of the opinion that the public interest requires that that
  money be used instead to repair the damage caused by an event that is
  not attributable to the corporation and is extraordinary, unforeseen
  and irresistible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Triennial report

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The annual report of the
waste management organization for its third fiscal year after the fiscal
year in which a decision is made by the Governor in Council under
section 15, and for every third fiscal year after that, in this Act
called the "triennial report", must include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a summary of its activities respecting the management
  of nuclear fuel waste during the last three fiscal years, including an
  analysis of any significant socio-economic effects of those activities
  on a community's way of life or on its social, cultural or economic
  aspirations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} its strategic plan for the next five fiscal years to
  implement the approach that the Governor in Council selects under
  section 15 or approves under subsection 20(5);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} its budget forecast for the next five fiscal years to
  implement the strategic plan;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the results of its public consultations held during
  the last three fiscal years with respect to the matters set out in
  paragraphs (a) and (b); and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the comments of the Advisory Council on the matters
  referred to in paragraphs (a) to (d).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's statement

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall,
within 90 days after receiving a report, issue a public statement
regarding the report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of reports in Parliament

**[[19.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19.1 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall
cause a copy of each report to be laid before each House of Parliament
within the first fifteen sitting days of that House after the Minister
has received the report.

## [Change in Approach]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368022 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New approach --- technical difficulty

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  waste management organization is unable, for technical reasons beyond
  its control, to implement the approach that was selected by the
  Governor in Council under section 15, the waste management
  organization shall so report in its triennial report and shall, in
  that report, propose a new approach.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New approach --- technical innovation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a new technological method is developed that has
  been the subject of a scientific and technical review by experts from
  international governmental organizations that deal with nuclear
  matters and has received their support, the waste management
  organization may propose, in its triennial report, a new approach for
  the management of nuclear fuel waste that is based on that new method.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of other provisions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 12(3) to (7) and sections 13 and 14
  apply, with such modifications as the circumstances require, in
  respect of an approach proposed under subsection (1) or (2). The
  approach must be accompanied by the comments of the Advisory Council
  on that approach.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission to Governor in Council

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is satisfied that the new approach
  referred to in subsection (1) or (2) is technically and economically
  feasible in Canada, the Minister shall submit the new approach to the
  Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision of Governor in Council

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of
  the Minister, approve an approach proposed under subsection (1) or
  (2), in which case the decision of the Governor in Council shall be
  published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

## [Withdrawal by Beneficiary]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368034 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawals from trust funds

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding
subsection 11(1), the Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of
the Minister, authorize a beneficiary of a trust fund to withdraw all or
part of the balance in the trust fund if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Governor in Council has approved an approach
  under subsection 20(5) and the total balance in the trust funds
  exceeds the estimated total cost of implementing that approach; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the waste management organization has completed the
  implementation of an approach that the Governor in Council selected
  under section 15 or approved under subsection 20(5).

## [Records, Books and Financial Statements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368039 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records and books to be kept

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  waste management organization, every nuclear energy corporation and
  Atomic Energy of Canada Limited, as well as every financial
  institution that holds a trust fund, shall keep, at its place of
  business in Canada, records, books of account and other documents for
  at least six years after the end of the fiscal year to which they
  relate, in such form and containing such information as will enable
  the verification of the accuracy and completeness of the information
  that is required to be submitted or provided to the Minister under
  this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False entries, omissions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall make a false entry, or fail to make
  an entry, in a record, book of account or other document required to
  be kept under subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}WMO financial statements

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  waste management organization shall provide the Minister, within three
  months after the end of each fiscal year of the organization, with
  financial statements audited at its own expense by an independent
  auditor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Trust fund financial statements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every financial institution that holds a trust fund
  shall provide the Minister and the waste management organization,
  within three months after the end of each fiscal year of the trust
  fund, with financial statements relating to that trust fund, audited
  at its own expense by an independent auditor.

## [Documents To Be Made Public]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368050 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents to be made public

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The waste management
organization shall make available to the public

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the study, reports and financial statements that it
  is required to submit to the Minister under this Act, simultaneously
  with submitting them to the Minister; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} financial statements provided to the waste management
  organization under subsection 23(2), as soon as practicable.

## [Inspection of Records and Books]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368055 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Auditors

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate as an auditor for the purposes of this Act any
  person that the Minister considers to be qualified.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of auditors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of ensuring compliance with this Act,
  an auditor may, during normal business hours,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter any premises of a body referred to in
    subsection 22(1), after having given reasonable advance notice to
    the person in charge of the premises; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inspect, make copies of, and take extracts from,
    any records, books of account and other documents that the auditor
    believes on reasonable grounds are required by subsection 22(1) to
    be kept.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation to be produced

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An auditor shall, if so requested either before or
  after entering any premises under this section, produce to the person
  in charge of the premises evidence of the auditor's designation by the
  Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to assist auditor

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person shall give all reasonable assistance to an auditor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibitions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall obstruct or hinder an auditor, or
  make a false or misleading statement, either orally or in writing, or
  provide false or misleading information, to an auditor.

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-368070 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to deposit amounts

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  nuclear energy corporation or Atomic Energy of Canada Limited fails to
  comply with subsection 10(5) or section 17, it is guilty of an offence
  and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding \$300,000 for
  each day on which the offence is committed or is continued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a body is convicted under subsection (1), the
  court may, in addition to any punishment imposed under that
  subsection, order the body to deposit to its trust fund, on or before
  the date fixed by the court, the amount that it failed to deposit as
  required, plus interest on that amount at the prime rate plus two per
  cent calculated from the day on which the amount was required to have
  been deposited.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to comply with court order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a body fails to comply with an order made under
  subsection (2), it is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
  conviction and liable to a fine equal to twenty per cent of the
  aggregate amount set out in that order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to submit study

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  waste management organization fails to submit the study of its
  proposed approaches within the period set out in subsection 12(1), it
  is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not
  exceeding \$300,000 for each day on which the offence is committed or
  is continued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to submit revised study, annual
  report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the waste management organization fails to comply
  with a direction of the Minister made under subsection 14(2), or fails
  to submit the report of its activities within the period set out in
  subsection 16(1), it is guilty of an offence and liable on summary
  conviction to a fine not exceeding \$100,000 for each day on which the
  offence is committed or is continued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to submit revised annual report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the waste management organization fails to comply
  with a direction of the Minister made under subsection 16(4), it is
  guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not
  exceeding \$50,000 for each day on which the offence is committed or
  is continued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawals, making documents public

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the waste management organization withdraws moneys
  from a trust fund without the Minister's approval where that approval
  is required under subsection 11(4), or fails to comply with section
  24, it is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a
  fine not exceeding \$100,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Auditors

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes section 26 is guilty of
  an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding
  \$100,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other offences

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes any other provision of
  this Act is guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a
  fine not exceeding \$50,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence by employee or agent

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** In a prosecution for an
offence under this Act, it is sufficient proof of the offence to
establish that it was committed by an employee or agent of the accused,
whether or not the employee or agent is identified or has been
prosecuted for the offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Due diligence

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** No person shall be found
guilty of an offence under this Act if it is established that the person
exercised all due diligence to comply with this Act or to prevent the
commission of the offence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for prosecution

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** Proceedings in respect of
an offence under this Act may be instituted within but not later than
two years after the time when the subject matter of the proceedings
arose or the Minister became aware of the subject matter of the
proceedings.
