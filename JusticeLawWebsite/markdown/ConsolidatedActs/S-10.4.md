## Sir John A. Macdonald Day and the Sir Wilfrid Laurier Day Act {#sir-john-a.-macdonald-day-and-the-sir-wilfrid-laurier-day-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2002, c. 2
:::

Assented to 2002-03-21

An Act respecting Sir John A. Macdonald Day and Sir Wilfrid Laurier Day
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS Sir John Alexander Macdonald was born on January 11, 1815;

AND WHEREAS he was one of the founders of Confederation;

AND WHEREAS he was the first Prime Minister of Canada;

AND WHEREAS Sir Wilfrid Laurier was born on November 20, 1841;

AND WHEREAS he was Prime Minister of Canada from 1896 to 1911;

AND WHEREAS he was the first Prime Minister of French ancestry and a
fervent promoter of national unity;
:::

::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-431382 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Sir John A. Macdonald Day and the Sir Wilfrid Laurier Day
Act](/eng/acts/S-10.4).

## [Sir John A. Macdonald Day]{.HTitleText1} {#h-431385 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sir John A. Macdonald Day

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Throughout Canada, in each
and every year, the 11th day of January shall be known under the name of
\"Sir John A. Macdonald Day\".

## [Sir Wilfrid Laurier Day]{.HTitleText1} {#h-431388 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sir Wilfrid Laurier Day

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Throughout Canada, in each
and every year, the twentieth day of November shall be known under the
name of \"Sir Wilfrid Laurier Day\".
