## Petro-Canada Public Participation Act {#petro-canada-public-participation-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1991, c. 10
:::

Assented to 1991-02-01

An Act respecting the privatization of the national petroleum company of
Canada
::::

:::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-391598 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Petro-Canada Public Participation Act](/eng/acts/P-11.1).

## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-391601 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
      Privy Council for Canada as is designated by the Governor in
      Council as the Minister for the purposes of this Act or, if no
      member is so designated, the Minister of State (Privatization and
      Regulatory Affairs); ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Petro-Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Petro-Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the corporation given
      that name by subsection 4(1); ([Petro-Canada]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Petro-Canada Limited]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Petro-Canada Limited]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the corporation
      given that name by subsection 4(2). ([Petro-Canada
      Limitée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Same meaning

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless a contrary intention appears, words and
  expressions used in this Act have the same meaning as in the [Canada
  Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operation of [Canada Business
  Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the event of any inconsistency between this Act
  and the [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44), or
  anything issued, made or established under that Act, this Act prevails
  to the extent of the inconsistency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operation of [Competition
  Act](/eng/acts/C-34)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in, or done under the authority of, this Act
  affects the operation of the [Competition Act](/eng/acts/C-34) in
  respect of the acquisition of any interest in Petro-Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1991, c. 10, s. 2
- 1994, c. 24, s. 34(F)
:::

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-391616 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Changes of Names]{.HTitleText1} {#h-391619 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of name of Petro-Canada Inc.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  articles of Petro-Canada Inc., a corporation incorporated under the
  [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44), are hereby amended
  to change its name from "Petro-Canada Inc." in English and French to
  "Petro-Canada" in both languages.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of name of Petro-Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The name of Petro-Canada, a corporation established
  by the Petro-Canada Act, chapter P-11 of the Revised Statutes of
  Canada, 1985, is hereby changed to "Petro-Canada Limited" in English
  and "Petro-Canada Limitée" in French.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights preserved

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the changes of names effected
  by this section do not affect an existing cause of action or claim or
  liability to prosecution in favour of or against a corporation whose
  name was so changed or its directors or officers or any civil,
  criminal or administrative action or proceeding to which the
  corporation or its directors or officers are parties.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1991, c. 10, s. 4
- 1994, c. 24, s. 34(F)
:::

## [Transfer of Shares to Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-391629 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of Petro-Canada shares

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The shares of Petro-Canada
held by Petro-Canada Limited are hereby transferred to the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dealing with shares

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister is hereby authorized to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} acquire, hold, dispose of and otherwise deal with
    shares and debt obligations of, and security interests in,
    Petro-Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into any agreement or arrangement necessary
    for or incidental to any activity referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration of shares

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Shares of Petro-Canada acquired by the Minister shall
  be registered in the name of the Minister in the books of Petro-Canada
  and shall be held by the Minister in trust for Her Majesty in right of
  Canada.

## [Sale of Shares by Petro-Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-391639 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issue and disposal of shares

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Petro-Canada is hereby
authorized to issue and sell or otherwise dispose of shares of
Petro-Canada.

## [Reorganization of Petro-Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-391642 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Articles of amendment

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Forthwith after this section comes
  into force, Petro-Canada shall submit to the Minister for approval
  articles of amendment prepared in accordance with section 9.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission to Director

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Forthwith after the Minister approves articles of
  amendment submitted pursuant to subsection (1), Petro-Canada shall
  send the approved articles to the Director.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Articles of amendment sent to the Director pursuant
  to this section are, subject to this Act, deemed for all purposes to
  have been sent under section 177 of the [Canada Business Corporations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-44).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1991, c. 10, s. 8
- 1994, c. 24, s. 34(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory provisions in articles of
amendment

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  articles of amendment for Petro-Canada shall contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provisions imposing constraints on the issue,
    transfer and ownership, including joint ownership, of voting shares
    of Petro-Canada to prevent any one person, together with the
    associates of that person, from holding, beneficially owning or
    controlling, directly or indirectly, otherwise than by way of
    security only, in the aggregate voting shares to which are attached
    more than twenty per cent of the votes that may ordinarily be cast
    to elect directors of Petro-Canada, other than votes that may be so
    cast by or on behalf of the Minister;

  - [(b) and (c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2001, c. 18, s.
    2\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provisions preventing Petro-Canada from selling,
    transferring or otherwise disposing of, whether by one transaction
    or event or several related transactions or events, all or
    substantially all of its assets to any one person or group of
    associated persons or to non-residents, otherwise than by way of
    security only in connection with the financing of Petro-Canada;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} provisions requiring Petro-Canada to ensure that
    any member of the public can, in either official language,
    communicate with and obtain available services from

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its head office, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any of its other offices or facilities, and the
      head office and any other office or facility of any of its
      wholly-owned subsidiaries, where Petro-Canada determines that
      there is significant demand for communications with and services
      from that office or facility in that language having regard to the
      public served and the location of the office or facility;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} provisions respecting the enforcement of the
    constraints imposed pursuant to paragraph (a); and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} provisions specifying that the head office of
    Petro-Canada is to be situated in Calgary, Alberta.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement provisions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Without limiting the generality of paragraph (1)(f),
  the provisions referred to therein may provide for the filing of
  declarations, the suspension of voting rights, the forfeiture of
  dividends, the refusal of the issue or registration of voting shares
  and the sale of voting shares held contrary to the constraints and
  payment of the net proceeds of the sale to the person entitled
  thereto.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption for small transactions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the directors of Petro-Canada are of the
  reasonable opinion, from the central securities register of
  Petro-Canada or otherwise, that a subscriber for or a transferee of
  voting shares of Petro-Canada would, on acquiring the shares, hold,
  beneficially own or control voting shares to which are attached not
  more than the lesser of two one-hundredths of one per cent of the
  votes that may ordinarily be cast to elect directors of Petro-Canada
  and ten thousand such votes, the directors are entitled to assume that
  the subscriber or transferee is not and will not be an associate of
  anyone else and, unless the address to be recorded in the register for
  the subscriber or transferee is outside Canada, that the shares will
  not be held, beneficially owned or controlled in contravention of the
  articles of amendment for Petro-Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No provision imposing constraints pursuant to
  paragraph (1)(a) applies in respect of voting shares of Petro-Canada
  that are held

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by the Minister in trust for Her Majesty in right
    of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by one or more underwriters solely for the purpose
    of distributing the shares to the public; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by any person who provides centralized facilities
    for the clearing of trades in securities and is acting in relation
    to trades in the shares solely as an intermediary in the payment of
    funds or the delivery of securities, or both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Associates

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, a person is an
  associate of another person if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one is a corporation of which the other is an
    officer or director;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one is a corporation that is controlled by the
    other or by a group of persons of which the other is a member;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one is a partnership of which the other is a
    partner;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} one is a trust of which the other is a trustee;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} both are corporations controlled by the same
    person;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} both are members of a voting trust that relates to
    voting shares of Petro-Canada;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} both, in the reasonable opinion of the directors of
    Petro-Canada, are parties to an agreement or arrangement a purpose
    of which is to require them to act in concert with respect to their
    interests, direct or indirect, in Petro-Canada or are otherwise
    acting in concert with respect to those interests; or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} both are at the same time associates, within the
    meaning of any of paragraphs (a) to (g), of the same person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (5), for the purposes of
  this section,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where a person who, but for this paragraph, would
    be an associate of another person submits to Petro-Canada a
    statutory declaration stating that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} no voting shares of Petro-Canada held or to be
      held by the declarant are or will be, to the declarant's
      knowledge, held in the right of, for the use or benefit of or
      under the control of, any other person of which, but for this
      paragraph, the declarant would be an associate, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the declarant is not acting and will not act in
      concert with any such other person with respect to their
      interests, direct or indirect, in Petro-Canada,

    the declarant and that other person are not associates so long as
    the directors of Petro-Canada are satisfied that the statements in
    the declaration are being complied with and that there are no other
    reasonable grounds for disregarding the declaration;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} two corporations are not associates pursuant to
    paragraph (5)(h) by reason only that under paragraph (5)(a) each is
    an associate of the same individual; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the directors of Petro-Canada are of the
    reasonable opinion, from the central securities register of
    Petro-Canada or otherwise, that any person holds, beneficially owns
    or controls voting shares to which are attached not more than the
    lesser of two one-hundredths of one per cent of the votes that may
    ordinarily be cast to elect directors of Petro-Canada and ten
    thousand such votes, that person is not an associate of anyone else
    and no one else is an associate of that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section,
  [[control]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means control in any manner that
  results in control in fact, whether directly through the ownership of
  securities or indirectly through a trust, an agreement or arrangement,
  the ownership of any body corporate or otherwise, and, without
  limiting the generality of the foregoing,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a body corporate is deemed to be controlled by a
    person if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} securities of the body corporate to which are
      attached more than fifty per cent of the votes that may be cast to
      elect directors of the body corporate are held, otherwise than by
      way of security only, by or for the benefit of that person, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the votes attached to those securities are
      sufficient, if exercised, to elect a majority of the directors of
      the body corporate; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a partnership or unincorporated organization is
    deemed to be controlled by a person if an ownership interest therein
    representing more than fifty per cent of the assets of the
    partnership or organization is held, otherwise than by way of
    security only, by or for the benefit of that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} In this section,

  [[corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a body corporate,
      partnership and unincorporated
      organization; ([société]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[downstream assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[downstream assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means assets used in the
      refining of crude oil or in the distribution and marketing of
      refined petroleum products and related goods and services; ([biens
      de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[non-resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[non-resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an individual, other than a Canadian citizen,
        who is not ordinarily resident in Canada,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation incorporated, formed or otherwise
        organized outside Canada,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a foreign government or an agency thereof,

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a corporation controlled by non-residents as
        defined in any of paragraphs (a) to (c),

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a trust

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} established by a non-resident as defined in
          any of paragraphs (b) to (d), other than a trust for the
          administration of a pension fund for the benefit of
          individuals a majority of whom are residents, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in which non-residents as defined in any of
          paragraphs (a) to (d) have more than fifty per cent of the
          beneficial interest, or

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that is controlled by a trust
        described in paragraph (e),

      but does not include

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a mutual company within the meaning of the
        [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), if its head office
        and chief place of business are situated in Canada and at least
        three quarters of its board of directors and each committee of
        its directors are Canadian citizens who are ordinarily resident
        in Canada, or

      - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a company within the meaning of that Act that
        is a subsidiary of a foreign institution within the meaning of
        that Act or a foreign company within the meaning of that Act,
        where the company or the foreign company is acquiring shares to
        form part of the assets of a segregated fund maintained pursuant
        to section 451 or 593 of that Act that has been established with
        respect to one or more policies or amounts for the
        administration of a pension fund for the benefit of individuals
        a majority of whom are
        residents; ([non-résident]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any individual,
      corporation, government or agency thereof, trustee, executor,
      administrator and other legal
      representative; ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual, corporation,
      government or agency thereof or trust that is not a
      non-resident; ([résident]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[upstream assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[upstream assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means assets used in the
      exploration for, or in the development, production and marketing
      of, crude oil, natural gas, natural gas liquids, bitumen,
      synthetic oils and sulphur; ([biens de
      production]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[voting share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[voting share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a share carrying a
      voting right under all circumstances or under some circumstances
      that have occurred and are continuing, and includes a security
      currently convertible into such a share and currently exercisable
      options and rights to acquire such a share or such a convertible
      security. ([action avec droit de vote]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

## [Transitional, Consequential and Coming into Force Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-391752 .Part}

### [Transitional]{.HTitleText2} {#h-391753 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[issuance
date]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[issuance date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the date on
  which shares of Petro-Canada are first issued after the coming into
  force of section 7 to any person, other than the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before the issuance date, the Minister may, with the
  approval of the Governor in Council, appoint the chairperson, chief
  executive officer and other directors of Petro-Canada to hold office
  during pleasure for a term not exceeding one year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Re-appointment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A director of Petro-Canada, including a director
  holding office on the coming into force of this section, is eligible
  for re-appointment under subsection (2) to the board of directors in
  the same or another capacity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of office

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The directors of Petro-Canada holding office on the
  coming into force of this section cease to hold office when the
  directors first appointed under subsection (2) take office, unless
  re-appointed under that subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (2), the term of office of
  a director who holds office on the issuance date ends at the close of
  the first annual meeting of shareholders of Petro-Canada held after
  that date, which meeting shall be held not later than six months after
  the end of Petro-Canada's financial year in which that date falls.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} In the event of any inconsistency between this
  section and the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), this
  section prevails to the extent of the inconsistency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In the period between the issuance date and the first
  annual meeting of shareholders of Petro-Canada held thereafter,
  Petro-Canada shall be deemed not to be a parent Crown corporation
  within the meaning and for the purposes of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pension rights

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  manner and to the extent provided by the regulations made under
  subsection (3), the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the [Supplementary Retirement Benefits
  Act](/eng/acts/S-24) and the regulations made under those Acts apply
  to any person who meets all of the following criteria:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person, on the coming into force of section 5,
    was employed by Petro-Canada Limited and was a contributor under the
    [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and, immediately
    before the coming into force of this section, was employed by that
    corporation or Petro-Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the President of the Treasury Board has not made a
    payment to Petro-Canada Limited pursuant to section 40 of the
    [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) in respect of
    the pensionable service to that person's credit under that Act
    immediately before the coming into force of this section;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person has not received or opted to receive any
    annuity or other benefit under section 12 or 13 of the [Public
    Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) in respect of the
    pensionable service to that person's credit under that Act
    immediately before the coming into force of this section; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person elects, within one year after the coming
    into force of this section and in such form and manner as the
    President of the Treasury Board may direct, to have the [Public
    Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the [Supplementary
    Retirement Benefits Act](/eng/acts/S-24) and the regulations made
    under those Acts apply to the person in the manner and to the extent
    provided by the regulations made under subsection (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Election irrevocable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An election referred to in paragraph (1)(d) is
  irrevocable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of
  the President of the Treasury Board, make regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the manner in which and the extent to
    which provisions, as amended, of the Acts and regulations referred
    to in subsection (1) apply to persons who make an election under
    paragraph (1)(d);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} adapting provisions of those Acts and regulations
    for the purposes of this section; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} generally for carrying out the purposes and
    provisions of this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retroactive application of regulations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Regulations made under subsection (3) may, if they so
  provide, be retroactive and have effect with respect to any period
  before they were made.
