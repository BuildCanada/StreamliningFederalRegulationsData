## Forestry Act {#forestry-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. F-30
:::

An Act respecting forestry development and research
::::

::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-236781 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Forestry Act](/eng/acts/F-30).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-30, s. 1
- 1989, c. 27, s. 14
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-236786 .Part}

Definition of [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Natural
Resources.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-30, s. 2
- 1989, c. 27, s. 15
- 1994, c. 41, s. 26
:::

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Development and Research]{.HTitleText1} {#h-236791 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Functions of Minister

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  exercising the powers and performing the duties and functions assigned
  to the Minister by the [Department of Natural Resources
  Act](/eng/acts/N-20.8), the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall provide for the conduct of research relating
    to the protection, management and utilization of the forest
    resources of Canada and the better utilization of forest products
    and may establish and maintain laboratories and other necessary
    facilities for those purposes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may undertake, promote or recommend measures for
    the encouragement of public cooperation in the protection and wise
    use of the forest resources of Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} may enter into agreements with the government of
    any province or with any person for forest protection and management
    or forest utilization, for the conduct of research related thereto
    or for forestry publicity or education;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} may provide for the making of forestry surveys and
    provide advice relating to the protection and management of forests
    on lands administered by any department or agency of the Government
    of Canada or belonging to Her Majesty in right of Canada; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} at the request of any department or agency of the
    Government of Canada, may assume responsibility for the protection
    and management of any forest on lands for which that department or
    agency is responsible, including responsibility for the disposal of
    timber and grass and for the granting of rights to the natural
    produce of the forest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Studies, investigations and aid

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may conduct economic studies relating to
  the forest resources, forest industries and marketing of forest
  products, make investigations designed to aid the forest industries
  and woodlot owners of Canada and assist external aid programs relating
  to forestry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Silviculture, forest

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister has, in relation to silviculture, the
  same powers, duties and functions as the Minister has under this Act
  in relation to the protection and management of the forest resources
  of Canada.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 1989, c. 27, s. 16\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation and conferences

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, in carrying out the Minister's
  duties and functions under this Act, consult with and inaugurate
  conferences of provincial or municipal authorities, universities,
  representatives of industry or other interested persons.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-30, s. 3
- 1989, c. 27, s. 16
- 1994, c. 41, s. 27
:::

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Forest Experimental Areas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-236809 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forest Experimental Areas

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
establish as a Forest Experimental Area

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} lands belonging to Her Majesty in right of Canada,
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} lands provided therefor, pursuant to an agreement
  with the government of any province, by the government of the province
  or any person in the province,

and may withdraw lands from or add lands to a Forest Experimental Area.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-30, s. 4
- 1989, c. 27, s. 17(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Minister

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to any regulations
made under section 6, the Minister may, on lands comprised in any Forest
Experimental Area or in respect of which the Minister has assumed
responsibility under paragraph 3(1)(e), do such acts and construct such
works as the Minister considers necessary for forestry research and
forest protection and management, including the disposal of timber and
grass and the granting of rights to the natural produce of the forest.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-30, s. 5
- 1989, c. 27, s. 18
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations for the protection, care and management of lands
comprised in Forest Experimental Areas and lands in respect of which the
Minister has assumed responsibility under paragraph 3(1)(e), including
regulations respecting

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cutting, removal and disposal of timber, the
  establishment and use of reservoirs, water power sites, power
  transmission lines and communication lines and any other use of those
  lands, and the granting of leases and permits therefor;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the protection of the flora and fauna;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the prevention and extinguishing of fires;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the regulation and prohibition of traffic, the
  carrying on of businesses and other activities and the abatement and
  prevention of nuisances;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the removal and exclusion of trespassers and of
  persons failing to comply with the regulations; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the prevention of trespass to property, the
  mutilation or destruction of trees and the destruction or damaging of
  buildings, materials or notices used in connection with the
  administration or management of those lands.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-30, s. 6
- 1989, c. 27, s. 18
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes any regulation made under section 6 is guilty of an offence
and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding five hundred
dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to
both.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. F-30, s. 7
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seizure of articles

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  officer employed in the administration of this Act or a peace officer
  as defined in the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) may seize any
  article by means of or in relation to which the officer believes on
  reasonable grounds an offence under this Act has been committed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Detention

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An article seized pursuant to subsection (1) may be
  detained for a period of one month following the day of seizure
  unless, during that period, proceedings under this Act in respect of
  the article are undertaken, in which case the article may be further
  detained until the proceedings are finally concluded.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a person is convicted of an offence under this
  Act, the convicting court, judge or provincial court judge may, in
  addition to any other punishment that may be imposed, order that any
  article by means of or in relation to which the offence was committed
  be forfeited, whereupon the article is forfeited to Her Majesty and
  may be disposed of in such manner and at such time and place as the
  Minister may direct, but no article shall be disposed of pending an
  appeal against the conviction or before the time within which the
  appeal may be taken has expired.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. F-30, s. 8
- R.S., 1985, c. 27 (1st Supp.), s. 203
:::
