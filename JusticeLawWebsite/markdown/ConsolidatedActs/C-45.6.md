## An Act respecting certain measures related to COVID-19 {#an-act-respecting-certain-measures-related-to-covid-19 .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2022, c. 2
:::

Assented to 2022-03-04

An Act respecting certain measures related to COVID-19
::::

:::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments out of C.R.F.

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Health may
make payments, the total of which may not exceed \$2.5 billion, out of
the Consolidated Revenue Fund for any expenses incurred on or after
January 1, 2022 in relation to coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19)
tests.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfers

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Health may
transfer to any province or territory, or to any body or person in
Canada, any coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) tests or instruments
used in relation to those tests acquired by Her Majesty in right of
Canada on or after April 1, 2021.
