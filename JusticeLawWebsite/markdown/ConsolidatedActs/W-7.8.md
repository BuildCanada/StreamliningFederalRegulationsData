## Western Grain Transition Payments Act {#western-grain-transition-payments-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1995, c. 17, Sch. II
:::

Assented to 1995-06-22

An Act to provide authority to make transition payments to owners of
farmland in Western Canada on which grain is grown

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted as Schedule II to 1995, c. 17, assented to 22nd June, 1995.\]
:::
:::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468820 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Western Grain Transition Payments Act](/eng/acts/W-7.8).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468823 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[eligible area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area comprising
    Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Alberta and those parts of British Columbia
    known as the Peace River District and the Creston-Wynndel
    Areas; ([région désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[farmland]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[farmland]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means land on which an annual crop
    of grain was cultivated during 1994 and land that was in
    summer-fallow during that year and on which an annual crop of grain
    was cultivated during 1993; ([terre arable]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[grain]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[grain]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning given that word by
    section 181.1 of the National Transportation Act,
    1987; ([grain]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Agriculture
    and Agri-Food; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[own]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[own]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of farmland, to hold
    the fee simple in the farmland, unless another interest in the
    farmland is prescribed, in which case it means to hold that
    interest; ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by regulations
    made by the Governor in Council under section 6. ([Version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468832 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons who may apply

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who owned farmland in the eligible area at the beginning of the day on
  February 28, 1995 may apply to the Minister for transition payments.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be in a form approved by the
  Minister and be received by the Minister not later than September 15,
  1995, or any later day fixed by the Governor in Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of ownership

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may require the applicant to provide
  proof of the applicant's ownership of the farmland.

## [Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468840 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may make payments

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may make an interim transition payment and a final transition
  payment to an applicant if the Minister is satisfied that the
  applicant owned farmland in the eligible area at the beginning of the
  day on February 28, 1995 and meets the prescribed criteria and
  conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment deadlines

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Governor in Council may establish deadlines for
  making the payments.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of amounts

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The total amount of the interim transition payment
  and the final transition payment must be calculated in accordance with
  the regulations on a basis that reflects the productivity of the
  farmland and its distance from port.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tax treatment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a transition payment received in respect of
    farmland that is capital property of the applicant shall be
    considered to be an amount required by subsection 53(2) of that Act
    to be deducted in computing the adjusted cost base of the farmland
    to the applicant after the payment is received;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a transition payment received in respect of
    farmland that was, immediately before its disposition by the
    applicant, capital property of the applicant shall, where the
    farmland is disposed of before the payment is received, be
    considered to be an amount required by subsection 53(2) of that Act
    to be deducted in computing the adjusted cost base of the farmland
    to the applicant immediately before the disposition;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a transition payment to which neither paragraph (a)
    nor (b) applies, received by the applicant, shall be considered to
    be assistance received in the course of earning income from a
    business or property in respect of the cost of the property or in
    respect of an outlay or an expense; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where, pursuant to an equitable arrangement
    referred to in paragraph 6(c), a portion of a transition payment
    received by an applicant is paid to a person or partnership that is
    leasing farmland from the applicant, that portion paid to the person
    or partnership is required to be included in computing the income of
    the person or partnership from a business for the taxation year of
    the person or partnership in which it is received and the amount so
    paid is deemed not to be a transition payment received by the
    applicant for the purposes of paragraphs (a) to (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment limit and apportionment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The total amount of all transition payments must not
  exceed 1.6 billion dollars and the total amount must be apportioned as
  follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 16.10% for farmland in Manitoba;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 56.42% for farmland in Saskatchewan; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 27.48% for farmland in Alberta and those parts of
    British Columbia known as the Peace River District and the
    Creston-Wynndel Areas.

## [Appropriation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468861 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appropriation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The amount of 1.6 billion
dollars is appropriated for the purposes of this Act, to be paid out of
the Consolidated Revenue Fund from time to time as required.

## [Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-468864 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Governor in Council

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make regulations for carrying out the purposes of this Act, including
regulations prescribing

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} interests in farmland for the purposes of the
  definition [[own]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} criteria to be used by the Minister in deciding
  whether applicants are entitled to transition payments;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} conditions to be met prior to receiving a transition
  payment including the condition that an equitable arrangement must be
  made by an applicant in respect of any person who or partnership that
  is leasing the farmland that is the subject of the application for the
  transition payment; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} how interim and final transition payments are to be
  calculated, including which port is to be used for the purpose of
  determining the distance of farmland from port.
