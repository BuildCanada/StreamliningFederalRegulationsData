## Canada Student Financial Assistance Act {#canada-student-financial-assistance-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1994, c. 28
:::

Assented to 1994-06-23

An Act respecting the making of loans and the provision of other forms
of financial assistance to students, to amend and provide for the repeal
of the Canada Student Loans Act, and to amend one other Act in
consequence thereof
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-441655 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada Student Financial Assistance Act](/eng/acts/S-22.7).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-441658 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Act,

  [[appropriate authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[appropriate authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
      province, means an appropriate authority designated for the
      province pursuant to subsection 3(1); ([autorité
      compétente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated educational institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated educational institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      an institution of learning designated pursuant to subsection 3(1),
      and includes a specified educational institution within the
      meaning of the [Canada Student Loans
      Act](/eng/acts/S-23); ([établissement agréé]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a financial institution
      within the meaning of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) or other
      corporation that is a party to an agreement with the Minister
      entered into under section 5; ([prêteur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
      Privy Council for Canada as may be designated by the Governor in
      Council as the Minister for the purposes of this
      Act; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in the case of a form or the
      information to be given on a form, means prescribed by the
      Minister and, in any other case, means prescribed by the
      regulations; ([Version anglaise
      seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

  [[qualifying student]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[qualifying student]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} who is a Canadian citizen, a person registered
        as an Indian under the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), a
        [[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
        subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
        Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) or a protected person within the meaning
        of subsection 95(2) of that Act,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} who is qualified for enrolment or is enrolled
        at a designated educational institution as a full-time student
        or part-time student for a period of studies at a post-secondary
        school level, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} who intends to attend a designated educational
        institution as a full-time student or part-time student for a
        period of studies described in paragraph (b) if it is
        financially possible for that person to do so. ([étudiant
        admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Appropriate Authorities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-441679 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of appropriate authorities

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of this Act, the Minister may, by order, designate for a
  province

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an appropriate authority, which authority may
    designate as designated educational institutions any institutions of
    learning in Canada that offer courses at a post-secondary school
    level, or any class of such institutions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an appropriate authority, which authority may
    designate as designated educational institutions any institutions of
    learning outside Canada that offer courses at a post-secondary
    school level, or any class of such institutions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocations and exclusions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An appropriate authority may revoke any designation
  made by it under subsection (1), and any designation made in respect
  of the province under the [Canada Student Loans Act](/eng/acts/S-23)
  and, in the case of a designation of a class, may exclude any named
  institution from that designation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may enter into an agreement with an appropriate authority, or
  with an appropriate authority and the government of the province for
  which the authority was designated, respecting the exercise or
  performance of any of the authority's powers, duties or functions
  under this Act or the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directives

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may give directives to any appropriate
  authority respecting the exercise or performance of any of its powers,
  duties or functions under this Act or the regulations, and such
  directives are binding on the appropriate authority.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Statutory Instruments
  Act](/eng/acts/S-22)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22) does
  not apply in respect of directives given under subsection (2).

## [Agreements with Lenders]{.HTitleText1} {#h-441694 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreements

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, if the
Minister considers it appropriate, with the concurrence of the Minister
of Finance, enter into an agreement with any lender respecting the
making of student loans or the provision of other forms of financial
assistance to students by the lender, and such an agreement may, without
limiting the generality of the foregoing, provide for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the payment by the Minister to the lender of all or a
  portion of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} amounts equivalent to interest on a student loan
    made by the lender in respect of the period before April 1, 2023 for
    which no interest is payable by the borrower under subsection 7(1),
    as it read from time to time before April 1, 2023,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} amounts equivalent to interest on a student loan
    made by the lender in respect of any period for which no interest is
    payable by the borrower under regulations made under paragraph
    15(1)(n), as that paragraph and those regulations read from time to
    time before April 1, 2023,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} principal and interest on a student loan made by
    the lender to a borrower whose obligations in respect of the loan
    are terminated in the circumstances set out in section 10 or 11,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} amounts in respect of a student loan made by the
    lender that are paid under regulations made under paragraph
    15(1)(l),

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a risk premium for lending risks on a student loan
    made by the lender,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} amounts necessary for the purchase of a student
    loan made by the lender that is payable and on which no payment has
    been made by the borrower in at least one year, and a percentage of
    amounts recovered by the Minister in respect of that loan,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} adjusted amounts in respect of principal and
    interest on a student loan, where an inadvertent error concerning
    the rate of interest or the amount to be repaid is made by the
    lender in drawing up the consolidated student loan agreement or the
    student loan agreement,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} amounts in respect of the loss suffered by the
    lender on a student loan made to a full-time student who, before the
    month following the month in which the borrower ceases to be a
    full-time student,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} makes an assignment under the [Bankruptcy and
      Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3) that is filed and not cancelled, is
      deemed under that Act, by reason of circumstances occurring before
      that following month, to have made an assignment, or is the
      subject of a bankruptcy order made under that Act,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} makes a proposal under Division I or II of Part
      III of that Act that is approved or deemed to be approved by a
      court under that Act,

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} is granted a consolidation order under Part X of
      that Act, or

    - [(D)]{.lawlabel} is granted relief under a provincial law relating
      to the orderly payment of debts, and

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} any other amounts that the Minister considers
    appropriate in respect of a student loan made, or other financial
    assistance provided, by the lender;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} audits and adjustments in respect of payments made to
  the lender by the Minister;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to the regulations, the circumstances in
  which the lender is, on application, required to provide financial
  assistance to a qualifying student in an amount not exceeding the
  maximum amount determined in accordance with section 12;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2022, c. 19, s. 155\]]{.Repealed}

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the repayment of student loans by borrowers or
  classes of borrowers on an income-contingent basis;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the provisions to be included in an agreement between
  a borrower and the lender relating to a student loan, the making of
  amendments to the agreement, and the conditions and consequences of
  making an amendment;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the procedures to be followed by the lender in
  making, collecting or otherwise dealing with a student loan;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the making of reports to the Minister respecting
  student loans; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the terms and conditions respecting the obligations
  of the Minister and the lender under the agreement.

## [Interest and Periods of Deferral]{.HTitleText1} {#h-441759 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No accrual of interest

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Beginning on April 1, 2023, no
  interest is payable by a borrower on a student loan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, nothing in this section
  relieves a borrower from liability for interest that accrued before
  April 1, 2023 on a student loan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No fees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No fee of any kind may be charged to a borrower on a
  student loan in respect of any period of studies during which the
  borrower is a full-time or part-time student or in respect of any
  subsequent period ending on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a student loan that is made to a
    full-time student, the last day of the sixth month after the month
    in which the borrower ceases to be a full-time student; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a student loan that is made to a
    part-time student, the last day of the sixth month after the month
    in which the borrower ceases to be a student, whether a part-time or
    full-time student.

## [Death or Disability of Borrower]{.HTitleText1} {#h-441797 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Death of borrower

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All

## [Certificates of Eligibility]{.HTitleText1} {#h-441820 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issue of certificates

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, the
  appropriate authority for a province designated under paragraph
  3(1)(a) may, on application of a qualifying student, issue or cause to
  be issued a certificate of eligibility in the prescribed form, for a
  period of studies at a designated educational institution in or
  outside Canada, to or in respect of the student if that authority
  considers the student

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to have attained a satisfactory scholastic
    standard; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to be in need of financial assistance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount needed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the appropriate authority considers that a
  qualifying student meets the criteria set out in paragraphs (1)(a) and
  (b), it shall determine the amount the student needs, taking into
  consideration

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the program of studies in which the qualifying
    student is registered;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cost of books and other course-related
    supplies; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the qualifying student is enrolled in an
    institution outside the province of residence of the student.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A certificate of eligibility must set out the social
  insurance number of the qualifying student and the maximum amount of
  the financial assistance that may be given to the student.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amount for certain full-time
  students

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a full-time student, the maximum
  amount, for a province, of a student loan prescribed by regulations
  made under paragraph 15(1)(j), and in respect of which a certificate
  of eligibility is issued, is the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount prescribed for that province, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the product obtained by multiplying

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined under subsection (2) for
      the student

    by

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the percentage prescribed for that province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Escalator

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The amount referred to in paragraph (4)(a) may be
  adjusted annually by an escalator determined in accordance with the
  prescribed formula.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amount for part-time students

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a part-time student, the maximum
  amount, for a province, of a student loan in respect of which a
  certificate of eligibility is issued is the prescribed amount.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amount

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The maximum amount of financial assistance in respect
  of which a certificate of eligibility is issued, other than a loan to
  which subsection (4) or (6) applies, is the prescribed amount, or the
  amount calculated in accordance with the prescribed formulas.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The maximum amount of financial assistance in respect

## [Alternative Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-441855 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative payment

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the government of a province has, at least twelve months before the
  beginning of a loan year, notified the Minister in writing that a
  provincial student financial assistance plan will be in operation in
  that province in that loan year and that it does not wish, or no
  longer wishes, to participate in the plan established by this Act and
  the regulations, the Minister shall pay to the province, not later
  than six months after the end of that loan year and each following
  loan year in which it does not participate in the plan, an alternative
  amount for the loan year, calculated as provided in this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-participating provinces

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The government of a province that, on the coming into
  force of this section, is not participating in the student loan plan
  established by the [Canada Student Loans Act](/eng/acts/S-23) is

  respect of the loan year in which this section comes into force.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The government of a province that is not
  participating in the plan established by this Act and the regulations
  but later wishes to participate may do so by so notifying the Minister
  in writing at least six months, or such shorter period as the Minister
  may fix, before the beginning of the loan year in which it wishes to
  participate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of alternative amount

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The alternative amount for a province for a loan year
  (in this subsection referred to as the "current loan year") is the
  amount, as determined by the Minister after consultation with the
  Chief Statistician of Canada, obtained by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} multiplying

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the net per capita costs for participating
      provinces for the loan year beginning on August 1, 1990,

    by

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the product obtained by multiplying the
      estimated number of persons in that province who, on the first day
      of the current loan year, have attained eighteen years of age and
      have not attained twenty-five years of age by the escalator
      determined under subsection (5), for each loan year in the period
      beginning on August 1, 1991 and ending on July 31 of the current
      loan year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subtracting from the product obtained under
    paragraph (a) the net costs for that province for the current loan
    year.

      participating provinces for that loan year, and

      participating provinces for that first loan year, and

at the time the agreement was entered into.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

- **[[16.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section and section
  16.2, no action or proceedings shall be taken to recover money owing
  under a student loan more than six years after the day on which the
  money becomes due and payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction and set-off

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Money owing under a student loan may be recovered at
  any time by way of deduction from or set-off against any sum of money
  that may be due or payable by Her Majesty in right of Canada to the
  borrower or the estate or succession of the borrower.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment of liability

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a borrower's liability for money owing under a
  student loan is acknowledged in accordance with subsection (4), the
  time during which the limitation period has run before the
  acknowledgment does not count in the calculation of that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of acknowledgments

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An acknowledgment of liability means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a written promise to pay the money owing, signed by
    the borrower or his or her agent or other representative;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written acknowledgment of the money owing, signed
    by the borrower or his or her agent or other representative, whether
    or not a promise to pay can be implied from it and whether or not it
    contains a refusal to pay;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a part payment by the borrower or his or her agent
    or other representative of any money owing; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any acknowledgment of the money owing made by the
    borrower, his or her agent or other representative or the trustee or
    administrator in the course of proceedings under the [Bankruptcy and
    Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3) or any other legislation dealing with
    the payment of debts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment after expiry of
  limitation period

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a borrower's liability for money owing under a
  student loan is acknowledged in accordance with subsection (4) after
  the expiry of the limitation period in respect of the loan, an action
  or proceedings to recover the money may, subject to subsections (3)
  and (6), be brought within six years after the date of the
  acknowledgment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period suspended

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The running of a limitation period in respect of a
  student loan is suspended during any period in which it is prohibited
  to commence or continue an action or other proceedings against the
  borrower to recover money owing under the loan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement proceedings

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply in respect of an action
  or proceedings relating to the execution, renewal or enforcement of a
  judgment.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2003, c. 15, s. 11
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[16.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This section applies only in respect
  of the recovery of money that became due and payable under a student
  loan before the coming into force of this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section, no action or proceedings
  shall be taken to recover money owing under a student loan more than
  six years after the day on which the limitation period that applied
  before the coming into force of this section started to run.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior acknowledgments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), the day of the
  most recent acknowledgment of a borrower's liability in respect of
  money owing under a student loan is deemed to be the day on which the
  limitation period started to run if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the acknowledgment was made before the coming into
    force of this section; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} under the law applicable at the time of the
    acknowledgment, the time during which the limitation period ran
    before the acknowledgment did not count in the calculation of that
    period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction and set-off

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Money owing under a student loan may be recovered at
  any time by way of deduction from or set-off against any sum of money
  that may be due or payable by Her Majesty in right of Canada to the
  borrower or the estate or succession of the borrower.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment of liability

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If, on or after the day on which this section comes
  into force, a borrower's liability for money owing under a student
  loan is acknowledged in accordance with subsection (6), the time
  during which the limitation period has run before the acknowledgment
  does not count in the calculation of that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of acknowledgments

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (5) and (7), an
  acknowledgment of liability means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a written promise to pay the money owing, signed by
    the borrower or his or her agent or other representative;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written acknowledgment of the money owing, signed
    by the borrower or his or her agent or other representative, whether
    or not a promise to pay can be implied from it and whether or not it
    contains a refusal to pay;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a part payment by the borrower or his or her agent
    or other representative of any money owing; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any acknowledgment of the money owing made by the
    borrower, his or her agent or other representative or the trustee or
    administrator in the course of proceedings under the [Bankruptcy and
    Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3) or any other legislation dealing with
    the payment of debts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment after expiry of
  limitation period

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If, after the expiry of the limitation period in
  respect of a student loan --- including any limitation period that
  expired before the coming into force of this section --- and on or
  after the day on which this section comes into force, a borrower's
  liability for money owing under the loan is acknowledged in accordance
  with subsection (6), an action or proceedings to recover the money
  may, subject to subsections (5) and (8), be brought within six years
  after the date of the acknowledgment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period suspended

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The running of a limitation period in respect of a
  student loan is, commencing on the day on which this section comes
  into force, suspended during any period in which it is prohibited to
  commence or continue an action or other proceedings against the
  borrower to recover money owing under the loan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statutory bar

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (7), if the limitation period
  in respect of a student loan expired before the coming into force of
  this section, no action or proceeding shall be taken to recover money
  owing under the loan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement proceedings

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply in respect of an action
  or proceedings relating to the execution, renewal or enforcement of a
  judgment.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2003, c. 15, s. 11

  to a student, knowingly makes any false statement or

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrative measures

- **[[17.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  a person, in respect of a student loan or other financial assistance
  to a student, knowingly makes any false statement or
  misrepresentation, including by omission, in an application or other
  document or knowingly provides any false or misleading information,
  including by omission, the Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deny the person financial assistance for a
    prescribed period;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2022, c. 19, s. 161\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} deny the person the deferral of payments of
    principal or interest under section 8 for a prescribed period, or
    terminate the deferral;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} deny the person payments of interest under
    subsection 9(2) for a prescribed period, or terminate the payments;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} deny the person a gratuitous payment referred to in
    paragraph 15(1)(l);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2022, c. 19, s. 161\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} deny the person repayment of a student loan on an
    income-contingent basis referred to in paragraph 15(1)(o) for a
    prescribed period, or terminate repayment on an income-contingent
    basis;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} require the person to immediately repay any
    outstanding amount of a student loan that they obtained by reason of
    the false statement or misrepresentation or the false or misleading
    information; or

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} require the person to immediately repay any grant
    that they obtained by reason of the false statement or
    misrepresentation or the false or misleading information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Outstanding student loans

  force, a person knowingly made any false statement or
  misrepresentation, including by omission, in an application or other
  document or knowingly provided any false or misleading information,
  including by omission, in respect of a student loan that is
  outstanding on that day, the Minister may take any measure set out in
  paragraphs (1)(a) to (h).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may not take any measure under
  subsection (1) or (2) without having given the person 60 days' notice
  of the Minister's intention to take the measure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submissions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The person may make submissions to the Minister in
  respect of the measure at any time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rescission or modification of measure

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may rescind or modify a measure taken
  under subsection (1) or (2) if new facts are presented or the Minister
  considers that the measure was taken without knowledge of some
  material fact or on the basis of a mistake concerning one.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation or prescription

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may not take any measure under
  subsection (1) or (2) later than six years after the day on which he
  or she becomes aware of the false statement or misrepresentation or
  the false or misleading information.
