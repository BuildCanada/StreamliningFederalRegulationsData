## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-333790 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Industrial Design Act](/eng/acts/I-9).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-8, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-333795 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,

[[article]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[article]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any thing that is made by
    hand, tool or machine; ([objet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention of the Union
    of Paris made on March 20, 1883, including any amendments and
    revisions made from time to time to which Canada is a
    party; ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[country of the Union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[country of the Union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a country that is a member of the Union for the
      Protection of Industrial Property constituted under the
      Convention, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a member of the World Trade Organization as
      defined in subsection 2(1) of the [World Trade Organization
      Agreement Implementation Act](/eng/acts/W-11.8); ([pays de
      l'Union]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[design]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[design]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[industrial
    design]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means features of shape, configuration,
    pattern or ornament and any combination of those features that, in a
    finished article, appeal to and are judged solely by the
    eye; ([dessin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[kit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[kit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a complete or substantially
    complete number of parts that can be assembled to construct a
    finished article; ([prêt-à-monter]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means such member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada as is designated by the Governor in Council
    as the Minister for the purposes of this
    Act; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by the
    regulations and, in relation to fees, includes determined in the
    manner prescribed by the regulations; ([Version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[set]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[set]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a number of articles of the same
    general character ordinarily on sale together or intended to be used
    together, to each of which the same design or variants thereof are
    applied; ([ensemble]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[useful article]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[useful article]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an article that has a
    utilitarian function and includes a model of any such
    article; ([objet utilitaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[utilitarian function]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[utilitarian function]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
    article, means a function other than merely serving as a substrate
    or carrier for artistic or literary matter; ([fonction
    utilitaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[variants]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[variants]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means designs applied to the same
    article or set and not differing substantially from one
    another. ([variantes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-9, s. 2
- R.S., 1985, c. 10 (4th Supp.), s. 20
- 1993, c. 15, s. 12(E), c. 44, s. 161
- 2014, c. 39, s. 102
:::

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Industrial Designs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-333813 .Part}

### [Registration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-333814 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Register

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall cause to be kept a register called the Register of
  Industrial Designs, which shall contain the prescribed information and
  statements in respect of designs that are registered under this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Register of Industrial Designs is evidence of its
  contents, and a copy of an entry in the Register is evidence of the
  particulars of the entry if the copy is certified as a true copy by
  the Minister, by the Commissioner of Patents or by an officer, clerk
  or employee of the Commissioner's office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Admissibility

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A copy appearing to have been certified under
  subsection (2) is admissible in evidence in any court.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-9, s. 3
- 1992, c. 1, s. 79
- 2014, c. 39, s. 103
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obvious error

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may,
within six months after an entry is made in the Register of Industrial
Designs, correct any error in the entry that is obvious from the
documents relating to the registered design in question that are, at the
time that the entry is made, in the Minister's possession.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2015, c. 36, s. 44
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to register design

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  proprietor of a design, whether the first proprietor or a subsequent
  proprietor, may apply to register the design by paying the prescribed
  fees and filing with the Minister an application that contains

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the finished article in respect of
    which the design is to be registered;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a representation of the design that complies with
    any prescribed requirements; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any prescribed information or statement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Substituted applicants

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application shall, subject to any prescribed
  terms and conditions, be considered to have been filed by a person
  other than the person who filed it if, before the design is
  registered, it is established to the satisfaction of the Minister that
  the other person was the proprietor when the application was filed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing date

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The filing date of an application in Canada is the
  date on which the Minister receives the prescribed documents,
  information and statements or, if they are received on different
  dates, the latest of those dates.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-9, s. 4
- 1992, c. 1, s. 79

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal of application

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall refuse an application for the registration of a design
  and notify the applicant of the refusal if the Minister is satisfied
  that the design is not registrable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration of design

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is not so satisfied, the Minister
  shall register the design and notify the applicant of the
  registration.

::: HistoricalNote

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registrable design

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A design is registrable if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application is filed in accordance with this Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the design is novel, within the meaning of section
  8.2;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the design was created by the applicant or the
  applicant's predecessor in title;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the design does not consist only of features that are
  dictated solely by a utilitarian function of the finished article; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the design is not contrary to public morality or
  order.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-9, s. 7
- 1992, c. 1, ss. 81, 143(E)
- 1993, c. 15, s. 15
- 2014, c. 39, s. 105

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Priority date

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  priority date of a design in an application for the registration of a
  design (in this section and section 8.1 referred to as the "pending
  application") is the filing date of the application, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the pending application is filed by a person

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} who, on the filing date of the pending
      application, is a citizen or national of, or is domiciled in, a
      country of the Union or has a real and effective industrial or
      commercial establishment in a country of the Union, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} who has, or whose predecessor in title has,
      previously regularly filed an application for the registration of
      a design disclosing the same design in or for a country of the
      Union;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the filing date of the pending application is
    within six months after the filing date of the previously regularly
    filed application; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has made a request for priority in
    respect of the pending application on the basis of the previously
    regularly filed application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing date of previously regularly
  filed application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the circumstances set out in paragraphs (1)(a) to
  (c), the priority date of the design is the filing date of the
  previously regularly filed application.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-9, s. 8
- 1993, c. 15, s. 16
- 2014, c. 39, s. 105
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for priority

- **[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of section 8, an applicant for the registration of a
  design may submit to the Minister a request for priority in respect of
  the pending application on the basis of one or more previously
  regularly filed applications.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request for priority shall be made in accordance
  with the regulations, and the applicant shall submit to the Minister
  the filing date, the name of the country or office of filing and the
  number of each previously regularly filed application on which that
  request is based.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request deemed never filed

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A request for priority is deemed never to have been
  filed if the request is not made in accordance with the regulations or
  if the applicant does not submit the information, other than the
  number of each previously regularly filed application, that is
  required under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of request

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An applicant may, in accordance with the regulations,
  withdraw a request for priority, either entirely or with respect to
  one or more previously regularly filed applications.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Multiple previously regularly filed
  applications

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If more than one application has been previously
  regularly filed either in or for the same country or in or for
  different countries,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 8(1)(b) shall be applied using the
    earliest filing date of the previously regularly filed applications;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subsection 8(2) shall be applied using the earliest
    filing date of the previously regularly filed applications on which
    the request for priority is based.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Previously regularly filed application
  deemed never filed

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 8, a previously regularly
  filed application shall be deemed never to have been filed if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the filing date of the pending application, more
    than six months have elapsed since the filing date of the previously
    regularly filed application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} before the filing date of the pending application,
    another application for the registration of a design, disclosing the
    design in the pending application applied to the same finished
    article,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is filed by the person who filed the previously
      regularly filed application or by that person's successor in title
      or predecessor in title, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is filed in or for the country where the
      previously regularly filed application was filed; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the filing date of the other application
    referred to in paragraph (b) or, if there is more than one such
    other application, on the earliest of their filing dates, the
    previously regularly filed application

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has been withdrawn, abandoned or refused without
      having been made available to the public and without leaving any
      rights outstanding, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has not served as a basis for a request for
      priority in any country, including Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 105
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Novel design

- **[[8.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  design in an application for the registration of a design is novel if
  the same design, or a design not differing substantially from it,
  applied to a finished article that is the same as or analogous to the
  finished article in respect of which the design is to be registered,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has not been disclosed, more than 12 months before
    the priority date of the design in the application, in such a manner
    that it became available to the public in Canada or elsewhere, by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the person who filed the application,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that person's predecessor in title, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a person who obtained knowledge of the design
      in the application, directly or indirectly, from the person who
      filed the application or their predecessor in title;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has not been disclosed by any other person, before
    the priority date referred to in paragraph (a), in such a manner
    that it became available to the public in Canada or elsewhere; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to the regulations, has not been disclosed
    in an application filed in Canada for the registration of a design
    whose priority date is before the priority date referred to in
    paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application deemed never filed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(c), an application
  referred to in that paragraph is deemed never to have been filed if it
  is withdrawn before the earlier of the date on which it is made
  available to the public under section 8.3 and the date on which a
  design in it is registered.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 105
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application and documents made available
to public

- **[[8.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall make available to the public, on the prescribed date,
  an application for the registration of a design and all documents in
  the Minister's possession relating to the application and to the
  design's registration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-disclosure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except with the approval of the applicant or the
  registered proprietor, the Minister shall, before the prescribed date
  referred to in subsection (1), refuse to disclose the application for
  the registration of the design and any information or document
  relating to the application or to the design's registration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The prescribed date referred to in subsection (1) may
  not be later than the later of the date of registration of the design
  and 30 months after the filing date of the application for
  registration or, if a request for priority is made in respect of the
  application, the earliest filing date of a previously regularly filed
  application on which the request for priority is based.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of request

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a request for priority is withdrawn on or before
  the prescribed date, it shall, for the purposes of subsection (3) and
  to the extent that it is withdrawn, be deemed never to have been made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawn applications

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If an application for the registration of a design is
  withdrawn in accordance with the regulations on or before the
  prescribed date, the Minister shall not make the application and
  documents referred to in subsection (1) available to the public and
  shall refuse to disclose the application and documents, as well as any
  information relating to them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed date

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A prescribed date referred to in subsection (4)
  or (5) is to be no later than the prescribed date referred to in
  subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 105
- 2017, c. 26, s. 60(E)
:::

### [Exclusive Right]{.HTitleText2} {#h-333925 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusive right

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The registration of a
design, unless shown to be invalid, gives to the proprietor an exclusive
right in relation to the design.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-9, s. 9
- 2014, c. 39, s. 105
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of exclusive right

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the term
  limited for the duration of an exclusive right

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} begins on the later of the date of registration of
    the design and the prescribed date, referred to in subsection
    8.3(1), on which the application for the registration of the design
    is made available to the public; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ends on the later of the end of 10 years after the
    date of registration of the design and the end of 15 years after the
    filing date of the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintenance fees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The proprietor of a design shall, to maintain the
  exclusive right accorded by the registration of the design, pay to the
  Commissioner of Patents such fees, in respect of such periods, as may
  be prescribed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expiration of term

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the fees payable under subsection (2) are not
  paid within the time provided for by the regulations, the term limited
  for the duration of the exclusive right shall be deemed to have
  expired at the end of that time.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-9, s. 10
- 1993, c. 15, s. 17, c. 44, s. 163
- 2014, c. 39, s. 106
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-10-20021231.html "Link to previous version of section 10"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Using design without licence

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During
  the existence of an exclusive right, no person shall, without the
  licence of the proprietor of the design,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make, import for the purpose of trade or business,
    or sell, rent, or offer or expose for sale or rent, any article in
    respect of which the design is registered and to which the design or
    a design not differing substantially therefrom has been applied; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} do, in relation to a kit, anything specified in
    paragraph (a) that would constitute an infringement if done in
    relation to an article assembled from the kit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Substantial differences

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), in considering
  whether differences are substantial, the extent to which the
  registered design differs from any previously published design may be
  taken into account.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-9, s. 11
- 1993, c. 44, s. 164
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on protection

**[[11.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.1 .sectionLabel}** No protection
afforded by this Act shall extend to features applied to a useful
article that are dictated solely by a utilitarian function of the
article or to any method or principle of manufacture or construction.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 107
:::

### [Proprietorship]{.HTitleText2} {#h-333954 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First proprietor

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  author of a design is the first proprietor of the design, unless the
  author has executed the design for another person for a good and
  valuable consideration, in which case the other person is the first
  proprietor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acquired right

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The right of another person to the property shall
  only be co-extensive with the right that the other person has
  acquired.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-9, s. 12
- 1993, c. 15, s. 18
:::

### [Transfers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-333962 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Design transferable

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  design, whether registered or unregistered, is transferable in whole
  or in part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recording of transfer of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, subject to the regulations,
  record the transfer of an application for the registration of a design
  on the request of the applicant or, on receipt of evidence
  satisfactory to the Minister of the transfer, on the request of a
  transferee of the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration of transfer of design

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, subject to the regulations,
  register the transfer of any registered design on the request of the
  registered proprietor or, on receipt of evidence satisfactory to the
  Minister of the transfer, on the request of a transferee of the
  design.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer void

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A transfer of a registered design that has not been
  registered is void against a subsequent transferee if the transfer to
  the subsequent transferee has been registered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal of recording or registration

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall remove the recording or
  registration of the transfer of an application for the registration of
  a design or the transfer of a registered design on receipt of evidence
  satisfactory to the Minister that the transfer should not have been
  recorded or registered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is not authorized to remove the
  registration of a transfer of a registered design for the reason only
  that the transferor had previously transferred the registered design
  to another person.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-9, s. 13
- 1993, c. 15, s. 19
- 2014, c. 39, s. 108
:::

### [Action for Infringement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-333979 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action by proprietor or licensee

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  action for infringement of an exclusive right may be brought in any
  court of competent jurisdiction by the proprietor of the design or by
  an exclusive licensee of any right therein, subject to any agreement
  between the proprietor and the licensee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proprietor to be a party

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The proprietor of the design shall be or be made a
  party to any action for infringement of the exclusive right.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-9, s. 15
- 1993, c. 44, s. 166
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of court to grant relief

**[[15.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.1 .sectionLabel}** In any proceedings
under section 15, the court may make such orders as the circumstances
require, including orders for relief by way of injunction and the
recovery of damages or profits, for punitive damages, and for the
disposal of any infringing article or kit.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1993, c. 44, s. 166
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Concurrent jurisdiction

**[[15.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.2 .sectionLabel}** The Federal Court has
concurrent jurisdiction to hear and determine

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any action for the infringement of an exclusive
  right; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any question relating to the proprietorship of a
  design or any right in a design.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1993, c. 44, s. 166
:::

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, 1993, c. 44,
s. 167\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defence

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In any
  proceedings under section 15, a court shall not award a remedy, other
  than an injunction, if the defendant establishes that, at the time of
  the act that is the subject of the proceedings, the defendant was not
  aware, and had no reasonable grounds to suspect, that the design was
  registered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if the plaintiff
  establishes that the capital letter \"D\" in a circle and the name, or
  the usual abbreviation of the name, of the proprietor of the design
  were marked on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all, or substantially all, of the articles to which
    the registration pertains and that were distributed in Canada by or
    with the consent of the proprietor before the act complained of; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the labels or packaging associated with those
    articles.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proprietor

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), the proprietor is
  the proprietor at the time the articles, labels or packaging were
  marked.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-9, s. 17
- 1993, c. 15, s. 21, c. 44, s. 168
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** No remedy may be awarded
for an act of infringement committed more than three years before the
commencement of the action for infringement.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-9, s. 18
- 1993, c. 44, s. 169
:::

### [Extension of Time]{.HTitleText2} {#h-334016 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time period extended

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  time period fixed under this Act for doing anything ends on a
  prescribed day or a day that is designated by the Minister, that time
  period is extended to the next day that is not a prescribed day or a
  designated day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to designate day

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, on account of unforeseen

  public interest to do so, designate any day for the purposes of
  subsection (1). If a day is designated, the Minister shall inform the
  public of that fact on the website of the Canadian Intellectual
  Property Office.

### [Procedure as to Rectification and Alteration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-334024 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Federal Court may rectify entries

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Federal Court may, on the information of the Attorney General or at
  the suit of any person aggrieved by any omission without sufficient
  cause to make any entry in the Register of Industrial Designs, or by
  any entry made without sufficient cause in the Register, make such
  order for making, expunging or varying any entry in the Register as
  the Court thinks fit, or the Court may refuse the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In either case, the Federal Court may make such order
  with respect to the costs of the proceedings as the Court thinks fit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Questions to be decided

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Federal Court may in any proceedings under this
  section decide any question that may be necessary or expedient to
  decide for the rectification of the Register.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Jurisdiction

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Federal Court has exclusive jurisdiction to hear
  and determine proceedings under this section.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-8, s. 22
- R.S., c. 10(2nd Supp.), s. 64
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to alter design

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  registered proprietor of any registered industrial design may apply to
  the Federal Court for leave to add to or alter any industrial design
  in any particular not being an essential particular, and the Court may
  refuse or grant leave on such terms as it may think fit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notice of any intended application to the Federal
  Court under this section for leave to add to or alter any industrial
  design shall be given to the Minister, and the Minister is entitled to
  be heard on the application.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-8, s. 23
- R.S., c. 10(2nd Supp.), s. 64
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consequent rectification of register

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A certified copy of any
order of the Federal Court for the making, expunging or varying of any
entry in the Register of Industrial Designs, or for adding to or
altering any registered industrial design, shall be transmitted to the
Minister by an officer of the Registry of the Court, and the Register
shall thereupon be rectified or altered in conformity with the order, or
the purport of the order otherwise duly entered therein, as the case may
be.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. I-8, s. 24
- R.S., c. 10(2nd Supp.), s. 65
:::

### [Electronic Form and Means]{.HTitleText2} {#h-334047 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic form and means

- **[[24.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, any
  document, information or fee that is submitted to the Minister or the
  Commissioner of Patents under this Act may be submitted in any
  electronic form, and by any electronic means, that is specified by the
  Minister or the Commissioner of Patents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collection, storage, etc.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the regulations, the Minister and the
  Commissioner of Patents may use electronic means to create, collect,
  receive, store, transfer, distribute, publish, certify or otherwise
  deal with documents or information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[electronic]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[electronic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
  in reference to a form or means, includes optical, magnetic and other
  similar forms or means.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2014, c. 39, s. 110
:::

### [Regulations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-334057 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council
may make regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} governing titles of designs;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the form and contents of applications for
  the registration of designs, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the manner of naming finished articles,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the manner of identifying features of shape,
    configuration, pattern or ornament of all or part of a finished
    article, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the manner of identifying that an application
    relates to only some of the features of shape, configuration,
    pattern or ornament that, in a finished article, appeal to and are
    judged solely by the eye, or to only some or all of those features
    of a part of a finished article;

- [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} respecting the processing and examination of
  applications for the registration of designs, including the
  circumstances in which applications shall be deemed to be abandoned
  and the circumstances in which they shall be reinstated;

- [(b.2)]{.lawlabel} respecting the circumstances in which paragraph
  8.2(1)(c) does not apply in respect of a design that has been
  disclosed in an application for the registration of a design that was
  filed in Canada by a person referred to in subparagraph 8.2(1)(a)(i)
  or (ii);

- [(b.3)]{.lawlabel} respecting the withdrawal of an application for the
  registration of a design and, for the purposes of subsections 8.3(4)
  and (5), prescribing the date, or the manner of determining the date,
  on or before which a request for priority or an application for the
  registration of a design shall be withdrawn;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the payment of fees and the amount of
  those fees;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} respecting the return of any fees paid under this
  Act;

- [(d.1)]{.lawlabel} authorizing the Minister to waive, subject to any
  prescribed terms and conditions, the payment of a fee if the Minister
  is satisfied that the circumstances justify it;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} respecting the registration of sets and of variants
  of a design;

- [(e.1)]{.lawlabel} respecting the correction of obvious errors in
  documents submitted to the Minister or the Commissioner of Patents,
  including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the determination of what constitutes an obvious
    error, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the effect of the correction;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} respecting requests for priority, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the period within which priority shall be
    requested,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the information and documentation that shall be
    submitted in support of requests for priority,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the period within which that information and
    documentation shall be submitted,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the withdrawal of requests for priority, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the correction of requests for priority or of
    information or documentation submitted in support of them and the
    effect of corrections on the application of section 8.3;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} respecting certificates of registration;

- [(g.1)]{.lawlabel} respecting the recording of documents relating to a
  design;

- [(g.2)]{.lawlabel} respecting the recording or registration of
  transfers of applications for the registration of designs or transfers
  of registered designs;

- [(g.3)]{.lawlabel} respecting the provision, including in electronic
  form and by electronic means, of documents and information to the
  Minister or the Commissioner of Patents, including the time at which
  they are deemed to be received by the Minister or the Commissioner of
  Patents;

- [(g.4)]{.lawlabel} respecting the use of electronic means for the
  purposes of subsection 24.1(2);

- [(g.5)]{.lawlabel} respecting communications between the Minister or
  the Commissioner of Patents and any other person;

- [(g.6)]{.lawlabel} for carrying into effect, despite anything in this
  Act, the Geneva (1999) Act of the Hague Agreement Concerning the
  International Registration of Industrial Designs, adopted at Geneva on
  July 2, 1999, including any amendments and revisions made from time to
  time to which Canada is a party; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} prescribing anything else that is to be prescribed
  under this Act and generally for carrying out the purposes and
  provisions of this Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. I-9, s. 25
- 1993, c. 15, s. 23, c. 44, s. 170
- 2014, c. 39, s. 111
- 2015, c. 36, s. 47
- 2017, c. 26, s. 61(E)
:::
