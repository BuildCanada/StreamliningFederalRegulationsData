## Pest Control Products Act {#pest-control-products-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 2002, c. 28
:::

Assented to 2002-12-12

An Act to protect human health and safety and the environment by
regulating products used for the control of pests
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
WHEREAS the availability and use of pest control products pose potential
risks, both directly and indirectly, to the health, safety and
well-being of individuals in Canada and to the environment;

WHEREAS pest management plays a significant role in diverse areas of the
economy and other aspects of the quality of life throughout Canada;

WHEREAS pest control products of acceptable risk and value can
contribute significantly to the attainment of the goals of sustainable
pest management;

WHEREAS the goals of sustainable pest management are to meet society's
needs for human health protection, food and fibre production and
resource utilization and to conserve or enhance natural resources and
the quality of the environment for future generations, in an
economically viable manner;

WHEREAS Canada and the provinces and territories have traditionally
administered complementary regulatory systems designed to protect
individuals and the environment, including its biological diversity,
from unacceptable risks posed by pest control products, and it is
important that such an approach be continued in order to achieve
mutually desired results efficiently, without regulatory conflict or
duplication;

WHEREAS it is in the national interest that the primary objective of the
federal regulatory system be to prevent unacceptable risks to
individuals and the environment from the use of pest control products,

the attainment of the objectives of the federal regulatory system
continue to be pursued through a scientifically-based national
registration system that addresses risks to human health and the
environment both before and after registration and applies to the
regulation of pest control products throughout Canada,

pest control products of acceptable risk be registered for use only if
it is shown that their use would be efficacious and if conditions of
registration can be established to prevent adverse health impact or
pollution of the environment,

in assessing risks to individuals, consideration be given to aggregate
exposure to pest control products, cumulative effects of pest control
products and the different sensitivities to pest control products of
major identifiable subgroups, including pregnant women, infants,
children, women and seniors,

pest control products be regulated in a manner that supports sustainable
development, being development that meets the needs of the present
without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own
needs,

the federal regulatory system be designed to minimize health and
environmental risks posed by pest control products and to encourage the
development and implementation of innovative, sustainable pest
management strategies, for example by facilitating access to pest
control products that pose lower risks, and encouraging the development
and use of alternative, non-toxic, ecological pest control approaches,
strategies and products,

applicable policies of the Government of Canada that are consistent with
the objectives of this Act be duly reflected in decisions respecting the
regulation of pest control products,

there be cooperation among federal departments in the development of
policies to pursue the attainment of the objectives of this Act, and
that those policies take into account advice from diverse sources
throughout the country,

the provinces and territories and those persons whose interests and
concerns are affected by the federal regulatory system be accorded a
reasonable opportunity to parti­cipate in the regulatory system in ways
that are consistent with the attainment of its objectives, and

the federal regulatory system be administered efficiently and
effectively in accordance with the foregoing principles and objectives
and in a manner that recognizes the various interests and concerns
affected and, where consistent with the primary objective of the system,
minimizes the negative impact on economic viability and competitiveness;

AND WHEREAS Canada must be able to fulfil its international obligations
in relation to pest management;
:::

NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-418017 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Pest Control Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-418020 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in this Act.

  [[active ingredient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[active ingredient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a component of a
      pest control product to which the intended effects of the product
      are attributed and includes a synergist but does not include a
      solvent, diluent, emulsifier or other component that is not
      primarily responsible for those effects. ([principe
      actif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[advertise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[advertise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes making a representation

      product. ([publicité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  :   [[biological diversity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the variability

      they form a part and includes the diversity within and between

      biologique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  :   [[biotechnology]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the application of
      science and engineering in the direct or indirect use of living
      organisms or parts or products of living organisms in their

      lang="fr"})

  [[conditions of registration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[conditions of registration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any conditions specified by the Minister under

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other requirements stated by this Act or

        d'homologation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[confidential business information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[confidential business information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      information to which access may be refused under the [Access to
      Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1) and that meets the requirements of
      subsection 43(4) or (5). ([renseignements commerciaux
      confidentiels]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[confidential test data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[confidential test data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means test data to
      which access may be refused under the [Access to Information
      Act](/eng/acts/A-1). ([données d'essai
      confidentielles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[distribute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[distribute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means distribute in any way,

      sale or distribution, and expose, display or advertise for sale or
      distribution. ([distribution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means anything on which
      information that is capable of being understood by a person, or
      read by a computer or other device, is recorded or
      marked. ([document]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[ecosystem]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[ecosystem]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a dynamic complex of
      plant, animal and micro-organism communities and their non-living
      environment interacting as a functional
      unit. ([écosystème]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[environment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[environment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the components of the
      Earth and includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} air, land and water;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all layers of the atmosphere;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all organic and inorganic matter and living
        organisms; and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the interacting natural systems that include

        (c). ([environnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[environmental risk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[environmental risk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a pest
      control product, means the possibility of harm to the environment,

      use of the product, taking into account its conditions or proposed

      environnemental]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[formulant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[formulant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any component of a pest

  [[government policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} efficacy;

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acceptable risks

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Act, the health or
  environmental risks of a pest control product are acceptable if there
  is reasonable certainty that no harm to human health, future
  generations or the environment will result from exposure to or use of
  the product, taking into account its conditions or proposed conditions
  of registration.

## [Mandate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-418081 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Primary objective

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  administration of this Act, the Minister's primary objective is to
  prevent unacceptable risks to individuals and the environment from the
  use of pest control products.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ancillary objectives

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Consistent with, and in furtherance of, the primary
  objective, the Minister shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} support sustainable development designed to enable
    the needs of the present to be met without compromising the ability
    of future generations to meet their own needs;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} seek to minimize health and environmental risks
    posed by pest control products and encourage the development and
    implementation of innovative, sustainable pest management strategies
    by facilitating access to pest control products that pose lower
    risks and by other appropriate measures;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} encourage public awareness in relation to pest
    control products by informing the public, facilitating public access
    to relevant information and public participation in the
    decision-making process; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} ensure that only those pest control products that
    are determined to be of acceptable value are approved for use in
    Canada.

## [Advisory Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-418095 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  carrying out duties under this Act, the Minister may establish an
  advisory council of persons whose interests and concerns are affected
  by this Act, and may specify the functions of the council and the
  means by which it is to perform those functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The advisory council shall give any report that it
  makes, including its recommendations and the reasons for them, to the
  Minister, who shall place it in the Register.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-418101 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unregistered pest control products

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall manufacture, possess, handle, store, transport, import,
  distribute or use a pest control product that is not registered under
  this Act, except as otherwise authorized under subsection 21(5) or
  41(1), section 48 or 51, any of sections 53 to 59 or the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of registration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise authorized under section 53.3 or
  54, no person shall manufacture, import, export or distribute a
  registered pest control product unless it conforms to the conditions
  of registration respecting its composition and the person complies
  with the other conditions of registration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Packaging and labelling

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise authorized under section 53, 53.3
  or 54, no person shall store, import, export or distribute a pest
  control product that is not packaged and labelled in accordance with
  the regulations and, if it is registered, the conditions of
  registration.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2016, c. 9, s. 35\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Misuse of pest control products

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} No person shall handle, store, transport, use or
  dispose of a pest control product in a way that is inconsistent with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the regulations; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the product is registered, the directions on the
    label recorded in the Register, subject to the regulations.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2016, c. 9, s. 35\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Packaging, labelling and advertisement

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} No person shall package, label or advertise a pest
  control product in a way that is false, misleading or likely to create
  an erroneous impression regarding its character, value, quantity,
  composition, safety or registration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activities that endanger health, etc.

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} No person shall manufacture, possess, handle, store,
  transport, distribute, use or dispose of a pest control product in a
  way that endangers human health or safety or the environment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alteration, destruction or
  falsification --- required documents

  [(8.1)]{.lawlabel} No person shall alter, destroy or falsify a
  document that they are required under this Act to keep, maintain or
  provide.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alteration, possession or
  use --- official documents

  [(8.2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for a person to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} alter a document issued or made --- or in any
    manner given --- under this Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have in their possession, or use, a document issued
    or made --- or in any manner given --- under this Act that has been
    altered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Possession or use of documents that
  resemble official documents

  [(8.3)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for a person to have in their
  possession, or use, any document that has not been issued or
  made --- or in any manner given --- under this Act if the document so
  resembles a document issued or made --- or in any manner
  given --- under this Act that it is likely to be mistaken for such a
  document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} A person who contravenes any provision of this
  section is guilty of an offence and liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine of not more than
    \$200,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than six months,
    or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine of not more
    than \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than three
    years, or to both.

## [Registration of Pest Control Products]{.HTitleText1} {#h-418133 .Part}

### [Applications for Registration or Amendment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-418134 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to Minister

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application to register a pest control product or to amend the
  product's registration must be made to the Minister in the form and
  manner directed by the Minister and must include any information or
  other thing that is required by the regulations to accompany the
  application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of information provided by
  registrants

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines that the active ingredient
  of the applicant's pest control product is equivalent to the active
  ingredient of a registered pest control product, the Minister shall,
  subject to and in accordance with the regulations, permit the
  applicant to use or rely on any information referred to in
  subsection (1) that has been provided by any registrant if the
  Minister is satisfied that the information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is relevant to the registered pest control product
    that contains the equivalent active ingredient; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is necessary to support the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foreign review or evaluation

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the applicant
  may include information that is available from a review or evaluation
  of a pest control product conducted by the government of another
  member country of the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and
  Development if the proposed use of the pest control product in Canada
  would be under conditions similar to those under which the foreign
  review or evaluation was conducted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evaluation of pest control product

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister is satisfied that the application has
  been made in accordance with subsection (1), (2) or (2.1), the
  Minister shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with the regulations, if any, conduct
    any evaluations that the Minister considers necessary with respect
    to the health or environmental risks or the value of the pest
    control product;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} expedite evaluations with respect to a pest control
    product that may reasonably be expected to pose lower health or
    environmental risks; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} carry out any consultation required by section 28.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, by delivering a notice in writing,
  request an applicant to provide the Minister with other information in
  support of the application within the time and in the form specified
  in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Denial of application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall deny an application if the
  applicant does not comply with a notice under subsection (4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Burden of persuasion and consideration
  of information

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} During an evaluation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has the burden of persuading the
    Minister that the health and environmental risks and the value of
    the pest control product are acceptable; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall consider the information
    provided by the applicant in support of the application and may
    consider additional information, but the Minister shall give the
    applicant a reasonable opportunity to make representations in
    respect of the additional information before completing the
    evaluation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scientific approach

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In evaluating the health and environmental risks of a
  pest control product and in determining whether those risks are
  acceptable, the Minister shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} apply a scientifically based approach; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to health risks, if a decision referred
    to in paragraph 28(1)(a) or (b) is being made or has been made in
    relation to a pest control product,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} among other relevant factors, consider available
      information on aggregate exposure to the pest control product,
      namely dietary exposure and exposure from other non-occupational
      sources, including drinking water and use in and around homes and
      schools, and cumulative effects of the pest control product and
      other pest control products that have a common mechanism of
      toxicity,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} apply appropriate margins of safety to take into
      account, among other relevant factors, the use of animal
      experimentation data and the different sensitivities to pest
      control products of major identifiable subgroups, including
      pregnant women, infants, children, women and seniors, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a threshold effect, if the
      product is proposed for use in or around homes or schools, apply a
      margin of safety that is ten times greater than the margin of
      safety that would otherwise be applicable under subparagraph (ii)
      in respect of that threshold effect, to take into account
      potential pre- and post-natal toxicity and completeness of the
      data with respect to the exposure of, and toxicity to, infants and
      children unless, on the basis of reliable scientific data, the
      Minister has determined that a different margin of safety would be
      appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Government policy to be given effect in
  evaluation

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} In evaluating the health and environmental risks and
  the value of a pest control product, the Minister shall give effect to
  government policy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Comparative risk and value assessment

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} In determining whether the health and environmental

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (9), the Minister

  information referred to in that subsection.

### [Re-evaluation and Special Review]{.HTitleText2} {#h-418226 .Subheading}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may delay the effective date of the
  amendment or cancellation if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no suitable alternative to the use of the pest

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister considers that the health and

    the effective date of the amendment or cancellation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions on delay

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A delay is subject to any conditions that the
  Minister considers necessary for carrying out the purposes of this
  Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continued possession, etc., of existing
  stocks

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} When cancelling the registration of a pest control
  product under this section or any other provision of this Act, the
  Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} allow the continued possession, handling, storage,
    distribution and use of stocks of the product in Canada at the time
    of cancellation, subject to any conditions, including disposal
    procedures, that the Minister considers necessary for carrying out
    the purposes of this Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require the registrant to recall and dispose of the
    product in a manner specified by the Minister; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} seize and dispose of the product.

  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons for discontinuation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may deliver a notice in writing to the

  discontinuation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation or amendment of
  registration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of notification under subsection (1), the
  Minister shall cancel or amend the registration, as the case may be,
  as of a date to be determined by the Minister and, pending that date,

  carrying out the purposes of this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-payment of fees, fines, etc.

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violation or offence

  who committed the violation or offence is the registrant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cancel or amend the registration of any other pest
  control product in respect of which the person is the registrant; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} refuse to consider any application made under this

  appropriate.

### [Offences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-418372 .Subheading}

  information about such a test.

  than \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than three
  years, or to both.

      data, or make the test data available to others, in order to
      register a pest control product in Canada or elsewhere or to amend
      a registration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right to inspection

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall permit the person to inspect
  confidential test data in the Register if the Minister is satisfied
  that the person does not intend to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} use the test data in order to register a pest
    control product in Canada or elsewhere, or to amend a registration;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make the test data available to others for the
    purpose of registering a pest control product in Canada or
    elsewhere, or of amending a registration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to registrant

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister permits a person to inspect
  confidential test data in the Register, the Minister shall make a
  reasonable effort to immediately notify any registrant who provided
  the data that the Minister has permitted a person to inspect the data.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Denial of application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall deny an application if the
  Minister is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant intends to use the test data for a
    purpose referred to in subsection (2); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has used test data obtained from a
    prior inspection for a purpose referred to in subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidential business information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (5) and (6), confidential
  business information is information provided under this Act that is
  designated as confidential business information by the person who
  provided it, or information provided under the [Pest Control Products
  Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01), chapter P-9 of the Revised Statutes of Canada,
  1985, and that concerns

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} manufacturing or quality control processes relating
    to a pest control product;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} methods for determining the composition of a pest
    control product; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the monetary value of sales of pest control
    products provided to the Minister pursuant to subsection 8(5) and
    other financial or commercial information provided to the Minister
    pursuant to this Act or the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Formulants and contaminants

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise excluded by the regulations, if any,
  made under paragraph 67(1)(n), confidential business information also
  includes information that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is provided under this Act and is designated as
    confidential business information by the person who provided it or
    was provided under the [Pest Control Products
    Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01), chapter P-9 of the Revised Statutes of
    Canada, 1985; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contains the identity and concentration of the
    formulants and contaminants in a pest control product, other than
    those that the Minister considers to be of health or environmental
    concern and are identified on a list to be established and
    maintained by the Minister and made available to the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation not satisfactory

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister decides that information designated
  under subsection (4) or (5) does not meet the requirements of that
  subsection, the information is not confidential business information
  for the purposes of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister decides that designated information
  is not confidential business information, the Minister shall give
  written notice to the person who provided the information of the
  decision and the reasons for it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this Act shall be construed as

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} preventing the Minister from refusing to disclose
    confidential test data or confidential business information under
    the [Access to Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entitling a person to make or obtain a copy of
    confidential test data, other than confidential test data to which
    the public has access

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in documents referred to in subsections 28(6),
      39(2) and 42(3), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} under the authority of the regulations made
      under paragraph 67(1)(m).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} Every person who makes a false statement in an
  affidavit or a statutory declaration referred to in subsection (1) is
  guilty of an offence and liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine of not more than
    \$200,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than six months,
    or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine of not more
    than \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than three
    years, or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permitted disclosure

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, in accordance with the regulations, if any, disclose
  confidential test data or confidential business information that has

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person who provides services to Her Majesty in
    right of Canada for the purpose of protecting human health or safety

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an international organization or the government of

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a medical professional who requests the information
    for the purpose of making a medical diagnosis or giving medical

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a department or an agency of the federal or a
    provincial government that requests the information in order to
    respond to a situation that endangers human health or safety or the
    environment;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a review panel established by the Minister under
    subsection 35(3); or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other person or body, including an advisory
    council established under subsection 5(1), from whom the Minister
    requests advice for the purposes of this Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection of disclosed information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before disclosing information under paragraph (1)(b),

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition against disclosure

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition against use

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine of not more

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security measures

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every person who obtains information under

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

### [Inspections]{.HTitleText2} {#h-418647 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of inspectors

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For a
  purpose related to verifying compliance or preventing non-compliance
  with the provisions of this Act and the regulations, an inspector may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 49, at any reasonable time,
    enter and inspect any place, or stop any means of transport, in
    which the inspector believes on reasonable grounds there is a pest
    control product or other thing to which this Act or the regulations
    apply;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} open and examine any receptacle, package or other
    thing that the inspector believes on reasonable grounds contains a
    pest control product or other thing to which this Act or the
    regulations apply and take samples from it;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} order any person to present any pest control
    product or other thing for inspection in any manner and under any
    conditions that the inspector considers necessary to conduct an
    inspection;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} order the owner or person having possession, care
    or control of a means of transport that the inspector intends to
    enter, to move it to a place where the inspector can enter it;

  - [(d.1)]{.lawlabel} examine or test, or take samples of, anything
    that is in the place being inspected;

  - [(d.2)]{.lawlabel} examine a document that is in the place being
    inspected, make copies of it or take extracts from it;

  - [(d.3)]{.lawlabel} order the owner or person having possession, care
    or control of a pest control product or other thing to which this
    Act or the regulations apply that is in the place being inspected to
    move it or, for any time that may be necessary, not to move it or to
    restrict its movement;

  - [(d.4)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used a computer or other
    device that is in the place being inspected to examine data that is
    contained in or available to a computer system or reproduce it or
    cause it to be reproduced in the form of a printout or other
    intelligible output and remove the output for examination or
    copying;

  - [(d.5)]{.lawlabel} use or cause to be used copying equipment that is
    in the place being inspected and remove the copies for examination;

  - [(d.6)]{.lawlabel} take photographs or make recordings or sketches;

  - [(d.7)]{.lawlabel} order any person in the place being inspected to
    establish their identity to the inspector's satisfaction;

  - [(d.8)]{.lawlabel} prohibit or limit access to all or part of the
    place being inspected or to anything that is in the place;

  - [(d.9)]{.lawlabel} remove anything from the place being inspected
    for the purpose of examination, conducting tests or taking samples;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} conduct any tests or analyses or take any
    measurements.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons accompanying inspector

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An inspector may be accompanied by any person that

  perform their duties or functions under this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every person who fails to do anything the person was

### [Removal, Forfeiture or Destruction of Unlawful Imports]{.HTitleText2} {#h-418753 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unlawful imports

- **[[53.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53.3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector who has reasonable grounds to believe that an imported pest
  control product does not meet the requirements of the regulations or
  was imported in contravention of a provision of this Act or the
  regulations may decide whether to give the owner or importer, or the
  person having possession, care or control of the product, the
  opportunity to take a measure in respect of it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In making a decision under subsection (1), the
  inspector shall consider, among other factors

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the pest control product endangers human
    health or safety or the environment; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other prescribed factors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of inspector

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the inspector decides under subsection (1) not to
  give the owner or importer or the person having possession, care or
  control of the pest control product the opportunity to take a measure
  in respect of it, the inspector shall exercise, in respect of the
  product, any of the powers conferred by the provisions of this Act,
  other than this section, or of the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures that may be taken and notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} However, if the inspector decides under
  subsection (1) to give the owner or importer, or the person having
  possession, care or control of the pest control product the
  opportunity to take a measure in respect of it, the inspector, or any
  other inspector who is informed of the decision, shall decide whether
  the owner or importer, or the person having possession, care or
  control of it may remove it from Canada at their expense, consent to
  its forfeiture or take either of these measures, and shall notify or
  cause to be notified the owner or importer, or the person having
  possession, care or control of the product that they may take that
  measure within the period specified by the inspector or other
  inspector, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a person is notified under subsection (4) that
  they may consent to the forfeiture of the pest control product and the
  person consents to its forfeiture, the product is forfeited to Her
  Majesty in right of Canada and may be disposed of, as the Minister may
  direct, at the person's expense.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2016, c. 9, s. 52
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal or destruction of unlawful
imports

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector who has reasonable grounds to believe that an imported pest
  control product does not meet the requirements of the regulations or
  was imported in contravention of a provision of this Act or the
  regulations may, by written notice, whether the pest control product
  is seized or not, order its owner or importer, or the person having
  possession, care or control of it, to remove it from Canada at their
  expense or, if removal is not possible, to destroy it at their
  expense.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeiture

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the pest control product is not removed from
  Canada, or destroyed, within the period specified in the
  notice --- or, if no period is specified, within 90 days after the day
  on which the notice was delivered --- it is, despite section 53.1,
  forfeited to Her Majesty in right of Canada and may be disposed of, as
  the Minister may direct, at the expense of the person to whom the
  notice was delivered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of application of subsection
  (2)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An inspector may, for the period specified by the
  inspector, suspend the application of subsection (2) if the inspector
  is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the pest control product does not endanger human
    health or safety or the environment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the pest control product will not be sold within
    that period;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the measures that should have been taken for the
    pest control product not to have been imported in contravention of a
    provision of this Act or the regulations will be taken within that
    period; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the pest control product does not meet the
    requirements of the regulations, it will be brought into compliance
    with those requirements within that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An inspector may cancel the notice if the inspector
  is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the pest control product does not endanger human
    health or safety or the environment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the pest control product has not been sold within
    the period referred to in subsection (5);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the measures referred to in paragraph (3)(c) were
    taken within that period; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the pest control product did not meet the
    requirements of the regulations when it was imported, it was brought
    into compliance with those requirements within that period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The period for the purposes of subsection (4) is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the application of subsection (2) was suspended
    under subsection (3), the period of the suspension; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the application of subsection (2) was not
    suspended, the period specified in the notice or, if no period was
    specified, the period of 90 days after the day on which the notice
    was delivered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every person who fails to comply with an order in the
  notice is guilty of an offence and liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine of not more than
    \$200,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than six months,
    or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine of not more
    than \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than three
    years, or to both.

- **[[54.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

### [Compliance Measures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-418837 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspector may order measures

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  inspector has reasonable grounds to believe that a person has
  contravened this Act or the regulations, he or she may order the
  person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to stop or shut down any activity or thing involved
    in the contravention; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to take any other measures that the inspector
    considers necessary to prevent further contravention, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} modifying a pest control product or its labelling
      or disposing of the product so as to comply with this Act and the
      regulations, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} manufacturing, handling, storing, transporting,
      importing, exporting, packaging, labelling, distributing or using
      a registered pest control product in accordance with the
      conditions of registration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An order under subsection (1) may apply for a
  specified period or until the inspector is satisfied that no further
  contravention is likely to take place.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An order under subsection (1) shall be communicated
  by delivering a written notice to the registrant or, as the case may
  be, to the owner or person having possession, care or control of the
  pest control product, activity or thing that was involved in the
  contravention and the notice must be accompanied by a statement of the
  reasons for the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence and punishment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every person who fails to comply with an order in a
  notice delivered under subsection (3) is guilty of an offence and
  liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine of not more than
    \$200,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than six months,
    or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine of not more
    than \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than three
    years, or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prosecutions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An order under subsection (1) may be given whether or
  not the person has been charged with an offence relating to the
  contravention, but if the person is charged, the order may be
  confirmed, varied or rescinded by the court that tries the offence.

### [Review of Inspectors' Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-418878 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for review

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section, an order in
  respect of which notice has been delivered under subsection 53(2),
  57(3) or 59(3) shall be reviewed on the written request of the person
  to whom the notice was delivered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of and time for making request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request under subsection (1) shall state the
  grounds for the request and the decision that is requested and must be
  delivered to the Minister within 10 days after the date on which the
  notice referred to in that subsection was delivered to the person who
  made the request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reviewer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A review requested under subsection (1) may be
  conducted by the inspector who delivered the notice or by any other
  inspector or official to whom the review is assigned.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal when grounds already considered

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A review requested under subsection (1) may be
  refused if the request does not comply with subsection (2) or if the
  grounds stated in the request were presented to and considered by the
  inspector before the notice was delivered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal in case of emergency

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the reasons in a notice referred to in
  subsection (1) include the existence of an emergency concerning risks
  to human health or safety or the environment, the reviewer may refuse
  to undertake the review until he or she is satisfied that there has
  been sufficient compliance with the order in the notice to address the
  emergency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal not affected by inspector's
  prior knowledge of emergency

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (5), the reviewer may
  refuse to undertake the review no matter how long before delivery of
  the notice the inspector had known of the circumstances concerning the
  emergency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons for refusal

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A refusal under subsection (4) or (5) shall be
  communicated in writing to the person who made the request, with
  reasons for the refusal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review initiated by inspector

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} An inspector who delivers a notice referred to in
  subsection (1), or any other inspector or official assigned to do so,
  may review the order included in the notice regardless of whether a
  request has been made under that subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct of review

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} A review referred to in subsection (1) or (8) shall
  be conducted in accordance with the regulations, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision on completion of review

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} On completion of a review, the reviewer shall make a
  decision to confirm, amend, terminate or cancel the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} Written notice of the reviewer's decision under
  subsection (10), with reasons, shall be delivered to the person who
  made the request or, if there was no request, to the person to whom
  the notice referred to in subsection (1) was, or could have been,
  delivered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delay in proceedings under section 61

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} An application shall not be made under section 61 in
  relation to an order until

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the expiry of the 10-day period referred to in
    subsection (2) without a review having been requested under
    subsection (1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a request for a review has been refused under
    subsection (4) or (5); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a review that has been requested under
    subsection (1) and has not been refused under subsection (4) or (5),
    or a review that has been undertaken under subsection (8), has been
    completed and action has been taken under subsection (10).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of amendment

  [(13)]{.lawlabel} When the decision under subsection (10) is to amend
  an order, the notice of that decision under subsection (11) is deemed,
  for the purpose of section 61, to have been delivered under subsection
  53(2), 57(3) or 59(3), as the case may be, and the amended order is
  subject to review under this section.

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may make an interim

  regulation made under this Act if the Minister believes that immediate
  action is required to deal with a significant risk, direct or
  indirect, to health, safety or the environment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of effect

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An interim order has effect from the time that it is
  made but ceases to have effect on the earliest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 14 days after it is made, unless it is approved by
    the Governor in Council,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which it is repealed,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which a regulation made under this Act,
    that has the same effect as the interim order, comes into force, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} one year after the interim order is made or any
    shorter period that may be specified in the interim order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of unpublished order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall be convicted of an offence consisting
  of a contravention of an interim order that, at the time of the
  alleged contravention, had not been published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) unless it is proved that, at the
  time of the alleged contravention, the person had been notified of the
  interim order or reasonable steps had been taken to bring the purport
  of the interim order to the notice of those persons likely to be
  affected by it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption form [Statutory Instruments

    Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) within 23 days after it is made.

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-419055 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-419056 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention causing risk or harm

- **[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person is guilty of an offence if, in contravening this Act or the
  regulations, they cause

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a risk of imminent death or serious bodily harm to
    another person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a risk of substantial harm to the environment; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} harm to the environment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who commits an offence under
  subsection (1) is liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine of not more than
    \$200,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than six months,
    or to both; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine of not more
    than \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than three
    years, or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence committed wilfully or
  recklessly

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person is guilty of an offence if, while
  contravening this Act or the regulations, they wilfully or recklessly
  cause

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a risk of imminent death or serious bodily harm to
    another person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a risk of substantial harm to the environment; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} harm to the environment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every person who commits an offence under
  subsection (3) is liable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine of not more than
    \$300,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than six months,
    or to both; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine of not more
    than \$1,000,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than
    three years, or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contravention of regulations

**[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes a provision of the regulations is guilty of an offence and
liable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on summary conviction, to a fine of not more than
  \$200,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than six months,
  or to both; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on conviction on indictment, to a fine of not more
  than \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term of not more than three
  years, or to both.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Due diligence

**[[69.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69.1 .sectionLabel}** A person is not to be
found guilty of an offence under this Act --- other than an offence
under section 30 or subsection 33(8), 40(1) or 44(7), an offence under
subsection 47(4) as it relates to a contravention of subsection 47(3) or
an offence under subsection 68(3) or 70(3) --- if they establish that
they exercised all due diligence to prevent the commission of the
offence.

    Control Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01), chapter P-9 of the Revised
    Statutes of Canada, 1985; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a report on significant scientific developments
    relating to the evaluation of the health and environmental risks and
    the value of pest control products and the integration of those
    developments into decision-making under this Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permanent review of Act

- **[[80.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The administration of this Act
  shall, every 7 years after the day on which section 1 comes into
  force, stand referred to such committee of the House of Commons, of
  the Senate or of both Houses of Parliament as may be designated or
  established for that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review and report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The committee designated or established for the
  purposes of subsection (1) shall, as soon as practicable, undertake a
  comprehensive review of the provisions and operation of this Act and
  shall, within one year after the review is undertaken or within such
  further time as the House of Commons, the Senate or both Houses of
  Parliament, as the case may be, may authorize, submit a report
  thereon, including a statement of any changes to this Act or its
  administration that the committee would recommend.
