## National Day of Remembrance Act {#national-day-of-remembrance-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1991, c. 36
:::

Assented to 1991-10-29

An Act respecting a national day of remembrance and action on violence
against women
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
WHEREAS on December 6, 1989, fourteen women died as a result of a
massacre at the University of Montreal;

AND WHEREAS it is important to denounce violence against women;

AND WHEREAS the Canadian people wish to reflect on the event in the hope
of preventing further violence against women;
:::

::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374207 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [National Day of Remembrance Act](/eng/acts/N-4.6).

## [National Day of Remembrance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-374210 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National Day of Remembrance

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Throughout Canada, in each
and every year, the 6th day of December shall be known under the name of
\"National Day of Remembrance and Action on Violence Against Women\".
