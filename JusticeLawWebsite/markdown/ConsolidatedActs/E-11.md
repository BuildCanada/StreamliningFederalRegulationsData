## Canadian Environment Week Act {#canadian-environment-week-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. E-11
:::

An Act respecting Canadian Environment Week
::::

::::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canadian Environment Week Act](/eng/acts/E-11).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1970-71-72, c. 28, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian Environment Week

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Throughout Canada, in every
year, the week that includes June 5 shall be kept and observed under the
name of Canadian Environment Week.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., 1985, c. E-11, s. 2
- R.S., 1985, c. 10 (1st Supp.), s. 2
:::
