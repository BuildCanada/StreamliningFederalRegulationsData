## Gwich'in Land Claim Settlement Act {#gwichin-land-claim-settlement-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1992, c. 53
:::

Assented to 1992-12-17

An Act to approve, give effect to and declare valid the Agreement
between Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada and the Gwich'in, as
represented by the Gwich'in Tribal Council, and to amend an Act in
consequence thereof
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
###### Preamble {#preamble .MarginalNote}

WHEREAS the Gwich\'in, from time immemorial, have traditionally used and
occupied lands in Yukon and the Northwest Territories;

WHEREAS the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/) recognizes and affirms
the existing aboriginal and treaty rights of the aboriginal peoples of
Canada;

WHEREAS Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada and the Gwich'in, as
represented by the Gwich'in Tribal Council, have negotiated in order to
achieve certainty and clarity of rights with respect to ownership and
use of land and resources;

WHEREAS Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada and the Gwich'in, as
represented by the Gwich'in Tribal Council, on April 22, 1992, entered
into a comprehensive land claims agreement that, in exchange for the
release by the Gwich'in of certain rights and claims as set out in the
Agreement, defines certain rights that the Gwich'in shall have, and
confirms the treaty rights of the Gwich'in that are unaffected by that
release;

WHEREAS the Agreement further provides that the Agreement will be a land
claims agreement referred to in section 35 of the [Constitution Act,
1982](/eng/Const/) and that approval by Parliament is a condition
precedent to the validity of the Agreement;
:::

:::: Enacts
NOW, THEREFORE, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the
Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

::: HistoricalNote
- 1992, c. 53, Preamble; 2002, c. 7, s. 175(E).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247212 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Gwich'in Land Claim Settlement Act](/eng/acts/G-11.8).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247215 .Part}

Definition of [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Act,
[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Comprehensive Land Claim
Agreement between Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada and the
Gwich'in, as represented by the Gwich'in Tribal Council, signed on April
22, 1992, tabled in the House of Commons for the Minister of Indian
Affairs and Northern Development on November 16, 1992, and includes
amendments made from time to time pursuant to the Agreement.

## [Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247218 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Binding on Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Act is binding on Her
Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247221 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agreement is hereby approved, given effect and declared valid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, where the Agreement confers on
  any person or body a right, privilege, benefit or power, requires any
  person or body to perform a duty or subjects any person or body to a
  liability, that person or body may exercise the right, privilege,
  benefit or power, shall perform the duty or is subject to the
  liability to the extent provided for by the Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, title to lands vests in the
  Gwich'in Tribal Council as set out in the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Renewable Resources Board

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
carrying out its objectives, the Renewable Resources Board established
by the Agreement has the capacity, rights, powers and privileges of a
natural person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Orders and regulations

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Governor in Council may
make such orders and regulations as are necessary for the purpose of
carrying out the Agreement or for giving effect to any of the provisions
thereof.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of Agreement and amendments

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Indian
Affairs and Northern Development shall cause a certified copy of the
Agreement and any amendments thereto to be deposited in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the library of the Department of Indian Affairs and
  Northern Development that is situated in the National Capital Region;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the regional offices of the Department of Indian
  Affairs and Northern Development that are situated in Yukon and the
  Northwest Territories;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the legislative libraries of the Government of Yukon
  and the Government of the Northwest Territories; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} such other places as the Minister deems necessary.

## [Other Laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-247241 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency or conflict

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where there is any
inconsistency or conflict between this Act or the Agreement and the
provisions of any law, this Act or the Agreement, as the case may be,
prevails to the extent of the inconsistency or conflict.
