## National Wildlife Week Act {#national-wildlife-week-act .Title-of-Act}

[R.S.C.]{.abbr title="Revised Statutes of Canada"}, 1985, c. W-10
:::

An Act respecting a National Wildlife Week
::::

::::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [National Wildlife Week Act](/eng/acts/W-10).

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-18, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National Wildlife Week

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Throughout Canada in each
year, the week in which April 10 occurs, being the anniversary of the
birthday of the late Jack Miner, shall be known and observed as National
Wildlife Week, so that interested clubs, associations and organizations
may, on the day of that week most suitable to them, disseminate
information pertinent to wildlife conservation.

::: HistoricalNote
- R.S., c. N-18, s. 2
:::
