## Canada Small Business Financing Act {#canada-small-business-financing-act .Title-of-Act}

[S.C.]{.abbr title="Statutes of Canada"} 1998, c. 36
:::

Assented to 1998-12-10

An Act to increase the availability of financing for the establishment,
expansion, modernization and improvement of small businesses
::::

:::::::::::: section
::: Enacts
Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-53864 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short title

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Act may be cited as
the [Canada Small Business Financing Act](/eng/acts/C-10.2).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-53867 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Act.

[[lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Canadian Payments Association,
      established by subsection 3(1) of the [Canadian Payments
      Act](/eng/acts/C-21),

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} referred to in paragraph 4(1)(b) or (c) or any
        of paragraphs 4(2)(a) to (c) of that Act, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} referred to in any of paragraphs 4(2)(d)
        to (h) of that Act if the member meets the prescribed
        conditions;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a local cooperative credit society, within the
      meaning of subsection 2(1) of the [Canadian Payments
      Act](/eng/acts/C-21), that is a member of a central cooperative
      credit society, within the meaning of that subsection, if that
      central cooperative credit society is a member of the Canadian
      Payments Association; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other organization designated by the Minister
      as a lender for the purposes of this
      Act. ([prêteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a loan, including a line of
    credit, that meets the conditions set out in subsection 4(1) and is
    made to a borrower that meets the criteria set out in subsection
    4(2). ([prêt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the member of the Queen's
    Privy Council for Canada designated as the Minister for the purposes
    of this Act. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prescribed by the
    regulations. ([Version anglaise
    seulement]{.DefinitionOtherLangOnly})

[[small business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[small business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a business carried on
    or about to be carried on in Canada with an estimated gross annual
    revenue

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} not exceeding \$10 million or any prescribed
      lesser amount for the fiscal year of the business during which a
      loan is approved by a lender in respect of the business; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a business about to be carried on,
      not expected at the time a loan is approved by a lender in respect
      of the business to exceed \$10 million or any prescribed lesser
      amount for its first fiscal year that is of not less than 52 weeks
      duration.

    It does not include the business of farming. ([petite
    entreprise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-53885 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loans made after March 31, 1999

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This Act
  applies only in respect of loans made after March 31, 1999.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collection of annual administration fee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), section 12 applies,
  with the consent of the lender, in respect of any guaranteed business
  improvement loan that was made by that lender after March 31, 1995
  under the Small Business Loans Act and that is outstanding after March
  31, 1999.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claims for loss

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any provisions of the regulations with respect to
  interim claims apply in respect of claims submitted after March 31,
  1999, notwithstanding that the claim may be in respect of a guaranteed
  business improvement loan made under the Small Business Loans Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed eligibility

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), a loan is deemed to
  meet the conditions set out in subsection 4(1) and to be made to a
  borrower that meets the criteria set out in subsection 4(2) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the loan is approved before April 1, 1999 and is
    made on or after that date but before July 1, 1999; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} either the loan meets the conditions set out in
    subsection 3(2) of the Small Business Loans Act that would have
    applied to the loan if it had been made before April 1, 1999, or
    subsection 3(7) of that Act would have applied with respect to the
    application of those conditions to the loan.

## [Eligibility]{.HTitleText1} {#h-53897 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loan conditions

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following conditions must be satisfied in respect of a loan, in
  addition to any further conditions that may be prescribed:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the loan must be made by and with the consent of a
    lender to a borrower who meets the eligibility criteria set out in
    subsection (2);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the loan must be in respect of a small business;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the loan must be for an expenditure or commitment
    that falls within the scope of one or more prescribed classes of
    loans; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the portion of the cost of the expenditures and
    commitments to be financed by the loan must not exceed the
    prescribed limits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria for eligibility

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A borrower is eligible for a loan on application to a
  lender if, at the time the loan is made,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the borrower meets the prescribed eligibility
    criteria;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a loan made before April 1, 2009,
    the outstanding loan amount in relation to the borrower does not
    exceed \$250,000 or any prescribed lesser amount;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a loan made after March 31, 2009 but
    before the day on which paragraph (d) comes into force, the
    outstanding loan amount in relation to the borrower does not
    exceed \$500,000 or any prescribed lesser amount, of which a maximum
    of \$350,000 or any prescribed lesser amount is for a purpose other
    than the purchase or improvement of real property or immovables of
    which the borrower is or will become the owner;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a loan made after June 22, 2015 but
    before the day on which paragraph (e) comes into force, the
    outstanding loan amount in relation to the borrower does not
    exceed \$1,000,000 or any prescribed lesser amount, of which a
    maximum of \$350,000 or any prescribed lesser amount is for a
    purpose other than the purchase or improvement of real property or
    immovables of which the borrower is or will become the owner; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a loan made on or after the day on
    which this paragraph comes into force, the outstanding loan amount
    in relation to the borrower does not exceed \$1,150,000 or any
    prescribed lesser amount, of which maximum amounts may be prescribed
    for a loan other than a line of credit, a line of credit and
    prescribed classes of loans.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of outstanding loan amount

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The outstanding loan amount referred to in any of
  paragraphs (2)(b) to (e) is the aggregate of the amount of the
  proposed loan and the principal amount outstanding, in respect of the
  borrower and all borrowers that are related to that borrower within
  the meaning of the regulations, of all loans made under this Act and
  guaranteed business improvement loans made under the Small Business
  Loans Act.

## [Liability of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-53915 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability of Minister

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Minister is liable to pay a lender any eligible
  loss, calculated in accordance with the regulations, sustained by it
  as a result of a loan in respect of which the requirements set out in
  this Act and the regulations have been satisfied.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of liability

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, with the approval of the Governor
  in Council, give notice to a lender of the termination of the
  Minister's liability in respect of loans of a prescribed class or of
  any prescribed classes made by the lender on or after the date set out
  in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When notice to be given

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice under subsection (2) is not effective unless
  the date set out in the notice is at least twenty-four hours after
  receipt of the notice at the head office of the lender.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Program liability ceiling

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister is not liable to make any payment to a lender in respect of a
  loss sustained by it as a result of a loan made and registered after
  the Minister's aggregate contingent liability in respect of the
  aggregate amount of the loans made by all lenders and registered by
  the Minister during each consecutive five-year period, starting with
  the period beginning on April 1, 1999, has exceeded \$1.5 billion or
  any other amount that is provided by an appropriation Act or another
  Act of Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation of liability in respect of
  each lender --- loans other than lines of credit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The liability of the Minister to make any payment to
  a lender in respect of losses sustained by it as a result of loans,
  other than lines of credit, made by it and registered by the Minister
  during each consecutive five-year period, starting with the period
  beginning on April 1, 1999, is limited to the total of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 90%, or any prescribed lesser percentage, of that
    part of the aggregate principal amount of the loans --- other than
    lines of credit --- made by it during that period that does not
    exceed \$250,000,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 50%, or any prescribed lesser percentage, of that
    part of the aggregate principal amount of the loans --- other than
    lines of credit --- made by it during that period that exceeds
    \$250,000 but does not exceed \$500,000,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 10%, or any prescribed lesser percentage, of that
    part of the aggregate principal amount of the loans --- other than
    lines of credit --- made by it before April 1, 2009 that exceeds
    \$500,000, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} 12%, or any prescribed lesser percentage, of that
    part of the aggregate principal amount of the loans --- other than
    lines of credit --- made by it after March 31, 2009 that exceeds
    \$500,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation of liability in respect of
  each lender --- lines of credit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The liability of the Minister to make any payment to
  a lender in respect of losses sustained by it as a result of lines of
  credit authorized by it and registered by the Minister during each
  consecutive five-year period, starting with the period beginning on
  April 1, 2019, is limited to the total of 15%, or any prescribed
  lesser percentage, of the aggregate amount of the lines of credit
  authorized by it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loss-sharing ratio

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The liability of the
Minister in respect of losses sustained by a lender as a result of a
loan made by it is limited to the lesser of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 85%, or any prescribed lesser percentage, of its
  eligible loss, calculated in accordance with the regulations, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a prescribed maximum amount.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other limitations --- registration fee,
etc.

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to the regulations, the Minister is not liable to make any payment to
  a lender in respect of any loss sustained by it as a result of a loan
  made by it unless the lender has, in respect of that loan,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} paid to the Minister the registration fee in
    accordance with section 11; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} complied with the other requirements of this Act
    and the regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other limitations --- annual
  administration fee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is not liable to make any payment to a
  lender in respect of any loss sustained by it as a result of a loan
  made by it unless the lender has, in respect of all loans made by it,
  paid to the Minister the annual administration fee in accordance with
  section 12.

## [Fees and Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-53955 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition re: fees and charges

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every loan is subject to
the condition that no fee or charge is payable by a borrower in respect
of the loan other than

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} interest;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the registration fee; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other prescribed fee or charge that is specified
  in the regulations as being payable by the borrower.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration fee

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every lender shall pay to
the Minister, at the time a loan made by that lender is submitted for
registration, a registration fee of an amount calculated in accordance
with the regulations in respect of the loan. The registration fee may be
charged by the lender to the borrower.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual administration fee

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Every lender shall, in
accordance with the terms and conditions set out in the regulations, pay
to the Minister, in respect of a loan, an annual administration fee of
an amount calculated in accordance with the regulations. The lender may
not charge the borrower for that fee otherwise than through the
imposition of interest in respect of the loan.

## [Pilot Projects]{.HTitleText1} {#h-53965 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding anything in this
  Act, the Governor in Council may, on the recommendation of the
  Minister, make any regulations that the Governor in Council considers
  necessary respecting the establishment and operation of one or more
  pilot projects for testing whether to guarantee loans made to
  borrowers in the voluntary sector or capital leases and to determine
  the legislative and regulatory measures required to do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Program liability ceiling

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister's maximum aggregate contingent liability
  in respect of a pilot project may be established by an appropriation
  Act or another Act of Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum duration of pilot projects

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), regulations made under
  subsection (1) have a maximum duration of five years from the time
  they come into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of regulations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, as a result of a pilot project referred to in
  subsection (1), the Minister decides to guarantee loans made to
  borrowers in the voluntary sector or capital leases, the Minister
  shall cause to be published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) a notice of that decision and the
  regulations made under that subsection in relation to that pilot
  project will continue to have effect until the earlier of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the coming into force of the legislative and
    regulatory measures referred to in that subsection, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one year after the day on which they would
    otherwise cease to have effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tabling of proposed regulations

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause a copy of each regulation
  proposed to be made under subsection (1) to be laid before each House
  of Parliament before it is made and that House shall refer the
  proposed regulations to the appropriate Committee of each House.

## [Audit or Examination]{.HTitleText1} {#h-54010 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's powers

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, after giving at least twenty-one days notice in writing
  to a lender, conduct an audit or examination of the lender's
  documents, records and books of account to verify that this Act and
  the regulations are being complied with in respect of a loan,
  including that the lender has exercised due diligence, as provided in
  the regulations, in the approval and administration of the loan and
  that the documentation submitted to the Minister in respect of the
  borrower is accurate and complete.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records to be made available

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The documents, records and books of account shall,
  for the purposes of the audit or examination, be made available at all
  reasonable times to any person authorized by the Minister for the
  purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assisting authorized persons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every lender shall, for the purpose of an audit or
  examination under this Act, give all reasonable assistance to any
  person authorized by the Minister to carry out the audit or
  examination, provide access to all relevant sites, answer, orally or
  in writing, as required, all questions relating to the subject-matter
  of the audit or examination and provide all information and
  documentation in their possession and all copies required for the
  purpose of the audit or examination. Without the permission of the
  lender, the authorized person shall not remove the documentation or
  copies from the relevant sites.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report of audit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall provide the lender with a copy of
  the report of the audit or examination within twenty-one days after
  the report is completed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-compliance

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a lender refuses or intentionally fails to comply
  with any requirement of this section, the Minister may notify the
  lender in writing that the Minister is not liable under this Act to
  make any payment to the lender in respect of any loss sustained by it
  as a result of any loan made by it.

## [Offences and Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-54022 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offence

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person commits an offence who, in respect of a loan,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} knowingly makes any false statement or
    misrepresentation in an application, report or other document or
    wilfully furnishes any false or misleading information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} being a borrower, without the consent of the lender
    and with fraudulent intent, disposes of any assets taken as security
    for the loan where the disposition is not permitted by the
    regulations; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} being a borrower, uses the proceeds of the loan,
    with fraudulent intent, for a purpose that does not fall within the
    scope of any prescribed class of loans.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Punishment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who commits an offence under subsection
  (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an indictable offence and liable to a
    fine not exceeding \$500,000 or to imprisonment for a term not
    exceeding five years, or to both; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is guilty of an offence punishable on summary
    conviction and liable to a fine not exceeding \$50,000 or to
    imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or to both.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any proceedings by way of summary conviction in
  respect of an offence under subsection (1) may be instituted at any
  time within but not later than three years after the time when the
  subject-matter of the proceedings arose.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-54035 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment out of C.R.F.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Any amount payable by the
Minister to a lender under this Act is to be paid out of the
Consolidated Revenue Fund.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall,
within twelve months after the end of each fiscal year, cause a report
on the administration of this Act during the preceding fiscal year to be
made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Five-year review

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall,
within twelve months after March 31, 2004 and every five years after
that, cause a comprehensive review and report of the provisions and
operation of this Act during the preceding five years to be made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laying of reports before Parliament

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall cause
a copy of each report referred to in sections 18 and 19 to be laid
before each House of Parliament on any of the first fifteen days on
which that House is sitting after the day on which the report is made.
