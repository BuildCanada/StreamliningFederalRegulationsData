### [DIVISION 3]{.HLabel2}[International Development Financing Agreements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-39304 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Agreements]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[177]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-177 .sectionLabel}** In this Division,
[[Agreements]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the following agreements, as
amended from time to time:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Administration Agreement for the Financial
  Support of the Private Sector Window of the Global Agriculture and
  Food Security Program (TFC-3), entered into between Her Majesty the
  Queen in right of Canada, as represented by the Minister of Finance,
  and the International Finance Corporation on March 14, 2011;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Administration Agreement for the Financial
  Support of the Financial Mechanisms for Climate Change (FMCC) Facility
  --- Concessional Finance (TFC-4), entered into between Her Majesty the
  Queen in right of Canada, as represented by the Minister of Finance,
  and the International Finance Corporation on March 8, 2011; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Administration Agreement for the Financial
  Support of the Financial Mechanisms for Climate (FMCC) Change Facility
  --- Technical Assistance (TF 071589) entered into between Her Majesty
  the Queen in right of Canada, as represented by the Minister of
  Finance, and the International Finance Corporation on March 8, 2011.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers, duties and functions

- **[[178]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-178 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Foreign Affairs is responsible for the Agreements.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Share --- acquisition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 90 of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), shares of a corporation that, on
  acquisition, would be held by, on behalf of or in trust for the Crown
  may be acquired in the course of the administration of the Agreements.
